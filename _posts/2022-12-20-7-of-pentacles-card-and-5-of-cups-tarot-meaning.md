## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Cups mean together?

When the 7 Of Pentacles tarot card and the 5 Of Cups tarot card appear together in a reading, they suggest that while life may bring loss and grief, there is light at the end of the tunnel. Hard work and patience are required to make success happen, and although the process may be slow, there is a reward at the end. It is important to remain focused and patient, and not give up when faced with challenges. Even when you feel that all is lost and that no one loves or cares about you, it is important to trust that the Universe will take care of you in time.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health

The Five of Cups tarot card is symbolic of loss and regret and is connected to health in two primary ways. The first is in terms of physical health. This card suggests that you have had a physical loss that has caused you regret and grief. This may be a disruption to your health, a chronic illness, or even a physical ailment that has resulted in a loss of a part of your body. It can suggest that the road to recovery will be long and hard, and that you are mourning the loss of what used to be.

The second way the Five of Cups card relates to health is in terms of emotional and mental health. This card suggests that you have been through a difficult time and have suffered a great loss. You may be feeling isolated, and have a deep sense of regret and sadness. These feelings may lead to depression, anxiety, and other mental health issues if left unresolved. In order to turn around the effects of the Five of Cups card on your health, it is essential that you take time to understand what has happened and take active steps to process and heal your grief.

Finances

The Five of Cups suggests that you have suffered a financial loss or setback, likely through no fault of your own. You may have been taken advantage of, or have made a poor decision in the past that has had a long-term negative impact. Regardless of the cause, this card suggests that you will be feeling regret and sadness as a result of this loss.

The impact of this regret and sadness may be felt in a variety of ways in your finances. This card could be indicative of a struggle to stay afloat financially, or a fear to make investments or take risks that could benefit you in the long run. It can also hint at difficulties in managing your resources and budgeting, as it may feel like a constant reminder of the mistake you made that has had such a deep impact.

Relationships

The Five of Cups suggests a loss in your relationships. This could be a direct loss, such as the end of a relationship or the death of a loved one. Alternatively, it could be a more subtle loss, such as a feeling of being abandoned or unloved. You may feel like you have been wronged or taken advantage of, and this has left you feeling rejected and alone.

The impact of this loss may be manifested in your relationships in the form of trust issues, feelings of insecurity, difficulty in forming new connections, or a belief that you are undeserving of love. You may be struggling to open up to others, or you may find yourself pushing away those who care about you. In order to transform the impact of the Five of Cups card on your relationships, it is essential that you take the time to understand what has happened and learn to forgive yourself and others.
