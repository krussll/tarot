A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card when paired with the Page Of Swords tarot card can be interpreted as a need to step back from a situation to gain a more objective view. The Hanged Man’s wisdom and self sacrifice may be required in order to get to the truth that the Page of Swords reveals. The need for cautiousness and a critical eye are indicated, as well as the ability to remain calmer and focused in the face of difficulty. By combining the wisdom and insight of the Hanged Man with the truth-seeking of the Page of Swords you can gain a new and more complete perspective.

When these two Tarot cards come together, it is a reminder that we need to be less impulsive and more thoughtful when making decisions about our love lives. We should strive for wisdom and truth, and take our time to get a new perspective. With this combination, our relationships can remain strong, healthy, and filled with unconditional love.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card can be interpreted differently by different people, but generally speaking, when it comes to health, finances and relationships, it speaks of the need to make sacrifices in order to gain a better understanding of the underlying causes and effects of these matters.

When it comes to health, the Hanged Man Tarot card can signify a willingness to turn away from less beneficial practices in order to make a healthier and more balanced lifestyle. It might refer to the need to give up a certain habit in order to become more in tune with one’s body and health needs. It might also be a call to take time to meditate and gain clarity about how to approach health matters.

When it comes to finances, the Hanged Man Tarot card can tell you that, in order to better manage your money, you should be willing to accept a certain amount of financial sacrifice in order to gain a better understanding of what you’re doing wrong. This can be achieved through making the right decisions when it comes to investments, budgeting or even cutting back on what you want in order to make sound financial decisions.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Hanged Man Tarot card can be interpreted as a reminder to take the time to self reflect and evaluate if you are indeed sacrificing enough to foster and maintain meaningful relationships. This can be done through making concessions, figuring out what your partner needs and wants and having an honest conversation with them. It could also refer to a need to set and maintain healthy boundaries in relationships.

Overall, The Hanged Man Tarot card can be seen as a call to step back and look at the “bigger picture” in order to gain a better understanding of the cause and effect of personal matters. It can be an indicator of the need to make certain sacrifices in order to gain a holistic understanding of one’s life.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health:

The Page of Swords Tarot card is associated with health in a few different ways. First, the Page of Swords calls for us to be vigilant about our health and to pay attention to potential signs that something might be wrong. The card speaks of being critical and analytical about our health, and to not ignore any potential signs that something might be wrong. We are also encouraged to take a proactive approach to health, which means being proactive about our health and wellbeing instead of reactive. It also calls for us to be honest with ourselves about our health and to not ignore any symptoms or signs that something might be wrong. Furthermore, this card reminds us to not ignore any advice from our doctor and to really listen to their advice.

Finances:

The Page of Swords Tarot card is associated with finances in a few different ways. Firstly, the card suggests that we should be incredibly critical and analytical when it comes to our finances. This means taking stock of our financial situation and really assessing our financial goals and objectives. It also speaks to being smart and savvy when it comes to handling our money, as well as being open to learning and understanding more about finance. Furthermore, the card suggests that we should be honest and upfront about our financial situation and not hide or lie about it. And finally, the Page of Swords encourages us to take the initiative to talk to professionals about our finances, such as financial advisors or bankers, in order to best prepare ourselves for any potential financial changes or shifts.

Relationships:

The Page of Swords Tarot card is associated with relationships in a few different ways. Firstly, the card speaks to being open, honest and truthful in all of our relationships and not to keep secrets or lie about anything. The card also suggests that we should be cautious and analytical in our relationships, to make sure that we are not being taken advantage of or manipulated. Furthermore, this card encourages us to be curious and to ask questions in order to gain a better understanding of the people we are involved with and of the dynamics of our relationships. And finally, the Page of Swords reminds us to be vigilant and to pay attention to any potential red flags that could indicate that a relationship may not be healthy or beneficial to us.
