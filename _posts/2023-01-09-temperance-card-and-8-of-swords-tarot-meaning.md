A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Temperance card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card together signify a need for balance and harmony, as well as a need for patience and self-control, especially in the face of self-imposed restrictions and isolation. While the 8 of Swords might feel like a prison, Temperance encourages us to use self-discipline to stay in control and work our way out of our current situation. Embrace the balance between taking responsibility for our choices and reflecting on the power of self-restraint to find our freedom and succeed.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Temperance and 8 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Temperance tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card can give you insight into your love life. The Temperance tarot card means balance, harmony, patience, and self-control. It suggests that if you have patience, you are more likely to have a harmonious relationship. This card encourages you to take a step back and assess the situation before rushing into a decision. This can help you make decisions from a calm and rational place.

At the same time, The 8 Of Swords tarot card is a warning. This card means self-imposed restrictions, isolation, and imprisonment. It can signify that you are placing unnecessary restrictions upon yourself and isolating yourself from your partner. This can lead to feelings of imprisonment, which can be damaging to a relationship. It can also be a sign that you are not allowing yourself to be truly open and vulnerable with your partner.

The message from both of these cards is to take some time and focus on yourself. It suggests that you need to be honest with yourself and to take true responsibility for your emotions and your relationship. Take this time to connect to yourself and cultivate a sense of balance and harmony within. This can help you create healthier boundaries and learn to express your needs and desires to your partner. By taking the time to practice self-care, you can create a beautiful sense of balance and harmony in your relationship.

With The 8 Of Swords, however, the message is to be wary of self-imposed restrictions and avoid feeling isolated or imprisoned by your finances.

The Temperance tarot card encourages you to seek a balance between saving and spending and to be patient, as sudden changes aren’t always the best solutions. You should take the time to research and weigh your options, and then make decisions that benefit both your short-term and long-term financial security. It also means that you should have the discipline and willpower to delay gratification and save for a larger goal.

The 8 Of Swords encourages you to be aware of the risks of self-imposed restrictions. Instead of focusing solely on cutting costs and forgoing luxuries, think about how to generate additional income and focus on creating more opportunities for yourself.

Be mindful of your budget, while also having the patience to wait and make wise decisions, and remain open to new opportunities. With the right balance of Temperance and The 8 Of Swords, you’ll be better positioned to thrive with your finances and career prospects.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Temperance and 8 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health: The Temperance Tarot card is an important reminder to strive for balance when it comes to health. The card has an underlying message to be mindful of how we choose to nourish our bodies, both physically and mentally. It also encourages finding a means of moderation, such as engaging in physical activity, avoiding overindulgence of unhealthy foods, and getting adequate sleep. Taking time to practice mindfulness and self-care should also be included as a way to stay in tune with our true selves. The Temperance card emphasizes that when we strive for balance we can be rewarded with optimum energy and wellbeing.

Finances: The Temperance Tarot card emphasizes the importance of making sensible decisions with money. Prudence and temperance are key when it comes to handling finances. This card encourages careful planning and budgeting of resources so that there is an equal balance between expending and accumulating. It’s important to practice self-control and not succumb to the lure of impulse purchases. It’s also wise to save for unexpected costs and emergencies, as well as setting aside money for fun and leisure.

Relationships: The Temperance Tarot card reminds us to seek harmony and restraint in our relationships. It urges us to practice tolerance, patience, and diplomacy when interacting with others. We should take time to express our feelings in a considerate manner and be mindful of the needs and wants of those around us. It is important to be open to compromise and establish a level of mutual understanding. The Temperance Tarot card also implies that we should practice forgiveness and be willing to accept that sometimes things won’t always go our way. When we strive to remain balanced, we can learn to form strong connections with those we love.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 8 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that we should be extra mindful of our self-care, and the choices we make in regards to our bodies. When this card appears, it can indicate that self-imposed restrictions, neglect and isolation have been allowed to take their toll on our physical and mental health. This card is symbolic of our own refusal to assess our own physical, mental, and spiritual needs, in favor of those who, for one reason or another, have taken control of our well-being. We may need to look back and evaluate how our own decisions and actions may have had an impact on our health, and how we can better take care of ourselves in the future.

Finances

The 8 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning that unforeseen difficulties or losses in our finances are imminent. This card can indicate that any efforts to free ourselves from our current financial situation may feel impossible. We may be stuck in a cycle of self-imposed restrictions, where we are unable to move past our current financial problems. The 8 of Swords serves as an encouragement to assess our options and to work towards freeing ourselves from the burdens we have created for ourselves.

Relationships

The 8 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning for relationships. This card can indicate that we have created a prison for ourselves by withholding our affections, isolating ourselves from loved ones, and generally avoiding any meaningful relationships. We may be fearful of letting others in and vulnerable to the possibility of being hurt. This card serves as a reminder to assess our relationships, and to begin to open up and embrace the connections we have with others.
