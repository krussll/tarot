A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 7 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 7 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Queen Of Wands tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card combined suggest that success may not come easy or quickly, but with patience, hard work, and confidence, it can be achieved. It also encourages you to trust your own instincts and to be brave and assertive, showing that your own strength and willpower can help you get through anything. The combination of these two cards is a reminder to stay optimistic and to remain focused on your goal, assuring that your efforts will eventually pay off.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

For Health:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card signifies that your health is in good hands and that you’re taking good care of yourself. You are able to make home-cooked meals, try different types of exercise and find the perfect balance between rest and activity. This card also indicates health in the longterm, meaning that your health should stay strong in later years if you continue to practice maintaining a healthy lifestyle. You are likely to feel strong and energized with the Queen of Wands in your life.

For Finances:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card implies that you have control over your finances. You are confident and organized when it comes to money, and are often able to make wise investments. You think about the future, and you have a handle on your money and financial investments. You’re likely to be comfortable in financial discussions and to make lucrative investments.

For Relationships:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card suggests that you are confident in yourself, which draws people to you. Your relationships are likely to be strong and meaningful because you are able to communicate honestly and effectively with your loved ones. In addition, your strong self-assurance and inner strength give your relationships balance and stability. You are able to focus on the positive aspects of your relationship, and to take action when needed.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks to the value of having patience in all aspects of life. This card symbolizes hard work, perseverance, delayed success, and waiting for results. When it comes to health, this card suggests that while it is important to focus on self-care and nourishment of the body and mind, one should also be patient when it comes to health goals. Working at a steady pace and taking breaks when necessary will build both physical and mental strength. This card advises that success in the health domain will take dedication and hard work, but that results will be worth the wait.

In regards to finances, the 7 of Pentacles encourages financial accountability and taking steps to build financial stability. Hard work, delayed gratification and investing your money wisely will bring long-term success. This card advises that short term gains or quick fixes will not bring lasting financial success and that one should create a plan and work steadily towards the end goal.

When it comes to relationships, the 7 of Pentacles speaks to the importance of having patience and understanding in all relationships. The card suggests that trust, communication, and mutual effort will be necessary for any relationship to be successful. Results will take time, but with patience and openness, success can be achieved. This card also acknowledges that all relationships will require hard work and dedication, but that the rewards will be worth it in the end.
