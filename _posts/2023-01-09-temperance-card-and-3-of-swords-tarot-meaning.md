## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Temperance and 3 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is a powerful symbol of balance. It encourages us to be mindful in our lives to create stability and harmony. This card reminds us that patience and self-control are paramount to a successful life.

For health, the Temperance Tarot card is a sign that we need to carefully consider our decisions and take a balanced approach to life. We should ensure that exercise, nutrition, sleep, and relaxation are all in balance. The card reminds us to take care of ourselves and to be mindful of our choices with regard to our health.

For finances, the Temperance card encourages us to be prudent with our money. It reminds us to focus on our long-term goals and to look for ways to save money, invest, and grow our wealth. The card is a reminder to be patient and to not give in to short-term impulses, as this could lead to money mistakes.

For relationships, the Temperance card indicates that we should take a balanced approach. We need to be mindful of our communication and actions, and we should strive to make sure our relationships are harmonious. The card also reminds us to be patient and understanding of our partners, and to always ensure that love and respect are part of our relationships.

In conclusion, The Temperance Tarot card is a powerful reminder for us to focus on balance and harmony in our lives. We should pay attention to our health, finances and relationships, and take a proactive approach to creating a stable and successful life.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords tarot card is a card that often times represents a difficult period of transition. This transition is often associated with heartbreak, loss, and a feeling of depression. On the physical level, this card can represent a difficult medical operation or procedure, a battle with an illness, or a significant shift in health. Financially, the card can signal a period of struggle caused by a significant loss or change in income. Relationships are also often affected by this card, with the potential for friendships and romantic partnerships to be tested, broken, or even permanently damaged.

When it comes to health, the 3 of Swords tarot card provides a warning that whatever is being faced may be difficult, lengthy, and costly. However, the card also speaks of hope, as it serves as a reminder to focus on what can still be controlled and to work through the hard times. Financially, the card could be an indication of an immediate financial crisis, or a warning of tough times ahead and a need to adjust spending habits. It can also be a signal to prepare for unexpected expenses, or to be careful with how and where money is being spent.

In terms of relationships, the 3 of Swords tarot card can signify a painful and difficult time. Disagreements, arguments, and a feeling of disconnect can be present. It can also symbolize a parting of ways, or a need to let go of something or someone that is no longer serving a purpose. In any case, it is important to focus on the larger picture and understand that although some relationships may not survive, it is possible to find new and meaningful connections that better serve our needs.

The 3 of Swords tarot card is a reminder to focus on the bigger picture amidst adversity and to stay strong while facing difficult times. It also serves as a warning to be aware and prepare for changes, whether in health, finances or relationships. In dealing with these changes, the card encourages us to strive for balance and to rely on our inner strength to get us through.
