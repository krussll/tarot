A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The The World card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and King Of Swords mean together?

The World tarot card and the King of Swords tarot card represent the culmination of a purposeful journey. In this combination, the King of Swords suggests the intentional leadership and strategic thinking needed to successfully complete a long-term goal or lesson. The World card highlights the importance of setting and working towards a goal, and when these two cards appear together, it can mean that you have achieved success through diligent effort. This combination can also signify the end of a difficult journey, and a celebration of the rewards that come with it. Whether it’s travel, emigration, or the successful completion of a project or task, the combination of these two cards suggests that you have completed a significant achievement.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The World and King Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The World mean?

The World Tarot card is a symbol of achievement and success, representing the completion or end of a cycle, idea, or journey. This symbol can represent success and completion in all aspects of life, including health, finances, and relationships.

In matters of health, The World Tarot card can represent making a full recovery from an illness or injury. It could also signify the end of a period of ill health and the beginning of a period of now health. The card represents a victory after months of hardship, and it is a sign that one’s body is finally in a good state of health.

In terms of finances, the card could symbolize the completion of a goal such as saving for a big purchase or reaching a certain amount of money in one’s financial accounts. It could also represent the end of an era of financial hardship and the beginning of a period of financial success. The World Tarot card symbolizes reward after hard work and dedication to financial goals.

Finally, The World Tarot card can signify the culmination of a relationship or the final ending of a relationship. It could represent all the work put into a relationship and the success of a relationship or the realization that a relationship has run its course. The card symbolizes the end of one chapter and the beginning of a new one. It is a sign of the journey taken and the lessons learned.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health: The King of Swords symbolizes an individual who has mastered the power of thought to control his own health and wellbeing. He stands firm in the face of adversity and actively works to ensure his body is nourished and well cared for. As a leader and strategist, he knows how to maintain balance in his life and how to prioritize his health needs. He may be one to embrace and understand the power of holistic healing, relying on herbs, exercise, diet, and adequate rest to keep his body in peak condition. He understands the importance of self-care and listens to his body, making sure that he isn’t overworked and depleted.

Finance: The King of Sword is a shrewd planner when it comes to his finances. He is the one to research and understand the ins and outs of money management. Not one to be driven by emotion, he looks to the facts, assesses risk, and puts his money into investments that he believes are secure and have a good return. He is unemotional about his money and takes responsibility for his financial future. He understands the importance of saving for retirement, as well as for shorter-term goals. He plans for the future and takes calculated risks.

Relationships: The King of Sword approaches relationships with the same strategy he applies to health and finances. He values honesty and integrity and is not afraid to speak his mind. He will step in when he sees injustice or unfairness and is willing to put in the work to ensure the relationships in his life remain healthy. At the same time, he understands that he cannot control the people in his life and that everyone has their own goals and motivations. He is patient and open-minded and knows how to be both supportive and firm in his relationships.
