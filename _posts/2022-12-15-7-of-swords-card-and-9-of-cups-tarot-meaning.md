A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together?

When The 7 Of Swords tarot card and The 9 Of Cups tarot card are drawn together, the message is that it is time to take action and make your wishes come true. Despite the betrayal and deceit implied by the 7 Of Swords, the 9 Of Cups can represent the promise of optimistically receiving material abundance and good health. This is an invitation to set boundaries and address past betrayals, and be open to new experiences and opportunities available to you. It is time to find new ways to manifest your wishes and break through the challenges that have been preventing your true desires from manifesting.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The combination of The 7 Of Swords and The 9 Of Cups tarot cards in relation to your love life speaks of a tricky situation. The 7 Of Swords can suggest that there is a potential for betrayal and dishonesty in your relationship. You may feel as though someone you love is hiding something important from you, or that they may be giving false information or deliberately deceiving you in some way.

This could be a warning to take extra caution and to pay close attention to those in your life. At the same time, it could also point to a need for clarity and communication. A key to unlocking the issues may lie in honest conversations.

The 9 Of Cups tarot card speaks, however, of a hopeful sign. It suggests that all of your wishes may come true in your love life. If you have been working hard on having a fulfilling and happy relationship, then this combination of cards could be an indication that all of your efforts may pay off. There is potential for good health and abundance in your life, and for your relationship to be a source of strength and joy.

Ultimately, this combination of tarot cards points to an underlying need for trust. It is important that you learn to trust yourself and to trust your intuition. Open yourself up to clarity and communication and allow the good to come into your life.

### What does 7 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

Reading the 7 of Swords and 9 of Cups cards together in regards to your finances and job can be a bit tricky as they have very different connotations. The 7 of Swords card is all about theft, betrayal, and dishonesty, so this could indicate that someone is trying to take advantage of you financially. It could also be a warning to be cautious with your money and to watch out for any suspicious activity or people.

The 9 of Cups card, on the other hand, is about wishes coming true, material abundance, and good health. This could be a sign that you have the potential to achieve financial abundance if you put in the effort and stay vigilant. However, the 7 of Swords card right next to it is a reminder to be mindful of how you’re spending your money and to be wary of any scams or dishonest people.

Overall, the combination of these two cards suggests that you may be able to achieve some financial success in the future, but you’ll need to be aware of any potential theft, betrayal, and dishonesty that could stand in your way. Be sure to watch your spending, be cautious of anyone trying to take advantage of you, and stay focused on your goals. With hard work and determination, it is possible to reach the financial heights represented by the 9 of Cups card.

### What does 7 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together for your health?

The 7 of Swords and the 9 of Cups tarot cards can be interpreted to mean that you should be careful with your health, as there may be some potential for theft, betrayal, and/or dishonesty surrounding it. Those that are in positions of power or influence that may be taking advantage of you may be at work, and it would be wise to be vigilant about your health and wellbeing.

At the same time, the 9 of Cups is a reminder that with diligence and caution, your wishes and hopes for good health may be fulfilled. This is a time to focus on taking care of yourself and doing the things that nurture and replenish your mind, body and spirit. Consider taking up a new exercise routine, eating healthier, and rejecting anything that doesn’t line up with what you know is best for your health.

Whatever decisions you make now will have a direct impact on your physical and emotional wellbeing. Don’t give in to the temptation of shortcuts that may seem tempting in the short term, but may have disastrous long-term consequences. Rely on your own judgement and strength to steer you away from situations that could negatively affect your health, and use the 9 of Cups to keep your positive mindset and outlook alive.

The 7 of Swords and the 9 of Cups tarot cards together remind you to take care of your health on all levels. Being wary of those that may have malicious intent, trusting your own judgement, making healthy decisions and having faith that your wishes will come true- these are the steps you should take to ensure your physical and emotional health is taken care of.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 7 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot Card is a card associated with theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and running away. It suggests a feeling of being victimized, manipulated or taken advantage of in some way. This can lead to feelings of insecurity and vulnerability, as well as a sense of being weary of trusting a person or situation.

When it comes to health, the 7 of Swords Tarot Card can signify a lack of awareness of one’s own physical and mental well-being. It can indicate that an individual is preoccupied with their own personal gain and safety over the needs of their body and mind. This can lead to neglecting their own health and well-being, either through effort or attitude. When this card appears health-wise, it can be a reminder to take time for self-care and pay attention to even the smallest of body aches or mental unease.

In terms of finance, the 7 of Swords Tarot Card can imply dishonesty, or a feeling of being taken advantage of or manipulated financially. It can be associated with running away from responsibility and hefty debts, or it can suggest a lack of trust in financial situations. When this card appears in relation to finances, it is paramount to be honest, transparent and responsible with your finances, and to make sure to check the details of any important financial documents.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 7 of Swords Tarot Card can signify a lack of trust and a feeling of betrayal. It can be related to running away from issues in a relationship, and feeling like one is being taken advantage of in some way. When this card appears in relation to relationships, it requires a focus in communication and making sure to create a secure, trusting bond with another person. It also can serve as a reminder to be mindful of one’s own feelings and needs, as well as those of the other person in the relationship.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 Of Cups Tarot card is associated with material abundance, as well as good health and wishes come true. In terms of health, the energy of this card encourages us to value our physical, mental and emotional wellbeing. It reminds us to take active steps to protect and nurture our health, such as getting enough sleep, exercise, rest and eating nutritious foods. Doing this will ensure we have the energy and vitality necessary to confront the challenges life can bring.

When it comes to finances, the 9 Of Cups Tarot card suggests that you are able to manifest the prosperity you seek. This can come from working hard and investing wisely. You may be rewarded with increased resources, money and investments, especially if you are humble and recognize that you are always in a state of abundance.

In relationships, the 9 Of Cups Tarot card encourages us to foster meaningful connections with others that offer mutual support, understanding and compassion. We may find ourselves drawn to people that reflect the highest parts of ourselves, so we can achieve a deeper level of connection and trust. This card helps us recognize the importance of nurturing our relationships and actively participating in their growth. It also encourages us to be mindful of our words and actions, as they will have an impact on our relationships.

Overall, The 9 Of Cups Tarot card encourages us to manifest our wants and needs in any area of life, whether it be health, finances or relationships. As we focus on manifesting our desires, we can enjoy a sense of material abundance and a deeper connection with ourselves and those around us.
