Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Hanged Man card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Hanged Man, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Hanged Man.

## The Hanged Man And Health – An Overview

The Hanged Man tarot card is a reminder that we must take a step back in order to look at our health from a different perspective.

It encourages us to be willing to sacrifice our current approaches and to gain wisdom from the experience.

This could mean anything from giving up a certain food or drink, to trying a healthier lifestyle, to seeking the guidance of professionals.

When it comes to health, The Hanged Man tells us to be willing to give up something in order to gain a greater understanding of our health and to make beneficial changes in the long run.

## Upright Meanings For The Hanged Man And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Hanged Man tarot card is a symbol of wisdom and self-sacrifice when it comes to health.

It suggests that when it comes to maintaining your health, taking a step back and viewing things from a different perspective can be invaluable.

This can help you to make wise decisions about how you choose to care for yourself and your wellbeing.

It may also be a reminder to put yourself first, and to be mindful of the balance between giving to others and taking care of yourself.

By taking the time to pause and reflect on how you can make healthier choices, this card can be a positive reminder to prioritize your health and wellbeing.

### What It Means For The Future

The Hanged Man tarot card suggests that in order to be healthy for the future, I must take a step back, gain a different perspective, and make some self-sacrifices.

It encourages me to look at my health from a different angle and to consider different approaches to achieving my health goals.

It suggests that I can gain wisdom by changing my perspective and seeing the bigger picture.

It also suggests that I should not be afraid to make some sacrifices in order to improve my health now and in the future.

I can be sure that the effort and dedication I put into my health today will have a positive impact in the future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Hanged Man tarot card is a reminder to look at the bigger picture when it comes to the health of those close to me.

It is a sign that I should be willing to make sacrifices and put the health of the people I love before my own.

It also encourages me to take the time to gain perspective and a greater understanding of the situation.

With the wisdom I can gain from this tarot card, I can make the best decisions for my loved ones to ensure their wellbeing and health.

## Reversed Meanings For The Hanged Man And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed Hanged Man tarot card is a sign that one’s health is out of balance and needs to be re-evaluated.

It could be an indication that one is too focused on the physical aspects of health, and not enough on the mental and spiritual aspects.

It can also mean that one is feeling stuck in an unhealthy lifestyle and needs to make changes in order to move forward.

The reversed Hanged Man can also indicate that one is being overly self-critical and should take a step back and look at their health from a different perspective.

The reversed Hanged Man could be a reminder that health is not only physical, but emotional and spiritual as well.

It can be a sign to take a break and look inward to discover what is really important to one’s health.

This could be a time to practice self-care, mindfulness, and to explore alternative forms of healing.

The reversed Hanged Man can be a call to explore different ways of nourishing one’s body, mind, and soul to create a balanced and holistic approach to health.

### What It Means For The Future

When The Hanged Man tarot card appears in the reversed position, it can indicate a lack of balance and focus in regards to health and wellness.

This card suggests that you may be neglecting important aspects of your health due to a lack of discipline and self-care.

For example, you may be focusing too heavily on exercising but forgetting to eat well and get enough rest.

Alternatively, you may be neglecting your physical health in favor of mental health, such as meditation and mindfulness.

The reversed Hanged Man can also indicate that you may be stuck in unhealthy patterns and routines.

You may be in denial about the way that your health is affecting your life, or you may be ignoring warning signs from your body in order to continue a certain lifestyle.

It may be time to take a step back from your habits and take a hard look at what is and isn’t working for you.

This card can also signify that you are feeling overwhelmed and out of control, so take a deep breath and focus on what you can control.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed The Hanged Man tarot card appears in a health reading, it can be an indication that an individual is sacrificing their health and wellbeing for a greater cause.

This could be a sign that they are putting too much pressure on themselves to take care of others and neglecting their own needs in the process.

It may be a sign that a person is struggling to find balance in their life and is ignoring the importance of self-care.

The reversed The Hanged Man can also be a warning that a person is getting stuck in the same patterns and believes that the only way to make progress is to keep pushing harder and harder.

This can be particularly dangerous when it comes to health and wellbeing, as it can lead to burnout and exhaustion.

It is important to take a step back and reassess the situation, finding a way to care for yourself without sacrificing your overall health and wellbeing.

The reversed The Hanged Man is an opportunity to gain a different perspective and to make sure that you are making time to take care of yourself before everything else.
