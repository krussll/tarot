However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

Your wishes can come true if you use your strategic thought and intention to lead yourself and others to success. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health  
The King of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a person who values their physical and mental health. They will take the necessary steps to stay in shape, eating healthy and getting regular exercise. They are wise enough to know the importance of self-care, such as getting enough sleep and taking the time to relax and meditate. They will be strongly determined to maintain both their physical and mental health, looking for ways to improve and investing the time and energy needed to do so.

Finances  
The King of Swords Tarot card symbolizes fiscal responsibility, careful planning, and a well-thought-out strategy when it comes to finances. They are mindful of their spending, creating and adhering to a budget and looking for ways to save money. They are also wise to investing and will look for high-yield opportunities to grow their finances. They take the long view and aren’t swayed by quick fixes and schemes.

Relationships  
The King of Swords Tarot card symbolizes someone who values open communication, honesty and understanding when it comes to relationships. They are clear about their beliefs and expectations and aren’t afraid to express them in a respectful way. They understand the importance of compromise and don’t take things too personally. They take a thoughtful approach when it comes to dealing with disagreements, looking for common ground and positive outcomes. They are reliable and can be trusted to keep their word.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes wishes being fulfilled, material abundance, and good health. When it comes to the aspect of health, the 9 of Cups is a powerful sign that the body, mind and soul are in harmony. It signifies that physical and mental wellbeing are both in a positive state and that any ailments that may be occurring are being addressed. It is a reminder for the querent to take care of themselves and to make sure to prioritize their health and wellbeing.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Cups means that the querent is in a good financial situation and that money is coming in and out in balance. It suggests that the querent has a healthy relationship with money, and is more than capable of creating material abundance. The 9 of Cups also encourages the querent to be mindful of how they spend their money, encouraging them to think the long-term rather than the short-term.

In terms of relationships, the 9 of Cups is a reminder to the querent to cultivate their relationships with those around them. It is a sign that good relationships will be coming into their lives, and that existing relationships are strong and supportive. It is a reminder to nurture these relationships and to be mindful of how they are treating their loved ones. In addition, the 9 of Cups encourages the querent to be open to new forms of relationships – whether that be friendships, romantic relationships, or other types of partnerships. The 9 of Cups is a reminder to the querent that their relationships are a source of abundance and fulfillment.
