However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does 10 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The 10 Of Wands tarot card and The Queen Of Swords tarot card bring a message of perseverance in the face of stress and exhaustion. It implies that although you may feel that there are too many burdens and responsibilities, you possess the strength and self-sufficiency to handle them. Your courage and sense of honesty will help lead you through the hard times. Ultimately the cards tell you that everything will be OK if you keep going and don’t give up.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

When it comes to our health, the 10 of Wands card serves as a warning that if we do not take steps to alleviate the burden we have put upon ourselves, we may suffer from physical and emotional health issues. Not taking the time to rest and replenish our energy will result in mental exhaustion, physical exhaustion, and can even lead to depression and anxiety.

The 10 of Wands card also implies that our finances may be affected if we continue to take on too much. Carrying the burden of too many commitments can result in missed deadlines or lack of efficiency in our work, which can lead to lost wages or even job loss. It can be difficult to focus on financial planning and to make decisions about our future when we are overburdened with obligations, making it difficult to achieve financial security.

Finally, the 10 of Wands card may affect our relationships as well. Because we are taking on too much and becoming overwhelmed with obligations, it can be difficult to make meaningful connections with others and to maintain existing relationships. We may become short-tempered or too exhausted to be present in the moment with our loved ones. This card also serves as a warning that if we do not take the time to rest and reflect, we may begin to build walls and become isolated from the people who are most important to us.

The 10 of Wands tarot card serves as a warning that if we carry too much on our shoulders, it can have far-reaching consequences in our health, finances and relationships. It is important to remember to take care of ourselves and to recognize our limits, in order to avoid the potential pitfalls of being overburdened and overwhelmed.

This card signifies the need to take responsibility and be proactive in making sure you are taking care of your body and wellbeing. The Queen of Swords wants you to take full ownership of your finances, to stay mindful and make decisions that are in keeping with your long term goals and not just your immediate desires.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Swords in a relationship context stimulates the need to speak your truth, to stay honest and open with your partner. This card can signal a need to stay true to your values and beliefs in a relationship, and to set boundaries when needed. It can be a reminder to stay honest and open in communication, and to be aware of any controlling behaviours that can keep both partners from feeling their full freedom. Ultimately, the Queen wants us to lead with integrity and authenticity, making sure we don’t settle for anything less than true intimacy and acceptance.
