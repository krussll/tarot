However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 10 Of Cups tarot card together suggest finding harmony in following traditions and conventional expectations. However, you should also look to your family and loved ones to find contentment, love, and support. Listening to and adhering to the wisdom of tradition and your family will bring you total satisfaction and joy. While one card advises you to follow the traditional path and the other advises you to seek love and support within your family, taking both these paths together can truly lead to a fulfilling and meaningful life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hierophant and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health:

The Hierophant Tarot card can represent traditions or conventional expectations when it comes to health. This card suggests that you should look to the lessons of the past and embrace traditions and habits that have been passed down through generations. This card could also be a reminder to adhere to well-known advice on nutrition and fitness in order to stay healthy. It might also be a warning against conforming to unhealthy or unsafe habits that could have a negative impact on your health.

Finances:

When it comes to finances, the Hierophant Tarot card could be a reminder to respect traditional financial models and invest in sound financial options, such as stocks and bonds, rather than speculative investments. It could also be a warning against succumbing to peer pressure and making risky investments. Establishing a good financial plan and sticking to it can be one of the best forms of financial advice in this situation.

Relationships:

The Hierophant Tarot card could represent that traditional expectations and social norms should be observed when it comes to relationships. This could include respecting gender roles, following courting rituals, and adhering to social class boundaries. It might also be a warning against acting on impulse when it comes to relationships, and instead seeking out strong foundations and committing to long-term plans.

It can mean that regardless of what life throws at you, you are able to find the strength and emotional balance to handle it. It suggests that whatever health issues you may face, you’re able to lean on the support of your family and friends to get through each challenge.

For finances, The 10 of Cups Tarot card suggests that you have the resources and support to make sound financial decisions. You have the potential to achieve financial security, and the support of family and friends can provide the emotional stability, guidance and reassurance you need to take a measured approach to investing and success.

For relationships, The 10 of Cups Tarot card encourages you to appreciate and celebrate the personal and intimate relationship you have with your partner or family.
