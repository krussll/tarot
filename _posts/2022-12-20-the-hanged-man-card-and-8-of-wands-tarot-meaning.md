A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 8 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 8 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 8 Of Wands mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card and The 8 of Wands tarot card together signify the need for a change of perspective in order to reach wisdom. By sacrificing yourself and looking at a situation from a different angle, you may be able to understand a situation quickly and reach a resolution that is beneficial for all. The 8 of Wands tarot card reinforces this by suggesting that quick action is necessary. News may be incoming, so you do not have much time to make decisions, but with a little self-sacrifice, you can act swiftly and be rewarded with the gift of wisdom.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and 8 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

It suggests taking some time out of our day to focus on our physical and mental health, such as through yoga, meditation or simply a long walk in nature. We can use this self-care to gain perspective not just on our own health, but also on the bigger picture of our lives. This is represented in the card by the figure of the hanged man hanging upside down from a tree, looking at life from a different angle. The card also suggests taking a step back and looking at the whole picture of our health, including the physical, emotional, mental and even spiritual aspects.

Finances  
The Hanged Man Tarox card can represent taking a step back from the pressures of finances. It is a reminder to take the time to reflect on our current financial situation and think about the bigger picture. This can involve considering our goals and objectives in relation to our finances and developing a plan to reach them. The card also suggests being open to creative solutions to financial issues and looking at our money in a different way. It may be beneficial to seek advice from a financial expert to ensure that our plans are sound and to ensure that we are making the best decisions for our finances.

Relationships  
The Hanged Man Tarot card can represent a willingness to be open and flexible when it comes to relationships. It suggests considering how our behavior affects our relationships, and how to approach them in a way that is positive and compassionate. It also encourages us to be willing to compromise when it comes to our interactions, be they with family, friends or partners. It is a reminder to view our relationships from a different perspective, and to not be afraid to step out of our comfort zone in order to find a better understanding of ourselves and our relationships. We can use this card to remind us to stay open to new ideas, to be willing to give and take in relationships, and to be honest and true about our feelings in order to create a more meaningful connection with others.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 8 of Wands Tarot card indicates a time of sudden action and quick changes. In terms of health, this card could represent a sudden shift in your physical health, such as a recovery from an illness. It could also point to the need for a change in lifestyle or diet, such as an exercise routine or a change to healthier eating habits. The 8 of Wands card can indicate that a health issue that was previously blocked or stagnant is now ready to be addressed and resolved.

Finances: The 8 of Wands Tarot card encourages financial activity and risk-taking. This card often signals a time of financial changes and news about money matters. It suggests that opportunities for investment and speculation are on the horizon. If you are looking to make a financial move, this card can indicate a successful outcome, as long as you act swiftly and take advantage of available resources.

Relationships: The 8 of Wands Tarot card indicates a period of rapid change and unexpected news. In terms of relationships, this card suggests that sudden developments are at play and that the status quo may be on the brink of shifting. If a relationship has been stagnant or in a period of confusion, the 8 of Wands card encourages decisive action. It can also mean that long-distance relationships are coming to an end or that new ideas are being presented. Ultimately, the 8 of Wands encourages swift and decisive movement in all areas of life, including relationships.
