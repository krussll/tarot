You may feel grief and regret over what you have lost, but you also recognize that you need to carry on and complete the task ahead of you. You may be feeling abandoned or unloved, but you know that you must power through to ensure that your legacy is left behind. In the end, it may all have been worth it, as your legacy will outlive you and be your inheritance for future generations.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The 5 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes physical and emotional health issues that can arise from loss. Physically, it can represent a decrease in energy and vitality, as well as feelings of exhaustion and lethargy. It can also manifest in a sense of depression, isolation, and in extreme cases, even physical illness. Those who are suffering from the 5 of Cups Tarot card should seek professional help or advice to cope with their feelings and address potential health issues.

Finances:

The Five of Cups Tarot card also symbolizes a difficult time in one’s finances. This can manifest in money lost, through investments gone wrong or unexpected expenses. The Five of Cups Tarot card can also symbolize a stagnancy in one’s financial progress, with the lack of any dramatic increase in wealth. It is important for those suffering from this card to focus on creating and maintaining a budget, saving for the future, and finding ways to increase their income.

Relationships:

The Five of Cups Tarot card can symbolize deep emotional loss, particularly in relationships. This could be the end of a relationship, the death of a loved one, or even the loss of a friendship. It can represent a feeling of emptiness and sadness, as well as a feeling of being unloved or abandoned. Those who are having difficulty dealing with the emotional loss associated with this card should take some time to focus on personal growth and healing. It is important to take time to reflect on what brought us to this point and to identify what needs to change in order to move forward. With the right outlook and attitude, we can use this time to set new goals and to create new, healthier relationships.
