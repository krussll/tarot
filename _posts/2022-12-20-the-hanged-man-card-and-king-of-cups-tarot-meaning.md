However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and King Of Cups mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card and the King of Cups tarot card together represent a unique combination of wisdom and leadership. The Hanged Man card indicates that you should be willing to make sacrifices in order to gain a more expansive outlook on life. The King of Cups brings a more compassionate and supportive attitude to the equation, indicating that you are capable of making decisions that will benefit your loved ones as well as yourself. Together, these cards suggest that through careful consideration and a willingness to be generous with your time and compassion, you can maintain a balanced perspective and use your leadership abilities for the benefit of all.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hanged Man and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hanged Man and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

The Hanged Man reminds us to make decisions and choices that are informed and beneficial for the long–term health of our bodies.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health  
The King of Cups Tarot card is all about healing and renewal. It indicates that now is a time to nurture your body and mind, and to place emphasis on emotional and spiritual growth. Its presence suggests that any physical or psychological ailments can be healed through patience and dedication to self-care. It invites you to access your inner wisdom and trust your instincts when it comes to making decisions about your health and wellbeing.

Finances  
The King of Cups Tarot card is one of stability and prosperity. It suggests that financial and business matters are going well and that now is a great time to focus on financial security and stability. It encourages you to take risks and invest in yourself and your future, but to also practice balance and moderation in spending. It also offers a reminder to remember that wealth isn’t always measured in material possessions, and to look to your spiritual and emotional wellbeing instead.

Relationships  
The King of Cups Tarot card is a message of emotional balance and understanding. It suggests that any relationships you have will benefit from communication and trust. It invites you to open your heart and mind to those around you, express yourself clearly, and listen to the needs of your partner. It encourages you to practice empathy and kindness, and to show compassion to those who need it. This card is a reminder that relationships of any kind are built on mutual understanding, patience and respect.
