A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Empress card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Empress card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Empress and 10 Of Swords mean together?

When I combine the meanings of the Empress tarot card and the 10 of Swords tarot card, I sense that I am going through a period of deep, painful endings which are making way for new beginnings. Though the endings may seem overwhelming, I realize that this process of ending, letting go, and resetting is necessary for me to move forward and create something wonderful. I take comfort in the knowledge that although the endings may be difficult, there is a great blessing and potential for growth on the other side. I trust that the abundance, fertility and good parenting that The Empress tarot card represents will help me through these painful endings, and create the foundations for something beautiful.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Empress and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Empress mean?

The Empress Tarot card symbolizes abundance and fertility, representing creation both in tangible things, like wealth and success, and intangible aspects, such as relationships and personal growth. For health, The Empress Tarot card can signify balance and an overall sense of being content and in tune with one’s own body and mind. This card can indicate that a person may be taking better care of themselves, both in terms of physical and mental health. It could also suggest an increase in energy and vitality, which can help a person to handle life’s challenges and stresses a bit better.

In terms of finances, The Empress Tarot card is associated with a feeling of abundance and prosperity. A person who draws this card when seeking guidance about their financial situation may be encouraged to take greater opportunities to strengthen their financial security. It could also mean that a person is able to bring their creative ideas for wealth-building to fruition.

In relationships, The Empress Tarot card can symbolize a sense of nurturing and a willingness to tend to the needs of one’s partner. It can be an indication that a person is willing to provide support and security to the people they care about, while also encouraging them to follow their dreams and passions. This card can also suggest that a person is engaging in positive communication and setting healthy boundaries in their relationships. The Empress Tarot card can be seen as a sign that a person is ready to move forward and create something beautiful with their partner.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 10 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a health issue that has reached a crisis point. You may have pushed yourself too hard or ignored the warning signs of a physical, mental, or emotional problem that has now become a major issue. You may feel overwhelmed and unable to handle the situation on your own. This card suggests that seeking professional help would be beneficial and can be a blessing in disguise, leading to a recovery and improved sense of wellbeing.

Finances: The 10 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a financial situation that has reached an all-time low. You may have made mistakes or taken unnecessary risks that have now caused you to find yourself in a difficult position. Your finances may have reached a rock bottom, you may be facing repossession or bankruptcy, and your only option is to start over from scratch. This can be an incredibly difficult time and it can feel like no matter what you do, you can’t seem to get ahead.

Relationships: The 10 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a relationship that has reached a breaking point. You may have encountered too many issues or pushed each other too hard, and now the relationship is beyond repair. You may feel as if all your hard work and effort has been for nothing and it is time to accept that the relationship is over. This card suggests that it is time to move on and find closure, so that you can open yourself up to new relationships and experiences.
