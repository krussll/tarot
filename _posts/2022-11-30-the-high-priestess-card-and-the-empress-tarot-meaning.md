A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The High Priestess card and The Empress card together in particular.

The The High Priestess card and The Empress have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The High Priestess and The Empress mean together?

The High Priestess and The Empress tarot cards together mean that you should trust your intuition and secrets to create abundance in your health and relationships. You should listen to your inner voice and trust your gut instinct. These tarot cards also mean that you are fertile and can create new life. You have the ability to be a great parent and to create abundance in your life.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The High Priestess and The Empress mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The High Priestess mean?

Health: The High Priestess Tarot card means that you should trust your intuition when it comes to your health. If you feel like something is wrong, then it probably is. Don’t be afraid to get a second opinion from a doctor or another health professional to be sure.

Finances: The High Priestess Tarot card can mean that you have some hidden talents or abilities that you can use to your financial advantage. Now is the time to start using them to make some extra money. It is also a good time to save money, as your intuition can help you to know when a good deal is coming your way.

Relationships: The High Priestess Tarot card means that you should trust your intuition when it comes to your relationships. If you feel like something is off, then it probably is. Don’t be afraid to talk to your partner about your concerns. You may also want to consider getting some counseling to help you work through any issues that may be present.

## What does The Empress mean?

Health: The Empress Tarot card is a very positive card for health, as it represents fertility and abundance. If you are pregnant or hoping to become pregnant, The Empress is a very favorable sign, indicating a healthy pregnancy and a bountiful supply of breast milk. The Empress also signifies good parenting, so if you are a parent, you can expect to be loving and nurturing towards your children. Finances: The Empress Tarot card is also a very positive card for finances, as it suggests abundance and prosperity. If you are hoping to start or grow a business, The Empress is a very favorable sign, indicating that you will be successful in your endeavors. If you are worried about money troubles, The Empress suggests that you will soon be prosperous and able to live a comfortable life. Relationships: The Empress Tarot card is an excellent card for relationships, as it signifies love, fertility, and abundance. If you are hoping to start a family, The Empress is a very favorable sign, indicating that you will be able to have children. If you are in a relationship, The Empress suggests that your relationship will be loving and fruitful.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The High Priestess and The Empress has helped you.
