A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together represent a journey of realization and celebration. The Hanged Man tarot card encourages you to look at things from a different perspective and to be willing to sacrifice a little in order to gain a greater understanding of yourself and the world around you. The 3 Of Cups tarot card suggests that as you go through this journey, you will find a circle of support, friends who will celebrate your growth and stand by you as you learn more about yourself and the world. It’s a powerful reminder that true wisdom and growth come from the combination of self-sacrifice, understanding, and appreciation from those around us.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health

The Hanged Man Tarot card speaks to a need to pause, to step back from our worries and anxieties around health. It asks us to be perfectly still, to take a break. To see beyond what we immediately think or feel. It encourages us to look inward and to explore alternative ways of thinking and being. We may need to slow down and pay attention to our body and its needs and to listen to its wisdom. This card can remind us to take the time to find balance in life and to nourish our mind, body and soul.

Finances

The Hanged Man Tarot card can be interpreted as a call to put our financial concerns in perspective. To look at our resources from a different perspective and to get creative. This card asks us to broaden our view and to take an unconventional approach. To explore a different way of interpreting the situation. It can also remind us to consider the long-term benefits of our choices and to be patient in times of difficulty.

Relationships

The Hanged Man Tarot card speaks of balance and understanding. It can suggest that we take a step back in relationships and allow connections to develop organically. We can remember to trust in ourselves and to trust in the power of our relationships. We can seek out balance between giving and receiving and to recognize meaningful connections. The card can also encourage us to be aware of the give and take in relationships and to strive for a level of mutual understanding and support.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health

When it comes to health, the 3 of Cups Tarot card encourages us to nurture our wellbeing through the support of our friends and family. By seeking out those who can provide us with a safety net and the affirmation we need, we may find our own strength and inner confidence to tackle any medical issues or physical challenges we may face. Our circle of loved ones can create a powerful source of emotional support that can help us maintain healthy relationships with our own body and mind. In times of hardship or uncertainty, we can know that our friends and family are there to provide encouragement, guidance and much needed positivity.

Finances

The 3 of Cups Tarot card can also be a sign that we should pursue our financial goals with the help of others. By forming a strong support system, our goals can be more attainable and less overwhelming. A team of friends and family who are committed to helping each other can provide a positive environment to discuss our financial plans and come up with innovative solutions. As we cooperate and collaborate, we can benefit from each other’s unique knowledge, skills and resources to gain opportunities, create wealth and financial stability.

Relationships

In terms of relationships, the 3 of Cups Tarot card indicates that it’s time to celebrate the special people in our lives. We need to be open to forming meaningful connections and building strong social circles with those who offer understanding, respect and unconditional love. Those genuine friendships can be like a family, offering a sense of security and emotional support that can help us find peace and contentment in our romantic relationships. Times of celebration with friends and loved ones can bring us immense joy and remind us that we are never alone.
