A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The 7 Of Swords tarot card and the 10 Of Cups tarot card together speak to a situation of conflict between loyalty and betrayal. On one hand, the 7 Of Swords suggests that you may feel tempted to take a dishonest path to success, or to abandon a current path or relationship. On the other hand, the 10 Of Cups reminds you that the most important thing in life is the love and support of your family. This could be a difficult tug-of-war between your own feelings and the feelings of those around you, making you wonder what the “right” course of action is. However, it is important to remember that you cannot deny the importance of family loyalty, no matter what the situation.

The 7 Of Swords tarot card and The 10 Of Cups tarot card together in your love life could mean both good and bad news. The 7 of Swords tarot card is a warning that you could experience dishonest, sneaky, and possibly even betrayal in your intimate relationships. In order to protect yourself, you need to be wary and cautious. Your boundaries should be clearly communicated and respected.

At the same time, The 10 Of Cups tarot card signals that you have the capacity to experience contentment and joy in love. You can find total love and support in your relationships which could lead to a deeper connection, greater stability and long-term happiness.

The best way for you to find fulfillment in love is first to get clear on your boundaries and what you will and will not accept, and then to be brave enough to take risks and open yourself up to the possibility of true love. You will have both the courage and tenacity to guard your heart against any dishonesty, and to stay optimistic when it comes to finding real, lasting love.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card generally indicates a theft, betrayal, or dishonesty that could have serious consequences, both in the short-term and long-term. When it comes to health, the 7 of Swords may foretell of a deep betrayal that poses a health risk. This could be an individual’s partner cheating on them, someone stealing their identity, or even their physician prescribing medications that are harmful. Whatever the case, an individual’s health is at risk when this card appears in a Tarot reading.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Swords can imply a loss of money due to someone deceiving or taking advantage of an individual. This could manifest as a dodgy investment, a business scam, or a dishonest loan shark. Whatever the situation, the individual is likely to suffer a financial loss or have their money stolen from them.

The 7 of Swords can also portend of an unbalanced relationship. In this case, someone could be lying or dishonest to their partner, be unfaithful to them, or not be truthful about their intentions or their feelings. This could result in the breakdown of a relationship, as trust has been broken and someone has been betrayed.

In general, the 7 of Swords is a card that warns of deception, dishonesty and betrayal, which can have serious repercussions in all aspects of an individual’s life. It is important to take extra care when this card appears in a Tarot reading, to ensure that the individual is well aware of the potential danger they may face.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 10 of Cups Tarot card can mean health in a variety of ways. Firstly, it can be a sign of ultimate physical and emotional health. When this card appears, it signifies a period of balance and homeostasis, in which all aspects of life are in harmony. It can be a sign of feeling your best, with plenty of energy, peace and joy. It can also mean psychological health and contentment, as family and community, the cornerstones of emotional wellbeing, are at the centre of this card.

Finances:

The 10 of Cups Tarot card indicates financial security, abundance and stability. The focus of the card is on relationships and support, and financial security is built when those associations are strong. When this card appears, it implies that with the right assistance, focus and support, financial goals can be achieved and maintained. It can also mean strong financial planning, and creating a solid foundation for financial success.

Relationships:

The 10 of Cups Tarot card symbolises the ultimate in relationships; total love and support. When this card appears, it can mean an effortless feeling of connectedness and comfort with a significant other. It can also mean strong and supportive family relationships, whether that’s with parents, siblings or even extended family. It can be a sign of deep understanding and appreciation of one another, and a feeling of being completely accepted and supported by those around us.
