A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and 4 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and 4 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and 4 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 7 Of Wands and the 4 Of Pentacles tarot card together symbolize a need for self-defense and the desire for long-term security. The 7 Of Wands suggests that in order for you to achieve your desired level of security, you must be prepared to protect yourself against competition. You must be willing to put up a fight, both mentally and physically, to ensure that your security is not threatened by outside forces. On the other hand, the 4 Of Pentacles tells you that in order to be successful in achieving your security, you must have a strong desire to find it. You must be willing to pursue the security that you require, without letting your greed cloud your judgment. In conclusion, these two cards together suggest a balance between self-defense and the longing for secure, long-term stability.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 7 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the need to take charge of your own physical and emotional health. When this card reveals itself, it is a sign that you must be proactive and take responsibility for your own well-being. To maintain good physical health, it is important that you are mindful of what you put into your body; ensure that good nutrition is a priority and resist the temptation to give in to unhealthy cravings. Further, it is important to make regular visits to your doctor, be mindful of symptoms, and take preventative measures to protect from illness.

In regards to emotional health, self-care and self-compassion are of the utmost importance. Take time to meditate and reflect, and practice mindfulness and gratitude. When difficult emotions arise, take some time to process and understand underlying causes, rather than immediately turning to unhealthy coping mechanisms.

Finances: The 7 of Wands Tarot card serves as a reminder to be mindful of your financial resources. This card encourages you to take charge of your finances and make wise investments. In your financial decisions, you must balance short-term gratification with long-term planning. Practicing disciplined spending habits and developing an emergency fund are essential steps to taking control of your finances. Additionally, avoid relying on credit cards and accumulating too much debt; instead, focus on growing your own wealth.

Relationships: The 7 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes self-defense in relationships. This card serves as a reminder to stand up for yourself, as well as to set and maintain healthy boundaries when it comes to interpersonal relationships. When faced with difficult personalities or conflicts, take pause and draw from your inner strength before responding. Further, practice healthy communication skills such as expressing yourself directly and honestly. Finally, when faced with competitive relationships, practice self-love and self-care; recognize that you are special and unique, and your worth does not depend on the opinions of others.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The Four of Pentacles tarot card shows a figure with two hands gripping a golden disc, which represents the material world and its trappings. This figure is also crowned with four pentacles, each symbolizing a different aspect of physical security. This card encourages a person to use caution and preserve their resources.

The Four of Pentacles tarot card is a warning that too much focus on physical, materialistic security can lead to rigid thinking and a closed-off attitude. It is an invitation to examine what the individual truly needs to feel safe and secure.

In health, the Four of Pentacles suggests that feeling secure and safe in one’s own body is an essential foundation for true well-being. It is important to take care of one’s body, both through good physical health habits and through positive psychological practices. A person can cultivate a strong sense of inner security that is independent of physical possessions.

In terms of finances, the Four of Pentacles encourages a person to be frugal and to be mindful of the importance of saving. If one is too focused on material security and accumulating wealth, there can be an imbalance between the amount one is saving and the amount one is spending. It is important to find a sense of stability and security in one’s finances while still being open to new experiences and opportunities.

The Four of Pentacles card also speaks to relationships. This card suggests that security in relationships is essential and can come in many forms. A person can seek out relationships that are based on mutual respect, a shared sense of security, and open communication. This kind of security is a solid foundation for any lasting relationship.

In conclusion, the Four of Pentacles tarot card encourages caution, security, and balance in all aspects of life. This card warns that too much emphasis on physical security can lead to a closed-off attitude and stagnation. True security comes from finding a balance between physical possessions and inner balance and stability.
