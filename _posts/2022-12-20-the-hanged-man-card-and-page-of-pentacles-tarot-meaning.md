A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and Page Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and Page Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and Page Of Pentacles mean together?

The combination of the Hanged Man and the Page of Pentacles is one of great potential. It tells of a moment of wisdom and self-sacrifice that could potentially lead to a newfound gain. This could mean a job, a raise, or even money news in general. It is a call to look at things in a different perspective and use the wisdom one has won to make the most out of a situation. The patience of the Page of Pentacles also allows for this focus, as it encourages one to consider the long-term effects of making any decisions.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and Page Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card is an ancient and powerful symbol of wisdom, self-sacrifice, and learning to see things from a different perspective. It is often associated with letting go of old patterns and beliefs and embracing change. When it comes to health, finances, and relationships, The Hanged Man can encourage letting go of unhealthy habits or toxic relationships, or sacrificing something in order to make a positive change. It speaks to the importance of maintaining a healthy balance in life and learning to trust the universe.

When it comes to health, The Hanged Man Tarot card can be seen as an encouragement for self-care. It encourages you to make wise decisions about your physical and mental health, and to take ownership of your overall wellbeing. If a health issue is taking up too much of your time and energy, The Hanged Man suggests that you might need to make some changes in order to bring yourself back into balance. This can involve taking a break from work or obligations, or making a conscious effort to slow down and make healthier decisions.

When it comes to finances, The Hanged Man encourages you to make wise investments and maintain a healthy balance between saving and spending. It speaks to the importance of making smart financial decisions that will benefit you in the long term. The Hanged Man also suggests that sometimes it pays to take a risk and move away from the status quo. This might involve taking a leap of faith into a new career or side gig, or investing in something new and unique.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Hanged Man Tarot card can be seen as an encouragement to take a step back and evaluate your current situation. It speaks to the importance of being honest with yourself and those around you, and of making conscious choices rather than lashing out in anger. It also encourages you to let go of relationships that bring negativity or drama into your life, and to make wise choices when it comes to your time and energy. By embracing change and learning to see things from a different perspective, The Hanged Man Tarot card can be a powerful reminder of the importance of self-care and wisdom in our finances, health, and relationships.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

The Page Of Pentacles Tarot card represents a person who is stable and reliable, who is willing to put in the hard work and time to achieve their goals. It is a card of security, patience and success through hard work.

When it comes to health, The Page Of Pentacles Tarot card can show that taking the time to focus on one’s physical wellbeing and health is essential. This involves being conservative with diet and making time for exercise, as well as looking out for mental health, too. It’s important to take all aspects of health seriously, and this card suggests that doing so will pay off in the long run.

When it comes to finances, The Page Of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that opportunities for a new job or raise are on the horizon. This is a time for taking long-term steps to ensure financial stability, through budgeting and planning. Taking the time to assess one’s finances and finding ways to grow them is essential for financial security.

When it comes to relationships, The Page Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that taking the time to really get to know someone is a worthwhile investment. This is a time for creating solid foundations for a relationship, as well as being flexible and patient in order to develop and strengthen it. For those who are single, the Page Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that now is a great time to invest in self-care before diving into the dating scene.
