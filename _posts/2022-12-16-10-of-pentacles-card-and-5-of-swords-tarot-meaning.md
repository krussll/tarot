## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a powerful symbol for wealth, family and legacy. It refers to the legacy of success in one’s life, whether it be through financial means, relationships or health.

For one’s health, the 10 Of Pentacles can represent putting in the effort to maintain good health in order to live a long and healthy life. It also signifies taking heed of wise counsel from those that have gone before, such as seeking medical advice and taking preventative measures to avoid illness.

For one’s finances, the 10 Of Pentacles can be interpreted as being smart with the money one earns. It may be a sign to save for the future, to invest wisely, and to give to charity. In addition, it may remind one to not only secure a comfortable retirement, but to leave a legacy of wealth to future generations.

In terms of relationships, the 10 Of Pentacles can represent a strong foundation of security and trust. It can be a reminder that relationships should be built on a trusting, honest and dedicated foundation. One should invest energy in order to ensure that their loved ones and family members are taken care of for many generations.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

The 5 of Swords tarot card can be a very concerning omen when it comes to health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to the health of an individual, the 5 of Swords tarot card can mean that they may be dealing with a situation such as bullying, theft, violence and/or abuse. In this situation, they may need to seek professional help to help them cope with the situation and get them back on track. They may need to talk to a therapist or counsellor who can help them find ways to build their self-confidence and resilience so they can once again feel good about themselves and be able to stand up for themselves.

Financially, the 5 of Swords tarot card can indicate that a person is dealing with someone who is taking advantage of them or is being abusive in some way. This can mean that their finances are being drained from a situation where they are being taken advantage of, or someone is mishandling their finances by taking away their assets without permission. If a person finds themselves in this situation, they should speak up for themselves and take action to protect their money.

In relationships, the 5 of Swords tarot card can mean that a person is in an abusive relationship. This abuse can include physical, emotional, verbal or financial abuse, or any combination of these types of abuse. It is important to recognize this sign and to reach out to their support network or seek professional help to help them get out of the situation and find a healthier, more fulfilling relationship.

No matter what the situation is, the 5 of Swords Tarot card is an important reminder to take care of our mental, physical and financial health. If a person is feeling overwhelmed or is in an unsafe situation, they should not hesitate to get professional help in order to get back on track.
