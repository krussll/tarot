## What does Temperance and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The 7 Of Wands tarot card together represent the need for patience, balance and self-control in the face of opposition or adversity. They suggest that while it is important to protect yourself and assert your rights, doing so in a measured and tempered way is necessary in order to achieve the best outcome. As such, they remind us that we can achieve more through thoughtful, considered action than through rash, impulsive behavior. With self-control and balance, we can navigate challenging situations with a sense of grace and presence.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card represents balance. It symbolizes the need for moderation, harmony and patience in all aspects of life. This Tarot card is particularly important for one’s health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, The Temperance Tarot card encourages balance between healthy and unhealthy practices. It suggests that one should maintain a lifestyle that is not too extreme in either direction. Eating a balanced diet of healthy foods, coupled with exercising regularly and getting enough rest, are all important health-related practices that this card promotes.

When it comes to finances, The Temperance Tarot card calls for balance. It encourages one to save and invest while also spending money wisely and having a healthy relationship with money. This card warns against going into debt and urges caution when making financial decisions.

Finally, The Temperance Tarot card suggests that balance is important for healthy relationships as well. It speaks for keeping open communication lines, being patient, understanding and compromising with others. It also encourages people to be mindful of their own actions and to take responsibility for them.

In sum, The Temperance Tarot card is a powerful symbol of balance. It encourages moderation, harmony and patience in all aspects of life, including our health, finances and relationships. By living in balance, one can achieve greater overall health, wealth and emotional well-being.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 7 of Wands is a card that speaks to the significance of protecting one’s health. It suggests that you be prepared to defend your health against unhealthy life choices, such as bad eating habits, lack of exercise, or neglecting to have regular checkups and screenings. This card suggests that you recognize the importance of taking care of yourself, and to be aware of any outside influences or pressures that could have a negative impact on your physical or mental well-being.

Finances

The 7 of Wands card is also a reminder to protect your finances, a reminder to stay wary of any outside influences or competition that could undermine your financial security. This card encourages you to act proactively in safeguarding your financial security. This might mean taking the time to research investments, create a budget and maintain a sensible spending plan, or setting goals for the future to ensure your financial security.

Relationships

Finally, the 7 of Wands is a reminder to protect our relationships. It encourages us to be aware of any external influences or competition that could potentially damage our relationships. It also suggests that we are prepared to protect our relationships from lapses in communication, shifts in expectations, or unspoken resentment. This card suggests that we defend our relationships by remembering the importance of respect, understanding, and open communication. It encourages us to be mindful of the important role our relationships play in our lives, and prioritize their health and wellbeing.
