You will have to be willing to make some voluntary changes in order to achieve success. This could involve taking risks and expanding your horizons to explore previously unexplored areas. By working together with others, using your intuition and making the right changes you could move forward to a more successful future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health: The Moon Tarot card symbolizes unseen problems in the realm of health, especially those relating to emotional wellness. The Moon warns of possible secrets, emotional issues, and miscommunications, which can manifest in physical issues. The Moon can represent emotional instability, insecurity, and feelings of confusion. These feelings can manifest in physical ailments such as headaches, insomnia, digestive issues, or fatigue. To stay healthy, it is important to be in tune with your inner self and to trust your intuition. Acknowledging any issues and communicating them to a trusted health professional can help you find a resolution.

Finances: In the realm of finances, The Moon card reflects unseen issues and the need to be conscious and aware in order to succeed. This card is a warning that trust and caution need to be employed when dealing with money matters, and that careful ideas, strategies, and decision-making are essential. The Moon also suggests that voluntary changes may be necessary to free yourself from financial obstacles and to provide for a more secure and prosperous financial future.

Relationships: The Moon Tarot card indicates that there may be unseen problems, miscommunication, and secrets in relationships. Unresolved issues can cause feelings of insecurity, confusion, and instability. To cultivate healthy relationships, trust your intuition and voice your feelings honestly and openly. Communicate and be mindful of the feelings of others, and be willing to make the necessary changes to help the relationship grow.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

The 3 of Wands Tarot card is a powerful card that represents teamwork, commerce, expansion, and travel. It is a card of optimism, striving for better and greater things in life, and having the courage to move forward.

Health: The 3 of Wands Tarot card can symbolize health and well-being. It is a great card to turn to when embarking on a health journey. It speaks of the willingness to strive for greater health, and to have faith that things will improve if we take the right paths. It also suggests using teamwork to achieve health goals. Working with someone else can be a great way to stay motivated and to help each other through challenges.

Finances: The 3 of Wands Tarot card can also be a sign of financial good fortune. It speaks of ambition and believing in the possibility of greater prosperity. It is a card of trust; believing that if we take the right steps, success will come. It also suggests that commerce and teamwork can help us reach our financial goals. Relationships: The 3 of Wands Tarot card can represent relationships. It speaks of trust, understanding and mutual commitment. This card is a reminder that partnerships need to be nurtured, and that with the right amount of work and communication, great things can be achieved. It also reminds us that relationships need to expand, grow and travel together in order to thrive.
