## What does 5 Of Wands and 10 Of Wands mean together?

The 5 of Wands and the 10 of Wands represent a challenging time in life, full of obstacles and rivalry. It’s a time of intense competition and the pressure can be immense. It might be an individual challenge you’re facing or a group you’re in competition with. You will need to work hard to overcome the difficulties and stress of this period, as the 10 of Wands warns that too many burdens can lead to exhaustion. A balance needs to be found between the need to take on the challenges and obstacles of life and being able to step back, take a break and rest.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

In terms of finances, the 5 of Wands Tarot card is an indication that you may need to be prepared for unexpected challenges and obstacles. In terms of relationships, the 5 of Wands Tarot card suggests that you should be aware of potential conflicts and power struggles. ## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 10 of Wands tarot card is a sign of stress and exhaustion. It suggests that the person has been exerting too much energy and has taken on too many tasks and obligations. This can lead to physical and emotional health issues such as fatigue, lack of motivation, sore muscles, headaches, insomnia, and depression. It is imperative for this individual to take the time to reflect on their current situation and prioritize the tasks that are most important to them. Additionally, it is important to take breaks and practice relaxation techniques such as mindfulness meditation and yoga to reduce stress and improve their overall wellbeing.

Finances: The 10 of Wands tarot card can signify financial burden and a sense of being overwhelmed. This might be due to hefty expenses, or because the person is taking on too many obligations that leave them feeling worn out and unable to focus on what’s important. It is important for this person to create a budget and determine which areas of their finances they need to prioritize. Additionally, they should take the time to reassess their current financial situation, and identify any areas of overspending or where they can cut back on their spending.

Relationships: The 10 of Wands tarot card reflects a sense of feeling overwhelmed and overburdened. This can lead to difficulty in maintaining relationships. The person may start to feel like they do not have the energy or time to devote to their relationships, leading to feelings of disconnection. It is important for this individual to prioritize the relationships in their life and set aside time to make their loved ones a priority. Additionally, communicating openly with their loved ones about how they are feeling can help to create stronger and more meaningful bonds.
