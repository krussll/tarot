A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The 4 Of Swords and The 10 Of Swords tarot cards together suggest a period of difficulty and transition. This could be a time of sorrow and hardship, where a painful ending is finally reached and complete. The 4 Of Swords indicates that this is a necessary stage, where you must retreat and take time for yourself to renew and recover from whatever you’ve been through. This renewal won’t come without a price, but it will be worth it in the end as you emerge from this stage with a newfound strength and inner peace.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the need for rest and recovery in order to maintain and enhance physical health. It often appears when someone needs to take a break from their day-to-day activities, whether it be due to physical or mental exhaustion. It can also point to a need for a period of solitude in order to heal from an illness or injury. This card encourages us to step away from routine for a period of time in order to allow our bodies to rest and rejuvenate.

Finances

The 4 of Swords Tarot card often appears when a person may need to step back and re-evaluate their financial situation. It is a reminder to take a pause and practice caution when making any big financial decisions. This card often signals that it is not the right time to take risks or make big financial investments. It is an invitation to take some quiet time and assess the situation carefully.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card suggests that a period of rest and retreat may be necessary for relationships. It can indicate a need for some space, whether it be with a partner, family member, or friend. This card encourages us to pause and assess the situation before acting. It is an invitation to step back and reassess how to best nurture the connection and move forward in a healthy, constructive way. This card can also suggest that relationships run more smoothly when we take some time to pause and reflect on how best to move forward.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords Tarot card is often seen as a sign of complete and painful endings. When it appears in a reading, it suggests that a particular situation has been taken as far as it can go and is no longer salvageable, regardless of any efforts to save it.

Health

The 10 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes complete and painful endings in terms of health. When it appears in a reading, it can indicate that something has been taken to its rock bottom and is no longer viable. It can also indicate something has come to a sudden or unexpected end, or that it has decayed beyond repair or recovery. It can also suggest a health issue that has been pushed too far and is no longer possible to fix.

Finances

The 10 of Swords Tarot card indicates complete financial ruin. When it appears in a reading, it can mean the end of a particular financial arrangement, investment, or business venture. It can suggest a financial situation that has been pushed beyond its limits and is no longer viable. In terms of investments, it could indicate a strategy that has been taken too far and caused a devastating financial loss. In terms of a business venture, it can indicate that the enterprise is no longer worthwhile and should be abandoned.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, the 10 of Swords Tarot card indicates complete and painful endings. It suggests relationships that have been pushed to their absolute limits and are no longer salvageable. It can also indicate a sudden or unexpected end to a relationship, or the dissolution of a long-term connection. It could even suggest an unhealthy and unworkable relationship that needs to be released.
