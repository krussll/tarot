A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Magician card and 7 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Magician card and 7 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Magician and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The Magician and The 7 of Wands together indicate a need for focused action and creativity in order to achieve your goals. This may be a time of competition or challenge, so it is important to be prepared and to defend your position. You have the power to make your dreams a reality, so use it wisely.

This combination can also represent a battle between your head and your heart. The Magician is all about logic and reason, while the 7 of Wands is about passion and emotion. You may find yourself torn between what you know is right and what you feel is right. Trust your intuition and go with your gut instinct.

This is a time of great potential, so make sure you are prepared to seize any opportunities that come your way. The Magician is a master of manifestation, so use your powers of visualization to attract what you desire. The 7 of Wands reminds you to stay focused and to stand your ground. Trust that you have what it takes to succeed.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Magician and 7 Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Magician and 7 Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Magician mean?

The Magician Tarot card means focused creativity, turning visions into reality, inspired action. In terms of health, this suggests that if you have a clear vision of what you want to achieve, you can take steps to make it happen. If you’re feeling stuck or uncertain about what you want, The Magician can help you focus your creative energy to find a way forward. In terms of finances, The Magician suggests that you have the power to manifest your desires into reality. If you can focus your energy and take inspired action, you can create the financial situation you want for yourself. In terms of relationships, The Magician suggests that you have the power to create the relationship you want. If you can focus your energy and take inspired action, you can create the kind of relationship you desire.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

In terms of health, The 7 of Wands suggests that you may need to defend yourself against illness or competition. This may be a time when you need to be more vigilant about your health and take steps to protect yourself. This may also be a time when you need to be more mindful of your finances and relationships. In terms of finances, The 7 of Wands suggests that you may need to protect your assets and income. This may be a time when you need to be more cautious about spending and investing. In terms of relationships, The 7 of Wands suggests that you may need to defend your boundaries. This may be a time when you need to be more assertive about your needs and wants.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The Magician and 7 Of Wands has helped you.
