A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The 8 Of Wands tarot card and The 9 Of Cups tarot card together point to good news arriving quickly and bringing on a period of rapid change in your life. This change will bring with it a feeling of abundance and contentment as your wishes will begin to come true. You can expect a period of good health and a higher level of satisfaction as everything starts to come together in the way you have envisioned. Now is the time to be open to the changes that are coming and to embrace them with enthusiasm.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 8 of Wands Tarot card signifies the speed of the changes and progress that can be made in your health. This card suggests that you can make progress with your health quickly and can be beneficial for long-term health if you take timely, positive action. It encourages you to take the necessary steps towards improving your health and wellness. It advises you to take decisive action and not be scared of changes that you need to make.

Finances  
The 8 of Wands Tarot card reminds you to make sound financial decisions and to be mindful of your expenditures. When this card falls, it is a sign that you may have to make changes to your spending habits in order to gain more financial stability. This card can also signify good news when it comes to financial matters, such as a job offer or an increase in pay. It signifies that you should be open to new opportunities and be ready to take action.

Relationships  
The 8 of Wands Tarot card can be representative of quick changes in relationships. It signifies that there is a need to take decisive action in order to maintain and improve relationships. This card encourages communication and encourages you to be honest and open with your partner. It reminds you that relationships can benefit from change, but it is important to be willing to adapt and go with the flow when it comes to making changes. It is also an indication that news may arrive in regards to the relationship, so be sure to pay attention to subtle clues from your partner.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot Card is often seen as a card of wishes coming true. It symbolizes material abundance, good health, and general contentment. Health wise, this card promises that any health issues will be resolved or healed in a positive and timely manner. It represents a healthy and balanced state of being, and is an encouragement to pay attention to one’s physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual needs.

Financially, The 9 of Cups Tarot Card foretells unexpected wealth and abundant success. Money will likely come from many different sources, with little effort. It encourages good money management and offers reassurance that good fortune and abundance will come into your life.

Relationships wise, The 9 of Cups Tarot Card brings joy and contentment. This card symbolizes strong, harmonious relationships that are both fulfilling and lasting. It is a sign that one’s current relationships will continue to bring happiness and satisfaction. It can also refer to new relationships, which can bring lasting joy and happiness into one’s life.

The 9 of Cups Tarot Card represents a time of contentment, joy, abundance, and satisfaction. Through health, finances, and relationships the 9 of Cups brings the promise of joy and fulfillment.
