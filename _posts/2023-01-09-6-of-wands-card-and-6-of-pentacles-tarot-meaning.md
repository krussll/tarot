However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 6 Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 6 Of Wands and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot cards together signify a time of victory and success, coupled with generosity and help. This could be a time of triumph in your life, where you have achieved some great success, perhaps from hard work and dedication. This success may bring you recognition and perhaps even an award or prize. You may also be in a position to offer help to those in need, either by way of financial aid, advice, or other gifts. You should be able to feel very proud of your accomplishments now, and that feeling of achievement should be balanced with the generosity that you can give to others.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

For Health:  
The 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the victory and the victory of health over sickness. It’s a card that speaks to the healing process, as it indicates that a person is on the road to recovery from an illness or injury. In terms of physical health, the 6 of Wands can indicate that a long-term health issue is likely to be resolved, resulting in a period of improved health. This card can also indicate a return to overall wellness, such as the healing of physical, spiritual, and emotional issues.

For Finances:  
The 6 of Wands Tarot card denotes a victory in terms of finances. The card implies that one has achieved a certain success in terms of financial matters. It can show that a person has overcome financial difficulties or has achieved a financial goal. This card indicates that past sacrifices and hard work have resulted in a period of abundance and prosperity. This card can signify a successful financial investment or a well-chosen business venture.

For Relationships:  
The 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the victory and the victory of relationships over conflict. It speaks to an improved relationship, where communication has improved and both parties are working together for the benefit of the relationship. It conveys a sense of harmony and amicable resolution, suggesting that relationships have been mended. This card can also show that a person has achieved some sort of acceptance or recognition from others, whether it be in the form of a promotion or recognition in a social setting. The 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the rebuilding of connections and improved understanding.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes health as sharing and exchanging resources and knowledge. This card is a reminder to recognize our limitations, and to seek help or advice when needed. It suggests that it’s ok to ask for and receive help in times of physical distress or illness. Asking for assistance is not a sign of weakness but instead an opportunity to grow and heal. It is essential to rely on the support of family, friends, and professionals when managing one’s health.

Finances: The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests there is a need to be generous in sharing and earning resources. It implies that there is an imbalance of power and resources in the world and the card suggests the need for giving and taking to balance the scales of wealth. It can also be a reminder to not just share our money, but also to share our time, effort, and energy.

Relationships: The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card points towards the importance of relationships, because when it comes to success and wealth, we are only as strong as our connections. It encourages us to show generosity and compassion in our relationships, while also reminding us that we don’t have to always give to others one-sidedly. Instead it suggests that it’s important to also receive what we give, as there needs to be a balance between giving and taking. The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card can also stand as a reminder to be mindful of the dynamic between you and your partner, as this is an essential aspect to a healthy and successful relationship.
