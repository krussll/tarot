A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 6 Of Wands card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 6 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The 5 of Wands and the 6 of Wands tarot cards represent the cycles of struggle and victory in life. The 5 of Wands indicates that we are in a period of rivalry, challenges, obstacles, and sport. We may be in a situation where we feel pressure to perform, a fight for power, or a competition for recognition. We must work hard, put in the effort, and show our prowess to get ahead. The 6 of Wands then reveals that our efforts have not been in vain and that we have emerged successful. We will be rewarded with victory, recognition, and good news. Through diligence and hard work, we have achieved success and can take pride in our accomplishments.

If a couple is competitive with each other in their love life, the fire of their love could be extinguished by the heat of their competing egos. This could lead to a lot of stress, arguments and difficulty in communication.

On the other hand, the Six of Wands speaks of a time of victory and success in love. If a couple has over come the obstacles and rivalries within their relationship, then they will have been rewarded with a time of peace and harmony. The recognition and success that come from this time of peace is an indication that the couple have put a lot of effort into their relationship and have been rewarded for it. With the Six of Wands present in a reading, it is a reminder that the love between two people is worth working for and that a relationship can endure even when faced with the toughest of trials.

When the Five of Wands and Six of Wands are seen together in a reading, they indicate that a relationship has been through many trials and tribulations, but with hard work and determination, eventual success will be achieved. This is a reminder to stay strong and try to keep the love alive, for it is only through effort that true and lasting love can be achieved.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

The 5 of Wands Tarot card represents hustle, competition and obstacles in all aspects of life, including health, finances, and relationships.

When it comes to health, the 5 of Wands suggests that there is a need to stay in peak physical condition. There may be inner or outer battles to overcome, and the card predicts that you or your loved ones will have to work hard to stay healthy. Whether it be diet and exercise, or going to the doctor, good health requires a constant level of effort.

When it comes to finances, the 5 of Wands indicates that you will be facing challenges and competition. Money may be tight and it will take effort to get ahead. You may have to juggle multiple jobs or market yourself in order to bring in the income you need. Gambling should also be avoided, as this card suggests a higher degree of luck and risk.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 5 of Wands suggests that it may be difficult to maintain. You may have to work hard to make sure that a relationship is strong, as there is a tendency to become complacent. There will likely be moments of tension and competition; it’s your job to navigate them with grace. The card encourages you to cultivate a shared understanding and be open to compromise.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

For Health:

The 6 Of Wands Tarot card signifies that now is the time to honor your commitment to yourself and your health. This card indicates that you are on the right track towards feeling healthier and stronger. Health is best served when taken into account with all the other aspects of one’s life, such as finances, relationships, and professional life. This card encourages you to take a look at how integrating these different aspects of your life can help support your health journey. It may be time to make changes in your diet, exercise habits, and lifestyle that will create a harmonious balance between taking care of both your body and mind.

For Finances:

The 6 Of Wands Tarot card signifies that now is the time to reap the rewards of your hard work. This card is a reminder that your financial situation is improving and you are on the path to success. You may have had to make difficult decisions or take risks that have now paid off. This card encourages you to rely on your intuition when it comes to money. You may find new opportunities on the horizon that can help you continue to grow and increase your wealth.

For Relationships:

The 6 Of Wands Tarot card signifies that relationships are a source of great joy and satisfaction right now. This card indicates that relationships can bring recognition, accomplishment, and success into your life. It is likely that you are now surrounded by supportive people who will be there for you when you need them. This card encourages you to celebrate and be proud of the connections you have made. Taking the time to appreciate your relationships will bring you even more joy.
