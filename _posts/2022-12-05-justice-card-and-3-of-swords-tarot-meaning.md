A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Justice card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card together represent a difficult time in life, full of loss, sadness, and upheaval. It is a time of facing the truth, no matter how hard it may be, and coming to terms with difficult reality. What comes out of this situation, however, is a chance to use the newfound truth to create win-win solutions and to heal the damaged heart. It is an opportunity to come away with a deeper understanding of the cause and effect of our choices, and a chance to make better ones in the future. It is a chance to bring justice to a situation, even if it brings with it pain and heartbreak.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This can be difficult and even painful, but it is the only way to come to a place of understanding and peace. If a relationship is no longer serving its highest purpose or isn’t working out, this card suggests it is time to move on. The 3 of Swords may indicate that it is time to go through a process of grief and letting go in order to be renewed.

This card combination can also indicate that, if your love life is currently in an unhealthy state, then it is time to make changes. The Justice card is a reminder that the truth must be embraced and healing must happen before love is possible. It can be hard to be honest with yourself and with your partner, but it is often the only way to create a healthier, happier and more loving relationship.

By embracing the messages of these two cards, you can use this knowledge to create true and lasting love in your life. Facing the truth of your current situation and making the changes you need to make will ultimately bring you peace, and move you towards a better and brighter tomorrow.

It may also suggest that one needs to confront the pain caused by past financial decisions, face the truth, and take steps to make reparations in order to create a balanced situation. It may also serve as a reminder that life is cyclical, and that for every loss, there may be an opportunity for gain if we look for one. In any case, both cards represent a need for a truth-facing and fair approach to one’s finances.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 3 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 3 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice tarot card represents the importance of looking after your health in order to gain balance and harmony. It encourages you to take responsibility for your own well-being, and to make decisions that will bring about a healthier and more satisfying way of life. This card suggests that by being mindful of your lifestyle choices, you can ensure that any decisions you take will have positive long term benefits for your overall health. You may need to take a step back to make sure that your decisions are for the best, and that any short term gains don’t come at the cost of your long term health.

Finances: The Justice tarot card is associated with matters of finance, and highlights the importance of making decisions that are fair and just. It suggests that you strive to achieve a win-win situation whenever possible, where both sides come out of a financial agreement in better shape than when they started. This card encourages you to bring a sense of balance and fairness to the table, so that all sides gain from the outcome. It also warns against taking advantage of someone else’s situation for your own gain, as this will ultimately result in karma coming back around to you.

Relationships: The Justice tarot card reflects the need for fairness and balance in all relationships, both personal and professional. It suggests that compromise is essential in order to ensure that both parties get what they need from the relationship. It encourages you to ensure that both sides are heard, and that any decisions are taken with mutual respect. This card also warns against speaking falsely or telling lies, as the truth will always come out in the end. It encourages you to strive for justice amongst your relationships, both with others and with yourself.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The Three of Swords Tarot card is a card of deep sorrow and pain. It often represents a time of loss, heartache, and grief. In terms of physical health, this card can suggest surgery or other medical treatments that may involve loss or discomfort. In terms of finances, one could be facing financial loss either in terms of job loss or financial difficulties due to an unexpected event or fallout from a relationship. In terms of relationships, the Three of Swords Tarot card can signify the breakdown of a relationship, divorce, or the end of a love affair. This card can also be a warning not to be too trusting of another, as even well-intentioned people can cause deep pain and sorrow.

The Three of Swords Tarot card can also indicate a time for healing and self-reflection. This card can suggest taking time to process what has happened and to heal from it. It can be a time to reassess one’s beliefs and values in terms of relationships and to take time to sort out how best to move forward. In terms of physical health, it can represent a time to take care of oneself with good nutrition and to focus on healthy habits like exercise.

In terms of finances, the Three of Swords Tarot card can be an indication of a need to make changes to spend more wisely and to conserve resources. It can also be a reminder to look at long-term goals and dreams and to devise plans to achieve them.

The Three of Swords Tarot card can signify a very painful period, but also a period of transformation and growth. It can remind one to be compassionate with oneself and to allow for healing to take place. It can also be an indication of the need to make changes in order to ensure more stability and peace.
