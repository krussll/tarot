A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and The Star card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and The Star have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and The Star mean together?

The Hermit and The Star tarot card together can mean a journey of introspection and reflection, allowing space for spiritual growth and personal revelation. This is a time to look within and find the clarity, hope and faith you need to trust your intuition and move forward. As you go within to gain knowledge and understanding, like the Hermit, you must also extend your reach to the outer world with the help of The Star and be confident that the universe is supporting you in your journey. This is a time to focus on your inner truth and find the promise of miracles that lies within.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

With these tarot cards in mind, you can take the time you need for yourself and then come back renewed, ready for the great possibilities that await you in your love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and The Star mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card serves as a reminder that physical health is deeply connected to one’s spiritual health. The Hermit card encourages one to go within to find and nurture their holistic health. It suggests that physical health and spiritual health are intertwined, and in order to improve physical health, one should take the time to focus on their spiritual needs. This includes setting aside time for self-reflection, meditation, and spiritual practices, such as yoga and prayer. Taking the time to deeply connect with one’s spiritual self through these practices can help to give the body and mind the rest, balance, and inner peace that it needs in order to stay healthy.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card can also provide guidance and clarity when it comes to finances. It reminds us to take a step back and assess one’s financial goals and priorities. It suggests that one should take the time to go within and reflect on their current financial situation, and what changes need to be implemented in order to reach their financial goals. It is also a reminder to be mindful of our spending, to avoid overspending and make sure that our money is being spent in wise and meaningful ways.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card is also a reminder to take some time to focus on our relationships. It encourages one to go within and assess which relationships are healthy, and which may need to be re-evaluated or let go of. The Hermit card suggests that it is important to take the time to get to know oneself, and to make sure that one’s own needs are met before getting into any relationships. It is also a reminder to be mindful of how one interacts with others; to make sure that there is a balance between giving and receiving in all relationships.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot Card has plenty to offer for each of the three areas of our lives: health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Star card is a sign of renewal, reminding us of our ability to heal and recover. It is a message to take the time to nurture and care for ourselves, and to look after our physical and mental wellbeing. The card is a reminder that we are resilient, and no matter how hard things might get, we have the power to heal. It inspires us to seek out the guidance of professionals or supportive friends and family to see us through tough times.

When it comes to finances, The Star Tarot is a reminder to put our trust in the universe and to believe that abundance is coming our way. It encourages us to be generous and not to hoard money, because these actions will create more wealth in our lives. The Star is a sign of hope, and encourages us to take risks and make bold investments, should we feel the urge.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Star Tarot card is a reminder to have faith and trust in each other, even during the tough times. It encourages us to look for ways of healing and restoring broken relationships. It is a sign of hope that even in challenging times, we can overcome our differences and find common ground. The Star also helps us to have the courage to enter new relationships, and to share our dreams and goals with others, in order to create something beautiful together.

All in all, The Star Tarot card is a reminder to trust in the transformation and renewal that is available to us, no matter what the current circumstances or challenges may be. It encourages us to have faith in ourselves, and to nurture our relationships and our dreams, no matter how small or distant they may seem. The Star is a symbol of miracles, and shows that with open hearts and minds, anything is possible.
