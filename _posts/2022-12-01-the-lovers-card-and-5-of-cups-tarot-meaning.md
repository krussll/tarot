A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 5 Of Cups mean together?

When The Lovers tarot card and The 5 Of Cups card appear together, it can represent a difficult decision between two potential partners. There is a sense of loss and regret, because no matter which partner you choose, you are still leaving the other behind. This can indicate a time of deep sorrow, but also a time of learning the strength of two being together. It is possible to create something beautiful out of the sorrow and regret, if you keep an eye on the future and the potential of a new partnership.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Lovers and 5 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The Lovers tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card both appear together in a reading about love, it suggests that you may be struggling to find balance in your current relationship. The Lovers tarot card is a strong indication that partnership and genuine love are available to you, but the 5 Of Cups indicates that you may have previously suffered some kind of loss or abandonment in your love life that is causing you to hesitate in fully trusting and committing to your current relationship.

The presence of the 5 Of Cups may indicate that the fear of being hurt again is governing your interactions and decisions. It’s important to recognize this fear and to be realistic about the fact that no relationship will be perfect and free from any kind of loss or suffering. The Lovers tarot card is a sign that if you’re willing to take a leap of faith and trust in yourself and your partner, your connection and love have the potential to be very strong.

This combination of cards suggests that it is time to look to the future and focus on forging a fulfilling and meaningful partnership. To do this, it’s important to be honest with yourself and your partner about past hurts and anxieties. With honesty, courage, and a commitment to growth, the two of you can work through any issues and build a strong, loving bond that can sustain you both in the long run.

### What does The Lovers and 5 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

For your finances, the pairing of The Lovers tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card could indicate that many of your financial decisions have been driven by an emotional attachment. While this can mean that some of your choices have been the right ones, it has also likely led to some costly mistakes. Your emotional attachment could also mean that any losses that you suffer financially feel much more devastating than they likely are. The card pairing could thus be urging you to make more objective decisions when it comes to managing your money.

For your job, the card pairing could be indicative of a difficult work situation. To some degree, the emotional attachment to certain aspects of your job might be preventing you from seeing what is truly best for you in the long run. It could also suggest that you may have to make a difficult choice in order to fully achieve your career goals. Your job could also be contributing to feelings of loss or regret, as you may not have been able to achieve the level of success or satisfaction that you hoped for. The card pairing could thus be encouraging you to take a step back and objectively assess the situation in order to make the best decision for yourself and your career.

### What does The Lovers and 5 Of Cups mean together for your health?

The Lovers tarot card and the 5 Of Cups tarot card together represent the push-pull of emotions in health. This combination encourages balance between the ups and downs of life, the ability to make positive choices, and the power of partnership.

The Lovers card emphasizes the importance of connection and encourages making conscious decisions that reflect both the needs of oneself and one’s partner. In health, this can refer to the practice of self-care and communication with one’s partner when it comes to health decisions. It is vital to take responsibility for one’s wellbeing, and for a couple, this can mean sharing habits and goals that help both individuals stay healthy.

The 5 Of Cups tarot card speaks of sadness and loss, but it is also an invitation to personal growth. It can be a reminder of the importance of mourning losses, accepting that change is a part of life, and learning to move on more gracefully.

Therefore, the Lovers tarot card and the 5 Of Cups tarot card together signify that when it comes to health, one should take responsibility for one’s wellbeing, practice self-care, communicate with one’s partner, and learn to be resilient in the face of obstacles. This combination encourages individuals to practice balance and harness the power of partnership to build and maintain health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and 5 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health

The Lovers Tarot card can represent partnership when it comes to health. This implies that two individuals sharing their journey together can create a strong and transformative bond. This card encourages individuals to find a partner to help them cultivate a healthy lifestyle. This could include a friend, family member, spouse, or even a coach or mentor. Sharing the experience of wellness with another person can provide the support and motivation needed to achieve one’s goals. Additionally, learning to nurture and take care of oneself through self-love can be just as powerful.

Finance

The Lovers Tarot card can suggest a partnership in finances. This does not necessarily imply that two individuals have to have a joint bank account, but rather it shows the strength of two individuals working together towards a common goal. It could also mean that there is a need for trust, security, and support in financial decisions. This could be between two partners, or between an individual and a financial professional.

Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card implies deep love, mutual respect, and companionship. This card stands for whatever kind of relationship that two individuals might be in, be it romantic, platonic, or otherwise. It suggests that a strong bond and connection can be created when two individuals work together in a relationship. The card also speaks of a choice – that whatever kind of relationship one desires is possible when someone is willing to take a chance and put in the effort to build a relationship.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 5 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a sense of loss in terms of health. It could represent a chronic condition or illness that has you feeling helpless and out of control. This card often appears when someone is feeling emotionally drained, drained due to the physical strain of illness. It could also signify a feeling of guilt or shame associated with the health issues and how they are affecting your life.

Finances:

The 5 of Cups Tarot card in terms of money can represent a loss or decrease in money or assets. It can represent a financial setback due to unexpected expenses. This card can often indicate a situation in which it was not possible to anticipate the financial strain or loss of income. It can also symbolize a time when it feels impossible to recover from a financial misstep or setback.

Relationships:

The 5 of Cups Tarot card in relationships symbolizes a sense of abandonment or feeling unloved or unappreciated. This can often be a result of a broken relationship or a sense of betrayal. This card can signify a time in which it feels impossible to heal the wounds of a past relationship that is no longer and the person feels an emptiness. It could also represent a feeling of loneliness or despair in terms of not being able to find a connection or someone to care for them.
