A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and 3 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The World card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and 3 Of Wands mean together?

The World tarot card and The 3 Of Wands tarot card together can mean that you are completing a goal or challenge at the same time as exploring new opportunities. This could mean learning a new skill, enrolling in a course, travelling to a new country, or taking part in a new project. There is a sense of accomplishment, as well as a sense of working together with colleagues, business partners, or peers to expand your knowledge and reach a new level of expertise. You may find yourself on a journey of self-discovery and following your own dreams, while also learning to function in a wider business environment.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The World tarot card can also signify a deep, complete connection with another person that is just waiting to be discovered. The 3 Of Wands can represent teamwork, suggesting that if you are in a relationship, you may need to work together and collaborate in order to have the relationship that you desire. It can also mean that you may have to cooperate with others in order to bring the love you seek into your life, and that you may have to rely on your social circle or friends in order to find your romantic partner.

In summary, the World tarot card and the 3 Of Wands tarot card together in your love life could be a sign that you are ready to expand and find your true love. It could mean that you are ready for travel, adventure, and new experiences that can bring about more love and passion in your life. It could also suggest that now is the time to work together and cooperate with others, in order to open yourself up to the possibility of deeper and lasting connection.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

The World Tarot card is a symbol of an all-encompassing conclusion and represents great success in whatever one has set out to achieve.When interpreted in the context of health, finances, and relationships, this card can be seen as an indication of the successful completion of a current journey in any of these areas.

In regards to health, The World Tarot card indicates that one has now achieved a healthy balance or equilibrium in their life. This could be the successful conclusion of a healing process or a full recovery from an illness. It is also a reminder to take the necessary steps to maintain your physical and mental wellbeing.

When interpreted in terms of finances, The World Tarot card is an indication that a financial goal has been reached or a financial situation is now stabilized. This could mean the successful completion of a debt repayment plan, a change in career or the achievement of financial goals.

As far as relationships are concerned, The World Tarot card indicates the successful completion of a lesson or goal in this life area. It could be a successful ending to a difficult period in a romantic relationship or the successful conclusion of a friendship. It is also a reminder to appreciate the relationships in your life and maintain a sense of balance and harmony.

Overall, when The World Tarot card is seen in any reading, it symbolizes the successful completion of a journey, the achievement of a goal or lesson, and the reminder to maintain a sense of balance and harmony in all areas of life.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

The 3 of Wands tarot card is associated with health, finances, and relationships. It is a card of exploration, a challenge of the current state, and a call for growth and evolution. In terms of health, the card suggests that one should take the time to explore and understand their body and its needs. This can mean anything from eating better, to taking up a new exercise regimen, to taking time off to rest. It is important to maintain a healthy balance and not become too caught up in any one pursuit.

In terms of finances, the 3 of Wands tarot card implies that collaboration and teamwork are the paths to achieving success. Taking on new business partners, and working with them to develop a solid future plan are great steps towards financial security. The card emphasizes the need for an open mind and flexibility.

Finally, in regards to relationships, the 3 of Wands tarot card suggests that one should take the time and effort to explore each of the relationships they are a part of. This includes romantic relationships, family relationships, and friendships. One should take the time to learn about the motives and needs of their partner, and strive for mutual understanding and respect. Growth and understanding of each other should be the focus of any relationship the card indicates. This can help to strengthen connections and foster a deeper and more meaningful connection. With this in mind, the 3 of Wands tarot card is a great card to turn to when looking for health, financial and relationship guidance!
