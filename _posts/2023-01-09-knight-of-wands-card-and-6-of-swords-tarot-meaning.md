A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Knight Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Knight Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

Your free spirit is being called upon to make a bold, but necessary, change. You may need to move to a new location or perhaps just a new way of thinking to make the most of your talents. Let your creativity flow and use the wisdom of your current circumstances to make the best use of what you have. This is a time for exploration, discovery, and liberation. Take a chance and make something beautiful out of the transition.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Knight Of Wands mean?

Health: The Knight of Wands Tarot card signifies a person’s physical and mental vitality. It suggests a general healthiness and vibrancy, as well as a sense of ambition and drive. In terms of physical health, this card suggests staying active and inspired to stay in shape and maintaining a good diet. It encourages taking risks, pushing yourself to your limit, and trying different things to stay healthy.

Finances: The Knight of Wands Tarot card symbolizes financial success. It encourages taking risks with investments, starting a business, and not being afraid to explore new opportunities. It suggests staying focused on a aim and having the courage and confidence to pursue it. It is also a reminder to make sure your finances are in order and to prepare for the future.

Relationships: The Knight of Wands Tarot card symbolizes passionate, fiery relationships. It encourages taking risks in relationships, being adventurous and exploring new avenues. It also suggests having open and honest conversations and keeping a sense of independence and self-confidence in all relationships. It encourages you to take initiative and follow your heart in shared endeavors.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a necessary transition in health. This transition could be anything that helps one to achieve better physical or mental health, such as necessary changes or treatments that may need to be made. This card reminds us that, although these changes may be difficult, eventually the results will be positive, and the individual involved will be better off in the long run. This card symbolizes taking action to improve the health and wellbeing of ourselves and others, through diet, exercise and lifestyle changes.

Finances  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a necessary transition in finances. This transition may involve changing one’s job, investments or debt. It is a reminder that although these changes may be difficult and stressful, it can lead to a better financial future in the end. Taking proactive steps towards improving one’s finances, such as budgeting and saving, can help one to achieve financial freedom and security.

Relationships  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a necessary transition in relationships. This transition may involve changes in the way one interacts with others, or even steps to end a relationship if needed. This card encourages one to take the necessary steps to find healing and closure, as well as allowing time to focus on oneself and reflect on what they want and need in a relationship. It encourages one to have difficult conversations and make changes that are beneficial for both parties.
