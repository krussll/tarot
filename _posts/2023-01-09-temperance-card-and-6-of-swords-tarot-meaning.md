A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Temperance card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The 6 Of Swords together represent a necessary transition or relocation which requires balance, harmony, patience, and self-control. Moving from one place to another can be both exciting and intimidating. This transition requires you to be mindful of your emotional energy, being disciplined in order to preserve your peace of mind throughout the process. Both cards invite you to find the right balance between stability and progress, allowing the transition to occur in an orderly manner.

The 6 of Swords card can be seen as an indication of change or transition. In conclusion, when looking at the two cards together, The Temperance and The 6 Of Swords can be seen as a reminder of the importance of balance, self-control, and patience in any relationships, especially when going through periods of transition. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

For Health:  
The Temperance Tarot card signifies that good health is a balance of physical, mental, and emotional health. It encourages individuals to practice self-care, and to make decisions that promote health and wellbeing, like exercising regularly, getting enough sleep, and eating a balanced diet. It suggests that individuals take the necessary steps to achieve and maintain optimal health, and to be patient with their own progress.

For Finances:  
The Temperance Tarot card highlights the importance of balance and moderation when it comes to finances. It advises individuals to practice financial self-control, and to be wise with their money. It encourages them to plan ahead and to act responsibly when it comes to budgeting, saving, and spending. It also suggests that individuals should think before they buy, and consider the long-term financial implications of any purchase.

For Relationships:  
The Temperance Tarot card encourages individuals to strive for harmony and balance in their relationships. It emphasizes the importance of open communication and respect between partners, as well as being patient and understanding with each other. It suggests that individuals remain committed to resolving conflicts, and to negotiate compromises that are agreeable to both parties. It also encourages individuals to recognize when it may be necessary to take a step back and give each other space, in order to maintain a healthy, balanced relationship.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 6 of Swords Tarot Card is about making a necessary transition for better physical and mental health. It can signify taking the steps towards committing to a healthier lifestyle, such as eating a balanced diet, exercising regularly, getting enough rest, and engaging in healing activities. It can also represent an emotional transition such as working through a traumatic event or dealing with feelings of anxiety or depression. Taking steps to protect your emotional and physical health is necessary for overall well-being.

Finances: The 6 of Swords Tarot Card is about making a necessary transition to improve a person’s financial situation. It can be about starting a new job, taking steps to reduce debt, or putting yourself back in control of your money. Making a plan and taking action to gain financial freedom can be incredibly empowering. By leaving the “boat” of debt, fear, and lack and transitioning to a more stable financial situation, you can help secure a more secure future.

Relationships: The 6 of Swords Tarot Card indicates there is a need for a transition in relationships in order to progress in a positive direction. This could mean leaving a toxic relationship, such as an abusive partner, or it could mean finding a more harmonious way to relate to those around. It could also mean healing from past hurt or addressing communication issues. If a relationship is no longer serving you, taking the steps to transition to a better one is essential for emotional, mental and physical wellbeing.
