A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 Of Swords tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together signify that you may be starting a new chapter in your life, and relocating to a new area that will bring you closer to your friends and circle of support. You may need to make an important transition to reach this new destination, but the 3 of Cups tarot card assures that when you do, you will be surrounded by a vibrant, loving community. It’s a time of celebration, so embrace the adventure and find joy in your new home.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 6 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 6 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 6 Of Swords Tarot card signifies a necessary transition in health. This could mean starting a new diet and exercise plan, seeking out alternative treatments, or changing your lifestyle in some way. It is important to recognize that this transition is necessary in order to improve your overall health. This could also mean transitioning away from unhealthy habits and practices, such as smoking or drug use. Change can be difficult, but the rewards can be great if you are willing to make the effort and commitment to make the necessary changes.

Finances:  
The 6 Of Swords Tarot card signifies a necessary transition in finances. This could mean taking on a second job in order to get back on track financially, or making an effort to save more money each month. It could also mean taking a risk and branching out from your comfort zone in order to generate new streams of income. Transitioning away from bad financial habits, such as excessive spending or gambling, is also important for financial security. Making the commitment to make these necessary changes can have the potential to greatly improve your financial situation.

Relationships:  
The 6 Of Swords Tarot card signifies a necessary transition in relationships. This could mean taking a step back to reassess your current relationships, as well as exploring different types of relationships. It could also mean transitioning away from toxic or unhealthy relationships in order to create more healthy and fulfilling ones. It is important to recognize that making these changes can be difficult, but the rewards can be great. Making the effort to make these necessary changes can help to strengthen your existing relationships and open the door to new ones.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a card of celebration and joy. It can be indicative of good health and physical well-being, typically when people are surrounded by supportive friends and family. The card can represent a time of healing and recovery when a person is supported through difficult times by their loved ones. Even if a person is not feeling well, they can find solace in knowing they have the backing of a strong support system. It is important to reach out to friends and family and make sure they know they are appreciated and valued.

Finances:

The 3 of Cups Tarot card can often be associated with financial stability or success. It can be indicative of the rewards that come with hard work and dedication to achieving financial independence. The card can also represent a time of abundance and prosperity. A strong support system can help a person achieve financial success, as friends and family can provide advice, promotion, and networking opportunities. However, the card can also symbolize carelessness or overspending if not managed carefully.

Relationships:

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a card of strong relationships, both platonic and romantic. It can represent a close-knit circle of friends and family, with an emphasis on the importance of quality time with one another. The card can represent a time of joy, celebration, and shared experiences. It can also be considered a love triangle due to its three cups, often symbolizing mutual respect and appreciation between two people with the third cup being a symbol of the bond that connects them. The card can be indicative of a successful relationship, though it’s important to remember that relationships still require effort and dedication in order to thrive.
