Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Temperance card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Temperance, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Temperance.

## Temperance And Love – An Overview

The Temperance tarot card is a reminder of the importance of balance and harmony in life, particularly in regards to health.

It encourages us to practice patience and self-control when it comes to our physical and emotional wellbeing.

It is a sign that we should take the time to nurture ourselves, make healthy decisions, and focus on creating a lifestyle that is in line with our values.

Taking care of ourselves is not always easy, but The Temperance card encourages us to focus on moderation and not to be too extreme in our efforts.

With balance and harmony, we can create a lifestyle that is fulfilling and healthy, both mentally and physically.

## Upright Meanings For Temperance And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Temperance tarot card is all about balance and harmony in relationships.

It speaks of the need to be patient with the person you love and to practice self-control.

Relationships are a delicate balance of give and take and Temperance encourages us to practice our own balance in order to maintain the harmony in our relationship.

It also encourages us to take the time to understand our partner’s needs and to be willing to compromise.

Temperance is a reminder that love is a two-way street and that when it is balanced and harmonious, it can bring us a great deal of joy and peace.

### What It Means For The Future

The Temperance tarot card suggests that your future love life will be based on balance, harmony, patience, and self-control.

This suggests that you will choose your partner carefully and take the time to understand their needs and feelings, as well as your own.

You will strive for balance in your relationship, understanding that you both need to give and take.

You will also have the patience to work through problems and disagreements and have the self-control to not let your emotions cloud your judgement when it comes to making decisions.

With this in mind, you are sure to find a lasting and fulfilling relationship.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Temperance card is a reminder of the importance of balance and harmony in our relationships with those close to us.

It encourages patience and self-control in order to maintain healthy and strong relationships.

It cautions us to practice moderation in our interactions with our loved ones, to be mindful of our words, and to be aware of our own emotions.

The temperance tarot card reminds us that in order to experience true love, we must strive to achieve balance in all our relationships.

It emphasizes the need to create an environment of understanding and respect, where we can be honest and open with each other, and where we can trust and rely on each other.

## Reversed Meanings For Temperance And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The Temperance card appears reversed in a reading about love, it can be a sign of imbalanced relationships.

This could be a sign that one partner is giving more than the other, or that one partner is trying to control the other.

In either case, it indicates that the relationship is unbalanced and needs some work.

The reversed Temperance card can also indicate a lack of patience or self-control in a relationship.

If one partner is getting too overbearing or demanding, or if one partner is too willing to forgive and forget, this could lead to a lack of harmony in the relationship.

Overall, the reversed Temperance card suggests that there is a need to restore balance and harmony in a relationship.

It can indicate that one partner needs to regain patience and self-control, or that both partners need to make more effort to communicate and understand each other’s needs and desires.

By doing this, it is possible to restore balance and bring back the harmony that was once present in the relationship.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Temperance card is reversed, it can be an indication of possible discord in your future love life.

It can suggest that you may be too impatient, and that you could be prone to making decisions too quickly, without considering the consequences that may arise.

You may also be too willing to give in to temptations, which could lead to heartache and emotional turmoil.

It can be a sign that you need to take time to consider all aspects of a situation before making any decisions.

The reversed Temperance card can also point to a lack of balance in your relationships.

You may be too focused on yourself, and not take the time to listen to your partner or loved one.

It can be a warning that you need to be more aware of the needs of your partner, and to be willing to make compromises to keep the relationship healthy.

It can also be a sign that you need to practice self-control, and learn to be more patient in order to ensure that your relationships are strong and lasting.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Temperance tarot card is reversed, it can indicate a disruption of balance and harmony in the loves of those close to me.

This can mean that my loved ones are out of sync with one another, and tensions may be running high.

It could also mean that my loved ones are struggling to communicate their feelings and emotions clearly, leading to further misunderstandings and disagreements.

Reversed Temperance can also mean that my loved ones are having difficulty finding patience and self-control in the face of difficult situations.

If one of my loved ones is feeling overwhelmed and stressed, they may be reacting in ways that are causing more conflict.

This disruption of harmony could lead to a lack of trust, increased distance, and even a breakdown of the relationship if it isn’t addressed.

Ultimately, reversed Temperance suggests that my loved ones need to take a step back and re-evaluate their relationship and communication.

They need to make an effort to create balance, understanding, and patience between them in order to get back on the same page and build a stronger relationship.
