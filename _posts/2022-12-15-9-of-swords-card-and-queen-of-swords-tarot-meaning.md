The Queen of Swords confront this situation with total honesty and a strong self-sufficiency allowing her to confront her fears head on and to cope with them. It is a reminder that we all have an inner strength and resilience that can help us through any difficult situation.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 9 of Swords card is often a reminder for physical and mental health. On a physical level, it can suggest a need for rest, relaxation, and detoxifying from unhealthy substances. On a mental level, it can be a reminder to break out of patterns of negative thinking, and to find ways to better manage stress and anxiety. For those who are struggling with mental health issues, the 9 of Swords can be a warning that counseling or therapy is needed to help break out of unhealthy patterns of thinking.

Finances

The 9 of Swords Tarot card suggests financial stagnancy or lack of financial growth. It can signify being stuck in a cycle of making bad money decisions, or not having the funds to make necessary changes. It can be a warning to practice mindful spending and to create a budget to prevent overspending. Additionally, it can suggest a need to reassess current financial habits and find ways to shift toward wiser financial decisions.

Relationships

The 9 of Swords Tarot card suggests caution and the need for inner strength. It can be a reminder to take a step back and assess relationships in order to see if they are truly healthy. It can suggest fear-based relationships or behavior, such as over-dependence, jealousy, or codependency. In order to move forward, it’s important to be honest and brave enough to create positive boundaries in order to improve relationships. It can also remind someone to look within and heal any inner issues that may be sabotaging relationships with others.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health – The Queen of Swords card can suggest a period of self-healing. This Queen symbolizes the need to be true to oneself in order to heal and grow. This can mean listening to the needs of your body and giving yourself the rest and nutrition that you need. She also encourages you to be honest with yourself and acknowledge any issues that are getting in the way of ideal health.

Finances – The Queen of Swords symbolizes the need to be levelheaded when it comes to money. Her direct demeanor indicates that you should be honest and realistic about your financial situation. The Queen also encourages you to take a disciplined approach to financial decisions and to not give in to impulse buying.

Relationships – The Queen of Swords card implies a desire for total transparency and truth in relationships. This doesn’t mean you should go around revealing every detail of your life to everyone, but more that you should be honest about your feelings, intentions, and expectations. It can also suggest that you need to stand up for yourself and not let others take advantage of you. The Queen of Swords wants you to know that it’s ok to be yourself and be honest in your interactions with others.
