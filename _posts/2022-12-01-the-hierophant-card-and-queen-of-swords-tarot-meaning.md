## What does The Hierophant and Queen Of Swords mean together?

This can sometimes be a difficult balance to strike, as you may feel pressure to conform to the expectations of others, while your desire for honest self-expression and autonomy remain strong. However, by recognizing both elements, you can achieve a productive balance that is beneficial for yourself and those around you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and Queen Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and Queen Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

The Hierophant Tarot card points to traditions and conventional expectations when it comes to health. This could be in the form of abiding by the advice and protocols of medical professionals, or going with time-tested natural remedies rather than experimental treatments. What this card suggests is that you should adhere to tried-and-true methods, which have been tried and proven over time and have stood the test of time. It is a reminder to be wise in making decisions related to health and not to be too daring in trying the latest trend or miracle cure. Traditional methods such as eating a balanced diet, getting regular exercise, and getting enough rest are essential for maintaining good health.

It is a reminder to be prudent and wise with your money, and not to be swayed by the latest trends or get-rich-quick schemes. This card suggests that you should establish a budget and stick to it, while being mindful of your debts and obligations. It is also important to save for the future and build a savings cushion to be prepared for unforeseen costs. Building a strong financial foundation through sound investments and financial planning is essential for achieving financial stability and growth.

Relationships that are rooted in tradition, conventional expectations, and conformity tend to be more stable, secure, and predictable. The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that when it comes to relationships, it is necessary to approach them with the same commitment and seriousness as you would any other sort of partnership. Building relationships is a process that requires trust, respect, and dedication, and this card suggests that following traditional methods for doing so is the wisest course of action. This includes open communication, setting mutual expectations, taking the time to get to know each other, and investing effort and energy into the relationship. Such relationships tend to be long-lasting and committed.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a powerful symbol of personal strength and wisdom. It emphasizes that we must take responsibility for our own lives and our own decisions, no matter what the consequences may be. It shows that integrity and truth are paramount when it comes to achieving success.

When it comes to health, The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take good care of our bodies, both physically and mentally. It suggests that we take a proactive approach to our wellbeing, monitoring and recognizing the signs of stress and exhaustion before they become unmanageable. It also encourages us to provide ourselves with the healthiest environment possible, free from external pressure or interference.

In terms of finances, The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages us to be savvy and honest. It is a reminder to look after our money and not take risks for the sake of a quick gain. When it comes to relationships, The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages us to be honest, open and direct in our dealings but to also be mindful of others’ feelings. It is a reminder to treat others how we would like to be treated, even if it is difficult in the moment.

The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a great reminder that we are our own best source of strength and guidance. It encourages us to be honest, responsible and self-sufficient, while always being mindful of the impact our decisions have on ourselves and those around us.
