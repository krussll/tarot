## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The person is reflecting inwardly on their choices and contemplating the outcomes of their decisions. It can also suggest a period of transition, during which one must navigate new challenges and opportunities.

In terms of health, The Two of Wands Tarot card suggests that one is either waiting for the outcome of a health-related decision made before, or considering a current health choice. Whatever the case may be, it is important to seek out information and resources that can help one make the most informed decision possible. Taking the time to consider one’s options can be beneficial to one’s overall health and wellbeing.

When it comes to finances, The Two of Wands Tarot card implies that one is weighing their choices when it comes to finances. It can signify that one is considering a financial decision, such as making a large purchase or investing in a risky venture. It is important to be mindful and deliberate when it comes to financial decisions, and to make decisions that are in line with one’s long-term financial goals.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Two of Wands Tarot card suggests that one is pondering their choices when it comes to love and interpersonal connections. It could be a situation where one is considering which potential partner to pursue, or weighing the pros and cons of staying in a current relationship. Taking the time to carefully consider the impacts of one’s decisions can lead to healthier, more fulfilling relationships.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 10 of Swords tarot card can represent a complete end to one’s current health situation. This may be the result of sustained health problems that have reached a boiling point, or a sudden and severe health condition. This card signals that all choices have been exhausted, and there is nowhere left to turn. It can be a sign that more extreme measures may need to be taken to find relief, such as surgery or a new course of treatment. It can also suggest that health issues are causing emotional pain and distress, and that talking to a counsellor or therapist may be required to cope with this.

Finances  
The 10 of Swords tarot card can mean that an individual has hit rock bottom financially. This card signals that all resources have been exhausted, and there is no more left to give. It can be a sign that help is needed in order to get back on track financially, such as from family, friends, or financial advisors. This card may also indicate a need to scale back spending and focus on paying back debts. It can also suggest that it is time to let go of material possessions or investments that are not providing a financial return.

Relationships  
The 10 of Swords tarot card can mean that a relationship has reached an end and all hope is lost. This card signals that the relationship has been drained of all energy, and all attempts to make it better have been in vain. It can be a sign that it is time to let go and accept the relationship for what it is. It could also suggest that there are unresolved issues that need to be addressed in order to move forward. This card can also indicate that talking to a counsellor or therapist may be necessary to process and cope with the emotional pain caused by this situation.
