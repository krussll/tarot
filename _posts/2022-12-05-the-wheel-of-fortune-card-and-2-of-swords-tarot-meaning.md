## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is one of the most auspicious cards in the Tarot deck and is associated with great luck and good fortune. This card usually heralds a turning point in life, signifying that the wheel is turning and a new era of luck and abundance is arriving.

When it comes to health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can mean a turning point in health matters. It may signal a new period of increased energy and vitality or a period of healing and recovery. It can also signal a change in physical circumstances, such as an impending relocation, or the start of a new health regimen.

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can also signify a turning point in finances. It might signify an unexpected windfall of money, a change in income or a new job opportunity. Whatever the financial situation, this card usually heralds a time of abundance and growth.

When it comes to relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card indicates a change in emotional circumstances. It may signify a strengthening of an existing relationship, or a new relationship just beginning. It may also signify a change of emotional focus, such as a shift away from the past and towards the present. Whatever the case may be, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card often brings a sense of hope to a relationship, signaling that better days ahead.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

The 2 of Swords tarot card shows a woman sitting in the middle of two swords. This card is a sign that a decision needs to be made, or a compromise must be reached, in order to bring peace and balance to a situation.

Health: When the 2 of Swords appears in a health reading, it could be an indication that something needs to be done in order to find a healthier balance. It could signify a difficult decision, such as whether to continue a particular treatment or change to something more suitable. It could also be a warning to take a step back and weigh out all of the options before making a decision. Too much of one thing can be harmful, and this card is an indicator to find a middle ground.

Finances: When it comes to money, the 2 of Swords can suggest a need to weigh out all the pros and cons before making a decision. It is a sign that you should make sure you are being mindful with your finances. This card can also warn against being too extreme with spending or saving, and it implores you to make a wise decision that allows for both security and flexibility.

Relationships: In a relationship reading, the 2 of Swords can indicate that a compromise needs to be made in order to maintain a balance between two partners. This card can also signify a difficult decision that has to be made, or an opinion that needs to be heard. It could be a sign that both partners need to take a step back and assess the situation from an outside perspective in order to find a solution that works for them as a couple.
