A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

When the 7 Of Swords tarot card and the 3 Of Cups tarot card appear together, it signals a warning of betrayal and dishonesty, likely in a friendship or close relationship. This means that, despite the strength of the friendship, someone may still be willing to go against your trust and run away with something that doesn’t belong to them. This card combination may also suggest that even though you are in a circle of supportive friends, you should take extra care when it comes to trusting the people around you, as even those closest to you can’t be trusted fully.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The 7 of Swords and the 3 of Cups tarot card can tell us a lot about what to expect for our love life. The 7 of Swords implies that there is potential for deception and betrayal, either from our partner or from someone else. It is a warning to remain vigilant and to not be too trusting. On the other hand, the 3 of Cups suggests that our love life will be supported by a strong circle of friends and family. This can offer us a sense of security and belonging, which may help to mitigate the potential for betrayal.

In terms of the relationship dynamic, the 7 of Swords and the 3 of Cups point to a balance between trust and caution. The 7 of Swords warns us to remain vigilant and to not let our guard down, while the 3 of Cups provides assurance that we will have a supportive, loving circle of companions. When it comes to communication, the 7 of Swords may indicate that our partner is not always honest with us, or that there is potential for dishonesty in our relationship. The 3 of Cups encourages us to communicate openly and honestly, and not to neglect the importance of friendship in our relationship.

Overall, the 7 of Swords and 3 of Cups tarot card point to a relationship that is rife with potential and risk. While there is potential for betrayal, the presence of a supportive circle of friends can help to keep the relationship strong and secure. It is essential to remain vigilant and aware of any potential issues within the relationship, but also to remember the importance of friendship, fun, and celebration within the relationship.

### What does 7 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When the tarot card combination of The 7 of Swords and The 3 of Cups comes up in a reading when it comes to finances and job, it could indicate that someone has been dishonest or has betrayed you in some way, or that there could be some sort of theft involved. This could mean that you have been the victim of some sort of financial fraud or malfeasance, or that someone has taken advantage of your trust or goodwill in some way.

On the other hand, it could also mean that, even in the presence of this dishonesty or betrayal, you have a strong network of support. This suggests that there are still people around you who care, who are willing to help, and who are prepared to stand by you as you work to overcome the difficulties and challenges presented by the 7 of Swords.

The combination of these two cards may also indicate that, while facing such difficulties, you need to avoid taking too many risks. This could mean refraining from certain investments or job opportunities, or avoiding certain people or organizations. It is important to maintain a sense of caution, particularly if there is evidence of wrongdoing or dishonesty in your finances or job.

At the same time, despite the difficulties, this combination of cards can also be interpreted as a sign of eventual success. This suggests that if you remain patient and use the strength of your support network to weather the storm, eventually you will be able to emerge in a better position. The presence of the 3 of Cups should act as a reminder that you are not alone, and that with the right resources and the right plan, things will eventually improve.

When The 7 Of Swords and The 3 Of Cups tarot cards appear together, it generally reflects a tension between betrayal and friendship. This can be particularly relevant when it comes to our physical and mental health. On one hand, The 7 Of Swords tarot card warns us of the dangers of engaging in acts of dishonesty and betrayal that might jeopardize our health, such as hiding information or engaging in activities that are harmful to our physical and mental wellbeing. On the other hand, The 3 Of Cups tarot card reminds us that any form of true, honest, and supportive friendship can be beneficial for our bodies and minds.

When it comes to our health, it is important to ensure we are surrounded by people who care for us, support us, and are honest and open with us. The combination of The 7 Of Swords and The 3 Of Cups suggest that if we can find that balance between being honest and respectful in our relationships, then we can create supportive, healthy environments in which we are free to express ourselves and take care of our mental and physical health.

On the other hand, if we are surrounded by relationships built on dishonesty, betrayal, or secrecy, then this can be a danger to our mental and physical health. When The 7 Of Swords and The 3 Of Cups appear together, we are reminded that it is important to make sure our relationships are based on honesty, respect, and friendship, and that we should stay away from any relationships that could be potentially damaging to our health. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 Of Swords Tarot card is typically associated with theft, betrayal, dishonesty and running away, and this card is a warning to all its viewers that they should be careful and suspicious.

Healthwise, The 7 Of Swords Tarot card advises individuals to be aware of negative influences, as this card symbolizes health issues which can arise from problems related to stress and anxiety. It can be a sign that an individual is neglecting their health due to being dishonest or deceptive and it can lead to problems such as insomnia, depression and other physical ailments. Individuals should take caution to manage their health and remain honest with themselves, in order to stay healthy.

Financially, The 7 Of Swords Tarot card advises individuals to be aware of any deceit or betrayal in regards to finances. It can be a warning sign that an individual is running away from their financial problems rather than facing them. It can also be a sign that one is acting dishonestly in regards to finances or is taking risks or shortcuts to get ahead. It is essential for individuals to remain truthful, honest and responsible in all their financial decisions in order to remain financially stable.

Relationshipwise, The 7 Of Swords Tarot card suggests that individuals should be careful of any deceit or betrayal in regards to their relationships. It could be a warning sign that an individual is not being honest or trustworthy with their partner, or that they are trying to run away from their relationship issues instead of facing them. It is important for individuals to remain honest, truthful and faithful with their partners in order to ensure a successful relationship.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a very optimistic card when it comes to health. It suggests that if you put your trust in yourself and your circle of supportive friends and family, you will be able to improve your health and well-being. You are likely to be looking after yourself and those around you, with a focus on healthy habits, routine and positivity. There may be times of difficulty or illness, but if you are surrounded by love and support, you will be able to traverse these challenges with more ease.

Finances:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card indicates that, with the right attitude and openness to receive support, finances should be relatively stable. You may be offered financial advice from a friend or family member, and it is important to consider their positive input. There is a suggestion that you are working together with those around you to manage money more effectively, and this will allow you to enjoy the fruits of your labour. It is important to be responsible and accountable, but to also recognize that you don’t have to do it alone.

Relationships:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card suggests that relationships with family and friends will be highly supportive and rewarding. There is a real sense of joy and celebration that comes from being around people who understand and appreciate you. Peace and harmony should be found in relationships, and you may find yourself open to meaningful conversations. There is a sense of acceptance within these connections, and you will feel encouraged and buoyed by those around you.
