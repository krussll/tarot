A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean together?

When the 4 of Wands Tarot card and the Queen of Swords Tarot card appear together, it can signify a celebration of homecoming. It may be a reunion with family and close friends that you haven’t seen for a while, or bringing together the people you love for an occasion that is special to you. The Queen of Swords tarot card is a reminder to be honest and truthful throughout the festivities, and also to remain independent and strong in your own convictions. The 4 of Wands Tarot card signifies joy and happiness, allowing yourself to go forth and trust that you will find joy at the heart of this union.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Queen of Swords shows a self-sufficient person who is open and honest about their feelings. When the Queen of Swords is combined with the 4 of Wands it could suggest that your partner is now in a position where they can openly express their love without shame or fear of judgement. They understand that a relationship should be based on honesty and trustworthiness.

The 4 of Wands and the Queen of Swords together in a love reading suggests that the homecoming is a time to celebrate the reunion of love. The relationship will benefit from a feeling of security and warmth that comes with welcoming home a lost loved one. It is a reminder that love is worth the wait and no distance is too far to travel if it brings two people back together.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 4 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the health benefits of celebrating life with family and friends. It suggests taking the time to stop and appreciate the moments of joy, goodwill, and contentment that come from socializing and coming together. While partaking in activities such as attending festivals, having dinner together, or simply catching up over a cup of coffee, these occasions can be beneficial for one’s physical and emotional health. Through strengthening the bonds with loved ones and engaging in a supportive environment, an individual can improve their psychological wellbeing, which in turn can improve overall physical health and lifestyle.

Finances

The 4 of Wands Tarot card encourages a sense of bounty, abundance and prosperity in financial matters. Gathering together with friends and family to celebrate can bring a wealth of positive energy and financial stability. This card is a reminder to focus on the abundance and gifts that one already has in their life, rather than the material items they may be lacking. Having something to look forward to, such as a party or event, can bring a sense of hope and optimism in financial matters.

Relationships

The 4 of Wands Tarot card speaks of the importance of celebrating our relationships with those closest to us. Taking the time to come together and rejoice in the good times strengthens relationships, creating a feeling of intimate connection, safety and joy. The card encourages individuals to express their love and appreciation for one another, to create a deeper emotional bond between them. This card reminds us that our relationships with our partners, family, and friends can be further enriched by simply celebrating together.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health

The Queen of Swords Tarot card speaks of self-sufficiency and total honesty, two important qualities for healthy living. Self-sufficiency means that you rely on yourself for your own health, physical and emotional well-being. You do not allow outside pressures to influence how you take care of yourself and your health. Instead, you take on the responsibility for making your own decisions in regards to your health. You do not allow yourself to be manipulated or influenced by other people’s opinions. Additionally, total honesty is essential for true well being. You are honest with yourself about how you are feeling, what you need, and how to take care of yourself. This honesty allows you to make healthier decisions for yourself, rather than relying on others.

Finances

When it comes to finances, the Queen of Swords Tarot card speaks of the need for self-sufficiency and total honesty. Self-sufficiency means that you are in charge of your financial security. You do not allow others to dictate your financial decisions or to take advantage of you. Instead, you take full responsibility for your own financial decisions, and you are honest with yourself about what you can and cannot afford. Total honesty is also essential when it comes to finances. You are honest with yourself about any bad financial decisions you may have made in the past, and you take the appropriate steps to rectify them. You also remain honest about your financial situation, and you take full responsibility for the consequences of any choices you make.

Relationships

The Queen of Swords Tarot card can also provide guidance for your relationships. Self-sufficiency means that you do not let others define who you are and do not allow them to manipulate you in any way. You rely on yourself for security and strength and do not allow others to weaken your sense of self. Total honesty is just as important when it comes to relationships. You must be honest with yourself and with others about what you want and need in a relationship. This honesty allows you to make informed decisions, and it can help you weed out those who are not compatible with you. It also helps you build stronger and healthier relationships with those that are compatible with you.
