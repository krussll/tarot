## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Finances:

The Ace of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take risks and invest in yourself and your future. ## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 9 of Cups Tarot card signifies a great sense of health and well-being. It symbolizes the achievement of overall balance and the ability to nourish the body, mind and spirit. When this card appears, it can indicate a period of good health, where energy levels are high, and the body is able to take care of itself without effort. It suggests the presence of a strong immune system and the ability to easily overcome physical and mental obstacles. This card also symbolizes spiritual healing, as it suggests the possibility of deeply cleansing the body and soul of impurities.

Finances: The 9 of Cups tarot card is associated with financial abundance and wealth. When this card appears, it can indicate a steady and prosperous flow of financial energy that will bring good fortune and success in all matters relating to money. This card symbolizes the ability to attract wealth and to maintain financial security in times of hardship. It suggests the power to manage one’s finances successfully and to create a safe and secure future. The 9 of Cups can signify a strong opportunity for investment and success, providing the necessary financial stability to continue on the path of success.

Relationships: The 9 of Cups tarot card symbolizes a great connection and understanding between two people. It suggests a strong bond that is based on trust, respect and generosity. This card can signify a period of sympathetic understanding between two lovers, allowing for deep, meaningful conversations and fulfilling conversations. It can also symbolize the ability to support one another through difficult times, providing each other with a sense of comfort and security. This card can signify a deep connection between two people that will last through time.
