A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Queen Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The Queen Of Pentacles and The 4 Of Swords tarot card together could mean that you are in a state of renewal and recovery in terms of your resources and fertility. You have recently taken time out for yourself and have found a place of rest and solitude to recenter yourself. This is an important time to take stock of all the resources you have, from the physical to the emotional and spiritual, and to plan for the future. This is a time for you to use your resourcefulness and creativity to create a more fruitful and abundant life for yourself. Take the time to evaluate your progress and be gentle with yourself as you do.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles mean?

The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of resourcefulness and abundance. In terms of health, this card indicates that the querent is nurturing their physical and emotional well-being. They have tapped into their potential to create a life that is abundant with vitality and health. They have found ways to maintain balance and create a healthy lifestyle.

In terms of finances, the Queen of Pentacles Tarot card signifies that the querent is in a financially stable position. They have found ways to create financial stability and abundance for themselves, through wise investments and decision-making. This card can also indicate a time for the querent to take risks and start new business ventures.

When it comes to relationships, the Queen of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that the querent is in a stable and comfortable relationship. They are nurturing a relationship with creative ideas and through taking care of the other person. This card can also suggest that the querent is in the process of building a meaningful relationship, one that is rooted in mutual respect.

Ultimately, the Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of resourcefulness and abundance. It is a reminder to the querent that when they make wise decisions, both in health, finance and relationships, they can create a life of abundance and prosperity.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health: The Four of Swords Tarot card suggests taking a break from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and allowing yourself to restore your energy and health. This card speaks to taking a step back and giving yourself a break from the stress that often accompanies a busy lifestyle. The Four of Swords encourages us to be mindful when it comes to our physical and mental health and to dedicate time to physical rest and relaxation. In terms of health, the Four of Swords Tarot card can represent the need to pause, to get enough sleep and to engage in activities that nurture and replenish your energy levels.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the Four of Swords Tarot card speaks to pulling back and taking a break from attempting to keep up with the financial demands of life. This card encourages you to downshift and give yourself time away from money matters for a period of restoration and rest. Rather than overworking or seeking to make money to fill the empty spaces in your financial coffers, the Four of Swords advises you to look within and take a respite from the material world. During this period of recovery, it may be beneficial to explore alternative ways to approach your finances or to look for ways that you can cut expenses rather than seeking to make more money.

Relationships: In matters of relationships, the Four of Swords asks that we take a step back, assess the situation and go within. This card speaks to setting aside time for solitude and taking a break from our relationships and external connections to focus on our own inner renewal. The Four of Swords can represent a period of retreat and rest, a time to restore the balance within our relationships and to make sure that we are not sacrificing ourselves in order to make our connections work. During this period of replenishment, it may be helpful to take some time out to connect with yourself, to nurture and heal, and to build a stronger foundation for yourself before engaging in relationships anew.
