A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Page Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Page Of Swords and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together are a sign of social support and respite in difficult times. It signals that you should use the guidance of your friends and family to help you through difficult moments. It also speaks to the power of truth and honesty. That only by being open and honest with your loved ones, you can find the answers and strength of mind to successfully manage through any situation. Lastly, this combination advises that you should be weary of gossip and prying eyes, as these things can lead to nothing but trouble.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health

The Page of Swords is a card that speaks to physical and mental health. It can indicate feeling drained, exhausted, and overwhelmed. It is about pushing yourself too hard, doing too much, and having too little rest. It cautions against over-exertion and suggests that a healthier balance needs to be struck. However, it can also suggest the curiosity and ambition of youth and the determination to take risks, to push boundaries and grow. In terms of health then, the Page of Swords is a reminder of the importance of rest and balance, while also encouraging creativity and ambition.

Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card relates to finances in a way that is grounded in investigation and strategy. It suggests the willingness to think outside of the box, to assess different approaches, and to be open to change. It can indicate a need to be vigilant, to keep current with market trends, and to be proactive about making smart financial decisions. It also cautions against overspending, unwise investments, and emotional impulses that can lead to financial instability.

Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card speaks to relationships in terms of boundaries. It reminds us to be mindful of our own needs, to speak up when boundaries have been crossed, and to listen carefully to our partners and loved ones. It is a card of communication and honesty, of openness and compromise. It warns against intellectualizing our relationships, of being overly analytical and overly critical, and of prying too far and too deep. Most of all, it reminds us to practice empathy and compassion, and to show our love and support in ways that we would long to receive.

A physical health issue can seem daunting, but this card suggests you will have plenty of support from your friends, family and peers. This card encourages you to seek emotional and even spiritual support as well, as emotional and spiritual health are essential for physical health. This card suggests that your friends and family will be able to provide assistance when you are facing tough financial decisions. You may find that by opening up and asking for help, you will gain new perspective and unique solutions. This card implies that with the right network of support, even the most challenging of financial situations can be faced with strength, power and understanding.

For Relationships:  
The 3 of Cups tarot card encourages you to seek out the support of those around you in order to nurture and strengthen your relationships. This card suggests that a strong support system is essential for healthy relationships and it encourages you to seek out meaningful connections rather than superficial ones. It suggests that honest, open and supportive communication with those closest to you can help you build a strong foundation for meaningful and loving relationships. It encourages you to accept and appreciate the circle of love and support you have created, and to allow it to help you build more powerful relationships in the future.
