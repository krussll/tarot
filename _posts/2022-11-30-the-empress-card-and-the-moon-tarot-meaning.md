A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Empress card and The Moon card together in particular.

The The Empress card and The Moon have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Empress and The Moon mean together?

The Empress tarot card and The Moon tarot card together suggest that you should trust your intuition and intuition when it comes to your health and your relationships. You may need to make some voluntary changes in your life in order to create a more positive and healthy lifestyle. The Empress tarot card suggests that you focus on your creative endeavors, while The Moon tarot card suggests that you focus on your intuition and your emotions. Trusting your intuition will help you make the best decisions for your health and your relationships.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Empress and The Moon mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Empress mean?

The Empress is a card that embodies all that is creative, nurturing and abundant. She is a symbol of fertility and pregnancy, and is also associated with good parenting. She represents all that is needed to create and nourish new life.

When it comes to health, The Empress is a reminder that we are all connected to the cycle of life and death. She reminds us to appreciate our bodies and the miracle of life. She is also a reminder that we need to take care of our bodies, as they are the temples of our souls.

When it comes to finances, The Empress is a reminder that we are all connected to the flow of money and energy. She reminds us that we need to be responsible with our money and use it in a way that benefits all. She is also a reminder that we need to be generous with our money and share it with those who are in need.

When it comes to relationships, The Empress is a reminder that we are all connected to one another. She reminds us that we need to be compassionate and loving with our words and actions. She is also a reminder that we need to be grateful for the relationships we have.

## What does The Moon mean?

The Moon Tarot card is often associated with unseen problems or changes that may be coming your way. This can be a difficult card to interpret, as it can often be difficult to trust your intuition when things seem to be going well. However, if you are able to see the signs that The Moon is trying to show you, it can be a very powerful tool in help you make necessary changes in your life.

When it comes to your health, The Moon can be a sign that there are some underlying issues that you may not be aware of. It is important to trust your intuition and get checked out by a professional if you feel something is off. The Moon can also be a sign of major life changes, such as a new job or a move. It is important to be open to change and trust that The Moon is leading you in the right direction.

In terms of your finances, The Moon can be a sign that there are some things you need to change in your spending habits. It is important to be mindful of your spending and make sure that you are not putting yourself in a difficult financial situation. The Moon can also be a sign that you need to start saving for your future. It is important to be proactive and start planning for your long-term financial security.

When it comes to your relationships, The Moon can be a sign that there are some unseen issues that need to be addressed. It is important to be honest with your partner and communicate openly about any concerns you may have. The Moon can also be a sign that you need to take some time for yourself and focus on your own needs. It is important to nurture your relationship and make sure that you are both happy and fulfilled.
