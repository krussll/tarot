Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 3 Of Wands card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 3 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 3 Of Wands.

## 3 Of Wands And Love – An Overview

The 3 Of Wands tarot card is a reminder that there is strength in numbers, and it can be beneficial to reach out and collaborate with others in order to achieve your health goals.

Through teamwork and collaboration, you can open yourself up to new ideas and resources, and expand your awareness.

Working with others can also give you the motivation and support you need to stay on track with your health goals.

Travel can also be beneficial for your health, as new environments and experiences can help to refresh your body and mind, providing a much-needed boost of energy and perspective.

## Upright Meanings For 3 Of Wands And Love

### What It Means For Me

The 3 Of Wands tarot card in regards to love symbolizes a partnership that has grown and is ready to expand.

It suggests that the relationship is strong and the individual is ready to make a commitment to the other.

This card may represent a couple that is ready to take the next step in their relationship, such as getting engaged or married.

It also suggests that the couple is ready to do whatever it takes to make their relationship work, whether it be through teamwork, communication, and understanding.

This card can also mean that the couple is ready to take a journey together, such as traveling to new places or embarking on a new adventure.

The 3 Of Wands suggests that the relationship is strong and that the couple is ready to embark on a beautiful journey together.

### What It Means For The Future

The 3 Of Wands card suggests that my future love will be based on the principles of teamwork and collaboration.

We will be able to support each other, create meaningful connections and build a strong foundation of trust.

Our relationship will also have a commercial aspect, as we will seek to find ways to expand our resources and support each other’s ambitions.

We will also enjoy traveling together, discovering new cultures and places to explore.

Overall, this card indicates that my future love will be filled with companionship, creativity and adventure.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 3 of Wands tarot card encourages us to look to our relationships and the people we love and cherish.

This card speaks to the relationships that we have built and the team we have put together to help us reach our goals.

It is a reminder to nurture these relationships as they can provide us with tremendous support and help us reach new heights.

The 3 of Wands also speaks to the need for us to explore, expand, and travel together.

Whether we are traveling for business, pleasure, or simply to explore, this card encourages us to do it with the people we love so that we can create beautiful and lasting memories.

## Reversed Meanings For 3 Of Wands And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The 3 of Wands appears in the reversed position, it could be an indication that obstacles or difficulties are blocking your path to a successful relationship.

It suggests that you may be working against yourself by being overly critical or judgmental of your partner, or even of your own feelings.

You may be putting up walls to protect yourself from getting hurt, but in the process, you are preventing yourself from experiencing the joys of a loving connection.

The reversed Three of Wands suggests that it is time to take a step back and evaluate your expectations for a relationship.

Are you trying to fix something that isn’t broken, or are you trying to control the outcome of a situation that is out of your control? It is important to remember that relationships take time and effort to grow, and that they can never be forced into being something they are not.

Instead, focus on what you can do to create a secure and trusting relationship with your partner, and be open to the possibility of love and happiness.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Three of Wands tarot card is a sign of potential obstacles in your romantic future.

You may have difficulty finding the right person and maintaining a relationship.

This can be because of a lack of teamwork and communication between you and your partner or because of a fear of commitment.

You may also be reluctant to take risks in love, or you may be too focused on success and security that you don’t take the time to nurture a strong connection.

Your current relationship may be experiencing a series of setbacks, which could be the result of a lack of trust, understanding and cooperation.

You may also be influenced by outside forces that are preventing you from achieving the kind of love you desire.

This could be due to a fear of change and uncertainty, or a tendency to stay in your comfort zone, rather than exploring new possibilities.

The reversed Three of Wands suggests that it’s time to take a step back and re-evaluate your current situation.

Make sure to be honest with yourself and your partner, and express what your needs and expectations are.

Don’t be afraid to take risks and step outside of your comfort zone.

Once you have the courage to explore new possibilities and put in the effort, you can create a strong connection with someone special.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Three of Wands appears in a Tarot reading, it can often indicate a lack of teamwork between those close to you.

You may be having difficulty collaborating with those around you and your relationships may be strained.

You may feel like you are going it alone, that your attempts to reach out to others for help or support are falling on deaf ears.

There may be a feeling of loneliness or alienation from the people who are closest to you.

The reversed Three of Wands can also indicate that you are putting too much pressure on yourself and not allowing yourself to enjoy the love of those close to you.

You may be feeling bogged down by your own expectations and responsibilities and not allowing yourself to appreciate the support that those around you are offering.

You may be too focused on the practical aspects of life and missing out on the joy that comes from spending time with those you care about.

Take a step back, take a deep breath, and focus on nurturing your relationships with those close to you.

Allow yourself to be vulnerable and to open yourself up to the love of those around you.

Find ways to work together to reach your goals and to provide emotional support to each other.

Releasing the pressure you’re putting on yourself and showing gratitude for the people in your life can help bring back the harmony and joy that comes from being part of a team.
