A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 3 Of Pentacles mean together?

When the 5 of Wands and 3 of Pentacles tarot cards appear together, they represent a challenge that requires a team effort. This could be a team sport, an obstacle course, or any other activity that requires skill and collaboration. The 5 of Wands suggests that you will face competition and rivalry, but the 3 of Pentacles encourages you to persevere and work together to overcome any obstacles. The success of the team depends on the skill and craftsmanship of each member, and the quality of the team’s work will be reflected in the final result. This combination of tarot cards encourages you to be creative, take risks, and work together for the best possible outcome.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

The 5 of Wands tarot card symbolizes rivalry, obstacles, and challenges. It can be applied to all areas of life, including health, finances, and relationships. Each interpretation of this card has different implications.

Regarding health, the 5 of Wands tarot card signifies a challenging health situation, such as trying to maintain a healthy lifestyle with a hectic schedule, or needing to make difficult lifestyle changes in the face of health issues. This card suggests that there is an underlying cause of the health issues that will need to be addressed in order to promote health and wellness. Although the challenges may be difficult, it is important to face them head on and not be discouraged.

In terms of finances, the 5 of Wands tarot card indicates a struggle related to money. This could be related to finding steady employment, trying to save up for a big purchase, or handling unexpected expenses. It could also signify a situation where there is a need to compete with others financially. Whatever the case may be, it is important to be persistent and work on finding the most cost-effective solutions.

For relationships, this card suggests competition and obstacles that need to be overcome. It is likely referring to a situation where two people have differing opinions or desires in regards to a certain thing or situation. It is important to remember that communication and compromise are key to resolving any issues in a relationship. Even though the challenges can feel overwhelming, it is important to not let pride get in the way of having open and honest conversations to reach a resolution.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The Three Of Pentacles tarot card represents teamwork and collaboration, as well as skill and craftsmanship. It also indicates quality, structure and organization, which can be beneficial when it comes to health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Three of Pentacles tarot card suggests that teamwork is the most effective way to achieve your health goals. This could be teaming up with a doctor, nutritionist or health coach to create an effective and achievable health plan. It also points to the need for discipline and commitment to your goals, and the importance of always striving for excellence.

For finances, The Three of Pentacles tarot card encourages collaboration and structure, reminding us that it is often best to work through challenges with a clear and organized approach. It speaks to the need for discipline and order in our financial lives, reminding us to be smart and mindful with our resources.

When it comes to relationships, The Three of Pentacles tarot card indicates quality over quantity and the importance of understanding the needs of the other. It speaks to the need for balance and communication, and the importance of working together to create a strong and lasting bond. It also asks you to be open and honest with your partner, encouraging you to be realistic and to seek out collaboration and understanding.
