A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the 4 of Swords tarot card together suggest a need for taking time out of your normal routine to go on a quest for personal truth and spiritual illumination. It is time to set aside all other obligations and focus on the inner work that must be done. This is a period of retreat and rest, where you can take the time to renew your spirit and find peace in solitude. This period of recovery will help to gain clarity and provide the insights necessary for profound inner growth.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 4 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card is a reminder to invest in your health and connect with yourself in order to make the best decisions for yourself. In terms of your health, it may be a sign to spend some quiet time and reflect on how you feel physically and emotionally. Take the time to think about your diet, exercise routine, and sleep pattern. Additionally, the Hermit card may be a reminder to seek advice from a professional if needed – be that a doctor, counsellor, or nutritionist. It is important to take time to listen to your body and make decisions that are based on what you truly need and desire.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card is a reminder to take a step back and focus on what is truly important. When it comes to finances, it is a sign to pause and reflect on what it is that matters to you in your financial life. Ask yourself what it is that you truly value and how you can best nurture those values. This card also suggests avoiding making rash decisions and getting in touch with what you need, as well as setting financial boundaries and knowing your limits. The Hermit card also encourages you to take financial advice from a trusted professional rather than making important decisions in a moment of haste.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card is an invitation to take a step back and look within for what truly resonates for you in your relationships. It is a reminder to listen to your intuition and start to make decisions based on your deeper needs and desires. It is important to take time to yourself and nurture your relationships without neglecting your own personal growth and development. This tarot card encourages you to know when it is time to take a break and practice self-care, as well as when it is time to reach out to others. It is important to take some time to reflect on how you are communicating your feelings and needs without expecting others to read your mind.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 4 of Swords tarot card symbolizes a need for rest and recovery. It’s indicative of a time in which you need to step away from the busyness of daily life in order to give yourself time to heal, recover and restore balance to your physical and mental health. This card serves as a warning that continuing to push yourself when you are exhausted and run-down will only lead to more health problems down the line. If you feel like you need a break, don’t be afraid to take it. Give yourself the opportunity to rest and rejuvenate so that you can come back to your life with renewed energy and a better perspective.

Finances:

The 4 of Swords tarot card can represent a need to pause or take a break when it comes to financial matters. This can be a time to take a step back and assess the situation before making any big decisions. The card suggests that if you wait and take a more measured approach, you will be able to plan out your finances more effectively and not risk making any moves that could be detrimental to your financial health. It’s important to stay informed on the latest economic trends and market conditions, but it’s also important to be mindful of your own wellbeing and not overextend yourself.

Relationships:

The 4 of Swords tarot card can indicate a need to take a step back from any interpersonal relationships that are draining or putting strain on your wellbeing. This is a time to assess whether the relationship is fulfilling and healthy or if it needs to be put on hold while you prioritize taking care of yourself. It’s important to remember that all relationships require effort, but if you’re feeling overwhelmed or burnt out, then it’s likely time to take a break. This gives you the opportunity to reflect on the relationship and come back to it in a clearer, healthier state of mind.
