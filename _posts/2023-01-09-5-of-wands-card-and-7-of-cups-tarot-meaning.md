## What does 5 Of Wands and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of the 5 of Wands and the 7 of Cups tarot cards represent a situation of being in the midst of internal conflict and struggling with competing desires. It can indicate a situation where you are facing multiple challenges, be it in love, work, or personal goals. You will find yourself unsure of which direction to take and having to navigate between the competing interests and ambitions in front of you. This situation can lead to feelings of being overwhelmed and bogged down with too many choices, emphasizing the importance of commitment and focus in order to reach any sense of clarity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

For Health

The 5 of Wands Tarot card can indicate a healthy competitive spirit in a person. This competitive drive can manifest in activities such as exercise and sports, and could be beneficial to improving overall health and wellbeing. It can also represent a need to be the best at what one does, making it necessary to push oneself beyond personal limits and boundaries. This can be seen as a positive energy as it encourages a person to stay focused and motivated in order to achieve their health and fitness goals.

For Finances

The 5 of Wands Tarot card can represent a competitive and ambitious approach to money and finances. This can mean being driven to make the most profitable investments or taking risks to ensure greater financial gain. However, it can also suggest that obstacles and challenges may be in the way when facing a financial situation. Financial success requires patience and resilience and this card advises to be prepared to work hard in order to reach one’s financial goals.

For Relationships

The 5 of Wands Tarot card can indicate a conflict that could arise in relationships. This could be due to competition between two parties for the same thing. This can often lead to feuds and disagreements, which can manifest in one’s personal relationships. It is important to remember that this card does not represent an aggressive energy, but rather a need to be the best in any situation. This can be used as an opportunity to strengthen relationships and bond through a shared competitive energy.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 7 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a lack of focus when it comes to health matters. It may denote times of indecision or recklessness, when a person is unable to commit to a healthy lifestyle and thus, their health and wellbeing suffer for it. It can also signify vagueness about what should be done and how best to go about doing it. This Tarot card marks a time when one is scattered and unfocused in their approach, which can lead to poor nutrition, excessive stress, lack of regular exercise and sleep, and other symptoms of unbalanced health.

Finances:

The 7 of Cups Tarot card can indicate times of financial recklessness, when a person is unable to commit to a financial plan and thus, their financial health suffers for it. It can also signify confusion over where to invest, how to save and when to spend. This Tarot card marks a time when one is scattered and unfocused in their approach to money, which can lead to erratic spending, haphazard investments and other symptoms of financially unhealthy behavior.

Relationships:

The 7 of Cups Tarot card can symbolize commitment issues in relationships. It may denote a time when a person is unable to commit to moving their relationship to the next level, whether that be taking it exclusive, getting married, or even just planning for the future together. This Tarot card marks a time when one is scattered and unable to make a commitment, which can lead to feelings of insecurity, self-doubt and mistrust. It may also signify an inability to stay focused and present in the relationship, which can lead to feelings of distance and distance between the two people.
