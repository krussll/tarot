On the other hand, you must be willing to open up and let love come into your life. This card suggests that in order to be successful in both your personal life and professional career, you must find a balance between standing up for yourself and being vulnerable to love and partnership. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

In the context of health, this card can suggest a need to defend yourself from unhealthy habits or situations in order to remain healthy and strong. It can be a reminder to take care of yourself and strengthen your physical and mental resilience so that you can stand up to any health issues that come your way. Taking preventive health measures and proactively protecting your health can be recommended to ensure that you stay strong.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Wands Tarot Card suggests that it is important to defend your finances from any financial risks or uncertainty. This could include setting up a budget, monitoring your spending and avoiding any dangerous investments. It can also be a reminder to protect yourself with insurance, such as life and health insurance, to make sure that you are covered in the event of any unfortunate circumstances.

In terms of relationships, the 7 of Wands Tarot Card can be a reminder to stand up for yourself in the face of any competition or challenges. It can be a reminder to protect yourself and your relationships, be assertive when necessary, and not let anyone take advantage of you. It can also be a reminder to be confident and strong in the face of any relationship issues, so that it does not end up damaging your relationship. ## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

The 2 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the coming together of two distinct but complimentary energies, and typically symbolizes a harmonious partnership. It’s a card that speaks of balance, and harmony between the two halves of a relationship – whether those halves are two people, two families, two finances or two different aspects of the self.

When it comes to health, the 2 of Cups suggests that a healthy balance can be found between physical and emotional aspects of our wellbeing. The card suggests that there are two sides to a healthy lifestyle – the physical and the emotional. It’s important to recognize that both of these aspects are essential to achieving a healthy balance. Reaching out to partners, family and friends can offer emotional support, and can help to create a healthy, wholesome environment that encourages both physical and emotional wellness.

When it comes to finances, the 2 of Cups is a card of balance. It suggests that in order to achieve financial success and stability, it’s important to balance our spending, saving and investing. The card encourages us to take an even-handed approach to our finances, and to recognize that there is power in working together, rather than trying to go it alone. This can mean budgeting, having a financial plan or seeking professional financial advice.

In relationships, the 2 of Cups is a card of connection and commitment. This card symbolizes a bond between two people, and is often associated with love and romance. The card encourages us to open our hearts, nurture a bond, and to be supportive and understanding of our partners. It suggests there is a balance to be had between giving and taking, and that both parties should be able to give and receive respect, love and support.
