## The Chariot – An Overview

The Chariot tarot card symbolizes action and change, often attributed to a journey or a new beginning. It suggests an unstoppable force and ambition, the ability to take control of a situation and make it happen. The card often signifies an important decision that needs to be made, and the energy to do so. It could denote a journey, a new vehicle, a new job, a new relationship, or a new project. The energy of the card is often seen as strong and powerful, characterized by an unstoppable momentum and determination that will drive the individual to success. The Chariot card suggests that if one is willing to take initiative and put in the effort, success will follow.

The card also suggests that I may be about to acquire a new vehicle, whether literal or figurative, to help me on the journey. This could mean a job promotion, a new car, or even a new way of thinking that propels me forward. Whatever the case may be, I feel an energy of vroom-vroom, of ambition and momentum, pushing me forward and propelling me to my destination.
