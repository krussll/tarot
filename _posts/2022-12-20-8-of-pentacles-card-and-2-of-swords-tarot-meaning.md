## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 8 Of Pentacles and 2 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

Health

The 8 of Pentacles in the Tarot represents mastering your craft, dedication and focus when it comes to health. This card encourages us to invest the time and energy necessary to better our own health and wellness. This includes taking the time to become educated on healthy lifestyles, the importance of nutrition and exercise, learning how to manage stress, and surrounding ourselves with resources and support to help us stay focused on our health goals. The 8 of Pentacles encourages us to be mindful of our physical, mental, and spiritual health, and to use our skills, abilities, and resources wisely in order to achieve the ultimate end goal of greater health and well-being.

Finances

The 8 of Pentacles in the Tarot is a great message when it comes to finances. This card encourages us to apply the same dedication, focus and craftsmanship to our financial well-being that we might apply to any other areas of our lives. This includes investing time and energy into developing a financial plan, understanding the basics of debt and credit, staying on top of bills and budgeting, and investing wisely based on our financial goals. The 8 of Pentacles encourages us to be wise with our investments, and to use our resources and skills to work towards our financial objectives.

Relationships

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is a message about the importance of investing in relationships in order to master our craft. This card encourages us to take the time and energy necessary to learn more about our partners, our family and friends, and how to make our relationships work. This involves fostering understanding of how to communicate effectively, creating healthy boundaries, and utilizing our resources to keep relationships healthy and thriving. The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card reminds us to take the time to nurture existing relationships and create strong connections with others.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 2 of Swords tarot card often signals a time of difficult choices in regards to health. Often, this refers to a choice between two options, and both of which require a great deal of consideration. It could be the decision to take a riskier course of action in regards to a medical treatment, or the decision to stay with a conservative approach if that is what is best for the individual. It indicates a need for balance between doing too much and not doing enough, both for the physical body as well as for the emotional state. This is a card of finding the middle ground in health matters, and to be careful not to go too far in either direction.

Finances:  
With the 2 of Swords tarot card, it signals a time for compromise and balance when it comes to making financial decisions. This could be a situation where two differing strategies are being presented, and each has different potential outcomes. Rather than choosing one or the other, it is important to look at them both and decide on a middle course that takes both into consideration. This could be a tough decision to make, but it is also essential to ensure long-term financial stability. There is a need to weigh both sides of the argument carefully, and make a decision that is best for the individual’s situation.

Relationships:  
The 2 of Swords tarot card can represent a time where a difficult choice needs to be made in a relationship. This could be a choice between two different paths, or a decision that needs to be made that carries a great deal of weight. This card indicates that while it is important to stay true to one’s feelings, it is also necessary to think of the other person involved in the relationship and find a way to balance the needs of both parties. It is important to take the time to reflect on the situation and make sure that the decision that is being made is the best one for the relationship as a whole.
