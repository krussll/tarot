A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 4 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 4 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 4 Of Wands mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The 4 Of Wands tarot card together can suggest a time of transition and upheaval in your life. You may be experiencing an unwanted change or ruin that causes destabilizing upheaval, or even violence. As such, you may find yourself separated from family and friends, perhaps feeling ashamed or disgraced. Yet, you can take heart knowing that the 4 Of Wands tarot card indicates that ultimately, despite the tribulations, your family and friends will open their arms in a homecoming celebration once the transition period is over. This card is a reminder that no matter what, family and friends will be there for you during the most trying times.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Tower and 4 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The Tower tarot card and the 4 Of Wands tarot card can mean different things when they appear in a reading concerning love. When these cards come together in a reading, it can indicate momentous changes in one’s love life.

The Tower is a card of disruption and upheaval, and in a love reading, it can suggest an unwelcome change that can result in distress and ruin. Whether this involves the end of a relationship or a significant relationship-altering event, the Tower indicates that whatever is happening is out of one’s control and will have a lasting impact on their life.

At the same time, the 4 Of Wands, which is a card of celebration, family, and homecoming, can bring a sense of hope in the midst of the unwanted changes. When this card appears in a love reading, it suggests that one may find joy and comfort in spending time surrounded by friends and family. These times of togetherness can help one rebuild and heal from any damage caused by the Tower tarot card.

When the Tower tarot card and the 4 Of Wands tarot card appear together, it can be a reminder that unwanted changes can often lead to new beginnings. Although the process of recovery can be difficult, the 4 Of Wands in the reading can signify that with the right support, love and joy can be found in the midst of disruption.

### What does The Tower and 4 Of Wands mean together for your finances?

When the Tower tarot card and the 4 of Wands tarot card come together in a reading, it suggests a period of transition and change in the area of finances and job. This usually signals a period where the status quo of your finances is no longer in balance, and it will take effort to restore equilibrium.

The Tower card can stand for unwanted, abrupt change, and it can suggest a period of upheaval and ruin when it relates to finances and job. This may mean that unexpected events force a dramatic shift in your financial and job situation and you will have to make difficult and often fast decisions to adjust your lifestyle and resources. There may be drastic changes in your career and sources of income, and even if difficult, the upheaval might be necessary for long-term growth and stability. You might have to take a leap of faith and step out of your comfort zone to make the changes necessary for progress.

The 4 of Wands, in contrast, is a more positive companion to the Tower card as it denotes a coming together and reuniting of people, family and friends. This suggests that though money and job may be undergoing transition or even disruption, you can look to your strong relationships to keep you buoyed. Often the 4 of Wands card comes when you have earned the respect of your peers and colleagues and enjoy the acknowledgement of your accomplishments, so know that you can find steady support from your inner circle as you go through the changes.

All in all, when the Tower tarot card and the 4 of Wands tarot card appear together in a reading, it serves as a reminder that though change can be unsettling, it is necessary and it can bring about beautiful connections and reunions that will help you in your journey towards achievement and stability in the area of finances and job.

Though it is a difficult time, it may also bring with it an opportunity for growth. Change can be difficult, but can also bring new and exciting opportunities with it. Once the upheaval has settled, you may find yourself in a better place with a new perspective on health and wellbeing.

The 4 of Wands card signals a time of homecoming, reunions and celebrations. Despite the disruption and crisis that comes with The Tower, this card reminds you that the people who love and care for you will always be with you. Though it is a challenge and it may be difficult to see at the time, the combination of The Tower and The 4 of Wands tarot cards can be seen as an invitation to examine your health in a new and deeper way. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Tower and 4 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: When The Tower Tarot card appears in a health context it warns of a potential disruption in well-being and comfort. The Tower is a symbol of forced change, and in the context of health, this could mean the onset of an illness or injury, the results of medical procedures, or the need to make lifestyle changes to benefit your overall health. It could also denote an unexpected health scare such as a sudden high blood pressure or the onset of severe anxiety. In most cases, The Tower indicates a period of health turmoil, during which people should pay extra attention to their health, take extra precautions where necessary, and stay away from careless and hazardous behaviors.

Finances: The Tower Tarot card in the context of finance suggests that there is an impending upheaval in the financial realm. This could indicate a loss of money due to poor decision-making, or due to an unexpected surprise such as a rise in taxes or a surge in costs. It is a warning to be extra mindful with finances, to not take unnecessary risks, and to plan ahead to make sure that any potential losses are kept to a minimum. It is a sign that chaos and disruption is coming, and it is important to stay vigilant and be prepared.

Relationships: The Tower Tarot card in a relationship context suggests that there is an imminent breakdown in the relationship due to a sudden event or surprise. This could be due to a misunderstanding or miscommunication between the partners, or due to an outside event that affects both parties. It is a warning to be mindful of the needs of one another and to be prepared for any potential conflicts or disagreements. It is important to stay open to one another and keep the lines of communication open to prevent any major issues. This card warns of massive change coming, and it is important to be prepared and show goodwill towards one another.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 4 of Wands Tarot card indicates that health will be very strong. Any issues or illnesses you may be dealing with will soon pass, leaving your body feeling energetic, happy and healthy. There is a sense of balance and equilibrium when it comes to your physical health. You will feel energized and at peace with your health, allowing you to focus on more important issues.

Finances:

The 4 of Wands Tarot card is a message of growth and expansion in the financial sector. You will find yourself in a fortunate position where your finances are concerned. It could be that you have inherited something of value, got a promotion or won a lottery. It could also be that a long-term investment or gamble is paying off. In any case, it is a time when you will feel optimistic about your finances and see possibilities for growth.

Relationships:

The 4 of Wands Tarot card is associated with family, friends and community. You will find yourself surrounded by many people who are supportive and bring joy to your life. This is a time of celebration, reunions, and coming together. You may even find yourself renewing old friendships or reuniting with family members who have been absent for some time. Any relationships that have been strained or stressed are likely to be repaired and strengthened. This is an ideal time to nurture existing relationships and enjoy the love, companionship, and support that they provide.
