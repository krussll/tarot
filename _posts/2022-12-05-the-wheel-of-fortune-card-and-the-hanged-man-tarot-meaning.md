A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Wheel Of Fortune card and The Hanged Man card together in particular.

The The Wheel Of Fortune card and The Hanged Man have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune and The Hanged Man mean together?

The combination of The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card and The Hanged Man tarot card means that it’s time to take a chance and take a risk. You have the opportunity to use your wisdom and knowledge to make a wise decision that will bring you luck and good fortune. It’s time to take a step back and look at the situation from a new perspective to gain insight that will help you find the right path. Sometimes taking a risk is necessary to create a new path, and with this combination of cards, it’s a sign that you can trust your intuition and make a change that will bring better luck and success.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Wheel Of Fortune and The Hanged Man mean together for your love life?

The Wheel of Fortune tarot card and The Hanged Man tarot card together represent a shift in your love life. It is a sign that you are ready to take a risk and that luck is on your side. You may be at a crossroads where you must sacrifice something in order to move forward. This could be emotional, mental or physical energy, but you should know that whatever you do will pay off in the end.

With the combination of these two cards, expect some kind of turning point. You may find yourself dealing with a decision that is delicate and difficult. The Wheel of Fortune indicates that taking chances and playing the game of love is worth it – victory is guaranteed if you trust in the cosmic forces. It’s a sign that it will take some risks for you to get to the other side of your love story. The Hanged Man tarot card serves as a reminder that even when times feel tough, there will be something valuable learned from the experience.

At this time, it is important to stay open and listen to your intuition when it comes to taking action. Even though luck is on your side, The Wheel of Fortune tarot card and The Hanged Man tarot card together suggest that you should also weigh your options carefully and make sure you are confident in the decision you make. Be patient and remember that even when it doesn’t feel like it, your love life is progressing in the right direction.

### What does The Wheel Of Fortune and The Hanged Man mean together for your finances?

When The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card and The Hanged Man tarot card are combined in terms of finances and job, it suggests that opportunities for luck and good fortune are arising. This could be in the form of a new job position, a promotion, an unexpected financial windfall, or some other form of gain for you. It is important to understand that the Wheel of Fortune represents a turning point and not just ‘luck’ as such – it is an indication of the risks taken and the chance to make, or even break, your financial future.

More importantly, The Hanged Man Tarot card suggests that this change is not inevitable and requires a different perspective in order to turn your luck into success. The Hanged Man suggests wisdom and self-sacrifice, and in this regard the combined meaning of the two cards could suggest some specific decisions to be made which may require you to change your outlook and priorities in relation to your job and finances.

For example, it might suggest that you might need to take a chance and make a risky decision or change direction in order to maximise your potential for success. You may also need to invest some personal time and effort into realising your goals, as this is the only way to reap the rewards of the Wheel Of Fortune. It might also suggest that you might need to make a sacrifice of your current security in order to progress onto bigger and better opportunities.

Ultimately, The Wheel of Fortune and The Hanged Man Tarot cards combined suggest that the chance for good fortune is here, and a wise and self-sacrificing approach will be necessary to ensure success. This may require a degree of risk-taking as well as a willingness to change direction and make the necessary investments in order to realise your goals.

### What does The Wheel Of Fortune and The Hanged Man mean together for your health?

When The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card and The Hanged Man tarot card appear together, it can be a sign of major change for the better. This symbolizes the fated fate of good fortune and luck coming your way, but the caveat to this positive change is that you need to be willing to make a sacrifice. The Wheel of Fortune symbolizes a potentially beneficial turning point, something that has the potential to truly help you out. But, this change comes with the necessity to let go of some of your own desires and accept what is being presented. The Hanged Man tarot card symbolizes the need for critical thinking and wisdom, requiring you to take a step back and look at your situation from different perspectives to make the most of the opportunities the Wheel of Fortune is presenting. When combined, these two cards can indicate that to take full advantage of the changes coming your way, whether it be in health or other areas of your life, you need to put aside your own ideas and desires and look at what needs to be done, and while this self-sacrifice and wisdom can be difficult, it has the potential to reward you greatly. The combination of these two cards also encourage you to trust in the process and that the changes being presented are ultimately for the best.

When it comes to health specifically, the Wheel of Fortune and the Hanged Man tarot cards can be a sign of a fresh start and the potential for improved well-being, but it requires a willingness to accept what is being presented and to look at the situation from multiple angles. It might signify a drastic change in lifestyle and habits, such as switching to a healthier diet, more frequent exercise, getting more rest, or managing stress levels in more effective ways. This can be a challenging process, especially if you are used to a routine and if what you are being asked to change seems challenging or uncomfortable. The combination of the two cards indicates that, even if it is difficult, self-sacrifice and wisdom will lead to the best outcome – whether it is the improved health you are looking for, an easier physical routine, or the ability to finally take control of your wellbeing. With an open mind and a willingness to let go of your stubbornness and fear, you can use these cards to make a positive and lasting change, and to truly reap the rewards of the Wheel of Fortune.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Wheel Of Fortune and The Hanged Man mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot Card is associated with the power of fate, luck and the potential for change and transformation. When it appears in a Tarot reading, it signifies the turning of the wheel of fortune and the belief that no matter what life throws your way, you will be able to handle it and make the most of the situation. For health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot Card indicates a time where a healing cycle is beginning, and where the natural healing energy of the Universe is beginning to flow into your life. This could be the start of a period of healing for physical, mental and spiritual ailments, and could even be the start of an entire new chapter in your life.

For finances, the Wheel of Fortune card suggests that you could be in for an unexpected windfall or a major change in your financial situation. This could mean a raise or promotion, a new business venture, or a surprise inheritance. In addition, it could mean that you will be presented with a gambit of chances to make money, or that you may choose to take a gamble in the hopes that it will pay off.

Finally, the Wheel of Fortune card can reveal information on relationships. It could mean that a current relationship is in a state of flux, or that a new beginning is on the horizon. It could also mean that taking a chance on a new relationship could pay off in the end and bring new love into your life. Additionally, the card could suggest that it is time to move on from an unhealthy relationship, or that the Universe is blessing you with an opportunity to start fresh in an existing relationship.

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot Card is a symbol of change and of luck and fortune, and when it appears in a Tarot reading, it can give valuable insight on the areas of health, finances, and relationships.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card is symbolic of the need to look at any given situation from a different perspective. This card is often interpreted to mean wisdom, self-sacrifice, and the willingness to give up the “comfortable” status quo in order to grow and learn. Thus, when considering the implications of The Hanged Man card in regards to health, finances and relationships, it suggests that a shift in perspective and a willingness to sacrifice or change certain beliefs or habits may be necessary in order to achieve greater balance and growth in all three of these arenas.

When it comes to health, The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages us to consider how we can adjust our routines and behaviors in order to seek greater health benefits. This means being willing to let go of unhealthy habits in order to make space for healthier ones, even if this involves sacrifice or the temporary uncomfortable feelings associated with change.

When it comes to money, The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages us to take a step back and re-evaluate our finances from a different point of view. It asks us to think about how our current spending habits may be limiting our financial potential in the future, and whether it would be beneficial to sacrifice some current comforts in order to create a more sustainable financial future.

Finally, The Hanged Man Tarot card, when related to relationships, encourages us to look anew at our existing relationships and consider how we may be able to make them more meaningful and supportive. It asks us whether there are any changes that need to be made in order to make our connections more fulfilling and beneficial. Ultimately, this card advises us to be willing to let go of certain entrenched beliefs or behaviors which may be limiting our ability to build strong, lasting relationships.
