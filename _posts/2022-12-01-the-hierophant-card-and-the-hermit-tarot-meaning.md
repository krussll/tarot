A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and The Hermit card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and The Hermit have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and The Hermit mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The Hermit tarot card together suggest a journey of balance. On the one hand, we must adhere to the traditions and expectations of our society. On the other hand, we must also give ourselves the opportunity for spiritual growth by attuning ourselves to our personal truth. The Hierophant tarot card encourages us to take our rightful place in society and acknowledge its norms, while The Hermit tarot card reminds us to take the time to go within and explore our inner world. We need to take a balanced approach when it comes to conformity and personal truth; otherwise, we can quickly feel caught between two opposing forces.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Hierophant tarot card and The Hermit tarot card offer a unique combination of messages when it comes to your love life.

The Hierophant card indicates a need to conform to the traditional expectations of relationships and partnerships within your social circle. It’s a reminder to follow societal conventions when it comes to romance, to take the accepted paths when it comes to interactions, and to honor customs when it comes to courtship. Even if the outcome might be more comfortable than passionate in the conventional sense, the Hierophant card suggests that sometimes it is best to trust in the tried and true in matters of the heart.

On the other hand, the Hermit card alerts you to the importance of an inner journey and solitude in matters of the heart. As meaningful and fulfilling as relationships can be, the Hermit card reminds us that true honesty, integrity, and truth come from within. The Hermit is a reminder to take the time to reflect on yourself, your identity, and your emotions. To look inward and to figure out what really matters to you when it comes to love.

Together, the Hierophant and Hermit cards offer a nuanced and balanced approach to matters of the heart. Yes, there is wisdom in conforming to the expectations and norms of love, but there is also great power in self-inquiry. Both cards remind us that there is no universal formula for love. That each relationship and individual is unique, and that understanding ourselves is the cornerstone of every healthy connection.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and The Hermit mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and The Hermit the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health: The Hierophant Tarot card can represent the importance of maintaining a healthy lifestyle in many ways. It encourages us to stick to healthy eating habits, exercise regularly, and get enough rest. It also shows us the importance of taking care of our bodies by seeing a doctor for check-ups, going to the dentist, and making sure our vaccinations are up to date. Following the advice of health professionals is key when it comes to our health and The Hierophant Tarot card can remind us to do so.

Finances: The Hierophant Tarot card can represent the need to stay within our financial means and stick to a budget. It is important to think about our spending habits and plan for the future. The Hierophant Tarot card reminds us to save for emergency funds and retirement, as well as investing wisely and using our money to our best advantage. It can be beneficial to consult with a financial advisor or look into creating a financial plan that works for us.

Relationships: The Hierophant Tarot card can also remind us to be mindful of our relationships with others. It encourages us to practice healthy communication and maintain respectful boundaries with our friends, family, and partners. It is important to remember the importance of compromise and to be considerate of one another’s feelings. The Hierophant Tarot card is a reminder to practice open and honest communication and promote mutual respect.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card is a call for a person to reflect inwards and gain clarity in their life. This can be applied to all aspects of life, including health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Hermit Tarot card suggests that one takes time to reflect on their physical, mental and emotional well-being. This means taking time to listen to one’s body and inner voice, and responding accordingly. If a person is feeling unwell or out of balance, they need to take time to go within and find the answers they need. Perhaps they need to take better care of themselves, or perhaps they need to ask for help. Whatever the case, the Hermit Tarot card encourages the individual to listen to their body and make any necessary changes to improve their health.

When it comes to finances, the Hermit Tarot card suggests taking a moment to reflect on how one treats their money. It encourages one to be truly honest with themselves and take steps to make financial decisions that truly reflect their values and needs. This means going within and listening to one’s inner wisdom and choosing a course of action that is responsible and beneficial.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Hermit Tarot card advises one to go within and make sure that their relationships are based on healthy, honest self-reflection. This means taking the time to consider if a particular relationship is nourishing and healthy for the individual. If it is not, then the Hermit Tarot card suggests taking the necessary steps to reassess and make any changes that are needed. It also encourages the individual to be honest with themselves and their partners in order to foster authentic and meaningful connections.
