A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 2 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 2 Of Swords tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card together suggest a difficult situation regarding a loss or abandonment. You may feel as though there is no good decision that can be made, or that too much has been lost. This can be a difficult time where you feel stuck because you need to make a decision, yet it will cause further loss or feelings of regret. It is time to think carefully and consider options that will lead to the best outcome, no matter how difficult the decision may be. Compromise, understanding, and patience can get you through this trying time.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 2 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The 2 Of Swords tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card appear together in a love reading, it could mean that you have reached a stalemate in your relationship. You may feel that your emotions and desires are at odds, and that you cannot reach a mutual understanding. The 2 Of Swords tarot card asks you to analyze the situation and make a difficult decision if you are to continue. It is not enough to simply remain in a state of limbo, and you must take steps to move forward.

At the same time, the 5 Of Cups tarot card highlights the negative consequences of this impasse. The feeling of loss and bereavement in this card suggests that you may feel abandoned and unloved. There is a sense of regret that comes from not being able to resolve the issue and create a satisfying outcome for you both.

The combination of these two cards urges you to proceed cautiously and with sensitivity. You must be willing to compromise if you are to reach a resolution but also to accept the possibility that the relationship may not be able to continue in its current form. This is a difficult time, but you must take the time to weigh up the situation and follow your intuition. It is possible to come back from the brink and find a new way forward together.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

Health: The 5 of Cups card can mean that you are feeling overwhelmed and overwhelmed with the current physical and mental state that you are in. Physically it could be a sign of being run down, having a weakened immune system, or even suffering from a severe illness. Mentally, this card could indicate that you feel like you have been abandoned by the treatments and solutions that you have sought out in the past. You could be feeling unwell, exhausted, and without hope for improvement.

Finances: The 5 of Cups tarot card could refer to the loss of finances that you have recently sustained. It could mean that you are feeling the pinch of budgeting your income and expenditure, or that you feel like you have been left with no resources to get by. This card could allude to feeling a sense of loss, an inability to make up for what was previously had, and ultimately being left in a position of financial difficulty.

Relationships: The 5 of Cups tarot card can mean that relationships have taken a turn for the worse. It could mean that you feel like you have been abandoned by those close to you, that you feel a sense of loneliness, or disconnectedness from your social circle. This card could also refer to the loss of a relationship, to the feeling of sadness and regret experienced upon the end of the relationship. Ultimately, it could refer to the feeling of being lost, without a sense of love or connection from those around you.
