However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card signify a sense of being constrained by social expectations and pressures. There is an element of imprisonment in the strictures of societal norms, making it difficult to break free from the traditional constraints and choose one’s own path. The struggle between the values of tradition and personal autonomy will be difficult to reconcile, and one must be aware of the self-imposed restrictions that come with conforming to the expectations of others.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and 8 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 8 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health: The Hierophant Tarot card can signify traditional approaches to health. This could mean seeking out conventional treatments from trusted medical professionals, or sticking to treatments that have worked in the past. It could also mean being cautious and avoiding activities which are not deemed healthy, such as substance abuse.

Finances: The Hierophant Tarot card can signify a conservative approach to finances. This could mean avoiding taking risks with investments, or instead allocating resources gradually and in small doses, depending on what has worked in the past. The card could also signify the importance of living within one’s means and budgets, to ensure financial stability and security.

Relationships: The Hierophant Tarot card can symbolise using traditional methods to build and maintain relationships. This could mean relying on well-established methods of communication, such as face-to-face meetings, instead of texting and social media. It could also mean relying on tried-and-tested relationship-building behaviours, such as following etiquette, expressing gratitude, and allowing for mutual understanding.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, The 8 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take care of yourself. It may indicate that you are feeling exhausted, overwhelmed or possibly a little “stuck” in some way. It suggests that you are preventing yourself from making progress or receiving the help you need. You may be limiting yourself by not taking the necessary steps to care for your mental and physical health. You can benefit from seeking help, reframing your thoughts, talking to someone you trust, or taking a break to recharge.

When it comes to finances, The 8 of Swords Tarot card implies that you may be too rigid with your budget. It could mean that you are not taking risks in order to reach financial goals, or you may be routinely sticking to a tried-and-true financial plan despite the potential for growth. This card encourages you to be open to seeing financial opportunities in new ways, and to consider taking methods beyond the norm in order to find financial stability.

Finally, The 8 of Swords Tarot card brings warnings when it comes to relationships. This card emphasizes the need for communication and self-expression. When it comes to relationships, it can indicate a person’s reluctance to open up or take chances in order to make connections. You may find yourself stuck in a pattern of self-isolation, where you are not allowing yourself to be vulnerable or to put yourself out there in order to experience more meaningful connections. The 8 of Swords requires you to reach out, break down your walls and take risks if you want to experience fulfilling relationships with others.
