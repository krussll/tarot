## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace Of Pentacles Tarot card is a card that symbolizes abundance and new beginnings when it comes to health, finances and relationships. On a physical level, this card can suggest that one is now in a position to take better care of themselves, either through increased financial stability or through an increased awareness of one’s physical health. This can be the start of a new diet, or the beginning of a fitness routine that can bring about much needed improvement in physical well-being.

In terms of finances, the Ace of Pentacles can be an indication that something new is coming into one’s life that will bring stability and abundance. This could be anything from a raise or promotion, to an investment that will bring in long-term returns. However, it could also be a warning sign to be aware of any shady or risky financial decisions or investments that can potentially harm one’s financial stability.

Finally, the Ace of Pentacles can suggest that the start of a new relationship could be in the cards, or that the relationships one already has are about to enter a new phase. This card could bring about a healing of any past issues, a period of growth, or a deepening of existing bonds. It could also mean that the person is ready to enter a new phase of their life in which they are ready to open up to the idea of a loving relationship.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

The Ace of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the power of intelligence and reason. It’s associated with new ideas, mental clarity, and the ability to break through mental barriers. In the Tarot, this card is associated with the element of air, which symbolizes objectivity and clarity of thought.

When it comes to health, The Ace of Swords Tarot card is associated with mental clarity and the ability to make decisions quickly and efficiently. It can be a card of healing and transformation and provides a reminder that mental health is just as important as physical health. This card may represent going in for surgery or other health treatments for an ailment. It can mark a time of physical healing and mental clarity.

When it comes to finances, The Ace of Swords Tarot card may indicate that a sudden, unexpected change-of-fortune is coming your way. This card can suggest that it’s time to make a decision and that you need to think through the available options carefully. It can also be a sign that the best decision is to cut your losses, or that a new idea or strategy is needed to meet your financial goals.

Relationships can be complicated, and The Ace of Swords Tarot card can represent the need for clarity and objectivity in resolving conflicts. This card can be a sign that it’s time to cut ties with a toxic partner, or to find a new way of approaching a difficult situation. It can also be a reminder to think before speaking and to focus on finding solutions instead of arguing. The Ace of Swords may also represent a newfound understanding and harmony in a relationship, as well as a deepening of understanding and empathy.
