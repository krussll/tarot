However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

In regards to health, The 6 of Wands Tarot card suggests that the individual is making progress in managing their health. They have taken proactive steps to make positive changes in their lifestyle, diet, exercise and/or medical treatments, and are now seeing the fruits of their labor. The 6 of WandsTarot card is an indication that the individual should stay the course and maintain their hard work, as their health will soon be fully restored and their energy levels will skyrocket.

When it comes to finances, The 6 of Wands Tarot card suggests the individual has been diligent in their money management and they are now experiencing the rewards of their hard work. The individual has been able to pay off debts, set up a budget and build a savings, and is now able to enjoy the newfound stability. The 6 of Wands Tarot card is an indicator that the individual should now focus on investments, exploring ways to make their money work for them in the long run.

Lastly, The 6 of Wands Tarot card in regards to relationships signifies deep emotional connection and understanding. The individual is in a healthy relationship that is built on trust, respect and mutual understanding, and this is allowing them to bloom and grow. The 6 of Wands Tarot card is an indicator that the individual should take the time to nurture and nourish the relationship, making sure they are fulfilling their partner’s needs while still meeting their own. It is also a reminder that relationships take work and effort, meaning the individual should not take it for granted.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Knight Of Swords Tarot card symbolizes physical and mental changes that will affect your overall health. It represents a swift and sudden transformation, so even if the transformation is positive, be prepared for some bumps along the way. It suggests that a fast pace will be needed to keep up with the changes, and that you’ll need to focus on taking care of your body and mind. Be sure to make time for regular exercise and healthy eating habits, as this will help to make the transformation easier.

Finances  
The Knight Of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a major financial change. It could be a job offer that you never expected, a pay raise or a sudden windfall. Either way, it signals a need to make a quick lifestyle adjustment to accommodate the sudden financial change. Financially, it might suggest taking a risk or making a smart investment. It could also signal a time to be more frugal and save for the future. Regardless, the message is to be prepared for the changes, and to make decisions that align with your long-term goals.

Relationships  
The Knight Of Swords Tarot card symbolizes new relationships full of passion, challenge and adventure. This could be a time to explore the potential of new relationships or to rekindle existing ones. However, it also suggests that these relationships will move quickly, so be prepared for passionate, intense conversations and debates. This could be a time to take risks and try new things, so don’t be afraid to explore new ideas, perspectives and activities. Just make sure to focus on communication and respect, as these are the foundations for successful relationships.
