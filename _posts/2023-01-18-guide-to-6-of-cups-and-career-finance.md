Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The 6 Of Cups card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 6 Of Cups, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 6 Of Cups.

## 6 Of Cups And Career And Finances – An Overview

The 6 Of Cups tarot card is indicative of a need to get in touch with our inner child and practice self-care.

This card represents a time for reflection and nostalgia, reminding us to look back on our past with innocence and joy.

Taking time to reflect on our past can help us to heal and make better decisions for our future.

The 6 Of Cups also represents gifts, suggesting that we should treat ourselves to something that nurtures our mind, body, and spirit.

This could be anything from a therapeutic massage to a quiet walk in nature.

Finally, this card also symbolizes reunions and reconnecting with people in our lives that we haven’t seen in a while.

Reconnecting with those we love and care about can help to alleviate stress and anxiety, improving our overall health.

## Upright Meanings For 6 Of Cups And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The 6 of Cups tarot card suggests that my career and finances are likely to be marked with nostalgia, gifts, and innocence.

It could mean that I am in for a period of reunions and unexpected surprises that will bring me back to my roots and remind me of why I started this career journey in the first place.

This card speaks to the idea of luck and fortune, and I may be presented with unanticipated opportunities and gifts that will help me to succeed in my career and make financial gains.

I should keep an open mind, as these could be career-transforming or even life-changing moments, but I should also keep in mind that these are likely to be unexpected and will come out of the blue.

### What It Means For The Future

The 6 of Cups is a powerful card for those looking to make progress in their career and finances.

It indicates that nostalgia and memories of the past can play a role in your success.

It also suggests that gifts, either given or received, will be important in helping you achieve your goals.

The card is a reminder to not lose sight of your innocence and optimism, as these will be instrumental in achieving your dreams.

It’s also symbolic of reunions, which could indicate that networking and reconnecting with people from your past may be key in obtaining financial resources and career stability.

The 6 of Cups suggests that looking to the past could be a great way to secure your future success.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 6 of Cups tarot card is a sign of nostalgia, gifts, innocence, and reunions.

It can represent the careers and finances of those close to me in a positive light.

It suggests that those close to me have likely enjoyed financial stability and success in their careers.

The card suggests that they have likely done the hard work to get to where they are, but that their current financial situation is rewarding and stable.

Additionally, the 6 of Cups can point to reuniting with old friends or mentors in a career-related context that can bring about even more financial gain.

Ultimately, the 6 of Cups can be a sign of abundance and success for those close to me.

## Reversed Meanings For 6 Of Cups And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When The 6 Of Cups appears reversed in a reading related to career and finances, it indicates stagnancy and lack of progress.

It suggests that you may be stuck in a rut, feeling as though your career or financial goals are out of reach.

You may be finding it difficult to find motivation or make the necessary changes to advance in your career or financial situation.

It can also symbolize fear of change and the possibility of failure.

You may be clinging to the past, which is preventing you from taking risks in the present.

Reversed The 6 Of Cups encourages you to take a step back and reassess your career and financial goals.

It is important to remember that change is necessary for growth and that taking risks can lead to great rewards.

It is time to move out of the comfort zone and take action in order to reach your goals.

It is time to trust yourself and have faith that you can make your career and financial dreams come true.

With the right attitude, hard work, and dedication, you can reach success.

### What It Means For The Future

When The 6 Of Cups tarot card appears in a reading reversed, it can be interpreted as a sign that you may feel stuck in your current career path or financial situation.

You may be feeling a sense of stagnation or lack of progress, and could be stuck in a rut.

This could lead to a feeling of being weighed down by the same responsibilities or obligations, and an inability to move forward.

The reversed 6 Of Cups could also indicate a lack of strong financial investments or a lack of resources that could help to facilitate a change in your career.

There may be an opportunity to change but it could take some creative problem-solving to make it happen.

It could also mean that you’ll need to take some risks in order to achieve your goals, and you may need to be willing to step out of your comfort zone.

It could also indicate that you need to let go of any nostalgia or sentimentality that may be holding you back, and focus on the present and the future.

Despite the difficulties and obstacles, this card is ultimately a sign of hope and a reminder that with determination and dedication, you can make progress towards achieving your goals.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Six of Cups appears in a reading concerning the careers and finances of those close to me, it can indicate that there is a sense of stagnation in their lives.

They may be struggling to find a way to advance their career or increase their finances, feeling like they have hit a wall.

They may be feeling nostalgic for a past job or financial success, but unable to find the same level of success.

This card could also suggest that there is a lack of innocence in their financial situation, as the reversed Six of Cups can indicate a lack of trust in those close to them or a feeling of being taken advantage of.

It could also be a sign of a strained relationship with someone, preventing them from achieving the success they desire.

The reversed Six of Cups can also be an indication that gifts and reunions may not be coming as easily as in the past, and can suggest that any reunions or gifts are more likely to be in the form of emotional support rather than tangible items.
