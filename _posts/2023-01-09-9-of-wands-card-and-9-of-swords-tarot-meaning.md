A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 9 Of Wands and The 9 Of Swords tarot cards, when combined, tell a story of both resilience and despair. The 9 Of Wands symbolizes the need to keep your boundaries in order and to be persistent in protecting them, while The 9 Of Swords speaks of the nightmares, anxiety, grief and depression that may arise from such struggles of maintaining one’s boundaries. Although these cards emphasize the darkness of these challenges, they also show the strength of our capacity to confront the difficulties in our lives and to come out stronger, despite our suffering.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The 9 of Wands Tarot card can represent different things depending on the context and the rest of the cards in the reading, but generally it signifies the need for maintaining boundaries and persisting no matter the difficulties that arise. When it comes to health, the 9 of Wands indicates being mindful and proactive in taking care of one’s physical, mental, and emotional well-being. One should ensure to balance rest and activity, stick to healthy routines, and ask for help if needed in order to create the best conditions for optimal health. In the context of finances, this card suggests that careful planning and persistence can lead to financial stability. Building a budget and planning ahead can help to avoid costly mistakes and provide a sense of security and peace of mind. Relationships also benefit from the energy of the 9 of Wands. It encourages healthy boundaries and communication in order to ensure that all parties remain satisfied and respected. It also implies being persistent in the face of conflict, and patience and understanding in order to resolve any issues that arise.

All in all, the 9 of Wands Tarot card serves as a reminder that maintaining boundaries and persisting in the face of adversity can lead to positive outcomes, whether it’s in the realm of health, money, or relationships. Cupidity, careful planning, and an open mind can pave the way for a fulfilling and healthy future.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

In terms of health, the 9 of Swords tarot card can portend serious health issues. It can suggest that a physical ailment is causing emotional stress or conversely, it may indicate that mental anguish is causing physical symptoms. In either case, this card is indicative of a person feeling overwhelmed by the severity of the situation. It could also point to a person who is dealing with a long-term illness or condition that has taken a toll on their physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

When it comes to finances, the 9 of Swords tarot card can signal financial difficulties that are shaking one’s faith and stability. It can signal issues such as job loss, debt, or difficulty making ends meet. It can be an omen of a financial disaster looming on the horizon or it can illustrate a person’s fear of money and difficulty managing it.

In terms of relationships, the 9 of Swords tarot card can point to a person’s fear of communicating openly and honestly with their partners. It can signal that a person is struggling to be vulnerable and trusting in their relationships because of the fear of being hurt. It can suggest that a person is dealing with feelings of loneliness and isolation due to their inability to open up to those around them. This card can also mean that a person is expecting too much from their relationships without giving enough in return.
