A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 3 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and 3 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 3 Of Pentacles mean together?

When The Justice tarot card and The 3 Of Pentacles are combined, it symbolizes working together to create a win-win solution with collective skill and quality. As a result, the truth of any situation can be brought to the forefront with ease. This combination encourages cooperation, collaboration, and teamwork in order to achieve effective outcomes and achieve success. It also stresses the importance of craftsmanship, ensuring that your efforts are of the highest possible quality. The combination of Justice and 3 Of Pentacles illustrates the importance of building strong teams and working together to reach an equitable solution that benefits everyone.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 3 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It encourages us to take a holistic approach to our physical and mental health, taking into account the way that our physical health and mental wellbeing can intertwine and affect us. Justice can also be seen as a reminder to be mindful of our dietary and exercise charts and to ensure that these are not only in line with our own personal health goals, but also with regulations and laws that govern how we look after our bodies. It also speaks of using our intuition to determine what is best for our health, considering both the short and long-term effects that any health decision may have.

Finances:  
The Justice Tarot card is particularly helpful when it comes to looking at finances. It encourages us to make responsible decisions with our money and to consider the long-term impact of those decisions. This card can also be seen as a reminder to stay true to ourselves and our values, to think critically and objectively when considering financial matters, and to be aware of our own limitations when it comes to money. Justice can also serve as an invitation to look into potential solutions that can lead to a win-win situation, such as budgeting, renegotiating contracts, or researching investments.

Relationships:  
Justice Tarot is often seen as a reminder to treat ourselves and those around us fairly when it comes to our relationships. It encourages us to stay mindful of the effects of our words and actions on those around us and to be mindful of the impact that our relationships can have on us. This card also speaks to the importance of speaking our truth and of being honest in our interactions. It also serves as a reminder to not let our relationships become stagnant or one-sided, but to strive to maintain a healthy balance that is beneficial for all parties involved.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of teamwork and collaboration that suggests working together to achieve a common purpose. It is a card of quality and craftsmanship and reflects a deep level of skill and talent associated with any project, partnership or endeavor. When this card appears in a Tarot reading, it can indicate a time of organizational, financial and creative success.

Health: The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of teamwork and collaboration that encourages healthy partnerships and collaboration between teams of professionals. It can suggest that working closely with a specialist or a doctor to develop a health care plan is a good and beneficial option. Collaboration between health care professionals may also allow for a more balanced and holistic approach to health, and the team can find creative solutions to any health-related issues.

Finances: The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of teamwork and collaboration that can indicate a positive period in terms of financial stability. When this card appears, it can suggest that working with an accountant or financial advisor to create a budget and plan for future success is a wise choice. It can also indicate a strong team effort involved in achieving and maintaining financial stability, be it with a partner, family member or even a financial institution.

Relationships: The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of teamwork and collaboration that encourages communication and collaboration to maintain and strengthen relationships. This is a card that speaks of skill and craftsmanship in terms of relationships, and it can suggest that it is important to be open and honest with one another to resolve any problems that may arise. It is a card of compromise and negotiation, and it can suggest that the ability to work together as a team is key to the success of any relationship.
