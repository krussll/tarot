## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is often seen as a card of heartbreak, because it represents a deep emotional pain that comes from loss. This card can signify a separation from a loved one, a break-up, a divorce, or the death of someone close to you. It can also signify depression and sadness that has been brought on in life as a result of a deep emotional pain.

When it comes to health, the 3 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a need to heal from emotional pain or trauma that has been caused by a difficult situation. It can refer to a physical ailment or illness that has been brought on by emotional stress. It can also signify the need for surgery, or a medical procedure that has been done to correct an underlying issue that has been plaguing one’s health.

When it comes to finances, the 3 of Swords Tarot card can represent a financial loss due to a break-up or a divorce. It can also represent a need to cut back on expenses and focus on important needs, in order to heal from the emotional pain associated with the situation. It can indicate that one needs to accept that a difficult situation has happened and learn to move on from it.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 3 of Swords Tarot card can refer to a difficult situation that has caused a heartbreak or the end of a relationship. It can signify a need to accept that things have changed and let go of the pain that has been caused by the situation. It can also refer to a need to rebuild trust after a betrayal, or heal from any broken promises or hurt feelings. In any case, this card can represent an emotional pain that needs to be healed in order to move forward in a healthy relationship.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

The 4 of Swords tarot card can indicate a much-needed pause in life. It suggests that it is time to take a break and refresh before proceeding on to other activities. This card can promote rest and renewal, which is essential for physical and mental health, as well as overall wellbeing. It can be a reminder that sometimes it is important to take some time for ourselves, away from work and other obligations.

In terms of finances, 4 of Swords Tarot card encourages you to take a step back and evaluate your current financial situation. It is a sign that your finances need a break before you can move forward. You should take some time to assess your current financial situation, set achievable financial goals, and create a strategy to reach those goals. Taking a break will allow you to clear your mind and gain clarity on the best course of action to move forward with investments, budgeting and other financial decisions.

In terms of relationships, 4 of Swords Tarot card signifies that a period of introspection and internal healing is needed. It could be a sign that it is time to take a break from a toxic or difficult relationship and allow yourself some time and space to take care of your own needs. Taking a break is essential for any healthy relationship. It will allow for clarity, insight and new perspectives. This card may also suggest that it is time to look within and focus on strengthening your relationship with yourself, so that you can approach other relationships with a healthy and balanced mindset.
