A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Pentacles card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Pentacles card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 3 Of Pentacles and The 5 Of Cups tarot cards together represent the potential for growth, in spite of having felt loss, grief, and regret. While The 5 Of Cups can symbolise feeling abandoned and unloved, The 3 Of Pentacles provides a path forward – to find strength in the support and collaboration of others. By finding the skill and quality in the people and projects around us, we can work together to build a better, brighter future despite any feelings of heartache or abandonment. With the combined influences of these two cards, we can rebuild from our losses and allow ourselves to move forward.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The Three Of Pentacles Tarot card can mean working together when it comes to health. This card in a reading can suggest looking for help from medical experts to improve your physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing. It encourages you to look for support from trusted individuals, both medical professionals and in the community, to create a plan for improving your health and maintaining it. This card can also indicate that it’s time to take the initiative and make changes to your lifestyle and environment that will have a positive effect on your overall health.

Finances:

The Three Of Pentacles Tarot card can symbolize teamwork when it comes to finances. This card encourages cooperation and collaboration with others to create a plan and a strategy for financial success. It can indicate working with a financial planner or other financial professionals to build a plan that will help you achieve your financial objectives. It also urges you to look for support from colleagues or family members who can help you make and stick to better financial choices.

Relationships:

The Three Of Pentacles Tarot card can signify collaboration and quality when it comes to relationships. This card suggests that it is time to look for support from the people you care about so that you can build a strong, lasting connection. It can indicate that it is time to take a step back and analyze the relationship, including what is working and what is not and then making changes that will be beneficial for both parties. It also urges you to be open and honest with each other, paying attention to each other’s feelings and needs, and working together to build a fulfilling relationship.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The Five Of Cups Tarot card often implies a gloomy outlook when it comes to health. For example, if you are worried about a health condition, this card can suggest that those worries may be well-founded. You may find yourself feeling emotionally drained and drained of energy, as the card indicates a sense of loss with regards to your health. This card also suggests feelings of regret over past decisions that have had a negative impact on your health. It is a reminder to take time to pause and reflect on the choices you have made and what could change going forward.

Finances:

The Five Of Cups Tarot card in the context of finances can signify a feeling of regret or loss of financial security. It can indicate that you feel as if you have spent or squandered your resources recklessly or without thought. This card can also mean that you are staving off feelings of financial despair and insecurity. It suggests that you take time to evaluate your financial situation and find a path forward that can help to restore your financial strength.

Relationships:

The Five Of Cups Tarot card in the context of relationships can indicate a feeling of being abandoned, ill-used, or unloved. It can suggest that you underwent a difficult break-up, or that an important relationship has ended with a feeling of bitter disappointment. This card can also hint at the potential for a past lover to return, although it is not a guarantee. It may be a subtle reminder to consider the importance of love, compassion, and understanding in all relationships.
