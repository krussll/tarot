## What does 4 Of Wands and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The 4 Of Wands tarot card and The 4 Of Swords tarot card together mean a beautiful balance of homecoming and retreat. On the one hand, this card combination symbolizes celebrating with family and friends as we reconnect and reunite with those we love. On the other hand, it speaks to the importance of taking time for ourselves – retreating for rest, to recover and renew our energy, and be comfortable in our own solitude. Together, these two cards offer a reminder to enjoy the company of others and to retreat to nurture our own soul.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 4 of Wands Tarot card can be a symbol of good health, both mentally and physically. It suggests that we are on the right path and that we are taking good care of our bodies and minds. It could be a sign that we are gaining strength and vitality and taking time to rest and recuperate. We can look for signs that our physical and mental well-being is improving and that we should take time out to celebrate our progress.

Finances

The 4 of Wands Tarot card can also be interpreted to mean that our finances are in a stable and secure place. Although we may not be making massive gains, we are likely to have a consistent source of income and be on track to building long-term wealth and security. This card suggests that although we may not have the means to make extravagant purchases, we are capable of providing for our basic needs.

Relationships

The 4 of Wands Tarot card can be a symbol of family, friends, and reunions. We may be feeling a renewed sense of connection to people in our lives, especially those who have been close for a long time. This card can also represent reunion after a strained period of separation. Whether we have been separated from family and friends due to a disagreement, or just have been busy and haven’t had time to reconnect, the 4 of Wands Tarot card can suggest that we are making amends and finding our way back to each other.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

For Health

The 4 of Swords tarot card can represent taking a break from the normal pace of life and retreating to a quiet space for some self-care and renewal. In this way, it encourages us to take a step back from our current activities and take some time out for ourselves, in order to let our physical and mental health recharge. This could take the form of intentional and mindful rest, yoga, or taking a vacation. It can also indicate that a period of healing is needed, whether that is through traditional methods or alternative practices. This could mean seeking professional help if necessary, or simply finding moments in the day to actively practice self-compassion and patience.

For Finances

The 4 of Swords tarot card can indicate a time for financial retreat. This could mean making the decision to take a break from accumulating new debt, or drawing a line when it comes to taking on new financial commitments. It can also mean slowing down our spending and taking a step back from the pressure to get ahead. This can lead us to taking a more mindful approach to our finances, with the intention of creating greater financial security in the long term.

For Relationships

The 4 of Swords tarot card can indicate that a period of retreat is necessary in our relationships. This can be a challenging process, as it involves putting our needs before others and making the conscious choice to take a break from the frenetic pace of our connections. It can also mean setting boundaries and taking time out to focus on our own healing and growth, as well as understanding ourselves better through practices such as journaling and meditation. In doing so, we can make conscious decisions about which relationships are nourishing and which are draining, without causing too much distress or disruption.
