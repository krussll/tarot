A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and the 3 Of Cups tarot card combine to symbolize the balance between conformity and celebration, between tradition and friendship. This combination speaks to honoring established customs and conventions while also taking the time to share joy and love with the people closest to you. In this way, your experiences, beliefs and community can both be celebrated and embraced. Together, these cards remind us of the importance of both growth and comfort, of finding community and strength in the traditions that have been handed down to us.

On one hand, The Hierophant tarot card could symbolize actively engaging in the customary activities, such as going on dates to movies or dinners, and expressing your love for your partner in conventional ways. On the other hand, The 3 of Cups could symbolize having fun with your beloved, planning special celebrations together, and taking a break from the strictures of societal conventions.

In order to create a successful and meaningful love life, you will need to find a healthy balance between expressing your feelings for one another in traditional ways and speaking through unique expressions of love such as creating special moments or having fun activities together. This could involve anything from allowing yourself to be vulnerable and express your needs openly and honestly, or simply taking the time to treat each other to something special like a surprise date or a getaway vacation.

It’s important to note that this balance must be mutual. If you are the one primarily taking care of the more traditional expressions of love and your partner is only providing the more unconventional ones, it won’t work. A balanced relationship depends on both partners making an effort to express love in both traditional and unconventional ways.

Similarly, if you are both being too resistant to traditions or lack of appreciation for the more unconventional expressions of love, then it won’t work either. Finding a middle ground between the two is essential for a successful and healthy relationship. If you and your partner can find a happy balance between The Hierophant tarot card and The 3 of Cups tarot card, then you will be well on your way to creating a beautiful and meaningful relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card is associated with tradition, conventional expectations, and conformity. In health, the card suggests that traditional approaches to healing work best, such as going to a doctor for a physical or taking medication for a condition. It suggests that the most beneficial route may not be the most exciting or interesting, but the most tried and true. The card encourages us to be disciplined in our health care and to stick with a prescribed plan of action. It also warns us to not be too rebellious in trying new treatments if they have not been medically proven.

Finances

For finances, the Hierophant Tarot card means traditional approaches, such as budgeting, saving and investing in stable assets such as stocks and bonds. It tells us to resist the temptation of get-rich-quick schemes and advises us to play by the rules of financial security and stability. It encourages us to look long-term in our planning and reminds us that while money is important, it should not be our main focus in life.

Relationships

For relationships, the Hierophant Tarot card suggests that traditional values and conventional expectations should be observed for stability and longevity. It suggests using common sense and being wary of straying too far out of the boundaries of societal norms. The card reminds us to treat our partners fairly and with respect, and to make sure we are following through on our commitments, both to ourselves and to our relationships. It also encourages us to be open to learning from our relationships, taking the benefit from them without getting too tied up in the expectations and rules of society.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups tarot card symbolizes a time of joy, good will and of coming together, the start of a new cycle of friendship and harmony. In terms of health, this card is a reminder to find balance in your life and to recognize the importance of friendships and connections in your life. You may be feeling inspired to take up a new fitness or health regime, or to nurture yourself through self-care and positive habits.

In terms of finances, this card is a reminder to not get caught up in the hustle and bustle of working and making money but to also take the time to appreciate and foster your relationships. The 3 of Cups encourages you to approach your work with passion and enthusiasm but to also remember to take time out to celebrate successes and successes of others too.

In terms of relationships, the 3 of Cups offers a reminder that the best relationships are built on a foundation of friendship. This is a time to nurture and foster your existing relationships and to seek out new ones too. From romantic relationships to platonic friendships and family bonds, take the time to recognize and appreciate these relationships in your life.
