This is a reminder to practice patience and self-control to attain the peace and satisfaction that comes from celebrating and being with people you love. With a sense of balance and self-control, you are able to create a life full of effortless joy and peace that can only be achieved through harmony in your day to day activities.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health:

When it comes to health, The Temperance Tarot card is about finding balance. It is about moderation, understanding that there are limits to how much we can push ourselves and how much we should do for our health. To be healthy, we must take care of our bodies, both physically and mentally. This includes eating healthy, exercising on a regular basis, and getting enough sleep. Additionally, having a balanced emotional life is also essential for health and well-being. Temperance encourages us to be patient with ourselves, to monitor our stress levels, and to practice self-care.

Finances:

The Temperance Tarot card is a great reminder to practice fiscal responsibility and to develop healthy attitudes towards money. This involves understanding that money is a tool, rather than a goal, and that we should use it to build towards long-term financial stability. Temperance encourages us to be patient as we build our financial portfolio, while also being mindful of our spending habits. It also suggests that we save regularly and invest wisely.

Relationships:

The Temperance Tarot card is about finding balance between ourselves and our relationships. It reminds us to be patient and understanding with our partners, friends, and family. Moreover, Temperance urges us to practice active listening and to be aware of our words and behavior. We should strive to be respectful, honest, and compassionate in our interactions. By being purposeful and mindful in our conversations, we can build and maintain stronger relationships. Furthermore, Temperance counsels us not to be too attached to outcomes, and to instead focus on the journey of getting there.
