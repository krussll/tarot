A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 2 Of Swords mean together?

The 5 of Wands and The 2 of Swords tarot card could point to the need to find a balance between recognizing and managing any competitive dynamics that may be present in your life and understanding when it’s necessary to make a decision. Although it may seem like there are numerous obstacles and rivals, you can use the wisdom the cards offer to find the best way to proceed. The 2 of Swords cautions to use patience and consideration when making a decision, as you might be able to reach a compromise that suits everyone involved. The 5 of Wands also encourages a sense of sportiness, so you can use it as motivation to stay focused and take your time.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 5 Of Wands and 2 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 5 Of Wands and 2 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

In terms of health, The 5 Of Wands Tarot card represents a need to take on a challenge or to strive for a goal. This card suggests that healthy habits and goals may be difficult to achieve, and that at times it can feel like there is too much competition to be the healthiest version of yourself. When this card appears, it signals that you may need to push yourself to stay focused and disciplined in order to reach your desired health goals.

In terms of finances, The 5 Of Wands Tarot card cautions against taking on too much risk and suggests that financial competition can lead to increased stress and difficulty in achieving your desired financial goals. It is important to remember that in order to be financially successful, it is important to be strategic and realistic. This card reminds you to be aware of the risks that come with different investments and to not be intimidated by others who are in more competitive financial positions.

When it comes to relationships, The 5 Of Wands Tarot card suggests that competition and obstacles may be present in current and potential relationships. Although it is healthy to have a good sense of competition, this card advises against being overly aggressive or competitive in relationships. Instead, it is important to focus on communication, trust, and collaboration to ensure the success of any relationship.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

For Health

The 2 of Swords Tarot card often indicates a need to make a difficult decision regarding health, such as whether to pursue a particular treatment or procedure. It can also be indicative of a need to compromise when it comes to health, such as finding an acceptable middle ground between two opposing treatments. Taking the time to listen to all sides and weigh the options will be essential in achieving a positive outcome. It is also important to remember that there is no right or wrong answer, and to trust your intuition when it comes to making choices about health.

For Finances

When the 2 of Swords appears in a Tarot card spread in relation to finances, it typically indicates the need to make a difficult decision or a hard choice. This could include whether to invest in a certain stock, deciding how to allocate money towards debt repayment, or choosing how to spend available funds. Trusting instinct and considering all options thoroughly are key to making the best possible decision for financial security. Comparing pros and cons, as well as seeking advice from a financial professional, can also be helpful.

For Relationships

The 2 of Swords Tarot card is often indicative of a need to compromise in a relationship. This could mean finding a way to balance the needs of both partners, or finding a middle ground between two opposing views. It is important to remember that compromise is an essential part of any successful relationship, and that any difficult decisions made should be done fairly and with respect to both parties. Taking the time to clearly understand each other’s point of view and listen to each other can help in reaching a decision that is mutually beneficial.
