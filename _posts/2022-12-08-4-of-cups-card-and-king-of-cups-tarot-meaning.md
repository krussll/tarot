## What does 4 Of Cups and King Of Cups mean together?

The 4 of Cups and King of Cups tarot cards when read together can signify a strong need to take a step back and reflect on the situation at hand. The 4 of Cups can be a call to examine why feelings of apathy and unfulfillment are being experienced. It could also signify a surprise gift entering your life. The King of Cups offers the supportive energy of a leader or mentor to help you find the answers. His energy of empathy and tolerance can be invaluable during this time, as he guides you through your inner thoughts and emotions. In combination, these two cards can help you to rediscover your passions, and find the motivation to pursue your dreams.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

The 4 of Cups Tarot card indicates a stagnant and unfulfilled state in all aspects of life. It suggests that the individual is feeling apathetic and uninspired. This energy can manifest in health, finances and relationships, leading to feelings of disinterest and disillusionment.

When it comes to health, the 4 of Cups Tarot card can signal that the person is feeling disconnected from their physical and psychological wellness. They may not be motivated to prioritize their own health and may be feeling uninterested in making the necessary changes to improve their wellbeing. This could manifest in unhealthy habits such as avoiding exercise, poor diet choices or ignoring a doctor’s orders.

In terms of finances, the 4 of Cups Tarot card reveals that the individual is uninspired and unmotivated to take action. They may feel that no matter how hard they try, their financial situation will remain the same and thus, give up on any efforts to improve their finances. They may not be able to see the long-term benefits of responsible financial management or saving for the future.

When it comes to relationships, the 4 of Cups Tarot card reveals that the person is feeling uninterested and disconnected. They may be feeling unfulfilled and in search of something more. They may be too wrapped up in their own feelings of discontentment to make the effort to nurture relationships. As a result, they may be missing out on the surprise gifts that relationships can bring.
