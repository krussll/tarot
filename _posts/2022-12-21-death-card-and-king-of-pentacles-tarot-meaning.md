You are the wise investor now, and the solid and practical leader. A rebirth is taking place in which you can see the end of a cycle or project and use this opportunity to start something new. You have the strength and wisdom to make this a successful change, and with careful thought and planning you can reach a stage of success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and King Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

The Death tarot card can indicate significant changes and endings in all aspects of life, from health, to finances to relationships.

When it comes to health, The Death tarot card can be a reminder that our physical health is limited and subject to change, indicating that it’s important to take care of ourselves while we can. The card can also signify a major health issue or life-altering illness, like cancer, that may require treatment, extensive therapy and lifestyle changes.

When it comes to finances, The Death tarot card can represent a significant ending, such as a job loss or a business closing. The card can also indicate a major financial change, like a large inheritance or a large debt that needs to be paid off. It can also signify a time of necessary financial transformation, such as budgeting, saving or finding new sources of income.

In relationships, The Death tarot card can mean the ending of a relationship or the start of a new one. It can also signify a major transformation in a relationship, such as a commitment like marriage, a change in physical location, or a dramatic shift in the dynamics. The card can also indicate a period of rebirth, where a relationship may need to be rebuilt and re-evaluated in order to strengthen the bond.

Ultimately, The Death tarot card can be interpreted in many different ways, but it is often associated with endings and new beginnings in all areas of life. Health, finances, and relationships can all see significant changes and transformations with this card, and it is important to remain open to change and be willing to make necessary adjustments.
