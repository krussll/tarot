## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Strength mean?

Health  
The Strength Tarot card means embracing physical and mental health. Self-love allows one to make healthy decisions in order to take care of their body, mind and spirit. This self-love and unconditional love leads to greater self-respect. With this respect comes the courage to do what’s best for your health. It can mean learning how to better manage stress and anxiety by developing healthy coping mechanisms, getting enough quality sleep and eating healthy. It also means going to the doctor and taking care of health needs in a timely manner. This ultimately leads to better overall health.

Finances  
Likewise, The Strength Tarot card can be used to guide one’s financial health. Self-love, self-respect and courage are necessary to make the money decisions that will benefit one’s finances. This means learning about money management, creating and adhering to a budget, paying debts on time and saving for the future. It’s also important to make smart investments for the long-term and have a plan for retirement.

Relationships  
The Strength Tarot card also provides insight into our relationships. Self-love, respect and courage can enable us to build and maintain healthy, meaningful relationships with those we care about. This means having the strength to stand up for yourself and speak up when something isn’t right. It also means having the courage to let go of relationships that are damaging and taking the risk to build relationships with people who can help us grow and support us. With these traits, we can start relationships on the right foot and nurture them as they continue to grow.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 10 of Cups is often associated with good health and a strong sense of physical and mental well-being. It indicates that one is surrounded by love, support and nourishment, both physical and emotional. Having a healthy diet and lifestyle is key to maintaining this feeling of contentment, as the card suggests that if one is healthy, both in body and mind, they will be able to enjoy the contentment of the 10 of Cups.

Finances: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is also thought to represent prosperity and financial success. In terms of wealth, it indicates that one is well taken care of and that they have enough to live comfortably. It is a card of abundance and is associated with a wealthy and secure lifestyle. Additionally, having a strong financial base can lead to a stable and secure emotional state, as the 10 of Cups suggests.

Relationships: The 10 of Cups is all about love, support, and contentment. It reflects the joy of being surrounded by close family and friends and the feeling of deep connection and commitment to those who are important to you. The card indicates that one will be able to find true happiness and joy, and that relationships will be strong and secure. With this card, one can know that they have a safe and secure relationship, with an abundance of love and support.
