A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Magician card and Ace Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Magician card and Ace Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Magician and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The Magician and The Ace of Swords together represent the power of focused creativity and mental clarity to turn visions into reality. This is a powerful combination for anyone seeking to manifest their dreams into the physical world. If you have been struggling to bring an idea to fruition, these cards offer hope that you can make it happen through hard work and perseverance. This is also an excellent combination for anyone seeking to improve their health, as the Ace of Swords can represent surgery or other medical procedures. If you have been considering making a change in your health regime, this is a sign that you have the inner strength and clarity to make it happen. In relationships, this combination suggests that you and your partner are able to communicate effectively and work together towards common goals. This is a relationship that is built on trust, respect and mutual support. If you have been experiencing difficulties in your relationship, this combination suggests that you have the power to work through them and come out stronger on the other side.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Magician and Ace Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Magician and Ace Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Magician mean?

For health, The Magician Tarot card means that you have the power to heal yourself. You are the architect of your own health and wellbeing, and have the ability to manifest your desires into reality. If you can focus your creative energy and put your mind to it, you can achieve anything you set your sights on.

When it comes to finances, The Magician Tarot card means that you have the power to create your own financial abundance. You are the master of your own destiny, and can manifest your dreams into reality. If you can focus your creative energy and put your mind to it, you can achieve anything you set your sights on.

In relationships, The Magician Tarot card means that you have the power to create the relationships you desire. You are the architect of your own happiness, and have the ability to manifest your dreams into reality. If you can focus your creative energy and put your mind to it, you can achieve anything you set your sights on.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

In terms of health, The Ace Of Swords Tarot card can signify new ideas and new conflict. This may manifest as someone having to make a tough decision regarding their health, or it may simply be a case of coming to terms with a new diagnosis. Either way, this card suggests that clarity is needed in order to make the best decisions for one’s health.

When it comes to finances, The Ace Of Swords Tarot card can again suggest new ideas or conflict. This time, however, it may be about making a fresh start or coming to terms with a financial setback. Either way, this card suggests that financial matters need to be carefully considered in order to make the best decisions for one’s future.

Finally, in terms of relationships, The Ace Of Swords Tarot card suggests that new ideas or conflict may arise. This could manifest as a relationship going through a tough time, or it may simply be a case of two people needing to have a difficult conversation. Either way, this card suggests that communication is key in order to maintain a healthy relationship.
