A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 2 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The two of Swords and the three of Cups symbolize the balance between compromise and celebration. This balance signifies the need to make a tough decision, while still recognizing and celebrating the people in your life who are surrounding you with love, support and friendship. Having both of these cards together in a reading emphasizes the importance of making a thoughtful and well-informed decision, while understanding and appreciating the people who will accompany you on your journey. When it comes time to make a hard choice, it’s important to keep your friends and family in mind and remember that even though it may be difficult, the payoff will be worth it in the end.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Ultimately, the combination of The 2 of Swords and The 3 of Cups Tarot card serves as a reminder that we can make decisions without compromising our love lives. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 2 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 2 of Swords tarot card can represent the need to make a difficult decision related to your health. This could be agreeing to a risky medical procedure, changing your diet, or even just deciding whether or not to go to the doctor. It could also indicate that a hard decision has already been made, but that you are still struggling with it. This could be a choice related to whether to take medication or not, or to take a break from exercise. You may be feeling stuck or unable to move forward without further clarity. Whatever the case, the 2 of Swords indicates that you need to take a moment to weigh your options and make a decision for your own health and well-being.

Finances: The 2 of Swords tarot card can indicate that there is a difficult financial decision ahead of you. It could suggest that you need to decide whether to make a risky investment, take out a loan, or even just make a tough budgeting decision. This card may also suggest that a decision has already been made and that you are now feeling the effects of it – and having difficulty coming to terms with it. Whatever the situation, this card is a reminder that difficult decisions sometimes need to be made in order to secure your financial future.

Relationships: The 2 of Swords tarot card can mean that a difficult decision needs to be made in order to improve a relationship. If a situation is causing tension, it can be difficult to make the ‘right’ decision – especially if it means compromising your own needs. The 2 of Swords reminds you that hard decisions need to be made and that both parties will have to compromise in order for the relationship to move forward. It can also indicate that a decision has been made, but that you are still struggling with it. Taking the time to reflect, talk things through and come to a resolution can help you move forward in a positive way.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 3 Of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the importance of friendship and having a circle of support when dealing with any physical or mental health problems. Having a support system in place can help people better cope with any health issues that may arise. On an emotional level, this card reminds people of the importance of developing and maintaining healthy relationships – both with themselves and with others. It advocates treating our own body and mind with care and respect, as well as showing kindness and understanding to those around us.

Finances

The 3 Of Cups Tarot card can also be interpreted as a sign of abundance and prosperity. When it comes to finances, it suggests that the individual is surrounded by a circle of supportive people and resources which can be utilized to help achieve financial stability. It also encourages risk-taking in pursuit of success, as well as trusting in our own abilities and striving to reach our goals.

Relationships

The 3 Of Cups Tarot card speaks to the importance of developing and nurturing healthy relationships. Friendships, partnerships and strong bonds with family members are all highlighted in this card. It encourages people to look for guidance and support from those close to them, as well as offering their own support to those who need it. On an emotional level, it can also imply the need to let go of any negative relationships, as well as to forgive any grievances which may have caused harm.
