However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 8 Of Wands card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The combination of The 8 Of Wands tarot card and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card speaks of a period of quick change that brings with it news of a legacy or inheritance. It could be that you are receiving a surprise windfall, such as an unexpected financial gift. Alternatively, it could represent the news that you have come into an inheritance or legacy, such as a house or business. It may therefore be a time of retirement, financial or otherwise, where you can relax and enjoy the fruits of your past hard work. You may have achieved a goal that you have set for yourself and now receive the reward for your efforts.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is a powerful and positive card that signifies legacy, legacy building, and financial stability. It encourages you to use your resources wisely and to build something lasting that will benefit future generations. It also suggests that retirement is near and that there is an inheritance coming your way.

For health, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card implies that you need to make sure you are taking the proper steps to ensure you live a long and healthy life. Make sure you are getting the proper nutrition, exercising regularly, and getting the proper rest. If you do this, you will be able to enjoy a good retirement and the legacy that you build for yourself.

For finances, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that you need to be very prudent with your money. This card also encourages you to save money now so that you have a cushion in the future when you are ready to start collecting your inheritance.

For relationships, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages you to invest in relationships that will last and that will bring you joy. Don’t waste your time on toxic relationships that don’t bring you joy and don’t build up your spirit. Invest in your relationships, and your relationships will build a better future for you. This card also encourages you to spend quality time with your loved ones, so that you can make memories that will last for a long time.

Overall, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is all about creating a lasting legacy for yourself, retiring comfortably, and having enough money for the future. As you make wise decisions now, you will be able to look forward to a more secure future.
