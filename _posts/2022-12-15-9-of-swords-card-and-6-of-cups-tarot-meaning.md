## What does 9 Of Swords and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The 9 Of Swords tarot card and The 6 Of Cups tarot card together could signify a period of intense emotional turmoil, with recollections of the past and old experiences returning to haunt you. It could be a difficult time when old wounds and unresolved issues come to the surface, and you feel overwhelmed by fear and sadness. At the same time, the 6 Of Cups tarot card brings hope that you can make it through this difficult period. It hints at the possibility of reunions with people from the past and moments of nostalgia that remind you of more peaceful times. In this way, the 9 Of Swords and the 6 Of Cups could indicate a period of intense anxiety and sorrow, but with moments of hope and comfort.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is associated with nightmares, anxiety, grief and depression. It usually indicates a difficult or dark time and signifies that one is experiencing deep emotional suffering.

When this card appears, it is important to take a pause and reflect on the current life circumstances. The 9 of Swords indicates that something needs to be addressed in order to move forward.

Health-wise, the 9 of Swords Tarot card can indicate physical and mental exhaustion, lack of energy, and feelings of hopelessness and despair. Negative thoughts and feelings can manifest in physical pain, sleeplessness and other ailments. The message of this card is to take the time to review one’s health and well-being and to prioritize self-care before any negative consequences can manifest.

Financially, the 9 of Swords Tarot card can suggest fear and stress over money and financial security. It may be a time for careful financial planning and budgeting in order to secure and maintain financial stability. It is also important to review and analyze investments in order to make the best financial decisions.

In relationships, the 9 of Swords Tarot card can be indicative of unresolved issues, feelings of insecurity and mistrust, communication breakdowns, and feelings of grief. It is essential to take the necessary steps to analyze and identify issues within the relationship in order to facilitate healing and growth.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 6 Of Cups Tarot card is a reminder that health is based on taking care of yourself physically, mentally, and emotionally. It suggests that nostalgia for the past can be a good thing – as it can provide physical and psychological comfort. It can also prompt a reflective pause, allowing one to reassess their current health situation. If something needs to be fine-tuned, now is a great time to act. This card suggests that the best way to nurture one’s health is to take time to appreciate the simple things in life. Gifts, whether given or received, can also provide comfort and a sense of care that can support one’s physical, mental and emotional well-being.

Finances

The 6 Of Cups Tarot card is associated with opportunity, innocence and financial security. The innocence that this card represents can help one to make decisions with a clear head and without fear. Financial opportunities may arise that can help one to better their financial stability, though one should not expect too much overnight. It is important to take care of the relationships that are important to one’s financial security, as these can provide helpful advice and guidance when making decisions. This card suggests that taking the time to recognize and appreciate the gifts that one has can be worth much more than money.

Relationships

The 6 Of Cups Tarot card is an important reminder to cultivate meaningful relationships, both old and new. It suggests that reunions can be a meaningful way to reconnect with loved ones. This card also implies that gifts can be a great way to express care and appreciation for someone. It is important to also take time to appreciate the relationships we have right now, even if they are not perfect. Though it may be difficult to do, it is important to look to the future and use past relationships as lessons for how to move forward in a healthy way.
