## 3 Of Swords – An Overview

The 3 Of Swords tarot card is a symbol of disappointment, pain and sorrow. It represents the shattering of a dream, a broken heart and the emotional devastation that comes with it. This card may appear when one is facing a difficult decision or the effects of a major setback. It can also symbolize the grief that follows the ending of a relationship, the loss of a loved one or a major life change. Other meanings can include depression, surgery, and the emotional turmoil of divorce. Whatever the circumstances, The 3 Of Swords reminds us to be kind to ourselves and to find strength in knowing that we can heal from such pain.

## Upright Meanings Of 3 Of Swords

### What It Means For Me

The 3 of Swords tarot card suggests that I’m currently going through a difficult period in my life, characterized by emotional pain, heartache, and loss. This card speaks to me of a period of emotional turmoil, and a deep sadness that I may be struggling to cope with. It could represent a difficult breakup or divorce, a loss of a loved one, or a difficult period of depression or anxiety. It could also point to a physical ailment or surgery, and the emotional toll that it can take. Whatever the cause of my emotional distress, it’s important that I make time to process my feelings and reach out for support if I need it.

### What It Means For The Future

The 3 of Swords tarot card can be an indication of a difficult future ahead. It may be an indication of a potential heartbreak or loss in the near future. It could also symbolize a period of depression or the possibility of a difficult surgery. Whatever the case may be, it’s important to remember that although the 3 of Swords may be a difficult card to interpret, it is not necessarily a negative omen. It can also indicate a need to process and move through difficult emotions in order to experience true growth and transformation. No matter what the future brings, it is important to remain hopeful and to focus on the good.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 3 of Swords tarot card speaks to the pain of loss, heartbreak, and depression that can occur when relationships with those we are close to are strained or end. It can signify a divorce between spouses or a falling out between friends and family. It can also represent the sense of loss that can come with a major life change such as the death of a loved one, the end of a job, or the sudden departure of someone close to us. This card speaks to the anguish and sorrow that can come with such losses, and encourages us to work through our feelings and use them to heal and grow.
