## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card often signals a time of financial worry, health and relationship troubles. When drawn, the card can represent unfortunate health and financial problems. Health issues can include short-term illnesses, chronic diseases, depression, anxiety, physical injury, and anything that prevents us from thriving. Financially, it can indicate growing debt, financial distress, bankruptcy, and a lack of access to basic needs.

The card can also represent isolation, loneliness and strained relationships. When the 5 of Pentacles shows up it can indicate that our relationships are strained, that we’re lacking support, or that our relationship is on the rocks. It can suggest that things have become too difficult to handle, and that perhaps we are in need of some form of support.

It is important to remember that the 5 of Pentacles does not necessarily signify a permanent state, but instead often signals a turning point. Although difficult times may be ahead, this card can suggest that there is light at the end of the tunnel and that recovery is possible. The card can indicate a need for change or restructuring of one’s finances, health, and relationships. It can mean taking a step back from a draining situation and allowing yourself some time to heal and recalibrate.

Finally, the 5 of Pentacles can be a reminder to give yourself compassion during this time. No matter how dark the situation may seem, it is crucial to remember that you are not alone and that you can and will get through this.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 8 of Swords Tarot card represents self-imposed restrictions and imprisonment that can manifest in your physical health. These disturbances can take the form of physical ailments or emotional afflictions such as depression or anxiety. You may find yourself unable to take action on your health, or feel too restricted to make the necessary changes in your lifestyle. The 8 of Swords can also represent a fear of failure, which in turn can cause you to limit your own growth and progress in terms of health.

Finances

In terms of finances, the 8 of Swords Tarot card speaks of self-imposed restrictions and lack of motivation to take action. It could be due to a feeling of being overwhelmed or overwhelmed, or a fear of failure that keeps you from taking the necessary steps to pursue greater financial success. It is important to become aware of what may be holding you back in order to identify any limiting beliefs and break free from them.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, the 8 of Swords Tarot card suggests that you may be cutting yourself off from other people, which can create feelings of loneliness, isolation, and frustration. It also carries with it a fear of commitment, as you may be afraid of getting hurt or having your heart broken if you take the risk of investing yourself in another person. It is important to be open to creating meaningful relationships and be willing to take a risk in order to benefit from the connection and love that comes with it.
