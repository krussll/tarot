A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 9 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 9 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 9 Of Wands mean together?

The combination of the Judgment tarot card with the 9 of Wands tarot card is a reminder that the clarity you have gained should be used to make a final decision and stand strong on it. It is also a wake-up call to remember your life’s purpose and to continue to persevere towards it. The 9 of Wands encourages you to keep and maintain boundaries, an action which requires strength and courage, and to persist regardless of any adversity. Together, these meanings illustrate the importance of holding true to your purpose and of having the courage and strength to stick by it. The Judgment and 9 of Wands remind you to stay focused and dedicated to achieve your goals, and that you are capable of overcoming any challenges that come your way.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 9 Of Wands tarot card is also emphasizing the importance of keeping and maintaining personal boundaries in your love life. To have a successful relationship and to avoid future heartache, it is important to be clear about what you will and will not tolerate in a partner. Your gut instinct is likely telling you whether or not this person is right for you, so make sure to trust your intuition and stay firm in your decision. The Judgment tarot card, coupled with the 9 Of Wands tarot card, is a call to look closely at yourself and your relationship with this person. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and 9 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health  
The Judgment Tarot card is a reminder that taking care of your health should always be a priority. It urges you to face any issues with your health with clarity and finality. Consider your physical and mental health, and make clear and actionable decisions about how to address any imbalances. Be honest with yourself about the impact of unhealthy behaviors and patterns, and take steps to make the changes needed to bring balance back into your life.

Finances  
The Judgment card is a wake-up call when it comes to finances. It encourages you to take a closer look at your budget, spending habits, and financial goals. Make difficult decisions about reprioritizing your spending and saving for the long term. Separate the wants from the needs and be sure to follow through on paying off debts. This card also suggests that you avoid making rash decisions when it comes to finances and to be thoughtful and deliberate in your financial planning.

Relationships  
The Judgment Tarot card often indicates that you are looking to take a more conscious and deliberate approach to relationships. Questions of purpose and values should be considered, and difficult decisions may need to be made in order to balance physical and emotional needs. This card can also bring clarity when it comes to friend and family relationships, as it encourages you to accept and learn from difficult experiences. As you make decisions, consider what qualities and characteristics you most value in these relationships and take the necessary steps to nurture them.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 9 of Wands Tarot card in the health context suggests that it is important for us to maintain boundaries involving our health. We should be aware of our own limitations and strive to be mindful of our physical and mental health. It is important to stay within our comfort zone and not push beyond our own capabilities. The 9 of Wands also suggests persistance in keeping a healthy lifestyle. This can be achieved by setting achievable goals for ourselves and then sticking to them.

Finances: The 9 of Wands Tarot card in the financial context suggests maintaining boundaries with finances. We should strive to stay within our financial means and not let ourselves become overwhelmed with debt. This card also urges us to be persistent in achieving financial security. We should set clear goals, such as saving up a certain amount of money each month, and then make sure to stick to those goals.

Relationships: The 9 of Wands Tarot card in the relationship context suggests maintaining boundaries, both physical and mental, in our relationships. We should strive to be honest and open with our partners and respect both our own and their boundaries. The 9 of Wands also suggests persistance and dedication when it comes to relationships. This means not giving up easily and trying to work through any problems that may arise.
