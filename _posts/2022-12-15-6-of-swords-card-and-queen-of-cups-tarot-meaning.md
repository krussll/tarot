A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The 6 of Swords and the Queen of Cups tarot card combo represent a necessary transition and healing. It suggests that a change must occur for you to progress and become healthier. This transition could be physical, such as a move to a new home or location, or it could be internal. The focus of this transition should be directed to restoring your sense of peace, nurturing yourself, and providing the support you need. The Queen of Cups symbolizes the healing and support you will receive or will provide yourself during this period of change. Allow yourself the patience and comfort necessary to make the transition. The combination of the two cards suggests a successful journey towards a more peaceful, content and enriched life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 6 Of Swords Tarot card is about changing and transitioning, as well as taking a new approach to health. This card suggests that you need to make a change in order to improve your health and wellbeing. You may need to change your diet, exercise routine, or lifestyle habits in order to achieve better health. Additionally, this card suggests that you should be open to different avenues of healing; try out different remedies, experiment with different practices, and be willing to try something new. It’s important to be open to the idea of healing and self-care, as there are a multitude of ways to take care of your body and mind.

Finances: Financially, the 6 Of Swords Tarot card suggests that it’s time to make a move, and to make a change. You may need to change the way you budget or make investments in order to achieve financial stability. It’s important to understand that it’s not just about making more money; it’s about understanding your current financial situation and working to improve it. Additionally, this card suggests that you may need to take a new approach to money, such as consciously spending and saving.

Relationships: In terms of relationships, the 6 Of Swords Tarot card suggests that it’s time to move on and make a change. This card symbolizes the need to refocus and reevaluate your relationships, and to find new, healthier ways to relate to those in your life. You may need to make a conscious effort to open up, be vulnerable, and communicate more clearly with those around you. Additionally, it’s important to focus on relationships that are positive and uplifting, as this will create an environment of growth and transformation.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

The Queen of Cups is a Tarot card that has strong implications in several different aspects of life. Health, finances, and relationships all come into play when this card appears.

Health: This card speaks to emotional and spiritual health, although it can also have physical implications. In terms of emotional health, the Queen of Cups suggests that you are caring and nurturing of yourself and others. You are aware of your own emotional needs and are willing to work to fulfill them. You have a strong sense of compassion and kindness which allows you to provide comfort and understanding to those in need. Physically, this card also indicates a strong connection to your own body and an ability to listen to its needs.

Finances: The Queen of Cups is a card of abundance, prosperity, and financial stability. You have an abundance of resources at your disposal and the wisdom to use them wisely. You are also willing to take risks and invest in yourself and your future. This card suggests that you don’t just focus on immediate gratifications but also consider long-term goals.

Relationships: This card signifies strong, healthy relationships. You have strong, supportive relationships with the people in your life, and you use your intuition to help guide you in your interactions. You are also very connected to your own emotions and can empathize with others. You may be a nurturing and supportive partner, friend, or parent, who is devoted to understanding the needs of those around you.

The Queen of Cups is a Tarot card of compassion, abundance, and emotional intelligence. It is an important card for those looking to improve their physical and emotional health, finances, and relationships.
