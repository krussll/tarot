However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does Page Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The Page is a harbinger of potential and opportunity, while the 9 Of Cups is a sign of well-wishes coming into reality. This combination suggests that you can have the drive and enthusiasm to bring your ideas to life, because you have the resources to do so. A project or endeavor that you were hesitant to start due to lack of resources or opportunity can now be taken on without worry. Best of all, you can do so with enthusiasm, creative energy and good health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 9 of Cups tarot card is a signifier of good health and abundance. It is a symbol that health can be obtained and improved. Physically, it means feeling good about yourself and taking steps to maintain good health. Eating a balanced diet and getting plenty of exercise are important elements of good health. When the 9 of Cups appears, it’s a reminder to stay focused on your health and to take time to look after your physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing.

Finances  
The 9 of Cups tarot card is a signifier of financial abundance. It encourages you to be proactive in obtaining the wealth and prosperous life you desire. It is a reminder to stay focused and take control of your finances. Financial stability is within reach if you are willing to take steps to achieve it. It is a sign that investing in yourself and your future will pay off in the long run. It is also a reminder to use money responsibly and to not let wealth and material possessions become a source of unhappiness.

Relationships  
The 9 of Cups tarot card is a signifier of strong and healthy relationships. It encourages you to seek out true connections that bring joy and fulfillment to your life. It is a reminder to be open and honest with yourself and with others, and to leave space for mistakes and compromise. When the 9 of Cups appears, it is a reminder to nurture relationships in a meaningful way and to be willing to build trust and respect within them. It also emphasizes being grateful for loved ones and meaningful connections in your life.
