A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and Queen Of Swords mean together?

When The 6 of Pentacles tarot card and The Queen of Swords tarot card are combined, they bring to mind a picture of a generous, honest person who is not afraid to ask for or give help. This person is independent and self-sufficient, yet still aware of the importance of community and of helping others out. They understand that giving and receiving help is necessary for creating a better world. They are open to giving and receiving whatever help is necessary, whether it be in the form of money, advice, or support. This combination of cards suggests that it is possible to be both generous and honest without sacrificing your own independence or integrity.

This combination encourages you to be generous with your time, energy, and resources in your relationship, while also keeping a healthy boundary and maintaining your own autonomy. You must learn to be open and generous in your relationships and accept help when needed or happily give it when requested. This combination speaks to creating a balanced relationship, of both giving and receiving, and finding the right point of equilibrium between them.

The 6 of Pentacles shows us that it’s important to be generous, both with ourselves and with others. In our love life, this card is asking us to be open to giving but also open to receiving when necessary. It speaks to not just always giving but also allowing your loved one to give to you in return. It’s about allowing yourself to be vulnerable and allowing the other person into your heart, so that the giving and receiving of love can be an equal exchange of energy and resources.

The Queen of Swords is reminding us to stay grounded and true to ourselves in our relationships. It is about finding trust within ourselves and being honest and open with our partner, while also respecting our own boundaries and not giving too much of ourselves away. It’s important to remember to be independent and not give away too much of ourselves, while still making sure we are generous with our love and support.

Ultimately, the combination of the The 6 of Pentacles and the Queen of Swords is asking us to strike a balance between the giving and receiving in our love life. We must be generous and open to both giving and receiving love, while also maintaining our boundaries and independence. This combination speaks to creating a healthy relationship where love and support is readily exchanged between partners.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Pentacles and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

When it comes to health, The 6 Of Pentacles Tarot card reveals a need for balance. On a physical level, it could indicate the need for rest, both physical and mental. It could also symbolize the need to take care of ourselves and find a way to come back into balance. This could be the start of a new and healthier lifestyle or the end of an unhealthy one. By finding a balance between work, rest and play, we can find a way to achieve physical and mental health.

Finances:

The 6 Of Pentacles Tarot card is all about balance when it comes to finances. On the one hand, it suggests a need to be generous and share with others, but it doesn’t encourage reckless spending. On the other hand, it advises that financial decisions should not only be practical, but generous and beneficial for the people in our lives. It encourages us to take the time to earn and save our money in a responsible way and to use it to support ourselves and others.

Relationships:

When it comes to relationships, The 6 Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests a need to practice generosity. This could mean taking the time to be generous with our time, attention and love. It could also mean lending a helping hand with a problem or situation. This could be a relationship with a partner, family member or friend. It’s a reminder to give and receive in equal measure, because this is how relationships are kept healthy and balanced.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Queen of Swords card implies that if you want to maintain optimal health, you must be honest with yourself and have the discipline to face your own weaknesses. You may need to be your own coach, weeding out self-destructive habits and behaviours. Look at the areas of your life that need to be addressed, and make the necessary changes. Place your trust in yourself and be honest with your own physical and emotional needs.

Finances:  
The Queen of Swords card indicates that making sound financial decisions requires clear and honest evaluation and planning. Whether you are making a decision about whether or not to invest or trying to find ways to create a stable financial future, you must be able to weigh all the facts and figure out the truth. Avoid being impulsive and instead use your levelheadedness to make prudent financial decisions.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Swords card encourages you to use your honesty, independence, and judgement in order to create strong and harmonious relationships. Though it is often hard to be the one who stays levelheaded and does not allow yourself to be influenced by someone else, it is often the best choice for a long-term healthy relationship. Don’t be afraid to stand up for yourself and make sure your partner is always being honest and fair to you.
