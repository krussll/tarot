## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health

The Moon Tarot card indicates that underlying health issues may be present. Even though you may not be aware of it, these problems could be having a negative impact on your health. To unearth these deeper issues, it’s important to trust your instincts and pay attention to any messages your body is trying to send you. Take time to pause and listen to what your body needs, and don’t be afraid to make changes if they’re necessary.

Finances

This card suggests that changes may be necessary to achieve financial stability. It often points to unseen problems that may be impacting your finances such as debts, investments, or taxes. To find solutions, it’s important to trust your intuition and be willing to make changes if necessary. This could include creating a budget or evaluating current investments and debts.

Relationships

The Moon Tarot card emphasizes the importance of trusting your intuition when it comes to relationships. It can indicate that you may be feeling conflicted or uncertain about a relationship and you should listen to your instincts. Be mindful of any red flags, and don’t be afraid to make changes if necessary. This could include setting boundaries, having uncomfortable conversations, or making the decision to end a relationship for the sake of your mental and emotional health.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 6 of Swords Tarot card is an indication that it is time for a transition in the area of health. This transition might include an individual making changes in their diet, exercising, or even seeking out medical help. This card indicates that although the process may be difficult, the end result will be beneficial. The individual needs to be mindful of their physical and mental well being, and be willing to make a shift in lifestyle or attitude to improve their situation. It is important to listen to their body, and be willing to make necessary changes to maintain balance and health.

Finances

The 6 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder of the need to make a transition in the area of finances. This transition may include a shift in the way one views money, or a need to create a budget and stick to it. This card suggests that although this transition may be difficult, it is necessary in order to secure financial stability. It is important to be mindful of one’s spending, and be willing to make necessary changes to one’s financial habits and practices.

Relationships

The 6 of Swords Tarot card is an indication that it is time to make a transition in one’s relationships. This could be a situation in which one realizes the unhealthy nature of a relationship and is ready to move on, or a situation in which one is going through a transition with a significant other. In either scenario, the card suggests that although this transition may be difficult, it is necessary in order to create healthier, more fulfilling relationships. An individual must be willing to take the risk of releasing that which no longer serves them and being open to new opportunities.
