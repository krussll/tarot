A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 3 Of Pentacles mean together?

For me, the combination of the 2 of Wands and the 3 of Pentacles suggests that I am about to make an important decision and the results of that decision depend on the teamwork and collaboration of others. I also feel that I need to put in the dedication, skill and craftsmanship to make sure it is a successful outcome. The journey ahead of me may be long and I must be prepared for any challenges that arise in order to get the best result. The idea of travel is also likely to be connected to this decision and I need to be prepared for this as well.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 2 Of Wands and 3 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It can suggest that a health decision needs to be taken, or that a new approach to health is needed. This card often comes up when it is necessary to take a step back and consider all the facts before making a decision.

For finances, The 2 Of Wands Tarot card suggests that someone with this card may be in the process of making a choice or considering options that could be beneficial to their financial situation. It can be a sign of weighing out all the various options in order to make the best choice. This card can also point to the need to look carefully at investments or business plans to make sure that the financial decision made is a wise one.

In relationships, The 2 Of Wands Tarot card can point towards taking a journey or going on a trip with a partner. It suggests that the relationship needs to move to a new level or take a new course of action, and travel is necessary for this. The card can also mean that a relationship needs to be examined from a different angle in order to find a better solution. The 2 Of Wands can be a sign that it is time to make a conscious effort to break away from destructive patterns that may be hurting the relationship, while opening up to new possibilities.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 3 of Pentacles tarot card encourages us to take part in team activities and to come together to achieve a common goal. In terms of health, this card suggests that we work together with others to create a healthy lifestyle. We should collaborate to find ways to improve our overall wellbeing, build meaningful relationships, and stay fit and active. Working as a team can help motivate us and make it easier to stay on track with our health goals.

Finances:

The 3 of Pentacles tarot card encourages us to be mindful with our finances. This card suggests that we take a collaborative approach to tackling any financial challenge we may face. We should think of our finances as a team and work together to solve any monetary issue. We should also be conscious of our spending habits and look for ways to save money if possible. Utilizing our skills, knowledge and craftsmanship, we can come up with creative and effective solutions for managing our finances.

Relationships:

The 3 of Pentacles tarot card encourages us to build strong, collaborative relationships. It suggests that we take a team approach in our relationships and come together to create mutual understanding and respect. We should be open to learning from one another and ensuring that each partner in the relationship has a voice. We should also be willing to put in the work necessary to help the relationship grow and thrive. Working together and using our skills and creativity, we can build relationships that are built to last.
