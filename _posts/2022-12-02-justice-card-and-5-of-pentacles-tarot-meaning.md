A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card together indicate a situation where a difficult financial situation needs to be solved. It might be a period of instability, where you’re feeling like you don’t have much power or control. Even though it may feel like a lost cause, the Justice card encourages you to seek out win-win solutions and to trust that justice will be served, regardless of the external forces at work. The 5 of Pentacles also implies that healing and restoring prosperity will require self-care and careful consideration. Things may be tough now, but The Justice card promotes the idea that truth and justice will prevail and that you can turn this period of hardship around if you are brave enough to seek out solutions.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card speaks of balance and fairness, two tenets that can help one to retain their health, finances and relationships during trying times. When it comes to one’s physical health, the Justice Tarot card encourages a person to take responsibility for taking care of themselves, whether it’s through diet, exercise, or other healthy habits. The Justice Tarot card can also be used as a reminder to make sure that medical bills are paid on time and in full, so that one’s credit score and other financial relationships remain strong.

The Justice Tarot card can also assist in forming relationships with others based on shared principles and values. For example, it can be used to help one make the decision to take a job that pays less but offers a better work-life balance. It can also remind people to think of their relationships with others as partnerships, in which each person contributes equally and works together towards a shared goal.

In terms of one’s finances, the Justice Tarot card can be a reminder to always play fair and to be open to sharing resources for mutual benefit. This could involve anything from negotiating salary to sharing stocks or other investments. The idea here is to actively look for win-win solutions that help both oneself and the other party. Ultimately, the Justice Tarot card encourages one to lead a life of balance and fairness, something that is ultimately beneficial for one’s health, finances and relationships.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card represents failing health, poverty, loneliness, and financial loss. It may represent physical health issues, such as chronic illness, injury or disability that results in a lack of ability to earn income or complete daily activities. Money worries can also manifest in the form of job loss, unexpected bills, or other financial difficulties. In addition, relationships can become strained when one person is burdened with illness or financial woes.

The 5 of Pentacles tends to represent a time of trial and hardship, but this is not an indicator of future despair. It is a reminder that life can be difficult and so it is important to lean on supportive people for help. During this trying period, focusing on physical and mental wellness is essential. Exercise, good nutrition, and connecting with a supportive circle can help to uplift and invigorate.

On the financial front, the 5 of Pentacles encourages its holder to search for creative solutions in order to make ends meet. This may involve applying for financial aid, taking on odd jobs, or applying for a loan. No matter the situation, it is important to accept help from those who can lend a hand.

Finally, the 5 of Pentacles reminds its holder that even when things are difficult, there is still hope. Reaching out to trusted loved ones and focusing on self-care can be incredibly valuable during times of hardship. With time, dedication, and hard work, it is possible to overcome the difficulties associated with the 5 of Pentacles.
