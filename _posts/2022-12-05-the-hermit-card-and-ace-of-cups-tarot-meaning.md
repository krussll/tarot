A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Ace Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Ace Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Ace Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Hermit and The Ace Of Cups tarot cards is one of internal reflection and introspection as well as joy and emotional fulfillment. It suggests that it is time to look inside yourself, to gain clarity and to understand what is right for you. Once you have done this, you will be ready to move forward with a new relationship, or even a proposal or marriage. The emotional fulfillment that can be achieved when your innermost soul is fully realized is immense. By listening to your own inner truth and intuiting what is right for you, you will be able to make the most of this exciting time and the joy that comes with it.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and Ace Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card is a powerful symbol of withdrawal and introspection. In matters of health, The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to take a step back and seriously assess our own well-being. Physically this could mean taking the time to properly nourish our bodies and get the necessary rest our bodies need. Emotionally and mentally, The Hermit Tarot card urges us to practice self-care and self-reflection. Meditation and reflective exercises can be particularly beneficial in cultivating internal peace, enabling us to more readily tap into our inner wisdom.

When considering finances, The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to go within to examine our relationship to money and to take a hard look at our spending and saving habits. We may find that conventional financial advice isn’t necessarily in alignment with our individual values, prompting us to create our own ethical financial framework. Additionally, The Hermit Tarot card underscores the importance of being mindful of our material possessions and making responsible investments with the goal of promoting long-term stability.

Finally, The Hermit Tarot card emphasizes the importance of listening to our inner voice as we navigate our relationships. This entails tuning into our intuition and examining our feelings and motivations with honesty, so that we can discern our true needs. While The Hermit Tarot card may suggest it’s necessary to take time away from others, it’s important to note that the purpose of such a retreat should ultimately be to generate greater self-awareness and insight, and to reconnect with our inner truth, which in turn can help us become better relational partners.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

The Ace of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of hope, happiness and emotional health. It suggests that there is an opening to receive good fortune, healing, and love.

When it comes to health, the Ace of Cups is a positive omen of abundance. It can indicate a time of improved physical health, with a focus on wellness and preventive self-care activities. The card also is a reminder to trust in the process of both healing from physical ailments and emotional ones. Additionally, it signals that a new frame of mind is necessary for transformation and spiritual growth.

In terms of finances, the Ace of Cups is an indication of abundance, as it contains a strong message of prosperity. It shows that there is an opportunity to accept a new direction in finances, and to take advantage of fresh resources and ideas. This card also encourages one to open up to generous and beneficial streams of income.

The Ace of Cups is especially fitting for relationships, as it signifies new starts, beginnings and growth. It can signal a new connection or the deepening of an existing one, as one is open to receiving love. It suggests that one must clear away old hurts and pains in order to make way to a new, healthier relationship. Additionally, the card shows that one may be ready to take the next step in a relationship, such as thoughts of marriage or making a new commitment.

The Ace of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of hope and abundance, no matter the circumstance. It suggests that one is open to receiving love, resources, healing, and prosperity in all realms of their life.
