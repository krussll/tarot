A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The Chariot tarot card and The 7 Of Cups tarot card together symbolize action and change, a journey, and too many choices. They might be telling you that you have lots of potential directions to explore and just need to figure out which path to take. You’re like the chariot driver, ready to take off in a new vehicle and explore, but you’re feeling overwhelmed with the amount of choices and have commitment issues. It’s time to focus on what you really care about and commit to it. With the power of the Chariot, you can navigate the journey and make the most of your choices to get to your desired destination.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Chariot and 7 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 7 of Cups Tarot card is a warning sign to those who are struggling with their health. It signifies that individuals may have too many choices when it comes to their health, and they may be afraid of committing to a plan of action. This is because they are overwhelmed with the variety of programs, diets, and treatments available for any given condition. This can lead to a fear of being overwhelmed if they choose the wrong option, causing feelings of indecision. Without a clear plan of action, individuals will not be able to make progress and can become stuck in a cycle of inaction and poor choices.

Finances:

The 7 of Cups Tarot card is also a warning sign when it comes to finances. When individuals have too many options, they can become overwhelmed and make poor financial choices. This card is a sign that individuals may not be committing to a budget or financial plan, instead being swayed by the lure of various investments and steals. Without a clear financial plan, individuals can become stuck in a cycle of poor spending and saving habits, leading to financial instability.

Relationships:

The 7 of Cups Tarot card can also be a warning sign when it comes to relationships. Individuals may be overwhelmed with the choices of potential partners, leading them to become afraid of making a commitment. This can lead to feelings of indecision, causing people to become stuck in a cycle of never feeling ready for a relationship. Without committing to a meaningful relationship, individuals can become frustrated and lonely, missing out on the potential for love and companionship.
