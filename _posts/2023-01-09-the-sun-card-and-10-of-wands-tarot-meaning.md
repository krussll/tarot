However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Sun card and 10 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 10 Of Wands mean together?

The Sun tarot card and The 10 Of Wands tarot card together suggest that you are currently bearing a heavy burden and feeling overwhelmed. You may be feeling the pressure to succeed, and that’s causing you stress and exhaustion. However, the combination of these two cards indicates that although your problems are serious, if you keep pushing and persevering, joyous success and material happiness are in your future. It may require hard work and dedication, but the rewards of success will be worth the effort.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Sun and 10 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

In terms of health, this card symbolizes a general sense of well-being, joy, and vitality. It may also indicate a period of healing and recovery, as well as increased energy and focus on maintaining a healthy lifestyle. In terms of finances, the Sun Tarot card suggests a prosperous period in which success and abundance will come your way. It is a sign of luck and good fortune in the financial realm. Lastly, in terms of relationships, the Sun Tarot card suggests joyous and fulfilling relationships that bring contentment and lasting happiness. It suggests a period of mutual understanding, trust and appreciation, as well as a strong bond of connection and harmony between two people. ## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 10 of Wands tarot card can represent a need to take better care of yourself. This card can signal that you are feeling bogged down by stress and exhaustion due to a heavy workload or obligations and you need to take some time to focus on your physical and mental health. Doing things that make you feel good, such as yoga, exercise, or getting a massage, will help reduce your stress, increase energy levels, and will restore balance in your life.

Finances: The 10 of Wands can also be symbolic of taking on too many financial burdens. You may find yourself feeling overwhelmed and overstretched financially. The 10 of Wands can be a reminder to take a step back and look at the big picture. Make a budget and stick to it, create a plan of action for paying off debts and make sure you are saving money for the future.

Relationships: When the 10 of Wands appears in a relationship context, it can refer to feeling weighed down by the expectations of a partner. You may feel obligated to always put your partner’s needs before your own, to the point that it is wearing you down. If this is the case, then it is important to remember to also take care of yourself. Your relationship is only as healthy as each individual in it. Nurture yourself and make sure to take the time to prioritize and respect your own needs.
