However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

By learning to see things from a different perspective and surrendering rigid ideas, one can be rewarded with leaving a lasting legacy that can be passed on to future generations. This legacy could be financial, personal or spiritual and is earned through hard work and dedication. Through this process of self-sacrifice, one can be assured of a comfortable retirement, which will be further supplemented by an inheritance that the previous generation has left behind.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health: The Hanged Man Tarot card symbolizes a period of reevaluation in order to reach an understanding of how to best care for one’s physical and mental health. It speaks to the importance of self-care and personal growth, as well as the necessity of resilience and patience. People who draw the card may need to take a step back, disconnect from the pressures and stresses of everyday life, and take time to reflect on their current health choices and habits. When this Tarot card appears, it is best to reconsider one’s current path and to make sacrifices in order to better realign one’s personal health journey.

Finances: The Hanged Man Tarot card signals a time for taking a stepped back and assessing one’s current financial situation. It is a time for detoxing from the anxious energies that come with financial planning, and focusing on personal values and a long-term vision. The card speaks to the need for practicality and the grounding of one’s actions in universal truth. People should reassess their spending habits, become more mindful of their finances, and cultivate a sense of patience with their progress.

Relationships: The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages one to reevaluate their current relationships and to assess which relationships truly serve one’s growth and purpose. This is a time for understanding one’s true needs and desires, and creating boundaries to ensure that those needs are met. It is also a time for reflecting on how one’s individual values and beliefs affect their interpersonal relationships. The card speaks to the need to forgive, both oneself and others, in order to move forward with clarity. It also encourages the importance of self-reflection and patience, in order to make sense of one’s current relationships and determine which ones are worth taking the time to nurture.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card can represent robust health. This is a card that speaks of abundance and having the resources to focus on preventative care and wellness. It is a card of security, and the availability of funds to invest in lifestyle tweaks to promote health and vitality. Exercise, nutrition and quality sleep are all within reach. It can also signal the end of an illness or injury.

Finances:

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of financial security and stability. This card can signal the arrival of a financial windfall, such as an inheritance, large bonus, grant or investment returns. It can also mean a successful financial venture, a large payout as a result of a lawsuit, or the successful sale of a business. It can also represent a long-term savings plan that has paid off and the resulting financial comfort and stability.

Relationships:

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of long-term stability and commitment. It symbolizes the ability to provide for one’s family or to provide for a long-term relationship. It is a card that speaks of inter-generational exchange and the ability to leave a legacy or inheritance to family or loved ones. It also signals commitments and responsibilities that are honored, as well as the appreciation of family bonds and values that are passed on from one generation to the next. It is a card of relationships that have stood the test of time, and are built on mutual respect and trust.
