However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean together?

The 6 Of Swords and The Page Of Swords tarot cards together could be a sign that it is time for a necessary transition in your life. It could mean that you are preparing to move to a new place and will need to rely on spies and prying eyes to find out information about this new location. The cards could also mean that you will need to be more curious and ask more questions about the truth in order to make the transition easier. In any case, the cards are advising you to make the necessary changes and to be open to discovering the truth in order to make the transition successful.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 6 of Swords Tarot card is often associated with physical health and healing. This card can denote a period of transition when it comes to our physical bodies, such as starting a new diet or taking up a new exercise routine. This transition can be a difficult one, as it often involves releasing old habits and making lifestyle changes that may seem strange or uncomfortable at first. This transition can also signify emotional and spiritual healing, as we might need to step back and reflect on our current habits and how they may be affecting our overall wellbeing. The 6 of Swords encourages us to take a break from the same old routine, and perhaps seek out alternate forms of healing, such as yoga, meditation, or therapy.

Finances

The 6 of Swords Tarot card can indicate financial transitions. It may represent a change in employment or income, such as starting a new job or investing in a new business venture. This card could also signal a need to re-evaluate our spending habits, or even a transition away from certain material possessions and towards more frugality and financial security. The 6 of Swords Tarot card may often imply taking the necessary steps to ensure stability in our finances and peace of mind in the long run.

Relationships

The 6 of Swords Tarot card can suggest a period of transition in our relationships. It can represent a decision to move away from a toxic relationship, or the need to communicate our needs more clearly. This card can be an indication that we need to take time for ourselves and reassess our relationships, including the ones with family and friends. The 6 of Swords can also suggest the need to set boundaries, even with the people we care about most, in order to ensure our emotional health and welfare. Overall, this card encourages us to be mindful of our relationships and to assess what works for us and what doesn’t.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health

The Page of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a person’s ability to stay alert and take care of their health. It suggests maintaining a healthy balance between a person’s physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. The Page of Swords represents the need to take action in order to achieve health and wellbeing. This card shows the power of observation, of listening and understanding – skills that can help when making decisions regarding health and wellbeing. Furthermore, this card may also signify the need for truth-seeking and an honest examination of one’s health and lifestyle.

Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card signifies that money matters need careful consideration and good judgement. It is a symbol of financial risk-taking and of using one’s resources wisely. This card suggests that money needs to be managed with discipline and steps taken to ensure that financial goals are achieved. It also suggests that one should take the time to do research and to be curious in business dealings and financial pursuits. This card also encourages a person to be honest and up-front when dealing with financial matters.

Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card represents communication and truth in relationships. It symbolizes the need for two people to be open and honest with each other in order to maintain a strong, healthy relationship. This card suggests that communication should be clear, direct and open in order to ensure that both parties understand and respect each other’s points of view. The Page of Swords could also mean that one should remain curious and attentive to ensure that the relationship is one of mutual understanding and trust. Furthermore, this card could be a warning to not believe everything one is told as gossip and lies can easily damage a relationship.
