However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

Alternatively, it could mean that the investment of time and energy into a new job or relationship is met with theft or violence. The Ace of Pentacles and The 5 Of Swords could also mean that a promotion is accompanied with a toxic environment. It is important to take the time to assess any situation before investing further to ensure safety and security.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Ace Of Pentacles and 5 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card means a great deal for health, finances and relationships. When it appears in a reading, it indicates that a new opportunity is on its way related to one of these aspects of life.

In terms of health, the Ace of Pentacles symbolizes the power of financial wealth to create stability and well-being. With the additional resources, you may be able to purchase better healthcare or make lifestyle changes that enhance your overall health. The card represents a newfound ability to take control of your well-being and make changes to improve it.

For finances, the Ace of Pentacles indicates a new job, raise, promotion or investment that brings increased wealth and security. This card can also represent a new source of income or an opportunity to make a wise investment. Whatever the case, it’s a card of abundance and financial stability that indicates you can achieve the life you want.

In terms of relationships, the Ace of Pentacles represents the power of wealth to open up possibilities for a deeper connection. It can lead to financial security for a relationship, or it can help strengthen existing bonds by enabling couples to do more with their time together. On a larger level, it can also indicate a new level of financial stability in more than one relationship.

Above all, the Ace of Pentacles symbolizes optimism and success. It speaks to the possibilities of abundance and growth in all aspects of your life. So, if you’re looking for a new job, a raise, a promotion, an investment or a new source of income, the Ace of Pentacles is a card of hope and abundance that indicates your dreams can come true.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 5 of Swords Tarot card is a sign of potential health issues and stresses related to bullying, violence, theft, and abusive relationships. There may be physical and emotional wounds that a person may need to heal as a result. This card encourages people to be aware of how these situations can negatively impact their health. It is important to seek out professional help and support, if needed.

Finances: The 5 of Swords Tarot card signifies potential financial losses due to bullying and abusive situations. It encourages the person to take action to protect their financial security. This card could also refer to unethical behavior or manipulation of finances in an effort to gain advantage over someone or something. It is important to take caution when dealing with these types of people or situations and to have reliable support around them.

Relationships: The 5 of Swords Tarot card is an indication that relationships are at risk due to bullying, violence, theft, and abusive relationships. It is important to recognize these risks and protect oneself and any other parties involved in the relationship. If any of these situations exists, it is important to take action and reach out for help, as soon as possible. It can also signify a lack of trust and communication between the parties, which should be addressed quickly to ensure the health and safety of all involved.
