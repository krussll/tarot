A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 10 Of Cups tarot card together represent a happy, harmonious relationship and a strong sense of balance. They suggest that when the truth is revealed and each party takes responsibility for their actions and works towards win-win solutions, harmony and contentment can be achieved. The combination of these cards symbolizes a strong family bond where there is total love and support, and an understanding of cause and effect. You can create a strong foundation of balanced justice and contentment within your family relationships.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card is a powerful symbol of fairness, balance, and consequence. It serves as a reminder that all actions have consequences, and that you should be mindful of the effects of your words and actions. When we talk about what The Justice Tarot card means for health, finances, and relationships, it is important to note that this card is not only about making decisions that are just, but also about understanding the consequences of our choices.

When it comes to health, The Justice Tarot card encourages us to take responsibility for our wellbeing. It serves as a reminder that we have a responsibility to take care of our physical and mental health, and to make choices that are in our best interests. We should strive to make choices that will bring us lasting wellness and fulfillment, and be mindful of the potential consequences of our decisions.

In terms of finance, The Justice Tarot card is an important reminder to make wise financial decisions, and to remember that our choices can have repercussions that last for years. It is important to understand contracts, to use resources responsibly, and to be mindful of the consequences of taking a risk. This card encourages us to make financially responsible decisions, but also reminds us that sometimes taking measured risks can be beneficial.

Finally, The Justice Tarot card also applies to our relationships. It reminds us that the decisions we make in all of our relationships have consequences, and that we should strive for win-win solutions whenever possible. We should be mindful of how our words and actions may affect other people, and work towards creating relationships that are based on trust, understanding, and mutual respect. It reminds us to strive for fairness and equality in all of our relationships, as well as to respect boundaries and be mindful of the consequences of our actions.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

The 10 of Cups Tarot card suggests that good physical health is possible, as long as you remain focused on caring for yourself and nurturing your physical body. It is a reminder to honor your body and to be mindful of the how the food you eat, the rest you get, and the resources you have may affect your overall wellness. The 10 of Cups can indicate a period of stability and abundance, as you learn to cultivate and maintain a healthy lifestyle.

Finances

The 10 of Cups Tarot card carries with it the message of abundance, financial stability, and long term security. This card can signify a period in which you have the power to control your financial health, allowing you to make the choices that support your overall financial goals. The 10 of Cups suggests that you should use this period of financial stability and abundance to create plans and manifest your goals.

Relationships

The 10 of Cups Tarot card implies that meaningful, lasting relationships are possible. This card encourages you to cultivate connections that are based on mutual trust and understanding, communication, and deep respect. The 10 of Cups suggests that your relationships should be a source of joy and comfort, rather than a source of stress or anxiety. The 10 of Cups Tarot card also encourages you to form meaningful relationships with yourself, by cultivating a healthy relationship with your thoughts and feelings.
