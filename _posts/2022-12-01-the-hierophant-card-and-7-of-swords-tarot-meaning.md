A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Hierophant tarot card and The 7 Of Swords tarot card reveals a dichotomy between following one’s path and making a decision that may not fit within societal norms. In this case, the traditional expectations of conformity and traditions can sometimes lead to dishonesty and the need to run away from those expectations. The choices made may be difficult, but ultimately necessary to find one’s true path and a sense of freedom.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

You may be feeling like the traditional expectations are too rigid for you or like you’re being forced into a certain role that you don’t necessarily fit into. This can manifest as feeling a disconnect from your partner or feeling like you’re compromising too much in order to make the relationship work.

On the other hand, The 7 Of Swords tarot card can also mean that there is betrayal in your relationship or the potential for betrayal. In a love reading, this could mean that you or your partner have been secretive or dishonest. It could also mean that you have been betrayed in the past and it’s causing you to struggle with trusting your partner or yourself in the relationship. Your fear or suspicion of betrayal can cause a barrier between you and your partner.

In conclusion, The Hierophant tarot card and The 7 Of Swords tarot card together in a love reading can mean that you’re struggling with societal expectations and traditional expectations in your relationship, while also being suspicious that you or your partner could be betrayed. It’s important to address the trust issues and to work on building an honest, open, and healthy relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

In terms of finances, The 7 of Swords Tarot card can suggest that you are in a situation where you need to make decisions that could impact your finances. It can also suggest that you are not taking your financial situation seriously enough and need to start making more responsible decisions.

Finally, this card can symbolize deception and betrayal when it comes to relationships. This means that someone close to you may be deceiving you for their own interests or that you are not being honest with yourself about your relationships. It could also indicate that you need to start being more honest with yourself and those around you in order to build healthier relationships. Taking the time to evaluate your relationships and be more open and honest with yourself and others can help to improve existing relationships and avoid deception and betrayal.
