A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The 8 of Pentacles and the 7 of Cups tarot cards together symbolize the importance of looking inward and finding satisfaction in mastering your craft. The 8 of Pentacles shows us the importance of honing our skills, while the 7 of Cups encourages us to look at our life choices and make sure we are taking the right steps. They remind us that while life can offer us many options and choices, it is important to focus on mastering the craft and taking joy in the work we have to offer. We should commit to something we feel passionate and devoted to in order to find our sense of purpose and fulfillment.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Work on cultivating your own self love and self-worth, strengthen your core values, and allow yourself the time to come to terms with your commitment issues. Take time to reflect on what you are looking for in a relationship. Instead of feeling overwhelmed by the plethora of options available to you, find a path you can commit to.

The 8 of Pentacles and The 7 of Cups together suggest that if you take the time to explore yourself, you will be able to find the type of relationship you truly desire, be it a traditional monogamous relationship or something outside of the box. You can be the lover you always wanted to be but have not been able to, due to commitment issues. If you take the time to master your craft and form a deeper connection with yourself, you will find the relationship that you want to be in without feeling too overwhelmed by the options.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

The 7 of Cups Tarot card often symbolizes an overwhelming number of choices or a sense of being overwhelmed. Many view this card as a sign that one may be having difficulty committing to one option or course of action. This could be true in many areas of life, including health, finances, and relationships.

In regards to health, the 7 of Cups can signify a tendency to delay making decisions when it comes to one’s health. This could be due to fear of making the wrong decision or an unwillingness to commit to taking care of one’s health. This could manifest in terms of procrastinating health checkups or tests, choosing unhealthy lifestyle habits, or not taking necessary medications or treatments.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Cups Tarot card can signify a reluctance to commit to a budget or a savings plan. This could be due to feeling overwhelmed with the options available or feeling like none will really work. It may also mean being unable to commit to just one way of reaching financial goals. This could mean an inability to focus on one investment or a lack of commitment to a particular budget.

Finally, the 7 of Cups can represent commitment issues when it comes to relationships. This card can signify a fear of committing to a relationship or a fear of making the wrong choice. This can be seen in difficulty committing to a romantic relationship, a reluctance to prioritize relationships, or difficulty trusting someone in a relationship.

Overall, the 7 of Cups Tarot card signifies an overwhelming number of choices and a fear of choosing wrong. This can manifest in many areas of one’s life, including health, finances, and relationships.
