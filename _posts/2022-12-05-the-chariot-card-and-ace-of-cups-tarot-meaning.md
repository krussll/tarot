A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and Ace Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and Ace Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

Whether this is a literal journey or a symbolic one, you’re ready to make big strides in whatever you’re aiming for. The Ace of Cups card adds something special – a new baby, relationship, proposal or marriage. This means that whatever you’re pursuing, it will bring you a great sense of connection, love, and joy.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Chariot and Ace Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Chariot and Ace Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Chariot mean?

Health  
The Chariot Tarot card means action and change when it comes to health. It suggests the need to get moving, to take the initiative and to be proactive. It may indicate the need to make changes in one’s lifestyle or diet, or to take up a new exercise routine. The Chariot Tarot card can also be a reminder to take care of yourself and your health, to listen to your body, and to make decisions that are beneficial for you.

Finances  
The Chariot Tarot card in regards to finances symbolizes new opportunities and the need to take action in order to succeed. It can suggest the need to create new strategies or plans when it comes to money, or to invest in something that may bring long-term gain. It indicates the potential for success, but also the need for discipline and hard work in order to reach your financial goals.

Relationships  
The Chariot Tarot card in relationships suggests a need to take action, to move forward and to make changes. It may indicate a need to establish new boundaries or to create balance in a relationship. It may also symbolize a journey of self-discovery and growth, as well as the potential for a positive outcome. The Chariot Tarot card can also suggest the need to be assertive and to make decisions that are beneficial for both parties.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Ace of Cups Tarot card is associated with health, wellness and emotional well-being. It symbolizes the possibility of a healthier, more balanced life and is a reminder to take the time to nurture yourself and those around you. On a physical level, it can signify an improved diet, taking up a new exercise routine, or even getting more rest. It is a sign that it is time to pay attention to your health, both emotionally and physically.

Finances:  
The Ace of Cups Tarot card is associated with abundance and prosperity. It is a reminder to take the time to consider your financial position and take a balanced approach to money management. The card encourages us to look at our resources and assess how we handle our finances. It is a reminder to balance saving and spending, to take a proactive approach to planning our finances, and to put effort into generating more income.

Relationships:  
The Ace of Cups Tarot card is associated with relationships, love and commitment. Its presence encourages us to take the time to assess our relationships and to consider our partner’s needs and feelings. It can signify a new relationship, an engagement or marriage, or even a pregnancy. The card also encourages us to communicate openly and honestly with our loved ones, to share our feelings, and to show compassion and understanding. It is an indicator that it is time to nurture our relationships and take them to the next level.
