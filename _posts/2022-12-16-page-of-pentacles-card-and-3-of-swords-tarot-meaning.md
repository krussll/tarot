## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

When it comes to relationships, The Page Of Pentacles Tarot card could indicate that the person will be making important connections in their life that will benefit them. It could also suggest that the person may form a friendship or professional relationship that helps them achieve their goals.

In conclusion, The Page Of Pentacles Tarot card is an encouraging card that has the potential to bring success and growth in the person’s life. It could bring improved physical and mental health, more financial stability and success, and meaningful connections in relationships.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The Three of Swords Tarot card is a card that symbolizes the pain, suffering and sorrow associated with heartbreak, divorce, loss, and depression. It is a warning that there may be a difficult time ahead and that choices must be made.

When it comes to health, the Three of Swords Tarot card warns of physical and emotional pain due to an illness, surgery, depression, or loss. It can be an indication that a person may be struggling with a serious condition of body or mind and should take the necessary steps to seek help. The card can also indicate a warning of potential health-related consequences from certain life choices.

In terms of finances, the card can indicate a time of serious financial hardship. This is a warning that should never be taken lightly, because the stress brought about by financial strain can often have a direct impact on physical and emotional health. To prevent this, the three of Swords Tarot card advises to make wise decisions and be mindful of the consequences of any financial transactions.

Finally, the Three of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of potential problems in relationships due to heartbreak, betrayal, or divorce. It can be a warning that relationships, whether romantic or platonic, may need extra care and attention. It can also point to the need for open communication and serious, honest conversations to ensure that the relationship remains strong.

The Three of Swords Tarot card is a warning for a time of distress, but it is also a reminder to be mindful and take the necessary steps in order to heal and move forward.
