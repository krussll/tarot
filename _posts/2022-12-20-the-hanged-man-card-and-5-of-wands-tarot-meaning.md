## What does The Hanged Man and 5 Of Wands mean together?

It may be difficult and require some self-sacrifice, but there is a greater understanding and even reward to be gained from facing your obstacles head on. Seek out different angles and don’t be afraid to try something different: it may bring more success than you expected.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hanged Man and 5 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hanged Man and 5 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

Lastly, The Hanged Man is a sign that taking risks can sometimes be necessary in order to achieve financial goals, but it is important to think through the risks one is taking and make wise decisions.

It suggests that by being willing to make sacrifices in order to keep a relationship strong, we can achieve deeper emotional connections with those we love. Additionally, this card encourages us to take a step back from our relationships from time to time in order to gain perspective and allow for the relationship to grow and mature. Lastly, learning to respond to difficult situations in relationships with love and respect rather than anger is an important lesson that the Hanged Man encourages us to embrace.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

When it comes to health, The 5 of Wands tarot card indicates that there are some physical challenges and obstacles in the way. These could reflect a competitive atmosphere among family, friends or colleagues where everyone is striving to do better than the others. The individual may be dealing with physical exhaustion, especially if they have been pushing themselves too hard. This card can also indicate periods of physical strain, like illnesses or injuries that need to be worked through.

When it comes to finances, The 5 of Wands tarot card indicates some challenges or obstacles appearing in a person’s financial life. The person’s finances may be under strain due to competition. This could be in terms of salary negotiations, investment or business deals that haven’t gone as expected, or other forms of financial difficulty. This card suggests that it’s important for the individual to focus on creating stability and security in their finances.

In relationships, The 5 of Wands tarot card often reflects difficulties that stem from competition. This could be in the form of a romantic rivalry, or conflicts between family members. It suggests that the individual is likely to experience some kind of difficulty in their relationships if they are not careful. This card warns against being too competitive and advises the individual to focus on collaboration and compromise.
