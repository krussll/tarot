A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Swords card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Swords card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 9 Of Swords tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card suggest that you are struggling with anxiety, grief, and depression, yet you still have a strong supportive leader in your life. The King Of Cups encourages you to have patience and tolerance with yourself, as well as to seek out an empathetic listening ear to help make sense of the difficult emotions you are experiencing. This leader will be able to provide you with strength and comfort during this difficult time, and to help you navigate the challenging path ahead.

The King of Cups could represent a partner who has been supportive and understanding, or a relationship that started with warmth and compassion but perhaps has become colder in recent times.

The combination of the two cards could indicate a dynamic that cycles between arguments and despair, to moments of warmth and understanding. It could be a situation in which your partner is being supportive but sometimes says or does something that triggers your own dark emotions. On the other hand, it could mean that the moments of understanding and connection are much more fleeting and that it is the arguments and distress that are more persistent.

Ultimately, the combination of these two cards could be a warning to take a step back from the relationship and make sure you’re taking care of yourself emotionally as well as dealing with any issues that are arising in the relationship. Finding a sense of balance and self-care in the midst of the emotional turmoil is important. Having a supportive partner is invaluable, but make sure that it is a relationship that is ultimately healthy and not damaging to your mental health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the fear, anxiety and despair that can accompany mental health illnesses such as depression and PTSD. It can be associated with nightmares and panic attacks, as well as a general feeling of dread and isolation. Physically, it can indicate pain and exhaustion, as well as difficulty sleeping and persistent thoughts that won’t go away. It’s a sign of rising stress levels, and may be a warning to take care of yourself and get the help you need before things get worse.

Finances  
The 9 of Swords can be a warning to be aware of your financial worries and to be careful not to make costly mistakes out of fear and anxiety. It’s a reminder to take control of your finances and not let them control you. It can signify that you are letting your worries about money get in the way of making sound decisions. It can be a motivating force to make sure you’re taking steps to secure your financial future.

Relationships  
The 9 of Swords can be an indication of fear and jealousy that is ruining relationships. It can mean that you are letting paranoia and insecurity damage your relationship and prevent you from seeing the best in your partner. It can be a sign that you’re letting fear or depression keep you from truly enjoying the relationship. It’s a reminder to take stock of your mental health and to make sure that you’re not letting fear control your relationships.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health:

The King of Cups Tarot card can be a very positive omen for one’s physical health. It suggests a leader who is supportive and has a caring attitude. This person will have a deep empathy for the needs of others and will strive to help them achieve physical health and well-being. The King of Cups can be seen as a guardian angel who will go out of their way to ensure that those around them remain in good health. This person has the capacity to provide understanding and practical advice to those with physical ailments in order to promote a sense of healing.

Finances:

The King of Cups Tarot card indicates tolerance when it comes to finances. This person has the ability to be realistic in their approach to financial matters and will strive to maintain a balance between spending and saving. They are able to provide a safe and secure environment for those in need and are willing to provide guidance and support to ensure their financial stability. This person does not put themselves first, but instead looks out for the needs of others, allowing them to balance their finances in a more healthy and stable manner.

Relationships:

The King of Cups Tarot card encourages people to strive for healthy and meaningful relationships. This card indicates that an individual is able to provide support, understanding, and empathy to those they are close to. This individual is patient and tolerant when it comes to issues within relationships and is willing to work to find a solution. The King of Cups can indicate a deep appreciation for others and their needs, allowing the individual to foster healthier and more meaningful relationships. This person is able to provide guidance and understanding when it comes to dealing with any issues that may arise.
