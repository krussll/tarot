A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 3 Of Diamonds card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 3 Of Diamonds have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of The 7 Of Spades and The 3 Of Diamonds indicates a journey through complex circles of interaction.

The 7 Of Spades, representing deceit and victory, hints at a situation where truth may be obscured or distorted.

This may involve overcoming complicated situations through strategic moves, which may not necessarily be considered entirely honest.

This is compounded by the Winter season it denotes, portraying a period of hardship or internal introspection.

The Water element adds depth to the reading suggesting emotional undercurrents and layers of hidden meanings.

Conversely, The 3 Of Diamonds adds a layer of tension and disagreement to the reading.

The card speaks of faith, implying the necessity of belief in oneself through these challenging scenarios.

The Fall season indicated by The 3 Of Diamonds suggests a period of change and transition, and the element of Air brings in ideas, communication, and possible conflict.

Together, these cards draw a picture of navigating through tumultuous situations using shrewd strategies, effective communication, and propelling into a transformative state fueled by self-belief.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together for your love life?

The 7 of Spades and the 3 of Diamonds in a shared reading could hint at an ongoing situation in your love life that needs close attention.

The former represents deceit or victory in line with the element of water, suggesting deep emotions or subconscious desires playing a part.

There may be elements of misunderstandings, secrets or hidden feelings that are influencing your current situation.

But the latter half, victory, implies that with patience and perseverance, you may be able to swim through these troubled waters successfully.

Moving on to the 3 of Diamonds, it represents disputes and faith, pointing towards a struggle or argument that could be plaguing your relationship.

This dispute could be a result of the deceit hinted by the 7 of Spades.

However, faith here is the redeeming factor, it signifies the need for trust in your partner during these harsh times.

It also suggests that if you can maintain your belief in your love and relationship, you can overcome the challenges.

Given that it’s associated with the element of air and the season of Fall, it’s a reminder of change and the value of communication.

To sum up, facing hidden truths and holding strong faith in each other will eventually get you past the test of time in your love life.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, the 7 of Spades is a direct indication of deceit and victory in the realm of your job and finances.

This might suggest that you may not have been given the full picture regarding your financial state or your job.

It could be that you are facing certain hidden realities that remain undisclosed or that someone might be trying to deceive you.

Guidance in such a situation is to rethink things over, reassess, and use your intuition to discern the truth.

However, the victory note also indicates that despite these deceptive scenarios, you will ultimately overcome hindrances.

This could take the shape of financial success, promotion, settlement of debts, or resolution of any lawsuits that you might be dealing with.

On the other hand, the 3 of Diamonds is a sign of disputes and faith.

This suggests that you may find yourself in disagreements or conflicts, which could be at your workplace or could involve your finances.

This could mean bickering, unnecessary quarrels with coworkers that are potentially affecting your ambitions, or financial disputes concerning financial agreements or transactions.

While this may cause worry, the mention of ‘faith’ is an encouragement.

It is an advisory note to not lose hope, maintain your positivity, and keep your belief intact that these disputes will eventually resolve.

The faith element cautions you to be patient, keep your spirit up, and persistently work toward finding an amicable solution, which will ultimately create a harmonious situation in your finances and job.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together for your health?

The 7 of Spades in combination with the 3 of Diamonds can have significant implications for health in a cartomancy reading.

The 7 of Spades, associated with deceit and victory as well as the element of Water and the season of Winter, may suggest hidden or underlying health issues that have not yet surfaced or been recognized.

However, the idea of victory infers that these issues, once identified, can be overcome or managed.

Winter indicates a time of introspection and going inwards, suggesting that it might be a good time for health checkups or to pay closer attention to any signs your body might be showing.

On the other hand, the 3 of Diamonds represents disputes and faith, and is associated with the Fall season and the element of Air.

These could point to disagreements or difficulties with health management plans or healthcare providers, but the idea of ‘faith’ here indicates trust in the process or reassurance that things will work out in the end.

The Air element usually indicates thoughts, communication and change, suggesting that you should be open-minded about health suggestions or lifestyle changes.

Hence, in combination, these cards suggest a period of health challenges but that, with a positive mindset, and necessary changes, recovery and management are entirely possible.
