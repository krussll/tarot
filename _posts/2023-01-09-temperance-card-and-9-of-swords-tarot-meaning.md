## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is a powerful symbol of balance, harmony and self-control. It encourages us to find ways of finding equilibrium in all areas of our lives, from physical health to emotional well-being to our finances and relationships.

When it comes to physical health, the Temperance Tarot card suggests finding a balance between healthy and unhealthy habits. This could mean finding a way to balance regular exercise with occasional indulgences, or eating healthy foods along with occasional treats. Sleeping, drinking and eating in moderation are also good habits to cultivate in order to find a balance for physical health.

When it comes to finances, The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to save and invest, but also to enjoy the rewards of our hard work. Balance is key, and finding a way to spend within our means while still being able to enjoy life is important. It suggests finding a way to plan for the future, while still allowing ourselves to have some fun today.

Finally, The Temperance Tarot card also has something to say about relationships. It encourages us to find balance between offering support and asking for support. This means finding a way to share our feelings and expectations with others while also being open to understanding their experience. It also suggests being patient and tolerant when it comes to interpersonal issues, and to remain mindful of our boundaries.

Overall, the Temperance Tarot card tells us to strive for balance in all aspects of our lives, from physical health to our finances to our relationships. By cultivating balance and moderation, we can find a meaningful and sustainable way of living.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card can represent physical and emotional health problems associated with anxiety, grief, and depression. Physically, it can indicate a lack of restful sleep, exhaustion, fatigue, and headaches. The emotional toll of these states can take a huge toll, leading to decreased concentration, poor decision-making, and irritability. The card can be a warning of the risk of burnout and breakdown.

Finances  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of financial worries and instability. It may indicate a lack of funds, or a feeling of being overwhelmed by money concerns. The card may also signify money-related arguments, or a feeling of being stuck in a precarious financial situation.

Relationships  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card can represent issues and disconnection in relationships. It can point to a lack of communication and understanding, feelings of betrayal or hurt, arguments, or even the ending of a relationship. The card can be a reminder to take appropriate steps to process emotions and create an atmosphere of understanding and trust.
