It is important to remain vigilant and observant, and to be aware of the prying eyes and gossipy words which can so easily lead to chaos and disruption of harmony. The Temperance tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card together symbolize the importance of staying mindful of our surroundings and using our best judgement when it comes to truth and accuracy.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Temperance and Page Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Temperance and Page Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card suggests an equilibrium of energies in all areas of life. It is a reminder that moderation, patience and self-control are essential to achieve a harmonious and balanced life.

Health

The Temperance Tarot card is associated with balance, and it emphasizes the importance of achieving and maintaining balance in terms of health. It encourages us to be mindful of our lifestyle choices and to make sure that we don’t overindulge in unhealthy activities. This card advises us to practice moderation in our diet and exercise routine, to get enough sleep and to set boundaries in our lives in order to prevent burnout and health issues.

Finances

The Temperance Tarot card is also related to finances. This card encourages us to take a balanced approach towards money. We should think before we impulsively spend our money and avoid being reckless. It also emphasizes the importance of creating a budget and following it in order to maintain financial balance. We should also use our money to invest in projects that will bring long-term success, instead of relying on short-term gains.

Relationships

The Temperance Tarot card advises us to nurture our relationships in a balanced way. We should strive to compromise and cooperate in order to prevent arguments and disagreements in our relationships. We should also take the time to get to know each other on a deeper level and practice patience and empathy. This card reminds us to remain firm in our boundaries and be mindful of our words and actions, in order to build strong, trusting relationships.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Pag of Swords Tarot card is a card of caution and careful reflection. In terms of health, it can mean that you need to be mindful of your physical well-being. Even if you have been feeling fine, it is best to take extra precaution and be aware of any changes in your body. Listen to your intuition and pay close attention to potential signs of illness or injury. If a particular symptom persists or gets worse, do not hesitate to seek medical attention immediately.

Finances  
The Page of Swords Tarot card is a warning to be mindful of your finances. It can be easy to get caught up in your own spending habits, but you must be mindful of your budget and prioritize needs over wants. Don’t be afraid to ask for help if you need it. It’s also important to watch out for others taking advantage of you, such as scams and false promises. Financial security is important and the Page of Swords is warning you to stay informed and be careful.

Relationships  
The Page of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to be mindful and honest in your relationships. It can be easy to get lost in gossip and judgment, but you must balance curiosity with respect. Don’t let your insecurities or cynicism run wild, as it can have a negative impact on your relationships. Be open and honest with your partner, friends, and family. Don’t rush relationships and take time to get to know each other. Respect their boundaries and privacy, and never feel the need to pry or spy on them.
