A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and Ace Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Devil card and Ace Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and Ace Of Wands mean together?

The Devil tarot card and the Ace Of Wands tarot card together symbolize the danger that lies in acting on primal urge and allowing ourselves to be consumed by our desires. The Devil card warns us of the potential perils of addiction, envy, and obsession, while The Ace Of Wands urges us to set our creative goals and chase our dreams with enthusiasm and passion. While these two cards are both powerful and can either lead us to great success or dangerous pitfalls, they remind us that it is important to be mindful and disciplined when it comes to our ambitions and goals. The Devil and the Ace Of Wands together remind us to enjoy our creative work and passions, but to keep our goals in focus, and to avoid becoming too engrossed in any particular activity.

You may also be indulging in obsessive behavior that isn’t healthy for your partnership.

At the same time, this pairing could indicate a new start in terms of your relationship. The Ace Of Wands card encourages creativity and a fresh approach to your love life, and this could be just what’s needed to steer it away from the unhealthy environment that The Devil card indicates. With the burst of energy that comes from The Ace Of Wands, you have the chance to channel your passion and enthusiasm into something that could bring joy and fulfillment to your relationship.

It’s important to remember that The Devil card doesn’t have to be seen as a warning sign or a death knell for your relationship. Instead, it’s a reminder that if you’re willing to confront the darker aspects of your love life, it can be transformed into something beautiful. With The Ace Of Wands energy coming in to lend its creative spark, you can start to seriously put your energy into building a healthier and more satisfying romantic connection with your partner. Don’t be afraid to take risks and put yourself out there in order to make that happen.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Devil and Ace Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card can represent a negative spiral in all areas of life, including health, finances and relationships. When this card appears in a reading, it often indicates that unhealthy patterns of behavior or thought are preventing one from achieving the success and satisfaction they desire.

In regards to health, The Devil Tarot card can suggest that addiction, whether it be to drugs, alcohol, food or another unhealthy habit, has taken over and is preventing an individual from achieving optimal health. The card can also signify a tendency to indulge in unhealthy behaviors such as over-eating, lack of exercise, or poor sleep habits. These behaviors can lead to physical and emotional ailments, such as depression and anxiety.

The Devil Tarot card can also imply that an individual is being controlled by greed and materialism, and will never be satisfied with the money and possessions they have, leading to financial instability. The card can represent an excessive focus on things that do not truly bring joy and fulfillment, such as overspending or trying to impress others with unnecessary purchases. The individual may also be enticed by get-rich-quick schemes, unable to see that these schemes can never bring lasting wealth and security.

When it comes to relationships, The Devil Tarot card can signify an unhealthy influence, such as envy or obsession. These negative emotions can lead to obsessive thoughts or behavior, which can be damaging for the individual’s relationships. The Devil Tarot card can also indicate a tendency to be controlled by sexual lust and an inability to move beyond physical desires. This attitude can lead to relationships where the individual only desires physical pleasure and is not interested in forming a deeper connection.

The Devil Tarot card can suggest a negative cycle in all areas of life. It can be a warning to pay attention to unhealthy behaviors and habits, and to work towards breaking the cycle. The card encourages individuals to focus on positive behavior, and to strive towards reaching their highest potential in all areas, including health, finances, and relationships.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

Health: When the Ace of Wands shows up in relation to your health, it can mean a great boost to your energy and vitality. It’s a card that symbolizes a new beginning, so it could indicate a health regimen that you need to get into to help improve your well-being and overall health condition. Taking up a new fitness class or adopting a healthier diet could be among the new projects it suggests.

Finances: The Ace of Wands Tarot card advises you to stay alert and ready to seize a new opportunity that could bring financial fortune and stability, especially if you have been struggling to make ends meet recently. The card suggests that it is time to get creative and use your imagination to come up with new ideas to increase your income. If a project needs to be started, set your sights on gathering resources, finding the right people and picking up the skills needed to make it happen.

Relationships: The Ace of Wands is all about passion and the spark of new beginnings, so it is a great card for relationships. It suggests an enthusiasm for exploring and growing together, and reminds you to keep your eyes open for new possibilities and experiences. Spending less time at home and going out to explore new places and cultures together could be a great way to bring excitement and stimulation into both of your lives.
