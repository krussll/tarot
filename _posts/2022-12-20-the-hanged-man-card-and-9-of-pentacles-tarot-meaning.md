Through wisdom and self sacrifice you can gain financial gain, but it is important to also remember to take time for yourself and find time to enjoy the smaller things in life. The importance of both cards is to remember to find balance in all aspects of your life, have a different perspective, and understand that it is okay to enjoy the rewards of your hard work.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health: The Hanged Man Tarot card is a reminder that to stay healthy, you must be willing to make sacrifices, sometimes of your own comfort. This often involves making changes in diet, adopting healthier habits, or even using external resources such as professional medical help. It is also a sign that you need to take a step back and look at your health from a different perspective. When you’re feeling stuck, it’s time to pause and take a different approach.

Finances: Finances can be complicated and intimidating, but the Hanged Man Tarot card is a reminder to approach them differently. You may need to give yourself permission to take a break from your traditional financial plans and look for new opportunities or out-of-the-box ideas. This card also reminds you to be patient and to trust that the current financial situation is a necessary lesson to help you see the bigger picture.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages you to self-reflect and take responsibility for your own actions. It is a reminder that in order to have healthy relationships, you must be willing to sacrifice your own comfort and make difficult changes. This card also symbolizes understanding and empathy, which can benefit you when it comes to maintaining relationships. It encourages you to look at the situation from different points of view and understand that no matter the circumstances, everyone’s choices are valid.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

The 9 of Pentacles is a Tarot card that symbolizes luxury, self-sufficiency and financial gain. This card encourages personal growth, independence and opportunities to increase wealth. In terms of health, it encourages excellent self care and the ability to take advantage of moments of relaxation and solitude. It speaks to proper nutrition, adequate sleep and general health, as well as spiritual and emotional wellbeing.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Pentacles signifies the fruits of hard work and wise investments. This card encourages practicality and financial security. It suggests that one is on the way to true financial stability, and is able to enjoy it in the present moment. This card is also a reminder that financial success can be enjoyed without compromising one’s values.

As for relationships, the 9 of Pentacles speaks to solo pleasures and activities. It speaks to being happy with your own company and enjoying things like vacations, hobbies, and other activities that can be done alone. It also encourages having the space to reflect and really get to know oneself. Further, it encourages setting boundaries in relationships and financial independence. In relationships, this means appreciating time alone and without others, while still nurturing strong relationships with others.
