A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together signify that there is a way to find success and fulfillment through collaboration, communication, and mutual understanding. This emphasizes that solutions can be found when different points of view are held in respect, and that friendships can be formed and strengthened through celebrations. Through this, the truth emerges, and everyone benefits. With the right mindset and understanding, a win-win situation can be achieved.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

When it comes to health, The Justice Tarot card speaks to the need to maintain balance and fairness. It suggests that one should exercise their right to make choices that are not only conducive to their own wellbeing, but also to the wider community. This includes making sure that health care is accessible and affordable to everyone, regardless of background or income level. The card also highlights the importance of seeking out solutions that are mutually beneficial and fair to all involved – a win-win approach to achieving better health and wellbeing.

When it comes to finances, The Justice Tarot card suggests that one should strive for balance and fairness in all aspects of financial decisions. This includes striving for fairness in relation to taxation, investment, and other money management strategies. The card also speaks to the need to make sure that the money you have is being managed in a responsible and ethical way, to ensure that all affected party’s needs are met equitably.

Relationships are a major component of life and The Justice Tarot card suggests that fairness is essential for any relationship to thrive. This means creating solutions that are mutually beneficial and understanding that compromise and fairness are key elements of a successful relationship. It also speaks to the need to seek out justice when it comes to interpersonal disputes, by assessing the situation fairly and finding a mutually beneficial solution that is fair to both parties.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a card of celebration, friendship and support, and those same themes can be applied to your physical and mental health. This card encourages you to reach out to those around you — friends, family or even a therapist — to discuss any issues that you may be struggling with. It can also signify emotional healing through connecting with those who lift you up. It is also a reminder to take time to celebrate the successes and victories, no matter how small, that you achieve in your health journey.

Finances:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card signifies celebration, friendship and support. When it comes to finances, these themes can help remind you to avoid going it alone. It can be tempting to deal with financial hardships on your own, but this card urges you to reach out to those around you for advice, assistance or just a listening ear. It can also be a reminder to recognize and celebrate any successes that you have achieved as it relates to your finances.

Relationships:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card signifies friendship, celebrations and a circle of support. It is a reminder that relationships do not have to be perfect for them to be meaningful. This card encourages you to lean on those around you for support and to foster friendships with those who understand and accept you. It is also a reminder to appreciate and celebrate the important relationships that you have and to take the time to strengthen the bonds that you have with those who are important to you.
