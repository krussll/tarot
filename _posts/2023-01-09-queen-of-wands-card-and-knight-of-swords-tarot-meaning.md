A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean together?

It may represent a current period of your life in which you are about to embark in a lifestyle change. You have a lot of confidence and self-assurance in yourself, which allow you to take risks and take action. In addition, you possess a sharp tongue and are ready to speak your truth with clarity and power. Your current life stage is an exciting one, as you are about to make changes that will bring great rewards for you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health  
The Queen of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of health and vitality. It suggests good physical health, but also emotional and spiritual health. This is a reminder to take care of your body, mind, and soul. It can indicate a need for more self-care and a well-balanced lifestyle. Eating nutritious meals, getting enough rest, exercising, and spending time in nature can all help to restore your well-being. Additionally, it’s important to focus on your emotional and spiritual health. Spend time reflecting on your emotions and what you need to do to make yourself feel better.

Finances  
When it comes to finances, The Queen of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to be confident and independent. It encourages you to rely on your own abilities. Take the lead in managing your finances and make smart investments. Don’t be afraid to take risks and make bold decisions. The Queen of Wands suggests that you have the power to increase your wealth.

Relationships  
The Queen of Wands Tarot card encourages you to be bold and confident in all relationships. It suggests that you can handle any situation with grace and poise. Don’t be afraid to voice your opinions and take charge in your relationships. Also, be sure to spend time nurturing your relationships with friends and family. No matter what the situation, be sure to show respect and kindness. Ultimately, The Queen of Wands encourages you to lead with confidence.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

Health: The Knight of Swords Tarot card is a strong warning and reminder to be mindful of our personal health and wellbeing. The card symbolizes a fast-paced lifestyle and a need to tackle large goals quickly and efficiently. This energy can often lead to burnout and exhaustion, so it may be wise to take some time for rest and relaxation as well. This card can also signify a lifestyle change, such as making positive choices in regards to diet, exercise and overall health. It is important to listen to our intuition and be aware of potential changes in our health before pushing ourselves too hard and too fast.

Finances: The Knight of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a risk taker and someone who is willing to make quick decisions with their finances. This card can be an indication of a shift in financial circumstances due to the need to take calculated risks. Although it can be a sign of wealth, this card reminds us to be mindful and to not let our ambitions and greed blind us, as they can easily lead to poor financial decisions. It also suggests that we should carefully consider the risks and rewards of our financial decisions before making them.

Relationships: The Knight of Swords Tarot card can represent a fast-talking and passionate lover who is ready to make quick decisions. They may often speak before thinking and can be quite impulsive in their relationships. This card encourages people to be mindful of the emotions of their partners and take the time to listen and understand their needs. It is important to remember that relationships require patience and understanding, rather than impulsive decisions and hasty communications. The Knight of Swords Tarot card can signify a new beginning for relationships and can also signify a need for a lifestyle change in order to improve existing relationships.
