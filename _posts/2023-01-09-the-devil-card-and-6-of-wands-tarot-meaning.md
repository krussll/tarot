## What does The Devil and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The combination of The Devil tarot card and The 6 Of Wands tarot card offer a message of hope that despite our materialistic, self-destructive urges, we can still achieve our goals. The Devil suggests that we need to face and take ownership of our vices and weaknesses in order to reach our greatest potential. The 6 Of Wands indicates that by taking responsibility for our actions and being honest and true to ourselves, we can gain the recognition and rewards that come with success. We can gain the victory we desire, but it will take hard work and dedication to break free of our addictions and destructive patterns in order to achieve it.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card is an ominous sign that signals a period of difficulty and darkness. It points to a time in our lives when we may struggle with feelings of addiction, greed, envy, materialism and obsession. While these feelings can be natural, they can also be harmful.

We should focus on self-care and be aware of the potential for destructive behavior and practices that could bring harm to our bodies and souls. We should strive to keep ourselves healthy and practice healthy habits such as eating nutritious foods and getting enough exercise.

The Devil card can also be a warning about finances. Greed and envy can lead us to make impulsive decisions with money that we will regret. We should be mindful of our spending, focus on earning and saving, and practice fiscal responsibility. Looking only to our own material gain, instead of paying attention to our own needs as well as the needs of those around us can lead us down a dark path. We should strive to find balance between wanting to obtain material things and being satisfied with our current situation.

When it comes to relationships, The Devil Tarot card can be a warning that we are overly possessive or sexually obsessive. We should learn to balance passion and intimacy with respect and responsibility. We shouldn’t let our desires run away with us and we should strive to maintain healthy relationships with our partners.

The Devil Tarot card is a sign of potential danger. We should be aware of how our darker emotions may lead us down paths of destruction and try to keep ourselves healthy, financially secure, and in healthy relationships. By being conscious of our feelings and being aware of the potential for harm, we can avoid the darkness that The Devil card warns against.
