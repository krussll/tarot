This combination of cards speaks to a reliance on a mature individual who has your best interest in mind and wants to help you make the best of your situation. With the supporting presence of someone in authority, you can trust that the transition will be as smooth and successful as possible.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This card advises to not blindly accept all health advice, but to actively seek out new ways of improving your physical and mental health through transition and movement. Taking up a new exercise regimen or healthy habit can be one way to transition and move towards improved health. Sticking to a regular routine and embracing the journey to better health can help move away from unhealthy habits or cycles of ill health.

Finances: The 6 of Swords Tarot card is a sign of financial transition and change that can lead to improved financial health. It could signify a need for a new approach to managing money, such as budgeting, taking on a side hustle, or investing in a more lucrative market. It could be a sign of a new opportunity to make money, such as starting a business or finding a new source of income. Taking action and moving away from past financial mistakes can lead to improved financial health.

Relationships: The 6 of Swords Tarot card is a sign to transition and move away from unhealthy relationships dynamics in order to improve the state of one’s relationships. This could be a reminder to let go of past grudges or toxic patterns of behavior, while also transitioning and growing in healthy, positive ways with others. Introducing new activities or forms of communication can help bring relationships in a new direction and can open up a new world of understanding and respectful dialogue. Moving away from stagnation and into the realm of growth is a necessary part of improving the quality of relationships.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health:

The King of Cups tarot card is a powerful symbol of balance and peace. In terms of one’s health, this card suggests learning to care for and nurture oneself with acceptance, understanding and empathy. Not only should individuals understand their needs better, they should also be more receptive to the needs of others. This kind of attention to self and others fosters a deep connection and trust that allows individuals to be in better tune with their physical and mental needs. This sense of balance and harmony can provide a strong foundation for health and well-being.

Finances:

For those who are seeking financial advice, the King of Cups tarot card may be a helpful sign. This card suggests understanding and tolerance when it comes to money matters. It encourages individuals to be conscious of their spending habits, and to recognize their financial goals and ambitions. This card may also symbolize the need to depend on others for help, either through financial advice, support, or even a loan. In this sense, it suggests a degree of generosity, as well as a level of trust and loyalty that can help people to better manage their finances.

Relationships:

The King of Cups tarot card can bring a sense of peace and balance to relationships. This card symbolizes the importance of understanding, empathy and tolerance in intimate relationships. Couples should strive to cultivate a spirit of acceptance and understanding, and to show compassion and respect for one another. They should also be willing to be open to communication and to listen to each other without judgement. This kind of openness and understanding fosters a deep connection and trust that can help couples stay together in the long run.
