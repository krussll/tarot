## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of luxury, self-sufficiency, and financial gain. The card often suggests that you are financially secure and have invested wisely. It is also a reminder to take care of yourself and enjoy the pleasant things in life. This card encourages you to take pride in your accomplishments and revel in the rewards of your hard work.

When it comes to your health, The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes taking the time to nurture and care for your body and mind. It advises you to slow down and practice self-care on a regular basis. Consider investing in treatment or therapies that help reduce stress and relieve tension.

In terms of finances, The 9 of Pentacles implies that you are in a good place. You have worked hard and are now reaping the rewards. You have a secure financial standing and can treat yourself to something special. Most importantly, this card reminds you to stay invested and be mindful of your spending.

In the realm of relationships, The 9 of Pentacles encourages you to value yourself and your own needs. This card is a message to recognize that you are strong and capable of taking care of yourself. It’s important to recognize that you don’t need anyone else to make you happy or fulfill your needs. This card also suggests that it is ok to spend time alone and relish in your own accomplishments.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

In terms of health, the 8 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a desire to withdraw from any medical treatments that are not helping to improve one’s condition. It could reflect a feeling of being stuck in a health rut, and a need to pull back and reassess one’s treatment plan. By withdrawing from the treatments or appointments that are not helping, one can take a look at what options are available and find a more suitable path to better health.

In terms of finances, the 8 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a need to retreat from certain financial situations. This could mean avoiding certain investments or taking a step back from high risk or speculative activities. It can also represent a pause to look over one’s current financial situation, find ways to reduce debt and create a more stable financial foundation.

When it comes to relationships, the 8 of Cups Tarot card can have a negative connotation, as it can reflect one’s desire to withdraw from a relationship they feel is no longer beneficial. It could signal a need to have a break, or to gain some space to reassess the situation and decide if the relationship is worth continuing. Alternatively, it could also represent a desire to move on to a more fulfilling relationship.
