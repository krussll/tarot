A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 Of Wands and The 5 Of Cups card together represent a difficult and complex journey of both victory and loss. They remind us of the hardships, obstacles, and sacrifices that must be faced in order to attain success. It speaks of the necessity of learning from past failures and losses in order to move forward. At the same time, it encourages us to take pride in our accomplishments, however small, and to recognize the value of our efforts, no matter how difficult the path. Though it is a reminder that we must grieve our losses, it also emphasizes the importance of looking ahead and striving for the successes that will follow.

We may find ourselves overwhelmed by a sense of sadness, as if there’s something that’s missing from our romantic lives. We may even feel like we’ve been let down or abandoned in some way.

In looking at these cards together, we may be faced with the idea that things could be both positive and negative. We may ultimately be successful in our love life, but we may also have to go through a period of personal growth and pain as we strive toward our goal. We may have to overcome obstacles, accept losses, and face our sadness in order to ultimately achieve the success that we are striving towards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands Tarot card means good news when it comes to health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, this card often indicates that a period of healing is coming soon. It may be a physical healing, a spiritual one, or a mental one. The 6 of Wands Tarot card encourages us to take the time we need to recuperate and to focus on our well-being. However, it also indicates that the hard work we put in to achieve this is acknowledged and appreciated, perhaps even rewarded.

When it comes to finances, the 6 of Wands Tarot card indicates a period of financial success and stability. This could mean getting a pay rise or finding an unexpected windfall of money. It also implies that hard work and dedication have paid off and that financial rewards are coming our way.

In terms of relationships, the 6 of Wands Tarot card suggests that we are being recognized and admired for who we are and what we have accomplished. It suggests that others view us in a positive light and they appreciate our efforts. Our relationships will benefit from this, with more mutual respect and understanding.

Overall, the 6 of Wands Tarot card offers us a message of hope and recognition. It tells us that our hard work and dedication will not go unnoticed, and that good things are coming our way, in terms of health, finances and relationships.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: The Five of Cups Tarot card can imply physical or mental exhaustion. One may feel a sense of being overwhelmed, which is often accompanied by feelings of sadness and apathy. A person can also become drained, or suffer from a complete lack of energy or motivation. The card also indicates a lack of self-care and can be a sign of burnout.

Finances: The Five of Cups Tarot card suggests that financial losses may be imminent. This card can warn a person to take caution when it comes to making any big financial decisions. One may encounter financial difficulty, a setback, or the loss of assets. It can also represent a lack of financial stability or difficulty in reaching one’s financial goals.

Relationships: The Five of Cups Tarot card may reflect emotional pain within a relationship. This card can signify disappointment, regret, feeling of abandonment, or emotional distance. It can indicate that a relationship is in need of attention and emotional healing, or that one should take a step back from the relationship to reassess it. If the card is present, it could also indicate that a person needs to take a proactive approach to overcoming the difficulties in their relationship.
