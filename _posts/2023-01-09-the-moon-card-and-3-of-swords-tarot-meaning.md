A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Moon card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Moon card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Moon and 3 Of Swords mean together?

When you draw The Moon tarot card as well as The 3 Of Swords, it can indicate that you may have some unseen problems, or issues that you don’t understand yet, but that may require some voluntary changes in order to find a resolution. You may have to trust your intuition in the face of heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression or surgery, as these experiences may be unavoidable. It may be a difficult, emotional journey, and it is important to keep faith that everything will work out for the best in the long run.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Moon and 3 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Moon tarot card in combination with the Three of Swords tarot card can indicate that a difficult period is coming up in your love life. This could be in the form of a breakup, divorce, or other form of relationship separation. However, even though this is a difficult and sad time, there is also a sense of hope implied by The Moon card. It suggests that there are unseen problems that will have to be addressed before the situation can be fully healed. This break is seen as necessary for growth and progress.

The Three of Swords card indicates that sadness and grief will be part of the process, but it also provides a source of strength. It is a reminder that heartbreak is a natural part of life, and that it can teach us important lessons about relationships and self-care. The Moon card helps us to trust our intuition when making difficult decisions, and understand that leaving a situation is not always a bad thing.

The combined energy of The Moon and Three of Swords tarot cards suggests that although this is a difficult and emotional time, it is also an important part of the healing process. There is a promise of new beginnings, and an understanding that the struggles of the present are necessary for growth in the future. This period of separation and healing can be used to reflect on our emotions and find new ways to connect with ourselves and others.

### What does The Moon and 3 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When The Moon tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card come together in a reading, it can be a sign of financial and job instability. The Moon tarot card can represent unseen problems that may be lurking beneath the surface of your life, which can affect your job and service. These might be related to underlying issues in your relationships, hidden debt or other forms of financial instability. You may also find yourself dealing with voluntary changes in your career or finances, such as switching jobs, taking a pay cut or changing your budget.

The 3 Of Swords tarot card can represent heartbreak and loss, which can also have a significant impact on your finances. This card warns of potential divorce, depression and even surgery, which can have a negative effect on your professional and financial life. This card can also be indicative of difficult decisions you may have to make regarding your job or money, such as whether to quit your job or take a financial risk.

Together, these two cards can be a cautionary tale of the potential for unexpected financial and professional instability. It is important to trust your intuition when it comes to money and career matters, and to be prepared for anything unexpected. Though it may be difficult to accept that financial and professional changes are a part of life, it is important to stay informed and keep a close eye on your finances to ensure that any potential problems can be identified and rectified quickly.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

The Moon Tarot card represents unseen problems, voluntary changes and a need to trust your intuition. When the Moon card appears in a Tarot reading, it suggests that it is time to be introspective and pay attention to what is going on with your inner state. It can also mean that you are being asked to explore the depths and darkness of your emotions and psychic energy.

For health, The Moon Tarot card can bring messages of taking a closer look at your physical and spiritual health. It advises trusting your intuition and uncovering any unseen problems that may be impacting your total well-being. This could include anything from physical ailments to negative emotions, so it’s important to take the time to explore what may be causing an imbalance. You could be asked to make some voluntary changes that could improve your health and help you take better care of yourself.

In terms of finances, The Moon Tarot card can bring messages of paying closer attention to your finances and managing your money carefully. It warns of possible unseen dangers related to financial matters, such as risky investments or unwise decisions, and advises using your intuition to guide the course of action. This card could also represent a time of voluntary changes, where you look at different alternatives to improve your financial situation.

For relationships, The Moon Tarot card can be an indication of exploring the depths and trusting one’s intuition about the relationship. It warns of any potential unseen problems, such as hidden feelings of mistrust or feelings that you may not be able to express. This card invites you to look more closely at how you can make voluntary changes in the relationship that could lead to more trust and understanding. It can also mean that it is time to be more honest with yourself and your partner about how you feel.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords Tarot card sends a message of pain and sorrow when it makes an appearance in a reading. It symbolizes heartbreak, loss, and emotional turmoil, and it can indicate a difficult period in one’s life. When it comes to health, this card can signify physical pain, depression, surgery, and other health issues. It can also be a warning of illness that can be difficult to overcome.

When it comes to finances, the 3 of Swords Tarot card can represent a financial setback or a period of financial insecurity. It can also suggest difficulties in maintaining financial stability, or the possibility of a devastating loss that could affect one’s finances.

Finally, this card can be indicative of strained relationships. It suggests that some relationships may be in need of work, or that a romantic relationship or marriage may be on the rocks. Conversely, it can also indicate an end to a relationship that has been causing emotional pain, allowing one to move on to a happier and healthier relationship.
