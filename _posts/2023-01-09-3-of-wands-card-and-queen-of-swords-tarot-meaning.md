A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean together?

When the 3 of Wands tarot card appears alongside the Queen of Swords, it indicates a situation in which team work and honest communication will bring about expansion and new opportunities. This powerful combination indicates that taking risks and traveling beyond the comfort zone can produce rewards. There is a strong chance of success when working together with this combination, as the Queen of Swords suggests a self-sufficient and honest person who is able to guide the process through clear communication. The 3 of Wands encourages you to come up with innovative ideas to maximize the potential of the team and take advantage of any opportunities.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health:  
The 3 Of Wands Tarot card is associated with general well-being, physical and spiritual success, and inner balance. It is said that this card implies a strong and healthy system, free of any imbalances. Having the 3 Of Wands Tarot card in a health context, symbolizes the need to work in a team, to collaborate, and to trust that your body knows best. It suggests that physical and spiritual health are integral parts of overall well-being. This card encourages us to seek out successful partnerships that nourish our health and wellness needs.

Finances:  
The 3 Of Wands Tarot card signifies success in financial matters, as well as the ability to make and receive investments. It encourages us to take measured risks and to expand our opportunities in order to create financial stability. This card is a sign of abundance and wealth, achieved through careful planning and sound team-work. It’s a reminder that we need to be aware of our financial goals, and to trust the process of achieving them.

Relationships:  
The 3 Of Wands Tarot card speaks to relationships, signaling the importance of strong bonds between people. It promotes healthy, fruitful relationships that are founded upon collaboration, trust, and mutual respect. It suggests that relationships are strengthened through honest and open communication, and that working as a team is essential for relationship growth. For those in a relationship already, the 3 Of Wands Tarot card encourages each partner to respect the needs of the other and to embrace the strengths and weaknesses of their relationship.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a person who is determined to take care of their own health, through self-sufficiency and total honesty. This card represents a person who is willing to take responsibility for their own health, by being aware of their physical needs and caring for their body accordingly. A Queen of Swords type individual is likely to be mindful of their nutrition, exercise, sleep, and mental health. They also readily confront any health issues they may have, and strive to find the best course of action to address them.

Finances:  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card suggests a person who has their finances under control and is wise with their money. This card symbolizes a person who is honest with themselves and others about their finances, and is able to prioritize their needs and wants when it comes to spending their money. This is a person who understands the importance of budgeting and savings, and is not afraid to ask for help or advice if needed. The Queen of Swords Tarot card is for someone who is in control of their financial situation, and is using their resources to create opportunities for themselves.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card represents a person who is strong and independent when it comes to relationships. This Queen is open to meeting new people and forming meaningful connections, but is also fiercely protective of her emotional boundaries. This card suggests a person who will communicate their feelings clearly and honestly, and is not afraid to express their needs in a relationship. The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages a person to value their own intuition and feelings, and to choose relationships that are both fulfilling and respectful.
