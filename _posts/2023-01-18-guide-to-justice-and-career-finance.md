Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The Justice card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Justice, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Justice.

## Justice And Career And Finances – An Overview

The Justice tarot card speaks to the importance of cause and effect when it comes to health.

This includes the idea that when we make healthy decisions, the effects are positive, leading to a win-win situation.

Moreover, it means that if we make unhealthy decisions, there are consequences; the truth will come out in the form of physical, emotional, and mental repercussions.

When we make decisions with our health in mind, we can expect to see a positive outcome.

In essence, The Justice is a reminder that what we do today affects our health in the future, so it’s important to make choices that are in our best interest.

## Upright Meanings For Justice And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The Justice tarot card symbolizes cause and effect, win-win solutions, and the truth coming out.

In the context of my career and finances, this card speaks to the importance of making decisions that are in alignment with your true values, and the need to seek out win-win situations in any exchanges.

As the truth comes out, I will be able to assess my current financial and career situation objectively and make decisions that are in my best interests and for the benefit of all parties.

By making decisions that are based in truth and fairness, I can create a path of success and prosperity in my career and finances.

### What It Means For The Future

The Justice tarot card is a sign that I should consider all of the causes and effects of my decisions, both in my career and financially.

This card indicates that I should strive for win-win solutions in all aspects of my life, as this will bring me the most long-term success.

It also suggests that I should be honest and truthful with myself and with others, as this will help me to create the best possible outcome for all involved.

In terms of my career and finances, this card suggests that I should make decisions that are fair and beneficial to all, and that taking the time to think things through and to weigh up the options will bring about the best possible outcome for me.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Justice tarot card speaks to the importance of finding cause-and-effect solutions and working toward win-win outcomes.

In terms of careers and finances, this card encourages those close to me to seek balanced decisions that honor both parties and weigh the potential consequences before making any moves.

It is also a reminder that truth and honesty are paramount when it comes to financial decisions; any attempts to hide or manipulate information can lead to disastrous results.

Ultimately, The Justice encourages a balance between ambition and responsibility, and advises my loved ones to keep their financial and professional transactions open and honest for best results.

## Reversed Meanings For Justice And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When The Justice tarot card is reversed, it can suggest that the balance between career and finances is out of alignment.

This may mean that you’re focusing too much on one or the other, or that you’re not looking at the bigger picture when it comes to your finances and career.

Rather than seeking quick wins, The Justice reversed encourages you to look for win-win solutions.

You may need to re-evaluate your priorities and make sure that you’re investing time and energy in both your career and your finances.

It’s important to weigh the pros and cons of each decision and look at the long-term impact they will have.

The truth may be hard to accept, but The Justice reversed reminds us that we need to be honest with ourselves and honest with others if we are to achieve balance.

This could mean taking a step back to look objectively at your career and finances and seeing where you need to make changes.

It is only through honest self-reflection and acting upon our findings that we can achieve true balance between our career and finances.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Justice card in my future career and finances reflects a need for me to be mindful of the consequences of my actions.

This card encourages me to consider the long-term effects of my decisions, and to strive for win-win solutions.

It may be important for me to explore all options before making a decision, and to be honest and truthful with myself and others.

In terms of my finances, this card could be a reminder to me to think before I act, and to be mindful of my spending decisions.

It might be a sign to take a more balanced approach to money, to be sure that I’m investing in myself, but also taking the time to save and plan for the future.

The reversed Justice card could also suggest that I should be more honest with myself and others about my finances, and to make sure I’m living within my means and not getting into debt.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed Justice tarot card can indicate a lack of balance and fairness in the lives of those close to you when it comes to their careers and finances.

This could mean that they are dealing with power struggles in the workplace, or that there is an uneven distribution of income that is causing stress and tension.

It could also indicate that hidden truths and secrets are causing issues for them, such as a lack of transparency or unfair practices.

It may be time for them to take a closer look at their work and financial situation, and make sure that they are being treated fairly and with respect.

They should also be aware of the impact their actions have on others, and strive to create win-win solutions.

Ultimately, the reversed Justice tarot card suggests that they will be able to find equilibrium and establish balance in their lives, but they must be willing to make changes and challenge the status quo.
