Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Hierophant card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Hierophant, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Hierophant.

## The Hierophant And Love – An Overview

The Hierophant tarot card indicates that when it comes to health, traditional expectations and conforming to established norms is important.

This card encourages us to follow the rules, do the research, and consult with experts in order to maintain our health and well-being.

It speaks to the importance of taking preventive measures and understanding the consequences of our actions.

This card suggests that while health trends may come and go, adhering to the conventions and conventional wisdom is the most effective way to maintain good health.

It demonstrates that sometimes the best results come from following the tried and true path.

## Upright Meanings For The Hierophant And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Hierophant tarot card symbolizes traditional and conventional expectations when it comes to love.

In a relationship, The Hierophant encourages us to follow social norms and consider the expectations of our family and friends when making our decisions.

This card reminds us to respect the expectations of our culture and to not be too rebellious when it comes to love.

It also encourages us to reflect on our relationship to ensure it is fulfilling the expectations of those around us.

Ultimately, The Hierophant encourages us to find balance between our desires and the expectations of our culture and community.

### What It Means For The Future

The Hierophant tarot card suggests that your future love life will be filled with traditional expectations and a sense of conformity.

You may find that you are drawn to someone who adheres to social conventions, who is comfortable with traditional roles, and who is content with a stable and conventional relationship.

You could find that you are deeply attracted to someone who is familiar with your culture and way of life, and is comfortable with the same expectations and ideas that you have.

Ultimately, The Hierophant suggests that your future love life will be filled with familiar and comfortable traditions, and a sense of conformity that will ensure a stable and secure relationship.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Hierophant tarot card speaks to the importance of maintaining traditional values and customs in the relationships we have with those close to us.

It speaks to the importance of adhering to the expectations of society and the people around us, rather than making rash decisions that could disrupt the harmony of our relationships.

This card suggests that we should strive to maintain a balance between following our own beliefs and respecting the beliefs of those closest to us.

Ultimately, this card reminds us that the love of those close to us should be guided by conventions and expectations that have been established over time, and that by following these conventions we can build stronger and more meaningful relationships.

## Reversed Meanings For The Hierophant And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The Hierophant tarot card appears reversed in a reading related to love, it indicates that the querent is ready to break free from traditional expectations or any conventional notions of love.

The card may also suggest that the querent is looking for something more unconventional or out of the ordinary in their relationship.

This may mean a yearning for something wild and passionate, or it could suggest that the couple seeks a more unique or spiritual connection.

The reversed Hierophant can also indicate that the querent has grown tired of traditional norms and expectations when it comes to love.

They may feel that they need more freedom, space, and autonomy in the relationship, and could be attempting to break out of the mold of what is expected of them.

Additionally, this can point to the need for more creativity and exploration in the relationship, and a desire to shake things up and experience something new.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed Hierophant tarot card appears in a reading about your love life, it can indicate a future free of traditional expectations and conventions.

If you’ve been feeling restricted by the expectations of others, this card is a sign of liberation.

You have the freedom to break away from what society deems as “normal” and create a love life that is unique to you.

This card is a reminder to trust your inner voice and follow your own heart when it comes to matters of the heart.

This is your chance to break out of the box and explore possibilities that you may have previously dismissed.

You can expect a unique and unconventional relationship with someone who encourages you to be yourself.

This person is likely to accept you unconditionally and be supportive of your individual goals and dreams.

Your future love life is sure to be filled with an adventure and excitement that comes from breaking away from the traditional expectations and conventions of society.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Hierophant tarot card is reversed, it suggests that the traditional expectations and conventions of my closest relationships are being disrupted.

The reversed Hierophant suggests that I am no longer willing to conform to the expectations of those in my life, and instead, I am starting to make my own decisions and forge my own path.

This could mean that I am questioning the conventional expectations of commitment, loyalty, and intimacy that I used to take for granted in my relationships.

The reversed Hierophant also reminds me to remain open-minded and flexible in my relationships.

It suggests that I can still honor the traditions and conventions of my relationships while also allowing for new and different ways of being together.

I may find that by questioning the traditional expectations of my relationships, I can create a connection with my loved ones that is even deeper and more meaningful than before.
