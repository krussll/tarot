They suggest that when you put in the hard work and dedication, it will pay off. The King of Pentacles implies that you are a wise and practical leader who values the rewards of working your way up to success. You understand the importance of investing in yourself, becoming an expert in your field and reaping the rewards of your labor. The 8 of Pentacles encourages you to enjoy your occupation and find satisfaction in the work you do, knowing that you are on the path to success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This card encourages taking the time to understand the principles of money management and to develop strategies that are tailored to your particular financial goals. It suggests considering different investment options and attending to the details of any financial arrangements. With the 8 of Pentacles, the message is to attend to each financial decision with care and to do the hard work necessary to build financial security.

Relationships: The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is about maintaining a relationship through the practice of the craft of relationship building. This card indicates that it takes time, patience, and effort to grow mutual respect and care. The 8 of Pentacles encourages each person to take meaningful action to show their love and appreciation for the other person. ## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The King of Pentacles Tarot card means that health and wellness will be a priority. He takes the long term view and employs preventative measures to stay in top physical, mental and emotional shape. He knows the value of diet, exercise, and rest and invests his time and money in maintaining a healthy lifestyle. He is also careful about who he shares his health information with, as he wants to protect his privacy.

Finances: The King of Pentacles Tarot card signifies a smart investor who is able to make wise decisions regarding his money. He typically has a good eye for what will bring him a good return and is not afraid to take calculated risks. With money, he is disciplined and knows how to save, even when tempted to spend. He understands the power of compounding interest and the importance of budgeting so he can reach his financial goals.

Relationships: The King of Pentacles Tarot card is a leader and provider who can be supportive and reliable. He values his relationships and is willing to invest time and energy in order to nurture them. He has a good balance between romance and practicality and is willing to go the extra mile to show appreciation. He is capable of long-term commitment and makes sure both parties are happy in the relationship. He can be trusted to keep his word and make sure his actions match his intentions.
