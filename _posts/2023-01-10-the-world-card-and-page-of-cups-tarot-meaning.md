## What does The World and Page Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The World and The Page Of Cups Tarot cards can mean a time of completion and growth. This could mean completing a goal or lesson, such as graduating from school or finishing a creative project. It could also mean emigrating to another country, undertaking a spiritual journey, or completing a volunteer project. The Page of Cups suggests this could involve a studious and dedicated individual, such as a young person or newly found love. As you complete this journey there will be a sense of growth, achievement and joy that will stay with you for a long time.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

The World Tarot card symbolizes a journey coming to an end, and an opportunity for growth and learning. This card can have a variety of meanings depending on the context in which it appears in a reading, but generally it speaks of completion, balance, and moving forward. When it comes to health, finances and relationships, the World Tarot card can represent a period of transformation and healing.

Health: The World Tarot card indicates that a health issue or challenge is coming to an end, or is being transformed in some way. This could be a physical illness, mental health issue, or even a spiritual challenge. The card suggests that all is coming to a place of balance and harmony, with the potential to find healing and peace in the process.

Finances: The World Tarot card often speaks of financial success, sometimes indicating that a legal matter has been resolved, or that a period of financial difficulty is coming to an end. Money may come to you through new opportunities or investment, and the card suggests that all debts have been paid and all commitments have been fulfilled. The card provides the potential to move forward with a new level of financial stability.

Relationships: The World Tarot card suggests that a relationship is transforming or coming to a close. This could indicate the ending of a romantic relationship, or a business partnership, or simply the ending of a phase in a current relationship. The card speaks to the potential for renewal, and for both parties in the relationship to find closure, joy and harmony as they move forward.

The World Tarot card indicate a period of transformation, balance and healing in all aspects of life, particularly in terms of our health, finances and relationships. It is a card of hope and possibility, as it speaks of new beginnings and the potential for growth and learning.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health: The Page Of Cups Tarot card speaks of a youthful energy and a deep inner quest for health and healing. It can represent a period of exploration into new dietary options, or a time of researching and understanding the body’s needs. This card indicates a willingness to learn new concepts and open up to new possibilities. It symbolizes a desire to take care of oneself both physically and emotionally.

Finances: The Page Of Cups Tarot card can also signify a fragile financial situation, as it is a card of beginnings and new ventures. It can indicate a need to be mindful with resources, or a time to explore new options when it comes to money. It can also symbolize new opportunities, as well as an inner motivation to take charge of finances. It can also suggest potential limitations, and the need to take sound advice when making financial decisions.

Relationships: The Page Of Cups Tarot card can signify a new, budding relationship. It symbolizes a period of trust-building and communication. It encourages the individual to be open to new types of interactions and to explore new dynamics with their partners. It can also indicate that it is a time for tenderness, playfulness and light-heartedness. It can suggest that one takes the time to listen deeply and to be patient with one another.
