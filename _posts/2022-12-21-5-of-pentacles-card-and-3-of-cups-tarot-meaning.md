A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Cups mean together?

However, you do have friendship and a circle of support around you which can help you through this time. Celebrations with your friends can be a way to help lift your spirits, as well as providing you with meaningful connections and companionship. A strong network of people can be a great resource when dealing with difficult times, and the 3 of Cups tarot card reminds you that you do have this kind of support in your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card often symbolizes a difficult time in one’s life, especially with regards to health, finances, and relationships. It suggests that despite hard work and effort, there may be an inability to get ahead in life. Ill health, poverty, worry, isolation, and financial loss can be the result of this difficult time.

When it comes to health, The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card can signify a physical or mental ailment that has left the individual feeling depleted. They may be feeling worried about their health and unable to access the resources necessary to improve their situation. They are likely to be isolated from their usual support network, and feeling disconnected from friends and family.

Financially, this card suggests a lack of resources, whether it’s in terms of income or opportunities. The individual may be feeling worried about money, unable to pay bills, or struggling to make ends meet. They could be feeling alone in their financial situation, unable to ask for help due to either pride or shame.

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card can also be an indication that relationships are in trouble due to poor health and financial pressure. The individual may be feeling unable to connect to their partner due to these problems. They may also be feeling disconnected from the world, either due to a lack of access to social activities or simply due to a feeling of powerlessness and isolation.

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card can be a difficult card to interpret since it suggests a difficult time, but it can also be a sign of strength and perseverance. If one is able to endure this difficult time, they may be able to find the resources and support they need to get back on their feet and reclaim their health, finances, and relationships.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a card of celebration, friendship and support. It is a card that symbolizes the joy of being together as we share our lives with each other. It is a card that encourages us to reach out and embrace the people around us, to be with them, to feel supported and to find joy in our relationships.

When it comes to health, the 3 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a strong emotional support system. It signifies the strength of friendship, as well as the presence of family and friends who are there to help us when we need it. Having this emotional support can be a powerful tool in helping us to cope with any physical or emotional struggles we may be facing. It can give us the extra strength and motivation we need to stay healthy and well.

In terms of finances, the 3 of Cups Tarot card suggests a sense of security and financial stability. It indicates the presence of financially savvy friends and family members who can offer guidance and assistance, providing us with a support system which can be relied on in times of difficulty. It is a card that encourages us to also reach out and extend our own financial knowledge and expertise to those who may need it.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 3 of Cups Tarot card encourages us to be open to companionship and friendship. It suggests taking the time to bond with those close to us, and to open our hearts to creating and sustaining meaningful relationships. It indicates reaching out to those around us and creating a circle of support, understanding and unconditional love. It encourages us to embrace whatever relationships bring us, and to be grateful for the love and support they provide us.
