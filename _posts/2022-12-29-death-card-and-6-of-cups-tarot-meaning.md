However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

You may be leaving something behind that has brought you nostalgia and gifts, but it is now time to move on for new beginnings and opportunities that could help you achieve transformation. You may reunite with things from your past, allowing for a sense of innocence and nostalgia. It is a time for big changes and endings, but could also mean the potential for new growth and opportunity ahead.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and 6 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health:

The Death Tarot card indicates that it is a time for change and healing. It is a reminder that sometimes, the only way to heal is to let go and move on from an unhealthy situation or pattern. This can include physical, mental, and emotional health issues. A person may need to make big changes, such as quitting a job, ending a destructive relationship, or even getting medical help. It is a time to take a deep breath and realize that new beginnings are possible, even when it may seem difficult or impossible.

Finances:

The Death Tarot card is a reminder that sometimes financial health is tied to letting go of old habits. It can be hard to admit that something is just not working and to take the necessary steps to make a change. If debt has become a problem, it could be time to cut back spending and start making a budget. It could also be time to sell off some of the items that aren’t being used or that are only taking up space. When it comes to finances, the Death Tarot card indicates that it is time for a change, and to focus on healthier money habits.

Relationships:

The Death Tarot card can be a reminder that even though it can be painful, sometimes the best thing for a person to do is to end a toxic relationship. It is important to be honest with oneself and to know when it is time to move on from a situation that is not good for one’s wellbeing. It can also be a reminder that sometimes a break from someone can be necessary in order to heal. The Death Tarot card is a reminder to stay strong, and to know that it is ok to let go of people, situations, and habits that are no longer serving one’s highest good.

The 6 of Cups Tarot card is a card of nostalgia and innocence, representing a feeling of contentment. It often appears when you are looking for closure or a sense of renewal in your life. In terms of health, the 6 of Cups Tarot card can be a reminder to take care of yourself and your emotional health. It suggests that it is important to take time out from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and to reconnect with yourself, your environment and those around you. This card may also signify a period of rest and recuperation, allowing the body and mind to heal and find balance.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Cups Tarot card expresses optimism and positivity. It is a reminder that no matter what struggles you face, there is always a chance of something better coming your way. It encourages you to trust your intuition and to understand that sometimes taking a risk can be beneficial. The card also suggests that you take a look at any recurring patterns in your life and make sure you are taking the right steps to create financial stability.

In terms of relationships, the 6 of Cups Tarot card suggests that you are probably in a good place with your relationships and should take the time to nurture and build upon the ones that are important to you. It encourages you to rekindle any relationships that may have been lost and to create meaningful connections with those around you. This card also reminds you to maintain a healthy balance between work, relationships, and your own personal needs.
