A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and King Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Devil card and King Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and King Of Pentacles mean together?

The Devil tarot card and The King Of Pentacles tarot card together suggest that you may be struggling with an addiction, greed, envy and materialism. The combination of these cards shows a warning that the material wealth that you are seeking may be unfulfilling or destructive. However, with the influence of the King Of Pentacles, it is also a reminder that you can use your wisdom, caution and material resources to help you overcome these struggles and take advantage of opportunities to make wise investments and become a leader in your life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

You may be facing temptations to give in to indulgences of all kinds in the pursuit of pleasure. If you have become so obsessed with the material side of relationships that you have neglected their emotional side, then it is time to take a step back and reassess your goals.

At the same time, the combination of these two tarot cards can also suggest that you are a wise investor of both your emotions and your finances. You may be in a position to make well-considered decisions and investments that will benefit your love life. As you are both practical and wise, you could benefit greatly from understanding the true value of certain assets, both in terms of money and relationships.

In other words, the combination of the Devil tarot card and the King of Pentacles tarot card in your reading suggests that you have each side of the coin. While you may have a tendency to succumb to temptation and overindulge, you have the wisdom and self-control to make the right decisions when it comes to your relationship. If you can maintain this balance and make wise decisions, then your relationship will benefit from it.

On the other hand, the King of Pentacles tarot card signifies a practical leader, wise investor, and someone who is driven by logic and reason. They have the ability to make smart decisions and invest wisely. When combined with The Devil Tarot card, this combination can lead to both unbearable greed and selfishness, but also can create the potential to use one’s resources wisely and responsibly. With this combination, you may find that you become more conscious of your decisions when it comes to your finances and job. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Devil and King Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Devil mean?

Health  
The Devil Tarot card can symbolize a lack of self-care, a warning for poor decisions or a sign that you’re treating yourself too harshly. It suggests that you’re self-sabotaging your health, giving in to temptations and cravings, and not looking after your physical and mental well-being. In terms of physical health, The Devil Tarot card can signify addiction to drinking, drugs, smoking, or unhealthy eating habits. It can also represent the impact of a mental health issue, such as depression or anxiety, on your physical body.

Finances  
The Devil Tarot card can also have an impact on your finances. It suggests that you’re allowing greed and obsession to drive your financial decisions, leading to reckless spending, mismanagement, and overspending. It may also be a sign that you’re giving into materialism and looking to satisfy your needs through excessive shopping or investments. The card is a reminder to take a step back, look at your priorities and try to get your finances back on track.

Relationships  
The Devil Tarot card may also point to the need to be mindful of your relationships. It can represent a sign of envy and jealousy, suggesting that you’re trying to control the outcome of your interactions with others. Additionally, it can also signify obsession with another person or a toxic relationship dynamic that is based on power and manipulation. The card is also an invitation to practice forgiveness and acceptance, and to cultivate healthier and more nurturing connections with the people in your life.

## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

The King of Pentacles Tarot card represents a very solid and reliable force in life. He is the master of practicality, stability, and security. He is a wise and experienced leader who may bring wealth and prosperity to those he advises. He is also a reliable investor and can help bring financial stability and security to life.

When it comes to health, the King of Pentacles encourages us to take an active and responsible approach to our health. He is wise and has a deep understanding of the body and its needs. He encourages us to invest in the health of our physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being. He teaches us the importance of taking good care of ourselves, both through diet and lifestyle choices. He encourages us to exercise, eat well, and get enough rest and relaxation.

In the area of finances, the King of Pentacles is a master of sound investments and fiscal responsibility. He is wise and experienced, and if we turn to him for advice, he will guide us to make wise investments and take a more conservative approach to our spending. He can help us create a solid and secure financial future for ourselves and our families.

In relationships, the King of Pentacles is a steady and reliable partner. He is someone who will always be there to offer support, guidance and understanding. He is a pillar of strength and security, and he will not be easily swayed by any emotional turmoil. He will be the one to offer advice and sound judgement when it comes to making important decisions. He is the reliable and steady presence that can be counted on in any relationship.
