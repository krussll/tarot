A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and 2 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and 2 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 Of Swords and The 2 Of Cups tarot card together can signify an important transition in the relationship between two people. The Six of Swords can signify movement to a new location, suggesting that the two people involved are in the process of relocating and starting a new chapter in their lives together. The 2 Of Cups indicates that this change is coming from a place of romantic love and partnership, and could mean that the two have made the decision to move in together or even to get married. It’s a time of great change and growth, and is an indication that the two will be embarking on a new and exciting journey together.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 6 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The 6 of Swords and the 2 of Cups tarot cards together in a love reading suggest that you are about to embark on a necessary transition in your relationship. This could mean anything from breaking up and moving on from a previous relationship to starting a new one with a new partner. You may feel like you need to move on from a current situation or that something is not quite right, and this is the sign that you need to take a step back and make a change.

On the other hand, the 2 of Cups tarot card brings a sense of hope and renewal to the 6 of Swords card. Despite the fact that a transition is necessary, there is still the potential for a new and exciting romantic connection. You may be feeling like you are ready to take a new relationship to the next level or even that a proposal and marriage is on the horizon. The new relationship could be with a person you already know or a complete stranger, but either way, it could bring great joy into your life.

Overall, the combination of the 6 of Swords and the 2 of Cups in your love life suggests that you are going through a period of transition and change, but don’t forget the potential for a new and passionate relationship. By making the necessary changes and taking risks, you could find yourself in a much better place than where you began.

### What does 6 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When looking at these two cards together in regards to finances and job, it can mean that there is a need for a major transition when it comes to career and earning money. The combination of these two cards suggests that a current job situation may not be fulfilling and that a change is necessary. This could involve relocating to a new area, or even a different country, in order to take advantage of better job opportunities and potentially higher pay.

It is important to balance the need to improve one’s financial situation with the desire for a loving relationship. The 6 of Swords and the 2 of Cups suggest that a committed partnership can support the transition, allowing one to take risks and make changes in order to achieve the desired career goals. This combination could mean that if one plans and prepares with their partner, a new job and a better financial situation can be attained.

The combination of these two cards can also reflect the need to attract a loving partner during the transition. Making a successful transition is often easier with a supportive partner, so focusing on creating a loving relationship can help make the transition smoother. This can provide emotional security, while also allowing both partners to dream and plan together.

Overall, The 6 of Swords and The 2 of Cups tarot cards suggest that there can be a need for a major transition in terms of finances and career, but that this transition can be supported by a loving partnership. If one plans and prepares with their partner, this combination can lead to improved financial stability, and the possibility of achieving greater success and prosperity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 6 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 6 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 6 of Swords Tarot card can represent a necessary transition in health. This transition could be relocating to access better healthcare, focusing on preventive health, or making the decision to take charge of one’s own health and well-being. It could also signify the need to seek out a different kind of treatment or care if one’s current approach is not working. As with any transition, it is important to trust one’s intuition and rely upon the support of family and friends.

Finances: The 6 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a necessary transition in finances. This could mean relocating to gain better job prospects and more financial stability. It could also suggest that it is time to make a change in investments or other financial pursuits. It could also be an indication that a period of austerity is required in order to provide financial security. As with any transition, it is important to have a plan and to trust in one’s ability to make wise decisions.

Relationships: The 6 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a necessary transition in a relationship. This could mean relocating in order to gain more relationship freedom and security. It could also suggest that a period of transition is required in order to put relationships on a more solid footing. It could also be a sign that dramatic changes are needed in order to create lasting and fulfilling relationships. As with any transition, it is important to have realistic expectations and to trust in the process of growth.

## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 2 of Cups is a card of partnership, so this Tarot card often speaks of partnerships in regards to physical and mental health. This card suggests that two people, or two entities such as two doctors, must come together to support one another in order to achieve peace and health. An alliance of professionals and experts can work together to help create a balanced lifestyle, which will ultimately lead to physical and mental well-being. This card encourages being open to forging new relationships and seeking out help when needed.

Finances: The 2 of Cups card speaks of teamwork and cooperation when it comes to financial matters. This can mean building strong partnerships with financial advisors, investors, and colleagues to tackle any financial difficulty. These relationships must be well managed to reap the rewards of hard work and dedication. Working together with others can help you achieve financial success by pooling resources, pooling knowledge, and pooling support.

Relationships: The 2 of Cups speaks of strong relationship connections and a willingness to be vulnerable and intimate with another person. This card suggests that two people must come together, in a loving and committed way, in order to strengthen the bond and rapport between them. Showing appreciation, acceptance, and dedication to one another leads to a strong long-lasting relationship. This card encourages communication, understanding, and appreciation of the other person, which in turn leads to stronger and healthier relationships.
