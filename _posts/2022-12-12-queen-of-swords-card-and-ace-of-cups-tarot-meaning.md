A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and Ace Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and Ace Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and Ace Of Cups mean together?

The Queen of Swords tarot card and The Ace of Cups tarot card when combined speaks of a life-changing event, likely a new relationship or marriage proposal, that requires total honesty and a self-sufficient attitude. This event will bring excitement and joy, but also demands that you be strong and independent in order to fully enjoy it. It also encourages you to be open to a new experience and to work on developing a level of self-confidence and independence in order to sustain the relationship or build a secure future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Swords and Ace Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health

The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a person who is determined to remain in control of their own physical health and wellbeing. This card is often associated with an individual who will take responsibility for their own health, rather than relying on anyone else to do it for them. This person will likely take preventative measures such as regular exercise, eating healthy, and getting enough sleep in order to maintain their optimal state of health. It can also symbolize a person who knows their body and mind very well, and is not afraid to use their intuition to make decisions regarding their health.

Finances

The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a person who is financially independent and self-reliant. This person will likely management their finances with great accuracy and responsibility. They will likely be very organized when it comes to keeping track of their money, budgeting and saving. This person is also likely to look for ways to increase their income and make smart investments, ensuring the stability of their financial future.

Relationships

The Queen of Swords Tarot card may indicate that a person is independent and direct when it comes to their relationships. This person will likely voice their opinions in a straightforward and honest manner. They may also be able to set boundaries and maintain a sense of self-control when it comes to their relationships. This person may also be loyal and trustworthy, placing great value in honesty and integrity. They are likely to appreciate intellectual conversations and will seek out deep connections with their partners.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Ace Of Cups Tarot card is, in essence, a card of renewal and new beginnings. If you find yourself drawn to this card, it is an indication that you are likely on a journey of physical, emotional, or spiritual healing. It can be a sign that you are about to make a breakthrough in some way, be it an improvement in your physical health, a new journey of psychological exploration, or a spiritual awakening. As the card is associated with the element of water, it can also symbolize experiencing a sense of emotional stability and clarity.

Finances:  
The Ace Of Cups Tarot card suggests that a new financial opportunity is on the horizon. It may be in the form of an investment or a job offer. You may find yourself in a situation of increased financial freedom whereby you are able to pursue projects that you would otherwise not have considered. Whatever the situation, the card carries an element of good fortune and abundance. This card is a reminder to take risks, to have faith in yourself, and to be patient as the new opportunity unfolds.

Relationships:  
The Ace Of Cups Tarot card speaks of a new and exciting relationship that is about to blossom. Whether it be a new romantic relationship or a new level of closeness within an existing relationship, this card suggests that something new and exciting is on the horizon. The card can also be seen as an indication of renewal in an old relationship, bringing with it the possibility of a positive resolution. For those who are single, this card can indicate the potential of a new love, or a new understanding of the need for self-love.
