A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Fool card and 6 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Fool card and 6 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Fool and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The Fool tarot card means fresh hope. No matter what situation you are in, The Fool reminds you that there is always hope. Things can always get better. The 6 Of Wands tarot card means victory. Whatever you are striving for, whether it is in your career, your relationship or your health, The 6 Of Wands assures you that you will find success. These tarot cards are a reminder that you should never give up on yourself or your dreams. You have the power to make all your dreams come true.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Fool and 6 Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Fool and 6 Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Fool mean?

The Fool Tarot card is a very positive card when it comes to health. It suggests that if you have been feeling down or unhealthy lately, now is the time to start fresh and take care of yourself. It is also a good time to start new healthy habits, such as eating better or exercise. This card can also suggest that a new health condition may arise, but it will be nothing to worry about and will eventually pass.

When it comes to finances, the Fool Tarot card suggests that this is a good time to take risks and try new things. If you have been feeling stuck in a rut financially, now is the time to take some chances and see where they lead you. This card can also suggest that you will come into some money unexpectedly. Whether it is through winning the lottery, an inheritance, or a lucky gambling streak, you are likely to come into a large sum of money.

In relationships, the Fool Tarot card suggests that this is a good time to take some chances and try new things. If you have been feeling stuck in a relationship rut, now is the time to take some chances and see where they lead you. This card can also suggest that you will meet someone new and exciting. Whether this is a new love interest or just a new friend, you are likely to meet someone who will bring some fresh energy into your life.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is a very positive card when it comes to health, finances and relationships. It indicates that good news is on the way and that success is achievable. This card suggests that any goals or plans you have set are attainable and that you will be rewarded for your efforts. The 6 of Wands also signifies recognition and approval from others, so expect to receive compliments and praise from those around you. This is a great time to start new ventures or to take risks, as they are likely to pay off. When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Wands suggests that things are going well. If you are single, you may meet someone special who will sweep you off your feet. If you are in a relationship, it is likely to be a happy and prosperous time. There is a strong chance of a pregnancy or the birth of a child. Overall, the 6 of Wands Tarot card is a very positive and optimistic card that indicates good things to come.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The Fool and 6 Of Wands has helped you.
