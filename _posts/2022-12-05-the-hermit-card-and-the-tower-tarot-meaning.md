A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and The Tower card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and The Tower have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and The Tower mean together?

The Hermit and The Tower tarot cards together represent a journey towards personal growth amidst radical, abrupt change. In this combination, The Hermit encourages you to look within to seek the spiritual understanding and clarity needed to face The Tower’s disruptive power. The Tower signifies something deep within you that must be accepted, embraced, and ultimately transformed to make the desired changes. The difficult times brought forth by The Tower will demand of you a willingness to open up and explore the depths of yourself with The Hermit’s guidance, allowing you to rediscover your true power, purpose, and identity.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Tower tarot card, on the other hand, signifies a period of upheaval and unwanted change in your life. It is a warning against clinging to outdated ideas or beliefs and instead encourages you to embrace any shifts that need to take place. This may not be an easy journey and can bring with it a feeling of ruin or disgrace, but at the end of it you will end up in a better position.

The Hermit and The Tower, when combined, suggests that it is important to approach any changes with courage, clarity and understanding. There may be a need to be honest with yourself and others in order to ensure a positive outcome. It is important to take some time away from the situation and ground yourself in a place of personal insight. Then, take action on the new perspective that you have gained.

Overall, The Hermit and The Tower card together can represent a significant shift in the direction of your love life. It signifies the need to evaluate the current situation and embrace any changes that may come. It is important to stay connected to your feelings and intuition throughout this process in order to ensure a successful outcome.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and The Tower mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and The Tower the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health: The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to look within ourselves to find the answers to our health and well-being. To listen to our bodies, minds and hearts, to understand how we can best care for ourselves. To acknowledge that by taking the time to be still and be reflective and go within, we can gain greater insight, clarity and understanding of our body’s needs and how we can best meet them. Seeking the wisdom of our inner selves can provide us with knowledge about how to make better choices for overall health, fitness and nutrition.

Finances: The Hermit Tarot card advises us to look within when it comes to our finances. To set aside time for contemplation and self-reflection in order to gain greater insight and understanding into our financial situations. To be mindful of our spending habits, to acknowledge the impact our actions have on our financial health, and to consciously make decisions that serve our best interests in the long run. The Hermit Tarot card suggests that with greater insight and transparency, we can strive for balance and achieve greater financial stability.

Relationships: The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to look within and examine how our relationships with others influences our overall wellbeing. To understand how we can best show up and be present in our connections with others. To be mindful of our feelings, responses and behaviors, and to strive for a balance between being our authentic selves and honoring the needs and feelings of the other person. The Hermit Tarot card reminds us that by listening to our intuition and connecting with our inner wisdom, we can gain greater understanding and insight into how we relate to others.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: The Tower Tarot card symbolizes the potential for unwanted chaos, upheaval and violence when it comes to our health. This card can represent sudden and possibly severe illness with no warning, as well as injury and disability. It can also refer to crisis points in our lives, such as a medical emergency or serious medical diagnosis. These events can have a huge impact on our well-being and spur us to make major changes to our lifestyle and health habits.

Finances: The Tower Tarot card warns us to be prepared for unexpected and possibly drastic changes in our financial life. This could mean the loss of a job, an unexpected financial setback or perhaps the discovery of a previously unknown debt. It can also signify the sudden expense that comes with a medical emergency or other crisis. Whether it’s an unexpected expense or a major blow to our savings and assets, the Tower Tarot card helps to warn us to be prepared for financial upheaval.

Relationships: The Tower Tarot card can also be a warning sign of crisis points in our relationships. This card can refer to massive fights and upheavals within a relationship, even to the point of a breakup or divorce. It can also imply a change of status between friends, or the sudden appearance of a new rival or enemy. It can also refer to the sudden presence of an unwanted third party, such as a nosy relative or a disrupting ex. Whether it’s the end of a relationship or the start of a new one, the Tower Tarot card indicates a major shift in our relational dynamics.
