A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The 3 Of Wands and 7 Of Cups tarot card, when interpreted together, is a symbol of the need to make a commitment to something in order to move forward in life and achieve success. It can be a warning against having too many options and not committing to any of them, which could stop you from achieving your goals. Teamwork and cooperation are essential to fulfill your ambitions, and travel can bring great rewards. The risk of too many choices and making the wrong commitment needs to be weighed against any potential rewards that could come with making the right choice. So it is important to take the time to select the best option, thus ensuring a successful and prosperous journey.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

The 3 of Wands Tarot card is associated with teamwork, commerce, expansion and travel, which can have an impact on all aspects of life including health, finances and relationships.

Health: When the 3 of Wands Tarot card appears in a health reading, it may suggest the need for teamwork to maintain good health. This could be by teaming up with a doctor or other health professional to create and maintain a healthy lifestyle. It is also possible that the card may indicate a need to explore alternative treatments such as herbal medicine or aromatherapy. Additionally, the card could denote the benefit of taking a work trip or extended vacation for a change of pace and perspective.

Finances: This card can point to the need for collaboration, both in the workplace and in personal finance. It could indicate that working together with both coworkers and other professionals to maximize financial gain is essential. The card also speaks to expansion, implying that the individual should broaden their research into opportunities beyond the current scope and take a chance to invest in something with potential growth. Finally, it could symbolize a potentially profitable and beneficial venture that the individual could pursue with the right resources and partnerships.

Relationships: The 3 of Wands Tarot card suggests that a couple may need to work together to get through challenging times. This could mean finding a way to balance work and life so that the relationship can be a priority, or simply taking the time to build strong communication skills so that both parties can be heard and understood. It could also indicate that, by taking the time to expand their views of each other, the couple can discover that there is more to their relationship than just the differences between them.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 7 of Cups Tarot card represents too many choices when it comes to health, as well as difficulty in making a commitment. There could be a lot of options to take, with so many choices it is difficult to make a long-term decision. We may be stuck in the analysis paralysis of research, looking for the perfect solution for our health challenges. Too many options can cause us to feel overwhelmed and we may end up procrastinating the decision making process. We may lack focus and become scattered, unable to choose a beneficial option and take action on it.

Finances

The 7 of Cups Tarot card also points to an excessive use of options for money management and expenditure. We may be prone to overspending, indulging too much in the luxuries of life, without proper planning and forethought. We may have commitment issues and be unable to decide on the best course of action when it comes to investments, savings, and budgeting. We may be unable to make a long-term commitment and choose instead to pursue short-term solutions, such as gambling, stock market speculation and impulse purchases.

Relationships

The 7 of Cups Tarot card in relationships can signify difficulty in making a commitment. We may examine too many options and feel overwhelmed, unable to decide what is best. We could be uncertain if we have chosen the right partner, always looking for the ‘perfect’ person to be with. We may have trust issues or a fear of commitment due to our past experience, unable to commit to one relationship at a time. We may also be prone to idealize a relationship, unable to see the relationships for its true complexity.
