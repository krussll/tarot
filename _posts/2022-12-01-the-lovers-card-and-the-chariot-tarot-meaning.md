However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Lovers card and The Chariot have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Lovers and The Chariot mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Lovers and The Chariot the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health

The Lovers Tarot card is all about strength in partnership, so when it comes to health, the card offers a reminder of the power of finding support. Whether this is taking on a friend or loved one as an accountability partner to help reach health goals, or potentially even seeing a therapist to seek professional help, the card encourages you to look to others as a form of support. This doesn’t mean you can’t be proactive on your own when it comes to taking care of your health; The Lovers Tarot card simply encourages you to think about who you can share the journey with to help you reach your goals.

Finances

When it comes to finances, The Lovers Tarot card offers a reminder to be mindful when making decisions. It’s easy to rush into decisions, especially when it comes to money, but the card encourages you to take a step back, breathe and take your time when assessing the potential consequences. The card also encourages you to seek support when it comes to making decisions with your finances. Whether it’s talking to a professional or simply taking on a trusted friend as a sounding board, having support can often help to make the right decision.

Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card is all about balance and harmony in relationships, so it’s an excellent card to draw when considering the state of your relationships. The card serves as a reminder to listen, value, and appreciate your partner and take the time to really nurture the relationship. In terms of relationship advice, the card encourages a sense of balance; this means taking time for yourself and your needs, as well as for your partner. It’s important to understand that the relationship will only be successful if both parties are content and supported.

## What does The Chariot mean?

Health  
The Chariot Tarot card points to the need to take action in order to improve one’s health. This could mean being proactive about getting regular checkups and wellness visits, taking on a new diet or exercise regime, or seeking out alternative methods of healing, such as acupuncture or meditation. It may also indicate that the time is right to embark on a journey of self-discovery or self-care in order to heal or improve one’s overall wellbeing.

Finances  
The Chariot Tarot card suggests that it is time to take action in order to improve one’s finances. This could mean taking a further look at one’s financial situation and taking a realistic approach to budgeting, investing, and saving money. It is also an indicator that one should have the courage to take a risk and try something new in order to potentially reap greater financial rewards.

Relationships  
The Chariot Tarot card speaks to the need to take action in order to improve one’s relationships. This could mean being proactive about communicating better with those close to you, being honest about what you want and need from a relationship, and taking steps to create a more harmonious and balanced life overall. It is also an indication that one should have the courage to take a risk and try something new in order to reap greater rewards from the relationships in one’s life.
