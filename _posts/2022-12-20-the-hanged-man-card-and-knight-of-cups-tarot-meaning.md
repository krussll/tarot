A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and Knight Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and Knight Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and Knight Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Hanged Man and The Knight Of Cups tarot cards suggest that you may be faced with a situation where you need to make a self-sacrifice in order to experience love. You may need to get a different perspective on the situation and take a step back to reflect on your situation in order to make a wise decision. This can bring a balance between your own desires and the well-being of others. The Knight of Cups is a reminder that an act of self-sacrifice can be rewarded in the form of a romantic relationship, a proposal, or a “Prince/ss Charming”. It is a sign to be generous, kind and understanding in order to experience the bliss of love.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and Knight Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health

The Hanged Man Tarot card indicates that it is time to step back and get a different perspective on your health. It can be easy to become overly focused on one issue, such as a particular physical ailment or persistent mental health issue. Instead, take a step back and examine your situation as a whole. The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages you to be willing to make changes and sacrifice your current habits and beliefs if they are not serving you. You may need to make a change in diet, exercise, or even lifestyle. Don’t be afraid to look within and ask yourself, “What do I need to let go of so that I can start fresh and make better decisions for my health?”

Finances

The Hanged Man Tarot card suggests that it is time to take a step back and look at your finances from a different perspective. Are you sacrificing long-term security in favor of short-term gratification? Are you letting fear or prejudice dictate your choices? It is time to reevaluate your financial habits and create a plan that will lead you to financial security and abundance. This may require sacrificing some of your current habits and beliefs regarding finances, so be willing to let go of those and develop new, healthier financial practices.

Relationships

The Hanged Man Tarot card indicates that it is time to take a step back and look at your relationships from a different perspective. Instead of focusing on the negative aspects, look for the opportunities to grow, learn, and become stronger together. What have you been sacrificing in order to maintain the status quo? Are you afraid to make changes because of your fear of the unknown? This is a time to ask yourself what you need to let go of in order to create healthier relationships, both with others and with yourself. It is a time to put aside the need to be right and focus instead on understanding and compromise.

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

If a person is facing health challenges, this card can suggest a time of re-balancing, which could manifest in a physical or emotional way. The message of this card when it comes to health is to take time to nurture and take care of yourself, and make sure that you prioritize your needs.

Finances: The Knight of Cups Tarot card is a message that when it comes to finances, it is time to up your level of creativity. It is a card full of potential and suggests that there is a light of opportunity at the end of the tunnel. This card can indicate that you are capable of finding a solution to any financial situation that arises, as long as you are willing to think outside the box and tap into the creative potential of your mind.

Relationships: The Knight of Cups Tarot card can be a sign of romance, proposals, and a new beginning. It is a card of courage, initiative, and taking a leap of faith in relationships. This card suggests that if you are feeling stuck in a certain relationship, now is the time to throw caution to the wind and see what life has to offer. It is a reminder that with bravery and openness, great things can happen.
