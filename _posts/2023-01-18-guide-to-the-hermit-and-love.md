Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Hermit card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Hermit, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Hermit.

## The Hermit And Love – An Overview

The Hermit tarot card is a reminder to take the time to go within and discover true personal clarity and spiritual illumination.

This can be especially beneficial for our physical health.

Taking the time to go inward and reflect can provide us with the insight and understanding we need to become more mindful of our bodies and how our lifestyle choices affect our physical health.

By connecting with our deeper selves, we can gain a greater understanding of what our bodies need in order to stay healthy, from proper nutrition and exercise to rest and relaxation.

The Hermit encourages us to take time for ourselves so that we can create healthier and more balanced lives.

## Upright Meanings For The Hermit And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Hermit tarot card can represent a time of introspection and self-reflection when it comes to love.

It suggests that you take some time away from your romantic relationships and go within to gain clarity and understanding.

It may indicate a period of solitude, where you can explore your own personal truth and rediscover yourself and your feelings.

Taking a step back can help you gain a new perspective on your relationship and make decisions that truly reflect your own heart and soul.

The Hermit also encourages you to seek spiritual illumination and strengthen your inner connection with yourself and the divine.

By reconnecting with yourself, you can gain a better understanding of your feelings and what it is you truly need and want in your relationship, allowing you to find true love.

### What It Means For The Future

The Hermit tarot card is a sign of hope for a future love.

It is a reminder to look within for clarity, to go on a quest for personal truth, and to seek spiritual illumination.

It encourages you to take time for yourself and to reflect on your deepest desires and feelings so that you can find a love that will bring you true joy and fulfillment.

By trusting your intuition and exploring your innermost thoughts and feelings, you can uncover what you need to make the right decisions to find happiness in the future.

With the Hermit guiding you, you can trust that the path to true love will be illuminated.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Hermit is a reminder that true love does not come from outside of us but rather from within.

This card signifies the importance of taking the time to go inward and connect with our own inner truth, so that we can discover the true depths of our feelings for those close to us.

This is a journey of spiritual exploration, and it can help us to gain clarity on our relationships and to better understand and appreciate the love that we have for our friends and family.

Taking the time to go within and nurture our inner truth can help us to recognize the true beauty of the love and connection that we share with those close to us.

## Reversed Meanings For The Hermit And Love

### What It Means For Me

When the reversed Hermit tarot card appears in a reading about love, it can represent a period of loneliness and introspection.

The reversed Hermit suggests that the querent may be facing a period of isolation from those they love and care about.

As the card is typically symbolic of a quest for personal truth, the reversed Hermit can indicate that the querent is not relying on their intuition and is instead allowing other people to define their relationship.

The card also often warns against a lack of self-reflection and too much focus on external sources for guidance.

In terms of a love relationship, the reversed Hermit can point to a period of stagnation and an inability to move forward.

It can be a sign that the querent is stuck in their ways, unable to accept new perspectives and unwilling to put the work into the relationship.

It is important to remember that the reversed Hermit is not necessarily a bad omen, and that it can represent a necessary period of personal growth.

The querent must take the time to look inward and reflect on their feelings and hopes for the relationship.

With honest self-reflection, they will be able to reconnect with their inner truth and make choices that are best for their relationship.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Hermit card appears in the reversed position in a reading about love and relationships, it indicates that the seeker is being called to step back and take a break from the relationship.

This could be a warning sign that the seeker is allowing themselves to become too dependent on their partner and needs to take some time to reconnect with themselves and their own sense of purpose.

The reversed Hermit also suggests that the seeker is in need of a period of self-reflection and solitude in order to gain greater clarity over their emotions and motivations within the relationship.

This could be a sign that they need to spend some time alone, or perhaps reach out to a trusted friend or mentor for advice.

Ultimately, the reversed Hermit is a reminder to take a step back and reflect on why they are in the relationship in the first place, and to ensure that their own needs are being met and fulfilled.

Through this period of introspection, the seeker can gain greater insight into what is and is not working in their relationship, and make decisions from a place of clarity and personal truth.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the Hermit card appears in the reversed position, it indicates that the love of those close to you is being blocked.

This could be because of an inability to let go of the past and move forward, or because of a need to keep emotions and relationships at a distance.

This can lead to feelings of loneliness and isolation, as well as a fear of intimacy.

You may be feeling disconnected from your loved ones, and uncertain of how to re-establish those connections.

The reversed Hermit card is a reminder to open your heart to the love of those close to you and to allow yourself to be vulnerable.

It is time to let go of any pain or hurt that you may be holding onto, and to open yourself up to the possibility of true connection.

Be patient and understanding, and try to trust that those around you have your best interests at heart.

With an open heart, you can begin to mend old bridges and create new ones.
