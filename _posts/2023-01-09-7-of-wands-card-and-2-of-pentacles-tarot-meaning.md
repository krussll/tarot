A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and 2 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and 2 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and 2 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 7 Of Wands and The 2 Of Pentacles tarot cards together can be seen as a representation of the need to find a balance between standing your ground and weighing your options. Although it may feel challenging to safeguard your own interests while still considering the opinions and reactions of others and pursuing the multiple paths available to you, the combination of these two cards reminds you that it is possible to find the right balance. The 7 Of Wands encourages you to take the necessary measures to protect yourself, while the 2 Of Pentacles encourages you to approach it in a way that looks at all the possible outcomes and various options.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands tarot card is a reminder to those in all aspects of life that protection and self-defense are necessary. Although in the tarot it can represent combat and adversity, it also serves to remind us that in order to achieve success, we must protect and defend ourselves from competition.

In terms of physical health, this card is a reminder that it is important to take care of yourself and be assertive in self-care. By engaging in healthy habits such as regular exercise and having a nutritious diet, we are better able to ward off illness. Additionally, physical self-defense techniques can be beneficial for those wanting to further protect their bodies from harm.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Wands tarot card is a reminder to be wise with one’s money. This can mean setting a budget and not spending beyond one’s means. Additionally, it can also mean investigating investments and insurance options to protect against any competition that may occur in the field of finances.

When it comes to relationships, the 7 of Wands tarot card is a reminder that in order to keep a healthy partnership, one must be willing to protect the relationship. This can mean setting boundaries and being honest with each other. Additionally, it can mean being willing to communicate in a healthy way and ready to defend oneself if needed. Being willing to stand up and defend oneself when necessary is key to building and maintaining healthy relationships.

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to take a balanced approach to address health concerns. The card suggests that we need to find a balance between our physical and spiritual needs. We should not neglect either of these aspects of our overall health in favor of the other. To achieve this balance, we may need to explore different treatments, therapies, and solutions for our medical issues in order to find the best option for our individual needs.

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card warns us to be aware of our financial situation and to look for ways to make wise decisions. It suggests that we consider carefully where our money is going and find a balance between saving and spending. We need to explore options to increase our income while carefully analyzing potential risks. This card encourages us to think outside the box and come up with creative solutions that work for our personal finances.

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card emphasizes the importance of finding balance in relationships. We should be honest with ourselves and our partners about our needs and how to meet them. We need to take into consideration our own wants and desires, as well as those of our partner. It is also important to be able to negotiate and come to a middle ground. The card encourages us to explore different things and be open to new experiences to keep our relationships fresh and vibrant.
