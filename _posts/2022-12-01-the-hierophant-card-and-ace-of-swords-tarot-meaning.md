A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and Ace Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and Ace Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and the Ace of Swords tarot card together suggest a balance between tradition and innovation. On one hand, the Hierophant card reminds us to stay grounded in our cultural norms and traditional values. On the other hand, the Ace of Swords speaks to the importance of taking risks to cut away what no longer serves us, to break out of old habits and think outside the box. It is a reminder that sometimes innovation and progress require us to challenge our patterns and find a new way of thinking that can take us further. When these two cards come together, it is a reminder to find the balance between staying grounded in traditions and daring to change them.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hierophant and Ace Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card has a strong connection with a commitment to a healthy lifestyle. This card is a reminder for us to keep our health in check, to adhere to regular check ups, and to maintain the best health possible. It can symbolize the importance of taking care of our physical and mental wellbeing, of establishing routines and sticking to them, but also being flexible to adjust when necessary.

It can also symbolize being aware of our physical and mental limitations, and making sure to not push ourselves too hard. The Hierophant Tarot card is a reminder for us to adhere to the best health practices, and to take the time to care for ourselves.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card is often interpreted as being a symbol of stability and security. It is a reminder to set and follow a financial plan and to invest in something that will bring long-term success and security. It also speaks of wise investments, to take the time to research and make sure that our financial decisions are sensible and secure.

The Hierophant Tarot card is also a reminder to stick to financial conventions, to pay taxes in a timely manner and to keep our financial records up to date. It can also be a reminder to save and create a financial security net for ourselves, to guarantee our future wellbeing.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card is related to relationships and signifies the need for commitment and loyalty. It is a reminder to think about our behaviour within relationships, and to always strive for respect, honesty and trust. This card speaks to us about the importance of looking for long-term relationships, either romantic or friendships, and to look for serious connections rather than surface ones.

The Hierophant Tarot card is a reminder to stick to values, to stay true to ourselves and to our beliefs, and to make sure that these don’t shift depending on our partners’ opinions. It is a reminder to build relationships that are based on mutual understanding and trust, and to look for strong connections with those in our lives.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

Health

The Ace of Swords can indicate breakthroughs in the realm of health, providing us with new revelations and understandings. On a physical level, this card can signify surgery or other medical procedures needed to restore balance, heal an injury, or correct an issue. It can also point to mental clarity, improvements in our emotional and mental states, and the courage to make necessary changes. If we are experiencing a period of stagnation in our well-being, the Ace of Swords can offer breakthroughs and the opportunity to begin anew with a fresh perspective.

Finances

In regards to finances, the Ace of Swords can indicate a need for us to redefine our relationship to money. It can represent a change in our current approach and a willingness to try something new or to see our situation with greater clarity. A sense of determination and focus is needed to navigate through the changes and possibilities that this energy brings. The Ace of Swords also has a tendency to bring powerful new ideas and innovations which can be used to increase our finances when combined with hard work, determination and resilience.

Relationships

The Ace of Swords indicates a need for us to take a closer look at our relationships, both in terms of the dynamics of the relationship and our own individual needs. It can represent the potential for breakthroughs and understanding, but also the willingness and strength to have difficult conversations and confrontations. This card can also signify justice, fairness and truth in our relationships, allowing us to move forward in more honest and healthy ways. With the Ace of Swords, we can gain the clarity and courage needed to make changes and take the right steps.
