A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and Judgment card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and Judgment have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and Judgment mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The Judgment tarot card, when taken together, encourages you to recognize the power of traditions and societal expectations. It can also be seen as a reminder of the importance of adhering to these standards, but with a deeper understanding that it is in these moments of conformity that you can take the time to reflect on yourself and your life’s purpose. The Judgment tarot card can act as a wake-up call and an impetus to make that all-important final decision – to clarify your life’s true purpose and to take a different path if need be, despite the pressures of societal norms.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Hierophant and Judgment mean together for your love life?

The Hierophant and Judgment tarot card combined in a reading for love speaks to the traditional and more conservative expectations of life. It could be that in this context, you feel like your love life is operating under predetermined rules and you need help to break free. It could be there are expectations you feel like you have to live up to, and you may find yourself in a holding pattern, unable to move forward.

The Judgment card in this context brings hope. It suggests that something will happen, maybe news or events that will shake things up in your love life or in the way you perceive it. This could provide the clarity you need to make a firm decision, and go on and create the kind of relationship you desire.

This card combination also indicates that your love life is also connected to your higher purpose and that there is a strong spiritual element to it. Taking the time to discover your life’s purpose and focus on it is an important part of understanding the real meaning of your relationships.

The Hierophant and Judgment tarot card combined suggest that you have to look within and answer the questions you have been avoiding. It is time to take a stand, be honest with yourself, and align with your true authentic self. This is the only path to a lasting and fulfilling love life.

### What does The Hierophant and Judgment mean together for your finances?

When The Hierophant tarot card and The Judgment tarot card appear together in a reading for your finances and job, the message is about being aware of the need to conform to traditional conventions. On the one hand, The Hierophant tarot card suggests that there is an expectation from society to be “normal” and follow the accepted rules and regulations of your industry and workplace. This may mean respecting the hierarchy, voicing your opinion in the right places, and socializing with the right people.

At the same time, The Judgment tarot card provides the clarity and the wake-up call that you need to make a conscious decision about your life’s purpose. It encourages you to think about what matters to you, what you want to accomplish, and your long-term goals. The card helps you to see the potential future impacts of following certain conventions and suggests that you may need to make adjustments or take risks in order to reach your intended destination.

When these two cards come together, they suggest that you need to carefully assess your financial and job situation and create a balance between the established traditions, conventions, and expectations and your own personal ambitions. Find the path that respects your beliefs and honors the traditional norms of the industry while still allowing you to find success, fulfillment, and happiness.

### What does The Hierophant and Judgment mean together for your health?

The combination of The Hierophant tarot card and The Judgment tarot card is a powerful force when it comes to looking at our health. Taken together, they offer guidance on how to navigate through the conventional expectations of health and develop a personal path of health that is right for us.

The Hierophant tarot card reminds us that we can find our health and wellness by looking to the traditional approaches to health. This could mean researching and implementing the approaches to health and wellness that have been used for generations, or that have been proven effective in modern times. It also means being mindful of the expectations that society and those around you set for you, and taking the time to understand how those expectations affect you and your health journey.

The Judgment tarot card, on the other hand, provides clarity on what we should ultimately decide when it comes to our health. It can be a wake-up call that encourages us to take ownership of our own health and make decisions that are right for us. It is a sign that we must consider our own goals and purpose in life and make decisions that align with our values and desires. In other words, it tells us that we need to trust our own judgment and find the right balance between tradition and innovation when it comes to our health and wellness.

The combination of The Hierophant and The Judgment tarot cards speaks to the importance of personalizing our approach to health and wellness. We can take the time to understand the expectations set by our culture and our community, while at the same time, using our own judgment to decide what works best for us. Ultimately, this combination of tarot cards can be seen as a reminder that we can find our own balance between tradition and personalization when it comes to taking care of our health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hierophant and Judgment mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health:

The Hierophant Tarot card can represent a need to abide by conventional standards of health and wellness. This card often suggests that someone is looking to a doctor, nutritionist, or other health professional for advice on how to best take care of themselves. It can signify the need to follow a routine for maintaining one’s health, such as going to the gym regularly or eating a balanced diet. Alternatively, it can also be a reminder to stay informed about health and wellness trends, and to stay up-to-date with any medical advice that is available from trusted sources.

Finances:

When it comes to finances, The Hierophant Tarot card often refers to adhering to a conventional financial plan. This can mean looking to a trusted financial advisor for advice on budgeting and saving. It can also mean using traditional payment methods such as cash rather than riskier options like credit cards. This card can also signify a need to be prudent and conservative when it comes to investments, which can help protect against market volatility.

Relationships:

The Hierophant Tarot card can signify the need to follow conventional standards of relationships. This could mean following social norms such as being courteous and respectful to one’s partner, and understanding that there is an expected way to behave in romantic relationships. It could also refer to the need to follow through with commitments, such as honoring wedding vows or sticking to an agreed-upon division of labor in a marriage. This card can be a reminder that relationships adhere to certain expectations of behavior, and to always treat one’s partner with respect and kindness.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health:  
The Judgment Tarot card indicates a moment of clarity or awakening that can be beneficial when it comes to our health. This card reminds us to take a step back and assess our current lifestyle habits and make an honest assessment of how they are serving us. It provides an opportunity to reflect on the choices we are making and whether they are in line with our ultimate well-being. This card encourages us to make decisions about our health that come from a place of understanding and knowledge rather than fear or pressure. It’s time to look at our diet, exercise habits, and use of supplements, and make changes where necessary to move towards a healthier lifestyle.

Finances:  
The Judgment Tarot card indicates the need for structure and discipline when it comes to our finances. It could be a sign for recognizing when it’s time to make a change or a call to action to create a budget and stick with it. This card can also signify an awakening to the importance of saving for retirement, categorizing expenses and keeping track of investments. It is a reminder to look at our financial situation realistically so that we can make sound decisions. If we are feeling overwhelmed, this card can be a reminder that it’s ok to ask for help and seek guidance to get our finances in order.

Relationships:  
When it comes to relationships, The Judgment Tarot card encourages us to look at our partnerships honestly and authentically. This card signals the need to consider all angles before making any final decisions. It can also mean that it is time to be honest with ourselves and with our partner, and to explore how we can make our relationships deeper and stronger. This card can also be a call to action to let go of old patterns and beliefs that are no longer serving us. On a spiritual level, the Judgment Tarot card can help us to come to terms with unresolved issues and to release any guilt or shame that we have been carrying. This card is an invitation to deepen our understanding of ourselves and of the relationships in our lives, allowing for a more meaningful connection and greater intimacy.
