A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Wands and The Knight Of Swords tarot cards suggest that a new opportunity involving teamwork, commerce, and expansion may be on the horizon. It could involve travel, either local or global, or a business venture that requires the cooperation and collective effort of others. Additionally, working with a fast-talking person may lead to a sudden and dramatic shift in lifestyle. With the potential of great rewards come the potential of great risks, but it could be an opportunity to expand, explore, and achieve goals that once seemed out of reach.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 3 of Wands and the Knight of Swords tarot cards bring positive messages of expansion and change in your love life. The 3 of Wands indicates teamwork, commerce, and travel. You should be open to trying new activities or visiting new places in order to keep your relationship fresh and interesting.

The Knight of Swords indicates a fast-talking person and change. This card encourages you to be open to change in your relationship. Your decisions should be thoughtful, but also bold and decisive. If you are feeling stuck or bored with your current relationship, then now is the perfect time to shake things up and try something new. Maybe this could take the form of a big move, or simply trying something new together as a couple.

Taken together, the 3 of Wands and the Knight of Swords tarot cards emphasize collaboration and change in your love life. Communication and teamwork will be key to success, but you also need to be open to new ideas and taking risks. Consider this as a sign that it is time to take your relationship to the next level and make it truly exceptional.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 3 Of Wands Tarot card can represent a time for healing and self-care for those who are feeling overwhelmed and stressed out. It encourages us to reach out to our support systems, ask for help, and take time to rest, relax and rejuvenate. Taking time to maintain our mental, emotional and physical wellbeing can be difficult, but it’s important for our long-term health. This card can also suggest that now is the time to start making healthier choices in our lives, such as eating better and exercising more.

Finances: The 3 Of Wands Tarot card can be a sign of financial growth and stability. It indicates that the hard work you’ve been putting in is finally paying off, and that it may be time to expand your business or investments. Teamwork is also a major theme of this card. This could mean that collaboration with others could be advantageous in achieving financial success. Planning and making wise decisions can be beneficial in order to make your money work for you.

Relationships: The 3 of Wands Tarot card can represent relationships that are dependent on teamwork. It’s a reminder that in order for a relationship to flourish, it is necessary for both partners to put in effort and work together. This card can also signify the importance of communication and openness in relationships, as well as having mutual respect. It can suggest that participating in activities with our partners or taking a trip together can be beneficial for the relationship and can bring you both closer together.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

The Knight of Swords Tarot card is a sign of change and a warning to be mindful of our actions. It speaks of a period of rapid movement and a need for rapid decision making. This is a time of increased energy, but it can also mean impulsiveness and recklessness.

Health: The Knight of Swords Tarot card warns us to be mindful of our health and to take stock of our physical and mental well being. It is a reminder to take a break and to find balance in our lives. This card advises us to take care with our diet, exercise and self-care to ensure that we stay healthy.

Finances: The Knight of Swords Tarot card signifies that there may be an opportunity for a financial windfall or a period of increased wealth, but this should be approached with caution and planning. It is wise to invest in sound financial strategies, save money and plan for the future. This card also advises against making any impulsive decisions or taking any high-risk investments, as they may not pay off in the long run.

Relationships: The Knight of Swords Tarot card brings an increase in energy, but it can also mean an increase in conflict. This card suggests that it is important to be honest and direct in our relationships, but to be mindful of how we express our opinions. It urges us to think before speaking and to be aware of how our words will affect the people around us. It is a reminder to be mindful of our actions and how they influence our relationships.

Overall, The Knight of Swords Tarot card signifies movement, change and a need to be mindful of our actions. It urges us to be cautious and to take a measured approach in our lives. Whether it’s in regards to our health, finances or relationships, this card is a reminder to take a step back, assess and make informed decisions.
