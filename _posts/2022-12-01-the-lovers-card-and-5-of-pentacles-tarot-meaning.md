A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and the 5 of Pentacles tarot card together can represent a challenging time in your relationship. You may feel isolated or that your worries are shared between the two of you, leading to financial strain or illness. However, the strength of two together is key here as you can work together as a team to build a stronger and more stable partnership. With love and understanding, you can move through this difficult period and come out stronger on the other side.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and 5 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

For Health

The Lovers card in a Tarot reading often signals an improvement in physical and mental health. It can represent a journey of healing, where strong and supportive partnerships with others are essential. This card often signals an inner battle between two choices. Taking the time to discover what your body and mind needs, without judgment, will be the key to unlocking the potential of this card. It may mean considering alternative treatments, Meditation and other forms of self-care.

For Finances

The Lovers card often indicates the need to make a difficult choice, or to come to a compromise between two different points of view. It is important to be aware of all available options and to trust your intuition when it comes to making financial decisions. This card can also refer to investments and partnerships in business. A good partnership and negotiation is essential to making good financial decisions that will benefit all in the end.

For Relationships

The Lovers card speaks of a strong bond and connection between two partners. This can represent an existing relationship or the potential for a deeper one. It is important to be aware of the strengths and weaknesses of any relationship, and to make sure that both sides are committed to a common goal. Communication is essential to creating a healthy relationship, as is trust and respect. This card can also symbolize the need to make a tough but important decision when it comes to relationships. It is important to be aware of your needs and desires, while also being open to compromise and understanding.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of ill health, financial hardship and strained relationships. On this card, two people are walking past a church, hunched over in the cold, and one of them is missing a shoe. This card suggests a state of physical, mental and financial exhaustion, as both of these characters are struggling with a lack of resources. Health-wise, this card could indicate illnesses, medical costs and general physical exhaustion. Financially, this card could suggest poverty, debt and a lack of access to resources. This could be a sign that you are struggling to make ends meet, that you are living paycheck to paycheck, or that you are having difficulty finding a job.

In terms of relationships, The 5 Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests isolation and loneliness. The two figures on the card are walking alone, and it is a sign that you may be feeling disconnected and unsupported in your current relationships. You may be feeling like you don’t have anyone to turn to, or that no one understands you. This card can also indicate that you are struggling to find intimacy and connection with others, or that you are overwhelmed by the demands of your relationships.

The 5 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of struggle and hardship. It is a call to re-examine your physical and financial health, and to make sure that you are taking care of yourself and your needs. It is also a reminder to reach out for help and to seek out meaningful connections in your relationships.
