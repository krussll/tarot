A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Swords card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Swords card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The 5 of Swords and the 4 of Cups tarot card when read together mean that you have been experiencing a lot of chaos and violence in your life. You might have been a victim of bullying or physical/mental abuse; while at the same time feeling a sense of apathy and feeling unfulfilled. You may have been taken aback by the sudden surprise gifts you have been receiving as a sign of comfort, but it is hard to trust and accept them due to the negative experiences you have had. The combination of these two cards signify a tumultuous situation that has sparked a need for you to take a step back and evaluate what is important for you to move on from this destabilizing period.

On the other hand, the 4 of Cups could represent a sense of apathy or feeling unfulfilled in your relationship, and a disconnection between you and your partner.

You may be feeling uninspired and stuck in a stagnant situation where you’re unable to get out of this unhealthy connection. This could be in the form of verbal or emotional abuse, or an imbalance in the relationship where one person has all the power. Whatever the issue is, this pairing of cards suggests that you have been feeling emotionally lost and unable to reach a place of joy or satisfaction in your love life.

The 4 of Cups could suggest that there could be unexpected gifts waiting for you in the form of new opportunities or potential relationships. This could be a sign that it’s time for you to begin exploring what will bring you true contentment in a relationship. It is important for you to become aware of any issues that may be causing a disconnect or imbalance in your current relationship and to find ways to overcome these issues, so that you can move towards a more fulfilling love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 5 Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 5 Of Swords and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

For Health: The 5 of Swords Tarot card is an ominous sign when it comes to your health. It suggests that you may be at risk for some form of physical or emotional abuse, which could be violent or even dangerous. You could also be dealing with unhealthy boundaries, such as overworking yourself or neglecting important self-care. This card could be signaling a need to evaluate your lifestyle so that you can take steps to become healthier and more balanced.

For Finances: The 5 of Swords Tarot card suggests that you are at risk of experiencing some form of financial hardship or loss. This could be the result of theft or fraud, or it could be due to bad investments, costly mistakes, or even indulgent spending. It’s important to take steps to protect your finances and to be mindful of the choices you make. Taking a long-term view and developing a plan to help you weather any storms ahead is key.

For Relationships: The 5 of Swords Tarot card suggests that you may be dealing with an abusive or bullying relationship. It could be a romantic partner, a family member, or even a friend. This card is telling you to take stock of the situation and to address it if it’s within your power. If it’s not, then it’s important to reach out for help to support you through this challenging time. In the end, it’s important to recognize that relationships should be based on mutual respect and understanding.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 4 of Cups Tarot card represents a state of apathy, boredom and feeling unfulfilled. It appears when one is not engaged with the present moment and fails to take advantage of exciting opportunities. When this card appears in regards to health, it can mean one is not actively working towards health goals and is not engaging with the world around them. This can lead to a lack of motivation and a reluctance to take necessary steps to get healthy. That said, it can also suggest that there might be an underlying condition that needs to be addressed, or that one is in a state of disconnection from their own body, making it hard to understand or appreciate their physical and mental health needs.

Finances

The 4 of Cups Tarot card can also point to financial apathy and lack of interest, possibly because one feels unable to change their financial situation. They could be feeling overwhelmed by the choices they have to make and they may be reluctant to take risks. It can also mean one is not utilizing their full potential to manifest money. On the flip side, this card can also mean that there may be potential gifts or surprise sources of income, which one can take advantage of if they have the courage and awareness to do so.

Relationships

The 4 of Cups Tarot card may indicate a feeling of stagnation in one’s relationships, particularly romantic ones. There may be a lack of interest, communication and initiative in the relationship, with both parties feeling unfulfilled. It can also mean that one is not taking advantage of the new opportunities and experiences that come with being in a relationship. Alternatively, this card can signify that there may be a surprise or unexpected source of joy coming into one’s life, even though it might be hard to trust and accept.
