## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card represents abundance and success in one’s health, finances and relationships. It reflects the potential for new opportunities to come your way in all three areas.

When it comes to health, The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of a physical transformation in which you are able to take better care of your body and enjoy better health. It advises you to seek professional medical advice if needed and to explore any new ideas you have on how to improve your physical health. The card encourages you to give yourself permission to make positive changes in your life that bring greater well-being.

For finances, The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card signals positive news like a new job, promotion, raise, or investment. It signifies that you are about to enter a prosperous phase of financial abundance and stability. The card may also symbolize that you are about to embark on an amazing financial journey through wise investments and careful planning.

In relationships, The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card promises the potential for joy and good fortune. It indicates the possibility of new relationships entering your life, or the blossoming of existing ones. It suggests that you are entering a period of openness and mutual understanding that leads to deeper connections and true intimacy. This card also suggests that you will find yourself enjoying deeper levels of devotion and commitment in your relationships.

Overall, The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of new beginnings, opportunity, and abundance in one’s health, finances, and relationships. It carries with it the potential for success, fulfillment and joy if you are willing to take the necessary steps towards achieving it.
