A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Pentacles card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 9 Of Pentacles card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 9 Of Pentacles and The 9 Of Swords tarot cards represent the duality of life; how one can find power and self-sufficiency in some aspects of life, yet still find themselves in times of fear and worry. The 9 Of Pentacles card can symbolize a time of plentiful riches, abundance and luxury that one is able to fully enjoy on their own in a solo pleasure. The 9 Of Swords card, on the other hand, can indicate that while one may feel they have it all, they may still experience a deep sense of anxiety, depression, and guilt. This can be a reminder that one should also be mindful of their mental and emotional health as well as any material gains.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

However, The 9 Of Swords warns of potential obstacles that may cause emotional distress, such as nightmares, anxiety, and depression. Your journey will require you to face your inner demons and confront the fear and pain they evoke.

The combination of these two cards can also represent the challenges that come with taking a risk in love. You may be emotionally vulnerable because of your worries and fears, but The 9 Of Pentacles encourages you to take the plunge and open your heart to someone despite the risk. This is the only way to attain true emotional and financial security, but it is a difficult path to take. The 9 Of Swords challenges you to examine the dark sides of your love life and take the necessary steps to healing and security.

Ultimately, the combination of The 9 Of Pentacles and The 9 Of Swords tarot cards suggests that your love life will require vulnerability, courage, and strength. It will be difficult at times, but if you are able to face your fears, the rewards of love and security will be worth the risk.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a card that speaks to fear, anxiety, and distress. It can be a warning to those who are facing challenging times in terms of health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, this card may represent illnesses or physical pain that has been exacerbated by our own inner fears and anxieties. It could be a sign of a psychological disorder like depression or post traumatic stress disorder. It may also be indicative of nightmares or night terrors that we experience as a result of our pressing issues in life. The 9 of swords can also be viewed as a situation in which our own negative thoughts and worries are preventing us from fully living our life and enjoying our health.

In terms of finances, this card can represent a pessimistic attitude where we may lack the confidence to make sound financial decisions. This could lead to a lack of money or even financial ruin. Other times, this card could be an indication of financial anxiety or fear of the future that keeps us from investing in our future. Alternatively, it could be a sign of a dark time in your financial life where you are feeling overwhelmed and have difficulty seeing a way out.

Finally, in terms of relationships, this card can represent fear of or anxiety about relationships. You may feel like you are unable to connect with the people in your life and that you are unable to make meaningful connections. This could be indicative of feelings of guilt or insecurity or even low self-esteem. It could also be a warning for us to watch for codependency relationships and to make sure that our relationships are healthy and balanced.

Overall, the 9 of Swords can be a warning to those who are facing tough times in terms of health, finances, and relationships. It is important to pay attention to our thoughts and worries and to take action to get the help we need in order to make our lives easier and healthier.
