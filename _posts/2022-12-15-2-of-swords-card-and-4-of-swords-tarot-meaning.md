A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Swords card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Swords card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Swords and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of the 2 Of Swords and 4 Of Swords tarot cards suggest that it’s time to find a peaceful balance between opposing forces and make a decision. However, this does not necessarily mean that the decision should be made quickly – you are encouraged to take some time for yourself and retreat into solitude, in order to gain the clarity and insight needed in order to make the best possible decision. During this period of rest and renewal, ensure your mental and physical needs are met in order to avoid feeling overwhelmed or burnt out. Once you have taken the time to reflect and find balance, you will be in the perfect position to make a compromise and move forward in life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 2 Of Swords and 4 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

The 2 of Swords Tarot card represents a period of compromise, where you must make a difficult decision by weighing all of the options available to you. When it comes to your health, this card can be interpreted as a need to evaluate your lifestyle and the choices you have made in terms of diet, exercise, and any medications you take, and make changes to optimize your wellbeing. If you’re facing a tough decision in terms of your finances, the card urges you to think things through carefully, be honest with yourself, and make a decision that is best for your future. When it comes to relationships, this card can indicate a period of negotiation and compromise as all parties involved must do what is necessary to move forward, or potentially part ways. No matter the situation, this card reminds you to stay neutral and think clearly before making any final decisions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card indicates that it is time for a retreat from physical and mental strain and stress. It is time to slow down, rest, and find balance in one’s life. This card asks that you take a break from the chaos and demands of our day-to-day lives to rejuvenate and restore your energy. It also speaks to physical recovery, healing and restoration. It is an invitation to take a pause, a vacation or a break to restore balance and energy. Taking the time to rest and retreat can be beneficial for your physical, emotional and mental wellbeing.

Finances

The 4 of Swords Tarot card represents a time of regrouping, taking a step back and reassessing your financial situation. It is a time to be more mindful of how you are spending and investing your resources. The 4 of Swords suggests that you take a break from constant hustle and busyness in order to assess and plan your finances more carefully. This is not a time to take risks, but to be more conservative and stable with your money. Spend time reviewing your finances, creating a budget and setting goals to get back on track.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card indicates a need for a break from relationships. It encourages us to take the necessary time to rest, reflect and recharge. This card is reminding us to take a step back from our relationships and heal any wounds or areas of discord. This could be a time to take space away from a relationship that is not working or to refocus your attention on yourself. It can also be a time to simply rest and relax away from any obligations or demands. This is a time to restore and nurture the relationship, to find healing and peace.
