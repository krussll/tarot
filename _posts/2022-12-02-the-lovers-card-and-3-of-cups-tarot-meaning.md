A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together mean that you have formed a strong bond with your partner, and that you are able to rely on the support of your circle of friends and family. You have a strong connection with your chosen partner, and you both have the strength to make a choice that suits you both. You are able to celebrate and rejoice in the partnership you share, as well as your friendship group, which provides you with an unparalleled level of support and love.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Lovers and 3 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The Lovers tarot card and The 3 Of Cups card are in the same reading, they are an invitation to bring more collaboration into your love life. This dual card combo is a call to emphasize the strength of partnerships, rather than feel threatened by them.

The Lovers card represents partnership, deep love, and a choice. It encourages us to focus on the strength of two together and resist feeling intimidated by the power of a committed relationship. Whether it’s a romantic relationship or a friendship, the two together suggests that real strength comes through trusting and understanding the power of togetherness.

The 3 Of Cups card represents friendship, celebrations, and a circle of support. It reminds us that our love lives don’t exist in a vacuum, but are held up by a network of friends that provide us with a foundation of understanding and support. To find true love, it is essential to have a strong and understanding community.

The Lovers card and the 3 Of Cups card together signify a love life rooted in collaboration and stability. They invite us to find joy in partnerships and recognize that deep love is a choice between two individuals and can also extend beyond the traditional “couple”. Having a strong and supportive network of friends and family can enhance the love we have for our partners, as well as for ourselves.

The strength and support of your inner circle is a formidable resource when making important financial or job-related decisions.

By seeking out and listening to the advice of those who care deeply for you and your wellbeing you can make confident, well-informed decisions that will ultimately serve your financial and job related goals. The Lovers tarot card suggests that when you make decisions in partnership with those closest to you, it brings joy and satisfaction. When you combine the love and support of your inner circle with your own wisdom, you can make decisions that have real long-term positive implications.

The 3 Of Cups tarot card suggests that you can also draw strength and security from the circle of support that you can find among your colleagues and peers. Learning from the experience of those who have gone before you and seeking out their advice can help guide you towards decisions and strategies for success. Similarly, working with others who share the same goals and ambitions as yourself can help you to stay motivated and focussed when making decisions related to your job or finances.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

The Lovers Tarot card is about forming partnerships and embracing deep love connections, which are essential for healthy physical, emotional and mental health. From the physical perspective, it encourages us to form positive connections with our own bodies and to treat them with respect and kindness. It is also a message to reach out to others and build relationships that are supportive and filled with love. Financially, it indicates being open to influencers who can help us reach our goals. It speaks of tapping into knowledge that others can offer, as well as looking for ways to create healthy financial partnerships with others.

When it comes to relationships, The Lovers card is all about choices – making conscious decisions, such as choosing a healthy partner, staying with an existing partner or deciding to move on. It speaks of treating your partner with love and respect and building an emotional connection, as well as developing mutual understanding. It also encourages us to take responsibility for our decisions and to be honest with ourselves and with loved ones. The Lovers card implies strength in a relationship as it suggests that love is a two-way street and that both parties have a role to play.

Overall, The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder to cherish the partnerships we have already created and to build strong, healthy relationships with others. It encourages us to look for areas of common ground and understanding, as well as to accept our differences. It also speaks of taking responsibility for ourselves and for our part in a relationship, as well as of working to create balance and harmony.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups tarot card is an indication of friendship, solidarity, and support. It represents the positive energies that are created when we celebrate of the joys in life with those we hold dear. In terms of health, it encourages us to nurture our relationships, both old and new. This card serves as a reminder that we are not alone in any endeavor, and to reach out to others so we can draw strength and inspiration from them. In terms of finances, the 3 of Cups can suggest that support, both emotional and practical, is available. It denotes a situation where many are working together to achieve a collective goal, or a team that is united in its efforts to succeed. Finally, the 3 of Cups is a great card for relationships. It speaks of the power of shared experiences and of the reward that comes from collaborative celebration. It is a sign that if we are in a relationship, we should foster a sense of community, openness and warmth to ensure our relationship continues to thrive.
