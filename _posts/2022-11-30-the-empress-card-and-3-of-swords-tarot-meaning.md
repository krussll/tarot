## What does The Empress and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The Empress and The 3 Of Swords tarot cards together bring a unique combination of energies. On the one hand, The Empress brings an abundance of fertility and creativity, while on the other, The 3 Of Swords can show us a time of heartbreak and loss. This combination can represent both creating something new, such as a new relationship, while coming to terms with the ending of something else, such as the dissolution of a marriage. It can also show the potential for good and resilient parenting, even after experiencing the pain of a breakup, or the assurance that through surgery, we can find a new sense of hope and purpose. All in all, this combination of cards can remind us that even when faced with heartache, we can still create something beautiful, and that hope will always remain.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is a symbol of emotional pain and loss, typically in the form of heartbreak, divorce, or depression. In the health realm, the 3 of Swords may represent a necessary surgery or medical procedure that is emotionally difficult to go through. It is possible that the individual may be feeling grief and deep sadness around the decision to go through this medical procedure, as it could signify a change in their life. In terms of finances, the 3 of Swords could represent a major loss, such as the loss of a job or a financial setback. Although it may be difficult to cope with such a situation, it is important to remember that things can get better.

The 3 of Swords’ meaning for relationships is particularly painful. It can signify a relationship that has come to an end, often due to irreconcilable differences. It can also symbolize the pain of a break up, such as the grief of a sudden and unexpected split. This card can also reflect the pain of unrequited love, especially if one partner was much more invested in the relationship than the other.

The 3 of Swords can represent a difficult time in any area of life, but it also serves as a reminder of the importance of recovery and healing. After going through a painful experience, it is essential to take the time to process and heal, so that we can move forward with our lives. Even though the 3 of Swords can signify pain and loss, it can also be a sign of hope and resilience, as it shows that we can come through hard times if we persevere.
