When we first start tarot you may have questions about the process. “How does it work?” “Which spreads should I be considering” – but the most fundamental question I get asked most often is “What does a tarot card reader actually do?”

This question has a lot of potential answers as it could refer to any number of things. However when I hear it two potential threads come to mind:

1. what does a tarot reader do during a reading?
2. how does a tarot reader read the cards to answer your questions?

In this guide I’ll offer insight into those questions.

## What Does A Tarot Card Reader Do?

**A Tarot Card Reader is someone who uses Tarot cards to provide spiritual guidance and advice. Tarot cards are a form of divination, which is the practice of seeking knowledge of the future or the unknown through supernatural means.**

Tarot Card Readers use their knowledge of the cards and their intuition to interpret the cards and provide insight and guidance to their clients. During a Tarot Card reading, the Reader will lay out a spread of cards and interpret them based on their position, the order in which they were laid out, and the imagery that they represent.

The Reader will often ask the client questions to help focus the reading and create a meaningful dialogue between the Reader and the client. The Reader will then interpret the cards and provide guidance and advice to the client based on their interpretation of the cards. In addition to providing guidance, Tarot Card Readers may also offer spiritual counselling.

This type of counselling is based on the Reader’s understanding of spiritual concepts, such as karma and reincarnation, and how they may affect the client’s life and current situation. Tarot Card Readers also often provide healing services, such as energy healing and crystal healing. These services are based on the belief that energy can be used to heal physical and emotional ailments.

Tarot Card Readers may also use their knowledge of the Tarot cards to provide insight into their clients’ past and future.

Ultimately, a Tarot Card Reader is a spiritual advisor who uses their knowledge of Tarot cards and their intuition to provide guidance and advice to their clients. They can help clients gain insight into their current situation, provide healing services, and offer spiritual counselling.

## What do you call a person who reads tarot cards?

**A tarot reader or a tarot card reader.**

## Common questions asked by Tarot readers

The types of questions you ask during a tarot reading can impact the answers you receive and the overall accuracy of the reading. Formulating the right questions is key to getting the most out of your tarot reading. When asking questions during a tarot reading, it’s important to be as specific as possible.

Specific questions will give the tarot reader more information to work with and help them interpret the cards in a more meaningful way. It’s also important to focus on questions that will give you actionable advice. Questions such as “What do I need to know?” are too vague and won’t give you any concrete direction.

Instead, try asking questions such as “What can I do to make my relationship more successful?” Questions that focus on the present and near future are also good to ask during a tarot reading. This will help you gain clarity on any current issues you’re facing and give you insight into how to move forward.

Questions such as “What do I need to focus on right now?” and “What is the best path to take in the near future?” can be very helpful. If you’re looking for more specific advice, you can also ask questions about different areas of your life such as work, relationships, and finances.

Questions such as “What steps can I take to achieve career success?” and “What should I do to improve my relationships?” can provide you with valuable guidance. When asking questions during a tarot reading, always remember to focus on the present and near future and to be as specific as possible. This will help ensure that you get the most out of your tarot reading and receive meaningful guidance.

## Conclusion

In summary – A tarot card reader is someone who interprets the meaning of tarot cards. Tarot cards are a form of divination, which is the practice of seeking knowledge of the future or the unknown by supernatural means.

Tarot card readers use the cards to provide guidance and insight into the past, present, and future. They can help people gain clarity and understanding about their lives, relationships, and spiritual paths.

Tarot card readers can provide insight into difficult decisions and help people find solutions to their problems. They can also provide comfort and support to those in need.
