## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is an important reminder of the importance of balance and harmony in our lives. It suggests that balance is essential for both physical and mental health. For example, if we overwork ourselves and don’t take the necessary time to rest and recuperate, we can become ill. Similarly, if we don’t take the time to nourish our bodies with healthy food and stay active, we can suffer from poor health.

The Temperance card also encourages us to practice self-control in all areas of our life. This is especially true for finances. If we don’t practice restraint when spending our money, we can quickly fall into debt and find ourselves in an untenable financial situation. The Temperance card gives us the opportunity to make wise choices about our expenditures and to keep our finances in check.

Finally, The Temperance Tarot card reminds us to strive for harmony in our relationships. When we are able to communicate with each other honestly, respectfully and with understanding, we can foster meaningful connections. On the other hand, if we allow our egos to take over, it can lead to discord and disharmony in our relationships. The Temperance card encourages us to be honest and open with our partners and friends while also respecting each other’s boundaries.

In summary, The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to strive for balance, harmony, patience and self-control in all areas of our lives. By doing so, we can create healthier, financially secure and more fulfilled relationships with others.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 10 Of Cups Tarot card encourages you to take time to look after yourself and your health. Make sure to prioritize self-care activities, such as a good night’s sleep, exercise, and a nutritious diet. The 10 Of Cups also reminds you to be mindful of how you approach health issues. Being able to draw on family support during times of illness can be very beneficial.

Finances  
When the 10 Of Cups Tarot card appears in a reading, it indicates that you may have the support of family and friends to turn to when you are struggling financially. However, it also suggests that you should not become overly reliant on others. Instead, take the time to establish a good financial plan and be smart with your spending.

Relationships  
The 10 Of Cups Tarot card is a reminder that healthy, loving relationships should be nurtured and cherished. If a relationship has become strained, take the time to reflect on how you can strengthen it. Surround yourself with friends and family that make you feel safe and secure. This card also encourages you to nurture not just romantic relationships, but also the relationships you have with family, friends, and even yourself.
