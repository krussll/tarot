A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 2 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 2 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 2 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 8 Of Wands and The 2 Of Pentacles tarot card signify a period of change and news coming to you quickly. This could be a time to make a decision or take action. You likely have two or more options to consider, and you need to be able to move quickly and juggle multiple tasks if you are to make the best decision. This could be an exciting but also hectic time, where you need to consider all your options, weigh the pros and cons, and then make a decision- all at a faster pace than usual.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 8 Of Wands Tarot card may signify a sudden change in one’s health. It might mean a speedy recovery from a sudden illness, or a sudden worsening of an existing condition. It could also be an indication of a dramatic change in lifestyle, as an individual is pushed to take action to improve their health. If one is already maintaining a very healthy lifestyle, the card can represent a burst of energy and newfound motivation to take health to the next level.

Finances: The 8 of Wands Tarot card could signify a sudden windfall of money, or the sudden arrival of a business opportunity or investment. It could also represent a need to take a leap of faith and take a financial risk. It is a reminder to take action and short-term risks to secure long-term gains.

Relationships: The 8 of Wands Tarot card could represent an instant spark of intense attraction between two people, or a sudden realization that one is in love. It could also mean sudden news in a relationship, either positive or negative. It is a reminder to take action to communicate one’s feelings and to seize the moment of opportunity. It could also be a warning that things are moving too fast, and that one should take a step back and assess the situation before taking any drastic action.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles mean?

For Health:

The Two of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that the current situation may be complex and require careful consideration when it comes to making decisions about health. On the one hand, there is the temptation to just ignore health concerns due to the stress of other commitments. On the other hand, the card points to taking a balanced approach to maintaining physical and mental wellbeing. This could mean making time for positive activities such as exercise and eating healthy, while also carving out much-needed time to relax and refuel. This card suggests that with careful navigation it’s possible to strike a balance between work and pleasure, between activity and rest.

For Finances:

The Two of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that to stay in financial control requires a disciplined approach to money management. This card suggests the need for careful consideration when it comes to decision-making about money and investments. It encourages us to treat our finances with a steady hand, weighing up the pros and cons of different options, and considering the long-term implications of each decision. We may need to juggle multiple investments, or simply create a budget that allows for a certain degree of flexibility.

For Relationships:

The Two of Pentacles Tarot card cautions against making rash decisions in relationships. It encourages us to take our time, to weigh up all of the implications of any decision before committing to it. There may be a need to juggle multiple relationships or interests, and this card suggests a careful and considered approach when it comes to managing different commitments. Rather than jumping head-first into a relationship, the card encourages us to think practically about potential outcomes, allowing us to make decisions with greater clarity and insight.
