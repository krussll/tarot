## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Self-control is important when it comes to our health. Eating right and avoiding overindulgences, such as drugs and alcohol, help us stay in balance and achieve optimal health. It’s also important to practice self-care and to make sure our body is getting the necessary rest and time for relaxation. Keeping physical, mental and emotional health in balance is essential for an overall sense of wellbeing.

The Temperance Tarot card is all about balance when it comes to finances. It’s important to practice self-control and to be sensible with our money. The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to save money, budget wisely and resist impulsive spending. We should strive to find a balance between saving and spending, so that we’re able to be financially secure in the future. Being prudent and mindful of our spending habits can help us achieve financial stability.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to strive for harmony in our relationships. It’s important to practice patience and understanding in our relationships, as this is the key to maintaining balance and harmony. We should also practice self-control when it comes to our emotions, as it can be easy to become consumed by our feelings and forget about the needs of our partners. By keeping our emotions in check and showing respect for our partners, we can build strong and lasting relationships.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card often symbolizes physical and mental exhaustion. It can be an indication that too many burdens have been placed on the individual, whether they be responsibilities, expectations, or goals. This exhaustion can manifest in physical tiredness, tension and aches, or in emotional and mental exhaustion due to the over-extension of one’s energy. The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a warning of the need to stop and take a break. It is essential to allow the body and mind to rest, in order to avoid further exhaustion, burnout, and potential health problems.

Finances:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can also be a warning about financial burdens. The individual may be feeling overwhelmed by debt, or dealing with too many financial requests from other people. It is important to step back and evaluate one’s financial situation and obligations. It is sometimes necessary to make difficult decisions in order to get back on track. The 10 of Wands Tarot card encourages taking the necessary steps to restore financial stability.

Relationships:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can represent too many expectations, obligations, and commitments in relationships. The individual may be feeling overwhelmed by the pressure of trying to please everyone or having too many responsibilities. The 10 of Wands Tarot card warns about the importance of setting boundaries and learning to say no. It is important to prioritize your own needs and well-being, and to practice self-care. It is important to ensure that the relationship is built on mutual respect, instead of feeling pressured to do more than one can handle.
