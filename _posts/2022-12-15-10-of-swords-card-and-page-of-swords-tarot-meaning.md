A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean together?

The combination of the 10 of Swords and Page of Swords tells a story of rock bottom and loss of innocence. It speaks of a time when a complete and painful ending is at hand and the truth of the situation is laid bare before us. The Page of Swords represents a curious kid that has been snooping around for the truth and finally it has caught up with them. They are now left stunned and broken, their naivety gone, and it feels like the end of the world has come. This is a powerful reminder of how the truth will out and that the truth can be painful, but it can ultimately set us free.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 10 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Ten of Swords in the Tarot card is a sign of complete exhaustion, lack of motivation, and helplessness. It is a reminder to take a much needed break from the stresses of everyday life and to practice self-care in order to regain and maintain physical and emotional health. It is a warning sign that one should evaluate current lifestyle habits and make necessary changes in order to improve health. Neglecting one’s health is the surest way to experience the painful consequences of this card.

Finances  
This card warns of financial ruin and destruction. It is a sign of financial despair and suggests that one should take concrete steps to improve their financial situation. The Ten of Swords urges to be mindful of spending habits and encourages to focus on creating and following a budget, staying away from risky investments, as well as paying off any debts. It also paves the way to a brighter future by suggesting to start saving for the future and seeking financial advice from professionals, if necessary.

Relationships  
The Ten of Swords is the proverbial “rock bottom” in relationships and reflects a deep sense of disappointment, betrayal, and pain. It suggests that current relationships are not fulfilling, may be toxic, and should be evaluated. It is a warning sign that suggests to move away from relationships that are based in manipulation, control, and emotional abuse, as well as to think twice before entering into new ones. This card also advises individuals to take responsibility for their own emotional needs, to practice self-love, and to focus on building and maintaining relationships with individuals who will bring out the best in them.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page of Swords for health matters often speaks subtly of the mind-body connection, emphasizing the importance of mental clarity and peace for overall health. It signals that it is necessary to be aware of the language you use to talk to yourself and the impact it has on your physical wellbeing. If the Page of Swords appears in a reading related to health, consider whether you have been neglecting proper diet and exercise or exposing yourself to negative influence and toxic people.

Finances: The Page of Swords for finances often denotes a period of careful assessment and caution. In many cases, it is telling you to mind your own business and not get caught up in other people’s financial affairs. The Page of Swords also advises you to remain frugal and disciplined with your finances, and to protect yourself from people who may be taking advantage of you financially. It is a reminder to pay attention to the small details and to ensure that all bills and payments are taken care of on time.

Relationships: The Page of Swords for relationships suggests that you are entering a period of communication and truth-seeking. You need to take a step back and look at the situation objectively and dispassionately. The Page of Swords advises you to pay attention to the words you choose and the tone and intention of your communication. It is essential to be honest and clear in your communication and to listen carefully to the words of your partner. The card also beckons you to use your intuition when dealing with relationships, and to pay attention to the signs and warning flags. Additionally, it is important to remain vigilant and to ward off manipulative energy, gossip and other devious tactics that can disrupt relationships.
