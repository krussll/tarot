It’s an invitation to go on a quest for spiritual illumination and fulfillment. The 9 Of Cups card is a reminder that our deepest dreams can come true and that we may achieve material abundance, good health, and overall well-being. Together they inspire us to take the time to reflect internally in order to receive external rewards. By seeking inner clarity, we can gain access to true joy, peace and satisfaction.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health: The Hermit card can represent taking a step back from the rat race of life and engaging in some much-needed self-reflection and meditation to assess the state of one’s overall health and well-being. Taking a break from the hustle and bustle of everyday life can allow us to explore our innermost thoughts and feelings, become aware of our energy levels, and analyze our behavior and habits. By taking this time away from the noise and distractions, we can gain a clearer understanding of what will truly benefit our physical and mental health.

Finances: The Hermit card suggests taking a step back and looking at the bigger picture and reassessing our financial situation. With its message of inner exploration, this card encourages us to take a closer look at our spending and saving habits and make adjustments accordingly. It also encourages us to think outside the box and come up with creative solutions to ever-evolving financial challenges.

Relationships: The Hermit card can represent taking a pause in our relationships in order to reflect on ourselves, understand our own needs and motivations, and determine where our relationships are headed. Taking some time alone or with our partner can ensure that our relationships are based on mutual understanding, respect, and trust. Through this process of self-reflection and honest conversation, we can gain greater insight into our relationships and make informed decisions about their paths.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is a very positive card that speaks of good fortune and great expectations. When it comes to health, it indicates that you’re in a great place. You have been taking care of yourself and you are likely to enjoy a healthier lifestyle. This could mean that you are not feeling any major illnesses or that if you have something, you’re managing it very well. Additionally, it could also refer to your overall wellbeing. You may be feeling happy and contented with your life, and you are likely to have more energy and vitality than before.

When it comes to finances, The 9 of Cups Tarot card speaks of abundance and prosperity. It is likely that you will experience great financial stability and success. You could be making a good salary, or you may be enjoying rewards for hard work and dedication. You are likely to be very financially secure and you may even experience some exciting windfalls.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The 9 of Cups Tarot card also speaks of great prospects. You are likely to experience strong and healthy relationships, both platonic and romantic. You could encounter a person who makes you feel fulfilled and content, and it is possible that your current relationships could develop or become even stronger. You are likely to experience a level of mutual understanding, trust, and joy that is fulfilling.
