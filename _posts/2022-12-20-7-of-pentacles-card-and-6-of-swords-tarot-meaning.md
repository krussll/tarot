## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card means different things for different aspects of life, but it is almost always associated with the need for patience and hard work.

Health-wise, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that health-related goals may take longer to achieve than initially anticipated. It may feel like the journey towards better health would never end, but one must not give up. Patience, discipline, and consistency are essential for achieving any health related goal, and the 7 of Pentacles encourages that one must trust the process.

Financially, the 7 of Pentacles is a reminder that success never happens overnight. It might take time, effort, and dedication before reaching a desired financial goal. As with health-related goals, this card also encourages delayed gratification and patience. One should not expect immediate financial success, but instead be mindful and strategic when it comes to financial decisions.

Relationship-wise, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card might be interpreted as a sign of improvement. It can mean that even though it might take longer than expected, things will eventually fall into place. This card also indicates that it may take patience and hard work to sustain and nurture a relationship, but that it is possible. Ultimately, this card is a reminder that relationships are worth investing in and that it is important to stay true to oneself.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a necessary transition in all areas of life, including health, finances and relationships. When this card appears, it indicates the need to move on, both mentally and physically, and make changes to keep life in balance.

When it comes to health, this card can represent a need to make new lifestyle changes that will bring balance and well-being. This could mean cutting out unhealthy habits, such as smoking, drinking, or overeating, and replacing them with healthier ones. It could also mean taking the time to properly rest and recuperate from illness or injury. Finally, this card can be a reminder to prioritize mental health by finding time for relaxation, self-care and taking regular time for activities that bring joy and peace.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Swords Tarot card represents a shift in money management. This could manifest as an opportunity to grow and expand, as well as to streamline processes or adjust current plans to find greater financial stability. It could also mean taking the time to truly understand personal finances and savings plans, creating an emergency fund and exploring opportunities for passive income.

Finally, in relationships, the 6 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a need to re-evaluate relationships and move on if the situation is not conducive to growth. This card often points to a period of reflection and introspection, as well as a readiness and desire to find more meaningful connections. When this card appears, it could be a sign that it’s time to let go of unhealthy relationships and create space to pursue more fulfilling and balanced connections.
