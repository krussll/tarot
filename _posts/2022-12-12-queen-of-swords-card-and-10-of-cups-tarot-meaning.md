A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen Of Swords and The 10 Of Cups tarot cards together represent a balance between self sufficiency and contentment within a supportive family environment. It indicates that you can rely on the love and support of your family, while at the same time maintaining your autonomy and enjoying honest relationships with them. This card combination can symbolize the harmony that comes from having a secure family foundation, while also setting healthy boundaries and living a life focused on individual growth. Ultimately, it points towards a life of both personal fulfillment and familial bliss.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take a look at the various aspects of our health. This card suggests that by being honest and truthful with ourselves, we can achieve improved physical and emotional well-being. Honesty when it comes to our physical health is key. We should all be honest and forthcoming about our physical ailments or condition, so that we can get the best possible treatment or advice. By taking responsibility for our health, we can empower ourselves to make better decisions and to live a healthier life.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes taking control of our finances. This card recommends that financial decisions should be made honestly and with complete transparency. Being honest about our finances will help us to make sure we have enough money for the things we need. This card also encourages us to be responsible with our finances and to make sure that our decisions are well researched and thought out. Taking time to understand our financial situation can go a long way in avoiding unnecessary debt and overall financial stability.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card emphasizes the importance of honesty in our relationships. This card encourages us to be honest about our feelings, expectations and thoughts. This card gives us the strength and courage to stand up for ourselves and our beliefs, even if it may not be popular with others. It also teaches us the importance of respecting each other’s boundaries and being open and honest with our partners in order to nurture and maintain strong and healthy relationships.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: Health is a key component of the 10 of Cups Tarot card, as it represents a sense of balance and harmony within one’s physical and mental well-being. By channeling the energy of this card, one can create a foundation of health and prosperity capable of sustaining them through difficult times, while maintaining a level of inner peace. With the 10 of Cups, one can be assured that they have the support and love of family, along with all the necessary resources, to keep their health in balance.

Finances: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of financial abundance and security. This card implies that, with the support of family and friends, one can experience prosperity and stability for a long time to come. It is a sign that whatever the current financial situation, it is only temporary and does not have to stay that way. With a strong sense of faith and the guidance of the 10 of Swords Tarot card, one can pursue their goals, dreams, and desires, assured that their finances are in safe hands.

Relationships: Relationships are often strengthened and deepened when the 10 of Cups Tarot card is present. This card symbolizes the comfort and support of family, as well as the strong bond formed between those of similar backgrounds. As a result, relationships tend to be more supportive and loving. With the 10 of Cups, one can trust that they have the backing of others and that their relationships are valued and appreciated. This card also opens one up to the possibility of forming new relationships, creating a positive and supportive atmosphere with which to thrive.
