A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The 7 of Pentacles and The Queen of Cups together offer a reminder that the success we all seek in life needs patience and hard work, and the support of nurturing people. We should not expect overnight success, but rather be comfortable in knowing that we are in the process of achieving our goals, and that being surrounded by loving and supportive people is key. The Queen of Cups is a reminder to seek out healing, compassion and understanding in our relationships, as well as to be more nurturing and supportive of ourselves as we journey towards our desired outcomes.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

For health, The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a need for patience and perseverance. It may suggest that an individual is feeling frustrated and is ready for a payoff for all of their hard work and dedication. This card encourages reflection and introspection; there may be an answer or solution that has not been discovered yet. Knowing when to slow down is key to maintaining balance in one’s health; patience helps ensure that the right decisions are being made, rather than impulsively acting on emotions or desires.

In terms of finances, The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages the individual to take it slow. Results may not be seen right away and that’s okay! Patience and dedication will be rewarded; it may just take time. Avoid taking on too much at once, or being too quick to make a decision; a thoughtful, calculated approach is needed. Similarly, it also reminds us to take a step back and assess our progress, rather than focusing solely on the destination.

Finally, in regards to relationships, The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a need for patience and understanding. This card reminds us to take the time to learn about our partners, and recognize the importance of both give and take. Be prepared for moments of frustration, but don’t let the negative thoughts override any hope for the future; it’s important to keep our dreams alive, even in trying times. Learning how to adjust to the changes and unpredictability of life is key; relationships are a process that requires continued effort, learning, and understanding.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health: The Queen of Cups Tarot card symbolizes health related issues and is associated with both physical and emotional well-being. She often appears when we are working on bringing our body, mind and soul into a balanced state, as she is a representation of nurturing and healing. When this card appears in a reading, it’s usually indicative of the need for us to focus on self-care and health, both for ourselves and for those we love. We are reminded that our physical, mental and emotional health are intertwined, and that it is important to pay attention to all aspects of the body-mind-soul axis.

Finances: The Queen of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the realm of finances. In regards to finances, this card represents the need to pay special attention to our finances and to be very mindful of how we spend our money and what we value. This card encourages us to evaluate our financial goals and make decisions based on our priorities and values. With financial matters, this Queen also shows us that it is important to stay balanced, allowing us to keep our finances in check while also allowing ourselves to enjoy life and have fun.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, the Queen of Cups Tarot card symbolizes nurture, support, and empathy. She is the mother of the Tarot, the nurturer that we can turn to for advice and support. This card encourages us to be compassionate, to listen and understand, and to be non-judgmental. In relationships, the Queen of Cups reminds us to stay present and open-minded rather than getting lost in the drama of the moment and rushing to any conclusions. She is here to guide us in learning the importance of understanding and communication, which are key in engaging in successful relationships.
