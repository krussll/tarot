A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 6 Of Wands card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 6 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The 2 of Wands and the 6 of Wands suggest that you are on the cusp of a huge victory and are about to experience a great deal of success. You have been putting in a lot of effort, and it is now time to start reaping the rewards. You have arrived at a point where you must make a choice, and the choices you make now will determine your future. You are likely waiting for the results of your efforts, and may be considering future travel plans to enjoy the fruits of your labor. Soon, you will be receiving recognition, awards, and good news for your accomplishments and effort.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 2 Of Wands and 6 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The combination of The 2 of Wands and The 6 of Wands tarot card in your love life means that you have an exciting decision to make. That decision could possibly be related to travel plans, or even a decision to stay in one place. Whatever choice you make, the results will be rewarding. Either choice will bring you closer to celebration.

In terms of travel plans, the 2 of Wands is suggesting that you take a journey which will bring much reward upon your return. As you travel, be open to unexpected opportunities, such as meeting someone new or learning the local customs of your destination or even a chance to advance your career. As you make your choices, the result will bring joy and success.

The 6 of Wands is an excellent card for success in love, and it suggests that you will be able to enjoy the rewards of your decision. This journey which you are about to take may maintain a surprise for you, such as a meeting with a potential soulmate. The 6 of Wands can also symbolize recognition from another person that works positively for your love life.

No matter where your journey takes you, the combination of the 2 of Wands and the 6 of Wands indicate that it will be a journey worth taking. The decisions you make now, will bring you great success and love in the future. So, take this journey and make the best of it. Celebrate your good fortune, as it will be a journey which will bring you great riches in terms of new experiences, growth, and potential romantic connections.

### What does 2 Of Wands and 6 Of Wands mean together for your finances?

The 2 Of Wands and The 6 Of Wands both signify a turning point in matters of your finances and job. If you’ve been waiting for results in either area, the movements of the 2 of Wands foretell that the answer is about to arrive. It’s time to make a choice about where to put your efforts – which path to take. The 6 of Wands suggests a successful outcome, so no matter what decision you make, there’s sure to be recognition, victory, and good news.

The 2 of Wands is a card of travel and exploration, so this could be a sign that a journey is coming up: a new job opportunity, a chance to learn something new, or just a change of scenery. If you choose to pursue it, you’re likely to come out the other side with a sense of victory and achievement. The 6 of Wands indicates that you will be rewarded for your efforts, whether it’s in the form of a promotion, a raise, or something else.

The combination of the 2 and 6 of Wands also suggests that you have an opportunity to take on a leadership role and be a beacon of success. You may feel that you’ve plateaued and need to reach the next level. With enough planning and focus, the 6 of Wands indicates that you can reach the top and become an inspiration to others.

Overall, the combination of the 2 of Wands and the 6 of Wands suggest that you have the strength and means to make big strides in your finances and job. Make the right decisions, explore your options, and reap the rewards!

### What does 2 Of Wands and 6 Of Wands mean together for your health?

The 2 of Wands tarot card and the 6 of Wands tarot card together represent a period of transition and decision-making in terms of your health. This is a powerful combination of cards that suggests you are in a period of decisive growth, moving you closer towards achieving your health goals.

The 2 of Wands speaks to taking action and making choices in order to achieve results. It encourages you to think carefully about the effects of your decisions and take the necessary steps to reach your goals. You may be called upon to take risks, but you will be rewarded for it – and you can trust that your efforts will be beneficial in the long run.

The 6 of Wands follows this with a message of victory and the rewards of success. After taking the steps outlined earlier, you can expect to see positive changes and rewards as a result. This could indicate a health breakthrough or the successful implementation of a new health plan, as well as public recognition for your efforts. You may even receive an award for your hard work in reaching your goals.

By bringing the 2 of Wands and the 6 of Wands together, you can expect to enjoy a period of growth and success in terms of your health. With dedication, perseverance, and the right decisions, you can both achieve the desired results and be rewarded for your hard work.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 2 of Wands Tarot card can suggest a time of health maintenance and preventative measures. This card can represent taking a proactive approach to health, such as undergoing regular check ups and scheduling routine tests. Taking ownership of your health can prevent ailments before they occur. It can also signify a time of research and study, as one may be seeking to learn more about their health and how to take better care of their body.

Finances: This card can symbolize proactive financial planning and taking the time to create a budget and plan for the future. It is a reminder to think beyond the immediate, and make decisions that will benefit you and your finances in the long run. It can also be an indicator of investing and seeking greater returns for the future. It can represent the need to make plans for long-term savings and investments, instead of spending impulsively.

Relationships: The 2 of Wands Tarot card reminds us to take the time to think before we act. When it comes to relationships, it can signify holding back and being mindful of what you are contributing in a relationship. It can also be a reminder to not jump into a decision too quickly, but to consider all the options and how it will affect the relationship. Additionally, this card can represent a time of travel and exploration, which can be an enriching way to strengthen an existing relationship or cultivate a new one.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands in the Tarot is a card of great celebration and victory. It speaks to us of having achieved success after a period of effort and hard work. This card can speak to us of the many successes that life has to offer, whether it be in terms of health, finances or relationships.

For health, the 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the fruits of healthy living. It could be in reference to having worked hard and achieved good health, which is a huge victory. It is a reminder that the effort and hard work we put in always pays off and that success and good health can be achieved in the long run. It could also be a sign that our efforts in lifestyle changes and healthy habits are already working.

For finances, the 6 of Wands Tarot card speaks of achieving financial success. It could be a sign that whatever financial goal we have worked hard to achieve, we have reached it. It also serves as a reminder to stay focused and put in effort if we want to achieve financial freedom and success.

For relationships, the 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes victory and success in terms of our relationships. It could mean that things have gone well in our relationships and that our hard work in resolving issues and communication has paid off. It could be a sign of our relationship flourishing and doing better than ever before. It could be that our relationships have reached a new level of success and understanding.
