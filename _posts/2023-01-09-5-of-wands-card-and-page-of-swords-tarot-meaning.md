A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and Page Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The 5 Of Wands and The Page Of Swords cards suggests that there is a situation in your life that is filled with rivalry, challenges, and obstacles. You may be competing against others who are trying to get the same thing as you. Although there is competition, this situation can be seen as a game – a positive challenge. On the other hand, the Page Of Swords suggests that there is a curious person who is probing and watching you, trying to find out the truth. The combination of cards can mean that you need to be careful not to share too much information, while still pushing forward in the competition.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

You understand that the truth will eventually come out and that there is no point trying to hide it. It may be best to be honest and open with this person, as they will eventually know everything anyway.

In conclusion, the 5 of Wands and the Page of Swords tarot card together suggest that your love life will be full of challenges and obstacles. You also need to be aware that you could have someone in your life who is always prying and sniffing out the truth. Be honest and open with this person and don’t be afraid to take on any type of challenge that arises.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 5 of Wands Tarot card can suggest that you are feeling stressed trying to juggle many activities and responsibilities and that it is taking a toll on your physical and mental health. It can suggest that you are in a state of competition with yourself or with others, or that you are feeling overwhelmed with all the tasks you have set out to accomplish. You may be finding yourself running at full capacity and feel that you are in constant competition with yourself and your own sense of identity. The 5 of Wands Tarot card can also suggest that you take some time to relax and take a break from the mental and physical competition you may be experiencing.

Finances: The 5 of Wands Tarot card indicates that you are in a state of competition when it comes to your finances. It could signify that you are trying to compete with yourself or with others in order to make the most money possible. Or it could also signify that you are competing for resources, attention, or status. The 5 of Wands Tarot card can suggest that if you want to make more money, you may have to take more risks or get out of your comfort zone and actively seek out new opportunities.

Relationships: The 5 of Wands Tarot card can signify competition in relationships. It could mean that you are in the midst of a power struggle with someone or that there is a competitive atmosphere in your relationship. The 5 of Wands Tarot card suggests that while competition can be healthy, it needs to be kept in check in order to maintain harmony and balance in your relationships. It can also suggest that if you want to make your relationships stronger, you may need to take a step back and focus on working together towards a common goal, rather than competing against one another.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page of Swords Tarot card represents a person who is very observant and curious when it comes to their health. This card encourages individuals to be aware of their physical body, take notice of changes, and to seek out advice from professionals. This card suggests that being aware of your health can help one be proactive in taking care of their health, instead of simply reacting to illnesses and health issues. Additionally, this card encourages individuals to be honest with their doctors and to be wary of self-diagnosing their health concerns.

Finances: The Page of Swords tarot card indicates financial prudence and intelligence. People who are represented by this card understand the importance of making budget plans and avoiding unwise investments. They are careful with their money, ensuring that they stick to their budget and never overspend. This card also suggests being observant to trends in the stock market and other financial issues, such as stock prices and political policies that can affect their finances.

Relationships: The Page of Swords tarot card emphasizes honest communication when it comes to relationship dynamics. People represented by this card understand that relationships require honesty, openness, and clarity. They value knowing the truth, even in uncomfortable circumstances. People represented by the Page of Swords tarot card use keen observation to gain knowledge and aid in decision-making in regards to their relationship, ensuring that they have all the facts before making any choices. This card indicates a need to be aware of the facts before making assumptions or leaping to conclusions.
