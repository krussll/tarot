It is important to remember patience and self-control when considering any new job, raise, promotion, investment or new income opportunities. With the guidance of these cards, you may be able to achieve financial and emotional stability that comes with the right level of prudence and self-control.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Temperance and Ace Of Pentacles mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Temperance and Ace Of Pentacles the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health: The Temperance Tarot card symbolizes balance and harmony within your health, encouraging you to find a balance between physical, mental, and emotional health. It asks you to take stock of your physical and emotional health, to make sure that you do not overwork or overtax yourself. Making sure to practice moderation and moderation of diet, exercise, and personal care is key to maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Additionally, Temperance encourages finding balance with your mental health, reminding you to take time for yourself to relax and care for your emotional needs, as well as staying mindful of your thoughts and emotions.

Finances: The Temperance Tarot card encourages careful management of finances and encourages patience when it comes to making decisions. It advises that you create a budget and chart out a plan that allows you to have money left over for savings. Releasing money too quickly and spending without a plan can cause financial difficulty in the future, so Temperance advises that you review your plan regularly and remain flexible to adjust if financial needs change. With patience, you can make the most of your money and ensure that you have enough to save, invest, and splurge on something special now and then.

Relationships: The Temperance Tarot card reminds you of the importance of maintaining balance in your relationships. This card advises you to practice patience with your loved ones, understanding that we all move at our own pace and have different needs. Temperance encourages you to listen not only to what is being said but also to what is not being said. It suggests taking time and space to reflect on how your actions may affect another, and how you can contribute to balance and harmony in the relationship. Letting go of any egos and embracing compromise will help to create peaceful and fulfilling relationships.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card is an auspicious one, suggesting an abundance of potential for health, finances and relationships. It symbolizes wealth, material abundance, prosperity and success.

In terms of health, the card suggests nourishing foods, regular exercise, positive lifestyle habits and overall wellbeing. The Ace of Pentacles encourages you to take steps for your own health and well-being, as your physical wellbeing will help support your financial success.

In terms of finances, the Ace of Pentacles Tarot card represents potential for new opportunities and investments. It suggests money coming in rather than going out, with the potential for new job, raise, promotion, investment or business. The card suggests that with the right attitude and ambition, financial success can be achieved.

When it comes to relationships, the Ace of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that little effort can go a long way. It encourages generosity and thoughtfulness, which can help to strengthen and nurture relationships. With the right attitude and investment of time, relationships can become more solid and abundant.

Overall, the Ace of Pentacles Tarot card is a highly auspicious one, suggesting abundance in all areas of life. With the right attitude and effort, the card suggests that health, finances and relationships can all become successful and nourishing.
