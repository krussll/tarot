When looking at the Empress and the 6 of Pentacles together, it can represent fertility and abundance, but also a generosity when it comes to giving and receiving help. This might mean that if you’re struggling in one area of your life or in your relationships, these cards could be a reminder that you have support, be it in the form of friends, family or even just yourself. It can also mean that your creative endeavors could be blooming and flourishing with the support of your community.

When thinking of your health, this combination can represent physical and mental fertility and balance. For example, it might be a reminder to practice yoga to find balance between the mind and body. It can also mean that a balanced and consistent diet, exercise and sleep pattern, coupled with generous and loving self-care, can open up a strong sense of physical and mental fertility.

When thinking about your relationships, the Empress and the 6 of Pentacles can remind you of the importance of giving and receiving help and being generous with yourself and others. This could mean that if something is becoming strained, it might help to look for ways to give and receive support in whatever ways work for you, whether that’s a kind word or a hug. This can help to keep your relationships healthy, loving and nourished.

Overall, the combination of the Empress and the 6 of Pentacles speaks to the importance of fertility, abundance and the importance of being generous when it comes to giving and receiving help in the areas of your health, yourself and your relationships. It can be a reminder that you are not alone and that there is always support on all levels if you choose to look for it.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

It can be interpreted as an indicator of fertility, both literally in terms of pregnancy, as well as figuratively in terms of creative endeavors. It could also be indicative of a flourishing business, new job or moneymaking opportunity.

In terms of finances, The Empress card encourages you to use your resources wisely and in abundance. Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Empress card speaks of unconditional love and support. It is a card of nurturing and mutual respect which encourages our closest relationships to thrive. ## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles in a Tarot card reading can have many different meanings depending on the context of the subject asking, but overall the card is associated with generosity, charity, and giving or receiving assistance. In terms of health, it can suggest a focus on giving or receiving assistance to foster overall well-being. The 6 of Pentacles can indicate it’s time to practice self-care, or to seek outside help if needed. It could also mean that physical and mental health should be prioritized, and to not be too proud to ask for help.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Pentacles can signify financial assistance, either in the form of receiving a loan or charity. Alternatively, it could mean that it’s time to “pay it forward”, by offering assistance to those in need. Money can be a powerful tool for improving one’s life, and this card can be an encouragement to use it wisely. Rather than greed or hoarding, it’s about recognizing when and where help is needed and offering financial support whenever possible.

For relationships, the 6 of Pentacles can indicate that it’s time to give or receive help from friends or family. This help can come in the form of emotional or practical support, but it’s especially important to practice empathy and kindness in relationships. The card can also be a reminder to offer assistance to someone in need, as a sign of friendship or respect. It’s about recognizing the importance of generosity and the positive result it can have in our relationships.
