A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of the 5 of Swords and the 10 of Cups tarot cards suggest that you are in a difficult situation and need to remember what is truly important in life: family and love. You may be faced with bullying, theft, violence, and abusive relationships, but it is important to remember that, even in the darkest of times, love and family connection will help you to find your way out. With the support of your family, you can transform the chaos around you into joy and contentment. The 10 of Cups tarot card reminds you that one should not forget the power of family and love in times of adversity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 5 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a lack of control over one’s physical and mental health. It is a warning about the negative consequences of too much aggression and violence, including physical and emotional abuse. It can be a sign of someone who is feeling overwhelmed by their problems, and is unable to cope with them. When this card appears, it indicates that the individual may be engaging in behavior that is unhealthy for their well-being, such as self-harm or substance abuse.

Finances  
The 5 of Swords Tarot card suggests a lack of financial stability. It is a warning that if one is engaging in dishonest or manipulative actions in order to get money, they will eventually be found out and will suffer consequences. It also indicates that one is not in control of their finances and is acting recklessly with their money. When this card appears, it is a sign to stop and consider one’s financial decisions carefully, before taking any drastic measures.

Relationships  
When the 5 of Swords Tarot card appears, it can be a sign of violence and hostility in a relationship. It is a warning that the individual may be engaging in an abusive relationship, either with themselves or with someone else. It is also a sign that there may be a power imbalance in the relationship, with one person exerting control over the other. It can be a sign of bullying or manipulation, and is a warning to be aware of any potential signs of danger. It also indicates that the individual needs to be assertive in order to protect themselves, and to address any issues in the relationship.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 10 of Cups Tarot card indicates a strong sense of well-being and contentment with life. It symbolizes a healthy balance between physical health and emotional stability. The balance of a good diet, exercise, and relaxation is essential. With a feeling of contentment and joy, one will be more likely to make the right decisions when it comes to health and well-being.

Finances: The 10 of Cups Tarot card suggests an abundance of financial security and stability. This card suggests that the individual has achieved balance between spending and saving, making sure to keep money in the bank for future use. Financial security can lead to a healthier lifestyle, free from worry and stress. This card encourages people to be mindful of their financial decisions and be prepared for the future.

Relationships: The 10 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a strong sense of family and friendship. People who are surrounded by supportive and loving relationships are in a more advantageous position to achieve happiness and success. The 10 of Cups can be a great reminder to value the people in your life and take the time to nurture and strengthen existing relationships. As this card also suggests unconditional love, it’s important to remember that a strong relationship is built on trust and understanding.
