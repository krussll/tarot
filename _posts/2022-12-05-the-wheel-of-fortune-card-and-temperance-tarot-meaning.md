While the Wheel of Fortune may seem to imply risk and possibility, The Temperance card reminds you that the key to successful navigation through the unknown is finding a balance between the two and being wise with your choices.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune tarot card is a powerful symbol of fortune and destiny. It references the idea that the wheel of life is constantly turning, bringing with it both good luck and bad. In terms of health, the Wheel of Fortune can be a reminder of the cyclical nature of life and the importance of balance. It can offer hope for healing and a reminder of the power of the human body to heal itself. Those with chronic illness may find that this card provides comfort and encouragement, allowing them to accept and move through the changes in life in positive ways.

When it comes to finances, The Wheel of Fortune tarot card can represent a sudden windfall of luck and the potential for great success. It can also be a sign that despite current setbacks or low points, an upswing is likely. It encourages risk taking and the beginning of a new cycle of financial prosperity. This can bring a sense of empowerment to those dealing with financial instability as it signals that there is hope in spite of the current conditions.

In terms of relationships, The Wheel of Fortune tarot card speaks to the idea that we are all going through cycles and fluxes. It can be a reminder of the importance of flexibility and acceptance in all of our relationships, as life is constantly changing and evolving. It encourages us to trust that in spite of the challenges, there is always a chance for rebirth and new beginnings. It reminds us to be optimistic in our dealings with others and that even in the face of difficult circumstances, there is the potential for enjoyment and growth.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health  
The Temperance Tarot card represents balance and moderation in all aspects of life. This includes health. In terms of physical health, it implies that one should not overindulge in any one particular area of health. For example, it is important to maintain a balanced diet and to engage in a mix of aerobic and anaerobic activities. It also suggests that diet and activity be tailored to the individual’s individual needs, rather than following any given formula or set of rules.

Mentally, the Temperance card encourages individuals to practice moderation and temperance in their thoughts and emotions. It implies that mental health should not be neglected, as strong mental health is important for overall physical health. In particular, it suggests that one should practice self-reflection in order to identify and work through difficult emotions. Additionally, it implies that engaging in meditative activities can help to achieve inner peace and balance.

Finances  
The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to practice moderation and balance when it comes to finances. It suggests that individuals should not overspend, nor should they be overly cautious with their money. Instead, it implies that a balanced approach should be taken, one which allows for saving and investing some money, while still having enough to enjoy life. Additionally, it suggests that one should develop a financial plan and goals and then take the appropriate steps to achieve these goals.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card reminds individuals to practice balance and moderation in all of their relationships. This includes relationships with family, friends, and romantic partners. It implies that one should engage in relationships that are healthy, respectful, and mutually beneficial. Additionally, the card implies that one should practice temperance in the intensity of their emotions, so as not to be too impulsive or reactive in any given situation. Furthermore, it suggests that couples should practice forgiveness and understanding, and that couples should have clear expectations of each other so that both parties are honored, respected, and appreciated in the relationship.
