A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Swords card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Swords card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Swords and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The 9 Of Swords and The 8 Of Cups tarot cards, when experienced together, suggest a situation of intense inner turmoil. You may be weighed down by the anxieties and nightmares caused by the 9 of Swords, while feeling the need to retreat and move on, as represented by the 8 of Cups. The combination of these two cards could be highlighting your internal struggle between wanting to stay and wanting to move on. Whether you are dealing with grief or depression, you may be feeling overwhelmed and unable to process your emotions. It is important to take a step back and find the courage to make the changes needed in order to find peace.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Swords and 8 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Swords and 8 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a representation of oppressive and overwhelming feelings like insomnia, nightmares, fear, dread, and distress. It suggests that the querent is afflicted by dark, depressing thoughts and is struggling to make sense of their current situation and life circumstances.

For Health –The 9 of Swords tarot card implies the querent is emotionally and mentally afflicted and struggling to cope with the inner turmoil. There may be physical ailments brought about by such heavy emotional burden like insomnia, headaches, and chronic fatigue. The best way to address such issues is to seek help from a professional in order to determine the root cause of the burden and resolve it.

For Finances – The card signals a period of financial struggles. This can be due to a poor financial decision, being unable to make ends meet or being in debt. The best recourse is seeking advice from a financial expert to help the querent gain control of their finances.

For Relationships – The 9 of Swords tarot card alludes to disillusionment in the querent’s relationships. The querent may be feeling let down, disappointed, or betrayed. The querent should try to communicate their feelings with their partner and to find a way to resolve such issues. If it proves to be difficult to do so, it is recommended to seek professional help.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is an indication that physical or emotional withdrawal is necessary in order to achieve a greater understanding of yourself and the world around you. This card suggests that there is something within yourself that requires reflection, action and growth in order to move on from the current situation. It can represent a need to turn inward in order to gain clarity, and a readiness to let go of existing circumstances in order to create something more meaningful.

When it comes to health, the 8 of Cups can suggest a need to pause and identify what is important to your wellbeing. It can be interpreted as an awareness of signs in your body that are telling you to slow down, rest and take better care of yourself. It may also indicate the need to take time for yourself in order to reassess your goals, ambitions and lifestyle choices.

When referring to finances, the 8 of Cups can signify a need to look at your current financial situation from a fresh perspective. It could suggest that you need to withdraw from the current routines and habits that may be holding you back from achieving a greater financial security. This card can also point to a willingness to let go of existing investments in order to focus on something more meaningful.

When it comes to relationships, the 8 of Cups can indicate a need for space and distance. It could suggest that the relationship requires a period of reassessment in order to understand its true potential. It can also suggest that it is time to move on if the relationship is causing harm or is not meeting your needs. The 8 of Cups can also point to a willingness to invest in a new relationship that is based on mutual understanding, trust and respect.
