A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 4 of Swords and the 5 of Cups tarot card can be seen as a signal to take some time for yourself. It suggests that you need to retreat into a space of solitude and rest, in order to recover from your current feelings of loss, regret, and abandonment. It also encourages you to take a step back from the current situation and evaluate your relationship with the world around you. In doing so, it is possible to find the peace and renewal that comes from self-reflection and acceptance. The combination of these two cards is a call to reclaim your autonomy, and to find a way to heal the wounds that have been created.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 4 of Swords Tarot card represents a necessary time of retreat and renewal when it comes to health. It suggests that time away from external influences, such as work and obligations, is necessary in order to properly replenish one’s energies and heal both mentally and physically. It is a gentle reminder to take a break from stresses, overworking and other unhealthy habits that can be detrimental to health. Taking a step back from common responsibilities and stressors can help people focus on self-care and supporting their own wellness.

Finances: The 4 of Swords Tarot card also speaks to finances, suggesting that rest and recuperation is essential for success. This can include taking a break from obsessively thinking about money and instead focusing on calming and nurturing activities. Taking a break from financial tasks, such as budgeting and bill-paying, can help bring clarity, allowing people to create a more realistic financial plan. The 4 of Swords Tarot card can also encourage people to look inwardly, helping to identify financial stumbling blocks, such as unchecked spending and poorly executed planning.

Relationships: The 4 of Swords Tarot card emphasizes the importance of rest, renewal and solitude in relationships as well. This could mean literally taking a break from a relationship that has been feeling strained and struggling. Taking time to rest, reflect and nurture oneself can help create balance and detachment, enabling clarity and perspective that can help resolve relationship issues. On the other hand, the 4 of Swords Tarot card can also be an invitation to a deeper level of relationship; one of spiritual healing, growth and emotional connection. This can be accomplished through extended periods of connection and solitude that allow for relationship-based healing.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 5 of Cups Tarot card is associated with feelings of regret, loss and grief, and this can have a profound effect on a person’s physical and mental health. For example, when experiencing a sense of loss, the individual may feel a lack of motivation or an inability to concentrate which can lead to fatigue and depression. Other physical symptoms such as headaches, weight loss/gain, or an inability to sleep well can arise due to the stress and emotions experienced in response to the loss. As such, the 5 of Cups Tarot card can be a sign to pay careful attention to one’s health, as it is important to take steps to address any underlying issues that may be causing these physical problems.

Finances

The 5 of Cups Tarot card can also be indicative of potential financial troubles. The feelings of loss and regret associated with this card may lead to a decrease in motivation to find long-term solutions that can help improve one’s financial situation. For example, instead of actively looking for ways to save money, or to increase one’s income, the individual may be overwhelmed by these emotions and instead focus on short-term fixes such as spending money on unnecessary items or gambling. The 5 of Cups Tarot card can be a reminder to take a step back from the situation, take a deep breath and reassess one’s finances in order to create a long-term financial plan.

Relationships

In relationships, the 5 of Cups Tarot card can be a sign that there is a feeling of loss or abandonment that needs to be addressed. The individual may feel as if they are not loved, or that their partner is neglecting them or is distant. This card can be a reminder to focus on understanding and empathy in order to ensure the relationship is healthy and happy. It is important to discuss any feelings of loss or abandonment with the partner, in order to avoid any further hurt or resentment. Both parties should be willing to make an effort to address any underlying issues and build a strong relationship, as the 5 of Cups Tarot card can be indicative of the need to take a relationship off auto-pilot and put in the extra effort in order for it to be successful.
