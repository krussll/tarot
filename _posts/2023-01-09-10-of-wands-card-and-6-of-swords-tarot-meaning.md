A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The 10 Of Wands tarot card and The 6 Of Swords tarot card together signify a period of transition in which you’re feeling overwhelmed and exhausted from the burden of having too much on your plate. You may have been carrying too many responsibilities for too long and have finally reached a point where you need to downsize and make a change. The 6 Of Swords tarot card is the signal you need to make that change, whether that means physically removing yourself from a situation or simply lightening your mental and emotional load. It’s time to take a step back and make the necessary changes to relieve the stress you’ve been carrying.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 10 Of Wands and 6 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 Of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the burden of too much responsibility and a feeling of being overwhelmed. It signifies a weighty responsibility and physical exhaustion. When this card appears in a Tarot reading, it tells us that the burden of too much responsibility is having a direct effect on our physical and emotional health, as well as our financial and relationship stability.

When it comes to physical health, The 10 Of Wands Tarot card indicates that we may be taking on too much and it is beginning to take its toll. We may feel exhausted and overworked. We may have taken on physical tasks that exceed our strength and endurance, leading to fatigue and burnout. We may also be compromising our overall health, sacrificing proper rest, nutrition and exercise in order to keep up with life’s demands.

In terms of finances, The 10 Of Wands Tarot card reveals that we may have become too enmeshed in pursuing money, or securing our financial foundations, and neglected our own needs in the process. It warns of the effects of working too hard at the cost of our passions and joy in life. We may also be taking on too many financial responsibilities, and as a result, creating a heavy mental and emotional burden.

Finally, The 10 Of Wands Tarot card reveals that our relationships may be suffering due to our intense focus on overworking and responsibilities. We may be neglecting our relationships in favor of trying to meet the demands of our work and life obligations. We may be too exhausted to be present in our relationships, or to provide emotional support when it is needed. We may also be too preoccupied with work to engage in meaningful conversations or activities with our partners. In short, The 10 Of Wands Tarot card is a warning to take a step back and remember to take care of ourselves and our relationships in order to find balance in life.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords Tarot card is symbolic of a necessary transition; a movement away from that which is no longer serving our highest good. In terms of health, this card speaks to the need for a change in lifestyle; the need for us to make decisions that are beneficial, rather than harmful, to our physical and mental well-being. This could involve cutting out unhealthy habits or taking up a new, healthier routine. It also suggests that if we are feeling stuck in a rut – whether this is in terms of our physical health or our mental outlook – then it is time to make a change.

When it comes to finances, the 6 of Swords carries the message that it may be time to make a shift in our outlook and approach towards money. This could mean budgeting more carefully or taking steps towards financial freedom. It also suggests that we may need to take a closer look at our finances and reassess our relationship with money; it is time for us to make decisions about our financial future that will benefit us in the long run.

In terms of relationships, the 6 of Swords speaks to the need for us to make changes in the way we interact with others. It could mean cutting ties with those who are no longer serving our highest good and shifting our focus to relationships that are more fulfilling and beneficial. It can also suggest a need to reassess our current relationships and make decisions that will help us to build healthier, more loving connections. Whatever changes we make, the 6 of Swords reminds us that it is often necessary to make a transition in order to create lasting positive change in our lives.
