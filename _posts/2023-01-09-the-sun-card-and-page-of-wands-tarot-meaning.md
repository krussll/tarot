## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

Health  
The Sun Tarot card is associated with physical health and well-being. It is a card of good luck and fortune, which suggests that the person receiving it may be in for a period of healing and renewal. It is associated with clarity and constructive energy, so it can be used to help the person boost their physical health. Good nutrition, exercise, and a healthy lifestyle all come to mind with The Sun card. Additionally, the warmth of The Sun can be used to guide the person in setting realistic goals and making lifestyle changes to reach them.

Finances  
The Sun Tarot card often signals financial prosperity, abundance, and new opportunities to create wealth. It suggests that the person receiving the card may be on the brink of fortune and financial success. It is a powerful card that encourages ambition and hard work, as well as creativity. In addition, The Sun card also shines a light on fiscal responsibility, and encourages the individual to be mindful of budgeting and investing wisely. It is a reminder that ultimately, the person should take ownership of their finances and be in control of their own destiny.

Relationships  
The Sun card is a card of optimism and light, so it is often seen as a positive omen in the context of relationships. It is a card of joy and celebration, suggesting that a strong bond is forming or that existing relationships are growing ever stronger. It is a reminder that it is important to nourish relationships, and to recognize the importance of communication and understanding. In addition, The Sun card suggests that the person receiving it should recognize the importance of living in the present moment and savoring each interaction.

It encourages you to take ample time for yourself and to strengthen your physical and mental wellbeing. This card tells you to be proactive and encourages you to explore new ways of healthy living, such as trying new exercises, following healthier diets and engaging in activities that bring you joy. It’s time to focus on your own wellbeing and think of creative ways to stay physically and mentally healthy.

The Page of Wands Tarot card encourages you to take action by setting goals and taking concrete steps towards achieving them. It alludes to a period of new development, so now is the ideal time to start taking the steps necessary to increase your financial stability. It suggests that it may be beneficial to create a new budget or invest in new sources of income. Take a proactive approach by researching investment options and creating a plan for the future.

Relationships

The Page of Wands Tarot card encourages you to be creative, open and honest when it comes to your relationships. It encourages you to come up with unique and creative ways to show your loved ones that you care. Spend time talking and getting to know each other in new and interesting ways. You may also benefit from exploring a new hobby together. This card suggests that if you’re willing to take action now, you can be successful in your relationships.
