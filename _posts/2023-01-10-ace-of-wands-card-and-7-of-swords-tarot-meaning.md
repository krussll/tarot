A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Wands card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Ace Of Wands card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Ace Of Wands and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The Ace of Wands and the 7 of Swords tarot cards together suggest a situation of both fresh beginnings and hidden danger. On the one hand, this combination might signify the start of a new endeavor or project, filled with creativity and inspired ideas. On the other hand, there is a hint of something sinister lurking beneath the surface, likely to do with betrayal, dishonesty or even theft. This could be a warning to take caution when starting something new, as it could lead to unexpected consequences if precautions are not taken.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Ace of Wands represents new beginnings, new projects, and an urge to create something new. This could mean taking a romantic getaway together, or trying a new activity together.

However, the Seven of Swords also represents betrayal, dishonesty, and stealing. This could mean that one or both of you has been dishonest with each other, or that one person in the relationship is harboring some hidden feelings that they don’t feel comfortable expressing. This could lead to feelings of distance between you and your partner.

Ultimately this card combination suggests that you may need to take a step back and think about what is missing in your relationship. Consider why you are feeling distant and think about ways to bring the excitement and spark back. Talk to your partner and be open and honest in order to find a way to reconnect. It may be painful, but if you are willing to work hard and be honest with each other, the Ace of Wands can suggest that a new and exciting beginning is possible.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

It encourages us to take action to maintain our physical and mental health, and to keep up with our energetic and emotional balance. It suggests that it’s time to take some steps to strengthen our body, and to make sure we have a nutritious diet and regular exercise. This card encourages us to take initiative and to strive for our personal well-being, so that we can be alert and ready to face life’s challenges.

Finances: The Ace of Wands tarot card is a sign of financial abundance, and it’s a great card for manifesting prosperity. It indicates that now is the perfect time to focus on our aspirations, and it encourages us to take active steps towards securing our financial stability. It suggests being creative in finding new sources of income and having the drive to gain a competitive edge in our field. This card also encourages us to have a healthy attitude towards money, and use it to improve our lives.

Relationships: The Ace of Wands tarot card suggests that we should use our creative energy to spend quality time with our loved ones. It encourages us to strengthen our relationships by being open and honest about our feelings, desires and goals. It also emphasizes that we should also be willing to bend to compromise and to show our partners our affection and appreciation. This card reminds us that strong relationships don’t just happen, and that we need to take active steps to ensure they stay healthy.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 7 of Sword Tarot card speaks to health in terms of breaking boundaries and engaging in risky behavior. This card warns us that the physical results of such decisions can be catastrophic and can lead to disease, addiction, and illness. It is a reminder to always be mindful of the consequences of our actions, especially when we are on a quest for self-discovery. Neglecting to take care of our physicals health can cause us to lose touch with our deeper selves and this card is a reminder to always practice self-care and to find healthy, non-destructive ways to grow.

Finances: The 7 Of Swords tarot card speaks to finances in a somewhat negative light. This card suggests that financial problems can be caused by stealing, cheating, and deceit. The 7 Of Swords warns us to be honest and to stay away from any kind of illegal activity that might seem profitable in the short term, but could hurt us in the long run. It also suggests that we should be careful not to make uninformed decisions that could lead to financial ruin.

Relationships: In regards to relationships, The 7 Of Swords tarot card advises us to be honest and to not take advantage of those we care about. This card warns us of the potential for betrayal and dishonesty, and provides a reminder to always practice truthfulness and integrity in our relationships. This card also serves as a reminder to pay attention to the motives behind our actions, as selfishness and non-constructive behavior can be damaging to relationships. It is important to remember that relationships should be based on mutual trust and respect.
