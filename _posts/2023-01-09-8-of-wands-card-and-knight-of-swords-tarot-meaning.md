A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean together?

The 8 Of Wands and The Knight Of Swords tarot card combination suggests that you will soon experience a rapid shift in your life. Change is coming quickly and it is important to stay vigilant and be prepared for the sudden news that will arrive. This combination could represent an individual who is fast-talking and willing to make big changes in their lifestyle in order to make the most of whatever life throws their way. It is a reminder to take action and adapt to sudden changes in order to make progress in your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

For Finances:

The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of financial success. It suggests that if you are in need of extra money, it is coming your way at a rapid pace. It could be a windfall, an unexpected bonus, a raise, or a new business venture. This card is a reminder that you must take action to make the most of your financial opportunities as they come.

For Relationships:

The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of new beginnings and growth. It suggests that if you are stuck in a relationship rut, it’s time to shake things up. This card encourages you to take action, make changes, and work towards moving in a new, positive direction. It may indicate the beginning of a new relationship, or a shift in an existing one. The 8 of Wands Tarot card suggests that you should be open to changes that can bring new energy and excitement into your life.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

When interpreted through the lens of health, The Knight Of Swords Tarot card indicates a sudden lifestyle change that could have both positive and negative effects on one’s wellbeing. It can be a call to action for people to take better care of themselves and their physical health, or a warning about the dangers of ignoring physical needs in favor of other pursuits. On the other hand, the card can represent an ambitious person who is pushing themselves too hard, leading to potential problems with exhaustion and imbalance. This card serves as a reminder to keep regular check-ups, maintain a balanced diet and exercise routine, as well as to be aware of one’s mental and emotional health.

The Knight Of Swords Tarot card can also impact one’s financial situation. It can be seen as a sign of a sudden shift in the way one handles their finances and investments. It could represent a willingness to take calculated risks in the name of ambition, or it could point to a person who is prone to taking risks without considering all of the potential negative outcomes. Additionally, this card could encourage people to take a more active and organized approach to managing their wealth and taking steps to ensure financial stability.

The meaning of The Knight Of Swords Tarot card in relationships is a bit more mixed. On the one hand, it can be seen as a sign of optimism and ambition that could lead to some positive changes in relationships. On the other hand, it can be a warning about the dangers of being overly pushy or reckless with relationships, causing a strain in communication or mutual respect. This card encourages people to have honest conversations and strive for balance between assertiveness and respect for others.
