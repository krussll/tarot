## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

In terms of health, The 9 of Wands encourages us to be cautious about the decisions we make and the environment we are in. We can practice this kind of cautiousness by maintaining safe and healthy habits, such as regular exercise, healthy eating, and adequate rest. The 9 of Wands can also serve as a reminder to trust our intuition and to be wary of certain people or circumstances that could be detrimental to our health.

The 9 of Wands also serves as a reminder around finances. We must remain frugal and practical, while also being mindful of our spending habits. We must also be diligent with our finances, not getting into dangerous financial situations, but also being aware of where our money is going.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The 9 of Wands urges us to stay true to our values and beliefs. We must remain aware of how we are being treated and how our relationship is impacting our lives. We must also stay aware of how our partners are treating us and our situation, and remember to take the time to set boundaries to protect ourselves. Additionally, this card encourages us to stay open and honest in our relationships, allowing us to create trust and understanding.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is often associated with good health. It symbolises a balance between physical health and mental wellbeing. It is believed that when this card appears in a spread, it is a sign that one should focus on self-care and paying close attention to one’s own physical and mental needs. This could mean taking better care of one’s physical health by maintaining a balanced diet and exercising regularly, as well as investing in the mental wellbeing by exploring activities such as meditation or mindfulness.

Finances: The Queen of Swords is often viewed as a sign of financial success, but it also warns that this must come with total honesty and careful decisions. This means that if one wants to succeed financially, they must be ready to make difficult choices and take responsible opportunities, as well as stay genuine and true to their own principles. When this card appears in a spread, it is often a sign for one to think about the long-term effects of their decisions and to manage their finances wisely.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords is a sign of relationships built on honesty and self-sufficiency. This card serves as a reminder to be independent in relationships, to be honest and authentic with your partner, and not rely too heavily on their support. It also suggests that it is important to be able to stand on your own two feet and make decisions that come from within. When it appears, it is a sign to take a honest and thoughtful look at your relationships and to make sure that your partner is not relying on you for more than you are comfortable with.
