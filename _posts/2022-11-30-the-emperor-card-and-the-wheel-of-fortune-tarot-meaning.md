However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Emperor card and The Wheel Of Fortune card together in particular.

The The Emperor card and The Wheel Of Fortune have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Emperor and The Wheel Of Fortune mean together?

The Emperor tarot card and The Wheel of Fortune tarot card represent two important aspects of life that can help steer you towards success. The Emperor tarot card speaks of respect, leadership, strategic planning and the father figure, while The Wheel of Fortune tarot card speaks of good fortune, a turning point, luck and gambling. Together, these two cards offer insight in the importance of striving for success while also taking advantage of luck and opportunity that come your way. Respect and leadership are qualities to admire, but the fortune gained by luck or opportunity should not be taken for granted. By considering both cards the message is to use wisdom, courage and knowledge to plan a strategy for success and take advantage of the good fortune that may come to you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Emperor mean?

The Emperor Tarot card has implications for health, finances, and relationships. In terms of healthcare, it signifies one taking a strategic and mindful approach to wellness. This could mean things like having better habits when it comes to diet and exercise, seeking professional medical guidance as needed, and finding a system of self-care that works for them. The Emperor card also offers a reminder to take responsibility for one’s own health, and to not be complacent in taking care of oneself.

In terms of finances, the Emperor card can suggest the need to take an organized and disciplined approach to money management. This could mean budgeting responsibly, understanding the importance of saving, and being mindful of how one’s money is used. It could also mean taking advantage of opportunities that can increase one’s financial security. The Emperor card can serve as a reminder to take financial matters seriously, and to use resources wisely.

In terms of relationships, the Emperor card can mean seeking a sense of balance and stability. This could involve honest communication, respecting boundaries, and taking a mature approach to conflict resolution and compromise. The Emperor card suggests establishing a strong foundation on which a relationship can thrive, while also ensuring that both partners feel secure and respected. It can serve as a reminder to approach one’s interactions with this level of respect and an understanding of what it takes to create a successful, lasting relationship.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel Of Fortune Tarot card is all about luck, chance, and new beginnings. It is often seen as a card that brings hope and optimism for the future. This Tarot card speaks to the idea of a new cycle, where fate will bring about a welcome change in circumstances. Therefore, the Wheel of Fortune can have significant implications for one’s health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can represent a new lease on life. It may suggest that you have to make a few changes in order to improve your overall health and well-being, such as making better lifestyle choices or seeking medical help if the situation requires it. This card can also indicate a positive change in your health – perhaps your doctor has given you a clean bill of health or a new treatment has begun to take effect. Meanwhile, if health matters have been difficult recently, then the Wheel of Fortune could represent a break in the clouds – a welcome respite from the difficult times.

Financially, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card suggests that there could be an unexpected windfall of money – perhaps a lucky break in the stock market or a surprise inheritance. It can also mean that changes in your financial situation are on the way. This could be from a change in career, a bonus, or any other means of improving finances. On the other hand, this card can also refer to the idea of gambling, so it is important to ensure that you don’t take any foolish risks with your money.

Finally, in relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can signify a time of change. This could represent a new beginning, such as the start of a relationship or a renewed commitment in an existing one. On the other hand, it can also mean the ending of a relationship, as the wheel turns and a new chapter begins. In any case, changes in relationships can be a positive experience, as long as the individuals involved remain open-minded and willing to compromise.

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card indicates that change is on the horizon and that luck is in the air. Therefore, it is important to be mindful of this Tarot card’s message when it comes to health, finances and relationships, as it may bring about a welcome new direction in life.
