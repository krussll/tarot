A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 6 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Justice card and 6 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 6 of Wands tarot card together indicate that through cause-and-effect and win-win solutions, the truth will come out, resulting in victory and recognition. This could mean that if you are honest, upfront and make sure everyone is able to benefit from the situation, you will come out on top in the end. It is a reminder that seeking justice, equality and fairness will bring about good results, and you may be rewarded with success and positive news.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It suggests that issues in the relationship have been addressed, and there has been a fair resolution of the issues. The 6 of Wands Tarot card further reinforces this idea of equality in the relationship. It speaks of victory, a sense of accomplishment, and a feeling of recognition in each partner of the other’s efforts. This could symbolize the partners feeling respected and appreciated, which can often be a missing element in a relationship. The recognition that they are both truly equal in the relationship is what will bring the long-term success that this card can foretell.

By combining the Justice Tarot card and the 6 of Wands, it appears that the relationship is about to turn the corner and move towards greater understanding for each partner. The ground rules have been set and now the partners have the opportunity to move forward in the relationship, making sure that each partner is heard and respected. With all of the cooperation and mutual respect in place, the relationship can now look forward to great success, victory and recognition.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Health

The Justice Tarot card is a reminder to make healthy choices. It encourages an examination of the cause and effect of any health decisions. It is a reminder to look at the short-term and long-term effects of your health decisions. The card is a reminder to be mindful of how you take care of your body, mind, and soul. The Justice Tarot card reinforces the idea that you have a responsibility to yourself to choose action in the best interest of your health. It encourages looking at situations objectively and making choices without bias.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card encourages financial win-win solutions. It is a reminder to look at finances objectively and to consider the consequences of any decision. It is an invitation to make a decision that is in line with your true values, that brings a sense of justice and balance to your finances. This card encourages wise decisions so that you don’t experience any setbacks. It is a reminder to find solutions that are fair and equitable to everyone involved.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card is a reminder to always treat others with fairness and respect. It is an invitation to look at relationships objectively and create a sense of peace and justice within them. It is a reminder to practice honesty and truthfulness in relationships. It is also a reminder to look for solutions that are equitable for all involved, solutions that bring about a sense of balance and justice. The Justice Tarot card encourages healthy communication and dialogue as the best way to come to a resolution.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes excellent health – both physical and mental. Physically, it means that you’ve been able to make positive changes in your diet and/or exercise regimen and can now boast in the successes of your hard work. Mentally, it suggests that you’ve conquered any obstacles that were hindering your emotional or mental well-being, allowing you to take pride in how you feel. Additionally, the 6 of Wands tarot card can be a sign that you’ve overcome a health challenge or been victorious in the face of adversity.

Finances: Financially, the 6 of Wands Tarot card signifies a period of considerable accomplishment or windfall. It may mean that you’ve received a significant raise or discovered a way to increase your revenue. You may also have achieved success in negotiation, marketing, or investments, though it’s important to remain prudent in your spending. Additionally, the 6 of Wands Tarot card can indicate financial assistance from others or a generous gift coming your way.

Relationships: In relationships, the 6 of Wands Tarot card can mean everything from newly formed connections to the strengthening of existing ones. It may point to reconciliation after a period of tension or increased intimacy with a current partner. The 6 of Wands Tarot card can also suggest a feeling of celebration among family members or the recognition of your accomplishments by those close to you. Additionally, it can signify that you’re one step closer to achieving a relationship goal or dream.
