However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

It is a time to look forward with optimism, as your wishes and goals can come true if you work hard and stay focused. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 10 of Swords Tarot card indicates that a person’s state of physical or mental health is at its absolute lowest point. This can be manifested in either a physical ailment or a mental illness. A person may feel they have exhausted all of their options and may find themselves in a state of despair and hopelessness. They may feel physically and mentally drained, and may be unable to find the will and strength to continue struggling with their health issues.

Finances: The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests that a person’s financial situation is in a complete and painful ending. This card can suggest that money has run out, investments have failed, and there may be mounting debt. A person may have already taken drastic measures to correct their financial situation, but have found that this has not yielded any results. The 10 of Swords can also signify a person’s inability to move forward, as they may have reached the end of their financial rope.

Relationships: The 10 of Swords Tarot card can signify a relationship that has come to a sudden and painful end. This card suggests that a person may have felt betrayed and abandoned by someone they trusted, leaving them feeling intense pain and sorrow. These feelings may be so overwhelming that they cause a person to shut down emotionally, unable to trust again. The 10 of Swords can also signify a relationship where one partner is clinging onto hope even though the other person has indicated that they are done; the pain and heartache of knowing that a relationship is over can be intense and difficult to overcome.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

When the Nine of Cups appears in a Tarot reading, it symbolizes a time of great blessings and good luck in all areas of life. The energy of this card encourages us to appreciate and savor the successes that we have achieved, as it indicates a time of great abundance, joy and contentment.

For our health, the Nine of Cups tells us to take time for ourselves and enjoy our newfound blessings. By taking care of our physical and mental health, we will find that our sense of wellbeing is improved and we will be able to better handle the challenges that life throws our way. This card is also a reminder that our health is affected not only by our physical actions, but by our mental and emotional states as well. Therefore, we must take time for self-care and practice mindfulness and compassion during challenging times.

In terms of finances, the Nine of Cups speaks of abundance and great wealth. This card is a reminder to us to appreciate the wealth that we already have, both material and spiritual, as we look to manifest our new goals. It also encourages us to appreciate the wealth that can be found in our relationships. Caring and nurturing relationships are a priceless source of wealth and we must actively work to maintain these connections.

For relationships, the Nine of Cups encourages us to cherish the people in our lives and appreciate all that they bring to us. This card reminds us to be kind, compassionate, and understanding to others, as this allows the essence of the Nine of Cups to expand and flourish within our lives. It also encourages us to find ways to help our relationships grow, such as spending quality time with each other, communicating openly, and expressing our appreciation for one another.

The Nine of Cups speaks of great abundance and blessings in all areas of life. It encourages us to take care of our health, appreciate the wealth that we do have, and nurture our relationships so that we can all thrive. When this card appears in a reading, it is often a reminder that there is much to be thankful for and that our lives can be greatly enriched by finding the joy and contentment in the present moment.
