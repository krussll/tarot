A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Devil card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

When looking at the combination of The Devil card and The 10 Of Pentacles, a message of caution comes to mind. The Devil card speaks of the dangers of getting locked into addictive behavior, the need for contentment with the material world, and the potential for us to become blinded by lust. In contrast, The 10 Of Pentacles tells us that we can choose to use our materialistic tendencies for good, to build something that will last beyond our own lifetime. It reminds us that there can be more to our lives than satisfying our temptations. Together, these two cards remind us to strive for a balance between worldly indulgences and leaving a legacy that will make our lives meaningful.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Devil and 10 Of Pentacles mean together for your love life?

The Devil tarot card and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card together suggest a love life filled with materialism, greed, and obsession. This combination of cards points to a need to focus on materialistic values over emotional needs or connection. It speaks to an addicting and potentially destructive desire to acquire things and status, often at the expense of relationships, emotions, or overall well-being.

This combination warns of an obsession with acquiring the things which symbolize success in our society. Materialism can become an addiction when we become consumed with the need for bigger and better possessions, trapping us within a cycle of never-ending striving. The 10 of Pentacles brings forth the notion of leaving an inheritance, and when combined with The Devil card, can suggest a desire to secure one’s legacy through material acquisition. An unhealthy focus on money and material gains can prevent us from living a life full of meaningful relationships and experiences.

The Devil card is also linked to sexual lust, and when combined with The 10 Of Pentacles card, can suggest that a relationship is built on superficial satisfaction. This combination can represent a relationship that is centered around a shallow version of love, founded on the idea that with enough money and attention, any problem can be solved. While these types of relationships can offer temporary gratification, this combination is ultimately a warning that a lasting and fulfilling connection requires more than what material objects or momentary pleasures can provide.

When The Devil tarot card and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card appear in combination, it’s a reminder that our love lives should reflect a balance of material success and emotional nourishment. It’s important to acknowledge that although a certain level of comfort and stability is important, it should never be more important than the overall well-being of our relationships.

### What does The Devil and 10 Of Pentacles mean together for your finances?

The Devil and 10 of Pentacles are two very different tarot cards that can both influence our finances and job. When read together, the Devil suggests that we need to pay attention to our financial choices, and the 10 of Pentacles highlights the potential for creating a secure financial future.

The Devil often highlights an unhealthy relationship with money – one that is characterized by obsession and addiction. The Devil warns us to look for the warning signs of financial recklessness, including moving too quickly on investments, exposing ourselves to too much risk, and neglecting our savings. We must pay attention to what we are doing with our money, or we run the risk of losing it all in a moment of temptation or foolishness.

The 10 of Pentacles, however, speaks to our long-term financial security. This card often appears when we have the opportunity to invest in a secure, long-term source of income. It reminds us to think ahead as we make our financial decisions, and to make sure that our money is contributing to our future security and legacy.

When the Devil and 10 of Pentacles are read together, the message is clear – be mindful of your decisions, take calculated risks, and ensure that you are setting up a secure financial future for yourself. The Devil cautions us to not get too wrapped up in our current financial circumstances, and the 10 of Pentacles encourages us to create a strong financial foundation that will last. This means taking a balanced approach to our finances, creating a strong budget and diversifying our investments.

It is essential to have a long-term plan for your finances, or else you may find yourself in a difficult financial situation. The Devil and 10 of Pentacles remind us of this, and if we are strategic and mindful in our financial decisions, we can create a secure financial future for ourselves.

### What does The Devil and 10 Of Pentacles mean together for your health?

When The Devil tarot card and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card are combined, it speaks of a situation where the material pleasures and addictions of life have taken control and caused damage to our health and wellbeing. Our obsession with status, possessions, and achieving success has come at the cost of our physical, mental and emotional health. On the one hand, the 10 of Pentacles encourages us to strive to leave behind a legacy, of financial or personal accomplishments achieved through hard work and dedication. On the other, The Devil card is a reminder not to get too carried away with our ambition and to balance our lives with rest and relaxation so that we don’t compromise our own health and wellbeing in the process.

We must be aware of our own addictions and unhealthy habits and make sure that we don’t become trapped in them. It’s easy to get caught up in chasing money, power and recognition, but none of these are worth sacrificing our own health for. We must be mindful of the materialistic temptations in our lives and be willing to forgo short-term gratification for the sake of our long-term health. We must learn to listen to our body, mind, and spirit and be willing to step away from The Devil’s temptations so that we can focus on our physical and mental health.

Ultimately, when The Devil and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot cards come together, they remind us to take a step back and think carefully about what kind of legacy we want to leave. It’s not enough to just work hard and accumulate wealth and material possessions; we must also be mindful of our own wellbeing and look after our health both now and in the future. We must make sure to nurture ourselves and our relationships and seek balance between the material and the spiritual, so that we can create a lasting legacy of good health and wellbeing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Devil and 10 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card usually reflects the darker aspects of life that can come when we make decisions based on our own immediate desires, rather than on what is more balanced for ourselves and for others. In terms of health, this card can represent an addiction to substances, behaviors, or even certain types of foods. In other words, it’s about making choices that aren’t necessarily good for you in the long term. If one’s health is suffering as a result of these choices, it’s likely The Devil Tarot card is in play.

In terms of finances, The Devil Tarot card can reflect a tendency to focus on immediate needs and wants, rather than a long-term, balanced approach. It can represent an obsession with material things, or a desire to keep up with what everyone else has, which can lead to overspending and debt. Greed and envy become primary drivers and it can be hard to reign in the spending.

In relationships, The Devil Tarot card can manifest as an obsession with another person, or a sexual desire that is hard to control. It can also represent materialism and envy within the relationship, with one partner constantly wanting more from the other – either material things or attention. In short, The Devil Tarot card reflects a lack of balance and moderation in terms of health, finances and relationships, and this can lead to difficult and uncomfortable situations. For this reason, it is important to recognize the signs of this card and take steps to move away from temptation and toward a more balanced lifestyle.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of wealth and security, one that can bring about new beginnings in health, finances and relationships. When this card appears in a reading, it is typically a sign that everything is coming to fruition, and that a new cycle of stability and abundance is emerging.

When it comes to health, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card can indicate a time of healing and restoration. This is a period when individuals can draw on their resources and strength to create a healthier lifestyle and improved wellbeing. It is a chance to revitalize the body and soul with positive habits and healthy routines.

In regards to finances, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card can signify the ability to create wealth and accumulate resources. This card suggests a time of plenty, when earned income and unexpected bonuses can be used to create a sound financial base. It can also suggest the possibility of inheritance or other forms of legacy, allowing people to set their family up for generations.

On the relationship front, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card can denote a period of stability and security. This is a card of long-term commitments and the ability to create secure, loving relationships. It can suggest the possibility of retirement together and the joy of transitioning into a life of leisure. This card also highlights the importance of family and the joy of leaving a legacy, both in terms of finances and relationships.
