## What does Queen Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen of Swords and the 5 of Cups tarot card together represent a highly honest and self-sufficient person who is enduring a period of loss, regret and grief. This individual is likely feeling abandoned and unloved, and although they have the ability to be independent and honest, they can still feel the emotional consequences of these negative experiences. It is important for this person to allow themselves to experience the sadness of the situation, but remain hopeful and trust that in time they will find the closure that they need.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This individual takes full responsibility and control of their own health, providing their body with the necessary care and nourishment it needs. They are able to navigate their body’s needs without becoming dependent on outside sources or assistance. They understand the importance of consistency and routine, recognizing that these are key tools for maintaining healthy habits. Additionally, they do not hesitate to ask for help when necessary and remain unerringly honest with themselves and those they deal with regarding their health.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card indicates that a person is self-sufficient and honest when dealing with finances. They approach money with patience and discernment, understanding the intrinsic value of frugality and moderation. They are able to manage their money shrewdly, investing it in productive and profitable ventures. This person stays true to their purpose and does not allow their emotions to steer them in the wrong direction. As a result, they are able to establish a sound financial plan that best serves their needs and goals.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes an individual who is honest and direct in their relationships. This individual is self-sufficient and is able to maintain healthy boundaries. They strive to communicate their feelings and needs while also taking responsibility for the effect of their words and actions. They understand that relationships are a two-way street and are willing to get vulnerable and lay it all out there. Furthermore, they remain honest and unbiased in their dealings with others, which gives them the opportunity to form lasting and meaningful connections.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: When the 5 of Cups Tarot card appears in a reading, it’s a warning that you may be experiencing physical and emotional exhaustion due to the consequence of a difficult situation. This can lead to depression, anxiety and stress-related health problems such as headaches and insomnia. It can also indicate that you may be neglecting yourself and your own needs, potentially resulting in an overall decline in physical health.

Finances: The 5 of Cups Tarot card is a warning that you may be experiencing financial difficulties. It suggests that difficult decisions may need to be made to make ends meet. This could be a result of a mistake you may have made in the past, or due to a situation outside of your control. It’s important to take stock of your financial situation and evaluate how you can best manage your finances moving forward.

Relationships: The 5 of Cups Tarot card speaks of a sense of emotional loss and regret. It may indicate that you have suffered from hurtful experiences in the past, leading to a feeling of abandonment or loneliness. It is important to understand that these experiences do not define your future relationships and that there is a way forward. There may be a need to let go of past hurt and open yourself up to potential new relationships, even in the face of uncertainty.
