The Empress tarot card symbolizes fertility, creativity, abundance, and good parenting. It is a reminder of the power of nature, growth, and compassion and that we should take full advantage of these qualities in our lives. When coupled with the 3 of Pentacles, this card combination suggests that you are focusing on creating something of real value, whether it be building a business or a family.

At the physical level, when the Empress and the 3 of Pentacles are together, they suggest that you may be in a fertile period in terms of health. This could be a good time to start a new exercise routine, especially if it involves team sports and collaboration. Creative activities, such as dancing and painting, could also be beneficial.

From an emotional and mental standpoint, the Empress and the 3 of Pentacles together suggest that you are entering a period of great growth and abundance. You could find that any collaborative efforts you make will bear fruit in terms of increased emotional intelligence, communication skills, and overall confidence. This could even spill over into your relationships and enable you to improve them — or build new ones.

Finally, on a spiritual level, the Empress and the 3 of Pentacles combination hint at a renewed sense of purpose and a feeling of deep connection to the Universe. The act of working together with others to create something from nothing may be a profound reminder of the interconnectivity we each have with one another and with nature. You may find yourself having more profound and meaningful conversations with your loved ones and feeling a renewed appreciation for the simple things in life.

Overall, the Empress and the 3 of Pentacles together suggest that you will be in for a period of substantive growth and enrichment. Whether it is in the form of new relationships, increased mental and emotional clarity, or physical health, this is a time when your efforts pay off in the form of lasting solutions and fruitful investments.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.
