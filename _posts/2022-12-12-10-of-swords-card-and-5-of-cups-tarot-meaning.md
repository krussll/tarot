A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 10 of Swords and the 5 of Cups tarot cards signify a complete and final end with feelings of regret, grief, and abandonment. This combination is about facing your rock bottom, and coming to terms with a painful situation. You may feel a deep sense of loss and be experiencing a deep sense of sadness and loneliness. It may be time to accept that something has come to an end, and while it is difficult, it may be time to move on. This combination of tarot cards offers a chance to accept the current circumstances and find the strength to find a new path.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 10 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 10 of Swords and the 5 of Cups in a love reading could indicate a complete and painful ending. This could be a relationship that has ended abruptly and drastically, where there is no going back. You may feel like you have hit rock bottom and you are overwhelmed with sadness and regret. This could be a situation where you had your hopes and dreams invested in a particular person only to find out that they had been lying to you or that they could not commit to you.

The 10 of Swords suggests that you may now be in a situation where you feel helpless and alone in the face of a deep loss. You may feel betrayed, misunderstood, and unable to cope with the pain of the situation. The 5 of Cups reinforces this sense of pain and sorrow, as it often points to feeling abandoned or unloved. This could be a time where it feels difficult to move on from the darkness of the loss and to find a way to forgive the other person and make peace with the ending.

The combination of the 10 of Swords and the 5 of Cups suggests that it is important to take the time to process your grief and work through the pain in order to find your way forward. It is important to acknowledge your feelings and be kind and gentle with yourself during this difficult time. Once you are able to take care of yourself and allow yourself to heal, you will be on the path to finding renewed hope and joy in love.

### What does 10 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

The combination of The 10 of Swords and The 5 of Cups in your finances and job can be a difficult omen. In this instance, it indicates that the situation you’re in is beyond repair and will come to a complete and painful end. You are likely to experience a major loss in your finances and job, leaving you feeling dejected, abandoned and lacking in security. This may lead to a deep sadness, regret and despair.

The 10 of Swords speaks of an inevitable collapse, a breakdown too severe to be reversed. Your professional relationships may be fractured and you may find yourself betrayed by those you thought you could trust. Everything that you have worked hard for may be slipping away, leaving you feeling helpless and powerless.

The 5 of Cups hints at a deeper, hidden layer of emotion. The sense of loss you will experience as your job or finances crumble cannot be understated. You may feel as though you have been abandoned, as though you are no longer valued and appreciated by others. This may lead to feelings of grief, self-pity and misery.

The combination of The 10 of Swords and The 5 of Cups give a bleak outlook for your finances and job, but the message here is that it is not the end. There will be a chance for new beginnings and fresh opportunities, although in darker moments it may seem impossible. These cards are a reminder to hold onto hope and to surround yourself with those who will love, support and nurture you. Though the situation may be difficult now, a brighter future awaits.

### What does 10 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together for your health?

When the 10 of Swords and the 5 of Cups appear together in your tarot reading, it is a call to look more deeply at all aspects of your physical health. The combination of cards suggest that you have reached a point of complete and painful endings, a point where you have hit the rock bottom. This might be the result of the neglect, stress, or overwork that you have been subject to, or it could be a sign of making bad decisions that have put your health in jeopardy.

It is likely that you have also been feeling overwhelmed by a sense of loss, regret, and grief. You might have been feeling abandoned or even feel that you are not worthy of love. All of these emotions can lead to poor health and can manifest as physical ailments. The 10 of Swords and the 5 of Cups suggest that it is time to take a break from all of the external pressures that are causing you pain, and to take a step back and look at the bigger picture.

The combination of these two cards encourage you to make a commitment to taking better care of your health. This could mean taking the time to eat properly, get adequate rest and exercise, attending regular medical check-ups, and addressing any underlying emotional issues that could be taking a toll on your physical health. Investing in yourself and realizing that your physical health is not something to be taken lightly is the most important step in reclaiming your health.

The 10 of Swords and the 5 of Cups are a reminder that you can overcome the physical pain and emotional distress you are feeling. Through taking action and making an effort to prioritize your physical health, it is possible to restore balance and reclaim a sense of joy and wellbeing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Ten of Swords Tarot card signifies a need for self-care. It indicates that you are quite fatigued, both mentally and physically. You may feel very tired and exhausted, and you may struggle to make it through the day. It also suggests that there may be some underlying wounds you haven’t dealt with yet, and it’s time to do so. It’s time to take a step back and assess your physical and mental health, and take the necessary steps to make sure these issues are attended to before they reach a critical stage.

Finances  
The Ten of Swords Tarot card indicates a need to address a financial issue that you’ve been putting off. It could be a debt that you’ve been avoiding that needs to be addressed, or a financial situation that you need to take a closer look at and make sure it’s on track. You may need to make some tough decisions and take action, but it is necessary in order to uncover any issues and take steps to move forward.

Relationships  
The Ten of Swords Tarot card suggests a need to look within and examine any relationships in your life which you have been avoiding. This could include any family members that you have unfinished business with, or any friendships that may have suffered because of an unresolved conflict. It is important to recognize these issues and make an effort to work through them. It may be a difficult process, but it is essential in order to move forward in your relationships and create healthier, stronger bonds.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 5 Of Cups Tarot card is associated with grieving and regret, and when it appears in a health context, it indicates that one may be suffering from an emotional state that is impairing their overall wellbeing. Physically, the 5 Of Cups card can indicate a lingering mental illness or physical condition that has gone untreated or is not being addressed effectively. Furthermore, the card can suggest that someone is neglecting their own health and wellbeing in favor of working on other aspects of their life, be it their finances, relationships, or career.

Finances: When the 5 Of Cups Tarot card appears in a financial context, it can denote a feeling of loss or inadequacy that is preventing one from making the necessary steps towards financial security. It can also suggest that someone is struggling to accept or cope with a financial setback, such as debt or the loss of a job. In any case, the 5 Of Cups card requires one to take the necessary steps to heal from the losses and move forward once more, in order to begin building a more stable financial future.

Relationships: The 5 Of Cups Tarot card also holds meaning when it comes to relationships. It can be associated with feelings of regret, grief, and abandonment that is holding someone back from finding and creating meaningful connections. It can suggest a past relationship that has left a lingering sense of hurt and sadness, which can prevent someone from seeking out new relationships. In order to move forward and heal from these feelings, it is important for one to recognize and process their grief. Once the necessary healing has been done, it will be much easier to let go of the past and begin looking to the future.
