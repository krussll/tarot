However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Star card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Star card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The Star Tarot card indicates a period of healing, renewal and hope. The Star card indicates that we should take some time to nurture our physical, mental and spiritual health, by taking a step back, noticing our patterns and habits, and making adjustments as necessary. When we can see our lives as a whole, we can gain insight and clarity into which areas of our health need more attention.

Finances  
The Star Tarot card can be an encouraging sign that greater abundance and financial security are on their way. It is a reminder to believe in ourselves and have faith that the best is yet to come. The Star card encourages us to take the steps necessary to bring our financial dreams to fruition. This may include setting realistic goals for our income, investing our time and energy into meaningful work, and cultivating a sense of optimism and possibility in our relationship with money.

Relationships  
The Star Tarot card suggests that it is time to put some extra effort into our relationships. This could mean taking the time to nurture existing connections and perhaps making new ones. It is also a reminder to believe in ourselves and in the possibilities of our relationships. With our inner clarity and self-confidence, we can find peace and contentment in our relationships, without trying to control the outcome or any other person involved. We can also be open to miracles, trusting that the universe will provide us with the connections and opportunities that we need.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

For Health

The Queen Of Wands tarot card representing the fiery influence of potential, confidence and self-assurance is an important card when considering the health of an individual. A person with the Queen Of Wands influence will often approach health with an assertive and proactive attitude. They may be drawn to alternative or complementary treatments with a focus on holistic wellbeing. Most importantly, they will take charge of their own health and be determined to achieve a balance of physical, emotional and spiritual wellbeing.

For Finances

The Queen of Wands Tarot card speaks of financial confidence and independence. Its influences empower an individual to take control of their own financial destiny. With this card comes the determination to create a secure and abundant financial future. It encourages those under its influence to dream big, trust their instincts and set practical monetary goals. Those with the Queen of Wands influence will often approach money matters with a sound head, trusting their own judgment while also exploring all available avenues to secure their financial future.

For Relationships

The Queen of Wands tarot card encourages a confident and independent outlook in relationships. Those with this influence will be quick to create a secure relationship based on trust and communication. They will be eager to find a balance of give and take in any partnership, respecting that each person involved has their own needs. This card also speaks of loyalty and commitment. The Queen of Wands will be fierce in their protectiveness of those close to them, showing love and support in whatever form is needed.
