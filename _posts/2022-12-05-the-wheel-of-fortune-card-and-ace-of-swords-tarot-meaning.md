## What does The Wheel Of Fortune and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card and The Ace Of Swords tarot card together signify the potential for great change in your life. The Wheel Of Fortune suggests that luck and good fortune may be coming your way, and could signal a major turning point in your life. The Ace Of Swords adds a sharper edge to this, signifying the need for mental clarity and new ideas to help you move forward. You may have to confront a difficult conflict or situation, but the combination of the two cards suggest that you can come out of it with a positive result.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

The Ace of Swords Tarot card can represent the beginning of a new chapter in your health. The card symbolizes the idea of surgery or some sort of change in physical health. The card is a reminder to act thoughtfully and with purpose when it comes to any changes in health, as it has to do with mental clarity and proper decision-making. It can also suggest a strong sense of focus and determination in regards to any physical or mental health issues. The card can also be a symbol of growth and progress, as the starting point of any journey of health is to first become clear of what you want to achieve.

Finances

The Ace of Swords Tarot card can provide guidance when it comes to finances. The mental clarity associated with this card allows one to make informed decisions that are based on logic and reason. The card is a reminder to stick to a budget and stay within our means. It warns against being reckless with money and encourages financial discipline. The card encourages being mindful and deliberate in financial decisions, taking the time to consider the long-term impact of those decisions.

Relationships

The Ace of Swords Tarot card can represent the beginning of a new relationship. The card symbolizes the need for clear communication in order to start off on the right foot. It is a reminder to trust your instincts and make informed decisions that are based on facts, not feelings. The card also warns against being overly-quick to judge someone and instead encourages taking the time to get to know the person and build trust. The card encourages honest and respectful communication, allowing both parties to be heard and understood. The card is a reminder that conflict is natural in any relationship and the key to overcoming any difficult situation is to show empathy, understanding, and to stay focused on the end goal of the relationship.
