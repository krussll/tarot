A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Death card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The Death tarot card and the 7 of Swords tarot card together represent a time of great transformation. They signify the end of something, and the need for change. This could be in the form of letting go of a past relationship, leaving a job, or starting anew. At the same time, there is a suggestion of dishonesty or betrayal, and a need to be cautious while moving forward, as the 7 of Swords hints at theft or other forms of deceit. Ultimately these cards are telling you to trust your instincts, remain honest, and be open to new beginnings in spite of any potential difficulties.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Death and 7 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Death and 7 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

The Death Tarot card signifies a major transition in one’s life and the opportunity to move on to the next stage. In terms of health, it calls for a deep and thorough examination of one’s physical and mental state. It may indicate a need to let go of certain unhealthy habits, like the need to overeat or to overexert oneself. It can also signify a time of renewal, with a focus on healing and self-care. If one is taking medication or undergoing treatment for a health condition, this card implies that the time has come for a reevaluation and a potential change in course.

In terms of finances, this card can symbolize a period of endings, when it is time to say goodbye to money-wasting habits and to financial difficulties. It suggests that a person’s financial status is changing, whether it be positive or negative, and that the person should prepare for a new way of managing their money. It can signify a difficult transition to greater financial stability, such as downsizing, budgeting or cutting costs.

The Death Tarot card can also represent change or endings in relationships. This could mean that it is time to say goodbye to someone in one’s life, or that it is time to re-evaluate the dynamic of a current relationship. It can signify an opportunity for growth, a time when a person must break away from unhealthy relationships and move on to healthier connections. It could also mean a time when one is ready to let go of the past, to start anew and to focus on building meaningful relationships in the present.

The Death Tarot card is about transformation and growth, and its meaning is applicable to all areas of life. By understanding the implications of this card, one can prepare for the changes it may bring and be ready to embrace the opportunities that lie ahead.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card can signify a difficult period in one’s life, particularly in the realms of health, finances and relationships.

Health: This card can suggest that health problems may arise due to a lack of caution or neglect. People may be feeling vulnerable due to a lack of self-care and may be prone to accidents or illness because of this. Alternatively, this card can indicate that one has over exerted themselves, or taken too much on, leading to serious health concerns.

Finances: This card can warn of financial problems as a result of dishonesty or theft. It could suggest that one should exercise caution when dealing with money, as risk-taking behavior could lead to loss of money. Alternatively, it could signify that one may be taken advantage of by a person in a position of power, or that an exploitative deal may be proposed that could put the querent in a difficult financial predicament.

Relationships: This card can suggest a period of challenges in one’s relationships. It can indicate issues of betrayal, dishonesty, and a lack of trust. Alternatively, it can suggest that one may be tempted to run away from issues in their relationship and avoid dealing with them. It could also suggest that one is abdicating their own responsibility in the relationship and attempting to escape consequences that may arise.
