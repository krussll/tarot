## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is all about balance, harmony, self-control and patience. It is a reminder to practice moderation and self-discipline in every aspect of life.

When it comes to health, this card encourages you to listen to your body and practice healthy habits and behaviors. Take the time to develop healthy habits such as exercising and eating nutritiously. Balance is key when it comes to health. Moderation should be practiced in all aspects of health from diet to exercise. Avoid extreme behaviors that can cause harm to your body.

In regards to finances, the Temperance card reminds us to be mindful of our spending habits. This involves setting a budget and sticking to it. Balance is key when it comes to finances. Spend money on the necessities, save a portion of your income, and avoid impulse buying. Patience and self-control are also essential in order to build long-term financial security.

Finally, the Temperance Tarot card encourages us to practice balance, harmony and patience in our relationships. This card is a reminder to be mindful of our communication styles, practice active listening, and be open and honest with our partners. It also suggests that we reserve judgment and instead respond with understanding, compassion, and kindness.

The Temperance Tarot card is an important reminder that balance, moderation, and self-control are essential for a healthy, happy, and secure living. It teaches us that these traits can be applied to all aspects of our lives, from health and finances to relationships.

The 6 of Wands Tarot card suggests that when it comes to health, there is a certain level of pride in the effort being made to stay in peak condition. Financial success is within reach, as long-term investments start to pay off. In relationships, the 6 of Wands suggests that creative collaboration is a surefire way to achieve mutual goals, while giving recognition to one another’s hard work and dedication.

When it comes to health, the 6 of Wands encourages individuals to take pride in their good choices and trust that the efforts they make will eventually be rewarded. Physically following through with healthy habits like regular exercise, eating nutritious foods, and getting enough sleep will give the individual a sense of accomplishment as they see their efforts pay off in the form of improved energy, strength, and well-being.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Wands tells us to have faith in our long-term investments. This card suggests that rewards and recognition for our hard work may come in the form of financial success. Therefore, it is important to be patient and trust that the investments we have made have been the right ones.

In relationships, the 6 of Wands encourages open communication and creative collaboration with our partners. This card encourages individuals to recognize the dedication and hard work of their partner by offering recognition and praise. This card supports collaboration and partnership, as working together can help make mutual goals a reality. Furthermore, the 6 of Wands suggests that by recognizing one another’s strengths and continually providing support, relationships can grow and strengthen.
