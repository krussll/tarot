A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and Judgment card together in particular.

The Temperance card and Judgment have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and Judgment mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The Judgment tarot card suggest that you should use balance and patience to find clarity and make a definitive decision about your life. They urge you to take control of your behavior, to stay focused and disciplined, and to recognize what your true purpose is. Together, they are a reminder that trusting your intuition and using self-discipline to focus on your life’s goal will lead to ultimate success. They encourage you to stay balanced, be patient, and make wise choices that are not rash or impulsive.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Temperance and Judgment mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Temperance and Judgment the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health:

The Temperance Tarot card symbolizes the importance of balance and harmony in the pursuit of good health. This card encourages us to take a mindful approach to our health, understanding that while it is important to push ourselves physically and mentally, it is also important to be kind and compassionate to ourselves and practice moderation. The Temperance Tarot card signifies that it is essential to be in tune with our physical and mental needs and recognize when it is time to take a break and rest. When we take the time to ensure we are properly nourished, hydrated and getting enough sleep, our bodies and minds are better able to cope with stress and challenges.

Finances:

The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to strive for balance and harmony when it comes to our finances. This card suggests that the key to managing our finances is to find a balance between spending and saving. We should strive to save for our future, but also to enjoy the present. We should also recognize when it is time to take a step back and reassess our spending habits, and consider ways to cut back or find additional sources of income if we are feeling stressed by our finances.

Relationships:

The Temperance Tarot card urges us to remember the importance of balance in our relationships. We should strive to maintain a sense of harmony and understanding between ourselves and our partners. This card suggests that we should practice patience and give each other time and space in order to foster healthy relationships. We should also strive to be mindful of our actions and words, making sure that we are not taking the other person for granted and that we are treating them with kindness and compassion. This card encourages us to strive for harmony in our relationships and to remember the importance of compromise.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health  
The Judgment Tarot card indicates that it’s time for you to take control of your health. It’s time to make a decision about how to move forward in regards to your well-being. You may need to examine your habits, dietary choices, and lifestyle to determine the best path for you to take. It’s a time of intense reflection and often requires a willingness to make changes. It’s a time to take responsibility for our own health needs and make a commitment to take better care of ourselves.

Finances  
When the Judgment Tarot card appears, it’s time to take a more critical look at your finances. You’re being asked to reflect on your financial decisions, past and present, and make a plan for how to best continue with your money. It’s time to examine where you’ve been and make a commitment to take action, whether that be to pay down debt, save money, or invest in something that will increase your wealth. You’re being called to be financially responsible and accountable for where you’re at today and where you want to be tomorrow.

Relationships  
The Judgment Tarot card is urging you to re-evaluate your relationships, both past and present. It’s a call to reflect on the dynamics of your relationships and decide if any changes need to be made. This could mean making the tough decision to let go of unhealthy relationships or taking a look at how you can improve existing relationships. It’s also a reminder to take responsibility for your own actions and how they impact others. The Judgment Tarot card is a call to be honest, open and real with yourself and those around you.
