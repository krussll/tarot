A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 7 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 7 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

When it comes to a successful outcome, both The Sun tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card tell us to not give up and to keep pushing forward. The Sun card is a sign of material happiness and joyous success, while the 7 Of Pentacles shows that we must be patient and work hard in order to achieve our desires. Together, they are a powerful reminder that while we may have to wait and labor, our efforts will eventually be rewarded with the joy and success we strive for.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Sun and 7 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The Sun Tarot card indicates that good health and abundant energy levels are in your future. Its bright, warm rays symbolize vitality, growth, and the potential for healing. You may see your physical and mental health improve, as well as an increase in your confidence. Your relationships could also benefit from the Sun’s light and energy, with communication and understanding becoming second nature.

When it comes to finances, the Sun Tarot card can bring stability and security. You may find abundance and success with your endeavors. This card also encourages you to make investments that can increase your wealth, as well as set aside money for the future. If you’re already financially secure, this card may indicate new opportunities to increase your fortune.

In relationships, the Sun Tarot card symbolizes joy and lasting happiness. You may find that the bond between you and your partner strengthens, or you may reconnect with someone from your past. The Sun card can bring light and warmth to any type of relationship, from friendships to family ties. With the positive energy of the Sun on your side, you can expect happy, harmonious relationships for the foreseeable future.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card serves as a reminder that when it comes to one’s health, the path to success requires patience and hard work. Success cannot be reached overnight, and instead requires learning and investment of time and effort. This card encourages one to take the time to develop a comprehensive and informed plan of action when it comes to their health, and continually review their intentions, progress and results. The 7 of Pentacles encourages us to be patient and consistent with our health, understanding that it takes time for results to be achieved.

For Finances:

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card encourages one to be patient and consistent when it comes to their finances. It emphasizes the importance of developing a comprehensive and informed plan of action when it comes to one’s finances and continually reviewing its effects. The 7 of Pentacles reminds us that achieving financial success requires hard work and effort; a great deal of time may pass before changes to one’s financial landscape can be seen. It encourages us to continue to invest in ourselves, be patient and consistent, and take advantage of the opportunities available to us.

For Relationships:

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card is a reminder that successful relationships require investment, effort and hard work. This card encourages us to invest in our relationships, both with our significant others as well as our friendships, by taking the time to understand our goals and intentions and working together to make them a reality. The 7 of Pentacles serves as a reminder that relationships take time to develop and that our efforts may not be fully seen until much later. This card encourages us to stay patient and consistent with our relationships, understanding that it takes time for changes to manifest.
