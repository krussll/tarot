A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 5 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 5 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The two tarot cards, The 2 Of Wands and The 5 Of Swords, can be interpreted to suggest that you need to make a choice in order to move forward, but that you may be experiencing difficulties in doing so. The 2 Of Wands can indicate that you need to travel or explore different options before you make a decision, whereas The 5 Of Swords suggests that there might be obstacles in your way, such as bullies or abusive relationships. In this case, it could be wise to take your time and assess the situation carefully to be sure that you make the right choice and avoid any potential harm.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

**Both 2 Of Wands and 5 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Wands and 5 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 5 of Swords Tarot card can represent health issues that arise due to physical or emotional bullying, theft, violence or abuse. Physical bullying, for example, can lead to physical injuries, strained muscles, fatigue, and stress. Emotional bullying can lead to issues such as depression, anxiety, self-esteem issues, and a feeling of being overwhelmed. Theft can also be unhealthy if it leads to too much worrying or an inability to trust. Violence can lead to psychological trauma and physical injuries. Abusive relationships can lead to both physical and emotional harm, as well as an overall lack of well-being and feeling safe.

Finances

The 5 of Swords Tarot card can also represent financial issues that arise due to theft, bullying, violence, or abuse. Theft can lead to financial difficulties as money, jewelry, and other items of value can be taken. Bullying can lead to loss of jobs, money, and status in the community. Violence may cause physical damage to property and can also lead to financial losses due to medical expenses, lost time from work, and legal fees. Abuse can lead to financial instability, as the victim may have to leave their job or home in order to be safe, which can create a lack of money or income.

Relationships

The 5 of Swords Tarot card can also represent relationship issues due to bullying, theft, violence, or abuse. Bullying can lead to broken relationships with family, friends, or coworkers due to the fear and anxiety it can cause. Theft can lead to broken trust between friends or family members, or even between couples. Violence can leave long-lasting scars, both physical and emotional, making it difficult for relationships to be healthy and functioning. Abusive relationships can be very damaging, leading to feelings of shame, guilt, and mistrust – all of which can be hard to recover from.
