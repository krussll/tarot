## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is an incredibly powerful card in the deck, symbolizing good fortune, destiny, and a shift in luck. This card indicates that a person is at a crucial crossroads in their lives, an opportunity for transformation and growth. It can be thought of as the “beginning of the end” – with the potential for things to become much better, or for things to take a more difficult road.

When it comes to health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card speaks of cycles and changes. It can be indicative of a period of poor health coming to an end and a period of good health beginning. It can also represent a healing change in diet and lifestyle, such as more exercise or better nutrition. This can be a good time to start taking better care of oneself, as well as to seek out the help of medical professionals when needed.

When it comes to finances, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be a sign of luck, a turning point in financial abundance. It can represent a shift in wealth or the winning of a luck-based game, such as the lottery. It can also suggest a change in attitude or outlook when it comes to money, such as a desire to save and invest or to take risks that might pay off in the long run.

In relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card often points to changes in existing partnerships or the forging of new ones. It can bring a dream relationship into reality, or it can bring about a more positive attitude in existing relationships. It can also indicate a period of discernment, allowing for an individual to decide which path is best for him or her. It is an encouraging card, suggesting that no matter what the outcome, there will be positive movement in the near future.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card represents the deep inner turmoil of mental anguish, pain, and suffering. It is symbolic of nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression. It is a sign of distress and can reveal problems in the realm of health, finances, and relationships.

Health: The 9 of Swords governs the realm of mental health, and it can be a warning sign of a deeper psychological problem if it appears in a reading. When it appears in a reading, it may be telling you that it is time to take care of yourself and seek professional help. Those who are struggling with mental health issues, such as depression, anxiety, or intrusive thoughts should pay attention to this card.

Finances: The 9 of Swords can indicate financial worries and a fear of money issues. This card can be a sign of instability in finances, of debts, of fear of losing material possessions, or a fear of not having enough money. It can be a warning sign to be mindful of how you are managing your money, and to take a practical approach to financial concerns.

Relationships: When the 9 of Swords appears in a reading, it can be a warning sign that there may be distress in a relationship. In some cases, it can also be a sign of internal torment, with the individual feeling trapped and unable to escape the situation. It can also be a sign of communication breakdowns, where discussions are not productive and emotions are high. It is important to deal with issues head-on and not allow the situation to escalate further.

The 9 of Swords is a reminder to take care of your mental health, take a proactive approach to finances, and to address relationship issues before they become unmanageable. Taking the necessary steps to manage these issues can help you build a strong supportive network and a more balanced life.
