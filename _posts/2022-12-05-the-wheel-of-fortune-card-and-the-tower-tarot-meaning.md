A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Wheel Of Fortune card and The Tower card together in particular.

The The Wheel Of Fortune card and The Tower have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune and The Tower mean together?

The Wheel of Fortune tarot card and The Tower tarot card mean that an unexpected turning point is coming your way. This could be in your favor or it could result in some unwanted change. You may be faced with a situation that could bring either luck or misfortune and the outcome could be beyond your control. It is a reminder to prepare for a sudden shift in your circumstances, as the luck of the Wheel of Fortune could bring ruin and upheaval just as easily as it could bring fortune.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is an archetypal representation of a turning point, luck, and good fortune. For it is in these three areas of life that its presence can have an especially strong meaning.

When it comes to health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card could signify a number of things. It could represent the progress of a long-term illness or the sudden diagnosis of a serious health issue. It might also suggest a sudden physical transformation such as a dramatic weight loss or gain. Health issues can also manifest in emotional terms, such as sudden and unexpected changes in mood or mental wellness.

On the financial front, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can imply a fortunate turn of events. It could mean a sudden windfall of money, the purchase or acquisition of valuable assets, or a major shift in an economic situation. It could also represent a sudden change in career or source of income, such as winning a lottery, landing a dream job, or finding a new way to generate income.

In the realm of relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can signify a major shift in a current relationship, such as a break-up or the start of a new love affair. It could also symbolize a new and unexpected friendship, or a chance encounter with a soul mate. It could also denote a fortunate meeting or reunion with someone important to the querent, such as a family member, old friend, or lost love.

This card is brandished with the message that the fates will align with our favor, if we only approach it with a humble and open heart. It may allude to the exciting idea that anything is possible if we are willing to work for it, as The Wheel of Fortune’s spinning destinations will never stop turning.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card is usually a sign that life’s foundations are crumbling and are about to be completely destroyed. This can have serious implications for one’s health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the Tower card typically signals a period of illness, injury or debilitation that causes a person to suffer from intense physical pain, shock and exhaustion. It can also indicate a time of significant trauma or major life changes that have profound physical and psychological effects on the individual. Long-term health conditions are also often associated with this card, as it can reflect a period of intense stress and disruption that can create health issues such as depression, anxiety and chronic pain.

For finances, the Tower card usually suggests considerable financial loss or upheaval. Major investments may fail, or debt may accumulate, causing a person to suffer a serious financial setback that can shake the foundations of their financial security. Bankruptcy or radical changes in employment or home circumstances could also become a reality at this time, making it hard to regain financial stability.

When it comes to relationships, the Tower card can be quite destructive as it can signal a period of intense turmoil, confusion and destruction. Divorce, affairs and cheating are all possibilities, and it is not uncommon for relationships to end due to the extreme upheaval that this card represents. Old patterns of behavior and dynamics may need to be broken and rebuilt from scratch. In order to begin a new cycle of relationships, a person will need to move away from past hurts and learn to forgive and be more open and vulnerable to experience the joys of true connection.
