A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 9 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and 9 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 9 Of Pentacles mean together?

The combination of The Justice tarot card and The 9 of Pentacles tarot card is an indication of the reward one is likely to gain when they do what is right. It suggests that by following a path of fairness, truth and win-win solutions, one can create a luxurious and self-sustaining lifestyle. Even in the most unexpected and difficult of circumstances, the sensible and moral choices one makes can lead to financial gain. This is an encouraging reminder that even in the face of adversity, the truth will ultimately prevail and bring with it an abundance of rewards.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Being able to provide for yourself in a secure and stable way brings a sense of confidence and makes it easier to establish trust in a relationship. On the same token, ensure that you take time for yourself, indulging in solo pleasures every once in a while to maintain your inner happiness and balance.

Financial stability and trust should be an important factor in any relationship, and it’s important to remember to take the time to invest in yourself and feel self-sufficient and fulfilled.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 9 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card is a reminder to take a balanced approach to your physical and mental health. It suggests that finding a balance between work and play is the key to a healthy lifestyle.

When it comes to mental health, it’s important to be honest with yourself about how you’re feeling. This card encourages you to take steps to take care of yourself and find a healthy balance. It could mean seeking professional help, attending therapy sessions, or changing your lifestyle habits.

For Finances

The Justice Tarot card can be a reminder to take a practical approach to your finances. It suggests taking the time to make a financial plan, setting goals, and researching the best ways to save and invest. When it comes to spending, it is a reminder to question whether or not it is necessary and to think of the long-term effects.

The Justice Tarot card encourages respectful and honest communication when it comes to money. It suggests that you take responsibility for your finances, and to be honest with yourself and others when it comes to understanding the ins and outs of budgeting.

For Relationships

The Justice Tarot card encourages us to take a balanced approach when it comes to our relationships. It could mean taking the time to listen, compromise, and be honest with the other person. We should recognize the importance of healthy communication and be willing to make changes if necessary.

Taking a balanced approach to relationships includes being honest about your feelings and opinions and respecting the opinions of others. This card also encourages us to let go of past issues and move on to create healthier relationships with those around us. It is important to not let personal pride get in the way of healthy relationships.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card signifies a state of financial security, security, and comfort. It is an indication of your well-being and that you are in a place where you are comfortable and at ease with your finances. This card suggests that you have reached the point of self-sufficiency and financial stability, and can now enjoy the pleasures and luxuries that come with financial freedom.

When it comes to health, the 9 of Pentacles suggests that you are in a stable state of well-being and that you should relax and enjoy the benefits of your good health. The card encourages you to take the time to appreciate the physical and mental rewards of healthy living.

In terms of your finances, The 9 of Pentacles implies that you are in a strong financial position.You have been wise with your money and it has paid off. This is a time to focus on your financial goals and to take the steps necessary to build toward a more solid financial future.

The 9 of Pentacles also indicates that you have a sense of inner-contentment and self-sufficiency in your relationships. It suggests that you are comfortable on your own, allowing you to make the best decisions for yourself and for the relationships that you are in. This card also suggests that you are independent and can provide yourself with the security and comfort that comes with financial stability.

Overall, the 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of financial stability in all aspects of life. It shows that you are in a strong position and are able to enjoy the comforts and luxuries that come with financial success. It also suggests that your health and relationships are in a good place and the security you have gained will allow you to take care of yourself and the people you care about.
