## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 7 of Cups Tarot card is a card that represents too many choices and commitment issues. In terms of health, this card suggests that one can be feeling overwhelmed with too many choices and options, causing one to become indecisive and unable to commit to a lifestyle change that could potentially improve their health and wellbeing. It can also signify that one is avoiding or procrastinating making changes for their health, due to personal reasons such as lack of motivation, fear of failure, or uncertainty about which path to take. Ultimately, it challenges us to take a step back, to assess our current health situation, and to make one definitive choice that will move us forward.

Finances

The 7 of Cups Tarot card may also have an impact on one’s finances, as it can represent too many options and an inability to commit to one of them. It speaks of decision paralysis, where one may be presented with a number of different options but is unable to make a choice. This can lead to missed financial opportunities or not making the most of the money available. To avoid missing out and to take control of one’s finances, it is important to take a step back and assess one’s financial situation and make a definitive choice that will move one forward.

Relationships

In terms of relationships, The 7 of Cups Tarot card can represent an individual who has commitment issues and struggles to make a definitive decision about their relationships. It can speak of one’s fear of making a commitment and the fear of being hurt or let down. In order to move forward, it is important to look at one’s current relationship situation and make one definitive choice that will move them forward. This can involve taking a step back to assess the relationship and the person, and deciding if the relationship is worth investing in and if it is worth committing to.
