A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The 8 of Pentacles tarot card and the Queen of Swords tarot card signify a need to look inwards and focus on your personal development and honing your skills. The 8 of Pentacles speaks to mastering one’s craft and enjoying the process of learning, while the Queen of Swords brings forth her message of total honesty and self-sufficiency. Together, these cards portend that you should be proud of your hard work and be willing to be open and honest with yourself about your goals, ambitions, and drive—allowing you to shoot for the stars without getting bogged down by distractions. Through focus, hard work and determination you can create a life that is filled with success and joy.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 8 Of Pentacles and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is a fantastic card for understanding how to achieve mastery in any area of life, from health, to finances, to relationships.

For health, this card means taking charge of your body and becoming an expert in understanding how it works and how to take care of it. Doing the research, following through on the action necessary for peak performance – whatever that may look like for you – and staying dedicated to the process. It’s about understanding the value of taking your time and investing in yourself, rather than making rash decisions that can quickly become costly.

For finances, The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is all about taking charge of your finances and creating a good system to manage your finances. That could be as simple as budgeting your expenses and making sure you’re maximizing the value of your income. Or, it could mean investing time into learning about money management, investing, and creating streams of passive income. This card asks us to stay dedicated to the process, even if it’s slow going at first.

For relationships, The 8 of Pentacles is all about investing in your relationships and taking the time to understand the other person, as well as yourself. It’s about being patient and understanding that relationships don’t always move quickly, but rather are an investment with long term returns. It’s about having an open mind to learning and growing together, and understanding that you may have different points of view to the other person. It’s ultimately about mastering the art of relationships, which takes time, effort and dedication.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card signifies taking charge of one’s physical and mental health. It encourages us to take a more proactive role in our health, to take the responsibility to nourish ourselves, prioritize exercise, and to be honest about the current state of our health. The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages us to be proactive and honest about health issues and to seek out necessary treatments or therapies.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card represents being financially independent. This means being honest and making prudent choices in regards to our spending and investments. It also encourages us to be mindful of our finances and practice fiscal responsibility. We should be mindful of budgeting, avoiding debt, and striving to have multiple streams of income.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages us to be honest and authentic in our relationships. We should be open and honest with our partners, friends, and family, and practice boundaries and healthy communication. We should be mindful of other’s emotions and be open to any constructive criticism or advice. The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages us to be mindful and authentic in our relationships, and to be honest about our feelings and expectations.
