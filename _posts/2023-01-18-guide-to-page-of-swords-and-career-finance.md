Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The Page Of Swords card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Page Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Page Of Swords.

## Page Of Swords And Career And Finances – An Overview

The Page Of Swords tarot card is a reminder to take care of your physical and mental health.

This card speaks to being mindful of gossip, spies and prying eyes, which could mean being careful with the information you are sharing, both online and in person.

It also speaks to being honest with yourself and others – be honest about what you feel and what you need in order to be healthy.

Taking time for yourself and looking after your wellbeing is essential.

The Page Of Swords encourages you to be mindful of the energy you are putting into your body and environment and to seek out support if needed.

## Upright Meanings For Page Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The Page of Swords tarot card speaks to the importance of gathering information, asking questions, and staying vigilant about your career and finances.

This card encourages you to do your research, ask hard questions, and look out for yourself.

It warns that there may be spies, gossips, or prying eyes trying to get in the way of your success.

This card encourages you to remain vigilant and stay focused on the truth.

With dedication and hard work, you will find the success you are looking for in your career and finances.

### What It Means For The Future

The Page Of Swords tarot card is a sign that your future career and finances could be subject to a lot of scrutiny and gossip.

You may find that you are constantly being questioned and judged, and you must be willing to stand up for the truth in order to succeed.

Your career may require you to be particularly observant and analytical, as you will need to be able to pick up on subtle conversations, detect lies, and uncover secrets.

It is important that you pay attention to every detail, as this will help you to make the best decisions and protect your financial interests.

The Page Of Swords suggests that you should think before you act and be sure to speak the truth, even if it is unpopular.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Page of Swords meaning suggests that those close to you may have to be very careful with their careers and finances.

This card is associated with gossip, spies, and prying eyes, so those close to you may have to be vigilant about protecting their financial and professional interests.

They should be aware of the potential for people to be talking about their finances, or even spying on their actions, and be sure to take measures to protect their privacy and security.

Additionally, the Page of Swords is a card that is associated with truth, so those close to you should be honest and straightforward in their dealings and avoid any sort of deception.

With these precautions in mind, they should be able to effectively manage their finances and careers.

## Reversed Meanings For Page Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When the reversed Page of Swords appears in a tarot reading regarding one’s career and finances, it can be a sign that the querent is being hindered by gossip, secrets, and unreliable sources of information.

This card can indicate that the querent is being bogged down by too much information, which can lead to feelings of confusion and lack of direction.

Alternatively, the reversed Page of Swords can suggest that the querent is unaware of certain facts or is relying on information that is not entirely accurate.

The reversed Page of Swords warns of the dangers of being too trusting.

The querent should be wary of people who may be acting out of their own interests and not necessarily in the querent’s best interest.

It is important to stay focused and trust one’s own judgment when it comes to career and financial decisions.

The reversed Page of Swords can also suggest that the querent might be too focused on the details or too busy with external tasks that they are neglecting the bigger picture.

It is important to step back, take stock of the situation, and determine what is most important.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Page of Swords is reversed in a Tarot reading, it can mean that I may be heading down a path of financial hardship.

I may find myself in difficult situations that require careful decision-making and strategy in order to navigate.

I may be easily influenced by others, or too eager to please, which could lead to financial difficulties.

If I am not careful, I could be taken advantage of or easily manipulated.

On the other hand, the reversed Page of Swords can also represent an opportunity for me to learn from my mistakes and to use my intelligence and wit to create a more secure financial future.

I may find that I have a knack for spotting opportunities and understanding the best way to capitalize on them.

Through careful decision-making and strategy, I can use my intuition to find solutions that will lead to financial security.

As I gain more experience and knowledge, I can increase my chances of success and stability.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed The Page Of Swords appears in a Tarot reading, it is a sign that those close to you may be dealing with some financial and career obstacles.

This card can indicate that a person may be involved in gossip, spies, or prying eyes that are preventing them from succeeding.

It can also suggest that they are being held back by their own lack of enthusiasm, or that they are not utilizing all of their potential.

It could also indicate that they are not paying attention to the truth, and that they need to look deeper into the facts before making any decisions.

In regards to finances and careers, the reversed The Page Of Swords can point to a lack of focus, and many opportunities being overlooked.

Those close to you may need to focus on one particular area and pursue it wholeheartedly, instead of trying to do too much and spreading themselves too thin.

It could also indicate a tendency to rely too heavily on outside sources for advice, when the best advice comes from within.

They need to trust their own intuition and make decisions based on the facts, instead of relying on the opinions of others.
