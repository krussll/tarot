A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Queen Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The Queen of Pentacles and the Three of Swords represent a dichotomy of both positivity and negativity in life. On one hand, the Queen of Pentacles is associated with resourcefulness, fertility and domesticity, which can be a source of security, comfort, and growth. On the other hand, the Three of Swords symbolizes heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression, and surgery, emphasizing the negatives of life. Overall, these two cards represent the balance of both positives and negatives that life may throw at you, and a reminder to stay strong and resourceful in times of adversity.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Queen Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Queen Of Pentacles and The 3 Of Swords together in your love life could mean that you are feeling a deep heartache and depression due to a loss. Whether this is an actual physical loss, such as the end of a relationship or a divorce, or a metaphorical one, such as a job loss, the Queen Of Pentacles is here to remind you that there is always a resourceful way through the pain. The Queen of Pentacles is the embodiment of resourcefulness and fertility, and encourages you to embrace those qualities to create a better life for yourself.

At the same time, the 3 Of Swords reminds you that whatever your loss is, it is still valid and should be processed and respected. It is important to take the time to feel your pain, to identify what it is that you need to grieve, and to make room for your emotions. Taking care of yourself by talking to a friend or a therapist, or simply allowing yourself to feel whatever it is that you’re feeling is an important step in the healing process.

Though your situation may feel overwhelming, The Queen Of Pentacles is here to remind you that you can and will make it through this difficult time. The resourceful energy of the Queen of Pentacles will help you to find creative solutions to the challenges you are facing, and to create a positive future for yourself. It may take time, but with persistence and resourcefulness, you will be able to find a new way to go forward in life.

On the job front, this combination can signify a difficult time. It could mean that you’re having to make a painful decision to leave a position or that you’re dealing with a difficult colleague who’s putting a strain on your job satisfaction. It may even be a sign that the job you’re currently in isn’t the right fit for you and that it’s time to make a change. You will have to weigh the pros and cons of each situation carefully in order to make the best decision for your future.

Ultimately, it’s important to be mindful of the fact that The Queen of Pentacles and The 3 Of Swords tarot card combination can mean difficult times. Take the time to evaluate your current financial and job situation, take a step back, and make the best decisions you can for yourself. Don’t let emotions or stubbornness overcome good judgement and don’t let financial hardship define the rest of your journey. With the right perspective and careful planning, you can still turn these times around and come out stronger in the end.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles mean?

Health  
The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of health and wellbeing. It shows that you are taking care of yourself and your body, and focusing on maintaining a strong and healthy lifestyle. This card is a reminder that you should make sure to take care of yourself, both physically and mentally. Eating healthy, exercising regularly and getting adequate sleep are all important factors in keeping yourself in good health. This card also encourages you to nurture yourself and enjoy the simple pleasures in life, as this can also contribute to your overall wellbeing.

Finances  
The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of financial stability and prosperity. It suggests that you are in control of your finances and have a clear understanding of how to budget and save. This card is a reminder to remain diligent and disciplined when it comes to managing your money. It also encourages you to focus on long-term strategies to ensure your financial security, such as building an emergency fund and investing for the future.

Relationships  
The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of relationships that are built on trust, loyalty, and a deep understanding of one another. It encourages going beyond physical and material connections and cultivating emotional intimacy. This card is a reminder to nurture and protect your relationships, and to provide support to your loved ones. It also suggests that you should be aware of the needs of your relationships and be willing to compromise and make sacrifices in order to maintain a healthy and balanced relationship.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is a card of sorrow and loss, symbolizing heartbreak, divorce, depression, and surgical operations. The imagery of three swords piercing a broken heart symbolizes the pain of separation and loss. In terms of health, the 3 of Swords Tarot card illustrates the physical and emotional pain resulting from the loss of a loved one. It is indicative of illness, depression, surgery, and the difficulties these can bring to the individual’s life.

The 3 of Swords Tarot card brings financial difficulties, as this card shows a lack of wealth or resources. When it comes to relationships, the 3 of Swords Tarot card holds a warning that existing relationships may be in danger of breaking down due to hostility, misunderstandings, and resentments. This card also suggests that new partnerships may not be able to provide the stability and security that is desired, and the potential for heartbreak is high.

The 3 of Swords Tarot card suggests that any kind of loss or separation will be a difficult and emotionally draining experience. The best advice for a person receiving this card is to be mindful of their emotions and take time to reflect and heal after a break-up or loss of a loved one. It is also important to be aware of how these losses can affect physical and mental health and to take steps to seek professional guidance when necessary. The card also signifies the need to be open to new relationships, however, caution is advised, as the potential for disruption of a future relationship is strong.
