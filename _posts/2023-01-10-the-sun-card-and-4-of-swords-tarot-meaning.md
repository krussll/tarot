However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Sun card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The Sun tarot card combined with the 4 Of Swords tarot card can emphasize the importance of taking a break and finding joy in the small things. It can mean that it is time to take a step back and rest in order to achieve the happiness, success, and joy that you need. Through periods of solitude, rest, and renewal, you can come back stronger and ready to tackle anything that comes your way. Keeping a balance between hard work and rest will ensure that you are able to enjoy the successes that come your way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

When The Sun Tarot card appears, it usually signifies material happiness, hopefulness and joyous outcomes. This card is generally associated with positive energy and good news. In terms of health, The Sun Tarot card can indicate that you’re likely to be in good physical and mental health and that healing or treatment are likely to be successful. This card can be a reminder that physical and mental well-being are linked, and that taking care of yourself is important.

In terms of finances, The Sun Tarot card can often be a sign of good fortune, prosperity and success. This could be in terms of income, investments, or even a financial windfall. This card can also be an indication that you’ll be able to manage your money well and make wise decisions.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Sun Tarot card often symbolizes joyous and positive experiences with those you care about. This card can indicate that relationships will be filled with love, light and happiness. It could be a sign that you and your partner are ready to take the next step in your relationship, or even a sign that a new relationship is on the horizon. It can also signify that your friendships are strong, and that you’ll enjoy many wonderful experiences together.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is an indication of the need for rest, respite and recovery from physical, mental, and emotional stress. This card is a reminder that we all need time away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life to rest, restore and recharge. Taking time away from your usual daily routine can be beneficial for both your physical and emotional health. Dedicating time just for yourself each day can help to restore balance and clarity, enabling you to gain perspective on any health issues that you may be facing.

Finances

When it comes to finances, the 4 of Swords Tarot card suggests taking a step back and considering your financial situation. Take a break and evaluate your current financial situation and goals. Consider ways to save or invest money, and look for opportunities to increase your income. This card encourages you to take responsibility for your finances, think carefully before making purchases, and plan for your financial future. It can also be a reminder to take the necessary steps to protect your finances, such as creating a budget and building an emergency fund.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card can be an indication of the necessity for a break from relationships, in order to rest and restore both yourself and your connections. This card can be an opportunity to focus on yourself and build up your own strength and security, so that you can approach relationships more confidently. It can also be a reminder to reassess your current relationships, and consider whether they are causing stress and unhappiness in your life. If so, then it can be beneficial to take a step back and reflect on ways that you can create healthier, stronger connections.
