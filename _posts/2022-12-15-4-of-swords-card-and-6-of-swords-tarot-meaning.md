A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords mean together?

Taken together, the 4 Of Swords and the 6 Of Swords can be seen as a call to retreat, rest and refuel before beginning a necessary transition. In the face of pressure and change, the 4 Of Swords encourages a period of rest and renewal to be enjoyed in solitude, allowing us to recover our strength and recharge our batteries. The 6 Of Swords suggests that a change of scenery or a relocation journey is needed to help us make the transition needed to move on, allowing us to face what awaits us with courage and renewed vigour.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 4 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The 4 Of Swords and The 6 Of Swords cards appear together in a love reading, it is a message that you need to take some time for yourself before charging in blindly with a relationship. This is a call to retreat and regroup, to take some time for yourself and reflect.

The 4 Of Swords card encourages you to take time out and treat yourself with kindness, to take time to heal and renew your energy before taking a step forward. This card indicates that you may need to take a break from the relationship to assess the situation, to give yourself a chance to think and be alone.

The 6 Of Swords card, on the other hand, is a card of transition and movement. This card asks you to consider the possibility that it may be time to move on. It is possible that the relationship is coming to an end and it is time to make a change and begin anew. The 6 Of Swords is warning you to be willing to take a risk, to take a chance on something new and exciting.

All in all, taken together, these two cards indicate that you should take some time for yourself to reflect, to heal, and to make sure you are ready for the next step. If the transition is necessary, then the 6 Of Swords encourages you to be brave and move on. This can be a difficult decision, but ultimately it is an important one, as it will set you up for a more fulfilling, healthy relationship in the future.

### What does 4 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When you combine the meanings of The 4 of Swords and The 6 of Swords tarot cards, the message could be interpreted as a need for a period of rest and renewal before taking on a new financial challenge. The 4 of Swords encourages you to step away from any commitments or obligations that are causing you stress and take a break for the sake of your mental and physical health. This self-care may feel like a retreat from the world, but it is essential to your well-being and could energise you for the next part of the journey.

The 6 of Swords follows the 4 of Swords, symbolising a necessary transition that may involve moving to a new place or taking on a new job opportunity. The journey may feel daunting, but it is a necessary step in the right direction. This card encourages you to take stock of what you are leaving behind and to trust that the change is necessary. It is important to remember that rest and recovery is just as important as taking on new challenges.

The combination of these cards advises that your finances and job will benefit from taking a break and taking the time to rest and restore your energy before you move forward. It is important to take the time to properly assess the changes you are considering making in order to ensure you are moving in the right direction. This period of rest and renewal will help ensure your positive transition into the next stage of your life.

### What does 4 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The 4 Of Swords and the 6 Of Swords in combination for health signify the need for a temporary retreat, rest and renewal in order to better your physical and mental well-being. This could mean taking some time away from the demands of everyday life, dedicating yourself to relaxation, reconnecting with nature and taking part in activities that bring joy and peace to your heart. Whether it’s a nap, a yoga class, a massage, a day at the beach, a weekend away or just a simple walk in the park, taking time to recharge and re-energize can improve your physical and emotional wellbeing.

The 6 Of Swords also points to a need for transition, change, and relocation. This could be literal in the sense of moving to another home or city, but more likely refers to creating a shift in lifestyle and attitude. It could be something as simple as adjusting the way you view stressful situations, learning to recognize and accept difficult emotions, or finding healthier ways to cope. Making changes to your routines, decluttering your home, or adding regular exercise to your life can all promote healing and growth.

In sum, The 4 Of Swords and The 6 Of Swords signify the importance of taking a break in order to restore your energy and make necessary changes that will result in a healthier outlook and lifestyle moving forward. By embracing rest and solitude, finding ways to heal and nurture yourself, and transitioning to a more balanced life, you will be able to revive your spirit, restore your health, and find a renewed sense of wellbeing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 4 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 4 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

The 4 Of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to all of us to take a break and give ourselves a much needed break from the hustle and bustle of life. It is symbolizing that it is time for you to retreat and take a much needed rest for your physical, emotional and mental well-being.

Health:  
The 4 Of Swords Tarot card encourages us to take time for ourselves to heal and benefit from the time during which we can take a break from our everyday worries. It encourages us to take a break from our daily routine, to get enough rest, to nourish our body with the right nutrition and to get enough physical exercise. It also reminds us to take time for ourselves in the form of relaxation and meditation.

Finances:  
The 4 Of Swords Tarot card is also a reminder to take a break from our financial stresses and worries. It encourages us to take a step back and reassess our financial situation, to make sure we’re spending and saving wisely, and that our goals and budget are still aligned with our needs and desires. Taking a break from our financial worries can help us gain clarity and create realistic and achievable goals.

Relationships:  
The 4 Of Swords Tarot card is also a reminder to take a break from any toxic relationships we may be experiencing. It encourages us to take a step back and reassess our relationships to make sure we’re getting the love and support we need from the people we’re in relationships with. It’s also a reminder to make sure our relationships are healthy and that we’re not being used or taken advantage of. Taking a break from unhealthy relationships can help us gain clarity and create healthier relationships in the future.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card often symbolizes a necessary transition in one’s life. In terms of health, it suggests that a person may be moving from a state of ill health to improved health, either through changes in lifestyle or through medical treatment. It could also suggest a transition from an unhealthy state of mind or emotions to improved mental and emotional well-being.

When it comes to finances, the 6 Of Swords Tarot card indicates a change in current financial circumstances for the better. This could manifest in the form of an injection of funds, an inheritance, or a more secure job that provides stability and better pay. All of these scenarios signal change and higher financial security in the future.

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can also signify a transition or relocation in relationships. This could signal the end of a bad relationship and imply the beginning of a new and healthier one. It could also suggest that an individual is preparing to move forward in their current relationship, or to begin a new relationship. Either way, the transition promises improved relationships and positive future prospects.
