## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card indicates a general feeling of ill health, poverty and worry. It can represent physical, mental and emotional issues which are adversely affecting a person’s life. The person’s financial circumstances may be dire, with mounting bills, debt and the inability to meet their basic needs. This could lead to feelings of despair and hopelessness, as the individual attempts to survive in a world where resources are limited.

In terms of relationships, the 5 Of Pentacles tarot card symbolizes a time of isolation, as the individual grapples with their circumstances and fails to receive adequate support from those close to them. It is difficult for them to maintain any meaningful connections, due to their financial strain, and feelings of guilt and helplessness may arise.

When it comes to finances, the 5 Of Pentacles tarot card emphasizes the worry and stress of having inadequate funds. It may be difficult to find a way out of poverty and paying back debts can be overwhelming. It may be necessary to identify and investigate new sources of income, such as obtaining grants or loans, in order to make ends meet.

The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card provides an indication that some challenges may need to be overcome in order to move forwards, and progress is possible with diligent effort and the support of those around us. It is important to acknowledge the difficulty of the situation and to take practical steps towards improving it.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords Tarot card is a painful omen when it appears in a reading. It signifies a complete and utter ending, often referred to as “rock bottom”. This is especially true when looking at the card in relation to areas such as health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, the 10 of Swords Tarot card signals a time of complete exhaustion and physical depletion. It can indicate a health crisis or a period of illness that has left you feeling depleted and depleted of energy. This can also be a sign that poor health can result from making bad decisions or engaging in unhealthy habits.

In terms of finances, this card symbolizes devastating losses or a major financial setback. It can represent bankruptcy or the complete loss of all your financial assets. It can also suggest that you have taken on too much financial risk or made some poor financial choices.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the 10 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a complete and utter breakdown in a significant partnership. It can reflect a period of crisis and deep pain that has caused irreparable damage. It can suggest a time of intense loneliness and despair as a result of a failed relationship.

Ultimately, the 10 of Swords Tarot card is a warning about the danger of rock bottom. It tells us to be mindful of our decisions and to seek a better path forward. It also serves as a reminder that sometimes endings are the only way for something new to begin.
