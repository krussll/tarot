However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The Death card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and King Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Death card and The King Of Cups in your reading highlights the need for transformation and the importance of finding an understanding guide as you begin to move through a period of change. It’s a reminder that endings, no matter how difficult, can often lead to new beginnings. The King Of Cups speaks of a leader who is supportive and tolerant, who can offer empathy and compassion to those going through a difficult time. Use their wise guidance and understanding to help you cope with, and adjust to, the many changes life throws at you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Death and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Death and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health:  
The Death card can indicate a major change in one’s health. This card can indicate a need for a dramatic lifestyle change, such as quitting smoking or losing weight. It can also represent a physical health issue such as a chronic illness or a serious medical diagnosis. The card may signify a need to accept the inevitability of mortality and to cherish life, recognizing that it is a precious gift. The Death card can also represent healing and recovery, but it is important to take the process of healing seriously, as it is not a quick fix but a long-term endeavor.

Finances:  
The Death card can indicate financial changes and the need to take responsibility for one’s financial wellbeing. It can mean taking a hard look at one’s finances to assess where money is going, and to make some changes. It can also represent a need to let go of any financial problems that may be weighing one down, and to create a new budget and plan for the future. This can be a difficult process but one that can ultimately bring security and stability.

Relationships:  
The Death card can indicate a major shift in relationships. It can signify an ending of a relationship or indicate the need to take a break or have a hard look at the relationship. It can also mean that a relationship is no longer serving its purpose and that it is time to move on. The Death card can also signify a new beginning in relationships, as it can represent a willingness to let go of any hurt or pain that may be obstructing the relationship. It encourages one to accept the natural cycle of relationships and be open to the possibility of transforming them into something new and better.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health

The King of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of good health, physical and mental. This card reveals a deep understanding and empathy in regards to our health and well-being. It encourages us to practice self-care and to seek out medical advice when necessary. Of course, it is also important to practice mindfulness and moderation. The King of Cups encourages us to take care of our body and mind so that we can feel our best.

Finances

The King of Cups Tarot card symbolizes financial security, pragmatism and self-discipline when it comes to money matters. The figure on this card also represents a sense of generosity and an understanding of others’ financial needs. This card can be seen as a reminder to practice effective budgeting and money management in order to build a secure financial future.

Relationships

The King of Cups Tarot card encourages us to be tolerant and empathetic in our relationships. It suggests that we take the time to truly listen to our partners and to be willing to compromise. The King of Cups encourages us to be compassionate and understanding of each other, while also respecting our own needs and boundaries. This card can also remind us to practice kindness, especially during difficult times.
