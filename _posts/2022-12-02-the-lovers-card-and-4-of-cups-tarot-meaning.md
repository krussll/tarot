A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 4 Of Cups mean together?

When The Lovers tarot card and The 4 Of Cups card appear together, it suggests that you must make a decision about a relationship or partnership. On one hand, you are deeply in love and feel a strong connection with your partner. On the other hand, there is a sense of apathy and feeling unfulfilled within the relationship. It is important to assess the situation, and make a decision about which path to take. This could be a difficult choice to make, but it is necessary to make sure that both parties are completely satisfied. There may be a surprise on either path, and it is important to remember the strength of two when making a decision.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and 4 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health

The Lovers Tarot card is a symbol of health and wellbeing on every level. It speaks of a deep and profound connection to ourselves and to others. When this card appears, it may be a sign for you to take a step back and evaluate the condition of your health, physically, mentally, and emotionally. With the Lovers Tarot card, it is important to listen to your intuition and your body. Spend time exploring the connections between yourself and your environment, and seek to make a healthy balance between the two. In addition, take time to nourish both your mental and physical states with healthy activities and habits, such as getting enough rest, eating nutritious meals, and engaging in physical activity.

Finances

The Lovers Tarot card can also be associated with affairs of the finances. Here, it represents balance and stability, and the strength and power of two – the power of creating a strong financial structure when dealing with money matters. When you are presented with financial decisions, take the time to consider how the decision will affect both yourself and any partners or team members that you have. You must also remember to take into account all possible outcomes and potential risks. Take a step back and use the power of two to discuss, plan and make the best decisions for both the present and future.

Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card speaks of love, partnership and strong relationships. It is a reminder to take a step back and appreciate the unique connection that you have with those that are important to you. The key is to keep communication open between yourself and your partner or partner(s). Discuss issues, come to an agreement on decisions, and remain loyal and supportive. When this card appears, you may also receive insight into any issues that exist in your relationships. Acknowledge them, express them and work together with your partner(s) to find solutions.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 4 of Cups is a Tarot card that can represent feelings of apathy when it comes to your health. When this card appears, it’s an indication that you’re feeling unfulfilled in your current health situation and may be feeling resigned to your lot in life. It’s important to evaluate how to make changes so that you can fully engage in living a healthy life. Look for small steps and actions that you can take to move in a better direction and get out of the apathy that this card is pointing out.

Finances

The 4 of Cups Tarot card can be a warning when it comes to finances. It reflects a feeling of unfulfilled expectations and a sense of stagnation. You may be feeling like you’ve reached a financial plateau that’s hard to surmount. It’s time to get creative and find new ways to increase your finances. Could you focus more on passive income strategies? Or find a way to increase your income? This card is a reminder that you don’t have to stay stuck in your current financial situation.

Relationships

The 4 of Cups Tarot card suggests surprise gifts in terms of relationships. This card can suggest that you’re not getting the connection, reciprocation, or closeness that you may be looking for in relationships. But look for the unexpected surprise that might come your way in terms of relationships. Maybe you’ll meet someone new, or a current relationship will advance in an unexpected way. This card is a reminder to look for the surprises that life has in store for you.
