A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and Knight Of Wands card together in particular.

The Judgment card and Knight Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and Knight Of Wands mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and The Knight Of Wands tarot card together show that you have a great opportunity to make a final decision that is aligned with your life’s purpose. With the clarity of your purpose and the creativity of an impulsive, courageous and free spirit, you can make a decisive move towards your destiny. The combination of these tarot cards encourages you to let go of the past, to be courageous and to make bold choices in the service of something greater. It is time to answer the wake-up call and start taking action on your life’s purpose.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Judgment and Knight Of Wands mean together for your love life?

Combining The Judgement and The Knight of Wands tarot cards in reference to your love life can be rather revealing. The Judgement card speaks of a wake-up call, a need to become more aware of your life’s purpose, and a final decision to be made. With this card comes a need to be honest with yourself and look to the future. In terms of relationships, The Judgement card may mean that it is time to make a decision about your current love life.

The Knight of Wands speaks of a creative and free-spirited person. This card can reveal that the person in your life is full of energy, enthusiasm and optimism. They have a great deal of passion and a lust for life that is infectious. The Knight of Wands can also signify a sense of adventure, and a desire to explore and experience life in all its forms.

Combined, these two tarot cards speak of a potential relationship. They advise that it is time to take action and to make a decision about your love life. If you are in a relationship, The Judgement card could be urging you to move forward with a commitment. However, The Knight of Wands could be suggesting that you find a relationship that is more adventurous and passionate.

Overall, The Judgment and The Knight of Wands tarot cards suggest that you are being called to take action. It is encouraging you to take the initiative and to move forward with whatever decision you make, no matter if it is to stay as you are, or to seek out a new, more adventurous, relationship.

### What does Judgment and Knight Of Wands mean together for your finances?

When the Judgment card and the Knight of Wands appear together in a reading related to finances and job, it often indicates that you are at a crossroads of making a final decision about your career and financial future. The Judgment card brings clarity and encourages you to seek a deeper understanding of the choices you make and how the decisions you make today will influence the life you live tomorrow. This card brings a wake-up call about the reality of life and it nudges you to become conscious of your responsibilities and investments.

The Knight of Wands tarot card encourages you to channel your creative energy and use your natural talents to achieve your financial goals. This card also shows that you have a strong desire to progress and be successful. The Knight of Wands tells you that it’s ok to take risks, as long as you stay true to your values and use your initiative. This card encourages you to make moves and seize opportunities that may present themselves to you.

When combined, The Judgment card and The Knight of Wands tarot card give you the courage and determination to set yourself a path and move forward with your finances and career. This combination encourages you to be open to novel ideas, explore new opportunities and find ways to use your natural creativity to achieve your goals. Money and security may be a priority for you, but whatever you do, make sure you maintain your integrity and never forget your life’s purpose.

When the tarot card of The Judgment and The Knight Of Wands come together for your health, it’s a strong suggestion for you to do some soul-searching about what you can do to live a healthier lifestyle. It’s a call to gain clarity around what is essential for your wellbeing. What have been your health-related goals or habits that have been successful, and which ones need to be changed? The judgment card suggests it’s time to take responsibility for your health and be aware of how you are feeling mentally, physically and emotionally.

On the other hand, the Knight Of Wands tarot card encourages you to bring some creative juice to the process. Maybe it’s a new exercise routine that makes you excited, a new way to prepare healthy meals, or a way to make time for yourself that you take pleasure in. Additionally, the Knight Of Wands asks you to be generous with yourself – to be kind to your body and give it the care it needs.

Overall, The Judgment and Knight Of Wands tarot cards are a strong call for you to find clarity about what you need to do to improve your health, while also looking for creative and energizing ways to turn those intentions into reality. It’s the perfect combination of strength and balance that will guide you to a healthier life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Judgment and Knight Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Judgment and Knight Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health  
The Judgment Tarot card can help you gain clarity around health issues. It’s a call to reflection on the habits that you have established at all levels of your being – physical, mental, emotional and spiritual. Consider what has been keeping you from achieving optimal health. Ask yourself, “What changes do I need to make in order to take responsibility for my own health and wellness?” The card may also be a sign that you are ready to let go of any unhealthy attachments that have been holding you back from true holistic health.

Finances  
The Judgment Tarot card reveals the importance of making wise decisions when it comes to finances. It’s a calling to look at the current state of your finances and determine how you can best manage your resources. It may be a reminder to look honestly at the habits and behaviors you’ve been engaging in around money. It’s a message that receiving clarity about the financial tools you have available to you can help you make better decisions. Be honest with yourself about the steps you need to take to create a secure financial future.

Relationships  
The Judgment Tarot card can be a sign that it is time to make a decision in a relationship. It may be a message that it is time to take a deep look at the relationships you have and determine what is truly meaningful and valuable to you. You may need to let go of relationships that are no longer working in order to make room for new connections. It could also be a reminder to be mindful of the values that define relationships, such as respect, honesty, and trust. If a relationship is going to thrive and grow, then these values must be present. The Judgment Tarot card is a sign that you can make the best decision for yourself and your relationships.

## What does Knight Of Wands mean?

In terms of health, The Knight of Wands Tarot card often suggests a sense of creative energy towards healing and wellbeing. This card often encourages us to keep our minds and bodies active, take risks and experiment with our health. The Knight of Wands often counsels a positive outlook when it comes to health and the belief that anything is possible if we put in the effort. The energy of this card can spark new possibilities, leading to the exploration of healthier lifestyles, new diets, and exercise regimes.

For finances, The Knight of Wands Tarot card suggests that there is an opportunity to take a risk and push boundaries. This card encourages us to get creative with money management, to explore new ways of looking at finances, and to be inventive in creating wealth. It might also indicate a time of financial change, such as job changes, investments, or starting a new business.

In relationships, The Knight of Wands Tarot card is often representative of a passionate, intense connection. This card often suggests a need to take a risk and be bold in romance. It can also symbolize a level of freedom and independence, encouraging us to take risks, explore our most creative ideas, and free ourselves from inhibitions. With the energy of The Knight of Wands, relationships are bound to be dynamic, exciting, and full of adventure.
