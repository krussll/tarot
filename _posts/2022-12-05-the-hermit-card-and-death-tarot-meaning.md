A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Death card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Death have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Death mean together?

The Hermit and The Death tarot card combination brings a message of inward exploration and a time of transformation. The Hermit encourages going within for clarity, a quest for personal truth, and spiritual illumination. The Death prompts big changes, endings, and a time to move on. Together, this combination suggests that you may be embarking on a journey of self-discovery and personal growth. It is important to stay mindful and open-minded, as the insights you receive during this time can be powerful and life-altering. As you explore yourself and find the spiritual illumination from your inner being, you can shed old parts of yourself and bring about positive transformation that will last for a lifetime.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and Death mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and Death the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

When it comes to health, finances and relationships, this tarot card indicates the pursuit of personal truth in all three aspects of our lives. We may need to move away from the distraction of others in order to properly assess our physical, emotional and financial state. The Hermit card can also signify a retreat from the world and a restoration of physical, mental, and spiritual health.

In regards to health, this card advises us to take some time to go within and focus on our physical and emotional well-being. We may need to take a break from the hustle and bustle of everyday life in order to listen to our body and assess our overall health. The Hermit card can also be a reminder to take care of our bodies through exercise, proper nutrition, and adequate rest.

In terms of finances and relationships, The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to take time to pause and reflect on our current situation. Are our relationships strong and healthy? Are we making sound financial decisions? Taking a step back can give us the clarity needed to make sound decisions about our finances and relationships. We may need to take a break from the people and processes that are draining us and focus on things that will help us grow.

In conclusion, The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to step back and look at our lives from a distance. This can help us gain clarity and understanding on our physical, emotional and financial state. Taking a break from the distractions of our lives and focusing on the present moment can bring the insight needed to make sound decisions about our health, finances and relationships.

## What does Death mean?

Health  
The Death Tarot card can signify the end of an illness or a period of poor health. It reflects the need to move on to new and healthier habits. Change is an inevitable part of life, and this card reminds us to embrace it. Depending on the surrounding cards, it can also be a sign that it is time to seek professional help or to take a break and relax. Health, like all aspects of life, need to be constantly managed and sometimes a major shake-up is necessary.

Finances  
The Death Tarot card indicates a time of financial change. This could mean the end of a job, a major loss, or a need to adjust the way funds are managed. It signals the need for shifts in perspective, resourcefulness and risk-taking. A thorough review of all finances is necessary to ensure that money is being spent responsibly and that future goals are realistic. This card encourages the individual to be proactive in taking charge of the situation and making necessary adjustments.

Relationships  
The Death Tarot card symbolizes the death of a relationship or a significant change in the relationship dynamic. It can also be a sign that it is time to break away from unhealthy or toxic relationships. This card is a reminder that relationships can go through ups and downs, and that sometimes drastic steps need to be taken in order to move forward. It is a call for the individual to focus on self-growth and to evaluate whether a current relationship is healthy or worth the effort.
