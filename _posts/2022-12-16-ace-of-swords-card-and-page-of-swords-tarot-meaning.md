A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Swords card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Ace Of Swords card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Ace Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Ace Of Swords and The Page Of Swords tarot cards together could signify a period of transformation and clarity for you. You may be called upon to use your mental capacity and intellect to navigate a conflict. Whether that conflict is an internal or external one, or both, your sharp mind will be capable of finding the creative solutions and innovative ideas to move forward and resolve the issue. You may also be entering a period of truth-seeking and truth-telling, as the Page Of Swords represents gossip and prying eyes. Whatever is revealed to you will help you to make clear and decisive decisions, guided by the clarity brought by the Ace Of Swords.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Ace Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Ace Of Swords and Page Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

Health

The Ace of Swords Tarot card is a symbol of clarity, certainty, and new beginnings. It suggests that you may be entering a new phase of health, and that something is about to be cut away to make room for improved well-being. It could indicate the need for surgery or taking a new approach to health and wellness. It may be time to focus on the mental and emotional aspects of your health, and to look at ways to make positive changes in your routine.

Finances

The Ace of Swords Tarot card indicates that new ideas and solutions may come to you with regards to your finances. It suggests that you will be able to gain clarity about financial matters, and that you should be more decisive and careful when it comes to managing money. You may need to be more mindful of your finances and look into ways of budgeting and saving. New solutions may be found when it comes to tricky financial problems, but it is important to stay focused and not get overwhelmed by the task at hand.

Relationships

The Ace of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a new beginning when it comes to relationships. It suggests that you will need to be clear and decisive when it comes to relationships and that you should be ready for conflict. It may be time to make some tough decisions about where you stand in a relationship, and it could be time to cut away the excess and make sure that your connection with someone is clear and healthy. It suggests that you should be honest with yourself and your partner and be ready to move forward with any changes that need to be made.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health

The Page of Swords Tarot card in relation to health suggests physical and emotional intelligence. It implies that one needs to be careful with their decisions, as well as their own mental and physical health. The card speaks of the need to stay alert, as well as having a strong sense of self-awareness. It also suggests that the querant may need to take a step back from the situation at hand in order to gain clarity and understanding. Physically, the Page of Swords Tarot card may be a warning that one needs to take care of themselves, whether that means checking in with a doctor, getting enough rest, or simply being mindful of their health.

Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card can be a representation of financial difficulties in a person’s life. The card may be telling the querant to be smart with their money and to look before they leap into any investments. It is also a reminder that sometimes, even if the financial situation isn’t ideal, the querant should always look out for their own best interests and take small steps to better their economic life. The Page of Swords Tarot card speaks of one’s need to make sound financial decisions, as well as a reminder to be conscious of even the smallest spending habits.

Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of relationship troubles. In regards to this, the card may be warning the querant to be aware of any lies or secrets within their relationship, as well as to keep an eye out for any untrustworthy behavior. The card may also bring to light feelings of jealousy, gossip or an overall lack of communication between partners. Ultimately, the Page of Swords Tarot card speaks of the need to be honest and truthful in all relationships, and to be accountable to one’s own decisions.
