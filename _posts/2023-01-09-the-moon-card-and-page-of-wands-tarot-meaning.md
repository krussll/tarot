## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

For health, The Moon Tarot card can represent potential unseen issues, such as illnesses or other ailments that may be difficult to diagnose. It may also serve as a warning that it’s time to take some preventative measures such as an annual physical or checkup to make sure everything is alright. It could also signify that you need to trust your intuition above all else and pay close attention to your body and its needs.

For finances, The Moon Tarot card could be a sign that it’s time to make some voluntary changes in your financial life. It may be time to look into a budget or a savings plan, or even to reexamine your current decisions to ensure that your financial health is sound. This card also stresses the importance of trusting your intuition when it comes to money; if something feels off or too good to be true, listen to that feeling and make sure to do your research.

For relationships, The Moon Tarot card could be a reminder to pay attention to the subtle changes or issues that may be happening in the relationship. This card encourages you to trust your instincts and be observant of any signs that things may be amiss. It could also remind you to pay attention to any patterns of behavior that suggest an underlying issue that needs to be addressed. Ultimately, this card encourages you to take the necessary steps to ensure that your relationship is moving towards a healthy and fulfilling place.

The Page of Wands Tarot card can be a positive sign when it comes to health. It can signify an increased focus on physical health, as the Page of Wands encourages action. The Page of Wands suggests taking time to pursue activities that bring joy and pleasure, as well as finding ways to nurture one’s body to keep it in top shape. Additionally, the Page of Wands can be a sign that one should take a closer look at their daily habits, such as diet and exercise, and find ways to make them healthier.

The Page of Wands Tarot card can be a sign of luck when it comes to finances. The Page encourages taking the initiative and taking calculated risks. The Page also hints at being able to manifest hopes and dreams of financial success. On the other hand, the Page of Wands Tarot card can also indicate a need to be more prudent with money. This card suggests setting a budget and sticking to it, as well as taking steps to protect one’s financial security.

For Relationships:  
The Page of Wands Tarot card can be a sign of new beginnings in relationships. This card encourages communication, whether it is with a romantic partner, friends, or family. The Page of Wands suggests being open and honest in these conversations, finding common ground and creating understanding. The Page of Wands also implies being proactive in relationships, being willing to compromise and make changes to foster strong and fulfilling connections.
