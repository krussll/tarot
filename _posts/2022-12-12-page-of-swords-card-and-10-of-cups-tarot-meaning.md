A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Page Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

This card combination can also mean that you are surrounded by truth, no matter what it is, but you must be mindful of the eyes that are looking in on your life. This will help you stay grounded and know what is real from what is not.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page Of Swords Tarot card can be indicative of a tendency to poke into other people’s business which can be seen as a symptom of an underlying condition such as paranoia or depression. It can also be indicative of unhealthy behaviors such as being too nosy in an effort to uncover secrets or gossip. It can also suggest a need to be passed from one person to the next with little concern for the repercussions or the impact it has on the health of the individual.

Finances: The Page Of Swords Tarot card can mean that the individual is relying too heavily on information taken from others rather than forming their own opinion. As such, it can suggest that the individual is taking too much risk and not carefully weighing their decisions. Moreover, it could suggest that an individual is letting others influence their opinion and decisions, making them vulnerable to financial scams or misjudgments.

Relationships: The Page Of Swords Tarot card can indicate a tendency towards gossip in relationships which could be damaging to trust and intimacy. It could also suggest that a person is unwilling to accept criticism or feedback, instead trying to uncover dirt or secrets in order to gain an upper hand in the relationship. This card can also suggest that an individual may be overly defensive, not wanting to let others in or open up.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 10 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the attainment of physical, mental, and emotional health. It is a sign of perfect health and well-being. On the physical level, this card often indicates inner balance, relaxation, and steady energy to carry through the day. On the emotional and mental levels, this card represents a sense of contentment, joy, and unity with oneself and others. This card symbolizes the ability to take care of one’s health on a physical and spiritual level.

Finances:  
The 10 of Cups Tarot card suggests that your financial situation is in perfect balance. This card indicates that all your responsibilities and obligations are being taken care of in a timely manner. It is a sign of contentment and stability, and indicates that you can trust your intuition when it comes to making decisions about your finances. It may be a reminder that it is necessary to put in the hard work to achieve success in any aspect of life, especially when it comes to investments.

Relationships:  
The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of the highest level of love and connection. It represents a lasting relationship in which both parties are fully supportive of each other and willing to work hard to make their relationship stronger. This card signifies complete happiness and joy in the relationship, as well as full love and support. All relationships, whether it is between partners, family, or friends, have room to grow and this card can help guide that growth. It also symbolizes the commitment to make difficult decisions when needed and stay true to your loved one.
