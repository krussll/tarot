They tell you to take your leadership role seriously, combining strategic thinking and empathy. Take the time to consider all the ramifications of your plans and be willing to adjust them when necessary. Be open to others’ opinions, seek out advice from those around you, and accept that it may be difficult to make the decisions you must. Show your commitment to being an intentional leader and know that when you do so you can foster positive change and growth in the world.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both King Of Swords and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw King Of Swords and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

The King of Swords Tarot card typically signifies health matters and decisions related to health. It is a card that encourages one to take a logical and well thought out approach to health matters. It suggests that any health issue should be carefully considered, and all options weighed up, before any action is taken. It indicates that one should not be too hasty in making decisions about their own health, or the health of others. The King of Swords can also indicate that now is the time to seek advice from a trusted professional, who can give the best advice when it comes to health matters.

Finances

The King of Swords Tarot card can also signify financial matters. It is an indication that any financial decisions should be made with wisdom and sound judgment. This card encourages one to think carefully and strategically about money matters, weighing up all the options in a logical and analytical way before committing to anything. It suggests that one should not make any hasty or rash decisions when it comes to financial matters, and seek professional advice if needed.

Relationships

The King of Swords Tarot card may also suggest a need to consider relationships with a logical and analytical approach. It suggests that any relationship matters should be approached in a strategic and thoughtful way, with careful consideration of all the options before committing to a course of action. It is an indication that one should take the time to assess a situation carefully in order to make the best decision for all involved. It can also suggest that one should be honest and open in their communication with others, and seek advice from a trusted professional in order to make the best decisions.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health

The King of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of emotional and mental balance. It suggests that a person has a strong foundation and the capacity to maintain emotional equilibrium. It also speaks to the importance of self-care and cultivating a healthy lifestyle. People represented by this card often have an understanding of their own emotional needs, and the inner strength to meet them. They tend to have an ability to look after their physical health, observing healthy diet and lifestyle habits. This card encourages people to take time out for themselves, to rest and relax, and to stay connected to their inner guidance.

Finances

The King of Cups Tarot card suggests a balanced approach to money and finances. This card speaks to the importance of a strong foundation in financial matters, with a focus on careful, consistent planning and prioritizing of resources. People represented by this card are often savvy with their finances, working hard to ensure that their spending is aligned with their long-term financial goals. This card can also suggest that taking a creative approach to money matters can be beneficial.

Relationships

The King of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a strong foundation in relationships. People represented by this card are often emotionally mature and are able to extend tolerance and understanding to others. They are compassionate, patient and supportive partners, who value open communication and mutual respect. This card encourages people to prioritize balance and harmony in their relationships. It can also suggest that people take a creative approach to their relationships, looking for ways to strengthen and deepen their connection.
