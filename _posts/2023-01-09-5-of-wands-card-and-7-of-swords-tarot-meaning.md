A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The 5 Of Wands and The 7 Of Swords tarot cards together suggest that you may be facing obstacles, challenges or even rivalries that could involve some degree of dishonesty or betrayal. You may be dealing with a situation that requires you to be extra vigilant and proactive in order to defend yourself or your interests, or you may even need to consider running away in order to avoid a difficult situation. Ultimately, this combination of cards points to a potentially taxing challenge requiring you to rely on your strength and resilience to make it through.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

**Both 5 Of Wands and 7 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 5 Of Wands and 7 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 5 of Wands Tarot card in the health context often symbolizes obstacles or challenges related to health and fitness. It can represent a need for discipline and focus when it comes to maintaining health, or a need to accept and adapt to a chronic physical condition. It could also signify that a certain challenge must be faced or a competition must be won in order to achieve better health. This card encourages people to take a proactive approach to their health, to keep striving and persevere to reach their goals.

Finances:

The 5 of Wands Tarot card in the context of finances can indicate a competitive environment. This card usually means that you need to focus and remain competitive in order to attain financial success. It can signify that you may need to make difficult decisions for the greater good, or that you may need to work hard to achieve financial stability. Competition can be a healthy thing, as long as it doesn’t lead to reckless decisions or unhappiness.

Relationships:

The 5 of Wands Tarot card in the context of relationships can symbolize a certain level of rivalry in the relationship. It is possible that a partner may be jealous or possessive, or that a certain level of ‘friendly’ competition exists between partners. This card also signifies a need for both partners to remain adaptable and flexible, in order to work through any obstacles in the relationship. In any case, cooperation and working together is essential, as the 5 of Wands encourages partners to strive to achieve their goals together.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 Of Swords Tarot card can signify a time when it’s important to be honest, open and upfront. This card is all about the consequences of deceit, dishonesty and betrayal.

When it comes to health, the 7 Of Swords can be a warning to pay attention to any underlying issues that might be pushed aside in favor of a more convenient solution. In terms of physical health, this can be a reminder to take care of yourself and not take risks. It might also indicate the need to be honest with medical professionals to ensure the best course of action is administered. On the other hand, it can be a warning of a potential ailment or health issue arising, and a reminder to be mindful of any sudden changes in health.

Financially, this card could be an indicator of potential financial losses. This could be a result of a deception on the part of another person or party, or from risky and unethical investments. It could also be a warning to be conscious and aware of any financial decisions you make so as to protect your assets.

In terms of relationships, the 7 Of Swords can represent deception, betrayal and dishonesty. It can also signify a need to be honest with yourself and others. This card could indicate that if something doesn’t feel right, there’s probably a reason. It can also be a reminder to trust your instincts and be aware of potential deceit or betrayal in your relationships. Finally, the 7 Of Swords can serve as a reminder to be self-protective, to put your own needs first and to keep your boundaries in check.
