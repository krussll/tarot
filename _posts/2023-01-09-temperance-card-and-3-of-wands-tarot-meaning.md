## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card can have a profound effect on many areas of life, including health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Temperance Tarot card encourages an individual to be mindful of their health and to practice balance and moderation in the physical and emotional aspects of their life. This includes diet, exercise, sleep and relaxation, which when combined can lead to increased vitality, improved immunity and overall wellness. Furthermore, The Temperance card is an ode to balance, which encourages us to be mindful of our mental and emotional health as well, taking time to process emotions, and express them in healthy ways.

Regarding finances, The Temperance Tarot card encourages individuals to practice patience and remain mindful of their finances. This means being mindful of purchases, controlling spending, and practicing smart money management. It also encourages saving rather than spending recklessly and developing a budget plan to ensure financial stability.

When it comes to relationships, The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to practice patience and understanding with others. This means seeing all sides of an issue before making a judgement, being mindful of other’s feelings, and communicating honestly and openly. The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to practice self-control and look for common ground, understanding that relationships can be fragile and difficult to maintain.

All in all, The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to practice balance and moderation in all aspects of life. It encourages individuals to be mindful of their health, finances and relationships, taking care to practice self-control and patience in each.

The 3 of Wands represents a healthy balance between physical, emotional, and spiritual health. If you draw this card, it could be an indication that you are currently in harmony with yourself and your lifestyle. It may also signal that you are taking on the challenge of becoming more aware and mindful of your health. The 3 of Wands suggests that you should strive to make sure that each aspect of your health is taken care of. It is necessary to be aware of any imbalances in order to stay in good health.

The 3 of Wands is often seen to signify financial growth. It is a card of prosperity and success, indicating that a well thought-out plan and hard work have likely paid off. It can be a good sign that you are on the right track financially. It is an indication that you are expanding your business, investments, or savings. The 3 of Wands can suggest that your finances are healthy and that you are in a position to take risks and expand further.

Relationships:  
The 3 of Wands suggests that your relationships are strong and full of energy. It is a sign that you are working with your partner to create something together, whether it be a business venture or a different kind of venture. The card symbolizes teamwork, trust, and cooperation. It also relates to travel, which suggests that communication and understanding between you and your partner are strong. The 3 of Wands is a reminder to keep working together, in order to grow and strengthen the bond between you. The card also brings a sense of adventure and the potential for great rewards. It is a reminder that when relationships are based on trust and understanding, they will flourish.
