On one hand, the Page of Wands signals a time of newness and creative energy. You may be about to embark on an exciting project or idea that brings newness and enthusiasm into your life. On the other hand, the 3 of Swords speaks to a time of pain and heartache, perhaps with an impending divorce or loss that is looming large. This is an emotionally difficult stage in your life but it is also an opportunity to reinvent and recreate yourself. It is a time of growth and change and although it will be challenging, it will ultimately give rise to a new and improved version of yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

This card is often a sign of a difficult and painful situation that is beyond a person’s control. It can indicate a deep sadness or loss of a loved one, a physical ailment, or a major life event that has left a person feeling helpless. When it comes to health, this card can symbolize that something is wrong and that healing and recovery are necessary.

Looking at the financial aspect of The Three of Swords Tarot Card it usually can suggest that money matters may have to be reassessed or that a financial loss will take place. It could be a sign of major financial changes, and restructuring of accounts and investments will be necessary.

Relationships and The Three of Swords Tarot Card can show up as a sign of an impending breakup or divorce. It could also be a sign that a relationship has reached the end of its course and it needs to be let go of. The card can indicate that a relationship is at a crossroads and needs to be examined to see if it is worth continuing or not.

Overall, The Three of Swords Tarot Card carries a difficult energy and can signify heartache and loss. This can be related to health, finances, and relationships, and if dealt with courageously, can lead to healing.
