However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 2 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Hermit mean?

When it comes to health, The Hermit Tarot card indicates a need to reflect and understand yourself better. This card suggests that it is important to take the time to be introspective and identify any emotional blocks or patterns that are holding you back from living your best and healthiest life. It encourages you to take a close look at the underlying issues that may be the root of any physical or mental health problems in order to make lasting change. Furthermore, this card is a reminder to prioritize your own well-being above anything else and not neglect your needs.

When this card comes up in regards to finances, The Hermit Tarot card encourages you to take a step back and look objectively at your financial situation. It suggests that you take the time to identify any existing patterns that are creating the current state of your finances, and to make the necessary changes to put your finances back in order. It is important to create a budget that works for you and to be mindful of any unnecessary spending. The Hermit Tarot card also acts as a reminder to be patient with yourself as you go through this process and have faith that you can create a financially secure future.

When it comes to relationships, The Hermit Tarot card suggests that it is important to take the time to understand yourself better. The energy of this card encourages us to reflect on past relationships and to identify any patterns that have been detrimental to us. Additionally, it encourages us to take a step back and look at our relationships through a less emotionally charged lens in order to identify the root of any issues. This may require some time alone in order to gain a clearer perspective. Lastly, this card suggests that it is important to set healthy boundaries and to prioritize self-care.

## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 2 of Cups Tarot card is indicative of a positive energy when it comes to health and wellness. It symbolizes a more balanced and harmonious approach to physical and emotional well-being. The message of this card is that while it is important to take care of our physical health, it is just as essential to look after our mental and spiritual well-being as well. This card suggests that the integration of both aspects of our health is necessary for a more complete and fulfilling life.

Finances

The 2 of Cups Tarot card is associated with financial stability and security. It symbolizes a steady and secure flow of resources, and a comfortable but inviting lifestyle. The message of this card is that while it is important to have a good grip on our finances, it is equally important to realize that financial security is not just about having money — it is also about living within our means, and having the wisdom to spend away from our means. The card encourages us to not only think about wealth and material goods, but also to strive for a healthier balance between our material needs and our spiritual needs.

Relationships

The 2 of Cups Tarot card encapsulates the sentiment of a strong and loving relationship between two individuals. It indicates the mutual understanding, trust, and acceptance that forms the very foundation of a healthy partnership. The message of this card is that while relationships may not always be easy, they should still bring out the best in both individuals. This card encourages us to nurture these relationships, and to strive for a balance between our needs and the needs of our partners. Furthermore, it stresses the importance of not just focusing on what each partner can give to the other, but also recognizing and celebrating the different strengths and abilities each person has.
