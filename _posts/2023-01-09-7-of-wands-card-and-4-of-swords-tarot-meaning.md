A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The 7 Of Wands tarot card and The 4 Of Swords tarot card together represent a balance between self-defense and retreat. It is a reminder of the importance of knowing when to protect your interests and when to take the time to rest and recover. The 7 Of Wands reminds us to be aware of potential competition and take the necessary steps to protect ourselves in such situations, while the 4 Of Swords encourages us to take a step back and retreat in order to restore our energy and focus. Overall, this combination suggests that we need to strike a balance between being vigilant and taking a break in order to remain resilient and successful.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands Tarot card often indicates self-defense or protection against competition. This card suggests that one should be aware of their own weaknesses and be prepared to ward off potential dangers with strength and courage.

For health, The 7 of Wands encourages one to use their inner strength to continue pushing forward toward health and wellness. It is a reminder that we should be prepared to defend our physical and emotional wellbeing against any potential threats.

For finances, The 7 of Wands suggests that one should look to defend their finances against competition. This card warns against putting too much trust into someone else’s hands and encourages that one should be aware of their own strengths and weaknesses when it comes to money.

For relationships, The 7 of Wands encourages one to protect their relationships against any competitive threats. This card suggests that one should use their intuition and strength to protect their relationships, while still being mindful of the potential risks of trusting others.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

For Health:

The 4 of Swords Tarot card indicates a need for physical rest and renewal. It is a reminder that a break from intense physical and mental activity may be necessary in order to restore balance and well-being. Taking a break from excessive exercise and unhealthy habits will benefit both mind and body. It is important to take time for relaxation and to find a healthy balance between rest and activity.

For Finances:

The 4 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning to be more mindful of money. It is a reminder to take a step back and look more objectively at your finances. The need for rest and relaxation may also mean that you should take a break from spending for a while to give yourself time to think more deeply about your financial situation. It is a symbol of taking time to plan and make wise decisions regarding money.

For Relationships:

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is an indication that relationships require regular renewal in order to stay healthy and strong. This card encourages individuals to step back and take a break from relationships, in order to reflect on the dynamics and make necessary changes. Solitude and self-reflection are important for understanding our individual needs and communicating them with our partners. Taking time to prioritize relationships is essential and leads to healthier, more balanced connections.
