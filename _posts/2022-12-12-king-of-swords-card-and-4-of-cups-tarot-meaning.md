However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does King Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The King Of Swords tarot card and the 4 Of Cups tarot card working together suggest a leader who is aware of the feeling of being unfulfilled and knows that the surprise gifts that come their way will help them to find a solution. The leader will be strategic, taking the time to assess the situation and discover new opportunities before making a firm decision. It’s possible that the gifts presented will be difficult for the leader to accept, but with patience and thought, it’s likely that they’ll be able to make the most of them and progress in their mission.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both King Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw King Of Swords and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

This card can call for us to take the time to create effective, healthy habits to enhance our physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. It can also inspire us to think through our decisions and emotions before we act. It calls for honesty, clarity, and integrity in our relationship with ourselves and our choices.

Finances: The King of Swords Tarot card emphasizes the importance of being intentional and mindful when it comes to finances. It implies that making wise financial decisions should be made based on further research and understanding. The King of Swords can remind us to take the time to properly budget, plan, and invest, as well as to think twice before making any drastic decisions.

Relationships: The King of Swords Tarot card encourages us to ponder the potential outcomes of our actions before we make them. It can help us to be mindful of our words, and to think through our decisions to ensure that our relationships are based on strong communication, honesty, and respect. It can serve as a reminder that respect, clear boundaries, and understanding are key in maintaining a healthy relationship with others.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 4 of Cups can represent a feeling of exhaustion when it comes to physical and mental health. Someone may feel drained, unmotivated, or even a sense of apathy. This can indicate that there is an underlying issue that needs to be addressed. It could mean that the individual has been neglecting their health and wellness or that they have become complacent in their current lifestyle. In either case, it is important to take the time to assess what needs to happen in order to make positive changes in their health and wellbeing.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the 4 of Cups can denote a sense of stagnation. A lack of enthusiasm or drive may be indicative of an issue related to money. It may suggest that the individual is not making the most of their financial situation, or that they are not making the most of the resources they have available. It can indicate that they have become comfortable with their finances, rather than pushing forward and working to make positive financial strides.

Relationships: On a more interpersonal level, the 4 of Cups tarot card can be interpreted as a lack of connection or contentment. It could mean that someone feels disconnected from their significant other or family members, or that they are dissatisfied with their current relationships. It also can represent a surprise gift, as something unexpected could be on the horizon for that individual which could bring about a breath of fresh air. In other words, the 4 of Cups can indicate that something is either lacking or about to come into the individual’s life.
