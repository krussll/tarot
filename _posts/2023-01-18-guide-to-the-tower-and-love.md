Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Tower card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Tower, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Tower.

## The Tower And Love – An Overview

The Tower tarot card is often associated with health issues, as it indicates unwanted change, ruin, and upheaval.

It is a sign of disruption and destruction in the physical and emotional realms.

When this card appears in a health-related reading, it can signify a need to make a drastic change in order to restore balance and stability.

It could mean the destruction of an old health habit that is no longer serving you, or it could be a warning sign of a health crisis to come.

It is important to take this card seriously and take the necessary steps to protect your health and well-being.

## Upright Meanings For The Tower And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Tower tarot card represents an unwelcome and sudden change in love.

This could be anything from a breakup to a sudden shift in the relationship dynamic.

It is a sign of upheaval and can represent violence, ruin, and disgrace.

It can be a warning of an impending disaster or a sign that something needs to be done to save the relationship.

It can also indicate that the relationship is due for a significant change and that the couple must be prepared for the consequences.

In this case, the best approach is to remain open and honest with each other and to take the necessary steps to ensure the relationship remains strong and healthy.

### What It Means For The Future

The Tower tarot card is a sign of potential upheaval in my future love life.

It suggests that unexpected changes or sudden ruin may be on the horizon, and that the relationship could face disgrace or violence.

Although this might be frightening, the Tower card can also be a sign of a needed transformation, and the chance to start fresh with a more balanced, meaningful relationship.

It could be a sign that I need to let go of something that is not working in order to make space for something more fulfilling.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Tower tarot card symbolizes unwanted change, ruin, disgrace, and upheaval when it comes to love.

It implies that the relationships and bonds of those close to me may be subject to upheaval and destruction.

This could be a result of sudden and unexpected changes that could be damaging to the relationship, or it could be caused by some kind of disruption or violence.

This card warns me to be aware of the potential for negative forces that could lead to destruction of the love I share with those close to me.

It is important that I stay open and honest with my loved ones in order to maintain strong and healthy relationships.

## Reversed Meanings For The Tower And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The Tower tarot card is reversed in regards to love, it typically indicates that a relationship is undergoing massive changes and upheaval.

This card could symbolize a break-up, a dramatic shift in the dynamic of the relationship, or a major disagreement.

The upheaval can be overwhelming and difficult to navigate, but ultimately it could be a sign that the relationship is in need of some serious repairs.

When The Tower is reversed, it may also signal a need to break free from an unhealthy or unbalanced relationship.

This card can indicate that someone is feeling trapped in a situation and needs to take a step back and evaluate the relationship.

If a relationship is struggling and you find yourself stuck in a rut, The Tower reversed could be a sign that it may be time to let go and move on.

It may be a difficult decision, but it could ultimately lead to greater freedom and happiness.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Tower tarot card suggests that my future love may bring unexpected changes that could potentially be disruptive or traumatic.

It indicates that there may be a sudden upheaval or a period of chaos in my relationship that I may not have anticipated.

The reversed Tower can also suggest that a previously stable relationship could be threatened by outside forces or shattered by unexpected events.

It could be a warning that I may suffer public humiliation or disgrace as a result of my relationship.

However, the reversed Tower tarot card can also signify a period of healing and renewal.

It can suggest that, even though the changes that come with my future love may be unwanted, the end result could lead to greater stability and harmony in my relationship.

The reversed Tower can be an indication that the obstacles that I face in my relationship could help us to develop a stronger bond and an even more solid foundation.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Tower tarot card appears in a reading about love, it can signify something unexpected and unwanted coming into the relationship.

It can also mean that something is about to cause disruption and upheaval in the relationship dynamics.

This card is a warning to be prepared for the unexpected and to be prepared to make the necessary changes in order to keep the relationship healthy.

It can also indicate that there may be a conflict between the two lovers that needs to be addressed.

The reversed Tower tarot card can also suggest that there may be some underlying issues that are causing disharmony in the relationship.

It is important to take the time to look at these issues and to address them before they cause too much damage.

Acknowledging and dealing with these issues will help strengthen the relationship and bring the two lovers closer together.

It can also mean that it is time to take a step back and re-evaluate the relationship and what is truly important to both partners.

By doing so, it will help to ensure that the relationship is strong and healthy.
