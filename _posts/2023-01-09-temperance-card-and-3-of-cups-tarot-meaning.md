A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Temperance card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Temperance tarot card, combined with the 3 Of Cups tarot card, speaks to me of a harmonious balance between friendship and self-control. Having friends who are there to celebrate your successes and help you through the failures is important, but so is maintaining a sense of restraint and moderation in order to keep things in check. Together, these two cards are a reminder to enjoy the camaraderie of those around you without overindulging in any one aspect of life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Temperance and 3 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Temperance and 3 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health  
The Temperance Tarot card encourages a healthy balance between mind, body and spirit. It teaches us to take the time to nurture our physical and emotional health. This card encourages us to engage in healthy practices that promote self-care, such as maintaining a balanced diet, getting enough sleep, exercising, and participating in calming activities such as yoga and meditation. This card also reminds us that moderation is key when it comes to our health, and to avoid overindulging in anything that could have a negative effect on our wellbeing.

Finances  
The Temperance Tarot card indicates that establishing and maintaining a healthy balance between spending and saving will serve you in the long term. This card is a reminder to save regularly and to be patient when it comes to financial goals. This card suggests that it’s important to be mindful of impulsively spending, and to instead, develop a plan for saving and investing regarding money.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card reminds us to take our time when it comes to building relationships, and to be mindful of the balance between giving and taking. This card speaks to developing relationships in a way that’s balanced and respectful for all involved. It’s about finding the balance of being open and vulnerable with someone, while still allowing for your individual autonomy. This card also encourages us to maintain healthy boundaries and to practice patience and understanding in our relationships.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups tarot card speaks to the power of friendship, celebrations, and a strong support system. This card is a reminder that we are not alone and when we come together with family, friends, and loved ones we are stronger than ever.

When it comes to health, the 3 of Cups tarot card indicates that our health is best supported when we have the support of our community. It suggests that we make time to connect with friends and family and enjoy shared moments of laughter and joy. Doing so can boost our mental health and lift our spirits. Furthermore, it reminds us to take time to relax and enjoy life; even small moments of pleasure can have a positive impact on our physical health.

When considering finances, the 3 of Cups tarot card indicates that communal resources are always available to us. It suggests that we can look to our circle of support for help, advice, or a loan if it’s ever needed. We should also remember to be generous with our finances and to help out those in need.

For relationships, the 3 of Cups tarot card signifies the importance of communication and sharing feelings. It reminds us to be honest with our partners, friends and family, and to be open with our emotions. It’s also a reminder to take part in social activities with loved ones and to have a balanced lifestyle by not letting work or other commitments dominate our lives. This card encourages us to enjoy life and make time for fun.
