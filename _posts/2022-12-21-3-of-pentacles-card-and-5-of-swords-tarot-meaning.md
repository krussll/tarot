A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Pentacles card and 5 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Pentacles card and 5 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 5 Of Swords tarot card together represent two sides of the same coin: collaboration and collaboration gone wrong. On the one hand, the combination of these two cards signifies a team working together skillfully to craft something of high quality. On the other hand, it shows the consequences of collaboration when it is corrupted by bullying, theft, and violence. Working together in a cooperative and constructive way brings great rewards; however, when teamwork becomes tainted by abusive behavior, it can quickly lead to serious problems. When understanding these two cards together, it’s important to keep an eye out for the misuse of power and try to protect yourself from becoming a victim of it.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Five of Swords tarot card can be a warning that you might be engaging in behavior that is detrimental to your health: bullying, physical or verbal abuse, theft, or violence. As with any stressful, self-destructive behavior, it can cause a range of physical and psychological problems that can manifest in a variety of ways. Stress, anxiety, and depression are just a few of the illnesses that can result from this kind of behavior. Physically, the impact of this behavior can manifest in lack of energy, fatigue, and difficulty concentrating. Ultimately, it can cause physical or mental health problems that can have long-term effects.

Finances:  
The Five of Swords tarot card is a signal that your financial situation might be in trouble. Bullying or being taken advantage of financially can mean that your finances are not as strong as they could be. It could also mean that you are in a situation where you are being taken advantage of, either by someone you know or a company that is manipulating you financially. This could lead to debt, bankruptcy, or an inability to save for the future. Furthermore, the card could signify that you are not taking the necessary steps to protect your finances, either by not having a proper savings plan, not budgeting correctly, or not taking the time to protect your investments.

Relationships:  
The Five of Swords tarot card can be an indication that relationships in your life are not as stable as they should be. It could mean that you are in an abusive relationship, or in a relationship where bullying and manipulation are common. It could also indicate that you are in a relationship where your partner is engaging in behaviors that are not only harmful to you, but to the relationship as a whole. This could lead to the breakdown of trust, communication problems, and even a lack of respect. Furthermore, it could mean that you are in a relationship where you feel powerless and unable to fully express yourself or your emotions.
