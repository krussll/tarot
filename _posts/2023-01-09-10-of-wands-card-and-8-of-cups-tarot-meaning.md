## What does 10 Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean together?

You have taken on too many responsibilities and tasks and it has become overwhelming. You need to let go of some of these burdens and move on to something new. It is time to retreat and abandon what is no longer serving you in order to allow yourself to move forward. Taking this time of withdrawal and retreat can help you to rejuvenate and regain perspective.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Wands and 8 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The Ten of Wands in the Tarot is a warning of the dangers of taking on too much, implying that shouldering too many responsibilities in any area of life can lead to a sense of exhaustion, depression, and distress. Health wise, The 10 of Wands can signify an individual struggling with long term stress and an inability to relax or take time for themselves, leading to a decrease in overall wellbeing. There can also be an element of self-neglect, neglect of one’s physical and mental health, which can manifest in feelings of guilt or shame.

Financially, The 10 of Wands suggests a lack of balance when it comes to work and money. There may be an over-emphasis on making money at the expense of one’s own needs and desires, leading to financial stress and difficulty. This card can also signify someone who is in financial difficulty due to taking on too many obligations without the ability to pay them off.

In relationships, The Ten of Wands can indicate an imbalance in energy between two people, with one partner taking on too many responsibilities. This can lead to resentment, frustration, and a lack of connection with one another. It can also signify taking on too much work or too many commitments, leaving little time for meaningful interactions and conversations, thus creating emotional distance over time.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of retreating and withdrawing from unhealthy habits or situations. It can be an indication that something in your life is no longer working and it is time to make an effort to find something healthier. It is a warning that it is time to make changes and move away from any physical or emotional pain. This could be an eating disorder, unhealthy relationships, addiction, or an unhealthy lifestyle. The card is also a reminder that it is never too late to make positive changes in your life and that it is possible to find emotional and physical healing.

Finances: The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of leaving behind past financial mistakes and breaking away from bad financial habits. It could be a sign that it is time to reassess your financial goals and find a secure and sustainable financial path. This card can also indicate it is a time to retreat from investing in risky ventures and to focus on finding long-term financial stability. The card can be an indicator that it is time to create a new budget, save more, and work hard to get out of debt.

Relationships: The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign to move away from relationships that are no longer serving you. This card can be a sign to break away from toxic patterns, unhealthy dynamics, and any relationships that bring more pain than pleasure. It is a reminder that it is never too late to find healthier, more positive connections. The card can also be a sign that it is time to accept that some relationships have run their course and that it is time to part ways in order to find true happiness.
