A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and 3 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Devil card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and 3 Of Wands mean together?

The Devil tarot card and the 3 of Wands tarot card together suggest a need to focus on the collective rather than individual pursuits. While The Devil card speaks of addiction, greed, and materialism, the 3 of Wands card brings awareness of how, when focused on mutually beneficial goals, wealth and abundance can be generated. The two cards together encourage a transformational shift away from an individual focus on material gain, and toward greater awareness of the power of working together, trading and expanding with others in mutually beneficial ways. When these cards are combined, it reminds us to embrace the spirit of collaboration and to focus our energy on building something of lasting value that will benefit us all.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Devil and 3 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The Devil tarot card in combination with the 3 of Wands relates to two distinct drives in your love life – the drive for materialism and pleasure, and the need for togetherness and collaboration. At first, it may seem as if these two forces are at odds, but in reality, they can be used to create a positive and harmonious experience.

On the one hand, The Devil card symbolizes all kinds of desires, such as addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust. It warns you that these desires can cause you to become too focused on materialism, and you may find yourself sacrificing relationships and intimacy in order to pursue those goals. On the other hand, the 3 of Wands encourages you to embrace teamwork, commerce, expansion, and travel as ways to strengthen your bond with a partner or to enjoy new experiences together. This card suggests that it’s possible to find harmony between materialism and relationships, and that when you’re mindful and thoughtful about your goals, these two forces can create something greater together than either could create alone.

For your love life, the combination of The Devil and the 3 of Wands suggests that you should be mindful of your desires, and the effects that pursuing them can have on your relationships. It also encourages you to find a balance between the pursuit of materialism and the need for connection, and to embrace collaboration and teamwork in order to make your relationship stronger. By paying attention to both forces and working together with your partner to create something larger, you can find a balance between pleasure and support that will bring you both tremendous fulfillment.

### What does The Devil and 3 Of Wands mean together for your finances?

When The Devil tarot card and The 3 of Wands tarot card appear together in a reading, it could be a warning sign that our finances and job situation are potentially heading down a dangerous path. This combination of cards indicates that our financial and job security may be at risk due to a lack of discipline and unwise decision making. Our focus on financial gain and material possessions could lead us to becoming too deeply entrenched in the pursuit of excess and selfish ambition. We could be so driven by greed and envy that we are unable to see the dangers of our actions or the potential consequences of our decisions, resulting in disastrous financial losses in the future.

On the other hand, this card combination could also indicate a need to work together with others and capitalize on business opportunities. The 3 of Wands encourages us to venture outside of our comfort zone and explore new ventures, partnerships, and collaborations in order to increase financial and job security. Teamwork is key if we want to successfully expand our financial portfolio and venture into new, potentially profitable, opportunities. We must be aware and in control of our own desires and activities if we want to successfully navigate this journey.

This card combination warns us to pay attention to our own motivation and keep our eyes open for new opportunities. Successful collaboration and partnerships can bring great financial reward, but without proper discipline and control it can also lead to addiction, greed, and financial destruction. We must remember to remain mindful of our own destructive desires and have a clear plan and boundaries in place before entering into any business venture.

### What does The Devil and 3 Of Wands mean together for your health?

When The Devil tarot card and The 3 Of Wands tarot card intersect, they represent a warning that we may be focusing too much on materialistic pursuits and neglecting our physical and mental health. It is easy to become obsessed with accumulating material wealth, or entrapped by our addictions or lusts, and this combination of cards encourages us to step back and evaluate our priorities.

We must learn to balance our material goals and desires with our need for physical and mental wellbeing. The Devil card can also be seen as a sign of co-dependency, which may be playing a role in our current health situation. On the other hand, The 3 Of Wands card speaks of teamwork, cooperation and partnership, all of which are necessary components of a healthy, balanced life.

The Devil and The 3 Of Wands cards, together, suggest that we should strive to achieve a healthy balance between our material and spiritual pursuits. This means taking the time to nurture our physical and mental health, and to ensure that we are investing in relationships and pursuits that are beneficial to our wellbeing. If we can learn to live in harmony with our material desires, and prioritize our health and wellbeing, we can achieve greater joy and satisfaction in our lives.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Devil and 3 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Devil mean?

When it comes to health, The Devil Tarot card can indicate that a person is engaging in unhealthy behaviors or addictions that are damaging both their physical and mental health. This can include drugs and alcohol, as well as unhealthy behaviors like overeating, smoking, or gambling. The Devil card can also be a warning that the person is obsessed with perfectionism, which can lead to feelings of anxiety and depression.

In terms of finances, The Devil Tarot card can be an indication that a person is in deep debt or stuck in an cycle of unhealthy spending habits. They may also be so caught up in their materialistic desires that they don’t realize how their purchases are impacting their financial situation.

Finally, in relationships, The Devil Tarot card can signify that a person is caught in a cycle of unhealthy attachments, often based on greed and lust. They may be so obsessed with their partner or driven by sexual or emotional desires that they neglect all other aspects of the relationship. This can lead to conflicts, break-ups, and other destructive patterns.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 3 Of Wands Tarot card represents energies related to health and well-being. It encourages the practice of forming and maintaining meaningful connections with team members and those around you in order to achieve optimal health. If you are feeling low, the card reminds you to join forces with others to bring out the best in yourself. It also encourages taking risks in order to expand and grow your knowledge, skills and abilities in order to stay healthy.

Finances  
The 3 Of Wands Tarot card is associated with commerce and financial matters. It often appears when a business venture, collaboration, or financial partnership is needed. It encourages taking risks, as well as forming partnerships with those who can help you obtain your financial goals. It also encourages taking the time to assess and plan before taking action. It reminds us to not forget the importance of developing trust and honest communication between yourself and others in order to obtain financial security.

Relationships  
The 3 Of Wands Tarot card encourages us to nurture relationships and to be open to possibilities that come our way due to the strong element of teamwork and collaboration associated with this card. It encourages us to take the initiative to initiate and form relationships, whether with friends, family, or business partners. It urges us to take risks and to go the extra mile to ensure that these relationships are valuable and worthwhile. It also encourages us to take the time to nurture existing relationships and to take responsibility for our own happiness and that of those we love.
