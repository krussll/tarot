A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Star card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Star card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

Have faith that you can manifest and receive whatever miracles you most long for in life. You are surrounded by love from your family and community, and can depend on their contentment and support as you step into the spotlight and work towards what you truly desire.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Star and 10 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Star and 10 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Star mean?

Health:

The Star Tarot card speaks of health being restored after a time of being depleted or ill. It encourages us to have faith that our bodies will recover, be strong and healthy once again. It is also a reminder to take care of our health, not only physically but also spiritually and mentally. Taking time to meditate, do yoga, eat a healthy diet and get plenty of rest can all help us stay in top physical and mental health. The Star card can act as a reminder to listen to our bodies, practice self-care and recognize the connection between the mind and body.

Finances:

The Star Tarot card brings a message of renewal and hope when it comes to finances. It can act as a sign of new opportunities or a revenue stream that may have been unavailable in the past or that was not being tapped into. The Star card encourages us to have faith in ourselves and our ability to use our resources wisely and make smart decisions. It can even be a source of inspiration and help us to find creative solutions to financial issues.

Relationships:

The Star Tarot card speaks of the potential for renewal and growth when it comes to relationships. It can act as a sign that a stagnant relationship can be reinvigorated. It is also a reminder to have faith in our relationships, to take risks and to not be afraid of honest communication. The Star card reminds us to nurture our relationships, to give them the attention they deserve and to cherish the connections we have with others. It may also be a sign that it is time to start new relationships or to give old ones a second chance.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 10 of Cups Tarot card suggests that overall, your health is in a good place if this card shows up in a Tarot reading. There is a sense of emotional, mental, and spiritual wellness that is likely stemming from overall contentment in other areas of your life. It could mean recently overcoming a health challenge that you have been facing, or it could suggest that you are taking the time for self-care and self-love. Staying mindful and attending to your needs can help you maintain your positive health and energy.

Finances  
The 10 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes financial security and stability for the present and potentially for the future. This card is a sign that your hard work is paying off, and you may be able to live comfortably and not worry about the state of your finances. You have a solid foundation and a safety net in place that will help you continue to build success and security. Once you are content with your financial standing, you will be in a great position to create a sustainable future.

Relationships  
The 10 of Cups Tarot card speaks to strong and meaningful relationships. In a reading, this card is a representation of harmonious relationships, total love and support, and genuine connection. This card can also suggest that you’re being rewarded with meaningful relationships after all the hard work you’ve put into your own personal growth. It can also suggest that you are ready to embark on new relationships, and the hope of stability, contentment, and joy will follow.
