A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and Judgment card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and Judgment have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and Judgment mean together?

The Hanged Man and The Judgment tarot card together can signify a call for introspection and enlightenment. This is a call to take a step back and look at the bigger picture of your life. You are being asked to dig deep into the meaning and purpose of your existence and make a conscious decision about your next steps. This can require a certain amount of self sacrifice and patience, as you reflect on and make a wise choice for your future. In doing this, you will gain clarity that will help you make a final, informed decision.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This is a time to seek out a fresh perspective, to step back and really take the time to assess your relationship from a higher perspective. The Hanged Man card encourages you to be patient and self-reflective, giving guidance to recognize and extract the best of your love life.

The addition of the Judgment card in this combination further emphasizes the need for clarity and commitment. You are being called to decide if you can and want to continue on this journey together. This is a powerful wake-up call to look within and to identify if this is the relationship with which you are meant to proceed. Through great self-reflection and discernment, you can use this combination of cards to determine if the future of your relationship can be defined by greater love, understanding, and spiritual connection.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and Judgment mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health:

The Hanged Man Tarot card can symbolize the need to take a step back and find a different perspective on your health. It can mean surrendering to the process and trusting the journey, even if that means it is going to take longer to achieve the desired outcome. It suggests that one should be willing to make wise, informed decisions and sacrifice their own ego or expectations in order to make the best choices for themselves. This could mean cutting out unhealthy habits such as smoking and unhealthy eating, but also cutting out unhealthy mental habits such as self-criticism. Having the wisdom to listen to your body, to ask it what it needs, and to trust the answers, can be very beneficial on the journey to optimal health.

Finances:

The Hanged Man Tarot card can also pertain to finances. It can suggest the importance of being able to surrender to the unknown, trusting that circumstances will unfold in the best possible way. It can also suggest the importance of making wise financial decisions and having the courage to take risks. This card encourages one to be patient and to trust the process, instead of attempting to force a desired outcome. It can suggest that wise investments need to be made with caution, as well as self-sacrifice when it comes to impulse spending and planning for the future.

Relationships:

The Hanged Man Tarot card also has significance when it comes to relationships. It can suggest the importance of making wise decisions, even if that means sacrificing some of your wants and needs. It can also suggest having the courage to take risks, and the willingness to surrender to the process of a relationship, instead of forcing it to go in a certain direction. The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages one to look at the bigger picture and not get too caught up in the details or their own expectations. It also suggests to be mindful of the effects of their actions on those around them, having the wisdom to make wise choices that are beneficial for everyone involved.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health: The Judgment Tarot card symbolizes a time of personal transformation and clarity. Health-wise this could mean taking the time to reflect on how to further improve one’s health and lifestyle. It could also be a reminder that taking responsibility for one’s health is key. Taking a deeper look at any unhealthy habits and understanding how making changes to one’s diet, mentality and daily routine can greatly improve one’s overall wellbeing, is advised during this time.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the Judgment Tarot card could bring with it an opportunity to reevaluate one’s relationship with money. Taking a look at all expenses and understanding how to manage one’s budget more effectively is a great idea. It is also an opportunity to develop a savings plan or review any investments. Taking control of one’s finances by understanding the decisions that have been made in the past, and creating a plan of action to ensure financial stability and growth in the future is recommended.

Relationships: The Judgment Tarot card can indicate a clear and honest understanding of relationships and how they work. This could mean assessing both the positive and negative impacts of relationships that have been formed. Taking a look at any past hurts and forgiving one’s self and others for any wrong-doings is highly advised for emotional growth. It could also be a call to action to make a conscious effort to improve relationships in order to create healthier connections with others. Taking the time to get to know one’s self better and finding purpose and meaning in relationships is encouraged.
