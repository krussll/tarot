A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card and the Page Of Swords tarot card together signify the importance of collaboration and communication when working together. This card pairing implies that although it is important to work hard and create something of quality, it is also important to make sure to communicate effectively with those involved in the project. Having a curious mindset and being able to find the truth through gossip and prying eyes can help to ensure that the quality and craftsmanship of the work are at the highest standards. This card combination encourages us to use reasoning and logic to find the truth, while also understanding that teamwork and collaboration play essential roles in achieving success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The 3 of Pentacles tarot card is a positive omen, suggesting that team work, collaboration and high quality output are necessary for success in the areas of health, finances, and relationships. When this card appears, it hints that taking the time to work on projects with others and to create something of high quality is going to lead to positive results. It suggests that by joining forces and combining skill and craftsmanship, greater results can be achieved.

For health, this card suggests that when it comes to improving one’s physical health and wellbeing, it is important to enlist the help of others. Whether that means joining a local running group, or working with a personal trainer, or even taking a class together, having a team of people who can help to motivate and hold one accountable is incredibly beneficial. Working together, rather than alone, also allows for greater creativity, innovative ideas and constructive criticism.

For finances, The 3 of Pentacles tarot card means that it is important to form collaborations to get the most out of any financial projects. Working together with like-minded individuals ensures the best possible outcome, as each person can bring something unique to the table. When it comes to money, advice from an expert can be invaluable, so it pays to seek out the right people and make sure that goals are clearly established before beginning any project.

Finally, the 3 of Pentacles tarot card indicates that relationships thrive on collaboration. Whether it is a romantic partnership or a friendship, it is important to work together to ensure that both parties are receiving the support and respect that they need. Communication is key in any collaboration, and it is important to be open and honest with each other to ensure that any issues that arise can be quickly and effectively resolved.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

For Health

The Page Of Swords Tarot card can often signify that you need to be more aware of how your actions and intake can affect your health. If you’ve been getting yourself into unhealthy habits, it’s time to cut them out. Being aware of the truth and being honest with yourself can help you make better decisions that will benefit your physical and mental health. The Page of Swords Tarot card can also be a reminder to stay guarded and to protect yourself from infections and illnesses. If you feel unwell, it’s important to speak up and get the help you need.

For Finances

The Page Of Swords Tarot card can indicate that you need to think more deeply about your finances. Be wise and speak the truth, rather than making decisions that are not in your best interest. Be aware of what you are spending your money on and consider ways to save. In terms of people, the Page of Swords Tarot card may warn you to be careful and don’t be taken advantage of by others. Recognize who is telling you the truth and who is not.

For Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that the truth is essential for relationships. When it comes to your love life, it’s important to be honest and direct with your partner. Don’t gossip, be open and trustworthy with each other, and be aware of any attempts by your partner to spy on you or pry into your business. In terms of friendships, the Page of Swords Tarot card suggests that you need to be cautious when listening to gossip and rumors. Guard your own privacy and be open to hearing all sides of a story before making any decisions.
