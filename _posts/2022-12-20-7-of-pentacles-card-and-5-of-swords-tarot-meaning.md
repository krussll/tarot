## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The 7 of Pentacles and 5 of Swords tarot card combination means that hard work and patience may not be enough to ensure success in a challenging situation. There may be an element of bullying or an abusive relationship involved, as well as the potential for theft or violence. It implies that success in such a situation will come only with the help of those who are more powerful or influential, and that the path ahead is likely to be filled with obstacles. However, it is important to remember that even in adversity, success can still be achieved with the right combination of strategy, determination, and resilience.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 5 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card can represent patience, hard work and delayed success in regards to health. This indicates that it will take time to see the results of any efforts made to improve health. It may mean that those efforts are hard work, like following a strict diet, exercising regularly, and taking the time to prioritize wellness. The efforts may take off in the future, but for now, it is a slow and steady process. This card may also suggest taking a break if the energy and effort are becoming too much.

Finances:

The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes patience, hard work and delayed success when it comes to finances. This card advises being patient with money and other investments. Many of these investments may not have immediate results, but with hard work, they may lead to success later on. It encourages working diligently to reach financial goals such as savings, investments and even debt repayment. The card can also refer to caution when it comes to spending, and refraining from rash decisions.

Relationships:

The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card can also point to patience, hard work and delayed success in relationships. This is a card that reflects that relationships take time, effort and dedication to Grow. It suggests nourishing and tending to relationships with love and patience, knowing that the fruits of hard work may not be immediately visible. It encourages being mindful and taking the time to get to know someone, to allow for a gradual blossoming of a relationship. The card could also signify that you may need to step back and take a pause if emotion and expectations become too much.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 5 of Swords tarot card can indicate several concerning issues related to one’s mental and physical health. Physically, this card can highlight afflictions or illnesses that one may suffer from due to harsh treatment from others – including physical abuse, bullying, or violence. It can also indicate physical fatigue from unhealthy coping behaviors that are used to deal with the effects of such trauma. This can also include low immunity and a weakened immune system. Mentally, the 5 of Swords tarot card can point to mental health issues such as depression, anxiety, and other psychological disorders. It can even indicate a feeling of hopelessness and an inability to break free from the trauma.

Finances

The 5 of Swords tarot card can be a sign of financial instability. It can reveal that one is facing financial hardship due to the costs of dealing with abusive relationships or having to cope with the aftermath of a violent incident. The card also points to possible theft of one’s resources and assets, leaving one feeling vulnerable and exposed. Additionally, this card can signal that one is using unhealthy behaviors to cope with the financial strain, such as gambling or excessive spending, leading to further financial hardship.

Relationships

The 5 of Swords tarot card can indicate a toxic relationship. This card can reveal that one is in an abusive relationship, either verbally, emotionally, or physically. It can also signal that one is in a coercive and manipulative relationship, where one partner is trying to control the other. Additionally, it can point to a feeling of disconnection and a lack of mutual trust. This card can also highlight a tendency towards deception and dishonesty in relationships. It can also indicate an ongoing feeling of being insecure, anxious, and overwhelmed by the relationship.
