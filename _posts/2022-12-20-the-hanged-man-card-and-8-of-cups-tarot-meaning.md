A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Hanged Man and 8 of Cups tarot cards in combination may suggest that you are being called to move forward and take a step back from your current situation. You may need to take a break to gain perspective and open up to new ideas and possibilities. To do this may require a period of self-sacrifice, but the outcome will be a more enlightened view and greater insight. Even if this means moving on from a certain situation, it does not necessarily mean complete abandonment, but rather a deeper understanding of your current situation and the wisdom to decide which way is best for you to go.

The Hanged Man encourages you to look at the situation from a different angle and to make the necessary sacrifices required to reach a better understanding with your partner.

The 8 of Cups indicates that there may be some distance between you and your partner, or a sense of not being connected to the relationship. This is a sign that it is time to move on, to re-evaluate the relationship and your stance towards it. It may be necessary to take a step back or take some time alone to reflect on the past and the changes that can be made.

Ultimately, the Hanged Man and the 8 of Cups in a love reading suggest that changes need to be made in order to progress. It could be time to let go of certain expectations, to make selfless sacrifices, and to view the relationship from a new angle. With some work and determination, it is possible to get closer to the one you love, and to achieve a healthier and more fulfilling relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 8 of Cups Tarot Card strongly suggests the idea of personal retreat and withdrawal. When this card comes up, it often indicates that the querent is in need of taking a step back and focusing on their own health. It could mean that the individual should be taking a pause to properly assess their mental, physical and emotional states. This could include practices such as regular exercise, healthy eating, getting a regular amount of rest, meditation, taking time to be alone, and being mindful. If the querent is feeling overwhelmed or overworked, then this card suggests that time away from their usual routine can help them to come back with a better understanding of their own health.

Finances:  
The 8 of Cups Tarot Card could be indicating a period of financial setback for the querent. It is a call for them to take a step back and reassess their current financial situation. This card recommends that the individual should take the time to look at their current spending and saving habits, and investigate ways to manage their money more responsibly. They may need to make sacrifices in order to achieve their financial goals, and the 8 of Cups suggests that they should not be afraid to do so. Taking the time to properly plan their finances can help the querent to move forward with financial security.

Relationships:  
The 8 of Cups Tarot Card encourages the individual to take a step back and reassess their relationships. It may be necessary for them to retreat from certain connections in order to focus on themselves and their own needs. This could mean letting go of certain individuals, or just taking the time for themselves to reevaluate their own values and boundaries. It is important for the individual to take this time for themselves in order to become clear on what they truly want from their relationships. This can help to ensure that the relationships they enter into are healthy and supportive.
