A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Emperor card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Emperor card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Emperor and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Emperor tarot card and The 10 Of Swords tarot card speaks to a moment of surrender, when all of our carefully laid plans and strategies come to a complete and painful end. The Emperor’s respect and leadership are replaced with the sense of being at “rock bottom,” yet we can still find strength in the Father Figure he represents and the knowledge that this is an ending, rather than a stagnation. This is a necessary step in our journey that will allow for a new beginning, an opportunity for renewal and growth.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Emperor and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Emperor mean?

For health, The Emperor Tarot card signifies that one should take responsibility for their own health, which can mean something different for each individual. It’s a reminder to take preventive measures, be mindful of what one consumes, and take steps to prevent illnesses before they start. It’s also an indication that one should take the initiative and be pro-active when it comes to their health, and seek professional advice or assessments when necessary.

For finances, The Emperor Tarot card represents responsibility and sound judgement. It’s an invitation to plan and strategize, to be mindful of one’s financial goals, and to use wisdom and practicality when it comes to spending, investing and saving. It also suggests that working hard and sticking to a plan are key factors when it comes to achieving financial success.

For relationships, The Emperor can be a reminder to stay open-minded and clear-headed when it comes to interactions with others. It’s an indication that one should take a leadership role when it comes to relationships, preserving a sense of balance and fairness in all interactions. It also suggests that one should use their own sense of judgement and discretion when it comes to making decisions in relationships.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health: The Ten of Swords indicates a health crisis in your life, whether physical or emotional. It indicates that you are at a low point in your health, and that a major change is needed. You may be feeling overwhelmed due to the magnitude of the health issue you are facing, and it may seem like there is no way out. The Ten of Swords requires you to take a step back and re-evaluate your health in order to make the necessary changes.

Finances: The Ten of Swords tarot card serves as a warning that the financial situation you are in is not sustainable. It is time for you to assess your financial situation and make the necessary changes. You should be aware of your current financial situation, as well as creating a plan to restore your financial health. It is important to be realistic and make realistic goals. It is also important to remember that it takes time and effort to get out of a financial crisis.

Relationships: The Ten of Swords tarot card can represent a painful ending of a relationship. The relationship may have ended due to a lack of communication or because it has become toxic. It is important that you take responsibility for any part you may have played in the end of the relationship. This card serves as a reminder that it is better to end a relationship rather than prolong something that is not working. You must also acknowledge your feelings and mourn the loss of the relationship before you can move on.
