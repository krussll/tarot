A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The 5 Of Wands tarot card in combination with The 4 Of Cups tarot card suggests that you may be feeling like you are facing multiple obstacles and challenging rivalry in some area of your life. While the struggle may be wearing you down, you may find that unexpected gifts come out of this cycle, prompting you to take a step back and assess the situation with a fresh perspective. The 4 Of Cups invites you to take a break and recognize that apathy and feeling unfulfilled are not the only options, and that within the challenge lies the opportunity for a new and rewarding adventure.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 5 Of Wands and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 5 Of Wands and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

For Health

The 5 of Wands tarot card can represent physical and mental health challenges. It could be anything from a minor injury or illness to a serious medical condition. It can also signify endless mental and emotional stress due to competing demands and expectations. The 5 of Wands can suggest that you need to push yourself to take better care of your physical and mental wellbeing. Taking a break and allowing yourself to rest, exercise regularly, and eat a healthy diet are essential ways to improve your overall health.

For Finances

The 5 of Wands is a warning that you may face some financial challenges. It could be a sign that you’re in the midst of a competitive battle for money, such as a financial dispute or wage negotiations. It could also represent difficulty in managing your budget or making ends meet. The 5 of Wands can suggest that it’s important to take a step back and make smart financial decisions. Researching budgeting strategies, understanding the pros and cons of different financial products, and creating a financial plan will help you move towards financial stability.

For Relationships

The 5 of Wands tarot card can indicate that rivalry and competition are impacting your relationships. It could signify feelings of competition or jealousy between friends, rivals in romantic relationships, or conflicts in family dynamics. The 5 of Wands suggests that it’s important to take a step back and examine the root of the problem. Learning to communicate openly and honestly with those around you, manage expectations, and work through differences are essential components of healthy relationships.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 4 Of Cups Tarot card can be an indication of a lack of nurturing in health matters. It can relate to people who are unfulfilled in their current physical state and feel as though they are missing out on something important. This could include not taking proper care of themselves and not prioritizing their overall health, such as regularly exercising and eating well. This could lead to feelings of resentment and emptiness that can affect your physical and mental state.

Finances

When it comes to finances, The 4 Of Cups Tarot card can signify carelessness or apathy. This could be a sign that someone is not taking their financial future seriously enough and is not taking the necessary steps to secure their financial stability. This card can also represent being in a rut, where it seems the same financial situation is happening over and over again and no progress is being made.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, The 4 Of Cups Tarot card can be a sign that one or both partners are feeling unfulfilled or unappreciated. This could lead to feelings of apathy and a lack of connection between the two people. It could also indicate that someone is not getting the emotional support they need, leaving them feeling lost and alone. It can also represent unexpected surprises, such as someone coming in with a gift or a kind word to make the other person feel better.
