You might feel stuck in a rut of unfulfilled desires, abandoned dreams and a lack of love or appreciation. You may be surprised to receive unexpected gifts or blessings, but it is unlikely to fill the void within. It is important to take the time to process any losses and to recognize the feeling of emptiness it brings. Find the courage to move forward, without the fear of further loss. Recognize the power of vulnerability and courage in yourself and others, to fight the apathy you have been feeling.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

The 5 Of Cups Tarot Card is associated with ill health, as it symbolizes deep sadness and regret that can have a physical toll. This card can represent a feeling of being overwhelmed and hopeless, which can lead to a weakened immune system and decreased energy levels. It can also represent inactivity, as the person affected by this card may choose to stay in bed or sleep too much, rather than take action toward healthier habits.

The 5 Of Cups Tarot Card and Finances

The 5 Of Cups Tarot Card indicates a period of significant financial loss. This card can represent a sense of despair when it comes to money, with the person feeling as though they have no control over their financial situation. This card may also indicate a period of overspending, as an attempt to make up for the financial losses.

The 5 Of Cups Tarot Card and Relationships

The 5 Of Cups Tarot Card can signify a broken relationship or the end of a relationship. This card suggests that the person affected has experienced a feeling of abandonment or emotional betrayal, which can lead to feelings of sadness and grief. The person may also feel unable to trust new relationships, as they are unable to move on from their past experiences.
