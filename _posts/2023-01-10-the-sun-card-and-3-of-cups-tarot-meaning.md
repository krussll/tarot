A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Sun tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together symbolize a bright and joyful future in your life. This combination of cards is a reminder that your success and happiness come from having good relationships with others. With the positive energy of friendship and celebration radiating from The 3 Of Cups card, it is likely that you will find joy and accomplishment with the support of strong and caring people in your life. The Sun card offers an optimistic outlook that your endeavors will be successful and lead to true material happiness.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Sun and 3 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Sun and 3 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does The Sun mean?

Health: When The Sun card appears in a Tarot reading, it is indicative of good health, harmony and balance in both the physical and emotional realms. On a physical level, it may suggest that the health of the querent is excellent and they have great energy levels. It could also mean that any current medical issues the querent may have will be resolved positively. Emotionally, The Sun card can bring feelings of joy, happiness and security. Relationships with family and friends are likely to be harmonious and the querent may find themselves able to easily express their feelings and emotions.

Finances: The Sun Tarot card is associated with wealth and abundance; when it appears in a reading, it can indicate that the querent is in a good financial situation. It may also suggest that the querent has a job that provides a steady income, or that a new source of income is on the horizon. The Sun card can also be a sign of great success in their business dealings, investments, or even entrepreneurial endeavours.

Relationships: The Sun Tarot card can be a very strong indicator of positive relationships. In a romantic Tarot reading, The Sun card can mean that the relationship is thriving and full of joy. The couple in question may have found stability, as well as emotional and physical intimacy. The querent may also find that they have great chemistry with their partner, with little to no conflicts. In other relationships, The Sun card may signify that the querent is surrounded by supportive and loyal friends, who bring them much joy and happiness.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 3 of Cups Tarot card represents good health, prosperity, and abundance. It signifies the rewards that come with positive lifestyle choices such as exercise, nutrition and relaxation. This card may signify a time to connect with your body and nourish it in a positive way, such as self-care practices like yoga and meditation. It may show the importance of a balanced, fulfilling lifestyle that includes meaningful relationships, self-care, and celebration.

Finances:

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a reminder that financial well-being involves more than money. It is connected to the idea of abundance, both in the literal sense and in the sense of enjoying life’s experiences and social gatherings. Abundance is not only about making money, but about pursuing dreams and making lasting connections. This card encourages us to use our resources in a positive way, by planning and investing in ourselves and our relationships.

Relationships:

The 3 of Cups Tarot card encourages us to nurture meaningful relationships. It speaks to the importance of forming supportive circles that extend beyond just romantic or family relationships. In this card, we see three people engaging in a celebratory activity, illustrating the idea that joy and support are found in positive relationships. It is important to take time for the people who bring us joy and to return the favour to those in need. The 3 of Cups Tarot reminds us to cherish, honour and build meaningful relationships in order to augment our lives.
