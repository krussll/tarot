A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

When the 8 of Wands and 6 of Pentacles come up together in a reading, it suggests that a fast-paced situation is about to be resolved by benevolent help and generosity. There may be the need for a quick change of direction, such as a sudden offer of assistance or a request for help. This could be a business or personal matter, but whatever it is, it should bring good news. It could also be a way to make a partnership even stronger. It could be a way of accelerating the healing process in a relationship. It could be a way of creating a more equitable situation for both parties. It could be the opportunity for a new relationship of generosity, one that is mutually beneficial.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 6 Of Pentacles meanwhile denotes a period of mutual understanding, give and take, and act of genuine generosity between the two of you. In a nutshell, this card combination indicates that you and your partner are in a period of real growth and expansion. It suggests that you are both being generous and understanding towards one another, allowing the relationship to evolve and blossom. There is no telling what exciting breakthroughs you may make together in the coming days, weeks or months, so make sure to enjoy the journey and the changes that come with it.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health

When The 8 Of Wands card appears in a health reading, it indicates a quick and positive change in health. It suggests that a health issue, condition or illness will soon be resolved and can bring a sense of relief that healing and recovery is near. Alternatively, this card indicates that an effective, timely health plan will be implemented soon and there is no need to worry, as the transition will be smooth. The 8 of Wands card can also suggest that the querent will hear good news in regards to their health soon and it may come in the form of a medical test result or diagnosis, which will bring on a sense of vitality and energy.

Finances

The 8 Of Wands card in a financial context highlights the importance of acting quickly as any delay might hamper the success of financial goals. The 8 of Wands is a card of change and transition, so it can point to a transition of funds to a more beneficial position. It can mean that money is coming quickly and unexpectedly, such as an inheritance or a raise. Additionally, the 8 of Wands card can advise the querent to do the necessary research in order to make the most of the opportunity. It is a reminder to stay vigilant and act fast to capitalize on the given circumstances in order to achieve desired financial goals.

Relationships

The 8 Of Wands Tarot card in a relationship context suggests a time of swift change. This card suggests that something new is about to enter the relationship, either from the outside or from within the relationship itself. Could there be a new opportunity or a new venture? It could be a new development in a current relationship, or it could be the beginning of a new one. It denotes the start of something new, a fresh beginning in the relationship and encourages swift action. It can also indicate that news is arriving soon, which will bring clarity and direction to the relationship. Despite the sudden change, The 8 Of Wands Tarot card brings assurance that it will be a smooth transition as long as both parties keep an open mind and are willing to work together.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is about giving and taking in balance. It’s a reminder that balance is key to maintaining health, financial security, and positive relationships.

Health:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to look at all aspects of your health and wellness, including physical, mental, spiritual and emotional. It speaks to finding a balance between rest and exercise, leisure activities and work, healthy eating and indulging. This card encourages you to look out for yourself, find the balance between too much and too little in order to maintain a well-rounded lifestyle.

Finances:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card also relates to finances and money. It speaks to the importance of balance when it comes to your finances. It encourages you to look for opportunities for both saving and spending, and take advantage of both in moderation. Too much saving can prevent you from having fun, while too much spending can lead to debt. It’s important to practice self-control and find the middle ground when it comes to your finances.

Relationships:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card also applies to relationships with others. It encourages you to look for a balance between giving and taking when dealing with relationships. It’s important to maintain a healthy balance between helping others and taking care of your own needs. Too much “giving” can cause you to be depleted, while too much “taking” can make relationships one-sided. The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card reminds you to find a balance between the two in order to maintain healthy, balanced relationships with others.
