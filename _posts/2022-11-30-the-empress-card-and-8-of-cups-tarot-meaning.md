However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Empress card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Empress and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Empress and the 8 of Cups together represent the concept of necessary endings leading to a new cycle of beginnings. They represent the feeling of being ready to release the old in order to make room for the new. This could be in the form of letting go of a project or emotional attachment, or simply taking a step back from a situation that has become too overwhelming. This is not a time of abandonment; it is a time of necessary transition in order to create something even better, more fruitful, and more meaningful. The Empress and 8 of Cups signify the knowledge that in order to manifest abundance and fertility, sometimes one must take a retreat to gain new perspective and clarity. The energy of the Empress will protect and nurture you so that you can have a safe space to discover what is meant for you and take the necessary steps to fulfill your potential.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Empress and 8 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Empress mean?

Health:

When it comes to health, The Empress Tarot card is a symbol of fertility and abundance. It is the card of creation and as such, is associated with concepts of good health. This card may represent your own physical health that is strong and vibrant, or it may speak of health from within and the natural balance that your body maintains. Either way, The Empress’s energy is one of nurturing and protection, and it is said that those who draw this card are often blessed with good health and a long life.

Finances:

The Empress Tarot card is also associated with financial abundance, and those who draw this card may experience a sudden influx of wealth. This could mean a large inheritance, winning the lottery, a salary increase, finding a new job, or even a windfall. In addition, The Empress’s energy is one of creation and as such, it may point to creativity as a source of financial abundance. This could be through artistic endeavors, starting a business, or even a side hustle.

Relationships:

When it comes to relationships, The Empress Tarot card is all about nurturing. This card brings a sense of unconditional love, gentleness, and understanding. It speaks to the idea that in order for a relationship to thrive, it must be built on a solid foundation of mutual respect and unconditional love. Those who draw this card often find the strength to make their relationships stronger, more secure, and more meaningful. It encourages us to look deeply at our own needs and issues and to nurture our relationships with compassion and understanding. It is a reminder that we should strive to create relationships that are both mutually supportive and full of abundance and joy.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is all about making tough choices, retreating and moving on. This card implies that no matter how difficult the decision may be, it is best to accept it and move forward. This can be an especially difficult card to interpret in terms of health, finances, and relationships.

In terms of health, the 8 of Cups can be a sign that it’s time to withdraw, and accept that the situation is out of your control. It might be a sign that it’s time to slow down and focus on self-care, or a sign to move on from any unhealthy habits and relationships.

In terms of finances, the 8 of Cups can be a sign of withdrawing from a risky situation. It could mean it’s time to let go of a bad investment or to cease a bad spending habit. It could also be a sign to retreat from an emotionally draining situation and focus on financial security.

In terms of relationships, the 8 of Cups could mean it’s time to move on from a toxic relationship or to take a step back from a situation that is no longer bringing any happiness. This card could be a prompt to take the time to care for yourself and to focus on finding the right relationship.

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is all about recognizing that sometimes you need to let go and move on in order to find the right path forward. This card encourages you to focus on taking care of yourself and to trust that the right path will present itself eventually.
