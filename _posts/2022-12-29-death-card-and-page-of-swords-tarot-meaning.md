A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Death card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Death tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card combined indicate that it is time for you to end an outdated or stagnant situation and move on, but not before gathering some important information. You will need to subtly observe and pay attention to the people and circumstances around you, as they contain valuable truths that will help you during this time of transformation. You may feel as though you are a curious kid, learning and discovering new things as you go. The Death card reveals that the changes to come will be big and powerful, but the Page Of Swords encourages you to embrace the curiosity and excitement in this process.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and Page Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health

The Death Tarot card indicates a looming transition in health matters. This could be an illness or health issue coming to a close, or it might be the start of a new journey. It may be a physical or mental health crisis that leads to self-improvement and transformation. It doesn’t necessarily mean the end of life, but it does mean that old habits, routines, and ways of living must be let go of in order to make way for new and healthier ones. This card can offer insight into times when we need to let go and accept that our current health situation is no longer serving us, and that it is time to move on and make necessary changes.

Finances

The Death Tarot card often signals a period of transformation in financial matters. It could be the ending of a job, the dissolution of a business, or the end of a large financial transaction. It could mean a sudden and drastic change of lifestyle or a loss of money. But it could also mark a period of great growth, like the starting of a new business or the acquisition of a large sum of money. It could point to the death of old patterns of thought and behavior, allowing for new opportunities and more responsible handling of money. In most cases, the Death Tarot card indicates a new start and a period of transition where financial matters are concerned.

Relationships

The Death Tarot card can signal a dramatic change or end to a relationship. This could be the ending of a romantic partnership, of a friendship, or of any type of relationship. It could also indicate a period of transformation and renewal, when old patterns of behavior must be let go of in order to embrace new and more meaningful connections. This card calls for acceptance of the end of a relationship, and a willingness to move on and be open to new connections. It may represent a chance to start anew and create better, healthier relationships.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health

The Page of Swords Tarot card is associated with a need for a change when it comes to health. This change may involve a spiritual, physical or mental shift. On the physical level, this card can suggest illness and caution. On the spiritual level, the Page of Swords Tarot card can signify alertness and heightened awareness. This card can also signify a need for focused attention on matters related to health. The Page of Swords Tarot card can signify being inquisitive and observant about health, always looking for ways to improve and make progress.

Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card can signify caution and vigilance when it comes to matters related to finances. This card warns of a need to be watchful and alert to potentially misleading or deceptive financial information. It can also signify a need to think critically and objectively when it comes to financial decisions. This card can signify a need to be fair and honest in financial dealings, to make sure all involved parties are getting what they need.

Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card can signify communication and honest dialogue in relationships. This card can signify being open, honest and direct with loved ones. It can also signify being observant, noticing subtle shifts between yourself and your partner. The Page of Swords Tarot card can also signify being aware of underlying tensions in a relationship that may need to be discussed. This card can signify a need to express oneself without fear or judgment, and to be honest about one’s thoughts and feelings.
