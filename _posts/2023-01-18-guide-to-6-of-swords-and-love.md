Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 6 Of Swords card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 6 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 6 Of Swords.

## 6 Of Swords And Love – An Overview

The 6 of Swords tarot card is associated with the realm of health and wellbeing, representing a necessary transition to a healthier lifestyle.

It symbolizes the need to leave behind unhealthy habits and begin anew.

Whether this transition is physical, mental, or spiritual, it is an indication that a change needs to be made in order to achieve greater health, balance, and harmony.

It could represent a new diet or exercise plan, taking steps to improve mental health, or embarking on a spiritual journey.

Whatever the case, the 6 of Swords suggests that the time has come to make a move in the direction of better health.

## Upright Meanings For 6 Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

The 6 of Swords tarot card is often associated with a necessary transition or relocation in relationships.

This card can represent a change in the relationship, such as a break-up, a separation, or a move to a different location.

It could also represent the need for a fresh start, or for a couple to gain more emotional distance from each other in order to take a step back and evaluate the relationship.

This card is a reminder that sometimes, change is necessary in order for a relationship to grow and become stronger.

It also reminds us that even though a transition may be difficult, it can ultimately lead to a more fulfilling and meaningful relationship.

### What It Means For The Future

The 6 of Swords tarot card is a sign that your future love may involve a necessary transition or relocation.

This may mean that you have to move to a different city, state, or even country in order to be with the person you are meant to be with.

You may also have to make a big change in your life in order to make room for this relationship.

This card is a sign that the transition will be necessary for your future love, but it will be worth it in the end.

You may have to take a leap of faith, but the rewards will be worth the risk.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 6 of Swords tarot card is a reminder that sometimes love means letting go.

This card often symbolizes a necessary transition, and it can mean that it’s time to move on from a certain situation, whether it involves a relationship or something else entirely.

It’s a reminder that sometimes the most loving thing we can do is to allow ourselves and those close to us to make the moves necessary in order to find our true purpose.

It may be time to relocate, or to move away from a current situation.

Even though this can be a difficult and painful decision, it can be the best thing for the love of those close to us.

By allowing ourselves and those we care about to transition, we are allowing them to live out their true potential and become the best version of themselves.

## Reversed Meanings For 6 Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The 6 of Swords is reversed, it indicates a difficulty in transitioning in a relationship.

This can mean that a couple is having difficulty shifting from one stage of a relationship to the next, from dating to being exclusive, from being exclusive to being married, and so on.

This can also signify that both parties involved are having a hard time getting to the point where they feel comfortable and trusting enough to move forward.

It could mean that one partner is too afraid to take the next step, or that the other partner is too impatient to wait for the relationship to grow naturally.

It can also be an indication that both people involved are struggling to adjust to each other, or that there is a lack of understanding or communication between them.

This card often signifies that there is an imbalance in the relationship, such as one person trying to control the other or one person feeling like they have to carry the weight of the relationship.

In order to move forward, both parties will need to be willing to talk openly and honestly about their needs and desires in order to come to a mutual understanding.

With the right amount of patience and effort, this transition can be a positive one that will lead to a beautiful and lasting relationship.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 6 of Swords tarot card represents a period of stagnation and feeling stuck in the current state of affairs.

It is a warning that there is a need for a change in order to progress and find true happiness and fulfilment.

When it comes to love, this card is a sign that there may be a need to move on from the current relationship or situation in order to find the love you desire.

It is a sign that a shift in attitude and approach is needed in order to break free from the current state of stagnation and move closer to a new and more fulfilling relationship.

This doesn’t necessarily mean that the current relationship is doomed, but that it is necessary to take a step back and reflect on what is and isn’t working in order to find the right path.

The reversed 6 of Swords suggests that it may be necessary to take a break, or even permanently end the current relationship in order to find the level of love and connection you are looking for.

The reversed 6 of Swords encourages you to focus on what is genuinely important to you in a relationship, and to look for a partner who can provide that.

It is a reminder that true love is possible, and that it is worth taking a chance and making a move towards it.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed 6 of Swords appears in a tarot reading, it typically has to do with the love of those close to you.

This card indicates that a difficult transition or journey is about to take place in your life and it will involve those closest to you.

You may be faced with the difficult decision of separating from a loved one or moving away from them.

This could be due to a job relocation or a change in circumstances.

Alternatively, you may be presented with the opportunity to move closer to a loved one, allowing you to deepen and strengthen the bond between you.

The reversed 6 of Swords is telling you to take your time and think carefully before making any decisions.

It is important to make sure that any changes that are made will be beneficial in the long run and will not negatively affect the relationship.

If you are considering a move that will separate you from a loved one, make sure you communicate your feelings and thoughts with them.

It may be difficult, but it is essential that they understand your reasons and are comfortable with your decision.

The reversed 6 of Swords is a reminder to take care of yourself and your relationships during this period of transition.
