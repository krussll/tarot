A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 Of Swords tarot card combined with The 7 Of Cups card represents a need or desire to make a major transition, or relocating. You may feel the need to move away, either physically or emotionally. However, this transition requires that you first assess the many choices before you, which can be a difficult task due to your commitment issues. It is important for you to consider all of your options before taking the next step, but once you make the decision, you can then move forward with confidence and certainty.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a necessary transition in health matters. It could mean quitting an unhealthy lifestyle—whether that means bad dietary habits, smoking, or substance use—for a healthier one. There could be a need to move away from an environment that is not conducive to good health—for example, a location where air pollution is high or food options are limited. Physically relocating to an environment with clean air, clean water, and healthy food can be a step toward improved health. Switching doctors, treatments, or medications could be part of the transition.

Finances

The 6 of Swords Tarot card indicates a transition in finances, as well. It could involve a relocation or change of job that would lead to increased financial security. It might mean taking steps to reduce debt or investing in different financial products. It could also mean a shift in mindset around money, one that involves greater financial responsibility or planning for the future.

Relationships

The 6 of Swords Tarot card could signify a transition in relationships. It could mean moving away from a toxic relationship or leaving an unhealthy situation. It could involve leaving behind people or a place that is not supportive of positive relationships. On the other hand, it could also be about taking steps to build meaningful connections with people. It could also symbolize an emotional transition, letting go of old pain or processing unresolved issues. In all cases, the 6 of Swords Tarot card could be an indicator of necessary growth to move forward in life.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health: The Seven of Cups tarot card is a sign of too many choices in the realm of one’s health or in their diet or lifestyle. It signifies there are too many options, and it can be difficult to make a definitive choice. This card can also be a warning that one’s health is in jeopardy due to their refusal to commit to a healthier lifestyle or a healthier diet. It’s important for one to recognize potential harms that can come from too much indecision in regards to one’s health.

Finances: The Seven of Cups tarot card speaks to making choices regarding one’s finances. It can show that one is being too indecisive in regards to their finances, whether it’s due to too many different investments and financial projects, or it could be that one is indecisive on how to handle their debt. This card can be a sign that one is putting themselves in a precarious situation if they are not careful and thoughtful when it comes to making financial decisions.

Relationships: The Seven of Cups tarot card can be a symbol of commitment issues in relationships. It can also be a sign of too many choices in the realm of relationships and love. It can be a signal that one is refusing to commit to a relationship or maybe they are trying to juggle too many different relationships at once. It could be an indication that one needs to choose from the options they have in front of them and make a decision in order to move on and develop healthy, meaningful relationships.
