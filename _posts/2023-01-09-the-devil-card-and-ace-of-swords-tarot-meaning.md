A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and Ace Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Devil card and Ace Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The Devil tarot card and The Ace Of Swords tarot card together represent the challenge of combing addiction and materialism with mental clarity and new ideas. The Devil card reminds us of the importance of avoiding the pitfalls of addiction, greed, envy and obsession and instead embracing a more balanced approach to life. The Ace Of Swords teaches us that we can overcome our addictions and obsessions if we focus on creating a life with clarity of thought and new ideas. It is possible to be mindful of our behaviours and make choices that are in alignment with our long-term goals. Together, these two tarot cards provide a powerful message that we can break free of our addictions by opening our minds to new ideas and embracing mental clarity.

### What does The Devil and Ace Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The Devil and The Ace of Swords tarot cards are combined together in a love reading, they signify a complex situation. On the one hand, The Devil card can represent strong desires, obsession, and addictions that could be affecting your relationship. It could mean that you are feeling trapped in unhealthy patterns of behavior, perhaps due to an addiction to your partner or to passionate emotions. The Devil speaks to us of attachment, and it could be that you are having difficulty letting go of certain expectations or desires of your partner.

On the other hand, the Ace of Swords card brings with it a potential for new ideas, new ways of thinking, and a fresh perspective on life. It could mean that clarity is coming, and that you may soon find yourself presented with a new outlook on your relationship. This could be a time for you to take some time for personal reflection, to assess your wants and needs, and to decide if the existing situation is working for you or not. It could also be a time to have a honest and open conversations with your partner about your feelings, and to determine what changes can be made to ensure the health and success of your relationship.

The combination of The Devil and The Ace of Swords tarot cards can also mean that it is time to release destructive patterns, to be honest with yourself and your partner, and to make difficult decisions. It may be a time that requires courage and strength, but it can also be a time of enormous growth and self-empowerment. With a clear head and courage in your heart, you can move forward in your love life and create a path that works for both of you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Devil and Ace Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card may mean different things for health, finances, and relationships. When it comes to health, this card typically signifies addiction or an obsession with unhealthy habits. It might indicate a person who is enslaved by their substance abuse, or an individual who is unable to break free from unhealthy patterns that are leading to their decline in health. It could also represent an individual who is fixated on unhealthy ideas, such as getting thin at any cost, and is willing to do anything that will achieve this goal, no matter the cost.

When it comes to finances, the Devil card can suggest an unhealthy attitude towards money. It often symbolizes greed, materialism, and a tendency to prioritize money before anything else, even health and relationships. The card could represent a state of financial debt due to poor decision-making, or an individual who is recklessly spending money without any consideration for their future.

For relationships, the Devil card could indicate an unhealthy and controlling power dynamic. It may represent a person who is dominating and possessive, unable to give their partner the space and autonomy they need to grow. It can also suggest a lack of trust, or an individual that is ultimately trying to control their partner and the relationship itself.

The Devil card often suggests a state of entrapment, where a person is unable to break free from their self-destructive behaviors and habits. It can be a harsh reminder of how our destructive choices and unconscious behaviors can affect our health, finances, and relationships. The card is a reminder to be mindful of the decisions we make, and the consequences it can have on ourselves and those close to us. In this way, The Devil card is a powerful reminder to take responsibility for our actions and to make conscious and thoughtful decisions.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

Health: The Ace of Swords Tarot card represents a mental and physical sharpness, which can have an effect on your health. It suggests that you need to be clear-headed, decisive, and use your intellect to make the best decisions for your health. It can also signify surgery, and that it is time to make changes to your health so that you can be the best version of yourself and achieve optimal health.

Finances: The Ace of Swords Tarot card relates to finances because it signifies new ideas and a need to use your intellect. This card can represent the power to think outside the box and tap into new ways to make money. It could mean making a new business venture, creating a side hustle, and having the courage to try something different. Whatever the case may be, it encourages you to think critically and use your wits to create financial success.

Relationships: The Ace of Swords Tarot card also relates to relationships because it stands for clarity and truth. This card encourages you to express yourself honestly and speak up when necessary. It suggests that you talk openly and honestly in your relationships and be willing to resolve any conflicts that arise. This card can also mean having the courage to have difficult conversations and being honest and direct with friends, family, and partners.
