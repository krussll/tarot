## What does The Hanged Man and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The Hanged Man and the 6 of Wands tarot cards together signify a journey of self-reflection and introspection potentially leading to a powerful victory. In this journey, you may be called to make difficult decisions, accept and forgive life’s inherent injustices, and sacrifice a certain degree of comfort in order to gain a greater wisdom. This wisdom may prove invaluable when you ultimately achieve the recognition and success you desire. The cards together remind you to take pause and reassess before taking further action, and to remember that the rewards of inner peace and self-assurance always outweigh the external rewards of status and recognition.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card is full of meaning that can be applied to any situation, including health, finances and relationships. When it comes to health, this card speaks of the need for wisdom and self-care. It encourages us to think outside the box and to look for new ways to take care of our bodies. We should be willing to make some sacrifices in order to prioritize our mental and physical wellbeing. This could involve seeking out professional help to treat any medical conditions or seeing a counsellor to help us with emotional issues.

When it comes to finances, The Hanged Man Tarot card is a reminder that we must be wise with our money. It encourages us to put our financial future first, to look at our options and to make informed decisions. We should be willing to sacrifice some of our present time and lifestyle in order to put money away for the future. We can also use this card as a reminder that it’s okay to take some risks, but only if we have done our research and understand the risks associated with any investments or financial decisions we might make.

Last but not least, The Hanged Man Tarot card can offer us guidance when it comes to our relationships. It can point us to the importance of being honest with our partners, being willing to compromise, and looking for creative solutions to any disagreements that may arise. This card also serves as a reminder that relationships of any kind require some self-sacrifice if we want them to last. We must be willing to put in the effort and take the time to understand our partners if we wish to build meaningful bonds.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The Six of Wands Tarot card symbolizes victory, award, recognition, good news and success. In health, it is a sign that one has overcome a difficult illness or medical condition and is now on the path to recovery. It can also signify an upcoming medical advancement or healing journey that can bring an unexpected result. In finances, it signifies that one is achieving financial stability and a period of financial abundance. It can represent an increase in income or savings, a successful investment, or a potential windfall. In relationships, this card indicates that a two-way effort from both parties is now paying off. It can be a sign of harmony and mutual understanding, or even a significant milestone such as an engagement or marriage. It signifies a shared sense of accomplishment, the growth of a meaningful connection, and the strengthening of a bond between partners.
