Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Strength card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Strength, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Strength.

## Strength And Health – An Overview

The Strength tarot card is a reminder to take care of yourself, both mentally and physically.

It suggests that having a strong sense of self-love and self-respect will give you the courage to make healthy choices in your life.

It encourages you to nurture yourself, to practice self-care, and to show yourself the same unconditional love you give to others.

Having strength and self-confidence will help you to make decisions that are good for your health, such as following a nutritious diet, exercising regularly, and getting enough rest.

The Strength encourages you to stay true to your values and to take care of yourself, both physically and mentally.

## Upright Meanings For Strength And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Strength tarot card speaks to the importance of self-love, unconditional love, and self-respect in regards to health.

When we love ourselves, treat ourselves with respect, and have the courage to take care of our physical and mental health, we are setting ourselves up for a lifetime of health and wellness.

It is only through self-love and self-respect that we can make the necessary changes to our lifestyle that will lead to lasting health and wellbeing.

Furthermore, having the courage to make decisions for our health, even when it feels difficult, is a sign of strength.

So, when we embrace The Strength tarot card into our wellness routines, we can be assured that we are taking the necessary steps to cultivate a life of health and wellbeing.

### What It Means For The Future

The Strength tarot card is an encouraging reminder of the importance of self-love and self-respect in my future health.

It encourages me to use courage to face any challenges that may come my way, and to be confident in my own abilities and potential.

This card suggests that if I focus on creating an environment of unconditional love and self-respect, I will be able to nurture my physical and mental health and create a healthier, more balanced life.

By putting faith in myself and knowing that I can overcome any obstacle, I will open the door for a healthier future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Strength tarot card is a reminder to show those close to me an unconditional love that is rooted in self-respect and courage.

It means that I should offer them my support and understanding, even if it means making difficult decisions for the sake of their well-being.

It means that I should be there for them and be willing to have difficult conversations when needed, so that I can help them stay on the path toward a healthy and fulfilling life.

By being a source of strength and compassion, I can help them feel empowered and motivated in the face of difficult times.

## Reversed Meanings For Strength And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed Strength card can be a sign of unhealthy behaviors or habits, as well as a lack of self-awareness or self-control.

It is a reminder to take better care of ourselves and to check in with our bodies and our minds.

When this card appears reversed, it can be telling us to examine our behavior and ask ourselves if we are truly honoring and respecting our physical and mental health.

The reversed Strength card can also mean that we are facing difficulties in our relationships and need to take a step back and practice self-care.

We may be overreaching and pushing ourselves too far, or ignoring our own needs in order to please others.

This card is a reminder to prioritize our own well-being and to remember that we can’t be of service to others if we are not taking care of ourselves first.

In conclusion, when the reversed Strength card appears, it is a sign that it’s time to be honest with yourself and to take a moment for self-reflection.

It can be a reminder to take better care of our physical and mental health and to put ourselves first.

By recognizing our needs and taking steps to meet them, we can restore balance and strength in our lives.

### What It Means For The Future

When The Strength card appears in its reversed position, it is a sign that you may be struggling with your own health, both mentally and physically.

You may feel weak, powerless, and out of control of your body.

This can manifest in a number of ways, such as a lack of energy, difficulty sleeping, a weakened immune system, or physical pain.

It could also be a sign of ongoing emotional stress or mental health issues.

The reversed Strength card is a gentle reminder to practice self-care and self-love.

Take time for yourself to relax and to practice positive self-talk.

Spend time outdoors in nature, meditate, and make sure you are getting proper nutrition and enough sleep.

Taking proper care of your physical and mental health will empower you to be strong and resilient, and will help you to reclaim your strength and courage.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Strength tarot card appears in its reversed position, it can indicate feelings of cowardice, a lack of self-love, and a lack of self-respect.

This can be especially true when it comes to physical health and wellness.

When the card appears reversed, it can suggest that the seeker is neglecting their physical health and wellness due to feelings of fear and insecurity.

They may be struggling to find the courage to make healthy lifestyle changes, such as increasing physical activity, eating healthier, or seeking mental health treatment.

Reversed The Strength can also indicate feelings of powerlessness when it comes to health and wellness.

The seeker may be feeling overwhelmed by their health issues and unable to find the courage to take the steps needed to manage their physical or mental health.

They may be suffering from emotional imbalances such as depression or anxiety, and these emotional issues can cause them to neglect their physical health.

In this case, the reversed Strength card can indicate the need for self-love and self-respect in order to achieve physical health and wellness.
