A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Judgment and The 5 Of Cups tarot cards is a powerful blessing. It suggests that a situation that has been causing you grief and sadness is coming to a resolution and you will be able to move on. The Judgment tarot card encourages you to make clarity and a final decision about the issue, which will bring a sense of peace and understanding. It also gives you the opportunity to reflect on your life’s purpose and your experiences; it is a wake-up call from the Universe, reminding you to seek clarity and growth from every situation you find yourself in. The 5 of Cups tarot card indicates that you will have to accept the losses that have come from the situation, as well as any regret and grief, but it is in accepting this that you will be able to move on and find a new path.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Judgment and 5 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The Judgment tarot card in combination with The 5 Of Cups tarot card in the context of love is quite a difficult card combination to interpret. On the one hand, this card combination suggests a moment of clarity and a final decision in matters concerning your relationships. This might be an opportunity to take a hard look at your feelings and decide whether or not you are ready to make a commitment to someone. It is also possible that this moment of clarity could be a wake-up call that brings to light any unresolved issues or unspoken tension in your relationship.

On the other hand, the 5 Of Cups card brings a message of loss and regret. This could be a sign that you have unresolved feelings for an ex or that you feel abandoned or unloved in your current relationship. It is important to take a step back and truly examine your emotions in order to determine if your current relationship is truly worth preserving. You may discover that the path of least resistance is to draw a line and move on.

Ultimately, the Judgment tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card together signify a difficult decision. You may be faced with a choice between following your heart or letting go of what doesn’t make you happy. In either case, the message of this card combination is to find the courage to become clear on your life’s purpose and make a decision that will bring clarity to your love life.

### What does Judgment and 5 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When The Judgment tarot card and The 5 Of Cups come together in a reading about finances and job, it suggests that a difficult decision is looming and the outcome could be a source of great regret. It is hinting that if you don’t make a move now, you will miss an opportunity to find your life’s purpose and your career path. The Judgment encourages you to look at the facts rationally and weigh up the pros and cons. It may be the case that you have to make a sacrifice and accept a degree of loss to realize your ambitions.

The 5 Of Cups could suggest that tough choices need to be made and it might mean feeling like you have to leave something or someone behind in order to pursue a clearer path for yourself. Your current job may no longer suit your values and you may be feeling stuck in a rut with no way out. This card encourages you to look at the bigger picture and recognise that although there may be a period of sadness, the path ahead should be rewarding in the long-term.

This combination of cards is telling you to make a carefully thought-out decision and take a leap of faith. It is likely that the outcome may be daunting but know that you are taking steps towards something greater. It may be necessary to experience some hardships and losses so you can achieve the life purpose and your ideal career. Trust that you have the inner strength to face and conquer whatever lies ahead.

### What does Judgment and 5 Of Cups mean together for your health?

When Judgment and the 5 of Cups appear together, it hints at a sense of deep regret and loss related to one’s health. It can be thought of as a wake-up call, a reminder from the Universe to take a step back and reflect on your current choices, decisions and lifestyle. It is a sign that now is the time to take serious action in order to get back on track and to start making healthier choices for your body, mind, and spirit.

The Judgment card suggests that it may be time to take a long, hard look at any beliefs and habits that are counter to good health. It may be time to move away from any destructive behaviors that have been negatively impacting your physical, emotional and spiritual well-being. It could be time to seek out counseling and support, or to look into a healthier lifestyle that may include modifications to one’s diet and exercise plan.

The 5 of Cups suggests that this was not an easy journey and there may have been experiences of loss, abandonment, and deep regret along the way. These are important feelings to take seriously and to allow to be felt and honored as you move forward. This card can also be a reminder to be gentle with yourself, to forgive yourself for making mistakes, and to trust in the process of healing and transformation.

The combination of Judgment and the 5 of Cups tarot cards encourages a healthy balance between self-reflection and self-love as you make the necessary changes in your life aimed at restoring your sense of health and balance. The message here is to take the time to really assess the state of your physical, mental, and spiritual health and make the necessary changes, while at the same time, honoring any feelings of loss and comfort yourself in your choices.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and 5 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health

The Judgment Tarot card is a wake-up call that encourages us to take responsibility for our own health. It serves as a reminder to consider our diets and lifestyle choices and urges us to make changes where necessary. Taking preventative measures and being mindful of our bodies is key in achieving good health and wellness. This card also suggests that sometimes we need to be honest with ourselves when it comes to our health, both physical and mental. For example, if we have been avoiding a doctor’s visit or ignoring symptoms, it is time to take action. This card could be a sign that it is time to seek medical advice or request a check-up.

Finances

The Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to reflect on our financial situation and plan for the future. It implies that we must make tough decisions now rather than later. Setting realistic financial goals and staying accountable for our spending is important for financial success. This card also encourages us to take stock of our assets and liabilities, so that we can plan more effectively and make more informed financial decisions. Furthermore, it suggests that sometimes it is beneficial to seek professional financial advice, in order to build wealth and protect our finances.

Relationships

In relationships, the Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to be honest with ourselves and our partners. This card could be a sign to look within and assess our needs, our wants and our expectations. It is also an encouragement to voice our feelings and concerns in order to foster a more meaningful connection. Furthermore, this card implies that it is important to make decisions about our relationships that are in line with our values and life purpose. This could mean ending unhealthy relationships or restoring broken trust. Taking responsibility for our own happiness and that of our partners is essential to cultivate relationships that are full of love and growth.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

The Five of Cups tarot card is a card of loss and regret, grief and feelings of abandonment and being unloved. It is an indicator of a period of difficulty and hardship in life, and a warning that difficult times lie ahead.

In terms of health, The Five of Cups tarot card can indicate a period of ill health, or difficulties in overcoming an illness or injury. It can also signify a period of emotional difficulty, such as depression, anxiety, or feeling overwhelmed with life.

In terms of finances, The Five of Cups tarot card can indicate a period of financial hardship. It can mean difficult times ahead in terms of money, whether it be increased debt, a decrease in income, or difficult times in business. It can also be a sign that you are not taking enough responsibility for your own financial wellbeing.

In terms of relationships, The Five of Cups tarot card can indicate a period of difficulty and conflict. It can mean a feeling of abandonment, or a sense of dissatisfaction with the current state of your relationships. It can also be an indicator of turbulence ahead in relationships, such as a break up or a separation.
