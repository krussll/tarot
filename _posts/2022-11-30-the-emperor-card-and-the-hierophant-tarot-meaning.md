However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Emperor card and The Hierophant have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Emperor and The Hierophant mean together?

The Emperor tarot card and The Hierophant tarot card together suggest a need to combine traditional expectations with a strong sense of leadership. The combination of these cards indicates you must take the time to plan strategically, while also understanding the importance of honoring traditions and upholding the values of the past. This combination can bring respect and authority to your position, providing you with the potential to be a wise and respected leader in whatever field you choose.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Emperor mean?

The Emperor Tarot card signifies taking responsibility for one’s health and well-being. It encourages an organized and disciplined approach to overall health maintenance, including diet and exercise. Additionally, the Emperor Tarot card encourages a balanced approach to all aspects of life, such as allocating adequate time for both work and leisure activities. This card also encourages taking preventive measures to maintain health and avoid illness, such as getting regular checkups or seeking help from a professional health provider.

Finances

The Emperor Tarot card encourages strategic and disciplined financial planning. This includes setting short-term and long-term financial goals and taking steps to reach those goals. Additionally, the Emperor Tarot card emphasizes the importance of sound financial decisions, such as investing in the stock market or avoiding debt. This card also encourages self-sufficiency, investing in oneself and ones career, and relying on one’s own resources if needed.

Relationships

The Emperor Tarot card encourages respect and decency in all relationships. This includes treating one’s friends, family, and co-workers with respect. Additionally, this card encourages taking responsibility for one’s relationships and communicating openly and honestly with those around them. It also encourages taking the initiative to resolve conflicts and disputes, and seeing relationships as a way to learn and grow. Finally, the Emperor Tarot card suggests that relationships should be seen as a partnership – a two-way street where both parties are valued and respected.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health: The Hierophant Tarot card is about tradition and conventional expectations. When it comes to health, it means following the advice of trusted medical professionals, rather than taking a more alternative or creative approach. This may include following conventional treatments such as taking scientific medicines for illnesses and diseases, rather than more alternative medicine options. It also means being aware of any health risks that come with disruptive changes to your lifestyle, such as big dietary changes, being mindful of the risks associated with extreme exercises or drastic techniques.

Finances: The Hierophant Tarot card represents tradition, but in financial terms, it means sticking to a consistent budget and not taking unnecessary risks or shortcuts. This means investing time in understanding the basics of finance and investing, as well as studying the stock market and understanding the common trends. It also means taking advice from financial advisors and utilizing their guidance to make the best financial decisions for your own benefit.

Relationships: With the Hierophant Tarot card, relationships are based on trust, commitment, and traditional values. It means not taking shortcuts and following conventional understanding of relationships, such as being faithful, honest, and devoted to each other. It also means spending quality time together and setting clear boundaries, as well as being open to communication and expressing your feelings. The Hierophant Tarot card values loyalty and faithfulness, so it means finding someone to share your life with who you know you can trust and rely on.
