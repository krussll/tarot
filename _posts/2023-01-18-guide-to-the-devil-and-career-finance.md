Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The Devil card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Devil, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Devil.

## The Devil And Career And Finances – An Overview

The Devil tarot card is a reminder that addiction and obsession can be detrimental to our well-being.

It serves as a warning to resist the temptation of unhealthy materialism, envy and sexual lust, as these can lead to destructive behaviors and unhealthy relationships.

It also suggests that we should strive to live a balanced life and be mindful of our own desires, as well as the needs of others.

The Devil tarot card can serve to motivate us to make lifestyle changes that benefit our health and sustain our happiness.

## Upright Meanings For The Devil And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The Devil tarot card warns against becoming too obsessed or attached to material wealth and financial success.

It reminds us to be mindful of the consequences of addiction and greed, as well as to be wary of envy and lust for power.

The Devil’s message is to stay focused on our long-term goals and maintain balance in our career and finances.

It’s important to recognize when we are too attached to material success and to make sure we are taking care of our mental and spiritual health.

The Devil card is a reminder to stay humble and to be careful to not let our ambition overtake our sense of morality.

### What It Means For The Future

The Devil tarot card, when interpreted in relation to my future career and finances, suggests that I may be in danger of becoming overly obsessed with the material gains of my job and career.

I may lose sight of the bigger picture and become consumed with greed, envy, and materialism, which can lead me to become distracted and lose focus of my long-term goals.

Furthermore, I may be tempted to engage in risky behavior in pursuit of quick financial gains, which could lead me down a slippery slope of addiction.

In order to protect my future career and finances, it is important that I stay mindful of my behavior and remain focused on what is truly important to me.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Devil tarot card suggests that those close to me may be struggling with materialism, greed, and obsession when it comes to their careers and finances.

They may be driven by a lust for power and money, willing to do whatever it takes to get what they want.

Unfortunately, this can lead to dangerous temptations and risky and unethical decisions.

The Devil card warns that any obsession with money or power can lead to addiction, envy, and dangerous behavior that cannot be sustained in the long run.

It’s important to remember that money and power are fleeting and should not be pursued at the expense of your own wellbeing.

## Reversed Meanings For The Devil And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The reversed Devil tarot card indicates a turn away from the greed, envy and materialism that the original card embodies.

It is a sign that you are free from the shackles of your former state and are ready to move on from the temptations that were once holding you back.

Instead of being obsessed with the things that you do not have, you are now focused on what you do have and are open to achieving more in life.

In terms of your career and finances, this card can signify that you are now ready to take a positive step forward and make the most of the opportunities that come your way.

You may have become more mindful of how your actions affect your financial success and are willing to make changes in order to improve your situation.

You may find that your motivation to reach new heights in your career is stronger than ever before and you are willing to take risks necessary to achieve success.

The reversed Devil tarot card is a sign that you have the determination, ambition, and drive to make great strides in your career and finances.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Devil tarot card is a sign of a positive change in your future career and financial situation.

It signifies that you are ready to let go of your materialistic and obsessive desires that may have been holding you back from achieving success.

You are ready to make sacrifices and take risks in order to reach your goals.

You are also likely to find yourself making better decisions and understanding the value of hard work.

This is a great sign that you are ready to make the transition from a life of materialism and greed to one of balance and abundance.

In terms of your finances, the reversed Devil tarot card can indicate that you are ready to take control of your financial situation and make wise decisions that will lead to greater financial stability.

You are ready to make sacrifices and work hard in order to achieve your financial goals.

You are also likely to find yourself better equipped to handle the ups and downs of financial markets and will be able to make better investment decisions.

This is a great sign that you are ready to move away from the cycle of addiction, envy and materialism and onto a path of abundance and financial success.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed The Devil appears in a Tarot reading, it can be a warning to those close to me that their careers and finances are in jeopardy.

This card suggests that they may be too focused on materialistic pursuits, such as making money and acquiring possessions, at the expense of their personal and spiritual growth.

Their greed, envy, and obsession with material goods may be leading them down a dangerous path.

They may be too focused on short-term gains, rather than long-term stability.

It is important that they slow down and think through their choices, as the consequences of their actions could have unforeseen repercussions.

The reversed The Devil card can also suggest that those close to me may have become addicted to their career or financial pursuits.

They may be overly reliant on money and material possessions to make them happy, rather than focusing on the things that truly matter.

They may be lacking in self-control and discipline, leading them to make reckless decisions.

They may be tempted by quick and easy fixes, instead of taking the time to work on a lasting solution.

It is important for them to remember that their career and finances are only one piece of the puzzle and should not be the only thing they focus on.
