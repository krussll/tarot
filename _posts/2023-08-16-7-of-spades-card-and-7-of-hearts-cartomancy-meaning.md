A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 7 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 7 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 7 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the 7 of Spades and the 7 of Hearts together present a compelling narrative.

The 7 of Spades, with its association with deceit and victory, suggests an achievement shrouded in secrecy or dishonesty, likely associated with a situation or feelings experienced during the winter months.

This could indicate overcoming obstacles or challenges by means possibly deceptive or unfair.

Contrastingly, the 7 of Hearts, associated with illusiveness and achievement, signifies a triumph that may not be entirely clear or visible and potentially linked to events or emotions of the spring season.

Thus, these two together could indicate victory achieved under unclear circumstances, likely involving spiritual or emotional matters, given their association with the elements of water and fire.

The final interpretation would, of course, depend on the reader’s intuition and the specific context of the individual seeking the reading.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Spades and 7 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing both the 7 Of Spades and 7 Of Hearts cards together has significant meanings for your love life.

The 7 Of Spades symbolises deceit and victory linked to the coldness and harshness of winter, while having ties to the element of Water, indicating depth of emotion, fluidity and change.

This may suggest a period or a past of deception, possible heartache, or misunderstanding in your relationship.

However, the idea of victory tied to it indicates overcoming these challenges, coming out victorious with a stronger and more profound understanding of your relationship.

On the other hand, the 7 Of Hearts stands for illusiveness and victory, associated with warmth, growth, and the fresh energy of Spring, linked to the element of Fire which represents passion, intensity, and transformation.

This indicates a period of mystery, maybe your relationship is not transparent, or there may be feelings that are not fully expressed.

However, the assurance of triumph confirms that you will pass through this phase, reaching an understanding that brings growth and new energy into your relationship.

Together, these two cards suggest a journey through hardships, misunderstandings, and ambivalent feelings leading to a stronger, more passionate, and prosperous relationship.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 7 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of the 7 of Spades and the 7 of Hearts suggests a complex scenario concerning finances and job.

The 7 of Spades points towards deceit, perhaps insinuating that there might be some hidden elements or even dishonesty associated with your current financial situation or job.

This could mean there might be unforeseen expenses coming up, or perhaps a deal seems alluring but hides underlying issues.

Similarly, it could indicate a colleague or business partner might not be as honest or forthcoming as they appear.

Even so, the association with victory implies that overcoming these hidden obstacles could ultimately lead to a stronger financial standing or job position.

Similarly, drawing the 7 of Hearts represents the concept of illusion and victory.

This might suggest false hopes or expectations in regards to your career growth or financial status.

It warns to stay grounded in reality and not be swayed by appearances or promises without substantial proof or substantiation.

Additionally, the fire element linked to this card could signify that active steps and a persistent approach are needed to overcome challenges.

The victory associated with both cards indicates that these obstacles are not insurmountable and success can be expected after a period of struggle.

So, while the immediate outlook may seem tricky, these cards signal the ability to manoeuvre through these challenges to arrive at a victorious outcome.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 7 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading for your health, the appearance of The 7 Of Spades card indicates potential challenges or deceits masking your true health status.

Coupled with its Winter and Water associations, this might represent hidden ailments, suppressed symptoms, or misconceptions about your current health condition.

This card invites you to delve deeper into your physical well-being, urging you to avoid dismissing small symptoms or changes that might reveal a larger hidden issue, such as chronic illness or an unhealthy lifestyle.

Protecting your bodies during the cold winter months, hydrating sufficiently, and maintaining a balanced emotional state are also crucial steps dictated by this card.

Together with the 7 Of Hearts card, the reading suggests a victorious outcome over these health obstacles, but it insists on the need for clear discernment.

As the 7 Of Hearts card implies illusiveness, there could be potential distractions or delusions diverting your attention from the important health priorities.

Being associated with Spring and the element Fire, this card assures that a time of rejuvenation and energy surge is heading your way, like the new life that comes in spring.

However, you must harness this energy appropriately to fuel your health positively.

The energy signified by the Fire element could quickly burn out if not used wisely, leading to potential burnout or exacerbated health issues.

Therefore, the combination of these two cards invites you to navigate your health issues with honesty and wisdom, ensuring the use of your energy in a health-enhancing manner.
