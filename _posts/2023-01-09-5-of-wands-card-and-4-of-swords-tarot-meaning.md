A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The 5 Of Wands tarot card and The 4 Of Swords tarot card suggest that it is time for a period of introspection and rest. Challenges in the past have taken their toll, and now it is important to take a step back and reflect. In order to renew and refresh, you must take care of yourself and rest, retreat and recover in order to prepare for the next round of rivalry, obstacles and sport. Make sure to take the necessary time out to reflect and restore, so you can move forward with clarity and focus.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 5 of Wands tarot card suggests that when it comes to taking care of one’s health, there may be difficulty along the way. This card indicates challenges and obstacles, so it can suggest that maintaining good health may take more effort and self-discipline than expected. It can also suggest that the individual may need to push themselves to stay active or try to eat better. This card can symbolize competition, so it could be a great motivator to strive for a better quality of life.

Money  
When the 5 of Wands tarot card appears in a reading regarding finances, it suggests that there are challenges that need to be overcome to achieve financial success. This card is representative of obstacles and rivals, suggesting that to reach financial stability there may be a need to compete or fight for it. It can also suggest that it is important for those struggling financially to keep their focus and motivation on their financial goals in order to succeed.

Relationships  
In a relationship reading, the appearance of the 5 of Wands tarot card can indicate that there are challenges to be faced. This card suggests rivalry and conflict, so it can suggest that there may be competition or difficulty in the relationship. This card can also denote an argumentative relationship, where both partners may be competing for the other’s attention or attempting to prove they are right. It can also indicate a relationship where there is a power struggle between both partners, as they attempt to be the one in control. It is important to remember that the card is a sign of challenges, and that it is possible to overcome any obstacles and strengthen the bond between two people.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card is an indicator of physical health and healing. It suggests that it is time to take a break from the hustle and bustle of life and to retreat inward to rest and renew the body, mind and spirit. It is a reminder that rest is an essential part of a healthy lifestyle, allowing the body to recover and recharge. Taking a break from physical activities is beneficial, but it is also important to disconnect from the busy world of technology and modern life and to focus on restoring the health of the mind. This could include indulging in self-care rituals such as meditation and journaling, or engaging in therapeutic activities such as yoga or creative projects.

Finances:  
When it comes to finances, the 4 of Swords Tarot card indicates that it is time to take a step back from the demands of money and material pursuits and to focus on finding balance. It is a reminder to take time to assess current financial situations and to make sure that resources are being used in a way that brings peace and contentment. The card suggests making space for time spent in solitude, where one is able to rest, reflect and restore mental energy. This energy can then be put towards creative and mindful ways of managing finances.

Relationships:  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card is a message to give oneself the gift of rest and solitude. It is a reminder to step back, take a breath and restore the energy between two people in a relationship. It is a gentle reminder that relationships take effort, and when both partners are feeling exhausted, it is essential to take time to renew and recharge. A relationship is an ongoing journey, and there will be times when it is best to take time apart to reflect and to enjoy some time alone. Taking a break from one another can bring greater understanding, connection and appreciation for each other.
