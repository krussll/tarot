A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 5 Of Swords and The Queen Of Cups tarot cards indicate a need for healing and support from a nurturing person. It could be that you are in an abusive relationship, perhaps even a situation that involves bullying, theft and violence. It is important that you seek help from someone who is both understanding and caring. Put your trust in a person who is willing to help you out of a difficult situation and provide a safe and supportive environment for you to heal.

The situation may have spiraled out of control, leading to violence, theft, and other destructive behaviors.

Fortunately, the Queen of Cups is here to bring some love and healing into the situation. This card suggests that a nurturing person could be just what you need to help you through this difficult time. You may find solace and support from someone who is kind and understanding, who will listen to your story and help you find a way out of this unhealthy situation. With their help, you can create healthy boundaries and start to move on.

The 5 of Swords and the Queen of Cups offer a stark contrast, but they can also work together in your favor. The 5 of Swords shows that you need to face and confront the toxic aspects of your relationships, while the Queen of Cups brings in the warmth and love to help you heal. By embracing both cards and learning to discern between what is healthy and what is not, you can take back control of your love life and find true happiness.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

The 5 of Swords Tarot card is an ominous symbol of bullying, theft, violence, and abusive relationships, and offers a warning to be mindful of dangerous situations. In terms of health, this card suggests that physical and mental health may be negatively impacted by a bully or someone in an abusive relationship. The relationship dynamics could lead to physical, mental, and emotional distress, which could manifest as chronic headaches, depression, anxiety, and other forms of physical and mental suffering.

In terms of finances, this card can be a warning sign for theft and fraud. It can suggest that one’s financial safety might be compromised due to someone taking advantage of them. The card could also represent a difficult financial situation due to bullying, such as someone taking money or possessions without permission.

This card can also be a warning to watch out for abusive relationships which can take many forms. This could be a situation of physical or emotional abuse, or it could be a situation of financial or psychological manipulation. It can suggest that someone is in a situation of being dominated or controlled by another, as well as not being respected or appreciated.

Overall, the 5 of Swords Tarot card is a warning that bullying, theft, violence, and abusive relationships can have serious consequences in terms of health, finances, and relationships. If someone is experiencing any of these issues, it is important to seek help and support, either from professionals or from friends and family. It is essential to take action and find a healthy resolution to any dangerous or destructive situation.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health: The Queen Of Cups Tarot card indicates healing and nurturing, suggesting that individuals must take time to focus on their health and wellbeing. It can also be interpreted as a sign to take action in order to improve health, such as practicing mindfulness and finding time to be restorative and gently caring for your body. This card also suggests that it is important to focus on self-care, as it is often one of the first things to go when under duress.

Finances: In terms of finances, the Queen of Cups Tarot card suggests wise investments and taking time to consider important decisions before making them. This card also indicates that one should take the time to save, rather than impulsively spend. To succeed, one must take the time to be well informed and to do their research. This card also suggests that one should be non-judgmental and consider every point of view before deciding on a course of action.

Relationships: For relationships, the Queen of Cups Tarot card indicates nurturing, healing, and support, suggesting that it is important to express emotions and be patient with others during difficult times. This card also encourages trust and understanding, as it suggests that one should be mindful of the feelings of the other person and be willing to offer emotional support. This card also suggests that relationships should be based on compromise, as it is important to understand and respect the viewpoints of both parties. Furthermore, it suggests that individuals should stay open to growth, understanding that relationships often change and grow over time.
