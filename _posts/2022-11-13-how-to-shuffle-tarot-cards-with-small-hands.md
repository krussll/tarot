## Different Techniques for Shuffling Tarot Cards

There are a variety of techniques for shuffling tarot cards, each of which has its own benefits and drawbacks. Here are a few of the most common techniques for shuffling tarot cards:

- Overhand shuffle: This is the most common technique for shuffling tarot cards. To do this, start by holding the cards in your hands, then spread them out and mix them up. This technique is great for those with small hands, as it allows you to easily manipulate the cards.
- Ribbon spread shuffle: This technique is similar to the overhand shuffle, but instead of mixing the cards in your hands, you spread them out on a table or surface and mix them up using your hands or a card spreader. This technique is great for those with smaller hands, as it allows you to easily manipulate the cards without having to hold them in your hands.
- Riffle shuffle: This technique is more advanced and requires more practice. To do this, start by separating the cards into two equal piles, then use your hands or a card spreader to mix them up. This technique is great for those with small hands, as it allows you to easily manipulate the cards without having to hold them in your hands.
- Spiral shuffle: This technique is similar to the overhand shuffle, but instead of mixing the cards in your hands, you hold them in your hands and use a spiral motion to mix them up. This technique is great for those with smaller hands, as it allows you to easily manipulate the cards without having to hold them in your hands.
