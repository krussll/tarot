A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and The Star card together in particular.

The Justice card and The Star have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and The Star mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the Star tarot card coming together in a reading means that you need to focus on cause and effect and finding win-win solutions in order to achieve a greater hope and renewal. The inner clarity you gain from understanding the truths that come out of this situation will bring about miracles when you have faith in yourself and accept being in the spotlight. This combination of cards signifies that your commitment to the truth and your willingness to stay optimistic will be the driving force behind achieving your goals.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and The Star mean together for your love life?

The Justice tarot card represents cause and effect, win-win solutions, and the truth coming out. This is a card of balance and fairness in our relationships, as well as a reminder to look at all sides of the story before jumping to a conclusion. This card reminds us that we should take responsibility for our actions, and strive to create an equitable environment in our relationships.

The Star tarot card, on the other hand, offers us a different kind of message. This card represents renewal, hope, inner clarity, and the belief that miracles can happen. It is a reminder to keep our faith, even when times appear bleak, and to be confident that better days are coming. A combination of the Justice and Star tarot cards is a potent one for couples, as it encourages both sides of the relationship to look for balance, fairness, and common ground.

When these two cards come together in a reading, it is a sign that the foundation of your relationship is strong. There is a suggestion that win-win solutions can be found, and that together, you can create a space of understanding, trust, and growth. With renewed optimism and clarity, you can learn to talk honestly and openly, without fear of judgment. As you work together to find the truth and navigate through challenging times, miracles and blessings can follow.

### What does Justice and The Star mean together for your finances?

When The Justice tarot card appears alongside The Star, it indicates that a win-win solution to your financial and job situation may be closer than you think. The Justice card symbolizes cause and effect, meaning that whatever decisions you make today can create long-term consequences. This makes it all the more important to carefully consider your choices, especially when it comes to your finances and job. All of your hard work and dedication will be rewarded in the end, and you can trust that the truth will eventually come out.

The Star card represents hope and renewal, suggesting that you can trust that your circumstances will improve. It’s a sign of inner clarity, reminding you to stay positive and have faith in yourself and your path. The hope that The Star card brings allows you to stay open to the possibilities and opportunities that will come your way. Experiences like this often come with greater responsibility, including the potential of being in the spotlight. With the right attitude and mental focus, you can turn this into an advantage and leverage it to your financial and career advancement.

The influence of The Justice and Star tarot card together offer you a unique opportunity to find solutions that are beneficial for both parties. With the right attitude and consideration of your choices, you can make decisions that will lead to a positive outcome and create lasting impact. By having faith and trusting the process, you can create opportunities for yourself and unlock the potential for new pathways and miracles.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and The Star mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card is an omen of balance and equality. When it comes to physical health, it means striving to find balance between the body and mind. As a result, it could manifest as a balanced diet and appropriate exercise, or seeking help when needed. It also encourages being truthful with your doctor and not neglecting your health due to fear or denial. It also means being honest with yourself and others when making health-related decisions.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card signifies the law of cause and effect, and with finances it can mean a time for taking action to improve your financial status. It applies to both money management and investments; use wisdom and discretion, follow the laws of the land, and don’t be greedy or deceptive in any financial dealings. Furthermore, pay attention to what is being asked of you and ensure that you are giving fair returns for any services performed.

Relationships

This card suggests a time of fairness, balance, and justice—including in relationships. It means being honest and open with your partner and allowing each person to take responsibility for their actions, even if it results in a difficult conversation. It also means respecting each other’s differences and finding ways to compromise and work together. Additionally, it is important to look out for each other’s best interests and remember to practice fairness and kindness, even when it is hard to do.

## What does The Star mean?

Health: The Star Tarot card indicates a renewal of health. It may be a recovery from an illness or a state of balance that allows your health to remain strong. This card also symbolizes hope for the future and the inner clarity needed to make decisions about health and wellbeing. Having faith in yourself can lead to miracles that restore your body and mind to a state of vitality.

Finances: Financially, The Star Tarot card symbolizes the potential for new opportunities in your life. It can be a time of growth and success if you have the faith and patience to take advantage of these chances. Inner clarity can also help you make wise financial decisions. Don’t be afraid to step into the spotlight and make the most of your current situation.

Relationships: The Star Tarot card indicates a renewal of faith and hope in relationships. There is a renewed energy that can lead to a better understanding and connection between partners. Emotional clarity can also help you make decisions that are best for both of you. With faith and patience, you can create a stronger and healthier bond.
