A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The 5 of Wands and 9 of Cups tarot cards together represent a time of intense competition and ambition, but also a time of rewards for your hard work. Your focused efforts will bring you success and recognition, and your dedication and determination will be rewarded. There’s a good chance that your ambitions will be met with material abundance and good health, as well as the joy of having your wishes come true. So while the obstacles ahead may be challenging, the promise of satisfaction and enjoyment is there when you triumph and overcome the challenges you face.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

In the realm of romance, this combination suggests that it’s important to be prepared to confront any negative energy that may arise on your path.

When it comes to attracting a romantic partner, the 5 of Wands suggests that you are in competition with yourself and others. While there’s no need to become too overwhelmed by the prospect of rivalry, it’s important to recognize that you will need to put some effort into standing out from the crowd and achieving your own personal goals. It’s also important to understand that this effort shouldn’t come at the expense of others, but rather a way to improve yourself.

The 9 of Cups, however, brings good luck and the promise of something positive. In this context, it’s likely that the competition and rivalry suggested by the 5 of Wands will all be worth it in the end, as you stand to gain a wealth of good health, material abundance and wishes come true as a result. When it comes to love and relationships, this combination suggests that while circumstance and other people can pose a challenge, with dedication and effort you can create your own luck and be rewarded with something truly wonderful.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 5 of Wands tarot card can signify that you are in the midst of a period of challenge, competition or rivalry when it comes to your health. You may find yourself comparing your health goals to those of others and feeling that you need to give it your all in order to come out on top. It may be a case of pushing yourself physically or mentally in order to reach higher levels of fitness or to be able to achieve challenging goals. This could be an indication of a desire for perfection, and it’s important to keep that in balance with allowing yourself rest and recovery.

Finances

The 5 of Wands tarot card can indicate that you are facing a challenging time when it comes to your finances. It may be that you are facing competition for financial resources, or perhaps you feel that you need to outdo others in order to come out ahead. Alternatively, it could be something to do with the stock market or investments that are proving to be an obstacle – it could be that you are cautious and watching the market to see which way it will go, or that you are taking a risk in order to see if it pays off.

Relationships

The 5 of Wands tarot card can signify a period of conflict or rivalry when it comes to relationships. This could be with a partner, sibling or coworker – there might be something that you are competing over, either material or emotional in nature. This could be a challenge for the relationship, as you might both feel that the other person is out for themselves and not for the benefit of the relationship. It’s important to take a step back and recognize that working together is more beneficial than seeking personal gain. Alternatively, it could be a sign that you are both engaged in a healthy competitive spirit when it comes to the relationship – taking time to challenge and push each other in positive ways.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 Of Cups Tarot Card is often seen as a sign of great fortune, abundance, and good health. For those in good health, this card signifies a time of ongoing good health, luck and fortune. It is a reminder to take care of one’s physical and mental health, as the newfound abundance may bring with it increased stress and worry.

For those in need of some financial aid, the 9 of Cups Tarot Card is an encouraging sign of potential material abundance. This card suggests that with hard work, dedication, and staying true to one’s goals, financial success is possible. It also suggests that luck may be on the side of the receiver, and that unexpected sources of money may be available, such as help from friends, family or grants.

In terms of relationships, the 9 of Cups Tarot Card predicts the potential for strong partnerships and great happiness. Whether it be a romantic relationship or a friendship, those involved will be open to communication and compromise. There will be a sense of contentment and positive energy between the two that will allow for a stronger and lasting bond.

The 9 Of Cups Tarot Card acknowledges that good fortune exists, but it also serves as a reminder to practice self-care, be grateful for what has been achieved, and to not take anything for granted. With a little bit of luck and a lot of hard work, happiness can be found in all aspects of life.
