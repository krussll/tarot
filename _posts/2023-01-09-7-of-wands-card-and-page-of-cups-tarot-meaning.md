## What does 7 Of Wands and Page Of Cups mean together?

You must be prepared to stand your ground and refuse to be intimidated by competition. At the same time, The Page of Cups speaks to the need for study and learning in order to protect yourself against those who try to take advantage of you. In addition, the Page of Cups encourages you to consider new forms of love and to express your feelings in a more generous way. Finally, this card can also signify involvement in volunteer work for the benefit of others. These two cards together remind us that self-defense is not only physical, but can be expressed through knowledge, understanding, and kindness.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This could involve setting boundaries, developing resilience, and standing up for oneself in times of difficulty.

For finances, this card could reveal that one should strive to keep their financial situation secure and insure that their assets are protected. This could involve being mindful of how one spends money, setting up strong financial safety nets, and making sure that one is thoroughly informed on investments.

For relationships, this card could be an indicator that one should take steps to defend their boundaries in difficult situations. This could involve standing up for what one believes in and being honest about one’s feelings. Additionally, it could also be an invitation to protect the relationship from outside pressures and competition. It can also signify a newfound sense of financial security, or the need to look into different ways of increasing income.

In terms of relationships, the Page of Cups Tarot card can signify a period of new love, or the need to embrace a new level of commitment in existing relationships. This card encourages individuals to express their feelings and be open to different forms of connection, whether it be through a romantic relationship, a platonic one or even through a volunteer project.
