## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card often indicates the presence of a tremendous amount of worry, fear, and anxiety. The card’s imagery often portrays an individual paralyzed by fear, overwhelmed by their thoughts, and unable to break free of their distress. This can manifest in physical health concerns, often in the form of insomnia, headache, or panic attacks. Mentally, it can manifest in worry and panic that cause a person to ruminate endlessly over a problem that they are unable to change or solve.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Swords can represent financial hardship caused by anxiety and fear. This includes fear of failing to maintain financial security or fear of being overwhelmed by debt. It can be difficult for a person in such a state to take the necessary steps to address their financial situation and make progress.

Relationships are also affected by the 9 of Swords. Anxiety and fear can cause a person to be unable to focus on their relationships, and instead to be stuck in their own thoughts and worries. This can lead to distance in relationships and make it difficult for a person to reach out and connect with their loved ones in times of need. Additionally, a person in a state of fear and anxiety may come off as negative and pessimistic, which can be off-putting to friends and family.

In short, the 9 of Swords Tarot card speaks to a person who is stuck in fear and anxiety, unable to take positive action and unable to connect with the people around them. It is important to be aware of this card’s meaning and to be mindful of the ways in which fear and worry can negatively affect physical, financial, and interpersonal health.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 10 Of Swords Tarot card symbolises a difficult and complete ending in terms of health. It suggests suffering from serious illnesses, or being let down by ineffective treatments. The card indicates being unable to recover from an injury or chronic illness, and reaching a stage of hopelessness. It can also point to illness-induced financial woes, as a consequence of high medical bills or long periods of inability to work.

Finances

The 10 Of Swords Tarot card points to a financial situation that has reached rock bottom. It suggests hitting financial obstacles and hitting a dead end, with no way out. It can also mean that any investments have gone sour, and debt is piling up. The card symbolises a situation without a way out, and the repercussions of bad decisions and bad investments.

Relationships

In terms of relationships, the 10 Of Swords Tarot card signals a situation that has reached a complete and painful ending. It signals the breakdown of relationships that have gone sour, and a feeling of hopelessness. It is a warning that the relationship can no longer be fixed, and that letting go may be the only solution. It can also mean the end of a friendship, and the need to start again from the bottom.
