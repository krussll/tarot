A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and The Sun card together in particular.

The Justice card and The Sun have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and The Sun mean together?

When the Justice tarot card and the Sun tarot card come together, it can mean that all outcomes will be positive and beneficial. There is a feeling of equilibrium and balance that comes with the energy of Justice, which helps to ensure that any situation is handled in a fair and just manner. The Sun card brings a sense of joy, success and material happiness, making it the perfect accompaniment to the Justice card. With both of these cards working together, it can mean that the truth will always come out and will be handled with an eye towards finding win-win solutions that benefit all parties involved.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and The Sun mean together for your love life?

In this context, the Justice card can symbolize the need to create a healthy balance between each partner. This balance should allow the relationship to thrive and relies on the truth coming out in terms of the needs and feelings of both partners.

The Sun card offers hope that whatever balance is achieved through justice and truth coming out, will result in a joyous and successful outcome. In a relationship reading, the Sun card suggests that the relationship has a potential to be full of happiness, positive energy and an overall feeling of success.

When read together, the Justice tarot card and The Sun tarot card suggest that a situation of win-win solutions can be achieved in the love life through a combination of the truth coming out and manifesting the highest good of both partners. In such a situation, both partners should be open to compromise and to understanding the needs of their partner. This can bring a sense of achievement to both partners, creating a strong and successful bond.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and The Sun mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health  
Justice influences the health-related decisions in an individual’s life. It is associated with balance, planning and taking responsibility for one’s own well-being. In terms of health, the Justice Tarot card encourages individuals to make lifestyle changes to maintain balance, practice preventative measures and to consider all options available for healing. It suggests that individuals should be honest with themselves and with their loved ones regarding their health and make decisions that are in the best interest of their physical and emotional wellbeing.

Finances  
The Justice Tarot card encourages financial security, planning and responsible decision making. It encourages individuals to take responsibility for the current financial situation they are in, make well-informed decisions and take the necessary steps to secure a healthy financial future. It is important to take a balanced approach when managing finances- to invest, save and spend appropriately. The Justice card also suggests to be honest and open with loved ones when addressing financial matters- to avoid any kind of power struggle or imbalance.

Relationships  
The Justice Tarot card encourages honest and open communication in relationships. It suggests that all relationships should reflect fair and balanced give and take. It encourages individuals to stand up for what is right, to practice self-love and to put their own emotional needs first. The Justice card also emphasizes that individuals should take responsibility for their own actions and be honest with their partners about what is necessary for the relationship to survive and thrive. It also suggests that relationships should be based on trust, respect and understanding. The balance and fairness of the Justice card can help bring justice to any relationship.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card offers a degree of optimism for health, finances and relationships. The card suggests that any health issues will be addressed and overcome with confidence. It also implies that finances will improve and that there will be financial success in the future. The Sun symbolizes positive energies and feelings, which will help to strengthen relationships and bring about a more joyous outcome.

For health, the Sun suggests that any obstacles that have been preventing the person from achieving good health will soon be removed. This card encourages individuals to seek out the advice of medical professionals who can provide the best health care. With The Sun in the picture, any health-related issues can be addressed and resolved quickly and easily.

For finances, The Sun indicates that the person’s financial future is bright. Money can be earned, saved and invested to create wealth. The Sun card encourages the person to set clear financial goals and to work towards achieving them. The card is an indication that hard work and persistence will pay off in the end.

For relationships, the Sun card symbolizes joy and happiness. It suggests that any relationships that have been strained or have gone through difficult times will have a chance to be rekindled and strengthened. With the Sun card, individuals will be more open and willing to communicate openly with one another. This can help to create more positive relationships that are full of understanding and compassion.

The Sun Tarot card is indicative of a joyous outcome for all areas of life. Health, finances and relationships are likely to experience positive changes and the person will be filled with a sense of contentment. The Sun card can be looked to for guidance when times get tough, as it will always be a reminder that good things come to those who work hard and stay positive.
