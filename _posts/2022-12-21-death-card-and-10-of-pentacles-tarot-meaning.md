A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Death card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Death tarot card and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card together signify a time of great transition, where you are leaving behind the old and embracing the new. This is a time of endings and beginnings, of moving on from a chapter of your life and making way for something fresh. The 10 Of Pentacles brings with it the idea of legacies, of leaving something behind that will outlast you, that will keep your name and your memory alive. Although there may have been sadness in the passing of something, it’s time to focus on the future, on the opportunity that has arisen to start again, to create something meaningful. It is a time to be brave and to start anew.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and 10 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health  
The Death card in the Tarot suggests big changes, transitions, and endings when it comes to the health of an individual. In terms of physical health, this card could signal something as major as the end of an illness or the start of something new, such as a new diet or exercise plan. This card can also signal the public health crisis coming to an end or the start of a new wave of protection and awareness. On a spiritual level, the Death card can signal the need for a person to break away from unhealthy habits, attachments, and beliefs in order to make room for a new level of understanding and holistic wellness.

Finances  
The Death Tarot card also brings endings and new beginnings in the realm of finances. This card can signal a time to let go of certain spending habits and unproductive investments. It can also signal the end of a difficult financial period, or the start of new chapter of self-discovery and learning when it comes to money. This card can also signal a time to forgive yourself for any past financial mistakes and to let go of any limiting beliefs around scarcity and lack.

Relationships  
The Death Tarot card brings change, endings, and transformation in relationships, as well. This card can signal a time to let go of any unhealthy relationships, or to end relationships that no longer serve us. It can also signal a time of rebirth in relationships, whether that’s the start of a new relationship or the healing of an old one. This card can remind us to be open to change, and to allow ourselves to be brave and vulnerable in order to experience deeper connections with those around us.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes the ultimate goal in life: the legacy we create for ourselves. Represented by an individual surrounded by the wealth of abundant coins, the 10 of Pentacles reflects a state of prosperity, security, and peace.

For health, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card implies that one is taking good care of themselves. It could signify that they are improving their diet, exercising regularly, and preventing potential illnesses. It could also suggest that they are able to support their health through financial means, such as health insurance and preventative care.

For finances, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that one has taken the right steps to achieve financial stability. This could mean that the individual is investing wisely, budgeting effectively, and utilizing their resources to secure a comfortable future. It could also represent an inheritance or financial windfall, either of which would provide one with a boost in financial security.

For relationships, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card signifies that one is creating a lasting bond with another. This could refer to a romantic partner, a good friend, or a close family member. It implies that one is investing in relationships that will outlast them and will leave a lasting legacy for the individuals involved.

Ultimately, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card reflects a life well spent, where one is able to enjoy the fruits of their labor. It reflects security, abundance, and the peace of knowing that their legacy will carry on long after they have gone.
