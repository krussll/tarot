You are also likely to find yourself surrounded by a circle of friends and supportive people who are there for you during this time. Celebrate, enjoy and be open to the changes that come your way, as their presence is a sign of the positive growth to come.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

The Knight of Swords in the Tarot is a card about making swift and powerful changes in life. This card is a warning to make sure the choices you make are wise and based on accurate information.

Health: When it comes to health, this card can bring a warning to be mindful of what changes you make. It’s important to weigh your options and choices carefully in order to achieve good health. Making sudden changes can be difficult to adjust to and can have an adverse effect. Take the time to research and gather all the facts before making any drastic shifts.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the Knight of Swords Tarot card can represent a sudden windfall or a sharp financial shift. This card warns to be cautious when spending or investing, as impulsive decisions could have serious financial repercussions. Be sure to learn the facts, explore options, and consider the advice of others before making any major movements with your money.

Relationships: In relationships, The Knight of Swords Tarot card can bring a warning to take stock of the situation and make sure both partners are in agreement about any changes to be made. It’s also important to consider the other person’s feelings and thoughts when making decisions. A sudden shift in a relationship could create resentment and misunderstandings. Instead, the card advises to take the time to talk and weigh all options before making any changes.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The 3 of Cups Tarot card suggests that it is important to foster healthy and supportive relationships. This card encourages us to give and receive love and kindness, and to treasure the special bonds we share with people in our lives.
