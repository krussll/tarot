However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Empress card and 5 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Empress card and 5 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Empress and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The Empress tarot card and The 5 Of Swords tarot card together bring a powerful message of protection and security. The Empress card suggests that we create an environment of abundance, fertility and good parenting. At the same time, The 5 Of Swords indicates that we must also be aware of the potential for bullies, violence, and abusive relationships in our lives. While it is important to trust and create a secure space, it is also important to be mindful of potential threats and be proactive in protecting ourselves and our loved ones from harm. With the combined energies of The Empress and The 5 Of Swords, we can strive for balance and stay safe in our relationships.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Empress mean?

The Empress Tarot card encourages us to foster our physical health. It symbolizes taking care of ourselves and feeding our bodies with nutritious, healthy food. This card also reminds us to find balance in our lives and to engage in activities that make us feel inspired. These might include yoga, meditation, gardening, or other forms of self-care.

The Empress Tarot card also lends us its abundance and fertility in the realm of finances. It can mean that now is the time to invest in yourself and your pursuits, take entrepreneurial risks, or aim for a promotion. The Empress also encourages us to be smart with our money and to save for the future.

When it comes to relationships, The Empress Tarot card promotes nurturing, supportive partnerships. It is associated with strong communication and understanding between two people. It’s a reminder to create a safe space for growth and collaboration in all our relationships, both romantic and platonic.

The Empress Tarot card invites us to be bold and brave, to take steps to create a life we’re proud of, and to cultivate abundance and growth in all areas. With this card, we can feel encouraged and empowered to pursue our goals and dreams.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

The 5 of Swords Tarot card is a card symbolizing bullies, theft, violence, and abusive relationships. It is a warning that whatever situation you are in, you are in danger of being taken advantage of, manipulated, or even worse, hurt.

Health:  
The 5 of Swords Tarot card warns that unhealthy relationships and behaviors can have a major effect on health, both physical and mental. This card can indicate that someone is being subjected to bullying, manipulation, or physical abuse. It can also represent a situation where someone is feeling emotionally exhausted from constantly trying to fend off harm, threats, or abuse from others. This card is often a sign that someone needs to seek help and find a safe space to rid themselves of any unhealthy relationships or situations.

Finances:  
The 5 of Swords Tarot card is a warning about financial loss due to deception, manipulation, and theft. This card can suggest that someone is in danger of being taken advantage of in a business deal or is about to be scammed. It can also represent a situation when someone is so desperate for money that they are willing to do anything, including illegal activities, to get it. In either case, this card is a warning to be cautious and attentive with finances in order to avoid any major financial loss.

Relationships:  
The 5 of Swords Tarot card warns of unhealthy relationships. This card can point to a situation where someone is being taken advantage of or taken for granted in a relationship. It can also indicate a situation where someone is being manipulated or abused in any form. This card is a reminder to be mindful of the people you have around you, as unhealthy relationships can have a major effect on mental and physical health. It is important to seek help if you find yourself in an abusive or manipulative relationship and create a safe space for yourself.
