Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 6 Of Pentacles card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 6 Of Pentacles, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 6 Of Pentacles.

## 6 Of Pentacles And Love – An Overview

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card is a benevolent symbol of health, representing the generous exchange of resources and support when it comes to our physical wellbeing.

It suggests that taking care of our physical health is a collaborative effort that requires giving and receiving help in order to be successful.

Whether it is seeking out the support of a trusted friend or professional to help us through tough times, or offering our support to someone who is struggling, it is important to recognize that we are not alone in the journey of taking care of our health.

The 6 of Pentacles encourages us to practice generosity and kindness with ourselves and our loved ones, as it is only when we come together to share our resources and knowledge that we can truly reach our health goals.

## Upright Meanings For 6 Of Pentacles And Love

### What It Means For Me

The 6 Of Pentacles, when it comes to love, speaks to the need to be generous and supportive to one another.

It’s a reminder to us all to give help where needed, and to be gracious when receiving it.

It’s a sign that we should be open to giving and receiving assistance, understanding and consideration.

It speaks to the importance of communication and compromise, and of fostering a spirit of cooperation and understanding.

When we take this approach to love, we create strong and lasting relationships.

### What It Means For The Future

The 6 of Pentacles in the Tarot speaks of generosity and giving/receiving help which signifies that your future love life will be full of selflessness and understanding.

Your partner will be willing to lend a helping hand or offer advice and support when needed.

They will also be generous with their time, energy and resources, showing you how much they care for you.

In return, you will also be generous and understanding, offering unconditional love and support no matter the situation.

The 6 of Pentacles is a sign that your future love life will be filled with kindness and understanding, making it a beautiful and fulfilling experience.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card is a reminder to show love to those around us and to be generous both in giving and receiving.

This card encourages us to open our hearts and minds to the people who are close to us, to be generous with our time, energy, and resources, and to be receptive to the same from those we love.

It is a reminder to appreciate and support each other, to think of our loved ones first, and to be mindful of our words and actions.

Ultimately, the 6 of Pentacles is a reminder to share our love and kindness with those around us.

## Reversed Meanings For 6 Of Pentacles And Love

### What It Means For Me

The reversed 6 of Pentacles tarot card in a love reading represents a lack of generosity or balance in a relationship.

This could mean that one partner is feeling taken for granted or taken advantage of, as if all the giving is coming from one side.

They might feel as if their partner is not giving as much as they are, which can create resentment in the relationship.

This card can also indicate a need for more balance, as it suggests that one partner is getting more out of the relationship than the other.

Forgiveness and understanding are both necessary for the relationship to move forward.

The reversed 6 of Pentacles can also represent a lack of generosity in terms of emotional support.

One partner might be feeling neglected or unheard, while the other partner is not being sensitive enough to their feelings or needs.

It is important to be mindful of each other’s feelings and to provide equal attention and care in a relationship, in order to create a healthy, balanced dynamic.

It is also important to be generous with compliments and appreciation to make the other person feel valued and secure.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed 6 of Pentacles appears in a reading about love and relationships, it suggests that you may be feeling insecure in the current relationship.

This can manifest in many ways, including feeling like you are not getting enough support or attention from your partner.

It may also indicate that you are not fully trusting your partner and are attempting to manipulate the relationship to your advantage.

In the future, this card suggests that you will be able to find both love and security in your relationship.

You will be able to give and receive help generously, without feeling like you are being taken advantage of.

You will also be able to trust your partner more, allowing you to relax and let go of any feelings of insecurity.

You will be able to trust that your partner is there for you, and that you can rely on each other for the long haul.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the 6 of Pentacles is reversed, it can indicate a lack of generosity or support in close relationships.

This could mean that the people who are supposed to be the closest to you are not giving you the emotional or financial support you need.

It might also signify that you are not opening up to those who are trying to support you.

Whether it’s your family or your partner, there may be a feeling of disconnection or unwillingness to accept help.

Alternatively, the reversed 6 of Pentacles could mean that you are giving too much and not receiving enough in return.

This could be an indication that your partner or loved ones are not appreciating your efforts or are taking you for granted.

It could also be a sign that you are not being mindful of your own boundaries and needs when it comes to giving and receiving help.

It is important to recognize if you are being taken advantage of and to make sure that your relationships are balanced when it comes to the exchange of love and support.
