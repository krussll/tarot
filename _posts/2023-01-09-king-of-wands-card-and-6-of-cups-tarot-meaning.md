## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

The 6 of Cups Tarot card is associated with nostalgia, gifts, innocence and reunions. This card can represent a period of healing and renewal in our health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the 6 of Cups can suggest that we slow down and take some time to reflect on our current health. We may feel a sense of nostalgia for the body we used to have before our illness or injury, but it can also serve as a reminder to take care of ourselves with kindness and self-care. This card can also mean that it is time to repair the damage we have done to our bodies, including taking time to rest and recover. If we have been neglecting our physical and mental health, the 6 of Cups can remind us to take a pause and focus on our overall wellbeing.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Cups can suggest that it is time to take a look at our financial history and find a sense of balance. We can reflect on how we got to where we are today and make changes so we can move forward with confidence. This card can remind us of the importance of saving money and budgeting to ensure our financial security. It can also suggest that we should give ourselves the allowance to enjoy the luxuries that come with having a comfortable income.

Finally, when it comes to our relationships, the 6 of Cups can suggest that we take time to reconnect with our loved ones and enjoy the company of those we have grown distant from. The 6 of Cups suggests that we should break free from the patterns of our past and focus on the present. We may find ourselves feeling nostalgic for certain people and experiences, but it is important to remember that these feelings can provide comfort and reassurance in our current relationships. We should also be willing to make sacrifices in order to maintain our relationships and show those we care about how much they mean to us.
