A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Ace Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Ace Of Wands and 6 Of Swords mean together?

When The Ace of Wands and The 6 of Swords appear together in a reading, it means that you are in a period of transition, but that you have all the creative energy necessary to make it a successful one. You might be inspired to begin a new project or creative pursuit, or you might be relocating in order to expand your horizons. Though it might be a difficult transition, you can use your creative energy to make it a positive one and to make sure you come out on top.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

When it comes to health, the Ace of Wands indicates a time of renewal, both internally and externally. This card encourages us to pay attention to our physical health and listen to our bodies to make sure we’re getting the proper nutrition, exercise, and rest that we need to stay healthy. It also suggests taking time to focus on our mental health, such as attending therapy or engaging in self-care activities.

In regards to finances, The Ace of Wands Tarot card points to positive financial growth and opportunities. It symbolizes the spark of a new venture, inspiring us to take smart risks and take advantage of any potential opportunities that come our way.

Finally, the Ace of Wands Tarot card can inspire meaningful relationships. Its energies can propel us to meet new people and open our hearts to love and connection. This card encourages us to focus on our passions, be willing to take chances, and ultimately find the relationships that make us most happy.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The Six of Swords tarot card is a card of transition when it comes to health. It is telling you that it is time to make a change in your lifestyle. This may mean you have to transition to a healthier lifestyle and make changes that are more sustainable and beneficial to your body. Additionally, it is a sign that you have to transition away from any unhealthy habits you may have. This could mean diet, exercise, habits, or even limiting your exposure to stressful situations. It is a reminder to prioritize your physical and mental health, as this will be a key factor in improving your overall wellbeing.

Finances  
When it comes to finances, the Six of Swords Tarot card symbolizes transition. This card is telling you to transition from relying on your current financial situation to taking a more proactive role in your finances. It is a reminder to take control of your financial matters, as this will help you to prepare for the uncertain future. Additionally, the card can mean you need to transition away from risky investments and look for ways to create more secure and stable financial growth.

Relationships  
The Six of Swords tarot card is a sign of transition when it comes to relationships. This card is telling you to transition away from unhealthy relationships and prepare for the next step. It is a sign that it is time to move forward and invest emotionally in better, more meaningful relationships. Additionally, it can mean that it is time to transition from a comfort zone and become more open and honest with others. This will help you to build and strengthen relationships with others, as well as build your overall self-confidence.
