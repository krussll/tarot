A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Swords and The Queen Of Swords tarot cards together signify the end of a relationship or a phase of life cycle. This could portend a difficult period of sadness, loneliness and depression, as the combination of these two cards often suggest a break-up or divorce. The 3 Of Swords implies that intense emotions and sorrow must be felt and accepted before being able to move on and accept new beginnings. The Queen Of Swords speaks of honesty and being self-sufficient and independent, and can be seen as a sign of strength and resilience to cope with this change. This combination of the 3 Of Swords and the Queen Of Swords is a reminder to stay true to yourself and have faith that you will be able to overcome this difficult time and move forward.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 3 of Swords Tarot card can mean physical health issues, such as surgery or a chronic illness. It can indicate long-term health problems that may require a lifestyle change, or a major operation. On a mental health level, the card can refer to depression, sadness, or feeling overwhelmed and helpless. It might represent the need for therapy or some form of help to manage a difficult situation.

Finances

The 3 of Swords can signify financial loss or difficulty. It can indicate that a financial decision made in the past has left you in a difficult situation. It can also signify that you need to make a drastic change in order to recover from financial hardship. It may mean that you are going through a period of financial instability, or that a loan or investment has not paid off as expected.

Relationships

The 3 of Swords can refer to a traumatic event in a relationship, such as a divorce or break-up. It might indicate a deep emotional pain that needs to be addressed and healed. Alternately, it could signify a period of estrangement between two people due to misunderstanding or unresolved differences. It could also indicate a destructive pattern in a relationship, where one or both people are unable to move forward.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a self-sufficient person who is their own best advocate when it comes to their physical and mental well-being. The queen is an independent thinker and is able to make decisions based on what is best for them, instead of relying on the opinions of others. They are willing to work hard and make responsible choices about their health in order to maintain their physical and mental health. They will always strive for more knowledge and understanding about how their body works, and use that knowledge to make informed decisions about their lifestyle.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card indicates a person who is independent in their financial decisions. They are able to take control of their finances, including budgeting and planning for the future. They understand the importance of fiscal responsibility and will ensure that they are investing their money in a smart way. They have a realistic outlook on money and are comfortable with taking risks if it is ultimately going to benefit them in the long-term.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card represents a person who values total honesty in their relationships. They understand the importance of being open and honest with each other in order to maintain any type of relationship. They are confident in expressing their needs, desires, and feelings and will not be swayed by others’ opinions. They want to be respected and will go to great lengths to ensure that their boundaries are respected by others. They are not afraid to speak up for themselves and can cut ties with those who do not accept them for who they truly are.
