A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together?

The 9 Of Swords tarot and The Page Of Cups tarot card together could mean that you are currently struggling with difficult emotions, most likely grief, depression, or anxiety. You may be having nightmares or feel as though you cannot escape your worries. On the other hand, you may also be dealing with the start of a new relationship or being inspired to do volunteer work. All of these experiences can be a difficult balance, but ultimately it is important to focus on the new love and your inspiration to volunteer, as this could help you cope with the difficult emotions you are going through.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 9 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When the 9 of Swords and the Page of Cups appear in a reading regarding your love life, it could be a sign of difficulty ahead. The 9 of Swords is a card of inner turmoil and can indicate nightmares, worry, and grief. This might indicate challenges in your current relationship, or it could point to a fear of starting a relationship for fear of getting hurt.

The Page of Cups is more optimistic, however, and could suggest that you are ready to take a chance on love with someone new. This card could be a sign of a studious and devoted partner coming your way. There may have been difficulties in the past, but now you have an opportunity to begin a new relationship.

However, the Page of Cups also suggests being willing to give of yourself to help others. This card can also represent a volunteer project that you work on together with your potential partner. This could be a sign that you need to focus more on the relationship at hand than on your own worries and anxieties.

The 9 of Swords and the Page of Cups both suggest that you need to take a chance and move forward in your love life despite any fears or worries you may have. The past may have been difficult, but make sure to focus on the future and what you can create together with someone special. With a healthy dose of courage, you can make it through the night and be rewarded with great love.

The 9 of Swords symbolizes nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression, all of which can cause mental and physical strain, thus having an adverse effect on your job and financial life. You may find yourself constantly worrying about how your money will last, or if you have enough money to cover all your expenses. It is likely that this inner struggle is causing you to feel overwhelmed, frustrated and unable to make significant progress in your job or financial goals.

The Page of Cups, on the other hand, suggests that there is help available to you, in the form of either a studious kid, new love, or volunteer work. These things may not seem important at first, but they can be a great source of support and guidance. A studious kid can give you advice on how to save money, while a new relationship may provide emotional support. Volunteering can also help you to gain new skills and contacts in your field of work, which can lead to new opportunities.

The combination of The 9 of Swords and The Page of Cups suggests that you can get through this difficult patch in your life. You may be feeling overwhelmed, but there are things you can do to make your financial and job-related situations better. Utilizing the resources available to you and understanding that this is not an easy journey will help you cope with the inner turmoil and move forward. With the right guidance, people, and actions, you can make progress and find success in your finances and job.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 9 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

For Health:  
The 9 Of Swords Tarot card can signify great suffering, likely linked to physical, mental or emotional health issues. It denotes nightmares, overthinking, and deep-seated fear. It can be a warning to attend to physical health and take stock of mental health. Eating healthily and exercising are key to alleviating depression while therapy and/or medication can help to reduce anxiety. Self-care and self-love is essential in this regard.

For Finances:  
The 9 Of Swords Tarot card can represent financial insecurity and worries. It can indicate a need to reassess financial decisions and look for a new way to approach money matters. It is important to remember that problems can be solved through dedicated effort and that financial security should not be taken for granted. As long as one perseveres, money can be earned and managed with skill.

For Relationships:  
The 9 Of Swords suggests deep-seated anger and grief as well as difficulty in communication and/or trust. This card can represent an inability to express feelings or an unwillingness to confront issues that are causing distress. It is important to recognize when relationships are causing distress and work together to improve communication, trust, and understanding. It is possible to move from sadness to joy with the right attitude and support.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page Of Cups Tarot card indicates that there are opportunities for better health that can be gained through knowledge and understanding. It highlights the importance of taking a proactive role in the maintenance of one’s health. It suggests that it is important to be open to new possibilities and to study any information that comes your way. This can help you to find a healthier lifestyle and to develop better habits that can help to improve your overall wellbeing.

Finances

The Page Of Cups Tarot card also hints at financial opportunities that can be gained through knowledge, understanding and self-discipline. It is a reminder to be mindful of the financial decisions that you make and to be diligent in your research and planning. The energy of this card suggests that through self-control and proper management of resources, you can create a secure financial future for yourself.

Relationships

The Page Of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the emergence of love and new relationships. It is a reminder to be open to new possibilities and to be willing to be vulnerable and open up to others. This card encourages you to be accepting of new forms of love and understanding, both romantic and platonic. It is a reminder that to receive love we must first be willing to give it. This can be a daunting prospect, but if we take the time to nurture and develop our relationships, they will blossom and bring us joy and happiness.
