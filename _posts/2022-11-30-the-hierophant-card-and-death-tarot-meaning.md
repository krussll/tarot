They can represent the need to adhere to traditions and expectations while also recognizing the need for transformation and change in order to move forward. It reminds us to be mindful of the ways in which we can and should challenge conventional expectations and recognize when it is time to move on and experience something new. The Hierophant tarot card and The Death tarot card together suggest that although our paths may be different, it is important to be open to the possibility of change.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant can be a rather positive card for health and wellness. It can signify that one is following traditional treatments and remedies for whatever health issue they may have. This can be a sign that the individual is trusting in traditional techniques and has consulted health professionals. Another interpretation of this card is that it can represent someone following a strict traditional diet that helps maintain body health from a mind, body, and spirit perspective. The Hierophant card can imply that someone is staying within a healthy regimen and adhering to routines that keep the body and mind healthy.

Finances

The Hierophant card can offer advice in relation to financial matters. This card can indicate that one is leaving their financial planning up to expert sources and is relying on traditional financial strategies to provide them with financial stability. It also implies that the individual is working within a conventional system when making investments or instead of taking risks that could pay off, they may choose to stick to traditional investments. This card can be an indication that the individual is playing it safe with their money and not taking any major risks.

Relationships

The Hierophant card could be a sign of marital stability or that the couple is practicing traditional values when it comes to their relationship. It could indicate that both individuals are adhering to the expectations of society and can be a sign of commitment and dependability when it comes to their relationship. This card also can signify that the couple is partaking in traditional rituals to keep their connection strong such as weekly date nights or family get-togethers. The Hierophant could indicate a mature and enduring relationship for the couple.

When it comes to finances, the Death Tarot card can represent a time of change and transformation, as it may encourage us to pursue new opportunities or take risks that can lead to a more prosperous future. Although it may feel like the end of something, it can also bring the potential for great financial gain that can lead to security and stability.

In relationships, the Death Tarot card can signify a time of breakups and endings. It can also signify a time of transformations and healing for couples who have been struggling with a particular issue. The Death Tarot card encourages us to be open and honest with each other, and to let go of any negative patterns that are preventing us from having a healthy, happy relationship.

The Death Tarot card reminds us that change is inevitable, and that sometimes the most difficult times can bring us the most meaningful transformation. By embracing the uncertainty of life, we can move forward towards a more secure and prosperous future.
