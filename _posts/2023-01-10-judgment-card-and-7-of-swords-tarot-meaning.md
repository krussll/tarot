A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and the 7 of Swords tarot card together can be read as a warning that you need to be aware of the need to make a decision. In making this decision, you must be aware of any potential dishonesty or betrayal that could occur. As Judgment brings clarity, this is a wake-up call to you to focus on your life’s purpose instead of fleeing or stealing from others. The message is to stay true to yourself and make decisions that bring you closer to your goals.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Judgment and 7 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The Judgment tarot card and The 7 Of Swords tarot card appear together in a love reading, they indicate that a decision must be made. This decision will bring clarity to your life, and it’s purpose or meaning in regards to a relationship. It could mean that a betrayal or dishonesty has been uncovered that requires swift action. It does not necessarily mean the ending of the relationship, but could mean that changes need to be made to restore trust and fidelity.

These cards tell us that a final decision must be made, and this could come as a wake-up call. It may mean that one party will have to end their dishonesty and make an effort to restore the trust that has been broken. It could mean that one person needs to make a choice between staying in the relationship or walking away. If the decision is to stay, it could mean that boundaries and rules need to be set to prevent any further deception.

The Judgment tarot card partnered with The 7 Of Swords tarot card can also be a warning to keep an eye out for betrayal and deceit. If you feel that something is not quite right, it could be time to take a closer look at what’s going on. This may mean having conversations that may be difficult, but it is important to be honest and direct. Setting boundaries and establishing trust is key to having a lasting relationship.

Ultimately, The Judgment and The 7 Of Swords tarot card together in a love reading point towards making a final decision on what is best for your life, and how you want love to manifest. If there is dishonesty or betrayal present, it is time to confront it and make the necessary changes to move forward with clarity and purpose.

### What does Judgment and 7 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When The Judgment tarot card and The 7 of Swords appear together in a reading, it can signify a difficult period in your career or financial life. It could mean that someone has betrayed you and taken your money or resources without your permission. This could be a wake-up call for you to take control of your finances and your life’s purpose. You must now make a final decision about how to proceed and move forward.

It could also signify a time of clarity for you. You may start to see through the fog and lies of the person who betrayed you and gain insight into the truth of what has been happening. This can help you make a more informed decision in terms of how to protect yourself and your finances moving forward. It is important to remember that although it can be difficult to come to terms with a betrayal or loss, it may actually be the perfect opportunity to take control of your finances and make changes for the better.

With The Judgment tarot card and The 7 of Swords together in a reading, it could also mean that you are being called to look within yourself to find clarity and make a conscious effort to find your life’s purpose. This could mean finding a career or job that truly aligns with what you want and value in life. It could also mean taking a hard look at the way in which you manage your finances and making any necessary changes that could help you achieve financial security and independence.

The Judgment tarot card and The 7 of Swords together in a reading could signify a difficult transition in your finances and job. It can be a wake-up call to take ownership of your finances and to move on from past betrayals and losses. It can also give you clarity to make a final decision and purposeful choices about your finances and job.

### What does Judgment and 7 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The Judgment tarot card combined with The 7 Of Swords card can be a warning to reflect on your health and wellness. The Judgment card calls on you to take a moment and look at your life with clarity and objectivity. It may be time to re-evaluate and make a final decision about your health. It could be that your priorities are out of balance, and now is the time to take the necessary steps to restore that balance.

The 7 Of Swords card brings to light the danger of betrayal or dishonesty regarding your health. You may be faced with a temptation to prioritize other things over your own health and wellness. This could mean neglecting to eat healthily, or not getting enough exercise, or taking risks with your own physical or mental health.

The combined meaning of these two cards is to assess your own health and take charge of it. Protect yourself from betrayal and dishonesty of others and yourself. Don’t give in to the urge to take shortcuts and aimlessly drift through life. Make a conscious effort to keep your health a priority and make the right decisions with regards to your health and wellbeing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and 7 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health: The Judgment Tarot card is all about understanding how to use clarity and decision making to improve one’s health. This card encourages us to take a moment and ask ourselves if our current health decisions are in alignment with our true selves. We often make decisions out of fear or anxiety that ultimately don’t serve us in the long run. This card can be a wakeup call to pause and reflect on what we really want for our health. Taking the time to really listen to our inner voice and the wisdom we have available can lead to empowering decisions that could improve our overall health.

Finances: The Judgment Tarot card can be a reminder to step back and look at our financial habits objectively. This card encourages us to question whether our financial decisions are aligned with our values, goals and priorities. It can inspire us to make responsible and wise choices that set us up for a secure financial future. The Judgment Tarot card points to the possibility of uncovering underlying motivations or patterns that may be impacting our financial choices in negative ways, such as fear or insecurity.

Relationships: The Judgment Tarot card can remind us to take a step back and consider how our decisions are impacting our relationships. Sometimes we get stuck in patterns that keep us disconnected from the people that matter most. We can use this card to more clearly assess how our decisions and actions are impacting the relationships in our lives. Ask yourself, are you making decisions that are helping or hindering your relationships? Having this awareness can help guide you to make decisions that foster connection and understanding. The Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to recognize that our relationships should be a source of growth and fulfillment, not anxiety or guilt.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card typically symbolizes cheating, betrayal, and dishonesty. When it comes to health, this card can indicate a situation where you are deceiving yourself or someone else is misleading you with false information. It can be a sign that you have been avoiding taking responsibility for your health, and are instead denying reality and avoiding addressing issues. In terms of finances, this card can signify that you are engaging in shady business practices or have been taken advantage of financially. It could be a warning about the potential for fraud or identity theft. Finally, when it comes to relationships, this card often indicates that someone is not telling the truth or is engaging in secret activities that could be damaging to the relationship.

When it comes to health, this card could indicate a need to take a closer look at your lifestyle and make changes toward a healthier lifestyle. It is also a sign to be mindful of how you are taking care of yourself and to be honest with yourself about what is and isn’t working. In terms of finances, it could be a sign to watch out for potential scams or be more aware of what steps you are taking to protect your financial health. Additionally, it is a warning to be extra cautious and to not let anyone take advantage of you.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, it is a sign to be aware of any betrayals or dishonesty. This card could also suggest that you may need to have an honest conversation with your partner about any issues that may be hurting the relationship. The 7 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning to take extra care with your health, finances, and relationships and to be sure to practice honest and open communication in order to maintain and build trust.
