However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

For health, the 2 of Cups Tarot card indicates a healthy, balanced approach to life. This card is all about harmony, balance and integration. It suggests that you need to take care of yourself and be mindful of how your lifestyle and environment affect your physical, mental and emotional health. It can also indicate that you are paying attention to your physical needs, such as getting proper nutrition, exercise and rest. When this card comes up in a reading, it is important to focus on the positive aspects of your health and the steps that you can take to create a healthier life.

For finances, the 2 of Cups Tarot card suggests that financial balance is attainable and it is time to take a pragmatic approach to your finances. It can indicate that you have a good handle on your money and that you have created a budget that works to achieve your financial goals. It may suggest that it is time to take a closer look at your investments and make sure that they are diversified and in line with your financial objectives.

For relationships, the 2 of Cups Tarot card can indicate that a strong connection already exists between two people, and that it needs to be nurtured and developed. It suggests that a relationship is built on trust, understanding and mutual respect. It can symbolize a romantic relationship, a business relationship, or any situation in which two people need to work together. The card also indicates that communication is important in order to strengthen the bond, and that it is important to pay attention to the needs of each person in the relationship.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

For Health:

The Page of Cups Tarot card suggests a time of emotional healing, as well as gentle physical health and wellbeing. This card symbolizes renewal and rejuvenation, so it encourages us to take time to nurture ourselves and our bodies. Now is the perfect time to explore new forms of self-care, such as restorative exercise, yoga, meditation, and massage. We may also be able to benefit from paying more attention to our mental health and trying to think more positively. We should make sure to get plenty of rest, eat healthy meals and hydrate well.

For Finances:

The Page of Cups Tarot card suggests that now is a great time to look at our finances and make sure they’re in order. It is important to review our budget and make sure we are on the right track. This can include creating new budget goals, reassessing our spending, and evaluating how we can better save money and make our money work for us. We can also look at ways to increase our income, such as launching a side hustle. The Page of Cups encourages us to be creative and innovative as we explore these new opportunities.

For Relationships:

The Page of Cups Tarot card encourages us to view our relationships with a new light. Now is the perfect time to take a step back and reflect on what we need from our relationships, both personally and professionally. We can focus on nourishing our existing relationships and take the time to reach out to the people that we may have lost touch with. We can also explore new, healthier forms of relationships and embrace the joys of meeting new people. The Page of Cups tells us that, as we work on ourselves, we can create more positive and fulfilling relationships.
