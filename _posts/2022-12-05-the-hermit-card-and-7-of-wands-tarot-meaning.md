A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 7 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 7 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The message of these two tarot cards together is to recognize that the path to truth and spiritual growth can be a difficult one, and requires self-defense to protect oneself and one’s journey. It is important to remember that while it is important to look within for the answers you seek, it is just as important to protect and guard that journey so that others may not disrupt or impede your progress. The Hermit and the 7 of Wands urge you to seek knowledge and illumination while learning to stay true to yourself and defend your truth.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

While taking a step back and reflecting, you may realize that it’s time to make a stand for your relationships, whether it be to push back against external competition or to defend yourself against an internal conflict. It is essential to remain strong and firm in your beliefs and decisions about your relationships.

On an energetic level, the combination of The Hermit tarot card and The 7 of Wands tarot card encourage you to stay grounded, centered, and balanced while standing your ground. It is important to remember that while it is ok to defend yourself and to protect your relationships, it is also important to be compassionate and understanding. You have the power to choose how you move forward, so it is important to be mindful of your words and actions.

The Hermit tarot card and the 7 of Wands tarot card are a reminder that there is strength in being vulnerable, honest, and humble. It is time to go within and find a greater understanding of yourself and your relationships. The internal and external struggles in your love life are a chance to come out of the struggle wiser, stronger, and more connected.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 7 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card indicates a period of time during which solitude and introspection is necessary to gain clarity and understanding in all aspects of your life – health, finances, and relationships.

In regards to health, The Hermit card urges you to adopt a proactive role in your wellbeing. Take time to gain a better understanding of your physical, mental, and emotional needs and find ways to take care of yourself. That could mean visiting a doctor to learn more about your health, or perhaps trying new relaxation techniques and forms of exercise. The Hermit card can also indicate potential medical problems and a need for medical attention.

The Hermit card also speaks to your finances. It may be a warning to be more mindful of your spending habits and to research any financial decisions before making them. Pay attention to your intuition and use it as a guide. Developing a well thought out budget, and having savings put aside for emergencies, are signs of financial stability. Acknowledge any mistakes you have made and be honest about your current financial situation.

Finally, The Hermit card can also refer to your relationships. It is a reminder to listen to your inner wisdom when it comes to the people you choose to surround yourself with. Spending time alone can be a way to reflect on the relationships in your life, and to discover what is working, and what isn’t. It’s important to pay attention to how you feel and to be honest with yourself about what you need in order to remain healthy in your relationships.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands tarot card is associated with the element of fire and symbolizes determination, courage, and willingness to fight in the face of adversity. In health, the 7 of Wands card calls for a heightened awareness of our own physical and mental resources. This card encourages us to be mindful of our own health and to have the courage to step up if we are feeling overwhelmed with stress, exhaustion, or illness. From a financial standpoint, the 7 of Wands encourages us to stay competitive and to take proactive measures to protect our financial wellbeing. This card invites us to be mindful of our spending and remember that our finances can be both a blessing and a curse.

In relationships, the 7 of Wands tarot card is indicative of the courage it takes to stand up for ourselves and what we believe in. This card calls upon us to be honest and open with our feelings, and to take care of ourselves, even when faced with criticism or competition. It is a reminder that good communication and self-honesty are essential ingredients in any successful relationship. The 7 of Wands also encourages us to remain vigilant about our relationships, to ensure that we are not being taken advantage of or taken for granted. Above all, this card asks us to trust our intuition and to be true to who we are, no matter the cost.
