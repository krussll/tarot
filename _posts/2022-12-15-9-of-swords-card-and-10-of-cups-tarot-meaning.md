A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean together?

When The 9 Of Swords and The 10 Of Cups tarot cards come together, they can often bring a message of hope and reassurance through difficult times. This can be a reminder that no matter how dark and oppressive your current state of mind may feel, you have a built-in support system to help you through. Your family and loved ones are there for you and will provide you with love and support, which can be essential in helping you work through your anxiety, grief, and depression. With their help, you will be able to find peace and contentment.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 9 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 9 of Swords and the 10 of Cups tarot cards suggest that despite the darkness and depression brought on by the 9 of Swords, an underlying sense of love and support from family and friends is present. While the 9 of Swords speaks of nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression, the 10 of Cups offers a ray of hope. It indicates that your loved ones are there to help you through this difficult period, whether it be through providing practical support, offering emotional strength, or simply being a listening ear. It is important to lean into this support network during this time and keep in mind that it’s OK to take a break from life and its stresses if needed.

The 9 of Swords and 10 of Cups tarot cards also suggest that you need to practice self-care during this time. Taking time for yourself and doing activities that bring joy and calmness to your life will help to alleviate the looming darkness. Whether it be listening to music, exercising, journaling, or going for a walk, it is important to do whatever you can to bring peace and healing to your mind and soul.

Although the 9 of Swords speaks of pain and heartache, the 10 of Cups is a reminder that you are loved and held in unconditional love by those around you. This presence of love and understanding can be both a comfort and a reminder that you are strong and will eventually overcome the grief and depression. Although it may take time and effort, the combination of the 9 of Swords and 10 of Cups suggest that you will come out of this situation a stronger and more resilient version of yourself.

### What does 9 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

The combination of the 9 of Swords and 10 of Cups cards can spell out a warning for those looking over their finances and job prospects. The 9 of Swords warns that one could be dealing with nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression over their financial situation. This could be linked to a variety of things, such as a job loss, unexpected expenses, or difficulty in getting a loan. The 9 of Swords can indicate that a person needs to step back and assess their financial situation and make changes in order to make it more manageable.

The 10 of Cups can be a sign of relief for those dealing with the financial stresses of the 9 of Swords. It can show reassurance that there is a silver lining to the situation at hand, such as family contentment, total love, and support. This indicates of a sense of community that may be helpful in finding a solution to the financial woes that have crept up. With the support of family and friends, it may be possible to find alternate sources of income or to restructure current debt in order to make it easier to manage.

In conclusion, the combination of the 9 of Swords and 10 of Cups can be a potentially difficult one. On one hand, one needs to be aware of the financial hardships that could arise and be prepared to face them. On the other hand, one needs to take comfort in the knowledge that they have a supportive network that can help them through the tough times. With careful planning and resourcefulness, this combination of cards could be the start of a bright future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 9 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take care of your mental health. It speaks to the nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression associated with feeling overwhelmed. This card can signify a period of restlessness and pain, but also an opportunity for self-reflection. It signals a need to make time for yourself to take care of your mental health. This could include reaching out for help from family, friends, or professionals, engaging in stress-relieving activities, creating a self-care plan, and engaging in mindful practices such as meditation and yoga.

Finances:

The 9 of Swords Tarot card signifies potential financial struggles, especially if it is combined with other negative cards. The card might indicate feeling overwhelmed by debt or a lack of financial security. It speaks to the anxiety and distress of feeling unable to make ends meet. The card might point to a need to take some financial risks or look into creative ways to make money. It might also be a reminder that money isn’t the most important thing in life and to be mindful of the relationships that truly matter.

Relationships:

The 9 of Swords Tarot card indicates a period of caution in relationships. It symbolizes nightmares, anxiety, grief and depression that can disrupt intimate relationships and friendships. This card suggests that it might be necessary to take a step back and reassess if a relationship is bringing you joy. It could also be a reminder to be gentle with yourself, as well as with others. It might be difficult to open up and talk about challenging emotions, but it is important to communicate to prevent them from growing and damaging the relationship.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is associated with good health, both physical and mental. It indicates that the individual is enjoying a balanced lifestyle and is not overworking themselves. The composition of the card suggests that the individual is energized and motivated, keeping their body and mind in check. Furthermore, they have a supportive environment – both within their family unit and outside of it – encouraging and helping them keep a good healthy balance.

Finances: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of financial stability and security. The individual is content and confident with their current earning power and resources, knowing that they have enough for the present and for the future. Additionally, the stability of their financial situation is due to the support of their family and friends, which suggests that the individual is not afraid to ask for help when needed.

Relationships: The 10 of Cups Tarot card represents strong, long-lasting relationships that are based on mutual respect, kindness, understanding and happiness. The individual is content with their current home life and family situation, and is lucky enough to be surrounded by those who are emotionally available and supportive. They are able to count on their family and friends to be understanding and there to listen when they need it. This tarot card suggests that the individual is in a very secure and loving environment, and that their relationships are a safe haven for them.
