However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

You may be feeling at rock bottom or exhausted from the ending and you are ready to move on and abandon this past experience. It can signify a dark and heavy period in your life, and you are ready to start over and get out of this dark place. You may be feeling despondent, defeated and abandoned, but it is time to leave and start fresh.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Swords and 8 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Swords and 8 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

In Health:  
The Ten of Swords Tarot card symbolizes an end to health complications. A rock bottom moment of complete and utter exhaustion and a lack of resources to continue on. The Ten of Swords can represent a terminal illness or diagnosis that may cause physical and emotional exhaustion as you come to terms with the situation. This moment can feel like the last straw and can be incredibly intense and difficult to cope with. Ultimately, the Ten of Swords can bring a necessary closure to a situation and a moment of healing despite the immense pain and difficulty.

In Finances:  
The Ten of Swords Tarot card can represent a financial crisis. This could mean a period of extreme financial hardship and a lack of resources to help you make progress. This moment can symbolize reaching the lowest point of exhaustion and feeling powerless to make changes. The Ten of Swords could mean debts, bankruptcy, or the loss of resources necessary to financially survive. This card can represent the sense of being overwhelmed and feeling a lack of hope to move forward. Despite the difficulty of such a situation, the Ten of Swords can bring a necessary closure and the opportunity to start fresh.

In Relationships:  
The Ten of Swords Tarot card can signify the end of a relationship. This could mean that a relationship has reached complete and painful exhaustion and can no longer survive. This card could represent a break-up, a divorce, or the loss of a loved one. This moment can feel like a complete rock bottom with no light at the end of the tunnel. The Ten of Swords is a reminder that this type of closure can bring a necessary healing and the opportunity for new beginnings.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 8 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes negative or difficult health-related decisions. It suggests that an individual might turn away from a physician’s advice or a treatment plan after careful consideration. This card can also indicate that an individual is leaving behind unhealthy behaviors, such as substance abuse or unhealthy coping mechanisms. It can signify a retreat, withdrawal or escape from a situation that is holding a person back from achieving their desired health goals.

Finances: The 8 of Cups Tarot card can indicate that someone is leaving behind a financial situation that is not beneficial to them. It can suggest that they will be withdrawing from investments, stocks, or any other financial endeavor that has become unprofitable or high risk. It can also suggest that a person has chosen to retreat from a job or career path that is no longer meeting their goals or needs. The card is a sign that it is time for a change and a fresh start.

Relationships: The 8 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the ending of a relationship or the abandonment of a situation that is no longer beneficial to someone’s emotional or mental well being. This can be a difficult decision to make, but it is often necessary to create a healthy balance in our lives. It can also signify that someone is breaking away from unhealthy attachments, such as codependent relationships or a bad habit of needing the approval of others. The 8 of Cups encourages us to take action and to make the changes necessary to ensure that we are in healthy relationships.
