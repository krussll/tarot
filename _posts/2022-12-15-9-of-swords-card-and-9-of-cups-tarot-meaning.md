A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

The 9 Of Swords Tarot card reminds me that overcoming the inner monsters of sleepless nights, uncertainty, heartache and depression is possible. It also teaches me that finding peace within can only be done when I accept and process my pain and use it as a catalyst for self-growth. The 9 Of Cups Tarot card, on the other hand, serves as a reminder of the rewards of this journey. With it, I am encouraged to appreciate the moments of contentment, joy, and abundance that come with facing my fears and reaching a higher level of inner peace. Together, these cards provide me with an understanding of the power of resilience and the transformative potential that lies within.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 9 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The Nine of Swords Tarot card is associated with nightmares, anxiety, grief and depression and it is an indication of the overall state of mental and emotional distress that many people can experience. Within the physical realm, it suggests the feelings of exhaustion and sleeplessness that can accompany mental health issues. In terms of finances, this card is a warning of potential losses and financial stress due to a lack of clarity, foresight and planning. The Nine of Swords can be an indication of poor decisions and misplaced trust that can lead to monetary losses and scarcity. In relationships, this card signifies fear and worry, and it can manifest in mistrust, aggression and insecurity. The card can be a sign of an inability to communicate effectively, or of a lack of trust and commitment, which can lead to distressing and volatile relationships. This card is a reminder to be aware of our own emotions and take responsibility for our own choices. Seeking professional help is often a good idea in cases of mental health issues and troubled relationships, especially when the Nine of Swords appears in a reading.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The Nine of Cups Tarot card is a powerful symbol of good health. This card signals that emotional balance, harmony and wellbeing are at hand. It implies that the body is strong and resilient, and the mind is relaxed and content. The Nine of Cups signals complete emotional fulfillment and a deep contentment with the physical body. Health-wise, this card indicates a period of contentment and balance in all facets of life, where the body, mind and spirit are all in alignment.

Finances:

The Nine of Cups Tarot card also signals a prosperous period of financial abundance. This card suggests that material wealth and financial wellbeing is in abundance. It signifies that the materialistic side of life will be taken care of, and good luck in business and finance will come your way. The Nine of Cups also means that the hard work you have put in to reach your financial goals will finally pay off. Money will come your way in abundance, and life will be stable.

Relationships:

The Nine of Cups tarot card is also a powerful symbol of emotional fulfilment and contentment in relationship. It signifies a period where relationships flourish, harmony and balance is found, and there is a strong emotional bond between partners. The Nine of Cups also suggests that there will be an emotional security in relationships, and that the ties that bind will be strong and enduring. This card symbolizes emotional fulfilment and satisfaction in all relationships, both romantic and platonic.
