Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 7 Of Swords card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 7 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 7 Of Swords.

## 7 Of Swords And Love – An Overview

The 7 of Swords tarot card relates to health in that it speaks to issues of dishonesty, betrayal, and theft.

This can be interpreted as a warning to be aware of the ways in which one’s health can be taken from them or compromised.

These could include stolen information or data, the breaching of confidential medical records, or the theft of vital medical supplies.

It warns against trusting those who may be untrustworthy, and encourages one to remain vigilant in protecting their health and wellbeing.

By being aware of the potential pitfalls and dangers to one’s health, it is possible to protect oneself and ensure one’s wellbeing is not compromised.

## Upright Meanings For 7 Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

The 7 of Swords tarot card suggests that there is a lack of trust in a romantic relationship.

It symbolizes betrayal, theft, and dishonesty in love.

It could signify that one partner is not being honest or that someone is trying to get away with something.

It can also indicate that one partner is running away from the relationship instead of facing the issues head-on.

In summary, the 7 of Swords tarot card warns of potential deception and betrayal in a relationship and reminds us to pay attention to the signs of disharmony.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 of Swords is an omen that suggests that the future of your love life may involve theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and running away.

This card indicates that in order to protect yourself and your heart, you must be aware of the potential for deceit and dishonesty in your relationships.

You must be able to recognize the signs of a dishonest partner or a relationship headed in the wrong direction.

Whether it’s an emotional or physical betrayal, you must be prepared to confront what is happening and take action if needed.

Ultimately, being mindful of the 7 of Swords can help you stay guarded and find a more honest relationship in the future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 of Swords tarot card speaks of treachery and betrayal in the realm of love.

It can represent a lack of trust between partners, or a sense of dishonesty that has been brought into the relationship.

It can mean that one partner is running away from the relationship, either physically or emotionally.

It may indicate that someone is not being entirely honest about their feelings and intentions, or that there are elements of deceit and secrecy in the relationship.

Ultimately, this card warns that a relationship may not be as secure and trusting as it appears on the surface, and can be a sign of discord in the love between two people.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The 7 Of Swords is reversed in a love reading, it suggests a more positive outlook for the relationship.

Instead of being a source of betrayal, dishonesty, and running away, the reversed card suggests that a sense of trust, loyalty, and faithfulness is developing within the relationship.

This could suggest that any previous feelings of betrayal, distrust, or dishonesty are being worked through and resolved, leading to a stronger and more honest connection between the two partners.

The reversed 7 of Swords also suggests that the relationship is heading towards a much more secure and healthy future.

This could mean that the two partners are taking steps to rebuild trust and honesty between them.

They could be actively communicating with one another in a way that is constructive, rather than destructive, which fosters a safe space for them to be vulnerable and share their feelings.

In short, the reversed 7 of Swords suggests that the relationship is getting stronger, and that the two partners are actively working to make it more secure and honest.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 7 of Swords in the tarot is a sign of hope and possibility in your love life.

It suggests that you are in a period of transformation, and that you are about to experience a new level of trust and honesty in your relationship.

The reversed card encourages you to be open to receiving love without feeling the need to be overly protective or suspicious of others.

It indicates that your future partner will be trustworthy, loyal, and devoted to you, and that you will be able to have a relationship based on mutual respect and understanding.

The reversed card also indicates that communication will be key in your relationship, as it will serve to counteract any feelings of mistrust or deception.

You should be prepared to have honest and open conversations with your partner and to be willing to be vulnerable with them.

You should trust that your partner has nothing but the best intentions for you and will be willing to work together to build a healthy, lasting relationship.

The reversed 7 of Swords indicates that your future love life will be filled with loyalty, commitment, and a strong foundation of trust, and that you and your partner can build something special together.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed 7 of Swords appears in a Tarot reading regarding love and close relationships, it can indicate that things may not always be as they seem.

Someone close to you may be hiding something, or even betraying you in some way.

This can be the cause of much heartache if it is not dealt with in a healthy manner.

It may be time to take a step back and consider how much trust you are placing in those close to you, and perhaps even consider ending a relationship if it is not fulfilling.

It can also signify that it is time to be honest with yourself and those around you.

If there is something going on that is causing you pain and heartache, it is important to communicate in a healthy and honest way about it.

It’s important to speak up and make sure that your needs are being respected and met.

If you are feeling taken advantage of by someone close to you, it may be time to set some firm boundaries and re-evaluate what you need from this relationship.
