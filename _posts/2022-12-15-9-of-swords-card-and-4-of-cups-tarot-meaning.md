It is a message that we should not ignore our thoughts and anxieties and that we must instead strive to break through the cycle of grief and depression. This can often be a difficult process, and in some cases we may feel apathetic and unfulfilled. However, if we are willing to look deep within ourselves, we may be surprised to find gifts that we didn’t know we had. With a bit of courage and effort, we can turn our nightmares into a source of strength.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Swords and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card carries a heavy message of despair, fear, and isolation. It is a card of deep inner turmoil that can manifest in various ways in health, finances, and relationships.

When it comes to health, the 9 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a general feeling of physical and mental exhaustion. Stress, headaches, and insomnia are common physical manifestations that can arise from this card. It can also indicate a greater fear of the unknown, with a feeling of being overwhelmed or helpless in confronting life’s challenges and changes. Mentally, it can also point to a deep sense of anxiety, guilt, or even self-loathing.

When it comes to finances, the 9 of Swords Tarot card suggests a difficulty in making sound financial choices. Fear of the future can lead to impulsive or irresponsible behaviors when it comes to spending and saving. It can also lead to feelings of despair or hopelessness when it comes to improving or changing one’s financial situation.

In relationships, the 9 of Swords Tarot card can indicate feelings of loneliness and disconnection. A sense of being misunderstood or mistreated can lead to feelings of mistrust or even despair. Fear of failure can also lead to a need to control and manipulate situations to maintain order and security.

Overall, the 9 of Swords Tarot card speaks to a deep inner turmoil that can manifest in various ways. It is important to pay attention to the warning signs and to reach out for help when needed. With the right guidance and support, this difficult card can lead to a place of understanding and healing.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card could signify a period of stagnation and apathy when it comes to health matters. This card suggests that you’re feeling unmotivated to make any positive changes to your overall health and wellness, and instead embracing a generally passive attitude. It might be that the same old strategies and methods you’ve been relying on to stay healthy have become stale and uninspiring to you. Without a sense of surprise or excitement about nourishing your body and mind, you may be neglecting to take the necessary steps to stay healthy.

Finances:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card may suggest a feeling of discontentment with your financial situation. This card can denote an unwillingness to take the necessary steps to reaching your financial goals, instead settling for a certain level of unfulfillment. You may find yourself resistant to making necessary changes to your budget, investments and spending patterns to improve your financial situation. The 4 of Cups also suggests that you may have neglected to take advantage of any surprise gifts that have come your way financially.

Relationships:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card could signify a dullness or stagnation in your relationships. This card could denote a feeling of being unfulfilled by the relationships around you, and a general lack of excitement or surprise. You may be feeling unable to move forward in any of your relationships, stuck in a rut of repetitive cycles. It’s important to shake things up and look for opportunities for renewal and surprise. The 4 of Cups suggests that it may be time to take a chance on something new to rekindle your relationships and bring a level of excitement back into them.
