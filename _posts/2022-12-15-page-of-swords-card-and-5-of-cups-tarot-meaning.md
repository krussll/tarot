A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Page Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The Page of Swords and the 5 of Cups tarot cards together represent a difficult feeling of trying to uncover the truth about a particular situation, whilst dealing with a sense of loss, regret and grief. Perhaps you feel like someone has betrayed you, leaving you feeling hurt and abandoned. You want the truth, but don’t want to be hurt any further. This is a difficult balancing act, as you try to seek the truth and protect yourself at the same time. It’s important to move forward cautiously and remember that no matter how much you feel lost, the truth will eventually reveal itself.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This could be due to the curiosity of a third party, such as a nosy friend or family member, or even a spy or private detective getting involved in your relationship.

The Page Of Swords indicates that although there is loss, the truth will come out in the end and it is important that you remain vigilant and be prepared to confront and investigate any suspicious behavior. It is possible that the truth has already been revealed but you have avoided it due to your grief.

The combination of these two tarot cards suggests that with patience and an open mind, the truth will come out and your relationship may be mended. It also suggests that to move forward you will need to confront the issues that have caused the rift, resolve any misunderstandings, and rebuild trust and understanding. There may be some difficult conversations ahead, but ultimately they will result in a healthier relationship and a more meaningful connection.

At the same time, the 5 of Cups suggests that you may feel emotionally overwhelmed when it comes to your job or finances. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The Page Of Swords Tarot card can represent a need to keep a watchful eye on our finances. It can also be a sign to be more inquisitive and to ask questions and gain accurate information, so that our relationships are based on truth. It is important to take the time to get a clear understanding of our partners and to navigate any conflicts or issues that may arise.

Health: The 5 of Cups tarot card means that health problems can lead to feeling abandoned and unloved. These feelings can manifest in physical symptoms, such as headaches, fatigue, and depression. It can also mean that you need to pay closer attention to your physical health, as neglecting it can lead to further complications down the road. You must take time for yourself and seek medical advice when needed.

Finances: The 5 of Cups tarot card represents a loss of money and a feeling of regret. When this card appears, it can suggest that you may have made some bad financial decisions or that you have been careless with money. It is important to reevaluate your financial situation and make sure that you are taking the proper steps to secure your future. This card can also indicate that you need to look for a way to make additional income and be cautious with the money you have.

Relationships: The 5 of Cups tarot card suggests that there is a feeling of abandonment or lack of love in a relationship. It is also possible that you need to take a break from a relationship and look for ways to regain your independence. Ultimately, it is important to communicate openly and honestly with your partner in order to ensure that the relationship is healthy and fulfilling for both parties.
