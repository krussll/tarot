A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 of Swords and the 10 of Cups tarot cards together can signify a necessary transition or relocating, but with the love, support, and contentment of your family. This transition may come with a sense of relief and anticipation for a better life for yourself and your family. Moving can be a hard decision but your loved ones are by your side to make it a bit easier. With family by your side, you can find joy in a new place and a brighter future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This could be in the form of a physical or emotional relocation, or it could simply be a transition of a new era in your relationship.

The 10 of Cups in this reading also signifies that you will be able to do this transition with a sense of optimism and joy. The love that you share with your significant other will help you get through this period of change and come out the other side stronger and more connected than before. This new move may bring with it an even stronger sense of family and togetherness, allowing the two of you to share contentment, love and support for one another.

This journey will not be without its trials and tribulations, and it’s important to remember that it will take time and dedication from both of you to ensure its success. However, with the 6 of Swords and the 10 of Cups working in tandem, you can be sure that the changes you make will be in your best interest and that you have the support of your loved one to make sure it works out for the better. Your relationship will undoubtedly flourish in the new chapter that awaits you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card is a positive sign for health. It suggests that a transition is necessary for you to become healthier. This could mean improving your lifestyle habits, such as exercising more, watching what you eat, and getting adequate sleep. It also suggests that it may be time to consider changing careers or reducing stress levels, as this can have a positive effect on your overall wellbeing. Additionally, the transition could be related to the use of alternative therapies or treatments, such as massage, meditation, or acupuncture.

Finances

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card indicates a transition that could be beneficial in terms of your finances. You could be considering a career change or investing in a new and profitable venture, both of which could increase your potential for financial gain. It also suggests that you may want to look at ways to reduce your debts, by working out a payment or debt consolidation plan, or by shaving off recurring expenses. Whatever you decide to do, the 6 Of Swords Tarot card offers a renewed hope and optimism in financial matters.

Relationships

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card encourages a transition in relationships, especially if a stagnant period has been occurring. It suggests that there is a need to move on from certain relationships that may be hindering your emotional health. This could mean leaving a toxic relationship, starting a new one, or simply reconnecting with old friends. The 6 Of Swords Tarot card encourages an honest assessment of where your relationships stand and encourages you to make changes if necessary. Additionally, it suggests a need to invest in yourself, such as becoming more independent, to further benefit your relationships.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

For health, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card signifies physical and mental well-being. On a physical level, it encourages us to keep our bodies in harmony and balance with the world around us. It is a reminder to nurture our bodies with proper nutrition, hydration and sleep, and to be mindful of our physical limitations. On a mental level, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card encourages us to stay connected with our families and friends, and to foster a sense of deep, lasting love and support.

For finances, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card suggests a secure, steady, and prosperous flow of income. It implies that financial goals can be achieved with ease and grace if one nurtures strong relationships with others. It is a reminder to take care of one’s resources and to wisely manage one’s finances.

For relationships, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card signifies a deep, unconditional, and trusting connection with the people in our lives. It encourages us to open our hearts to the people around us and to foster an environment of deep understanding, respect, and love. It reminds us to think of our family, friends, and partners as an integral part of our lives, and to commit to nurturing strong, loving relationships that can last a lifetime.
