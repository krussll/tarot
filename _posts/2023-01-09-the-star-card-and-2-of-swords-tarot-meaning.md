However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Star card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Star and 2 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Star mean?

Health

The Star Tarot card is a card of hope, renewal, and inner clarity. It encourages those feeling hopeless to have faith and trust that things will get better. It encourages people to focus on their mental health and well-being, and to believe in the power of miracles. The Star card speaks to the power of resilience, being able to overcome any obstacle, and finding strength within. It encourages us to stay positive, to keep our minds open to new possibilities and to stay focused on our goals. With the Star card, our physical health can benefit from the sense of inner peace and clarity it instills.

Finances

The Star Tarot card speaks to the potential of new beginnings, which can be applied to one’s financial situation. It is a reminder to keep faith, to have hope and to remain optimistic. It can help one to look at their financial situation from a different perspective, and offer guidance to see opportunities where there seemed to be none. It encourages us to take action and to make decisions with a clear and balanced state of mind. With this in mind, it also speaks to the importance of good financial planning and budgeting.

Relationships

The Star Tarot card is a reminder that the relationships we have with others come down to trust, faith, and having hope. It encourages us to be open to new experiences and to trust in the power of renewal. It serves as a reminder that with patience and communication, we can get through even the toughest of times. It speaks to the importance of being vulnerable with one another, and of showing kindness and understanding towards our loved ones. All in all, the Star card encourages us to keep our relationships strong and healthy, and to look to the brighter side of things.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health: The Two of Swords Tarot card is an indication that you need to assess your overall health and make decisions about how to move forward in a more balanced way. It can be difficult to make decisions at times, especially if you feel stuck in a rut. It’s important to weigh the pros and cons of any decisions that you make, as making a rush decision could have unforeseen consequences. Take some time to really consider options and think about consequences if you make the wrong choice.

Finances: The Two of Swords Tarot card is an indication that it is time to make a financial decision if you find yourself in a bind. It’s important to take into account the various options you have and weigh them out before making the decision. This could mean budgeting, cutting back on expenses, or looking into possible investments. Don’t be afraid to ask for help if you don’t feel like you can make tough decisions on your own – enlisting the help of a financial advisor can be invaluable.

Relationships: The Two of Swords Tarot card is an indication that a decision is necessary in order to resolve a conflict or to bring a relationship to the next level. In order to make the right decision it is necessary to be aware of the various options and to consider the emotions of both parties. Don’t be afraid to compromise, as this is often the best way to move forward. It’s also important to be able to trust your instincts and to trust that the decision you make is the right one for both individuals.
