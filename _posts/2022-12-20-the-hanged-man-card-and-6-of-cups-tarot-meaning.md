It is also a reminder to not forget the little moments, to appreciate the people and moments in life that matter most, and to be open to the possibilities of reuniting with those you may have lost touch with. This combination of cards encourages a moment of pause and encourages us to reconnect with our spiritual side and reflect on the importance of nostalgia.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and 6 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health

The Hanged Man Tarot card is often seen as a sign of transformation, and when it comes to health, it is no exception. This card indicates that we need to take a step back and rethink our approach to our health. We may be stuck in old patterns that are no longer serving us and need to find new, healthier ways to manage our health. The Hanged Man can also signify that it’s time to make some sacrifices in order to improve our overall well-being. We may need to make lifestyle changes, such as eating better, exercising more, and getting proper rest. Taking the time to focus on our health and take care of ourselves is essential when this card appears.

Finances

When it comes to finances, The Hanged Man Tarot card suggests that we need to reconsider our approach to managing our money. It’s time to take a step back and look at our current financial situation from a different perspective. We may need to make some changes in order to increase our income or reduce our expenses. We may also need to sacrifice some luxuries in order to save money. The Hanged Man can also indicate that it’s time to start planning for the future by investing or saving money in order to ensure our financial security.

Relationships

The Hanged Man Tarot card suggests that we need to reassess our approach to our relationships. It’s time to take a step back and consider how we are interacting with the people in our lives. Are we getting along with them, or are we constantly in conflict? Are we making an effort to nurture our relationships, or are we taking them for granted? It may be time to make some changes in order to grow our relationships and deepen our connections. We may need to be willing to sacrifice our own needs in order to let the other person’s needs be met as well.

This could be an old recipe that you make with your family, a hobby or sport that you used to enjoy, or even just something that brings you joy like a simple walk in the park. It can also mean that it’s a good time to reassess your financial goals and make sure that you’re on track with them.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Cups Tarot card can be a reminder to take a step back and appreciate the simpler things in life. It can be a great way to reminisce and remember the good times that you’ve shared, as well as to rebuild relationships that may have fallen apart. It’s also a great way to rediscover a sense of joy and innocence in your relationships.
