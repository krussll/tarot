A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 9 Of Wands and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together speak to the importance of having strong boundaries around your relationships. It is important to remain both persistant and mindful of what is acceptable in all of your friendships, and the way in which you choose to interact with the people who are important in your life. Embrace the power of celebration and friendship, but also recognize the significance of keeping and maintaining your boundaries. Celebrate yourself and others in ways that honor your individual experience and respect the space that you have filled out for yourself.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This card encourages you to establish a circle of support, such as a group of friends or family members, who can provide emotional and moral support. The combination of these two cards in a tarot reading encourages you to find the balance between maintaining boundaries and finding support in your social circle. When you know where you stand, it makes it easier to appreciate the relationship and all the things that come with it. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 9 Of Wands and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 9 of Wands tarot card is a great reminder to stay connected to your own well-being and practice self-care. It asks us to take appropriate boundaries in order to maintain our health and well-being. It’s important to stay mindful of our own physical and mental health and to be aware of what energies and activities may be affecting us. It can also be a reminder to seek professional help if needed and to put our health first.

Finances:

The 9 of Wands tarot card encourages us to take control of our financial wellbeing and to be mindful of how our finances affect us. It asks us to create financial boundaries and to prioritize our own self-care in terms of expenses and investments. It encourages us to take the time to create a financial plan that is realistic and tailored to our own needs. It also advises us to be mindful of our spending habits and to save for a rainy day.

Relationships:

The 9 of Wands tarot card encourages us to create and maintain healthy boundaries in our relationships. It reminds us to be mindful of what we are giving and taking and to ensure that both parties are happy and feel respected. It is a reminder to take the time to assess our relationships and to ensure that they are healthy, equitable and supportive. It also asks us to be mindful of our own needs and to trust our instincts if we feel as though something is off or needs to be addressed.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups Tarot Card represents friendship, celebrations, and a sense of support amongst those around us. For those seeking to improve their health, finances and relationships, this card can be a sign of encouragement and positivity.

When it comes to health, the Three of Cups card encourages people to look to the people around them for support and guidance. This can be friends, family, or even medical professionals. This is a reminder that together with the help of those around us, we can work together to reach our health goals.

For matters concerning finances, the Three of Cups card suggests that we look for support from those around us. This could mean speaking to financially savvy friends or family members for advice, or taking part in online money forums for guidance. The Three of Cups card encourages us to take advantage of the collective knowledge and experience of those around us to help reach financial stability.

As for relationships, the Three of Cups card encourages us to celebrate the good people in our lives. We should celebrate the friendships and support that we share with others, and reflect on our relationships to ensure they are healthy. This is a reminder that the strong relationships in our lives should be celebrated and nurtured.

In conclusion, the Three of Cups Tarot Card can be seen as an idea of collective health, financial stability, and strong relationships. In order to build our own lives, it is often helpful to take advantage of the support network surrounding us. This card encourages us to celebrate those we hold dear, and to make use of the relationships in our lives to help support us in the way we need.
