## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 5 of Cups Tarot card suggests that there is a feeling of physical and emotional exhaustion. It may relate to a health issue that is causing feelings of being overwhelmed and despair. The card can indicate that one is not taking proper care of their health and that changes need to be made. It can also be a sign of depression, either through a mental health issue or from being in a state of constant sadness due to a physical illness.

Finances  
The 5 of Cups Tarot card can suggest that financial loss is possible. It can signify a period of financial instability or difficulty. This can relate to a major setback such as losing a job, or an unexpected expense such as a medical bill. The card can indicate that one is not dealing with their finances in an effective way and that changes need to be made.

Relationships  
The 5 of Cups Tarot card can indicate feelings of abandonment and loneliness. It can indicate that one is feeling neglected and unloved. It can suggest a relationship has ended or that there is a lack of communication in the relationship. It can also suggest that one has experienced a great loss, either through the death of a loved one or the end of a romantic relationship. The card can indicate that one should take the time to process their emotions and heal from this experience before entering into new relationships.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

In health, the 7 of Cups Tarot card can mean a lack of commitment to taking care of one’s health. It suggests a person has a lot of options when it comes to health, but does not commit to any of them. They may not make time for regular checkups with a doctor or make an effort to exercise regularly. This can result in an unhealthy lifestyle, with the person making poor health decisions that may have consequences down the line.

In finances, the 7 of Cups Tarot card can suggest a lack of commitment to achieving financial success. It implies a person has a lot of options when it comes to money, but they are not prepared to commit to any of them. They may spend too much on impulse purchases, compromising their long-term financial goals. They may also lack the discipline to save money regularly or make investments that can help them achieve financial security.

In relationships, the 7 of Cups Tarot card can suggest a fear of committing to a relationship. It implies a person is more interested in the idea of a relationship than actually committing to one. They may prefer to keep their options open, exploring different people without fully committing themselves to any of them. This can lead to instability and unhappiness in the long run, as the person is unable to sustain any meaningful relationships.
