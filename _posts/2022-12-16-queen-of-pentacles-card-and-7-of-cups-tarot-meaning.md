## What does Queen Of Pentacles and 7 Of Cups mean together?

When The Queen Of Pentacles and The 7 Of Cups cards are drawn together, they suggest a need to make a decision on how you will use your resources and energy. You may have too many choices and feel overwhelmed, but The Queen of Pentacles encourages the utilization of fertility and domesticity to get things done and make a commitment. You may need to prioritize and focus on what is most important to you, while maintaining your resourcefulness. This pair of cards promotes the importance of making a choice and sticking with it, even if there are numerous potential paths ahead. This can be applied to all areas of life, from career and relationships to more mundane matters such as what to have for dinner. It is important to take the time to carefully weigh your options and make a solid decision that best fits your resources.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 7 of Cups tarot card is a sign that you may be dealing with too many options or ideas in the area of your health. You could be feeling overwhelmed by the number of diets and health plans to choose from, leaving you unable to commit to one, or feeling uncertain about which one is best for you. You may be feeling stuck, not knowing which direction to take, and unable to make a decision that will benefit your overall health. Additionally, this card can be a warning of some health issues, and that you should be paying more attention to your overall health, in order to nip any problems in the bud.

Finances  
The 7 of Cups tarot card can also signify financial uncertainty. You may be feeling overwhelmed by the amount of options in front of you, or by limited resources. You could be facing a financial decision or crossroads, or even feeling stuck in a cycle of debt. This card is a reminder to take a step back, look at all of your options and make a conscious decision on how best to move forward. It can also be a warning to be careful and proactive with your spending and investments, rather than letting them slip away, or feeling yourself getting pulled into a risky situation.

Relationships  
The 7 of Cups tarot card can also be a warning in relationships. It can mean that you are dealing with too many choices or options, or that you can’t seem to commit to one particular person. This can also mean that you may be dealing with commitment issues, or feeling stuck in a relationship that has become too routine and predictable. This card can also be a sign that you should take some time to evaluate your relationships and identify which ones are worth investing more time and energy into. It can also point to the need for more communication and openness in your relationships so that you can create a stronger bond.
