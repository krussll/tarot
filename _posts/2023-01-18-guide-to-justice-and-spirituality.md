## Justice And Sprituality – An Overview

The Justice tarot card is an important reminder to keep our health in balance.

It speaks to the idea of cause and effect, and how our actions have consequences on our physical and mental well-being.

It encourages us to make wise decisions that will bring about win-win solutions for our health.

With this card, we are reminded that even if we are dishonest or try to hide the truth, it will eventually come out in the end.

Thus, we must always be honest with ourselves and those around us to ensure that our health is in the best possible shape.

## Reversed Meanings For Justice And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

When the Justice card appears reversed in a tarot reading it can indicate a lack of balance in one’s life.

It could mean that an individual is not recognizing the true consequences of their actions, or that they are not seeing the balance between cause and effect.

They may be avoiding the responsibilities that come along with making decisions, or be engaging in behavior that could cause an imbalance in their lives.

This can lead to feelings of guilt and regret, as well as a lack of clarity in terms of making decisions.
