## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health:

The Moon Tarot card advises that hidden problems may be lurking beneath the surface in regards to one’s personal health. This could be anything from an undiagnosed health condition, to a lack of knowledge about maintaining a healthy lifestyle. It is important to trust your intuition when it comes to your health, and to be aware of any changes in your body that could be signs of a physical problem. Get regular check-ups, take care of yourself and don’t be afraid to seek medical advice if something doesn’t seem right.

Finances:

The Moon Tarot card also warns of unseen problems that may be affecting a person’s finances. This could be anything from an unscrupulous business partner, to unwise investments or bad financial choices in the past. The card advises to be extra cautious when making financial decisions, and to trust your gut when it comes to money matters. Voluntary changes in how money is handled are also advised, in order to ensure that cash flow is steady and secure.

Relationships:

In terms of relationships, The Moon Tarot card warns of underlying issues that may be affecting the relationship in a negative way. These could be anything from past indiscretions to misunderstandings or wrong assumptions. It is important to trust your intuition when it comes to relationships, and to be willing to make voluntary changes in order to ensure the relationship is successful. Communication is key, and it is important to be willing to work through any issues in a calm and rational manner.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

The 8 of Wands Tarot card indicates speed and quick changes when it comes to health, finances, and relationships. It suggests that something unexpected will soon arrive and will cause life to quickly shift or change direction. This card can indicate that you need to be prepared for sudden news and be quick to act in order to make the most of the opportunity before it passes.

For health, the 8 of Wands indicates that a sudden breakthrough or discovery may be on its way. This could be a new medical procedure or treatment that could revolutionize the way you manage your health. The card signals that you should stay open to new solutions and possibilities, and be ready to act quickly if the opportunity arises.

In regards to finances, the 8 of Wands suggests that you may receive an unexpected windfall or investment. This could range from a big bonus to an unexpected inheritance, or even the sale of an asset that you weren’t expecting to make at this time. In any case, the best way to use this opportunity is to act quickly and make wise decisions so you can get the most benefit from it.

For relationships, the 8 of Wands indicates that a sudden development could occur. This could be in the form of a new relationship, or the renewal of an old one. It could also be sudden reconciliation between two people who have been estranged from one another. It suggests that you should be open to new possibilities and be aware that something unexpected could come your way.
