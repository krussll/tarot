A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean together?

When The Queen of Wands tarot card and The 6 Of Cups tarot card come together for you, it’s a reminder to bring balance to your life. On one hand, the Queen of Wands inspires you to stand up for yourself and to be fiercely confident in whatever you do. On the other hand, The 6 Of Cups encourages you to remember the past and to find comfort in nostalgia and reunions. Together, these two cards suggest that you can combine your inner power and confidence with your fond memories and sentimental experiences to find good, sustainable balance within yourself.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Either way, both parties involved are interested in investing in the relationship, and they are committed to it. They are both driven to make the relationship work, and will bring their own unique gifts and skills to the relationship. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Queen Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Queen Of Wands and 6 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is an indication of vitality, energy and health. It suggests that the person is full of energy, and has a strong sense of self-confidence. This energy can be channeled in a positive way to help them to stay healthy and in shape. There is also a feeling of resilience, and the person is likely to be able to manage stress in a productive and healthy way.

Finances

The Queen of Wands Tarot card also has a positive message when it comes to finances. It indicates that the person has good money management skills, and knows how to use their resources to their advantage. They understand that money is only a tool, and are likely to have a good sense of when to take risks, and when to play it safe.

Relationships

The Queen of Wands Tarot card speaks to a person’s relationships in their life, as well. This card suggests that the person is assertive, confident, and knows what they want and need from their relationships. They have a strong sense of self-worth, and are able to communicate their needs and desires clearly and effectively. In relationships, they may be able to resist manipulation, or understand how to best handle difficult situations that arise. Overall, they bring a sense of stability to their relationships.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 6 of Cups Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of taking time to pause and remember that life is a journey filled with moments of joy and celebration. It suggests that regressing in time can bring much-needed moments of peace and gratitude. When it comes to health, this card encourages us to take a step back and recall the small wins that have kept us going throughout our journey. Remembering the moments where we’ve achieved something, no matter how small, can bring back the motivation to keep going and remain focused on our overall goal.

Finances

The 6 of Cups Tarot card is a sign that we should pause and appreciate the little successes that we enjoy. Recalling times when we’ve achieved financial security can give us the drive to stay focused on our personal goals and dreams. Although money might not always equal happiness, it sure does help us live a more comfortable and secure life. Remembering our past successes and setting small achievable goals can be a great way to stay motivated towards achieving financial stability.

Relationships

The 6 of Cups Tarot card suggests that we should look back on the happy moments shared with our close ones. Taking time to remember the precious memories and all the lessons learned from our relationships can help us appreciate the good times and move on from hurtful ones. This card can be a reminder to us all to go out of our way for those we love and to share gifts of kindness even when we’re feeling down. Relationships should be a top priority, an appreciation of the bonds we’ve created with those we love can help us start fresh and move forward.
