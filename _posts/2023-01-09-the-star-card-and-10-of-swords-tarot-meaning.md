The Star tarot card and The 10 Of Swords tarot card have an interesting combination if read together. They both symbolize an ending, however, The Star tarot card promises hope and renewal after that ending. This could signify that even though there is a painful and complete end, it can be followed by a period of clarity and healing. The 10 Of Swords tarot card is a rock bottom moment, where you must have faith that the light of The Star tarot card will shine on you and bring renewal, hope and a sense of being in the spotlight. Have faith that even the darkest moments can lead to miracles and a greater inner clarity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Star and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card is a powerful card that holds many meanings for health, finances, and relationships. For health, this card can represent a period of renewal and a sense of hope. It can suggest that when we are feeling stuck in a cycle of poor physical or mental health, that we may find the inner strength and clarity to make positive changes to improve our well-being. Miracles can happen and it is important to have faith in the process and trust that the right course of action will be revealed.

For finances, The Star Tarot card can mean that something positive will happen in our life financially. Perhaps an unexpected windfall or a new opportunity that could give us the boost we need. We should stay open to the possibilities and trust that our luck may turn for the better.

For relationships, The Star Tarot card symbolizes a time of being in the spotlight. It could mean that visibility in a relationship will be important and that any obstacles or issues will have a chance to be seen clearly. It could also indicate a feeling of being connected and supported by the Universe at this time; with the potential for new beginnings, healing and growth. It could be a time to focus on making peace and repair any damaged relationships, so that they can be more fulfilling and mutually beneficial.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card is an indication that your health has reached a rock bottom point, a place of complete exhaustion and pain. It is likely that you have experienced serious physical or mental health issues, or have overindulged in unhealthy habits to the point of no return. It may be necessary for you to completely overhaul your self-care practices in order to begin to move away from this depths. This could involve a shift in diet and lifestyle, an overhaul of your existing health care regime, or more radical changes such as detoxing from drugs and alcohol.

Finances:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card means that your finances have reached the point of no return. You have likely dug yourself into a financial hole that seems impossible to get out of. You could be at the brink of bankruptcy or foreclosure, or at the very least you are feeling the sting of serious debt. At this point, it is essential to take stock of your situation and take drastic measures in order to overhaul your financial status. This could include budgeting and cutting back your spending, perhaps enlisting the help of a financial advisor or doing a debt consolidation program.

Relationships:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests that your relationships have reached a painful ending point. It is likely that you have experienced some kind of betrayal or broken trust, or have been faced with a situation that has taken a toll on your emotional health. In order to move beyond this point and make the necessary repairs, it is essential to take the time to reflect on the situation objectively and make changes from within. This could involve getting professional help if needed, such as couples counseling, or having honest conversations with your closest confidantes in order to make sure you are ready to move forward.
