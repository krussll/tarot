However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 3 Of Swords mean together?

When reading The Hermit tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card together, it can bring a message of wisdom and understanding combined with deep emotional pain and challenge. The Hermit suggests that you take a step back and take some time for yourself to reflect and gain clarity on what you’re going through. With the 3 Of Swords, you may be feeling a sense of loss, heartache, and defeat and the Hermit can help to give clarity to allow you to process and heal from these painful experiences. The combination of these two cards can bring a message of confronting and dealing with the hard truths and painful emotions that life may bring and having the courage to explore your inner depths to find a way to the peace and freedom you desire.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 3 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 3 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is an incredibly heavy card relating to heartbreak, loss, and great sadness in life. When this card comes up in a reading, it is important to look at the situation from a big-picture perspective, as this card signifies significant upheaval.

When it comes to health, the 3 of Swords Tarot card can signify a major health challenge or illness. It can also refer to a necessary operation, such as surgery. This card can also indicate a period of emotional and physical stress, so it can be a reminder to take extra care of yourself physically, emotionally and spiritually during such times.

Financially, the 3 of Swords Tarot card can indicate losses and setbacks. This could be the result of a financial crisis, divorce, or another type of major event that disrupts your financial stability. This card can be a reminder to be mindful of your finances and make sure you are taking all the necessary steps to protect your financial future.

Relationships can also be affected when the 3 of Swords comes up. It can indicate a period of heartache and sadness, such as the end of a relationship or divorce. It can also signify a need to reassess relationships, as sometimes drastic events can bring about new perspectives. It can be a reminder to be kind and loving to those around you, even in the most difficult times.

No matter how it appears, the 3 of Swords Tarot card is never an easy one. It can signify a period of intense difficulty, heartache and sadness, but it can also be an opportunity for personal growth and resilience.
