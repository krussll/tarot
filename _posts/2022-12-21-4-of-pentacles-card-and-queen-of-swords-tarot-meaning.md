You must use your sharp intellect, discernment, and inner strength to remain honest and self-sufficient, no matter how hard it may be. Strive to keep a balance between focusing your energy on material desires and maintaining your independence, inner strength and impartiality. Find a way to manage your resources wisely, and use your intellectual power and wisdom to discover a path to stability that is both honest and sustainable.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that, if we cling too tightly to our possessions or to the idea of security, it can prevent us from truly achieving our goals or enjoying our lives.

When it comes to our health, this card can represent an unhealthy craving for perfectionism or an unwillingness to let go of unhealthy habits. If we’re too focused on our physical appearance or aesthetic, we may forget to take care of our bodies in more meaningful ways such as eating right, exercising, and getting enough sleep. Alternatively, this card may also signify a lack of enthusiasm or motivation to take control of our own health and well-being.

The 4 Of Pentacles in regards to finances can be a warning against a tendency to hoard money or to become too obsessed with managing our finances. We may be so focused on saving and security that we become too stingy in our spending and don’t allow ourselves to enjoy the fruits of our labor or share with others.

Finally, when it comes to relationships this card can signify a fear of commitment or an unwillingness to trust in the process. We may be too caught up in striving for long-term security and too afraid to take risks or trust in our partner. This card may also represent a tendency to cling too tightly to the past or to a relationship out of fear of change or heartache. We may be too caught up in the idea of security to the point where it actually begins to wall us off from true love.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes good health through persistence and diligence. She is represented by the suit of swords, which symbolizes the power of the mind. Her focus is on a balanced lifestyle that includes physical activity, proper nutrition, and positive mental attitude. She has the courage to make whatever changes are necessary to maintain physical health and well-being.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card’s interpretation for finances reflects farsightedness and careful consideration. She encourages taking a long-term view rather than making hasty decisions. The Queen of Swords is wise with her finances and always makes sure to understand the agreement before committing. She is a master planner who takes the time to research and understand the consequences of any financial decision before making it.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is the epitome of truthfulness, frankness, and honesty. When it comes to relationships, the Queen of Swords encourages her partner to be open and honest. While she may come off as stern, she is seeking and appreciative of real and sincere connections. She doesn’t have time for superficial interactions and makes sure her relationships are rooted in trust and mutual respect.
