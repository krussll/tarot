## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Take time to realign yourself and your health goals. Focus on creating a healthier lifestyle through physical activity and diet. Give yourself time to rest and recharge. Allow yourself the opportunity to practice self-care and positive self-image.

Finances: The Star Tarot card can signify guidance towards a successful financial path. The starlit night stands for clarity and focus. Take an honest look at your financial situation and strive to make better decisions. Make a plan and follow it. You may have to take risks, but stay focused and endeavor to make sound financial decisions. Once you have a plan, have faith – things will work out.

Relationships: The Star Tarot card speaks to the need for renewal and hope when it comes to relationships. It may signify the need to rekindle a connection or the start of a new one. Trust yourself and make the necessary changes to bring balance to your relationships. Find the courage to express your true feelings and to forgive. ## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Five of Cups tarot card is a symbol of emotional distress and a reflection of inner turmoil. It suggests that your emotional health can be negatively impacted by a variety of difficult experiences, including loss, abandonment and sorrow. These feelings may manifest in physical symptoms, such as fatigue, depression and anxiety. There is an opportunity to use this time to heal and to nurture yourself by taking time to rest and reflect. Pay attention to your emotions and allow yourself to grieve and express those feelings in healthy ways.

Finances:  
The Five of Cups tarot card is a sign of loss and regret in financial matters. It indicates that there is a feeling of despair or a sense of having made a major mistake. This could be in the form of a risky investment or an irresponsible spending decision. The card encourages us to take a step back, look at the situation objectively, and seek advice from a trusted financial professional. Accepting responsibility for any losses and learning from past mistakes can be a powerful way to rebuild your financial health.

Relationships:  
The Five of Cups tarot card can be a sign of grief and pain in relationships. It can indicate feelings of abandonment, betrayal or isolation. It warns us that our relationships may be negatively impacted if we don’t take the time to explore our inner world and consider how our emotions are influencing our interactions with others. Instead of numbing our feelings or avoiding difficult conversations, the card encourages us to explore our emotions, reach out for support, and open up to communication and understanding. Taking the time to heal and rebuild the relationship is essential to the long-term health of any connection.
