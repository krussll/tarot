A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The union of The Sun tarot card and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card suggests that you are about to experience both material happiness and generosity. Through giving and receiving help, success and joyous outcomes are likely to be achieved. It is a reminder to stay positive and to continue to trust in the flow of life. This is the perfect time to show gratitude and kindness to others, and your positive actions will be returned to you in kind.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card is a positive card associated with material happiness and success. It suggests that the seeker will find joy and abundance in all areas of life, including health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Sun Tarot card is a sign of good health and vibrancy. It suggests that any ailments you may have been suffering from will soon begin to fade away, and that an overall feeling of physical well-being will take over. It is also a sign that any treatments you may be undergoing will be effective, yielding positive results.

In terms of finances, The Sun Tarot card means abundant wealth and material success. It promises financial growth and security, and indicates that any investments or financial decisions you make now could prove to be hugely beneficial in the long-term.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Sun Tarot card suggests that all relationships – be it friendships, family or romantic – will be filled with joy, love and connection. It suggests that long-term commitments will be fruitful, and that any disputes or issues you may have encountered in the past can now be resolved amicably.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card can have a positive influence on health, finances, and relationships.

Health

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card suggests that you should reach out to your support system and accept help when needed. Ask for help in areas you need assistance or guidance and don’t be afraid to accept help or advice from those who are offering it. Know that if you are showing generosity and kindness to the people around you, it will be reciprocated.

Finances

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card can indicate that it is time to give back to those who have helped you in the past. Helping others financially or through whatever resources you have can be immensely beneficial to your life and relationships. It can be uplifting to share your wealth and see how it brings joy and abundance to those around you. Practice generosity in whatever form it may come and you will be rewarded with positive results in your finances.

Relationships

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card can signify a balance of giving and receiving. It is important to have a healthy balance of giving and receiving in order to build a strong relationship. This card is a reminder to be generous with your emotions and give your full attention to your loved ones. It is also a reminder to ask for help and show vulnerability when needed. In relationships, it is important to show respect and appreciation for each other and practice random acts of kindness to strengthen the connection.
