A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 8 Of Wands card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 8 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 8 Of Wands mean together?

The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The 8 Of Wands tarot card working together signify a time of great progress. This is a time when teamwork is essential and new business opportunities and collaborations should be explored. With the help of a team, you can make greater strides in achieving your goals. Expansion is possible, whether that is travel, international connections, or business success, and the arrival of news will mean quick, decisive action must be taken. This is an exciting time and a great opportunity for growth and progress.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This suggests that a new venture, involving a partnership with your partner, is likely to materialize. The combination of these two cards implies that the venture has the potential to bring about a great deal of fulfillment, both for you and for your partner.

This could be a shared project that you both take on together such as starting a business or perhaps even embarking on a travel adventure. Alternatively, it may signify a long distance relationship, with both of you venturing away from home to explore new cities and cultures. Regardless of what is in store, the promise of new and exciting experiences stands as an exciting prospect.

The 8 of Wands brings dynamic energy to the mix and suggests that events are likely to happen quickly and without warning. There is an air of excitement and anticipation around this card and it could mean that unexpected news is on the horizon that will propel you and your partner forward.

So, when the 3 of Wands and the 8 of Wands join forces in your love life, it suggests that a time of growth, expansion, and exciting change is coming your way. Be ready to take the plunge and make the most of this opportunity as it could be the start of something very special.

When The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The 8 Of Wands tarot card are drawn together, it is an indication that there is extremely positive news and change ahead in your finances and job. Taken together, The 3 Of Wands and The 8 Of Wands tarot cards signify a period of transition, growth and advancement in your finances and job. With the help of others and by taking clever and timely action, you will be well placed to make the most of the changes to come.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 3 Of Wands and 8 Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 3 Of Wands and 8 Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 3 of Wands tarot card is all about expanding your horizons and taking your health to the next level. This card is a reminder to invest further into your body and mind, as well as to look for opportunities for growth. It can point to trying out a new exercise routine or a new diet, travelling to a new place for a health retreat, and seeking out alternative healing methods. It also encourages you to look outside of your comfort zone and try something new, as this can often lead to health and well-being.

Finances:

The 3 of Wands is a strong reminder to take your finances to the next level. It suggests that it’s time to up the ante and start investing in yourself and your future. This card encourages you to expand your understanding of money and to look further afield for commercial opportunities. It could indicate taking a risk or investing with a business partner, as well as looking into different ways to make money. Remember that with this card, it’s important to be mindful and to work with someone you trust.

Relationships:

The 3 of Wands tarot card is a message of growth and expansion in relationships. It encourages you to look for opportunities for personal growth within your relationships, such as opening yourself up to different cultures and stepping out of your comfort zone. It can also represent a journey in a relationship, where it’s important to remain open to the possibilities of what lies ahead. This card encourages you to explore new ways to connect and deepen your relationship, as well as to never give up on a partnership even when the going gets tough.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health:  
When it comes to health, The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take action and move quickly. This card signifies that now is the time to take control of your health and make changes with the expectation of quick results. This could mean starting (or restarting) an exercise routine, implementing a healthier diet, or finding more balance and relaxation. The 8 of Wands also signifies that news may arrive, whether that’s a positive or negative report from your doctor or an encouraging announcement from a health care provider.

Finances:  
The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of impending change in financial matters. It implies that now is the time to take a step forward and take actions that will help improve your financial situation. This could mean starting a budget or saving plan, negotiating for a raise or finding ways to increase your income. The 8 of Wands can also signify a positive financial opportunity coming your way, or an unexpected windfall soon arriving.

Relationships:  
When it comes to relationships, The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign that meaningful progress can be made quickly. It signifies that action is required on your part to improve and repair relationships where needed. This could mean expressing yourself honestly and openly, making amends, or being more proactive in reaching out to your loved ones. Reaching out to those who are distant or have been estranged can bring unexpected speed and healing. The 8 of Wands also symbolizes a fast-moving and exciting new connection coming your way, so be open to new opportunities.
