A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Knight Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and Knight Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Knight Of Cups mean together?

When The Justice tarot card and The Knight Of Cups tarot card appear together in your reading, they are likely to offer positive news. It suggests that two parties are going to come together, and that justice is going to prevail. This could be a business deal, a romantic relationship, or even a legal situation. Whatever the case, the combination of the Justice card and the Knight of Cups suggests that a win-win solution is in the works, where both parties will benefit from the outcome. The truth will come out and the situation will be resolved in a fair and balanced way. This card pairing is also associated with romantic proposals and love, suggesting that a beautiful new relationship is on the way.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Knight Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card is a powerful symbol of fairness, balance and the law. When it appears in a reading, it typically suggests that we’ve reached an important juncture in our lives, and it’s time to weigh our options carefully in order to make the best decisions. The card can be interpreted in various ways, but in all cases, it emphasizes the need for a healthy and balanced approach.

For health, The Justice Tarot card implies that we should strive to maintain a healthy balance between our physical and mental states. We should mindfully practice self-care, and strive to maintain healthy habits in order to support our wellbeing. This card can also be a reminder to stay informed about our own health and to seek guidance when we need it.

For finances, The Justice Tarot card implies that we should carefully consider our financial options. We should strive to make choices that are both ethical and smart, while also looking out for our own best interests. This card can also be a reminder to stay informed about our finances and to seek professional advice if we feel we need it.

For relationships, The Justice Tarot card suggests that we should take a mature and conscious approach to our relationships. We should strive to foster understanding, respect and equality within our relationships, and ensure that we are not taking advantage of anyone. This card can also be a reminder to stay informed about any relational issues we may have and to seek guidance if things become complicated.

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

Health: The Knight Of Cups Tarot card can signify a period of well-being and good health. It can also suggest that you’re likely to experience physical healing in some way, either through treatments, therapies, or changing your lifestyle. Physically, this can mean anything from improving your diet to finding a new exercise routine. It can also indicate spiritual health and finding balance and harmony in life.

Finances: The Knight Of Cups Tarot card is a great omen when it comes to finances. It suggests that good luck, unexpected income, and long-term investment opportunities are coming your way. It may be a sign that a period of financial success and stability is coming up, so be sure to make the most of the opportunities that come to you.

Relationships: The Knight Of Cups Tarot card suggests a period of courtship and romance. If you’re single, then this card can show that new beginnings in love life are on the cards, while if you’re already in a relationship then it can signify deepening connections and a growing attraction between you and your partner. In platonic relationships, The Knight Of Cups can mean new friendship opportunities, something which could bring great joy and happiness into your life.
