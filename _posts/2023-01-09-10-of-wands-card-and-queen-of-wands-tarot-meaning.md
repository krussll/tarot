## What does 10 Of Wands and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The 10 Of Wands tarot card and The Queen Of Wands tarot card, when read together, tell a story of a person in need of assistance. On the one hand they are feeling the stress, exhaustion and burden of their responsibilities, but on the other they remain resilient and determined, drawing upon the strength of their inner confidence and self-assurance. The combination of these two means that the person needs to find a way to balance the stress of the situation with their own inner power to find a way out of their current predicament. With the help of the Queen Of Wands, they are reminded that they have the potential to carry the weight of their situation and come out the other side.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands Tarot card represents a heavy burden that can have a negative effect on someone’s health, finances and relationships.

Physically, the 10 of Wands can represent stress, exhaustion, and being overworked. When this card shows up in a Tarot reading, it can be suggesting that there’s too much on your plate and you should take a break and look at what needs to be done and prioritize. If you don’t, it’s likely that your physical and mental health could suffer.

Financially, the 10 of Wands suggests that too many of your resources are being used up and you’re feeling cash-strapped. You may be feeling overwhelmed with financial obligations and struggling to make ends meet. This can lead to a feeling of anxiety and can cause you to make rash decisions that could have negative financial consequences.

Relationships can also be affected by the 10 of Wands. If you’re under a lot of pressure, it can be easy to take it out on the people around you and you may find yourself feeling short-tempered and irritable. This can lead to arguments and a breakdown of relationships. In order to avoid this, it’s important to take time to take care of yourself and manage your stress levels.
