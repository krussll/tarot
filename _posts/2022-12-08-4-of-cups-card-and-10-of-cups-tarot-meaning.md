A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Cups card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 4 Of Cups card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

You may feel unfulfilled, unmotivated and apathetic, but something is about to shift and help you appreciate what you have and break out of this stagnant state. It could be a surprise gift, a new relationship, or simply the realization that you have been surrounded by love and support all along, provided by your family. Take the time to appreciate it and be open to the joy that contentment and security can bring.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Cups and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a lack of motivation or indifference when it comes to health. This could manifest in various ways, such as avoiding the doctor or not taking care of oneself physically or mentally. It could mean feeling like there is nothing more to be done to improve one’s health, or that nothing is worth the effort. It could lead to feelings of inertia, depression and fatigue.

Finances  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a feeling of apathy when it comes to finances. This could manifest in various ways, such as not wanting to manage money, not paying attention to budgeting or spending, and simply not caring about the future. It can mean feeling like there is nothing more to be done to improve one’s financial situation, or that nothing is worth the effort. It can lead to feelings of hopelessness and financial instability.

Relationships  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a lack of emotional connection when it comes to relationships. This could manifest in various ways, such as not wanting to invest in relationships, not paying attention to the needs of other people and simply not caring. It can mean feeling like there is nothing more to be done to improve one’s relationships, or that nothing is worth the effort. It can lead to feelings of indifference and disconnection from the people around them. It can also mean the possibility of something unexpected entering one’s life, such as an old flame coming back or an unexpected gift.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

When it comes to health, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card is associated with overall well-being and contentment. This card can suggest that all is well in one’s life and that the physical health of yourself and those around you is balanced. It can indicate an overall sense of satisfaction, often with one’s diet, exercise and lifestyle. This card can also point to a homeostasis and feeling of safety and harmony amongst the people in your life.

On a financial level, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card can reflect feelings of security and abundance. It could suggest that you are surrounded by people who are supportive of your financial endeavors. This card can signify stability, as well as a steady flow of incoming money and a secure financial future. It can also denote generosity towards yourself and others, which can bring a sense of comfort in the home and amongst loved ones.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card can point to a deep connection with friends, family and partners. This card embodies the power of love, and can suggest moments of happiness and joy with the people that you choose to bring into your life. It can also indicate an effortless harmony between yourself and those around you, as well as an appreciation for one another. This card reminds us to cherish our relationships, as these are the root of our contentment and satisfaction in life.
