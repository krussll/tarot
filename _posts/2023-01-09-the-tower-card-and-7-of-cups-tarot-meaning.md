A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The Tower tarot card and 7 Of Cups tarot card together suggests an unwelcome yet possibly necessary shift in your life. The Tower signifies a period of upheaval and unexpected change, while the 7 Of Cups suggests a difficulty in making decisions or committing to a specific path. It may be that the changes you are facing now are forces beyond your control, and the 7 Of Cups brings with it a fear of choosing the wrong path. It is important to remember that whatever the outcome of your decision may be, it will bring with it some form of growth.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

When The Tower tarot card and The 7 Of Cups tarot card show up in your love life, it can signify an unusual and unexpected turn of events. This can mean having to make an abrupt and unwelcome change in your relationship. It could manifest itself in the form of an upheaval, betrayal, or other cruel circumstance. In terms of love, The Tower symbolizes unwanted trials that could potentially take away the harmony of your relationship and leave you feeling shocked and disoriented.

On the other hand, The 7 Of Cups also indicates a time of difficult choices and a possible inability to commit. It reflects a period of doubt and lack of direction. In the context of your love life, this card could signify a struggle to decide on a course of action that is right for your relationship. You may be having difficulty committing to someone, or you may be entertaining multiple options and struggling to make a decision.

When combined, these two cards signify an unwanted change paired with an inability to commit. Depending on the other cards in the reading, these two cards can indicate an ending or a disruption to a relationship that forces you to reassess your choices. It’s a sign to be mindful of the impact that decisions can have, and to keep in mind that big changes can lead to unexpected outcomes.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card symbolizes an unwanted and destructive change, one that can have a profoundly negative influence on all aspects of life, such as health, finances, and relationships. It serves as a reminder that the world is constantly changing, and that sometimes the most fundamental elements of life can be altered in an instant.

In terms of health, The Tower Tarot card warns that sudden and unforeseen health problems could arise, which could include sudden illnesses, injuries, or traumas. These health issues could be brought on by lifestyle changes, as well as environmental and genetic changes. The card also implies that habits and routines must be adjusted in order to maintain good health and to endure the disruptions and upheavals that come with the card.

Financially, The Tower Tarot card indicates the potential for financial ruin and upheaval. It is a warning to practice financial responsibility, as the card suggests that unwise decisions, bad investments, and impulsive behavior could lead to sudden financial distress. It may also be a sign that financial authorities are about to impose new or stricter regulations that could cause a sudden shift in the financial landscape.

Finally, The Tower Tarot card has implications for relationships as well. It implies that the sudden changes brought on by The Tower Tarot card could have a strain on relationships, as it warns of sudden upheavals, disagreements, and quarrels. It serves as a reminder to be respectful and mindful of the feelings of others, as the card suggests that deep-seated issues and feelings could be brought to light.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

The Seven of Cups Tarot card is a complex card that can have various meanings depending on the context of the reading. In general, this card often carries a message of not having a clear sense of direction and focus, or feeling overwhelmed by too many options. This can apply to all areas of life, including health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Seven of Cups Tarot card can indicate that a person may be feeling overwhelmed by all of the paths they could take to achieve their health goals. This can mean considering too many different diets and exercise regimens, or unclear which treatments they should pursue and which they should avoid. The key here is to focus on the end result, and to choose the path that will get them there the fastest while still allowing room to make adjustments along the way.

For finances, The Seven of Cups Tarot card can indicate that a person is feeling stuck and overwhelmed by all of their options. This could include investing too heavily in one particular venture, without considering all of the other possibilities for their money. The message of this card is to take a step back and look at the whole picture before making any important decisions. It’s important to be sure all options are being explored and evaluated, and that the decision is based on evidence rather than emotions.

In terms of relationships, The Seven of Cups Tarot card can suggest that a person is struggling with commitment issues. They may be considering too many options and not sure which path is the right one. The key here is to focus on what truly matters and to be honest with themselves and their partners about their intentions.

The Seven of Cups Tarot card is a reminder that too many choices can be just as paralyzing as too few. It is important to take the time to make sure all options are considered, and that decisions are made based on what is best for the long-term.
