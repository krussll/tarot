## Can you mix tarot card decks?

**Yes, there is no reason why you can’t mix cards from different tarot decks, so long as the subject of the reading is comfortable with the setup. This often leads to more insightful and meaningful readings.**

The key is to make sure the cards are the same size, as this makes it much easier to shuffle and draw the cards in the deck.

## What Is a “Standard” Tarot Card Deck?

A standard tarot card deck is a set of 78 cards that are used for divination. They are usually hand-painted with images of people, animals, and objects that reflect different aspects of our lives. Tarot cards originated in Italy during the 15th century, when card games became popular pastimes for the elite.

At the time, the Catholic Church was very powerful in Europe, and many priests condemned card games as sinful and ungodly. As a result, many people began using playing cards as a form of divination.

Because playing cards were common, accessible, and inexpensive, they soon became the most popular way to interpret the future. Over time, playing cards became known as “Tarot cards” due to the large number of Italian words that begin with “Tar.”

Tarot cards are usually 78 cards consisting of Major Arcana, Minor Arcana, and Court cards. The Major Arcana are 22 important cards that contain imagery and themes that are common throughout many decks.

The Minor Arcana are 56 cards that represent things happening in each person’s daily life. And the Court cards show people in different roles, like the prince and princess.

## 3 Different Ways to Mix Tarot Card Decks Together

There are 3 different ways you can mix Tarot card decks together:

- Mixing two different decks at the same time

Mixing two different decks at the same time is like cooking with two different sets of spices: you can create something great by using both at once!
