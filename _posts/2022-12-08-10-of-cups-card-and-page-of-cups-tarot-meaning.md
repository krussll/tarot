## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 10 of Cups Tarot card has a strong connection to wellbeing, both physical and mental. It implies that one is content in their current state of health, and any minor issues will be resolved quickly and with minimal stress. The card speaks of harmony within the body, free from illness and chronic fatigue, and a general feeling of peace and wellbeing.

Finances  
The 10 of Cups indicates a time of balance and abundance when it comes to money. This card encourages one to be conscious with their spending and financially responsible, while also having enough to meet their needs without worry. It is a sign to save while also splurging on occasions, and to be generous with those less fortunate.

Relationships  
This card is a strong indication of fulfillment and harmony in relationships. It implies that family or friends are a source of contentment and that relationships are based on real love and support. It’s a sign that relationships are built on a strong foundation of mutual trust and respect. It encourages one to maintain open and honest communication with their loved ones and to spend quality time together.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page Of Cups Tarot card is an indicator of good health. It suggests that overall you are in good physical shape, but to maintain this you should focus on balancing emotional and spiritual health with physical health. In terms of specific health issues, the Page Of Cups Tarot card could suggest a focus on preventative health, such as regular check-ups and paying attention to the warning signs of illness. It could also be a reminder to get outside and enjoy nature, as this can help to balance one’s energy.

Finances

The Page Of Cups Tarot card is an indication that you are on a good path financially. It suggests that you will be successful in managing your finances and that there is potential for growth and abundance. However, it also serves as a reminder to keep your spending in check and to stay mindful of where your money is going. It could be beneficial to get an additional job or look for ways to invest your money in order to build wealth.

Relationships

The Page Of Cups Tarot card is an indication of fresh and new relationships. The card suggests that you have the potential to form meaningful, long-lasting relationships. It is a reminder to stay gentle and open-minded with any potential partners, as this will help foster a connection. This card may also be a reminder to watch out for codependency and to maintain healthy boundaries. It is also a reminder to be willing to forgive and to practice self-love.
