However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does The Devil and 3 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Devil tarot card and the 3 of Pentacles tarot card have an interesting combination of meanings. These two cards can represent a conflict between indulgence and restraint, between desire and discipline. The Devil card speaks to the temptations of addiction, greed, envy, materialism and obsession, while the 3 of Pentacles suggests collaboration, teamwork, and skill. It may be that you are struggling between giving into your desires and indulgent urges, and the necessity of controlling them in order to achieve success. It could also reflect a need for balance between the two extremes, with a focus on quality and craftsmanship, as well as personal responsibility. The two cards together suggest that you need to be mindful of your behavior in order to succeed.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card typically indicates a person’s recent struggles with addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust. These can manifest in an array of ways in terms of health, finances, and relationships.

When it comes to health, the Devil Tarot card can point to issues such as alcohol and drug abuse, gambling addiction, compulsive eating, and other addictive behaviors. These addictions can cause physical and mental health problems and create an unbalanced lifestyle. Additionally, those who are too focused on the pursuit of materialism and wealth can often forget to take proper care of their physical health, leading to long-term health problems.

In terms of finances, the Devil Tarot card can indicate a person’s unhealthy fixation on money and material gain. This can lead to unwise financial decisions, as well as greed and envy, as people compare themselves to those who have more than they do. Additionally, reckless spending can often be a consequence of these behaviors, leading to long-term debt or financial ruin.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the Devil Tarot card can indicate an individual’s unhealthy obsession with materialistic desires, which can damage relationships. This can lead to feelings of jealousy and resentment, as well as mistrust and sabotage of relationships. Additionally, those who are too focused on material gain can often forget to put enough effort into their relationships, leading to a lack of emotional connections and intimacy.

Overall, The Devil Tarot card can be a warning sign of potential problems when it comes to health, finances and relationships. The individual should take the time to examine their behavior and focus on developing healthier habits and attitudes.
