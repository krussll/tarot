A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 7 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 7 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Judgment Tarot card and the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card together indicate a time of making profound decisions that will lead to clarity and purpose in your life. This could be a time of hard work and patience while you wait for a final decision to be made. The 7 of Pentacles may be warning you that success won’t come easy or overnight, so it is important to remain patient and understand that there will be a delay in the results of your hard work. The Judgment card is a wake-up call, telling you to be mindful of your actions and intents, and to keep striving for your life’s purpose, even when the outcome may seem uncertain.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and 7 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card represents a call to action when it comes to your health. It encourages you to take a step back and assess your current situation, and then make a final decision on how you want to move forward. It is a reminder that you should be in tune with your body, understanding what your limits are and making conscious decisions about your health. Acknowledging your physical and emotional needs and taking the necessary steps to care for yourself is crucial in order to live a balanced and healthy life.

Finances:  
The Judgment Tarot card can mean a final decision on how you want to manage your finances. It is an invitation to take a long hard look at your current situation and ask yourself, “Is this the best way I can be handling my money?” This card encourages you to make smart and informed decisions about your finances; it is a call to action to plan for the future, creating goals and organizing strategies to help you reach them. This card is a reminder that you are responsible for taking control of your finances, and any decisions you make should be based on reality rather than fantasy.

Relationships:  
The Judgment Tarot card can be a wake-up call when it comes to relationships. It encourages you to take a moment of introspection and ask yourself, “What do I need in order to feel fulfilled in this relationship?” This card brings clarity to any confusing relationship dynamics and helps you to make decisions based on what is best for both parties. It is also a reminder that relationships are based on communication, understanding and compromise, and that you should be open and honest with your partner in order to build a strong and lasting bond.

Ultimately, when The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card presents itself, it is a reminder of the need for patience, hard work, and delayed success. The card encourages us to be mindful of our health, finances and relationships, and to be persistent and resilient in working towards our goals. With patience and dedication, success is sure to come.
