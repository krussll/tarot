## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups Tarot card signifies friendship, celebration, and a supportive circle. When this card appears in a reading, it can indicate that it’s time to come together with those that you trust and that have been there for you in the past. It may mean that it’s time to celebrate your successes, no matter how small they may be.

In terms of health, this card suggests that it’s important to appreciate the supportive people that are willing to be there for us in times of physical or mental illness. It may mean that it’s time to build more meaningful connections, seek support from friends, and share our stories with those that we trust.

In terms of finances, this card suggests that it may be a time to count our blessings and recognize our successes. It could be a good time to take stock of our financial situation and look to others for assistance if needed. It may also be time to be more mindful of our spending and look for ways to cut costs.

In terms of relationships, this card suggests that it may be time to look for connections that are mutually supportive, and that bring joy to both parties. It could also mean that it’s time to celebrate the relationships in your life, no matter their size, and that it’s important to appreciate the long-term friends, family, and partners that have been there for you, no matter the circumstances.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

The 10 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes abundance, contentment and happiness. When this card appears in a Tarot spread, it is a sign that all areas of life are blessed with joy and bliss. If a person is struggling with their health, finances and relationships, the 10 of Cups can be a message from the Universe that, with some effort and dedication, everything will eventually fall into place.

When it comes to health, the 10 of Cups signifies that even in times of stress, anxiety and illness, a person’s physical and mental well-being can be strengthened and improved. Through gentle and mindful self-care, a person can begin to feel the benefits of spiritual, emotional and physical healing. This card can also be a prompt to speak to a professional or join a support group if help is needed.

For finances, the 10 of Cups encourages a person to stay positive and remain open to new opportunities. It can signify a time of abundance, when income flows through in a steady stream. It can also be a sign to take extra care when it comes to money matters and to make sure it is managed responsibly.

In terms of relationships, the 10 of Cups represents a deep and lasting connection that is built on trust, loyalty and mutual admiration. It is an indicator that any problems between those involved are being resolved and a strong bond is being restored. It is also a reminder to value the people in our lives and to remember to let them know how much we care.
