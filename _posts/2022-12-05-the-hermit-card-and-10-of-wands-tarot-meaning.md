A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 10 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 10 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 10 Of Wands mean together?

The Hermit and The 10 of Wands tarot cards together invoke a feeling of being weighed down by too many burdens. The Hermit card encourages you to go within and seek personal truth and spiritual illumination, while The 10 of Wands suggests that this process can be hindered by the stress of carrying too many burdens. It is important to release yourself from these burdens and gain clarity through inner exploration. This will help you to find a greater understanding of your life and the path ahead.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 10 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 10 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health  
The Hermit Tarot card in relation to health can be interpreted as a need for us to look inward, to explore our innermost thoughts and feelings, and to gain clarity of understanding about what is best for our overall health. It suggests that we should take some solitary time to reflect on how we can make positive changes in our life and how we can take care of our physical, mental and emotional wellbeing. This could involve examining our diet and exercise habits, engaging in activities that bring peace and joy, and understanding what physical, mental and emotional needs must be met to maintain our health.

Finances  
The Hermit Tarot card in relation to finances is an encouragement to look inward and gain insight into our financial situation. It suggests that we must be honest and open with ourselves while evaluating our current financial position, before taking advice from anyone else. It encourages us to pay attention to our intuition and to learn from the mistakes we’ve made in the past, so that we can make better decisions for our future. This could mean gaining an understanding of how to budget, how to save, how to invest and how to effectively manage our money and resources.

Relationships  
The Hermit Tarot card in relation to relationships suggests that we should take some time away and look inward to gain insight into what kind of relationships we want and need in our lives. This can help us to identify the patterns of behavior that are either helping or hindering our interpersonal relationships. It encourages us to examine our communication and trust issues, the values and needs that we must have met, and the expectations that we must set in order to foster healthy and meaningful relationships. This type of inner reflection can then help us to make meaningful connections and build relationships with others that are mutual and supportive.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a very telling card when it comes to health, finances and relationships. On the surface, it is a card that indicates a situation in which the person has too much on their plate. Too many burdens, stress, fatigue and exhaustion. It can be viewed as a sign that a person is carrying the weight of the world on their shoulders and is struggling to move forward.

When it comes to health, the 10 of Wands Tarot card points to being weighed down by physical and mental exhaustion. It can be a warning of burnout and the need for rest, relaxation and self-care. It can be a sign to take a break from obligations, to take some time off from work and to reduce stress. For those dealing with chronic health issues, this card urges you to take on less and look after your body.

When it comes to finances, the 10 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of being overburdened. It can mean that a person is taking on too many financial obligations or is trying to do too much. It can be a warning to take stock of the situation and make necessary changes to reduce the burden. It can mean cutting back on expenses, creating a budget and taking on a new job or side hustle.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 10 of Wands Tarot card can indicate a feeling of being overwhelmed with responsibility. It can mean taking on too much, having too many commitments and not having enough time for loved ones. It urges you to take stock of your relationships and prioritize quality time with your partner, family and friends. It can also be an indication to take a break from commitments and spend time on yourself and your hobbies.
