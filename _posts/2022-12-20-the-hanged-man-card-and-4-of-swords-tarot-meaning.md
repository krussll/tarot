However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Hanged Man card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The Hanged Man and The 4 Of Swords tarot card combination indicates a period of deep reflection and contemplation. It is time to take a step back and gain a new perspective on matters before you. By trusting your inner wisdom, taking some time away, and embracing a period of rest and renewal, you can gain greater understanding and insight. This is a period of self-sacrifice, in which it is important to take some time for yourself and regroup before continuing on your journey.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

For Health

The Hanged Man Tarot card is a reminder that health is an important part of life, and taking the time to look after ourselves is essential. It encourages us to take a step back and assess our current health situation to ensure that we are taking the necessary steps to maintain our well-being. We should look at our lifestyle to assess if our behaviour is helping or hindering our health, and make any changes if necessary. It also suggests that we think about our mental health, including how we think and process emotions. Being aware of how physical and mental health work together helps us to stay in balance and maintain overall health and wellness.

For Finances

The Hanged Man Tarot card asks us to take a step back from our finances and to reassess how they are currently being managed. It encourages us to focus on budgeting, prioritising our financial goals, and looking at ways to save money. It may also suggest that we look at alternative ways to earn money, such as freelancing or developing our personal side hustle. The Hanged Man suggests that we should be open to different perspectives and to think outside the box when it comes to managing our finances.

For Relationships

The Hanged Man Tarot card reminds us to take a step back in our relationships and to reassess how they are currently being managed. It encourages us to look at our behaviour and the way we communicate with our partners, and to make any changes if necessary. It also suggests that we take the time to get to know each other better and to communicate our feelings openly. We should be open to different perspectives, values, and points of view, and look for ways to improve communication and strengthen our relationships.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card appears when we need to take a break and withdraw from the demands of our daily lives in order to restore our physical and mental wellbeing. It is a sign that we need to take some time to rest and relax, to come back to a place where we can find our balance and health. This card is a reminder to pay attention to our physical and emotional needs and treat ourselves with kindness, compassion, and care. Taking regular breaks from work and other obligations helps recharge our batteries and can improve our overall health and wellbeing.

Finances  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card relates to finances when we need to withdraw from trying to push our financial situation forward. It is a reminder that it may be necessary to pause, take a step back, and review the circumstances. To use this card as a practical tool, one could start to create a budget in order to identify where money is being spent and assess what areas can be trimmed or eliminated to reduce expenses. This could help create more financial stability and time to make decisions on how best to move forward.

Relationships  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card signifies a time of retreat in relationships. It can be a warning that we need to step back from a relationship in order to maintain our own sense of balance. This can be especially true when the relationship has become imbalanced or demanding. Taking a break from a relationship for a period of time can be beneficial for both parties, allowing a chance for reflection and a fresh perspective. It is important to remember that relationships are built on trust and understanding, and taking a break from a relationship does not necessarily mean it has failed. If a relationship is worth investing in, both parties should be willing to take a step back, rest, and then re-evaluate the situation when the time is right.
