The Temperance tarot card and The Devil tarot card represent a unique combination that speaks of the need for careful balance in our lives. It is a reminder that we must not allow ourselves to become too attached to any one thing. The Temperance card encourages us to practice patience, moderation, and self-control in all areas of our lives. The Devil tarot card, on the other hand, represents the darker side of temptation – the dangers of addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust. Taken together, these two cards present us with a powerful reminder that our lives can easily become unbalanced if we allow ourselves to succumb to these temptations. Striking the perfect balance between excess and restraint is key to a fulfilling and balanced life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to practice balance, moderation and self-care when it comes to our health. It reminds us to be mindful of our choices and to move towards a healthier lifestyle. We should strive to find the right combination of diet, exercise and rest that will nourish our physical and mental wellbeing. Through temperance and self-control, we can gain optimal health.

Finances  
The Temperance Tarot card teaches us to be prudent with our money. We should practice moderation and resist succumbing to temptations or reckless spending. With careful consideration, we can strike a balance between saving and spending that will give us the financial freedom and stability to create long term wealth.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card imparts wisdom that we should strive for balance in our relationships. It encourages us to practice self-control and to be mindful of our words, actions, and emotions. It also reminds us to show patience and understanding towards those close to us, and to take the time to define our boundaries and agree on expectations. Through understanding, acceptance and mutual respect, we can cultivate strong and stable relationships.

## What does The Devil mean?

Health: The Devil Tarot card is associated with addictive behavior, poor decision making and overall health problems. For example, the card can indicate an unhealthy relationship with food that can lead to obesity, or an obsession with drugs or alcohol that can destroy one’s health. In some cases, the card can warn of an underlying physical problem that needs to be addressed. In all cases, the card points to destructive, self-destructive behavior.

Finances: When The Devil Tarot card is pulled, it could be a sign of financial troubles. This could be taking on too much debt, making poor investments or living beyond one’s means. Financial problems can cause a lot of stress and anxiety and can have a negative impact on a person’s relationships.

Relationships: When The Devil Tarot card is drawn, relationships can suffer. This can be anything from a lack of trust and inability to communicate, to destructive behaviors like gambling, spending or drinking. It could also mean envy and materialism that put a strain on relationships. The card can be a warning sign of a toxic relationship or even a way to identify underlying issues that need to be addressed.

Overall, The Devil Tarot card is associated with negative behavior or thinking, and it is important to be aware of the potential consequences of such behavior. If you are having difficulty in any of these areas, it may be time to reach out for help. Professional counseling can help you work through any issues you may be facing and help you make positive changes in your life.
