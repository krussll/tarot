However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Lovers card and 7 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The 7 Of Wands tarot card together represent a powerful partnership – one that has been built on love, choice, and strength. While the Lovers encourages you to embrace the connection and trust in the power of two, the 7 Of Wands reminds you to stay protected against anything that could come your way. The combination of these two cards brings harmony and security, allowing you to feel safe and supported. It is an invitation to trust in the strength of forging strong relationships, and to embrace the beauty of being in love.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

When it comes to your health, The Lovers Tarot card signifies a balanced attitude and lifestyle. It suggests that you are not going it alone as it relates to your physical, mental, and emotional well-being. This card encourages you to seek out a partner (whether this be a friend, family member, or professional therapist) to help you stay on track and prioritize your health. This card also implies working together towards wellness, such as exercising together, forming a healthier diet plan, or creating a supportive and nurturing environment at home.

When it comes to finances, The Lovers Tarot card suggests unity between you and a partner, whether it is a spouse, friend, or family member. It suggests that you might want to consider seeking out a financial advisor to help you both come to an agreement about your finances. This card also encourages you both to set financial goals together, to create a savings plan, and to work together to achieve financial security and stability.

When it comes to relationships, The Lovers Tarot card suggests that you may be in a period of choice. This card encourages you to take some time to evaluate the relationships in your life before making a commitment. It urges you to look at how each relationship enhances your life and encourages you to be honest about what you need from your partners. This card also signifies being open to the possibility of love and feeling safe and secure in relationships, knowing that your partner has your best interests at heart.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to practice self-care and nurture your health. It encourages you to take a step back and take a good look at your current physical, mental, and emotional health. It advises you to defend yourself from physical and mental attacks, both real and imagined. Taking time to nurture and protect yourself, and to stay mindful of your health, is vital.

Finances: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is an indication of competition and the need to be defensive with your finances. This may mean being more aware of how and where you spend your money. It could also mean standing up for yourself, especially when it comes to business deals or negotiations. Being aware of the competition and protecting yourself from their tactics is essential.

Relationships: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to protect your relationships and guard against competition. It encourages you to take a step back and evaluate what is healthy for you and your relationship. It also advises you to stand up for yourself and be assertive when necessary. Be aware of how you are being treated in the relationship and remember that your needs are just as important. Being willing to stand up for yourself and communicate your needs will help protect the relationship from outside competition.
