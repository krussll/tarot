A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 4 Of Cups tarot card together indicate a time of change and transformation. The Justice card indicates that cause and effect will be at work, and that win-win solutions are needed in order to create lasting balance. The 4 Of Cups card indicates that you may be feeling unfulfilled or apathetic about current circumstances, but that you should be open to unexpected surprises. This card combination encourages you to take action to find the balance you crave, and to look for the gifts that could be awaiting you. Be prepared for the truth to come out, and for the balance of Justice to be restored.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health:

The Justice Tarot card signals the need for balance and moderation when it comes to health. It’s a reminder to think carefully before taking any drastic measures and to weigh the pros and cons of any course of action. It encourages us to find a path that works for our individual needs and be aware of the consequences of our choices. The Justice Tarot card indicates that our health is an investment and requires thought, dedication and planning. It is a reminder to seek professional advice where needed and to make an effort to pursue physical and mental well-being.

Finances:

The Justice Tarot card suggests that the key to a successful financial life is careful consideration and thoughtful decisions. It encourages us to create a plan that works for our individual circumstances, understanding the risks and rewards of different options. It reminds us to be practical and not to be swayed away by emotion. With the Justice Tarot card, we are encouraged to practice financial literacy, budgeting, and responsible investing.

Relationships:

The Justice Tarot card advises us to be honest and fair in our relationships. It indicates that the key to successful relationships is mutual respect and understanding. We are encouraged to communicate openly and clearly, ensuring that everyone’s needs and rights are respected. We must be aware of the effects of our decisions and be willing to make compromises and listen to the needs of others. The Justice Tarot card advises that we should strive to find solutions that are beneficial for both parties in any conflict and be mindful of the consequences of our actions.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

The 4 Of Cups Tarot card can signify stagnation and feelings of being unfulfilled in all areas of life. When this card appears in a reading, it may point to feeling trapped and not having the means to move forward. In terms of health, The 4 Of Cups Tarot card may be a warning to slow down and examine lifestyle habits that may be preventing progress. This card is a sign that it is time to seek professional help if needed and maybe take a break from chaotic activities to nurture self-care rituals.

When it comes to finances, The 4 Of Cups Tarot card may be a hint to look over all spending habits and develop a budget that would help regain control over one’s finances. A wise budget and strategic investments can help ensure financial security in the future.

The 4 Of Cups Tarot card can also signify feeling stuck in a relationship, whether it’s romantic or platonic. This card suggests that one must look at their own actions to see what is not working in the relationship, as well as shift their perspective to move forward. If the relationship is worth saving, both parties must be willing to compromise and make an effort to stay connected. Additionally, The 4 Of Cups Tarot card could indicate that a surprise gift could be on the way, whether it’s a surprise getaway or a sentimental gift.
