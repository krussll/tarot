However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does The Hanged Man and 10 Of Wands mean together?

This is likely causing a great deal of stress and strain in your life. The Hanged Man card can offer you insight and wisdom when it comes to this challenging situation. In order to find respite from the burden, you may need to sacrifice something or take a step back. By getting a different perspective, you can make changes that will help to relieve the stress and pressure that you are currently under.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hanged Man and 10 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hanged Man and 10 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health:  
The Hanged Man Tarot card speaks to us of health in terms of a need for an adjusted perspective. It suggests that for us to have a healthy life, we must be prepared to sacrifice certain things. These might include bad habits, unhealthy relationships, or furthering our own interests above those of others. With a renewed outlook, we can learn to embrace the struggles and obstacles of life, understanding that this improved viewpoint can help us to achieve balance and clarity, ultimately leading to a healthier life.

Finances:  
When it comes to finances, the Hanged Man Tarot card can be interpreted as a caution to not get too fixated on money. Sure, it’s important to plan ahead and make wise financial investments, but it can be easy to become obsessed and lose sight of what it is that really matters. This card encourages us to take a step back and look at our financial situation from a different angle, to make sure that we are not sacrificing our wellbeing in our pursuit of wealth.

Relationships:  
In terms of relationships, The Hanged Man Tarot card suggests that we need to get a different perspective on our connections. It can be easy to become too focused on our own needs and desires, but we must remember that we are in relationships with other people who also have their own feelings and needs. Therefore, the Hanged Man card encourages us to take a step back, suspend our opinions and judgments, and look at our relationships from the other person’s standpoint. This can help us to make decisions that are beneficial for both parties, and ultimately lead to healthier, more balanced relationships.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

For Health

The 10 of Wands tarot card represents stress, exhaustion and carrying too many burdens. It can suggest that your body and mind are overwhelmed with too much responsibility or too many obligations, leading to physical and mental exhaustion. You may be trying to do too much or take on more than you can handle in the physical realm of your life. Too much stress can lead to physical problems, such as headaches, insomnia, muscle tension, and difficulty concentrating. This card can also suggest that taking a break from your physical duties is necessary in order to restore your health and sense of balance.

For Finances

The 10 of Wands tarot card can also represent financial burdens and responsibilities. It may symbolize feeling overwhelmed with financial obligations or too much responsibility for managing your finances. It can suggest that you are taking on too much responsibility or too much debt at one time, leading to an imbalance in your financial life. You may need to take a step back in order to assess your current financial situation and create a plan to reduce your burden and move towards financial balance.

For Relationships

The 10 of Wands tarot card can also represent feeling overwhelmed in relationships. Too much responsibility or too many obligations can lead to exhaustion and even resentment. This card can suggest that it’s time to take a step back and reassess the relationship to make sure that you are not overburdening yourself or taking on too much responsibility for the other person. It can also mean that it is time to take a break from certain relationship responsibilities in order to restore balance and harmony.
