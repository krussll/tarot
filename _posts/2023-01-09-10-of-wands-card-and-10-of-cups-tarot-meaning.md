A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The 10 Of Wands tarot card and The 10 Of Cups tarot card together suggest a need to find balance between too many burdens and family contentment. The 10 Of Wands card expresses stress and exhaustion from carrying too much at once, while the 10 Of Cups card speaks of the joy that comes from having love and support from family. There is an important message to be aware of here — in order to find the total love and support of family, one must first find the balance between fulfilling their own life’s responsibilities and enjoying the joys of family life. To do this, it is important to be mindful of how much one can carry and when to take a break, before being overwhelmed by too many duties and responsibilities.

It’s a sign of responsibility and hard work, but it can also be a reminder that if you work too hard, you will eventually burn out. The 10 Of Cups represents a sense of family contentment and total love and support. It suggests that even during tough times, you can find solace and comfort in the arms and presence of those who love you.

This combination of cards is an indication that while a stressful situation may be looming, you won’t have to face it alone. You can rely on your family and friends to provide you with unconditional love and support, which can help you to get through it with ease. The 10 Of Wands is a reminder that some things may become too much, so it’s important to be mindful of how you are managing your workload and commitments. At the same time, The 10 Of Cups signals the importance of taking some time out to relax and let your loved ones provide you with the nourishment and care that you need. This combination of cards suggests that it is important to draw strength from the people who love you when things become too overwhelming.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 10 Of Wands and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health – The 10 of Wands Tarot card warns of the stress and exhaustion that can come with feeling overwhelmed. Feeling overburdened can take a toll on both physical and mental health. Those burdened with too much responsibility may find themselves struggling to make it through the day, resulting in tiredness, lethargy, and a lack of focus. It is important to recognize these signs and to take steps to reduce stress and create balance. This can include taking breaks, exercising, and engaging in activities that bring joy.

Finances – The 10 of Wands Tarot card is also a sign of financial strain. It may indicate that the individual is struggling to manage their money and responsibilities, leading to difficulty paying bills, spending beyond their means, and neglecting financial goals. It is important to create a budget and to have realistic expectations about what can and cannot be accomplished each month. Taking a step back and evaluating the overall financial picture can help to reduce the burden and create a more manageable financial situation.

Relationships – The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to prioritize relationships. When feeling overwhelmed, it can be easy to neglect one’s relationships, pushing away those who could be a source of support. Taking time to spend with close friends and family can be beneficial, and can help to reduce stress and bring balance to life. Additionally, it is important to accept outside help and delegate tasks to allow for more free time.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of overall good health for yourself and your loved ones. It can indicate that a healthy lifestyle including proper diet, exercise, and plenty of rest is what’s required for optimal well-being. This card also speaks of finding balance between physical and spiritual health, which can help you to stay cheerful and energized.

Finances: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of financial stability and abundance. It suggests that you may have the resources and the energy to make the most of your financial situation. You may be able to save up and plan for the future. It could be a great time to make investments that could help secure your financial future.

Relationships: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a representation of strong and supportive relationships. This card speaks of love, emotional understanding and mutual respect, which are essential elements of any successful bond. It can also point towards honesty and transparency in relationships, as these are important components of any healthy relationship. It also means that it could be time to take a look at how you communicate and how you’re able to express yourself with your loved ones. Overall, The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of happiness and satisfaction in love, whether it’s with a partner, family member, or a friend.
