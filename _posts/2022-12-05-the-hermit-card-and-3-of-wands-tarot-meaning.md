Together, these two cards signal a need to go beyond the self and push boundaries with outside help and expertise, offering boundless potential for spiritual growth and illumination.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 3 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card has been said to represent moments of profound contemplation and introspection. When this card appears, it can indicate a time to turn inward and discover the personal truths that bring about spiritual awakening.

Health & Wellbeing

The Hermit Tarot card can represent a need for the individual to delve deep into their inner self and look for their truth. It can be a reminder to take time for self-care; to nurture the physical, mental, and spiritual aspects of oneself. This means eating well, getting enough sleep, practicing self-reflection, and engaging in activities that fill us with joy and happiness. It can also be a reminder that we must dig deep into any physical or mental ailments and work to find solutions that will help them heal.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card can represent a need to reflect upon our true values and motivations around money. It can serve as a reminder to evaluate our spending habits and to learn to find a balance between meeting needs, while still allowing room for occasional indulgences. Furthermore, The Hermit card can also signify a need to examine our relationships with money, and to ask ourselves if our current economic situation is really in line with our deeper values and ambitions.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card can indicate a need for self-reflection when it comes to our relationships. It can be a reminder to step back and to assess our current relationships and the roles we are playing in them. It can also suggest to go inward and explore how we can become more mindful of our needs and how we can better communicate them to our loved ones. The Hermit Tarot card can be a reminder that having a healthy relationship is about acknowledging our true selves and making sure that there is emotional and spiritual balance in our interactions.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 3 of Wands Tarot card can represent a need to connect with others in order to take care of one’s physical and mental health. Working with others to achieve a common goal can lead to greater well-being, as it stops us from feeling isolated and without help. Ideas should be exchanged between like-minded individuals so that new paths to healing can be explored and implemented. This card encourages us to look outside of ourselves and work together in order to better our own health.

Finance:

The 3 of Wands Tarot card highlights the importance of teamwork when it comes to financial success. Working with others allows for greater opportunities and a more reliable income stream. Whether it’s through investments, entrepreneurship or contract work, collaborating with others can provide a much-needed boost to one’s wealth. This card also encourages us to think about expansion, being willing to explore new markets and avenues for potential revenue.

Relationships:

The 3 of Wands Tarot card suggests that relationships can benefit from teamwork and collaboration. Working together on joint projects and goals strengthens the connection between two people, resulting in positive communication and increased trust. This card can also symbolize travel, which can provide couples with an opportunity to explore new places and ideas together. The 3 of Wands encourages us to strengthen our relationships through engaging activities and investing in each other in order to foster a strong, lasting bond.
