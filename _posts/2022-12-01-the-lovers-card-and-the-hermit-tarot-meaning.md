A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and The Hermit card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and The Hermit have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and The Hermit mean together?

The Lovers and Hermit tarot cards together represent a balance between partnership and personal reflection. This represents a need to take time to examine your relationships, to make sure that the strength of two together is also honoring your own journeys and personal truths. This is a time to explore what partnership means to you and to make the best choice for yourself and your relationships. Taking the time to go within yourself and listen to your intuition brings you the clarity and spiritual illumination to make sure that any choices are right for you and your partnerships.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The combination of The Lovers and The Hermit tarot cards is an interesting one when it comes to your love life. The Lovers card symbolizes partnership, deep love, and the strength of two together, while the Hermit card symbolizes a quest for personal truth and spiritual illumination. Together, they suggest an invitation to explore the depths of your relationship with yourself and your beloved.

The Lovers card reminds you to appreciate the beauty and strength of your relationship. It also encourages you to take the time to nurture your connection and appreciate the unique qualities that each partner brings. Your relationship should be a source of joy and fulfilment, so take the time to savour each moment together.

The Hermit card encourages you to take some time to go within and reflect on your relationship. Consider the dynamics between you both and what needs to be done to create and maintain a healthy relationship. Ask yourself if you’re honoring your truth and if your actions are in keeping with that truth. Explore your innermost desires and dreams for your relationship.

This is a time to be honest with yourself and your beloved. Be willing to be vulnerable and open to the other’s perspective. The combination of The Lovers and The Hermit card invites you to explore all aspects of your relationship – the good, the bad, and the ugly – and use the wisdom gained to nurture the bond between you. In doing so, you’re setting the foundation for a long and thriving relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and The Hermit mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health: The Lovers card speaks to the beauty of partnering with someone to improve your health. This could mean having a buddy to work out with and motivate each other to stay active, or it could be working with a coach or nutritionist to help you get your diet and lifestyle on track. The card emphasizes the importance of having a partner to help you reach your goals and make lasting changes for your overall health and well-being.

Finances: The Lovers Tarot card can represent the financial partnership between two people. It speaks to the need to work together in order to make smart money decisions, such as creating a budget and investing wisely. It is important to remember to consult with each other on major financial decisions and work together to stay on the same page at all times.

Relationships: The Lovers Tarot card stands for deep and meaningful relationships. It suggests partnerships are most successful when built on trust, communication, and true love. This could be in a romantic relationship, a close friendship, or even a relationship between two family members such as a parent and their child. Working together, two people can create a strong and lasting bond that can withstand any challenge.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card can be a powerful symbol for understanding our health, finances, and relationships. Health is a very personal matter. In this context, The Hermit card encourages us to go within and take time to reflect on our physical and mental wellbeing, to listen to our inner voice, and to gain clarity and understanding on what course of action to take to improve our overall health.

Finances can be a complex area of our lives, and The Hermit Tarot card can offer insightful guidance in this area. It suggests that we need to dig deep, look within, and gain a clear understanding of our own fiscal behaviors. We need to assess our current financial situation, and make informed decisions on how to structure our financial investments or plans in order to create effective positive outcomes.

Relationships are often a delicate matter, and it is important to remember that no two relationships are the same. The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to take time to evaluate our current and past relationships, to consider what aspects of these relationships we need to let go of, and to identify what we need to nurture and strengthen. We should also make use of the space that The Hermit card represents, and use it to reconnect with ourselves, so that we can better understand the needs of our relationships, and make informed decisions about what we need for personal growth.
