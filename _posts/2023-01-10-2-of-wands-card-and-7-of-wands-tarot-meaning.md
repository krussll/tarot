A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 7 Of Wands card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 7 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The combination of the 2 Of Wands and the 7 Of Wands tarot cards suggests that you are facing an important decision, and the time has come for you to make a choice. You are waiting for the results to determine the outcome, and it is a good time to reflect on the journey you have been on, and the decisions you have already made. As you wait for the results, it is also important to be prepared to protect yourself against any competition or negative energy that could come your way. Be sure to take all the necessary steps to defend yourself during this time, and to make sure your travel plans are in order.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 2 Of Wands and 7 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The 2 of Wands Tarot card is all about making choices in love and being ready for the results. This card indicates that it is time to make a conscious decision about where you want to go in your love life. Whether this means taking a chance on someone new, or ending an unhappy relationship, you need to make a decision that is right for you. Once you’ve made a decision, be prepared to wait to find out the results.

The 7 of Wands tarot card is all about protecting yourself against competition and defending your place in your relationship. This card is a reminder that even if you are in a committed relationship, you still need to stand up for your own needs. Don’t let your partner take you for granted – you need to be assertive and make sure that your needs are being met. It’s also a reminder that it’s important to remember to treat your partner with respect and kindness.

These two cards bring a powerful message for your love life. Making tough decisions and standing up for yourself may be hard, but it will be worth it in the end. You need to be brave, make choices that are right for you, and take care of yourself and your partner. If you do this, you will be rewarded with a strong, healthy, and fulfilling relationship.

### What does 2 Of Wands and 7 Of Wands mean together for your finances?

The 2 Of Wands tarot card, in combination with the 7 Of Wands, is an indication of making a major transition in your work and financial lives. It suggests that you may be at a crossroads between two major decisions – should you stay in the situation you are currently in, or should you take the opportunity to move on to something bigger and better? You will need to make a choice based on your own instincts and judgment, without allowing outside competition to influence your decision.

The 2 Of Wands tarot card also speaks of waiting for results, indicating that your current situation may be in some form of stalemate. This can mean that you have reached a plateau in terms of your career progression and financial goals, and it is time to consider what changes you can make to move forward. It is important to remember that this is a choice that only you can make, as these cards are pointing to the power of your own decisions.

The 7 Of Wands tarot card in this combination is a warning that you must be ready to protect your current situation against unwanted competition. It can be difficult to make the right decision when faced with competition, as it can be tempting to bow to external forces. However, the 7 Of Wands is a reminder that you must be willing to stand up for yourself and your own values, in order to make the right choice.

In terms of finances and job-related matters, The 2 Of Wands and The 7 Of Wands in combination suggest that you need to take a step back and examine the situation from all angles before making a decision. It is an indication that taking the path of self-defense and protecting your own interests is the best way to ensure that the results of your decisions will be worth the effort. Be sure to weigh up all the pros and cons before making your choice, and consider the long term implications of any choice you make.

### What does 2 Of Wands and 7 Of Wands mean together for your health?

The 2 of Wands and the 7 of Wands tarot card together bring a message of waiting for results, making a choice and taking steps to protect your health. This combination encourages you to take proactive steps to stay healthy and move forward with your intention.

When it comes to protecting your health, the 2 of Wands indicates that you may need to wait for the results of an examination, such as a blood test or MRI, before making any decisions. It is important to stay patient and trust that the results will give you the information you need to make an informed choice about your health.

In the meantime, the 7 of Wands suggests taking preventive measures to protect yourself against potential health hazards. Be mindful of your diet, exercise regularly, and take other steps to boost your immune system. As you wait for results, having a plan in place to stay healthy is key.

In conclusion, the 2 of Wands and 7 of Wands card combination indicate a period of patience and proactive action when it comes to your health. It is important to be mindful of the choices you make while still remaining open to the results of any tests or screenings that you have. Be sure to take steps to protect yourself, such as eating well and exercising, as you await the results and be sure to take action based on the information you receive.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

The 2 of Wands Tarot card is symbolic of delaying a decision, making plans for the future and considering the different options available. This card can provide an indication of health, finances, and relationships and how these three areas of life will be affected by any decision that may be made.

When referring to health, the 2 of Wands encourages the querent to take time to consider the various options before making a decision. It suggests that there may be multiple routes to take, and careful consideration of each route will result in the best outcome for their health. This card can also suggest that the querent should take caution when it comes to their health and take the time to really think through the consequences of any decision they make.

In terms of finances, the 2 of Wands implies that the querent may need to be patient when it comes to making a decision. It can suggest that the resources they have available or the potential they envision are likely to become available if they weigh the options carefully. This card can indicate that the querent should take their time and make sure that any decisions they make regarding their finances are well researched and planned out.

When considering relationships, the 2 of Wands indicates that the querent should take the time to consider any decision made and be mindful of how it will affect the other party. This card can suggest that the querent should think carefully before speaking and ensure that any actions taken are in the best interest of both parties. This card can also warn that being too hasty could create more problems than it solves.

Overall, the 2 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take the time to consider all the different choices available before making a decision. It implies that the querent should carefully research and plan out any consequences that may happen from any decision they make, whether it be in regards to health, finances or relationships.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder that taking care of one’s health is also an act of self-defense. When it comes to health, the 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to focus on long-term health and preventative health care to protect one’s physical and mental wellbeing. Taking preventive measures like eating a balanced diet, getting regular exercise, and avoiding risky behaviors like smoking can help protect the body from harm and the mind from stress. The 7 of Wands Tarot card also encourages us to be proactive about our health and take action to protect ourselves from illness and injury.

Finances: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to protect our finances from potential financial difficulties. Making smart financial decisions and developing a sound budgeting plan can help guard against negative financial outcomes. The 7 of Wands Tarot card encourages us to be mindful about our spending and to invest in our future. Staying aware of changes in the financial market and the global economy and knowing our financial goals can help be better prepared for potential risks.

Relationships: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to protect our close relationships and be mindful of how we treat those we care about. When it comes to relationships, it’s important to be respectful and considerate of our partner’s needs and take active steps to protect the trust and connection between us. The 7 of Wands Tarot card encourages us to look at our relationships objectively and take the necessary steps to strengthen the relationship, whether it’s through communication or finding common ground and compromise.
