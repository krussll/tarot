A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and The Chariot card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and The Chariot have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and The Chariot mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The Chariot tarot card together suggest a hybrid of tradition and progress. They advocate for the acceptance of traditional values and expectations, but also suggest taking action to move forward. It is recognizing the importance of respecting the conventions of the past, but also feeling comfortable with the idea of taking risks and embarking on a new journey. Ultimately, it is about finding a balance between adhering to conventional norms while still striving to make changes and create something new.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hierophant and The Chariot mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that it’s important to adhere to traditional health practices. This could mean care for your body through exercise, healthy eating habits and regular check-ups. Additionally, the Hierophant indicates that conventional medicine may be the most appropriate route to take. Alternative medicines such as Ayurvedic, Chinese and Homeopathic may have their place, but the Hierophant suggests that it may be more beneficial to stick to established medical practice.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card might be suggesting that you should stick to traditional financial strategies when it comes to matters of money. This could mean that you should rely on conventional sources of income such as a regular paycheck or savings account. Avoid risky investments and stick to what you know best. Taking calculated risks is fine, but it’s best to stay within your financial comfort zone.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card could mean that it’s time to conform to established conventions when dealing with relationships. This could mean that it’s time to settle down, get married and have children. Alternatively, it could mean that it’s time to work on existing relationships and make sure that they are solid before taking any risks. The Hierophant suggests that it’s best to take things slow and be sure of your commitment before making any major decisions.
