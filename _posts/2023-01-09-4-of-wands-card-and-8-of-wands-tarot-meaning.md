## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

The 4 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of stability, harmony, and abundance in the realms of health, finances, and relationships. This card is an indication of an especially beneficial period of life when all aspects of one’s life are in balance, and these areas are likely to experience a boost.

In terms of health, the 4 of Wands Tarot card speaks of revitalization of energy and well-being. If one is feeling ill or has been recently ill, this card can be an indication that they are on the mend, and will be able to make a full recovery. For those who are already healthy, this card can forecast a period of increased energy and vitality.

In the area of finances, the 4 of Wands Tarot card signifies the best of luck at this time–for money and wealth to increase and grow. While it’s important to note that the 4 of Wands can’t guarantee financial success, it does mean that any efforts you make to make money will likely be rewarded. It can also suggest that now is a good time to take a risk, as it is likely to pay off in more ways than one.

Finally, the 4 of Wands Tarot card can symbolize strong relationships and healthy connections. Whether this is family, friends, or romantic connections, the 4 of Wands speaks to the potential for healing and strengthening of ties. This is a card of joy, celebration, and harmony; those who are connected to you will feel the positivity that is coming from the 4 of Wands. This period of life is the perfect opportunity to build relationships and create strong bonds that will last long into the future.

This could mean taking up a new activity quickly, or seeing a doctor as soon as possible. The card also suggests that any health news should be taken in stride, as it is signaling a change in your life—whether it is good or bad.

This means a change in fortune could be coming your way, or that the current situation could be quickly resolved through a bit of action and quick thinking. The card also suggests that taking risks, though not recommended, could be beneficial when things look their bleakest. Also, this card is a reminder to not act impulsively with money as things can change quickly.

Relationships: The 8 of Wands Tarot card is often a sign of how quickly relationships can change. It suggests that taking action and communicating clearly can help keep a relationship healthy. This card is also a sign that news will come soon and could bring changes, although it could be good or bad news. It suggests that quick actions are needed in order to maintain the relationship and not let it drift away. It is also a reminder that relationships need to be constantly nurtured and that taking a proactive approach will be beneficial.
