## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is all about the generous exchange of resources and support, both giving and receiving. When it comes to health, this card encourages us to look after ourselves as well as others. It could represent the need to ask for help when we are feeling stressed or overwhelmed by our health problems, or it could suggest that we need to provide support and compassion to those around us who are struggling. Ultimately, it encourages us to reach out to those who may need our help and to accept assistance from those who are offering it.

Finances:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that money is a form of energy, which can be shared and exchanged. This suggests that when it comes to our finances, we should be generous with our resources and open to receiving assistance when needed. It could suggest that a financial gift from an unexpected source may be coming our way, or that we should be willing to invest or lend money to someone in need. It may also remind us that financial success is often linked to our ability to network and share our resources, so it is important to invest in our relationships.

Relationships:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages us to think about the balance between sharing and taking within our relationships. It suggests that we should be willing to provide assistance to those around us who may need our help, as well as being willing to ask for help when needed. In a romantic relationship, it could represent the importance of compromise and understanding, as well as the need to be generous with our time and attention. In a family context, it could suggest that we should focus on the act of giving and receiving support, rather than simply taking or demanding too much.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a powerful symbol of despair and fear. It can indicate that the querent is overwhelmed by their worries and fears, often to the point of mental exhaustion. It can represent a period of dark nights of the soul; a time of feeling lost, lonely, and without hope.

For health, this card can represent a period of physical and mental exhaustion, due to the constant worrying and stress. The querent may be feeling physically and mentally drained, may be having difficulty sleeping, and could potentially be exhibiting symptoms of anxiety and depression. This card may indicate a need to take a break and to give oneself time to rest and recuperate.

For finances, the 9 of Swords tarot card can indicate a feeling of financial insecurity. The querent may feel that their finances are out of control and that they are unable to get a hold of their money. This card may be a sign that the querent needs to take a step back and reevaluate their financial situation. It can be a call to create more financial stability and security in their lives.

For relationships, this card can indicate a feeling of insecurity and fear. The querent may feel that their relationships are not working out and that they are unable to find a solid sense of security in them. It can be a sign that the querent needs to take a step back and reexamine their relationships. If the querent is able to identify the source of their fear, they can begin to take steps to create the security and stability that they need.
