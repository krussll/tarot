## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

For health, The Temperance Tarot card suggests you need to pay attention to your body and make sure that you maintain balance in your diet and exercise habits. Eating too much or too little can lead to health problems and make it difficult to maintain a healthy lifestyle. The card also suggests that it’s important to take time to relax and care for yourself. Taking time to do yoga or meditate can help you stay balanced and healthy.

For finances, The Temperance Tarot card encourages you to look for ways to maintain balance and be sensible with your money. It also reminds you to be patient with investments and not expect immediate results. It’s important to be mindful of how much you are spending and plan ahead to save money. The card suggests that you can use good judgement and common sense when it comes to your finances.

In relationships, The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to focus on harmony and balance. It’s important to be patient with each other and respect each other’s needs and opinions. It is also important to be able to control your emotions and not let them get the better of you. Being able to compromise and negotiate with each other is important for the balance and harmony of the relationship. The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to find balance and peace in your relationships.

The King of Wands Tarot card is a very powerful card, signifying a bold leader with the ability to charm and courageously take charge of the situation. This card often represents an ability to take control and use your determination to achieve success. When this card appears in a Tarot card reading, it typically signals a time of creative activity, ambition and determination.

Health: The King of Wands can be a reminder to take charge of your own health by having the courage to make positive changes. If a health problem is causing stress, the King of Wands can help to bring clarity and courage to face whatever health challenge you are dealing with.

Finances: The King of Wands signifies a time of financial stability. You may be in a place where your finances are looking quite healthy and you should use this opportunity to invest wisely and take control of your financial situation. The card encourages you to wisely make use of your resources to ensure that you reach your financial goals.

This card can also be a reminder to not let feelings of possessiveness or jealousy take over when in relationships. Instead, use this energy to create a healthy, harmonious dynamic and to gain the respect of your partner.
