A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Lovers tarot card and the 6 Of Swords tarot card signify a transition in a partnership. It suggests that you are both making a choice to strengthen the bond between the two of you by moving through a necessary transition. It could mean that you are relocating together, either geographically or emotionally, in order to create a deeper level of love and intimacy. It is a sign that together, you can overcome any obstacle that may be standing in the way of true happiness and lasting love.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Lovers and 6 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Lovers tarot card and the 6 Of Swords tarot card together indicate a period of transition in your love life. It suggests that the two of you are on the cusp of something new, which means you must both look to the future and make a choice. The two of you may be considering bigger changes in your relationship, such as moving in together, getting married, or having children. This could also represent a physical relocation as it is likely that a move of some kind is coming.

The Lovers tarot card indicates there is potential for deep love and partnership if you can make the right choices. This means your relationship needs to be strong enough to withstand the transition. The 6 Of Swords tarot card indicates a challenge and a need to pick up and move forward. This could mean leaving the comfort of the familiar and venturing into the unknown. It encourages you to come together, rely on each other, and navigate the new path ahead as a team.

The combined forces of these two cards could also indicate that the transition you’re facing involves a commitment or even a vow of some kind. The Lovers card suggests that the transition should bring the two of you to a new level of intimacy or commitment. The 6 Of Swords conveys a sense of moving forward, so it could symbolize a transition from one type of commitment to another.

Ultimately, these two cards suggest that there is an opportunity for a deeper level of commitment and love. However, it will likely take a lot of effort and understanding for the two of you to move through the transition successfully. If you can make it to the other side, the rewards will be great.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Lovers and 6 Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Lovers and 6 Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health: The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder that there are two sides to achieving optimal health: physical and emotional. A strong physical health requires good nutrition, exercise and relaxation. Emotional health requires self-love, strong relationships with others, and a positive attitude. The Lovers Tarot card reminds us that these two aspects of health are intertwined and must be balanced to achieve the highest level of health possible.

Finances: The Lovers Tarot card represents the strength of two joined together. This can be interpreted in a financial sense as two people working together as a team to make sound decisions that will put them on the path to financial success. The Lovers card encourages us to be smart with our finances, work with our partner to create a sound financial plan and remember that two heads are better than one.

Relationships: The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder that relationships require a commitment from both individuals. This card encourages us to work on our relationship together in order for it to be successful. The Lovers Tarot card also reminds us that relationships involve communication, trust and mutual respect in order to deepen and strengthen. The Lovers Tarot card reminds us to choose love and compassion over fear and to never give up on a relationship.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the need for a transition in life, whether it’s about relocating or moving on from a difficult situation. It is associated with a feeling of moving forward, but also with a sense of discomfort. In terms of health, this card can represent a difficult medical situation that requires a shift in lifestyle or attitude, or even a change of environment. This transition might not be easy, and it could involve a hefty dose of soul-searching and introspection to recognize the next step.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Swords Tarot card indicates that a financial position or certain debt might require a transition. It is a reminder to look at the bigger picture and carefully consider the best way to manage finances. Though the situation is not ideal, this card encourages taking the necessary steps to move on and improving the financial status.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Swords Tarot card suggests that it might be time for a transition. This could be about parting ways with a toxic relationship, or about taking steps to move on from a difficult situation. It might also symbolize a need to step away from an unhealthy attachment and allow both parties to find a healthier space. This card encourages the recognition of a needed change and taking necessary action. Ultimately, the 6 of Swords reminds us that we are stronger than we think and equipped to find solutions even in the toughest of times.
