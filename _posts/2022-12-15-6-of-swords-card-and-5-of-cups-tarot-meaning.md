A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 of Swords and the 5 of Cups together signify a time of healing and transition. You may be feeling regret and grief as you accept a recent significant loss, while also feeling called to move forward in your life. There is an acknowledgment that you need to make a difficult change so you can start anew and find peace. This shift may involve both a physical and emotional relocation. Despite the sadness that you are feeling, the cards indicate that you can find healing and strength in the process of relocating to a new place and beginning a new chapter in your life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

At the same time, the 6 of Swords can mean that this transition may lead to something better. Even though it’s hard, it’s necessary to move on in order to progress. This can also represent a new beginning, bringing the promise of a fresh start and healing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card indicates a necessary transition in various areas of life. In matters of health, this card suggests that this is a time to take action and make necessary changes. It could mean making changes to our dietary and exercise habits, or taking more proactive steps to improve our physical, mental and emotional health. This is a card of movement and progress, so it is a call to take the steps needed to ensure that we are living in the best possible state of health.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Swords tarot card can signify transitioning to a more secure financial situation. It is a sign that it is time to take action and make strong financial decisions that will benefit us in the long term. This could be anything from paying down debt, creating a budget, or investing in stocks or other assets.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Swords tarot card suggests a time to assess what is working and what is not in our existing relationships, and make changes or adjust our approach as needed. This card can also signify a relocation to help us move on from the past and create a new, more positive environment for ourselves. The 6 of Swords can also symbolize the courage and strength it takes to walk away from a toxic relationship if necessary.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 5 of Cups tarot card presents a bleak outlook on health. It suggests that physical and mental health are unstable and lacking a sense of stability or fulfillment. This can be especially true for those who are dealing with chronic conditions or long-term illness. It is important for those suffering from health issues to take time for self-care and to find ways to cope with their condition in order to regain some sense of balance and peace.

Finances: The 5 of Cups tarot card can indicate financial loss and difficulty in managing resources. This could be due to difficult economic times, unexpected expenses, or reckless spending and impulse buying. It encourages the reader to practice financial discipline and to seek guidance from trustworthy sources to ensure that their resources are being managed responsibly.

Relationships: The 5 of Cups tarot card can signify feelings of sadness and disappointment in relationships. It may point to a sense of abandonment or feeling unloved, as a result of losses or disappointments in relationships. It can be seen as a reminder to take a step back and evaluate how relationships serve us. It is important to foster healthy relationships that nurture and honor each individual’s needs.
