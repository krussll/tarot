A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The 9 Of Wands tarot card and the 7 Of Cups tarot card can mean several different things when taken together. On one hand, it can represent the dilemma of having too many choices. You are presented with multiple options and possibilities, and yet you cannot decide which one to commit to. On the other hand, it can stand for the importance of setting limits and boundaries. The 9 Of Wands encourages one to stay strong and defend their boundaries, while the 7 of Cups calls for taking a step back and reassessing the situation before moving forward. Taken together, these two cards illustrate the need for both forbearance, persistance and careful deliberation when making important decisions.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This card combination could also mean that you are experiencing difficulties with maintaining boundaries in your love life. You may be in a situation whereby you feel the need to take care of someone else’s needs before your own, and this could be causing you to put your own happiness last. It could be a good idea to focus on some self-care and to think about what is truly important to you and why.

The Nine of Wands suggests that you should try to build some resilience and courage and to stick to your guns in your relationship. You need to be clear about your boundaries and be willing to defend them, no matter how much pushback you may receive from your partner. Don’t be afraid to put your foot down and do not allow yourself to be taken advantage of.

The Seven of Cups is telling you to consider what you really want out of a relationship and to commit to it. This card implies that it is time to let go of the many possibilities and make a decision as to what it is that you truly want. Do not be afraid of making and committing to a decision – it can be the first step to a more fulfilling and loving relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Wands and 7 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Wands and 7 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

The 7 Of Cups Tarot card indicates too many choices and commitment issues. When this card appears in a reading, it suggests that the querent should be wary of taking on too much and may be overwhelmed by the amount of options they have to choose from.

In terms of health, the 7 Of Cups Tarot card could be a warning to take a step back and evaluate your health in an unbiased way. It could be a sign that there are too many choices when it comes to diet, lifestyle, or treatment options, leaving the querent feeling overwhelmed and unable to make a decision. Taking time to investigate each option and to get a second opinion from an expert or trusted friend can help the querent to make a healthier decision.

In terms of finances, this card suggests a situation where the querent has too many options in front of them and is unable to decide on the best course of action. It can also suggest problems with commitment and long-term goals, as well as an inability to stick to one’s budget. In order to make the best financial decisions, the querent should take some time to evaluate their finances, make a plan, and stick to it.

In terms of relationships, the 7 Of Cups Tarot card suggests a situation where the querent is having difficulty committing to a relationship or is unable to choose between multiple options. This can be a sign that the querent needs to take some time to think about their decision and evaluate all the options before making a commitment. It can also be an indication that the querent is avoiding deeper emotional connections, which can lead to issues in their relationships. Taking time to work through any internal issues and to make a concrete decision could help the querent to have successful and meaningful relationships.
