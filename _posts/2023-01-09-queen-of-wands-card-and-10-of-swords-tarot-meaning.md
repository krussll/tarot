A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The Queen of Wands and the 10 of Swords show a contrast between confidence and a sense of complete despair. The Queen of Wands represents a strong and independent woman whose boldness and self-assurance help her face difficult situations head-on. The 10 of Swords, however, suggests a shattering and total end to something, revealing a deep sense of abandonment and hopelessness. Together, these cards suggest that although you may face difficult times, you always have the inner strength and power to confront them. You must take a risk and stand tall, knowing that a painful ending may be the result, but you will still have the confidence to carry you through.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The Queen of Wands and The 10 of Swords appear together in a Tarot reading, it could very well indicate a tumultuous period in your love life. This combination of cards is not an ideal one, as it speaks of painful endings, difficult decisions, and the need to make a clean break from what wasn’t working out.

The Queen of Wands is a card of self-assuredness and confidence, which is great if you want to take control of your current situation and make the necessary changes. Still, it could also mean that you may be too confident in your own ideas and ways, which can lead to outbursts and conflict.

The 10 of Swords signifies a complete and final end to something, a rock bottom that can be painful. This card is warning you that the time has come to let go of a situation that was no longer working out and make a clean break. This could mean breaking up with someone or cutting ties with a friend.

Ultimately, The Queen of Wands and The 10 of Swords tarot card combination could be indicating that while it may be difficult and painful, the time has come to make the necessary changes to your love life. It might take a great deal of courage and self-assurance, but it is for the best and will help you find a much better partner and relationships in the future.

### What does Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When the Queen of Wands and the Ten of Swords appear together in a financial or job reading, it can represent a difficult situation that necessitates a complete ending or turning point so one can move ahead. It indicates that the querent has reached the full limits of their situation, whether it’s due to inadequate resources, mismatched skills, or not having the right environment for success. The Queen of Wands represents self-assurance, confidence, and the ability to push forward in the face of adversity. However, she is also a reminder to take stock of the situation and to recognize and accept when it is time to move on, even if this is a painful process that requires letting go of the past.

At the same time, the Ten of Swords indicates that it is time to go through a period of grief, as accepting a painful ending is a part of the process. It warns that clinging to something that no longer serves one’s purpose will only lead to additional emotional and financial pain down the line. The Ten of Swords calls for the awareness that acknowledging an ending is the only way to make space for beginning again.

Overall, when the Queen of Wands and the Ten of Swords appear together, it is a reminder that a difficult period is coming to an end and leaves the querent open to move into a more prosperous and rewarding future. They remind the querent to use their inner strength and confidence to navigate the difficult periods while they move into new, more profitable avenues. While the situation may feel challenging and painful, it is necessary in order to create a better situation for the future.

### What does Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The combination of the Queen of Wands and the 10 of Swords can signify a moment in our personal lives where we’ve come to the end of a difficult journey. This difficult journey may have left us feeling profoundly exhausted, insecure and vulnerable. We may have come to the end of it feeling drained and uncertain, as we have been tested to our emotional and physical limits.

The Queen of Wands reminds us to draw on our inner strength and courage to help us deal with these challenging circumstances. Despite this difficult challenge, the Queen of Wands encourages us to stay focused and find our inner power, as we have the ability to make a full recovery. The Queen of Wands reminds us to be fearless and confident in our abilities, no matter how challenging the goal may seem.

The 10 of Swords points to this difficult period having come to a definitive end. This card asks us to embrace the emotional freedom and peace that will come once we have put this difficult period behind us. There may be pain and sadness that still lingers, but we must remember that it is necessary in order to fully heal.

The combination of the Queen of Wands and the 10 of Swords therefore serves as an important reminder that difficult times don’t last forever. There may be dark times and painful endings, but if we stay confident in our abilities and take care of our mental, physical, and emotional wellbeing, we will find our way out of the darkness and into a more positive future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health: The Queen of Wands Tarot card stands for confidence and self-assurance and these traits often manifest as an overall sense of physical health. Those who embody the characteristics of the Queen of Wands often exude a vibrant and glowing energy. They have a positive outlook, ambition, and a passion for life. These qualities are often beneficial to one’s physical health and can be used to promote a healthy lifestyle.

Finances: For finances, the Queen of Wands Tarot card encourages one to take a step back and reassess their financial goals. Those representing this card have the ability to remain focused and motivated and are likely to take calculated risks while maintaining a firm belief in their own abilities. With confidence in oneself and a clear financial goal, the Queen of Wands can bring positive financial results.

Relationships: In matters of relationships, the Queen of Wands Tarot card stands for confidence and self-assuredness. Those who embody these traits will be able to push through tough situations and remain true to themselves. They will also be capable of making healthy and informed decisions. They will be able to stand up for themselves and their own beliefs and will not succumb to the needs of others. These traits will bring stability to any relationship.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The Ten of Swords tarot card indicates a complete and painful ending, often symbolizing rock bottom. This card can often foretell of adverse circumstances in health, finances, and relationships.

When talking about health, the 10 of Swords tarot card stands for the end of an illness or physical struggle. It can represent a complete recovery, but don’t expect to feel normal for some time after healing. As the 10 of Swords signifies a difficult and painful experience, it may represent a long and hard journey to get back to physical health and well-being.

In terms of finances, the 10 of Swords generally means that a financial situation has reached its worst point. This could mean significant debt, or an economic crisis that has taken a toll on one’s financial wellbeing. Even if the situation seems to have reached its lowest point, this card often signifies that if one perseveres and takes the right steps, they can climb out of the financial difficulties they face.

Finally, the 10 of Swords can represent the end of a relationship, whether it be a friendship, a family bond, or a romantic one. It stands for an abrupt and painful ending, often one-sided, and can suggest that there is no hope of the relationship being salvaged. It is important to remember that while this card can represent an ending, it can also signify a fresh start in life, and it is necessary to accept and heal from the pain experienced in order to move forward.
