A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Temperance card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Temperance have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Temperance mean together?

The Hermit tarot card symbolizes a need to go within, to look within and find clarity; while the Temperance tarot card symbolizes the need to maintain balance, harmony and self-control. Together, these two tarot cards mean that in order to find personal truth and spiritual illumination, one must first balance the inner and outer worlds with patience and self-control. This requires a willingness to be open and honest about oneself and to take responsibility for one’s actions. It also requires time for introspection and personal growth. By combining the two tarot cards, one can gain great insight into one’s own inner being, as well as the world around them.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card is a reminder to slow down and take a step back from our busy lives. This can have positive implications for our health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, The Hermit teaches us the importance of taking time for ourselves and looking inward for clarity. Rather than seek external solutions to our health issues, the Hermit urges us to pause and go within for a deeper understanding of our own well-being. This can mean finding quiet moments for introspection and self-care, such as meditation, walking in nature, or taking moments to connect with our own life force. It can also mean honouring our inner wisdom, trusting our instincts and following our own personal path towards health and vitality.

Financially, The Hermit can remind us to assess our lives with greater objectivity and clarity. This card can show us how to make mindful and wise decisions around money and investments. Rather than act impulsively or be swayed by external influences, the Hermit encourages us to look at the facts and figure out the best path for us that is aligned with our highest intentions. This card can also be a gentle reminder to conserve our resources and to trust that our own inner guidance can help us create good financial foundations.

In terms of relationships, The Hermit can encourage us to take a step back and reevaluate our relationships in a more objective light. This can mean looking at our own contribution to our relationships, how we show up in them, and the dynamics that we create. It can also be a reminder to ask ourselves if our relationships are truly supportive, or if they need to be adjusted or reassessed. Taking a step back and looking at our relationships with clarity can help us to determine what it is that we truly want, and to make decisions that honour both our own truth and our relationship.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health

The Temperance Tarot card represents balance in all aspects of life, including health. This card is a reminder to be patient and take care of yourself when it comes to eating, sleeping, exercising, and living a healthy lifestyle. Being mindful of what you put into your body and how you treat yourself is important when aiming for good health. The Temperance card encourages you to strive for a balanced approach to taking care of your body and staying in tune with your needs. It also can be interpreted as a reminder to practice moderation. The goal is to not overindulge or push yourself too hard when it comes to physical activities and to instead find a middle ground that works for you.

Finances

The Temperance card also has a strong connection to financial stability. This card stands for discipline and moderation, suggesting that when it comes to your finances, it is best to practice restraint and live within your means. It can be easy to get caught up in wanting more material possessions or to overspend, but it is important to keep sight of your long-term financial goals and to make responsible decisions. The Temperance card encourages us to stay patient, have patience, and practice self-control when it comes to our finances.

Relationships

The Temperance card is a reminder to practice moderation and self-control when it comes to relationships. This card encourages us to take our relationships seriously and to be mindful of how we interact with others. We should seek to create healthy, balanced relationships, avoiding becoming too angry or too forgiving. The Temperance card reminds us to strive for harmony and understanding, to be patient and understanding with others, and to work at maintaining a sense of balance and peace in our relationships.
