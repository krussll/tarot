A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The 6 Of Swords and The Queen Of Swords tarot cards together signify a time of necessary transition, of relocating and moving forward. The Queen Of Swords card emphasizes the power of self-sufficiency in making this shift and being able to take care of yourself in the new environment. This card also points to the importance of complete honesty with yourself, and the ability to overcome your fears and insecurities, as you make this move. Ultimately, this card combination signifies a journey of growth and transformation, as you learn to rely on yourself and conquer any uncertainties that may arise.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This transition could be arising from the need to gain distance from a situation that is becoming increasingly difficult to manage, or it could be as a result of a change of location for a relationship in which one or both partners must physically relocate for work or other reasons.

During this transition, it is important to maintain total honesty in all of our communications, both with ourselves and with our partner. The Queen of Swords brings clarity to our feelings and helps us to be honest and direct in our communication with our partner, allowing us to identify problems and begin to form solutions. This clarity will be invaluable when making difficult decisions related to our relationship.

Additionally, the Queen of Swords reminds us to be self-sufficient and to trust ourselves. We must recognize our own strength, intelligence, resourcefulness and resilience in order to make the best decisions for ourselves and our relationship. Clear communication and relying on our own strength will promote a successful transition, allowing us to move forward with a more secure relationship.

The 6 of Swords and the Queen of Swords together indicate that the transition ahead of us will be difficult, but its results will be worth it in the end. With honesty, strength, and a focus on self-sufficiency, we can make the necessary decisions to ensure that both us and our relationship move forward in a healthier, more secure way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card is associated with physical and emotional transitions. It is a reminder to be aware of your mental and physical well-being, and take steps to ensure they are both taken care of in the present moment. It suggests now is the time to make healthy lifestyle changes and commit to them. This could range from getting more rest, eating a balanced diet, and exercising regularly, to seeking professional help. It’s important to listen to your body and your emotions, and be attentive to their needs.

Finances:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card may indicate a necessary transition in finances. This could mean a reorganization of your financial life, ranging from creating a budget and getting out of debt, to investing in smart money-making opportunities. It can also symbolize a change of job, or a relocation, which could bring financial growth and stability. The card advises to be mindful of the decisions you make, and be wise and realistic in your approach.

Relationships:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card is associated with a necessary transition in relationships. It could be that you are moving away from unhealthy relationships and seeking out more positive ones. It could also suggest that it is time to put your relationships in perspective and evaluate if they are serving their intended purpose. It could be time to communicate with those around you in a clear and healthy manner, as well as being able to accept change and have faith that things will work out in the end.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords is a card that indicates taking good care of oneself on a physical, emotional and spiritual level. When it appears in a reading concerning health, it can be a reminder to take an honest look at your lifestyle and identify areas where a more balanced approach is needed. It also warns against allowing pride and ego to get in the way of doing what is best for your health.

Finances: The Queen of Swords can represent financial independence and a no-nonsense approach to money matters. It suggests taking a practical and realistic outlook and setting realistic goals for achieving long-term wealth. It can be a reminder to evaluate your finances and ensure sound investments and proper management of resources.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords can suggest a need for honesty and directness when it comes to relationships. It suggests taking a clear-eyed view of a situation before taking any steps so that you can make decisions that work best for yourself and for those around you. It also serves as a reminder to stand up for yourself and be true to yourself.
