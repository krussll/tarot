However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and The Tower card together in particular.

The Death card and The Tower have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and The Tower mean together?

The Death tarot card and The Tower tarot card represent two sides of a major transition in your life. The Death card symbolizes the end of something, signaling a necessary change in the way you have been living that can bring in new beginnings and transformation. The Tower card, on the other hand, is a warning of unwanted and abrupt change that can cause great difficulty and disruption, but often leads to a new perspective and lessons to be learned. This combination is a reminder to be open to the unexpected and use the power of the transition to your benefit. If you are willing to meet change with courage and resilience, then you can emerge from this experience with newfound wisdom and growth.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Death and The Tower mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Death and The Tower the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health:  
The Death Tarot card usually relates to drastic changes that must be made in order to improve your overall health. It shows that it is time to let go of any unhealthy habits or routines and make new and healthier choices. It often indicates a time for personal transformation and to begin fresh with a renewed commitment to self-care. This card suggests that “out with the old and in with the new”. In order to progress forward, it is necessary to let go of anything that is a hindrance to maintaining a healthy and balanced lifestyle.

Finances:  
The Death Tarot card is frequently associated with changes to one’s finances. This card shows that it is time to make a fresh start, let go of any bad financial habits such as impulsive spending and budgeting carelessly, and move on to more secure, financially responsible choices. It is important to think of this card as a warning sign, rather than a sign of the end, as it can lead to positive changes that will provide stability in the long run. It is a sign to take a look at how you are managing your money, examine any patterns that might be creating a problem, and create a plan to make your finances stronger.

Relationships:  
The Death Tarot card is often interpreted as a need to let go and move on from relationships that no longer serve you in a positive or constructive way. This card often suggests that something needs to be released in order to make room for something new and better. It encourages a time of reflection and analysis, to determine whether a relationship is still healthy and beneficial or if it is time to move on. Anything unhealthy should be released, moved on from, and the cycle of life should be allowed to continue and grow. This card indicates that although change can be difficult and painful, it is ultimately necessary and necessary for progression.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card is a warning of unwanted and difficult change that may be about to come into one’s life. This card often denotes ruin, upheaval, violence, and disgrace. In terms of health, The Tower Tarot card could imply the sudden onset of a serious illness or injury. It could also indicate a health crisis that would require a dramatic shift in lifestyle and habits. Financially, The Tower Tarot card typically heralds a time of financial ruin and upheaval. One might find themselves losing their job or having a sudden and unexpected drop in income. It is also possible that a significant financial investment or endeavor could turn sour.

In relationships, The Tower Tarot card could signify a major disruption in an existing relationship. This disruption could be caused by the introduction of an outside person, the unearthing of secrets or lies, or a major argument or disagreement. It could also be interpreted as an inevitable breakdown of a relationship that had already been struggling. With the Tower Tarot card, there is a sense of betrayal and upheaval that surrounds it, which is likely to leave a profound impact on any existing relationships.

The Tower Tarot card is definitely a card that nobody wants to draw, as it is a difficult and unwelcome indication of the imminent changes to come. It is important to remember, however, that although this card signifies a difficult time, it is not necessarily a sign that all hope is lost. Despite the unwanted change, growth and transformation can come from difficult situations; so it is important to remain open to the possibilities that can arise in the face of this card.
