A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Spades card and 7 Of Spades card together in particular.

The 5 Of Spades card and 7 Of Spades have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the presence of both the 5 of Spades and the 7 of Spades signifies a significant energy of transformation and dominance but within a cloud of deception and slyness.

This combination suggests a time of change or transition which may not be entirely straightforward due to underhanded tactics, suggesting possible manipulation or trickery in order to achieve victory.

As both cards are associated with the cold, reflective season of Winter, this might hint towards a crucial period of introspection.

Being aligned with the element of Water might hint towards navigating through the ebb and flow of emotions, intuition, and mystery.

Viewing these two cards together suggests a convoluted path towards success, filled with alterations and possibly deceptive strategies.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 5 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, the 5 of Spades signifies a time of change and sneakiness.

Paired with the 7 of Spades, which symbolizes deceit and victory, your love life may be undergoing a tumultuous period.

The element water and season winter associated with these two cards indicate that emotions are running high and a cold phase is ahead.

There’s a sense of something hidden or secretive – whether it’s a secret admirer or a more serious, deeper, concealed matter that concerns your relationship.

However, the presence of the 7 of Spades also suggests victory.

With sneakiness and deceit in the picture, it could possibly mean that although you might currently be amidst emotional turmoil, there is a light at the end of the tunnel.

You are likely to come out victorious and succeed in overcoming the challenges in your relationship.

It is a call to stay strong and see through the changes and hardships by maintaining clear communication and understanding each other’s perspectives.

After all, every relationship sees ups and downs; your current scenario is just a hurdle you have yet to cross.

### What does 5 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading focusing on finances and career, pulling the 5 of Spades suggests that you may need to be careful of underhanded activities in the workplace or unscrupulous actions related to your finances.

This could denote an environment where cunning or deceit is rife, like office politics or financial deals that seem suspicious.

However, this card also represents change, which can mean a potential shift in your financial status or career.

You may be due for a job promotion or be careful, you could also be facing a demotion or job loss.

Drawing the 7 of Spades in addition to the 5 of Spades amplifies the theme of deceit.

Thus, remain alert to any inconsistency in your financial records or duplicity among colleagues in your workplace.

But despite the deceit and underhanded activities, this card signifies victory.

Hence, whatever the situation you might find yourself in your career or finances, you’ll eventually triumph.

The key to navigating this phase is to be as open, honest, and direct as possible in your dealings and not let the potential slipperiness of others cloud your judgment or actions.

### What does 5 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing both the 5 of Spades and the 7 of Spades can point to a challenging situation related to your health.

The 5 of Spades stands for sneakiness and change, implying that there might be unexpected turns in your physical wellbeing.

It could mean a hidden or underlying health issue that you did not anticipate, or perhaps, an unexpected change in your current health situation.

The connection to the winter season and water element could suggest a need for introspection or it may symbolize a potential cold or related illness.

The 7 of Spades represents deceit and victory, a combination that suggests overcoming a health struggle that’s not currently apparent or that you’re being kept unaware of.

On the bright side, the presence of victory in its meaning suggests eventual triumph over your health obstacles.

This card doesn’t necessarily mean you need to panic, but it strongly advises you to pay more attention to what your body tells you.

Adopt a proactive attitude towards your health, get regular check-ups, watch your diet closely, and understand your body’s signals, for they are the best indicators of any underlying issues.

The joint presence of these two cards may represent a wake-up call regarding your health.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
