## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Moon and 8 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Moon and 8 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Moon mean?

For Health:

The Moon tarot card is a sign of unseen problems at play in your health. It could refer to hidden physical issues that have not been detected yet, or mental issues that you may not have become aware of. It can be a warning to take extra care of your health and to pay attention to your intuition. Don’t hesitate to seek out professional help if you feel something is off. It can also indicate that you are in need of a change in your routine or lifestyle. Take stock of your current habits and make adjustments to ensure your health is in top shape.

For Finances:

The Moon tarot card indicates that you need to take care of your finances, and that you may have some problems that have gone unnoticed thus far. It can be a warning to trust your intuition when it comes to financial matters. Try to be mindful of how you are spending and saving, and take control of your finances. There may be opportunities to make voluntary changes in spending and investing that will increase your financial security. Listen to your intuition and make the necessary changes in order to ensure your financial wellbeing.

For Relationships:

The Moon tarot card can be a warning for relationships as well. It suggests that you may have some underlying issues that have gone unnoticed. It can be a reminder to trust your intuition and to be mindful of any potential red flags. It is important to take action if something doesn’t feel right. Take time to reflect and make voluntary changes in your relationships. This can be hard but ultimately beneficial if done with an open mind and honest communication.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

The 8 of Swords Tarot card is an indicator of self-imposed restrictions and isolation. It suggests that we may be limiting our possibilities due to fear or avoidance of certain challenges. This card often speaks to the idea that our obstacles are mostly of our own making, either through our negative thinking or our refusal to take risks.

With health, the 8 of Swords indicates that we may be limiting our possibilities because of fear of the unknown. We may be avoiding a necessary medical treatment, diet change, or exercise routine because of fear that the outcome will not be what we expect or desire. This card encourages us to take a chance and be open to new possibilities for health and wellness.

With finances, the 8 of Swords implies that we may be avoiding taking risks or making investments because of fear. We may be getting in our own way when it comes to creating financial stability or success for ourselves. This card encourages us to take a chance and focus on building wealth instead of avoiding it.

For relationships, the 8 of Swords warns against self-imposed isolation. We may be avoiding social situations because of fear or anxiety, or we may be creating an artificial boundary between ourselves and the people we care about. This card encourages us to move beyond our fears and be open to making meaningful connections with others.
