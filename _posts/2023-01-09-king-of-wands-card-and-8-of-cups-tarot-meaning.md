A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the King Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The King Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does King Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The King Of Wands tarot card and The 8 Of Cups tarot card together suggest that it is time to move on from a situation that no longer brings you satisfaction or a sense of fulfillment. This is a time to take the bold step to retreat and redirect your energy into new endeavours. Although there will be moments of uncertainty, this card combination promises that your courage and charm will be your most powerful assets on your journey. You have the strength, power, and charisma to make the most of this new beginning.

### What does King Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The King of Wands tarot card and the 8 of Cups tarot card together typically indicate a situation in which a strong leader is abandoning their current situation. This could be a relationship in which one partner has become too controlling and is pushing their own agenda and desires, often at the expense of the other person’s feelings. As a result, the other person has made the decision to move on and abandon the relationship.

This kind of situation could be emotionally difficult and could cause deep feelings of sadness and regret. The power of the King of Wands can make the idea of leaving difficult, and the courage to actually do so is not always easy to come by. However, the 8 of Cups speaks to the idea of making a difficult but necessary decision that is ultimately in the best interest of all involved. It speaks of the power of walking away, and how it can be the right thing to do in certain circumstances.

In a love life, the King of Wands and 8 of Cups tarot card combination suggests that while there is still a strong connection between two people, it may be time to move on from the relationship. This could be because one person has become overbearing, or due to some other dynamic that has caused the relationship to become toxic or unbalanced. It is time for a new direction, and this may be the only way to preserve both parties’ peace of mind.

Ultimately, the combination of these two cards can indicate that while the relationship may be ending, it doesn’t have to mean an end to the love. It is possible to move on, preserve the respect and admiration that was there initially, and find a newer and healthier connection.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

Health:  
The King of Wands is a card of personal power and physical strength. This Tarot card encourages you to take charge of your own health and well-being. It suggests that you use your energy and enthusiasm to take the initiative in looking after your health and making positive lifestyle choices. By taking charge of your health, you will be able to create greater balance and well-being in your life.

Finances:  
This Tarot card also has implications for finances. It represents the ability to take charge of your own financial life. The King of Wands encourages you to come up with new and creative ways to further your financial success. It suggests that you should take risks, try out new ideas and be bold in your financial decisions. It is a reminder to you that you have the power and the motivation to take control of your financial life.

Relationships:  
In terms of relationships, the King of Wands is a symbol of charm and charisma. It suggests that you have the power to influence and attract people. This card encourages you to use your personal power and charm to strengthen your relationships with others. It also suggests that you should be assertive and confident in your interactions with others. It may serve as a reminder to you that the relationships you have should be mutually beneficial and should be approached with respect and assertiveness.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign that it’s time to abandon unhealthy habits in order to pursue a more balanced and healthy lifestyle. This card might signify withdrawing from behaviors that are not supportive for your health, such as poor diet and exercise habits, or even excessive sun exposure or substance abuse. If you have been avoiding going to the doctor or seeking out mental and emotional wellness support, this could be a sign that it’s time to take a step back and focus on your own health and wellbeing.

Finances

Whenever The 8 of Cups Tarot card appears, it could be a sign that it’s time to withdraw from activities that are not financially rewarding. This could be a sign that it’s time to let go of a particular job, business venture, or even an idea that can no longer sustain you as you continue to grow and expand. Alternatively, this card could also signify withdrawing from unhealthy financial habits such as debt, impulsive spending and gambling.

Relationships

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign that it’s time to retreat from unhealthy relationships that no longer serve you. This card may signify that it’s time to move on from an unhealthy partnership, a toxic friendship, or even an unfulfilling family dynamic. The 8 of Cups Tarot card encourages you to distance yourself from any situation where you are not being supported, respected and nurtured in order to take care of yourself and protect your mental wellbeing.
