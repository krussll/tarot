Many of us do tarot readings to understand our feelings, our current situation, or to make sense of the past – however some also like to use tarot to understand the future. This is often done to help prepare for what is to come.

However this can open a can of worms, especially if you don’t like what the reading predicts.

Are tarot reading predictions set in stone? Or do you have the power to change or influence what is to come?

I this guide I will take you through the big questions and how you can apply them to your practice, and everyday life.

## Can you change your tarot card reading?

**Yes, you can certainly change your tarot card reading. Tarot card readings are not set in stone, and the interpretations of the cards can be changed or altered depending on the situation**. It’s important to remember that tarot cards are tools for divination and guidance, and they can be used in a variety of ways.

The best way to change your tarot card reading is to ask yourself how the cards are being interpreted. Do the cards you have drawn truly reflect the situation you are in, or are they being interpreted differently? If the interpretation is not quite right, you can alter it by either changing the context of the cards or by looking at the cards from a different perspective.

Another way to change your tarot card reading is to draw more cards. If you feel that your current cards are not giving you the answers you need, you can draw additional cards to bring clarity to the situation. This can help you gain a better understanding of what the cards are trying to tell you.

Finally, if you still feel like your tarot card reading is off, you can ask for assistance from a tarot reader. An experienced tarot reader will be able to provide insight and guidance into the cards you have drawn, and can help you interpret them in a way that is more meaningful to you.

## Can tarot readings change?

Yes, tarot readings can change. Tarot readings are based on the interpretation of the cards and the reader’s intuition and are not set in stone. As the reader gains experience and practice with tarot readings, their interpretations can evolve and change over time. The tarot reader’s understanding of the cards and how they interact with each other can also change.

As they gain experience, they may start to see patterns in the cards that they didn’t see before or gain a better understanding of how the cards can relate to the querent’s life. This can lead to a different interpretation of the cards and a change in the tarot reading.

The querent can also influence the tarot reading by changing their attitude and outlook on the situation. If the querent approaches the tarot reading with an open and positive attitude, then the tarot reading can reflect that new attitude.

This can lead to the tarot reading changing from the original interpretation.

Finally, the tarot cards can change depending on the context of the reading. If a card appears in a different context, the interpretation of that card can change as well. For example, if the same card appears in a different position in a spread, the interpretation can be different.

## Is it okay to redo a tarot reading?

Yes, it is perfectly okay to redo a tarot reading. The tarot is a powerful tool used to gain insight into a situation and to gain clarity on decisions that need to be made.

By repeating a tarot reading, you are able to gain a more in-depth understanding of the situation and gain further insight.

When redoing a tarot reading, be sure to focus on the same questions and topics that you were asking in the first reading. This will help to gain further clarity on any areas that were unclear in the first reading. Additionally, you can use the same cards that were used in the first reading or ask the cards to reveal something new.

When redoing a tarot reading, it can be helpful to take notes and to compare the two readings. This will help you to see how the situation has progressed and how the cards have changed. It can also provide insight into how you can move forward in a positive manner. All in all, it is perfectly okay to redo a tarot reading.

The tarot is a powerful tool to gain insight and clarity into a situation. By redoing a tarot reading, you can gain further insight and clarity into the situation and make decisions that are right for you.
