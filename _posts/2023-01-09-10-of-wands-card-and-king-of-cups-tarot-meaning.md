## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Wands and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Wands and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 10 of Wands symbolizes the burden that comes with trying to do too much. When this card appears in a Tarot reading, it can indicate a sense of exhaustion and strain that comes from juggling many responsibilities or trying to take on too much at once. Physically, this card may signify the effects of long-term exhaustion or burnout, such as fatigue, insomnia, and lowered immune system. It could also be an indication of muscle tension or back pain from carrying the weight of the world on one’s shoulders. It is a reminder to take care of one’s health and to seek balance.

Finances:

The 10 of Wands symbolizes the burden of financial responsibility. This card can tell us that we are taking on too many financial obligations, or that we are too stressed about money. It can also indicate financial strain from having too many credit cards and loans, as well as from trying to work too many hours. It is important to remember that financial strain is not healthy, and it can be beneficial to seek out help or advice if necessary.

Relationships:

The 10 of Wands symbolizes the feeling of being overburdened in a relationship. This card can tell us that we are trying to take on too many responsibilities in our relationships, and that it is causing us stress and strain. It can be a warning sign that we are losing sight of our own needs, or that we are trying to control or dictate too much in the relationship. It is important to remember that relationships are a two-way street, and to be mindful of our own needs as well as the needs of our partner. We should strive for a healthy balance between our relationships and our other life responsibilities.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

The King of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to establish balance and harmony in one’s life, especially in terms of health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, the King of Cups represents an optimistic attitude towards life and an understanding that health is one of the most important aspects of life. Instead of pushing one’s body to the brink of exhaustion, the King of Cups encourages an attitude of rest and relaxation. This Tarot card also stands for moderation in food, drink, and exercise, meaning that one should strive to maintain good health by choosing the right balance.

In terms of finances, the King of Cups is a reminder to be responsible with money. It suggests that one should be smart with their financial decisions and practice self-discipline when making purchases. Rather than impulsively buying something that one may not necessarily need, the King of Cups encourages people to take the time to weigh their options and make smart decisions. The card also stands for being mindful of one’s expenses and sticking to a budget to ensure financial security.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the King of Cups is a reminder to be understanding and tolerant of others. It encourages people to listen to their partners, rather than imposing their own beliefs and values on the relationship. Furthermore, it suggests maintaining a level of emotional balance and not allowing any one emotion to overwhelm the relationship. The King of Cups also stands for being a supportive partner and being there for someone when they need it most.
