A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 4 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 4 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 4 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Three of Wands and the Four of Pentacles together suggest a journey towards a successful alliance. This could be a partnership between two individuals, a group, or even a business. The focus is on teamwork, collaboration, and commerce—all the elements that are necessary for growth and expansion. The Four of Pentacles suggests that this journey should be taken with a level of caution. Greed or a desire for long-term security should be kept in mind and carefully managed to ensure a rewarding result. Ultimately, this combination of cards speaks to a need to take risks and create something greater than what is currently in place. With the right balance of planning and collaboration, this journey should be one of success and progress.

It is important to remember that true love is not static, but dynamic, and that true love means taking risks. While it can be intimidating to make big changes, remember that love is ultimately a journey, and the rewards of taking that journey may be worth it in the end.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

Health

The 4 of Pentacles, often times referred to as the “Lord of Avarice,” is a Tarot card that speaks to the idea of wanting to take control of one’s health by any means necessary. The illustration of this card often shows a person sitting atop a throne while they clutch four pentacles, often referring to the material possessions they have accumulated over time in order to gain a sense of security. In a health sense, this card can refer to a hoarding mentality when it comes to lifestyle and diet, often trying to cling to what has worked in the past, even if it may be working against one’s health in the long run. It can also be indicative of an unwillingness to take necessary risks or try out new treatments or therapies that could be beneficial. It’s important to note that this card does not represent someone who is inherently unhealthy, but rather is just indicating a certain resistance to change or taking any risks for improvement.

Finances

The 4 of Pentacles is a Tarot card that speaks to a desire for financial security and stability. This card can suggest a tendency to hoard one’s finances, clinging to what has worked in the past in order to create a sense of comfort and safety. It can refer to an individual’s desire to save money rather than spend it, often leading to an unwillingness to take risks that could be potentially profitable. This card is not indicative of someone who is completely irresponsible with their money, but rather is a symbol of wanting to stick to what one knows and trusts, often leading to stagnation in wealth and ultimately a fear of taking any risks that could put one’s finances at risk.

Relationships

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card that speaks to a person’s desire for security and stability in their relationships. In a relationship context, this card can refer to a person’s desire to stay within their comfort zone, trying to make what works within the relationship as safe and secure as possible. This could manifest as an unwillingness to take risks or go outside of one’s comfort zone in a relationship, instead trying to stick to what one knows works. This card can also be indicative of a certain possessiveness in a relationship, wanting to keep one’s partner in their clutches and not allowing them to grow and explore other possibilities. The 4 of Pentacles is a warning to be mindful of one’s tendencies to cling to what has worked in the past, and to take risks where one can in order to create a healthy and secure relationship.
