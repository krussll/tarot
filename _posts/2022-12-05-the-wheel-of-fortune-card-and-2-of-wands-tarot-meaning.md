A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Wheel Of Fortune card and 2 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Wheel Of Fortune card and 2 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

You are at an important turning point, and your luck could be improving. Whether you decide to take a gamble or make a wise choice, you can expect results soon. You may also find yourself making plans for a journey of some kind, whether physical or metaphorical. Use the Wheel Of Fortune to help guide your decisions and trust your gut instinct. You never know what good luck may be headed your way!

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Wheel Of Fortune and 2 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is most often associated with luck, good fortune, and a turning point. For health, it could indicate a positive shift in health after a prolonged period of illness or a new opportunity to explore a healthier lifestyle. It could indicate a period of recovery that has been long awaited and a lucky break in terms of health. In terms of finances, it can suggest unexpected windfalls and a new financial opportunity. It could also be a sign of improved luck when it comes to money, investment, or gambling. Relationships wise, the Wheel of Fortune card could point to an unexpected chance to encounter love, the potential for a relationship to change for the better, or the luck of finding a relationship that is true and lasting. All in all, this card is most often viewed as a sign of luck and opportunity that can bode well for all aspects of life.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 2 of Wands tarot card is an indication that you need to make decisions in order to benefit your health. It is not necessarily a warning sign, but an indication that you need to make choices about your health that will have positive long-term effects. This could mean that you make changes in your nutrition, exercise habits, or other lifestyle changes that will lead to improved physical well-being. Now is the time to plan for a healthier future and make the decisions necessary to improve your overall quality of life.

Finances  
The 2 of Wands tarot card symbolizes a need to make decisions about finances. This card encourages you to be patient and to make well-thought-out decisions that will be beneficial in the long run. This could mean making investments or taking on new challenges that could help you to increase your wealth. It is important to consider all of your options and make sure that the choices you make are right for you.

Relationships  
The 2 of Wands tarot card encourages you to focus on your relationships and make decisions that will help them to flourish. It is important to take the time to assess both short-term and long-term needs and to plan accordingly. This could mean making decisions about how to spend more quality time with your partner or how to give your relationships the attention they deserve. Taking the time to nurture your relationships will lead to a more fulfilling life in the long run.
