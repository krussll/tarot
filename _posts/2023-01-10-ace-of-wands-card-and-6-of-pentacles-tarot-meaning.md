## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

The Ace of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the power of new beginnings. It is a sign of opportunity, inspiration and the energy to take on new projects. In terms of health, it can be a sign of renewed vigor and energy, and a sign that it is time to take better care of oneself. It can remind us that our physical, mental and emotional health need to be nurtured.

In terms of finances, the Ace of Wands Tarot card suggests that it is time to be creative and explore new opportunities. It encourages us to be open to new ideas and to welcome the chance of taking risks in order to realize our financial goals. It also encourages us to be mindful of our finances, to save and invest wisely.

When it comes to relationships, the Ace of Wands Tarot card reminds us to take time to nourish our relationships. It encourages us to take initiative, keep our spirits high and to explore new ways of connecting and interacting. It is a sign that it is time to begin a new project with those we love, to focus on the positive aspects of our relationships and to remain open-hearted and generous.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

When it comes to health, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to us that there is always help available if we need it. The card shows a wealthy man giving charity to two people who are in need. It is a reminder that, no matter our situation, we can always reach out to others for assistance. It may not always be financially-based, but can come in the form of emotional support or even just a listening ear who will offer advice. Whatever the form, there are people out there ready and willing to help.

When it comes to finances, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card conveys a message of abundance and generosity. It speaks to the idea that we can give and receive support when it comes to our finances. We may be able to help someone else out financially and, in turn, can receive that same help from others when we need it. It is a reminder to allow ourselves to receive and to also give when it is within our means to do so.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks to the idea of balance and mutual support. This card suggests that we can find a healthy balance between taking care of our own needs and providing for and helping out others. It encourages us to be generous with our resources and to be open to both giving and receiving in our relationships. It is a reminder that nurturing our relationships is important, and that we can create a healthy connection with others which is rooted in both give and taking.
