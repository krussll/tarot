A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Strength card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The 7 Of Cups tarot card together signify that you have a challenge ahead of you to find the courage and commitment to make a difficult decision. It is important for you to focus on self-love and self-respect in order to make sure your decision is rooted in the principles of unconditional love. It may take some effort for you to rise above the confusion and distraction of too many choices, but if you stay true to yourself and focus on the core values that make up your identity, you will be able to make a choice that is best for you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and 7 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

Health  
The Strength Tarot card symbolizes self-love and self-care. It encourages us to take care of our physical and mental health by treating ourselves with respect and kindness. On a physical level, the Strength card indicates the importance of taking care of our bodies with proper nutrition and exercise. On a mental level, it means that we should be mindful of our thoughts and practice positive self-talk and self-affirmation. It’s important to also take breaks and give yourself time to rest and rejuvenate. Setting healthy boundaries and honoring our needs and emotions is a form of self-care that this card prompts us to do.

Finances  
The Strength card suggests a need to take ownership of our finances. This card encourages us to be mindful of our spending habits and set realistic financial goals. It also encourages us to make responsible and confident decisions with our money. This card promotes a mindset of financial discipline, planning and self-discipline. Lastly, it suggests that we should take time to evaluate our spending habits and ask ourselves if these habits are serving our long-term financial goals.

Relationships  
The Strength card encourages us to be compassionate and understanding with ourselves and others. It also signifies the importance of respecting and honoring our own boundaries as well as those of others. This card reminds us to listen to our instincts and be honest about our needs and feelings in our relationships. Additionally, this card suggests that we should be aware of our own power and the power of others and use it with respect. Lastly, this card encourages us to take the time to nurture and nourish all relationships, whether friendship or romantic.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 7 of Cups Tarot card points to a lack of focus and commitment when it comes to managing one’s health. It suggests that an individual is overwhelmed by the sheer number of health options that are available, and is having difficulty deciding which direction to go. A person may find themselves stuck in a cycle of habits that are not beneficial for their overall health and wellbeing, and may feel unable to make changes. The card also implies that the person is likely to avoid making decisions, or will do so without fully committing to the choice they have made.

Finances: The 7 of Cups Tarot card is a warning when it comes to finances. It suggests that an individual may have a habit of overspending or have too many financial commitments. They may struggle to make prudent decisions and may be quickly influenced by the latest trends or fads. The card also speaks to a lack of discipline when it comes to money, such as making impulse purchases or failing to budget or save for the future.

Relationships: The 7 of Cups Tarot card speaks to an inability to make lasting commitments when it comes to relationships. This card warns that an individual may be prone to getting into relationships impulsively, or find themselves unable to make a meaningful connection with another person. This can make it difficult for them to maintain healthy relationships, as they may be unwilling to face their fears and make commitments. The card also points to a tendency for people to focus on the short-term rather than the long-term implications of their relationships.
