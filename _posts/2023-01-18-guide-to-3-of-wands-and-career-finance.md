Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The 3 Of Wands card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 3 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 3 Of Wands.

## 3 Of Wands And Career And Finances – An Overview

The 3 Of Wands tarot card is a positive omen when it comes to health.

It suggests that working together with a team can lead to positive results.

Working together in a collaborative effort can help to create new opportunities that can lead to healthy lifestyle changes.

This card also suggests that travel and exploration can help to boost overall well-being.

By expanding one’s horizons, it is possible to gain new insights and develop better habits that can help to improve overall health.

Overall, The 3 Of Wands suggests that with teamwork and collaboration, health goals can be achieved.

## Upright Meanings For 3 Of Wands And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The 3 of Wands suggests that you are in a great position to capitalize on your hard work and effort.

It indicates that team work, collaboration, and expansion are key to success.

This card can be interpreted as a sign of good fortune and abundance when it comes to your career and finances.

It encourages you to explore new opportunities and take risks that could lead to greater rewards.

It also suggests that travel may be necessary to reach your financial goals, which could bring success and prosperity.

You have the potential to make great strides in your career and finances if you are willing to explore and take risks.

### What It Means For The Future

The 3 Of Wands tarot card suggests that my future career and finances will be the result of a collaborative effort.

I should look for opportunities to work with others and leverage their talents to reach my goals.

This could include teaming up with likeminded peers or joining forces with larger entities to tackle difficult business objectives.

Additionally, I should look for ways to expand my scope of influence and explore new horizons.

This could involve taking on a leadership role in a new venture, utilizing technology to reach global customers, or even traveling to meet with potential partners.

With the right team, resources, and attitude, I can position myself for success in the future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 3 Of Wands tarot card is a reminder that no matter what career or financial path one takes, teamwork and collaboration are essential to success.

This card encourages those close to me to work together in order to achieve their goals.

The card also indicates that there is potential for commerce, expansion, and travel on the horizon.

Whether it’s a business venture, a new job, or an investment opportunity, it is important to remember that collaboration can open up many doors and bring about great success.

With the help of others, the possibilities are endless.

## Reversed Meanings For 3 Of Wands And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When The Three of Wands appears in the reversed position, it is a sign that my current career and financial ambitions are stuck in limbo.

I may feel like I’m at a standstill, with no clear direction or plan to move forward.

I may worry that I am working hard without seeing any tangible results.

This could be a sign that I need to be more patient and wait on the right opportunity to come along before I can achieve success.

The Three of Wands reversed could also indicate a lack of teamwork or support from colleagues.

I could be feeling alone in my endeavors and discouraged that I have to shoulder all the responsibility for my career and finances.

It also suggests that I may have been too focused on short-term gains, and that I need to take a long-term view of my ambitions.

In order to move forward, I need to take the time to assess my situation and make sure that I am taking the right steps.

I should also consider reaching out to others for support or advice, as this could help me to develop a new perspective and get my plans back on track.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Three of Wands is reversed, it can signify a lack of progress in your career and finances.

You may feel stuck or stagnant, as if there is no progress being made.

You may also be facing obstacles and delays, preventing you from achieving your goals.

The reversed Three of Wands can also indicate a lack of teamwork and collaboration.

You may be working too independently or not relying on the support of others.

This can lead to feeling overwhelmed and frustrated, as you may be unable to move forward without the help of others.

The Three of Wands reversed can also mean a lack of financial stability.

You may find yourself relying on others for financial support or struggling to stay afloat financially.

It is important to take a step back, reassess your finances, and create a plan to get back on track.

Taking initiative and collaborating with others is key to achieving success in your financial goals.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed 3 of Wands Tarot card suggests that those close to me may be struggling with their careers and finances.

They may be dealing with a lack of teamwork, communication, and collaboration, which can lead to a lack of progress and stagnant growth.

They may also be feeling a bit discouraged and overwhelmed by the challenges they are facing.

The reversed 3 of Wands Tarot card can also suggest that those close to me could be dealing with a lack of financial success, difficulty in achieving their goals, and difficulty navigating the world of commerce.

They may be feeling stuck, unable to move forward, or feeling blocked from achieving their desired outcomes.

It may also be a sign that they may need to adjust their goals and expectations, or take a step back and reassess the situation.

It can be a reminder to keep an open mind, stay flexible, and take risks in order to make progress.

Alternatively, this card could be a sign that it is not the right time to make any major investments or decisions, and that patience and caution should be exercised.
