## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

This card suggests that through dedication and hard work, one can achieve great results in physical health and general well-being. It also encourages a person to take charge of their own health and to make sure that they are taking all the necessary steps to stay healthy and energized. This includes eating a balanced diet, getting adequate exercise, and maintaining a regular sleep schedule. Overall, the King of Wands Tarot card signifies someone that is committed to bettering their health through personal responsibility and dedication.

Finances:

The King of Wands Tarot card symbolizes financial stability and success. This card encourages one to take charge of their financial well-being and to be proactive in managing their finances. This card advises that through hard work and dedication, one can create a positive financial future. It also suggests that having a positive attitude towards money and taking risks in order to gain financial rewards will pay off in the long run. The King of Wands Tarot card signifies that with a little bit of investment and hard work, one can achieve their financial goals.

Relationships:

The King of Wands Tarot card symbolizes commitment and leadership in relationships. It encourages one to take the initiative in relationships and to be the leader in a partnership. This card signifies a strong connection between two people, as well as the ability to communicate effectively and be open with one another. It encourages one to be honest and direct when interacting with their partner, as well as to be aware of their partner’s feelings and needs. The King of Wands Tarot card suggests that a relationship based on trust and understanding will be much more successful than one based on doubt and uncertainty.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 5 Of Cups Tarot card can suggest that one’s health is in a state of decline. This could be physical, mental or emotional as the card often denotes feelings of grief and regret. One may lack the motivation to take care of their bodily health, which could lead to physical symptoms and further feelings of despair. The card also hints at neglect of one’s own well-being, as one may feel as though there is no point in attempting to take care of themselves.

Finances  
The 5 Of Cups Tarot card can suggest a feeling of failure when it comes to finances. One may have experienced a loss of money due to poor investment decisions or bad luck. This could lead to feelings of regret, anxiety and despair. One might feel that financial stability is impossible, leading to a lack of focus or plan for the future. Additionally, one might feel abandoned or unsupported in terms of their financial struggles, as if no one is willing to help.

Relationships  
The 5 Of Cups Tarot card can also suggest a feeling of loneliness in relationships. This could be due to the loss of a loved one or a feeling of abandonment caused by a lack of support from family or friends. One might feel unloved or as if no one truly understands them or their struggles. This could lead to feelings of isolation and discouragement when it comes to forming meaningful connections. Furthermore, one may feel as though they cannot trust anyone with their innermost thoughts or feelings.
