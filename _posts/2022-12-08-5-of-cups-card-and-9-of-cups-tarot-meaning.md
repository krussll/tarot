A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Cups card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Cups card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Cups and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The 5 Of Cups tarot card combined with the 9 Of Cups tarot card is a powerful message that encourages you to not give up on yourself or your dreams. Although you may be facing a great deal of sadness, loss and regret, the promise of this card pairing is that your wishes can come true if you have the courage to keep going. It suggests that although you may feel as though you are unloved and abandoned, you have the power to manifest abundance in your life through hard work, determination and inner confidence. With the guidance of this card pairing, you can enjoy good health, success and inner peace.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Cups and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 5 of Cups is associated with physical health in several ways. It can refer to an emotional or mental health issue such as depression, anxiety or an addiction that is having a detrimental effect on the individual’s physical health. It can also refer to a physical health condition that is depleting energy, creating pain and affecting the individual’s overall wellbeing. In both cases, the 5 of Cups suggests that the individual should take the necessary steps to start healing, such as seeking professional help or making lifestyle changes.

Finances: The 5 of Cups symbolizes financial difficulty. It can represent the loss of money, financial mismanagement, or unexpected expenses. It can also indicate a struggle to make ends meet, or being stuck in an unstable financial situation. The card suggests that the individual should seek help to gain a better understanding of their financial situation and look for ways to make positive changes.

Relationships: The 5 of Cups symbolizes unresolved grief or disappointment in relationships. It can refer to a break-up, a betrayal, or a relationship gone bad. It can also refer to a fear of abandonment or the feeling of not being loved. The card suggests that the individual should face their feelings of grief and disappointment, move past the pain and make an effort to rebuild their trust in relationships.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is associated with wishes coming true, material abundance and good health. This card symbolizes a person’s inner strength, fulfillment, and an overall sense of contentment.

When it comes to health, The 9 of Cups Tarot card emphasizes the importance of taking care of oneself. It encourages manifestation and reminds us that we are in control of our own well-being. This card advises us to nurture our physical and mental health, tending to any issues that arise in order to achieve balance and optimum wellbeing.

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is also associated with financial wealth, implying that with the right mindset, hard work and commitment, one can achieve financial stability. This card encourages us to make sound decisions about our money, invest smartly and be watchful for opportunities that can bring rewards.

When it comes to relationships, The 9 of Cups Tarot card indicates a time of emotional growth and increased self-awareness. It encourages us to take an honest look at what we need and want from our relationships. This card reminds us that we are capable of forming strong connections with those around us, but it also warns us to be mindful of our boundaries and to not be afraid to set them.

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is a powerful symbol of joy and contentment, reminding us to appreciate what we have and to trust that our dreams are achievable if we strive for them. In terms of health, finances and relationships, this card encourages us to be positive and proactively work towards our goals, while also being open and honest with ourselves and those around us.
