However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does Justice and King Of Swords mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the King of Swords tarot card together mean that you should use your intentional leadership and strategic thought to achieve win-win solutions. Through careful consideration and analysis, you will be able to discover the truth behind any situation and make decisions that will benefit everyone involved. This combination of cards encourages a balanced and fair approach that is focused on creating positive outcomes. Ultimately, you will be able to make decisions that are both just and wise.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

The King of Swords tarot card symbolizes an intentional leader who is always looking for holistic strategies when it comes to their health. They are an independent thinker who has a keen interest in the workings of their body, and are highly focused on their physical and mental wellness. This card signifies a natural ability to be able to think ahead, calculate risks and make decisions in a timely fashion. This card encourages those with a King of Swords cardiovascular to focus on their physical health, as well as their emotional, mental and spiritual health as part of their overall strategy for optimal health. Taking the time to attend to their physical needs and also explore alternative ways of managing their stress, anxiety and depression is what this card is all about.

Finances

This tarot card speaks to a person’s ability to be calculative and shrewd when it comes to financial matters. They have a keen eye for identifying potential risks and taking the necessary steps to protect their financial resources. They are highly strategic in their investment and saving strategies and have a knack for being able to foresee possible opportunities. With the King of Swords at the forefront, a person can expect to sustain their financial resources over time as they have the ability to be able to actively participate in the management of their wealth.

Relationships

The King of Swords tarot encourages someone to be honest and have a clear understanding of their boundaries within relationships. This card prompts someone to take a look at how they interact with others and how deep the emotional connection is that they are willing to make. They are able to make sound decisions when it comes to who they would like to share themselves with, and are able to look at their relationships from a more objective viewpoint. This card is an indicator of someone who is able to remain emotionally detached and can keep the peace when it comes to relationship issues. They value active communication and clear, direct dialogue in order to ensure clarity and mutual understanding between themselves and the other person.
