Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The Temperance card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Temperance, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Temperance.

## Temperance And Career And Finances – An Overview

The Temperance tarot card is a reminder to maintain balance in our lives, particularly when it comes to health.

It encourages us to be patient and practice self-control when it comes to taking care of our bodies, as well as our mental and emotional well-being.

It also serves as a reminder to be mindful of the various choices we make regarding food, exercise, and other lifestyle habits that can affect our overall health.

Striking a balance between these various aspects of our lives can lead to greater harmony and wellbeing.

## Upright Meanings For Temperance And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The Temperance card in regards to my career and finances is a reminder to stay focused and patient.

It encourages me to maintain balance and harmony in my life and to use self-control when it comes to decisions related to my finances.

This card is a reminder to keep my goals in perspective and to be mindful of the importance of taking things one step at a time.

The Temperance card also encourages me to save for my future and to be aware of the importance of financial planning.

By maintaining a sense of balance, harmony, patience, and self-control, I can ensure that my career and finances are on track.

### What It Means For The Future

The Temperance card is indicative of a positive outlook in your future career and finances.

It suggests that with patience, balance, and self-control, you can achieve harmony and stability in your life.

You should take the time to create a budget and plan out your future goals, as this will help you gain control of your income and financial stability.

Additionally, this card suggests that you remain patient and keep an open mind when it comes to making decisions related to your career, as this will help you stay focused on the big picture.

With the Temperance card in your spread, you should be confident that you will achieve stability and success throughout your career.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Temperance tarot card is a reminder that, when it comes to career and finances, balance and moderation are key.

It encourages us to control our impulses and be patient and responsible in our decisions.

It reminds us that if we want to be successful, we need to take the time to think through our choices and make sure that we are making decisions with our long-term goals in mind.

For those close to us, The Temperance card suggests that they stay disciplined and vigilant when it comes to their financial decisions.

They may be tempted to make impulsive choices with their money, but The Temperance card reminds them to stay focused and commit to making sound financial decisions that will help them achieve their long-term goals.

## Reversed Meanings For Temperance And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When the Temperance card is reversed, it can often signify an imbalance in one’s career and financial life.

It can mean that you have been taking too many risks or acting impulsively, leading to instability and potential consequences.

You may be having difficulty managing your finances and making wise decisions.

Alternatively, the reversed Temperance card can indicate that you have been overly cautious and not taking enough risks to achieve success.

The reversed Temperance card can be a sign for you to be patient, practice moderation and balance, and set realistic goals when it comes to your career and finances.

You need to take a step back, assess the situation, and come up with a plan to move forward.

It is important to be mindful of risks and rewards, and to think through your decisions before taking action.

By taking the time to get organized and practice self-control, you can ensure that your career and finances are on the right track.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Temperance card is reversed, it can mean that there is a lack of balance and harmony in your future career and finances.

This could manifest as a feeling of being overwhelmed or frustrated with your current situation, or it could relate to a lack of patience and self-control when it comes to making decisions about your finances or career.

You may find that you are more impulsive with your decisions and not taking time to think things through, or you may be trying to take on too much at once without taking the time to focus on one thing at a time.

The reversed Temperance card could also mean that you need to be more mindful of your finances, or that you need to develop better financial habits.

It could indicate that you need to focus on the long-term stability of your career and finances, rather than short-term gains or losses.

You may need to take a step back and reassess your current situation before moving forward.

Taking a balanced and harmonious approach to your financial and career decisions can help ensure that you don’t make any rash or impulsive choices that could have a negative effect on your future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the Temperance card appears reversed in a tarot spread, it can be an indicator of financial instability and unrest in the lives of those close to me.

It may suggest that they are having difficulty finding balance and harmony in their professional and personal lives.

They may be feeling overwhelmed and uncertain about their financial prospects.

The reversed Temperance card can also point to a lack of patience and self-control when it comes to money, leading to impulsive decisions or overspending.

This can leave them feeling anxious and overwhelmed, leading to further financial instability.

It is important to remember that the reversed Temperance card does not necessarily signify a looming financial disaster, but rather an opportunity for those close to me to take a step back and re-evaluate their relationship with money.

They may need to seek help from a financial advisor in order to get their finances back on track, or to establish a budget and a plan for saving.

Above all, they should strive for balance and harmony in their finances.

With patience and self-control, they can create a stable financial future.
