A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Star card and Judgment card together in particular.

The The Star card and Judgment have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Star and Judgment mean together?

The Star tarot card and The Judgment tarot card combined symbolize the chance to find inner clarity and have faith that miracles can and will happen. They speak to a time of personal renewal and growth, a time to make a final decision based on your purpose and passions. It is a wake-up call to put yourself in the spotlight and reclaim what you want for your life, believing that the universe is conspiring in your favor and helping you to create something beautiful and meaningful. Take a leap of faith and trust that the outcome will be remarkable.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Star and Judgment mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Star and Judgment the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card is associated with hope, renewal, and faith, and these three qualities can be applied to health, finances, and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Star Tarot card can signify that any current health issues will be alleviated in the near future. It also indicates that an individual may experience an increase in their energy levels, and that any healing process should proceed smoothly with little to no complications. There could also be an element of faith and belief in one’s own self-healing powers that is necessary to find solace and well-being.

Regarding finances, The Star Tarot card encourages individuals to have faith and trust in the unpredictable nature of life and finances. It is a reminder that any financial hardships can be overcome, as long as an individual holds onto a sense of hope and optimism. This card can also be interpreted as a sign of abundance, suggesting that unexpected financial gains can be achieved with hard work and determination.

The Star Tarot card is also associated with a renewed sense of love and connection in relationships. It can signify that any conflicts or issues within relationships will soon be resolved in a peaceful manner. It encourages individuals to keep a positive outlook on their relationships and to make sure that they are enriching and filling their relationships with love and positivity. Furthermore, it also suggests that any new relationships should be given a chance as long as it is based on mutual respect and understanding.

Overall, The Star Tarot card is a reminder of hope, renewal, and faith which can be applied to one’s health, finances, and relationships. With these qualities, any current struggles may be alleviated and healed in the near future.

It’s time to take an honest look at your habits, decisions, and lifestyle and make a change for the better. It may also point to discovering your life’s purpose and living in line with that. The Judgment Tarot card encourages you to look after your physical self and live from a place of self-love.

This card could signal the need for a budget or a more sound plan for your spending and saving. Be sure to take the time to review your finances and come up with an actionable plan that works for you.

Relationships:  
The Judgment Tarot card can be a wake-up call when it comes to your relationships. It’s time to examine the relationships you’re in and be honest with yourself and the other person. Do they bring out the best in you? Or do they bring out the worst? The Judgment Tarot card encourages you to think long and hard about the relationships you are cultivating and make sure they are healthy. It may also be a sign to discover your life’s purpose and to create relationships that help fulfill that purpose.
