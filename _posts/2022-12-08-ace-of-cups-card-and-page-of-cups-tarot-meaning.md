However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

The Ace Of Cups Tarot card is a card associated with emotion and overflowing feelings. It is often interpreted as a sign of good fortune, joy, opportunity and love. In terms of health, it can signify that there is emotional balance, a time of healing and self-care and a general feeling of contentment and well-being.

In terms of finances, it can mean generosity, abundance, monetary luck and increasing wealth. The Ace Of Cups Tarot card signifies a period of financial prosperity and the possibility of investing in something stable and secure.

When it comes to relationships, the Ace Of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as the potential for a new start or the beginning of something special. This could mean a new baby or a developing relationship, a proposal or marriage being on the horizon. It symbolizes a time of unconditional love, emotional intimacy and spiritual connection. It can also mean that there is good luck and pleasant feelings in the relationship, such as mutual understanding and a deep connection.

All in all, the Ace Of Cups Tarot card means good fortune, emotional balance and unconditional love. In terms of health, finances and relationships, it signifies a period of healing, abundance and the potential for a lasting connection.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a healthy lifestyle, both physically and mentally. On the physical level, this card suggests that you take care of your body, including eating a balanced and nutritious diet and engaging in regular exercise for both physical and mental growth. It also encourages you to focus on self-care, such as getting enough rest, taking time for relaxation, and paying attention to how your body feels. On the mental level, this card encourages you to be extra mindful and aware of the signals your body is sending you. It also reminds you to be understanding and compassionate towards your body and to take time to understand what it needs.

Finances

The Page of Cups Tarot card suggests that it’s time to focus on understanding your finances and money management. This card encourages you to be realistic and honest with yourself when it comes to budgeting and spending. It also encourages you to think before you act and to be careful with investments. Asking for advice and getting help from trusted resources and mentors will be beneficial when it comes to financial decision-making. Additionally, putting aside some money for savings and emergencies is an important step to take for financial stability.

Relationships

The Page of Cups Tarot card encourages you to bring a greater level of understanding and communication to your relationships. It symbolizes a need for self-reflection and self-awareness when it comes to relationships. It also encourages you to be mindful of how your actions and words are affecting your relationships. This card suggests that it’s important to be open and honest with your partner and to deeply explore the emotions and feelings that exist between the two of you. Additionally, this card can represent a new level of love, so it’s important to show a gentler and more compassionate side to your relationships.
