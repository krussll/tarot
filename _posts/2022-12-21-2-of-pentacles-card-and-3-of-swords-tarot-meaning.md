A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of the 2 of Pentacles and the 3 of Swords tarot card can represent a difficult and confusing situation. The 2 of Pentacles suggests that you are dealing with a difficult decision that requires careful consideration of different options. The 3 of Swords suggests that you may be dealing with some form of heartache, possibly due to a difficult relationship. It is important to weigh your options carefully in this situation and make an informed decision. This combination can also refer to the need to make a difficult decision in a situation with high emotional stakes. Although it can be difficult, it is important to make sure you are making the best decision for yourself in the long run.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles mean?

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card implies two choices and the potential to balance and integrate them into a new perspective. In terms of health, this card speaks to incorporating healthy activities and making decisions that will promote and enhance wellness. This might include establishing a regular exercise routine, changing lifestyle habits, and/or seeking out preventative care. The 2 of Pentacles is a reminder that balance is key when it comes to maintaining and improving health.

When it comes to finances, the 2 of Pentacles imbues us with the necessary wisdom to make informed decisions. It speaks to multitasking, enjoying the process of weighing options in effort to make sound fiscal decisions. This could include finding a balance between spending and saving, making investments, and allocating funds in meaningful ways. The 2 of Pentacles reminds us to keep our eye on the prize, keeping our goals in clear sight as we create, craft and construct our financial future.

Relationships, too, can benefit from the wisdom of the 2 of Pentacles Tarot card. It speaks to the importance of balance and the need to understand and incorporate different perspectives into any relationship. Part of the process of achieving balance and creating relationships that are built to last involves careful and timely consideration of a variety of perspectives. The 2 of Pentacles won’t give us all the answers, but it does the remind us that all relationships require work, effort, and understanding.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, The 3 Of Swords Tarot card can mean more than just heartbreak and loss. It can be an indicator of a physical illness or injury that has caused significant pain and suffering. It can also be an indicator of a chronic health condition that has been difficult to manage, as well as the need for surgery. In any of these occasions, the 3 of Swords can be a warning to be mindful of one’s health and to seek medical assistance if necessary.

When it comes to finances, The 3 Of Swords Tarot card can be a warning of financial loss or hardship. It can indicate a period of difficulty when it comes to money or a pending financial crisis. This could be linked to the physical illness or injury previously mentioned, as medical costs could be immense, or it could be a sign of a job loss or other unforeseen financial circumstance.

From a relationship standpoint, The 3 Of Swords Tarot card can be a warning of divorce or the ending of a relationship, although it can also be a sign of separation or a lack of communication. This could be due to the financial hardships mentioned prior, or it could be a sign of a growing rift between two parties. In any case, it is a sign of sadness and heartbreak that could take time to heal.

No matter the circumstance, The 3 Of Swords can be a warning to take action and to be mindful of the potential risks of neglecting one’s health, financial wellbeing, and relationships. With the right attitude and determination, difficult times can be overcome and a more positive outlook can be gained.
