However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

You may have the support of family and friends encouraging you on your journey, providing you with love and contentment. Through the power of transformation, you can emerge into a new phase of your life feeling supported, loved and stronger than ever.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health:

The Death Tarot card can represent a period of change or transition in terms of health. It could point to a dramatic shift in physical or mental health related to a condition or circumstance. It could suggest that you are entering a new phase in your health journey, one that is accompanied by significant changes. This could be a time to focus on healing and rebuilding, as ending old patterns and habits can open up space for fresh energy and approach to health. This may take some time and effort to manifest, but the end result could bring about a much healthier and fulfilling lifestyle.

Finances:

The Death Tarot card could point to financial changes or transformations that are taking place. Endings may be necessary in order to simplify and improve financial circumstances. This could involve making hard decisions, such as selling off assets, changing investments, or cutting back on spending, in order to reinvest in a more sustainable future. It could also signify that opportunities for financial growth are available, whether through investment or a novel opportunity, as long as you are willing to take a risk and let go of what no longer serves you.

Relationships:

The Death Tarot card could point to change and transformation in your relationships. It could mean that some relationships may be entering a period of endings. These endings may be difficult, but necessary for the growth and betterment of both parties. Alternatively, it could mean that a relationship is shifting in a more positive direction, with opportunities for maturation and deepening. It could signify that you need to be willing to let go of what no longer serves you in order to move forward, whether this be a toxic relationship or a stagnant one. It could also mean that you are in the process of building a more fulfilling and supportive relationship with yourself.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

For health, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card symbolizes good mental health, physical well-being, and emotional balance. The 10 Of Cups is a card of completion, which can represent achieving a level of fullness, or a holistic approach to health. As a result, this card often indicates a feeling of satisfaction in regards to our well-being. Eating healthily, exercising regularly, and getting enough rest are all supported by the 10 Of Cups, as it suggests a connection between our physical bodies, the environment, and our emotional and spiritual states. The 10 Of Cups also indicates a sense of contentment and peace, which can lead to improved mental health and well-being.

In terms of finances, The 10 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a period of satisfaction, financial stability, and abundance. The 10 of Cups suggests a period of peace and security, as things are going smoothly and no major changes are expected. With the 10 of Cups, you can be relaxed and content knowing that your finances are secure, and you can focus on enjoying the fruits of your labor. This card can also indicate that you are able to attain financial success with the right mindset and attitude, and that you will eventually be rewarded for your hard work.

Relationships wise, The 10 Of Cups Tarot card suggests strong, loving relationships with family, friends, and romantic partners. This card is all about emotional connection, support and understanding. This card is a sign that you are being surrounded by people who love and care for you and who will always be there for you no matter what. The 10 Of Cups can also represent the feeling of contentment and satisfaction, as it is a sign that the relationships in your life are working out and you are surrounded by loving people.
