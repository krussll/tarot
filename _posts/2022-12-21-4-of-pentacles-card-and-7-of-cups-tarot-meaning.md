A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 4 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The 4 of Pentacles and the 7 of Cups together can tell us that you are caught between pursuing your dreams or making a commitment to the security of long-term wealth. You may feel greedy for both and can’t decide which one to focus on. You may find yourself focusing too much on accumulating more wealth, while postponing your pursuit of your dreams as a result. On the other hand, you may be also afraid of commitment and have too many options to choose from, making it difficult to decide which one to focus on. This card combination is suggesting that you should take a step back and decide on a direction that is right for you, rather than letting yourself to be torn between the two.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This card can symbolize an attitude of hoarding or extreme caution when it comes to using resources, and a fear of potential health risks or illnesses. You may be overly focused on yourself and ignore the general well-being of others, so it’s important to take some time to relax and consider the big picture.

Finances:

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card means that you are focused on long-term security when it comes to your finances. You may be focused on making sure that you have a steady stream of income and are protecting your money, or you may be striving to preserve your wealth and attain financial security. You could be putting too much emphasis on material objects and not enough on the emotional and relational aspects of your life.

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card can be a warning about your relationships, as you may be too focused on security. You may be putting too much pressure on yourself to be perfect or to maintain a certain kind of control over your emotions and your relationships. This card can also represent a fear of vulnerability and a reluctance to open up to others, or a fear of commitment and stability with another person. It’s important to open up and share your feelings in order to deepen the connection and foster trust in your relationships.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health: The message of The 7 Of Cups Tarot card when it comes to health is that you need to take a step back and evaluate what is really important for your well-being. This card can signify that you are spending too much time indulging in things that might be detrimental to your health, whether it is unhealthy habits or poor diet. It is important to step back and look at the bigger picture, as it can be easy to get caught up in the moment to make unhealthy decisions.

Finances: The 7 Of Cups Tarot card in regards to finances suggests that you might be overconfident and taking too many risks. You might be taking on too many financial commitments that you cannot keep up with, or spending too much money on unnecessary indulgences. This card cautions against taking too many risks, or overreaching your financial capabilities.

Relationships: The 7 Of Cups Tarot card in regards to relationships warns against having too many choices and commitment issues. You might be having difficulty in settling down and committing to someone, as you feel there is always something better out there. This card suggests that you should take a step back, evaluate your options and make a choice. Don’t rush into something without taking the time to consider all of your options.
