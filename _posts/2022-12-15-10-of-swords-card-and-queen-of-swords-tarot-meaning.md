## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords tarot card symbolizes a difficult and painful ending to a physical health issue that has been difficult to manage. This card suggests that all of the efforts to try to overcome whatever physical ailment or condition that you may have been struggling with may now have reached rock-bottom. It can also suggest that a long-term health issue has finally come to its inevitable conclusion, either in the form of a recovery or an unfortunate worsening of the condition. It is a reminder that there will be a period of rest and recovery needed before any real healing can take place.

Finances:

The 10 of Swords tarot card can symbolize a financial situation that has reached its darkest hour. This card suggests that you may have exhausted all possible resources in an effort to regain financial stability. It can also symbolize a debt situation that has reached the point of no return, where attempts to recover are becoming increasingly more difficult to manage. It can also suggest that an unfortunate financial setback has forced you to hit rock bottom and that you will have to make difficult decisions in order to recover.

Relationships:

The 10 of Swords tarot card can symbolize a relationship ending on a bitter and painful note. This card suggests that whatever conflict or issue that you may have had with a significant other or other important person in your life has now reached a point of no return. It can also suggest that all attempts at reconciliation have been to no avail, and that whatever relationship was present has now come to an end. It is a reminder to take one day at a time and to allow yourself time to heal from the wounds and move on from the painful situation.

It is important to be transparent and set healthy boundaries when it comes to your finances, and also to create a budget and plan that fits your current financial situation. The Queen Of Swords also invites you to evaluate any financial goals or plans you may have and make sure they are realistic and achievable.

In terms of relationships, The Queen Of Swords Tarot card encourages you to be totally honest and open with your partners.
