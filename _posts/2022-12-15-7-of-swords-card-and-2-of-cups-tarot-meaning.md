A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Swords card and 2 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Swords card and 2 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean together?

The 7 of Swords tarot card and the 2 of Cups tarot card is a stark contrast to one another. The 7 of Swords is a warning to be aware of deceit, betrayal and running away from situations. Whereas, The 2 of Cups speaks of a coming romantic union, perfect partnerships and promises of marriage. Together, these cards tell of a situation where a union on the cusp of forming may be impeded by the threat of dishonesty and betrayal. One must be wary of potential pitfalls and go into such a relationship with their eyes wide open, ready to balance the pros and cons. A period of testing for trust may be necessary before any commitment is made, yet, if it is meant to be, it will be worth facing the challenge of the 7 of Swords.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The 7 Of Swords tarot card and The 2 Of Cups tarot card appear together in your love life, it can signify a relationship under duress. On one hand, there is a need to heal and move forward in partnership and understanding. On the other hand, there is a risk of not being fulfullied as the two of you explore a partnership whether in marriage or love.

It is possible that there may have been some betrayal or dishonesty in the relationship that has caused a hurt that needs to be addressed and healed before the two of you can establish a meaningful union. It could also mean that one of you has made the decision to run away or withdraw, whether physically or emotionally, which could severly damage the relationship dynamic.

However, this card combination also shows potential to come out of the darkness and move towards a relationship of harmony and balance. The 2 Of Cups tarot card signifies the potential of a romantic love and connection, while The 7 Of Swords card can signify the need to enter into this relationship with caution and understanding. The two of you will have to work through any underlying issues of distrust and betrayal to truly embrace each other and move forward.

Ultimately, this card combination illustrates the power of developing a relationship together, although it may take some effort and a lot of communication to get there. As long as you can keep an open mind and look for the best in each other, you can find the connection that will allow you to stay together in love and equality.

### What does 7 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When The 7 Of Swords tarot card and The 2 Of Cups tarot card appear together in a reading, they bring a warning that financial security could be at risk. This could be through theft, betrayal, dishonesty, or simply running away from a situation. As such, it is important to be aware of any potentially deceitful elements in your finances and job, and to be wary of any offers made by strangers.

The 7 Of Swords also speaks to hidden challenges and the need for extra caution when it comes to financial decisions and matters. This card can signify the need to keep close counsel when it comes to money and job opportunities, and to look out for any shady or questionable dealings. This card warns against risking too much in a gamble and advises to look out for any situation that could lead to unexpected losses.

Meanwhile, The 2 Of Cups brings a message of partnership and collaboration, particularly when it comes to career-related matters. This card not only speaks of finding new professional opportunities, but also forging genuine and meaningful partnerships that could help to advance your career. It is important to recognize the potential of a business alliance or collaboration and to act on any potential chance to make a positive impact in your finances and job.

When The 7 Of Swords and The 2 Of Cups appear together, it is an important reminder to be mindful of your financial security and to look for genuine opportunities of collaboration. With an attentive and wise approach, these cards indicate that these areas of life can be improved with openness and cooperation.

### What does 7 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean together for your health?

When it comes to a health reading, the combination of The 7 Of Swords and The 2 Of Cups tarot cards can be interpreted as an indication that ongoing health issues are a result of a lack of trust. This may manifest as betrayal or a lack of communication between partners in a relationship. Both of these cards indicate that it may be necessary to step back from the situation and assess what needs to be done to ensure an honest and open dialogue.

The 7 Of Swords can also represent a fear of commitment, which can be a barrier to achieving optimal health. A fear of betrayal can cause a person to hold back from being vulnerable with a partner, leading to a sense of isolation. Alternatively, it can represent a tendency to cut and run to avoid difficult or uncomfortable conversations. This can also be damaging to one’s health, as it can create feelings of insecurity and alienation.

The 2 Of Cups is a reminder that resilience is key to long-term health. It encourages an honest, committed relationship that can provide a safe foundation for communication and growth. It indicates that true love and mutual support will help to increase feelings of security and bolster the capacity to endure difficult times.

By recognizing and understanding the meanings of The 7 Of Swords and The 2 Of Cups tarot cards, it is possible to identify ways to create healthier interpersonal relationships. This, in turn, will help to create an environment that is more conducive to physical and emotional health. Working toward strong, honest, and supportive relationships is essential for maintaining long-term health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 7 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card often reflects the idea of betrayal, overt or covert dishonesty, and running away from difficult situations. In terms of health, this card can symbolize the need to be more honest with yourself and others about what’s going on. There may be a fear of letting others know the truth, or struggles to take care of your own mental and physical health while trying to abide by external expectations. It can also suggest some kind of illness which is kept secret, either out of fear of judgement or a lack of understanding of what is happening. In terms of finances, this card suggests a need to be more mindful of how you are spending your money. There may be a need to process difficult money conversations and be honest about what you can and can’t afford. It can also indicate there is a sense of running away from financial responsibilities as a form of escape. In terms of relationships, this card can represent a need to be more honest about your feelings, or for an open and honest dialogue about what is going on. You may be trying to hide something that can no longer be kept secret. Alternatively, the card may highlight the importance of taking responsibility for your own actions in relationships, and not running away from the conversations which need to be had.

## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

For health, The 2 Of Cups Tarot card is a sign of balance and contentment. This card can represent physical and mental wellbeing, with the two cups symbolizing inner harmony and peace. You may find that taking time for yourself, nurturing yourself with healthy activities and eating a balanced diet can bring out the best in you.

For finances, The 2 Of Cups Tarot card suggests the coming together of resources in order to create something far more valuable. This could mean pooling money together with a partner to purchase something more meaningful, investing in a business, or working together to save money over time.

For relationships, The 2 Of Cups Tarot card is an excellent sign of connection and mutual understanding. This card indicates the merging of two parties in order to create something strong and stable. In a romantic partnership, the Two of Cups suggests a relationship based on mutual respect and communication, and can also indicate an engagement or marriage.

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw 7 Of Swords and 2 Of Cups has helped you.
