A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 5 Of Wands tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card mean that life is a mix of obstacles and celebrations. When facing a challenge or rivalry, you can draw strength from your circle of supportive friends. Together, the two cards show that life is a mixture of competition and joy, of effort and love. Life is often a combination of hard work and play, of striving and achievement. When you have a solid circle of friends, you can use their support to help you face any obstacle. Celebrate the successes you have while keeping an eye out for potential rivals.

The 5 Of Wands encourages taking on these challenges with a sense of play, rather than a sense of competition.

Though disagreements may happen, it is important to maintain a spirit of friendship and celebrate each other’s successes. The 3 Of Cups suggests that relying on the support of a strong network of close friends, family, and loved ones can help to keep the relationship healthy and strong.

The combination of The 5 Of Wands and The 3 Of Cups speaks to the importance of balance in any relationship. Keeping in mind that love isn’t just about passion and romance, but about understanding and communication, can help both partners maintain a healthy relationship. Though there may be times of challenge and rivalry, there should always be times of celebration and joy.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 5 of Wands Tarot card indicates that there may be some challenges related to our physical health. It may mean that we need to be more aware of our bodies and take steps to prevent illness. It may also be a sign that we need to be more active and engage in some form of physical competition or sport, either individually or with others. This could be a way of maintaining our overall health and keeping our bodies in shape.

Finances: The 5 of Wands Tarot card can signify there will be obstacles and challenges to overcome in relation to our finances. It can serve as a warning to us to stay vigilant and conscious of our financial situation. It may be a sign to be frugal and save our money to protect our financial stability. If we are feeling overly ambitious, this card may be telling us to stay within our limits and approach our finances in a balanced and constructive way.

Relationships: The 5 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the presence of competition in our relationships. Whether it be in our friendships, our romantic relationships, or even within our family dynamic, it could mean that there is a degree of competitiveness present which needs to be managed in a constructive manner. It may be a sign to give in to our natural rivalry and use it to our advantage to grow and develop in our relationships. We should remember to be conscious that the competition is healthy, and ensure that it doesn’t create any tension or animosity within a relationship.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

For Health: The 3 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes good health, joy, and an abundance of physical, mental and emotional balance. This card is a representation of the trinity as well as the joys and challenges associated with health. On a physical level, the 3 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a balanced diet, plenty of physical exercise, and general well-being. On an emotional level, it may suggest a sense of security, joy, and support from those around you who can help you to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

For Finances: The 3 of Cups Tarot card suggests financial stability and security, as well as the potential for growth. This card can represent good luck and support in financial matters, allowing for the stability and security necessary to stay on top of your finances and achieve success in wealth. It may indicate the opportunity for financial investments, or the need to plan and be prudent with money.

For Relationships: The 3 of Cups Tarot card signifies friendship, trust, and mutual respect. This card indicates that a relationship is founded on the mutual love, respect, and support of those involved. This card may also signify a strong sense of community and connection, with a strong circle of support coming in from friends and family. It can be a sign of celebration, joy, and deep connections that a person has with those important to them.
