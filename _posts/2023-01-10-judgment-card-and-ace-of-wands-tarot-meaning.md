A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and Ace Of Wands card together in particular.

The Judgment card and Ace Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and Ace Of Wands mean together?

The Judgment tarot card can be seen as a call to examine your life and gain clarity on your purpose. It is often seen as a wake-up call, asking you to make the decision to move towards your own destiny. On the other hand, The Ace of Wands represents the urge to create and start something new, to flow with inspiration and passion. When the two cards are together, this could mean a powerful new project in which you have the drive, focus and creative energy to bring it to life. This could be an opportunity to start anew and create something meaningful in alignment with your life purpose.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Judgment and Ace Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Judgment and Ace Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

For Health:

The Judgment Tarot card signifies clarity and a wake-up call when it comes to health. This card speaks to the need to assess your lifestyle and habits, and it signifies a time to come to terms with how those habits are impacting your health. It may be time to draw a line in the sand and make a tough decision, like switching to a healthier diet, getting more exercise, or taking time for self-care. It can also symbolize the need for forgiveness and releasing old grudges. If you’ve been holding onto anger, resentment, or negative emotions, Judgment prompts you to consider letting go of them.

For Finances:

The Judgment Tarot card can signify a financial wake-up call and a time to consider the long-term consequences of your financial decisions. This card indicates a need for clarity and decisive action. It may be time to make a budget or get out of debt. It may also be a sign that you need to be more mindful of how you’re spending money and how it’s impacting your stability. Judgment can also signify a time to assess investments and make changes to your financial plan.

For Relationships:

The Judgment Tarot card suggests that it’s time to come to terms with the relationship’s status, either by taking decisive action or by releasing the relationship altogether. It may be a sign that you need to have an honest and difficult conversation with your partner or that you need to let go and move on. If you have been struggling to forgive someone for something they did, Judgment reminds you that holding onto grudges and anger will only hurt you. It may be time to forgive and move on.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

The Ace of Wands Tarot card means something different for health, finances and relationships. In terms of health, this card implies that you should seize new opportunities for self-care, such as starting an exercise or diet program. It also indicates that taking up a new hobby or activity can be beneficial for your physical and mental well-being.

When it comes to finances, the Ace of Wands Tarot card can signify the initiation of a new business venture or investment opportunity. It suggests that now may be the ideal time to take some sort of financial risk and that this could yield great rewards. It also encourages you to develop creative strategies for increasing your income.

As for relationships, the Ace of Wands Tarot card indicates that you should be open to the possibilities of meeting someone new or finally taking the plunge and committing to a current partner. If you’ve been considering making a major change in your partnership, now is the time to do it. The Ace of Wands Tarot card also suggests that new friendships could also be forming and that they can bring you joy, support and growth.
