A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The 7 of Wands tarot card and the 8 of Swords tarot card together can often mean a need for self-defense from outside forces as well as restricting oneself in order to protect against potential competition. Even though it may feel like a burden to be so wary of potential opponents, this self-defensive mentality is important to keep in mind so that one can remain safe and secure in times of danger and uncertainty. At times, it may be necessary to cut oneself off from the world in order to remain safe, even if it means being isolated or imprisoned in a metaphorical sense. The 7 of Wands and the 8 of Swords urge one to take all necessary precautions in order to protect themselves and survive any potential threats.

### What does 7 Of Wands and 8 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The 7 Of Wands tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card together show a need to protect yourself in a relationship. Your love life is about to come under attack from outside sources – such as competition from other suitors – and the 7 Of Wands encourages you to stand your ground and defend against any and all intrusions. This card is a reminder that you have the power to protect and defend your relationship, and the strength within to do so.

The 8 Of Swords, however, suggests that you have limited yourself in some way in your relationship. Perhaps you have placed boundaries on yourself and perhaps you feel confined, as if you are locked in some way and unable to move forward. This card is a reminder that you can only control yourself, and not the actions of others. Releasing yourself from these restrictions will give you the freedom to find a deeper, more meaningful level of connection.

In conclusion, these two cards together suggest that in your love life, self-defense is an important element. Protect your relationship from outside forces and then release yourself from any self-imposed limitations. Doing so will help you and your partner move beyond the confines of your current relationship and create a deeper bond.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder that we need to look out for ourselves and our own health. It encourages us to be proactive in taking preventative measures to guard against physical and mental illness, as well as looking out for our overall wellbeing. Taking steps such as getting regular check-ups, eating nutritious food, exercising and getting good rest are all ways that we can protect our health and well-being.

Finances: The 7 of Wands Tarot card can also be taken as a sign of protection for our finances. It reminds us to be aware of our own financial situation and take steps to protect our money and investments. This could include understanding our budget and savings, creating financial plans, budgeting and being mindful of our spending habits.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, the 7 of Wands Tarot card speaks to the need for self-defense and protection. It reminds us to be mindful of our boundaries and to ensure that we are not taken advantage of in any way. We need to be aware of how we are communicating with others and not be afraid to set our expectations for any kind of relationships that we have, both personal and professional. Ultimately, protecting ourselves is key to maintaining healthy relationships with others.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can signify self-imposed restrictions, isolation, and imprisonment. When the 8 Of Swords card appears in a reading, it can indicate a feeling of limitation and being held back in a certain aspect of life. The area in question could be physical, emotional, mental or financial, and the card can suggest that a person feels stuck in an area of their life and unable to move forwards.

When it comes to health, the 8 Of Swords Tarot card can signify holding back on a course of action or treatment out of fear, despite evidence suggesting that it could be helpful. It can also suggest that a person is over-restricting themselves and consequently not receiving the necessary nutrition. There are also emotional and mental limits placed, such as not allowing oneself to take risks and instead helping to stay status quo, no matter how unhealthy it is, or not allowing yourself to feel and experience emotions.

In terms of finances, the 8 Of Swords can represent feeling limited and unable to increase one’s wealth due to too many restrictions placed upon oneself. A person could be avoiding taking risks for fear of failure, or hanging onto possessions and money out of fear of lack and scarcity.

When it comes to relationships, the 8 Of Swords might suggest that a person is restricting their feelings and emotions and not allowing themselves to be open and vulnerable with someone. It can also suggest that a person is in too much of a rush and is pushing for a relationship to take a certain path too quickly and not allowing it to unfold naturally. They may also be too closed off and unwilling to allow themselves to truly let someone in, leading to potential problems in the relationship.
