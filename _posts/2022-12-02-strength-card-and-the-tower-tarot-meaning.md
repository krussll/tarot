## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Strength mean?

Health: The Strength Tarot card is a reminder to take care of yourself from the inside out. It encourages you to build up your physical and mental strength, giving you the courage and confidence to take on any challenge. It urges you to be mindful of your own health, to take time to nourish your body and mind. By finding balance within yourself and having faith in yourself, you can create a strong foundation that will help to sustain you through times of difficulty.

Finances: The Strength Tarot card is a reminder to be mindful of your financial decisions. It encourages you to build a solid financial foundation, to plan ahead and to seek advice when needed. It also encourages you to be wise with your money, to be mindful of where it is going and to take constructive steps to improve your finances. By investing in yourself and being aware of your financial situation, you can create a strong financial position that will help you to be more secure in the future.

Relationships: The Strength Tarot card highlights the importance of self-love and mutual respect in all relationships. It encourages you to nurture your relationships and to show your commitment to them by treating them with unconditional love. It also encourages you to build a strong inner foundation, to practice self-care and to be your authentic self, so that you can bring that same authenticity to your relationships. By focusing on cultivating a healthy balance between yourself and others, The Strength Tarot card encourages you to create strong, lasting relationships.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card generally symbolizes unwanted and sometimes catastrophic changes. In terms of health, this card often indicates the sudden onset of an unexpected illness, the unfortunate side effects of a medical treatment, or a tragic accident. It can be a warning sign of an unhealthy lifestyle, such as an addiction, or even a warning sign about a life-threatening condition such as cancer. The message behind The Tower Tarot card is that if we fail to take care of ourselves and take the necessary preventative measures, our health will suffer.

In terms of finances, the Tower Tarot card indicates financial ruin, bankruptcy and disastrous investments. It is a warning sign that our financial stability may be at risk if we do not make careful decisions, or if we take huge financial risks without proper forethought. This card can also indicate sudden losses due to natural disasters, or even the unexpected collapse of companies or markets.

In terms of relationships, The Tower Tarot card can indicate a sudden and catastrophic upheaval. It can indicate that a relationship is falling apart, or that a particular dynamic is no longer healthy. It can be a warning sign that a relationship is on the brink of destruction, and that it is necessary to take action to avoid disaster. This card can also indicate that it is time to let go of someone who is no longer beneficial to our lives.
