A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Justice card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The Justice tarot card and 10 of Swords tarot card together symbolize the idea that all actions have consequences, and the truth will eventually come to light. The Justice card reminds us that the consequences of our actions should be fair, that there are win-win solutions for any situation. The 10 of Swords card, however, serves as a warning that when our actions are not in line with truth and justice, we will eventually face a difficult and complete ending. This can represent any type of ending, from financial ruin to the dissolution of relationships. Ultimately, the Justice tarot card encourages us to always strive for truth, justice, and fairness, so that when our actions inevitably come to an end, we will not be left with the deep pain of rock bottom.

### What does Justice and 10 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When the Justice tarot card is combined with the 10 of Swords tarot card, it can signify a difficult and painful ending in matters of the heart. The 10 of Swords suggests that whatever has gone on in your love life is coming to a definitive finish. This can be a hard concept to come to terms with, but it is necessary. This ending signals a major life shift, where you can start fresh with a clearer sense of what you want.

The Justice tarot card suggests that you make sure that the conclusion to this chapter in your life is fair and just. This means taking responsibility for your own part in whatever has happened and striving for win-win solutions if there is more than one party involved. All issues need to be dealt with in the most honest and open way possible so that the truth comes out.

Unfortunately, the combination of the Justice tarot card and the 10 of Swords tarot card suggest that it is necessary for you to experience this painful ending in order for you to move forward and have new, positive experiences in love. However, this ending can bring closure and a resetting of the expectations you have for your love life. It can be difficult, but ultimately it is necessary for the benefit of your own wellbeing. As a result, this ending can be seen as a kind of release, where you are free to create a more authentic, meaningful and satisfying relationship in the future.

### What does Justice and 10 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When the Justice tarot card and the 10 of Swords tarot card are paired together, it can represent a major change in one’s financial and employment situation. This could be a “win-win” situation where a difficult decision is made, one that might seem painful but ultimately leads to growth.

In this pairing, it’s likely that the 10 of swords tarot card is representing a time of endings—a difficult time when you have to make a hard choice in order to move forward. This could be the decision to quit a job, take a pay cut, or even cut back on unnecessary spending. It’s likely that whatever decision you make is accompanied by a feeling of sadness, but it can also be the right choice for you in the long run.

At the same time, the Justice tarot card indicates that cause and effect will be at work here. That is, the consequences of your decisions will have an impact on your financial and job situation. Whatever choice you make could be beneficial in the long run, as it could lead to greater financial stability and even more opportunities in the future. It’s possible that this situation could require you to make a difficult decision, but it could be the best one for you in the grand scheme of things.

In conclusion, when the Justice tarot card and the 10 of Swords tarot card are paired together, the message is to be willing to make a difficult decision in order to find financial and job stability in the long run. This could involve cutting back on spending, taking a pay cut, or even quitting a job, but ultimately it could be what’s best for you in the end.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 10 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 10 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

Health: The Justice Tarot card provides guidance for individuals to make decisions for the best health outcome for all parties. It encourages individuals to assess how the decisions they make affect their own health, as well as those around them. It is important for individuals to be honest in their choices and to weigh their options carefully. This means that individuals should strive for win-win solutions where everyone comes out ahead. By recognizing the importance of respect, fairness, and mutual benefit when making decisions related to health, individuals can make sound decisions that will be beneficial to everyone in the long-term.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card provides insight when making financial decisions. It suggests that individuals should act with integrity and make sure that their actions are fair and beneficial for all concerned. It encourages individuals to assess the consequences of their actions and seek out solutions that are mutually beneficial. This could mean taking time to consider all options, while keeping your values and objectives in mind, and avoiding making decisions based solely on greed or impulsiveness.

Relationships: In relationships, The Justice Tarot card encourages individuals to be honest and forthcoming in their interactions. It emphasises the need for both parties to apply sound judgement and be mindful of their words and actions, as these can have far-reaching consequences. This means that individuals should strive for understanding and respect, and in turn, be respected for their own views, values and beliefs. By embodying fairness and integrity, relationships can become stronger and more harmonious.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

In looking at The 10 of Swords Tarot card, it is largely interpreted as the ultimate representation of despair, failure, and sorrow. For health, The 10 of Swords Tarot card often symbolizes health problems that feel insurmountable, a chronic illness, or a health crisis that seems impossible to overcome. Although it is bleak, the message of The 10 of Swords Tarot card is that we must learn to cope with the pain and sadness of our ill health to ultimately reach a place of healing and recovery.

For finances, The 10 of Swords Tarot card can be interpreted as a time of financial ruin, when all resources and strategies to get out of debt have been exhausted. This card suggests that the querent must come to terms with the difficult situation they have created, look internally to accept responsibility, and find creative solutions to improve the financial situation.

In relationships, The 10 of Swords Tarot card often symbolizes the end of a relationship and the unavoidable pain that comes along with it. This card can mean a separation due to irreconcilable differences or a dead-end relationship that has reached its end. For those in a troubled relationship, The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests that it is time to accept the situation for what it is and take decisive action to either mend it or move on.
