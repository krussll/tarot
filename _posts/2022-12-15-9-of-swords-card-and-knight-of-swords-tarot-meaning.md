## What does 9 Of Swords and Knight Of Swords mean together?

When looking at The 9 of Swords tarot card and The Knight of Swords tarot card together, it is important to recognize the meaning of both cards and how they interact together. The 9 of Swords tarot card speaks to a sense of dread and anxiety, a period of intense grief and depression. The Knight of Swords tarot card on the other hand speaks to a person who is fast-talking and may be advocating for a lifestyle change. Taken together, this can mean that in your current state of intense grief and depression, a person is quickly talking you into making a drastic lifestyle change. This could be to distract you from your pain and urge you to take a different path. It is important to take this suggestion with a grain of salt and remember that it is coming from a place of intense emotion and should be carefully considered before moving forward.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Swords and Knight Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Swords and Knight Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is associated with extreme stress, insomnia, nightmares, and mental and physical exhaustion. It can be a sign that your health is suffering due to overwhelming worry and fear. It may indicate an ongoing battle with a health condition such as a chronic illness or even that you are at risk of developing a mental health issue. The card can be a warning sign to take care of yourself and seek proper medical treatment to address any physical or mental symptoms you are experiencing.

Finances

The 9 of Swords Tarot card can be seen as a warning to take stock of your financial situation. This card can be a sign of financial insecurity or instability, especially when it comes to long-term plans. You may be struggling to make ends meet or be overspending in a desperate attempt to cope with your worries. It is important to take an honest look at your finances and develop a plan that works for both the short and long term.

Relationships

The 9 of Swords Tarot card can represent feelings of loneliness and despair, as if your current relationships are not enough to help you cope with your worries and pain. It can be a sign to take time to introspect and figure out why you are feeling so isolated. Take the time to grow closer to those you love by connecting with them on a deeper level. This card may also be a warning to reflect on how your anxieties and fears are impacting your relationships and look for ways to build understanding, compassion and trust.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Knight of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a fast-paced lifestyle that can lead to health problems. The card can suggest a need for changes in lifestyle to address potential health issues. It can urge one to move quickly to address any potential health issues and to pay attention to signs of ill health that may be quickly developing. Additionally, the card can serve as a sign to take physical action and exercise in order to stay fit and healthy.

Finances  
The Knight Of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a fast-paced lifestyle that can lead to financial problems. The card can warn of too much risk-taking and a disregard for the consequences. It can urge one to make wise financial decisions and to be more aware of their spending. It also suggests that one should take time to really assess any possible financial investments and to consider the potential risks and rewards of any financial decisions.

Relationships  
The Knight Of Swords Tarot card can symbolize a fast-paced lifestyle that can lead to strained relationships. The card can warn that one should take time to be more mindful of their relationships and to take the time to communicate and express their feelings openly. The card can urge one to be honest and to take responsibility for their actions and to communicate clearly with those around them in order to foster strong and meaningful relationships.
