A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean together?

When I look at the combination of the 6 of Wands and the 6 of Cups cards, I am reminded of the joys of achieving victory and learning to appreciate the simple pleasures of life. The 6 of Wands is a card of success and recognition, and the 6 of Cups is a reminder of the nostalgic moments from our past, when we made can make beautiful memories with our loved ones. Taken together, the 6 of Wands and the 6 of Cups suggest that we should strive for our goals and recognize our successes, but also take time for the simple joys of life, like reunions and gifts. They remind us that it’s important to take a break every now and then and recognize the beauty and love in life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 6 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 6 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 6 of Wands tarot card can be interpreted as a sign of health-related success. It speaks of reaching a new level of wellness and balance through healthy lifestyle choices such as exercising, eating right and getting enough rest. It can also signify the completion of a medical treatment, the successful recovery from an illness or even the achievement of a new level of fitness. In its negative form, it can indicate blocked energy and the need to take action in order to restore balance.

Finances  
The 6 of Wands tarot card can represent a great financial success or an unexpected reward. It can indicate winning a large sum of money from a lottery or other gambling activity, as well as receiving a large windfall from an inheritance, tax refund or other unexpected source. In its negative form it can suggest financial difficulties or barriers, and the need to take action in order to improve one’s financial situation.

Relationships  
The 6 of Wands tarot card can be seen as a sign of recognition in relationships. It can mean being acknowledged for a kind gesture, achieving a milestone together or receiving a public show of appreciation. It can also signify a successful collaboration or a job well done. In its negative form it can point to a lack of recognition or a feeling of being taken for granted. It can indicate a need to take initiative, and take action in order to move the relationship forward.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Six of Cups Tarot card represents a period of health and wellness that is rooted in nostalgia. This card serves as a reminder to take a step back and reconnect with the things that gave us comfort and joy in the past. Whether it’s engaging in the activities you used to do as a child, giving yourself the gift of quality rest, or trying out a new wellness routine — whatever you decide to do, make sure it’s something you truly enjoy. Taking a break from the hustle and bustle of life and bringing back the joy of your childhood can do wonders for your emotional wellbeing.

Finances:  
The Six of Cups Tarot card is all about finding balance in our finances. It encourages us to think about what areas of our finances require the most attention and to take an introspective look into our spending habits. It’s important to prioritize our needs versus our wants and to be honest with ourselves about our financial goals. With the Six of Cups, it’s vital to find a balance between spending and saving. You may have to give up some luxuries in order to secure a solid financial foundation, but that doesn’t mean you can’t enjoy life. Spend responsibly, without sacrificing the joy of giving yourself a gift every once in a while.

Relationships:  
The Six of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to nurture relationships and to be open to new ones. It encourages us to be receptive to the people who bring joy into our lives, both old and new. As this card has an element of nostalgia, we should take the time to reconnect with the people who have been in our lives for a long time. These relationships can offer us a sense of security and companionship that can be invaluable for our emotional wellbeing. The Six of Cups also reminds us to look for new relationships and opportunities that can enrich our lives and help us grow. Enjoy the feeling of nostalgia as you reconnect with old friends, and open yourself up to the new possibilities that await you.
