Tarot cards are big business in the New Age world. Whether you have a standard deck or one of the numerous specialty versions, there’s no denying that tarot has become one of the most popular methods of divination and personal insight.

Beside being a fun pastime, tarot has many benefits. It can be used to gain new insights into your life, uncover hidden negative emotions, or even as a form of meditation to bring clarity and peace. But how do we know if it’s an accurate reflection of our inner self?

There are many who believe that using your own tarot cards is bad luck or brings negative energy into your life. Is this true? Let’s take a look at some common myths about tarot so you can practice with confidence.

## Is it bad to read your own tarot cards?

There are no hard and fast rules when it comes to reading the cards. The only rule of thumb is to abide by your own intuition and trust your inner voice. If using your own deck makes you feel uncomfortable or bring you bad vibes, then you should choose another deck.

What’s more, the idea that your cards have to be “new” or “fresh” is a modern invention. Unless you’ve been reading your cards since they were made, they’ve already been handled by many people before you.

This doesn’t mean that you have to practice with your own deck, but you should avoid reading other people’s cards if possible.

## It’s bad luck to read the same card twice

This is one of the most common tarot myths that people believe. Some believe that you shouldn’t reread the same card, even if it is a different position. Others believe that you shouldn’t read the same card again unless you shuffle the deck.

The truth is that there is no hard and fast rule about rereading the same card. Whether you shuffle the deck or leave it as it is, it is really up to you.

However, if you don’t shuffle the deck and read the same card twice, it is likely that you will draw a different meaning from the card. If you want to reread the same card, there is no reason to feel bad about it.

Just make sure that it makes sense in the context of your reading. You might be rereading the card to make a point more clear or because you need to think more about the card.

## Tarot cards are always right

Tarot cards aren’t always right, but they do have the potential to reflect your situation. They are a tool that can be used to help you reflect on your life, feelings, and relationships.

When used as a method of divination, they can also help you to make decisions easier. While some of us have a natural ability to read the cards, most people learn how to read tarot cards by practising.

It takes time and patience to fully understand the cards and how they work. It’s important to note that tarot cards reflect your current life situation. If you are feeling emotional or weak, the card you draw may not fully represent your situation.

If this happens, you may want to wait until you are in a better state of mind before reading the cards again.

## The deck is only a tool

The many benefits of reading the cards are often overlooked. When you read the cards, you are reflecting on your feelings and looking at your life situation. This helps you to understand what is holding you back or causing you stress and anxiety.

You can also use the cards to help you make decisions and plan out your future. But what if you have a specific question in mind? Will the deck provide an accurate answer? Although tarot cards can be used to answer specific questions, they may not provide the answer you are looking for.

When reading tarot cards, you are not attempting to control the situation or see what will happen. Instead, you are looking at the potential outcomes and identifying your solution. You may also want to consider reading the cards for somebody else.

## The deck will reveal your future

While it is true that the cards will reveal your current situation, it is unlikely that they will reveal your future.

Reading the cards can help you to identify the best course of action to take in your life. It will also help you to identify negative emotions that you may not otherwise realise exist.

While tarot can be used as a form of fortune-telling, it is not a crystal ball. If you are hoping for specific answers about your future, you may be disappointed.

However, the cards can provide you with a greater understanding of your current situation so that you can make the best decisions moving forward.

## Bottom line

Reading your own tarot cards is a great way to connect with yourself and gain clarity. Whether you are hoping to use them to make a decision or simply to reflect on your life, they can be a valuable tool.

The important thing to remember is that the cards are a reflection of your current situation. You should look at the cards as a mirror that can help you gain insight into your current feelings and emotions.
