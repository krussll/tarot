A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Star card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Star card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Star and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The Star tarot card and the 8 Of Swords tarot card together represent a journey of renewal and liberation. This combination of cards suggests that while you may currently feel trapped or restricted by your own choices, there is hope that you can break free of these self-imposed constraints and move forward with a greater sense of clarity and purpose. You may feel like you are in the spotlight and that miracles can happen to help you break away and start again. Have faith in yourself and allow yourself to be renewed and liberated, and this will lead to a better future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Star and 8 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Star mean?

Health: The Star Tarot card is an excellent card for health and can be seen as a reminder to take the time to focus on your own wellbeing. A reminder that taking care of yourself is not only important, but will also bring balance and healing within. This card indicates that through self care and self-awareness, you can unlock a greater sense of harmony and health. This can mean anything from regular exercise, diet, and time for self-reflection. The Star card encourages you to take a moment to tap into your own inner strength and take actions that nourish your mind, body and spirit.

Finances: The Star Tarot card is a signifier of hope and financial stability. It indicates that through careful planning and understanding of your financial situation, you can create a firm foundation for your finances. This card is an indication that by taking the time to assess your financial situation, you can bring clarity and focus. This card also encourages you to trust in the universe and believe that any financial worries can be taken care of in the end. That you are supported regardless of the situation.

Relationships: The Star Tarot card is a signifier of joy, clarity and renewal in relationships. It encourages you to trust that things can be sorted out in the end, and that you are able to enjoy the moment with your partner. This card also indicates that by taking the time to communicate your feelings and needs with your partner, you can bring greater harmony and understanding in the relationship. This card reassures you that relationships can bring a renewed sense of clarity and that through open communication, understanding and love, your relationships can bring greater joy and balance.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 8 Of Swords tarot card signifies a sense of being trapped in one’s own unhealthy habits and patterns, whether physical or mental. It reflects an inability to break out of an unhealthy cycle and suggests a need for a shift in one’s lifestyle habits and behaviors. The card can represent a lack of self-care and awareness, an unwillingness to make decisions, and a tendency to self-sabotage. It can point to an individual holding themselves back from making the positive changes needed for their own wellbeing, allowing their own negative thoughts and beliefs to get in the way of taking the necessary steps towards better health.

Finances:

The 8 Of Swords tarot card can signify an inability to take the necessary steps to make financial changes, either out of fear of failure or the reluctance to take responsibility. It can be a sign that one’s financial goals are weighed down with self-imposed restrictions, making it difficult to progress in achieving their long-term financial goals. The card can represent a lack of confidence and a lack of financial education, leading to making decisions that are motivated by fear rather than rationale. In some cases, it can refer to an addiction to instant gratification and an inability to save or invest in a meaningful way.

Relationships:

The 8 Of Swords tarot card can be a sign of dissatisfaction with one’s current relationships due to self-imposed restrictions or fear of taking risks. It can be a sign of holding back from engaging in healthy relationships, either out of fear of being vulnerable or of getting hurt. It may suggest an unwillingness to open up or invest in relationships, instead choosing to remain in the same patterns of interactions out of familiarity rather than progress. The card can be a sign that one has emotionally walled themselves off and have difficulty in trusting or allowing someone in. It can also mean a fear of commitment, making it difficult to take a relationship to the next level.
