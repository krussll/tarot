## What does 10 Of Wands and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The 10 Of Wands and The 7 Of Swords tarot cards can indicate a time of overwhelming stress and exhaustion due to heavy burdens, including feelings of betrayal, theft and dishonesty. This could represent a difficult time of feeling burdened and taken advantage of, leading to wanting to escape the situation. There could be a feeling of being overloaded with responsibilities, but also being unable to escape the stress due to the dishonesty of others. It is important to take a step back and ask for help when feeling overwhelmed by these burdens.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 10 of Wands Tarot card represents physical, mental, and emotional fatigue. It suggests feeling overwhelmed, overextended and struggling to cope with too many demands. A person who draws this card is likely feeling stretched thin and exhausted, both physically and psychologically. They may have taken on too many tasks, commitments, or responsibilities in an attempt to please others, and are now struggling to keep up. They may be feeling burned out and unable to find the energy to do the things they want or need to do. On a physical level, they may be struggling with tension headaches, fatigue, insomnia, back pain, or any number of other stress-related illnesses.

Finances  
The 10 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes a lack of financial control and an overburdened sense of responsibility. A person who draws this card may be feeling overwhelmed by debt, or an inability to keep up with bills or other financial commitments. They may be feeling like they don’t have the money or resources to cover their costs, and are having difficulty managing their expenses. The card indicates that poor financial planning has led to an over-commitment of resources and too little left to cover the necessary bills.

Relationships  
The 10 of Wands Tarot card implies strained relationships and a lack of time for meaningful connections. A person who draws this card may be feeling too worn out and stressed to deal with interpersonal issues, burdened by their responsibilities. They may be feeling like their relationships take a back seat to their commitments, and that they are unable to give their relationships the attention they need. The card suggests a need to prioritize relationships, to make sure they are not neglected in order to take care of other matters.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 Of Swords Tarot card is associated with health in the sense that you may be tempted to take short cuts with your health. Perhaps you are being tempted to take risks that could potentially harm you. Alternatively, it may be related to a general attitude of disregard for one’s health. You may be tempted to put off important check-ups and examinations, or neglect taking care of your own health.

It can also be related to finances, as the 7 Of Swords signifies theft, betrayal and dishonesty. In this context it may be a warning to be extra vigilant with your financial affairs. Be aware of potential scams and be sure to ask lots of questions before making any commitments or purchases.

The 7 Of Swords can have a negative impact on relationships. It can signify lack of trust and dishonesty, which can make it difficult to maintain any kind of strong bond. Secrets can be revealed when this card appears and you may find yourself feeling betrayed by someone close. It’s important to be aware of the potential for betrayal and maintain open and honest communication where possible.

Overall, the 7 Of Swords is a difficult card to see but can bring important lessons of caution and awareness. It is a reminder to take care of your health, financial affairs and relationships, otherwise you could risk a great deal of pain.
