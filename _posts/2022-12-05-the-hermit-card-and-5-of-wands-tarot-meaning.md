A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 5 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 5 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 5 Of Wands mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the 5 Of Wands tarot card together point to a journey of self-discovery. The Hermit highlights the importance of personal truth and spiritual enlightenment and in tandem with the 5 Of Wands, this journey is likely to include challenges, obstacles and rivalries along the way. We are being asked to look within and become aware of what matters to us the most in order to find clarity and gain knowledge from the tests set before us. While the road may be tough, the reward will be greater when we make it to the end.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Hermit and 5 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The Hermit and the 5 Of Wands in your love life signify that you are in a time of self-reflection. You are viewing your relationships from the perspective of your own personal truth. You are assessing which dynamics serve you, and which do not.

This period of self-reflection is not without its challenges, as symbolized by the 5 Of Wands. You may find yourself in situations of rivalry or competing interests. This could mean trying to balance the demands of your lover with your own needs, or making efforts to maintain harmony with your partner. It could also represent navigating the external expectations of those around you, that might try to influence or shape your romance.

It’s important to maintain an understanding of the personal truth that you have gained during your reflections. Be mindful of the obstacles, but don’t be discouraged by them. The Hermit is a reminder that it is possible to find balance and peace through inner wisdom. Continue to maintain strong communication with your partner and focus on what is truly important for your bond of love.

### What does The Hermit and 5 Of Wands mean together for your finances?

When the Hermit tarot card and the 5 of Wands appear together in a spread, this combination suggests that the querent is seeking illumination in their finances and job life. The Hermit encourages the seeker to go within for clarity and a quest for personal truth. This card can be interpreted as a call for a break from the hustle and bustle of day-to-day life, in order to gain a deeper understanding of what matters most. This could involve taking some time off work and reassessing the current job situation, or it could simply mean pausing to reflect on which job and financial goals are worth pursuing.

The 5 of Wands adds to the combination, suggesting that the process of finding clarity and personal truth in the job and financial arena will not be easy. This card warns of potential obstacles and challenges standing in the way of success. It can also suggest rivalry, or a need to stay competitive in order to achieve one’s goals. The 5 of Wands is a reminder to stay focused and push through any obstacles that arise.

Ultimately, the combination of the Hermit tarot card and the 5 of Wands tarot card serve as a reminder to the querent to stay in tune with their inner voice and to use the power of their will in order to reach their financial and job goals. By accessing the clarity that can be found within, and staying focused and competitive in the job market, they can find the success they seek.

The Hermit tarot card encourages time spent in reflection or meditation in order to gain clarity and achieve spiritual illumination. When these two tarot cards are combined, the message for our health is clear: take the time to observe and reflect on both our physical and mental states, and use this knowledge to make positive changes towards better health. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 5 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 5 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card has a wide range of implications for health, finances and relationships. On a health level, this card can be interpreted as an invitation to slow down and pay attention to our bodies. It implies that there is an inner wisdom that is available to us, and that by tuning in to the subtle messages of our bodies, we can gain insight and understanding into our overall health and well-being. Perhaps it is time to take a breather and focus on self-care.

When it comes to finances, the Hermit Tarot card can indicate a need to take a step back and analyze our financial situation. It can be a warning that our financial decisions need to be made with thoughtfulness and caution. It can be a reminder to create a budget and to be mindful of our current situation in order to make sound decisions that will benefit us in the future.

For our relationships, this card suggests that it is time to go deeper within and pay attention to our personal truth. It can be an invitation to spend time alone in order to gain clarity and perspective. It can be a reminder to pause and ask ourselves important questions about our relationships and how we want to show up in them. This can help us get to know ourselves better and make better choices when it comes to our relationships.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

When it comes to health, The 5 of Wands Tarot card suggests that you may be facing some challenges in your physical health journey. For example, you might be dealing with a prolonged medical condition, a difficult exercise routine, or an eating disorder. You may need to use every ounce of your energy and determination to overcome these obstacles, as trying to make positive changes can be a frustrating process. With a strong will and patience, you can find success, however.

In terms of finances, The 5 of Wands Tarot card can indicate that there are some setbacks and struggles along the way. You might be confronted with difficult decisions and be presented with some unexpected obstacles that need to be dealt with in order to move forward. Such challenges could include financial hardship, difficult investments, or even legal issues. It’s important to remain aware that despite the difficult times, there is always hope and you can find ways to turn your financial situation around.

When it comes to relationships, The 5 of Wands Tarot card can indicate that you may have to deal with some challenges. You may be in a situation of competition or rivalry with another person, and it is important to remember that these conflicts should be addressed head-on. It is important to realize that there are two sides to every story and to bring an open-minded attitude to the situation. With the right conversations, compromise and understanding, you can navigate through the challenges and come out stronger than before.
