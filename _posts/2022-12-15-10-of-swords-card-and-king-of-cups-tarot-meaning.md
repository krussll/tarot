A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together?

The 10 of Swords and the King of Cups tarot cards can represent a painful and complete end, which leads to an emotional rock bottom. However, the King of Cups offers an opportunity for support and empathy in this difficult time. This figure is a supportive leader, with a capacity for emotional tolerance and deep understanding for the difficult situation you may encounter. They have a healing perspective that can help you make it through the darkness and sorrow of such a difficult transition. With their compassionate understanding, you can eventually make it through the difficult emotions of the dark ending and accept the love and understanding that the King of Cups has to offer.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Swords and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Swords and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 10 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the rock bottom of a physical decline. This could refer to a serious illness, injury, or exhaustion from trying to cope with a stressful situation. Emotionally, the 10 of Swords can represent feelings of loneliness, depression, and despair in the face of a severe health crisis. On a mental level, this card can represent a deep sense of overwhelm from trying to cope with a difficult physical or emotional health issue.

Finances: The 10 of Swords Tarot card signifies a painful financial setback. This card can indicate a financial loss, a major expense, or a debt that can’t seem to be repaid. This card can also signify the struggle of trying to navigate an uncertain financial future. This can cause feelings of despair, hopelessness, and helplessness. Ultimately, the 10 of Swords can represent financial ruin and the need to start over.

Relationships: The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests a complete and painful end to a relationship. This could mean the end of a marriage, a break-up, or a major betrayal. On a deeper level, this card can signify the emotional pain of a broken heart. This could be in the form of betrayal, abandonment, or the end of a meaningful connection. In any case, the 10 of Swords signifies a deep and heartbreaking loss.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

The King of Cups Tarot card represents a figure of authority and wise judgment. It symbolizes the compassionate and empathetic side of a person and the ability to bring healing and balance to a situation. The King of Cups points to a generous and understanding leader.

For health, the King of Cups means that you need to be supportive of yourself and take care of your health. It advises to be mindful of what you put in your body, how much rest you get, how you manage your stress, and to look for the good in life. Additionally, the card suggests that you find ways to be kind and gentle with yourself.

For finances, the King of Cups encourages you to be mindful about how you handle money. Taking care of your financial health can be a difficult task, but the King of Cups reminds you to develop a plan, be disciplined and save for your future. It also advises that you practice patience with yourself and nurture a sense of abundance.

For relationships, the King of Cups is a reminder that strong relationships involve lots of communication, understanding and compromise. It suggests to be the bigger person, to be generous with your time and attention, and to be patient and tolerant with the other person. The King of Cups also encourages you to look at the positive aspects of the relationship and to seek out mutual understanding and support.
