However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 9 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 9 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 9 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the 9 of Pentacles together show that you are taking a moment to go within and find clarity and personal truth. This is a time to focus on yourself, your spiritual growth, and learning the lessons that life is sending your way. At the same time, you are being rewarded through luxurious self-sufficiency and financial gain. This could be through a salary bonus, savings or investments, or other types of financial rewards. This could also manifest as solo pleasures, such as a relaxing spa day or a night out alone with friends. It is a time to honor your individual growth, while also being rewarded for it.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card is a powerful symbol of inner contemplation, a reminder to examine one’s life in order to gain clarity and insight. It urges individuals to look within and not to rely on the opinions of others when making decisions. When this card appears it can represent a time of turning inward to search for greater meaning.

When it comes to health, The Hermit Tarot card can symbolize a time to take a break and focus on one’s own well-being. It can suggest that an individual should take stock of their habits and lifestyle and make an effort to learn more about their own body and health needs. It can also be a sign that seeking professional help and guidance can be beneficial in order to make the right choices for one’s own self-care.

In the realm of finances, The Hermit Tarot card can indicate a period for self-reflection and a need to consider one’s resources and to create a financial plan. It may be the time to look at the facts objectively and make changes and decisions that are best for the individual in the long-term.

Finally, in terms of relationships, The Hermit Tarot card can mean that a period of solitude is needed in order to process emotions and to gain insight into one’s own needs and boundaries. It can be a sign that it is time to take a break and to focus on understanding one’s own feelings and desires in order to create healthy relationships. This is a time to connect with oneself and to understand one’s individual gifts before connecting with others.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card stands for luxury, self-sufficiency, and financial gain. It suggests that it is the perfect time to treasure and celebrate the small successes and joys of one’s hard-earned success and accomplishments. This card encourages a person to acknowledge and reward themselves for the success they have achieved and the financial and material gains they have made.

When it comes to health, The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card reflects the importance of taking care of one’s body and health. It signifies that one should be mindful of their physical and mental health, self-care, and well-being. It is a reminder to nourish and prioritize those needs, so that one can be financially and emotionally secure.

In terms of finances, The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card reflects the importance of investing in something that can yield lasting financial security. It directs one to create a budget and plan for the future. It encourages investment in tangible things that can increase one’s net worth, such as assets, investments, and business ventures.

In terms of relationships, The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that it is important to create meaningful and long-lasting relationships. It encourages a person to be mindful of their relationships and to create strong, supportive bonds with their family, friends, and peers. It suggests that one should focus on creating an environment of trust and respect in their relationships, so that their bond can endure for the long run.
