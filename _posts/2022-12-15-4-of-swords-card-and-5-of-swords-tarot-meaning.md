## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This could involve taking a day off or taking a vacation, allowing yourself some time to disconnect from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. Alternatively, it could mean taking some time to focus on self-care and looking after your physical, mental and emotional health in whatever way works best for you.

The 4 of Swords indicates that it might be time to take a step back and reassess your current financial situation. This could include taking some time to review your budget to ensure you are managing your money in the best way possible, as well as assessing any investments or debts you might have. It can also mean that now is the time to make sure you are saving for the future and making sure your financial security is taken care of.

The 4 of Swords suggests that it might be time to take a break from relationships and take some time for yourself. This could mean taking a break from any romantic relationships, or it might mean taking some time away from close friendships and giving yourself some space to recharge. It could also mean taking some time to assess the relationships you have and make sure they are healthy and beneficial for both parties. Ultimately, the 4 of Swords suggests that it is important to take some time for yourself and make sure the relationships you have are ones that make you happy.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, the 5 of Swords Tarot card represents a lack of balance in the body. It can be a sign of physical and emotional exhaustion, mindless aggression and a lack of concern for one’s own wellbeing. It is an indication of neglecting one’s own health, leading to physical and mental health issues. This card warns against any type of abusive behavior, whether it be self-inflicted or directed at someone else.

When it comes to finances, the 5 of Swords Tarot card warns of a lack of respect for personal and financial boundaries. Someone might be in need of money but go about getting it in a non-ethical fashion, such as theft or deception. This card can also be a sign of a lack of planning when it comes to finances, leading to chaos and potential legal problems. Neglecting money management and trying to get ahead through unscrupulous methods can lead to financial instability.

When it comes to relationships, the 5 of Swords Tarot card is a warning sign of abusive relationships. This card can represent verbal and physical aggression, seizing of power, manipulation, bullying and intimidation. It is a sign of imbalance in relationships, where one person is taking advantage of another. This card is a reminder to look out for signs of abuse and reach out for help when needed. It also indicates a need to take responsibility for one’s own actions and be more conscious about the way one interacts with others.
