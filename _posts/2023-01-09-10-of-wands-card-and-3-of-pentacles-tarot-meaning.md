A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and 3 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 10 of Wands and the 3 of Pentacles tarot card combination suggests that although you may be feeling overwhelmed and exhausted by the current burdens that you have, you might have a great opportunity to develop your skills and collaborate with others in order to find more sustainable ways to manage them. By being willing to work with others and by making use of your own skills, you can turn a potentially crushing situation into something more positive and productive, and you can find creative solutions to come out ahead.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands in the Tarot is a card that reflects the feeling of being overwhelmed, encumbered by too many burdens and responsibilities. For many, this card has a heavy energy that can sap energy, putting strain on both physical and mental health. People carrying these burdens often find themselves overworked, exhausted, and drained. With so much effort required to fulfill their obligations, the spark that makes life enjoyable and worthwhile can be lost.

In terms of finances, this card often symbolizes a heavy debt burden. Whether it is debt from student loans, credit cards, or other obligations, this card often points to the need to take a hard look at finances. Taking the time to look for ways to relieve the burden can bring a sense of relief.

When it comes to relationships, this card can indicate a feeling of being weighed down by the demands of loved ones. Without proper communication, this can cause feelings of isolation and resentment. It may be necessary to learn to set boundaries, both with yourself and with others, in order to keep relationships healthy.

The 10 of Wands is a reminder that life is a balance – that too much of one thing can be detrimental in the long run. It is important to take the time to take a step back and assess the state of both your health and your relationships. Taking the necessary time and effort to create a balanced life can help bring back a sense of peace and wellbeing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card signifies teamwork, collaboration and quality. In terms of health and wellness, it can indicate a need for collaborative work with medical professionals. The card can represent the healing of illnesses through proper diagnosis and treatment, as well as the need to invest in quality medical care and equipment. It can also be a sign of successful lifestyle changes, such as starting an exercise routine and creating healthy habits, as these require a team effort.

When it comes to finances, the 3 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that investments should be made in smart, quality products. It may represent the need to partner with people who have a specific area of expertise, in order to ensure success. This could include hiring a financial advisor to help you make the right decisions when it comes to investing your money. Additionally, the card can indicate that the time is right to pursue a business venture, as long as it is backed by the right team of professionals.

In terms of relationships, the 3 of Pentacles Tarot card can signify working together with a partner to create something of quality. Whether this is a creative projects, a financial goal, or a new venture, teamwork is necessary for success. The card can be seen as a reminder to work together to achieve a mutual goal, or to invest in the quality of the relationship. Additionally, it might be a sign to seek outside help, such as therapy, if a problem needs to be ironed out.
