A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Spades card and 7 Of Spades card together in particular.

The Ace Of Spades card and 7 Of Spades have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Ace Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the convergence of The Ace of Spades and The 7 of Spades paints a picture of a daunting yet transformative journey ahead.

The Ace of Spades symbolizes misfortune and desire – the hardships that one needs to go through, driven by a strong desire that serves as the guiding light in the midst of adversity.

Paired with The 7 of Spades, a card that stands for deceit and victory, it indicates the possibility of treachery and dishonesty leading to victory.

The shared season of Winter and the element of Water among these cards further intensify the essence of hardships and adaptability, emphasizing emotional resilience and hidden strength.

The combination suggests a challenging path marred by deceit, yet filled with personal desires and eventual victory, requiring inner strength and perseverance to see it through.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Ace Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, the Ace of Spades suggests a period of potential misfortune in your love life.

This could be represented by a variety of scenarios such as hardships, misunderstandings, or break-ups.

This card implies that these challenges may arise from a deep desire or longing you have, which could refer to a particular individual, or an ideal relationship that you yearn for.

As the Ace of Spades is linked with the season of Winter and the element Water, it signifies a period of introspection, emotional deepening and perhaps a sense of sorrow or melancholy that resonates with winter’s cold and introspective nature.

On the other hand, the Seven of Spades suggests that deception might be afoot.

This could mean that perhaps you or your partner might not be entirely truthful with each other.

This could lead to a false victory or a victory that leaves a bitter aftertaste.

It’s crucial to remember that honesty is the foundation of any successful relationship.

The Seven of Spades also resonates with the season of Winter and the element Water, suggesting a period of emotional struggle and introspection, and perhaps the need to look beyond the surface and confront some hard truths.

Together, these cards are pushing for emotional growth and urging for a more sincere and straightforward approach in your love life for your own well-being and the health of the relationship.

### What does Ace Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your finances?

In a Cartomancy reading, pulling both the Ace Of Spades and the 7 Of Spades can indicate a challenging period in terms of finances and job stability.

The Ace Of Spades symbolizes misfortune, suggesting that you may face unexpected financial difficulties or struggles at your workplace.

Your job situation might get affected by unforeseen circumstances, probably lowering your income.

It prompts caution regarding your expenditures and signifies the need for planning and budgeting.

At the same time, the presence of the 7 Of Spades in your reading echoes deception.

This could imply a possible deceit in your professional path—be it a dishonest co-worker, an unfair boss, or a fraudulent deal.

However, this card also carries the significance of victory, suggesting that you can productively navigate these unfavorable conditions if you tread cautiously.

The symbol of victory hints at overcoming these problems, provided you’re diligent enough to spot any potential deceit or fraud at your work.

In essence, through hardship and careful scrutiny, you can weather this storm and overcome the financial and professional obstacles presented by the Ace and 7 of Spades.

### What does Ace Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, if you draw both the Ace of Spades and the 7 of Spades in relation to your health, it might signify a challenging period ahead.

The Ace of Spades, with its association to misfortune, could indicate an upcoming illness or health complication.

This card suggests a significant change, like the cold darkness of winter, and can warn of a tough battle that will require courage and strength to overcome.

Given its association with the element of water, it might suggest that this health issue could be linked to issues of emotional well-being or fluid balance in the body.

However, receiving the 7 of Spades in the same reading can suggest a victory is in sight, perhaps after a period of deception or confusion about your health.

While there may be initial fear or misinterpretation of symptoms, the end result will likely be triumph over the problem at hand.

Like the Ace, this card is linked to winter and water, reinforcing that this health journey might take some time and patience and could involve dealing with deep emotional issues.

Ultimately, though, drawing these two cards together suggests that while the road ahead may be tough, there is light at the end of the tunnel.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
