## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace of Pentacles is a positive card that has a variety of meanings when it comes to health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the Ace of Pentacles suggests that your health is in a very positive state, and that taking care of yourself is essential for continued success. It can indicate that it is time for you to make some changes, as well as taking steps towards preventative care. It is a good time to look at your diet, exercise habits and overall lifestyle, as this card suggests that now is the time to make a change to ensure your future health.

Financially, the Ace of Pentacles can represent an uptick in your financial fortunes. It can indicate the start of a new job, a raise, a promotion, an investment or a new source of income. It suggests that this is the time to begin taking steps towards financial stability. It is also a good time to begin making plans and exploring investment opportunities.

Relationship wise, the Ace of Pentacles can represent stability and commitment. It suggests that if you are already in a relationship, it is a time of growth and progress. For those who aren’t in a relationship, it can indicate a new potential for a relationship. It is also a good time to look for potential partners and to work on strengthening your existing relationships.

Overall, the Ace of Pentacles is a positive card that has many different meanings for a variety of situations. It suggests that change is in the air and that now is the time to take action. It is a card of positive energy that suggests that health, finances and relationships are all in a very positive place, and that taking action now is the key to ensuring continued success.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

This card can be a reminder that health involves more than just physical ailments. Taking a holistic approach to health by considering the well-being of your mental, spiritual, and emotional health is important to long-term health. Revisiting old practices such as yoga, meditation, or journaling can give you a much-needed break from the stressors of everyday life.

Finances: The 6 of Cups tarot card can be a beacon of hope for financial matters. It can indicate a reunion with more money coming your way. This could mean a long-lost inheritance or a windfall of some kind. It can also suggest the power of working together with others to reach a positive financial outcome. Joining forces with your financial advisor, accountant, or even a friend can help open doors to new financial opportunities.

Relationships: The 6 of Cups Tarot Card can represent nostalgia in relationships. It can be a reminder to reconnect with old friends and colleagues or to remind yourself of the beauty in long-term relationships. This card can also indicate the arrival of gifts with the intention of giving and receiving joy. Taking the time to appreciate the relationships in your life and cherish them can bring a new depth to your connections.
