A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 9 Of Wands and The 9 of Cups tarot cards represent an amazing sense of balance and harmony in regards to both progressing forward and self-care. It symbolizes that through a strong sense of boundaries and persistence one can achieve their dreams and wishes. It also suggests that material abundance and good health can be achieved if a goal-oriented approach is taken, with enough dedication and determination. This combination is a reminder to trust yourself and believe in your capabilities, as focusing energy on your goals can create a lifetime of success, prosperity and joy.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

With this card combination, one is likely to be a desirable partner due to their ability to set boundaries and still maintain a loving and passionate relationship. This combination can also bring a heightened sense of trust and security to a relationship, and encourages both parties to feel secure and supported within it.

The 9 Of Wands and The 9 Of Cups Tarot card combination is an indication of the culmination of a relationship, in the sense that it is well established, and both parties are in a comfortable and trusting place. This card combination speaks of a reciprocal respect and understanding between two people, which is the key to any long-lasting and meaningful relationship.

This card combination encourages one to find balance in their relationships by learning to value both their own personal boundaries and the boundaries of their partner. It is important to remember that a relationship should bring fulfillment, without either party feeling as if they are giving too much or too little. This card combination calls for both parties in the relationship to find a perfect balance between trust, respect and freedom.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 9 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

Health:  
The 9 of Wands Tarot card can represent a need for maintaining boundaries in regards to health. It may be an important time to remind yourself of what practices and habits you need to keep up in order to remain healthy. This could include things like moderating your diet, exercising, meditating, and getting adequate rest. Additionally, it could be a time to be mindful of who you allow to have an influence on your actions and decisions when it comes to your health and wellbeing. The card acknowledges that life can be unpredictable, but encourages you to remain resilient and follow through with your health commitments by perseverence and dedication.

Finances:  
The 9 of Wands Tarot card can signify that it is an important time to take control of your finances and be wise with your money. It serves as a warning that forces from outside can be working against you. It encourages you to be diligent and maintain a tight grip on your funds so that you can continue to make wise decisions and remain financially stable. This card may suggest that it might be beneficial to set up systems such as budgeting and developing a savings plan in order to further protect yourself and your money.

Relationships:  
The 9 of Wands Tarot card indicates that boundaries need to be established in relationships. It serves as a reminder that boundaries can help protect you from harm and ensure that you are respected in relationships. This could mean setting limits when it comes to how much time and energy you are willing to give and receive, or setting personal boundaries that respect your own needs and values. Additionally, the card could indicate a time to stand up for yourself and exercise personal empowerment in the face of opposition. It encourages you to be resilient and persistant in order to maintain a healthy and balanced relationship.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The Nine of Cups tarot card signifies good health and vitality. It is an indicator of excellent physical and mental well-being, with vibrant energy and a strong immune system. It reflects good health habits and a balanced lifestyle, with diet and exercise being important components. When this card appears in a reading, it can mean that you are in a good spot with your health and that you should continue to use preventative measures to protect it. This card can also indicate a recovery from an illness or serious health concern. Overall, this is a positive sign for health.

Finances:

The Nine of Cups tarot card is a sign of abundant wealth and prosperity. It indicates that the seeker might have a strong financial situation, with the ability to support comfort and luxury. It is a positive sign for those looking for success in business and in life, with positive rewards for all efforts. Money can come from unexpected sources, and financial stability can be achieved with long-term investments and wise decisions. The nine of cups can indicate that you have the power to manifest your own success.

Relationships:

The Nine of Cups tarot card speaks to strong relationships and the ability to find true contentment. It symbolizes a mature, healthy connection between people, with a strong foundation of trust and understanding. It indicates a balanced relationship, with both partners having their needs fulfilled, and nourishing each other in a supportive and loving way. This card is also a symbol of joy and celebration, where you can experience true pleasure in each other’s company. When this card appears, it can mean that you have found true happiness, with no need for fear of abandonment or loss.
