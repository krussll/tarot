## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

The Ace of Cups card in a Tarot reading suggests that you may be in a place of emotional healing. The card symbolizes the flow of love, compassion, and emotional energy that brings healing, fulfillment and joy to your life. It may signal a period of emotional growth and understanding, or a need to take a break in order to nurture yourself. You may find yourself paying more attention to your physical, mental and spiritual health, and taking time to relax. The Ace of Cups can be a reminder to honor your feelings, to be authentic and honest with yourself, and to pay attention to the quality of your relationships.

Finances:

The Ace of Cups in Tarot readings can symbolize new opportunities and money approaching in the near future. It can be a reminder to be open to receiving abundance and to appreciate the blessings in your life. Taking advantage of this new financial opportunity requires caution and sound judgment. Don’t rush into any business deals that feel too good to be true. With a little bit of planning and organization, you can ensure that your finances are secure and stable.

Relationships:

The Ace of Cups in a Tarot reading often signals new beginnings in the realm of relationships. It could be a marriage proposal, the start of a new romantic relationship, or the birth of a baby. It is important to approach any new relationships with an open heart, patience, and trust. Be willing to take risks and to communicate your feelings and expectations clearly. The Ace of Cups can also be a reminder to nurture existing relationships, to foster warm and loving energy, and to celebrate the joy that comes from being in a committed, trusting relationship.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

The 10 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a period of abundance and contentment in one’s life. Specifically, the 10 of Cups speaks to the importance of healthy, supportive relationships in achieving inner peace and stability. In terms of health, this card represents the idea of family and community support in times of need, whether it be physical or mental health. It can also signify the presence of healthy lifestyle habits, such as regular exercise and a balanced diet, that keep the body strong and resilient. In terms of finances, the 10 of Cups suggests balance and long-term security. It indicates a harmonious relationship between one’s material needs and the resources available. This could manifest in the form of a steady income, investments, or a well thought out budget that ensures the future financial stability of one’s family. Finally, in terms of relationships, the 10 of Cups symbolizes lasting joy and contentment. It speaks to the importance of nurturing meaningful connections with loved ones that are both emotionally and spiritually fulfilling. This could represent a healthy marriage, parent-child relationships, friendships, or any other type of relationship that brings comfort and support. In summary, the 10 of Cups Tarot card serves as a reminder to embrace the joy and abundance in one’s life, cherishing meaningful connections and practicing habits of self-care.
