A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and 7 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The World card and 7 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

The World tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card together represent a time of completion and a period of hard work and patience. It can signify that you have achieved the goals you have been working toward and have passed a significant lesson or milestone in your life. This success has come with its own form of hard work and patience and now you can look forward to embarking on a new journey, taking with you all that you have learned. It can also represent a period of emigration or travel, where you are rewarded for your labor and efforts with new opportunities.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 7 of Pentacles cautions against rushing into this next stage of life without having thought it through properly. It points to the need for patience and hard work to get things right. It also reminds us to be comfortable with delayed success. Savor the moment when the time is right, and understand that the reward for your hard work will come in time.

Overall, this combination of tarot cards suggests that it is time to take your relationship to the next level. It will require patience, dedication and hard work, but if you keep at it, the rewards for your efforts can be great. Take the time to consider your options carefully, and appreciate each day as it comes. With the right balance of effort and acceptance, you can achieve your end goal and make the most of this new, significant stage in your relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

Health: The World Tarot card can symbolize a sense of wellbeing and the completion of a personal health goal. It may be time to review accomplishments and find satisfaction in the efforts taken. The card may also make a person aware of changes that could be beneficial in developing a healthier lifestyle. These might include getting more exercise or finding ways to cut back on stress. The World Tarot card also suggests taking stock of physical, mental, and spiritual health and making the necessary changes to achieve balance.

Finances: The World Tarot card can signify financial success. It may indicate the completion of a long-term financial goal, like paying off debt or reaching a savings goal. Other times, the card implies an opportunity to make a substantial financial change. This could involve investing, changing careers, or taking a risk that could produce a large reward. The World Tarot suggests using sound business judgement and that the time is right to make a wise and far‐reaching decision.

Relationships: The World Tarot card can mean that a relationship has reached a level of completion. This could indicate progression in a relationship, such as celebrating a marriage, or signify that a relationship has come to a natural conclusion. In romantic relationships, the card can suggest a time of fulfillment and satisfaction. Friendships may have reached a new level of understanding and trust and it is a time to appreciate the bond that has been formed.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that when it comes to striving for health and wellness, it requires patience and hard work. When working on health goals, this card encourages us to remember that progress often comes gradually, and we should not give up after the first setback. We should strive to maintain motivation and stay consistent with our commitment to making healthy choices. We must also note that it often takes a while before we can see the results, and that our efforts will be rewarded when the right changes are made and enough time passes.

Finances: The 7 Of Pentacles reflects the need for a patient approach to money management. The card encourages us to take the necessary steps to strengthen our finances, such as creating and executing a budget, investing wisely, and taking calculated risks. By being diligent in our financial planning, this card suggests that we can eventually reap the rewards of financial security, albeit after a period of hard work and perseverance. We must trust in our plans, and commit to being patient and consistent in our approach.

Relationships: The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that hard work and persistence are key to building lasting relationships. We should not expect instant satisfaction and instead be willing to put in the effort to nurture our bonds. This card reminds us that relationships are about give and take, and that the best way to strengthen and deepen the bond is by being patient and showing care and understanding. We must also accept that sometimes it just takes longer for relationships to reach their fullest potential, and to remember that when we are dedicated to our relationships it will eventually pay off.
