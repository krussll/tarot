## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 6 Of Wands and Page Of Pentacles mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 6 Of Wands and Page Of Pentacles the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

It speaks to the strength of the individual and the ability to rise above adversities. Whether it is a physical ailment, mental state of mind or spiritual fulfillment, this card is an encouragement to press on and remain optimistic. With the aid of supportive relationships and caring professionals, the card is a reminder that it is possible to triumph in one’s health.

Finances:  
The Six of Wands tarot card is symbolic of financial successes and victories. It speaks of rewards, recognition and a sense of accomplishment in the area of finances. It carries the message that hard work, dedication and perseverance will bring forth abundance. It is a reminder to keep striving towards one’s goals and to remain focused on the desired outcome. This card may point to an external factor that could influence one’s financial situation for the better, whether it is a raise or promotion, an investment opportunity or a chance to start a successful business venture.

Relationships:  
The Six of Wands tarot card speaks of achievements within relationships, whether with friends, family or romantic partners. It is an indication of success in communication, trust and understanding. With the help of mutual effort, respect and mutual understanding, the relationships can flourish and reach new heights. This card signals that one should be open to making compromises and expressing feelings in order to gain mutual respect and peace. It can also encourage self-confidence and assertiveness in order to take control of one’s relationships.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with new beginnings, a strong focus on the future, and a dedication to making the best of one’s life. It is also an indication that one should pay attention to the details and learn from their mistakes.

Health: Health wise, the Page of Pentacles Tarot card encourages one to take care of their body, mind and soul. As with any new venture, it’s important to set aside time to maintain physical fitness and mental wellbeing in order to stay healthy. Taking the time to understand one’s diet and exercise regime can be beneficial for long-term health, and taking the time to nurture relationships with family and friends is another way to strengthen one’s wellbeing.

It may also serve as a reminder to be wise with any money that comes in, and to consider investments that could provide a healthy return in the future. It is also an indication that one should take a close look at their budget and maintain a good money-saving routine.

It is important to maintain respect in relationships and to communicate effectively with those who are important in one’s life. This card also encourages one to trust their instincts when it comes to making decisions and to focus on becoming the best version of themselves.
