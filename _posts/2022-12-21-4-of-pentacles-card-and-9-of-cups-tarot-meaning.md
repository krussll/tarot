## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card represents the need for security and stability that many people struggle to achieve in their lives. It suggests that a person may be experiencing a strong sense of insecurity or fear of change, leading them to try to control their environment and cling to the familiar. This card could represent an attempt to control one’s health, finances and relationships, all in an effort to protect their security.

The 4 of Pentacles indicates an unhealthy preoccupation with one’s financial, physical and emotional security. It is a sign of someone who is obsessed with keeping their financial, physical and emotional conditions the same and is unwilling to take risks or adapt to change. This could manifest in someone who is very protective of their money and averse to investment or financial risk, or someone who holds onto their possessions tightly and is unwilling to part with them.

In terms of health, the 4 of Pentacles suggests someone who is overly cautious and unwilling to make changes to their lifestyle or diet, even when it is necessary for their long-term wellbeing. It can also reflect someone who is very protective of their physical and emotional boundaries, making them feel uncomfortable whenever someone else tries to get too close.

In relationships, this card can signify a fear of commitment, as well as an unwillingness to open up and share one’s emotions with others. Someone who is fixated on the 4 of Pentacles could be clinging to a relationship that is no longer working and they may find it difficult to break away because they fear the unknown. They may also have difficulty allowing someone else into their life, as they are too scared of any potential changes that could threaten the security of their relationship.

Ultimately, the 4 of Pentacles Tarot card points to a deep-rooted desire for security and stability, but one that can become unhealthy and lead to controlling and manipulative behaviour. It is important to remember that with change comes growth and that it is alright to take risks in order to reach one’s full potential.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Cups tarot card signifies abundance and prosperity. It suggests that the individual has the ability to create wealth and to receive the rewards of their hard work. This card can also be a sign that the individual has the necessary resources to achieve their financial goals.

When it comes to relationships, the 9 of Cups tarot card symbolizes harmony and mutual understanding. It suggests that the individual is in a comfortable and loving relationship that provides the level of emotional support needed to move forward. This card can also mean that the individual is in a stable and secure partnership, and that the relationship is strong and lasting.

In conclusion, the 9 of Cups tarot card suggests good health, financial security, and stable, loving relationships. It is a reminder that the individual has the resources and strength to create a life full of abundance and happiness.
