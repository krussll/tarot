A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and King Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Tower card and King Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and King Of Wands mean together?

The Tower tarot card and the King of Wands tarot card represent the potential for unexpected and disruptive changes in our lives, as well as the power and courage to make the most of them. The Tower card symbolizes unwelcome changes that are wrought through upheaval, ruin and even violence, while the King of Wands stands for bold leadership, charm and the strength to overcome such chaos. Together, they suggest that while life may bring unexpected change, we can use our strength and courage to make the most of the situation and still come out on top.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: The Tower Tarot card could be an indication of an unwelcome health issue or sudden health issue arising. It signifies a health crisis that has caused devastation and chaos, and its effects are wide-reaching and dramatic. As the card implies suddenness, it could refer to a health condition that suddenly affects an individual’s life, leading to a period of upheaval and disruption. The Tower Tarot card brings a warning that health-related decisions taken in this crisis period should be done wisely and carefully, as the outcome is likely to be significant and lasting.

Finances: The Tower Tarot card can be associated with financial ruin, loss and upheaval. It implies a financial crisis that arises out of nowhere and causes a major disruption to a person’s financial security. The effects of this crisis are likely to be severe and long-term, potentially resulting in bankruptcy or debt. As such, it is wise for individuals to exercise caution and prudence when it comes to financial decisions, as any errors or missteps could result in devastating consequences.

Relationships: The Tower Tarot card could signify a period of upheaval when it comes to relationships. In particular, it could mean a period of disruption and even violence when it comes to intimate relationships. This card can signify a destructive force entering a relationship, causing a breakdown and potential rupture of the relationship. In attempting to navigate this period of disruption, individuals should consider each step carefully and treat each other with respect, as the consequences of any rash decisions could cause lasting damage.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

Health: The King of Wands Tarot card is a sign of good health and strong vitality. This card encourages you to remain confident in your mind and body. It urges you to take care of yourself and your environment, to nourish your body, and to take time out of the day to be in and enjoy the present moment. The King of Wands suggests that health and vitality come from internal and external balance.

Finances: The King of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of financial stability and success. It encourages you to be bold with your decisions, to take risks where you see opportunities, and to stay true to your financial goals. It is a reminder to create balance between your spending, savings, and investments, to remain mindful of your values, and to trust your intuition when it comes to financial decisions.

Relationships: The King of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of strong, positive relationships. It encourages you to be open, honest, and sincere with those around you, to be proactive when it comes to communication, and to stay true to your values. This card also reminds you to be a leader in your relationships, to set boundaries, and to use your charm to bring people together. The King of Wands encourages you to stay bold and brave in your relationships, and to strive for mutual respect and understanding.
