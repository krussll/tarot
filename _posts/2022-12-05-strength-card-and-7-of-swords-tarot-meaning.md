## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and 7 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

Health  
The Strength Tarot card speaks to the power of staying physically and emotionally strong. It’s a reminder that having a healthy, balanced lifestyle is the foundation for good health. So it tells us to take the time to nurture our bodies and give ourselves the best conditions for good health. Eating nutritious meals, exercising regularly, getting enough sleep and taking time out for relaxation and self-care are all essential for staying physically and emotionally healthy. The Strength Tarot card also serves as a reminder to face our challenges with courage, resilience and compassion. It tells us to take charge of our health, to courageously pursue treatments that are best for our well-being, and to not be afraid to ask for help and support when needed.

Finances  
The Strength Tarot card encourages financial self-knowledge, self-love and careful guidance when it comes to our financial decisions. It reminds us to be conscious of our spending, savings and investments. We need to be independent thinkers, so that we can make smart choices in our financial lives. This card also tells us to be constructively competitive and take intelligent risks. It reminds us that fiscal responsibility and fiscal discipline can help us create wealth and financial stability. Additionally, it encourages us to pursue strength and confidence in our interactions with the financial world.

Relationships  
The Strength Tarot card is a reminder to draw from strength and wisdom to build healthy relationships. It speaks of the power of unconditional love and self-respect. It tells us to practice courage, resilience and compassion when it comes to the relationships we nurture. This card encourages us to be open-hearted and honest with ourselves and those we love, and to courageously express our feelings. Additionally, it tells us to practice good communication, respect and trust in our relationships. Finally, it serves as a reminder that with strength and courage, all relationships are possible.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 7 of Swords Tarot card indicates a lack of commitment to taking care of our health. This card suggests that we’re running away from our health problems, refusing to take responsibility for them and ultimately leading to further illness or dis-ease. We may be ignoring symptoms, turning to quick fixes instead of holistic medicine and neglecting our overall wellbeing. This card warns that the consequences of our actions can have a negative impact on our health, both physical and mental.

Finances

The 7 of Swords Tarot card can indicate financial trouble. This card symbolizes someone who is looking to cut corners to make ends meet, potentially resulting in theft or dishonesty. We’re warned to beware of any unscrupulous money dealings or risky investments as these could lead to financial disaster. We need to be aware that there are some short-term financial gains to be had by taking shortcuts, but the long-term consequences of our decisions could be damaging.

Relationships

The 7 of Swords Tarot card suggests that we may be looking to take the easy way out of our relationships. This card warns us that dishonesty, betrayal, and running away from our problems can all have a detrimental effect on our emotional bonds with others. We’re advised to be honest about our emotions, communicate openly and confront our issues head on. This card also warns that if we don’t learn to trust and commit to our relationships, it could lead to the breakdown of our connections in the long term.
