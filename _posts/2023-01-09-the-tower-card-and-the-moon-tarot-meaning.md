A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and The Moon card together in particular.

The The Tower card and The Moon have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and The Moon mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The Moon tarot card can be interpreted collectively as a warning to be mindful of unwanted change and upheaval looming in your future. This could be a sudden and unexpected shift in your life, potentially bringing ruin, disgrace and even violence. As you prepare to face these potentially difficult transitions, The Moon tarot card advises that you trust your intuition and be open to voluntary changes. These shifts may be necessary to right the balance of your life, even if their true purpose and outcome are initially unclear.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Overall, The Tower Tarot card is a warning to be prepared for the unexpected. With proper preparation, you can make the most of any unwelcome surprises and come out of challenging situations with a greater sense of strength and resilience.

## What does The Moon mean?

The Moon Tarot card is associated with the astrological sign of Pisces and signifies unseen problems, voluntary changes, and trusting your intuition. In terms of health, the Moon Tarot card can signify that the physical body is vulnerable and needs to be tended to with care. On a psychological level, the card can symbolize fear, insecurity, and possible deception. It is important to pay attention to messages from the subconscious and rely on intuition to make decisions that will ultimately lead to healing.

In terms of finances, the Moon Tarot card indicates that people should not hide from financial challenges but instead find inner strength to face them, regardless of how difficult they may seem. This card can show a need to trust one’s intuition and inner guidance when making decisions about money. It may also suggest that there are hidden problems and traps associated with finances, and that one should use caution when entering into any financial transaction.

Finally, relationships can be a struggle when the Moon Tarot card appears. It can signify that partnerships may have hidden issues, and one should rely on intuition to navigate through any difficulties. It can also indicate that one’s partner may be deceiving them, or that there could be secrets that need to be uncovered in order to make the relationship successful. It is important to find balance between trust and intuition, and it can be a challenge to manage all of the different aspects of a relationship.

In summary, The Moon Tarot card can signify a need to rely on intuition and inner guidance when facing any health, financial, or relationship challenges. It is important to understand that there could be hidden problems and traps associated with all areas of life, and caution should be taken in order to make sure that desired outcomes are achieved.
