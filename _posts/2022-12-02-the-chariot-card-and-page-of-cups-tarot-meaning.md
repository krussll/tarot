A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and Page Of Cups mean together?

The Chariot and Page of Cups tarot card together suggest a journey of love and service. You have the drive and ambition to make a difference in your community, and have the power to initiate change and action. There may be some kind of vehicle you need to take the journey, whether it be physical or mental. Love, friendship and joy are all possible outcomes – you are heading into a new chapter of growth and learning that may be accompanied by romance, art, music and creativity.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Chariot and Page Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Chariot and Page Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page Of Cups Tarot card is a sign of overall wellness and vibrant health. When this card appears, it is a sign that taking care of your body is very important in the weeks ahead. This could mean getting more rest, engaging in physical activity, eating better, and even getting regular checkups with your doctor. It may also be a sign to pay attention to your mental and emotional well-being, as any feelings of sadness, lack of motivation, or anxiety should be addressed with the help of a mental health professional.

Finances

When The Page Of Cups appears in a Tarot reading, it is a sign of financial security in the future. It is a reminder that any investments you have made or are considering should be carefully thought out and vetted. The Page Of Cups is also a sign that you may need to be more frugal and save more, as unexpected expenses and difficult times can come up. Gainful employment is also a message of this card, so if you are considering a new job or business venture, it is a sign to take action.

Relationships

The Page Of Cups Tarot card signifies new and exciting relationships, especially with the potential for love. This card is a reminder to be open to new connections, as it is an opportunity for growth, learning, and fulfillment. It can also be a sign that it is a good time to work on existing relationships, as communication, understanding, and compassion are essential for any successful relationship. The Page Of Cups is a reminder that relationships should be based on trust and that it’s important to take the time to get to know someone before fully committing.
