A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The Judgment card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The Judgment and Queen of Swords tarot cards together indicate that it is time to make a life-changing decision and to be honest with yourself in order to move forward. This could be an emotional moment where you receive clarity on your life’s purpose. It demonstrates that it’s essential to make an informed decision and to have the courage to follow it through. The combination of these two cards signify that you must be self-sufficient in order to take the right actions towards your goals and to make the choices that will best serve your long-term interests. The Judgment tarot card and the Queen of Swords remind you that now is the time to make a final decision and to be truthful with yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card represents clarity, a final decision, and a wake-up call to our lives and our purpose. In terms of health, this card can be interpreted as a sign of renewal and a chance to begin anew. It can be a reminder to make healthier choices and to take a closer look at our health. We need to evaluate our current habits and check if there are areas of our health that need improvement, and take the time to make real changes that will benefit our overall physical and mental health.

In terms of finances, this card can represent a hard truth, but one that can be used to help reform the way we handle our money. It can be a reminder to let go of old destructive patterns and replace them with better ones that will allow us to grow and thrive financially. We need to take this time to assess our current financial situation, including our incoming and outgoing expenses, and see where and how we can save more. The Judgment card can also be seen as an invitation to take a closer look at our investments, as well as to start planning for the future.

In terms of relationships, this card can represent a chance to build something better. It can be a reminder to let go of any past wrongs and reach towards a healthier, more meaningful future. We need to take a closer look at our current relationships and assess how they are holding us back or pushing us forward. It can be a sign to release the negative patterns that are stagnant in the relationship, and focus more on the positive. The Judgment card tells us to be more mindful of our actions and words so that we can create healthier and more meaningful bonds with those around us.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a representation of an independent, self-sufficient woman who is known for her honesty, integrity and strength of character. She is a symbol of justice and makes decisions based on rational thinking. She is an excellent problem-solver and has the ability to cut through illusions and see the truth of a situation.

The Queen of Sword’s impact on a person’s health can be quite positive. It is a reminder for individuals to take good care of themselves and their own physical and mental health. It encourages people to be honest with themselves and with others about how they are feeling and to make decisions that are best for their overall wellbeing. This can be especially important for those struggling with their health, as it can be hard to stand up for yourself without being honest and transparent.

The Queen of Swords can also have a positive effect on finances. This card encourages individuals to handle their resources responsibly and with integrity. It promotes good decision-making that can help individuals to have and maintain financial stability. It is important to be mindful of how your choices can affect your financial situation and to not be too impulsive when making decisions related to money.

Finally, the Queen of Swords can be a powerful influence in relationships as well. It encourages honesty, communication and trust. This can be especially important in personal relationships, as it can be difficult to remain honest and open with a partner or close friend. The Queen of Swords also encourages individuals to approach relationships in a rational, logical way and to make decisions that are in the best interest of all involved. Essentially, it reminds individuals to use their head, as well as their heart, when making decisions related to relationships.
