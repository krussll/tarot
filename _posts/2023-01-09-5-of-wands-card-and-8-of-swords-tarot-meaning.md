## What does 5 Of Wands and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The 5 of Wands and the 8 of Swords tarot cards together signify a difficult situation of having to contend with multiple challenges or obstacles at once. You may be feeling restricted and isolated, as if you are trapped in a situation that you are struggling to get out of. The environment of competition around you can make it difficult to stay focused and motivated, as you will be facing off against rivals who are dead set on outdoing you. It may be difficult to make progress in this situation, and it may be easy to allow yourself to become overwhelmed or discouraged. You must remain resilient and determined to make progress, despite the pressure and obstacles that will be thrown in your way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 5 of Wands tarot card can represent a period of challenge and competition in terms of health. It can represent the struggle to stay in shape and reach fitness goals, or it can represent an actual competition such as a race or sporting event. It can also be an indication of physical disputes or struggles with illness or injury. The card reminds us that any health-related goals or challenges require a strong will and continued effort to see them through.

Finances

The 5 of Wands is a card about competition and rivalry in the financial realm. It can represent competing for the same job, jockeying for a promotion, or having to prove yourself in the industry. It can also suggest a healthy rivalry between businesses or a more hostile battle of competing agendas and interests. The 5 of Wands stresses the importance of remaining competitive and being vigilant with your finances.

Relationships

The 5 of Wands tarot card is associated with competition, rivalry and struggle in relationships. It can suggest a time of testing, such as when two people are vying for the same person’s affections. It can also indicate a power struggle within a relationship, when two people are vying for control. This card encourages us to stay balanced and aware of the tension that may exist in our relationships, as well as the potential for a positive outcome if we continue to work together.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can be indicative of poor mental health, or physical health that has been neglected. It can suggest a feeling of being trapped in one’s own body, or dealing with a chronic illness or disease. It can also be a warning sign of potential self-harm or low self-esteem. It could represent suppressed emotions, or feeling stuck in a cycle of bad habits or negative thoughts.

Finances:  
The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can be a warning sign of financial struggles. It could represent being overly cautious with money, fear of debt and feeling trapped by a lack of money. It could also be a sign that money is being squandered, or that over-spending is a problem. It could be indicative of being too afraid to invest, or feeling helpless in the face of financial hardship. It could be a sign to take a step back, assess one’s finances, and make a plan to improve the situation.

Relationships:  
The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can signify negative patterns in relationships, such as feeling stuck in unhealthy patterns of behavior, or being too afraid to make a change. It can also represent feeling isolated and cut-off from meaningful connections, and feeling like one is in a prison of their own making. It could also be a warning to take a step back and assess where the relationship is the most strained or stuck, and to put some effort into rebuilding the connection.
