## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health  
The Temperance Tarot card is a sign of good health, balance, and moderation. It suggests that we should practice moderation in all aspects of life, particularly in our health. This means eating and drinking in moderation, avoid over-exercising, and getting enough rest. The message is that if we pay attention to our diet, exercise, and sleep requirements, our overall health will improve significantly.

Finance  
The Temperance Tarot card is an indication of financial balance. This card is a sign that to have financial success, we should practice moderation and self-control with our spending and savings. It is important to put money away for when we need it, and not spend it frivolously. It is a reminder to take responsibility for our actions, and to think carefully about our investments and how to use our money wisely.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card is symbolic of harmony and balance in all relationships, whether that is family, friends, or romantic partners. This card is a reminder to practice patience, understanding, and compassion with those around us. It is important to remember that relationships should not be rushed, and the best relationships are the ones that take time to develop and grow. We should be aware of how our words and actions affect others and take responsibility for our own behavior.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

Health

The Ace of Wands Tarot card is associated with new beginnings, inspiration, and the will to take action. In terms of health, this card can mean that you are feeling energized and inspired to take steps towards better health. It can be a reminder to move your body, to implement a healthier diet, or to see a doctor to get a check-up. The Ace of Wands Tarot card can also be an indicator that your body is feeling stronger and more powerful, allowing you to take a more active role in your health instead of being passive.

Finances

The Ace of Wands Tarot card is also associated with money and finances. This card can signify a new project or idea related to making more money or improving your financial wellbeing. It can be a reminder to take steps towards creating a budget, start investing, or even to look for a new job opportunity. It can also be a sign that you are feeling motivated to tackle the financial challenges in your life, allowing you to make positive progress in this area. This card can also signify that your ideas and creativity can be used to enhance or improve your financial situation.

Relationships

The Ace of Wands Tarot card can represent new relationships and the opportunity to create something meaningful with someone new. It can signify that you are feeling inspired and excited to deepen your connection with someone or start a new connection. It can also be a reminder to create boundaries, communicate clearly, and to be open to change. This card can also be an indication that you are feeling empowered to take control of the relationships in your life, allowing you to move away from outdated patterns and into healthier connections.
