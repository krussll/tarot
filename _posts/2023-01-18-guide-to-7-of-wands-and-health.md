Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The 7 Of Wands card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 7 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 7 Of Wands.

## 7 Of Wands And Health – An Overview

The 7 of Wands tarot card symbolizes self-defense and protection, and this can be applied to health and wellness.

It suggests that we should take steps to safeguard our physical and mental health, and to defend ourselves from unhealthy behaviors.

This could be taking the necessary precautions to protect ourselves from the virus, or making sure we are taking care of our physical health by exercising and eating healthily.

It could also be taking the steps to protect our mental health by engaging in activities that bring us joy, and making sure we are taking time to relax and reconnect with ourselves.

The 7 of Wands encourages us to be proactive in protecting our health, and to take the necessary steps to ensure we remain strong and healthy.

## Upright Meanings For 7 Of Wands And Health

### What It Means For Me

The 7 Of Wands tarot card is symbolic of self-defense and protection against competition, but it can also be interpreted in regards to health.

This card can be a reminder to take care of ourselves, both physically and mentally.

It can be interpreted to mean that we need to be diligent about keeping our bodies healthy and strong in order to protect ourselves from illness or injury.

It also encourages us to take a proactive approach to our health and to be mindful of our habits, such as eating a balanced diet, getting enough sleep, and exercising regularly.

It’s also a reminder to protect our mental wellbeing, too, by taking time for self-care and engaging in activities that bring us joy.

By taking care of ourselves and protecting our health, we can be better equipped to handle any competition or challenge that comes our way.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 of Wands is a reminder that it is important to protect yourself, both physically and mentally, against any competition that may arise.

This card encourages you to take proactive steps to stay healthy.

It is important to maintain a healthy lifestyle by eating healthy, exercising regularly, and getting enough rest.

It is also important to create a positive mindset and to stay focused on your goals.

With the help of The 7 of Wands, you can stay strong and healthy and protect yourself against any competition that may come your way.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 of Wands tarot card suggests that those close to me need to be prepared to defend themselves and protect against competition.

This could be taken to mean that relationships and interpersonal dynamics require extra attention, which involves self-awareness and vigilance.

This card encourages me to be proactive in looking out for the well-being of those around me, making sure that I am keeping up with the needs of those close to me and helping them to stay strong and secure in their relationships.

By being aware of possible sources of stress and competition, I can help to nurture healthy relationships and create a safe and supportive environment for those I care about.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Wands And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed 7 of Wands speaks to a moment of reflection.

It suggests that in order to protect and nurture your physical and mental health, it is important to take a step back and take stock of where you are at.

It is a reminder to pause, to rest and to evaluate your current situation.

It is also a reminder to be mindful of your energy levels and to find a balance between pushing yourself and taking the time to recharge.

The reversed 7 of Wands suggests that in order to maintain good health, it is important to be aware of the impact that stress and pressure can have on our bodies and minds.

It is essential to take a step back and to recognize when our capacity to cope with additional demands begins to diminish.

This card is a reminder to stay mindful of our limitations, and to be aware of when we need to take a break and to put our own wellbeing first.

### What It Means For The Future

When The 7 of Wands is reversed, it can indicate a lack of self-defense and protection against physical or mental health issues.

This card can indicate a feeling of powerlessness in the face of life’s challenges, or a fear of competing with others and making the wrong decisions.

It can be a sign of surrendering to the challenges of life and not taking steps to defend or protect oneself.

Reversed, The 7 of Wands can also be an indication of weakened physical or mental health.

It can mean that the person is not taking the necessary steps to protect their physical and mental health, and instead might be surrendering to the challenges and difficulties that life brings.

This could be a sign of poor diet or lifestyle habits, or a lack of self-care practices such as getting enough rest and exercise.

It could even be a sign of mental health issues such as depression and anxiety, or of not taking the necessary steps to get the support and help needed to manage them.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed 7 of Wands appears in a tarot reading, it could be a warning sign that one has been neglecting their own health.

A strong emphasis is placed on protecting oneself and being aware that one’s health, both physical and mental, is in danger of becoming compromised.

It is time to take a step back and reevaluate the situation in order to make sure that the proper steps are taken to protect the body and mind from any unnecessary stressors.

The reversed 7 of Wands is a reminder to be mindful of one’s own needs and to prioritize oneself over any external needs or pressures.

Self-care is key, and one should be sure to take the time to relax and nurture the mind and body.

It is important to keep up with regular check-ups, listen to the body, and get plenty of rest.

Engaging in activities that bring joy, peace, and restoration is also essential in order to keep the body and mind healthy and strong.
