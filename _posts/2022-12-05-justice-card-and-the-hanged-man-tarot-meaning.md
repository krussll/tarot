A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and The Hanged Man card together in particular.

The Justice card and The Hanged Man have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and The Hanged Man mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the Hanged Man tarot card together can be seen as symbols of balance and enlightenment. The Justice tarot card reminds us that we must be conscious of the cause and effect of our choices and that we can create win-win solutions with effort and care. The Hanged Man tarot card encourages us to take a step back, to look at our lives from a different perspective, and to be willing to make sacrifices if it’s necessary to reach a greater understanding. This combination of tarot cards calls us to rise above personal tendencies and biases and to seek a higher truth that can bring peace and harmony to our lives.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and The Hanged Man mean together for your love life?

Combined, the Justice and the Hanged Man tarot cards reminds us that in our love life, we should strive for win-win situations, balanced solutions, and the truth. This means understanding the cause and effect of our actions and the actions of our partner, and the need for both of us to come out feeling equal and satisfied in the end. Taking a step back to look at the situation from a different perspective and being willing to sacrifice something of our own needs in order to make it work can help us to do what is best for everyone.

This combination of cards also draws attention to our need to be wise and discerning when faced with difficult decisions. We should take the time to examine the situation and consider the consequences of our actions, as well as those of our partner. We have to be mindful of when to stand our ground and when we need to make some compromises for the greater good, and have faith that the truth will eventually out.

Both cards also invite us to be honest and open in our expression of feelings, and be willing to talk things through with our partner. We need to be sure that we understand the other person’s viewpoint and respect the fact that they are entitled to their own opinion. The Justice card reminds us that it’s important to maintain fairness, while the Hanged Man encourages us to practice patience and view the issue from different angles.

Reaching a satisfactory outcome and a successful resolution in our relationships can be achieved by combining our natural abilities and strengths, creating a balanced relationship that works for both of us. By striving for justice, self sacrifice when necessary and a sensible approach to decision making, we can create an atmosphere of love, trust, and understanding.

The Justice card suggests that there is a need to focus on cause and effect and to use win-win solutions to gain positive results. It also implies that the truth will come out, so it is beneficial to make wise decisions based on facts and not on emotion or impulse.

The Hanged Man card conveys the importance of being selfless and using wisdom to get a different perspective when it comes to finances and jobs. In order to reach a better understanding of the situation and how to navigate it, one must step back from the situation and look at it from a bird’s-eye view. This can help them to bring balance and harmony to a situation by using their own self-sacrifice to help others.

When taken together, the Justice card and The Hanged Man card can be associated with making decisions based on the truth and what is most beneficial for all parties involved. It suggests that one must use wisdom and self-sacrifice to get a clear understanding of the situation, and then work to create win-win solutions. This could be applied to any financial or job-related situation, and it is a reminder that decisions should not be made in haste or based on emotions. Instead, it is best to take your time, think through the consequences of each decision, and make decisions that will benefit the most people.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and The Hanged Man mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and The Hanged Man the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice Tarot card can suggest a plan of action, taking responsibility for our health and making sure our decisions are right for us. It indicates a need to be mindful of our health and to invest in taking responsibility for decisions such as what to eat and how much exercise we get. It reminds us that if we are in good health, then the outcome is usually a win-win situation for both our physical and mental wellbeing.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card encourages us to take responsibility for our financial decisions. We need to weigh our options and make sure that the outcome of our decisions is financially beneficial. This may involve understanding our financial situation, considering the trade-offs and making sure to focus on our long-term goals. It suggests that if we take a balanced approach, then our financial outcomes can be positive.

Relationships: The Justice Tarot card encourages us to be honest and true in our relationships. We should strive to find win-win solutions, taking responsibility and ensuring that we are both respectful and fair. It indicates that the truth will come out, making it important to focus on building strong, mutually beneficial relationships.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health

The Hanged Man Tarot card can be an important reminder to stay healthy. It suggests the need to take a step back, assess our current state of health, and change course if necessary. It can provide the needed reminder to get an annual physical, to ask your doctor questions, to get regular screenings, or to alter your diet and lifestyle. It does not mean to abandon our health or go without care, but requires a certain amount of self-sacrifice and understanding.

Finances

For financial matters, The Hanged Man reveals the need to take a different approach. The card’s meaning suggests that a change in attitude is necessary to make sound financial decisions. This card suggests that the best way to navigate financial matters is with patience, flexibility, and understanding. You must be willing to part with certain possessions and make certain changes in order to get back on track. It encourages you to evaluate your current financial state and find ways to save money and build wealth.

Relationships

The Hanged Man also speaks to relationships. It emphasizes the need to keep an open mind while also exhibiting patience and self-sacrifice. If a relationship is struggling, it suggests to evaluate the situation from a different perspective and to consider what each partner can do to improve the situation. It is also a sign to seek a different point of view and to be willing to question your own beliefs and assumptions. Furthermore, this card encourages the need to be willing to make the necessary sacrifices and adjustments to keep the relationship strong and healthy.
