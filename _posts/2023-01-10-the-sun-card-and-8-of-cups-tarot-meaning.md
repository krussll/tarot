A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Sun tarot card, along with the 8 Of Cups tarot card, can signify an emotional victory and a successful ending to a journey. The Sun card represents joy, success, and material happiness, which indicates a positive outcome despite the need for emotional withdrawal that is present in the 8 Of Cups card. This could mean that after a difficult but necessary emotional retreat, you will find yourself ultimately in a better place with more emotional strength and clarity. It could also mean that, after leaving something behind and closing one chapter of your life, you will start a new one that brings with it greater contentment and joy.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

When looking at the Sun and 8 of Cups in the context of your love life it could also represent a time of healing, self-love and understanding. This could be a time to reflect and take some time out for yourself and your own satisfaction. As the 8 of Cups represents a withdrawal and retreat, this could be a time to take a step back and focus on yourself rather than solely on a relationship. This could be a time to assess what you need and want in a partner and to create the ideal balance in order to find the happiness and joy that you desire.

The Sun and 8 of Cups, when looked at together in the context of love, could also be an indication of a major shift in the way that you view and approach relationships. The 8 of Cups could represent an awakening, a realization and understanding of what type of relationship is truly fulfilling for you, and the Sun card could represent the joy and happiness that is to come after understanding this. You may have gone through a difficult time of withdrawal, retreat and self understanding and now, through the Sun card, you have found the courage to move on to something that is more rewarding, balanced and fulfilling.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

A good diet and a healthy lifestyle are recommended, as the Sun card indicates a period of increased energy levels and renewed physical health.

When it comes to finances, The Sun Tarot card portends prosperity, wealth, and financial success. You may experience an increase in your money and material security in the near future. You may be given an unexpected windfall, or you may receive a promotion or new job offer. You may also be able to use existing resources with great efficiency and make wise investments.

In relationships, The Sun Tarot card denotes a period of joy and happiness. Romance, fun, and celebration are encouraged. It may mean that a difficult prognosis is about to be made and that the best path for recovery is to make changes in lifestyle and accept the cards that have been dealt. The 8 of Cups can be a sign that it is time to let go of unhealthy habits and attitudes, and to look for healthier options. This card may refer to the need to withdraw from stressful or unhealthy situations, or to accept difficult changes with grace.

Relationships: The 8 of Cups card indicates a time where it is necessary to take a step back from a relationship that is no longer working. This card can indicate a need for greater self-care and for a break from the pressures of a relationship. It can also indicate the need to move on and to focus on one’s own happiness.
