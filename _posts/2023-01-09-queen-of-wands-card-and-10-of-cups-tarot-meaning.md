A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Queen of Wands tarot card and The 10 of Cups tarot card is a powerful one. It indicates a person who is confident, feisty, and sure of themselves, surrounded by a loving family who provides them with total love and support. It implies that this person has found the perfect mix of an empowering environment in which to grow and thrive. For them, family contentment is a top priority and the bond between them and their loved ones is strong and unflappable. This combination of tarot cards promises a life of joy, affirmation, and vibrant self-expression.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Queen Of Wands and 10 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health:

The Queen of Wands in tarot cards symbolizes a confident, self-assured person. When it appears in a reading related to health and wellbeing, it often indicates a person who is strong, powerful and in full control of their life and health. Physically, it suggests an individual who is fit, healthy and likely very active. They take good care of their body and prioritize their health. They are likely to have a good diet and nutrition, an exercise regime and a generally healthy lifestyle. They are likely to have a balanced approach to fitness, health and wellbeing.

Finances:

The Queen of Wands is a symbol for financial strength and security. It suggests that someone is confident and secure in their finances. They are likely to have a good understanding of how to manage their money and are likely to be successful in their financial endeavors. They are seen as being reliable, responsible and hardworking when it comes to their finances. They are able to make well-thought out financial decisions with the ability to take risks and make the most of the opportunities presented to them.

Relationships:

The Queen of Wands in tarot symbolism is a representation of a strong and confident woman who is sure of her place in a relationship. It can represent a woman who knows her worth and is not afraid to express it. She stands up for herself and is not afraid to have difficult conversations. This can make her appear assertive and unapproachable, but she is simply secure in her own beliefs and values. People often find her powerful and inspiring, but also intimidating. It is important to remember that she is someone who is also capable of love, compassion and warmth and will be faithful and loyal when in a relationship.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

For health, The 10 of Cups Tarot card indicates a physical, emotional, and mental well-being that comes from having strong, supportive relationships. It can symbolize healthy habits like exercise, diet, and rest. The card can suggest that family and friends support your efforts to stay healthy and help motivate you when the going gets tough.

When it comes to finances, The 10 of Cups Tarot card suggests that your family and close friends are a source of financial stability and security. With their support, you can come up with strategies for financial success and make sound investments that will benefit everyone. This card also indicates that you have the resources to create financial stability and security that will last.

In relationships, The 10 of Cups Tarot card is all about lasting, supportive, and loving relationships. It is a great card to draw when looking for a long-term relationship or wanting to deepen a current one. It suggests that family and friends provide a loving and supportive foundation to build a happy, healthy and fulfilling life together. With their support, you can enjoy all the joys of being part of a loving and committed relationship.
