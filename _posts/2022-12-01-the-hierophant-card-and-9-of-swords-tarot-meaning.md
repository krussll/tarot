## What does The Hierophant and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 9 Of Swords tarot card together highlight the often conflicting feelings between following tradition and expectations and our own inner turmoil. The Hierophant represents the desire to fit in, to adhere to the established order of things and to conform to the expectations and standards of our environment. The 9 Of Swords, on the other hand, signifies the fear and negative emotions that come with those expectations and trying to live up to them, such as the dreaded heartache of nightmares, the crippling anxiety, and the intense depression that can result from having to choose between our own needs and respecting the established order. Ultimately, these cards together remind us that while seeking guidance from tradition and conforming to certain standards can be helpful and comforting, we must also be mindful of our own needs and make sure that they are taken into account as well.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

**Both The Hierophant and 9 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 9 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a representation of being overwhelmed with fear and anxiety that can be so debilitating that it can lead to illness. It can signify complete mental breakdown and exhaustion that can lead to physical repercussions such as insomnia, chest pains, digestive issues, and weakened immunity. All of these physical symptoms can also be caused by stress and depression, which the 9 of Swords can often be associated with. It can be an indication of a mental health condition, such as PTSD or depression, that can lead to physical symptoms if not addressed.

Finances: The 9 of Swords Tarot card can symbolize financial setbacks and worries. It may indicate that current expenditures are higher than income, or that there’s a looming debt that can’t seem to be resolved. This card is a warning to watch out for financial traps and to take precautions to protect one’s resources. Making wise decisions about investments and long-term savings is also highly recommended. If a person has recently been laid off or is dealing with a health issue that is impacting their job, this card can be a reminder to focus on providing for themselves and their family.

Relationships: The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a representation of paranoia and worry that is damaging to relationships. It can be a sign of mistrust and a feeling of insecurity that can lead to communication breakdowns and perpetual mistrust. In an individual’s personal life, the card can indicate that one is not living up to their own expectations and is left feeling disappointed, leading them to distance themselves from those they love. Alternatively, it can be a sign of being too critical of a relationship partner, projecting one’s own issues onto the other person. It is a reminder to take some time to assess the relationship and consider where improvements can be made.
