A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The World card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The World tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card together suggest that the journey you are on is the end of a long journey that has brought you to the point of being able to complete a goal or lesson. You have been able to confront any barriers, limitations, or self-imposed restrictions that have been holding you back, in order to make progress and achieve your goals. You are finding yourself on a new path, so while this may feel like a moment of liberation, it may also bring feelings of isolation or imprisonment due to the changes in your life. Embrace the freedom while also being mindful of which paths you go down, so that you don’t end up back in the same trap.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The World and 8 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The World tarot card and The 8 Of Swords card appear together, it can mean a time of completion in a relationship. There is a lesson to be inferred from the restrictions and obstacles that have been put in place, which may be either of an emotional, physical, or spiritual nature. This could mean the end of a long-term relationship or the successful conclusion of an emotional journey that has been fraught with difficulties.

The World tarot card suggests a time of travel and exploration, while the 8 Of Swords tarot card warns of the dangers of self-imposed restrictions and isolation. This could mean that the ending of a relationship is something that needs to be accepted and embraced as a natural and necessary part of life, or it could be a warning that staying in a restrictive situation is causing more harm than good.

Together, these two cards can be a sign that it’s time for a change. It might be time to separate from a partner, if it’s been determined that it’s no longer beneficial for either of you, or it could mean that it’s time to explore new opportunities and possibilities. It can also be a warning to avoid self-imposed restrictions which can be damaging to relationships.

The World tarot card and the 8 Of Swords tarot card come together to remind us that relationship endings are often part of the grand journey of life. It’s best to take the lessons learned, accept the completion, and move forward with a newfound understanding of yourself and your needs. In the end, this newfound journey can ultimately lead to a better and more fulfilling love life.

### What does The World and 8 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When looking at the Tarot cards, The World and The 8 Of Swords, together they indicate completion of your financial journey, but a need to be mindful of any isolation or restrictions you might place on yourself. It can mean a reward or a change in the way you earn money. Travel or emigration could be on the cards but be aware that you may need to undertake it alone, or have to make some internal changes before progress can be made.

On the financial side, these two cards can signify a successful completion of a financial goal or lesson that you had been working towards. You may have achieved something significant, like your first promotion or a pay raise. It could mean that you are ready to embark on a journey and look for a new job that allows you to increase your income and provide you with more opportunities.

However, The 8 Of Swords can indicate that you need to be mindful of any self-imposed restrictions, such as feeling stuck in a particular area and not wanting to take the risks necessary to progress. This card could be warning you to break away from the mindset that comes with feeling that you are stuck in one place. It is important to take the time and make the effort to let go of any fear or anxiety that you have around taking on a new challenge, or stepping out of your comfort zone.

The World and The 8 Of Swords together urge you to make the most of the opportunities that come your way and not to let any negative thoughts limit your potential. This is the time to be brave, to spread your wings and to fly towards the goal that you have been working towards. With courage and confidence, you can complete your financial journey and close the cycle.

### What does The World and 8 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The tarot cards The World and the 8 of Swords suggest that your health is undergoing a process of completion and self-imposed restrictions. This could mean that you’re getting to a point in your health journey where you’ve reached the end of one stage and are preparing yourself to move onto the next. You may be completing a plan of action, such as a diet or exercise program, or you may have finished a course of medication and ready to move on.

The 8 of Swords appears in this combination of cards to remind you to take a moment of personal reflection and self-care. This can be especially important when you’re transitioning between stages, as the emotional and psychological implications of a health journey can be just as important as the physical ones. You may need to spend some time on your own to get a sense of where you are now, making sure to listen to your intuition and focus on what you feel is most important for your health.

By combining the messages of The World and the 8 of Swords, you can take this opportunity to create a plan for continued success in your health journey. While it can be tempting to jump into the next stage immediately, it’s important to take the time to really consider what you need to do to be successful. This could range from scheduling self-care time to creating a detailed plan of action to achieving your next goal.

Ultimately, the tarot cards The World and the 8 of Swords suggest that you have come to an important level of completion in your health journey. However, it’s important to take the time and energy to truly lay the groundwork for the next phase and ensure that you are setting yourself up for success. Taking the time for self-reflection and self-care will be invaluable as you move into the next stage of your health journey.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

Health: The World Tarot card is a symbol of renewal and an indication that you have reached the end of a cycle and are now ready to move on to a new phase of your life. This card can represent physical and spiritual health, as it signifies that you have achieved a level of mental and physical balance. This could mean that you have completed any treatments and therapies necessary and are now ready to begin a new period of healing and growth. On the other hand, it can also indicate that you should take care of your health before embarking on any new projects.

Finances: The World Tarot card signifies completion, success, and the attainment of goals. This card is a reminder to be mindful of the financial decisions that you are making and to make wise investments. The World Tarot card indicates that you are taking the necessary steps to secure your financial future, but it also serves as a reminder to not become complacent. Money and financial success come with responsibility, so it’s important to stay focused and maintain your financial goals.

Relationships: The World Tarot card is a reminder that relationships, like all things, must come to an end in order for new ones to begin. This card can signify a new relationship, or the end of an old one. It can also signify the need for balance and stability in a current relationship. The World Tarot card is a sign that it is time to make the changes necessary to bring harmony and happiness back into your relationships. It is an indication that you are ready to move on and experience something new.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card brings a message of possible health-related issues that are caused by self-imposed restrictions, isolation and imprisonment. This can refer to physical health but more likely it relates to mental health. Self-imposed restrictions often lead to depression, anxiety and even a feeling of hopelessness. Mental health is heavily impacted when these restrictions are combined with isolation and imprisonment, which can cause a person to become so overwhelmed that they can no longer make decisions and are unable to take care of themselves.

Finances  
Financially, the 8 of Swords Tarot card warns that self-imposed restrictions and isolation could lead to an overwhelming debt burden. This could come from not being able to budget properly, or from not being able to get out and pursue new opportunities. It could mean missing out on financial opportunities that would have otherwise been available. In extreme cases, it could also mean a complete loss of income due to being unable to meet the demands of a job or other obligations.

Relationships  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card also warns of strained relationships. Self-imposed restrictions, isolation, and imprisonment can make it hard for someone to emotionally connect with others. They may be afraid of talking to new people, or feel intimidated in social settings. They may also feel too overwhelmed to even think about making and sustaining meaningful connections. This can lead to a feeling of loneliness and can be overwhelming for the other person in the relationship, who may feel like they are not being heard or valued.
