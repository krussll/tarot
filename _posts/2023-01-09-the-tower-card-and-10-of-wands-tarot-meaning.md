## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

In terms of finances, the Tower tarot card can signify the risk of bankruptcy, loss of investments, the need to make difficult budgeting decisions, or perhaps sudden bills that must be paid. Any of these events can be stressful and leave one feeling helpless, especially if the person is unprepared for such a drastic change.

Finally, the Tower tarot card can represent the sudden collapse of relationships. This could be the end of a romantic relationship, or a disagreement among friends or family members. This card often signals that a relationship is no longer serving its purpose, and that a new course of action must be taken in order to find a better balance.

Ultimately, the Tower tarot card can represent a powerful transition in many aspects of life. It can bring with it disruption, upheaval, and often times distress. However, it can also be a call to face your fears, take risk, and grow in the process.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 10 of Wands Tarot card indicates feeling overwhelmed and worn out due to the immense amount of stress and pressure that is being put upon you. When this card appears in a reading, it is a warning that too many burdens and responsibilities can quickly lead to physical and mental exhaustion, including burnout. It is important to recognize when you are feeling overworked and make time for yourself to rest and relax. This is especially true if you are dealing with chronic health conditions, as it can be difficult to manage the pressures of life without taking care of yourself.

Finances

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can be a sign that you are facing financial stress due to the amount of responsibilities you are taking on. It is important to remember that money isn’t everything, and that it is important to find balance in your life. Spend time focusing on how you can make your finances work in your favor, and how you can make the most of your money. Being mindful of your spending can help you manage your finances more effectively and ensure that you are not overburdened.

Relationships

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can indicate that the relationships in your life may be feeling the strain of too much pressure and stress. It is important to take a step back and look at the bigger picture in order to realign your priorities and focus on what matters most. Too often we become so caught up in life that we forget to nurture our relationships. Take some time to show your loved ones that you care and that you are dedicated to making your relationship work.
