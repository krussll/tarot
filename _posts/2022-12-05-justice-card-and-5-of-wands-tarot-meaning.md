A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 5 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Justice card and 5 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 5 Of Wands mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 5 Of Wands tarot card together could be interpreted as a reminder that life is a balance of struggle and victory. You may be able to reach a win-win situation if you are willing to go through the necessary challenges and do the work to overcome any obstacles. The truth will come out through your efforts, and by embracing fairness and accepting responsibility for your actions, you can establish a sense of justice and success.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and 5 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

When the Justice tarot card and the 5 of Wands tarot card come together in your love life, it can indicate a very complicated dynamic. In terms of relationships, Justice is a card that encourages us to work things out fairly, to reach the truth and an eventual win-win situation. Because of the obstacles and rivalries that can be created by the 5 of Wands, it could be harder to come to this sort of balanced agreement.

In a situation where there’s competition for the affections of a third party, for example, this card combination might suggest that it is best to be honest about each other’s feelings. The Justice card suggests that no one should be allowed to gain an unfair advantage from the truth of the situation, and that everyone should be clear about what is happening with respect and fairness. It also implies that through dialogue, you and your rival might be able to come to a balance.

It could also be that the 5 of Wands is speaking to some kind of emotional struggle between partners. If one or both of you feel as if you are competing with each other, it might be that the struggle is stemming from underlying issues between you. The Justice card suggests that these issues can be addressed and that an understanding can be reached, but it will take effort on both sides.

However, if the 5 of Wands is instead more strictly interpreted as adversity in the sense of challenges to be overcome, the Justice card can suggest that through honest and amicable dialogue, you and the other person might be able to find mutually beneficial solutions. It implies that by being open and taking responsibility for your actions, you might be able to make it through whatever battles you have together with a better understanding of each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

The card serves as a call for responsible decision-making and warns against taking unnecessary risks or overspending. ## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

The 5 of Wands Tarot card is a card of conflict and competition, representing challenges and obstacles that must be overcome in order to find balance. Those who draw this card may find themselves in a season of strife, where rivals and adversaries lay barriers in their path.

When it comes to health, this card suggests that there may be obstacles in your attempt to achieve your health goals. You may be finding it difficult to make the right choices when it comes to diet and exercise, or may be struggling to find the motivation to stay on track. The 5 of Wands encourages you to remain determined and to ultimately find creative solutions to continue moving forward.

Financially, this card suggests that you are likely to face challenges when it comes to managing your money. You may be tempted to overspend or gamble with your finances, as the competition of others can lead to a sense of ‘keeping up with the Joneses’. This is a time to prioritize financial goals and to take a sensible approach to budgeting and investing.

In terms of relationships, this card comes with a warning that there may be temptations to compete for power or control within the relationship. Conflict within relationships can be healthy when controlled, but it is important to ensure that any competition does not become too extreme. It is possible to use this card to find creative solutions to disagreements and to prioritize communication and understanding.
