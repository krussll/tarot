## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that when it comes to health, one may be overly focused on their health and well-being, seeking to take every precaution and measure to ensure that their physical and mental health are maintained in optimum condition. Home remedies and holistic treatments may be favored over medical advice, with one taking all possible measures to stay healthy. This can be a good thing, as long as the focus is not only on preventative measures and one makes sure to seek out expert medical advice when necessary.

When it comes to finances, the 4 of Pentacles suggests that one may be very focused on ensuring long-term financial security. This can manifest as an obsessive desire to save, an unwillingness to take risks, or a reluctance to let go of material possessions. It is important to find a balance between short-term and long-term financial goals. There is nothing wrong with wanting to build a solid financial foundation, but there is also a need to assess the present and to recognize that the present is just as important as the future.

In relationships, the 4 of Pentacles can point to a desire to cling or to control. One can become overly possessive, wanting to hold on to the relationship and to keep it static. It is important to be open to exploring new possibilities in relationships and to recognize that relationships are a process of growth, change and evolution. It is important to remain open and flexible, allowing both people in the relationship space to explore and to have their own separate lives.

Overall, the 4 of Pentacles Tarot card can point to a desire to cling or to control, whether it be in the realm of health, finances or relationships. It is important to find balance and to recognize that balance is key in any life endeavor.
