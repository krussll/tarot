A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Magician card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Magician card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Magician and King Of Cups mean together?

The Magician and The King of Cups together represent focused creativity and the ability to turn visions into reality. The King of Cups is a supportive leader who is tolerant and empathetic, while The Magician is able to take inspired action and make things happen. This combination suggests that you have what it takes to achieve your goals, but you will need to be patient and put in the work to make it happen. Keep your eye on the prize and don’t let anything get in your way. With determination and a positive attitude, you can make your dreams a reality.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Magician and King Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Magician mean?

The Magician Tarot card is all about taking focused, inspired action in order to turn your vision into reality. This is an incredibly powerful card when it comes to manifesting your desires, whether they relate to your health, finances or relationships. When you work with the energy of The Magician, you can expect miracles to happen in all areas of your life.

If you are seeking to improve your health, The Magician Tarot card can help you to take the necessary steps to manifest your desires. It is important to be clear about what you want to achieve, and then take inspired action towards your goal. The Magician is a powerful ally in helping you to create health and wellbeing in your life.

If you are looking to manifest abundance in your life, The Magician Tarot card can help you to take the steps needed to make your dreams a reality. Whether you are seeking financial abundance, or abundance in your relationships, this card can help you to take inspired action and create the life you desire.

The Magician Tarot card is also a powerful ally when it comes to manifesting loving, healthy relationships. If you are seeking to attract a soulmate or improve an existing relationship, The Magician can help you to take the necessary steps to create the love you desire. This card reminds us that we are all connected, and that we must work together in order to create harmonious relationships.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

The King Of Cups is a supportive leader, which means that he is always looking out for the best interests of those around him. He is tolerant of differing opinions and is always willing to lend a listening ear. His empathy allows him to understand the feelings of others, and he is always quick to offer a helping hand.

When it comes to health, The King Of Cups is always looking out for ways to improve the wellbeing of others. He is quick to offer advice and support, and is always willing to go the extra mile to ensure that everyone is healthy and happy.

When it comes to finances, The King Of Cups is a responsible and generous leader. He is always looking out for ways to improve the financial situation of those around him, and is quick to offer advice and support. He is also generous with his own resources, and is always willing to help others in need.

When it comes to relationships, The King Of Cups is a caring and supportive partner. He is always looking out for the best interests of his loved ones, and is quick to offer advice and support. He is also a great listener, and is always willing to lend a shoulder to cry on.
