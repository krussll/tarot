A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and The 6 Of Swords tarot card together suggest that you are at a point in your life where a final decision and clarity are needed for you to move forward. It is a wake-up call for you to take ownership of your life’s purpose and make a change. The 6 Of Swords tarot card speaks of a necessary transition that is required of you to take you to the next chapter of your life. It may mean relocating or moving physically but also emotionally and spiritually. The two cards together suggest a time of reflection and change.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Judgment and 6 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Judgment tarot card in combination with The 6 Of Swords tarot card can be seen as a call to rise up from the depths of a stagnant situation in your love life. This card emphasizes clarity and a final decision, and the 6 of Swords calls for a necessary transition from wherever you are now to a place of greater fulfillment. This transition is both emotionally and geographically necessary. It could mean the choice between two partners, or between continuing the same patterns of negativity or rising above them. It could also mean that you need to make a difficult but necessary relocation away from current pain in order to find true love.

The Judgment tarot card indicates that you have used the experiences of your past to reach a clarity and understanding of what you need in a relationship. You have come to a point of realization where you know your life’s purpose and you can bring yourself closer to it. You can now make the necessary moves that will bring you closer to your true love. The 6 Of Swords tarot card symbolizes the relocation and mental journey that you are taking in your search for love. It may not be an easy transition, or a journey without hardships, but it is essential for finding deep and meaningful love.

The combination of the Judgment tarot card and the 6 Of Swords tarot card therefore symbolizes a journey of clarity, a period of transition as you move from one place of comfort to another, and decision-making that will bring your closer to your true love. This is a time to be brave, be open and allow the changes to free you from the past and into a more meaningful future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Judgment and 6 Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Judgment and 6 Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card means clarity in terms of health, finances and relationships. This card is a wake-up call to reconsider our current life choices and to think deeply about our life’s purpose and our ultimate goals.

When it comes to health, the Judgment Tarot card signals that the time has come to take a long, honest look at the current state of our health, and how we can make changes to ensure a healthier life. It also encourages us to consider the health of our minds and to prioritize self-care and relaxation. Financial decisions should also be evaluated with a clear and objective eye, with an understanding of the long-term effects that they may have. The Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to take the time to carefully consider our finances and make decisions based on a long-term strategy.

With relationships, the Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to honestly evaluate the relationships in our lives and to choose carefully the people we spend our time with. It encourages us to be conscious of what’s best for us and to prioritize those relationships that bring out the best in us. It also reminds us to be honest about our needs in relationships and to walk away from those relationships that don’t promote our overall well-being.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take time for yourself, to focus on your mental and physical health. It may be necessary to take a break from your life and rejuvenate. Look at your current health regimen and decide if it is truly serving you. Maybe it’s time to research different approaches and make changes as needed. It’s okay to let go of habits that aren’t serving your health any longer.

Finances:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a period of transition and change. When it comes to your finances, it’s time to be strategic. Take a close look at your budget and trim the unnecessary expenses, while also setting aside money for a rainy day. It’s also a great time to look for new and innovative ways to make more money. Research business opportunities or job markets, where you can apply your skills and experiences.

Relationships:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card suggests that now is the time to have honest conversations with your loved ones. Take some time to reflect on the relationships that you have and see if it’s time to move on from some of them. It might be time to clear away harmful relationships and create new, healthier ones. Communicate your goals and values to your current partners and see if it’s possible to continue in a new direction. This is also a great time to open yourself up to new people and social activities.
