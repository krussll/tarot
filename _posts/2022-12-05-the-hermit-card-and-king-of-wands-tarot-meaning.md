However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does The Hermit and King Of Wands mean together?

The Hermit and King of Wands tarot cards represent two sides of the same coin – on the one hand, an inward journey of personal discovery and illumination, and on the other, a courageous and powerful leader. These seemingly contrasting archetypes remind us of the importance of balance between introspection and action, and that to become a strong leader requires us to first go within and discover our own personal truth. The Hermit and King of Wands blend together to form a powerful energy that can help you harness your strength, courage, and charm to become a bold leader in your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and King Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card is an excellent indicator of focusing on health and wellbeing. This card suggests that one should look inward and focus on addressing any physical and emotional health issues before trying to solve them externally. This can be accomplished by taking time for self-reflection and meditation, accepting the limits of our bodies and getting to the root of any illnesses or physical issues. This card also encourages us to take a proactive approach to our health, as well as focusing on preventative measures and lifestyle habits that will improve our overall wellbeing.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card suggests that we should take a step back and look inwardly at our finances. This card is an indication of the need to be honest and confront our financial situation head-on. It is a reminder to be mindful of our spending and to create practical strategies and plans to ensure financial health. This card is a reminder to look for a deeper and more meaningful understanding of our finances, and to take the time to reassess our current financial goals and values.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card highlights the importance of taking time for self-reflection and personal growth. This card is a reminder to take a step back from relationships and look within for answers. It is a sign that we should take time for ourselves to understand our own needs and values without sacrificing our sense of self. It encourages us to reconnect with our inner voice in order to make decisions and set boundaries that will foster healthy relationships. This card suggests that it is important to take the time to truly listen to our own intuition and to make decisions that are driven by our own values.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

Health

The King of Wands Tarot card is a powerful sign of physical health and vitality. This card is associated with an unrivalled strength and courage to overcome obstacles that stand in the way of personal health. Health-wise, this card stands for a powerful sense of self-efficacy and discipline, which can lead to better eating habits, improved exercise routine, and better rest. It can also be seen as a reminder to take responsibility for one’s own health and to prioritize it above all else.

Finances

The King of Wands is a sign of ambition and success when it comes to finances. This card speaks of the courage and discipline to pursue financial success and security. It can indicate that with determination and a focus on achieving financial goals, there is no end to what one can do. The King of Wands is a card of big dreams and hard work, and it is often seen as a sign to make a bold move that will bring greater abundance and prosperity.

Relationships

The King of Wands Tarot card is a sign of power and charm when it comes to relationships. This card stands for a leader in relationships, and one who is willing and able to assert his authority while still being charming and nurturing to his partner. The King of Wands speaks of courage and a willingness to take risks in order to pursue a fulfilling and lasting relationship. He may not always win, but has the strength and courage to get back up and try again. This card encourages us to take responsibility for our own relationships, during both the good times and the bad.
