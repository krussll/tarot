However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Wands and 10 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 4 of Wands tarot card is often associated with a time of health and wellbeing. It is a card of celebration, joy and health, and when it appears in a reading, it often signifies a period of recovery or healing. This card can represent a time of rebuilding, restoring balance and finding inner health. It often suggests that the querent is at a place in their life where they can focus on their physical, mental and emotional health without distractions.

Finances:  
The 4 of Wands tarot card often indicates that finances are stabilizing and improving. This card can suggest that the querent is entering into a financially secure state and is capable of meeting their financial needs. It can signify that the querent is receiving or will receive money or support from unlikely sources. This card can also point to good fortune when it comes to financial investments or ventures.

Relationships:  
The 4 of Wands tarot card is often seen as a card of reunion, family, and celebration. When it appears in a reading, it can suggest that the querent is coming together with family, friends, and loved ones. It can signify harmony in the home, and it can point to the strengthening of existing relationships. The 4 of Wands tarot card can be an indication of the start of a new relationship, or signify the strengthening of an already existing relationship. It can be a sign that the querent is in a period of healing and repair in their relationships.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 10 of Wands tarot card can indicate a period of physical and mental exhaustion. This card often signals that you are carrying a heavy burden that is beginning to take its toll on your health. If you are feeling overworked and overwhelmed, you may need to take a much needed break from whatever responsibility has been weighing you down. Taking care of yourself mentally and physically is essential for maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Acknowledging and respecting your energy levels can go a long way in helping you stay out of emotional and physical burnout.

Finances:

The 10 of Wands tarot card can represent a period of financial strain. This card can indicate that you are feeling overwhelmed by financial obligations that are becoming very difficult to manage. You may be feeling your resources are stretched thin, causing you to become over-burdened. This card is asking you to direct your attention to your spending and look for ways to better manage your financial resources. Because of the strain being placed on your resources, now is the time to practice restraint and discipline when it comes to your budget.

Relationships:

The 10 of Wands tarot card can also represent a period of strained relationships. This card can often symbolize feeling overburdened and restricted by relationships, whether it is with a romantic partner or another close relationship. The card may be asking you to take a step back and consider if all of your relationships are helping you to feel supported and fulfilled. It may be time to reevaluate what dynamics are healthy, and which are oppressive and not beneficial to you in the long run. The card is asking you to take a hard look at all of your relationships and prioritize what can bring you the most joy and stability.
