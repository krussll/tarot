A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 8 Of Swords tarot card in combination with The 3 Of Cups tarot card can indicate that you are feeling trapped in a situation, feeling like you are isolated and have self-imposed restrictions, but that you have a great circle of support in the form of close friends and family. They can be your source of strength, helping you through this period of self-imposed imprisonment. Celebrations with your friends can also be a reminder of the joy of life, lifting your spirits and helping you to break free from this cycle of feeling restricted.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

They may feel as if their physical health is holding them back from reaching their fullest potential. When it comes to financial matters, The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can indicate that someone is feeling trapped in their current financial situation. They may be feeling limited by debt or lack of resources and cannot find a way to make progress. They may feel stuck and unable to move forward financially. This can cause frustration and tension within relationships if one partner is feeling financially restrained while the other is able to pursue their own goals.

Lastly, when it comes to relationships, The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can indicate that someone is feeling restricted by the boundaries of their current relationship. They may feel trapped in the relationship and unable to make progress. This can lead to feelings of loneliness and isolation if the person does not feel as if they are free to express their true feelings or wants. It can also lead to feelings of mistrust and lack of communication if the person believes their partner does not understand them or their situation. Ultimately, The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can indicate that someone needs to re-evaluate their situation and find a way out of their current restrictions in order to move forward with their life.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 3 of Cups tarot card is an invitation to treat health with care. It signifies that now is the time to take care of oneself, to celebrate and to seek support from friends and family. When it comes to health, it is important to focus on prevention, self-care and healing. The 3 of Cups encourages regular exercise, healthy eating habits and good sleeping habits. It also points to the importance of finding balance and taking the time to nurture oneself and tend to physical and emotional needs.

Finances: Financially, the 3 of Cups speaks of balance and moderation. It is a time to carefully consider expenditures and to make sure that the financial needs of the present and future are met. The card also suggests assessing any investments that have been made and adjusting them accordingly. It is important to focus on long-term financial stability, rather than short-term gains. Also, it encourages seeking the counsel of financial experts or family and friends if necessary.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, The 3 of Cups encourages strong bonds and mutual support. It is a card of friendship, companionship and emotional connectedness. The 3 of Cups implies that relationships should be celebrated, cherished and developed. It suggests nurturing bonds with family and friends, paying attention to the emotional needs of others, and maintaining a sense of understanding and openness with loved ones. It also speaks of taking time to celebrate the joys of relationships and making sure to create quality time with those closest to you.
