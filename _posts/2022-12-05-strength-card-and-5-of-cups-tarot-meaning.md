## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and 5 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

Finances: This tarot card is an emblem of perseverance and inner strength. When it comes to finances, the Strength card encourages us to take ownership of our financial situation, be it good or bad. It can remind us to stay steadfast in our pursuit of financial success, while being mindful of the needs of others. This can include things such as budgeting and creating a sensible financial plan. The Strength card is a reminder that with inner strength and courage, we can tackle financial issues, big or small.

Relationships: The Strength Tarot card signifies that relationships of all kinds, including platonic and romantic, require strong emotional foundations. This can be achieved through trusting and being vulnerable with one another. It also serves as a reminder of the need to extend kindness to oneself and to others. Having the courage to speak up and tackle issues head-on, rather than burying them, is essential to making relationships work. With the Strength card, we are reminded of our inherent resilience and power to create and sustain healthy relationships.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 5 Of Cups Tarot card can indicate that physical health is wavering. This could manifest through exhaustion, illness, or chronic pain. It may be that the person has been neglecting the need to nurture and care for themselves, and this neglect may have led to physical issues. Emotionally, the 5 of Cups suggest that the person is feeling overwhelmed with regret, sadness or grief. They may be drowning in their sorrows and unable to see any practical solutions to their problems.

Finances: Financially, the 5 of Cups Tarot card can indicate losses, reverses and a lack of motivation. The person may be feeling overwhelmed and unable to find their way out of a financial pit. They may have made some bad decisions or gambled recklessly in the past, and now they are facing the consequences of those actions. Because of their emotional turmoil, they may find it difficult to focus on the present and make wise decisions about their money.

Relationships: The 5 of Cups Tarot card can suggest that relationships are strained. The person is feeling abandoned and unloved and has difficulty expressing their pain and vulnerability. They may not be getting the emotional support and understanding they crave from their loved ones and this can lead to feelings of alienation and disconnection. The person may be carrying around a lot of resentment and anger, which can make it difficult for them to communicate their needs and for others to understand their situation.
