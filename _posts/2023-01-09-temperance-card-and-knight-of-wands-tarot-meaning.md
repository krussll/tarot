## What does Temperance and Knight Of Wands mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The Knight Of Wands tarot card together suggest a need to find harmony between wild impatience and creativity. The Temperance card is a sign of balance and moderation, urging you to be patient and maintain self-control when things don’t go your way. The Knight Of Wands, on the other hand, symbolizes an unbridled and passionate spirit, encouraging you to embrace your creative energy and use it to your advantage. Together, these two cards invite you to find a way to combine their energies and strike a balance between the wild and the calm. With patience and self-control, you can bring your creative ideas to life, harnessing both your passionate energy and balancing it out with thoughtful, practical planning.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

It encourages you to exercise self-restraint and not overindulge in unhealthy habits such as smoking, drinking, and overeating. It gives you the strength to resist the temptation of short term gratification and reminds you to take care of your body and mind in order to maintain your wellbeing. Practicing temperance in your health can help you to stay healthy and in good shape both mentally and physically.

Finances: The Temperance Tarot card encourages financial prudence and balance. It reminds you not to overspend, to avoid debt, and to make sure you are setting aside savings for the future. It suggests that you practice wisdom and self-control when it comes to managing money and suggests that you save rather than splurge. It also advises patience, so that you can avoid making impulsive decisions that could negatively impact your finances.

Relationships: The Temperance Tarot card encourages harmony, balance, and mutual understanding in relationships. It reminds you to practice self-control and reign in your emotions. It also encourages patience and diplomacy when dealing with difficult individuals, rather than succumbing to anger or reactivity. It suggests that you learn to cultivate peace in every interaction, no matter how difficult the situation. Finally, it encourages open communication and understanding in order to keep the relationship strong and healthy.
