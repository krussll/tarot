A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and The 9 Of Cups tarot card mean that you have clarity on your life’s purpose and that your wishes will be granted. This is a sign that it is time to make the final decisions for your future and to take action towards achieving your goals. It also symbolizes that you will be blessed with material abundance, good health, and a sense of personal joy and satisfaction. Overall, these two cards signify that if you listen to your inner voice and stay true to your own path, you will be able to manifest your dreams and create a rewarding and fulfilling future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card is all about clarity, a wake-up call and your life’s purpose. This card can have an important impact on our lives, particularly when it comes to health, finances and relationships.

Health: The Judgment tarot card is a reminder that we all have choices when it comes to our health. It encourages us to take responsibility for our physical and mental wellbeing, and make the right decisions for ourselves. It can be a warning sign to take better care of ourselves and to listen to the advice of our doctors and other healthcare professionals. It also symbolizes that we have the power to make positive changes to our lifestyle, such as exercising more and eating healthier foods.

Finances: This tarot card is an indication that it is time to take a closer look at our finances. It encourages us to be mindful of our spending and to look at ways to save money. It can be a wake-up call to appreciate the value of money and the need to plan for our financial future. This card can also represent the possibility of having to make difficult financial decisions, such as making a budget and cutting back on certain expenses.

Relationships: The Judgment card can signify a time for reflection when it comes to our relationships. It encourages us to take a step back and evaluate the situation. It may be a warning sign to pay attention to the advice and opinions of others. It can also indicate a need to make important changes within our relationships and to work on communication, compromise and understanding. This card can also reveal when it might be time to let go of certain relationships, or to make amends with those we may have wronged.

The Judgment Tarot card is an important reminder to take stock of our health, finances and relationships. It is a call to action and an invitation to find clarity and purpose in our lives. It is a reminder that we are in control, and that we can make positive changes if we make the right decisions.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card signifies that wishes can come true. It’s a card of abundance, fullness, and contentment in all aspects of life and as such, can bring great health, financial stability, and lasting relationships.

Health

Health is a precious gift and when the 9 of Cups appears in a reading, it indicates that the querent is in excellent physical and mental health. It suggests the querent is taking good care of themselves, is eating right, exercising regularly, and has a positive outlook on life. With the 9 of Cups comes a period of physical and emotional renewal, allowing the querent to experience life to its fullest without worry of illness or ailment.

Finances

Finances are also an important part of life and the 9 of Cups serves as a reminder to be wise and mindful with money. Those who find this card in their reading should be confident in knowing that their financial future is looking bright. Stability is the key to financial success, so the querent should focus on creating a budget and sticking to it. Additionally, the 9 of Cups encourages the querent to be vigilant when it comes to investment decisions or any other kind of financial planning.

Relationships

When the 9 of Cups appears in a reading, it is an omen of strong, lasting relationships in all areas of life. This card is a reminder to nurture and cherish the relationships that already exist, while also living life with an open heart. Fostering meaningful relationships with friends, family, and even strangers is a key component to experiencing a full and satisfying life. The 9 of Cups also encourages the querent to speak their truth and strive for honesty and understanding in their relationships.
