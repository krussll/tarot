A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together?

The Queen Of Swords and The Page Of Cups tarot card together represent a unique combination of characteristics that can lead to great success and joy. The Queen Of Swords implies you’re a self-sufficient and honest person, while The Page Of Cups suggests you are a studious kid who is just starting to experience the joys of love, as well as a willingness to volunteer your time. This combination of traits indicates you are passionate, hard-working and have strong values that guide your choices. By leveraging the power of these two cards, you have the potential to make the most out of your life and embark on a journey of success and happiness.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Queen of Swords can be seen as a symbol of mental and physical health. She encourages strong mental and emotional resilience, as well as clear communication about any health issues one may have. She teaches us to take responsibility for our health and reminds us to stay honest with ourselves and our medical care providers. Looking after oneself, both physically and mentally, with clarity and honesty can create optimal health.

Finances:  
The Queen of Swords can be seen as a symbol of wise fiscal responsibility and financial management. She brings with her the power of logic and clear-headedness in making financial decisions. She encourages self-discipline, focus and long-term planning. She can also be seen as a symbol of total honesty in financial matters. She reminds us to be conscious of our spending and to be clear and honest when involved with money.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Swords can be seen as a symbol of strong, honest and open communication in relationships. She encourages us to speak our truth clearly and thoughtfully, and to listen actively to the perspectives of others. She also brings with her the quality of self-sufficiency and independence, reminding us to maintain balance in relationships. She encourages us to be honest with our partners and to be mindful of the needs of both parties in the relationship. She teaches us to be mindful of our actions and to never make assumptions about what our partner may think or feel.

It can stand for an opportunity for a person to take care of their body and nurture their mind, body and spirit. The Page of Cups Tarot card can also signify a time for a person to explore new job opportunities, gain new skills, and push themselves to achieve success.

Relationships: The Page Of Cups Tarot card can signify the beginning of a new relationship or the strengthening of an existing one. It can represent a period of communication and understanding, where both parties can share what they want and need. It may also be a sign of a time of learning and exploration, as the couple continues to grow and develop together. Ultimately, the Page of Cups Tarot card encourages couples to create a foundation of honest and open communication.
