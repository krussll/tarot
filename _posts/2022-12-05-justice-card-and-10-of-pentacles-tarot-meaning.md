A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card together represent the idea of leaving a legacy and the long-term rewards that come from making wise decisions in life. The Justice card speaks to seeking out causes and effects and creating win-win solutions, which ultimately leads to a positive outcome. The 10 Of Pentacles card symbolizes retirement, inheritance, and the lasting effects of one’s actions. Together, the cards point to a future that is prosperous and secure, but only if the individual is willing to seek out truth, diligently pursue justice, and make wise decisions.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 10 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

For Health:

The Justice Tarot card is all about cause and effect when it comes to health. It suggests that when we take proactive measures to maintain our health, we can expect the positive results to manifest relatively quickly. This means that small choices like selecting a healthy snack over junk food, taking a daily walk, or getting enough sleep can have a profoundly beneficial effect on our physical and emotional health. It also suggests that when we don’t make the best decisions for our health, the consequences can be swift and long-lasting. The Justice Tarot card encourages us to ensure our health is a top priority, as any medical issues or ailments can quickly compound and cause major disruption in other areas of our lives.

For Finances:

The Justice Tarot card suggests that when it comes to finances, our choices have a significant impact on our financial future. This card encourages us to make mindful, wise decisions when it comes to our spending and saving habits. It encourages us to think carefully before making any major purchases and to make sure that our financial goals are realistic. We should also be mindful of how much money we are spending on our day-to-day expenses and strive to create a balance between saving and investing. The Justice Tarot card is a reminder that the choices we make today have a direct impact on our financial future; the decisions we make now will determine our financial success or failure tomorrow.

For Relationships:

The Justice Tarot card encourages us to be honest and just in all of our relationships. It suggests that we should strive to listen as much as we speak, and to approach any conversations or disagreements with calm, level heads. It also encourages us to think twice before taking any unnecessary risks or making decisions that could have unintended consequences. The Justice Tarot card suggests that relationships can benefit from communication and compromise, as the truth carried forward through negotiation helps to ensure both parties feel respected and valued. This card encourages us to be kind and understanding in any relationship, as our words and actions can create a ripple effect that can drastically alter the course of our relationships.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

When it comes to health, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card signifies that a person is entering a time of stability and equilibrium between body, mind and spirit. This period of health should bring a sense of security and independence. It is a sign of good fortune that a person’s health is likely to remain strong and relatively unchanged for the foreseeable future.

In terms of finances, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that a person is in a secure financial position. This is a period of economic success and prosperity. With financial stability, a person can focus on bigger picture concepts such as leaving behind a legacy or investing in the future.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that a person is likely to be enjoying a period of peace, contentment and security. It suggests that a person is in relationships that are based on mutual respect and understanding. This is a period of gratitude for the people in your life, as well as a time to invest in the relationships that are important to you.
