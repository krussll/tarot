It could mean that it’s time to move on, to start fresh in a new place, with a new perspective. This transition could be necessary to protect your well-being and emotional security, or to escape fraudulent behavior. As you move away from falsehood and dishonesty, you can focus on making positive strides in a much brighter direction.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords tarot card often means a necessary transition or relocation, both literal and figurative. It is typically associated with a sense of change, a need to move away from what we’ve been doing, to something new and untouched.

On the health front, this card can be a reminder that a change in lifestyle or perspective may be necessary for healing. It can suggest that you should take a break from a certain activity or routine and try something different. This can be a highly beneficial shift for your health, as it can lead to mental clarity, energy, and general optimism. It can also mean to more carefully consider boundaries and to prioritize your own needs and wellbeing.

When it comes to finances, the 6 of Swords can be a reminder that sometimes a fresh start can be a good thing. It can be a signal to consider creative solutions, such as investing, budgeting, or finding a new job. It can also remind you that sometimes risks have to be taken in order to make a big change, and it’s worth it.

In relationships, this card can signify that a change is coming or is necessary. It can mean to re-evaluate boundaries, communication preferences, or roles. It can also mean to re-connect with long distance friends, or to be open to meeting someone new. In short, the 6 of Swords can be a reminder to approach relationships with an open heart and a willingness to make space for positive changes.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The Seven of Swords Tarot card typically hints at dishonesty, betrayal, and theft. This card gives insight into how these matters may be reflected in aspects of health, finances, and relationships.

Health: The Seven of Swords is a warning to remain vigilant and proactive when it comes to health matters. The card suggests that it is important to stay aware of health alerts, regularly check in with medical professionals, and take care of yourself properly. At its worst, the card could represent neglect due to being overwhelmed with the demands of stolen resources, such as time and money.

Finances: The Seven of Swords brings up the potential for theft and betrayal when it comes to finances. It could be a warning to pay attention to financial documents and be aware of potential scams. It could also indicate running away from financial responsibility or recklessly making financial decisions.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, the Seven of Swords could be a warning to be extra mindful to avoid betrayal or dishonesty. It could represent a need to be honest with yourself and others about personal needs, boundaries, and wishes, so that relationships remain healthy and loving.

Overall, the Seven of Swords Tarot card is an important warning to pay attention to any red flags and remain aware of potential issues in various areas of life. It is important to be mindful of the signs of dis honesty, betrayal, and theft, and commonly represents a need to be extra diligent in personal and interpersonal matters.
