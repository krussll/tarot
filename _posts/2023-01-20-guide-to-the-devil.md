## The Devil – An Overview

The Devil tarot card is a symbol of the darker side of our nature. It is a reminder that we must be aware of our innermost desires and how they can lead us down a path of addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust. It is a warning to not let our egos take control of our lives and to be careful of the choices we make. The Devil tarot card also serves as a reminder that we have the power to rise above our base desires and to make choices that will lead to healthier and more fulfilling lives.

## Upright Meanings Of The Devil

### What It Means For Me

The Devil tarot card indicates that I may have a tendency to become addicted to certain things, whether it be materialistic goods, obsession with sexual pleasure, or even something as simple as a desire to accumulate more and more wealth. I may be prone to envy those who possess more than I do or become greedy and materialistic, fixating on the physical world and the objects that it holds. The Devil card also suggests that I could become too focused on the pursuit of pleasure and pleasure seeking activities, leading to an unhealthy lifestyle. Ultimately, The Devil card is a warning sign to pay attention to my impulses and to strive for balance in my life.

### What It Means For The Future

The Devil tarot card warns of the potential to be consumed by the material world and succumb to unhealthy addictions. It cautions against being seduced by the allure of greed, envy, and obsession. The card serves as a reminder that life should be enjoyed in moderation and that any kind of addiction, whether it be to material possessions, sex, or something else, can lead to ruin. This card speaks to the need to find balance in your life and to be aware of the consequences of your actions. The Devil tarot card can also be seen as a sign that you may need to reassess your lifestyle and make changes to avoid becoming trapped by your own desires.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Devil tarot card symbolizes the dark, negative aspects of human nature that can often lead to damaging behavior. Those close to me who show signs of addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust could be in the grips of something that is hard to break away from. If I notice these traits in people I am close to, it is important to reach out and offer support, as these issues can be difficult to overcome alone. It is also important to protect myself from being influenced by these negative traits, in order to maintain a healthy and balanced relationship.
