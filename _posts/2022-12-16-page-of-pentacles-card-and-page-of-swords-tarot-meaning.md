A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Page Of Pentacles tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card combined represent an adventurous and inquisitive child who may be starting a new job or receiving a raise. Money news and gossip may play a role in this new venture, and the child should be aware of the dangers of prying eyes and facts that may not be true. The combination of these two cards point to a bright future for the curious kid, provided that they take the time to be patient and to be wise with their choices.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

The Page Of Pentacles Tarot card, which is associated with the Earth element, suggests that even though you are going through some difficult times, if you pay attention to the signs around you and be patient, the efforts you make will eventually be rewarded. This card can indicate that health and stability will be achieved if you approach your physical and emotional well-being with care and discipline.

In terms of finances, the Page Of Pentacles Tarot card means that good news is on the horizon. This card often turns up when a person is offered a new job or a raise in salary, suggesting a new and improved financial situation. It is also associated with prosperity and abundance and encourages a desire to save rather than splurge.

In terms of relationships, the Page Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that if you are open and honest with those around you, your relationships will blossom and grow. This card encourages a steady and stable approach to partnerships, where both parties work hard and make compromises. If you focus on the present moment, you will be able to form genuine and lasting connections with those around you.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

On Health

The Page of Swords Tarot card can represent a period of curiosity and exploration in regards to health. This could mean a time to take stock of any health-related matters, researching and pursuing new options, treatments, and ideas. It may also signify a change in strategy or habits, such as you’ve had a health scare and are now looking to make more positive lifestyle choices. Be mindful of your energy levels and make sure to take good care of yourself during this journey of self-discovery.

On Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card suggests that finances may be a time of gathering information and making smart decisions. Do your research – read the news, ask for advice and find out the facts – so that you can make the best plans. This period could also bring tidying up any loose financial ends, from paying off debts to sorting out bank accounts. Don’t be afraid to ask for help if you’re struggling with a financial issue – do what you can and trust that you’ll get the answers that you need.

On Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card could mean that relationships are about looking for the truth right now. This could be the case if there’s something that needs to be uncovered or discussed. It could also signify an honest conversation with a partner or friend on a challenging topic. Don’t be afraid to speak up and express your feelings. Be wary of gossip, though – it’s a slippery slope and the truth could easily be distorted. Use your intuition and make sure to be kind when you communicate.
