## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

The 5 of Swords Tarot card has a wide range of meanings when it comes to health, finances and relationships. In terms of health, the 5 of Swords can indicate that a person is not taking good care of themselves. It may represent tension, illness, and general physical discomfort. It could even suggest an individual’s health is being adversely affected by the presence of a bully or an aggressive person in their environment. Alternatively, it could also signify that they are engaging in self-destructive behaviors, such as overindulging in substances to cope with stress.

When it comes to finances, the 5 of Swords may mean that a person is at risk of financial losses from theft, fraud, or other manipulative tactics. It could indicate there is a hostile person or influence in their life who is putting their finances at risk. It could even be a reflection of their own habits and attitudes towards money management and financial security. In any case, it suggests that a person may need to review their financial decisions and make any necessary adjustments in order to protect their financial wellbeing.

Finally, the 5 of Swords can represent an abusive relationship of some kind. It could reflect a situation where one person uses intimidation and violence to control, manipulate, or otherwise harm their partner. It could also refer to a one-way relationship where one person has all the power and the other doesn’t have any say. It could even be a relationship where the individuals are in competition with each other, always competing to be the dominant force. In any case, it suggests that a person needs to reassess their relationship and make any necessary changes to protect their own wellbeing.

Overall, the 5 of Swords Tarot card can represent a wide variety of meanings across different aspects of life. In terms of health, it can indicate physical problems and self-destructive tendencies. With finances, it can signify the potential for financial losses from theft or fraud. And in terms of relationships, it can represent a power imbalance and abusive dynamics. Ultimately, it serves as a warning to pay attention to one’s own wellbeing and to make any necessary changes in order to protect it.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Eight of Swords Tarot card speaks of self-imposed restrictions in matters of health, including a fear of being open to change, growth and progress. It suggests that we limit ourselves and our options by our own thoughts and beliefs, creating a kind of ‘mental prison’ of sorts. We may feel trapped, stagnant, and unable to make the necessary shifts and changes to experience health, wellbeing and happiness. In this instance, it is important to work on shifting our focus onto the ideas of self-care, self-acceptance, the ability to recognize our own needs, and the courage to take action and make healthy choices.

Finances:  
When The Eight of Swords Tarot card appears in a financial reading, it suggests a feeling of being stuck, unable to move forward and make progress. There can be an overwhelming sense of pressure and limitation, where our options seem incredibly limited. There may be a need to stop, reassess our options, and look for potential opportunities. It is essential to focus on the idea of being conservative with our resources, finding ways to be more mindful and responsible with our spending, and in general operating from a position of stability and sustainability.

Relationships:  
The Eight of Swords Tarot card speaks of self-imposed isolation in matters of relationships. We may feel trapped in a situation, unable to move forward and express our true feelings. It is important to take a step back and ask yourself what is really going on, and how can you find a resolution or healing. There is a need to trust yourself and trust your own feelings, and making sure your voice is being heard. It is also essential to look for ways to break through any self-imposed boundaries, or to break away from any unhealthy patterns or habits of co-dependence. It is time to be honest and open with yourself, and work on creating relationships of trust, understanding and compassion.
