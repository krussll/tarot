Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Temperance card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Temperance, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Temperance.

## Temperance And Health – An Overview

The Temperance tarot card means that balance and harmony are important to overall health.

It encourages patience and self-control when it comes to our habits and behavior, as this can lead to lasting health benefits.

Instead of feeling overwhelmed by the many aspects of health, the Temperance card advises us to take things one step at a time.

Self-discipline and moderation are key when it comes to eating and exercise, both of which are essential to our health and well-being.

The Temperance card also reminds us to take a break when needed and to give ourselves grace in times of difficulty.

Ultimately, the Temperance card encourages us to find balance and harmony in all aspects of our lives, which will lead to greater health and happiness.

## Upright Meanings For Temperance And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Temperance tarot card symbolizes balance and harmony in life, qualities that are essential for health and wellness.

It encourages self-control and patience, and urges us to take time to slow down and appreciate the delicate balance between our mind, body, and spirit.

It is a reminder to practice moderation and self-discipline, to create a well-rounded lifestyle that nourishes our physical and emotional health.

It reminds us to pace ourselves and consider the consequences of our actions.

This card encourages us to listen to our intuition and identify our needs for self-care.

Ultimately, it reminds us that health and wellness is a journey, and one which requires the right balance of nourishment and discipline.

### What It Means For The Future

The Temperance tarot card is a reminder that all things need to be in balance in order to achieve true health.

It encourages patience and self-control, as any major changes need to be taken slowly and deliberately.

This card also suggests that there is still hope, as long as I am willing to make adjustments to improve my health.

I need to be mindful of my emotional, mental, and physical well-being, as they are all equally important.

Emotional stability will help me to manage stress and maintain balance in all areas of my life.

I need to be mindful of how I use my time and energy, and practice moderation in all things.

With self-discipline and dedication, I can achieve a more balanced life that allows me to enjoy lasting health and happiness.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Temperance card is a reminder of the importance of balance and harmony in all aspects of life, especially in relationships with those close to us.

It encourages us to remain patient, practice self-control, and take the time to evaluate the health of our relationships.

This can mean taking a step back to reflect on the state of our relationships and being mindful of how we interact with those around us.

This card is an important reminder to be conscious of our own emotional health and the wellbeing of our loved ones, and strive for a healthy and balanced relationship with those close to us.

## Reversed Meanings For Temperance And Health

### What It Means For Me

When The Temperance tarot card appears in the reversed position, it speaks to a lack of balance and harmony in one’s life.

It also points to a lack of self-control and patience.

In terms of health, this indicates that an individual is likely feeling out of balance and may be struggling with issues related to a lack of self-discipline.

This can manifest itself in different ways, such as difficulty sticking to a healthy diet and exercise routine, or having a mental health issue that leads to emotional outbursts.

It is important to note that the reversed Temperance card is a sign that a person needs to take a step back and reevaluate their lifestyle and state of health.

The reversed Temperance card also suggests that an individual is lacking in moderation and may be prone to overindulgence in either physical or mental activities.

This can lead to a range of potential health risks, such as the development of compulsive behaviors like overeating, under-exercising, or over-exercising.

It is important to take the time to develop healthy habits that will help to restore balance and harmony in all aspects of life, including physical and mental health.

Taking the time to practice self-care and create a healthy lifestyle can help to prevent further health complications.

### What It Means For The Future

When The Temperance card is reversed, it can indicate a lack of balance, harmony, patience, and self-control in the querent’s life.

This could be both physically and emotionally.

Physically, this lack of self-control could manifest itself in the form of bad habits such as overindulging in unhealthy behaviors such as smoking, drinking, drug use, and overeating.

Emotionally, it can refer to a person’s inability to regulate their emotions, leading to outbursts of anger, depression, and anxiety.

When The Temperance card is reversed in a health reading, it suggests that the querent is out of balance and needs to take steps to regain control.

This could mean making lifestyle changes such as exercising regularly, eating a healthy diet, and managing stress levels.

It could also mean engaging in activities that promote inner peace, such as meditation, yoga, and mindfulness.

By taking the necessary steps to regain a balanced lifestyle, the querent can lead a healthier and happier life.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Temperance card is reversed, it suggests that you may be out of balance in your life, and that this imbalance is causing health issues.

You may have been neglecting aspects of your physical and mental well-being, such as getting enough sleep, exercising regularly, and eating a healthy and balanced diet.

This card can also be a warning that you need to take a step back and assess the state of your health and make positive changes accordingly.

It can also indicate that you may be attempting to overcompensate for your health issues by taking extreme measures in an attempt to improve your health.

This could include attempting to control every aspect of your diet or lifestyle, or relying on substances like alcohol or drugs to cope with stress or emotional issues.

This card reversed is an indication that you need to find a healthy balance before you can truly experience good health.

It is important to remember that health is holistic and requires attention to all aspects of body and mind.
