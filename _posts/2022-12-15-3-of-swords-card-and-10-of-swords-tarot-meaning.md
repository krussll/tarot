## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 3 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 3 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is a difficult card when it comes to health, as it typically denotes major physical or emotional issues and possible surgery. This is not a positive card for those in the medical field and warns about the possibility of major illnesses, surgery, emotional issues or physical trauma. Those with health issues should take extra care to ensure their emotional, mental and physical well-being.

Finances

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is equally as indicative of financial losses or unexpected expenses. It can refer to a loss of a job, an unexpected financial burden, or bankruptcy. Those dealing with financial issues should be extra careful to not overspend and remain vigilant in budgeting and saving.

Relationships

The 3 of Swords Tarot card can indicate heartbreak and divorce, though it can also portray issues within a relationship. The card may suggest a relationship on the brink of dissolution and can refer to any type of relationship, be it a romantic relationship or a business one. The card could be an indication of a deep rift or misunderstanding between two people that may need to be addressed and repaired. Those involved in a relationship should not ignore any warning signs from this card, and must be prepared to work hard in order to keep the relationship intact.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health

The Ten of Swords tarot card can signify a health crisis that has reached its breaking point. It could be a physical health crisis such as a chronic illness or injury that has reached a point where healing is no longer possible. It could also be a mental health crisis such as severe depression or anxiety that has reached a point where recovery is impossible. In either case, the individual would need to accept that they have reached rock bottom and they will likely have to learn to live with the health problems they have.

Finances

The Ten of Swords tarot card can symbolize a financial crisis that has reached its breaking point. It could be an individual’s financial situation that has gotten so out of control that all attempts to get back on track have failed. It could also be a business venture or investment that has tanked to the point where there is no hope of recovery. In either case, the individual may need to accept that they have reached rock bottom and will have to make significant lifestyle changes in order to survive.

Relationships

The Ten of Swords tarot card can symbolize a relationship that has reached its breaking point. It could be a romantic relationship that has become so toxic and filled with conflict that it can no longer be salvaged. It could also be a friendship that has become so strained that it can no longer be repaired. In either case, the individuals involved may need to accept that they have reached rock bottom and move on with their lives without the other person in it. If they choose to continue trying to repair the relationship, they will likely have to make significant changes and put in a lot of hard work.
