However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 6 Of Swords card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 6 Of Swords tarot card and the 9 Of Swords tarot card together signify a difficult journey that involves necessary transition and relocation. This transition could be emotionally difficult, as it is often accompanied by feelings of anxiety, nightmares, grief, and depression. However, it is important to remember that these feelings will eventually pass, and the journey towards a better life may be difficult but ultimately rewarding.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords Tarot card is a card of transition and relocation. For health, it suggests that you are making a serious move to improve your health, either through lifestyle and diet changes or through a more pronounced transition such as relocation or medical treatments. Whatever you decide, the 6 of Swords encourages you to make the necessary changes to achieve better health and happiness.

For finances, the 6 of Swords suggests a movement towards a more balanced financial situation. You may be transitioning from debt to abundance, investing in yourself and your assets or relocating to a new place with a higher salary. Whatever the case, this card encourages you to make the necessary changes to improve your financial wellbeing.

For relationships, this card implies a need for change in order to progress and grow. This could be changing the expectations in a relationship, relocating to be closer to family and friends or finding a new job that allows for better balance in your life. Ultimately, you will need to make the leap and make the necessary transitions and relocations to ensure that your relationships reach their full potential.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health: When it comes to physical and mental health, The 9 Of Swords Tarot card can represent lingering, intense nightmares or struggles with anxiety or depression. These issues may manifest as insomnia, trouble concentrating, or even a persistent negative outlook. The individual may be having a difficult time in processing their thoughts or emotions, leaving them feeling paralyzed with fear and unable to move towards solutions. This may also cause feelings of guilt, shame and worthlessness, leading to an overall feeling of stagnation.

Finances: In terms of finances, the 9 Of Swords Tarot card can represent a lack of confidence in financial decisions, leading to an inability to take action for fear of making the wrong choices. This difficulty in trusting oneself can lead to a lack of progress and growth as the individual avoids taking any necessary steps. Additionally, this card can also represent a feeling of helplessness; despite doing all the right things, the individual may feel that success is not necessarily forthcoming, thus leading to feelings of despair and distress.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, The 9 Of Swords Tarot card can signify fear, anxiety and feelings of hopelessness. This can manifest itself in the individual avoiding forming close connections with others, or shutting themselves off completely. This card can also represent a constant fear of being judged by others, or a fear of being misunderstood and rejected. These feelings can lead to a feeling of loneliness and isolation, as well as a fear of being judged or hurt. This can have an impact on relationships of all kinds, whether intimate or familial.
