A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Pentacles card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Pentacles card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 8 of Pentacles in combination with the 9 of Swords can represent the challenges of mastering your craft and enjoying your employment, while at the same time struggling with nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression. It can be difficult to stay focused on your goals and take pleasure in your work when the darkness of these emotions creeps into your life. This card combination can serve as a reminder that while it may be difficult, it is possible to find balance between mastering your craft and dealing with your mental health. With hard work and dedication, you can continue to excel in your chosen field, while also managing your mental health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 8 Of Pentacles and 9 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is an incredibly positive card, one that speaks to the industrious spirit within each of us. It is a message that if we persist in our hard work and diligence, we will be rewarded for our efforts. This card is a reminder to stay focused and keep developing our skills, so that we can reap the rewards of our dedication.

For health, the 8 of Pentacles card is all about taking the time to invest in ourselves. It reminds us to be good to our bodies, to exercise regularly and eat healthy, balanced meals. Taking the time to invest in ourselves, to practice good self-care and live a healthy lifestyle can have many positive effects on our overall wellbeing.

For finances, the 8 of Pentacles card encourages us to view money and wealth as something that we can cultivate through hard work and sustained effort. It suggests that if we take the time to focus on and improve our financial life, we will be rewarded for our efforts. It is about developing skills, being resourceful and wise with our finances, and being consistent and disciplined in our approach to money.

For relationships, the 8 of Pentacles card is a reminder to stay focused, dedicated and committed to our loved ones. It is a reminder to work hard to maintain and strengthen our relationships, to be reliable, trustworthy and honest. The 8 of Pentacles card reminds us to take the time to invest in those closest to us, to nurture our bonds and to be faithful and loyal to each other.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 9 of Swords signifies physical and emotional exhaustion, having been overwhelmed by worry and fear. It suggests a feeling of helplessness and an inability to face problems. This can manifest in physical symptoms such as headaches, insomnia, and loss of appetite. It can also impact mental health, leading to deep depression and an unshakable sense of dread.

Finances: The 9 of Swords can suggest a looming financial crisis. It can portend difficulty in managing debts or unexpected expenses that the person can’t manage. It can also foreshadow job loss or financial instability, forcing the person to turn to desperate measures to make ends meet.

Relationships: The 9 of Swords suggests that a relationship is in an unhealthy state. It can signify a feeling of hopelessness and stagnation, with the person feeling unable to move forward. It can also suggest that the person is overwhelmed with fear and worry of their partner’s loyalty, or of the future of the relationship. The person might struggle to be loving or communicative, instead keeping their feeling of fear and isolation to themselves.
