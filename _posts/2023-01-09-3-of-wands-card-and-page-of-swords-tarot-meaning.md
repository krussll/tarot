## What does 3 Of Wands and Page Of Swords mean together?

It may be that you have the chance to have meaningful conversations with others, but it is important that you do so with an open and honest mind. Furthermore, this opportunity involves some level of expansion, likely involving travel and the exchange of ideas. The Page of Swords tarot card warns that you should be aware of the gossip, spying, and prying eyes that could get in the way of your success, as well as the truth that needs to be faced in order to make progress. This will require courage and discipline, two things that this card and the 3 of Wands can bring in order for you to make the most of this opportunity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

In terms of finances, the 3 of Wands Tarot card suggests an opportunity for growth and success. It encourages us to take risks, seek out investments, and join forces with others for greater financial stability. This card can also be a reminder to take stock of our current financial situation and to start planning for the future.

When it comes to relationships, the 3 of Wands Tarot card encourages us to seize any opportunity for growth, both within and outside of our current relationships. This card encourages us to be open to different forms of connections, whether it be developing new friendships, starting or joining an organization or team, or exploring different types of relationships. At the same time, it also reminds us to have an open and trusting relationship with ourselves. ## What does Page Of Swords mean?

The Page Of Swords Tarot card represents a young, sprightly and ambitious individual who is eager to learn and explore the world around them. This card can symbolize the need to stay vigilant and alert in all aspects of life, especially in relationships, finances and health.

When it comes to health, The Page Of Swords Tarot card can represent being proactive and vigilant, looking out for any signs of a health issue before it becomes serious. This card can also signify being honest with yourself and your doctor about any health concerns and being willing to ask questions and seek advice.

In finances, The Page Of Swords Tarot card can mean being conscientious about reading the fine print, looking for any hidden fees or charges, and being committed to researching and comparison shopping to get the best deal. It can also represent having an eye for business opportunities, being willing to take risks, and being open to creative solutions and strategies to increase your financial security.

When it comes to relationships, The Page Of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the need to be honest and forthcoming with your partner. This card can signify keeping an eye out for any signs of emotional or physical infidelity and speaking up about honest communication. It can also mean being able to openly discuss any issues and being willing to listen and learn from your partner.
