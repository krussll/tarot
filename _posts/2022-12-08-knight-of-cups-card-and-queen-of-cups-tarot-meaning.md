## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Knight Of Cups and Queen Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

This Knight embodies a balance of both, as they are often portrayed as keeping a healthy diet and maintaining a level of emotional acceptance. This card can also be interpreted as a reminder to take care of yourself mentally, physically and spiritually. On a spiritual level, The Knight of Cups encourages us to pay close attention to our thoughts and feelings, as they are integral to our overall health and well-being.

Finances: This card is often a sign that something positive is coming financially. You might be open to new opportunities, or you might be presented with an exciting and advantageous financial opportunity that can be developed into something profitable. When The Knight of Cups appears in a Tarot reading, it is a reminder to be mindful of your finances and to seek out constructive ways to improve your income.

Relationships: The Knight of Cups Tarot card speaks of romance and commitment. It could represent a knight in shining armor, swooping in to sweep you off your feet and make all your dreams come true. This card symbolizes that you are open to new relationships, that it is safe to express your emotions, and that you are ready to give and receive love.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card means that healing and nurturing energy is present in all aspects of a person’s life. This card signals that taking care of oneself and finding ways to relax, practice self-care and stay healthy will bring abundance and peace. This card encourages a person to take control of their own health and make decisions that will bring about an optimal outcome. It also remembers them to be kind to themselves and take care of any mental, physical, or emotional imbalances that are present in their life.

Finance:  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card encourages a person to approach their finances from a place of balance and abundance. It reminds them that money should not cause stress but rather be used to express their creativity and help bring others joy. This card indicates that the person should be mindful when dealing with money and to not be too impulsive when making financial decisions. It also advises them to stay focused on their goals, learn to budget and save, and look for ways to use their money to benefit those around them.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card symbolizes expressing one’s love and compassion for those around them. It encourages a person to nurture the important relationships in their life and to look for opportunities to give and receive love. This card also reminds the person to practice unconditional love and understanding in their relationships, while also honoring each individual’s boundaries and feelings. It also emphasizes being open and honest in communication, being present in the moment, and allowing others to speak their truth without judgment.
