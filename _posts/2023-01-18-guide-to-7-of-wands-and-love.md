Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 7 Of Wands card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 7 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 7 Of Wands.

## 7 Of Wands And Love – An Overview

The 7 of Wands tarot card is a reminder to be proactive and protect your health.

It encourages us to take a defensive stance when it comes to our wellbeing and to be mindful of any potential threats that could threaten our physical, mental, and emotional health.

This card reminds us to stay vigilant with our health and to be aware of any potential risks that could arise.

It also encourages us to take the necessary precautions to protect ourselves from harm, such as eating a balanced diet, exercising regularly, getting enough sleep, and managing our stress levels.

Ultimately, this card serves as a reminder to take care of our health and to always be prepared for any potential threats that could arise.

## Upright Meanings For 7 Of Wands And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Seven of Wands tarot card is a reminder that even in love, you need to defend yourself.

In relationships, you must be mindful of your own boundaries and protect yourself from any competition that may arise.

It is important to remember that you have the power to choose how you respond to difficult situations and to make sure you are not taken advantage of.

Don’t be afraid to stand up for yourself and be assertive if necessary.

With the Seven of Wands, you can learn to better evaluate your relationships and determine when it is time to defend yourself and your love.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 of Wands tarot card is a sign that you may need to protect yourself against any competition in the realm of love.

Your love life could be subject to obstacles from outside forces and you need to be prepared to fight for the relationship you desire.

It is important to remember that love is a journey, and that any challenges you face should be seen as opportunities to grow and become stronger.

With focus and determination, you will be able to overcome any obstacles that may arise and find the love that you are searching for.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 of Wands tarot card is a symbol of self-protection when it comes to love and relationships.

It speaks of the need to be vigilant and protective of the love and affection of those close to us.

It encourages us to safeguard our relationships from outside competition and to strengthen our bonds with our loved ones.

It also reminds us that we must be ready to defend against any potential threats that may come our way, whether it be from another person or simply from our own insecurities.

This card serves as a reminder to always remain firm and sure of ourselves so that we can protect and nurture the relationships that we have.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Wands And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The 7 Of Wands is reversed in a reading of love, it can suggest that you may be feeling insecure in your relationship and are feeling the need to protect yourself.

You may be uncertain of the future of your relationship and are taking a defensive stance in order to protect yourself from potential hurt or disappointment.

This defensive attitude can manifest itself in the form of defensiveness when your partner tries to talk to you about something or a general reluctance to open up and be vulnerable.

You may feel like it is safer to keep your guard up, rather than taking a chance and allowing yourself to get close.

In order to make your relationship work, it is important to be able to let go of any fear or doubt and be open to communicating and connecting with your partner.

It is essential to find a balance between self-protection and vulnerability, so that you can both experience the love and intimacy that you are looking for.

It is okay to be wary of the future, but it is important to also take a chance and trust that the connection you share with your partner is strong and resilient enough to last.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed 7 of Wands appears in a Tarot reading about love, it suggests that you may be feeling defensive and resistant to new relationships.

You could be worried that any potential partners will not measure up to your standards or that they will not be able to offer the kind of commitment you desire.

This could be causing you to put up walls and close yourself off from potential love interests.

The reversed 7 of Wands indicates that it’s time to relax and trust in your own judgement when it comes to relationships.

You can recognise when a potential partner is not right for you, but it’s important to also be open to new possibilities.

If you’re willing to take a chance and let someone in, you could be amazed by the strength and depth of the connection you can form.

Let go of your protective barriers and take a leap of faith into the unknown.

You never know what could be waiting for you on the other side.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed 7 of Wands can indicate a lack of self-defense and a lack of protection from competition.

It can mean that your closest relationships are vulnerable, and you may feel helpless in the face of the competition that exists.

You may find yourself easily pushed aside and taken advantage of by those who are more powerful or influential.

You may also start to feel overwhelmed by the challenges and obstacles that come with trying to maintain a healthy relationship.

This card can also suggest that you’re failing to use your own strengths and abilities to defend yourself and protect your loved ones.

You may be too quick to give in to others’ demands, or too afraid to stand up for yourself and your loved ones.

If you’re feeling like your relationships are not as strong as they could be, this card suggests that it’s time to take a stand and fight for those close to you.

It’s time to take back the power and be assertive in the face of opposition.

With courage and perseverance, you can overcome any challenges that come your way and create a more loving, secure relationship with those around you.
