A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The 2 Of Pentacles tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card suggests that you have to make a decision between two options, and must weigh all of your options before making a decision. It also suggests that it is important to be aware of the gossip and truths that can influence your decision; be sure to do your research and know the facts before making a decision. The combination of these two cards encourages you to discern what is true and what is false before making your choice.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Perhaps you’re at a crossroads and there are several paths you can take in your relationship. The Page Of Swords symbolizes gossip, prying eyes, and the need for truth. Therefore, make sure to look for honesty in the people around you and be wary of any unsolicited advice. It may be that your friends or family have their own agendas and aren’t giving you the full picture. Take the time to step away from the situation and think logically about what’s best for you and your relationship.

The 2 Of Pentacles encourages you to take a break from the chaos of everyday life and focus on your current situation. This card suggests that the best way to make a wise decision is to understand the pros and cons of each option. Make sure to be honest with yourself, as well as your partner, when evaluating the different paths you can take.

Ultimately, the combination of The 2 Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords means that your decision should be based on logic and truth. Take the time to reflect on the different choices you have and be open to outside perspective. Follow your intuition, but remain aware of the people around you who may have their own hidden motivations.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 2 Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles mean?

Health

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card signifies a major decision when it comes to health. This usually means a choice between two treatments, two paths or two pieces of advice. This could be anything from deciding between a traditional approach or the latest controversial alternative health treatment. The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card may also point to considering the risks and rewards of a different lifestyle choice such as exercising more regularly or following a strict diet. It asks us to weigh our options carefully and make the choice that is right for us.

Finances

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that when it comes to finances, it is time to make a decision. This could be anything from deciding which investments to make or which mortgages to choose, to considering whether to put some money aside for a rainy day fund or a vacation. This card is a reminder that weighing our options and making a decision is an important aspect of financial planning.

Relationships

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card can represent two potential relationships. This could be a choice between two potential partners, two roads of a relationship, or two opinions of a problem within a relationship. This card indicates a need to carefully consider what is important for you and your partner and choose which route to follow. It also encourages us to communicate openly with our partner and make sure our needs and wants are met.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health

The Page of Swords tarot card can be a warning sign regarding overall health. It warns that mental health and energy levels may be affected. On the physical side, it indicates that you may have to pay attention to health issues, such as colds, fatigue or minor aches and pains. Taking preventive action at this stage can help to keep illnesses from developing or worsening. It can also be a sign to pay close attention to any health advice or guidance from health professionals.

Finances

When it comes to finances, the Page of Swords is a reminder to take time to analyze and assess financial decisions. It encourages you to search for the facts and find out the truth about any financial investments or offers before making a commitment – so no decisions should be made in haste. Consider all information carefully, look at the bigger picture and weigh up any potential risks and rewards.

Relationships

In relationships, the Page of Swords can be a reminder to be honest and understanding with friends, family, and partners. It can be a sign that you need to ask questions and get to the truth of any potential misunderstandings. It can also act as a warning to not be too judgmental or gossipy, as this can have a negative effect on relationships. Take time to get to know someone and consider their individual opinions, values and feelings. Maintain an open mind and be prepared for different beliefs, behaviours and lifestyles.

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw 2 Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords has helped you.
