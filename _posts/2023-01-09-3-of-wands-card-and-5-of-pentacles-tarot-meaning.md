A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 3 of Wands and the 5 of Pentacles when together suggest that you may experience a shift in your relationship with money, that is providing you with concerns in regards to your health, security and financial stability. The 3 of Wands urges you to build a strong team or network of people which can help to alleviate your worries and potential financial losses. It also encourages you to keep your eyes open for potential opportunities that may arise as a result of your new ventures and collaborations. Whilst the 5 of Pentacles cautions against any impulsive money decisions, this card also encourages you to find a way to travel and explore, to find yourself again, and to open your eyes up to a world of possibilities.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

In terms of health, the Three of Wands card suggests that creating a healthy lifestyle and improving your physical health is within reach. As far as finances are concerned, this card can mean that you are finally able to take the next step in your financial journey. When it comes to relationships, the Three of Wands card suggests a time of growth and expansion. ## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The Five of Pentacles tarot card often symbolizes the struggle to maintain physical and emotional health, financial security, and meaningful relationships. This card is indicative of a challenging time where financial instability, illness, and feelings of alienation can be experienced.

When it comes to health, the Five of Pentacles tarot card may suggest that an individual or family is suffering from a physical or mental illness. This card can imply that the healing process is long and difficult, as the person or family members may be financially unable to access adequate medical care. Physical health can be adversely affected by inadequate nutrition, lack of rest, and a lack of preventive health care due to financial constraints. Poor mental health, such as anxiety, depression, and insomnia, is also often associated with the Five of Pentacles due to the pressures of financial or emotional insecurity.

When it comes to financial security, the Five of Pentacles tarot card can be an indication of a difficult financial situation. This card may suggest that individuals or families are struggling to pay their living expenses, such as rent and utilities, as well as any medical expenses incurred due to illness. As a result, saving money may be exceptionally difficult and debt may pile up, leading to more stress and financial insecurity.

The Five of Pentacles tarot card can also be an indication of strained relationships. Individuals may feel isolated and unable to rely on their friends or family members for emotional and economic support. This card can also suggest that an individual or family is experiencing distressing events that prevent them from participating in social and cultural activities, leading to isolation and loneliness.

In conclusion, the Five of Pentacles tarot card suggests a difficult period in terms of health, finances, and relationships. While this card may illuminate a particularly challenging time, it also serves as a reminder to seek out the necessary resources to help improve one’s physical and mental health, financial security, and relationships.
