A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 2 Of Swords mean together?

The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 2 Of Swords tarot card together mean that you have put in a lot of hard work and you need to practice patience. Even though it may feel like success is delayed, it is time to make a decision and compromise to see the reward of your hard work come to fruition. All of the time and effort you have spent working towards a goal is about to pay off, but you must be willing to make a decision and make concessions if necessary in order to reach the desired outcome. Remember that with patience that success can come and be the reward for your hard work.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 2 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 2 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

It encourages us to take consistent action and put in the effort, as success will come in its own time. Long-term health is not instant and it is important to keep striving for progress and avoid shortcuts. It can also be a warning sign to seek medical advice if we are feeling the strain and not to wait to long before going to a doctor.

Finances: The 7 of Pentacles is a sign of hard work and delayed success when it comes to finances. It can represent a long-term investment or project that needs consistent effort and time in order to see a positive return. We are reminded to have patience and not to expect immediate gain. Taking risks too early can be detrimental, and instead the card encourages us to wait until the time is right before making a move.

Relationships: The 7 of Pentacles tarot card is a reminder that relationships take time and effort. It warns us not to expect immediate success or that relationships will happen overnight. We must be patient and put in the necessary work in order to build strong and meaningful connections. The card encourages us to let relationships develop in their own time, as this is the only way for them to be successful.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

The 2 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a stalemate or crossroads in life, where an important decision needs to be made. It can also be a sign of caution, as it implies that a difficult decision needs to be carefully considered before proceeding. With respect to health, it can mean that a difficult course of action needs to be chosen in order to maintain one’s health. It could be a sign to change one’s diet or start a new exercise regimen, or even pursue a major surgery or lifestyle alteration. It is a reminder to carefully weigh the pros and cons of a decision before proceeding.

In terms of finances, the 2 of Swords Tarot card can imply that a compromise is needed when it comes to budgeting or taking on new financial responsibilities. It can be a sign to take a step back and reassess one’s current financial situation, and to not rush into any irreversible decisions. On the other hand, it can also be a sign that a financial decision which had been put off for too long must now be made.

In relationships, the 2 of Swords Tarot card can signify that a compromise needs to be reached between two parties in order to reach a mutual understanding. It can mean that both parties must be willing to budge and make sacrifices in order to reach a common goal or sort out a conflict, as this is the only way to reach a resolution. It can also be a sign to be mindful of the delicate balance of a relationship, and to not act impulsively or recklessly.
