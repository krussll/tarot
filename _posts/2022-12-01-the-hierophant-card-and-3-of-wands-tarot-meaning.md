A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 3 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 3 Of Wands mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and the 3 Of Wands tarot card together may signify the importance of living according to traditions and conventions while also focusing on collaboration, progress, and change. The Hierophant card can remind us of the importance of adhering to our past and the rules of society, while the 3 Of Wands encourages us to work together, to expand our horizons, to explore new ideas, and to pursue excellence. These two cards together can be seen as a reminder of the need for balance between following the status quo and pushing ourselves to strive for more.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health:  
The Hierophant Tarot card often implies that a person is following medical traditions or conventional expectations in regards to their own health. This can mean anything from seeing the same doctor for years and trusting them to make the proper decisions for the best care, to blindly following the latest fad diet or adhering to other outdated traditional health methods. This card can also imply that one is failing to break out of the unhelpful rut of their own health expectations; they may be refusing to explore alternative methods of treatment or preventive measures due to the fear of the unknown.

Finances:  
The Hierophant Tarot card can also mean that a person is following conventional expectations when it comes to finances. This could be anything from blindly following the advice of a financial advisor without doing research themselves, adhering to outdated beliefs about how to manage money, or failing to challenge conventional thinking when it comes to investment strategies. This card could also be an indicator that a person is so set in their financial beliefs that they are failing to take advantage of new opportunities or to explore alternative ways of managing their money.

Relationships:  
The Hierophant Tarot card can be an indicator that a person is stuck in the conventional expectations of relationships. This could mean fitting into a traditional gender role, falling for the same type of person over and over, expecting things to work out the same way with each relationship, or failing to challenge their own beliefs about what constitutes a successful relationship. The card also implies that someone is likely to follow the advice of others in matters of love, rather than trusting their own judgement, which could lead to unfulfilling relationships.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 3 of Wands tarot card can indicate that health improvement and progress are just around the corner. This card symbolizes working together to find the best solution possible. This can mean that seeking out professional advice or working in a group environment could help you make great strides in improving your health. Working with others can help you stay motivated and focused on your goals even when times are tough.

Finances

The 3 of Wands tarot card can indicate that there is room for improvement in your financial life. This card symbolizes teamwork, commerce and expansion and can mean that by networking and working with others you may be able to increase your financial security. It is a reminder to stay open to new opportunities and to make use of tools such as budgeting, savings and investments. The 3 of Wands can suggest that by engaging in commerce, you can open up new channels of finance for yourself.

Relationships

The 3 of Wands tarot card can point to the idea of working together in order to strengthen existing relationships or build new ones. This card can be a reminder to stay open and to reach out to others with the intention of finding a shared solution or common ground. It is a reminder to collaborate with others in order to expand and strengthen bonds. The 3 of Wands can indicate that travel or exploration of new locations can be beneficial in deepening relationships, or even opening up the possibility of making new friends.
