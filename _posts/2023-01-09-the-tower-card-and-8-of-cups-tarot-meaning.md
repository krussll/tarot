## What does The Tower and 8 Of Cups mean together?

Taken together, the Tower and the 8 of Cups tarot cards suggest a period of unexpected and unwanted change, which will likely result in ruin or upheaval. The 8 of Cups speaks to a need to retreat, move on, or abandon something, suggesting that this change is unavoidable. While the Tower often carries with it feelings of violence or disgrace, the 8 of Cups reminds us that it’s important to face the difficult situation with grace and acceptance, and to look for the opportunity for renewal and growth that lies within the upheaval.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card often symbolizes upheaval and unwanted change. This is particularly true of health, finances and relationships. When The Tower appears in a reading, it often indicates a significant disruption or dramatic event that will cause these areas of life to change abruptly and sometimes in a negative way.

Health-wise, The Tower Tarot card can signify a very abrupt or sudden illness or injury that could lead to a complete upheaval of your life. It may indicate a long-term illness or disability. Alternatively, it may signify a mental health issue or a life-threatening health condition that will require a major adjustment to your lifestyle and daily routine.

When it comes to financial matters, The Tower Tarot implies a potentially damaging event or circumstance that could lead to ruin. This could be anything from sudden job loss or bankruptcy to debt and financial strain. It could even symbolize complete financial collapse. It is important to prepare for the worst and look for solutions as soon as The Tower appears in a reading.

In terms of relationships, The Tower Tarot symbolizes a major breakdown in communication or a traumatic event that could lead to the end of a relationship. It could also signify a relationship that is in danger of ending, or one that is headed towards a serious confrontation or heated argument. It is important to address the underlying issues and find ways to resolve them before it is too late.

The Tower Tarot card is a warning that unwelcome change is coming, and it is important to be prepared for it. It is important to keep an open mind and to be willing to make the necessary adjustments to ensure that you can protect yourself from potential damage to your health, finances and relationships.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

For health, The 8 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes taking a step back from your current lifestyle and exploring alternative healing methods for your mind and body. This could mean engaging in yoga and meditation, trying a new holistic approach to health, or simply taking a break from the chaos of everyday life. This is an invitation to focus on yourself and your own needs, rather than worrying about impressing or pleasing others.

For finances, The 8 of Cups Tarot card suggests that it is time to reevaluate your financial situation and determine if you’re on the right track. Taking a step back and looking at the situation objectively can help you make informed decisions that are in alignment with your long-term goals. It can be difficult to break free from the everyday grind, but this card encourages you to take a break, clear your head and come up with a plan that works for you.

For relationships, The 8 of Cups Tarot card suggests that you might be feeling dissatisfied with your current relationship. This could be due to a lack of communication, a lack of shared interests, or simply feeling stuck in a rut. This card encourages you to take your time to reflect and consider what changes need to be made for a healthier, more fulfilling relationship. Whether you decide to work on the relationship or move on, this card emphasizes that it is important to explore your feelings and make decisions based on what is best for you in the long run.
