A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 8 Of Cups tarot card together suggest a time of looking within to make decisions that are objectively right, regardless of how difficult they may be. It may mean you need to move on from a situation that is no longer working for you, being guided by truth and justice. The win-win solution you are seeking may require you to take some steps back, and you will know it is the right thing when the truth comes to light. This is a time of evaluating your decisions and allowing the universe to take care of the rest.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and 8 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When Justice and 8 of Cups come up together in a love reading, the message is that it may be time to understand how your choices have had an effect on your relationship, and to call into question the way in which things have been playing out in your romantic life. It suggests that you may have been too focused on the needs of your own desires, and overlooking the needs of your partner.

Justice counsels that you take a good look at how your choices have been impacting your situation, as well as what could be done to turn it around and make it more fair. You have a chance to evaluate the situation objectively and make changes that will benefit both parties. Seek win-win solutions and trust that the truth will be revealed.

At the same time, 8 of Cups indicates that it may be time to move on from an outdated way of thinking and try something new. The symbolism of the card suggests that it might be time to withdraw from a situation that no longer serves you, or to abandon a way of being that no longer brings you joy. It could be time to assess the relationship and decide whether it’s time to part ways, or if you can still find a way to make it work.

In conclusion, when Justice and 8 of Cups appear together in a love reading they are suggesting that you take a step back, reflect on the current situation, and make changes that will bring balance to the relationship. If a compromise or a new way of looking at things is necessary to make the relationship work, it may be time to try something new. Ultimately, both parties need to be heard and respected in order for a successful outcome.

At the same time, The 8 Of Cups tarot card in this combination indicates that it may be time to withdraw or retreat from an existing venture, or at least to explore other options. Moving on from current projects or reallocating resources to more rewarding pursuits can be beneficial. Overall, this combination of tarot cards urges us to make decisions based on careful thought and reflection. True success and happiness can often be found in moving on with new goals, in light of the facts at hand. At the same time, the Justice card promises that, in the end, the truth will come out and justice will be served. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 8 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 8 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.
