## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

When it comes to health, The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is indicative of a high degree of self-care and health maintenance. The figure on the card stands in a beautiful garden surrounded by powerful symbols – a dove, a falcon and grapes – which suggest physical regeneration and a healthy lifestyle. It points to the fact that a person has taken the time and effort to nourish their body with an abundance of nutritious food, plenty of rest and regular physical exercise. Overall, the card is a sign of good health and well-being.

When it comes to finances, The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of financial security and wealth. The luxurious garden on the card represents abundance and affluence, while the figure of the woman is a symbol of being in control of one’s finances. The card implies that a person has taken the necessary steps to ensure their financial stability and is reaping the rewards of their investments and sensible financial decisions.

In terms of relationships, The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of pleasure and self-sufficiency. The figure of the woman on the card is indicative of an individual who is comfortable in their own skin and does not need to rely on the approval of others. It suggests that a person has taken a holistic approach to their own self-care, and that part of this includes engaging in activities and relationships that are enjoyable and gratifying. The card is a sign of contentment and personal satisfaction.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups tarot card is a major arcana card which means this card is associated with issues of great importance and significance. This card indicates wishes coming true, and is associated with material abundance, good health and positive relationships.

When it comes to health, the 9 of Cups tarot card is associated with good health, overall vitality and a feeling of peace and wellbeing. It’s an indication of a person’s physical, mental, and emotional well-being, and suggests that illness and injury are less likely. This card serves as a reminder to practice good self care and to prioritize your health and wellness.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Cups tarot card suggests that abundance and prosperity are in the air. It is generally associated with luck and the ability to manifest one’s desires into reality. This card is a reminder that it is possible to create financial stability and wealth, and to use your resources wisely.

When it comes to relationships, the 9 of Cups tarot card is associated with positive, supportive, and rewarding connections. This card implies that while challenges may come up, these issues can be worked through with understanding and mutual respect. It is a reminder to nurture your relationships and to practice kindness, understanding, and forgiveness.

Overall the 9 of Cups tarot card is a powerful card which suggests wishes coming true, material abundance, and good health. It is a reminder that it is possible to make positive changes, to nurture relationships, and to prioritize physical and emotional health.
