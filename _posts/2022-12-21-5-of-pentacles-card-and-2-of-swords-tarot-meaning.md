## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 2 of Swords tarot card says that it is time to compromise or make a decision when it comes to health. This card is an indication that there are two choices or paths open to you, but that you must pick and choose between them. It could be a choice between two treatments for a medical condition, or two approaches to a lifestyle change. This card implies that careful contemplation and thought should be given to both approaches before making a decision. It is also wise to use other sources of guidance, such as talking to a medical professional and researching possible treatments, to ensure that the most appropriate and beneficial choice is made.

Finances

When it comes to finances, the 2 of Swords Tarot card implies that it is now a time to make a decision. This could be a decision that involves two competing financial decisions, such as whether to invest or save. This card indicates that it is important to carefully consider the advantages and disadvantages of both options. It would be beneficial to seek out professional advice where possible, or to seek help and guidance from trusted family and friends. This card is a reminder that compromise may be necessary and, ultimately, it is important to make a decision that will best serve one’s long-term financial goals.

Relationships

The 2 of Swords tarot card indicates that it is a time to compromise or make a decision when it comes to relationships. This could be a decision between two paths or different ways of dealing with a current situation in a relationship. This card reminds that it is important to take time to consider both possibilities before making a final decision. This card is a reminder to weigh up all of the pros and cons of both options and to be mindful that compromise may be the best way forward. It is also important to seek advice from those who are familiar with both parties involved and to ensure that all parties are in agreement with the decision.
