## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Swords and 3 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Swords and 3 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

The 2 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a period of weighing options and choices, leading to the necessity of making a decision or a compromise. In many readings, this card can bring a message of inner conflicts, hesitation, and indecision. When it comes to health, The 2 of Swords can signify that a difficult choice must be made in order to move forward and improve one’s wellbeing. For example, it could represent a patient who has to determine which medical treatment is the best option for them, weighing the pros and cons of various options with caution.

In terms of finances, the 2 of Swords suggests that a difficult decision must be taken in regards to resources. This card may point to the need to make a sacrifice in order to maximize financial gain or minimize expenses. This could be in the form of making a budget and cutting back on unnecessary spending, or investing carefully but strategically.

When it comes to relationships, the 2 of Swords urges for compromise. This card suggests that people must come to an understanding in order to move forward in a balanced and healthy way. This could take the form of working together to resolve conflicts or differences of opinion, or simply taking a step back and giving space to each other in order to achieve a better understanding. Ultimately, the 2 of Swords Tarot card means that tough decisions must be made in order to move forward in the right direction in all aspects of life.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 3 of Swords tarot card is a warning that the seeker may have a physical health issue that is causing emotional turmoil. This could be in the form of a surgery or an illness that has left them feeling drained and emotionally depleted. Physically, this card is a warning to take care of yourself and make sure all medical tests are done to put your mind at ease. In some cases, this card can also mean depression or a deep sadness that needs to be addressed and healed.

Finances: This card is a warning to be mindful with how needlessly spend or invest your money. If we are not careful with how and where we place our money, we can find ourselves in a difficult financial situation. This card is a reminder to think before we act and be mindful of the consequences of our decisions. This card can also mean that financial solutions are not necessarily in the immediate future and we must be patient and wait to make the right decisions.

Relationships: On a relationship level, the 3 of Swords tarot card often speaks of heartache and pain. This card warns that if we are already in a relationship, then it is time to look a little deeper and be honest with ourselves about our partner and the relationship. It can also be a sign of betrayal or the ending of a relationship. This card cautions against taking any negative actions or decisions without first considering the consequences. Ultimately, this card is asking us to deal with any emotions that are coming up and try to reach a solution through honest conversation and understanding.
