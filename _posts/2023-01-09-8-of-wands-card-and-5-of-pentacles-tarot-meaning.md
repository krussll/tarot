A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 8 Of Wands tarot card and The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card signify a time of sudden, drastic change combined with feelings of isolation and worry. This can signify a time when news arrives that leads to some kind of financial loss, resulting in a period of ill health and poverty. However, despite this difficult transition, it also suggests that there is an opportunity for increased speed and action to help you overcome the difficult situation. It is important to take advantage of the quick changes and use them to your benefit.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 8 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together for your love life?

When the Eight of Wands meets the Five of Pentacles in your love life, it means that rapid change and news of the future are coming into your life. It can mean that either you or your partner is going to have to make some swift decisions, as this card combination suggests that time has become of the essence. It can also be a sign that you and your partner will have to make some financial decisions. Depending on the context of the reading, it is possible that the Five of Pentacles indicates a period of financial hardship or loss that the two of you must face together.

It is important to remember that these two cards do not mean the situation will be negative. The Eight of Wands indicates that news and action is on the way, while the Five of Pentacles may signify that it is time to face and overcome some obstacles together. It is possible that the news brings closure to a past issue, and that the financial burden is something that can be managed with both of your hard work. Despite the difficulties, this card combination may signify the end of a period of ill health, worries or isolation, and can mean that a new period of your relationship is beginning. If anything, it can mean that both of you will be tested as a couple, and that you will come out of it stronger and ready to start a new chapter in your relationship.

### What does 8 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together for your finances?

The 8 of Wands and the 5 of Pentacles tarot cards together are both a message of warning and a call for action when it comes to finances and job. The 8 of Wands suggests that a period of speed, action, and quick change is coming, but it could just as easily mean the end of an opportunity or financial venture. The 5 of Pentacles, on the other hand, is a clear warning to watch out for ill health, poverty, worry, isolation, and financial loss.

To actually benefit from this combination of cards, it is important to take into account the message of speed that the 8 of Wands conveys and to take action quickly in order to avoid any of the negative consequences that the 5 of Pentacles could bring. This may mean taking the steps to ensure a steady job or income, or making sure that you have enough financial security to weather any potential storm. This could be anything from securing a second job or side hustle to putting more money into savings and investments.

At the same time, this combination of cards could be a warning to watch out for any financial scams or schemes that could promise quick results with little effort. Ultimately, this combination of cards is a cautionary one, to take all the necessary steps to ensure financial security and to stay alert in case of any unexpected changes or dangers.

It could also signify a financial loss that results in difficulty in affording medical treatment.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of the struggles we can face in life. It symbolizes a difficult period in life and the need to find a way out. This card often appears to people who are in a period of uncertainty and confusion. Its message is to be aware of the challenges that may come, and to be prepared for them.

When it comes to health, this card symbolizes a state of physical or mental ill health. It is an indication that health care should be taken seriously, and preventative measures should be taken to ensure that health remains optimal. It is also a warning that if health is neglected, it could lead to serious consequences.

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card also indicates financial difficulties. It can symbolize a period of poverty, debt and financial insecurity. This card encourages people to be mindful of their spending and to make sure that essential expenses are taken care of. It also suggests that it is important to remain resourceful and creative in order to find a way out of financial hardship.

Finally, this Tarot card can represent a sense of isolation in relationships. It may suggest that friends, family and partners are not supportive or understanding of the challenges one is facing. It is important to reach out for help and to strengthen relationships during difficult times, in order to remain connected and strong.

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that difficulties can be overcome, and that it is important to be self aware and to use creativity and resilience to overcome any challenges in life.
