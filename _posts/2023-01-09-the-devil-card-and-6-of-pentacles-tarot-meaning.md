A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Devil card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Devil tarot card and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card together represent an important warning to be aware of our own temptations. On one hand, The Devil tarot card cautions us to take care of ourselves and not become consumed by potential addictions, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust. On the other hand, The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card encourages us to find a balance between our own needs and the needs of others, to be generous and open to accepting help when needed. The combination of these two cards ultimately offers a reminder that it is possible to find the harmony between our own self-care and the needs of those around us.

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card suggests the possibility of giving and receiving help, generosity, and equality. It could suggest that it is possible to help each other move beyond the temptations of the outer world and towards deeper connection. You and your partner may need to work together to break the cycles of addiction and greed and find true balance.

When these two tarot cards come together they can suggest a potentially tempestuous relationship, yet one in which it is possible to reach a higher level if both parties are willing to work together. This could include growing more generous and equal, allowing yourself and your partner to express your love in more meaningful ways, rather than through materialism. It is possible to reach a point of true understanding, allowing your relationship to flourish and reach its full potential.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card means that a person’s health can be harmed by their obsession with materialism, addiction, and excessive sexual lust. Without proper attention, the physical health of a person can suffer dramatically. Furthermore, the materialism and greed associated with the Devil Tarot card can lead to financial problems. For those who have become obsessed with acquiring possessions, money can easily become an object of worship, leading to financial hardship and debt. Lastly, the Devil Tarot card can affect relationships by creating an imbalance between partners. One partner may become too focused on possessions, while the other may be neglected or ignored. This imbalance can lead to resentment and ultimately, a breakdown of communication and trust.

Overall, the Devil Tarot card can signify a person’s unhealthy obsession with materialism and lustful behavior. This can lead to physical and mental health issues, financial hardship, and strained relationships. Those who find themselves too focused on these things should make an effort to pay more attention to their physical and mental health, as well as their relationships with others. Doing so can help keep them from falling into the darkness associated with this card.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card is a sign of generosity, giving, and receiving help. It symbolizes paying it forward and helping others in need. In health, this card encourages us to give our time, energy and resources to those who need it most. It can also mean offering assistance to your friends, family and community members. This card can represent donating to causes that are close to your heart such as a local charity or organization.

In finances, The 6 of Pentacles tarot card can signify investment in yourself and your future. This card can mean setting aside money to pay off debt or investing in new skills and opportunities. It can also suggest that it’s time to start a savings account and take control of your financial situation. The power of this card lies in recognizing the abundance that is available to us, and taking the steps to make the most of it.

In relationships, The 6 of Pentacles tarot card is a sign of giving and receiving support. This card encourages us to reach out to our loved ones and lend a helping hand when needed. It can mean being there for someone in a time of need, or being open to taking advice from trusted friends or family. This card can also suggest that it’s time to share your love and affection with those around you, and to be generous with your time and attention.
