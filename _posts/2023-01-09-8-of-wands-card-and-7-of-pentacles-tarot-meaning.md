A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 7 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 7 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 8 Of Wands tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card together signify a journey of progress and growth that takes patience and hard work. They suggest that although news and quick changes will come soon, it is important to take the time and dedicate the effort necessary to make sure that the work and effort will pay off in the end. The two cards together serve as a reminder that a balance of speed and patience is necessary to achieve success, and that the rewards of patience will come soon.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

The 8 of Wands Tarot card indicates quick action and a sense of urgency when it comes to physical health, finances and relationships. On a physical health level, the 8 of Wands suggests that you take a proactive approach to health issues, either through exercise, dietary changes or seeking medical advice. The 8 of Wands encourages you to act swiftly and make changes in order to improve your overall wellbeing. This card also warns that procrastination will only lead to further problems, and the longer you wait, the harder it will be to take action.

In terms of finances, the 8 of Wands encourages you to take advantage of opportunities. If you have been thinking about investing, now is the time to act as this card is a sign of rapid change and development. The 8 of Wands also suggests that it may be time to review your financial situation and make wise decisions.

In matters of relationships, the 8 of Wands suggests that it is time to take swift action in order to repair any misunderstandings or differences. If you have been considering making a move, such as asking someone out, now is the time to do so. If there have been issues in a current relationship, this card encourages swift action in order to resolve the issue before it becomes worse. It may be time to take the initiative and make the first move.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card represents hard work, patience, and delayed success. In terms of health, it encourages the importance of investing time and effort into health. This card advises to focus on holistic health by nourishing the body, mind, and spirit. This includes eating a balanced diet, exercising regularly, getting enough sleep, and taking time for self-care. It insists that we must be patient with our health, as it takes time to experience real results.

In terms of finances, the card speaks to the value of delayed gratification. It can be tempting to go after quick, get-rich-quick schemes and to ignore our long-term financial goals. However, the 7 of Pentacles advises us not to take shortcuts. Instead, it encourages us to take the time to save and invest wisely, and to be patient with our financial progress.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the card encourages us to approach relationships with both patience and respect. We should be careful not to rush into relationships and instead, take our time to get to know someone before jumping into a relationship. The 7 of Pentacles also encourages us to be patient with others, even when they make mistakes. Instead, it advises us to be understanding and show patience in order to have successful and healthy relationships.
