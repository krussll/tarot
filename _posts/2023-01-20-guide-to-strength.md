## Strength – An Overview

The Strength tarot card represents the inner power and strength that lies within each of us. It is a reminder of the importance of self-love and self-respect, as well as the courage that it takes to stand up for ourselves and to pursue our dreams. Strength speaks of the power of the heart and how we can use this power to overcome obstacles and challenges. It encourages us to take action and to use our inner resources to manifest what we truly desire in life. Strength is also a reminder that we have the capacity to forgive, to heal and to create a better version of ourselves. Ultimately, Strength is about embracing our inner strength and loving ourselves unconditionally.

## Upright Meanings Of Strength

### What It Means For Me

The Strength tarot card is a powerful reminder to love and respect myself unconditionally. It encourages me to be brave and strong, no matter what life throws my way. It reminds me to take the time to nurture myself and to be compassionate with myself, as well as with others. This card serves as a reminder that I am capable of great strength and resilience, and that I have the power to overcome any obstacle that stands in my way. It is a reminder to be brave and to trust in my own abilities, and to have faith in myself and my decisions. Ultimately, The Strength tarot card is a reminder for me to stay true to myself, to stay strong in the face of adversity, and to always value my own self-love and self-respect.

### What It Means For The Future

The Strength tarot card is a reminder that I have the power to take control of my future and create the life I want for myself. The card symbolizes self-love, unconditional love, courage, and self-respect. It reminds me that I have the strength within me to make my dreams a reality. I am reminded that I have the power to be brave and to stand up for what I believe in. With the strength of self-love and courage, I can trust that I have the capability and the potential to create a bright and prosperous future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Strength tarot card speaks to the importance of having unconditional love, courage, and self-respect in my relationships with those close to me. It encourages me to cultivate strong and healthy relationships rooted in love and mutual respect, and to have the courage to stand up for what I believe in. To embody the Strength card, I can demonstrate strength of character by being honest and open in my communication, and by looking for the best in the people around me. When I have the courage to love unconditionally, it allows me and my close companions to grow together and to support each other in times of difficulty.

## Reversed Meanings Of Strength

### What It Means For Me

When the Strength card appears reversed in my Tarot reading, it signals that I need to work on my self-esteem and self-worth. I need to focus on developing a more accepting, compassionate, and understanding attitude towards myself and the world around me. I am being called to embrace my imperfections and vulnerabilities, so that I can move forward with the courage and strength to become my best self.

The reversed Strength card is a reminder that I must learn to forgive myself and others, seek out sources of healing, and practice self-care. I need to take time out of my day to nourish my body and soul. This will help me to be more self-aware and to recognize how I can best tend to my own needs. I must also recognize my strengths and limitations and accept them with grace. By doing this, I can empower myself to make positive changes in my life and to learn to love and appreciate myself just as I am.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Strength tarot card indicates that in my future there may be struggles with self-love, self-respect, and courage. I may find it difficult to take care of myself, cultivate self-love, and be strong in the face of challenges. I could be feeling weakened, drained, and without the power to make changes in my life. It is important to remember that although I may not feel powerful right now, I have the potential and capacity to tap into my inner strength.

The reversed Strength card can also indicate a fear of my own power and strength. I may be putting up blocks and barriers to keep me from accessing my inner strength and manifesting my dreams. I may be feeling overwhelmed and unable to take the necessary steps to make progress. It is important to recognize this fear and to take the time to nurture myself and step into my power. I should take time to practice self-love and self-care and recognize the strength and power within me. With patience and dedication, I can unlock my own true strength and power and use it to move forward in life.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the Strength card appears in reverse, it can be a sign that people around you lack self-love, self-respect, and courage. This can manifest in many ways, such as having difficulty standing up for themselves, being overly dependent on others, or struggling to express themselves in healthy ways. It can also indicate that they struggle to demonstrate unconditional love towards others, often leaving them feeling isolated and alone.

It’s important to note that this doesn’t mean that the people around you are bad or wrong in any way. It simply suggests that those close to you may need more support and encouragement when it comes to developing their own sense of self-worth. It’s also important to remember that it is ultimately up to each individual to take the steps necessary to cultivate a sense of self-love and self-respect. As such, it is important to provide a safe and supportive environment in which others can develop these qualities. Offer them kindness and understanding as they work towards becoming their best selves.
