A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The High Priestess card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The The High Priestess card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The High Priestess and Queen Of Cups mean together?

When The High Priestess tarot card and The Queen Of Cups tarot card appear together in a reading, it is a message to trust your intuition and go with your gut instinct. The Queen Of Cups is a supportive and nurturing figure, and she will be there to help you through whatever challenges you face. This combination of tarot cards suggests that you are about to embark on a journey of self-discovery, and that you should go into it with an open mind and heart.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The High Priestess and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The High Priestess and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The High Priestess mean?

The High Priestess Tarot card is often associated with secrets and mystery. In terms of health, this means that there may be some hidden health concerns that you are not aware of. It is important to trust your intuition and listen to your body. If you have any concerns, it is best to consult a medical professional.

In terms of finances, The High PriestessTarot card can indicate that there are some hidden financial concerns that you are not aware of. It is important to trust your intuition and be careful with your spending. If you have any concerns, it is best to consult a financial advisor.

In terms of relationships, The High Priestess Tarot card suggests that there may be some hidden issues that you are not aware of. It is important to trust your intuition and be honest with your partner. If you have any concerns, it is best to seek professional help.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health: The Queen Of CupsTarot card suggests that you take care of yourself and those around you. You are a natural healer and have a strong interest in keeping people healthy. You may find yourself working in the medical field or becoming a health consultant. You are a compassionate person who is always willing to lend a helping hand.

Finances: The Queen Of CupsTarot card suggests that you are good with money and know how to manage it. You are thrifty and make wise investments. You may find yourself working in the financial sector or becoming a financial advisor. You are a practical person who is always looking out for the best interests of those you care about.

Relationships: The Queen Of CupsTarot card suggests that you are a loving and caring person. You are supportive and always there for the people you care about. You may find yourself in a leadership position in a relationship or becoming a counselor. You are a great listener and always make time for those you care about.
