A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Spades card and 7 Of Spades card together in particular.

The 3 Of Spades card and 7 Of Spades have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the appearance of both the 3 of Spades and the 7 of Spades cards together suggests a complex and challenging narrative.

Both cards are associated with the cold and introspective season of winter, as well as the deep, flowing element of water.

The 3 of Spades indicates heartbreak and faith—perhaps the difficult end of a relationship or undertaking, but with the trust or hope that things will ultimately get better.

The 7 of Spades, on the other hand connotes deceit and yet victory.

It could mean that some form of dishonesty might factor into the situation or a person may be hiding something.

At the same time, the victory suggests that despite the deceit, you will ultimately triumph or find success.

The combination of these two cards ultimately points to a journey of emotional turmoil, faith testing, hidden truths, and eventual success.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your love life?

The combination of the Three and Seven of Spades in a cartomancy reading paints a challenging picture for your love life.

The Three of Spades, symbolizing heartbreak and faith, suggests that you may currently be undergoing emotional turmoil or a painful ending.

However, the element of faith indicates the necessity of trust and belief in something greater to guide you through this difficult time.

This could mean entrusting your heart and emotions to the natural flow of life or holding onto your faith in a higher power or the universe.

The Seven of Spades further complicates the reading by introducing deceit but also victory.

This card could represent dishonesty or betrayal either from your end or from your partner’s which is causing the heartbreak.

Yet, it also indicates victory, signifying that you will overcome this difficult phase, suggesting that pain and betrayal could lead to growth and self-evolution.

It stands as a testament that heartbreaks often pave the way for personal development and emotional growth.

In short, this pairing signifies that you may be going through a rough patch now, but keep faith, as the troubles will lead to personal victory in the long run.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, the 3 of Spades often symbolizes heartbreak, pain, and unusual struggles.

Given it’s tied to finances and job, the cards suggest you might be experiencing hardship in your workplace or with your financial situation.

The heartbreak could imply losing a job, the downfall of a business venture, or unpleasant discrepancies in your current economic situations.

Its symbolic relation with faith signposts the need to retain your inner strength, never lose hope, and brace yourself for the future challenges that lie ahead.

On the other hand, the 7 of Spades leans towards deceit and victory.

It warns of potential deceit within your financial or occupational realms.

Perhaps a deal that’s too good to be true or dishonesty in some professional matters.

But, it also holds a promise of victory.

Despite the possible deceit, there are strong chances of overcoming these obstacles and emerging victorious.

Together, these cards suggest navigating through hardship and deceit, but ultimate triumph lies ahead if you retain your faith and stay vigilant.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Spades mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading concerning your health, the 3 of Spades and 7 of Spades appearing together might suggest a challenging period.

The 3 of Spades signifies heartbreak or emotional distress, which can directly impact your mental health and indirectly affect your physical health as well.

It may be a wake-up call to acknowledge and address emotional wounds or trauma that have perhaps been neglected or suppressed.

As this card is associated with water, an element connected to emotion and intuition, it calls for inner exploration and healing on an emotional plane.

On the other hand, the 7 of Spades implies deceit, which could mean self-deception about your physical state of health.

Are you ignoring certain symptoms or disregarding professional medical advice? Alternatively, it could mean external deception: be aware of potential inaccurate or misleading health information from unverified sources.

However, it’s not all ominous; this card also denotes victory.

Despite the initial hardships or hurdles, there’s potential for overcoming health issues.

It indicates triumph over adversity, meaning whatever health struggles you face, you have the ability to surmount them.

The winter season this card is tied to may imply a somewhat slow recovery process, requiring patience and perseverance.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
