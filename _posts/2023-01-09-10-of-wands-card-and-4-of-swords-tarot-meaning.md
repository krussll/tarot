## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 Of Wands Tarot card represents a period of great stress and an inability to cope with the burdens that have been placed on an individual. It symbolizes feeling overwhelmed and exhausted, usually from taking on too much responsibility or having too many commitments.

Health-wise, the 10 of Wands card indicates that a person is at risk of burning out due to taking on too much. It is a warning to slow down and prioritize self-care so that they do not become overwhelmed and overwhelmed by stress. When this card appears, it is a reminder to take breaks, rest, and take time to relax and practice healthy coping skills.

Financially, the 10 of Wands card indicates that a person may be shouldering too much responsibility and is likely feeling overwhelmed by this. It serves as a reminder that it is important to take the time to plan and budget so that you do not fall behind on payments. It can also be a sign to look into ways to make extra money to help you manage the burden of overspending.

Relationship-wise, the 10 of Wands card indicates that there may be a lack of balance in the relationship due to one or both people feeling overwhelmed. It is a reminder to find ways to better communicate and divide up responsibilities so that both parties are not taking on too much. It also suggests that there may be a need for both parties to slow down and practice self-care in order to avoid any further stress.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is an indication of respite, rest, and renewal. It speaks of taking a break from the bustle of everyday life, allowing yourself the chance to reset. Whether it’s a mental or physical health issue, a financial setback, or a difficult relationship, the card is encouraging the need to retreat and recover.

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card could serve as an indication that your body is tired and in need of some downtime. A good night’s sleep and some restful moments in the day can do wonders for healing the body. Consider taking a vacation or visiting a spa so you can recharge and energize your mind and body. Letting your body rest for a bit can be key to reconnecting with your physical health.

Finances

The 4 of Swords card indicates that a financial reset may be needed. Take some time to evaluate where your money is going and make sure it’s going to the right places. Break any bad spending habits, get your budget in order, and ensure your finances don’t take a toll on your mental health. Taking an honest assessment of your finances can help you get back on track and create sound, sustainable solutions for your finances.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of needing a break in your relationships. It could be time to take some time away from your partner and allow yourself the space to consider what’s best for you. A break can give you clarity and help you to assess the relationship in a different way. Taking the time to reflect and consider how to move forward can be the key to restoring and strengthening your relationship.
