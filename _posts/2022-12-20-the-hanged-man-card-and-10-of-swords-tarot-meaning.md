## What does The Hanged Man and 10 Of Swords mean together?

When the Hanged Man tarot card and the 10 of Swords are combined, they typically indicate a difficult sacrifice that must be made in order to gain a greater perspective or a deeper understanding. This difficult decision may require giving up something important to you, but the experience of doing so can provide significant wisdom in the long run. The 10 of Swords may be pointing out that although this loss can be disheartening and bring about a period of suffering, it is usually necessary for growth. The message of these two cards suggest that you can find strength in sacrificing something you hold dear, and that ultimately, this will lead to a deeper understanding of yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man tarot card is a card that represents wise choices and sacrifice. When this card appears in a tarot reading it usually symbolizes that difficult changes are needed in order to move forward. It can often be interpreted to mean that the querent must sacrifice something in order to gain something in the future.

Health:  
When The Hanged Man tarot card appears in a reading it can be a warning to take better care of your health. It can mean that it is time for the querent to make real changes in their lifestyle in order to become healthier. A sacrifice must be made, such as giving up unhealthy habits, eating more balanced meals, or practicing mindfulness and self-care.

Finances:  
The Hanged Man tarot card could mean that the querent needs to look at their finances and make some difficult decisions. This could mean cutting back on spending, creating a budget, or finding a better job. Whatever it is, it could require a great deal of sacrifice to achieve, but it could lead to greater financial stability in the long run.

Relationships:  
When this card appears in a reading, it can mean that the querent needs to make some changes in their relationship. It could mean sacrificing something in order to keep the relationship going, such as being more open and honest with your partner or taking more initiative in the relationship. It could also mean that they need to reevaluate their relationships to decide if it is worth their time and energy or not.

Overall, The Hanged Man tarot card is a reminder to make wise choices and wise sacrifices. In order to create a better future, the querent must be willing to make difficult decisions and be willing to sacrifice something they value in order to gain something that will help them in the long run.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

For Health:  
The Ten of Swords tarot card means a complete and painful ending in health. It symbolizes a devastating health setback or the reaching of rock bottom in terms of physical health. It is a card that signals that all hope is lost and nothing more can be done. It can be a warning that if health care is not sought soon, the consequences may be dire. This card appears when medical care is needed as soon as possible, and it may be a sign of the need to take a break and rest.

For Finances:  
The Ten of Swords tarot card means a complete and painful ending in finances. It is a card that symbolizes bankruptcy or reaching rock bottom financially. This card appears when a person is in desperate need of a financial bailout or assistance. It is also a warning that if steps are not taken now to make better financial decisions, it could lead to an even worse situation. It is a sign that a person is at the end of their rope and needs to make some hard decisions regarding their finances in order to save themselves from further financial ruin.

For Relationships:  
The Ten of Swords tarot card means a complete and painful ending in relationships. It signals that a once strong and meaningful relationship has finally reached its climax and is coming to an end. It is a sign of bitterness, heartache, and despair as the relationship has reached rock bottom and is no longer salvageable. This card typically appears when a relationship has gone on for too long and has reached its final breaking point. It is a warning to take some time apart in order to rebuild and fix the relationship before it is too late, or else the consequences could be devastating.
