A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Page Of Cups mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the Page of Cups tarot card together suggest that making time for yourself is the path to achieving a deeper level of clarity and truth, as well as discovering your own spirituality. This can come in the form of new love, volunteering, or any other meaningful pursuit that opens your heart and connects you to the greater world. Ultimately, it is about the journey of self-discovery and allowing yourself the time and space to explore the depths of your own soul.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

For Health:

When the Hermit Tarot card presents itself in a reading on health, it often indicates that it is time to take an inward journey. This is a signal to look deeply within yourself and find clarity on any physical or emotional health issues that you may be facing. The Hermit suggests a period of introspection and reflection, taking time to build a deeper understanding of your own body, mind and spirit connection. In doing so, you will be able to look at your health from a more holistic perspective, making it easier to identify the root cause of any physical or emotional issues you may be facing. If your health is poor, look to the Hermit card as a reminder that you need to dedicate time and energy towards healing yourself from the inside out.

For Finances:

The Hermit Tarot card can represent a need for financial clarity. It suggests that you should take some time to reflect upon your finances in order to gain a better understanding of your current financial situation. This could also be a signal that you may benefit from seeking guidance from a trusted advisor who can provide you with a different perspective on your financial issues. By looking within you will be able to identify any areas where you could make improvements to your financial situation, such as understanding where your spending habits could be refined, or how you can better manage your savings and investments.

For Relationships:

When the Hermit Tarot card appears in a reading on relationships, it is a reminder to take time for personal introspection and reflection. Rather than focusing on your external relationships and interactions, the Hermit suggests that it is more important to look within yourself and understand what kind of relationships you want and need in your life. This could be a sign that you are expecting too much from your relationships and partners, and that it is time to look more closely within yourself in order to understand how you can bring more balance and stability to your partnerships. The Hermit can also indicate that you need to spend more time alone and focus on self-care in order to foster a healthier relationship dynamic.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page of Cups Tarot card is connected to our emotional and physical well-being. The Page of Cups challenges us to tap into our inner emotions and discover what is really going on beneath the surface. In terms of health, it encourages us to pay close attention to our physical body and our mental and emotional health. This can mean making time for activities that help nourish our inner self, such as yoga and meditation. It also recommends we focus on positive affirmations and positive self-talk to reduce anxiety and stress. Additionally, it reminds us to take time for self-care, whether that’s getting enough rest, eating nourishing meals, or generally just taking time to relax and recharge.

Finances

The Page of Cups Tarot card is associated with our finances and the idea of making smart decisions in order to ensure our long-term success. It reminds us that, although the short-term may provide temptation, it’s important to consider the long-term effects of our decisions. Instead of taking risks, this card encourages us to practice more conservative and responsible money management. It also asks us to pay attention to our intuition when it comes to financial decisions as it can help us make wise, informed choices.

Relationships

The Page of Cups Tarot card is associated with relationships and represents an opportunity to create strong, meaningful connections. This card encourages us to be open to the possibilities of love and trust. It also encourages us to be more accepting of new people, even if they are different from us. Additionally, it reminds us to focus on mutual respect and kindness to cultivate a genuine and meaningful connection. Lastly, it encourages us to pay attention to our intuition when it comes to developing relationships and to trust it.
