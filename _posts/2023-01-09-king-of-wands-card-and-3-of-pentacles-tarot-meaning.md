A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the King Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The King Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does King Of Wands and 3 Of Pentacles mean together?

The King Of Wands tarot card and The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card together can be seen as a strong leader who has the power, charm, and courage to bring together a team of skilled and talented people in order to produce quality results. This combination can also be seen as a person who has the ability to bring out the best in those around them and work alongside them in order to achieve a common goal. The King Of Wands lends the boldness and assurance needed to lead, while The 3 Of Pentacles provides the skills, craftsmanship and expertise needed to complete the task. This combination indicates that together great things can be achieved.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

Health  
The 3 of Pentacles tarot card is a card of team effort, cooperation and skill. Physically, it relates to good health maintenance, with a strong focus on diet, exercise and preventative care. The card encourages you to work with your doctor to ensure that you are implementing the best health practices for your individual needs. This card speaks of working together with medical professionals to maintain optimal health. It also suggests investing in quality resources, such as health care coverage, professional guidance, and community-based services designed to promote overall health.

Finances  
The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with taking a holistic approach to money management. This involves working with financial advisors to create a solid plan, as well as being mindful of where your money is going and being aware of potential opportunities to make wise investments. The card implies that you should be proactive and take advantage of available resources to manage and grow your financial portfolio. It also promotes collaboration with others to help structure a plan and evaluate profitability.

Relationships  
The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card deals with the idea of a team effort when it comes to relationships. It is important to be mindful of what is going into your relationships, like giving time and energy. This card indicates the need to work together and have clear communication, understanding, respect and open-mindedness. It implores you to be patient and collaborative in order to build a healthier, more positive relationship. The 3 of Pentacles also reminds you to take into consideration the needs of the relationship when making decisions. It suggests investing in quality resources to help create a mutually beneficial relationship.
