A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and Temperance card together in particular.

The Strength card and Temperance have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and Temperance mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The Temperance Tarot Card represent two sides of the same coin: they both advocate the need to cultivate an internal sense of balance. Strength reminds us to love and respect ourselves and to have the courage to make difficult decisions. On the other hand, Temperance encourages us to be patient and practice self-control, so as to maintain balance within ourselves and with the world around us. Together, these cards serve as a gentle reminder to draw on both our courage and our patience to be our best selves.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Temperance reminds us to be patient and to be mindful of our words and actions when interacting with our partner. This card teaches us to use self-control and discipline to prevent us from causing harm to our relationship—it also encourages us to take breaks and step away from heated conversations and arguments.

The combination of Strength and Temperance in a love life encourages us to practice self-reflection and to be mindful of how our actions and emotions may affect our relationships. Strength reminds us to be compassionate with ourselves, and to foster unconditional love for our partner—and Temperance reminds us to be mindful of our words and actions when interacting with our partner and to not let our emotions get the best of us. Ultimately, these two tarot cards remind us of the importance of balance and self-control in any relationship. By being patient and understanding in our relationships and by understanding our own worth and embracing self-love, we can cultivate a fulfilling and healthy love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Strength and Temperance mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Strength and Temperance the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

Health:

The Strength Tarot card is all about finding inner strength and self-love. On a physical level, this card speaks to the overall strength and durability of the body. It suggests that one should focus on physical health not only by taking care of one’s diet and exercise routine, but also by seeking out proper medical attention if needed. The Strength Tarot card encourages one to build resilience and empower themselves to make conscious decisions that lead to healthier habits and lifestyles.

Finances:

The Strength Tarot card is an empowering reminder that financial security can be within reach. This card encourages one to take calculated risks in order to further their financial goals, and to remain positive and hopeful during challenging times. The Strength Tarot card also speaks to the importance of financial planning and budgeting, and of using the power of patience and confidence to reach one’s financial goals.

Relationships:

The Strength Tarot card suggests that one should focus on building trust and resilience in their relationships. This card encourages one to open up to others and to create a safe and secure space where both parties can express their feelings. The Strength Tarot card also reminds us to cultivate understanding, patience, and unconditional love in relationships. With this strength and self-love, one can confidently pursue healthy, fulfilling relationships with others.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is a representation of the key to achieving a balanced and harmonious life. It is a reminder to practice self-control in all areas, especially those that can bring us great rewards.

When it comes to health and wellbeing, The Temperance Tarot card can be seen as a symbol of mindful and conscious eating. It encourages us to take the time to understand and appreciate our body’s nutritional needs and to practice moderation when it comes to indulging in unhealthy food and drink. The card also asks us to search for balance when it comes to exercising; to seek out activities that will make us feel good and not to become over-reliant on one particular form.

For finances, The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to take a responsible approach to our finances. We are asked to develop strategies and systems that help us stay in control, while respecting our financial goals. There is an emphasis on patience and planning; no quick fixes, no gimmicks and no taking risks. Above all, the card encourages us to take a long-term view and to think ahead.

When it comes to relationships, The Temperance Tarot card can help us find harmony with our loved ones. It encourages us to take time out for ourselves, to take stock of our feelings, fears and needs. To nurture and cherish our relationships, we must be willing to compromise and to give and take. The card reminds us to stay true to ourselves and to be open and honest about our feelings. It also brings to the forefront the importance of self-care, reminding us to not neglect our own needs in trying to uphold the needs of others.
