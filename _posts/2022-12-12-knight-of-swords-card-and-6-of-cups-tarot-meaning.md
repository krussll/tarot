A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Knight Of Swords card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Knight Of Swords card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Knight Of Swords and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The Knight of Swords tarot card combined with the 6 of Cups tarot card means that a period of significant change is coming, one that requires you to be honest, open and true to your core values. Change is inevitable, but it is a welcomed change. It is associated with nostalgia and a reminder of the past, as well as gifts, innocence and reunions in the present. A fast-talking person may come into your life and help you through the transition, so be sure to stay true to yourself and trust your instincts. Ultimately, the combination of these two cards is a reminder that life is always changing, but with the right mindset, anything is possible.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Knight Of Swords and 6 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Knight Of Swords card suggests that it’s time to make some lifestyle changes to improve your health. To begin with, this means thinking rationally and objectively about the decisions you make with regards to your health. Think of how the decisions you make today will affect your health tomorrow and in the long run. Lifestyle changes you make should include making smarter food choices, getting more exercise and sleep regularly. Taking the time to relax and de-stress is also important.

Finances  
The Knight Of Swords card also suggests being fast-thinking when it comes to your finances. This means making decisions quickly and thoughtfully. Prioritize your financial goals and make sure you are investing resources in the most important areas. Additionally, take time to research financial products before investing in them and be wary of people trying to take advantage of you with their fast-talking.

Relationships  
The Knight Of Swords card also suggests that it’s time to evaluate the relationships in your life. Consider the people you have surrounded yourself with and take a good look at the relationships you have created. Evaluate whether they are helping or hindering your growth and development. Make sure that your relationships are based on mutual respect and understanding. Additionally, be aware of the way you communicate with others. Be sure to think before you talk, and make sure that your words are true and meaningful.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 6 of Cups tarot card is often associated with physical and mental renewal, and a return to an innocent state of health. The card is a reminder to take time to slow down, to reconnect with yourself and to focus on positive wellbeing. It is a reminder that nourishing the body helps to nourish the mind and spirit too. This can come in many forms such as spending time in nature, taking time to meditate and practice yoga, or engaging in healthier activities such as cooking balanced meals rather than junk food. It is also a sign to be more conscious of what we put into our bodies, in terms of food, supplements and medications.

Finances: The 6 of Cups Tarot card is a reminder that sometimes, we don’t need to be looking for more, but rather to appreciate and use what we already have. Its meaning is that by being grateful for what we have and living a simpler life, can actually be more rewarding in the long run. This card can also reveal a need to look at our finances from a different angle, such as discovering new ways of generating an income and managing our money in a more conscious and mindful way.

Relationships: The 6 of Cups Tarot card is often seen as a symbol of nostalgia and reunions, reminding us of our childhood innocence. This can be applied to relationships too, as it suggests that by considering our past relationships and letting go of any resentment or hurt feelings, we can open ourselves up to new, healthier experiences. It is a reminder that healthy relationships bring joy, understanding and a sense of deep connection. This can be achieved through open-mindedness, honesty and regular communication. Taking time to nurture existing relationships and fostering new ones is also key.
