A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card together can signify a choice between staying with the one you love and breaking free to explore yourself and your desires. This can be a difficult decision, as the 8 of Swords implies a sense of isolation and self-imposed imprisonment, implying that making the wrong decision could lead to feeling trapped in the relationship. However, when looking at both cards together, it also suggests that a partnership with the one you love can be worth the risk, as the Lovers card implies the strength of two together and the potential for a deeply committed and loving relationship. Ultimately, the cards imply that you need to decide what is truly best for you and your partner, and that both choices have potential for a bright future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health:  
The Lovers Tarot card symbolizes the interconnectedness of our physical and emotional well-being, and the importance of balance in our lives. When this card appears in a reading, it may be suggesting that two perspectives can provide potential solutions for any health challenges faced. For example, it could be suggesting that the combination of traditional and holistic medicine could provide the best course of action for a particular health issue. Alternatively, it could suggest that two people working together (i.e. a patient and a healthcare provider, a patient and a support system, etc.) can create a supportive environment that facilitates the healing process. Overall, The Lovers Tarot card indicates that the power of two working together can provide balance, healing and improved overall health.

Finances:  
The Lovers Tarot card suggests that a combination of two perspectives can create powerful financial results. It is not just about having two incomes, but rather a combination of two different mindsets and approaches to how finances should be managed. When this card appears in a reading, it could signal the importance of a financial partner for sound decision-making and long-term success. This partner could be a partner or spouse, a professional advisor, or a trusted friend. It is also important to note that this is not about recklessness, but rather two minds working together to make sound and informed decisions about how to manage finances.

Relationships:  
The Lovers Tarot card symbolizes the power of relationships. This card suggests that two people can achieve much more when they work together as a team. It can also be indicative of a balanced relationship, as it takes both partners to forge a strong and healthy connection. This card can represent a romantic relationship, as well as many other types of relationships (friendship, family, colleagues, etc). When this card appears in a reading, it suggests that increased communication, mutual trust, and respect will help bridge any gaps and bring the two people closer together. It can also indicate that joint decision-making and the ability to empathize with each other will create the strong foundations for any relationship.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 8 of Swords Tarot card carries with it a sense of imprisonment and confinement, something that can be strongly felt in regard to physical and mental health. The 8 of Swords speaks to a state of mind where we feel confined and constrained, not only by our physical illness or ailments, but also by our own thoughts and emotions. In terms of physical health, this card often indicates a situation where we feel restricted by our own body or physical limitations, such as chronic pain or illness. We may feel trapped in a cycle of symptoms and treatments, without any real hope of improvement. Mentally, we may feel stuck in a negative mindset, struggling to break through our own restrictive thought patterns and beliefs that keep us from achieving our goals.

Finances

The 8 of Swords Tarot card may also indicate a financial situation where we feel stuck and constrained. This card can signal a period of financial insecurity and difficulty, accompanied by a sense of not knowing what to do and feeling trapped in a particular financial situation. There may be an unwillingness to take risks or an inability to look for solutions, due to a feeling of being limited by our resources, skills or knowledge. Additionally, the 8 of Swords can point to a situation in which we are making self-destructive financial decisions, such as overspending, taking irresponsible risks, or avoiding necessary changes.

Relationships

Finally, the 8 of Swords Tarot card can be associated with relationships where we feel confined and restricted. This card can point to situations where we feel like we’re in a “prison” of sorts, unable to fully express ourselves or feel that our needs are being met. We may experience a general sense of disconnection and isolation, as we feel unable to break through the restrictions that have been placed on us. In terms of our relationships with others, the 8 of Swords indicates a lack of communication, trust or understanding that can leave us feeling emotionally and spiritually imprisoned. Overall, the 8 of Swords Tarot card serves as a reminder that we may need to take a step back in order to gain the clarity and freedom we seek.
