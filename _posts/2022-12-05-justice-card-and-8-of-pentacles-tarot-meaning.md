A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 8 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and 8 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 8 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 8 of Pentacles tarot card together bring a message of balance, structure, and dedication. The Justice tarot card reminds you that there is a natural cause and effect in life, and win-win solutions can be achieved by recognizing the truth. The 8 of Pentacles tarot card encourages you to focus your efforts and to do what is necessary to master your craft. Enjoy each step of your employment, knowing that diligent effort will be rewarded. Together, these cards represent a harmonious balance in your life where you are working hard and authentically and reaping the rewards.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 8 Of Pentacles tarot card is encouraging you to enjoy the process of mastering your craft of communication. This could be through various activities such as counseling sessions, reading books on relationships and understanding more about your partner’s needs. Committing to understanding and mastering the communication between the two of you will bring your relationship to a deeper level of understanding and intimacy.

Altogether, these two cards signify that to move your love life in the right direction, you must take steps to ensure that honest communication is the foundation of your relationship. When both partners are dedicated to mastering the craft of communication, then it creates a win-win solution that benefits both. Only then will the truth come out, and your relationship will be built on trust and understanding.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 8 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

Making healthy choices involves finding a balance between taking care of ourselves, while also meeting the demanding needs of our lives.

In terms of finances, The Justice Tarot card is a call to consider our financial decisions carefully. This card advises us to practice thrift and budgeting, in order to ensure that our decisions benefit us in the long run. This card also suggests that it is important to be honest and transparent regarding our finances, and to make sure that we are being fair to all involved.

Finally, The Justice Tarot card speaks to relationships. In order to maintain healthy and secure relationships, it is important to value fairness and honesty. This card suggests that we strive for equality in our relationships, and that we actively work to resolve any issues that arise. It is important to communicate openly with our partners, and to strive for mutual understanding. Ultimately, it is important to remember that all relationships require effort in order to grow and thrive.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card speaks to us of physical, mental, and emotional health, financial stability, and strong relationships with those around us. This card is a symbol of self-discipline, dedication, and mastery. It encourages us to maintain a steady focus on our goals, and to work hard to reach our dreams.

In terms of physical health, the 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages us to stay true to our health goals. Whether it be a balanced diet, regular exercise, or proper rest, this card reminds us that our physical health is a craft that must be mastered in order to reach our fullest potential.

For finances, the 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card represents a strong focus on being frugal and wise with our resources. This card emphasizes the importance of maintaining financial stability. It encourages us to be mindful of our spending and to work hard to ensure that our finances remain in good standing.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card urges us to be dedicated to those around us. This card speaks to a commitment to creating and maintaining strong relationships with family, friends, and romantic partners. It reminds us of the importance of communication, trust, and loyalty to those whom we love.

Overall, the 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card stands for the power of dedication and hard work. It is a reminder of the rewards that await us when we are willing to take the time and effort to master our craft. When we remain true to our goals, both in our physical and emotional lives, we can achieve great success.
