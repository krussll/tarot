A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 9 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Justice card and 9 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 9 Of Wands mean together?

When the Justice tarot card and the 9 of Wands tarot cards appear together, they indicate a need for balance, equality, and fairness. The Justice card stresses the importance of evaluating situations objectively and in a fair manner, while the 9 of Wands encourages us to be firm and set boundaries in order to maintain control of situations. This combination of cards encourages us to make decisions based on fairness and objectivity, while also recognizing that personal boundaries are necessary to ensure that everyone involved is treated equally and with respect. By understanding the importance of both of these cards, we can strive to create win-win solutions that promote balanced and equitable outcomes.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and 9 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The Justice tarot card and The 9 of Wands tarot card together can represent the idea of win-win solutions in your love life. This could mean that both partners come together to find solutions that benefit both of them. The Justice card encourages the idea of cause and effect, that every action has a reaction. It encourages you to look at the whole situation objectively, to come to a decision that works best for all parties. The 9 of Wands tarot card speaks of maintaining boundaries – both in terms of not crossing lines you’ve set for yourself, and allowing the other person space to do the same. It suggests that even in the most difficult of times, we can stay strong in our convictions and remain resilient.

When these two cards come together they can suggest that it is important to find solutions through thoughtful deliberation, and to always remember to stay true to yourself. You’ll need to work together to balance the needs of both partners, while still understanding what it is you want as an individual. Despite difficulties or obstacles, you’ll need to remain steady and work on solutions that make both of you happy. Finding a win-win solution in love isn’t easy but it is possible – you just need to stay honest and be open to the idea that things are bound to change over time. With balance and understanding, you can reach a mutually beneficial solution.

Justice is all about finding win-win solutions, and taking responsibility for the consequences of one’s choices and actions. The 9 Of Wands card is all about keeping and maintaining boundaries, particularly in regards to finances and jobs.

So when these two cards come together in a financial reading, they represent the idea of finding a balance between fairness and responsibility. It’s important to be mindful of any contracts or agreements you have with employers or customers. Make sure that everyone involved is treated fairly and that no one is taken advantage of. Make sure that you have clear boundaries in place regarding your finances and job, so that you can protect yourself while still providing a valuable service.

It is also important to remember that truth always comes out. Justice represents truth and honesty, and the 9 Of Wands card indicates that we need to be accountable for our actions and decisions. Be open to feedback and criticism – this will help you to grow and succeed in your career.

Financial success does not come easily, and it is important to remain persistent and disciplined in pursuit of your goals. Justice and the 9 Of Wands are asking you to create a financial plan that is both responsible and fair. Do your research and make sure you are aware of the current market conditions. Take measured risks, be mindful of contracts and boundaries, and remain honest, genuine and responsible. Ultimately, this tarot card combination is encouraging you to take charge of your finances and job, and to create a sustainable and prosperous future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 9 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 9 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card encourages a sense of balance and fairness in regards to all aspects of health. It shows that, in order to be healthy, we must consider the needs of our own bodies as well as the needs of others. This card shows us that making sure all parties are respected, heard, and taken care of leads to greater overall health. This could involve talking over health decisions with family members or friends, getting opinions from experts, or considering the best actions for the well-being of the entire community. It suggests that maintaining an equitable balance of needs for all involved helps us to better achieve our own desired health outcomes.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card encourages us to be fair and just when it comes to our finances. This card implies that individuals must be honest, ethical and responsible with their financial dealings. It also asks us to consider the needs of others, such as family or colleagues, when making important financial decisions. The Justice Tarot reminds us that in order to ensure long-term financial stability, we must examine our finances with a mind towards balance, fairness, and justice.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card reminds us to practice fairness and equity in our relationships. It suggests that to create meaningful, lasting relationships, individuals must be honest and respectful of their partners and of one another. This card also tells us to bear in mind the needs of both parties before making any major decisions. For instance, when negotiating an issue or trying to find a compromise, it is important to make sure that both parties feel heard and respected. In this way, we can create healthy, strong relationships that are built on mutual trust, respect and understanding.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The 9 of Wands Tarot card has a lot to say about health, finances and relationships. Health-wise, the 9 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take care of your physical and mental health. This card suggests setting boundaries to maintain your health and protect yourself from physical and mental exhaustion or illness. This often means taking time to yourself where you can recharge and reset. It is important to stay true to your boundaries in order to keep your health and energy levels in balance.

The 9 of Wands Tarot card also has a lot to say about finances. This card is an indication that you have been putting in the work and effort to stay on top of your finances. It is important to maintain this persistence, as it is key to having financial stability. It may also be time to set and keep boundaries around your financial decisions and habits. Being mindful of how you spend or save your money can be a great way to remain financially secure.

Finally, the 9 of Wands Tarot card speaks to relationships. This card encourages maintaining and strengthening boundaries within relationships. This can be done by expressing your needs, wants and expectations in a clear and consistent manner. It is also important to respect others’ boundaries and not impose your own will onto them. It is essential to create a safe and honest space to nurture and grow relationships.
