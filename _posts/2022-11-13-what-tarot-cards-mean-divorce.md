Divorce is a complex and emotionally charged issue, and tarot cards can provide some insight into the potential for divorce. Different tarot cards can indicate different factors that play a role in a potential divorce, and reading them together can help provide a more complete picture.

## What Tarot Cards Mean Divorce?

Here are a few cards that might signify divorce:

- Ten of Swords
- The Tower
- The Four of Swords
- Six of Cups

One of the most common tarot cards linked to divorce is the Ten of Swords. This card often indicates a feeling of hopelessness and despair, and can point to a relationship that has reached a breaking point. It can also suggest that one partner has given up on the relationship, and the other partner may not be far behind.

The Tower card can also be an indicator of divorce. This card often symbolizes sudden change and upheaval, and can point to a traumatic event, such as an infidelity or a financial crisis, that has caused irreparable damage. It can also suggest that a relationship has reached a point where it is no longer salvageable.

The Four of Swords can also indicate a potential for divorce. This card often symbolizes a period of reflection and a need for time apart. It can suggest that a couple is taking a break from their relationship in order to gain perspective and decide whether or not the relationship is worth salvaging.

The Six of Cups can be an indicator of divorce as well. This card often symbolizes nostalgia and a longing for the past, and can suggest that a couple is stuck in the past, unable to move forward and create a happier future.

## Ten of Swords

The [Ten of Swords](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-10-of-swords/) tarot card is generally seen as a card of great loss and sorrow, and as such, it can be interpreted as a prediction of divorce. The Ten of Swords carries a feeling of deep despair and finality, suggesting that a relationship has come to an end.

The card typically depicts a figure lying on the ground, with ten swords piercing their back. This imagery can represent the end of an emotional or psychological journey, and in the case of marriage, the end of a partnership.

The Ten of Swords can symbolize the harsh realities of divorce and the death of a shared dream. Divorce often involves a period of grief and mourning, and the Ten of Swords can symbolize this emotional experience.

The card can also indicate that a relationship has reached a point where it is no longer salvageable. This can be due to a number of factors, such as infidelity, communication issues, or a lack of trust and respect.

The Ten of Swords can also be interpreted as a warning that the divorce may be difficult and painful. It can be a reminder to consider the emotional impact of the decision, and to take steps to protect oneself during this process.

Overall, the Ten of Swords is a powerful card that can represent a difficult but necessary decision. It can foretell the end of a relationship, and a need to accept that closure and move on.

## The Tower

[The Tower](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-the-tower/) tarot card is often associated with the idea of a sudden, harsh ending. This can be interpreted to symbolize the end of a marriage through divorce.

The card can represent the dissolution of a relationship due to its tumultuous energies, indicating that the relationship can no longer continue. The Tower tarot card can also be interpreted as a warning that something is not quite right in a relationship.

It can symbolize the presence of a problem that has been ignored for too long, leading to the breakdown of the relationship. The card can indicate that the relationship has become too rigid, too constricting, too confining, and that the only way to move forward is to accept the end of the relationship.

The Tower tarot card can also represent a sudden, unexpected breakup. This can be due to a lack of communication, a sense of disconnection, or an inability to resolve the underlying issues in the relationship. It can also signify the end of a relationship due to the presence of an outside force, such as an affair, deceit, or betrayal.

The Tower tarot card can also suggest a delayed divorce. This can be due to the presence of unresolved issues that are preventing the divorce from occurring.

This could be due to financial issues, worries about the children, or a lack of agreement on the terms of the divorce. In conclusion, the Tower tarot card can be interpreted to predict divorce due to its tumultuous energies and its ability to symbolize the sudden end of a relationship.

It can warn of the presence of problems in the relationship, a sudden or unexpected breakup, or a delayed divorce.

## Four of Swords

[The Four of Swords](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-4-of-swords/) Tarot card is an indication that a relationship is in need of some rest and repair. It suggests that the couple is in a state of stagnation and suffering from a lack of communication or a breakdown of trust.

This card can also be a warning that the relationship is headed for divorce. Divorce can be caused by a variety of factors, but the Four of Swords card reflects a lack of communication and trust. When a couple has a breakdown of communication, they can struggle to understand each other.

This can lead to disagreements and arguments that can, over time, destroy the relationship. Trust is also a key factor in a successful relationship, and when it is broken, a couple can struggle to recover.

When trust is broken, it can be difficult for a couple to rebuild the relationship. This can lead to a lack of motivation to try and repair things and eventually, divorce.

The Four of Swords can also be an indication that a couple is living separate lives. When a relationship is lacking in communication and trust, couples often find themselves living lives that are separate from each other.

This can lead to a sense of disconnection and further weaken their bond. Ultimately, the Four of Swords card is an indication that a relationship is in need of some rest and repair.

## Six of Cups

[The Six of Cups](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-6-of-cups/) card in the Tarot deck is often associated with nostalgia, innocence, and childhood memories. In a relationship reading, this card is a warning of a relationship that is stuck in the past. The relationship is no longer growing, and the couple has lost their connection.

This card can also predict that one or both people in the relationship are unwilling to move on and embrace the changes that come with time. Without growth, the relationship is doomed to fail. The Six of Cups is a card that warns of a breakdown in communication.

Both parties in the relationship may be holding on to old resentments and refusing to talk about them. This can cause a divide between the partners, and without any room for compromise, the relationship will eventually fail.

The Six of Cups can also point to a lack of effort on both sides. The relationship may have become stagnant because one or both partners have stopped putting in effort. They may take each other for granted and not pay attention to each other anymore.

In this case, it is unlikely that the relationship will be able to be saved, and a divorce may be the best option.

Finally, the Six of Cups can indicate an imbalance in the relationship. One partner may be more dominant or possessive than the other, which can cause feelings of resentment and insecurity.

Without equality in the relationship, the couple may find it difficult to move forward and may eventually decide to part ways.

## Conclusion

No single tarot card can predict divorce with certainty; however, when read together, they can provide important insight into a couple’s relationship and help determine whether there is a potential for divorce.

Ultimately, it is important to remember that tarot cards are meant to be read as guidance, not as a prediction of the future.
