A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and The Devil card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and The Devil have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and The Devil mean together?

The Chariot tarot card and The Devil tarot card together speak to the need for action and change, a journey into the unknown, for the purpose of breaking down any unhealthy addictions and freeing oneself from the grip of materialism and obsession. In order to make the necessary changes to move forward you must confront your own inner demons and acknowledge the source of any envy or lustful desires. It is only through the power of action and movement, whether it be through a literal physical journey or a mental journey, that you can break free from the chains of the Devil and transform your life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Chariot and The Devil mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It could also indicate a change in location, such as moving to a healthier environment. Additionally, the card could symbolize seeking medical help or professional guidance when you need it. The Chariot card signals a desire to take full control of your physical health, and the power to do so through action.

Finances

The Chariot Tarot card symbolizes a forward movement towards financial success. It calls for action and initiative to make changes in your finances, such as cutting back on unnecessary expenses, maximizing investments, or finding a new job. The card could also indicate the need for a new vehicle, such as a car or truck, to help get you from place to place and make money. The Chariot encourages you to take charge of your finances and believe in your ability to succeed.

Relationships

The Chariot Tarot card symbolizes the need for change and movement in relationships. It could indicate an opportunity to rekindle an old flame or explore a new relationship. The card encourages you to take the initiative and make the necessary changes to create a successful and meaningful relationship. It could also indicate a journey or adventure with your partner, or a move to a new place together. The Chariot signals the power to take charge of your relationships and make the changes needed to find the love you want.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card is a strong reminder to be aware of our worst habits and weaknesses. In terms of health, this card can be a warning about the negative impact of addictions, such as smoking or substance abuse. It can urge us to break free from the bonds of unhealthy habits which can have a majorly detrimental effect on our physical and mental health. It can also refer to negative health behaviours and attitudes that are linked to our environment or social context, such as excessive drinking or poor diet.

When considering finances and The Devil Tarot card, it may be a sign that we are overly driven by greed and materialism. We may be striving for more than we really need and in doing so, may be risking our financial security. This card cautions that when there is too much emphasis on money and possessions, our relationships become strained and ultimately suffer. Overindulgence and a lack of balance can lead to unhealthy relationships and even financial ruin.

Finally, The Devil Tarot card is an important reminder to be conscious of our relationships and how our behaviour can affect them. Lust, obsession and envy can be destructive forces in any relationship. When we are driven by these negative impulses, our relationships can become superficial or even abusive. This card can serve as a warning for us to be mindful of our behaviour and practice self-control in our relationships.

The Devil Tarot card is a reminder to take stock of our behaviour and examine our motivations. It can help us to stay conscious of our health, finances and relationships and to be wary of any negative influences that may be at play. Paying attention to this card’s warnings can help us to stay on the right path and ensure that our lives remain balanced and healthy.
