However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does King Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The King of Pentacles and the 8 of Swords can have a unique meaning when combined. It suggests that one must use their wisdom and practical leadership skills to deal with the feeling of self-imposed restrictions and isolation. It is important to recognize the limitations you have set for yourself and then use your leadership capabilities to find a way to overcome them. This could be something as simple as taking a break from a particular situation or task and looking at it with a fresh set of eyes, or seeking the advice of another wise and trustworthy individual. With the King of Pentacles leading the way and the 8 of Swords providing guidance, you can use your intuition, experience, and judgement to slowly break out of the self-imposed prison and reach a prosperous end.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The King of Pentacles tarot card means a person that is grounding, disciplined, and focuses on the physical rather than the mental or spiritual. This individual is likely to have a great deal of endurance and the ability to stay level-headed in difficult times. Health-wise, the King of Pentacles tarot card signifies success in achieving optimal health and wellness through disciplined habits such as maintaining a balanced diet, exercising regularly and getting adequate rest. Even if they don’t have the best genetics, they are likely to make up for it by taking the best possible care of their body. This card also encourages individuals to pay attention to their inner needs and to find a healthy way to express them.

Finances: The King of Pentacles tarot card stands for financial stability due to good planning and wise decisions. This individual is likely to have entrepreneurial skills and make good investments, allowing them to save and benefit from their money. They know the value of hard work and are willing to take the necessary risks to make the best of their financial resources. This card suggests that one should not be afraid to take risks in order to gain financially, but to do so in a well-thought and balanced manner.

Relationships: The King of Pentacles tarot card is about stability, reliability, and commitment. This person is likely to build meaningful and long-lasting relationships with those around them, either romantically or platonic. They understand the importance of communication, compromise and selflessness in a relationship and they are willing to invest their energy and attention into it. This individual is likely to be very affectionate and supportive of their partner, and their relationships are often based on mutual respect and trust.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

The 8 of Swords in the Tarot is a card of confinement and restricted choices. It indicates stagnant situations, self-imposed walls and insecurities that block progress. This card points to being stuck in a sense of hopelessness and fear, and can indicate a situation in life in which personal power and freedom have been taken away.

When it comes to health, The 8 of Swords suggests that a person is not able to take action or make decisions that might benefit their well-being, because of emotional and mental barriers. This card can point to physical restrictions, such as long-term illness or disability, but also to emotional blockades, such as feeling overwhelmed by symptoms or a lack of options. It can also mean that a person is being held back by their own doubts and fears, or by external forces, such as social or cultural expectations.

For finances, The 8 of Swords indicates a lack of control or power over one’s finances. Money seems to come and go without much influence, leaving a person feeling helpless and powerless. This card can suggest a situation of poverty or financial entanglement, such as a large debt or bad credit, or it can point to insecurity in terms of making wise investments or taking risks.

Finally, for relationships, The 8 of Swords can indicate feeling powerless and unable to make decisions or take action in matters of the heart. It could suggest feeling betrayed or restricted by a partner, or feeling that one’s freedom is being taken away. It can also point to a fear of commitment, or of letting someone in, or to blockages in communication, such as an unwillingness to express oneself or understand the intentions of the other.
