A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 9 of Wands and 6 of Pentacles tarot cards together suggest that maintaining strong boundaries while being generous with help when the need arises is important. This combination indicates that we should be cautious and be mindful of our own limits when it comes to giving help and support, allowing ourselves to be open to receive help where necessary and resist any pressure to overcommit. With this combination, we are urged to maintain a balance between giving and taking, understanding that in order for us to nurture and care for ourselves, it is important to also stand up for our boundaries.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The 9 of Wands tarot card symbolizes the need to have healthy boundaries in life. This can take many forms including the need to prioritize self-care and to set limits on your time and energy. It can also be a reminder to take appropriate breaks when needed and to recognize when you’ve taken on too much. Additionally, this card can suggest the need to advocate for yourself in order to achieve desired results and to recognize when it’s time to walk away. Overall, the 9 of Wands is a reminder to recognize your limits and establish healthy boundaries in order to maintain your wellbeing.

For Finances:  
The 9 of Wands tarot card symbolizes the need for financial caution and strength of will. This card can be a reminder to be careful about taking on too much debt and to be mindful of the decisions made with money. Additionally, this card can signify a need to maintain personal boundaries when it comes to finances. This can include, for example, asking for help when needed, not taking on too much financial responsibility without help and being aware of the need for security and stability. Overall, the 9 of Wands is a reminder to be fiscally responsible and to practice financial discernment.

For Relationships:  
The 9 of Wands tarot card symbolizes the need to have healthy relationships. This card can be a reminder to maintain personal boundaries and to understand when to say no. It can be a reminder to stop trying to please everyone and to be comfortable with setting limits and holding firm to them. Additionally, this card can suggest the importance of protecting oneself from being taken advantage of by others. It can also be a reminder to be honest, respectful and kind when communicating with people. Overall, the 9 of Wands is a reminder to build and maintain healthy relationships based on trust and respect.

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card suggests a period of balance, where giving and receiving are in harmony. This card suggests that it is important to understand the value of both giving and taking from the universe in order to stay on the path of abundance.

In terms of health, this card is a reminder to stay mindful of our body’s needs for balance. Eating healthy meals, exercising regularly, and getting enough sleep will help to keep the body in balance. The 6 of Pentacles suggests that having a sense of generosity when it comes to caring for one’s own wellbeing is important, as it can encourage healing and preventive care.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Pentacles can represent balance and stability in our finances. This card encourages us to plan and budget, and to give and receive help in order to keep our finances in check. It suggests that taking the time to plan ahead and be aware of our spending habits can lead to financial stability and growth.

In terms of relationships, the 6 of Pentacles encourages us to have a sense of balance in our relationships. This card suggests that being generous and offering help to those in need can lead to deeper and more meaningful connections. The 6 of Pentacles also urges us to be open to receiving help and support when needed, as this will help to keep us in balance. Overall, the 6 of Pentacles encourages us to be generous in all aspects of our lives, and to remember the value of giving and receiving in order to create abundance and balance within ourselves and our relationships.
