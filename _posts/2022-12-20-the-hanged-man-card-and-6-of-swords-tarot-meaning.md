A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card combined with the 6 Of Swords tarot card suggests that you are in need of some reflection and a change of perspective. You may have found yourself in an uncomfortable or difficult situation and the cards indicate that it is time to make a necessary transition, to move in a different direction. You need to take a step back and look at your current situation with a new set of eyes. Wisdom and self-sacrifice are called for as you change your mind and your environment. It may be time to relocate, either mentally or physically, in order to gain more knowledge, understanding and clarity of purpose.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man tarot card is a powerful symbol of enlightenment and transformation. It suggests that the time is ripe for re-evaluation and taking a step back from the current situation. Its message is to find a new perspective, allowing a bigger picture to come into focus. This new insight is often related to the surrendering of ego and the embracing of an open and enlightened approach to life.

Health

The Hanged Man tarot card in regards to health relates to the idea of making a conscious decision to become mindful of one’s body and health. This means taking a step back from unhealthy habits and behaviours and seeking a new way to approach health. It suggests that in order to be truly healthy, we must find a balance between physical, mental, and spiritual aspects of our lives. This card is a reminder that our health needs to be taken seriously, and we must be willing to give ourselves the time and care that is required to stay healthy.

Finances

When it comes to finances, The Hanged Man tarot card encourages us to take a step back and look at the bigger picture. It warns us against being too focused on the day-to-day dealings, but instead to contemplate our goals and ambitions. The card also encourages us to be mindful of our money, to pay attention to how we spend it and to be aware of our financial obligations and responsibilities. This card also suggests that in order to meet our financial goals, we must practice patience and discipline and be willing to make sacrifices.

Relationships

The Hanged Man tarot card encourages us to take a step back and look at our relationships from a different perspective. It suggests that we must be willing to surrender our ego and practice open-mindedness and understanding. This card also suggests that in order to have healthy relationships, we must learn to accept and embrace the differences between people and relationships. It further encourages us to be mindful of our words and actions and be willing to take responsibility for our mistakes and make amends when necessary. Finally, this card reminds us that our relationships should be based on mutual respect, understanding, and compromise.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: When The 6 Of Swords in the Tarot card is encountered, it can indicate a time to move on from a situation that may have been holding one back in health. It could be a sign to take a break from strenuous physical activity, to focus on a different form of exercise, or to take time to de-stress and reflect on how to achieve better balance in order to achieve optimum health. It could also indicate a need to break out of a rut, such as a sedentary lifestyle, and move forward with something new and exciting.

Finances: The 6 Of Swords in the Tarot card could also be a sign that one needs to make a change in their financial situation. It can be a warning to take a closer look at spending habits and to make sure that money is being used wisely. It could also be a sign to take a look at other investments and to explore options for increasing one’s financial stability.

Relationships: When the 6 Of Swords appears in the Tarot card, it could be an indication of a need to move on from a relationship that is not beneficial. It can be a reminder to evaluate the relationships in one’s life, and to move away from damaging individuals or dynamics. It can also serve as a sign to look for new relationships that can bring happiness and joy, rather than stress and conflict.
