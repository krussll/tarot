However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 10 Of Cups mean together?

This could manifest as family or friends joining forces to take on a collective goal, leading to successful commerce and expansion. This could also mean success in travel for these joint endeavors. The 10 Of Cups also offers a sense of deep contentment and satisfaction within the home as a result of this combined effort. The strong connection between the two cards will ensure that the outcome of their combined efforts will be joyful and uplifting for everyone involved.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The 3 of Wands encourages the exploration of different types of holistic practices like yoga and meditation that can help bring one’s physical and emotional well-being into balance.

Finances: The 3 Of Wands tarot card suggests that one’s financial situation is stable and that careful planning and smart investments will bring success. It’s a good time for taking calculated risks and making courageous decisions about investments that could bring long-term returns. When it comes to money, the 3 of Wands means taking a long-term view and focusing on stability and security.

Relationships: The 3 Of Wands tarot card symbolizes teamwork and collaboration, which means that relationships are flourishing. It symbolizes a positive, stable relationship where both partners are communicating openly and honestly and bringing their best selves to the table. It encourages taking the time to nurture relationships and cultivate trust and understanding through shared interests and experiences. It also suggests that you and your partner need to be proactive and take the lead in finding ways to grow and expand the relationship.
