However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does King Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The King Of Wands tarot card combined with The 9 of Swords tarot card represent a person who is dealing with a difficult situation, yet is capable of overcoming it with courage and charm. This person has the power and leadership to battle any difficulty that is in their way, however, their fear and anxiety have the potential to cripple their progress and cause misery. While this person may struggle with grief and depression, they must remember that their courage and charm are the keys to conquering their internal demons and that their boldness and strength can help them rise above any situation they may face.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card typically symbolizes anguish, fear, grief and torment. It speaks to situations of deep despair or unrest, of being in the throes of a negative, overwhelming thought loop. Physically, it can signify insomnia and exhaustion, which can lead to further distress.

The impact of this card can be particularly acute in one’s health, finances and relationships. Health-wise, a person in despair may find themselves struggling to take care of themselves physically, emotionally and mentally. They could experience sleeplessness, low mood, lethargy, impaired decision-making and a lost sense of hope. Financially, this may lead to overspending, impulsive decisions, gambling, and an overwhelming feeling of financial insecurity. Relationships may suffer from a lack of communication, mistrust, anxiety, and disconnection.

The 9 of Swords Tarot card can be a difficult one to confront, but it is extremely important to understand the gravity of the situation in order to move forward. It is a reminder to take care of yourself, to take solace in your own resilience, and to reach out for outside help. With the right support, a person can find ways to work through the issues the card is symbolizing, allowing them to make positive changes and create better outcomes in health, finances and relationships.
