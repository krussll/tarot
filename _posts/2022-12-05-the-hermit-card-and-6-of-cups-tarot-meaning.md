A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the 6 Of Cups tarot card together represent an important journey of self-discovery. The Hermit encourages us to look within and search for personal truth and spiritual guidance. The 6 Of Cups is a reminder of our past, a reminder to let go of nostalgia and use it as a tool to access the inner wisdom of our hearts. It’s a call to value gifts, innocence and reunions so that we can become more connected to our true selves and gain greater insight into our journey. This combination of cards encourages us to go deep into ourselves and use our inner guidance to discover our true purpose and gain clarity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card can indicate a time of going inward and taking a look at our health. This can mean getting a physical to make sure everything is okay or getting more rest. It can mean taking the time to meditate and practice mindfulness to reduce stress and promote well-being. It can also mean setting aside time to indulge in our favorite hobbies or activities, allowing us to recharge and be kind to ourselves. By taking a step back and looking inward, we can take the time to evaluate our health and make sure it’s on track.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card can indicate a time of taking a step back and re-examining our finances. This doesn’t mean to be reckless or make any sudden decisions, but rather to take the time to analyze our current financial situation and work out a plan for the future. It can be beneficial to sit down and create a budget, set goals, and think about ways to save money. This is also a time to identify areas of improvement, as well as take stock of what’s already going well with our money.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card can also be a sign to take a step back from relationships and analyze the state of our current connections. This is the time to consider the type of relationships we’re in and the roles we play in them, as well as any changes that need to be made if things aren’t going well. We can also take this time to focus on ourselves and any areas of self-improvement that need to be attended to if we want better relationships. By going inward, we can make sure our relationships are healthy and meaningful.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health: When The 6 Of Cups Tarot card appears in a reading related to health, it could indicate that you need to take a step back and look at your life from a more nostalgic perspective. It could mean that you are too focused on future goals and need to take a break from the hustle and bustle of life in order to remember what truly matters to you. This card could also imply that you need to take care of yourself and find a way to make time for relaxation and self-care. When it comes to health, The 6 Of Cups Tarot card encourages you to give yourself the gift of time to reflect and regroup.

Finances: The 6 Of Cups Tarot card in a financial reading could suggest that you’re being too hard on yourself when it comes to money management. The card could indicate that you need to spend more time enjoying the rewards of your hard work, by looking back at the positive changes you’ve made in your finances. You may need to take some time to appreciate the gifts that you’ve been given in life, such as financial security or the opportunity to save money. It can also mean that you need to be more mindful and realistic when it comes to creating financial goals and plans.

Relationships: The 6 Of Cups Tarot card in a relationship reading may imply that you need to give your relationship the opportunity for renewal and reunion. Perhaps you and your partner have been too focused on the future and need to take a step back to remember why you started the relationship in the first place. The card could also mean that you need to reflect on the innocence of your relationship; take time to appreciate the gifts that you’ve been given and the ways in which you’ve grown together. Finally, the 6 Of Cups Tarot card can suggest that you need to allow your relationship space to breathe and develop naturally.
