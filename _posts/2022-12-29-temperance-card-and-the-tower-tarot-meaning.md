A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and The Tower card together in particular.

The Temperance card and The Tower have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and The Tower mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The Tower tarot card together suggest a situation in which balance and harmony must be restored after undergoing a period of major upheaval. They can be interpreted as a warning that a lack of patience, self-control, and foresight can lead to unprecedented destruction and unwanted change. This can be a difficult journey, but it can also be an opportunity to learn from our mistakes and make choices that will help us create a better future. With the guidance of these two cards, we can strive to find balance amidst chaos and use our newfound understanding to achieve balance and harmony.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Temperance and The Tower mean together for your love life?

The Temperance and Tower tarot cards, when viewed together, can bring a powerful message to your love life. They speak to the need for balance and harmony between partners, while warning of the potential for dramatically unwanted change. This harmony is not necessarily easy to come by, and requires patience and self-control on both sides. To keep the relationship healthy and strong, you must be willing to commit to hard work and understanding.

At the same time, this combination of cards can suggest that unwanted change is looming. This could be the result of a partner’s own decisions, or an outside force that will not be easily resisted. In either case, the Tower card warns of ruin and upheaval that can cause great disgrace and violence.

When taken together, the Temperance and Tower tarot cards suggest that balance and harmony must be sought in your love life, or you risk facing difficult and unwanted changes. While change can often lead to growth, it can also bring destruction and ruin. Patience, self-control, and understanding between partners is needed to prevent this from happening. With each person contributing their part, you can keep your love life from toppling down.

### What does Temperance and The Tower mean together for your finances?

The Temperance tarot card encourages patience and self-control when it comes to your finances and job. It is important to have a balance and harmony between the spending and saving and it suggests that you should avoid overindulgent behavior and make sure to stay within your financial limits. It is also important to be patient and not be too impulsive when it comes to making decisions about money, as it is a long-term game and not a quick fix.

The Tower tarot card on the other hand is a warning to be prepared for change – often, sudden and unexpected changes that may result in unwanted upheaval, ruin or disgrace. It is important to understand that we can’t control everything and that sometimes, you may have to accept a change even if you don’t want it. It is important to remain flexible and open to any changes that may come your way.

When taken together, the Temperance and Tower tarot cards are reminding us that while we should focus on maintaining balance and harmony in our finances and job, we should also be prepared for unexpected changes. It is important to stay mindful of your decisions and be careful not to get too caught up in financial and work decisions that could have negative impacts on your finances and job. It is also important to be prepared and remain flexible with any changes that may come your way.

The Temperance tarot card and The Tower tarot card can provide insight into one’s overall health. When combined, these two cards suggest that the need for balance and harmony must be achieved in order to maintain one’s physical health. The Temperance tarot card advises those in need of health guidance to practice patience, self-control, and moderation when it comes to all matters related to their physical wellbeing. Without it, an individual’s health can experience an unwelcome and disruptive change.

The Tower tarot card serves as a warning against the consequences of neglecting one’s health. Without balance and moderation, unwelcome and potentially dangerous changes can occur. Such changes may include physical ailments, injury, illness, and even ruin or disgrace. When taken together, these tarot cards can be a powerful tool for those seeking insight into their physical health. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Temperance and The Tower mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Temperance mean?

For Health:

The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of creating balance and moderation in life. In terms of physical health, this card advises taking a balanced approach to diet and exercise and promoting a healthy lifestyle overall. Temperance encourages one to not veer too far in either direction, whether it is overexercising or under-eating. Taking a holistic approach to health means ensuring that all parts of the body’s system are in check, including the mind, body, and spirit. This card also encourages taking the time to nurture and nourish one’s physical and mental wellbeing. For example, one should make sure to take breaks and get adequate rest, as well as practice relaxation techniques such as yoga and meditation.

For Finances:

The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to practice financial moderation. When it comes to managing money, it is important to practice temperance by striking a balance between saving and spending. This card suggests that it is important to spend within one’s means and not to get too caught up in a lifestyle that is beyond one’s budget. Instead, one should strive to make wise investments and budget for long-term financial security. This card also recommends avoiding impulsive purchases and focusing on building a secure financial foundation.

For Relationships:

The Temperance Tarot card is an invitation to create balance and moderation in relationships. This card reminds us that it is important to take the time to nurture and cultivate relationships rather than engaging in an unbalanced dynamic that can lead to conflicts. One should strive to create a healthy give and take in relationships, both between romances and friendships. When it comes to work relationships, temperance suggests that one should temper any extreme emotions or reactions that could lead to a lack of cooperation and communication. This card also encourages people to practice non-judgment and find compromise whenever possible.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health  
The Tower Tarot card is a warning that health issues may be upon you. Health issues can range from minor ailments like a cold or the flu to a major illness that could require you to make changes to your lifestyle. It is a sign to take care of your body, to pay attention to warning signs, and to get help if you need it. This is a card of disruption and can indicate that an ailment might arise suddenly and unexpectedly.

Finances  
The Tower Tarot card can be an omen of financial disruption. It may signal that you could face financial ruin or loss of a job, especially if more negative cards are present in your reading. It could also signal that some big changes are coming your way and that you must be prepared for them. It is a warning to protect your assets, to be aware of any risks you take, and to keep your finances in check.

Relationships  
The Tower Tarot card can be seen as a symbol of upheaval and transformation in relationships. It could signal that a relationship is coming to an end or that there may be major changes that need to take place in order to save it. If a couple is facing difficult times, they may need to take a step back and assess the situation. This card also reminds us to stay aware of how our actions affect others and be willing to make changes to keep the relationship strong.
