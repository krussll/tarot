It means that while it is important to protect oneself and be independent, it is just as important to make sure that one’s family is taken care of and that all of the members come together in unity. It is a reminder that both one’s own security and the security of one’s family is essential for a happy life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands Tarot card can be a great guide to those looking to make positive changes in their lives in regards to health, finance and relationships.

Health: The 7 of Wands can help one to become more aware of their own physical and mental health. It encourages one to be aware of their body and mind, and make adjustments as necessary in order to ensure optimum health. Reaching out to professionals as needed is an important aspect of going beyond mere self-awareness to actively working to heal any ailments or mental health issues.

Finances: This card encourages us to be responsible with our finances, carefully planning and budgeting our income. It may be necessary to take a second job or invest in a business venture in order to better our financial situation, as competition in the business world can be fierce. It is also important to make sure one’s investments are well protected.

Relationships: The 7 of Wands may represent a time when we must protect our relationships from challenges that are thrown our way. Self-defense is important in this case, as it is possible to stand up for ourselves and our values while still protecting those we care about. It is important to be true to our own beliefs while being respectful of others’ boundaries. Taking time to self-reflect is important in this case in order to better understand our own emotions and how to best express them.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

The Ten of Cups Tarot card represents a time of complete contentment and joy in all aspects of your life. This card is a strong indication of being surrounded by close family and friends and having a strong support system. When this card appears in a reading, it can represent blessings in health, finances, and relationships.

In terms of health, The Ten of Cups Tarot card can suggest that the time is right for action on your wellbeing. This could include making changes to your diet or exercise routine to improve your health, or seeking medical advice. It could also mean that you are particularly healthy at this time, and the positive attitude that comes from physical health is aiding other areas of your life.

Financially speaking, The Ten of Cups Tarot card could indicate a period of stability. You may be able to save up for a big purchase, or be able to enjoy some financial success. This could be in the form of a promotion at work or a successful business venture. There may also be a sense of family stability, in that everyone contributes to the household income and are able to live comfortably.

Relationship wise, The Ten of Cups Tarot card could suggest that your relationships are solid and full of support and love. There is a sense of deep commitment and loyalty, and the feeling that each partner is with the other for better or worse. This could refer to a marriage or a more casual partnership, but either way, the card can indicate that the relationship is strong and nourishing.
