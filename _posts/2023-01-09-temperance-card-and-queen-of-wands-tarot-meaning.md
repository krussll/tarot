A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The Temperance card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and the Queen Of Wands tarot card combine to represent a balance of power and control in your life. With the Temperance card urging you to find moderation and harmony, and the Queen Of Wands suggesting confidence and self-assurance, it is important to find the right balance between these two energies. It is important to be patient as you learn what works best for you and develop your own sense of self-control. With these two tarot cards, you can make your life an equilibrium of peace and trust in yourself.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Instead, it suggests that both parties should take the time to consider the wishes of the other and look for a compromise that is mutually satisfactory. Rather than impulsively chasing one’s desires, the Temperance card encourages one to take the time to understand how best to move forward with grace and consideration.

The Queen of Wands card builds on this idea by reminding those looking for love to have confidence in themselves. The Queen is a feisty person who is not afraid to stand up for what she wants. This self-assurance is a must for anyone looking to find a lasting and healthy relationship. The Queen of Wands encourages the reader to own their desires and actively pursue a healthy and fulfilling connection.

When taken together, the Temperance and Queen of Wands cards offer a powerful reminder for those looking for love: take the time to understand both one’s own needs and those of their potential partner, and have the self-assurance to make sure that one’s wishes are satisfied. This is the key to finding balance, harmony and success in one’s love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

When it comes to health, The Temperance Tarot card indicates a need to find balance in how we lead our lives. This card suggests moderation in all areas of life, such as diet, exercise, sleep and other habits. We should strive to practice self-control and discipline in our daily lives, as it will lead to greater overall health. This card also advises us to monitor our progress and adjust as needed to find a lifestyle that is healthy and sustainable.

In terms of finances, The Temperance Tarot card suggests that we need to stay disciplined and focused on our spending. We should strive to keep our finances in balance, by avoiding impulse purchases and keeping track of our monthly spending. This card also reminds us to be patient and tolerant when planning for our financial future. A long-term, sustainable approach to money management will lead to greater financial security.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Temperance Tarot card signifies the need for harmony and balance. This card encourages us to be patient and understanding with our partners. We should also strive to be open-minded and tolerant when communicating, listening carefully and respecting our differences. In this way, we can build stronger and healthier relationships that are built on mutual respect and understanding.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health: The Queen Of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take care of your physical and mental health. She encourages you to take charge of your health, to be proactive in seeking out treatments and therapies that will help you, and to have the confidence to speak up and advocate for yourself when you need to. She encourages you to keep your spirit strong, and to have faith in the healing power of your own body.

Finances: The Queen Of Wands Tarot card is a reminder that your financial health is also connected to your physical and mental health. She encourages you to take charge of your finances, to be organized with your paperwork and to make wise investments that will bring you both short-term and long-term security. She also encourages you to take risks when it comes to your finances—but to make sure you understand the risks and how to manage them.

Relationships: The Queen of Wands Tarot card encourages you to bring confidence and self-assurance into your relationships. She encourages you to have healthy boundaries and to be honest with yourself and with your partners. She also encourages you to express yourself fully and to be willing to tackle any challenges that come your way. In general, she encourages you to approach relationships with an open mind and a willingness to learn and grow.
