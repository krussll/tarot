A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Magician card and The Sun card together in particular.

The The Magician card and The Sun have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Magician and The Sun mean together?

When The Magician tarot card and The Sun tarot card appear together in a reading, it indicates that your focused creative energy and inspired action are coming into alignment with your material goals and desires, bringing you closer to achieving success. This is a time to trust your instincts and follow your heart, as your hard work is sure to pay off. Keep your head up and your spirits high, as the sun is shining bright on your future.

In terms of your health, The Magician tarot card and The Sun tarot card indicate that you have the power to manifest your own wellbeing. This is a time to focus on your positive Intentions and take inspired action to improve your health. The Sun tarot card also suggests that you may be feeling particularly good physically at this time, so enjoy your vitality and make the most of it.

In terms of your relationships, The Magician tarot card and The Sun tarot card mean that you have the power to create the kind of relationships you want in your life. This is a time to focus on your positive Intentions and take inspired action to improve your relationships. The Sun tarot card also suggests that you may be feeling particularly good about your relationships at this time, so enjoy the love and connection you share with others.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Magician and The Sun mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Magician and The Sun the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Magician mean?

The Magician Tarot card is all about taking focused action in order to turn your visions into reality. In terms of health, this means taking care of yourself physically and mentally in order to achieve your health goals. This may require making lifestyle changes, such as eating a healthy diet and getting regular exercise, in order to improve your overall health. In terms of finances, The Magician Tarot card suggests taking proactive steps in order to manifest your financial goals. This may involve creating a budget and sticking to it, investing in yourself and your future, and being mindful of your spending. In terms of relationships, The Magician Tarot card indicates taking action in order to create and maintain healthy and fulfilling relationships. This may involve communicating openly and honestly with your partner, setting boundaries, and investing time and energy into your relationships.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun is a positive card, and as such it bodes well for all areas of your life. In terms of your health, The Sun heralds good news and a period of good health. This is a time when you can feel confident and relaxed about your physical well-being. Things are going well for you on the financial front too, and you can expect to see some positive movement in your bank balance. The Sun is also a great card for relationships. If you are single, it indicates that you will meet someone special who will bring joy and happiness into your life. If you are in a relationship, The Sun represents a time of happiness and contentment. Your bond with your partner will be strong, and you will enjoy spending time together.
