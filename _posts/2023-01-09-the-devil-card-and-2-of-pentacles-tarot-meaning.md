However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and 2 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Devil card and 2 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and 2 Of Pentacles mean together?

When The Devil tarot card and The 2 of Pentacles tarot card appear together in a spread, they could be signaling that you are stuck in a cycle of temptation and materialism. You desire things that your wallet and your heart cannot afford and you are trying to learn how to properly balance your desires and needs. This can be a difficult task as many desires, such as greed, envy, and sexual lust, can come up when trying to make decisions. It can be overwhelming as you are presented with different options and you need to figure out what is the best for you. The cards together could be saying to take a step back and be conscious of the choices you are making, as one wrong decision could lead to a downward spiral of addiction, obsession, and materialism.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

For example, a person experiencing an addiction might not be able to spend quality time with their family and friends, be unable to stick to a budget, or even be unable to take care of themselves. If a person is struggling with an addiction, it is important to reach out for help and get the necessary resources to heal and make positive changes.

In terms of finances, the Devil Tarot card warns of the dangers of materialism, greed, and obsession. It is important to keep perspective when it comes to money and material items. If we become too invested in these things, they may come to define us and take away from more important aspects of our lives. It is important to remember that money and possessions do not lead to ultimate happiness or fulfillment and should not be used to escape from life’s challenges.

Finally, the Devil Tarot card can have implications for relationships. Greed, envy and obsession can lead to distrust and disharmony between people. It is important to stay mindful of our behaviours and ensure that our relationship with our loved ones is based on respect, communication and understanding. If we become overly attached to material items or unhealthy behaviours, it can threaten the security and trust in our relationships. It is important to remain balanced, stay away from unhealthy behaviours, and take care of ourselves and the relationships we value.
