## What does The Hierophant and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 7 of Cups tarot card together suggest a conflict between traditional expectations and your own desire to explore the many potential paths available to you. The Hierophant encourages conformity and respect for tradition, while the 7 of Cups tempts you with the promise of a multitude of thrilling possibilities. It’s important to be mindful of traditional values and expectations, but it’s also important to remember that the world is full of exciting paths and possibilities to explore.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and 7 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 7 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

When it comes to health, The Hierophant Tarot card indicates that it is important to maintain traditional health routines to stay healthy. This could include activities like exercising regularly, eating nutritious meals, sleeping well, and seeing a doctor each year for a health check-up. It also serves as a reminder that taking care of yourself and following the fundamental health principles is essential for a healthy lifestyle. It can also represent the idea that sometimes we need to follow the “tried and true” methods when it comes to staying healthy, and that it’s not always necessary to try something new and different.

Finances

For finances, The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that it is best to practice financial responsibility and stick to traditional financial principles. This could include setting up a budget, tracking expenses, and saving for the future. It also encourages staying away from get-rich-quick schemes and instead using proven, sustainable methods to build wealth. It is important to stay committed to financial goals and to be consistent when it comes to money and investments.

Relationships

In relationships, The Hierophant Tarot card encourages us to stick to our values and commitments. This could include honoring promises and agreements, as well as avoiding gossip, dishonesty, and disrespectful behavior. This can also be a reminder to focus on the important aspects of relationships like building trust, communication, and mutual respect. We should strive to establish strong connections with the people in our lives, and the Hierophant Tarot card serves as a reminder that it is important to follow established principles when it comes to relationships.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 7 Of Cups Tarot card reflects the desire to have it all, and a fear of making the wrong choice. This card can signify that a person may be suffering from a lack of commitment in their ability to make healthy choices. This could manifest as difficulty sticking to healthy eating habits, wanting to workout but having difficulty finding the motivation, or constantly starting and stopping health regimens. This card can signify that a person must commit to a plan and stick with it in order to reach their health goals.

Finances:  
The 7 Of Cups Tarot card can suggest that a person has the potential to achieve their financial goals but lacks the drive to make their dream a reality. This card can signify a fear of making the wrong choices in regards to money and investing, or an inability to take risks. On the other hand, this card can also be a sign of the potential for abundance if the person is able to make the right decisions. It is important to remember that commitment and focus are necessary in order to achieve financial success.

Relationships:  
The 7 Of Cups Tarot card can mean that a person has difficulty committing to relationships. This can be due to a fear of making the wrong decision or a fear of being too vulnerable. This card can mean that a person needs to face their fears and commit to a relationship in order to grow and experience true love and connection. It is necessary to take risks and be vulnerable in order to build strong, meaningful relationships. Commitment and dedication are key for any relationship to be successful.
