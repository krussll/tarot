## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

In terms of health, the Wheel of Fortune can be interpreted to foretell a change in health that could be positive or negative depending on the overall spread. When it comes to finances, the Wheel of Fortune card is a harbinger of good luck, or of a major change in financial fortune. In any case, the Wheel of Fortune is a sign of a new financial beginning.

Finally, the Wheel of Fortune card has many meanings for relationships. It can predict a new and exciting relationship, a deeper understanding of existing relationships, or an end to a relationship that may have run its course. It suggests that it is time for a change and for taking a risk for something better. The Wheel of Fortune easily predicts a whole new chapter of relationships, one that could be full of unexpected surprises.

In summation, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card has a great deal to say about health, finances, and relationships. It is a sign of good luck and fortune, a turning point in life, and the potential for great success. No matter what the situation, the Wheel of Fortune is a positive omen that should be embraced and acted upon.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands tarot card is an indication of protection against competition, particularly when one finds themselves in a challenging position. Health-wise, this card symbolizes standing your ground to maintain your wellbeing or energy. It can also indicate the need to fight off illness, take preventative measures against any potential health issues, and make sure to take proper rest and nutrition.

Financially, this card can mean that one should operate from a place of security, rather than fear or recklessness. Planning for the future, setting achievable goals, and adhering to sound guidelines is necessary for success. It also symbolizes the importance of having a strong defense against financial scrambling, such as having consistent income streams and a savings plan.

In relationships, this card serves as a reminder to practice self-defense, both in ways we can maintain boundaries and in ways we can stop from becoming too codependent. It also stands as a reminder to fight off any incoming negative energy which can take any shape, whether in the form of jealousy or verbal attacks. Maintaining a secure sense of self is the best way to protect the relationship, as well as to understand one’s value and worth. All in all, the 7 of Wands tarot card serves as a reminder that it is important to take the necessary steps to build and protect oneself in order to keep a sense of security and wellbeing in all areas of life.
