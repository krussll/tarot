## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health

The Temperance Tarot card signifies a renewed sense of balance and harmony for the physical body. This can mean taking the time to get adequate rest and exercise, paying attention to mental and emotional wellbeing, and helping the body stay in a state of balance between opposing forces. It can also help us find the right balance between being too strict or overindulgent with our diet, habits, and lifestyle. This can lead to a greater understanding of our physical needs, and an ability to make healthier choices overall.

Finances

The Temperance Tarot card emphasizes moderation and responsible decision-making in financial matters. It suggests that we take time to look at the big picture, and set realistic goals with achievable timelines rather than make rash decisions. This could include learning more about budgeting, creating a savings plan, and being mindful of our spending habits. It encourages us to find a balance between short-term satisfaction and long-term stability.

Relationships

The Temperance Tarot card also symbolizes balance and harmony in personal relationships. This can mean finding a balance between assertiveness and flexibility, understanding different perspectives, and communicating in a non-judgmental way. It can also mean spending time with family and friends in a way that allows for meaningful connection and shared experiences. Taking a step back to observe our relationships and how we interact with one another can promote greater understanding and help us create healthier relationships overall.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is a card of retreat and rest, signifying a need to step back and take a break from physical and mental health stressors. This card advises its bearer to take a break and take time to renew their energy and refresh their mind, body, and soul. Taking care of one’s own health is an important part of being able to perform well in all areas of life. By taking this time for oneself, the 4 of Swords Tarot card promises that one will be able to regain the fortitude and vigor to move forward with their lives in a healthier way.

Finances:

For those who want to make meaningful changes in their financial life, the 4 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to step back and take a break to look at one’s overall financial picture. This card suggests taking an unbiased look and assess the current state of one’s financial life. This includes assessing both current and long-term goals, as well as one’s current resources and restrictions. Stepping back and taking stock of the overall picture can help one recognize patterns, anticipate future changes, and make decisions that will help one achieve their financial success.

Relationships:

The 4 of Swords Tarot card advises its bearer to take time to take care of oneself, both mentally and emotionally. This is especially important in relationships, where one may be feeling overwhelmed by the issues or expectations of the other person. Taking time to rest and reflect on one’s emotions can help one better understand what they need and can help them to better communicate their feelings. By taking this time to restore and rejuvenate, one will be more capable of understanding and respecting their own feelings, as well as those of the other person. This can result in a healthier, happier relationship for both involved.
