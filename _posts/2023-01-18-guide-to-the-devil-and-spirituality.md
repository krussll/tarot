Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The Devil card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Devil, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Devil.

## The Devil And Sprituality – An Overview

The Devil tarot card is a potent reminder that health is often lost in the pursuit of materialistic pleasures and desires.

The Devil is a reminder to be mindful of the need to keep our appetites and passions in check, lest they become unhealthy obsessions.

The card warns against becoming fixated on any particular need, whether it be money, power, sex, or any other material desire.

In the end, it is important to remember that health and balance come from within, not from without.

Too much of anything can lead to addiction and unhealthy behaviors, and The Devil is a reminder to take a step back and consider the consequences before indulging too deeply.

## Upright Meanings For The Devil And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

The Devil tarot card is a powerful reminder of the temptations that can lead us astray from our spiritual path.

It speaks of our basic drives for things like addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust.

These can be strong impulses that pull us away from our spiritual center.

We must be aware of these impulses and recognize that they can be detrimental to our spiritual health.

The Devil card is a reminder that we must be vigilant in guarding our spiritual selves against these urges and focus on cultivating a more balanced and harmonious relationship with our inner self.

### What It Means For The Future

The Devil tarot card indicates that your journey to spiritual enlightenment may be hindered by certain addictions and obsessions that are holding you back.

These obsessions could range from materialism, greed, envy, and sexual lust.

You need to be aware of these sources of temptation, and put in the effort to resist them.

This card is a reminder to practice self-discipline and focus on the inner journey instead of physical distractions.

If you can break free from these addictions, you will find the spiritual path much clearer and more rewarding.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Devil tarot card speaks to the darker side of human nature, particularly in relation to spirituality.

It warns us to beware of feelings of addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust when it comes to those close to us.

We must be mindful of our own spiritual beliefs and be cognizant of the spiritual needs of those around us.

It is important to guard against allowing these negative influences to become a source of power and control.

By recognizing the potential for such influences, we can create an environment of mutual understanding and respect that nourishes our spiritual growth.

## Reversed Meanings For The Devil And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

When the Devil card is reversed, it suggests that you are in the process of breaking free from the grip that materialism and obsession have had on you.

You are beginning to heal and reclaim your spiritual power and self-confidence.

You may be becoming aware of how these addictions and obsessions have kept you from experiencing your true potential and full self-expression.

The reversed Devil card also indicates that you are now in a position of strength, no longer enslaved to your negative thoughts and obsessions.

You are beginning to see the power of your spiritual self and are tapping into the universal source of divine energy.

You are also learning to practice self-love, self-care, and self-compassion.

You may also be learning to trust yourself, others, and the universe more and have greater faith that everything will work out for the best.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Devil tarot card is reversed, it can signify a positive energy coming into your life and a spiritual transformation.

This transformation could be the result of freeing yourself from materialism, envy, and obsession and instead focusing on personal growth and spiritual development.

Your newfound spiritual awareness may come from within and may lead you to the realization that true happiness and fulfillment come from within and not from external sources.

On a spiritual level, the reversed Devil card can be interpreted as a sign of liberation from addictions and destructive behaviors.

It is indicative of a time of clarity and insight, leading to a deeper understanding of your own spiritual needs and desires.

You may also be feeling more connected to your higher self, allowing you to make choices that come from a more enlightened place.

This could be a time of great spiritual growth, healing, and reconnection with the divine.

You may feel a newfound sense of purpose and direction, and discover a renewed sense of hope and joy in your life.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the Devil card is reversed, it can mean that those close to you are striving for a more spiritual connection in their lives.

This may indicate that they are becoming more aware of their own inner spiritual wisdom and that they are actively seeking out ways to express this in their daily lives.

They are likely to be making conscious efforts to improve their spiritual understanding of the world, and may be turning to spiritual practices such as meditation, journaling, yoga, or prayer as a means of doing so.

This reversal may also suggest that those close to you are becoming more aware of the need to break away from negative habits, such as addiction, envy, materialism, and obsessions.

They may have begun to recognize the need to find more balance and harmony in their lives in order to create a more authentic connection with the Divine.

They may be actively trying to free themselves from the bondage of materialism and focus more on spiritual growth and inner transformation.

This shift in focus may also help them to become more mindful of the world around them and the interconnectedness of all things.
