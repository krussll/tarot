A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 6 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 6 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The Hermit tarot card suggests that if you take some time to go within, to explore your own inner truth and your spiritual path, you can find the illumination you need. This can help to lead you to a victory, an award, or some form of recognition for your efforts, as suggested by the 6 Of Wands tarot card. By focusing on your inner truth and spiritual journey, you can gain the insight needed to achieve success and receive the recognition you deserve.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot Card is associated with the concept of “going within.” It is a reminder that seeking internal illumination and clarity can be more beneficial than external sources. When it comes to health, The Hermit Tarot Card encourages us to take a step back and self-reflect, rather than seeking an external cure-all. It encourages us to explore our own personal health needs and to look for the underlying causes of any physical or mental ailments, rather than simply masking the symptoms. This could involve seeing a doctor, taking supplements, meditating, or engaging in self-care activities.

When it comes to finances, The Hermit Tarot Card is all about being independent and self-sufficient, and taking the time to consider our financial goals and strategies. It encourages us to take a step back and analyze our investments, spending habits, and resources, and to use our own knowledge and advice to guide us. We should be careful not to rely solely on external sources, and instead look to our own internal guidance.

Finally, The Hermit Tarot Card encourages us to take a step back and reconsider our relationships, and to explore our own personal needs and wants. It encourages us to identify our true values and what makes us happiest, rather than compromising ourselves to please other people. It also encourages us to take time for personal growth, self-care, and solitude, which can help us to become more confident within our relationships.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is associated with victory, recognition, and success and carries positive connotations. Those who draw this card are likely to receive good news, awards and/or recognition in some capacity. This card symbolizes that victory and success are at hand and that an individual will be celebrating the fruits of their hard labor. While the 6 of Wands is certainly a favorable card, it is important to note that it is likely not a permanent state of victories and successes, but rather moments of triumph that are fleeting and must be worked for continually.

When it comes to health, the 6 of Wands can signify a period of improved health. This could mean anything from a full recovery from an illness to the successful implementation of a healthy lifestyle. This card also suggests that any health-related goals are achievable and can be achieved through hard work and dedication.

The 6 of Wands can also be a favorable card for finances. It could indicate that there is money coming in, whether it be through a bonus, a raise, a successful investment, or some other form of financial windfall. It is important to remember, however, that this card is generally associated with a momentary victory and not necessarily with an overall financial success. It is important to use any financial gains wisely, invest in growing one’s wealth, and to be mindful of how one can manage their finances in order to ensure a stable future.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Wands can symbolize success and recognition. This can be anything from a successful, happy relationship to a successful business partnership or collaboration. This card suggests that relationships are positive and that they are successful. It is important to remember, however, that relationships and collaborations require effort and dedication in order to remain successful in the long-term. Any successes and recognition achieved by a relationship should be appreciated and celebrated, and efforts should be made to ensure that the relationship remains healthy and prosperous.
