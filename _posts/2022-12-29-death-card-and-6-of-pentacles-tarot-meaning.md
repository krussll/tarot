A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Death card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Death tarot card and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card together signify the beginning of a new cycle of transformation in your life. The Death card highlights the end of something and the need to move on, while the 6 Of Pentacles encourages giving or receiving help, generosity and compassion in the process. This combination suggests a time of great opportunity and evolution, where you can accept offers of help and make changes without fear. Transformation is a natural part of life, and these two cards together offer further evidence of how endings can lead to something amazing.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

It could also signify the time for a restructuring of your current investments, or for a more conscious and balanced approach to your finances.

Alternatively, it could foreshadow a period of rebuilding, a time when a relationship is renewed or strengthened, with a newfound understanding and appreciation.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of health, finances and relationships. In the classic version of the Tarot card, it depicts a person giving and receiving help in the form of coins, indicating generosity between two people. The 6 of Pentacles is a message to give and receive help in a balanced manner.

When it comes to health, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to take care of our own bodies and seek help from outside sources when needed. This card suggests to listen to our intuition and ask for assistance in finding the best treatment possible. Additionally, the card encourages us to use our resources, whether it be time or money, to heal ourselves with physical, mental and emotional care. By accepting help and seeking assistance, we can make sure we are doing everything we can to improve our health.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of balance and generosity. This card suggests that it is important to establish a budget and stick to it so that you can ensure that all of your needs are met without overspending. Additionally, it is important to be generous and to give back to those who have helped us in the past. When we share our resources, we create a more equal balance between us and those we help.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages give-and-take and understanding. This card suggests that in relationships, it is important to set boundaries and to expect the same from the other person. Additionally, it is important to be generous with our time and resources, and to be open to the possibility of receiving help from those around us. The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card reminds us that a successful relationship is built upon the generous actions of both parties.
