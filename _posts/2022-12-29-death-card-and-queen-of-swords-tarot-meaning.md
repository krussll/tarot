You need to be honest with yourself and with those around you, and accept the transformation of your life. Being self-sufficient and relying on your own strength will help you overcome the obstacles you are facing and make the most of the new opportunities on the horizon.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Death and Queen Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Death and Queen Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health  
The Death Tarot card can signify a time of rejuvenation and transformation when it comes to health. It can mean a time to let go of unhealthy habits and behaviors, such as substance abuse, and to embrace healthier practices and lifestyles. It can also indicate a time to let go of restrictive dieting or unhealthy relationships with food. It can be a turning point for someone to take their health more seriously and make the necessary changes for their lifestyle and well-being, such as incorporating more nutrition, exercise and relaxation into their routine.

Finances  
The Death Tarot card can represent a time of financial change. It can mean a time to let go of bad money habits and practice better financial management. It can also mean it’s time to take a leap of faith and invest in something new and exciting, or to take on a new job or career path. For those who are already financially secure, it can be a call to transform the way they manage their finances, such as investing in a more sustainable or ethical way, or to find a way to use their money to benefit someone in their community.

Relationships  
The Death Tarot card can signify a time for transformation and renewal in relationships. It may mean a time for releasing unhealthy and toxic relationships, allowing each person involved to go their separate ways in peace. It can also indicate a time to let go of codependency and pursue a healthier and more independent path. It can be a sign to take a hard look at the state of a relationship and make the necessary changes for it to be more meaningful and fulfilling. It can represent a time to be honest and open with each other, to create an atmosphere of trust and respect.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card suggests that self-sufficiency and total honesty is important for relationships. Remember that relationships require a healthy balance between giving and taking, and don’t be afraid to stand up for yourself.
