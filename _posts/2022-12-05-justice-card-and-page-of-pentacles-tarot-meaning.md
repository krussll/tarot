A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Page Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and Page Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Page Of Pentacles mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The Page Of Pentacles tarot card taken together signify a situation where a win-win solution is possible, where the truth is paramount, but whereby patience and hard work will pay off. It is a sign that suggests you need to take your time to ensure that the solutions you come up with are fair, but upright. It is a positive sign that the things you set out to do will come to fruition, and you will be rewarded with financial benefits. The Page Of Pentacles tarot card brings with it news of a new job or raise, or other money related news. With Justice tarot card, it reminds you that you need to put in the necessary hard work and effort to achieve these ends, but it will pay off in the end.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Page Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice Tarot card can symbolize the importance of balance and moderation when it comes to health. It is a reminder that healthy choices and contemplative decisions should be made if one wishes to achieve physical and mental well-being. The Justice card encourages one to be mindful of the ripple effects that our health choices can have on others in our lives. Eating healthy, exercising regularly, getting enough sleep and managing stress are key components to physical health.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card signifies fairness and equity when it comes to handling one’s finances. It is a reminder to take responsibility for one’s financial situation and to make wise decisions in order to balance financial obligations. The card encourages one to create a budget, track spending and to seek advice from experts when needed. In addition, it is important to be mindful of how our finances affect those around us and to strive for win-win solutions whenever possible.

Relationships: The Justice Tarot card can be a reminder to practice fairness and honesty in relationships. It symbolizes the need to listen to our loved ones, to honor our commitments, and to take responsibility when things go wrong. It also encourages us to be mindful of the actions we take, as our actions can have ripple effects. The card is a reminder to communicate openly and to make sure that both parties feel heard, respected, and that their needs are being met.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

For Health: The Page of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of perseverance and loyalty in matters of health. When this card pops up in a reading, it could mean that you are being asked to take a step back and consider the long-term effects of your health-related decisions. It implies that you have the patience to look at the bigger picture and make wise choices that won’t have negative consequences down the line. The card may also be suggesting that you take a break to recharge and rejuvenate your body and mind before jumping into any health-related projects.

For Finances: The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is an omen of good news related to finances. It could mean that you are able to take on a new job or receive a raise. You may be presented with newfound opportunities that allow you to reap the rewards of your hard work and dedication. The Page is a reminder that with patience and perseverance, you are able to create and nurture financial stability and abundance.

For Relationships: The Page of Pentacles Tarot card encourages you to look at your relationship with a spirit of innocence and childlike wonder. This card symbolizes new beginnings and suggests that it’s time to put any past grievances behind you and start fresh with your partner. This card also encourages you to be patient and to take your time when it comes to dealing with any relationship issues; don’t be too quick to jump to conclusions, but rather take the time to really figure out what it is that both of you need from each other.
