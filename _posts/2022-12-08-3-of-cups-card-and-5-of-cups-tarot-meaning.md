A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Cups card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Cups card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Cups and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 3 Of Cups tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card together represent a balance of celebration and sorrow. On the one hand, they highlight the importance of friendship and the importance of coming together with others to celebrate life and its joys. On the other hand, they also emphasize the losses and griefs that are part of life, and remind us of the need to turn to friends and loved ones for support and comfort when we are feeling abandoned or unloved. Together, these cards remind us of the importance of being with one another in life and the balance of joys and sorrows that come with it.

### What does 3 Of Cups and 5 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The 3 Of Cups and The 5 Of Cups appear together in a reading, the combination suggests a period of mixed emotions related to relationships and love. On the one hand, you may be in a place of celebration and joy, surrounded by friends and loved ones. This could signify an engagement or wedding, a new romantic partnership, or a deepening of an existing bond. On the other hand, you may feel some sadness and loss. This could be due to a past relationship that didn’t work out, or a painful break-up.

The message of this combination is that it’s possible to feel both joy and sorrow in relationships, and both emotions are valid. As hard as it can be to accept, letting go of the past does not mean you cannot be happy in the present. The 3 Of Cups encourages you to carry on and to nurture your current relationships with joy and appreciation. Focus on the good times and celebrate your new or existing relationships.

The 5 Of Cups also offers an important reminder to be gentle with yourself as you go through this process. Acknowledge your feelings of loss and regret, but don’t let them prevent you from taking part in the festivities. Reach out to your friends and family during this time and let them provide you with the support you need.

Overall, the combination of The 3 Of Cups and The 5 Of Cups in a love reading speaks of a hopeful new beginning. It is a reminder that joy and sorrow can exist side by side, and that while there may be loss, there is also the potential for immense happiness.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is associated with friendships, gatherings, and teamwork. This card can indicate that a person is surrounded by a supportive circle of people and that the individual is feeling valued and fulfilled through meaningful relationships.

For health, The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to take care of oneself and to appreciate the healing power of supportive relationships. It can signify a time of physical, mental, and emotional well-being. Opening up to a friend or loved one can be a powerful way to heal and to find positive energy.

For finances, The 3 of Cups Tarot card represents a time of wealth and abundance. It is a reminder to stay positive and to trust that success and prosperity are within reach with the aid of supportive networks. This card is associated with luck and encourages us to take the opportunity when it arises.

For relationships, The 3 of Cups Tarot card encourages us to open up and share our thoughts, feelings, and experiences with others. This card is a sign of friendship and camaraderie, indicating that it’s okay to lean on your friends and family for encouragement and guidance. It is a reminder of the importance of having a strong social circle that is willing to offer help and support.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

The 5 of Cups Tarot card signifies loss, regret, and grief. It can indicate a feeling of being abandoned or unloved, or of disappointment and sadness. In terms of health, this card could be reflective of a physical or emotional issue, where one has the feeling of being overwhelmed and unable to move forward. It could also be indicative of a health crisis, or of feeling unsupported by one’s medical team. In terms of finances, this card could represent the loss of a job, the inability to make ends meet, or the feeling of being trapped in an unfavourable money situation. In terms of relationships, it could represent feeling disconnected, betrayed or abandoned by another. It could signify the end of a relationship, or indicate difficulty in forming lasting connections.

When this card appears in Tarot readings, it can point to letting go of what has been lost, allowing space for a new start. It can also be indicative of the need to forgive and to find ways to move forward from a painful situation. In terms of health, the 5 of Cups could be a reminder to seek healing and self-care, as well as to express feelings of sadness in a healthy way. Financially, this card may indicate the need to create plans for stability and to build our resources. In a relationship context, it could suggest the need to take steps towards rebuilding trust and to find inner healing. Ultimately, this card can be reflective of a difficult time, but also offers hope in terms of navigating the situation and finding a way forward.
