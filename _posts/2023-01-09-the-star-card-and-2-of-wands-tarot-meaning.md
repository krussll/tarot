## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card often symbolizes hope and a newfound sense of clarity. When the Star card appears in a Tarot reading, the advice is to trust in the process of life, to have faith in yourself and the Universe, and to look forward to upcoming miracles. In terms of health, this card serves as a reminder to take proper care of oneself, to trust the process of healing, and to embrace any opportunities for healthy growth and transformation. Financially, the Star card suggests that luck and abundance may be on its way. If a person is open to the possibilities, they may find themselves in a position to receive a financial windfall or a much-needed boost of financial stability. As for relationships, the Star card suggests a time of renewal and inner clarity. This could be a time for a person to reconnect with loved ones, set boundaries, and come to an understanding about the dynamics of their relationships. This card encourages one to have faith in the process, and to guide their relationships in the direction of peace, harmony and love.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

The 2 of Wands Tarot card indicates that in terms of health, it is time for one to take stock of their current situation and to make a decision. It could be that one is considering a change in diet or lifestyle, but is not sure whether they should take the plunge. This card suggests that one should take the time to think carefully before they make any decisions. It also suggests that one should take a step back and observe the results of their current choices, in order to decide whether any changes need to be made.

Finances:  
The 2 of Wands Tarot card signals a period of waiting. It encourages one to be patient, as certain situations related to their finances may take longer to play out. The card also indicates that it is time for one to make a choice regarding their finances. They may have to consider whether to take a risk or whether to be conservative. This card suggests that one should take time to weigh all their options before making any decisions.

Relationships:  
The 2 of Wands Tarot card suggests that now is the time to make a decision regarding a relationship. It could be that one is weighing up whether to take a relationship to the next level, or whether to end a relationship entirely. This card indicates that it is important to consider the potential outcomes of each option before making any decisions. It encourages one to consider the impact the choice could have on not only the other person, but also on themselves. It could also signal the need for travel plans, as this could provide clarity with regards to the relationship.
