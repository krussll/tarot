## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is associated with health and wellness, as it symbolizes friendship, celebration, and a supportive network of people. It suggests that having friends and supportive people around us helps us maintain our health more easily, as we have someone to talk to, share our problems with, and laugh with. Having a good support system is essential for our mental and physical health and also helps us cope with difficult times. Furthermore, having a strong community of peers keeps us accountable when it comes to staying healthy and taking care of ourselves.

The 3 of Cups Tarot card suggests financial comfort and security, as it symbolizes a circle of happiness and joy. It suggests that having a strong network of people to turn to in times of financial trouble or when a financial decision needs to be made, is beneficial. The presence of advice, resources, and understanding from friends and family can help turn any potential financial hardship into a success. Additionally, having someone to celebrate our successes with can help us to feel more secure about our financial endeavors.

Relationships:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes relationships and friendships in a positive light. It suggests that forming relationships with those who bring us joy and understanding is beneficial for us emotionally and psychologically. Having friends and family members to talk to, share our passions with, and just spend time with can help us build stronger relationships and experience positive and meaningful interactions. Furthermore, celebrating each other’s successes, as well as offering support and guidance during difficult moments, is a sure way to strengthen our relationships and ensure they stay healthy and happy.
