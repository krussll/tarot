## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

This strength comes from a loving partnership, either between two people, or between oneself and a higher power. When two people come together with a commitment to improving each other’s well-being and supporting one another through challenging times, it can be a powerful source of healing. When we are naturally vulnerable, loving support gives us the strength to face our own weaknesses and to improve our health and wellbeing.

The Lovers Tarot card also represents the power of two people working together to achieve financial success. The unity and connection between two individuals can allow them to reach higher levels of financial security than either could have achieved alone. Through a combination of teamwork, hard work, and communication, two people can make wise decisions, develop effective strategies and achieve their goals.

Finally, The Lovers Tarot card represents the power of true love in relationships. When two people come together with a deep understanding of each other, a strong connection and an unwavering commitment, their relationship can be a tremendous source of strength. When each partner is deeply committed to the other, they are able to support one another in times of need, give each other unconditional love, and strive for mutual growth. Through their unity and dedication, two people can experience a unique level of joy and fulfillment.

## What does Death mean?

The Death Tarot card is a card of transformation, and as such can have implications for health, finances and relationships. When it appears, it can indicate a need for changes in all or some of these areas.

In terms of health, The Death card suggests that some kind of transformation is needed. This could be anything from lifestyle changes such as diet and exercise, to a complete overhaul of your health care regime. It could even signal the need to seek out alternative forms of healing such as therapeutic massage or acupuncture. Whatever the case may be, The Death card indicates that a change needs to be made if you are to take back control of your health.

Financially, The Death card can be a difficult message. It can indicate either a need to let go of something that is draining you financially, or it could mean taking a chance on an investment that could yield huge rewards. Either way, it suggests a need to reassess your finances and make changes where necessary.

In terms of relationships, The Death card often signals the end of an existing relationship or the need to let go of something that is holding you back. It can also mean taking a chance on a new relationship, or investing in an existing one. Whatever the case, it’s a sign that some kind of transformation is necessary in order to move forward.

The Death card can be an unwelcome message, but it can also be a powerful one. By listening to its message and taking the necessary steps to make the changes it suggests, you can create a new and improved version of yourself.
