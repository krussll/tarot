## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card is associated with renewal, hope, inner clarity and miracles, reminding us to have faith, even when the horizon may look dark. In terms of one’s health, the Star Tarot card encourages one to see it as an opportunity to make positive changes, to commit to healthy behaviors, and to use the power of faith to heal. It reminds us to take care of our bodies, as well as our minds and spirits, and to seek appropriate medical care when needed.

When it comes to finances, the Star Tarot card encourages patience, and reminds us that, with enough effort and perseverance, our dreams of financial security can become reality. It also reminds us to be mindful of our spending choices, and to remember that financial success comes from both short and long-term plans.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Star Tarot card reminds us to appreciate and value the relationships we have, and to communicate openly and honestly with one another. It also encourages us to have faith in our relationships, to be patient, and to remember that even difficult conversations can lead to greater understanding and trust. It can also be a reminder to remember the importance of having hobbies and interests that bring us joy.

## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The King of Pentacles Tarot card often symbolizes the kind of health that comes with a balanced diet and a strong work ethic. Physical activity and healthy nutrition are important, and the card encourages a practical approach to being physically fit. The card also suggests that taking care of one’s body and mind is important, as is being mindful of stress and avoiding burnout.

Finances: The King of Pentacles Tarot card is a card that encourages wise money decisions. It suggests a sober, businesslike approach to financial management, being mindful of risks and being prepared for both success and failure. It also encourages focusing on long-term goals and making investments that will weather any storm, as well as seeking advice from wise financial advisors.

Relationships: The King of Pentacles Tarot card is a card that symbolizes a secure and reliable relationship. It encourages taking a practical approach to love and partnership, focusing on shared goals and building something lasting and secure. It emphasizes a secure base that can weather any storm, mutual respect and stability, being attentive to the needs of your partner, and having a level-headed approach to tough problems. Ultimately, it is a card that encourages a lasting and reliable relationship.
