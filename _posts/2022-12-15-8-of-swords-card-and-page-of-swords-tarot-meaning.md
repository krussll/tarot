A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Swords card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Swords card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean together?

The 8 Of Swords tarot card and the Page Of Swords tarot card together might represent a feeling of isolation and restrictions imposed on you by yourself. You might feel like you don’t want to take risks or make any changes because you don’t want to be gossiped about or judged. This combination of cards could also mean that you are feeling judged by those around you and are living in fear of the truth coming out if you make any sudden moves. You could have a fear of being exposed and feel as though you are being watched and monitored in some way. These cards are cautioning you to be mindful of your actions and think twice before you act.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 8 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 8 of Swords and the Page of Swords in your love life suggests that you may find yourself in a situation of self-imposed isolation, either literally or figuratively. Perhaps you are so afraid of getting hurt that you’re restricting yourself from fully engaging in the relationship, or you find yourself increasingly feeling trapped in your own thoughts and doubts. You fear that your partner may be hiding something from you, or that you’re not getting the full picture.

This combination could also suggest that you’re feeling the pressure of being judged or gossiped about by others. You may feel that you are constantly being watched and critiqued and it can be difficult to be your authentic self without feeling that your actions are being judged or misinterpreted.

Ultimately, this combination suggests that you need to be honest with both yourself and your partner. You need to examine the boundaries you’ve set on yourself or your relationship and ask yourself if they’re really necessary. Open communication and trusting the truth of your partner are important for growth and understanding. Being brave enough to take a risk and push past the boundaries and limitations you’ve put in place is key.

### What does 8 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean together for your finances?

The 8 of Swords is a card that speaks to the way that we can get in our own way when it comes to our financial and career paths. It cautions us against feeling trapped by our own decisions and feelings of self-imposed restriction. We can become bound by our resistance to leave a limiting situation or take a risk that could open up new opportunities.

The Page of Swords can offer some guidance here, encouraging us to be as honest and direct as possible when discussing our finances and career. This card can also point to feeling watched by others and the need to be mindful how we present ourselves. Though originally it may feel uncomfortable, being truthful and forthcoming regarding our finances can open us up to new opportunities.

Collectively the meaning of these two cards is that if we take off the blinders of self-restriction and fear, we can truly take a deep dive into our careers and finances. We can be honest about our needs and open to new possibilities, as long as we are mindful of our words and presentation. By taking a step outside of our comfort zone and letting go of our self-imposed restrictions, we can uncover a world of potential and fresh opportunities that we were unable to see before.

### What does 8 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean together for your health?

The 8 of Swords tarot card combined with the Page of Swords tarot card can be an indication for an individual to look to the dynamic between self-imposed restrictions and curious energy. On one hand, we have the 8 of Swords card speaking to the idea of imprisonment and feeling hemmed in by external and internal forces. On the other hand we have the Page of Swords which may be understood as pointing to the need for increased awareness and the active seeking of truth.

In the context of health, the 8 of Swords card can symbolize emotional or psychological stagnation, or an individual feeling trapped in their circumstances or a situation that is no longer serving them. In contrast, the Page of Swords can be interpreted as a call to step outside of this stagnation and take an active, investigative approach to uncovering new information.

From this, we can understand that when the 8 of Swords and Page of Swords cards appear together, it points to the need to pay attention to limiting thought patterns and behaviors that may be leading to a sense of entrapment. This can include mastering better ways of thinking, such as positive affirmations, improved stress management techniques, and eliminating negative self-talk. Additionally, it can be beneficial to take an active role in learning more about one’s health, such as by reading up on the latest research and talking to other health professionals.

By combining the 8 of Swords and the Page of Swords cards, we can recognize the importance of balance. Being aware of the potential for both self-imposed restrictions and curiosity, and by taking the time to recognize their impact, individuals can work to create a healthier, more balanced life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 8 Of Swords and Page Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

You are likely feeling trapped, blocked, and restricted in terms of what you can do with regards to your health and wellbeing. You may be feeling as if you are stuck in a loop, unable to make progress or find a way out of the cycle of poor health. It could be that you have been neglecting your physical and mental wellbeing or that you have been too restrictive in the decisions you make about your health. This card is encouraging you to take a step back and re-evaluate your current situation in order to determine how you can make positive changes.

Finances: The 8 of Swords Tarot card is a warning about your finances. You may be feeling like you are being held back financially or that you are unable to make any financial progress. You may also be feeling very restricted in terms of how much money you can make or save. This card is urging you to take a step back and reassess your finances and to look for ways that you can alleviate your financial burdens. It could be that you are too restrictive with your finances and need to be more open-minded when it comes to managing your money.

Relationships: The 8 of Swords Tarot card is encouraging you to reassess your relationships and to look for ways that you can break free from any self-imposed restrictions. You may be feeling trapped in an unhealthy or unfulfilling relationship and unable to make any progress or find a way out. This card is suggesting that it may be time to take a step back and re-evaluate the situation in order to determine if the relationship is worth your time and effort. It could also be a warning that you are too restrictive in the expectations you have for your relationships and need to be more open-minded about allowing others to express themselves.

Those with the Page of Swords in their Tarot spread should take steps to look after their health and well-being to prevent any serious issues from arising. The Page of Swords can also be an indication of the importance of being open and honest about one’s health concerns, as this can often hinder their healing process.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the Page of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of a great deal of insecurity. It can suggest that a person is constantly worrying about the state of their finances, and the need to make sure that their money is secure. It can also be a sign of financial struggles, as the Page of Swords reminds us that we need to be aware of our current financial situation and take steps to ensure that we are making wise financial decisions.

Relationships: The Page of Swords Tarot card can be a reminder that relationships can often be tricky to navigate. It encourages us to be honest and direct with our feelings, to not be afraid to speak up when we feel something is wrong, and to stay curious about our partner. It can suggest that relationships can often become tense or strained if one does not communicate their feelings in an open, honest way. The Page of Swords Tarot card can encourage us to take a step back and examine the situation from all angles, to ensure that we are being heard and taken seriously.
