This could be in any aspect of life where you need help from a team or a leader to reach your goals. Working together with a strong leader and team will help you achieve success and expand your goals. While working together, your leader will have an understanding and tolerant attitude, using empathy to help manage and foster a successful team. This is an opportunity to trust the process and let the team help you reach success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Wands and King Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

The King of Cups Tarot card is a card of balance, health and healing. It indicates that one is being managed by a benevolent figure and being guided in the right direction. This card symbolizes a feeling of being nurtured and cared for. It suggests that one should take time to pause and assess the situation before making any decisions. The King of Cups Tarot card also represents a person who has a balanced approach to finances. This card indicates a calm and steady management of money and resources. This card advises one to be careful with financial decisions and to be open to different kinds of financial management, such as investments or fiscal responsibility. The king of cups admonishes us to be cautious and to make sure our finances are managed responsibly.

Relationships:  
The King of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of empathy and patience. The King of Cups is often associated with a mature and understanding figure, who is willing to listen and be sympathetic. This card suggests that one should use this same approach when dealing with their relationships. It encourages us to take the time to understand and appreciate our relationships, taking a balanced approach to our interactions with others. This card is also a reminder to practice tolerance and forgiveness when it comes to our relationships.
