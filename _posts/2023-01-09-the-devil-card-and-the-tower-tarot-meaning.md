A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and The Tower card together in particular.

The The Devil card and The Tower have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and The Tower mean together?

The Devil tarot card and The Tower tarot card together symbolize a period of upheaval, where uncontrollable desires and obsessiveness lead to rapid, unwanted change. This could be due to the seductive power of addiction and greed, which lead to envy and materialism, or through unhealthy and obsessive relationships and sexual lust. In the end, what was once stable may be shattered and ruin may ensue. To avoid such a situation, one must be mindful of their own desires and take responsibility for their actions.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Devil and The Tower mean together for your love life?

The Devil tarot card and The Tower tarot card together in a love life reading represent an unhealthy or unbalanced relationship. This could be because of an addiction or obsession with a person or the material aspects of relationships. It could also be because of envy, greed, or lust for someone or something—this could be any material item, money, status, or even sex. When these two cards appear together in a love life reading there can be feelings of being trapped in a situation that you are unable to escape—or feeling enslaved by something.

It could be a partner that is hell-bent on controlling the relationship in a way that you are feeling uncomfortable with or have no control over. It could mean that you are stuck in a relationship because of power, money, or something else that is preventing you from leaving. It could also be that you are in a mentally unhealthy relationship that is leading to destructive habits, such as substance abuse, self-harming, or other dangerous activities.

The Tower tarot card warns of the destruction that might occur if the relationship is not carefully managed. The crashing of the tower symbolizes the crumbling of a connection and the devastating effects of its destruction. As The Tower card is a card of sudden destruction, it is important to take care of a relationship before it reaches this point.

Ultimately, The Devil tarot card and The Tower tarot card together in a love life reading represent a toxic relationship and the destruction of something special. The message is to be aware of the signs of an unhealthy connection and to realize when it’s time to let go. Change can be scary, but it is often necessary for growth.

### What does The Devil and The Tower mean together for your finances?

When these two tarot cards, The Devil and The Tower, are taken together when referring to finances and job, they can be interpreted as a warning that reckless behavior can lead to financial ruin. The Devil tarot card suggests a focus on materialism, greed, and obsession with money and possessions, focusing on the need to satisfy a craving for instant gratification and the accumulation of wealth to prop up the ego. The Tower tarot card is a warning of the consequences of materialism and a sign of unwanted change and total destruction of the plans one has made.

When The Devil and The Tower are taken together in regards to finances and job, it could be an omen that continued reliance on material possessions to prop up the ego, or pursuing a job solely for the money, could lead to an unexpected and unwelcome disruption in the lifestyle one has worked so hard to build. It is a warning that spending beyond one’s means, indulging in gambling, or becoming obsessed with a risky career path or even a single venture could lead to a financial downfall.

These two tarot cards together can be a warning of what could result from ignoring the consequences of living beyond one’s means. It is wise to take this advice seriously and to avoid making decisions solely based on money, wealth, and status. Instead, it is important to pursue opportunities for growth and to build a financial portfolio that promotes stability and longevity, not one that focuses on the desires of the moment. Without doing so, there is a risk of financial ruin and upheaval that can lead to a tumultuous future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card is a reminder to take a close look at the things in our lives that may be controlling us from within. When this card appears, it suggests that we are in the throes of addiction and excessive materialism, or have become obsessed with something or someone. We are being driven by our base impulses, and we are allowing material goods to become a source of security.

When it comes to health, The Devil Tarot card reminds us to pay attention to whether or not our impulsive behaviors are leading us away from making healthy choices. It is important to take stock of how any addiction or obsessive behavior might be affecting our physical and emotional wellbeing. We should look at where we might be overindulging, how our addictions are affecting our mental health, and how our actions may be creating physical detriment.

In regards to finances, the presence of The Devil Tarot card reminds us to take steps to ensure that we are managing our money responsibly and not allowing ourselves to be taken in by the siren song of materialism. We should ask ourselves if our materialistic impulses are keeping us from being financially secure, and consider what steps we can take to ensure that our finances are in order.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Devil Tarot card warns us that we may be allowing our obsessions or unchecked desires to interfere with the relationships we have with others. We should take time to assess our behavior and see if our needs are causing us to exclude others or be overly demanding in our relationships. It is important to recognize how our materialistic or obsessive desires might be keeping us from having healthy, meaningful relationships with others.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card is associated with unwelcome change and turmoil in life, and can have a significant impact on three major areas of a person’s life: health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Tower Tarot card can represent sudden illnesses, unexpected changes in health, or issues that arise suddenly and cause disruption in a person’s body. It can refer to poor nutrition, unhealthy lifestyles and taking risks that lead to bad outcomes. The Tower Tarot card suggests that a person should take a more mindful approach to their health and be more aware of the consequences of their decisions.

In terms of finances, The Tower Tarot card can represent sudden financial losses, bankruptcy, or having to face difficult money problems in a short period of time. Unexpected and unwelcome changes to the financial landscape can occur, and it isn’t always possible to be prepared for them. This card suggests that one must be mindful of their financial decisions and try to plan ahead as much as possible to help protect their assets.

Finally, The Tower Tarot card can signify unwanted changes to relationships. It can refer to breakups and divorces, conflicts and disagreements, or end of friendships and business partnerships. The Tower Tarot card indicates that a person should be willing to compromise and put in the effort to maintain relationships when difficulties arise, as well as understand when it’s time to let go and move on.

Overall, The Tower Tarot card can be a difficult card to interpret, as it can represent significant disruption to one’s health, finances and relationships. However, it also suggests that it is possible to turn such negative experiences into positive ones, if one is willing to make the effort to understand the underlying messages and make the necessary changes.
