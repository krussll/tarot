A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Moon card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Moon card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Moon and 5 Of Cups mean together?

When both The Moon tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card are present in a reading, it is a message to look inward and trust your intuition. There may be issues and potential losses that you remain unaware of, however, these can be successfully navigated if you stay true to your instincts. It’s possible that you may be feeling a deep sense of regret and grief, as if you’ve been abandoned or unloved. These are natural emotions to experience and this reading is asking you to be kind to yourself and trust that, despite these challenges, changes are in store for the better, and will come with a newfound sense of belonging and contentment.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 5 of Cups tarot card speaks of feelings of loss, grief, abandonment and being unloved. This suggests that there is potentially a period of hard times in a relationship, and that one or both partners may be struggling with their emotions. It’s possible that there is a sense of regret in the relationship, perhaps related to a past misunderstanding or disagreement. It’s possible that this feeling of loss could be attributed to the voluntary changes that have been made in the relationship, with both partners still needing to adjust and adapt to the new direction.

Overall, the combination of The Moon and The 5 of Cups suggest that it’s time to assess what is happening in the relationship and make the necessary changes to move forwards. Both parties should think carefully about their decisions, and trust their own intuition above all else. It’s important to remember that this period of difficulty will pass, and that it’s possible for a relationship to come out the other side even stronger than before. Relationships can be hard work at times, but if both parties are willing to put in the effort, it’s possible to come out on the other side even better than before.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

The 5 of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of loss and regret, grief and feeling abandoned or unloved. This card can be interpreted in a variety of ways, depending on the context in which the card is drawn. When it comes to interpreting this card’s meaning for health, finances, and relationships, this card can be seen as a warning about the potential for loss and disappointment in these areas.

When it comes to health, the 5 of Cups can indicate a period of disappointment and poor health. This can be brought on by an illness, injury, or even mental health issues. It is important to take any health issues seriously, as they can have lasting effects on both your physical and mental wellbeing. It is also important to remember that despite the setbacks, there will likely be a way to recover and heal.

For finances, this card can signify a loss of money or resources, which can lead to feelings of disappointment. This loss can be difficult to overcome, and it is important to look for ways to rebound in a healthy and sustainable way. This may involve creating a budget, cutting back on non-essential spending, and looking for other ways to make up for the lost money.

Finally, the 5 of Cups can indicate a period of relationship disruption and difficulties. This can be a time of feeling abandoned, unloved, or betrayed. It is important to remember that even during these tough times, with understanding and communication, positive changes can be made. It is also important to remember that relationships take work, and that even though there may be hard times, the rewards that come from a strong and healthy connection are worth it.
