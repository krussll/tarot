## What does 8 Of Wands and 8 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 8 Of Wands and The 8 Of Pentacles tarot card combined reveal a message of quick, but steady change. The 8 Of Wands encourages us to take smart, decisive action, while The 8 Of Pentacles reminds us to take the time to enjoy the journey of mastering our craft. Together, these two cards indicate that you can expect progress, but you must be patient and meticulous in your approach. There is a sense of newfound energy that comes with the combination of these two cards; news could arrive that could bring about a new adventure, but it is important to ensure that every step is taken with thoughtfulness.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 8 of Wands Tarot card means that your health is likely to improve quickly. It may be a sign of a swift recovery from an illness, an improvement in your fitness levels or simply a boost to your overall wellbeing. In any case, the 8 of Wands indicates that the path to improved health will be a fast one.

Finances  
The 8 of Wands Tarot card suggests that financial fortune is likely to come your way with speed. It may be an unexpected windfall, an unexpected raise or bonus at work, or even a significant inheritance. Whatever it is that brings sudden financial success, the 8 of Wands suggests that it will come quickly and be a welcome surprise.

Relationships  
The 8 of Wands Tarot card indicates that a relationship that is currently faltering or has become stagnant and stale will soon get a jolt of energy and excitement. The 8 of Wands suggests that this change might come from an outside source such as a new job, the ending of a long-term relationship or the introduction of a new romantic interest. Either way, the 8 of Wands signifies that the change will be fast and may potentially be life-altering.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card can indicate that you are in the process of mastering a craft or discipline related to your health. This could be anything from a holistic healing practice, to getting a certain medical certification. Whatever it is, the card tells you to focus your attention on mastering it so that you can experience the greatest benefit for your health. The 8 of Pentacles also suggests that by mastering a craft, you may be able to receive financial gain from it.

Finance:

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card can mean that you are on your way to mastering a particular job or project that will create a financial income for you. This could be the result of a side hustle or a career move, either way the card is encouraging you to focus on the task at hand and to make sure that you are investing the appropriate amount of time and effort into mastering your craft. This mastery will ultimately be rewarded with financial gain.

Relationships:

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card can also represent mastering a relationship, such as a marriage or partnership. It can indicate that you are spending considerable time and effort into understanding what your partner needs and desires, and that you are committed to giving your best efforts to make sure that you are both successful. Furthermore, mastering this relationship will not only be rewarding in the sense of personal fulfilment, but it can also bring about financial security and stability for both parties.
