## What does Page Of Swords and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The Page Of Swords and The 6 Of Cups tarot card together signify curiosity, nostalgia and gifts. This combination of cards suggest that a situation or person may be curious about something but there is a nostalgia or innocence surrounding it. It could be a situation where someone has been gifted some information that is valuable, but because of the Page Of Swords’ cunning and prying eyes, the gift of information must be handled very carefully, especially when it comes to gossip. Those involved should take care to preserve the nostalgia and innocence associated with the situation, and embrace the truth that the Page Of Swords tarot card represents.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card often suggests an overall sense of restlessness or unease in regards to physical and mental health. When it appears in a reading, it may be indicative of an imbalance of energy which can manifest as anxiety or insomnia. It might also indicate that a person needs to pay more attention to their health, or it may suggest that the person should be more mindful of the things they put into their body, such as food, drugs, and alcohol.

Finances:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card often suggests that a person needs to pay more attention to their money and finances. It can denote a person who is not paying enough attention to their financial health, or it may also suggest a person dwelling too much on the money they have and not seeing the bigger picture. A need for financial planning and budgeting may also be indicated.

Relationships:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card indicates a person who is often too nosy and overly curious about the relationships and lives of others, particularly those close to them. It can denote a person who is not trusting enough of the people in their life, or it may point to someone who is too eager to offer unsolicited advice to those they care about. It can suggest a lack of communication and trust, and it may indicate that someone is being too prying or gossipy.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 6 of Cups tarot card speaks to the importance of getting in touch with our innermost feelings and desires, both good and bad. There is a strong connection between our physical and emotional health, and this card encourages us to acknowledge and process our emotions in order to move past any physical health issues. The 6 of Cups also encourages us to find joy in the little things, to nourish our souls without relying on materialistic possessions. Appreciating the beauty in the world around us and taking time to relax can help to keep us healthy and well.

Finances

The 6 of Cups tarot card suggests that our financial well-being is deeply influenced by our emotional and psychological states. We need to be aware of how our emotional state can affect our ability to make sound financial decisions. This card encourages us to assess how our emotions influence our spending habits, and to make sure that we are spending in line with our values and goals. It also serves as a reminder to practice gratitude for what we have, rather than focusing on the money we don’t have.

Relationships

The 6 of Cups tarot card speaks of nostalgia and reunions, encouraging us to take the time to connect with those we care about. This card encourages us to open up emotionally and to be more vulnerable with our loved ones. When we are honest and open about our feelings, it is much easier to have truly meaningful relationships. This card also reminds us to appreciate the time we have with our loved ones, as nothing lasts forever. We should cherish the moments we have with our family and friends and not take them for granted.
