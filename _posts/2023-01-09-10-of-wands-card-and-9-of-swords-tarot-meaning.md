A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 10 Of Wands tarot card and the 9 Of Swords tarot card together represent a difficult moment in life where one may be feeling extreme stress and exhaustion due to carrying too many burdens. This can often be exacerbated by feelings of intense anxiety, grief and depression caused by nightmares. In this situation, it is important to take a step back, assess the situation and release some of the burdens that are weighing one down. It is also important to reach out for help and support from those around you, as it can be difficult to handle this pressure alone.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 10 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The 10 Of Wands and The 9 Of Swords appear together in a tarot reading for your love life, it can be a warning about the amount of stress and burdens you may be taking on in your relationship. This combination often symbolizes that you may be feeling weighed down by the amount of responsibilities and obligations in your relationship and this is causing your mental health to suffer. You may be feeling drained and exhausted from trying to keep everything together and give your best to your partner, or feeling anxious and sad because of the amount of work in front of you.

The combination of these two cards encourages you to take a step back, take a break and take some time for yourself to rest, relax and recharge. Consider how you can reduce the amount of stress you feel in your relationship and make sure you’re taking care of your mental health. Making sure that you take the time to do something for yourself and take a break from your responsibilities is essential in order to have a healthy and balanced relationship.

Remember that it is perfectly normal to feel overwhelmed and exhausted from time to time, and it is important to be mindful of your emotional and mental wellbeing. The 10 Of Wands and The 9 Of Swords showing up together in a tarot reading for your love life should be a reminder for you to take some time for yourself, to take a break and to prioritize your mental health in order to have a healthy, balanced and fulfilling relationship.

The 9 of Swords can indicate a sense of dread and anxiety associated with these financial and professional commitments, as well as a feeling of not being able to cope with the intensity and demands of the situation.

The combination of these Tarot cards suggests that although the amount of responsibility and burden one is carrying is overwhelming, it is important to try to focus on the present moment and stay in the present to manage the stress. The 9 of Swords may indicate fear and a feeling of being overwhelmed, but the 10 of Wands can also suggest that this pressure could lead to great success in the future if met with perseverance and resilience.

Investing in relaxation practices can be beneficial during this period of excessive financial and job-related stress. Practicing mindfulness, prioritizing important tasks, and taking regular breaks in order to rest and relax can help to ease any worries and anxieties associated with the situation. Self-care is important and can help to reduce the feeling of being overwhelmed.

By combining The 10 Of Wands Tarot card and The 9 Of Swords Tarot card, it is important to remember that although the financial and professional burden is heavy, it is also temporary and can be overcome with resilience and perseverance. Even though the cards indicate a difficult time, they can also point to future successes in the professional and financial sphere.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of overburden, fatigue, and stress. It is often seen as a warning to the reader to be careful of taking on too much, physically, mentally, and emotionally. When this card appears, you may need to reduce the load you are carrying and take a break from whatever it is burdening you.

For health, the 10 of Wands Tarot card suggests that taking on too much is weighing you down, both emotionally and physically. Your body is telling you that it can’t handle this much pressure any longer. It is time to prioritize your health and make sure you are getting enough rest and relaxation. If the 10 of Wands Tarot card appears in a reading, you should take this as a sign to slow down and focus on your physical and mental well-being.

For finances, this card often shows up when there are too many financial obligations that are weighing you down. It is a sign that you may be overextending yourself and it is time to take stock of where you are financially and make sure you are budgeting and managing your money responsibly. The 10 of Wands Tarot card is also a reminder to keep your financial goals in mind and to be careful of impulse spending.

For relationships, the 10 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes too much pressure or expectations being placed on a partnership. It suggests that you may be feeling too much responsibility for the relationship, such as trying to please the other person or taking care of the entire relationship. This card is a reminder to make sure you are both giving and receiving equally in a relationship and to make sure your needs are being met as well.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 9 Of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that our mental and physical health go hand in hand. It is a signal to take time to look after our mental health and recognize signs of distress that could lead to mental health concerns such as depression, anxiety and nightmares. It is time to take a step back and reconsider our lifestyle to ensure we are taking care of ourselves and our mental health is not being neglected.

Finances: On the financial front, the 9 Of Swords Tarot card is an indication to take a breather and assess our financial state. It can be a reminder to reduce our spending and take the time to create a budget that will help us see where our money is going. It is also an important warning to be careful of financial decisions that could have long-term consequences.

Relationships: The 9 Of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take care of our relationships by being honest with each other and taking responsibility for our actions. It is a sign to not let anxieties and worries stand in the way of our relationships and to make sure we are communicating effectively. It is also an indication to create a safe and supportive environment by showing empathy and understanding.
