A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and Temperance card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and Temperance have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and Temperance mean together?

The Hanged Man and Temperance tarot cards represent a powerful balance of wisdom, self-sacrifice, and patience. When combined, they encourage you to step back, reassess, and create a harmonious state of mind. The Temperance card encourages you to practice restraint and self-control, while The Hanged Man card suggests that perhaps you need to surrender and sacrifice something in order to gain a different perspective. Together, these cards represent the power of pausing and embracing the wisdom of patience, balance, and harmony. Only then can you find the answers you seek.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Hanged Man and Temperance mean together for your love life?

The Hanged Man and Temperance tarot cards when pulled together in a love reading speak to a deeper and more meaningful love life. It indicates that achieving a balance between self-sacrifice and self-control is essential to keeping the flame alive and growing. By understanding and accepting the need to sacrifice certain parts of your own desires, you can open yourself up to the possibility of a much deeper, more meaningful connection with someone.

Sacrificing for the good of the relationship does not mean giving up yourself, but rather allowing yourself to become vulnerable to another person and recognizing that this is sometimes necessary in order to fully open up and appreciate all the beauty and love that can be found in a relationship. Temperance is a card that paints a picture of the need for balance in all aspects of life, including love, particularly when it comes to making sure that both parties involved have their individual needs met.

In this respect, The Hanged Man and Temperance tarot cards suggest that giving up something of yourself to create a bond with another person requires harmony and patience in order to cultivate a healthy and fulfilling relationship. This could involve understanding that compromise is required in order to ensure both parties can benefit from the relationship. It speaks to the idea that, in order to enjoy love, we must let go of our preconceived ideas and notions of what we expect from love, and instead be open to developing the right connection with someone that works for both of you.

In conclusion, the combination of The Hanged Man and Temperance tarot cards emphasizes the importance of balance when it comes to your love life. It speaks to the idea that in order to fully appreciate and savor the beauty of a relationship, we must be willing to accept a certain measure of self-sacrifice and self-control, as well as being open to understanding and respecting the needs of our partners. Ultimately, a relationship will be far more meaningful when both parties are able to create a harmonious and balanced connection.

### What does The Hanged Man and Temperance mean together for your finances?

The Hanged Man and The Temperance tarot cards, when examined together, offer guidance on how to achieve and maintain fiscal balance, both in day-to-day life and in long-term plans. The Hanged Man reminds us not to be so attached to current expectations, but to be open to the possibilities of new and different options in order to make the best choices. This may involve a certain degree of self-sacrifice in order to arrive at a wiser, more beneficial outcome.

The Temperance card also asks us to be more mindful on how we live day-to-day. It encourages us to practice patience, self-control, and balance in our financial decisions. This means that we should strive not to live beyond our means and to avoid frivolous spending and debt. Instead, it suggests that we create a budget that allows us to balance our needs and wants, while still having some emergency or luxury funds.

The combination of The Hanged Man and The Temperance tarot cards might be especially helpful when considering your career. The Hanged Man encourages you to think outside of the box and explore new opportunities. It means that you don’t have to settle for simply what is expected, or what you think is expected of you. Instead, you can explore new ideas, take risks, and make the best decisions for your own success. The Temperance tarot card, on the other hand, advises that you make wise career decisions. This might mean being more mindful of how you advance in your current job or how you pursue new opportunities. It encourages you to practice patience and not rush into any decisions.

Both The Hanged Man and The Temperance tarot cards are beneficial when making decisions on your finances and career. They offer guidance on how to be wise with your money and how to make the best decisions for your future.

The union of The Hanged Man and The Temperance cards together suggest that a healthy lifestyle is best reached through a combination of self-sacrifice, patience, and getting a new perspective on our health. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and Temperance mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health

The Hanged Man Tarot card means to put your own health first and foremost. This doesn’t necessarily mean to cut yourself off from the rest of the world and become a hermit. It means to be aware and attuned to the needs of your own body. This could be a sacrifice of time and energy in order to look after yourself by getting enough sleep, exercising, and eating healthily. It also means taking a step back in order to gain a different perspective on the stressors in your life and what you can do to manage them. This could mean saying no to social engagements and activities that no longer serve your higher purpose or taking more time to connect with yourself.

Finances

The Hanged Man Tarot card could mean taking a step back and assessing your finances before making any drastic changes. This could be a time to look at your current situation, reflect on where it is heading and whether it needs to be altered. It might mean making a sacrifice of time and money in order to reassess your finances and take control of them. It also encourages taking a different perspective, such as looking for ways to reduce spending or actively seeking new ways to earn money.

Relationships

The Hanged Man Tarot card speaks to the idea of self-sacrifice and taking a different perspective when it comes to relationships. It could mean taking a step back in order to understand the dynamics of your relationships, particularly if they are strained or not functioning as they should. This could require a sacrifice of time, energy and resources in order to help restore balance and understanding. It could also mean looking at each relationship from a different angle, such as the other person’s perspective or setting boundaries in order to protect yourself. Above all, this card encourages introspection and reflection so that you can understand your own needs in each and every relationship.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health  
The Temperance Tarot card holds many implications for health and wellbeing. It symbolizes moderation, balance and harmony, and is associated with the element of water. As such, it encourages those who seek its counsel to maintain balance in their physical and spiritual health, taking care of both the body and soul. By engaging in temperate eating habits and physical activities, and nurturing the mind and spirit through meditation and social interactions, we can bring our physical and mental faculties into balance. As a result, we can achieve improved physical health, as well as better mental acuity, greater personal satisfaction, and more meaningful relationships.

Finances  
The Temperance Tarot card also has implications for personal finances. It suggests that purchasing decisions should be made with caution and restraint. Spending should be balanced, prioritizing essential needs over wants, with any excess income saved for future use. Furthermore, when dealing with investments or business opportunities, one should take care to weigh their options carefully, and to ensure that their choices are in line with their short and long term goals. The card also serves as a reminder to stay informed and informed of the market’s trends and fluctuations, to facilitate making wise decisions.

Relationships  
Finally, the Temperance Tarot card can offer insight into relationships. It promotes balance, peace and harmony within relationships, emphasizing that these three characteristics must be in place for a relationship to be healthy and prosperous. This balance is achieved through communication and a mutual respect for each other’s needs, wants, and boundaries. Patience and understanding are also essential when it comes to relationships, allowing us to exercise self-control and to remain level-headed when resolving issues. Adopting temperate behaviors when interacting with others serves to foster relationships that are positive, productive, and long-lasting.
