However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 8 Of Wands card together in particular.

## What does The Hierophant and 8 Of Wands mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 8 Of Wands tarot card together suggest a balance between the pursuit of tradition and the need for quick action. In the midst of trying to stay true to your roots, you will be presented with a situation that requires you to act quickly in order to achieve the best possible outcome. Despite the tension between these two forces, there is potential for growth if you can find a way to use the traditional aspects of your life to propel yourself forward. Taking a moment to pause and reflect on the wisdom of the past can guide you to a future that honors your traditions while allowing for opportunities for growth and change.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is a representation of structure, conformity, and traditions. It is related to the ideas of social and spiritual norms. When this card appears in the context of health, it is a reminder that it is important for us to take our physical wellbeing seriously and to respect established practices of living healthy. For example, it may be a sign that it’s time to see a doctor regularly, or to start eating healthier and exercising. Following medical advice and sticking to, rather than breaking, conventional health protocols generally leads to better overall health.

Finances:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is a symbol of structure, traditional values, and conformity. It is often associated with hard work, thriftiness, and the ability to stick to a plan. In terms of finances, this card can represent one’s discipline in budgeting and setting aside funds for the future. It is an indication to thoughtfully create financial plans and stick to them. Saving money and setting aside funds for necessary investments and unexpected expenses is a form of respect for the traditional values of prudent financial management.

Relationships:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is a sign of honoring traditions and social norms. In relationships, this card can represent a respect for the traditional values that make relationships strong. This card can be a reminder to practice fidelity, commitment, and understanding of one another’s needs. It also encourages us to embrace traditional values such as responsibility and empathy when relating to others. By honoring traditional values and having respect for the rules of relationships, couples can build strong, long-lasting relationships.

If you have been considering a new diet, now is the time to implement it. If you have been putting off getting a check-up, now is the time to do it. Take positive steps towards your health and the results will be seen in no time.

Whether you are looking to make a financial investment, paying off debt, or simply getting your budget in order, the 8 of Wands suggests that you act quickly and decisively. Relationships: The 8 of Wands tarot card is a reminder that fast paced relationships can be exciting and fulfilling. If you find yourself in a situation where you need to make a decision in your relationship, take the time to weigh your options and act quickly. Being decisive will bring positive results in the long run. Don’t be afraid to try something new and take the necessary steps to make your relationship a success.
