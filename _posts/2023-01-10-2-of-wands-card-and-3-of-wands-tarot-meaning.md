A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 3 Of Wands card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 3 Of Wands mean together?

The 2 Of Wands and The 3 Of Wands tarot cards indicate that it is time to make a choice about the plans and goals you have for the future. This could involve making a travel plan or starting a new business venture. You have been waiting and gathering information before making a decision, and now is the time to move forward. The 3 Of Wands tarot card suggests working with others to achieve your goals and that there is great potential for growth and success. It is time to take the plunge and make a bold move to create the future you desire.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 2 Of Wands and 3 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 2 of Wands and 3 of Wands in your love life can indicate a period of waiting and decision-making. You may feel as if you are stuck on a precipice, uncertain which course of action to take. This may relate to a major relationship decision, where you are struggling to choose whether or not to move forward. At the same time, it is a great time to think things through and evaluate the available options.

The 3 of Wands adds a sense of teamwork and collaboration to the mix, suggesting that communication and negotiation may be key for making progress in your relationship. This is a time to think about how you can best combine your strengths and resources in order to move forward. Consider how you can work together to make your relationship strong and successful.

The energy of the two cards combined could also be suggesting that you plan a joint adventure together. Whether this is a visit to a romantic destination or an opportunity to experience a new activity, it is a time to explore and grow together. This can be a great way to strengthen your connection and deepen your understanding of one another.

Overall, the 2 of Wands and 3 of Wands suggest a period of exploring the available options, trusting yourself and your judgement, and sharing experiences with the one you love. It is a powerful combination indicating a time of both decision and discovery in your relationship.

### What does 2 Of Wands and 3 Of Wands mean together for your finances?

The 2 Of Wands tarot card and The 3 Of Wands tarot card when combined can indicate a time of waiting for a result, making a choice, and contemplating travel plans related to your finances. This card combination is alerting you to take heed of the choices you are currently making and how you are managing your finances.

This card combination could mean that you are considering a job change or entrepreneurial venture and you are at a crossroads. This could be a moment of self-reflection where you are looking at your current financial situation and deciding if a change is right for you. You may also be considering expanding your current business or taking a position with a new employer.

This tarot combination suggests that to make the best decision, teamwork and collaboration are essential. You are likely not alone in this decision-making process and should consult family, friends, or colleagues. This card combination could indicate that you may have to travel for work or to expand your business, so be mindful of this potential expense.

Ultimately, The 2 Of Wands tarot card and The 3 Of Wands tarot card combination is urging you to be intentional and deliberate about your financial decisions. You are in the position to make a positive change for your financial wellbeing, but you need to take the time to make sure you make the right choice. Take the time to do your research, consider the opinions of others, and think through the cost-benefit of any job change.

### What does 2 Of Wands and 3 Of Wands mean together for your health?

The 2 Of Wands tarot card combined with the 3 Of Wands tarot card is a powerful combination for physical health. This combination suggests that you are at a point where you need to take action and make a choice for your health and wellbeing. It could mean that real change is possible and that you need to decide on a path and set your plans in motion.

On the one hand, the 2 Of Wands tarot card indicates that it is important to wait for the results before making any moves. This can be seen as gathering all the data before deciding on how exactly to move forward. This is wise, as it gives you a clear overview of the situation and reduces the risk of making the wrong choice.

On the other hand, the 3 Of Wands tarot card suggests that you should consider teamwork and cooperation when it comes to making a decision. Working with others can help you to develop a more comprehensive strategy and can also help reduce the risk of making mistakes. It also can mean that you are not alone, and that you can draw strength and courage from your community of support.

The combination of the two cards could also suggest that travel may be part of the plan. Going to different places and exploring new perspectives can be helpful in developing a more holistic approach to your health. This can be especially useful if you are attempting to make a life change or if you need a break from your usual routine.

Overall, the 2 Of Wands and the 3 Of Wands tarot cards together suggest that it is time to make some important choices. These choices may involve taking a step back, gathering more information, cooperating with others, and even planning to travel. When it comes to physical health, the ultimate aim of this combination is to make decisions that can lead to long-term success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

The 2 of Wands Tarot card can represent a period of waiting, contemplation, and decision-making regarding health, finances, and relationships. The card symbolizes a need to consider all the options and make a choice between them. It may indicate a journey toward finding the best solution, utilizing both internal and external resources, and taking the necessary steps to move forward.

Regarding health, The 2 of Wands suggests that it’s time to take inventory of current health issues and decide which course of action to take. This could mean seeking a second opinion, researching alternative treatments, or making lifestyle changes. It can also indicate taking a ‘wait and see’ approach, delaying a decision until more information is gathered.

Money is often linked to the 2 of Wands, urging the seeker to consider their finances carefully before making any decisions. It can indicate seeking out sound financial advice, creating a budget, or taking steps to reduce debt. The card can also signify exploring opportunities or investments.

In relationships, the 2 of Wands often represents having difficulty making a decision or committing to a particular course of action. It can also indicate exploring a potential relationship, considering the pros and cons of a partnership, or waiting for the other person to make a move. Alternatively, The 2 of Wands may also signify a need to pause and reassess current relationships, considering what changes need to be made in order to make them better.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

When it comes to health, the 3 of Wands tarot card is an indicator of maintaining balance and finding the right balance of work and rest. It is a reminder that we need to restore our bodies and minds so that we are in a healthy physical and mental state. This card tells us that we don’t need to push ourselves to the point of exhaustion, as it will only lead to physical and mental burnout in the long run. Instead, this card encourages us to take time off from work and engage in activities that make us feel energized and relaxed. This might mean taking a walk or a break from our daily routine and getting outside for a change of scenery.

When it comes to finances, the 3 of Wands tarot card encourages us to think about our money-management strategies. This card is a reminder to work smarter, not harder. It tells us to focus on making wise investments, cutting back on our expenses, and saving for the future. This card also encourages us to think about our long-term financial goals and make plans to achieve them.

When it comes to relationships, the 3 of Wands tarot card is a reminder to build strong relationships with those around us. This card encourages us to think about our communication strategies and how to get our point across without causing conflict. This card also reminds us to be flexible in our relationships and open to different perspectives. It warns us against getting stuck in an “our way or the highway” mentality, as this can lead to a breakdown in communication and ultimately, the end of a relationship. This card also encourages us to maintain a level of trust and honesty in our relationships.
