A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Swords card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Ace Of Swords card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Ace Of Swords and 8 Of Swords mean together?

When the Ace of Swords and the 8 of Swords come together, it indicates that a mental or emotional struggle is about to take place. You may find yourself in a position where you must face a new conflict that was previously unknown to you, but you are being restricted from doing so because of self-imposed limitations or imprisonment. This could lead to feeling stuck, unable to make progress in any direction, and feeling confused. However, when both of these cards appear together, they communicate a message of hope; the Ace of Swords promises that if you can break through your self-imposed restrictions, you will find mental clarity and a breakthrough of sorts. It may indicate a difficult process of self-surgery that you must undergo, but if you push through, you will be rewarded.

Overall, the Ace of Swords and 8 of Swords together suggests that your love life may be going through a rough patch, however it is also a reminder to remain positive and be open to the potential the future holds. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 8 of Swords tarot card is a warning to be watchful of one’s health. It suggests that one may be putting too much pressure on oneself, leading to a feeling of isolation or entrapment. It implies that one may be feeling helpless or stuck in a situation, leading to self-doubt or a lack of confidence in one’s own abilities. The 8 of Swords can sometimes illustrate a need to increase self-care, or to make sure that one’s own health needs are being taken seriously. In terms of health, the 8 of Swords can indicate that one’s stress levels are taking a toll, both physically and mentally. This card can encourage one to take time for self-care, such as getting enough rest and exercise, eating healthy meals, and taking breaks from work and other responsibilities.

Finances: The 8 of Swords tarot card symbolizes restrictions and entrapment when it comes to finances. This card can hint at a lack of control in one’s financial situation, as well as a feeling of powerlessness to make any meaningful changes. It can represent a lack of resources, or even a feeling of being stuck in a situation with no way out. This card may be asking one to take a look at their finances, understand their current financial reality, and develop a plan of action to make changes and move forward. It could also be a warning to be careful when taking any financial risks, as well as to be aware of potential debt or money troubles.

Relationships: For relationships, the 8 of Swords tarot card can represent a feeling of restriction or imprisonment within one’s relationship. It can indicate that one has become too isolated and is not spending enough quality time with their partner. It can sometimes be a sign that one is feeling trapped in a relationship, as if there is no way to make it better or no room to make any changes. This card can be a warning that one is in danger of losing connection with their partner or of making unhealthy decisions based on a lack of communication. It may be an indication that one needs to make an effort to reconnect and rekindle the relationship, to open up communication, and to focus on self-care and trusting one’s own judgment.
