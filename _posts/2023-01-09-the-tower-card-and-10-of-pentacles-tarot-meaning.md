A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The 10 of Pentacles tarot card together suggest a shift in fortunes. The uneasiness of the Tower foreshadows sudden and unwelcome change, while the 10 of Pentacles offers a glimpse of security and stability in the future. This tumultuous period of disruption and hardship may bring a sense of ruin or disgrace, and may even involve upheaval or violence. However, the 10 of Pentacles suggests that in time, this period of upheaval could lead to a lasting legacy, bringing a sense of comfort, retirement, and an eventual inheritance.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Tower and 10 Of Pentacles mean together for your love life?

When the Tower tarot card and the 10 of Pentacles tarot card are drawn together in a love reading, it means that changes are coming to your relationship. It could signify that a long-term partnership is about to come to an end, or maybe that you and your partner are about to go through a period of upheaval. During this time, it is important to recognize that you and your partner are in the same boat and are both equally effected by this upheaval.

The 10 of Pentacles tarot card suggests that after this period of upheaval, a legacy will remain. This legacy could mean material things or the lessons learned from this difficult time. It is comforting to know that, even though tough times may be ahead, a lasting impact will come out of it.

It is important to remember, however, that the Tower tarot card is not always a sign of negative change. It can also mean that you and your partner are ready to make a major step forward in your relationship- it just may not come as easily as you’d like. So, while the Tower may bring some unwanted changes, it can also be a sign of progress and growth.

In short, when the Tower tarot card and the 10 of Pentacles tarot card are drawn together, it’s a sign of significant change, but not necessarily the end of your relationship. Change can be an opportunity to develop your relationship even further and to leave lasting evidence of your union. Remember that you and your partner are in this together and this change can be a chance for you both to grow.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: The Tower Tarot card can indicate disruption in health. This can mean anything from sudden sickness to a chronic, long-term health condition. It can also indicate a breakdown in our mental and emotional state, manifesting as depression or anxiety. This card’s warning is to be aware of how our physical and mental health can be disrupted and how change can be traumatic and unexpected.

Finance: The Tower Tarot card could be a warning to those who are too reliant on finances. It can signify a sudden change in fortune that leads to financial ruin or distress. It can also represent a shift in our financial state, such as the loss of a job or sudden expenses. This card can reveal the need for financial planning and an understanding of how unexpected life events can have a huge impact on our financial resources.

Relationships: The Tower Tarot card can symbolise a breakdown in relationships and a need to look at the foundations of our relationships. It can indicate a period of upheaval, change and conflict that can either lead to the strengthening of existing bonds or signal the end of a relationship. It can also signify a need to look at power dynamics in relationships, and understand how our behaviour affects our relationships. This card offers a warning to be aware of how our behaviour and relationships can be impacted by change and unexpected events.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card is associated with the completion of a cycle and reward for hard work. It often symbolizes making an investment or leaving a legacy. It is a powerful card representing the accumulation of wealth and satisfaction in terms of finance, health, and relationships.

When it comes to health, the 10 Of Pentacles tarot card suggests an improved state of physical, mental, and spiritual wellbeing. It can represent taking a long-term approach to health, and investing in a healthier lifestyle. This card advises to look at overall health and not just physical health. It also implies taking time to relax and take care of yourself, to ensure that you have the energy and vitality to make it through each day.

In terms of finance, The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card suggests working hard to establish a secure financial position. It encourages investment that will increase financial security and create a lasting legacy. It can suggest the possibility of retirement, inheritance, or other forms of financial security.

For relationships, The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card suggests building a strong and lasting bond with a loved one. This could indicate marriage, committing to a long-term relationship, or investing in a solid friendship. This card portrays the potential for a secure and trusting relationship, and encourages finding ways to build a lasting bond.

The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card is a powerful card symbolizing the attainment of long-term goals. In terms of health, finance, and relationships, it advises investing in the long-term and taking the time to enjoy the rewards. It suggests creating a legacy, whether financially, in terms of relationships, or in terms of overall wellbeing.
