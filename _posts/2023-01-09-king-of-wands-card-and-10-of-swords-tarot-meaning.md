A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the King Of Wands card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The King Of Wands card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does King Of Wands and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The King of Wands and the 10 of Swords tarot cards together indicate that your current situation may be coming to an end, but it will not be without pain or difficulty. You may need to be brave and summon up your courage to make it through this difficult moment and see it through the other side. You have the power, charm and leadership skills to make it through this, and although it may feel like the end of something, it can also offer clarity and a renewed opportunity for personal growth. Hold onto your strength and remember that you are the one in control. The end of this moment can bring a new beginning.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

For Health:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes complete and painful endings when it comes to physical health. This card can be a sign that you are not taking care of yourself and will soon reach a “rock bottom” state of being. It could also mean that an illness or injury will bring you to a very low point and can take a long time to recover from. When it comes to mental health, this card could signify a bleak outlook and pessimistic attitude, signaling some sort of depression or anxiety that is difficult to get out of. Ultimately, this card can be a warning that you are not taking care of your health and need to refocus on ways to look after your physical and mental well-being.

For Finances:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card speaks of downsizing, bankruptcy and complete financial ruin. This card is not a good sign when it comes to money matters. It symbolizes a financial crisis that could involve debt, foreclosure, bankruptcy and all other kinds of financial hardship. This card can also represent a period of financial difficulty that does not seem to improve. It can be indicative of a time of necessity and desperation, when all other options seem to be exhausted, and the only way is to start all over.

For Relationships:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card speaks of complete and painful endings when it comes to relationships. This card could signify the end of a relationship, an argument, the death of a loved one, or a divorce. It could also represent a period of estrangement, where communication has ceased and hope seems to dwindle. When it comes to friendships, it could signal the end of a close bond, where the relationship has become strained and broken. Ultimately, this card speaks of painful endings and rock bottom, where closure is necessary and communication must be severed in order to move on.
