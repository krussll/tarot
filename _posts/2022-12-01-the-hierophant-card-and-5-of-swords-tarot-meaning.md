However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 5 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 5 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 5 Of Swords tarot card combined can be symbolic of how societal traditions, expectations, and conformity can sometimes lead to bullying, theft, violence, and abusive relationships. This could be seen when certain expectations are placed on someone that lead to a feeling of being backed into a corner and having no way out, resulting in struggles such as those mentioned above. Whether it’s the expectations of a family, community, or society that create this feeling, it’s important to remember that it’s never ok to harm ourselves or others, and that there is always a way out.

The 5 of Swords on the other hand points to more sinister connections, suggesting that there is bullying and violence involved, either physical or emotional. This combination in a love reading may indicate that you need to break out of the toxic cycle of unhealthy expectations, and to find a way to express your true feelings without fear of judgement or repercussions.

It is important to take this combination of cards seriously and to pay attention to your gut instinct. If you feel stuck in an abusive, oppressive relationship, this card combination is telling you to take action to protect yourself. You need to take a stand and do what is right for you and your partner, regardless of what society or anyone else expects. It is time to break free of the restrictive rules and find a healthier, more authentic way to express your love.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and 5 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 5 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health: The Hierophant card suggests that health is extremely important and that staying within the conventional methods of well-being is in your best interests. It can mean taking a more organized approach to your health and following traditional methods of healing, such as seeking professional medical advice, trying different methods of meditation, or taking up physical activities. The Hierophant encourages you to use tried and true methods for maintaining good health, as these methods have likely proved successful for many people.

Finances: The Hierophant card can be interpreted as advice to maintain a conservative financial approach. This could involve saving and budgeting carefully, being aware of your financial situation, and keeping within your means. It also could include finding a suitable job or investment that is secure, reliable and trustworthy. The Hierophant’s message is to be aware of scams, fraud and con artists, and to keep your finances safe and sound.

Relationships: The Hierophant suggests that relationships require a certain level of tradition and conforming to accepted expectations. This could involve displaying traditional forms of respect and courtesy, being consistent and reliable, and appreciating the importance of overall stability. It also could mean being open and honest in communication, avoiding gossip, and expressing appreciation for the people that you love. The Hierophant card advises to stay true to traditions and to strive for lasting, meaningful relationships.

Health: The 5 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning sign of physical, mental and emotional abuse. This card can denote the presence of a bully in your life, whether it be in the form of a partner, friend or family member and their dangerous and coercive behavior. It can also mean that you are putting yourself in danger either through reckless behavior or by allowing someone to manipulate and control you. It can be a warning sign to be mindful of your own safety as well as others, as physical and mental harm could be inflicted.

Finances: The 5 of Swords Tarot card indicates that there are risks involved when it comes to your finances. It could symbolize theft, fraud, or manipulation on someone’s part. It can be a warning to take extra caution when it comes to financial transactions, and to be aware of the potential for someone to take advantage of your generosity or naivety.

Relationships: This card can be a sign of toxic, abusive relationships. It can suggest that there is an imbalance of power, with one person taking control over the other and using abusive tactics to maintain their grip. It can also be a warning sign that someone is manipulating you into doing something that you wouldn’t normally do, or is inducing fear or guilt in order to get what they want. It can be a sign to reevaluate the relationships in your life and to cut out any unhealthy elements that could be damaging your wellbeing.
