A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and The Moon card together in particular.

The Justice card and The Moon have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and The Moon mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the Moon tarot card combined indicate the importance of understanding the result of our decisions. They remind us that while we may not be able to see the full outcome of our actions, we must remember that ultimately, win-win solutions are the best course of action. This can also serve as a reminder to trust our intuition and make changes that are voluntary rather than forced. In the end, the truth will be revealed and the justice system will always ensure the fairest outcome for all involved.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This tarot card combination also encourages you to be willing to make voluntary changes. If both you and your partner are willing to make these changes, then you can find a solution that works for both of you.

Overall, the Justice tarot card and the Moon tarot card together in a reading about your love life encourage you to search for the truth and be willing to make decisions from the heart. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and The Moon mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and The Moon the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

For Health:

The Justice Tarot card represents cause and effect in terms of health. When it comes to our health, we need to acknowledge that what we do affects our health outcomes. It is important to be aware of the consequences of our choices and to be mindful of the impact our actions have on our bodies. We need to acknowledge the consequences of unhealthy choices while also understanding that we are in control of our own health decisions. The Justice Tarot card encourages us to take responsibility for our health decisions by only engaging in activities that promote positive health outcomes.

For Finances:

In terms of finances, the Justice Tarot card suggests that we need to be aware of the effects of our financial decisions. We need to be mindful about how our spending affects our overall financial health. We must recognize that our money is a limited resource and that it should be spent carefully. We should strive for a win-win financial situation where we are able to meet our financial needs without sacrificing our long-term financial security. Additionally, the Justice Tarot card can also act as a reminder that we should be honest and truthful when it comes to our finances.

For Relationships:

The Justice Tarot card can represent the importance of fairness and equity in relationships. We should strive to create relationships where both parties feel safe, understood, and respected. We should approach our relationships with an open mind and try to find win-win solutions whenever possible. We should also be honest and truthful with our partners and recognize that our actions have consequences. The Justice Tarot card can remind us to stay true to our values and ensure that our relationships are based on trust, understanding, and kindness.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health: The Moon Tarot card symbolizes a need to become aware of any hidden/unseen problems that could be affecting your overall health. It is a reminder to start paying more attention to your body and its needs. This card can signify minor health problems that can be resolved by taking the right steps and consulting with a qualified medical professional. Similarly, you may need to make some voluntary changes to your habits and lifestyle to ensure optimal physical and mental health. This can include moderating alcohol and substance use, keeping a healthier diet, exercising regularly and getting enough rest. Most importantly, The Moon Tarot card encourages you to listen to your intuition and trust any inner messages or feelings you may have about your health.

Finances: The Moon Tarot card is a sign that unseen problems could be affecting your finances. This can be a warning to be careful not to fall into any traps set by others who are trying to take advantage of you. It is important to thoroughly look over any financial contracts or deals, ensuring that you understand exactly what you are signing up for. Similarly, you may need to make voluntary financial changes, such as deciding to save more and spend less, which will help you to improve your overall financial health. Additionally, The Moon Tarot card encourages you to trust your intuition when it comes to financial decision-making.

Relationships: The Moon Tarot card is a reminder to become aware of any unseen problems in any relationships you may have. This can be a warning that someone in your life is not being honest with you or is manipulating the relationship in order to benefit themselves. It is important to trust your intuition and take time to decide if the relationship is worth continuing. This card can also signify any voluntary changes that need to be made in a relationship in order to ensure it is healthy and beneficial for both partners. Most importantly, it encourages you to trust your intuition when it comes to any relationships, as it is often the best judge of character.
