Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The 8 Of Swords card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 8 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 8 Of Swords.

## 8 Of Swords And Sprituality – An Overview

The 8 Of Swords tarot card is a symbol of self-imposed restrictions and isolation, particularly in the realm of physical health.

It indicates a sense of being “locked in”, having difficulty breaking out of unhealthy habits and patterns.

It can mean that there are mental or emotional blockages that are preventing us from living a healthy lifestyle.

It can also refer to physical limitations and pain, such as chronic illness or a lack of access to medical care.

On the other hand, it may simply indicate feelings of helplessness and hopelessness when it comes to our health and wellbeing.

Ultimately, the 8 of Swords signifies the need to break out of our self-imposed prisons and take control of our health to find freedom and healing.

It is important for me to be mindful of my own limitations and respect them in order to continue making progress on my spiritual path.

It is essential for me to find the courage to break free from the self-imposed restrictions that hold me back in order to fully embrace my spiritual growth.

### What It Means For The Future

The 8 Of Swords tarot card is a reminder that although we may feel restricted and isolated, the truth is that these feelings are often self-imposed.

In terms of my future spirituality, this card is a reminder that I have the power to free myself from self-imposed restrictions and can choose to live a life that is spiritually fulfilling.

It is important to remember that spiritual growth often requires an open mind, a willingness to explore outside of one’s comfort zone, and a connection to something greater than oneself.

This card encourages me to look at the ways in which I may be inadvertently preventing those close to me from connecting with their spiritual selves and fulfilling their potential.

It is a call to recognize the spiritual needs of those around me and to create an environment of openness and understanding that will allow them to explore their spiritual selves.

It is a reminder that my spiritual journey is inextricably linked to the spiritual journeys of those around me, and that I should strive to create a safe and supportive environment that allows them to explore and grow.
