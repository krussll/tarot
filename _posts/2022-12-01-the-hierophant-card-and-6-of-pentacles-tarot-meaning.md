However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does The Hierophant and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card represent a connection between traditional expectations and generosity. They suggest that may find yourself participating in or being part of a traditional setting where you are expected to abide by certain customs, while at the same time being encouraged to express your generosity and provide assistance to those in need. It is not always easy to find a balance between traditional expectations and generosity, but this card combination encourages us to find a balance between the two so that we can meet our own needs, while also helping others.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that one should adhere to the traditions and expectations of health care. Seek help from a professional health care provider if you have any health problems. It may be necessary to accept traditional treatments that have been proven to be effective, rather than experimenting with alternative remedies. Embrace healthcare protocols, such as vaccinations and preventive checkups, that have been used for centuries and have been proven to work.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card urges one to stick to the established rules and conventions concerning financial matters. Adhere to sound money management principles such as budgeting and saving, and consider conventional investments—like stocks, bonds and mutual funds—as viable ways to grow your wealth. Don’t take any big risks with regards to your financial future; instead, focus on what’s been proven to be successful over time.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card advocates following the norms of relationships. Be a good listener and respect the opinions of others. Follow social conventions and interact respectfully. Be a good friend, and show support and loyalty to your loved ones. Show a dedication to the rules that have been in place for years and have helped many relationships to thrive. Respect the rules and traditions of the relationship, and be willing to compromise in order to keep the peace.
