The combination of these two cards suggests that you should be self-reliant and independent, as it will be the only way to make the most beneficial choices that will bring you closer to a brighter future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

The Moon Tarot card symbolizes trusting your intuition to make smart choices, particularly in areas of health, finances and relationships. Being in tune with your inner guidance system is essential for handling the unseen issues and potential changes that could arise in each of these areas.

When it comes to health, the Moon Tarot card encourages individuals to trust their instincts when it comes to care, prevention and treatment. This card implores us to be extra mindful and to not let existing medical conditions go unchecked. It may also indicate that alternative methods of healing should be explored, such as natural, plant-based remedies or energy healing techniques like Reiki and acupuncture.

When it comes to finances, the Moon Tarot card suggests that individuals should be mindful and not engage in investments or purchases on a whim. The card also implies that taking risks is okay as long as it is done with caution. Making voluntary changes to an existing budget in order to improve financial health is also recommended.

In relationships, the Moon Tarot card emphasizes the importance of trusting one’s intuition. It can indicate that changes are needed in order to improve the connection with others and to restore balance. It is also a reminder to be open to exploring new ways of communicating and interacting. Connecting with our inner truth is the key to building healthier relationships.

Overall, the Moon Tarot card advises individuals to trust their intuition and to follow their inner truth, especially when it comes to health, finances and relationships. It’s a reminder to be mindful, to take risks and to make voluntary changes that will help improve each of these aspects of our lives.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

The Queen of Swords Tarot card pertains to a person who is independent and honest. In terms of health, this card means that one should look for ways to become self-sufficient when it comes to their health care. This could include taking on proactive health practices like healthier dieting and exercise, or researching alternative methods of health care, such as homeopathy or acupuncture. They should have the courage to take responsibility for their own health, and should be aware of the power of honesty as it relates to their wellbeing.

In terms of finances, the Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes one who is in control of their finances and is honest about their financial situation. It suggests that one should have a clear understanding of their financial situation and take responsibility for it, rather than relying on others to take care of their financial needs. This could mean budgeting and saving, investing in stocks and bonds, or setting up a retirement plan. They should also be honest about their financial situation with those who depend on them. This could mean setting boundaries and taking responsibility for their own money rather than allowing others to control it.

In terms of relationships, the Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes honesty and self-sufficiency. It suggests that one should be honest and direct with those they are in relationships with, as truth will bring greater understanding and trust in such relationships. They should also be aware that they are in control of their relationships and should not allow the other person to have too much control or take advantage of the situation. Self-sufficiency in relationships will allow one to better understand what they want and need from their partner and can lead to a healthier and more fulfilling relationship.
