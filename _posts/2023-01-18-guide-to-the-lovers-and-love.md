Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Lovers card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Lovers, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Lovers.

## The Lovers And Love – An Overview

The Lovers tarot card symbolizes the power of partnership when it comes to health.

It shows that two people working together can achieve greater wellbeing than either of them can alone.

This can be seen in the many benefits of a supportive relationship, such as when couples exercise together, or when one partner offers emotional support to the other when going through a difficult time.

The Lovers card also suggests that when it comes to making decisions about our health, we should consider both our own perspective and that of our partner, to ensure that the best choice is made.

Ultimately, The Lovers card implies that when it comes to health, two is always better than one.

## Upright Meanings For The Lovers And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Lovers tarot card represents the power of two people in partnership and the beauty of deep, unconditional love.

It symbolizes a choice; whether it be to commit to a relationship, to accept or reject someone, or to move on after a break up.

It also speaks to the strength of two people when they are together, and the potential for growth that can come from such a bond.

Ultimately, The Lovers stands for the beauty of two souls united, and the joy and passion that can be found in such a connection.

### What It Means For The Future

The Lovers tarot card is a sign that you are on the path to finding true love.

You have the strength to choose the right person for you and the partnership that you build together will be strong.

Deep love will come from an understanding and trust that you have for each other.

With the Lovers card in your future, you can look forward to a meaningful, long-lasting relationship with someone special.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Lovers tarot card is a reminder of the strength of love between two people.

It is a representation of partnership and deep love, and the power of being together.

It is a reminder that, when faced with a tough decision, it is often best to take the advice of our loved ones.

This card encourages us to cherish those we are close to, and to value their opinion and their input.

It is a reminder that two people working together can often be greater than one, and that the love of those close to us is a precious and powerful thing that should be treasured and appreciated.

## Reversed Meanings For The Lovers And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The Lovers card appears in its reversed position, it can indicate a lack of love, or that a relationship is struggling.

It may suggest that a relationship is unbalanced or that the two people involved are struggling to find common ground.

It can also signify a lack of trust or communication between the two partners.

The reversed Lovers can also indicate a love triangle, or a situation in which two people are vying for the affections of the same person.

It can suggest a lack of harmony and unity between two people, and a reliance on power-plays or manipulation to get what each partner wants.

When reversed, The Lovers card can suggest that the two people involved need to reassess their relationship and make sure they are on the same page before they can find true love and happiness together.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed The Lovers card appears in a reading, it can be a sign of a rocky road ahead for romance.

It suggests that there may be some difficult decisions or choices to make when it comes to relationships.

It may also indicate a lack of trust or commitment, or a lack of balance between two partners.

It is a reminder that relationships are not always easy, and that we must be willing to work at them.

The reversed The Lovers can also be a sign that a current relationship may be strained or on the brink of breaking down.

This card can be a warning against rushing into a relationship too quickly, or not taking enough time to get to know someone before committing.

It is a reminder that it is important to take the time to nurture and cultivate relationships, and to ensure that both partners are getting what they need from the relationship.

Ultimately, the reversed The Lovers card can be a sign that the future of my love life may require some hard work and effort, but if I make the right choices, it can be a very rewarding experience.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed Lovers tarot card indicates that the love between those close to me is strained or distant.

It may be a sign of disharmony between partners, or a sign that something is preventing us from connecting in a meaningful way.

It could be a sign of a lack of communication or a lack of trust.

It may also indicate that there is difficulty in making decisions together, or that I am feeling disconnected from my loved ones.

The reversed Lovers tarot card could also be a sign that there is a need for more understanding and acceptance in my relationships.

It could be a sign that I am not truly listening to my loved ones or that I may be too quick to judge them.

It may also be an indication that I need to open my heart to my loved ones and to be more receptive to their needs.

Ultimately, the reversed Lovers tarot card is a sign that I need to give more attention, love, and understanding to those close to me if I want to strengthen my relationships.
