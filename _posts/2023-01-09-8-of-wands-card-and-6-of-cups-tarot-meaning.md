A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The 8 Of Wands and The 6 Of Cups tarot card combination mean that there is an exciting reunion on the horizon. This reunion will bring with it gifts of nostalgia, though the news of it is arriving quickly, likely due to a sudden change or action. Despite the speed with which it appears, this reunion is one that you can approach with innocence and joy, and promises to bring much happiness.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 8 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When the 8 of Wands and the 6 of Cups appear together in a love reading, it could indicate that things are about to move quickly and unexpectedly in your love life. This could mean that news, likely good news, will arrive, and it will come quickly. This could mean that it’s time to take action, whether that’s pursuing someone you’ve been admiring from afar, or beginning to plan a surprise for your partner.

The 6 of Cups often speaks of nostalgia and reunions, so this might be a time to revisit old feelings and reconnect with an old flame. It could also mean that your relationship will become more like it was in the past. If it was good, then this could be a positive thing, as it speaks of a renewed innocence and joy. If it wasn’t so great, then it might be a signal to take action and address the issues that caused the relationship to suffer.

In any case, the combination of the 8 of Wands and the 6 of Cups predicts a period of rapid change and movement. It could refer to a new or renewed relationship, or it could signify a period of increased closeness and joy in your existing relationship. There may also be gifts and surprises involved, as the 6 of Cups often suggests. In any case, you may need to take action and be ready to move quickly.

### What does 8 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

The combination of the 8 of Wands and the 6 of Cups is a powerful one when it comes to finances and career. For the 8 of Wands, it brings a message that fast changes and news arriving soon will be beneficial for your finances. This could mean a sudden increase in income, a sudden job offer or promotion, among other things. The 6 of Cups represents nostalgia and reunions, so something from your past may be coming back to give you a financial boost in the near future.

When it comes to job opportunities, the combination of the 8 of Wands and the 6 of Cups suggests that you may be presented with a job offer that is a bit out of the ordinary. This could be an old job you had before, a promotion from within your current company, or a brand new job offer from a fresh source. Either way, it is likely to be something that has long-term financial benefits for you, so it is important to keep an open mind about the possibilities.

For those already in a job, the 8 of Wands and the 6 of Cups suggest that you may be due for some kind of recognition for your hard work and dedication. This could be a raise, a bonus, or even a promotion. In any case, this is a sign that you are valued in your current position and it may be wise to take advantage of any financial opportunities that come your way.

In conclusion, the combination of the 8 of Wands and the 6 of Cups suggests that your financial future is full of potential and you should be ready to seize any opportunity that comes your way. Whether it is a job offer, a promotion, or a raise, it is likely to be something that has long-term benefits and allows you to secure your financial future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

When it comes to health, the 8 of Wands tarot card represents the need to move quickly. It signifies the need to be proactive in maintaining one’s health and suggests that any health issues should be addressed right away. It can also denote the arrival of news related to health, so it is important to be open and responsive to any pertinent information.

In terms of finances, the 8 of Wands implies that sudden changes are on the horizon. It is important to be prepared for difficult times, as well as opportunities for quick and profitable decisions. It suggests a time of transition, in which it is important to assess one’s current situation, as well as contemplate potential outcomes.

When it comes to relationships, the 8 of Wands signifies the need to move forward quickly. This can mean making a commitment, or making an important decision regarding an existing relationship. It can also suggest the arrival of news related to relationships, so it is important to be attentive and open to any new information. The 8 of Wands tarot card encourages action and looking toward the future.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

The 6 of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as a positive omen in one’s health, finances and relationships. In health, the 6 of Cups indicates an overall good physical and mental health. It could mean that you have been taking care of yourself lately and have a balanced diet, exercise, and rest time. This card could also mean that any illnesses or inconveniences that you have been experiencing are winding down due to your conscious efforts. Financially, the 6 of Cups suggests that your current financial situation is stable and secure. Your finances have improved and could be expected to remain that way for some time. It is a similar story with relationships – the 6 of Cups marks a faithful and strong partnership or relationship. It could mean that you have been getting along well with your partner or that relationship that means the world to you is as strong as it has ever been.

This Tarot card could also be interpreted as a call to reconnect with old friends, family and lovers. The 6 of Cups suggests that a pleasant surprise is waiting if you reach out and reconnect with those who mean a lot. Reuniting with old friends and family can bring amazing joy and nostalgia, helping to heal past hurts and bringing back fond memories. In terms of relationships with others, the 6 of Cups encourages us to be generous and compassionate with one another, show forgiveness for each other’s mistakes, and respect each other’s rights.

On the whole, the 6 of Cups Tarot card is a positive card that brings with it much joy and good news. It is a sign of good health, strong finances, and strong relationships. It can also be interpreted as a call to reconnect with old friends and family, and to show generosity and compassion with our fellow human beings.
