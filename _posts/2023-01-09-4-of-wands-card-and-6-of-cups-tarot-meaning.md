## What does 4 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The 4 of Wands and the 6 of Cups tarot card combine to bring a message of celebration and nostalgia. When these cards come together, it often means that it is time for a family gathering or a reunion with an old friend. Homecoming is the main theme here, as the 4 of Wands speaks of celebrations, family, friends, and happy reunions, while the 6 of Cups symbolizes nostalgia and gifts. This could be indicative of an upcoming holiday, birthday, anniversary, or other special occasion where family and friends will gather to create beautiful memories. The nostalgic gifts and innocent memories of the 6 of Cups combined with the joy and happiness of the 4 of Wands make for an truly wonderful time.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

The 4 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of homecoming and all it implies. When it comes to matters of health, this card can indicate physical recovery from a long-term illness. It can suggest a successful treatment plan has been accomplished, and the person is finally on the mend. This can also mean that the person is discovering a new state of balance and equilibrium as they return to their own inner health.

In terms of finances, The 4 of Wands tarot card can symbolize the coming of financial stability or increased prosperity. It could be that a long-term investment plan is finally paying off, or that a large amount of money is coming in at once. Whatever the case may be, the return on investment is likely to be substantial and rewarding.

In terms of relationships, The 4 of Wands tarot card can symbolize the return of a long-distance relationship or a reunion of two people who have been separated for a long time. This card can also indicate a renewal of a relationship that had become stagnant, which suggests that the two people involved are forging a new level of understanding and connection. With this card, it is likely that the two will find a newfound level of closeness and togetherness that was not present before.

Ultimately, The 4 of Wands tarot card is a reminder that return can often be a cause for celebration, no matter what the context. This card suggests that even in the wake of adversity or struggle, there is always potential for renewal, growth and abundance.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

The Six of Cups in the Tarot card can be a positive sign for health, finances and relationships. It is a reminder of all that is important in life, such as innocence and nostalgia, and how they can help to sustain the other aspects of life.

The Six of Cups encourages us to be mindful of our health and take care of ourselves. It advises us to stay grounded in our physical health, through simple things such as eating right, exercising, and getting enough sleep. It also reminds us to take a break and slow down, to give ourselves the time we need to recharge and nurture our physical and mental wellbeing. In terms of finances, the Six of Cups indicates a type of abundance. It encourages us to recognize the gifts that are available to us in our financial lives, and to be generous with ourselves and others. The card denotes the potential to receive gifts from others, and to share the gifts we have been given with the world.

In terms of relationships, the Six of Cups talks about reunions, of mending old ties and of reviving the innocence and joy of our past connections. This card encourages us to appreciate the people we have in our lives, to treat them with respect, kindness, and understanding. It reminds us to express our gratitude for the relationships we have and to embrace the potential for new experiences, connections and joy that it brings. The Six of Cups emphasizes the importance of cherishing and honouring both the past and the present.
