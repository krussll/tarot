Having a fully charged tarot deck is a wonderful feeling. Knowing both you and your cards are full prepared for a reading can give you so much confidence.

But cleansing your tarot deck is easily forgotten. Its sometimes time consuming, and those who are particularly eager my want to skip it to get straight into their reading.

However cleansing your cards should be integral to your tarot practice. Not cleansing your deck can have many nasty side effects, which I will go over in this guide.

## What Happens If You Don’t Cleanse Your Tarot Cards?

If you do not cleanse your tarot cards, you may find that your readings are not as accurate as they could be. This is because the cards can pick up negative energy from the environment, from people you have come into contact with, and from your own thoughts and beliefs.

If the cards are not cleansed on a regular basis, this negative energy can build up and eventually interfere with the readings. Not only can this cause inaccurate readings, but it can also lead to misinterpretations or even misinterpretations of the intended meaning of the cards. Additionally, not cleansing your tarot cards can cause the cards to become blocked or “stuck”.

This can happen when too much negative energy accumulates and the cards are unable to properly channel the information they are supposed to be providing. When this occurs, the readings will be unclear or inaccurate, and you may find yourself unable to interpret the messages the cards are providing.

Finally, not cleansing your tarot cards can affect your own emotional and spiritual well-being. When negative energy builds up in the cards, it can be transferred to you as well.

This can lead to feelings of depression, anxiety, and lack of clarity, which can in turn interfere with your own spiritual development and growth. It is important to cleanse your tarot cards on a regular basis to ensure that they are free from any negative energy that may be affecting their readings.

Doing so will help ensure that your readings are accurate and that you are receiving the messages the cards are intended to provide.

## Accuracy

Cleansing your tarot cards is an important part of the tarot reading process. When tarot cards are not cleansed, they can become blocked with energy from other readings and can prevent accurate readings.

Not cleansing tarot cards can cause the tarot reader to receive inaccurate readings due to the lack of clarity in the tarot cards. Tarot cards can pick up on people’s energy, which can be both positive and negative.

When the tarot cards are not cleansed, they can become “clogged” with other people’s energy and can prevent the tarot reader from receiving accurate readings.

This can be detrimental to the tarot reading process, as the tarot reader must be able to interpret the tarot cards accurately for the reading to be accurate.

Additionally, not cleansing tarot cards can be detrimental to the tarot reader’s personal energy. As tarot cards can pick up on energy, not cleansing the tarot cards can cause the tarot reader to become overwhelmed with energy from their readings.

Not only can this be distracting to the tarot reader, it can also prevent them from connecting with the tarot cards and receiving accurate readings. Cleansing your tarot cards is essential for accurate and insightful tarot readings.

## Emotional and spiritual well-being

Not cleansing your tarot cards can have a serious effect on your emotional and spiritual well-being. Tarot cards are a form of divination and can be used to gain insight into the future, uncover hidden truths, and gain spiritual guidance.

When tarot cards are not cleansed regularly, it can lead to a build-up of negative energy that can inhibit their ability to provide meaningful readings. This can lead to feelings of confusion, frustration, and anxiety as the readings become increasingly unclear and unreliable.

Additionally, when tarot cards are not cleansed, the user can become overwhelmed by negative thoughts and emotions. This can be detrimental to the user’s mental and emotional health, as the heavy negative energy can cause a feeling of being stuck and unable to move forward.

This can lead to a sense of discouragement, which can have a lasting effect on their spiritual journey and overall well-being. It is important to remember that tarot cards are a powerful tool, and they should be respected as such.

Regularly cleansing and charging the cards is essential to ensure that they are able to provide accurate readings. This will help the user stay focused and clear-minded, allowing them to access the guidance and insight that the cards provide.

Cleansing the cards can also help the user maintain an open and positive attitude towards the readings, ensuring that the messages received are free from any interference from negative energy. Overall, it is important to cleanse and charge your tarot cards regularly to ensure that you are able to access the most accurate readings.

This will help to ensure that you are able to remain focused, clear-minded, and positive, allowing for the best possible readings and insights. This will also help to ensure that you remain emotionally and spiritually balanced, allowing you to stay on your path towards personal growth and fulfilment.
