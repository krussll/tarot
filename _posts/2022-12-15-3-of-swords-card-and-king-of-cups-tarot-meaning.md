A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Swords card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Swords card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together?

The 3 of Swords tarot card and the King of Cups tarot card signify a time of deep emotional pain and challenging life changes. The 3 of Swords encourages us to accept the reality of heartbreak or a difficult divorce, recognize our feelings of loss, depression, and sadness, and allow our emotions to be fully expressed without shame. At the same time, the King of Cups is a gentle reminder that during this time of great upheaval and sadness, our compassionate leader with wisdom and understanding is within us. He offers us a safe place to receive emotional comfort, inner strength, and a sense of understanding and tolerance. Even when faced with the worst-case scenario, we can find solace in our own inner reserves of strength and find a new path forward.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 3 Of Swords and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 3 Of Swords and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Three of Swords Tarot card is a powerful symbol of physical and psychological pain and suffering. In the context of health, this card typically represents serious physical illness, particularly long-term and chronic conditions, or the resolution of serious illness through surgery. It can also signify being inflicted with an unexpected medical complication, or the emotional and psychological scars of a past medical experience.

Finances  
The Three of Swords Tarot card is a sign of financial burden, loss, and difficulty. It can signify the financial strain of expensive medical bills and procedures, as well as the heartbreak of losing out on a great financial opportunity. The card can also suggest that a person is stuck in debt, and may have to make tough financial choices in order to make ends meet.

Relationships  
The Three of Swords Tarot card can signify betrayal, heartbreak and emotional pain in relationships. It often appears when a relationship is coming to an end due to infidelity or dishonesty. It can also represent the emotional strain of a serious disagreement between two people, or the pain of a loved one’s death or serious illness. This card can also indicate depression, unhappiness and emotional turmoil in a relationship.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The King of Cups Tarot card represents a gentle, supportive approach to health that emphasizes compassion and empathy. It encourages us to nurture ourselves and our bodies, to take the time to listen to our own needs for healing, and to be open to exploring the healing potential of alternative treatments. It is a reminder to be patient with ourselves and focus on the healing journey rather than any immediate results. On a physical level, the card can warn of potential health issues related to an imbalance in emotions and the body, such as stress and anxiety.

Finances:  
The King of Cups Tarot card can represent financial security and security in the workplace. When the King of Cups appears in a Tarot reading, it is a sign to take a measured, balanced approach to finances. This could mean being wise with your money and mindful about where you put it. The King of Cups is also a reminder to be aware of the long-term effects of financial decisions, so that we can make informed, responsible decisions.

Relationships:  
The King of Cups Tarot card speaks of empathy, understanding, and compassion in relationships. It could mean that a certain relationship is ready to move to a new level or is in the process of growing and developing. The King of Cups encourages us to be open and honest about our feelings, and to make communication a priority. It encourages us to be tolerant of each other’s weaknesses and accept each other’s imperfections. The King of Cups reminds us to be loving and generous with our partners and to maintain a healthy balance between giving and receiving.
