## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Swords and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Swords and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card suggests that feelings of mental and physical exhaustion, deep-seated despair, and a general sense of dread may be present in one’s life. This card speaks to the debilitating effects that long-term stress, pain, and illness can have on the mind and body. It can suggest that physical health might be compromised due to poor lifestyle choices, poor nutrition and/or lack of sleep. These symptoms could lead to an overall sense of malaise and an inability to focus on important matters. In some cases, mental health issues may be present, such as anxiety and depression. These mental health issues can cause a person to feel trapped in a cycle of unhelpful thoughts, feelings and behaviors.

Finances  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card speaks to the deep-seated worry and distress one might experience in their finances. This card suggests that there may be financial setbacks or difficult times ahead. It can represent a period of financial insecurity and instability, as well as a feeling of being overwhelmed by debts and financial pressures. It can also speak to a feeling of helplessness or being stuck in a rut, unable to find a viable solution for financial woes.

Relationships  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card can suggest that interpersonal relationships might be hindered by anxiety and fear. This card symbolizes the emotional wounds inflicted by distrust, betrayal, or neglect. It can suggest that conflicts and disagreements could arise due to the tendency to overreact or shut down emotionally as a result of prior trauma. Insecurities and fear of abandonment can lead to resentment, passive-aggressiveness, and distance in relationships. An inability to make healthy connections could leave one feeling isolated and misunderstood.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

This card encourages the individual to take a deep and comprehensive look at their health and make sure that their diet is balanced, that they are engaging in regular physical activity, and that they are managing their stressors carefully. This card also suggests that an individual is willing to make decisions that are necessary for their long-term health, and that they will have the courage and fortitude to stick with these decisions no matter how challenging or difficult the circumstances.

Finances: The King of Swords Tarot card symbolizes an individual whose financial strategy is both thoughtful and considered. This card encourages the individual to take a proactive approach to their finances and to think through their long-term financial goals in order to ensure their fiscal security. This card also suggests that the individual is willing to make logical and calculative decisions when it comes to their finances and that they will be able to use their intuitive understanding of the market and industry to make sound investments in order to grow their wealth.

Relationships: The King of Swords Tarot card speaks to an individual who is willing to take an intentional approach to their romantic relationships and interactions. This card indicates that the individual will be willing to make tough decisions and to voice their opinion when necessary. This card suggests that this individual will be strategic and thoughtful when it comes to navigating interpersonal relationships, having the wisdom and understanding to know when to compromise and when to stand firm. This card indicates that this individual will take their relationships seriously and will strive to have meaningful and successful interactions with those around them.
