## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes our need for stability and security in life, especially when it comes to material possessions. It is often interpreted as the need to hoard resources and represent the attitude of “mine, mine, mine”. Through this, we can gain a better understanding of how this card may affect different aspects of our lives, including our health, finances, and relationships.

In terms of health, the 4 of Pentacles suggests an instinct to preserve and protect one’s own health. This could manifest in a number of ways, such as a desire to lead a healthy lifestyle, take preventative care, or to be wary of potential illnesses. This can be both beneficial and detrimental, as it encourages us to protect our well-being but can also lead to anxiety or paranoia.

In terms of finances, the 4 of Pentacles may symbolize a desire to build financial security, which can manifest as hoarding and stockpiling of resources. This card can signify being overly cautious with spending and saving too much, which can prevent us from cultivating a healthy relationship with money. It can also mean we are so focused on our own financial security that we may forget to use money to help us grow our relationships with others.

Finally, with regards to relationships, the 4 of Pentacles suggests a focus on material security over emotional security. It can indicate that one may have a need to feel safe in their relationships and a fear of change or commitment. It can also point to a tendency to be overly possessive or demanding in relationships, which can be damaging to any type of connection.

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to find a balance between our own needs for stability and security with our need to build healthy relationships. This balance can be attained by remembering to stay open and generous in our dealings with others. We can use our resources to build sustainable relationships, and be willing to let go of our controlling tendencies in order to allow for growth.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

For health, The 9 Of Swords Tarot card can indicate that the person is going through a difficult time in their life. It suggests worry, fear, and a feeling of being overwhelmed. It can represent physical and mental exhaustion and the person may be feeling increasingly disconnected from themselves. It may signify nightmares or insomnia and can lead to feelings of despair, apathy and hopelessness.

For finances, The 9 Of Swords Tarot card can represent feeling of being trapped in a situation from which there is no escape. It can symbolize a financial burden that cannot be overcome, giving rise to feelings of powerlessness and helplessness. There may be a sense of hopelessness about the future when it comes to money matters. It can also represent a lack of clarity about financial decisions and choices that need to be made.

For relationships, The 9 Of Swords Tarot card may signify difficulty in communication or a breakdown in relationships. It may represent feeling of isolation from others and difficulty in trusting others. The person may be wary of commitments or fearful to pursue a meaningful relationship. It can also suggest feelings of guilt and hurt that may lead to a sense of fear or insecurity in relationships.
