A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Judgment card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Judgment have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Judgment mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and The Judgment tarot card together mean it is time for you to go within for clarity and to make a final decision about your life’s purpose. It is a quest for personal truth and spiritual illumination and also a wake-up call, urging you to take a serious look at who you are and what you’re meant to do. The cards speak to the need to take some time for introspection and self-reflection, and to make the necessary changes to align yourself with your highest potential. To make a big decision, you must be willing to seek out and embrace all the wisdom within.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and Judgment mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health  
The Hermit Tarot card can be a reminder that health is often rooted in introspection. During times of physical, mental, and emotional stress, it can be beneficial to take time alone, away from distractions, to listen to the inner self, and to gain clarity on feelings, needs, and goals. By consciously and compassionately exploring one’s health and wellbeing, through meditation, journaling, or spending time in nature, it can be easier to manage stress, increase self-awareness, and create sustainable, healthy habits.

Finances  
The Hermit Tarot card encourages one to focus on the long-term goals of financial health and security. This card serves as a reminder to be wise with spending, invest in secure financial tools, and create a budget that aligns with one’s values. It also suggests taking some time away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life to calmly and thoughtfully assess finances and develop a plan for the future.

Relationships  
The Hermit Tarot card counsels that healthy relationships require mindful and honest communication. This card encourages us to take a moment to truly examine one’s relationships and discern their authenticity. True connections are based on mutual trust and respect, understanding, and appreciation. If one is feeling out of touch with a relationship, taking some time alone can help to clarify feelings and reassess priorities. The Hermit Tarot card serves as a reminder that by being honest and self-aware, we can create and sustain meaningful connections with others.

## What does Judgment mean?

It is a call to examine your lifestyle and make positive changes. This means eating a nutritious and balanced diet, getting regular exercise, and taking time to quiet the mind and focus on your physical and mental wellbeing. When it comes to health, The Judgment Tarot card encourages you to take responsibility for your own health and take charge of your body.

Finances: As with many aspects of life, the Judgment Tarot card is a wake-up call to take control of your finances. This means considering your financial goals, understanding where you are now, and creating a plan of action. The card encourages you to develop financial discipline and be mindful of how you spend and save money. This can include budgeting, setting aside money for retirement, and investing. When it comes to finances, the Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to make wise financial decisions and avoid succumbing to short-term spending.

Relationships: The Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to take time to reflect on your relationships and reevaluate what is most important to you. This card encourages you to prioritize meaningful relationships and let go of those that are no longer beneficial. The Judgment Tarot card is also a reminder to take responsibility for your own actions in relationships. The card encourages you to practice honest communication and to be mindful of how your words and actions impact others. The Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to treat your relationships with respect and to strive for meaningful connections.
