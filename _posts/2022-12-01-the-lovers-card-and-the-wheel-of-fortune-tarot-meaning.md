However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and The Wheel Of Fortune card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and The Wheel Of Fortune have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and The Wheel Of Fortune mean together?

The strength of two together creates the possibility for abundance, luck, and good luck, but only if you take that chance and make the right choices. The combination of these two tarot cards suggests that this relationship is a great opportunity for growth and success, and that it may take you to places you never imagined if you trust in it.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Lovers and The Wheel Of Fortune mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Lovers and The Wheel Of Fortune the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health

The Lovers Tarot card is a powerful symbol of balance, partnership and health. This card suggests that we take responsibility for our own health by nurturing ourselves, our partners and our friendships. It is a reminder of the power of the collective – that we are stronger together than we are apart. It encourages us to practice self-care and to establish healthy boundaries in our relationships so that we can take care of ourselves and our loved ones. It also suggests that we use our collective resources to support one another when it comes to health – whether it be mental, physical or emotional.

Finances

The Lovers Tarot card is a sign of financial security and growth. This card advises that we make wise investments, both in ourselves and others. It encourages us to practice financial responsibility and to create practical strategies for saving and investing that are beneficial for all involved. It also suggests that we use our resources to support each other and to build financial security by pooling our resources. This card is an encouraging reminder that when we work as a team, we can achieve great financial stability and success.

Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder that strong relationships require effort and dedication. This card advises us to cultivate and nurture our relationships. It encourages us to work together, communicate openly and be respectful of each other’s individual needs. It also suggests that we find ways to compromise and make compromises that benefit both parties. This card emphasizes the importance of trust, respect and honest communication in all our relationships. It reminds us of the power of connection and the strength that comes from being part of a team.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is an incredibly powerful card. It symbolizes possibility, change, and luck in all its forms. In readings, it often speaks of significant, sometimes unpredictable events that can dramatically alter the course of a person’s life. In the realm of health, it may indicate improved health, new treatments or therapies, or even the remission of a chronic illness. In the area of finances, it is a message to remain fluid and open to opportunities, while expecting good fortune to come your way. You may be poised to receive a financial windfall, or you may need to take risks in order to improve your financial standing. In terms of relationships, the Wheel of Fortune serves as a reminder that our relationships need to be nurtured, and that there are sometimes out-of-the-ordinary moments that can open up opportunities for deeper connection. It is an invitation to embrace change and stay flexible in your approach to relationship dynamics. Ultimately, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is an optimistic sign of spinning wheels, bringing with it the potential for both positive and negative experiences, but ultimately leading to greater good.
