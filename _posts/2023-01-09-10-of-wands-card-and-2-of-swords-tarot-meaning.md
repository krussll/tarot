A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and 2 Of Swords mean together?

The 10 of Wands tarot card and the 2 of Swords tarot card represent a difficult situation. You may feel overwhelmed by the number of tasks and responsibilities that you have taken on, and exhausted from the strain of keeping up with them all. The 2 of Swords tarot card suggests that it is time to make a decision or come to a compromise that will help you to manage the stress. By taking a step back and looking objectively at the situation, you can break down larger tasks into manageable chunks or delegate work to others. Taking on too much has put you in a difficult position, but the 2 of Swords reminds you that you still have the power to make a change.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 Of Wands Tarot card suggests that a person may experience stress and exhaustion when it comes to their health, finances and relationships. It indicates that too many burdens and obligations can pull a person in too many directions, leaving them overwhelmed and feeling as though they are drowning in obligations.

When it comes to health, The 10 Of Wands Tarot card can suggest that the individual experiences physical strain from worrying about too many things at one time, leading to exhaustion and burnout. It can even suggest physical ailments such as headaches, stomachaches, backaches and insomnia due to the weight of the stress.

When it comes to finances, The 10 Of Wands Tarot card can suggest that too many burdens and too many obligations are making it difficult for the individual to make ends meet. They may be struggling to keep up with all their bills and debts, leading to a state of financial chaos and anxiety.

And when it comes to relationships, The 10 Of Wands Tarot card can suggest that too many obligations can cause an individual to neglect the people they care about and the relationships they value. It can suggest that a person may not be able to make the time for their loved ones due to the sheer amount of work and obligations they feel they need to juggle. This can lead to feelings of guilt, frustration and resentment on both sides of the relationship.

The 10 Of Wands Tarot card warns that too many burdens and too many obligations can cause an individual to feel overwhelmed, exhausted and pulled in too many directions. It suggests that it is important to take a step back from the situation and prioritize what is truly important in life, in order to gain a sense of balance and perspective.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 2 of Swords Tarot card indicates that it is time to look at our health objectively and make a compromise. It could represent a difficult decision between western and alternative medicine, a certain diet or lifestyle, or even two competing medical opinions. We have to balance our own intuition, advise of health professionals and the common sense of our own decision making. This is a time to come up with a compromise that works best for us in order to maintain our physical and mental health.

Finances: The 2 of Swords Tarot card can also be interpreted as a need to make a difficult decision around finances. It is time to balance our needs and wants, and to make a compromise that works best for us in our current financial situation. We have to look honestly at what resources we have and be realistic about our current limits. This card can represent the need to make timely payments, cutting back on spending or even deciding to invest in something that is a risk but that can bring about positive financial growth.

Relationships: The 2 of Swords Tarot card also points to needing to make a compromise in our relationships. It can signify that it is time to make a decision between two opposing opinions or different paths, and to come to a mutual understanding. We need to listen to the needs and wants of all parties involved, and come to an agreement that works for everyone. It requires us to step away from ego and defensiveness, and to look at the bigger picture. This is a time to look honestly at our relationships and make decisions with clear understanding and heart-centered intention.
