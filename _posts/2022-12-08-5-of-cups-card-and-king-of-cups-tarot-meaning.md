## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: The Five of Cups represents a period of health when one has experienced loss, grief, or deep sadness. It may symbolize a physical or emotional illness, or a physical symptom such as fatigue, depression, or a weakened immune system. It is a reminder to take care of your body, mind, and soul and focus on self-care.

Finances: The Five of Cups in terms of finances signals a period of discouragement, sadness, and disappointment. You may be struggling to find financial stability and you may feel as though any money earned quickly goes back out. It is a reminder to take a step back and evaluate your financial situation. Make sure you are not staying in situations of financial loss, and look for ways to develop positive financial habits and become more secure.

Relationships: The Five of Cups in terms of relationships can represent a time when we feel unloved, abandoned, hurt, and heartbroken. It can also suggest a need for communication and understanding between two people. This is a time to explore our own needs and be honest about what we need from others. Speak up, ask for help, and take time to repair broken relationships.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health  
The King of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a balanced and harmonious approach to health. This card speaks to the importance of tending to mental and physical health. The King of Cups signifies that the individual should seek to maintain a balanced lifestyle by focusing on healthy eating, exercise, and stress relief. It represents a willingness to accept inner experiences, both positive and negative, without judgement. The card encourages individuals to be mindful of their physical and mental health, and suggests that they should seek out professional help when needed.

Finances  
The King of Cups Tarot card emphasizes the importance of wise financial decisions. On a symbolic level, this card reflects taking a measured and levelheaded approach to finances. The King of Cups encourages individuals to approach financial decisions with a high degree of care and thoughtfulness. It cautions against impulsive decisions related to money. The King of Cups also speaks to the importance of assessing one’s finances with the help of professionals and experts from time to time.

Relationships  
The King of Cups tarot card reflects the need for tolerance, understanding, and empathy in relationships. It encourages individuals to be open-minded and curious in their interactions with others. The King of Cups speaks to the importance of accepting others, despite the presence of differences or disagreements. It also speaks to the need for a sense of balance and harmony in relationships. The King of Cups cautions that one should take a careful approach to relationship conflicts. It encourages individuals to take the time to listen to one another and to explore solutions that could lead to a positive outcome.
