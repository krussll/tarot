A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Moon card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Moon card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Moon and King Of Cups mean together?

When The Moon tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card appear in a reading together, they urge you to trust your intuition and to take a leap of faith towards reaching your goals. The Moon card warns of unseen problems that may arise but The King Of Cups assures you that you are supported and you will be able to weather any storm. The King Of Cups encourages you to show tolerance and empathy towards yourself and others during this challenging time, and to be a supportive, comforting leader to yourself and those around you. The combination of the two cards suggests that in the face of any challenge, you should listen to your inner wisdom and make decisions with a clear, compassionate head.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

For Health:  
The Moon Tarot card is often associated with health, as it represents unseen problems or forces that are at play in one’s life. This could mean physical health issues that are not immediately apparent or subtle changes in the body that can surface without warning. In terms of physical health, The Moon Tarot card can be a sign that there is a need to pay more attention to one’s diet and exercise habits. It can also be an indication that stress levels need to be managed and checked for any underlying issues. In these cases, trust your intuition and pay attention to the small signs that may come from the body.

For Finances:  
The Moon Tarot card is associated with changes in the financial realm, which can result in either positive or negative outcomes. The card may signify a need to reassess existing financial arrangements or evaluate new investment opportunities. It can also be a warning sign that changes are needed in order to ensure financial stability. In this case, once again trust your intuition as it may give you an indication of what needs to be done in order to take care of your finances.

For Relationships:  
The Moon Tarot card is also associated with relationships and can indicate that voluntary changes are required in order to make the relationship stronger. This could mean taking a step back in order to gain a new perspective on the relationship, or discovering new interests or activities to enjoy with one’s partner. It can also be a warning sign that underlying problems are present in the relationship, and that more effort is required to make the relationship work. Here, too, following one’s intuition can be a key part of the process.
