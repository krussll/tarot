A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and The Devil card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and The Devil have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and The Devil mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card and The Devil tarot card together emphasize the importance of self-reflection and making wise choices. The Hanged Man encourages us to get a different perspective, to consider things from a higher point of view. The Devil card cautions us against making decisions based on materialism, greed, envy, and addiction. Together, they suggest that in order to make wise decisions, we must recognize the influence of these forces within us and strive to be guided by our own inner wisdom.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Hanged Man and The Devil mean together for your love life?

The Hanged Man tarot card and The Devil tarot card can be a warning message to couples, as they suggest a certain imbalance in your love life. When these two cards together indicate a power struggle between the two of you, it is essential to not get stuck in the same rigid positions. The Devil card can signify an imbalance of power that can cause you to become obsessed with someone. Your love life can become a vicious and destructive cycle, where one person dominates and dominates the other, leading to resentment and dissatisfaction.

On the other hand, the Hanged Man card means that you have the power to choose to step away and gain a new perspective. This card suggests that you understand the importance of self sacrifice; for example, by taking time out of your busy life to focus on the relationship and to value your partner as an equal. This can lead to a better connection between you both and a more harmonious relationship overall.

The Devil tarot card speaks of an unhealthy obsession with materialism and greed. It also reminds us to be aware of how our materialistic desires can affect our relationships. It is important to remember that money, power, and status should never be a priority in a relationship. Instead, it is important to focus on meeting the needs of your partner and finding ways to nurture and express love for one another.

By combining the meaning of the Hanged Man tarot card and The Devil tarot card in your love life, it is possible to find a balance between material and spiritual needs. It is also important to remember that self sacrifice and gaining perspective is key to creating a healthy and fulfilling relationship. If you can practice self sacrifice and understanding, then your relationship will have the potential to reach a new level of strength and contentment.

### What does The Hanged Man and The Devil mean together for your finances?

When considering the combination of The Hanged Man tarot card and The Devil tarot card in relation to finances and job, what comes to mind is the idea of maintaining balance. The Hanged Man tarot card urges the reader to take a different perspective and to think outside the box. We can apply this to our approach to money, and use this wisdom to inform our financial decisions. Self-sacrifice is also an element of this card, suggesting that disciplined decisions are in order when it comes to managing our financial lives — rather than quickly spending money on fleeting pleasures.

The Devil tarot card is a reminder of the pitfalls that can come with an obsession with material gain and wealth. Greed, envy and an overindulgence in material gain can have a negative effect on our finances and can lead to risky behavior, such as overspending or investment. Similarly, unhealthy habits such as addiction can have disastrous consequences for our financial well-being.

Thus, when it comes to managing our finances and jobs, it is important to heed the lessons of both The Hanged Man and The Devil tarot cards. To find balance, it is crucial to maintain a healthy work-life balance and to have realistic expectations when it comes to wealth. By being mindful of the dangers of greed, addiction, and envy, we can consciously avoid the traps of materialism and instead focus our energies on finding lasting success.

The Hanged Man tarot card and The Devil tarot card together can symbolize an unhealthy balance in our lives. Too often, we get lost in the petty dramas and worries of our immediate environment, neglecting our health and well-being in the pursuit of material comforts. The Devil tarot card urges us to recognize the negative perils of greed, envy, and obsession, and the warning that indulging these desires too often can draw us into dangerous, addictive habits. The Hanged Man tarot card, on the other hand, expresses the idea of looking at life from a different perspective, not just focused on the here and now, but rather with a long-term view that recognizes the importance of self-care and self-sacrifice.

When these two tarot cards appear together in a reading, they point towards a need to strike a balance between immediate gratification and long-term health. Too much indulgence in material comforts and pleasure can be damaging to our physical, mental, and spiritual health, but too much self-denial can lead to frustration, depression, and unhappiness. We must learn to find the middle ground, where we are able to enjoy the material comforts of life, but still prioritize our health and well-being over everything else.

The combination of The Hanged Man and The Devil tarot cards serves as a reminder that our highest calling is to become our true selves, free from the trappings of consumer culture and the ebb and flow of material comforts. We must remember that our physical and mental health is far more important than any physical possession, and take the steps needed to achieve a healthy balance. Only by striking this balance can we fully realize our potential and experience true fulfillment in life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hanged Man and The Devil mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hanged Man and The Devil the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

For Health:  
The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages us to pause, reexamine and make sure we’re taking care of our physical and mental health. It tells us to be mindful and make sure we’re making choices that work best for our well-being. The card also suggests that we may need to make some sacrifices in order to achieve a healthier balance. It suggests that we take a step back and look at the big picture to ensure that our decisions are in line with our long-term goals. The Hanged Man reminds us that it’s okay to slow down and take care of ourselves, even if it involves sacrificing something in the short-term.

For Finances:  
The Hanged Man Tarot card serves as a warning to be cautious when it comes to financial decisions. It tells us to think twice before investing or spending money. The card suggests that rather than rushing into a decision, we take time to consider the consequences of our choice and its implications for our finances. The Hanged Man also encourages us to adopt a more creative approach to finances. Rather than relying on conventional methods, consider a range of options and think outside the box.

For Relationships:  
The Hanged Man Tarot card asks us to maintain a good balance in our relationships. It encourages us to remain open-minded and be willing to try new approaches to make our relationships work. The card also reminds us to take responsibility for our own actions and look within to see how we can play a role in improving our relationships. It suggests that we may need to set healthy boundaries or make some sacrifices in order to create a harmonious relationship. Ultimately, The Hanged Man Tarot card reminds us to look for the best outcome for all involved and to remain open to new ways of doing things.

## What does The Devil mean?

Health  
The Devil Tarot card often symbolizes the dangers of addiction and obsession, which can manifest in a variety of unhealthy habits. Addiction to substances, such as drugs and alcohol, can have destructive consequences for physical and mental health. Similarly, unhealthy obsessions, such as an obsession with food or exercise, can lead to eating disorders or even physical injuries. In addition, when the Devil appears in a Tarot reading, it can indicate that a person is struggling with a psychological disorder such as depression or anxiety.

Finances  
The Devil Tarot card is also associated with financial struggles. The card suggests that someone is being controlled by their desires and is unable to manage their finances responsibly. This could be due to impulsive spending, gambling or borrowing money they can’t afford to pay back. The card is a warning that their reckless behavior could have serious consequences for their finances and relationships.

Relationships  
When The Devil Tarot card appears in a Tarot reading, it suggests that someone is struggling with unhealthy obsessions in relationships. They may be suffering from a lack of boundaries or have difficulty expressing themselves in a healthy way. They may be jealous, possessive or manipulative in their interactions with others, causing mistrust and resentment. The card also implies a tendency to form powerful and unhealthy addictions to sex, power or material possessions. It warns that these obsessions could lead to the destruction of even the strongest relationships.
