## The Tower And Health – An Overview

The Tower tarot card is a reminder to us that health can be fragile and that unexpected changes can cause upset and disruption in our lives.

This card is a warning to take care of ourselves, pay attention to our bodies, and be mindful of our habits.

It is a reminder to be ready for the unexpected, to be prepared for sudden shifts, and to be aware of how quickly our lives can be thrown into upheaval.

The Tower can indicate that poor health, or unexpected health issues, could arise and that caution must be taken to prevent such issues from becoming a long-lasting problem.

The Tower also suggests that, in order to maintain good health, we must be willing to accept and cope with change, and to be open to making the necessary adjustments to keep ourselves healthy and well.

The Tower can also indicate that someone may suffer a great loss or ruin in their health, such as a long-term disability or suffering from a chronic condition.

The Tower warns that any upheaval in one’s health is likely to bring great distress and can be difficult to handle.

To face such a significant change in health, it is important to be prepared and take measures to protect one’s wellbeing.
