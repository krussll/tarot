A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and The World card together in particular.

The Justice card and The World have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and The World mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The World tarot card together symbolize success, completion, and new beginnings. The combination suggests that after taking action to resolve a conflict or situation, you can now reap the rewards. This could mean that you have achieved justice, or that you have arrived at a win-win solution for both parties. It also suggests that the truth has been revealed, which is necessary for true closure. In addition, The World card signals the completion of a journey, goal, or lesson, and that you now have the opportunity to emigrate to a new level, or to travel to a new destination. It is a time of moving forward and of embracing the new possibilities that await you.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and The World mean together for your love life?

When it comes to love, the combination of the Justice and The World tarot cards can be interpreted as strong recognition that the truth will set you free. In many relationships, the truth can sometimes be difficult to face. With this card combination, the underlying message is that difficult truths will have to be confronted but this will ultimately lead to win-win solutions and the successful completion of an important journey.

This journey may take the form of a couple learning to better understand each other or admitting to a mistake and facing the consequences. With the Justice card comes the understanding that something must be done in order to rectify any misdeeds. In many relationships, this is a difficult step to take but it is always essential to move forward.

With the World card comes the reward; the successful completion of a difficult journey. This could be the end of a difficult phase in a relationship or the understanding that it is time to make a change. The combination of these two cards suggests that while the truth may be difficult to face, it ultimately leads to a positive outcome.

In short, the marriage of Justice and The World tarot cards in your love life stands for facing difficult truths and learning from them, in order to find win-win solutions and complete an important journey. In the end, this opens the door to a brighter, more fulfilling future.

### What does Justice and The World mean together for your finances?

When these two tarot cards come together in a reading, it is an indication that the querent is entering a period of their life that is full of possibility. The Justice card speaks to the concepts of cause and effect, finding win-win solutions, and discovering the truth. This suggests that the querent is in the process of making an educated decision about their finances and job.

The World card references the idea of completing one’s goal or lesson, and being ready to move onto the next stage in life. It is a time of transition, travel and emigration if necessary. Both of these tarot cards suggest that the querent is ready to close one chapter of their life and can now begin to work towards a more secure financial future.

It is likely that the querent’s current circumstances are making it difficult to find a job that will meet their needs and provide them with the lifestyle they desire. In these cases, the Justice tarot card advises that the querent can experience success and prosperity by taking a hard look at their current situation. By researching their options and considering all angles of the situation, the querent can make a wise decision that will allow them to move forward.

The World tarot card encourages the querent to approach their finances and job with an open mind. If the querent is willing to consider all possibilities, and take the necessary steps to make their goals a reality, they will be able to create a financial future that is both secure and fulfilling.

When the Justice and World tarot cards come together in a reading, the querent is being assured that now is the time to take action and make lasting changes in their financial situation. By discovering the truth, finding win-win solutions, and completing their goals, the querent can work towards a future full of possibility.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and The World mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card can be a reminder to practice good health habits. It indicates a need to balance and equalize the two sides of health – physical and mental. It speaks of the need for decision-making and awareness of the consequences of our actions. On the physical side, the card encourages the importance of healthy eating, exercise, proper rest and relaxation, and taking care of our bodies in order to promote good health. On the mental side, it speaks of learning from our mistakes, making wise decisions and taking responsibility for our health.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card encourages one to maintain transparency and fairness, in terms of financial matters. It speaks of understanding the consequences of all financial decisions, and being cognizant of the implications and effects that each decision will have. It also speaks of making ethical decisions in financial matters, and not taking advantage of others’ weaknesses. The Justice card also encourages balance and moderation when it comes to money, and not to overspend or make hasty decisions.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card encourages fairness and justice in all relationships, be they romantic, platonic or professional. It speaks of the importance of equality, truth and respect in all relationships, and being honest and open with one another. It also encourages one to take responsibility for their actions, and to take the time to reflect and learn from their mistakes. It also speaks of making decisions with compassion and understanding and not taking advantage of others. The Justice card encourages one to practice strong communication skills and not to jump too quickly to assumptions or conclusions.

## What does The World mean?

Health: The World Tarot card symbolizes the completion of a cycle in physical health. To interpret the card into a health related meaning, it can refer to the completion of a journey to one’s desired health goal. The card can also signify a successful outcome of a treatment or rehabilitation of a chronic condition. It can refer to the attainment of a balanced lifestyle: where one has achieved mastery over their mind, body, and soul.

Finances: The World Tarot card symbolizes the attainment of financial stability and success. To interpret the card into a finance related meaning, it can refer to the completion of a goal to reach an ideal financial state. The card can also signify the successful conclusion of a business deal, investment, or project. It can refer to the completion of a financial cycle- the ending of a period of struggle and the beginning of a period of prosperity.

Relationships: The World Tarot card symbolizes the achievement of emotional stability and completion. To interpret the card into a relationship related meaning, it can refer to the successful conclusion of a period of growth and growth in a relationship. The card can also signify the attainment of harmony and balance between two people. It can refer to the completion of a cycle where relationships have been mended and strengthened, allowing two souls to joyfully move forward together.
