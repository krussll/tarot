A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The 6 of Wands and The 6 of Swords tarot cards suggest that you are coming to a successful end in one area of life, and may be awarded recognition for your efforts. This success may be a precursor to a necessary transition or relocation, which you may be considering or have already begun. This change in circumstances may be managed with confidence thanks to your recent success, and you can look forward to the new opportunities and challenges that await you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 6 of Wands Tarot card can indicate a good time for health. It suggests that minor or ongoing illnesses will be overcome and that you will experience an overall improvement in your well-being. This card can also indicate that you will receive advice about the best way to take care of yourself and reach your optimal health. It could be a physical therapist, a nutritionist, or a spiritual mentor.

Finances

The 6 of Wands Tarot card can indicate a time of financial success. You may receive extra money or rewards, such as a promotion or bonus, that will help you secure a better financial future. It could also be a sign of good luck when it comes to investing. You may have an opportunity to invest in a business venture that could have great potential for growth.

Relationships

The 6 of Wands Tarot card suggests that your relationships will be positive and successful. People will recognize and appreciate your efforts and you will be able to build successful relationships with those around you. This card can also suggest that you may find a partner who shares your goals and ambitions. Overall, the vibes from this card are positive and successful.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords Tarot card has many interpretations in the different contexts in which it appears. In the context of health, the six of swords indicates that an individual is leaving behind a stagnating or unhealthy environment that has been having a negative effect on their well-being in order to seek out a healthier situation. This can be in the form of a physical relocation, such as leaving a toxic relationship or atmosphere, or it can be a mental shift. It suggests that even though the journey towards a healthier future may be difficult, it is necessary and will ultimately bring more positivity and clarity.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Swords can indicate a period of transition and change, as the individual decides to make a shift towards a more financially secure and stable future. This could be in the form of taking on a new job, entering a new field, or taking calculated risks in order to achieve greater financial gain. This card can also suggest a need to be aware of scams and other financial pitfalls that can arise during this period of transition.

When considering relationships, the 6 of Swords can suggest that an individual is transitioning away from an unhealthy or toxic situation. This could be in the form of leaving a bad relationship or moving on from an unfulfilling one. In this context, it suggests that it is important to recognize when it is time to move on, and that the individual should trust their intuition as they move into a new, healthier and more fulfilling relationship.
