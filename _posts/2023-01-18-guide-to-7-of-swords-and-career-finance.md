Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The 7 Of Swords card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 7 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 7 Of Swords.

## 7 Of Swords And Career And Finances – An Overview

The 7 of Swords tarot card is a warning about health that suggests there may be a risk of neglect or betrayal.

It is a reminder that it is important to be aware of our physical and mental health, and to take steps to protect it.

This card is a warning to look out for any signs of dishonesty or lack of commitment on our part, or anyone else’s, when it comes to taking care of our health.

It may also be a warning to look out for signs of running away from our health issues – a sign that we may be avoiding facing our problems head-on or taking responsibility for our health.

The 7 of Swords encourages us to be honest and open with ourselves, and to protect our health at all costs.

## Upright Meanings For 7 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The 7 of Swords tarot card is a warning that there may be some dishonesty and betrayal in my career and finances.

It could mean that someone is taking advantage of me or is stealing from me.

It could also be a warning to be wary of those I trust or to be careful when making decisions about my career and finances.

It could even be a sign that I need to run away from a situation or to be more careful with my money.

In any case, this card is a reminder to always be cautious in my career and finances and to look out for any signs of dishonesty or betrayal.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 Of Swords tarot card is a sign that my future career and finances may be affected by dishonesty and betrayal.

This could mean that I should be wary of people who may be trying to take advantage of me or take my money without permission.

It’s important to stay aware of any potential theft or betrayal, as it could have a detrimental effect on my career and financial success.

Additionally, this card could imply that I need to be prepared to take risks, but also take necessary precautions in order to ensure that I don’t fall prey to any dishonesty or thievery.

Lastly, this card suggests that it could be beneficial to be wary of running away from any potential challenges that may arise in my career or finances.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 Of Swords tarot card suggests that the careers and finances of those close to me are vulnerable to theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and running away.

This card could mean that someone close to me is facing an uncertain financial future due to an unexpected financial setback or a dishonest business partner.

It could also mean that a close one is feeling the effects of a recent betrayal or is dealing with the consequences of a dishonest decision.

Whatever the case may be, it is important to stay vigilant and take steps to protect oneself from further loss or damage.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When the reversed 7 of Swords appears in a reading related to career and finances, it is an indication that one should be careful of deceptive practices and dishonesty.

It can indicate that someone is trying to take advantage of you financially or professionally and that it is important to be aware of any duplicitous behavior that may be taking place.

The reversed 7 of Swords can also indicate that you may have to fight for the recognition and financial gain you deserve.

It is a reminder to stay true to yourself, no matter what challenges may come your way.

The reversed 7 of Swords is a warning to take extra precautions when it comes to money.

You may be tempted to take shortcuts or take risks that could put you in a precarious financial situation.

You should be mindful of any shady or unethical financial offers that come your way and make sure to do your due diligence before getting involved.

The reversed 7 of Swords can indicate that it is time to look for more reliable and trustworthy sources of income and to focus on building up your financial security.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed 7 of Swords card appears in a reading about your future career and finances, it suggests that you have the potential to achieve success in your career but you may have to overcome certain obstacles first.

You may find yourself in a precarious situation where you may be tempted to take shortcuts or resort to dishonesty or betrayal to get ahead.

This card cautions you to remain honest and ethical in your business dealings as dishonesty will only lead to trouble in the long run.

The reversed 7 of Swords also hints at a need to take a careful and calculated approach to your finances.

This card suggests that there may be risks involved in your financial decisions and investments.

You may feel the urge to take advantage of quick money-making schemes but they could end up costing you in the long run.

The reversed 7 of Swords encourages you to take your time and think things through before taking any actions.

This card also encourages you to be open-minded and creative when it comes to financial matters to ensure you are making the best decisions for your future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The 7 Of Swords is reversed, it indicates that those close to me are surrounded by honesty and integrity when it comes to their careers and finances.

There is no stealing, betrayal, or dishonesty present in their lives and they are able to feel secure and safe when making decisions and taking risks related to their career and finances.

They have the courage to take risks and are not afraid of failure, as they understand that failure is a necessary part of success.

They are comfortable with taking a calculated risk, and are able to honor their commitments and obligations.

This allows them to make wise decisions that will bring them closer to their goals.

Overall, they are able to be honest and trustworthy when it comes to their financial and career decisions and this is something that will benefit them in the long run.
