A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Wheel Of Fortune card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Wheel Of Fortune card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card and The 8 Of Cups tarot card together symbolize a turning point in your life, where luck and good fortune will be changing your current situation. This could mean that you are ready to take a risk, open yourself up to new opportunities, and accept the potential consequences of rolling the dice. It could also signify a time of withdrawal, retreat, and leaving certain aspects of your life behind in order to move forward. Regardless of what the cards mean in combination, it is up to you to decide how to use this understanding in a meaningful and productive way.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 8 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a time of retreat and withdrawal from physical health concerns. This could be a card associated with taking a break from excessive physical health regimes and instead focusing on the mental and spiritual aspects of health. This could be a time for healing and for restoring balance in the body, or it could represent a need to step back from something that has become overwhelming, such as an overly restrictive diet. During this period, it’s important to focus on rest and recovery, as well as activities that bring joy and relaxation.

Finances

The 8 of Cups Tarot might signify a need to withdraw from a financial situation that is starting to become too overwhelming or unsustainable. This could mean doing a thorough analysis of your current situation and taking steps to reign in spending and reduce debt. Alternatively, it could point to the need to take stock of investments and to shift away from a strategy that is no longer working. This is a time of retreat and letting go, and a reminder that financial security can come from living within our means.

Relationships

The 8 of Cups Tarot could indicate a time of withdrawal from a relationship that is no longer working. This could be a time for reevaluating one’s expectations and requirements from a relationship and deciding if it is worth the effort to continue or it might be time for a separation. It could also represent a time for retreat and self-reflection, using any lessons learned from the experience to inform the future. This period of reflection is an important step to taking care of one’s own needs and prioritizing personal growth.
