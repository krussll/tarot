## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Emperor mean?

The Emperor Tarot card symbolizes the need to tap into the power of authority and leadership in order to successfully navigate all areas of life, from health and finances to relationships. The energy of this card is often seen as being stern and protective, with its imagery of a seated figure wearing armor and a large crown. The Emperor is a reminder of the importance of strong, decisive leadership and the strategic planning needed to remain successful and in control of one’s destiny.

The Emperor card speaks to health in the sense that it encourages the use of strong, authoritative approaches when it comes to maintaining and improving overall wellness. This might involve actively taking charge of one’s health, such as scheduling regular check-ups, adopting a healthier lifestyle, or focusing on preventative measures. Additionally, the Emperor card serves as a reminder to stay focused on the end goal and to accept and embrace the challenges that come along with striving for better health.

In regards to finances and money, this card serves as a reminder to stay disciplined and to practice smart money management. The Emperor encourages a long-term approach, such as saving and investing money, rather than simply spending it on impulse buys. He also advises to research and study the best strategies for success and to take the time to assess each option and pick the best one in order to avoid any financial pitfalls.

On the relationship spectrum, The Emperor serves as a reminder to lead from an authoritative position. This means taking into account all perspectives and remaining open to different points of view, while at the same time remaining firm and confident in one’s own beliefs and principles. This is also a reminder to remain assertive and to not be afraid to enforce boundaries with others in order to maintain healthy relationships.

Overall, The Emperor Tarot card speaks to the need for strong leadership and strategic planning in order to have balance and success in all aspects of life, from health, finances and relationships. Ultimately, this card serves as a reminder to stay focused on the goal, to stay disciplined and take charge of one’s destiny.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is a warning of ill health, financial worry, and isolation. It is a message of caution, urging us to take care of our physical, mental, and financial health.

In terms of health, this card is a reminder that taking care of ourselves must be a priority. It is important to ensure our physical and mental well-being is in good shape, as this could be affected by financial troubles. In order to prevent a spiral into illness, we should practice self-care, get plenty of rest, and eat well.

In terms of finances, this card is a warning sign of financial loss and worry. We should take care to budget sensibly, and always be mindful of our spending. It is not wise to take unnecessary risks with our finances, as this could quickly lead to a downward spiral. We should also keep track of our investments and be mindful of any changes in our financial situation.

Finally, The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is also a reminder of the importance of relationships when dealing with difficult financial times. Although the concept of isolation is prominent in the card, it is important to remember that we do not have to go through this time alone. We should turn to our friends and family for support, and ensure we have a strong support network to rely on if needed.

In short, The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card serves as a reminder of the importance of taking care of our physical and financial health. It is a warning sign of potential trouble and isolation, so it is important to look after ourselves and practice self-care in order to stay healthy and financially secure. Additionally, it is important to remember that we do not have to go through this difficult time alone, and that our family and friends can be there to provide us with love and support if needed.
