## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The card urges caution and foresight when making decisions around your health, as it indicates a major change is coming in that area. The Ace of Swords is indicative of new therapies, treatments, or surgeries that may help aid in healing. This card may be an indication of a new beginning in a relationship, or an opportunity to gain a deeper understanding of yourself and your partner. ## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

The 5 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes many different types of personal and interpersonal issues. In terms of health, this card represents a lack of self-care and denial of difficult emotions. People who draw this card may be struggling with the symptoms of an illness or feeling overwhelmed by setbacks in their personal health journey. Physically, this card may suggest that a person has not been taking care of their body in a healthy way, as it can be a warning sign of addiction and/or self-destructive behaviors.

In terms of finances, the 5 of Swords Tarot card may symbolize financial hardship, dishonesty, and even theft. When a person draws this card, they may be dealing with debts or have suffered a major financial loss. This card can also be a warning sign of fraud or dishonesty, such as someone manipulating the financial system to their own advantage.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the 5 of Swords Tarot card can represent disagreements, bullying, and power struggles. It can represent abusive relationships or even physical violence. This card can also signify arguments, as it suggests that a person is not compromising with one another and is instead intent on getting their own way. People may also draw this card if they are being manipulative in their relationships or using guilt to control others. The 5 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of a person who is struggling to establish healthy boundaries and respect the autonomy of others.
