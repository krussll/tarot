Lets take a closer look at The High Priestess.

## The High Priestess And Sprituality – An Overview

The High Priestess tarot card is a reminder that health is a complex, mysterious matter.

It encourages us to rely on our intuition to make decisions about our health, trusting that our instinct will lead us to make the right choices.

We should pay attention to subtle messages from our bodies, as they can hold important clues about our health.

The High Priestess also encourages us to keep our health secrets to ourselves, which may include not divulging too much about our health on social media or other public platforms.

In doing so, we can protect ourselves from unwanted judgment or criticism, allowing us to trust our own wisdom and make decisions that are best for our overall health and wellbeing.

## Reversed Meanings For The High Priestess And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

In the reversed position, The High Priestess tarot card is a warning to me to not ignore the inner wisdom of my intuition.

It is a reminder to take a step back and trust what my gut tells me, even if the answer seems counterintuitive.

This card is a reminder to take the time to really listen to my intuition and inner wisdom.

By being open, I can tap into the collective wisdom of others and gain a deeper understanding of my spirituality.
