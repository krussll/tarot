## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 5 of Wands Tarot card signifies that health might come into play in the form of an obstacle or challenge. Health issues could arise and require extra effort and work to overcome. This card encourages you to focus on yourself and to actively work towards a positive outcome. It encourages meditation and physical activity as a way to heal and maintain balance and physical health.

Finances

The 5 of Wands Tarot card means that financial issues could present themselves as difficulties. This could mean either that there are obstacles in the way of achieving financial goals or that the road to financial success requires hard work and effort. The card encourages you to stay focused and to develop a plan to achieve financial stability and prosperity. It encourages caution in financial decisions and making sure that you are fully informed before making any large investments or taking on too much financial risk.

Relationships

The 5 of Wands Tarot card means that relationships, both romantic and friendships, could be a source of challenge and obstacles. Relationships could be strained by arguments and disagreements that require extra effort to overcome and maintain. This card encourages you to take the time to truly understand what values the other person in the relationship has and to try and find ways to bridge those differences. It suggests that working together to achieve a common goal and reinforcing shared values can help to strengthen a relationship.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen Of Wands Tarot card brings many positive attributes to one’s life. In terms of health, it can represent a strong sense of self-care, an unwavering commitment to personal well-being and physical activity. This Queen’s strength of will and self-assuredness allows her to take control of her health and make healthy choices, both mentally and physically. It can also point to a resilience in the face of adversity, signifying the individual’s ability to remain focused and maintain their sense of purpose throughout challenging times.

When it comes to finances, the Queen Of Wands Tarot card signifies abundance and success. By utilizing the Queen’s unwavering confidence, one can achieve their financial goals with ease. She represents a mature attitude toward money and financial matters, allowing those with her influence to confidently handle their wealth in a responsible and effective manner. This Queen also stands for the courage and enthusiasm to take risks in order to make financial progress, knowing that the reward will be worth it in the end.

Finally, in relationships, the Queen Of Wands Tarot card signifies strength, self-assurance and loyalty. She is a leader, not a follower, and will not be easily swayed to accept the beliefs of her partner without considering her own opinion first. She is passionate, yet holds onto her values, and is willing to listen to the other’s point of view. Her strong will allows her to remain committed to those she loves, and her confidence allows her to express her emotions clearly, calmly and without judgement. She is a faithful friend who is always loyal and unwavering in her support of others.
