A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card together suggest a difficult time in your life. The Hermit tarot card speaks of a need to go within for clarity and a quest for personal truth, and this can manifest as feelings of isolation or a need for spiritual illumination. The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card speaks of a period of ill health, poverty, worry, isolation, and financial loss. It is important to remember that these difficulties can be seen as an opportunity to go within and discover your true self. Going within can bring greater understanding and lead to spiritual illumination, allowing you to move forward in life with greater strength, resilience, and courage.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 5 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card is a powerful message to take time for ourselves and our own personal well-being. A time of inner-reflection that can allow us to gain insight into our health and overall wellbeing. Taking the time to really examine our physical, mental and emotional health can help us to recognize and address areas in our lives that may need some extra attention and care. Seeking out advice from a medical or mental health professional may also be of great benefit to gaining clarity on our overall health and wellbeing.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card implies a period of reflection, which can be beneficial to gaining clarity and understanding when it comes to our finances. It is important to take the time to understand our spending habits and to create a budget that works with our current financial means. We can look at our savings and investments and become aware of what is working and what may need to be adjusted in order to reach our financial goals. Keeping track of our monthly expenses and revisiting this from time to time can also benefit us during our process of gaining financial clarity and success.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card reminds us that taking a step back and examining our relationships can be a beneficial and powerful practice. Having an honest and open conversation with our partners or friends about how we’re feeling, what we need and want from our relationship, and how we want to work together to meet these needs can be a great opportunity for relationship growth. It can also be beneficial to take some time to ourselves to go within and examine our own thoughts, feelings and expectations of our relationships. We may find clarity in understanding our patterns and behaviors in our relationships and how we can better approach them in the future.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

When the 5 of Pentacles tarot card appears, it is a sign that physical and mental health, financial stability, and relationships may all be suffering. Those who draw this card may feel ill, distant, or cut off from their support systems. It can be a difficult time spiritually and emotionally, with a sense of loneliness and isolation.

Physical health can be greatly impacted by the 5 of Pentacles. The card warns of physical illness, fatigue, injury, or illness stemming from stress. Stress can also manifest in other ways such as depression or chronic illness. When this card appears, it is important to pay attention to the body and its needs and to reach out for help if necessary.

The 5 of Pentacles can also indicate financial losses, debts, or poverty. The financial situation may seem bleak, and it can leave those affected feeling hopeless and uncertain. It is important to find ways to cope with financial struggles, and to seek advice if needed.

Relationships can suffer in times of financial trouble. The 5 of Pentacles can cause friends and family to feel distant and alienation can take over. Jealousy and resentment may also arise. This can be debilitating, and it is important to reach out to loved ones and find ways to work on the relationships.

The 5 of Pentacles carries a warning that health and finances are at risk and that relationships may be strained. Ultimately, it is important to take care of the body, mind, and spirit during this time and to reach out for support when necessary.
