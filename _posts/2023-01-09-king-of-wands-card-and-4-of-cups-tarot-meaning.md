A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the King Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The King Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does King Of Wands and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The King Of Wands tarot card and The 4 Of Cups tarot card together signify a leader who has strength and power, with the potential to charm and courageously take on any challenge. But, they may also be feeling a deepening sense of apathy, unfulfilled – as though something is missing. It could be that they are in need of surprise gifts or blessings to help bring back their enthusiasm and motivate them to move forward with their projects.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as King Of Wands and 4 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 4 of Cups Tarot card suggests a feeling of apathy towards health and wellness. You may be lacking motivation and not feeling excited about your physical health. A lack of enthusiasm could be present when it comes to exercise, nutrition, and other self-care activities. Instead of viewing your healthy habits as a positive, energizing experience, you may be feeling like it’s just a chore. The card encourages you to look at health and wellness in a new light and try to approach it from a different angle.

Finances

The 4 of Cups Tarot card indicates a lack of enthusiasm when it comes to financial goals. You may be feeling unmotivated to take steps forward in your financial life, or even feeling blocked from taking any action at all. It could also mean that you are not noticing the gifts available to you – the opportunities to increase your financial wellbeing may be passing you by. The card encourages you to look for potential sources of creativity and innovation, and to develop your financial literacy.

Relationships

The 4 of Cups Tarot card suggests that you may be feeling unfulfilled or complacent in your current relationships. You may be stuck in a rut and not noticing the possibilities that come with deepening your friendships and partnerships. The card urges you to look for surprise gifts – moments of joy and beauty – that can be found deep beneath the surface of your relationships. Finding these moments can often help revive stagnant relationships and lead you to a feeling of greater satisfaction.
