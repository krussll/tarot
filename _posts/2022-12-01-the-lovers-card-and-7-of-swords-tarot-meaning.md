## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health:  
The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of balance in all aspects of life, including health. It symbolizes being in tune with our health and making choices around it from a position of love and strength. When present in a health reading, it encourages us to look at our lifestyle choices and seek for ways to create healthy and sustainable habits. We should consider our partner in this effort, and provide and receive support to reach our health and wellness goals.

Finances:  
The Lovers Tarot card is a sign of partnership in the financial side of life. It indicates a strong bond and mutual understanding between two people in making financial decisions. Balance is the key here, and it is important to remember that two people working together can achieve more than one person on their own. When present in a financial reading, it suggests that now is the time to come to an agreement and achieve a balance between saving and spending, in order to secure a stable financial future.

Relationships:  
The Lovers Tarot card indicates strong love and commitment between two people, and is an invitation for a deep and honest connection. It symbolizes trust and mutual admiration, as well as understanding and willingness to compromise. In relationships, it encourages us to look at our understanding of what love means and to create a connection based on respect and shared values. It is a reminder that relationships require time, commitment and work, and that no two people are the same.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card can symbolize a need to protect oneself in times of instability, such as when one is dealing with financial hardship, health issues or relationship problems. Health-wise, it can indicate that a person is afraid to face their health issues, and may be tempted to try to find a quick-fix solution in the form of skipping treatment, “running away” from the problem instead of confronting and dealing with it. Financially, it can refer to dishonesty, theft and betrayal, and may be warning someone to keep a close eye on their finances, or that someone close to them is not being honest about a financial situation. Relationships-wise, it can refer to dishonesty and betrayal, and can suggest that someone is not being truthful about their feelings or their intentions. It can also mean that one is afraid to trust or to commit to a relationship and is “running away” from commitment. A person who has this card in their reading should be wary of those around them and take caution when making big life changes.
