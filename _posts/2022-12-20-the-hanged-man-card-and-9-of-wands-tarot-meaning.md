A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 9 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 9 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 9 Of Wands mean together?

The Hanged Man and the 9 of Wands tarot cards represent a powerful combination when it comes to achieving successful outcomes. This combination encourages us to take a wise, self-sacrificing approach while establishing and maintaining boundaries. By taking a step back and getting a different perspective, we can understand what our boundaries should be and have the confidence and persistance in maintaining them. This combination promises that if we commit to making wise decisions and holding ourselves and others accountable, we can successfully reach our goals.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Hanged Man and 9 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

When the Hanged Man tarot card and the 9 of Wands tarot card appear together in your love life, it indicates that it is time to make some drastic changes. The Hanged Man tarot card may be telling you to make a sacrifice, whether that be of your ego, pride, or time, to move forward in your love life. This could be done by taking a step back and looking at the situation from a different perspective. Taking this pause will allow you to glean more wisdom and understanding, helping you to make an informed decision.

At the same time, the 9 of Wands tarot card is warning you to keep solid boundaries and be persistent in those boundaries. This card may suggest that you need to take a firmer stance in certain areas of your relationship, and not to be swayed by fear and emotion when it comes to making decisions.

By combining these two cards, it is possible to see that your love life is at a crossroads, and it will take wisdom, self sacrifice and perseverance to make the correct choice. This can be a difficult path to follow, but in the end it will lead to a happier, more fulfilling love life. This will require you to be honest and open with yourself and your partner, as well as with your emotions. Only then will you be able to make the best choices for you and your relationship.

The combination of these cards suggests that you should take a step back and look at the situation from a different angle. You may have to sacrifice time and money in order to refocus and reorganize your resources.

The Hanged Man is a reminder to look at personal sacrifice as something positive and necessary, rather than something negative. Taking a step back and making the sacrifices needed to reorganize your finances and career will help you become wiser and more informed. Utilizing this time to gain insight into your current situation can help you make more sensible and informed decisions about your future.

The 9 of Wands indicates the need to maintain and build boundaries. This may be in regards to your finances, job, or both. It is important to be mindful of how much time, energy, and money you are investing in work and your finances and to make sure that it is balanced. You may also need to look at how you are setting and maintaining boundaries with clients and employers, as well as with yourself.

The combination of the Hanged Man and 9 of Wands tarot card in a reading can point out that it is time to take a step back and look at your finances and job, so you can make informed decisions that will bring balance and long-term success. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hanged Man and 9 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hanged Man and 9 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health: The Hanged Man Tarot card indicates that you should take time to consider the real priorities in your life and consider sacrificing some less important aspects of your lifestyle in order to concentrate on your health, both mental and physical. You may need to meditate and/or take a break from your regular life to get some physical, mental, and spiritual perspective. Think about ways you can improve your health both overall and specifically, and try to take some small steps towards this goal.

Finances: The Hanged Man card urges you to take on a different perspective when it comes to your finances. Think about what really matters to you and how you can use the resources you have at your disposal to help you reach your goals. Consider radically changing the way you approach your finances. Sacrifice something that has been keeping you from making progress and use the freed up resources to help you reach your financial goals.

Relationships: The Hanged Man card signals that it’s time to pause, take a step back and gain some perspective on your current relationships. You may need to self-sacrifice in order to be able to focus on the most important relationships in your life. Be willing to let go of some relationships that are not worth your time and energy, and instead use the freed up resources to nurture and cultivate the relationships that do matter most to you. Consider the long-term implications of the decisions you make in this time and strive to create relationships with those that will be worth the sacrifice.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The 9 of Wands tarot card is an indication of the need to set and maintain boundaries in all aspects of your life. This card often appears to remind you to be persistant and trust in your own strength and intuition. In terms of health, the 9 of Wands suggests that you take steps to protect your mental and physical health by knowing your limitations and listening to your body. This means only taking on tasks that you know won’t unduly overextend you, being honest in your interactions with medical professionals and being mindful of your own needs above those of anyone else.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Wands encourages you to put boundaries in place that will help you stay on budget and have a healthy relationship with money. It may be time to create a budget, set aside a savings account, or speak to a financial advisor to get yourself on track. This card also indicates that it’s important to be cautious when it comes to making investments or entering into any financial agreements.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 9 of Wands indicates that you must establish a balance between giving and receiving. This card encourages you to speak your truth, set boundaries and not be afraid to say no if necessary. For example, if you’re in a romantic relationship, it’s important to be clear on what you are and are not comfortable with, be honest about your needs and make sure the relationship is mutually beneficial. In the end, the 9 of Wands reminds us to trust our intuition, be assertive and stay true to our own values.
