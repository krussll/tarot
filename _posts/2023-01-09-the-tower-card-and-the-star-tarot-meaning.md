A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and The Star card together in particular.

The The Tower card and The Star have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and The Star mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The Star tarot card represent the ultimate dichotomy of change and hope. On one hand, The Tower card can be seen as a warning that some unwanted changes and upheaval is coming, and it will cause ruin and disgrace. On the other hand, The Star card is an assurance that this change can bring about renewal, miracles and inner clarity. This card combination can serve as a reminder to have faith and believe in yourself, even in the face of potential violence and disruption. Although the changes ahead may be difficult, the promise of The Star card brings hope and a light at the end of the tunnel.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Tower and The Star mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Tower mean?

When it comes to health, The Tower Tarot card is associated with unexpected and unwelcome change. It is a sign that a major illness or injury could occur, or that a long-term health problem could worsen. Those who are already dealing with health issues could find their condition quickly deteriorating, often due to a lack of proper care or attention or the inability to manage their condition effectively. In the worst cases, The Tower Tarot card could signal a complete breakdown in health, with disastrous consequences.

In terms of finances, the meaning of The Tower Tarot card is not good news. It could indicate that a sudden financial windfall or unexpected expense is about to appear, with the potential to upend any plans or strategies. Alternatively, The Tower could signal an economic crisis, bankruptcy, or other financial disaster that could completely ruin an individual’s plans for their future.

When it comes to relationships, The Tower Tarot card is often a sign of upheaval and disruption. It suggests that a major rift is about to occur, or that a longstanding problem is about to be brought to light. It could even signal the end of a relationship, as its energies point towards a period of instability and unpredictability. A conflict of wills or irreconcilable differences could cause relationships to break down or end, with no way to repair or rebuild what has been lost.

## What does The Star mean?

Health:  
The Star Tarot card can be a great sign for health, as it often speaks to a renewal of energy and an overall positive outlook. It can symbolize hope, that things can be healed and improved. Healing can come from within, through self-care and education about wellbeing. It can also signify the power of miracles on the physical realm.

Finances:  
The Star Tarot card is often seen as a positive sign when it comes to finances. It speaks to the power of trusting in something larger than oneself, and having faith that money will come in time. It may symbolize financial stability, and the possibility of increased income. It can also be a sign of luck, or an opportunity to tap into a larger network of support that can help with all that money matters.

Relationships:  
The Star Tarot card is also an excellent omen when it comes to relationships. It speaks to the power of clarity, and seeing the connection between people more deeply. It can signify trust, wonder and awe, as well as an appreciation for the potential of growth with another. It’s a reminder to be in the spotlight, and to let the light of your relationships shine. It is a sign of hope, that even in difficult times, the power of love can be miraculous.
