## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

This can be attributed to finding balance in life, making good nutrition and exercise habits part of our routine, and knowing when to rest and relax. With proper self-care, our bodies, minds and souls can come into greater harmony. Ultimately, this card means vitality and a deep sense of contentment with one’s current physical state.

Financially, The Sun card suggests a rising tide of abundance. We may find ourselves taking the necessary steps to build wealth and achieve financial security. This could mean investing wisely, making sound financial decisions, and broadening our sources of income. Additionally, The Sun card suggests a period of recognition and appreciation, both inside ourselves and from others.

Finally, relationships are an essential part of our lives. The Sun card points to positive and nurturing connections, where both parties can show appreciation, understanding, and a sense of mutual fulfillment. With the Sun in play, relationships can become a source of joy and deep connection. ## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 6 of Cups Tarot card, symbolizing nostalgia and innocence, can provide insight into one’s current health and lifestyle. On a physical level, the 6 of Cups suggests that reverting to a simpler, more natural lifestyle can be beneficial. Avoiding processed and unhealthy foods can help to maintain energy, restore balance, and nourish the body. The card also encourages a return to simpler activities and routines that evoke childhood memories. Taking a moment to enjoy the simple pleasures in life can be a powerful self-care strategy and can promote overall health and wellness.

Finances: The 6 of Cups can represent a time of nostalgia and innocence, which can be interpreted as a time of financial success. It can suggest a return to financial planning and self-discipline that can lead to stability in the future. It can also be interpreted as being generous and openhanded with money and resources, as this can create a cycle of abundance that can bring in more money as time goes on. Appreciating the moments of joy and celebration can also provide for more financial stability as it can encourage people to spend within their means and take steps to save for the future.

Relationships: The 6 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes reunions and connections. This can suggest an opportunity to reconnect with those we may have fallen out of touch with or to restore the connections that we may have lost. It can also suggest taking the time to nurture familial or platonic relationships we may have neglected or hint at the potential of new relationships. The card can also symbolize a return to innocence and purity of companionship, where individuals treat one another with kindness and respect, devoid of any ulterior motives.
