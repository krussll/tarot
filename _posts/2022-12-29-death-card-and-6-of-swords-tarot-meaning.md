A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Death card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Death tarot card and The 6 Of Swords tarot card together suggest that you are about to embark on a journey of big changes and transformation. It is a time to end certain aspects of your life in order to create the space for new beginnings. The changes that are to come are necessary, so don’t let the fear of the unknown prevent you from moving on. It may involve a physical relocation to find better opportunities and make a fresh start. Embrace the transition and be ready for new experiences as you look ahead and make your way to the next chapter of your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and 6 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

The Death Tarot card is an important reminder that all things must eventually come to an end. In the context of health, it might be a sign that a health challenge must be addressed and that it’s time to make necessary changes to improve one’s health. It could also represent the need for understanding that the body is made up of energy, and that this energy is constantly changing and transforming. Even though the card seems to imply ‘the end,’ it really symbolizes a new beginning, a time to let go of unhealthy patterns and start anew with a healthy outlook and lifestyle.

Finances:  
The Death Tarot card symbolizes endings and fresh starts when it comes to finances. It could mean that it’s time to re-evaluate one’s financial state and make necessary changes in order to ensure that the future is secure. It might signify needing to let go of unhealthy financial habits and replacing them with more responsible ones. It could also represent an opportunity to seek out new financial opportunities and investments, as well as take the necessary steps to ensure long-term financial success.

Relationships:  
The Death Tarot card suggests that it is time to move on from a relationship that is no longer healthy or beneficial. It might mean that it is time to end unhealthy relationships, or it could suggest that it is time to start anew with a relationship in a new way. It could signify a need for self-reflection and understanding, a time to look within to see what needs to be changed or released in order to foster healthier relationships. Ultimately, the card symbolizes a time of rebirth and renewal and the potential for a new beginning within the context of relationships.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card represents a necessary transition in all areas of life, and it can portend different meanings depending on the context.

Health: This card suggests that a transition or change in approach to health may be necessary. It might be a physical change, such as adjusting your diet or exercise routine, a mental or emotional transition, or a medical treatment. It could also suggest a spiritual transformation; this could mean a path to greater self-awareness, or an exploration of alternative healing practices.

Finances: This card can suggest a change in financial status that is necessary for further progress. It could mean a shift in mindset or attitude, such as pursuing a new job or investment opportunities. It can also suggest a need to reevaluate your finances, from budgeting to savings, in order to identify any areas where improvement is possible.

Relationships: The 6 Of Swords Tarot card suggests that it may be time for a necessary change in relationships. This can include improving existing relationships, or making a difficult decision to end a relationship. This card also suggests a need for introspection, and a willingness to look at the issues that may be blocking you from having healthier, more fulfilling relationships.
