A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The Page Of Wands and the Queen of Swords tarot cards when read together suggest the emergence of a creative, active individual that is noticeably independent, but also open to new ideas and projects. This person will be honest in all their pursuits and have a go-getter attitude that can help them reach their goals. They will also be eager and enthusiastic to take on any challenge they come across, and will combine their creativity and ambition to go after their dreams.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Page Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Page Of Wands mean?

The Page of Wands Tarot card can signify a physical or mental health issue that may need to be addressed. It could be a sign that you need to be more active, or that you need to find a new outlet for creative expression. This card is a reminder to take good care of your health, both mental and physical. It is a reminder to stay active, stay creative and stay balanced.

Finances  
The Page of Wands Tarot card can signal a new venture or project to work on that could bring financial rewards. This could be a new business endeavor or a new way to invest your money. It may be wise to approach new ventures with caution and take the time to research your decisions before making any major changes to your financial portfolio.

Relationships  
The Page of Wands Tarot card can signify a new relationship starting or a new phase in an existing relationship. This could be a sign of a new love interest or a chance to work on building a stronger bond with an existing partner. A new relationship may require a lot of effort from both parties and it is important to communicate clearly and openly when taking the next step in a relationship. This card is a reminder to be creative, be open and be honest when building lasting relationships.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a powerful message for individuals seeking better health. It speaks to the need for self-discipline, hard work, and determination to succeed. This card encourages one to be willing to make the necessary changes to their lifestyle to achieve long-term health benefits. It also helps to maintain clear boundaries and have honest conversations regarding health.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to be fiscally responsible and prudent when it comes to spending and saving. This card encourages one to take a frank look at their financial situation and to practice fiscal responsibility. It also speaks to the need for clear communication when negotiating financial arrangements and making decisions about investments.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card reminds one to be honest and direct with their words and actions when it comes to relationships. This card encourages one to practice self-sufficiency and not rely too heavily on others for emotional, financial, or physical support. It also encourages one to be honest about their feelings and speak up if something is bothering them. By practicing clear communication, self-sufficiency, and honesty one can maintain healthy relationships both in business as well as in personal relationships.
