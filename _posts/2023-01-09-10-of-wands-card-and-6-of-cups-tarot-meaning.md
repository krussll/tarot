A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The 10 Of Wands tarot card and The 6 Of Cups tarot card together represent a situation of being overburdened with too much responsibility and stress at the same time as being reminded of a simpler past. You may experience nostalgia for moments of joy in the past and a longing for an innocent time when life was relatively free from the pressures of adulthood. It’s important to take some time to appreciate these happy memories and reunite with your old friends as well as getting any help you can in dispersing your burdens and lightening your load.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 10 of Wands palatable Tarot card can suggest physical exhaustion, aches, pains and strains. It could indicate a period of physical fatigue and overwork. It can also suggest a feeling of being overwhelmed and drained, leading to a lack of energy and motivation. Mentally, it can indicate a feeling of overburdenment, being weighed down with too many worries and responsibilities. It can represent being so worried and overworked that mental clarity and focus is difficult to achieve, leading to confusion and burnout.

Finances

The 10 of Wands Tarot card in its financial meaning suggests that an individual has taken on too many responsibilities and burdens. This can lead to an inability to focus on their financial goals, leading to a sense of being overwhelmed and bogged down by looming debts. It can represent a lack of financial responsibility, where one is spread too thin and unable to make wise decisions about their money. It could even suggest a period of financial struggle due to overspending, out of control debt and not making smart financial choices.

Relationships

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can suggest a period of exhaustion and irritability impacting relationships. It can indicate that an individual is feeling burdened, stressed and overworked, leading to an inability to maintain healthy relationships. Feeling overwhelmed and drained can lead to communication difficulties, passive-aggression and emotional disconnection, creating a strain on relationships. It can also suggest being too focused on work and not giving enough attention to the people in one’s life, leading to feelings of neglect.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

The 6 of Cups tarot card is a powerful omen in many different aspects of life, especially in the realm of health, finances and relationships. On the surface, this card can invoke a sense of nostalgia, innocence, and gift-giving. These positive elements can be powerful indicators of feelings of well-being, joy, and security.

In terms of health, the 6 of Cups tarot card is a sign that one’s health is in a good place. The card may be a reminder of the importance of taking time for self-care and joy, and to indulge in activities that bring about peace and relaxation. The card can also symbolize reconnecting with long-lost friends or family, and bringing about a sense of restoration for mind, body, and spirit.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Cups tarot card is a sign of stability and abundance. This card can be a reminder to practice patience and to allow the manifesting process to unfold. It can also symbolize generosity and gift-giving, reminding us to give to others what we would like to receive.

In terms of relationships, the 6 of Cups tarot card is a sign of renewing old bonds, reuniting with old friends, and giving someone a sense of comfort and protection. The card is a reminder to practice openness to new experiences, to let go of expectations, and to simply be present and enjoy the moment. It can also be a sign that past wounds are being healed, and old bonds are being strengthened.

All in all, the 6 of Cups tarot card is a powerful omen in many different aspects of life. Its message reminds us to practice kindness, to give gifts, and to reconnect with those we’ve lost touch with. When interpreted as a sign of health, finances, and relationships, the 6 of Cups tarot card can inspire a sense of healing, abundance, and strong connections to those we love.
