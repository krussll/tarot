A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Spades card and 3 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 5 Of Spades card and 3 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of The 5 Of Spades and The 3 Of Hearts suggests a tumultuous period ahead.

The 5 Of Spades, indicating sneakiness and change, combined with winter and water elements, suggests covert transformations or actions taking place under the surface.

This could suggest hidden motives or changes occurring without your knowledge.

On the other hand, The 3 Of Hearts denotes infidelity and faith, associated with spring and fire.

This implies a passion-filled scenario but with elements of betrayal or deception.

These could also suggest a loss of faith or trust.

In combination, these cards might be forewarning you about an impending deception or betrayal in your personal or professional life.

The change that is on the horizon may be due to the deceit uncovered, causing a shift in your life.

But remember, the fire and spring elements also imply a resurrection or rebirth, thus a silver lining of newfound strength and faith lies beneath the upheaval.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 5 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

Drawing both the 5 of Spades and the 3 of Hearts card in a cartomancy reading may present a complex forecast for your love life.

The 5 of Spades, symbolizing ‘change and sneakiness’, compounded with the typically cold and challenging nature of winter, suggests a time of transition and possible deceit.

This could indicate that your relationship is on rocky grounds, waiting to shift.

The water element signifies deep emotions and intuition; nevertheless, its muddled nature might imply an uncertain future and warn of possible underhand behaviors.

On the other hand, the presence of the 3 of Hearts card, symbolizing ‘infidelity and faith,’ and associated with the warmth and rejuvenation of spring, adds further complexity to this interpretation.

This card could point to a breach of trust or suggest the lack of loyalty and commitment in the relationship.

Yet, the fire element signifies passion, energy, and transformation; it also signifies faith.

This seems to offer some hope for revival and metamorphosis of your relationship, even after difficult times.

The fire’s radiating warmth can incite passion and rejuvenation, showing that even through struggles, there might still be room for love and forgiveness.

### What does 5 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

When the 5 of Spades card appears in a reading related to your career or finances, it often points to a period of change and transition that may not be very straightforward or clear.

This card encourages you to be cautious in your actions and make sure your motives align with your personal values and integrity.

In terms of finances, it suggests that you may have to deal with changes or fluctuations that may initially appear negative or challenging.

However, if navigated wisely, these changes may eventually work in your favor.

This card, being associated with the winter season and water element, indicates you to be more introspective, ponder over what truly holds value for you, and making any necessary adjustments in your financial decisions and career path.

On the other hand, the appearance of the 3 of Hearts card may bring some concerns.

It symbolizes a lack of faith, possibly indicative of insecurities or uncertainties about your job or finances.

This card encourages you to reassess these insecurities, and remind yourself of your capabilities.

Express your concerns if need be and clarify any misunderstandings.

A lack of trust might be holding you back from reaching your full potential.

The card’s spring association and fire element, however, bring a spark of hope and renewal.

Spring is a time to shake off the old and embrace the new, and fire signifies passion and determination.

Therefore, despite ongoing concerns, this card encourages optimism and a proactive approach to work and financial situations.

### What does 5 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, when the 5 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts cards are drawn together for your health, the message comes with varying levels of complexity.

The 5 Of Spades, associated with sneakiness and change, suggests there might be unforeseen changes or hidden issues regarding your health.

This could indicate a need to take action and delve deeper to unveil any unknown health issues.

This significance leans into the card’s association with the winter season, a time often characterized by stillness and introspection.

Its element, Water, is symbolic of deeper emotions and the subconscious, further indicative of an inward focus necessary for you.

In contrast, the 3 Of Hearts card with its representation of infidelity and faith is associated with the spring season and the fire element, juxtaposing the 5 of Spades.

The meaning could hint at a lack of consistency or neglect concerning health matters and a rightful need to re-evaluate your health routines.

Nonetheless, it also indicates the presence of faith, providing assurance that you possess the essential strength to handle these issues.

Spring is a season of renewal and rebirth and fire is symbolic of transformation and power, illustrating that the journey might be uncomfortable but the end result will be a healthier and stronger you.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
