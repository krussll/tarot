A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The Justice card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The Justice Tarot card and the Queen of Wands tarot card together signify a strong, confident person who is able to make the right decisions and take appropriate action. This person has the ability to balance cause and effect, create win-win solutions, and stand up for what is true and just. This individual is a source of strength, courage and drive, inspiring those around them to do their best and never give up. With the combination of these two cards, it is clear that this person is a leader who is not afraid to speak up and fight for what is right, driven by a fierce commitment to truth and justice.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Queen Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card indicates that we have the power to determine the outcome of our health. We must take responsibility for our actions and make choices that reflect balanced and wise decisions. This card also emphasizes that we must be mindful of the cause and effect of our decisions when it comes to our health. For example, if we choose to eat unhealthy foods and not exercise, it will likely have a negative effect on our health. But if we choose to eat nutritious foods and exercise regularly, it will have a positive effect on our health.

The Justice Tarot card also signifies that when we take a holistic approach to our health and make informed decisions, the result is often a win-win. We may be able to achieve our health goals, as well as prevent long-term health problems. Additionally, listening to our bodies and honoring our physical, mental, and emotional needs is also essential for our overall wellbeing.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card encourages us to invest wisely, be frugal, and take responsibility for our money decisions. This card emphasizes that when it comes to finances,

our choices today have far-reaching implications. For instance, if we choose to be reckless and make extravagant purchases, it could have a negative effect on our financial future. But if we choose to be responsible and invest our money wisely, then we can build a secure financial foundation for ourselves.

Furthermore, the Justice Tarot card suggests that win-win financial solutions are achievable when we are honest, fair and transparent in all our financial dealings. Honesty and integrity are essential in our financial dealings, which allows us to create sustainable financial success.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card is a reminder that relationships require a balanced approach. It symbolizes that we must be mindful of the cause and effect of our decisions when it comes to our relationships. We must always strive for win-win solutions and be fair, honest, and respectful in all our interactions.

This card also emphasizes that we must take responsibility for our actions and be accountable for the consequences. Additionally, we must strive to be understanding and patient, and recognize that the truth will eventually come out.

The Justice Tarot card encourages us to strive for balance, fairness, and truth in all our relationships. When we can acknowledge each other, respect each other’s boundaries, and approach conflicts from a win-win perspective, we can foster strong and meaningful relationships.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health

The Queen of Wands Tarot card indicates a strong sense of self-assurance and confidence which can be very beneficial for physical health. Those under this influence tend to have good nutrition, exercise regularly and take good care of their body and mind. They know their body and its needs and are willing to take the steps necessary to take good care of it. They understand the importance of self-care and are willing to practice it. With a strong self-confidence, they are able to channel their tenacity and will to strive for fitness and well being.

Finances

The Queen of Wands Tarot card also has positive implications in relation to finances. Those under this influence will have an entrepreneurial spirit, with the capacity to take risks and have the confidence in their abilities to manage their money and investments. They’re sure of their decisions and know how to manage their finances to create abundance and stability. This Queen is also likely to attract wealth and success due to their confident and bold attitude.

Relationships

The Queen of Wands Tarot card represents an independent and self-assured woman who is free to make her own decisions. She is passionate, strong-willed, and knows how to assert herself. She is someone who commands respect in relationships and will not be easily taken advantage of. Her strong sense of independence means that she is not easily intimidated and will not settle for anything in a relationship that does not make her feel fulfilled. She is confident in her own ability to make the right decision when it comes to love and will never stay in an unhealthy relationship out of fear of being alone.
