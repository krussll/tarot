However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Hierophant card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

This combination encourages you to recognize the benefits of knowledge and wisdom that come from tradition while still remaining confident in your individual decisions. You’re expected to conform to societal expectations, but you can still be a person with a unique, spirited outlook. It’s ok to be respectful of traditions and authority, but remember to be yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card speaks to the importance of tradition when it comes to health. This can include simple things like regular medical check-ups, physical activity, and balanced nutrition. It can also refer to seeking advice from qualified professionals when it comes to health issues. This tarot card encourages individuals to adhere to a traditional and tried and true approach when it comes to their overall health and wellbeing.

Finances

The Hierophant suggests that sticking to tried and true methods is the best way to handle finances. This could include consulting with trusted advisors, relying on traditional investment strategies, and diligently saving for the future instead of embarking on risky financial vehicles. It implies that conventional approaches to money management can be beneficial in terms of both long-term security and financial growth.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, The Hierophant encourages individuals to look to traditional values and customs as a guide. This could include conservative approaches to dating and marriage such as waiting for a certain age to get married or not living together before marriage. It could also relate to being traditional in how individuals show affection, such as being conservative in both physical intimacy and in the words they use. This tarot card suggests that in order to achieve long-term success in relationships, individuals should adhere to tried and true values.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen of Wands tarot card is a representation of a fiery, confident, and assertive woman. She has a bold and decisive nature, and it follows that this card symbolizes a brave, self-assured and determined demeanor. Across all aspects of life, this card can bring a sense of ambition and balance.

When it comes to health, the Queen of Wands suggests that we seek out solutions to our ailments and not just settle for temporary solutions and bandaids. We should be proactive in improving our physical and mental health. This card also encourages us to be generous with ourselves and find balance in our lives.

The Queen of Wands when it comes to finances speaks of the power of will and determination. It encourages us to take responsibility for our financial decisions and not be a passive recipient of our circumstances. We should be strategic and creative in our approach to money matters and make sure we stay focused on our goals.

Finally, in relationships, this card encourages us to be bold and to trust in our own intuition and power. We should never be afraid to express ourselves and stand in our truth. The Queen of Wands will remind us of the importance of honest communication and standing our ground in all aspects of our lives. We should be confident in our relationships and refuse to settle for anything less than what we deserve.
