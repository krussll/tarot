A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Strength card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The 10 Of Swords tarot card together signal the end of a difficult situation and the need to accept the situation for what it is. It is an opportunity to take stock of what you are feeling and reflect on how you can move forward with self-love, unconditional love, self-respect, and courage. This may require strength and resilience as you move through your rock bottom, but ultimately it is a chance for renewal and rebuilding. With the understanding that no matter what, you always have the power to shape your destiny, you can use this opportunity to start anew.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

When it comes to health and wellness, the Strength Tarot card is a reminder that having a strong sense of self-love means putting your health first. This means setting goals and establishing healthy habits that will leave you feeling positive and confident, while also making sure you’re taking care of yourself. You can do this by eating nourishing foods, getting enough rest, and engaging in activities that provide mental and physical stimulation. Such activities can be as simple as doing a daily yoga practice or journaling. Additionally, having the courage and self-respect to seek the help of a professional when needed can be instrumental to maintaining a healthy lifestyle.

In terms of finances, the Strength Tarot card represents the courage and discipline needed for successful financial planning. This is especially true when it comes to setting goals, budgeting, saving, and investing. To ensure success, discipline and determination is required, and it is important to have a good strategy in place. Additionally, understanding the importance of diversifying your investments, as well as being aware of potential risks, is essential.

When it comes to relationships, the Strength Tarot card speaks to the importance of self-love and respect. It is essential that you first and foremost develop a strong and healthy relationship with yourself, as this will lead to a strong foundation for any relationships that you may form. From here, it is important to continue to maintain self-love, as well as having the courage and motivation to set boundaries, forgive, and grow. Additionally, the Strength Tarot card encourages you to be vulnerable and compassionate, and to understand and accept the emotional needs of those around you.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 10 Of Swords Tarot card in relation to health indicates a complete and painful ending to a health journey. It can be seen as a way of showing rock bottom in terms of health issues and problems. This could indicate that a health crisis has happened or that physical and emotional exhaustion is at an all time high. It can also refer to a difficult time in recovery from a prolonged illness, or that treatment has not been successful in finding a cure. This card can be an encouragement and reminder to take care of yourself, seek support and focus on self-care to help rebuild and find what works best for your health.

Finances  
The 10 Of Swords Tarot card in relation to finances denotes a complete and painful ending to a financial situation. This could be a sign of hitting rock bottom in terms of money and resources. It could signify financial difficulties such as bankruptcy, debt, or other money issues that have caused a complete collapse. This card warns to take a step back and take a deeper look at your money and resources. It is a reminder to be mindful and responsible when it comes to managing your finances. Investing in knowledge and tools to help manage money and resources are necessary steps to create a secure financial future.

Relationships  
The 10 Of Swords Tarot card in relation to relationships indicates a complete and painful ending to a relationship. This could signify the rock bottom of a relationship, which could include breakups, divorce, or other difficult endings. It can symbolize a relationship that has been filled with anger and betrayal, leaving both partners feeling broken, confused and overwhelmed. This card can also be a reminder to take a step back and look at the situation objectively. It is a reminder to be honest and open with yourself and others to find solutions that work best for both parties. It can also be a reminder to focus on inner work and self-love to help navigate any healing process.
