A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 2 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 2 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 2 Of Wands mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card, symbolizing traditions and conventional expectations, in combination with the 2 of Wands card, which speaks of making a choice and awaiting results, calls on you to carefully consider how you will respond to a crossroads in your life. It suggests that you should take into consideration the expectations of those who have come before you as you make your choices, remaining mindful of the conventions and traditions that have been established along the way. By doing so, you will be able to make an informed decision based on what society has put in place. This is a great card combination to look at when you are contemplating a journey, be it literal or metaphorical. As you thoughtfully weigh the options before you, the 2 of Wands encourages you to move forward confidently with your chosen path and trust that you have made the right decision.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and 2 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 2 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant card indicates that when it comes to health, you should consider the traditions and conventions that have long been believed to be beneficial. Traditional medicine and traditional methods of healing are the wisest path to take. Health is not just physical—spiritual and emotional wellbeing are also important. Seek out guidance and advice from experts in their field and follow the tried and true. The Hierophant card is all about studying the past and using it to build the future.

Finances

The Hierophant card speaks of a need for financial stability. Taking a more traditional approach to your finances, such as keeping a budget and setting long-term goals, can be beneficial. Don’t be afraid to ask for help from those who are experienced in the field. Following the course of conventional wisdom is wise in this case. Invest in yourself and in your future, but don’t take any unnecessary risks. Planning and knowledge will help you make the decision that’s right for you.

Relationships

The Hierophant card indicates that in relationships, you should focus on honoring traditions, customs and rituals. It’s a reminder to keep things traditional and to rely on proven methods of communication and conflict resolution. Don’t be afraid to rely on the advice of others who know you best. There may be times when you want to take a more risky approach, but it is important to remember that following the well-established path can be beneficial in the long run.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 2 Of Wands Tarot card points to questions around health and wellness. It asks us to take a look at our current health patterns, and consider what changes we can make in the near future. It speaks of the importance of taking care of ourselves, and making wise decisions about our physical and mental well-being. The card may suggest that we need to make a conscious effort to make healthy changes. This could mean making a conscious effort to improve our diet, exercise routine, sleep habits and stress levels. The card may also point to waiting for results from a recent medical procedure or check-up, and suggests that we remain patient and trust that the results will be positive.

Finances: The 2 Of Wands Tarot card can indicate that we must make a decision around our finances. It speaks to us of delaying gratification, and points to our need to be prudent and discerning with our choices. It may point to the necessity of investing in the future and suggests that we should be mindful and responsible with our money. The card also points to the possibility of travel, and suggests that we consider taking a short break or vacation to get a better perspective on our current financial situation.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, the 2 Of Wands Tarot card suggests that we make a conscious choice around who we are investing our time in. It speaks of looking carefully at the relationships in our lives, and question whether we are truly getting what we need from them. It may point to a need to let go of certain relationships if they no longer serve our highest purpose. This card indicates that whatever decision we make around relationships, we will be rewarded if it is made from a place of clarity, responsibility, and self-love.
