## What does The Wheel Of Fortune and 3 Of Swords mean together?

Watching The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card together can be a powerful symbol of the mixed potentials of life. This alliance brings together the heavenly luck of the Wheel Of Fortune card with the sobering truth of loss, heartache and disappointment of the 3 Of Swords card. The Wheel Of Fortune signifies a turn of luck, a life-altering moment, a cycle of change and a fresh opportunity. The 3 of Swords is a symbol of the pain, sorrow and heartbreak that life can bring. Together these cards represent the twists and turns of life; always demanding us to be brave in the face of all that comes our way, holding on to faith in ourselves and our resilience. When these two cards come together they signify a powerful time of transformation and growth, and tell us to never forget the capacity within us to rise above our challenges.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card gives insight into all areas of life, particularly health, finances, and relationships.

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be an indication that changes are on the horizon. In terms of health, those changes could mean taking a different approach to diet and exercise, or seeking medical advice if necessary. It could also mean being open to alternative therapies or treatments that may not have immediately come to mind. Generally, the card indicates positive change and the possibility of better health.

In terms of finances, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is often seen as a sign of luck and good fortune. This could mean winning the lottery or receiving an unanticipated inheritance, but more likely it suggests that taking a chance, such as investing or starting a business, could pay off in the long run.

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is also associated with relationships. It can signal a new beginning, a chance to make a fresh start, or a time to take risks and explore new possibilities. It can also suggest an opportunity to try something new in a relationship or move on from a current one that no longer serves its purpose.

Overall, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a symbol of change and opportunity. When it appears in a reading, it can be a sign to take chances and look for new solutions. This card is a reminder that sometimes fortune favors the bold and that taking risks can bring about new possibilities in health, finances, and relationships.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The three of swords tarot card is a powerful card that reflects the very painful and often heartbreaking situations that occur in life. This card typically symbolizes the experience of deep loss, sorrow, trauma, and sadness. It is associated with painful experiences that can leave emotional and physical scars.

When it comes to health, the three of swords tarot card is a reminder that physical health can often be affected by emotional turmoil. Those who experience a period of great loss can suffer from more physical ailments than usual, such as headaches, stomach pains, and general malaise. Additionally, those who suffer from depression can often find it difficult to keep up with their physical health, both through nutrition and exercise.

In terms of finances, the three of swords tarot card can mean a period of financial difficulty in the midst of emotional hardship. Those who experience a great loss or trauma might find themselves struggling financially as grieving can impair decision making and efficacy. This can lead to a period of unbalanced finances that could cause debt and financial strain.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the three of swords tarot card is a reminder that those who experience hardship can often find it difficult to open up to those around them. Trauma or grief can lead to a period of loneliness and feelings of isolation, which can be damaging for relationships, both with family and friends. It is during these times that it is important for those around you to offer their support and understanding.

Overall, the three of swords tarot card is a powerful reminder that sometimes tragedy and loss can have an impact on our health, finances, and relationships. It is important to remember that these experiences can be incredibly difficult, but by seeking support and understanding, can help us to navigate these experiences and come out the other side.
