A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of the 2 of Wands tarot card and the 6 of Swords tarot card suggests that you are currently waiting for the results of a choice you have made, and are preparing for the necessary transition that comes along with it. You may be ready to make plans to travel or relocate, but need to wait for the results to confirm your decision. In the meantime, take time to plan what you will need to do in order to make the transition to your desired destination as smooth and successful as possible.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

The 2 Of Wands Tarot card can have a positive or negative influence on financial, health, and relationship matters.

When it comes to finances, the 2 Of Wands Tarot card indicates that one must be patient and wait for the results to come in. This could be related to making a career or business decision and waiting to see the results of it. It can also be indicative of a need for self-discipline or control in regards to money, as one must make a conscious decision to ensure their finances stay in order.

For health, the 2 Of Wands Tarot card can mean that one needs to be proactive in their health decisions. This may involve making the necessary changes to their lifestyle in order to stay healthy and fit. It could also mean that one needs to take time to rest and relax in order to give their bodies the time it needs to heal and recover.

In relationships, the 2 Of Wands Tarot card suggests that one needs to make a conscious choice in order to move forward. This could be in regards to making a commitment to a relationship or taking the time to really get to know someone before deciding if they are a good fit. This can also be linked to travel plans, as it may be beneficial to take time to explore new places and create memories with someone special.

Overall, the 2 Of Wands Tarot card can be seen as a reminder to make a conscious choice in regards to financial, health, and relationship matters. This can mean taking the time to evaluate one’s options or making the necessary changes to ensure their overall well-being. Ultimately, the key is to take the time to make the best decision for one’s self.

It could be a physical change such as starting a new exercise routine, or making changes to your diet. It could also be a mental health related change such as beginning psychotherapy or finding a support group. Whatever it is, the card is prompting you to make a necessary transition to better health.

When it comes to finances, The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can indicate a need to make a transition in your financial habits. It could be a practical change such as creating a budget or investing in a retirement plan. It could also be a psychological change such as shifting from a scarcity mentality to an abundance mentality. Whatever it is, the card is prompting you to make the necessary changes to better your finances.

When it comes to relationships, The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can indicate needing to make a transition with your associations. It could be a practical change such as setting up boundaries. It could also be a psychological change such as shifting from a fear-based mindset to a love-based mindset. Whatever it is, the card is prompting you to recognize the need for transition and transformation in order to create healthier, more meaningful and balanced relationships.
