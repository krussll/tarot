A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Ace Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The Ace Of Pentacles and the 7 Of Swords tarot cards combined could be a warning that a new job, raise, promotion, or investment could end badly. This could mean that someone is looking to take advantage of you, either by taking credit for your work or stealing something from you. It could also mean that someone may be trying to deceive you in order to gain some sort of financial advantage. Ultimately, it’s a warning to be careful and to trust your instincts if someone tries to offer you something that seems too good to be true.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace Of Pentacles Tarot card is a positive card symbolizing abundance, opportunity, and success in the areas of health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, this card can suggest that you are embarking on a path to improved health and wellness. An investment in yourself and your wellbeing is in order so that you can reach new levels of physical and mental health. Consider how making positive lifestyle choices, including exercise, proper nutrition, and ample rest, can help you achieve new heights of well-being.

Financially, this card can point to new business prospects, successful investments, and financial growth. A change in career, a raise, promotion, or bonus, or finding a way to increase your income is all suggested. This card can also indicate a more secure financial situation, with money coming in from multiple sources. Consider seeking advice from a financial advisor to ensure the best possible outcome.

In terms of relationships, the Ace Of Pentacles Tarot card can suggest that the time is right for a new venture together, such as starting a business or making a major purchase. It also points to a solid commitment to each other, as well total trust and solid communication. Both parties should be open to compromise and willing to work together for mutual success.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 7 of Swords Tarot card can signify an unhealthy state of mind and body, suggesting that an individual needs to take action to address their physical or mental health. This card can indicate that the individual is struggling with the pressures and demands of life, leading them to make irrational decisions. On a physical level, the 7 of Swords can point to physical exhaustion and poor self-care habits. For instance, the person may be overextending themselves, leading to burnout and exhaustion, or they may be neglecting their health and wellbeing, potentially leading to more serious physical health issues.

Finances

The 7 of Swords Tarot card could point to financial instability and a lack of security. This card can suggest that an individual is taking considerable risks with their money, either through reckless spending, gambling or investing. The individual may also be looking for a way out of their current financial situation, whether it be through debt or even stealing from others to make ends meet. Furthermore, it may indicate that the individual is making decisions without considering the long-term consequences, leading to further financial difficulty.

Relationships

The 7 of Swords Tarot card can signify deceit and betrayal in intimate relationships. It may indicate that one or both individuals are not being honest with each other, or that a third party is influencing the relationship, leading to destructive behavior. It can suggest a lack of trust and a feeling of insecurity, as the individuals may no longer feel safe in the relationship. Furthermore, the individual may be using manipulation or deception in an attempt to control their partner, potentially leading to an erosion of the trust and intimacy between them.
