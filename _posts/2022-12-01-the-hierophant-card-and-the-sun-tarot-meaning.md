A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and The Sun card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and The Sun have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and The Sun mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The Sun tarot card together represent the balance of honoring traditional expectations while also achieving success and happiness. They suggest that although you have to conform to the formalities of society, you can still be rewarded for your work. By staying open to guidance from those around you and by honoring traditions and conventional expectations, you will be able to reach the joyful outcome of achieving success and material happiness that you desire.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card symbolizes the importance of conventional health traditions and beliefs. It suggests that staying in tune with the truth of one’s body and mind through upkeep of conventional habits, such as exercise and the consumption of healthy foods, is the key to maintaining good health. Additionally, the card encourages one to trust in the wisdom of established health professionals and traditions, such as consulting with doctors and following medication instructions. Above all, the card encourages one to recognize the importance of honoring one’s core beliefs while also avoiding extremes in order to achieve balance in terms of health.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card speaks to the importance of adhering to long-standing, conventional financial wisdom when it comes to managing and investing money. It suggests that traditional methods, such as regularly saving money, investing in sound stocks or bonds, and avoiding financial extremes, are essential to creating and maintaining a strong financial foundation. The message of this card also encourages one to consult with trusted advisers, such as financial planners, to ensure that one’s financial decisions are well informed and guided by a more experienced source.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card symbolizes conventional relationship wisdom, such as the importance of communication, honesty, and respect. It suggests that staying in line with traditional relationship patterns is the best way to maintain meaningful and fulfilling relationships. The card implies that people should trust in the wisdom of tradition and accept their responsibilities as a partner in their relationship. This card also encourages one to focus on strengths of the relationship and respect differences. Lastly, it reminds people to remember to stay loyal and consistent in their relationships by following established patterns of behaviour.

It could also suggest that you may be about to embark on a new relationship which is rooted in mutual trust and respect.
