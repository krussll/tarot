However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and The Star card together in particular.

The The Devil card and The Star have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

By focusing on renewal, hope and inner clarity, one can start to make progress in understanding and fulfilling the potential that the universe has bestowed upon them. Miraculous occurrences can take place in one’s life, and faith and hope should be the main drivers for any journey. With this combination of energies, it is possible to reach heights of spiritual clarity and be in the spotlight for the right reasons.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Devil and The Star mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card can cause serious repercussions to one’s health, finances, and relationships. On a physical level, those who become fixated on materialism will become increasingly sedentary. This can lead to decreased physical fitness, weakening the individual’s immune system and leading to many physical ailments. In addition, greed and obsession often cause individuals to neglect their mental and emotional health as they chase material possessions.

On the financial side, The Devil Tarot card can lead to extreme debt accumulation. People become fixated on their desires, often placing obtaining material possessions before necessities. This often pushes individuals further into debt as they indulge in overspending. The Devil Tarot card also encourages individuals to cut corners and bend ethical rules in order to achieve the desired outcome. This can result in legal consequences that can lead to even more debt.

The Devil Tarot card has a negative effect on relationships as well. People who become fixated on materialism often become emotionally unavailable. They focus more on their own needs than the needs of their partner, creating an unbalanced and unhealthy relationship. Greed and envy can also result in strife and mistrust in relationships, as those under the influence of The Devil Tarot card strive to acquire the possessions of others.

Overall, The Devil Tarot card has an incredibly destructive impact on individuals’ lives. Not only can it take a physical, financial and emotional toll, but it can also create unhealthy relationships and lead to unethical behavior. Therefore, it is important to pay heed to the warning of The Devil Tarot card and strive to keep one’s life in balance and focus on the things that truly matter.
