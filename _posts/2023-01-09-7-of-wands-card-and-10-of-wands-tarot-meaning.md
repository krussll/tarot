## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands Tarot card has a lot to tell us about our health, finances, and relationships.

Health

The 7 of Wands is a card of protection and self-defense. It signifies that we need to take steps to protect ourselves from anything that might hurt us physically or emotionally. This can mean being more conscious of what we eat, getting regular checkups, or anything else we can do to maintain our health. Additionally, it can mean setting boundaries with other people in order to keep ourselves safe from hurtful words or actions.

Finances

This card symbolizes taking a stand and defending our resources. This could mean standing up for what we believe in financially and being vocal about it, or being prepared to fight for something if need be. It is a reminder to be cautious of people who might be taking advantage of us or trying to take away something that we rightfully own. We should be aware of our finances and make smart decisions, always keeping a watchful eye over our money and resources.

Relationships

The 7 of Wands reminds us to be protective of our relationships. This could mean setting boundaries between ourselves and others in order to avoid being taken advantage of. It also means being prepared to defend ourselves or our loved ones if they are threatened or hurt. We should also be mindful of not being too possessive or controlling, but still standing our ground and fighting for what we believe in. This card is a reminder that we should always speak up for ourselves and those we care about.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 10 of Wands Tarot card speaks of carrying too much weight and feeling overwhelmed. This can manifest itself physically and mentally, leading to fatigue, exhaustion, and possibly burn-out. Stress is a major factor and can manifest in various ways, from mild anxiety to full-blown panic attacks. It can also increase the risk of physical ailments, such as headaches and upset stomachs. Taking time to rest and recuperate is important in order to maintain good health.

Finances: The 10 of Wands Tarot card represents the struggle of carrying too much responsibility when it comes to money. It can signify too many debts or too few resources, and can lead to feelings of frustration and worry about being able to make ends meet. This card suggests that it’s time to take stock of your financial situation and make a workable plan for paying back debt, if necessary. It may also be a reminder to save more than you spend in order to build a financial cushion in case of emergency.

Relationships: The 10 of Wands Tarot card can signify the pressure of carrying too much of the load in relationships. This can include taking on too many responsibilities or feeling the need to please your partner at the expense of your own needs. This can lead to feelings of resentment, frustration, and exhaustion. It’s important to recognize when you need to ask for help and when it’s time to lighten your own load. It will be beneficial for both parties involved to make sure that both people have a sense of balance and equality in the relationship, with each person having time and space to take care of themselves.
