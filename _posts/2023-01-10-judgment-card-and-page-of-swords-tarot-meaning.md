A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Judgment card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and the Page Of Swords tarot card suggest a period of reflection and internal exploration. It is as if you are being called to awaken from a deep sleep and discover your true purpose in life. These two cards suggest a great clarity of thought and understanding that can be achieved through a combination of focused contemplation and gathering of facts. They indicate that you must use your intelligence and curiosity to seek the truth and make a final decision about your destiny. Through self-reflection, you will discover the answer to the ultimate question: what am I meant to do?

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Judgment card is all about a final decision, so it could be that you need to make a clear decision about the path you want to take with your relationship or you might need clarity around your feelings. You are being asked to look at the situation deeply and honestly before deciding where you want to go with it.

At the same time, the Page of Swords tarot card indicates that there are some spies or prying eyes in your environment, so it could be useful to take a step back and look objectively at the situation. The truth is that you don’t know who is really on your side and you need to be aware that there are people who are not looking out for your best interests.

With these two tarot cards, it is important to trust your intuition and make sure that you are following your life’s purpose when it comes to your love life. Focus on your inner voice and make decisions based on what is best for you. Be aware of the energy that surrounds you so that you are not being swayed by outside influences. You are the only one who can decide what is right for you and sometimes it is important to take a step back and gain clarity before making a final decision.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Judgment and Page Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Judgment and Page Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

When it comes to health, The Judgment Tarot card can symbolize a need for better self-care, a reminder to live a healthier lifestyle, and an overall wake-up call to take control of one’s health. This card may also mean that a major decision or transformation is required in order to make positive changes in one’s health. It can be an opportunity to reflect upon past habits and make decisions that will bring true balance, health and wellness.

In regards to finances, The Judgment Tarot card can mean that a major decision must be made in regards to one’s finances. This decision can be difficult but necessary, and can involve re-evaluating one’s spending, savings, and overall financial goals. This can involve being honest with oneself and taking proactive steps to ensure financial success.

When it comes to relationships, The Judgment Tarot card can symbolize a need for making an important decision in regards to a relationship. This decision can involve re-evaluating the relationship, deciding if it is beneficial, or deciding to end the relationship if it is no longer beneficial. This card can also symbolize a need for honest communication, a true understanding of one’s feelings, and an overall opportunity for growth.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page of Swords Tarot card can be a warning about our physical health. It may be telling us that we need to take better care of our bodies and minds. We may need to pay more attention to our diet and exercise, or to seek medical advice if we are feeling unwell. Neglecting our physical health can lead to emotional and financial problems, so it is important to take a proactive approach to our health.

Finances: In terms of finances, the Page of Swords Tarot card tells us to be vigilant and to pay attention to the details. It may be telling us to take steps to protect our assets and to be judicious in our spending. It could be a reminder to watch out for scams or bad investments and to be conscious of how our money is being used.

Relationships: In terms of relationships, the Page of Swords Tarot card can represent a warning to be mindful of the power of words. Be mindful not to gossip or to spread malicious rumors. We should be careful not to pry into the private lives of those we care about. This card can advise us to speak kindly and honestly in our interactions with others, and to respect boundaries and privacy.
