A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Swords card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 5 Of Swords card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 5 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords tarot cards together point to a situation that is incredibly difficult and overwhelming. It could be that you are facing a situation of violence, abuse, theft and bullying, all of which can cause you to experience deep, intense and recurring nightmares, anxiety, grief and depression. This could be due to a toxic relationship or and external situation, either way it is important to remember that you are not alone and that it is possible to move through the darkness to find healing.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It can mean that your partner has a tendency to be manipulative and controlling, and that your autonomy is being compromised. They may belittle you, criticize you, and make you feel like you can’t stand up for yourself. This can be an extremely difficult situation to be in, as it can make it hard to find the strength to end the relationship.

The Nine of Swords tarot card can further complicate matters by amplifying your fears and anxieties. This card often indicates that you are struggling to make sense of your situation, and that you may be experiencing nightmares or deep-seated anxieties. Even if your partner is not directly causing these feelings, it can be hard to find the courage to leave a relationship when you are feeling so overwhelmed by fear and doubt.

Ultimately, the combination of The 5 of Swords and The 9 of Swords tarot cards can suggest that you are in an unhealthy and potentially abusive relationship. It is important for you to take the necessary steps to create a safe and supportive environment for yourself, away from this situation. Find the strength to break free of this cycle, and reclaim your autonomy.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 5 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 5 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 5 of Swords tarot card often reflects potential physical health issues that can arise when faced with an imbalanced or toxic relationship. It can indicate a situation of physical or emotional abuse, or physical violence that has burdened a person with worry and fear. It is a warning to be aware of the risk of developing chronic illnesses due to the stress of a hostile atmosphere. This can manifest in physical ailments such as headaches, migraines, and depression.

Finances:  
The 5 of Swords tarot card can signify a situation of financial insecurity caused by theft or manipulative behaviour. It can point to a situation in which someone is taking advantage of another person or using unethical techniques to acquire money. It can also indicate the potential for a sudden and unexpected financial hardship due to unforeseen circumstances.

Relationships:  
When it comes to relationships, the 5 of Swords tarot card can signify a toxic dynamic between two people. It is a warning that the relationship is heading towards a difficult situation due to tensions, anger, and resentment. It can also indicate that a bully is present in the relationship and that one of the individuals is being manipulated or taken advantage of by the other. In certain cases, it can point to the potential for physical or emotional violence. The card can also represent a situation in which one person is having difficulty letting go of a relationship.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder of the strong connection between our physical and mental health. It warns of a time of sleepless nights, plagued by dark fears, nightmares and despair. It cautions that difficulty in managing our thoughts and patterns of behavior can have a serious impact on our physical energy and our overall well-being. A feeling of helplessness and being overwhelmed can lead to physical exhaustion, depression, and even serious health issues. As such, it is important to be mindful of our mental state and to seek help if needed.

Finances: The 9 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of worry, insecurity, and fear around financial matters. It can indicate a feeling of being trapped and constrained by the limitations of our resources. Money issues can cause immense anxiety, especially when we are trying to make ends meet and secure our future. This card can also represent debts, missed payments, and the burdens of having financial obligations. It is important to face our financial reality and develop a plan of action that involves both short-term and long-term goals.

Relationships: The 9 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a time of discord, distrust, and insecurity in our relationships. It can suggest feeling powerless in the face of uncertainty and fear. This card can indicate the need for mental clarity, resilience, and honest communication in order to navigate difficult relationships. It cautions against making decisions while under the influence of fear or feelings of worthlessness. It is important to be mindful of our words and actions when it comes to interacting with those around us. Taking a moment to assess and understanding our emotions, can help us make decisions with both our minds and our hearts.
