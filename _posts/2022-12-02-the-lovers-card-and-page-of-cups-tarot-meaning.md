A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and Page Of Cups mean together?

The Lovers and The Page Of Cups tarot cards together suggest that the relationship between two people is deep, meaningful and special. One partner may be more studious and analytical, while the other has the strength to keep the two together. Together, they make a great team and can take on the world as a couple. It also suggests that a new love could be blossoming and that volunteering work may be an aspect of the relationship. All in all, the two cards together mean that whatever the two choose to do, they will be able to achieve amazing things when they are together.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

The Lovers Tarot card is a symbol of partnership, commitment and deep love. It indicates a strong connection between two people, and implies that two people working together can achieve more than either could on their own. This principle applies to our health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The Lovers Tarot card suggests the strength of two in coming together to achieve our best health. Working together with a partner, a friend or a health practitioner can help us to reach our wellness goals far more effectively than if we were to go it alone. Two heads can be far better than one in managing our well-being, so we should seek to combine our efforts in order to reach our desired outcome.

In regards to finances, The Lovers Tarot card reminds us of the importance of strong communication and collaboration in order to ensure that our finances are well managed and balanced. Working in partnership with a partner or financial expert can help us to create an effective budget and financial plan. Two minds joining forces can help us to make wiser decisions, so that we can maximize our financial potential.

When it comes to relationships, The Lovers Tarot card calls us to be open, honest and vulnerable with our most intimate connections. It encourages us to create strong, trusting bonds, and to recognize the importance of two people working together to create a meaningful relationship. The strength of two, when committed to learning and growing together, can create a foundation for a beautiful and fulfilling connection.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

This card signifies that it is an important time to focus on personal health needs and take preventative measures to protect one’s wellbeing. It can also indicate that now is the right time to examine how one’s lifestyle and diet may be affecting their health and make changes to ensure good physical and mental health.

Finances: The Page Of Cups Tarot card can have implications for one’s financial health. It suggests that one can benefit from taking stock of their finances and being mindful of where their money is being spent. Looking for new opportunities to increase earnings, or to support new business ventures, can be beneficial now. Additionally, it can suggest that now is the time to start setting financial goals and creating a budget for the future.

Relationships: The Page Of Cups Tarot card can have a positive influence on relationships. It indicates that now is the time to take an honest look at our relationships and how they are impacting our lives. It suggests that we should be open to new ways of understanding and communicating with others and embrace the gifts of different perspectives and experiences. Additionally, this card can represent a new beginning and suggest that we create a vision for how we want our relationships to grow and develop.
