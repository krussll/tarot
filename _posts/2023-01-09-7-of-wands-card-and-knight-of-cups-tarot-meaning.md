However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 7 Of Wands card and Knight Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and Knight Of Cups mean together?

The 7 of Wands tarot card and the Knight of Cups tarot card together point to a difficult situation you are in that requires a delicate balance between self-defense and romantic pursuits. You may be in a situation where someone is competing for your attention or affection, and you need to protect yourself from them by staying true to your own desires. At the same time, the Knight of Cups card encourages you to remain open to the possibility of a passionate romance if the situation is right. It is important to keep your head up and maintain a confident attitude as you navigate this complicated situation. It is also important to keep your heart open to the potential of a beautiful and special connection.

The combination of the 7 of Wands and the Knight of Cups in a love reading can signify potential for a strong and long-lasting relationship, but it is important to be careful and protect your heart until you know for sure that the person is sincere in their affections. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health:  
The 7 of Wands Tarot card is an encouraging card when it comes to health. It advises that we take all necessary steps to protect our mental and physical health. It suggests to have a good defense system in place to guard against any kind of illnesses or diseases that could threaten our health. This could mean setting up a good plan of action to maintain our well-being, such as eating healthy, taking supplements, exercising, getting enough sleep and reducing stress. We should also be sure to have a good doctor and healthcare system in place so that we can be prepared to defend against any kind of health problem that could arise.

Finances:  
The 7 of Wands Tarot card signifies an ongoing struggle for financial security. This card suggests that it is important to set up a strong defense system to protect against financial problems, such as personal debt, unexpected expenses and market fluctuations. This could mean taking a proactive stance when it comes to money management, such as creating a budget, monitoring spending and investing wisely. Additionally, it is important to have an emergency fund and other investments that can provide us with a safety net should any financial problems arise.

Relationships:  
The 7 of Wands Tarot card suggests that relationships are an area in which we must remain on guard. This card suggests that it is important to have healthy boundaries in place to protect against any kind of emotional pain or energy drain that can come from being in unhealthy relationships. It is important to recognize our own worth and be willing to stand up for ourselves when it comes to relationships. This could mean learning when it is time to walk away from a relationship or conversation that is draining us, as well as being mindful of our own feelings and maintaining healthy communication with partners.

Relationships: The Knight of Cups Tarot card is all about romance and relationships.
