However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does 7 Of Wands and Page Of Wands mean together?

The 7 of Wands and the Page of Wands together represent the combination of self-defence and creativity. The 7 of Wands suggests that we should protect ourselves and our projects against competition, being aware of the external influences that could disrupt our process. The Page of Wands encourages us to be active and take on new ideas and projects with enthusiasm and a creative approach. It is important to have a combination of self-defence and a creative drive in order to achieve our goals, as this combination will give us strength and focus to build our projects and follow our dreams.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 7 of Wands Tarot card is an important and timely reminder to us to take care of our physical and mental health. It encourages us to be mindful of our own self-care and take appropriate precautions to protect our wellbeing. It tells us that in order to remain healthy we must respect our body, nourish it with proper food, exercise and adequate rest, and maintain our emotional equilibrium. We must also remain open to new methods of healing and preventative treatments, such as yoga, meditation, and therapeutic massage.

Finances

The 7 of Wands Tarot card reminds us that financial security does not come free of risk. We must be wise and take precautions to protect our savings and investments from possible losses. It is important to compare interest rates and investment opportunities, negotiate for the best possible deal, and consult with a knowledgeable financial advisor. The card also alludes to protecting your finances from theft, fraud, and financial predators. Taking these steps will ensure that you are better able to manage and grow your wealth.

Relationships

The 7 of Wands Tarot card encourages us to nurture strong and healthy relationships in our lives. It tells us that we must be open to new and different perspectives and be willing to compromise when differences arise. We should take care to protect our relationships from negative influences and conflict, such as jealousy, gossip, and power struggles. Additionally, the card implies that we should be wise when selecting friends and partners, taking time to get to know them and establish trust before committing to them.
