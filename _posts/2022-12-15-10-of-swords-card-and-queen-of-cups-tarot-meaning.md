A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The 10 of Swords and the Queen of Cups suggest a difficult but inevitable ending that can lead to a place of healing and support. The 10 of Swords tells us that we must accept the pain of finalizing something that we have been clinging to and make peace with its passing. The Queen of Cups encourages us to seek comfort in the arms of nurturing and supportive friends, family, or strangers, in order to care for our broken hearts and nurse them back to health. This combination of cards will bring closure and companionship, allowing us to grieve and find solace in a sea of love.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This is a difficult moment, no doubt, but with the support of the Queen of Cups, you can find the emotional healing and resolution you need to move forward in your journey. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

When referring to how The Ten of Swords Tarot card affects health, it generally signifies that it is a time of ultimate stress and strain that leads to a painful and devastating conclusion. The card indicates that there is no more struggle or strength to fight off the physical and mental ailments that have been accumulating for some time. It is time to accept these issues, feel the emotions associated with the situation, and move on. This could indicate a severe illness, long-term injury or a difficult recovery process that has finally been accepted as the only option.

When referring to finances, The Ten of Swords Tarot card suggests that the situation is in a state of collapse. All attempts to repair the situation have failed and the financial losses may be insurmountable. This card is a sign that the individual must accept the losses and begin the process of rebuilding and repairing the situation, but to do so will require a radical change in attitude and behavior.

In relationships, The Ten of Swords Tarot card indicates a situation that has become so unbearable that it needs to end. The relationship may have been doomed for some time and all attempts to repair it have been exhausted. It is now time to accept that the situation is no longer salvageable and to move on from the relationship before any further damage is done. This can be a difficult decision to accept, but it is the only way to ensure that further damage is not done to all individuals involved.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health: The Queen of Cups Tarot card is a sign of good health. It represents a person who is caring and nurturing, taking time to connect with their body and spirit. It also suggests that with the right support, healing is possible. The Queen of Cups suggests that inner health and balance are of utmost importance. Self-care is key to finding physical and mental well being. On a physical level, it reminds us to nurture our bodies and trust that when we do so, our bodies will heal naturally and stay healthy.

Finances: The Queen of Cups Tarot card is an indication of financial success. It stands for prosperity and abundance, both emotionally and financially. It stands for a secure and stable financial state, where one has a handle on their day-to-day finances. With the right guidance, financial stability can be found. For those in times of financial hardship, the Queen of Cups suggests that patience and diligence will help to secure a better financial future.

Relationships: The Queen of Cups Tarot card stands for compassionate and supportive relationships. It suggests a person who is capable of giving unconditional love and acceptance to those around them. This person is capable of fostering trust and open communication between two people. The Queen of Cups is all about building meaningful connections and understanding the emotion that lies beneath the surface. She encourages us to be patient and compassionate with those around us and to take the time to nurture special relationships. The Queen of Cups also stands for the healing power of relationships, as it encourages us to tap into our own inner strength to mend broken relationships and let go of past hurts.
