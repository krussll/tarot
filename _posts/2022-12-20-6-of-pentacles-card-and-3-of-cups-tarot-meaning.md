A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 Of Pentacles and The 3 Of Cups together speak of an abundance of generosity and support. The support can be both in the form of giving and receiving help and assistance. The 6 Of Pentacles encourages us to ask for help when we need it, while the 3 Of Cups reminds us that it is important to have a circle of friends and family with whom you can celebrate, share successes and give and receive assistance and advice. This combination is a reminder that we have access to a support network of people who are ready to help us when needed.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health  
The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card means health in the sense of looking after your own wellbeing, as well as being generous in sharing your health with others. Healthwise, it means generosity in the sense of taking care of your own health and also taking care of the health of those around you. It can also mean exchanging helpful advice and healing energy with others, such as offering a listening ear, providing some helpful nutrition tips, or sharing remedies and a holistic understanding of how to keep one’s body strong and healthy.

Finances  
The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card means finances in the sense of sharing financial resources with others. It can mean generosity in the form of giving or receiving help with money and exchanging financial advice with those around you. It also can mean being conscious of what you’re spending, budgeting wisely, and avoiding overextending yourself financially. When it comes to money, this card encourages you to look at the bigger picture and consider the benefits of openly sharing resources with others.

Relationships  
The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card means relationships in the sense of generosity. It encourages being open-hearted when it comes to relationships, both in the sense of giving and receiving support. It can mean being generous with your time, energy, and emotional resources, such as lending an ear to a friend or offering some helpful advice about a difficult situation. This card also suggests sharing resources where appropriate, such as offering to help out with a project or event, or contributing to a cause or charity. It emphasizes the importance of being generous and nurturing with the relationships that you have in your life, as well as being open to receiving help from others.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups tarot card is a symbol of joy and celebration in regards to health. It indicates that with the support of friends and a circle of people around you, your health will be improved. This card is a sign that you should reach out to others, find community and build relationships with people who will provide mutual support and encouragement. It is a reminder that strength can come from forming a strong network of people who are there to support and care for you in times of need.

Finances:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card is often associated with abundance and prosperity when it comes to finances. It suggests that your finances will be well taken care of so long as you have a good support system around you. This card encourages you to reach out to close friends and family to get advice and assistance when it comes to money and finances. It is a sign that you will find the resources and resources of others beneficial in acquiring the wealth you seek.

Relationships:  
The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of community and relationships. It suggests that by surrounding yourself with a strong network of people, your relationships will be healthier and stronger. This card also encourages you to open up to others and share your feelings with them as this has a positive impact on the quality of your relationships. It is important to reach out and build meaningful connections in order to find the love and happiness you desire. It is a sign of celebration, joy and support and is a reminder that a strong circle of people can be a great source of strength and stability.
