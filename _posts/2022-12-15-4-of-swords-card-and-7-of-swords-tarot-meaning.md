A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The 4 of Swords and the 7 of Swords together represent a time of rest and preparation that is necessary in order to move forward in life. This time is often a time of contemplation and withdrawing from the world to take time to reflect on yourself and your surroundings. The 7 of Swords in this context is a warning not to leave yourself vulnerable to betrayal and theft in your time of solitude. It is important to rest and renew, but to also remain alert and aware of any potential pitfalls during the process.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Balance the messages of the 4 Of Swords and The 7 Of Swords, and focus on healing, rest, and renewal, while also being aware of potential dishonesty and theft in your life. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is associated with the need for retreat, rest and renewal. This card is an indication that it is time to step back from our current situation and to take a break from the hustle and bustle of our lives. It encourages us to seek out avenues of solitude and respite, whether this be through physical or mental activities. It is a reminder that our bodies, minds and spirits often require rest in order to remain healthy and in balance.

When it comes to health, The 4 of Swords Tarot card reminds us of the importance of taking the necessary measures to ensure that our bodies and minds are given a chance to recover and recuperate. This could mean anything from getting adequate sleep, to taking regular breaks from exercise, to appointing time to relax and meditate. It is essential that we recognise and honour our own need for rest to maintain physical and mental wellbeing.

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is also associated with finances. It suggests that, if our financial situation is feeling overwhelming, it may be time to step back and look at the bigger picture. This card reminds us to take a holistic approach when dealing with our finances, to think about our long-term goals and to make sure we are doing all we can to ensure our financial security. It is a sign that, with the right plan in place, the strain of the current situation will eventually pass.

Finally, The 4 of Swords Tarot card is associated with relationships. It suggests that, if we feel that our relationships are becoming too strained, it might be time to take a break and to grow in solitude. This card encourages us to reflect on our individual needs and to take a step back if we feel that our own needs are not being sufficiently addressed. It is a reminder that, although relationships are important, there is a need to take care of our own selves first and foremost.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card typically represents a period of dishonesty and betrayal in one’s life, so it follows that has implications for one’s health, finances and relationships.

For health, the 7 of Swords Tarot card indicates that one may be facing some sort of physical or mental health struggle. It is possible that the person is trying to cope with the stress or strain of an unhealthy relationship or lifestyle and is looking for a way out. It could also be a warning sign that one is at a breaking point due to the dishonesty and betrayal they have experienced. If this is the case, it’s important to seek help and find ways to work on one’s mental health and well-being.

For finances, the 7 of Swords Tarot card serves as a reminder that it is important to be careful and mindful of one’s finances. This card may also be a sign that one is in the midst of a financial crisis due to deception or theft. It is important to take action and look for ways to minimize the damage and start rebuilding.

For relationships, the 7 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that one should be mindful of their thoughts and actions when it comes to their relationships. The card indicates that deception and dishonesty are present and as a result, one should be careful when dealing with others. It is possible that the person is in a toxic relationship and is looking for a way out. It is also possible that the person is betrayal-prone and needs to start looking within themselves and to take responsibility for their actions.
