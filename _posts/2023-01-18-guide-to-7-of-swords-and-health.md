## 7 Of Swords And Health – An Overview

The 7 Of Swords tarot card is a warning that health issues may arise due to dishonesty and betrayal.

It suggests that we should make sure that we are honest and truthful with ourselves and others when it comes to our health and well-being.

We should take responsibility for our own health, and not rely on anyone else for our well-being.

We should also be careful to avoid any form of deceit or betrayal that could have a negative effect on our health.

By being honest and taking responsibility for our own health, we can ensure that we are taking care of our physical, mental and emotional well-being.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Swords And Health

When the 7 of Swords is reversed, it suggests that a person’s health may have been undermined by some form of dishonesty or betrayal.
