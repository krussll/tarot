A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 10 Of Swords mean together?

Drawing both The Judgment and The 10 Of Swords tarot cards together can mean a difficult but necessary ending has been reached in your life, and you are now going through a powerful time of awakening and clarity. Your life’s purpose is emerging from the ashes of this hard ending, and you can start to see the bigger picture of what comes next. Although the pain is sharp and deep, there is the hope of a fresh start with a newfound understanding of what is important to you and what your true calling is.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health:

The Judgment Tarot card means it is time to take a look at your health and make decisions about how to best proceed. It may be time to assess your current lifestyle and make changes that will improve your long-term wellbeing. This could involve making dietary changes, getting more exercise, or consulting with a medical professional. It’s important to reflect on how your lifestyle is impacting your health and make a conscious effort to improve it. Taking ownership of your health and making decisions that prioritize self-care and wellbeing will be beneficial in the long run.

Finances:

When it comes to finances, the Judgment Tarot card signals that it is time to make a final decision about how to manage your finances. This could involve working out a budget, setting financial goals, or figuring out a plan to get out of debt. It is important to take a hard look at your current financial situation and be honest about where you are. It may be difficult, but taking ownership of your finances is essential for reaching a place of financial security and stability.

Relationships:

The Judgment Tarot card calls for an honest assessment of any relationships in your life. It can bring clarity and insight into the dynamics of your relationships and help you make decisions about how to proceed. Taking ownership of the situation and being honest with yourself can help you identify any areas in need of improvement. This could involve initiating or clarifying communication or simply recognizing boundaries. No matter what, it’s important to make decisions that are best for you and prioritize your own wellbeing. Taking ownership of your relationships and developing healthy communication is key to maintaining strong and secure relationships.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 10 of Swords Tarot card indicates that when it comes to health, a person may be at the worst possible point. It is the worst situation for a person to be in, as it means that the person has almost no hope for the current situation. It may be the worst possible diagnosis up to this point, or it may mean that a person’s body has reached the limit of its ability to heal. This card signals a need to take stock of an individual’s health worries, and to make the necessary decisions to address any health issues.

Finances

The 10 of Swords Tarot card is often a sign of financial despair, as it indicates that a person has hit rock bottom and may not be able to get back up. The person may have accumulated a large amount of debt and their financial situation may seem hopeless. This situation signals the need to take a hard look at one’s finances, and to make the necessary changes, such as budgeting and cutting back on spending, to get back on track.

Relationships

The 10 of Swords Tarot card can indicate that a person’s relationships have reached a dead end. It may mean that the person is in a toxic relationship, or it could mean that the person has gone through a painful break up. It signals a need for the person to take a step back and evaluate the situation, and to make the necessary changes to move forward in a healthy way. The person may need to take some time alone to heal and to gain clarity on what their next steps should be.
