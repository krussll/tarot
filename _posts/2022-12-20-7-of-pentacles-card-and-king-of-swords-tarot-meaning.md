A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and King Of Swords mean together?

The 7 Of Pentacles and The King of Swords tarot card indicate that patience and hard work are essential in achieving success. You may need to wait a while before you can reap the rewards of your efforts, but if you are able to stay focused and remain intentional in your actions, you’ll be able to strategize the best ways to make your dreams a reality. It is important to remember that success rarely happens overnight, and the partnership of these two cards highlights the importance of dedication to your craft and taking the time to make sure your decisions are smart ones.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that health requires patience, hard work, and being willing to wait for delayed success. It means recognizing that good health doesn’t happen overnight and that there may be times when progress is slow. It’s a reminder to be patient with your body, to listen to its needs, and to take the time to do the things that will get you closer to optimal health. Whether that means taking time to exercise, engaging in relaxation techniques, or eating whole and healthy foods – it all takes time and effort.

Finances: The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can also represent delayed success with finances. It can be interpreted as a sign to focus on saving and saving wisely, as well as being patient with any investments you make. It is suggesting that long-term success requires patience, hard work, and a strong and disciplined approach to finances. Take the time to develop a budget, create an emergency fund, or be smart with retirement savings. All of these require patience and dedication, but the rewards for doing so can be great.

Relationships: When it comes to relationships, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is all about being patient and willing to put in the effort to make a relationship successful. It is a reminder that to find long-term success, you need to be willing to work hard and nurture the connection. It means being willing to put in the time to listen, to talk, and to be understanding. Even when things move slowly, it’s important to stay focused on the big picture and the goals you have for the relationship. It also suggests that success may not come right away, but it will come with patience and hard work.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health: The King of Swords Tarot card signifies a need to take control of ones health. This could mean researching and studying ways to live a healthier life, learning more about nutrition and exercise, or taking measures to create a more balanced lifestyle. It also implies that a disciplined and focused approach is necessary to make lasting changes and achieve ones health goals. It may also indicate that it is important to practice self-care and to focus on one’s own needs, rather than trying to please or to take care of others.

Finances: The King of Swords Tarot card signifies the need for careful financial planning. This could mean budgeting, setting up a savings plan and/or investing, or looking at ways to reduce expenses. It also suggests that it is important to be thoughtful and intentional about how one’s money is being used, and to approach financial decisions logically and strategically. This card implies that success in finances is possible with careful thought, research and planning.

Relationships: The King of Swords Tarot card signifies the need for a thoughtful and strategic approach when it comes to relationships. This could mean setting boundaries, communicating more effectively and openly, and being honest in one’s interactions. It also suggests that it is important to remain objective and logical in personal relationships, rather than letting emotions and desires dictate decisions. This card implies that successful relationships can be achieved through tactical communication and honest self-reflection.
