A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Pentacles and the 8 Of Swords tarot cards mean there is a need for collaboration, skill, and quality to break through self-imposed restrictions and isolation. Working together in a team setting and drawing on the combined ingenuity and creativity of all parties can help to find constructive solutions to the obstructions we have placed upon ourselves, allowing us to break free and gain the freedom we need. Quality craftsmanship is essential to accomplishing this vital endeavor and can be achieved through the vibrant teamwork of many.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a period of self-imposed restriction and isolation in regards to health. This suggests a period in which an individual may not be receiving the optimal amount of care or nourishment that their body needs to maintain optimal health. It could suggest a period of worry and fear in which an individual may forgo activities, medical care, and therapies that could be beneficial for their health. It could also symbolize someone placing too much emphasis on negative ideas and feelings, causing them to restrict their lifestyle. Furthermore, it could symbolize an individual who is imprisoned by their own negative thoughts, unable to break out of this unhelpful mindset.

Finances:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a period in which an individual is stuck in an endless cycle of financial difficulties and hardships. This could be caused by the individual’s own bad financial choices, or it could be due to a feeling of powerlessness in the face of financial pressures. The 8 of Swords could indicate a sense of isolation, in which someone is blocked off from the resources they need to make sound financial decisions. It could also symbolize someone who is restricting themselves from actively pursuing any financial opportunities that could potentially be beneficial in the long run.

Relationships:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a period of self-imposed isolation in regards to relationships. This suggested a reluctance to open up to and engage with potential partners and those around them, leading to a feeling of loneliness and isolation. It could also symbolize a need to break away from unhealthy relationships and emotionally restrictive dynamics. Furthermore, it could symbolize someone who is imprisoned by their own negative thought patterns, unable to see beyond their own limiting beliefs. Finally, it could represent a feeling of being stuck in an unpleasant situation due to a fear of the potential consequences that come along with taking a risk.
