A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and 3 Of Swords tarot card represent a time of personal reflection and understanding when it comes to life decisions. The Judgment card speaks to a need to arrive at a final and clear decision. It could be a wake-up call to your life’s purpose and a call to action or a time of self-reflection. The 3 of Swords can represent painful situations that force us to make difficult decisions, such as heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression and surgery. Together, these two cards can signify a time of personal growth and honesty. They can be a reminder that we need to take a stand for ourselves and make tough choices so we can move forward and create a fulfilled and happier life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Judgment and 3 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The Judgment tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card appear together in a reading about your love life, it’s a sign that a difficult period of heartbreak and loss is almost over. It’s a wake-up call that you may have to make a final decision to move on and accept a difficult situation. It could signal that a painful divorce, breakup, or separation may be on the horizon, but at the same time, it also could mean that you have grown and learned from the experiences and will soon be on the way to finding clarity.

The Judgment tarot card associated with The 3 Of Swords tarot card can also be seen as encouragement that you are on your path to finding your life’s purpose. It’s a reminder that even though there may be loss, you will eventually reach a place of clarity. After going through the tough times, you will be able to look back on the experience with a newfound perspective, and be better equipped to pursue your ambitions and dreams.

The Judgment tarot card indicates that in order to move on and make decisions that are in your best interest, you must learn from your past experiences. You may experience depression or sadness during this difficult time, but ultimately, it’s a sign that you’re ready to take the next step in your life. The 3 Of Swords tarot card is a warning that you must prepare for the impending heartbreak, but The Judgment tarot card will help guide you through it.

### What does Judgment and 3 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

The Judgement card and the 3 of Swords card together represent an awakening, as well as a difficult period. The Judgement card suggests a clarity and finality that can have a transformative effect. While this could mean that a difficult decision has been made, it could also indicate that you may be set to take new, positive steps towards the future. On the other hand, the 3 of Swords card could indicate that this decision may have caused heartbreak, depression or loss.

When it comes to finances and job, the Judgement card and 3 of Swords card could mean that a difficult decision may have been made about your career. This could involve a career change, a layoff, or a move to a new city. On the other hand, it could also mean that you might have decided to take control of your finances and put yourself on a path to financial security. While this could be a step in the right direction, it could also have resulted in loss or heartbreak.

No matter the situation, the Judgement card and 3 of Swords card together could represent a period of sadness and difficulty. However, it could also suggest a time of new beginnings and potential. It is important to remain open to the possibilities, as well as to trust in yourself and your decisions. With hard work and dedication, you may be able to turn this difficult time into an opportunity to get your finances and career back on track.

This card encourages the individual to not run away from their problems and to instead approach them with courage and an open heart.

When these two cards come together, they can represent a crucial period in one’s life that could lead to great clarity regarding the individual’s health and wellbeing. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and 3 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card is an indication that it is time for an evaluation of our health, finances, and relationships. It signifies a time of clarity and the need to make a final decision. This card calls for the soul to embrace our life’s purpose and to take charge of our lives.

When it comes to health, The Judgment card is a wake-up call. It indicates that it is time to evaluate our health, check-in with our body and determine if we are on the right track. It is time assess if our current habits or lifestyle is helping or hindering our wellbeing. Questions we need to ask ourselves may include, “Am I getting enough rest?”, “Am I eating a balanced diet?”, and “Am I doing the things I need to do to stay healthy?”. This card is a call to action; we must make a conscious effort to improve our health so that we can be the best version of ourselves.

The Judgment card also encourages us to review our finances and take charge of our finances. We are encouraged to evaluate our current financial situation, to determine where we are spending and saving our money, and to create a plan on how to best manage our funds. This card advises us to make sound financial decisions, create a budget and to focus on our long-term financial goals.

Finally, The Judgment card is also an indication of a need to assess our relationships and whether or not they are fulfilling and supportive. We are encouraged to identify which relationships are positive and which relationships need to be addressed or improved. It is also a reminder to be honest and open with our communication, to be mindful of how we interact with those around us, and to put in effort to develop healthy relationships. This card is a call for us to take responsibility for our relationships and to determine if it serves our growth, our mind and our heart.

The Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to evaluate our health, finances and relationships. It is a clarifying call to align ourselves with our life’s purpose and to make decisions that serve our highest and best good.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords tarot card is one of the most difficult cards to interpret and is associated with heartache, loss, depression and separation. This card speaks of grief, loss and a feeling of emptiness, both emotionally and spiritually. As such, it can be seen as a warning sign to those who are experiencing a difficult period in their life.

Health-wise, the 3 of Swords can indicate physical and mental health issues, such as depression, anxiety, or mental exhaustion. It can also signify surgery, healing and recovery from an illness or injury. The card can be a warning sign that one’s health may be at risk or that healing needs to take place. It is important to listen to your body and pay attention to any signs of trouble before they become bigger issues.

Financially, the 3 of Swords can signify debt, loss of money, or expenses which are difficult to manage. It can indicate that sacrifices may need to be made in order to stay afloat, or that help may be needed in order to keep afloat. Prudence and good management may be required in order to get through the tough times.

Relationship-wise, the 3 of Swords can point to break-ups, divorce, or the experience of grief and sadness due to the loss of a loved one. It can also represent feelings of betrayal, or a lack of trust between partners. This card can be a reminder to be mindful of the feelings of those around you and to communicate honestly and openly with those in your life.

Overall, the 3 of Swords tarot card can be a difficult card to interpret and can signify many different things depending on the situation. It is important to pay attention to these warning signs and take action to address any issues that arise. By doing so, one can move through difficult times and come out the other side with a renewed sense of hope and optimism.
