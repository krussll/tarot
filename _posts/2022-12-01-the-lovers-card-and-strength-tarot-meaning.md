A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and Strength card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and Strength have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and Strength mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The Strength tarot card speak to the power and beauty of togetherness, and how true partnership can draw forth more love than we could ever find on our own. When these two cards appear together, it’s a reminder that love is stronger and more beautiful when shared, and that they can both give and receive it. But within that partnership, there also needs to be an outlet for self-love and self-respect as well. The Strength tarot card is there to remind us that we must first love ourselves in order to be able to share and receive love. Together, these two cards offer a strong message of the unconditional love of a successful partnership and the strength that comes from self-love and respectful relationships.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

When it comes to health, the Lovers Tarot card indicates that there is strength in numbers. Partners can benefit from understanding each other’s health needs and supporting each other by providing emotional and physical support. This card can also signify making a commitment to take better care of one’s health through treatments, therapies or lifestyle changes. This can be a difficult challenge, but doing it together helps to ensure that it is sustainable over time.

Financially, the Lovers Tarot card is a reminder to work together to combine resources and share expenses. This card can reflect a shared commitment to financial security, with both partners working towards a common goal. This card can also symbolize learning financial lessons together, including budgeting, investing and learning the basics of financial planning. It can also highlight the importance of having clear communication about money between partners and avoiding arguments about finances.

In relationships, the Lovers Tarot card is a reminder to maintain a strong sense of commitment and interdependence. This card represents two individuals coming together to create something special. It is a reminder to cherish the relationship, appreciate each other and show appreciation for the other person’s efforts. This card also symbolizes making decisions together, respecting each other’s opinions and supporting each other’s dreams and aspirations.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is associated with self-love, unconditional love, self-respect, and courage. This card signifies that one must have the strength to utilize self-love to build relationships, protect and restore health, and manage finances.

When speaking of relationships, the Strength Tarot card implies that one must build relationships with an open heart, willing to give and receive love. In order to have and nurture healthy relationships, one must have the courage to embrace the hard times that come with relationships, understanding that love is not always perfect. One should also bring their confidence, resilience and courage to the table, in order to be a loving partner.

In terms of health, the Strength Tarot card encourages self-care and self-love. Taking the time to invest in one’s mental, spiritual and physical health is essential for maximum energy and positive functioning. Building and maintaining healthy, healing habits such as regular exercise, eating well, and meditating, will help one to maintain balance and stay in tune with his/her body.

Finally, when it comes to finances, the Strength Tarot card encourages self-discipline and a sense of stewardship. Making mindful choices with spending and investing money wisely, while also protecting and managing money properly, is key to financial success. Setting goals, learning to save, planning investments, and building emergency funds are all important elements of having a secure financial future.

In summary, the Strength Tarot card encourages us to embody self-love, unconditional love, self-respect and courage in order to build healthy relationships, nurture physical, mental, and spiritual health, and manage finances. The Strength Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of taking care of oneself, in order to bring stability, growth and true success in life.
