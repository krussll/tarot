In order to look forward, you must be aware of your past and be willing to stand up for what you value and cherish. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands Tarot card holds special insight into various areas of life, all of which play important roles in our overall wellbeing. When it comes to assessing our health, the 7 of Wands is a reminder that we need to learn to defend ourselves when we are feeling vulnerable. By focusing on our own health, we can set positive boundaries that protect us from the physical and emotional stress that can come from unhealthy relationships, environments and lifestyle choices.

When it comes to our finances, the 7 of Wands is a warning to be aware of competitive forces that may be looking to take advantage of us. This card can be a reminder to not be swayed by clever marketing, to always read the fine print, and to make sure that our investments are managed responsibly. By taking the time to research our options, we can confidently choose the right decisions to make regarding our financial future.

Finally, the 7 of Wands can help us approach our relationships with strength and resilience. You may find yourself having to protect yourself from a partner who is overly competitive or aggressive. This card can remind us to stand our ground and set healthy boundaries in our interactions. Being strong in our relationships ultimately means having the courage to stand up for our values and beliefs, and to not let anyone take advantage of our kindness.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

The 6 Of Cups Tarot card is associated with nostalgia, gifts, innocence and reunions. While these themes can have a positive effect on our lives, it’s important to consider how they relate to our health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The 6 Of Cups Tarot card signifies a reminder of the importance of taking time off to relax and reconnect with our bodies. We should make sure to take moments throughout the day and week to ground ourselves in mindfulness and appreciation for our physical and mental well-being. This is especially important for those of us who are constantly on the go or overwhelmed with stress. Taking time off to simply appreciate the present moment can have tremendous benefits for our health.

When it comes to finances, the 6 Of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a renewed appreciation for the simple things in life. We should be mindful to not get so caught up in the pursuit of material wealth that we forget to enjoy the small pleasures in life. A reminder to stay conscious of our spending habits and be mindful of what we need versus what we simply want can be beneficial to our financial health.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The 6 Of Cups Tarot card signifies innocence and joy. It is a reminder to practice and nurture love and kindness in all relationships, no matter how deep or superficial the connection. Taking the time to reconnect with old friends and family members can bring back a sense of nostalgia and remind us of the important people in our lives. This attitude of reconnection, love and appreciation can lead to greater harmony in our lives and relationships.
