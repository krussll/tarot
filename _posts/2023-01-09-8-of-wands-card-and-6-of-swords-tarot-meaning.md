However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 8 of Wands Tarot card indicates that positive change is coming in terms of health. The card could represent arriving good news about a health issue, or a sudden reversal in one’s health. It could mean rapid healing, or increased energy. It could also indicate an unexpected medical breakthrough or a new development in terms of prevention or treatment of a medical condition.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the 8 of Wands Tarot card can be interpreted as a sign of long-awaited good news on the financial front. It could indicate an increase in income, or a new job opportunity. It might also represent investments that mature quickly, or a sudden influx of wealth. Whatever the form, the 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign that something is coming that will significantly improve one’s financial situation.

Relationships: In terms of relationships, the 8 of Wands Tarot card can be interpreted as the beginning of a new, more positive phase. It could signify a stronger connection between two people, or a sudden realization that the bond between them is much deeper than either of them realized. It might also mean that an argument between two people is quickly resolved, and that a stronger, healthier relationship is on the horizon. The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign that positive change and growth are imminent in any type of relationship.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 6 of Swords is an indication that transitions in health and well-being are possible, but there is often the need to take a step back and review current lifestyle habits. It could be time for a change in diet, exercise plan, or even a change in environment such as a move to a healthier area. This card can also symbolise a journey of self-discovery, encouraging you to explore the depths of your soul and make changes in lifestyle habits or attitude, as well as embarking on a spiritual journey.

Finances: The 6 of Swords meaning reveals a change in financial circumstances. It could be hinting at a need to review and alter your current budgeting habits, or it could be pointing at opportunities to increase your income. This card could also be suggesting that it is time to take a chance and invest in something, including yourself. Take calculated risks and research any opportunities you come across carefully.

Relationships: This card can signify a fresh start in relationships, suggesting that a transition is possible. The 6 of Swords as a relationship card often points towards a journey of understanding and reaching a new understanding of each other. If a relationship has been stale, it may be time to step back and work out ways to move to a healthier place. Partners can also use this card as a reminder to look internally and understand themselves – allowing them to better understand those around them. Additionally, this card can suggest the need for honest communication between partners, and the importance of being open and vulnerable in order to build long-lasting relationships.
