## Temperance – An Overview

The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to us of the importance of finding balance and harmony in our lives. It speaks to finding the right combination of patience, self-control, and moderation in all aspects of life. This card encourages us to recognize when we are too extreme in any one area and to strive for balance in order to create an overall state of peace, stability, and contentment. Temperance also brings a reminder that sometimes it is best to take a step back and take a moment to think and reflect on our decisions. Standing still and pausing to assess our feelings helps us find a path of moderation and self-control. Acting with temperance helps us to create a more balanced and harmonious life.

## Upright Meanings Of Temperance

### What It Means For Me

The Temperance tarot card is a reminder that I need to practice balance, harmony, patience, and self-control in my life. It encourages me to find a balance between the different aspects of my life, as well as to be mindful of how I react to different situations. It also reminds me to take the time to pause and reflect on the choices I make, and to consider the consequences of my decisions. Self-control is a key concept here, as it allows me to remain calm and composed even in the face of adversity. Temperance calls upon me to be mindful of how my actions might affect others, and to strive to live a life of harmony, balance, and compassion. Ultimately, this tarot card is a reminder that life is a journey, and that I should strive to live my life in a balanced, harmonious way.

### What It Means For The Future

The Temperance tarot card offers a glimpse into my future, suggesting that I should focus on achieving balance, harmony, patience and self-control. It indicates that I should take a step back and look at all angles before making decisions, and strive to find balance between the different aspects of my life. This card also suggests that I should strive to be more moderate in my behavior and decisions, so that I can create an equilibrium in my life. With patience, understanding and discipline, I can achieve a sense of inner peace and balance which will lead to success.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Temperance tarot card encourages me to be mindful of the balance and harmony in my relationships with those close to me. It reminds me to remain patient and to practice self-control, especially when emotions run high. It suggests that I take a step back and think before I act, in order to create a sense of peace and understanding between myself and the people I care about. The Temperance reminds me that relationships require hard work and attention in order to be healthy, and encourages me to be mindful of the choices I make and the words I speak.

## Reversed Meanings Of Temperance

### What It Means For Me

When the Temperance card appears reversed in a tarot reading, it typically suggests that I am experiencing a lack of balance in my life. It could indicate that I’m out of sync with the natural rhythms of life, and feeling overwhelmed by the chaos of the world around me. I may be having difficulty finding a middle ground between my own wants and needs and the needs of those around me.

The reversed Temperance card is also a sign that I might be struggling with issues like impatience, stubbornness, and impulsiveness. I may be having difficulty controlling my behavior and emotions. I may be overindulging in an unhealthy habit or behavior, or struggling to maintain healthy boundaries in my relationships. This card warns me to be mindful that my behavior is not throwing me out of balance. It’s time to take a step back, take a deep breath, and get back in touch with my inner harmony.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Temperance card suggests a period of imbalance in my future. It indicates that I will be struggling to find the harmony and balance I need in my life and will be tempted to act rashly and impulsively. This could be because of a difficult situation I am facing or because of a lack of self-control. This could lead to me making some choices I will later regret.

However, the reversed Temperance also suggests that I can learn to regain my balance if I can practice patience and self-control. I should take the time to evaluate my decisions and consider the long-term consequences before I act. By taking the time to think before I act and practice self-control, I can learn to create the balance I need in my future and steer away from the potential pitfalls of impulsivity.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the Temperance card appears in a reverse position, it can be an indication that balance, harmony, patience and self-control are lacking in the life of someone close to me. It could be telling me that the person is too focused on one thing or situation, and is having difficulty seeing the bigger picture. It also could mean that this person is focused on the short-term rather than the long-term benefits of a situation, leading to a lack of balance and harmony.

The reversed Temperance card could also be a warning that someone close to me is being too impulsive or rash in their decisions and actions, leading to a lack of patience and self-control. This person could be quick to anger, quick to react, or even too quick to make decisions without thinking them through. This could lead to them making mistakes and missing out on opportunities that could have been beneficial to them.

Overall, the reversed Temperance card is a warning to be mindful of the decisions and actions of those close to me, and to help them find balance and harmony in their lives. Offering patience and understanding could help this person regain their sense of self-control and make better decisions in the long run.
