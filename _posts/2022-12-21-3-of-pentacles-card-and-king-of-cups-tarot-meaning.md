However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and King Of Cups mean together?

The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card indicate that collaboration and teamwork are key to achieving success. With the King Of Cups tarot card, there is a supportive leader to help guide and inspire the team, as well as an emphasis on having empathy towards others. The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card encourages the team to focus on each person’s skills and craftsmanship, so that the end product will be of the highest quality. By combining the qualities of both cards, you can create a harmonious and productive environment where collaboration is valued, and everyone is respected.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

The King Of Cups Tarot card is often seen as a card of balance and harmony. For individuals, this card can represent an individual who is a supportive leader and maintain a sense of balance in all areas of life. In terms of health, this card represents a strong, healthy and balanced mindset that is necessary for any individual to have. This individual will maintain a sense of understanding and compassion when dealing with physical and mental health issues. Furthermore, they will be able to recognize their own emotions while also understanding the emotions of those around them.

In terms of finances, The King Of Cups Tarot card represents a person who is reasonably fiscally responsible. They will strive to maintain a sense of balance and stability when it comes to finances, understanding that there must be a balance between spending and saving. They will also be able to keep an eye on their budget in order to ensure that all obligations are met.

In terms of relationships, the King Of Cups card represents an individual who is tolerant and understanding of the people in their life. They will be patient with those around them, understanding that everyone is on their own paths. This individual will be able to remain impartial and understanding, able to listen and offer support when needed. They will strive to maintain balance and harmony in all of their relationships and will be a source of stability for those close to them.
