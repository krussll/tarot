## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

For health, The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages you to really take the time to master your craft and practice good health habits. This card is an indicator that to achieve true long-term health, one must take the time to develop healthy habits and avoid developing an unhealthy attachment to their habits. Learning about and understanding our health, and how to stay healthy, is a life-long process and requires dedication and discipline. As we learn better ways to nourish our bodies, eat healthy, and stay active, our health and well-being will benefit.

For finances, The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card stresses the importance of mastering your craft and taking the time to develop a plan that will help you build and maintain your financial security. This card encourages you to look for job opportunities that will bring you satisfaction and focus on financial education. Taking the time to properly plan your finances and learning how to make wise decisions when it comes to your financial security will provide you with greater peace of mind and stability.

As for relationships, The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card reminds us that if we are to grow and nurture relationships, we must first learn how to master our own craft. This card encourages us to take the time to learn the details of what makes a relationship successful and take the time to develop the skills needed to foster meaningful relationships. This includes learning how to communicate effectively, setting healthy boundaries, and learning how to properly express and handle our emotions. With patience and dedication, we can build and maintain meaningful relationships.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

The Ace of Cups Tarot card relates to emotions, so it can be a positive sign related to health, finances, and relationships.

Health: The Ace of Cups Tarot card can signify new beginnings when it comes to health. This card can be a sign of a new start to a healthier lifestyle or a renewed dedication to physical and mental wellbeing. It can also suggest that your health is improving and that you’re starting to feel better.

Finances: The Ace of Cups can signify an improvement in finances. It might be a sign of a financial windfall, a promotion, a new job offer, or the start of a new business venture. It’s also a sign that you’re becoming more mindful of how you spend your money and more responsible with your finances.

Relationships: The Ace of Cups Tarot card is connected to relationships, particularly the start of a new one. It could mean that a proposal or marriage is on the horizon. It could also suggest a renewed connection with an existing relationship or a new spark of passion for your partner. This card can also signify forming a connection with a potential new romantic interest.
