A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

Being patient and taking time to rest and recover will help you make the most of the financial security you’re seeking. Taking a break and focusing on self-care will also provide you with the energy and motivation you need to pursue your carefully thought-out goals.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

It implies that whatever health issues you may have been facing can be overcome through persistence and careful planning. You may need to take a step back from certain unhealthy habits, such as smoking or drinking, in order to find balance and success. With the energy of this card, you can stay motivated to work hard towards your health goals and come out of any current health struggles with a sense of renewal and victory.

When looking at your finances, The Page of Pentacles tarot card could be a sign that you are going to be experiencing a shift in the way that your finances flow. For those who are job searching, The Page of Pentacles could signal a new job offer or a raise. For those who have recently started a new job, The Page of Pentacles could mean that you will be receiving financial reward for your efforts. It also suggests that long-term investments or savings plans should be explored and nurtured to ensure that your wealth grows.

In terms of relationships, The Page of Pentacles tarot card suggests patience and persistence. This card could be a sign that with work and dedication, the two of you can overcome any obstacles that may stand in your way. It’s a reminder that, with the right attitude, the two of you can create a solid foundation for your relationship and make it last for the long haul. The Page of Pentacles also indicates that you may need to take some time to invest in yourself, so that you can bring balance and growth into your relationship.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card suggests that the best way to restore one’s health is to take a break from their current routine and take the time to rest and renew. This doesn’t necessarily mean taking a long vacation, but rather taking some time to relax and recuperate both mentally and physically. The card encourages one to give themselves the necessary time to recover and rejuvenate their physical and mental health.

Finances  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card encourages one to step back and take a break from their current financial circumstances. Taking a pause from their financial pursuits gives one the time to re-evaluate their current situation and to assess their current financial strategies. This valuable time can help one to figure out what they can do to improve their financial situation and increase their wealth, while still keeping their spending in check.

Relationships  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card signals a time of rest and renewal for relationships. This doesn’t necessarily mean to cut off communication or turn away from loved ones, but rather to take a step back and assess the current situation in order to see what can be improved. Taking the time to reflect can help to restore relationships that have become strained in the heat of the moment. It’s also helpful to take the time to re-establish boundaries and to find a better balance between what each person can give to the relationship. Taking a break can also help to build a stronger connection and to create an open forum in which both parties are more likely to be honest, open and understanding with one another.
