However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health:  
The Hierophant Tarot card indicates that a traditional approach to health and wellbeing is best. It suggests that using conventional methods such as a healthy diet, exercise and getting enough rest are essential for maintaining overall health. It also suggests that seeking guidance from professionals and leaning into support systems such as a close network of friends and family can be beneficial.

Finances:  
The Hierophant Tarot card indicates that success in finances requires the implementation of tried and true methods. It suggests that building a network of professionals such as accountants, lawyers, and financial advisors may be beneficial. Additionally, it suggests that taking an organized and structured approach to managing finances and taxes is important for long-term success.

Relationships:  
The Hierophant Tarot card indicates that traditional values place importance on relationships. It suggests that forging strong, healthy relationships requires honest communication, respect, and trust. It also suggests that taking the time to nurture relationships and make compromises is essential for keeping any bond healthy and strong. Additionally, it suggests that seeking out counseling or guidance may be beneficial during times of difficulty.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace of Pentacles tarot card is symbolic of new opportunities, both financial and material. It also stands for the potential and possibilities that come when one is open to creating a more abundant and prosperous life. From a financial perspective, it suggests that new and profitable possibilities are now available to the person. This could come in the form of a new job, a raise, a promotion, an investment, or a new income stream. All of these potentialities point to more financial security and abundance for the person.

When it comes to health, the Ace of Pentacles tarot card stands for taking care of one’s body and mind, and creating a lifestyle that supports that care. It is a reminder to nourish the body with healthy food, to move regularly and often, and to get enough rest. It also stands for having a positive attitude and perspective on life, opening up to the possibilities that come with good health and wellbeing.

When considering relationships, the Ace of Pentacles tarot card is a reminder to be open to new possibilities. Taking the chance on someone can be a risk, but with the Ace of Pentacles, there is confidence that the risk will be worthwhile. It is a reminder to create relationships that can bring abundance of joy, love and understanding.

In conclusion, the Ace of Pentacles tarot card is a reminder to trust the universe and take a chance on creating a more abundant and prosperous life. Whether it’s in regards to health, finances, or relationships, the potential for abundance and prosperity is there, as long as we remain open-minded and willing to take the chance.
