## What does 5 Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 5 Of Swords and The 7 Of Cups tarot cards suggest a situation of indecision, internal conflict and difficulty in committing to one path. This could reflect a pattern of behaviour in which one is confronted with a difficult choice, such as a relationship or a career move, and backs down due to fear or an unwillingness to confront the situation. It could also suggest a tendency to become involved in circumstances that have the potential to become dangerous or abusive, such as being in a relationship with someone who is prone to bullying or violence. Regardless of the specific situation, this combination of cards encourages one to face the hard choices courageously and commit to projects or relationships with authenticity and integrity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

The 7 of Cups Tarot card is a card that speaks to the idea of too many choices and commitment issues. In terms of health, this card is indicative of a lack of focus in taking care of one’s health. For instance, a person may struggle with making decisions about what kind of exercise to do and how to eat, or may struggle with following through on a plan for healthy living. It is important for those with this card in their reading to take time to really consider their options and choose one plan to focus on as opposed to trying to do everything at once.

In terms of finances, the 7 of Cups Tarot card suggests an inability to commit to a specific budget or financial plan. This card implies that a person is unable to determine what really matters to him or her financially, and instead spends haphazardly or keeps their finances in disarray. It is important for those with this card in their reading to take the time to assess their financial situation and make decisions that are in line with their values.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the 7 of Cups Tarot card tells us that a person may have a difficult time making commitments to others. This could manifest in a person bouncing from relationship to relationship, unable to decide who is truly worth their time, or it could mean that a person is hesitant to commit to a lasting relationship because of fear of the unknown. It is important for those with this card in their reading to take their time in finding someone that meets their needs and to get to know a person before committing to them.

Overall, the 7 of Cups Tarot card indicates that a person is unable to commit to choices in all areas of life. This often leads to feelings of being overwhelmed and uncertain of the future. It is important for those with this card in their reading to reflect on their values and strive to make decisions that honor them.
