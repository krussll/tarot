A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 3 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 3 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 6 Of Wands tarot card and The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card symbolize a period of great success and acknowledgment for your hard work. With collective efforts and dedication, you are able to create something of high quality. The result is sure to bring you recognition and accolades from your peers and superiors, taking you one step closer to attaining your goals. This combination of cards is a sign of imminent victory, success, and satisfaction from your efforts.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

When it comes to health, the 6 of Wands tarot card is indicative of positive news or successes when it comes to one’s health. This card brings news of a successful recovery from illness, healing from an injury, or a medical breakthrough. It also can be a reminder to take care of yourself and look for ways to stay healthy.

When it comes to finances, the 6 of Wands tarot card can signify a successful venture, such as a financial windfall or a profitable investment. It can also signify the accumulation of wealth, and a period of financial prosperity. It can be seen as a sign to work hard and be wise with your money, as well as to consider options to improve your financial situation.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Wands tarot card is associated with a period of harmony, understanding and mutual support. It can be a sign of reconciliation, or a peace offering to heal a rift. It can signify a time of celebration, success, and a period of agreement and good communication. This card encourages you to cherish and honor relationships, and to also take the time for self-care to stay connected with your partner.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The Three of Pentacles Tarot card is closely related to health, finances, and relationships. This card emphasizes the importance of teamwork and collaboration in all aspects of life.

Health: Working as a team is important to achieve a healthy lifestyle. Teamwork allows individuals to share their experiences, advice, and resources to improve their overall health. This can be achieved through physical activities, wellness activities, and even sharing healthy recipes. Each person brings something different to the table which helps the collective reach a healthier outcome.

Finances: Working as a team is important to achieve financial success. By communicating and collaborating with each other, team members can find solutions to financial issues and work towards a common goal. This involves understanding each others’ financial needs, setting a budget, and sticking to it. It also encourages people to think creatively and work together to find money-saving strategies such as negotiating better rates, cutting back on expenses, and creating different sources of income.

Relationships: The Three of Pentacles Tarot card encourages us to work as a team to resolve issues and communicate effectively in relationships. Working together involves listening and understanding each other’s point of view. It also means offering support, respect, and patience to each other. Collaboration helps create an atmosphere of trust and respect which can help build a stronger, healthier relationship.

Overall, The Three of Pentacles Tarot card emphasizes the importance of teamwork, collaboration, skill, quality, and craftsmanship in all aspects of life. It is a reminder to take the time to communicate, work together, and set goals as a team. In doing so, you can create lasting success in your health, finances, and relationships.
