It encourages unconditional love for yourself and respect for the decisions you make, even if they may be difficult. Moving to a new place or starting a new career can be a daunting prospect, but if you draw on the strength and courage of this card combination, you can know that you are capable and able.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health  
When The 6 of Swords Tarot Card appears in a spread, it indicates that a transition or relocation of some kind is necessary for the inquirer’s overall health. This card urges us to look at our current lifestyle and assess whether there are changes that need to be made to better ourselves. It can also signify a new way of looking at our health, such as learning to prioritize mental health over physical health or finding more time for self-care. It is important to pay attention to any warning signs our bodies might be sending us, and be willing to make the necessary changes to our lives in order to ensure our wellbeing.

Finances  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a necessary financial transition. It can suggest that the inquirer is in a difficult financial situation and needs to make changes in order to improve their situation. This card can represent making the necessary changes to budget and save money, as well as work towards long-term financial stability. It can also refer to taking risks that might be uncomfortable, but which are necessary for financial success.

Relationships  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card can symbolize a necessary transition in relationships. Relationships that are no longer healthy or fulfilling may need to be let go of, and new, more positive relationships need to be sought out. It can also signify a need to re-evaluate our existing relationships and make sure that they are providing mutual support and benefit. Overall, this card can refer to making the necessary transitions in our relationships in order to ensure our emotional wellbeing.
