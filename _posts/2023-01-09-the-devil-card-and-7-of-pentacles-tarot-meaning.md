A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and 7 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Devil card and 7 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Devil tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card taken together represent a warning of being careful to not fall trap to the temptation of materialistic and obsessive behavior. The Devil card is representative of the potential for addictions and deep desires that can quickly become damaging, while the 7 Of Pentacles card is a reminder to practice patience, focus on hard work, and remember that real success takes time. Together, these cards suggest that we be mindful about giving into our impulses and materialistic desires if we want to work towards true success.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This card can indicate an addiction or compulsion that must be addressed, or it can represent a temptation that should be avoided.

The Seven Of Pentacles card provides a counterbalance to the Devil’s temptation, offering a message of patience and delayed success. This card suggests that, if we take the time to nurture and cultivate a relationship, then it will eventually bear fruit. This card encourages us to put in the hard work and time required to build a lasting and meaningful bond. This can mean cutting out any unnecessary temptations and instead focusing on the small acts of kindness and love that build trust and intimacy.

The message of the Devil tarot card and the Seven Of Pentacles card together is an important one for any relationship. If we can find a balance between indulgence and patience, then our relationships can become something truly meaningful and lasting. By recognizing the power of temptation and understanding the importance of delayed gratification, we can create a strong foundation for our relationships to grow and flourish.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Devil and 7 Of Pentacles mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Devil and 7 Of Pentacles the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card suggests that an individual may have become enslaved to dark forces. They are being held captive by compulsions and obsessions that can be damaging to their physical, mental, and spiritual health. These forces can also manifest in their finances, relationships, and careers in negative ways.

Regarding health, The Devil Tarot card can represent addiction to substances or activities such as drugs, alcohol, gambling, sex, and other forms of risky behavior. This is because the card warns of enslavement to unhelpful habits. People may also become obsessed with achieving physical perfection or believing that they must be perfect in order to be happy. They can spiral into a negative cycle of unhealthy behaviors that can have dire consequences for their mental and physical well-being.

In terms of finances, The Devil Tarot card signifies extreme materialism and greed. Individuals may be focused on acquiring material possessions or seeking power and wealth to the detriment of their relationships and life goals. They can become preoccupied with meeting the latest trend and keeping up with a certain lifestyle, ultimately leading to financial strain and burden.

Relationships can also suffer the consequences of The Devil Tarot card. It can represent a level of possessiveness and jealousy that can be poisonous for romantic relationships. Individuals may become consumed with their partner, or be unable to open up to others as a result of trust issues. When it comes to friendships, the card can represent an attitude of superiority or manipulation, whether knowingly or unknowingly. By understanding the meaning of the card, individuals can take steps to prevent these negative dynamics from taking hold in their relationships.

The Devil Tarot card carries a strong warning for individuals. It urges them to be aware of the powerful forces of addiction, greed, envy, materialism and obsession that can have a detrimental effect on their health, finances, and relationships. The card serves as a reminder to take stock of themselves and seek out healthier alternatives that ultimately contribute to a more balanced and fulfilling life.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

For Health:

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is an indicator that your dedication and hard work towards achieving health goals are about to pay off. It could be that the results of your lab results are coming in and it is taking a while for them to be analyzed. It is important to keep your eye on the prize and to remain patient. There is a sense of delayed success, so whatever you have been working hard towards in terms of health, you can expect some positive results if you stick to it.

For Finances:

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign to take your time in making important financial decisions- anything that has to do with investments or long-term goals. Even if it takes longer than expected, it is important to be patient and to weigh the pros and cons of each decision you make carefully and wisely. This is not a time to be rash or impulsive, so take your time, consider your options and make sure you have the resources you need to move forward.

For Relationships:

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to take your time in building any kind of relationship, whether it be friendship, romantic, or professional. You may find yourself taking your time and getting to know someone before investing in them too heavily. This is a sign of patience, but also a sign that whatever relationship you are investing in has the potential to last if you are committed to nurturing it and growing it in a slow and steady way. It is important to give relationships the time and care they need in order to thrive and be successful.
