A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The 8 Of Swords and The Queen Of Swords tarot card together suggest an overall message of being confined to and isolated by your own thoughts and beliefs. The 8 Of Swords symbolizes the self-imposed restrictions and imprisonment that can be created in one’s mind while The Queen Of Swords symbolizes a strength of mind and a commitment to total honesty and self-sufficiency. This combination encourages you to take control of your thoughts and to break free of any mental prisons you have built by becoming more honest with yourself and relying on your own strength, judgement and confidence.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 8 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When the 8 of Swords and the Queen of Swords come together in a reading, it speaks to the need for honest communication and openness in your relationships. Your love life is experiencing a period of stagnation, as you feel a sense of self-imposed restrictions and isolation. You may be closing yourself off from potential partners, setting up invisible barriers to prevent yourself from getting hurt. This could be due to a fear of the unknown or the fear of being hurt again.

At the same time, the presence of the Queen of Swords indicates that you need to make a conscious effort to open up and be honest with those you care about. It is time to be honest with yourself as well as with your partner and other loved ones. You need to confront the anxieties and worries that have been stopping you from being fully honest. While being open is difficult, it is the only way for relationships to grow and develop.

The two cards together indicate that you need to find a sense of freedom and openness in your relationships. Rather than isolating yourself from others and protecting your heart, it is time to take the risk and trust that someone can love and accept you for who you are. Communication is key to building a strong and meaningful connection with someone. Let go of the fears that have been holding you back and be brave enough to let someone in. With honest communication and an open heart, you can find the strength and courage to pursue the rewarding relationships you deserve.

### What does 8 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together for your finances?

The 8 of Swords and the Queen of Swords tarot card together can signify a period of stagnation or difficulty in terms of finances and job prospects. This could be the result of self-imposed limitations or isolation, and it could be the result of having to become overly self-sufficient and cut off from external help due to a lack of resources.

The 8 of Swords often indicates a feeling of being stuck and unable to progress, or an inability to access the resources needed for financial freedom. The Queen of Swords follows suit in representing one who is isolated and in need of stern self-reliance. In this way, the combination of these two cards could indicate an environment of hardship and difficulty which must be tackled through individual efforts.

It’s important to remember that this combination of cards isn’t necessarily negative. It could signal a period of growth and maturity as one learns to stand independently and be resourceful in a job market that is either stagnant or uncertain. With total honesty and self-sufficiency, a person can come out stronger on the other side of difficult times. The 8 of Swords and the Queen of Swords could be a reminder to double down on your own efforts, be creative in finding resources, and look for potential opportunities in even the most challenging of financial environments.

### What does 8 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together for your health?

The 8 Of Swords and The Queen Of Swords together suggest that one is in the process of self-imposed restrictions and isolation. This could mean that the person is trying to cut out unhealthy habits from their life, or otherwise trying to be more disciplined. The Queen of Swords further implies that this process is being done with total honesty and self-sufficiency. This suggests that one is trying to make a conscious effort to better their life and health.

The 8 Of Swords and The Queen Of Swords together could suggest the idea of getting rid of unhealthy habits and distractions so that one can focus inward. This could mean that the person is trying to “detox” their life and body, maybe by limiting their reliance on technology, getting rid of unhealthy foods or indulgences, or even cutting out unhealthy relationships. By cutting out these unhealthy aspects of life, the person is essentially putting themselves in a self-imposed prison in order to get their health back on track.

Ultimately, the combination of The 8 Of Swords and The Queen Of Swords implies that the person is trying hard to better their health through self-imposed restrictions and isolation. Rather than letting these restrictions be damaging and oppressive, the Queen of Swords suggests that the person needs to use these restrictions as an opportunity for self-growth and personal development. This could involve developing a healthier lifestyle, such as engaging in exercise, eating more nutritious foods, meditating, and seeking out professional help if needed. Ultimately, the 8 Of Swords and the Queen Of Swords together suggest that if one takes the opportunity for improvement and growth, then it can lead to a healthier life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 8 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 8 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 8 of Swords card suggests feelings of powerlessness, feelings of being trapped, and feelings of being out of control. These feelings may manifest as physical ailments such as exhaustion, depression, or even serious illnesses. On the flip side, the 8 of Swords could suggest a period of healing or growth and could be interpreted as a time to take a break, to put up boundaries and to take a step back to evaluate one’s health.

Finances: The 8 of Swords Tarot card suggests restrictive financial situations, self-imposed boundaries and possibly, a period of austerity. This card suggests a period of financial strain, and a need to be financially responsible and to pay attention to spending habits. It may also suggest limiting any excessive spending, or a need to reassess current investments and financial strategies.

Relationships: The 8 of Swords Tarot card warns of emotional imprisonment or self-imposed boundaries in relationships. This card suggests that there may be fear or guilt surrounding a current relationship dynamic, or that one may feel powerless to change the course of the relationship. To break free from the bonds of the 8 of Swords, one may need to step back and reassess the situation, examine the boundaries and limitations that are in place, and work to create healthier, more balanced relations.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health

The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a sign of good health and strong vitality. It indicates that you have the strength and willpower to maintain a healthy lifestyle. The Queen of Swords does not only symbolize physical health but also emotional and mental health. It suggests that you should be honest with yourself and others, practice self-care, and take action when necessary. This card suggests that you should take the initiative to take better care of yourself and stay in tune with your needs and desires.

Finances

The Queen of Swords Tarot card indicates that you are capable of making sound financial decisions. You have a clear head and good judgement when it comes to money and investments. Take this opportunity to plan your financial future, but also be aware that there are risks and you should use good judgement when spending. The card encourages you to be wise with your money and think of the long-term instead of the short-term.

Relationships

The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a sign of total honesty and transparency in relationships. This card encourages you to be open and truthful when communicating with your loved ones and take responsibility for your actions. It suggests that you should be aware of the consequences of your words and actions and be mindful of how it affects your relationships. This card implies that integrity and honesty are always the best strategies for creating harmonious and lasting relationships.
