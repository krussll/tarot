However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does 3 Of Cups and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The 3 of Cups tarot card and the 8 of Cups tarot card together suggest a period of transition in which friendship and celebration need to be followed by a period of retreat and renewal. On the one hand, these cards signify the joys of close friendships and the importance of celebrating milestones, but on the other hand, they remind you that there may come a time to move on and make space for a new life chapter. This can mean letting go of those close bonds and withdrawing for a while in order to find a new direction or purpose. Ultimately, the 3 of Cups and the 8 of Cups encourage you to appreciate the support of your circle of friends, and to make the difficult choice of when it’s time to move on.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 3 of Cups tarot card is an encouragement to be surrounded by a supportive group of friends, to stay connected and to find joy in reflecting on the past and celebrating progress. It can signify a healing cycle, physical and emotional renewal, and a newfound appreciation for the body and its needs. It can also mean that one should take time to appreciate the little moments that make life so sweet, those that provide much-needed respite from the stress of daily life.

Finances  
The 3 of Cups tarot card signifies abundance, prosperity, and wealth. This card encourages one to use the resources available and to cultivate a sense of security, trust and abundance. It also suggests that one needs to create a plan and work hard to manifest potential. This card can also be an indication that one should be open to new financial possibilities, such as investments and partnerships.

Relationships  
The Three of Cups tarot card often appears when a relationship is on the rise and the celebration of love is in the air. This card encourages one to open themselves up to the joys of life, to sow the seeds of camaraderie and to be willing to recognize when it’s time to let go and start anew. It can also be seen as a reminder to be thankful for the relationships in our lives, to appreciate the lessons we learn from each other and to be generous in our love.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

The 8 of Cups tarot card represents a time of retreat, moving on, and abandoning of old ways of life, in terms of health, finances, and relationships. It can signify a need to take a step back from a situation and look at it from a new perspective. It is often a sign that something needs to be let go of in order to move forward and make progress.

In terms of health, the 8 of Cups tarot card can signify a need to take a break and allow the body a chance to rest and recuperate. It may be an indication that there are certain habits or routines that need to be overhauled to improve one’s overall physical and mental health. This could involve pushing oneself a little less and taking more time to unwind and relax.

In terms of finances, the 8 of Cups often suggests that a change in one’s financial outlook is necessary. It is a sign of a need to step back and examine where one’s funds are going and how they can be better managed. It encourages taking a long hard look at one’s spending and budgeting habits and making appropriate adjustments.

In terms of relationships, the 8 of Cups often indicates a need to take a step back and assess the dynamic of a partnership. It is a sign that a change of perspective may be needed in order to move forward and create a healthier, more balanced relationship. It could also indicate a need to end a relationship that is no longer beneficial or healthy and focus on tending to other areas of one’s life.
