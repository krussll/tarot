## 7 Of Swords – An Overview

The 7 of Swords tarot card symbolizes a moment in life when one is forced to make tough decisions while being in a difficult situation. It often implies that one is feeling uncertain, as if they are stuck in a no-win situation. The card can mean that one is considering cheating, lying, or even stealing as a way to escape their current dilemma. It can also represent betrayal and a lack of trust, as well as the idea of running away from a difficult situation. Despite these negative connotations, the 7 of Swords can also represent a moment of great courage and strength, as one is forced to make difficult decisions and face the consequences of their actions.

## Upright Meanings Of 7 Of Swords

### What It Means For Me

The 7 of Swords tarot card serves as a warning that I should be on the lookout for dishonesty and potential betrayal. The card can suggest that someone may be trying to steal from me or that I should be careful about trusting people too easily. It can also be a sign that I should be more vigilant of my surroundings and use discretion when it comes to sharing personal information. Additionally, the card could be a reminder that if I am feeling overwhelmed, I should look for an escape; although running away is not always the best option. All in all, The 7 of Swords is a reminder to stay alert and use wisdom in all of my decisions.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 of Swords tarot card suggests that there is a looming sense of betrayal and dishonesty in my future. It could be that someone I have trusted has been dishonest with me, or that someone I have trusted has betrayed me in some way. Additionally, the card suggests that I may be presented with opportunities to run away from difficult situations, rather than facing them head on. It is important that I try to remain vigilant and cautious in the face of possible threats or danger, as running away from a problem may only make it worse in the long run.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 of Swords tarot card is a warning to be wary of those who are close to me. It suggests a potential for dishonesty, betrayal, and theft from those I trust. It might indicate that someone I care about is not being truthful with me and may be using me for their own gain. This card also warns me to look out for any signs of evasion or running away, as this might be a sign of someone not wanting to take responsibility for their actions. Ultimately, The 7 of Swords tarot card serves as a reminder to keep my guard up and be aware of the motives of those close to me.
