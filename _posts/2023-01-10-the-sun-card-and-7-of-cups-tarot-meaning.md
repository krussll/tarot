## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

Health  
The Sun Tarot card symbolizes health, vitality and wholeness. It encourages us to take care of our physical, mental and emotional health. When this card shows up, it’s a reminder to take care of our bodies through exercise and a balanced diet, as well as give ourselves permission to rest and relax. The Sun’s message is to be grateful for the health we have and to work to maintain it through self-care and healthy habits.

Finances  
The Sun Tarot card brings news of financial success and abundance. When this card shows up, it’s usually an indication of money coming your way. This can be in the form of a raise, a bonus, or a successful investment. It can also signify that you’ll be able to enjoy the fruits of your labor in the near future. This card is a reminder to trust in yourself and your financial decisions, and to stay positive and optimistic about your financial situation.

Relationships  
The Sun Tarot card brings positive news in the area of relationships. It symbolizes joy, happiness and contentment in all of your relationships. When this card shows up, it’s a sign that you will be able to find peace and joy in your relationships with others. It reminds us that it’s important to take the time to nurture our relationships and that any conflicts should be resolved with kindness and understanding. This card also encourages us to spread happiness and positivity in our relationships and to be grateful for the support and love we receive.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 7 of Cups Tarot card points to too many choices, and potential issues with commitment. In terms of health, this could mean that you are so overwhelmed with all of the various health decisions you have to make that you can’t make any of them. Whether it’s deciding on a diet and exercise plan, or where to get your medical care from, the 7 of Cups means you are probably taking too much on and need to learn to say no. Taking on too much responsibility for your own health can be mentally and physically taxing, and it would be wise to reach out for support and guidance from friends, family, and professionals.

Finances

The 7 of Cups Tarot card means too many choices in your finances. This could mean you’re having trouble making decisions and committing to a particular budget or investment plan. You may be having trouble cutting out unnecessary expenses or sticking to a savings goal. You’re probably overwhelmed with the endless options available to you and it’s hard to make decisions when you’ve got too much information or too many choices. You should find an experienced financial advisor or budgeting app to help you make sensible choices and keep you on track.

Relationships

The 7 of Cups Tarot card speaks of too many choices and commitment issues when it comes to relationships. This could mean that you are unable to choose whether or not to commit to someone, or you’re feeling overwhelmed by all of the different relationships in your life. You may need to take a step back and try to evaluate your various relationships and decide whether you want to invest more energy and time into them. This card also suggests that it would be beneficial to seek guidance from a trusted source, like a good friend or a therapist, if you’re feeling overwhelmed and unable to make decisions.
