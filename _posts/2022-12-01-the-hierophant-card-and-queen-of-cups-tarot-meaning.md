## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card is a symbol of traditional practices, conventional expectations, and conformity. When it comes to health and wellness, The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that following conventional wisdom and traditional health practices is the best path to physical and mental well-being. This may mean following a prescribed diet and fitness plan, as well as receiving regular check-ups or consulting with a healthcare professional. It could also mean honoring traditional medicines and healing practices, such as homeopathic, holistic, or ayurvedic treatments. The card urges individuals to embrace the time-tested health practices that have been around for generations and to not fear trying something new.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that when it comes to finances, it is best to stick to what is tried and true, conventional wisdom, and established norms when it comes to money management. This could mean following a budget, investing in mutual funds, or seeking professional advice when needed. The card speaks to the importance of not taking big financial risks, but instead playing it safe and opting for financial security. The Hierophant advises that you consult with professionals or individuals with vast experience and knowledge regarding investments, banking, or anything involving money matters.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card speaks to the importance of honoring conventional expectations, norms, and traditions within relationships. This card suggests that relationships should be built upon trust, respect, and mutual understanding in order to be successful. It calls for couples to abide by generally accepted principles, such as not hiding things from each other or engaging in affairs. The Hierophant also speaks to the importance of compromise and communication in any relationship, which can only be achieved by adhering to established standards of behavior.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card is associated with a nurturer and healer who has a deep understanding of emotional health. She is connected to water, the earth and her own natural wisdom. The Queen of Cups is connected to the healing aspects of self-care and is an archetype of comfort and compassion. She has the ability to soothe anxiety, calm fears and bring peace of mind. The Queen of Cups is a powerful healer who can be called upon to help restore balance to the physical body and well-being to the emotional body.

Finances  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card symbolizes financial stability and steady growth. She is a card of intuitive decision making when it comes to money matters. This queen is a reminder that we can trust our own intuition as it relates to money and abundance. It is suggested that we take the time to do our own research and make well-informed decisions. The Queen encourages us to be brave and use our bravery to create a solid foundation for our financial life.

Relationships  
In relationships the Queen of Cups Tarot card represents a deep, nurturing connection. Those in relationships with this queen will find an abundance of emotional support and understanding. She is capable of providing guidance and insight in difficult times. She is not one to shy away from tackling the tough conversations and is comfortable with being vulnerable. Those in relationships with the Queen of Cups should find comfort in her understanding nature, abilities to heal and nurture, and willingness to always be there.
