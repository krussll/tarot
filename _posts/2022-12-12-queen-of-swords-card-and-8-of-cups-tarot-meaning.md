A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen Of Swords and the 8 Of Cups together represent a period of self reflection and honest assessment of your current situation. It marks a time for looking inward and evaluating what is worth keeping and what needs to be let go of. This combination of cards suggests that you must take a step back from specific relationships or commitments that are no longer serving your highest good. There may be some emotional difficulty associated with this transition, but it is indicative of a time of growth and change. Ultimately, you should come out the other side with a newfound sense of self-sufficiency and an improved relationship with yourself and your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Queen Of Swords and 8 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Queen Of Swords and 8 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a period of unwavering self-discipline when it comes to health. This card encourages individuals to take charge of their physical and mental wellbeing, and to be honest with themselves about their own needs and wants. Physically, this card suggests a commitment to preventative health care, such as regular check-ups, and an understanding of the importance of nutrition and exercise for overall wellbeing. Mentally, this card can indicate a period of rigor in terms of one’s thought patterns and the ability to take control of the way one thinks.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes financial stability and security. Those with this card in their reading will likely be encouraged to cultivate a clear financial plan, as well as a healthy attitude towards finances, coupled with complete honesty about one’s financial goals. This card suggests budgeting and saving for the future, as well as being open to taking risks and making investments when the right opportunities present themselves.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the need for clear personal boundaries when it comes to relationships. This card encourages individuals to be honest and direct in all of their dealings with others, and to remain true to themselves in any situation. It can also signify a period of self-sufficiency where individuals may be able to support themselves without relying too greatly on the help of others. In terms of romantic relationships, this card can indicate a period of commitment and open communication in order to create a successful and strong partnership.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a card of retreat and withdrawal. If this card appears in a reading related to health, it can indicate a need to take some time for oneself and to retreat from any physical or mental issues to better assess their condition and explore potential solutions. This card can also suggest that one must cut ties from any unhealthy habits or influences that may be negatively impacting their health. Taking a step back from any current stressors or situations that could potentially be harmful to one’s health, and refocusing on self-care methods and activities, is very important for restoring balance and wellbeing.

Finances: In matters of money, The 8 of Cups Tarot card can indicate that it is time to retreat from any current financial obligations or commitments that may have become overwhelming. It can also mean that it is time to assess one’s current financial situation and look for solutions or new opportunities outside of their current comfort zone. This card can also point to a need to release attachments to material possessions or to ideas and expectations they may have had that may no longer be viable, in order to explore better options in terms of financial security.

Relationships: In terms of relationships, The 8 of Cups Tarot card can point to a need to take a step back or retreat from a current relationship. It may also indicate a need to reassess a relationship and to look for new possibilities or perspectives that can lead to restoring balance, possibly through a healthier and more mutually beneficial dynamic. This card can also suggest that one must, at times, let go of emotionally draining relationships and commitments, in order to make room for more meaningful connections.
