A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The 2 Of Wands tarot card and the Queen Of Wands tarot card together suggest that you are waiting for the results of an important decision that you must make. It could be a decision about a travel plan or an important career or financial move that you are uncertain about. The Queen Of Wands tarot card encourages you to embrace your inner confidence and self-assurance. This card hints not to be too passive and to take a feisty and proactive approach to whatever you are contemplating. Think of the Queen Of Wands as a strong and spirited guide who will help you to make the right decision at this pivotal time.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 2 Of Wands and Queen Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

For Health

The 2 of Wands in the Tarot represents waiting for results, making a choice and planning for travel. When this card appears in a Tarot reading regarding health, it is important to pay attention to the results of any treatments or tests. If a Tarot reading reveals that you are awaiting results, it is important to stay positive and draw strength from the support of friends and family. It is also possible that the card suggests that you compare different choices and treatments before making a decision that is right for you.

For Finances

The 2 of Wands in the Tarot suggests a call to be cautious and patient in order to make wise decisions regarding finances. This could mean taking the time to analyze and compare different options before making a choice. It is also a reminder to be open to learning new ways to manage your money and find inventive solutions to your financial issues. Being mindful of your future goals, such as saving for retirement, will help you make decisions that will benefit your financial wellbeing in the long-term.

For Relationships

When the 2 of Wands appears in a Tarot reading for relationships, it could suggest a need for creating structure. This could involve setting clear boundaries and expectations for both parties involved. It is also a reminder to be mindful of your choices and the consequences of any decision. The card also encourages patience in navigating a relationship, and could indicate that travel or planning a vacation together would be beneficial. Overall, the 2 of Wands serves as a reminder to think before you act when it comes to relationships, and to be mindful of the power of choice.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health

The Queen of Wands tarot card represents a vibrant, confident and just attitude when it comes to health. She encourages us to take a proactive approach to our physical and emotional wellbeing. She stresses that we should never be afraid to pursue our goals, no matter how bold or challenging they may be. The Queen of Wands encourages us to take care of our bodies—get enough sleep, nourish ourselves with healthy food, and exercise regularly. She reminds us that our minds and bodies are powerful tools that, when used correctly, can make us feel strong, capable and ready to tackle anything.

Finances

The Queen of Wands tarot card is a reminder that financial success starts with a confident attitude. She encourages us to assess our current situation and determine our strategies for achieving our goals. She advises us to take responsibility for our financial stability and have the courage to take risks when necessary. The Queen of Wands also encourages us to use our creativity and problem-solving skills to construct solutions that have the potential to lead to financial independence.

Relationships

The Queen of Wands tarot card suggests an attitude of confidence and self-assurance when it comes to relationships. She advises us to be comfortable with our own strengths, weaknesses, and opinions. She encourages us to be comfortable in our own skin, recognizing our unique traits and having confidence in our ability to love deeply and authentically. The Queen of Wands reminds us to be open to new experiences, acting with kindness and patience, and remembering that no man or woman is an island.
