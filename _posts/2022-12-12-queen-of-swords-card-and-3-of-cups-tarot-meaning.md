A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen Of Swords and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together means that you have the ability to be self-sufficient and be guided by total honesty. At the same time, you are also supported by friendships and celebrations. You have a close circle of support and people who appreciate and love you just the way you are. You can rely on your own strength and determination to persevere and reach your goals, all while having caring people by your side.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

On the other hand, the 3 of Cups encourages you to open up and reach out to others, deepening your bonds and creating a level of comfort that will help you work through your problems without relying too heavily on your own judgment.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

The Queen of Swords is a powerful reminder to question everything and be mindful of what is beneficial and harmful for you. This card encourages you to be proactive with your health, take responsibility for your wellbeing, and make wise decisions when it comes to your health.

Finances: When the Queen of Swords appears in an financial reading, it is a sign to take charge of your finances and make responsible decisions. You may need to be honest with yourself and others; this includes getting help when needed, taking responsibility for your mistakes, and understanding the consequences of your financial decisions. This card encourages you to be more self-sufficient and make honest and practical decisions when it comes to your finances.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords in a relationship reading indicates that you need to be honest and direct about the relationships in your life. This card indicates that it is time to take a more independent approach and stand up for your own needs. You may need to be more open and honest with others, and become more self-sufficient in your relationships. The Queen of Swords reminds you that total honesty is important when it comes to creating and maintaining healthy relationships.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The Three of Cups Tarot card represents celebration, joy and a sense of harmony with the world. This card speaks to the idea of feeling connected to others, and of feeling supported by friends and family. When the Three of Cups appears in a Tarot reading, it can suggest a time of abundance and good fortune, and of feeling contented with relationships and finances.

Health: When the Three of Cups Tarot card appears in a reading, it can be interpreted as an indication of good health and overall well-being. This card can suggest that the individual is surrounded by supportive friends and family who provide emotional and physical support for their health journey. It can also signify a time of celebration and joy in life, which can have a positive impact on one’s overall health and well-being.

Finances: The Three of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as a very lucky card when it comes to finances. This card speaks to abundance and success, and it can indicate that the individual is blessed with good fortune when it comes to finances. It can suggest that the individual may be set to receive a windfall or an unexpected raise or bonus, or that they will be successful in their efforts to launch a new business venture.

Relationships: The Three of Cups Tarot card can signify a time of celebration and joy in relationships. This card can suggest that relationships are looking positive and prosperous, both in terms of emotional support and material benefits. It can also signify a feeling of connection to the wider community and a feeling of being supported and held up by friends and family. This card often appears when relationships are strong and secure, and it can provide a sense of security and contentment.
