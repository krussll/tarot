A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Knight Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Knight Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

The Knight Of Cups card encourages you to be open to the potential of true love and a romantic start of something new. You must look within and determine if your heart is open to new possibilities and consider the risks of getting your hopes up when it comes to romance. This will bring clarity and a clear understanding of what you need so that you can make the right decision for yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and Knight Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

For Health:

The Hermit Tarot card is associated with the need to go within and seek spiritual illumination and personal truth as it relates to your health. The card can be interpreted as a wake-up call, providing an opportunity to take a closer look at the habits, attitudes, and behaviors we have adopted that no longer serve us. The card suggests that time spent in personal reflection and contemplation can provide invaluable insight and knowledge that will help lead you to a healthier and more balanced way of life. This could be a call to reassess your diet, exercise routine, or joint a healthier lifestyle. It is important to recognize when it is time to take a step back and listen to what your body and mind need.

For Finances:

The Hermit Tarot card can be interpreted as a sign to take a closer look at your financial decisions. You may need to reflect on how you are budgeting or managing your money and whether you are ready for a change of perspective. This may require spending time in contemplation and re-evaluating how you make and manage financial decisions. The Hermit card suggests that the insights gained from this process will help lead to more prosperity and better financial planning. It is important to be mindful of your goals and to be honest with yourself about what you can and cannot afford; this will put you in better control of your financial future.

For Relationships:

The Hermit Tarot card speaks to the need to go inward and to reflect on the relationships we have in our lives. The card may be showing that it is time to take a step back and consider what changes we may need to make in order to nurture our bonds and relationships with those close to us. It could be an invitation to reassess the dynamics of your relationships, to look for ways to deepen and nurture the bond. Consider which values are important in the relationship, and whether you are meeting the needs of both people. More than anything, it is an invitation to self-reflection, to look for your truth and to be honest about your feelings. This will help lead to more authentic and fulfilling relationships.

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Knight Of Cups Tarot card is associated with overall well-being, good health, and a balanced lifestyle. It signifies that it’s time to take care of your physical and mental health and recognize your self-worth. This card encourages you to make healthier choices in terms of nutrition and exercise. By treating your body with respect, you can restore balance and harmony. It is also a reminder of the importance of rest and relaxation to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

Finances:  
The Knight Of Cups Tarot card signifies financial stability and abundance. It encourages you to look upon your finances with a sense of trust and security. Now is the time to evaluate your finances and make decisions that will benefit your future. This card suggests that it is wise to invest your money for the long-term rather than seeking out quick-fixes. It also encourages you to be generous and considerate with your finances, and not allow greed to take control.

Relationships:  
The Knight Of Cups Tarot card is all about romance, love, and emotional stability. This card encourages you to be open to new relationships and trust in the possibilities that come with them. It also encourages you to be honest and vulnerable in your relationships. This card reminds you to be mindful of your words, actions, and intentions, as this will help you form strong and meaningful relationships. All relationships require effort and respect – this card encourages you to take the time to nurture your existing relationships and to keep an open dialogue with those you love.
