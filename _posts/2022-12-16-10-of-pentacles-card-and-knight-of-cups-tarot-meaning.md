## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

When taking a look at the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card, several meanings come to mind, especially when it comes to health, finances, and relationships. This card is associated with the idea of leaving a legacy, retirement, and inheritance, and those three components can drastically affect how we live our daily lives.

When it comes to health, the 10 of Pentacles suggests that you have a strong foundation and the resources available to maintain a healthy lifestyle. It tells us to take the proper steps to ensure that our health is taken care of in the present and for the future. This can mean good nutrition, proper exercise, preventive medical care, and even proper lifestyle habits such as avoiding smoking and excessive drinking.

When it comes to finances, the 10 of Pentacles can suggest that you have established a strong financial base and are in a good position to start building your own future. This can mean proper budgeting, investing, and saving. It can also tell us to think ahead and plan for the future in order to ensure proper financial security.

Finally, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card can pertain to relationships. It can signify a strong, secure relationship, one that is built on love and understanding. It also reminds us to have proper communication and to always be honest with our partners in order to have a healthy, fulfilling relationship.

Overall, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of a strong foundation and the resources available to ensure good health, financial security and sound relationships. It is important to keep this card in mind when making decisions in these areas of life in order to ensure that our future is taken care of.

This card suggests that you need to take time to look after your own emotional health and wellbeing by practicing mindfulness and self-care. Freeing yourself from any negative feelings and embracing the positive ones is key to restoring balance in your life. With the right mindset, you will be able to cope with the daily stresses of life more easily and better make decisions that foster your health and wellbeing.

You should be patient and take time to weigh up the pros and cons of any decision before you commit to it. This can help you to avoid any hasty decisions that could have negative financial consequences. The card also encourages you to find stability by creating a budget and sticking to it.

Relationships: The Knight Of Cups Tarot card signifies romantic love and the potential for a new and exciting partnership. If you are looking to start a new relationship, the Knight Of Cups Tarot card suggests that you open up to the possibilities of love and make sure that you don’t let fear or worry stand in the way of true love. If you are already in a relationship, the Knight Of Cups Tarot card encourages you to cultivate a relationship based on mutual understanding, respect and affection. Blending emotion, intuition and sensitivity is key to creating a strong, lasting relationship.
