## What does The Devil and Knight Of Wands mean together?

You may need to be conscious of how your own behaviour could turn from creative and enthusiastic to selfish and driven if left unchecked. You may be particularly susceptible to temptations such as addiction or sexual lust, and so must be aware of the choices you make to ensure that you remain anchored to your values and goals.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card is a powerful indicator of the darker aspects of our lives. It is associated with addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust. It can have a negative impact on one’s health, finances, and relationships.

Health-wise, when the Devil card shows up in a reading, it often indicates that the querent is struggling with an addiction or has become overly dependent on something that is detrimental to their health. The Devil card could also point to compulsive behaviors such as overeating, overspending, and excessive drinking or drug use. These habits can cause significant physical and psychological harm and can lead to a host of health issues, both short- and long-term.

Financially, the Devil card can be an indicator of the need to break unhealthy spending habits. When it appears in a reading, it can point to the querent’s avid pursuit of material possessions and the tendency to overspend. The Devil card can also be a warning sign of financial ruin if the querent’s desire for material gain leads them to take extreme risks, such as gambling or taking out loans they cannot pay back.

Relationships can suffer if the Devil card appears in a reading. In this case, it usually points to the querent’s focus on themselves and desire for control. It can mean that the querent is unwilling to compromise or is more interested in their own needs than their partner’s. It can also mean that the querent has become overly jealous or possessive of their partner, which can cause significant strain on the relationship.

Overall, the Devil Tarot card is a difficult one to interpret, as it can indicate both good and bad things. However, no matter the context, it is important to remember that the Devil card is here to remind us to take a step back and re-evaluate our choices and behaviors.
