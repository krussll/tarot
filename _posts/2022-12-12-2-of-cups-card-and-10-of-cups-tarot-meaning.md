## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

It can signify that you and your physical and mental health are in a healthy and stable place, and will remain so for some time. It is also a card of renewal and rejuvenation, indicating that you are taking care of yourself and creating a healthy lifestyle for yourself. It can be a card of healing and, if there are any imbalances in your health or lifestyle, this card can be a sign that you should take steps to restore balance.

In terms of finances, the 2 of Cups card is seen as a card of abundance, prosperity, and success. It indicates that, with the right effort, you are likely to succeed financially and achieve your goals. It can also suggest that the best way to handle money is to be mindful of how it is spent and saved, as well as understanding the importance of budgeting and financial planning.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 2 of Cups is seen as a card of harmony, cooperation, and communication. It suggests that any problems within a relationship can be addressed in a positive and constructive way. It can represent a strengthening of the relationship and a sense of deeper understanding and connection, as well as a commitment to compromise and compromise on important issues. This card can also signify new relationships and a strong connection between two people, which can inspire both emotional and physical intimacy.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 10 of Cups card symbolizes good health and well-being. It means taking care of your body and mind, making sure to maintain a good balance between both. The 10 of Cups indicates a healthy lifestyle – eating nutritious meals, regularly exercising, maintaining an active social life, and finding ways to reduce stress. It also suggests avoiding unhealthy habits, such as smoking, drinking too much alcohol, and overindulging in sugar and processed foods. Ultimately, this card advises us to take care of ourselves so that we can live life to the fullest.

Finances

The 10 of Cups card is closely related to financial stability. This card encourages us to be mindful of our finances, setting up a budget and creating a savings plan. It suggests approaching money with a practical and realistic attitude, making sure that both short-term and long-term financial goals are taken into consideration. It also suggests taking action to increase financial stability and security, such as investing in the right places and learning how to properly manage our resources.

Relationships

The 10 of Cups card implies a sense of contentment and happiness within family and romantic relationships. It advises us to take a step back and look at the bigger picture, focusing on the things that are most important to us. This card encourages us to nurture our relationships, spending time with our loved ones and expressing appreciation for what we have. It suggests finding ways to create positive experiences and memories with other people and looking at relationships from a more positive perspective. It encourages us to be patient and understanding with those we care about.
