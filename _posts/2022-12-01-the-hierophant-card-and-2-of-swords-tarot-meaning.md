A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 2 Of Swords mean together?

When The Hierophant tarot card and The 2 Of Swords tarot card come together, it is a reminder that compromises must still be made even when traditional customs and expectations must be followed. It encourages us to consider making decisions based on what is expected of us, but also to find a way to meet our own needs. We must learn to take control of our destiny and make decisions differently if needed, while still adhering to the accepted norms. This duality brings forth a unique opportunity to honor both paths and make compromises in order to achieve a balanced outcome.

This could be anything from deciding to get married, to making a commitment to each other to remain in the relationship for an extended period of time, to deciding which traditions and conventional expectations you will observe in your relationship.

The combination of these two cards is asking you to evaluate your current relationship and the importance of traditional expectations and conformity to you both. It’s important to remember not to let outside influences or conventional expectations pressure you into making a decision before you feel ready. Rather, take the time to talk to your partner, consider all the options available to you, and find a compromise that works for both of you.

When it comes to making a decision, compromise can be a difficult process, but it doesn’t have to be impossible. The Hierophant tarot card and The 2 Of Swords tarot card combination is urging you to consider both your partner’s needs and yours, come to a mutual understanding, and have faith in the process. By being willing to cooperate with your partner, you can come up with a solution that works for both of you and helps cultivate your relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and 2 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 2 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It can urge us to build our health routines of self-care and look for ways to nurture our physical and mental well-being.

Finances: The Hierophant Tarot card stands for the rules and conventional wisdom that take shape in our finances. This can suggest sticking to your budget and seeking advice on managing your money from trusted advisors and professionals. It might also indicate that you need to look back to find solutions to financial problems and look to the tried and true methods of budgeting and saving. This card can show that you need to be aware of the financial conventions that are in place in your life and have a plan in place to help you manage your finances better.

Relationships: The Hierophant Tarot card speaks to the traditions and conventions that shape how we interact with each other. This can remind us to observe the conventional expectations about how to conduct ourselves with others and how to listen to and respect one another. It might also indicate that we should find our own ways to contribute to the building of strong relationships, whether that’s through communication, kindness, honesty, or offering support. This card speaks to the need to create conventions and rules that are beneficial to the relationship and allow it to grow and thrive.

The 2 of Swords Tarot card could be a signal to take a look at any financial decisions you’ve been putting off and to make a decision based on what is logical.

It could also be a sign to listen carefully to both sides of the story and to be honest with yourself and the other person in the relationship. It may also indicate a time to take a step back and to think carefully before making any commitment.
