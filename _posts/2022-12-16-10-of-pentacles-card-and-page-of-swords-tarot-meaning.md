A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords mean together?

The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card together suggest that you will find yourself in a position of leaving behind a legacy for the future. Although you are ready to retire, your legacy will live on through the information you have passed on to your family and younger generations. The Page Of Swords tarot card reminds you that there may be people who try to pry into your business, but remain vigilant in defending your legacy and the truth you have worked hard to promote. Although the transition may be uncomfortable, your legacy is what will remain long after you are gone.

On the one hand, the 10 of Pentacles speaks to the idea of leaving a legacy, retirement and inheritance; while the Page of Swords denotes a curious kid, gossip, spies, and prying eyes. When linked together, these cards may be pointing to a person who is content to passively observe in the relationship and let their partner do the talking, even when their presence may be needed.

This combination is not necessarily a bad thing, as it implies that the individual is allowing the other to speak their truth and is comfortable in their own skin. The 10 of Pentacles could be pointing to the idea that the individual is willing to wait until the other person is ready to open up, rather than pushing them to do so. This could be an indication of the trust and longevity in the relationship, with the individual in question being willing to be a supportive presence until their partner is ready to open up and share.

The Page of Swords further emphasizes this point, as it could be hinting at a tendency to gossip, spy, or intrude upon the other person’s privacy. This would suggest that the individual is content to wait until their partner is ready to share their feelings, rather than trying to force it upon them. Ultimately, this combination of cards speaks to a trusting and supportive relationship, one in which the individual is willing to wait until their partner is ready to open up and share.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

It encourages you to think of the future and plan accordingly so you can have stability and security in your health in the long run. This can mean getting regular checkups, taking preventive measures, and eating balanced meals. The 10 of Pentacles also asks you to think of your physical and mental well-being in the context of your relationships – perhaps with a partner or even within the family. Make sure to keep open communication with those who are important to you and encourage each other to stay on top of physical and mental health.

The 10 of Pentacles card also represents a secure financial future. It encourages you to think about savings, investments and retirement plans and urges you to be disciplined that your finances are taken care of. This can mean regularly contributing to your retirement accounts, educating yourself financially, budgeting and having multiple streams of income. The 10 of Pentacles card also asks you to reflect on how your relationship to money may be affecting your current situation. Do you take risks in the pursuit of wealth? Or do you shy away and stick to small-time investments? This card suggests that financial security begins with looking inward.

Finally, the 10 of Pentacles suggests that you take a look at your relationships and see where you need to build or strengthen connections. It might be time to reflect on the existing ones too, in order to be conscious of what’s working and what’s not. This card also encourages you to look at the idea of leaving a legacy and think about what kind of legacy you’d like to leave. Maybe it’s to be remembered as a great parent, a beloved partner, or an icon in a certain industry. Taking an honest look at your relationships and legacy can give you more clarity moving forward.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health-

The Page of Swords Tarot card in the context of health suggests that you should take time to thoughtfully examine your health. It could be a warning to take a closer look at any illnesses you may have, what you are eating, and your general lifestyle choices. The Page of Swords also reminds you to listen to your intuition when it comes to health decisions; if something doesn’t feel right, speak up and get the help you need.

Finances-

The Page of Swords Tarot card in the context of finances suggests that you should use your analytical skills to carefully examine the details of your finances. This can include budgeting, understanding where your money is going and what sources of income you may have. This card also warns against being too trusting with others and suggests that you look out for financial frauds or scams.

Relationships-

The Page of Swords Tarot card in the context of relationships can mean that you need to communicate honestly and openly, especially with new partners. This card suggests that it is important to examine the truth of a situation before jumping into a new relationship. It is also important to take time to assess if a potential partner has the same values and goals as you do. This card can also indicate that you need to stand up for yourself and use your intuition to speak up when issues come up.
