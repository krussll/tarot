A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The 9 of Wands and the 8 of Swords in combination represent the idea of keeping our boundaries and maintaining our own restrictions. It speaks to the need for us to remain conscious of our own needs and to be resolute about where we draw the line for ourselves. At the same time, this card combination cautions us against taking our restrictions too far, to the extent that we put ourselves in a state of self-imposed isolation and imprisonment. It’s important to find a balance between persistance and flexibility, allowing ourselves to create boundaries and also be open to new possibilities.

This is particularly true in love relationships, as they require vulnerability, trust, and understanding. On the other hand, the 8 Of Swords can indicate that you may be keeping yourself in a state of self-imposed prison without realizing it. You may be blocking yourself from trusting your partner and thereby keeping yourself from deepening the connection.

The two cards together suggest that it is important to be mindful of the boundaries that you have set in your relationship and make sure they are not blocking your connection with your partner. It is important to recognize the need to protect yourself but also to open yourself up enough to let your partner in. In order to make your relationship stronger and more trusting, you need to take risks and take down some of the walls you have built up so that you can grow closer to your partner.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Wands and 8 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Wands and 8 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

Health

The Nine of Wands tarot card is a reminder to look after your physical and mental health. It suggests that setting boundaries is important in maintaining balance in your everyday life. These boundaries may include setting healthier eating habits, exercising, getting enough sleep or taking a break from stressful situations. Being aware of your limits and respecting them can help keep you healthy and prevent possible illnesses.

Finances

The Nine of Wands tarot card can indicate that you need to adopt a more disciplined approach to your finances. This means staying within your budget, tracking your spending, and avoiding purchasing items you can’t really afford. This card also suggests that, if necessary, you should take a step back and take a more conservative approach to your finances. Setting boundaries is key here as it can help keep you from getting into debt or finding yourself in financial difficulty.

Relationships

The Nine of Wands tarot card suggests that it’s important to have healthy boundaries in all your relationships. This means being clear about what you are and are not willing to accept from others and being respectful of the boundaries of others. It also means setting and maintaining personal boundaries so that you can avoid getting into unhealthy dynamics or codependent behaviors. This card also asks you to nurture your relationships and invest in them, even when things get difficult or challenging.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can symbolize a feeling of being stuck in a stagnated state of health. This could indicate that an individual is refusing to seek medical help, despite their condition worsening. Often, this card can suggest that an individual is holding onto irrational fears that prevent them from taking positive steps forward in regards to their health. It could also signify externally imposed physical restrictions upon an individual due to health conditions.

In terms of financial matters, this card can represent feeling restricted financially. This could mean an individual feels trapped and unable to move forward due to financial constraints or limitations. Additionally, this card can also signify that an individual’s financial decisions are making them feel trapped and unable to get out of their current situation.

When it comes to relationships, The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can signify feeling trapped in a relationship, either due to expectations or obligations. This card can also represent feeling restricted and unable to communicate in a healthy manner with a partner. Similarly, this card can suggest that an individual is feeling alone and trapped, unable to break free from their current relationship.
