A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Knight Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Knight Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Knight Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Knight Of Pentacles tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card together symbolize a combination of reliability, hard work, and curiosity. The Knight Of Pentacles implies that you should be patient and persistent in your endeavors and trust that your hard work will eventually pay off. Meanwhile, The Page Of Swords indicates that you should be careful about who you choose to trust, as gossip and prying eyes can be an issue. You should also be on the lookout for people who are out to deceive you, as the truth is sometimes hard to find. Together, these cards suggest that you should balance your care and diligence with openness and a willingness to learn.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

For health, The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that one is taking the necessary steps to stay healthy in both mind and body. This card also encourages one to keep up with an exercise routine and dietary habits that promote longevity and wellbeing. For finances, The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card implies that you have to make sensible decisions with your money and put in the hard work to turn it into something of real value. The knight encourages you to cultivate financial stability with concrete plans, such as creating a budget or investing in something of value. It also promotes patience and discipline, two qualities necessary for successful financial management.

For relationships, The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages trust and stability. The knight stands for accountability and dependability, meaning that someone who follows this card is a reliable partner for both friends and romantic partners alike. This card also reinforces the importance of commitment, dedication, and loyalty when it comes to relationships. Furthermore, the knight encourages individuals to prioritize their partner’s needs and well-being over their own. By adhering to these qualities, one can form solid relationships with others.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to be cautious and conscious of our health. This card warns of potential gossip and spies that can be detrimental to our mental and physical wellbeing, and encourages us to take a closer look at our personal health. We should be aware of the risks in taking on too much, as too much physical, mental and emotional stress can cause serious health problems. Taking a step back and taking extra care in taking care of our health is the best way to approach dealing with this card. If a medical issue arises, seeking the opinion of a professional health care provider can help in alleviating any stress related to our health.

Finances: The Page of Swords Tarot card is associated with the need to pay attention to one’s finances. We must be aware of our spending, and not take on too much financial responsibility. We should always act with caution and be careful to keep our financial records up-to-date. It is advisable to be mindful of the people we associate with, as gossip and spies can be detrimental to our financial stability. Being suspicious of those with access to our financial accounts is a wise move.

Relationships: The Page of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to keep our relationships honest and open. We should be wary of gossip, spies, and prying eyes that can be detrimental to our relationships. We should use caution when engaging in close conversations and be certain of our interlocutors’ intentions. At the same time, we should make sure that we are not the source of gossip ourselves. It is important to always keep the truth intact when in relationships, as this will help maintain a healthy and honest bond between the two parties.
