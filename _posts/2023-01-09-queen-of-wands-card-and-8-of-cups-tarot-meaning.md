A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen Of Wands and The 8 Of Cups tarot card mean that you have the confidence, self-assurance, and feistiness to take a leap of faith and move on from what no longer serves you. Despite your normal feisty nature, you recognize that it may be time to retreat and abandon what has been holding you back. You know in your heart that it’s time to leave behind certain aspects in your life and move forward with confidence and self-assuredness. The Queen Of Wands is giving you the strength to take the steps you need to take in order to reach the next level of your journey.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Those who draw this card have the ability to bring out the best in themselves and in their relationships. When it comes to relationships, the Queen of Wands Tarot card encourages its bearer to be open to new experiences and perspectives, and to take the time to learn and grow from their romantic partners. ## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

When it comes to health, The 8 of Cups Tarot card can symbolize the need to step back and reevaluate your physical, emotional and mental wellbeing. This could mean taking a break from a stressful situation and allowing yourself to recuperate, refocus, and recharge. It could also mean quitting a habit that is no longer beneficial to your wellbeing and instead focussing on your physical, emotional and mental health.

In regards to finances, The 8 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a need to withdraw from a situation, especially if it has been causing stress or distress. This may mean withdrawing money from certain accounts, or cutting ties with an investment opportunity. It could also mean moving away from a particular job, job situation or lifestyle that can result in financial strain. Taking a step back and reevaluating your finances can prove beneficial, and sometimes drastic measures are required when it comes to your financial wellbeing.

When it comes to relationships, The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign to reflect on the current dynamics of your relationships and to see if they are bringing you joy, energy and connection. It could mean stepping away from a situation that has become toxic and is no longer productive. It could also indicate the need to put yourself first and to focus on your own wellbeing and goals. Moving away from a situation that is no longer beneficial for you can be a difficult and emotional step, but it could be necessary for your overall wellbeing and satisfaction.
