A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and The Page of Swords card together suggest that in order to gain clarity and spiritual illumination, one must dig deep and ask probing questions. It reminds us that obtaining personal truth will require a level of discernment, and that we should watch out for gossip, spies, and prying eyes that may stand in our way. The Page of Swords reminds us that one must be honest, but also prudent and careful when seeking the truth. In the end, we must stay true to ourselves and trust our innermost intuition to find personal growth and success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit card in the Tarot can represent a wide array of meanings related to health, finances and relationships. In terms of health, the Hermit can signify a desire to take a step back and take care of yourself. This might involve taking time off from stressful situations, implementing a healthy sleeping and eating schedule, or going on a retreat to spend time with yourself and your inner thoughts. It can also serve as a reminder to prioritize your physical and mental wellbeing, as ultimately, this will help you take better care of your relationships and finances.

In terms of finances, the Hermit can signify a need to take a break from spending and strive for more financial stability. This could mean creating a budget, limiting unnecessary shopping and investing more money in savings. It can also help guide you to be mindful about your current financial state, trusting the process of how your financial situation will unfold.

In terms of relationships, the Hermit card can represent a need for introspection and personal healing. It can be a reminder to examine how your current relationships or dating patterns are impacting your life and address any patterns that are no longer serving you. The Hermit card can also signify a period of pause and introspection as you come to understand and accept your own needs and desires. Ultimately, the Hermit card encourages us to take a step back and focus on our own personal growth and development before we can truly allow ourselves to be in healthy and fulfilling relationships.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

The Page Of Swords Tarot card is a complex card that can mean different things depending on its position in relation to other cards or the context of the reading. For health, it can represent a need to be honest with yourself and open up to those around you. It can also represent the need to take a realistic approach to any health problems you are experiencing, and to take a critical look at any advice or treatments you may receive. It’s also a reminder to stay vigilant and aware of your own physical and mental well-being.

When it comes to finances, the Page Of Swords Tarot card can be a warning of potential danger on the horizon. It can represent a warning of financial loss, theft, or fraud. It may also signify a need to be careful with your money, to look into all options before making a financial decision, and to be mindful of any potential risks.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Page Of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to maintain both honesty and boundaries. It may be an indication that secrets or misunderstandings could potentially be destructive, and that open communication is necessary in order to maintain a healthy relationship. It can also represent the need to stay curious and inquisitive, and never take anything at face value. This could be especially important when it comes to relationship issues, as it may be necessary to look deeper into the issue before making any decisions.
