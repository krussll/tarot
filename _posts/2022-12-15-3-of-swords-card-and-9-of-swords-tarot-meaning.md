## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords tarot card is a difficult card to interpret, as it denotes such negative emotions as nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression. In order to understand the implications of this card, one must look at it in the context of health, finances, and relationships.

When it comes to health, the 9 of Swords tarot card signifies mental and physical exhaustion. Those who are affected by this card are likely to suffer from insomnia, headaches, and a general feeling of unease. The card often represents a situation in which physical ailments are primarily caused by or worsened by mental or emotional distress. Stress and anxiety will be what is causing people to be fatigued, particularly in the context of health.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Swords tarot card suggests a period of financial difficulty and hardship. This card does not necessarily indicate complete financial ruin, however, it does indicate that the person is likely to struggle financially and have difficulty in managing their budget. It suggests a period of great financial strain, where resources are limited and there is simply not enough money to make ends meet.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 9 of Swords tarot card can signify a period of conflict, tension, and uncertainty. Those affected by this card may experience communication breakdowns in their relationships, where they are unable to express themselves clearly. This card can also indicate a situation where trust is broken and it can be difficult to rebuild. In personal relationships, the negative emotions of fear and anxiety can create feelings of disconnection and distance.

The 9 of Swords tarot card can be a difficult card to interpret, but it is important to remember that it does not signify complete despair and hopelessness. It simply encourages people to take a step back and recognize their emotions, so that they can work towards finding healthier and better solutions.
