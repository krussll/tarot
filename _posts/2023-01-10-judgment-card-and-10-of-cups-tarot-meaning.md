A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The Judgment tarot card alongside the 10 Of Cups represent a time of clarity and decision-making, as well as a call to discover your life’s purpose. It is a period in your life when you have access to your family’s love and support to make these decisions; they will be there to cheer you on as you strive towards your goals. The Judgment card can serve as a reminder to take heart and to understand that you are ready to pursue the direction of your dreams, safe in the knowledge that your family will offer their total love and support.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Judgment mean?

For Health:  
The Judgment Tarot card is a powerful card when it comes to health. This card can bring a wake-up call to make us aware of the importance of taking care of our physical, mental and emotional health. It could also be a call to get a medical check-up or to look into more natural healing methods. We may be prompted to look within and assess our overall health, to make sure we are taking care of our body, mind and spirit. This card can remind us to listen to our intuition and trust our inner voice when it comes to making decisions about our health.

For Finances:  
The Judgment Tarot card can bring clarity to our financial situation. This card can help us to make a final decision on our budgets, investments and savings. It can also be a sign that it is time to wake up and take control of our finances. This card can prompt us to make wise and informed decisions, while being mindful of our long-term goals. The Judgment card can also help us to overcome financial challenges and open up new pathways of abundance.

For Relationships:  
The Judgment Tarot card can be a powerful symbol for relationships. This card can bring clarity to our relationships and help us to make decisions about how to move forward together. It can remind us that our relationships have a deeper purpose, and encourage us to be more mindful of our own desires, needs, and boundaries. It can also be a wake-up call to be intentional in how we communicate with others, and to prioritize our relationships. Ultimately, the Judgment card can remind us of the importance of being honest and authentic in our relationships.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 10 Of Cups Tarot card is a sign of overall health and wellness. It symbolizes the attainment of peace and harmony, which in turn can lead to total physical and mental health. From this card, we can conclude that it means we are on the path of being in tune with our innermost spirits. We can expect deep relaxation, optimum nutrition and exercise, and a feeling of being whole. The 10 Of Cups Tarot card can also signify that we are on the road to recovering from any illnesses, or even preventing them from occurring in the first place.

Finances: The 10 Of Cups Tarot card can be seen as a sign of abundance in our finances. It is a reminder that money comes and goes, but overall, we are blessed with a secure financial future. This card can suggest that our investments are beginning to pay off, and that we have enough to pay our bills and indulge in our desires. In addition, the 10 Of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to be conscious of how we spend our money, and that when we turn to our faith and trust the universe, abundance flows.

Relationships: The 10 Of Cups Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of family, love, and support. It speaks of a strong foundation built on trust and understanding. Any couple or family unit represented by this card are likely experiencing a strong level of harmony and understanding shared between them. This card symbolizes that we are surrounded by genuine people who care for us and are there for us through any situation. It is also a reminder to always show appreciation for those that we love and be present in our relationships.
