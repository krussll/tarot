A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 9 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Strength card and 9 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 9 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The 9 of Pentacles tarot card together represent a powerful combination of self-love, financial independence, and courage. They speak of a self-respect that is not only strong, but also a source of pleasure in life. Self-love and self-sufficiency will lead to financial gain, while courage and strength are needed to make the most of these resources. This card pairing is telling you to move forward with confidence, and to enjoy the rewards that come with hard work and diligence. Having both these cards in your reading is a sign of a high potential for success, as your self-respect and courage work together to create opportunities for financial wealth and personal growth.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Strength and 9 Of Pentacles mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Strength and 9 Of Pentacles the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does Strength mean?

Health

The Strength card represents our ability to love ourselves and make decisions in accordance with our own physical and mental health. This means having courage in making decisions that prioritize our own wellbeing, even if they may not immediately make us feel the most comfortable. This could include making the difficult decision to end a toxic relationship, to change jobs or to seek help with mental health issues. The Strength card encourages us to recognize our individual strengths and use those to be decisive and prioritize our own health, despite any fear that may be present.

Finances

The Strength card encourages us to reflect on our financial decisions with care and to have courage when exploring new ways of managing our finances that prioritize long-term savings or investments. It also encourages us to be patient and take a step back to review our financial goals and objectives, to ensure our decisions are in line with our goals, and to have the resilience and dedication to stick to them.

Relationships

The Strength card encourages us to recognize that relationships require both self-love and unconditional love in order to be successful. This means that in order to be truly happy, we must be patient, understanding and kind to ourselves, and also to those around us. The Strength card also stresses the importance of honesty and communication in relationships, and encourages us to be brave and honest when discussing topics that may not be easy to talk about. It also reminds us that we are all worthy of love, and encourages us to seek out partners and friendships who will respect and honor that.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

Health: When The 9 Of Pentacles Tarot card comes up in a reading, it symbolizes that one has found a healthy balance in their life. This card speaks of a state of self-care, where one is able to invest in their own well-being and take pleasure in it. It may also suggest that one is taking a break from others and taking the time to enjoy some solo activities and pleasures. For example, one may feel the need to take some time to get away and enjoy a spa day, go on a nature hike, or read a favorite book.

Finances: Financially, the 9 Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that one is in a position of security and stability. This card symbolizes that one is actively striving for financial independence and working hard to achieve it. They may have just achieved a level of financial security and stability, and feel secure in the knowledge that they can provide for themselves. It also speaks of a growing level of wealth, where one’s hard work is reaping rewards.

Relationships: When The 9 Of Pentacles Tarot card appears in a reading, it suggests that one’s relationships are in a good, balanced state. This card speaks of feeling comfortable and secure in your relationships, so one can enjoy them without worrying about the future. It may also suggest that one is taking some time to focus on themselves and their own needs, rather than depending on someone else to provide all of their happiness. In this case, it may be time to take some time for yourself and just enjoy all the little pleasures life can provide.
