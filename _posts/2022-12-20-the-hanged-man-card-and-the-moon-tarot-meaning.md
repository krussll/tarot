A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and The Moon card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and The Moon have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and The Moon mean together?

The combination of The Hanged Man tarot card and The Moon tarot card suggest that wisdom, self-sacrifice, and getting a different perspective can help you better manage unseen problems and voluntary changes. They indicate that you need to remind yourself to trust your intuition during this challenging time and keep an open mind so that you can see the situation from a different angle. They also suggest that in order to find clarity and achieve a better outcome, you may have to make a few tough and sometimes selfless decisions. Ultimately, they urge you to pay attention to these inner signs and trust your instinctual wisdom as you work through these changes.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hanged Man and The Moon mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hanged Man and The Moon the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It signals a time for reflection and re-evaluation before making any major decisions related to health. It is a reminder to take advantage of this moment to pause and reassess the situation before taking any drastic measures. Finances: The Hanged Man Tarot card is a sign that it is time to look at finances from a different angle. It is time to find new ways to manage money and it is a warning that rash decisions or short-term gains should be avoided. The card may be indicating that by giving up something of value, another outcome may be more beneficial in the long run. It can point to a need to reassess investments and look for more creative ways to make and save money.

Relationships: The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages a complete assessment of any relationships in your life. It suggests that there may be a need to let go of any negative situations and focus on nurturing positive, supportive relationships. It counsels taking a step back, pausing to reflect and then looking at things from a different perspective. The card implies the need to be self-sacrificing in relationships in order to have joy and satisfaction in them. It is a reminder to practice patience and compromise in order to find harmony.

This can mean revisiting our diet, switching up our exercise routine or giving ourselves permission to make a change that we feel is necessary. It is also a sign that taking time to reflect on our health aspirations is beneficial.

In terms of finances, The Moon Tarot card speaks to hidden forces that may be influencing our money-related decisions. It can be a warning to trust our intuition and be aware of our financial choices. It can also be a sign to go out of our comfort zone and do something different when it comes to managing our money. We should take some time to reflect and reassess our financial situation and try to look at it from a different perspective.

When it comes to relationships, The Moon Tarot card encourages us to take a step back and explore our emotions in an effort to better understand the relationships we have with others. It is a reminder to pay close attention to our intuition and be open to the changes that can come out of our relationships. We should also be mindful of the ebb and flow of our relationships and give them space to grow as we learn more about ourselves.
