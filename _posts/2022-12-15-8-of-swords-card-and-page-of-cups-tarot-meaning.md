A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together?

When considering the 8 of Swords tarot card and Page of Cups tarot card together, it speaks to taking a break from the outside world and focusing on self-care. It suggests that now would be a good time to take some time to be alone, focus on self-love, and explore new creative endeavors. It also speaks to the importance of giving back and volunteering in some capacity. This can be a time to focus on the betterment of oneself and others around you. It encourages avoiding the self-imposed restrictions and isolation that often come with fear and doubt. With the help of the Page of Cups, this can be a time to find joy and love in the small moments that life has to offer.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

The 8 of Swords tarot card indicates that an individual is subject to self-imposed restrictions and may be feeling trapped, isolated and imprisoned in some area of their life. This could be reflected in their physical, mental, or emotional health, or their finances and relationships.

When it comes to physical health, the 8 of Swords implies a sense of suffocation and stagnation. The individual may feel as though they’re stuck in a rut and unable to improve their overall health and wellbeing. They may be putting limits on themselves when it comes to exercise, nutrition, and other aspects of their lifestyle. To break free, they need to become aware of their self-imposed restrictions and take steps to loosen them so that their physical health can start to improve.

When it comes to finances, the 8 of Swords suggests a sense of limitation and feeling tied down by the structure of budgeting and other financial obligations. The individual may be limiting themselves in terms of career opportunities or investments, or they may be avoiding taking any risks out of fear of failure. The key to improving their financial situation is to recognize their restrictions, take stock of their current circumstances, and then take action to create new opportunities and improve their financial situation.

In terms of relationships, the 8 of Swords implies a sense of being trapped in a stagnant dynamic. The individual may be feeling overwhelmed by their own expectations and those of their partner, or they may be avoiding new relationships out of fear of rejection or commitment. To break free, they need to become aware of their self-imposed restrictions and take steps to open up and create more meaningful connections.

The 8 of Swords tarot card is a reminder that it’s important to become aware of any self-imposed restrictions and take steps to break free from them. Once the individual is aware of their limitations, they can begin to make changes that will improve their physical, mental, and emotional health, their finances, and their relationships. Doing so can help the individual break free from feeling isolated and imprisoned and start to live their life with more freedom and joy.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health: The Page of Cups Tarot card is associated with health in terms of both physical and emotional well-being. Physically, it can represent the need for balance and taking time to care for one’s body. This can include eating nutritiously, getting regular exercise, and pursuing fun activities that promote a sense of happiness and contentment. Emotionally, the Page of Cups Tarot card can signify a willingness to explore emotions and feelings. It can mean a willingness to be open to the healing power of self-reflection and acceptance. By taking time to nurture one’s emotional wellbeing, the Page of Cups Tarot card can help one to become more in tune with one’s self and gain greater understanding of how to stay true to themselves in all aspects of their life.

Finances: The Page of Cups Tarot card indicates a period of financial growth and exploration. This card can represent the desire to take calculated risks with one’s finances, such as investing in a new venture or taking a risk in the stock market. It also lessons the need for security and the feeling that taking chances can bring rewards in the form of increased wealth and abundance. It is also a reminder to create a budget and practice fiscal responsibility, so that any decisions made are done so with a financial plan and strategy in place.

Relationships: The Page of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the beginning of a new relationship or the deepening of an existing one. This card suggests being open to exploring emotions, expressing vulnerability and building trust. It is a reminder to be honest and open with ones feelings and the feelings of others. This card also tells us to stay aware of the dynamics of a relationship in terms of power, respect and space. With the energies of the Page of Cups, it is important to be mindful of how we relate to others, and how we are communicating with them. It is also a reminder to stay loyal and patient, as relationships do not always develop overnight and can require nurturing.
