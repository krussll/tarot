Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The 10 Of Swords card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 10 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 10 Of Swords.

## 10 Of Swords And Career And Finances – An Overview

The 10 of Swords tarot card speaks to the idea of reaching a point of complete and painful endings in our health.

It speaks to the idea that we can reach a point where our health has declined to its absolute lowest, and that we can find ourselves in a position where we have no choice but to surrender to the situation.

It is a reminder to take care of our health, and to strive to achieve balance.

This card can also represent a time of healing, as we move through the pain and darkness of the situation and take the necessary steps to restore our health.

Ultimately, the 10 of Swords tarot card challenges us to learn from the experience and use it to inform our decisions moving forward to ensure that we don’t find ourselves in this position again.

## Upright Meanings For 10 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The 10 of Swords is a reminder that our career and financial situations can be subject to sudden and catastrophic endings.

It is a reminder to be aware of the risks and potential pitfalls associated with our financial decisions.

The card is a warning that even when we think we are at our peak, we can still find ourselves suddenly at rock bottom.

This card serves as a reminder to take calculated risks and to be prepared for the worst if our plans do not turn out as we had hoped.

It is also a reminder to stay resilient and to keep striving for the best outcome, knowing that even the most difficult of times can eventually lead to a better future.

### What It Means For The Future

The 10 of Swords tarot card is a sign that you may be approaching a complete and painful ending in either your current career or future finances.

It could mean that you may be headed for a career setback or a financial low point.

This card is a reminder that you won’t reach heights of success unless you first hit rock bottom.

To reach success, you need to accept and learn from failure, no matter how hard it may be.

Trust that this difficult period will eventually pass and that, in the long run, it will all be worth it.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 10 of Swords tarot card symbolizes a complete and painful ending, representing a person hitting rock bottom in their careers and finances.

This card suggests that those close to me may be nearing the end of a difficult period in their lives, with the possibility of a major financial setback or career-halting event.

It’s important to offer the support and guidance that may be needed to move forward and regain a sense of security and stability.

It might also be beneficial to provide advice and resources to help these people develop a plan for the future and to ensure that they understand the risks and rewards associated with their current financial and professional circumstances.

## Reversed Meanings For 10 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When the 10 of Swords is reversed, it can indicate a new beginning in terms of your career and finances.

This card speaks to the possibility of a fresh start, a reset of sorts.

It may be the message that you need to let go of the past, to move on from old beliefs and habits that are no longer serving your best interests.

This card can symbolize the end of a painful experience, and the beginning of a journey of exploration and self-discovery.

Financially, the 10 of Swords reversed suggests that you may be starting to move away from the burdens of debt and financial struggle.

This may be a time of taking responsibility for your financial situation and taking steps to improve it.

You may be learning to budget, investing in assets, or taking on new sources of income.

Your finances may be on the upswing, and you may be starting to see the results of your efforts.

Your career may also be on the upswing.

This card can indicate that you are finally getting recognition for your hard work, finding success in your field, and being able to pursue your passions.

You may be taking on new opportunities and gaining the skills and knowledge to excel.

This card may also point to you taking risks and investing in yourself, allowing you to reach new heights in your career.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 10 of Swords indicates that it may be time for a complete and painful ending in your career and finances.

You may be feeling a sense of hopelessness and despair about your career, but this is an opportunity for you to take a step back, reassess your goals, and start anew.

It could be that you’ve been working hard towards a goal that has resulted in a dead-end, or it could be that you need to shed the weight of a job or financial burden that’s been dragging you down.

Whatever it is, the reversed 10 of Swords is telling you that it’s time to let go and move on.

This is an exciting time to be creative and think outside of the box.

Maybe it’s time to pursue a new career path, take on a side hustle, or start a business.

It’s also a great time to get creative with your finances and explore new ways to manage and invest your money, such as starting an emergency fund, investing in the stock market, or exploring passive income streams.

The reversed 10 of Swords is a call to action.

It’s time to make a change, no matter how difficult it may seem.

There may be some pain and discomfort in the process, but it will be worth it in the end.

You are resilient, and you can make it through.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed 10 of Swords tarot card appears in a reading, it can indicate that a career or financial situation close to you is about to experience a period of complete and painful ending.

This could mean that the person in question may have hit rock bottom, and have to start over from scratch.

It could also indicate that a business venture or investment has come to a disastrous end, leaving the person in financial ruin.

On the plus side, the reversed 10 of Swords can also be a sign that this difficult situation is only temporary and that the person in question will eventually be able to rebuild their career and finances.

The person may need to be patient and not give up hope, as this period of difficulty could eventually lead to great opportunities.

It is also a sign that it is important to be honest and humble when dealing with the current situation, and to seek out advice and support from those around them.

With a bit of hard work and dedication, it is possible to come back from this difficult period and start anew.
