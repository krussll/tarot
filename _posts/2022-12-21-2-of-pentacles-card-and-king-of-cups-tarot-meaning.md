## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Pentacles and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Pentacles and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It serves as an encouragement to make conscious choices that benefit your physical, mental, and emotional well-being. This card indicates that it is important to stay flexible enough to adapt to changing demands, but not so adaptable that you take on too much. The 2 Of Pentacles Tarot card can encourage one to create a balance between their finances and their lifestyle. When this card appears, it is a reminder to weigh both your financial needs and desires before making a decision. This card is a not just an invitation to consider your current financial situation but to also anticipate any possible future changes. This card can remind you of the value of planning for the future and suggests that it is possible to enjoy the present without sacrificing opportunities for the future.

Relationships:

The 2 Of Pentacles Tarot card also speaks to the value of navigating relationships with care and balance. It encourages a person to assess their relationships in terms of give and take. It can also be seen as a reminder that we all need to nurture our relationships if we want them to continue to thrive. ## What does King Of Cups mean?

The King of Cups Tarot card represents a supportive leader, tolerance, and empathy. As a card of guidance, it reflects the power of understanding and listening to the needs of others, which can be incredibly important for healthy relationships, financial decisions and physical and mental well-being.

Health: The King of Cups encourages you to be empathetic and supportive of yourself and those around you who are facing physical or mental health issues. Taking the time to listen, help and be present in times of struggle and pain provides immense benefits to all parties involved. As a leader, it is also important to recognize when your help is no longer necessary. Knowing when to step back and give others space to heal and recover is a key attribute of the King of Cups.

Finances: This card can be interpreted as a sign to use careful consideration and restraint when making financial decisions. It suggests that exercising empathy and understanding can do wonders for your financial prospects. Before you commit to a purchase or investment, look for ways to support yourself and others who might benefit from the decision. While having money and security is valuable, it’s also important to recognize when you have enough and give back to those in need.

Relationships: The King of Cups encourages you to listen and be patient with other people. Give those you care about your full attention and support them no matter how difficult the situation. Being a good listener and exhibiting understanding can help repair strained relationships or build strong ones. Acting with respect, self-control, and forgiveness are all qualities that The King of Cups wants you to adopt in your personal relationships.
