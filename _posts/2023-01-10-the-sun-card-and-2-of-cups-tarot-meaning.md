A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 2 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 2 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 2 Of Cups mean together?

Together, The Sun and The 2 Of Cups tarot cards suggest a long-lasting and joyous union between two people. This could represent a successful marriage, proposal, or even the deepening of a romantic partnership. Whatever the result, the coming union will bring a great deal of material happiness and contentment to both parties involved. The cards are a promise that although times may be challenging, there is joy and positivity to be found in the union of two people in love.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Sun and 2 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Sun and 2 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card symbolizes good health. It could mean that your physical health is on the rise and the vitality you feel is unparalleled. You may be in a period of good health and energy, and you will enjoy the overall feeling of peace and well-being that comes with it. The Sun Tarot card also encourages you to take care of your physical and mental health. You should strive to be balanced in both aspects to ensure that you remain healthy and happy.

For Finances:

The Sun Tarot card indicates financial or professional success and abundance. This could mean that your financial situation is strong and that you are likely to make substantial gains or increase your investments in the coming months. You may be feeling secure and confident about your financial situation, and you may be feeling in control of your finances. It could also indicate that now is a good time to invest in something new and to take risks in your business.

For Relationships:

The Sun Tarot card speaks of relationships that are built on a solid foundation and trust. This could mean that you have a strong relationship with your friends, family, and romantic partners that is based on mutual respect and understanding. You may be enjoying the companionship and love of your friends and family and also of your significant other. It could also indicate that your love life is going well, potentially leading to marriage or a blossoming romance. It could also suggest that you may be surrounded by positive, supportive people who will help you to reach your goals.

## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

Health: In terms of health, The 2 of Cups Tarot card points to a strong balance and harmony of energy and wellbeing. It suggests that a healthy attitude and outlook can help improve overall physical health. It can be a sign of increased positive energy and vitality, suggesting the ability to recover quickly and stay healthy.

Finances: Money is also connected with The 2 of Cups Tarot card. Generally, it reflects an abundance of resources and an increase of wealth. When drawn in a reading, it could indicate that money is coming in through investment or a job offer. It also hints at a strong balance between savings and expenses, so it can be a sign of financial stability.

Relationships: The 2 of Cups Tarot card often signals the potential of making a lasting connection with someone else. This could be a romantic partner, a business partner, or someone you could form a close friendship with. It suggests that when approaching a relationship, we should look for a balanced exchange of emotions and resources. It also indicates that the relationship is based on mutual respect and understanding.
