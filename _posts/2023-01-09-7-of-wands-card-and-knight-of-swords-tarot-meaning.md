A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean together?

The 7 Of Wands and The Knight Of Swords tarot cards together urge you to stay firm and protect yourself when the competition grows fierce. As a fast-talking person, you can use your words to your advantage, and protect yourself from those trying to bring you down. This may mean moving towards a more positive lifestyle change, with a focus on self-care and bettering yourself. It also means being wise about how you defend yourself, and understanding when it is time to step back and regroup. The combination of these cards suggests that you can prepare yourself for success, and stand tall in the face of opposition.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 7 of Wands Tarot card is an indication of good physical health, but there is an important caveat: you should be prepared to defend yourself against any negative health habits or disease that may come your way. This card suggests taking preventative measures to safeguard your body and mind, such as eating well, exercising regularly, and getting adequate rest. You may also need to be wary of people or external forces that could threaten your health, whether it’s by spreading sickness or spreading bad health advice. This card is a reminder to be cautious and to take all the steps necessary to protect yourself and maintain your health.

Finances

The 7 of Wands Tarot card suggests that in order to protect your finances, you need to be aware of your competition, whether it is in business or in life. There may be people or organizations that you’ll need to watch out for, who could be trying to get a leg up on you financially. You need to be sharp and smart when it comes to money, and protect your assets from those who may be trying to take advantage of you. Don’t be afraid to take calculated risks, as long as you’re sure of yourself, but also be prepared to defend and protect the financial resources that you have.

Relationships

The 7 of Wands Tarot card is also a reminder to protect yourself and preserve your boundaries when it comes to relationships, both romantic and platonic. There may be people in your life who try to manipulate or influence you, or who may be pushing their own agendas in order to get what they want. In this case, you must stand your ground and be sure to safeguard yourself and your own best interests. At the same time, you should also be prepared to fight for a relationship that is important to you and protect it from outside forces that may be trying to come between you and a loved one.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

The Knight of Swords Tarot card has a powerful message for health, finances and relationships. This card is often seen as a card of change and courage, with its bold and swift energy.

When it comes to health, the Knight of Swords is often a reminder to take control of one’s health, to be more proactive and cut through any procrastination. This card can also be a warning sign that one’s health is not at its best, and that bold steps are needed to restore balance. The Knight of Swords can serve as a sign that it’s time to cut out unhealthy habits, to take better care of oneself and take charge of one’s health.

In terms of finances, the Knight of Swords is a reminder to act now. It can be a sign that one needs to be more aggressive in their financial decisions, to take risks and invest in the future. When it comes to relationships this card is often a reminder to be honest, to communicate clearly and to not allow emotions to stand in the way of truth. The Knight of Swords can also be a reminder to be bold and to take action to make changes that benefit relationships.

Overall, the Knight of Swords Tarot card is a card of change and courage, reminding us to take bold steps to change our lives and make positive decisions that will impact our health, finances and relationships.
