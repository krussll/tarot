Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The 8 Of Swords card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 8 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 8 Of Swords.

## 8 Of Swords And Health – An Overview

The 8 Of Swords tarot card can be interpreted as representing a period of health stagnation due to self-imposed restrictions.

It suggests that the individual is placing limits on their own health in some way, or that they are feeling trapped in a situation that is inhibiting their growth.

It can be seen as a warning sign that the individual needs to break free from the feelings of isolation and imprisonment in order to improve health.

Whether this means taking a step back from certain activities that are causing stress, seeking help from a professional, or simply taking time out to relax, it is important to remember that a person has the power to make a positive change for themselves.

## Upright Meanings For 8 Of Swords And Health

### What It Means For Me

The 8 Of Swords tarot card speaks to the physical and mental restrictions that we impose on ourselves when it comes to our health.

It suggests that we may feel that we are stuck in an unhealthy pattern, or that we are preventing ourselves from taking steps to improve our health.

It can also signify that we are living in isolation from the world and from our loved ones, or that we have imprisoned ourselves in a toxic lifestyle that is not beneficial for us.

It is a reminder to take a step back and to reflect on our current health situation and what we can do to make improvements.

Ultimately, it is an invitation to free ourselves from self-imposed restrictions and to make healthier choices for our well-being.

### What It Means For The Future

The 8 of Swords in my future health reading is a warning that I am in danger of self-imposing restrictions on myself that may unintentionally lead to a state of isolation or imprisonment.

This could manifest in a few different ways, such as self-destructive behaviors, like over-exercising, overeating, or avoiding healthy activities; or it could be something more extreme like developing an anxiety disorder or depression.

Whatever form it takes, it is important for me to be aware of the potential for such a situation and to take proactive steps to prevent it from happening.

I should focus on creating a balanced and healthy lifestyle, filled with meaningful and enjoyable activities that will help me stay connected with the world and keep my emotional and mental health in check.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 8 of Swords tarot card indicates that those close to me may be feeling trapped and isolated, as if they are stuck in a prison of their own making.

This could be due to a variety of reasons, such as a fear of failure, a lack of self-confidence, or a refusal to accept help from others.

It is important that I help those in my life to recognize the limitations they are imposing on themselves and provide the necessary support to help them break free of their self-enforced restrictions and take steps towards a healthier and happier life.

## Reversed Meanings For 8 Of Swords And Health

### What It Means For Me

When The 8 of Swords tarot card appears in a reversed position, it can be a sign of regaining control over one’s life.

It can mean that the individual is breaking free from self-imposed restrictions and overcoming their feelings of isolation and imprisonment.

This newfound freedom can create a newfound sense of freedom and power to make healthy choices.

In terms of health, The 8 of Swords reversed can be a sign of taking charge of one’s health and wellbeing.

It can mean that the individual is taking steps to make better lifestyle choices and create a healthier lifestyle.

It can also mean that the individual is breaking free from unhealthy habits and embracing new, healthier habits.

They are taking control of their own health and wellbeing and taking steps towards a healthier and happier life.

This card can also be a sign of healing and recovery from illness or injury, as the individual is taking charge of their own healing process.

### What It Means For The Future

When the 8 of Swords appears reversed in a Tarot reading, it indicates that the person may be experiencing a freeing from self-imposed restrictions or isolation.

This could manifest in the form of a newfound sense of liberation, or a loosening of the ties that bind them to certain habits or behaviours that no longer serve them.

This card suggests that the person may be ready to let go of feelings of overwhelmedness, confusion, or helplessness that can come with feeling too confined or restricted.

It may also be a sign that the person is beginning to recognize their own power and potential, and is ready to make changes that promote physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing.

The reversed 8 of Swords can indicate that the person is beginning to break down the walls they have built around themselves, and is now taking responsibility for their own health and well-being.

This could mean taking steps towards healthier habits and behaviours, or seeking out support and advice to help them on their journey.

Ultimately, the reversed 8 of Swords shows that the person is ready to break free from restrictive self-imposed isolation and begin to create a healthier and more fulfilling life for themselves.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the 8 of Swords appears reversed in a Tarot reading, it can be an indication that it’s time to break free from any self-imposed health restrictions.

The card often signifies that a person has been too hard on themselves, which has caused them to become stuck in a cycle of guilt and despair.

The reversed 8 of Swords can represent a need to take a step back and question any negative beliefs or attitudes that may be holding you back from achieving your health goals.

It can also symbolize that a person has been too focused on the end goal, at the expense of their own wellbeing.

This can mean that they are pushing themselves too hard, leading to feelings of exhaustion and frustration.

The reversed 8 of Swords indicates that it is essential for a person to take a break and take the time to focus on their own needs.

It is important to recognize that health and wellbeing is a journey, and that progress can be made through taking small steps every day.

Learning to listen to your body and honor its needs is an important part of self-care that can help to improve your health and wellbeing.
