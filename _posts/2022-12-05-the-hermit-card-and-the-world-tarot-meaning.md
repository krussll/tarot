A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and The World card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and The World have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and The World mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and The World tarot card combined, symbolize a journey of spiritual growth and enlightenment. The Hermit card encourages you to delve deeper within and explore the hidden aspects of yourself for ultimate spiritual awakening. Meanwhile, The World tarot card signifies that you have achieved a major goal or milestone in your spiritual journey and are ready to embark on a more meaningful endeavor. Together, these two cards show that you are ready to take your spiritual journey to a higher level of understanding and transcendence.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Hermit and The World mean together for your love life?

The Hermit and The World Tarot cards together represent a highly spiritual journey for your love life. The Hermit speaks to the need for introspection and inner reflection, as this is the only way to gain greater clarity and insight into your true feelings. This process of personal exploration is essential for navigating the complexities of relationships, as it will allow you to better understand your own desires, motivations, and expectations.

The World card, on the other hand, is a card of completion, travel, and emigration. It suggests that you must move beyond your comfort zone and be willing to step outside the boundaries of your familiar environment in order to gain the invaluable lessons that your journey has to offer. It is only through courage and exploration that you will gain the knowledge and understanding required to successfully build a strong and lasting relationship.

The combination of The Hermit and The World Tarot cards therefore indicates that you must be willing to embark on a highly spiritual journey in order to find true love and a healthy, lasting relationship. You must find the courage to explore beyond the boundaries of the known and go within to discover the answers that you seek. Only through this process of spiritual exploration will you gain the clarity and understanding needed to find a relationship that is true and lasting.

### What does The Hermit and The World mean together for your finances?

The Hermit tarot card and The World tarot card together can be interpreted to mean that if you are at a crossroads or struggling to make a financial decision, it is time to take some time to reflect and look inward for clarity. This means that you should take some time to focus on yourself and think deeply about what you want, what your goals are, and what makes the most sense financially and professionally.

In terms of finances and career, the combination of The Hermit and The World tarot cards suggests that you may have reached a point of completion and must now take a step back in order to gain a clearer perspective. This could point to the need to evaluate your current job and make sure it is still the right fit for you, or to evaluate your current financial situation and make sure that you are making the right decisions for your future.

The World tarot card in combination with The Hermit card also speaks to the need to travel and explore. This could mean traveling to a different country to gain some new perspectives and experiences, or perhaps traveling to attend a conference or educational event to learn more about your field. This could also be interpreted as a need to move for career opportunities or a better job.

Overall, the combination of The Hermit and The World tarot cards encourages you to take some time for yourself and go within for clarity about your finances and career. This combination can indicate a need to evaluate your current job and financial situation, as well as a need to explore and travel in order to gain perspective and potentially open up new opportunities.

Combined, The Hermit and The World Tarot cards suggest that you should take some time out of the external hustle and bustle to go within and reflect on your health journey thus far. You may be presented with insights and clarity on changes you could make that would be beneficial for your overall health. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card is associated with introspection and the process of looking within for answers. This applies to all facets of life, including health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the Hermit speaks of taking a step back to really assess how we feel, both physically and mentally. It can be a reminder to take some time to understand what we need to nourish our body, mind and soul. The Hermit encourages us to be mindful of our health and to seek out the best advice and treatment for our well-being.

Financially, The Hermit encourages us to take a look at our individual financial situations and to make sure that we are managing our resources in a sustainable way. It can be a reminder to investigate the best financial options for our individual goals and to make sure that we are taking the opportunity to save and invest in our future.

When it comes to relationships, The Hermit Tarot card speaks of the need to take time for ourselves and to reflect on our own values and desires. It encourages us to look within to ensure that our relationships are based on mutual understanding and trust. The Hermit also speaks to taking a step back to gain clarity on our relationships and to think about how we can nurture our connections with others.

Overall, The Hermit Tarot card reminds us to take a step back and to look within for answers. It encourages us to be conscious of our health, finances and relationships, and to make sure that they are sustainable and nourishing. The Hermit cards speaks of the importance of introspection and personal growth, and a renewed commitment to our well-being.

## What does The World mean?

Health: For health, The World Tarot card signifies the end of a health journey or lesson. It could indicate the completion of a medical treatment or the completion of a health-focused project, such as a diet or fitness regimen. It could also mean the end of a difficult situation or period of ill health. In any case, the card suggests a sense of relief, accomplishment, and balance in one’s well-being.

Finances: For finances, The World Tarot card could indicate the successful completion of a project or deal, the coming to fruition of an investment, or the ending of a financial cycle. It is a sign of financial stability, success, and even luxury. It is also a reminder to be mindful and grateful for the good fortune that has been achieved.

Relationships: The World Tarot card in relationships can represent lasting, secure relationships, such as marriage or partnerships. It could also signify the end of a difficult period in a relationship or the coming to a successful conclusion of a relationship issue. The card encourages patience, understanding, and mutual respect in order to ensure the lasting nature of any relationship.
