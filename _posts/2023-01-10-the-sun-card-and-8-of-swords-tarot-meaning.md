## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does The Sun mean?

Health

The Sun Tarot card represents a time of rejuvenation and renewal when it comes to health. Those with this card in their Tarot spread can look forward to a period of good health and vitality. It could mean an end to a long bout of illness or the start of a new healing process. This card can also mean that the person has the strength and wisdom necessary to handle any illness or injury they may be suffering from. Overall, this card is a positive sign that health can be improved with positive action and a sunny outlook on life.

Finances

The Sun Tarot card means a period of great prosperity and success when it comes to finances. This card is a sign that it’s time to take stock of your finances and gain a better understanding of where you are in order to move forward. It could also mean a windfall of money or the chance to take a big money-making opportunity. If this card appears in your Tarot spread then it’s time to focus your mental energy on money matters and enjoy the fruits of your labors.

Relationships

The Sun Tarot card indicates a time when relationships can flourish and move to another level. It can signify a joyous union between two people or the strengthening of a bond. This card is often seen as a sign of a successful outcome in love and marriage. It can also indicate a period of increased understanding and communication that can make relationships even stronger. Overall, it is a positive sign that will bring joy and harmony to all relationships.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card represents mental and physical self-restrictions related to health. When this card appears in a health-related reading, it indicates that you are limiting your physical and mental well-being by focusing on negative thoughts and feelings. The 8 of Swords represents a period of mental and physical stagnation, as well as feelings of being trapped in an uncomfortable and unhealthy situation. As a result, you may feel powerless to make changes or take action, leading to isolation and even depression. You may also be avoiding medical advice, refusing to admit that you need help, or avoiding asking for help. Despite these negative feelings and beliefs, the 8 of Swords can provide an opportunity to discover the importance of nourishing your body and mind, and finding a path to a healthier lifestyle.

Finances:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card is a warning about financial restrictions and how to avoid them. When this card appears in a reading about finances, it indicates that you are likely feeling trapped in a financial situation and unable to find a way out. It may be because of limited resources or a fear of making wrong decisions and losing money. This card suggests that you are allowing yourself to be stuck in an unsustainable or unstable financial situation, instead of taking steps to change it. The 8 of Swords encourages you to assess your financial situation, find and address areas of imbalance or overspending, and use self-discipline to create a more secure financial future.

Relationships:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card speaks to the feeling of imprisonment in relationships. When this card appears in a relationship reading, it may indicate that one or both parties are feeling trapped and powerless. There may be a lack of communication and a feeling that it is impossible to break free from a draining dynamic. This card can be a sign that you are avoiding confronting issues, instead of addressing and resolving them. It can be a warning that you are emotionally restricting yourself from taking actions that would benefit the relationship, or that you are defending yourself from the hurt that could come from those actions. The 8 of Swords can be a reminder to reflect on how you can open up to a more honest and healthy dynamic with others.
