However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

First, the Page of Pentacles signifies that you may be getting exciting news surrounding money or a new job or a raise. On the other hand, the Ace of Cups promises a fresh start in terms of relationships and/or family life, as it symbolizes new births, proposals, and marriage. These forces coming together in your life will ensure you have a bright future ahead of you with plenty of opportunities for growth and new experiences.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

The Page of Pentacles in the Tarot card is a card that represents good news. When this card appears in a reading, it is a sign of good luck, especially when it comes to health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, the Page of Pentacles symbolizes hard work paying off and that there is a strong potential for significant healing results. This card speaks of a newfound sense of vitality and potential, as well as an opportunity to be proactive in making positive changes in one’s life. It can be a call for one to put extra effort into taking care of their wellbeing and to focus on preventative health measures.

When it comes to finances, the Page of Pentacles encourages one to look for new work, a raise or promotion, or to increase their income in some way. It suggests that financial luck is on one’s side, and that there is potential for great success, especially through hard work and perseverance. It can also symbolize the need to take time to enjoy the fruits of one’s labors, and to use any extra money to reward oneself for their hard work.

When it comes to relationships, the Page of Pentacles cautions one to be patient and to remember that Rome wasn’t built in a day. It can serve as a reminder that all relationships require hard work and dedication in order to be successful and to last. This card can also signify a time of increased communication and understanding, as well as an opportunity to bring new life and joy into existing relationships. It encourages one to take advantage of the potential for positive and productive exchanges with one’s loved ones.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

The Ace of Cups Tarot card is a card of emotional fulfillment, abundance, and joy. It symbolizes a new beginning, a fresh start and the potential for happiness. In terms of health, the card suggests that emotional balance and happiness will lead to improved physical health. It can also signify the beginning of a new health care regime, such as starting an exercise program or eating healthier.

In terms of finances, the card suggests that emotional security and stability will lead to financial abundance. It can also signify the potential for investments, business opportunities, and the possibility of taking calculated risks.

In terms of relationships, the Ace of Cups Tarot card can symbolize the potential for a new relationship, the strengthening of a current relationship, the potential for a proposal of marriage, or a birth of a child. This card encourages an open heart, emotional health, and mutual understanding. It suggests that giving and receiving support and love will lead to fulfilling relationships.
