Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The Hermit card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Hermit, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Hermit.

## The Hermit And Sprituality – An Overview

The Hermit tarot card speaks to the importance of connecting with our inner selves and listening to our intuition when it comes to our health.

Our bodies are constantly sending us messages, and it is important to listen to them and be mindful of how we are feeling.

When we take the time to go within and search for our personal truths, we can gain clarity about our health and find ways to improve it.

The Hermit asks us to take a spiritual journey and discover how to nourish our physical and mental wellbeing.

In doing so, we can gain a better understanding of our health and how to make conscious decisions to improve it.

It encourages us to take a journey into our innermost selves, where we can find clarity, guidance, and illumination.

It also serves as a reminder that we should never forget to take the time to nurture our own spiritual growth, and to be open to learning from our own experiences.

Through this process of spiritual growth, we can learn to understand our true selves and gain insight into our own life paths.

### What It Means For The Future

The Hermit tarot card is a reminder that seeking clarity and a connection to your higher self is essential to achieving true spiritual growth and illumination.

This card is a reminder to go within to look for the answers you seek, and to trust that a greater wisdom exists within you.

As you embark on this journey of self-discovery, you will unlock the spiritual truth that resides within.

As you explore your inner truth, you will gain a deeper understanding of yourself and your purpose in life.

This spiritual journey will open you up to a new level of awareness and clarity that will lead to personal growth and transformation.

The Hermit is a reminder that the spiritual journey is a lifelong commitment, and its rewards come when you are brave enough to go within.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Hermit tarot card is a reminder of the importance of taking time for ourselves and our own spiritual growth.

It speaks to the importance of seeking out and finding our own personal truth.

For those close to me, The Hermit encourages them to take the time to explore their own spiritual journey, to find clarity within and to seek out spiritual illumination.

This could involve engaging in activities such as meditation, journaling and exploring spiritual texts to help further their understanding and connection to a higher power.

The Hermit encourages us to take the time to go inward and uncover our own personal spiritual growth, in order to create a better, more fulfilling life for ourselves.

## Reversed Meanings For The Hermit And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

When the Hermit tarot card is reversed, it can signify a tendency to run away from seeking spirituality and personal truth.

Whereas the right-side-up Hermit encourages an inward journey to uncover our inner wisdom, the reversed Hermit may suggest an avoidance of this process.

It can be a sign that we are not trusting ourselves and our own intuition, and instead of seeking out answers within, we are looking externally for clarity and guidance.

The reversed Hermit may also indicate that we are feeling disconnected from our spiritual path and are not engaging in practices that help us to remain connected.

We may be feeling a lack of motivation to explore our spirituality, and instead of seeking spiritual illumination, we are allowing ourselves to remain in the dark.

This can lead to feelings of stagnation and dissatisfaction in our spiritual practice.

To find our way back, we need to reconnect with our sense of inner truth, and find ways to nourish and nurture our spiritual side.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Hermit card is reversed, it signifies an emerging spiritual awakening.

You may feel drawn towards an exploration of your inner truth and the mystery of the universe.

This could be the start of a journey towards understanding your inner wisdom and the power of the divine.

The reversed Hermit card suggests that you are ready to dive deep into the realms of your spirituality.

You may find yourself being more curious and open-minded towards different beliefs, ideas and experiences.

It’s time to tap into your intuition and explore the depths of your soul.

The reversed Hermit card is an invitation to explore your inner self and all that the spiritual realm has to offer, including the secrets and mysteries of the universe.

It encourages you to be brave and trust yourself to find your own unique path and discover your own personal truth.

You are ready to look within and find the answers and inspiration you seek.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Hermit is reversed, it can indicate a lack of inner exploration and spiritual awareness.

This could symbolize those close to me who may have difficulty embracing their inner truths or seeking out their own spiritual enlightenment.

They may struggle to find the answers they need to make important decisions, often choosing to rely on outside advice instead of their own inner voice.

They could be stuck in a rut, feeling disconnected from their true purpose in life and not living up to their full potential.

The reversed Hermit can also signify a need to open up and allow others to help provide valuable insight and guidance.

Those close to me may feel too shy or embarrassed to open up and be vulnerable with others, often preferring to stay secluded, and this could be preventing them from experiencing all that life has to offer.

It’s important for them to step out of their comfort zone and trust that those around them can provide valuable insight and advice when needed.

Ultimately, The Hermit reversed can be a reminder for those close to me to take the time to go within and discover the answers they need to progress on their spiritual journey.
