However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does Temperance and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The 4 Of Cups tarot card together could mean that you are in the process of achieving balance, harmony, and self-control, while feeling a sense of unfulfillment and apathy. The message is to stay patient and keep working on achieving a desired state of balance – you might even be surprised by the gifts that await you if you stay focused and stay the course. This reflects a need to quit focusing on what is missing and instead stay steady and work on maintaining your state of balance and harmony.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health  
The Temperance Tarot card stands for finding balance in one’s health. It’s a reminder to practice moderation in diet, exercise, and overall lifestyle. The Temperance card symbolizes taking the time to reflect on how one’s body and mind are affected by what they eat, how often they exercise, and how they respond to stress. It encourages an awareness of body signals and an understanding of how and why one engages in self-care practices. This card suggests tempering the physical and the mental—both are important for a healthy lifestyle.

Finances  
The Temperance Tarot card stands for finding balance in one’s finances. This card encourages thoughtful and responsible planning when it comes to money and encourages one to look at their relationship with money itself. The Temperance card suggests doing research, asking for advice, and understanding the risks and rewards of various investments. It also emphasizes patience and self-control when it comes to spending and saving. This card encourages one to practice moderation and to make sure their financial goals are in line with their values.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card is particularly applicable to relationships of all kinds. It stands for finding balance and harmony between two individuals, or among groups of people. This card encourages one to reflect on the relationships that they have and encourages communication and understanding. It suggests considering the other person’s point of view, being open to compromise, and respecting boundaries. It also encourages one to be patient and to take the time to create meaningful connections. The Temperance card suggests that lasting relationships are built on mutual respect, trust and balance.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Cups tarot card is associated with feeling a sense of apathy or unhappiness in regards to one’s health. This card suggests that one may be feeling unfulfilled in terms of physical, mental or emotional wellness, or feeling as if they are not getting the results they desire from their health and lifestyle choices. This card may also be a sign that it’s time to take a break from any over-exerted efforts and take time to reassess one’s goals and motivations.

Finances:  
The 4 of Cups is related to feeling unfulfilled with one’s financial situation. This card suggests that one may be feeling complacent with their current financial state, and may not be taking the necessary steps to improve their wealth. This card may also be a warning that one needs to reassess their financial decisions and create a new plan to move forward. In addition, this card may be a sign that a surprise windfall is coming in the near future, but one should not get too comfortable relying on it.

Relationships:  
The 4 of Cups tarot card is related to feeling a sense of apathy or disinterest in one’s relationships. This card suggests that one may be feeling unfulfilled with their current relationships, or that their expectations are failing to be met. It is important that one does not allow this sense of disconnect to fester and should instead assess the situation and identify what is missing or wrong. Furthermore, this card may be a sign that surprise gifts are coming in one’s relationships, either in the form of new people entering the picture or a fresh start for existing relationships.
