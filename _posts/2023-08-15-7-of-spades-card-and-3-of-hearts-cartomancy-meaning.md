A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 3 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 3 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing the 7 of Spades followed by the 3 of Hearts presents an intriguing blend of potential outcomes.

The 7 of Spades, symbolizing ‘deceit and victory’, intimates that there might be some hidden aspects or veiled truths in circumstances that are leading to a successful outcome.

Its connection with Winter and Water signifies a period of dormancy or mystery, perhaps a situation that is more complex than it appears, immersed in deep thoughts and emotions.

On the other hand, the 3 of Hearts stands for ‘infidelity and Faith’, indicating a possible breach of trust or deviation from loyalty.

However, it also speaks of faith and hopes in relationships.

The association with Spring and the fiery element suggests a time of revival, growth and passionate new beginnings.

Together, these cards might suggest a victory achieved amid deceit and disloyalty, yet there is room for faith and renewed trust.

The challenging time of Winter is passing, giving way to the vibrant and vigorous energy of Spring.

It could be a reminder to remain steadfast in your faith and look forward to a brighter season.

These cards hint at prevailing over deceit through faith in oneself or others.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, having The 7 Of Spades and The 3 Of Hearts cards pull up together signifies a complex state in your love life.

The 7 Of Spades indicates elements of deceit and betrayal.

This card could mean that either you or your partner is not being entirely honest about something.

Yet, it is also a card of victory, suggesting that such deception may lead to an unintended positive outcome.

It is associated with the season of winter and water element, representing a time of stagnation or a cool and calm attitude to the situation.

On the other hand, The 3 Of Hearts stands for infidelity and faith, suggesting a possible breach of trust or fidelity.

However, the simultaneous presence of faith suggests the paradoxical possibility of a deeper understanding and strengthening of relationships despite such adversity.

Associated with the season of Spring and the element of Fire, it represents a period of growth, renewal, and passionate responses to circumstances.

Thus, pulling these two cards together in a love reading represents a period of turmoil, deception, and possible betrayal, yet also suggests the presence of potential growth, overcoming of challenges, and eventual stability in your romantic relationships.

It may be a period of storms, but also the promise of the calm that follows.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

The 7 of Spades in the financial setting may warn of potential deceit or dishonesty related to money matters.

In a job context, it could suggest a hollow or superficial victory where one feels dissatisfied despite seemingly achieving a goal.

It suggests a period of hard-work and struggle, symbolizing that one might be feeling unappreciated or undervalued at their place of work.

It could also hint at unscrupulous dealings or a warning to be careful about where you invest your money.

As this card is associated with the chill and darkness of Winter and the fluidity of Water, it can indicate emotions running deep and the need to be highly observant and cautious in financial matters.

Meanwhile, the 3 of Hearts, associated with Spring and Fire, talks about a transformation after a cold and difficult period represented by the 7 of Spades.

This card, signaling infidelity and faith, could mean a period of uncertainty and fluctuations in financial matters.

In terms of work, it could possibly foretell some form of betrayal, but also stresses the importance of faith in one’s own skills and abilities.

The link the card has to Fire signifies the energetic release, warmth, and growth one might feel in their career.

Despite the betrayal or deceit that the 7 of Spades may warn about, the 3 of Hearts reminds you to have faith and trust your instincts when it comes to your finances and work.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, the 7 of Spades is associated with deceit and victory.

While it is connected with the winter season and the element of water, its meaning when drawn in context with health can imply challenges.

Deceit can perhaps indicate hidden or underlying health issues that you might be oblivious to, or a misleading diagnosis.

At the same time, the victory tied to this card hints at overcoming these health troubles.

It might mean pending treatment or procedures will go well and result in improved health.

The 3 of Hearts, related to spring and fire, hints at infidelity and faith.

In a health sense, infidelity might point to inconsistency in maintaining healthy habits, perhaps negligence towards a nutritious diet or physical fitness regime.

On the other hand, the faith aspect of this card can suggest a strong belief in your potential to improve your health or a positive outlook that can greatly boost your recovery process.

Thus, together, these cards may indicate a need to reveal the truth about your health, confront any issues, stay consistent in health practices, and maintain a strong faith in your body’s ability to heal and overcome.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
