However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

It suggests that although pain and difficult times have caused suffering, it was all necessary for a greater good and finding true joy with the help and love of family. It implies that when times are difficult, it is even more important to turn to family and loved ones for support, which will help you reach the ultimate goal of contentment.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Finally, in terms of relationships The 10 Of Swords Tarot card could represent a major rift between two people. ## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 10 of Cups tarot card suggests that overall health is vibrant and strong. As a representation of joy, it implies that any health issues that you may be facing won’t be long lasting and will soon be overcome. The card can represent a stronger connection to your spiritual self, which in turn helps to create a healthier physical and emotional foundation. Being able to maintain a sense of gratitude and contentment also helps to ensure that your physical and mental wellbeing is in check.

Finances  
The 10 of Cups tarot card indicates that money is coming into your life in abundance. This could be related to financial gains such as a pay raise, inheritance or winning a lottery. It usually suggests that the money is coming in due to emotional satisfaction, rather than by any hard work or struggle. It symbolizes financial security and suggests that you have the capacity to handle any unexpected financial setbacks that may come your way.

Relationships  
The 10 of Cups tarot card indicates that the relationships in your life are supportive, loving and nurturing. It suggests that your family, friends and romantic partners are likely to be loyal and understanding and that any disagreement or disputes that come up are likely to be resolved quickly and without any major problems. The card also suggests that, for the most part, you are surrounded by people who bring a sense of joy and love into your life. It suggests that you are able to find a balance between giving and receiving love and that your relationships are providing you with a sense of satisfaction and fulfillment.
