A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and Ace Of Swords card together in particular.

The Temperance card and Ace Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The Temperance and Ace of Swords tarot cards together symbolize an opportunity to gain mastery over yourself and a chance to bring balance into your life. This combination encourages self-control, patience, mental clarity and harmony in order to help you push through and resolve difficult conflicts. With this combination, you have the opportunity to gain a more level-headed approach to life. The Ace of Swords can help you come up with new ideas, while the Temperance card helps you bring your mental clarity into action with patience and understanding. This balance brings newfound insight and understanding, allowing you to make decisions that are more in line with your personal values.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card indicates balance, harmony, patience and self-control. For health, this card can suggest a need for moderation and self-discipline in order to remain healthy and fit. Health is a delicate balance of mind, body and spirit, and The Temperance card encourages us to practice moderation and restraint. This could mean cutting back on unhealthy foods or drinks, stopping or reducing consumption of addictive substances, or balancing periods of exercise with periods of rest.

For finances, The Temperance card tells us to be patient and take the time to make informed decisions that are in our best interests. It is smart to practice delayed gratification, as well as moderation and self-discipline, in order to achieve good financial stability. This could mean making realistic budgets and sticking to them, or investing in something for the long-term rather than trying to get rich quick.

For relationships, The Temperance card suggests that patience, diplomacy and level-headedness are necessary in order to maintain successful and healthy relationships. This could mean not letting emotions run away with us and practicing self-restraint and moderation when communicating with others. It also encourages us to take the time to thoughtfully consider potential decisions and outcomes before acting. This can help us to be more understanding and tolerant of others, and to approach conflicts and disagreements calmly.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

Health

The Ace of Swords is a sign of great clarity and mental acuity. It is a sign of fresh starts and new ideas. For those in the health field, it can indicate opportunities to increase efficiency in the workplace or explore innovative medical solutions. It can also be a sign of upcoming surgery, which could bring a renewed sense of health and vitality.

Financial

The Ace of Swords is associated with new beginnings, clear thinking and decision making. It can be an indicator of an impending financial boon, such as an inheritance or a large settlement. It can also be a sign of hard work and dedication yielding results. The card is a reminder that when making financial decisions, it is important to approach them with clarity and precision.

Relationships

The Ace of Swords card is associated with clear thinking and renewal. It can be a sign that a relationship is going through a difficult period, and that it is possible to work through. It may also be a sign that a relationship is on the brink of a fresh start. It can be a reminder to approach relationships with clear communication and to take time to reflect on the past and the present.
