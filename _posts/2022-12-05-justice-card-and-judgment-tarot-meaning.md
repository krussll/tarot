A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Judgment card together in particular.

The Justice card and Judgment have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Judgment mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The Judgment tarot card combined mean that it is time to make decisions that are fair and beneficial to all involved. It calls for a synthesis of right and wrong, truth and justice. The ultimate outcome should be a situation where the truth is revealed and both parties are satisfied. This card also encourages you to take a step back and assess the situation from all angles in order to come to an informed decision. Furthermore, it is an invitation to look deep within and uncover your life’s purpose. Ultimately, these two cards remind you that true justice and clarity can only be attained when you have considered all of the facts and are conscious of your own inner truth.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Judgment mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health:  
The Justice Tarot card is a reminder of how our choices and actions have a ripple effect on our health. When we choose to take care of our body and make healthy decisions, we create an environment of self-care and wellbeing. We can also use the card to help us stay motivated by understanding our impact on the world around us. When we make decisions that keep our body, mind, and spirit well-balanced, we’re in a better position to help others.

Finances:  
The Justice Tarot card encourages us to think about the consequences of our financial decisions. It’s important to weigh the pros and cons of any major purchase or investment, and to create a budget that will help us achieve our goals. The card also reminds us to be mindful of our spending habits, as each purchase could have an impact on our financial future. We should strive for win-win solutions that allow us to meet our needs while also making wise decisions that will benefit us in the long run.

Relationships:  
The Justice Tarot card serves as a reminder of the importance of treating others fairly and with respect. Our relationships can be strengthened when we take the time to listen to each other’s perspectives and seek out win-win solutions. We should strive to practice integrity and honesty in our interactions, as the truth will always be revealed. The card also encourages us to look past superficial labels and judgements and to appreciate the beauty and diversity of every individual. When we act with kindness and compassion, we can create healing and growth in our relationships.

## What does Judgment mean?

For Health: The Judgment Tarot card is a sign of self-reflection and a call to take action on your health. It can signify a time for healing, whether it be physical, emotional, or spiritual. It is a reminder that you are responsible for taking care of your own health, and can’t rely solely on others for your wellness. The Judgment card is an indication that you are encouraged to recognize the choices and decisions you have made, and can provide a call to action to make changes that are necessary for your overall wellbeing.

For Finances: The Judgment Tarot card is a sign of accountability and responsibility. It can signify a call to take a look at your financial situation and evaluate your choices and decisions. It can encourage you to make decisions that are in line with your financial goals, and to make changes that will help to improve your financial security. It can also suggest that you take the time to review your spending habits, create a budget and make a plan to stay within it.

For Relationships: The Judgment Tarot card can serve as a reminder of how important it is to pay attention to your relationships. It can suggest that you spend time reflecting on how your relationships are helping or hindering your growth. It can also signify the importance of being honest and truthful in order to have healthier relationships. The Judgment card can indicate a need for communication, a willingness to resolve conflicts and a commitment to working on your relationships. It can encourage taking responsibility for your choices, and having the courage to make changes that will lead to healthier relationships.
