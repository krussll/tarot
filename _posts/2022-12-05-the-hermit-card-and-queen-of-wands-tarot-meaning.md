A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and The Queen Of Wands tarot card together signify a time for you to find clarity in your inner world and trust your instincts. The combination of these two cards suggests that you must have courage and be willing to face your fears in order to gain insight into your own personal truths. The Queen Of Wands symbolizes a strong and determined individual who is confident in their own power, so take her insight and combine it with The Hermit’s desire for spiritual illumination to help guide you in your inner journey. The combination of these cards suggests that you must have the courage to confront your inner darkness and gain a better understanding of yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and Queen Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health  
The Hermit Tarot card is associated with both physical and spiritual health. It encourages you to take a step back from your daily life and take some time for yourself. This is a time for reflection, introspection, and contemplating how our current decisions impact our wellbeing. This card advises us to take a break from our routines and instead focus on self-care. During this period of contemplation and introspection, we should explore what nourishes us both emotionally and physically, so we can make informed and conscious choices in terms of our health.

Finances  
The Hermit Tarot card can often signify a need to reevaluate our financial decisions and investments. This card is an invitation to take a step back and analyze our current financial situation and what we can do to improve it. It is a reminder that money should not be the end goal, but rather a tool to help us to achieve our dreams and goals. When faced with financial decisions, we should take time to consider all of our options, think through the pros and cons, and follow our intuition before committing to a course of action.

Relationships  
The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to look inward to better understand our relationships. In doing so, we can reassess whether we are in healthy relationships, and if we are making decisions that serve our highest good. This card calls upon us to remember that relationships require work, understanding, and honesty. It may be necessary to take a step back from certain relationships in order to gain clarity and understanding, so we can determine what is best for us. Ultimately, the Hermit Tarot card reminds us to take time for ourselves to reflect, reassess, and reconnect.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is associated with vibrant physical and mental health. Its energy suggests that a person is filled with confidence and vitality, which makes them resilient and strong. Its message encourages us to take a proactive approach to our physical and mental well-being, and to express our emotions honestly and clearly. In this way, The Queen of Wands Tarot card helps us to maintain balance in our lives and prevent illness.

Finances:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of abundance and financial success. This card’s energy encourages us to be enterprising and ambitious, and to seek opportunities to boost our income. It is associated with creative problem solving, helping us to come up with innovative solutions to our money troubles. This card also suggests that we should take risks and remain confident in our financial decisions.

Relationships:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is associated with strong relationships. Its energy encourages us to be courageous and honest in our relationships, and to reverence those we care about. This card’s symbolism also suggests that we should not be bullied or intimidated by those we care about, and instead stand up for ourselves and protect our own interests. Further, it encourages us to value our relationships, invest in them, and work to nurture them over time.
