A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Hearts card and 8 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 5 Of Hearts card and 8 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Hearts and 8 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, both the 5 of Hearts and the 8 of Hearts cards being drawn together suggest significant personal growth on the horizon.

The 5 of Hearts, symbolizing jealousy and change, points to possible difficulties or resentful feelings you may be harboring.

This could be towards others or yourself.

However, combined with the 8 of Hearts which symbolizes moving on and power, it indicates that you are about to transition from this difficult phase.

It suggests you are ready to let go of the negative feelings, grow from the experience, and regain your personal power.

Given that these cards are both associated with Spring, a season of growth and renewal, and the Fire element representing transformation, it only strengthens the message of imminent personal change and newfound strength.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 5 Of Hearts and 8 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, the presence of The 5 of Hearts signifies experiencing the emotion of jealousy, with a strong implication of change that’s yet to come.

This card reveals insecurities within feelings and relationships, and suggest the presence of rivals or competition in your love life.

Its association with Spring denotes a period of growth and renewal, possibly after a challenging time.

The element of Fire, meanwhile, symbolizes transformative energy, passion, and spontaneity.

Therefore, in terms of a love relationship, it may imply that the necessary change could lead to the strengthening of the bond or a passionate new beginning.

Meanwhile, The 8 Of Hearts signifies moving on and a sense of empowerment.

The appearance of this card in a reading indicates a transition phase in your love life, suggesting a strong necessity to leave behind past hurt and disappointment to move forward.

The element of Fire sparks passion and creativity, indicating an intense and passionate phase in your upcoming future.

The season of Spring hinting towards rejuvenation and freshness – a favorable time for applying changes and moving on from past issues.

In combination, these two cards may indicate an upcoming phase of transformation in your love life characterized by overcoming jealousy, embracing change, and finding the power to move forward.

### What does 5 Of Hearts and 8 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, pulling the 5 of Hearts and 8 of Hearts together can indicate significant changes and shifts within your financial or job situation.

The 5 of Hearts, symbolizing jealousy and change, could mean that your current dissatisfaction or envy of others’ financial or job status is a catalyst for impending transformation.

Perhaps you’ve been comparing your financial status or job position with others’; this comparison could be the impetus you need to reassess your current financial strategy or career path.

However, it is important to remain patient and grounded as these changes may not occur instantly.

The 8 of Hearts, on the other hand, represents moving on and power.

This could signify that you are ready to move away from an unfulfilling job or financial situation.

Maybe you’ve been feeling stuck in your present job or are unsatisfied with your existing financial conditions.

You are now ready to harness the power within you to make that crucial shift towards a more satisfying career or a more beneficial financial state.

This change can be as significant as switching career paths or implementing a new financial plan.

The Spring association suggests that this is an excellent time for these changes as it’s a season of growth and rebirth.

The Fire element further indicates the passion and energy you possess to effect these transformations.

Therefore, in essence, pulling these two cards together signifies transformation and the power to instigate changes in your financial and career situations.

### What does 5 Of Hearts and 8 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

In a health-related cartomancy reading, the combination of the 5 of Hearts and the 8 of Hearts suggests a significant transformation in your physical or emotional well-being.

The 5 of Hearts, representing jealousy and change, could indicate unhealthy comparisons leading to stress and anxiety.

Perhaps you’re comparing your health or fitness levels to others and feeling inadequate, or you’re going through a transition that is affecting your physical or mental health.

This card signifies that these changes or feelings of jealousy may lead to health issues if not handled appropriately.

On the other hand, the 8 of Hearts symbolizes moving on and power.

In the context of health, this card suggests the need or the ability to overcome health-related challenges and move forward.

It speaks of determination, resilience, and the power to take control of your health.

Perhaps you are about to recover from an illness, or you have decided to take control and adopt a healthier lifestyle.

Together, these two cards indicate a journey from feelings of inadequacy and change, to gaining power and control over your health.

They encourage you to stop comparing yourself to others, embrace change, and take proactive steps to improve your well-being.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 5 Of Hearts and 8 Of Hearts mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 5 Of Hearts and 8 Of Hearts the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.
