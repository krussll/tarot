However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 2 Of Swords mean together?

The 9 Of Wands and The 2 Of Swords tarot card together represent a difficult but necessary balance between keeping your boundaries and being open to compromise. This is a time to remain committed to your beliefs while also being flexible and willing to negotiate. Persistence and discipline are key, as is being open to negotiation and compromise. It is important to remain firm and steadfast in your beliefs while also being willing to work with others and find mutual understanding. This is a time to draw your inner strength and use it to stand up for yourself, but also to be open to alternate solutions.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Wands and 2 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Wands and 2 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

When the 9 of Wands appears in a reading, it can be encouraging those struggling with physical fitness and mental health to take their time and slowly build up their stamina and strength in order to achieve their desired goals. It may be a reminder to keep up healthy habits and to practice self-care, as well as taking time to reflect on any changes or improvements that need to be made to one’s life in order to stay physically and mentally healthy.

It is also a reminder that it is important to be resilient and to keep up the hard work in order to achieve financial security.

Its theme of persistance and resilience, as well as its message of setting boundaries, can be applied to all types of relationships, from intimate to platonic. When this card appears in a reading, it is often a reminder that it is important to build relationships on the foundation of respect and understanding. ## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 2 of Swords Tarot card can be an indicator of choosing between two approaches (or two different opinions) related to your health. You may be stuck at a crossroads where you cannot decide which way to go. You could be stuck between two physicians with opposing recommendations. It could also be between two diets, two treatments, or two lifestyle changes that you are considering. It is essential to analyze each side, weigh the pros and cons, and come to an informed decision. Without clear direction, it can be hard to move forward on your health journey.

Finances:

The 2 of Swords Tarot card can signify a decision between two different paths related to finances. It could be a financial decision between two different investments, two strategies, or two paths to reach your financial goals. This card can be an invitation to research both options thoroughly, consider the pros and cons of each, and come to a balanced decision. It is important to be patient and take the time to make an informed decision that best serves you.

Relationships:

The 2 of Swords Tarot card could signify that you are stuck between two conflicting ideas or viewpoints in a relationship. It could be between two different conversations with a partner or an outside opinion that you feel is necessary to make a decision. The 2 of Swords can be a sign to listen to both sides, take your time to make a balanced decision, and communicate it clearly to the other person. Compromise is essential to working through and resolving any conflicts or disagreements in relationships.
