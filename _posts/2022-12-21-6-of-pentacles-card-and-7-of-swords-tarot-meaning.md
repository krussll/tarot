A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The 6 Of Pentacles and The 7 Of Swords tarot card together emphasize the dichotomy between generosity and deceit. The 6 Of Pentacles indicates that, as we give and receive help, we should be mindful of the motivations of those around us. The 7 Of Swords indicates that some people seeking assistance may be acting out of selfishness and dishonesty, and we should be aware of potentially insidious behavior. These cards caution us to not be overly trusting, as even those close to us may be out for their own gain. Ultimately, these cards point to the importance of being mindful of our actions and those of others when engaging in charitable endeavors.

### What does 6 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 6 of Pentacles and the 7 of Swords in a love reading can be a difficult one to interpret. On the one hand, the 6 of Pentacles points to the need to give and receive help in a relationship, as well as a call to be generous towards your partner. This card encourages you to reach out, build trust, and create a bond that is rooted in kindness. On the other hand, the 7 of Swords suggests that your partner may be deceiving you or even stealing from you. This card could warn of potential betrayal or dishonesty, and could imply that your partner is being unfaithful.

In order to make sense of this combination, it is important to look at your current relationship dynamics and evaluate what could be causing the situation. Are there obstacles that the two of you are struggling to overcome together, or is there something larger at play that is causing a rift between the two of you? If the issue causing this card combination is within the relationship, then it is important to take a step back and assess whether or not trust has been broken. If steps have been taken to try to help and repair the relationship, then the 6 of Pentacles can serve as a reminder to remain generous and understanding of your partner’s struggles.

However, if the issue is coming from outside of the relationship and there is an external force causing distrust and deceit, then the 7 of Swords could be warning you to be on the lookout for potential betrayal. This card combination could be a sign that someone else is trying to come between you and your partner, or that there is a problem that could come between the two of you. In this case, it is important to be aware of what is going on and to be honest and communicative with your partner in order to keep the relationship strong.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 7 of Swords tarot card can signify a feeling of being overwhelmed. This could be related to physical, mental or emotional stress and strain. It could also indicate a difficult transition period in life, with a lack of clarity on how to best help oneself to move forward. This card can represent feeling uncertain, even helpless, in the face of health issues or other life challenges. Taking advice from trusted professionals may be necessary in order to help you find balance and peace.

Finances

The 7 of Swords tarot card can represent a feeling of financial instability. Mismanagement of money or resources is indicated, as well as a tendency to make impulsive decisions without full deliberation. It could also be an indication of theft, scams and frauds. It is important to be mindful of your spending and know where your money is going. A strong focus on budgeting and planning is necessary in order to bring financial stability back into your life.

Relationships

The 7 of Swords tarot card can represent dishonesty and betrayal in relationships. There may be a lack of trust, or a tendency to keep secrets and tell only half-truths. It is important to be honest and open with your close ones and to have strong boundaries in order to protect yourself. Holding yourself accountable for your actions is necessary in order to heal any wounds and rebuild trust.
