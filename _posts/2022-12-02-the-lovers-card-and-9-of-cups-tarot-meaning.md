You can expect that your wishes will come true, as you are now a team, and together this team will experience material abundance and good health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health: The Lovers Tarot card is an excellent card for those who need a push when it comes to taking care of their health. On the one hand, it encourages us to reach out and form partnerships with medical professionals and/or mentors to help us stay on top of our health. On the other hand, it also reminds us to be attentive to our internal needs, to create a strong and balanced relationship with our body. Fostering a loving and nurturing relationship with ourselves is the foundation for keeping ourselves healthy and in a state of balance.

Finances: The Lovers Tarot card also has significant implications for financial well-being. It encourages two approaches to financial matters. On one hand, it encourages us to look beyond our individual resources and embrace collaboration in order to create financial growth. This could take the form of joint investments or of reaching out to financial advisors and advisors in other areas of business that could help us make smart decisions. On the other hand, it reminds us to stay honest with ourselves and those we collaborate with, to create trust and respect in all financial transactions.

Relationships: Finally, The Lovers Tarot card speaks to the importance of creating and maintaining deep relationships with our partners, families, and friends. This card emphasizes the power of two hearts united as one, and reminds us to tend to our relationships as if they were our own garden. It encourages us to reach out and connect with those we feel close to, and to be attentive to the needs of our partners, while also taking care of our own needs. This card can help us to create more meaningful connections and to remain in a state of balance, understanding, and mutual respect when it comes to our relationships.

It signifies the ability to take care of ones self, to have enough energy to pursue a number of activities, and to be able to bounce back from whatever life throws your way. The appearance of The 9 Of Cups offers an assurance that whatever it is that you need to stay healthy and strong will come to you. You will find yourself able to reverse any ill health and turn it into something that benefits you.

When it comes to finances, The 9 Of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the ability to manifest abundance, the power to overcome financial worries, and the security of having the resources to build a future. It suggests the possibility of creating and manifesting wealth through hard work and dedication. It also highlights the importance of living within your means and saving for the future, rather than relying on luck or chance.

Finally, The 9 Of Cups Tarot card also points to strong and healthy relationships. This card symbolizes a time of understanding and appreciation, a time of forging new bonds and strengthening old ones. It suggests a time of support and mutual understanding, a time of enjoying the company of friends and family. It is a reminder of the importance of taking the time to nurture and care for your relationships, as well as allowing people to be who they are without judgement or pressure. Ultimately, the 9 Of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to focus on the things in life that truly matter, such as your health, wellbeing, finances, and relationships.
