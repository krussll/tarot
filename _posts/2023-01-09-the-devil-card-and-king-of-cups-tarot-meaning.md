A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Devil card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and King Of Cups mean together?

The Devil tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card together point to a need to exercise self-discipline and moderation in order to escape from the cycle of addictive behavior. This includes avoiding greed, envy, and materialism, which can lead to obsessive and compulsive behavior. The King Of Cups encourages you to seek out help and guidance from a supportive leader, and to cultivate a greater sense of empathy and tolerance. With such help, the Devil tarot card can be seen as a reminder of the importance of recognizing and overcoming your own temptations, and of developing a mindful approach to life.

The King Of Cups symbolizes a tolerant, supportive and empathic leader. In the context of a love relationship, this card can represent a partner who is deeply understanding and supportive of one another’s needs. Such a leader can help to prevent one from becoming overly reliant on certain things, such as material possessions or sex, which can lead to unhealthy and unbalanced dynamics.

In conclusion, The Devil tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card represent a warning about the danger of entering into relationships out of greed or obsession. The King Of Cups serves as a reminder of the importance of having someone in one’s life who is understanding, supportive and tolerant in order to maintain balance and emotional health. If one partner is able to provide this kind of leadership, then it’s likely that the relationship can be healthy and fulfilling.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card deals with the darker side of our nature: those impulses, emotions and behaviors that can lead us astray on the paths of health, finances, and relationships. The message of The Devil card is to beware: any vice you allow to take control can become an unhealthy obsession, dominating your life and leading to dire consequences.

In terms of health, The Devil warns against any activity or habit that can form an addiction, such as drugs, alcohol, smoking, or gambling. These activities can have a hugely detrimental effect on our physical and mental wellbeing, and when we become obsessed with them, our health can rapidly decline. The Devil also points to the danger of unhealthy eating habits and over-exertion in trying to achieve ideal body images or physical perfection. Allowing an obsession or addiction to take control of our lives can lead to serious physical and mental health issues, such as depression, anxiety, and substance abuse.

In terms of finances, The Devil warns us to be careful when we allow our material desires to take control of our lives. Greed, obsession and envy can all lead to destructive spending habits and the accumulation of debt, ruining our credit and our financial futures. It is important to recognize the signs of financial addiction, such as overspending, shopping impulsively and not living within our means. The Devil card also urges us to be wary of any get-rich-quick schemes, scams and frauds that can leave us deeply in debt and struggling to make ends meet.

Finally, The Devil card can symbolize the destruction of relationships. Greed, obsession and lust can lead to mistrust, dishonesty and a lack of communication in any relationship. The Devil warns us to be wary of ways that unhealthy behavior can sabotage our relationships, such as becoming too preoccupied with our own desires or needs. It is important to recognize when our actions are driven by selfishness or a need for control, and when our behavior becomes destructive or manipulative. When we allow our darker impulses to take control, our relationships can become strained and damaged beyond repair.

The Devil card is an important reminder that all of us have the capacity for destruction, and an unchecked tendency towards our vices can wreak havoc in our lives. We must be vigilant and aim to stay in control of our impulses, lest they become excessively damaging to our health, finances and relationships.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The King Of Cups Tarot card in the context of health represents a calming influence and a deep understanding of the mind-body connection. It speaks to the need to listen to the body’s cues and to trust in one’s own instincts. This card will provide guidance in managing stress and emotions, as it promotes tolerance and empathy. The power of this card is that it will encourage one to take a holistic approach to physical and psychological wellbeing. This can manifest as self-care strategies like meditation, journaling, getting proper rest, and restoring balance in the home environment.

Finances:  
The King Of Cups Tarot card when related to finances indicates a supportive leader with a well-rounded approach to money management. This card speaks to the need for strategic planning, wise decision-making, and the discipline to stick to plans. It also encourages patience and looking at the bigger picture to create long-term stability. With this card, the focus is on building a supportive financial foundation for oneself and one’s loved ones. The King Of Cups Tarot card may serve as a reminder to engage with financial support structures to develop sensible strategies for the future.

Relationships:  
The King Of Cups Tarot card when related to relationships stands for a supportive leader that creates space for open communication and understanding. This card encourages tolerance and empathy, and celebrates the potential of relationships when these characteristics are applied. It speaks to the need to approach an interpersonal relationship with patience and a willingness to listen, as well as to understand another person’s perspective. With this card, it is important to recognize that relationships are complex and the journey is often filled with challenges. However, with the right attitude and trust, these challenges can become opportunities for growth and development.
