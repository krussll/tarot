Tarot cards hold so much history and symbolism that most people find it hard to believe they are simply a game. The tarot has been around for centuries, and in that time, many people have developed their own practices when it comes to reading the cards.

In some cases, you do not have to cleanse your cards before every use – or ever if you feel like it’s not necessary. However, there are others who make cleansing part of their practice as often as possible. The choice is completely up to you. Here are some answers on the topic of cleansing your tarot cards.

## Do You Have To Cleanse Your Tarot Cards Every Time You Use Them?

**No, you don’t have to cleanse your tarot cards every time you use them. If you want to include it as part of your pre-reading ritual then it certainly wouldn’t do any harm – however cleansing your tarot cards once a month would suffice.**

## Why do you need to cleanse your tarot cards?

In order to get the most out of your tarot, it’s important to cleanse your cards before each reading. This is because tarot cards have a lot of energy, both positive and negative.

This energy is left behind in your cards after each reading, and if you don’t do a cleansing ritual, this energy can interfere with your next reading. Your cards may feel different to you after each reading, so cleansing them before the next one will put you in a clearer state of mind.

Another reason why you should cleanse your cards is because many people believe that negative energy can collect on your cards over time, which can bring harm to the owner of the cards.

By cleansing them, you are removing that negative energy, protecting yourself and your loved ones.

## How do you cleanse your tarot cards?

There are a few different methods you can use when cleansing your cards. The first method is by using white sage.

You can find white sage at most gardening or spiritual stores. Light the end of the sage and blow it out. Then, wave it over the cards in a clockwise motion.

Try to visualize any negative energy being removed from the cards. The second method is by using incense.

There are many different types of incense you can use for cleansing, such as sage, lotus, sandalwood, and cedar.

Just light the end of the incense and waft it over the cards in a similar manner to the sage.

## Do you have to use incense when cleansing your tarot cards?

While incense is the most common tool used during cleansing, it isn’t required.

You can use anything that you find significant, such as a candle or even water. The important thing is to have something that you feel is necessary to complete the ritual.

That said, most readers prefer to use incense as it provides a pleasant smell and is easier to perform than other cleansing methods.

## Can anyone do the cleansing ritual?

Yes. Anyone who uses tarot cards can cleanse their cards.

However, if you are new to tarot, it might be better to cleanse your cards after every reading for a few months so that you can truly get a feel for how the cards feel after cleansing them.

You may notice that the cards feel lighter or that they feel a little different to the way they normally do. If so, then you know you’re doing it right!

## Conclusion

While cleaning your tarot cards sounds like a chore, it can actually be a really enjoyable ritual. You can put on your favourite music, light a candle, and even try meditating while you do it.

The important thing is that you do it properly. Without cleansing, you might be missing out on important information that the cards could reveal to you.

So, if you have been wondering if you need to cleanse your tarot cards or not, there are many reasons why you should.

Make sure your cards are always clean, and they will last a long time.
