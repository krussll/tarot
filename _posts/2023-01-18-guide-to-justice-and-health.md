Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Justice card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Justice, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Justice.

## Justice And Health – An Overview

The Justice tarot card represents a balance of cause and effect when it comes to health.

It encourages us to find win-win solutions that are beneficial to both ourselves and others.

It reminds us that our health is in our hands, and that we have the power to create positive effects in our lives.

It also suggests that the truth of our wellbeing can come out through our actions, and that we should strive to be honest with ourselves and others about our health.

The Justice tarot card encourages us to take responsibility for our own health, and to strive for a balance between mind, body, and spirit.

## Upright Meanings For Justice And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Justice tarot card can represent a need for balance when it comes to health.

It speaks to the importance of win-win solutions and the cause and effect of our decisions on our health.

It can be a reminder that our choices, both good and bad, will have an impact on our overall wellbeing.

The Justice card also speaks to the truth that comes out from decisions made, as our health is a reflection of the decisions we make.

It encourages us to create a balanced lifestyle that nourishes and supports our health, as well as to be honest and mindful of the consequences of our decisions.

Ultimately, The Justice card can be a reminder that our health is in our hands and we have the power to create a healthy and balanced life.

### What It Means For The Future

The Justice tarot card speaks to the need to take responsibility for one’s own health and wellbeing.

It suggests that if I am willing to make choices that are beneficial to both myself and others, I can reap the rewards of improved health and happiness.

By finding solutions that work for everyone, the truth of the matter will reveal itself and I can move forward in a positive direction.

This card encourages me to be mindful of the ripple effects my choices will have on my health, and to be honest and open with myself and others.

Taking time to cultivate a balanced and healthy lifestyle will bring me closer to achieving the optimal physical and mental health I seek.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Justice card in a Tarot reading suggests that cause and effect, win-win solutions, and truth will come out when it comes to the health of those close to me.

It means that whatever choices I make, I should be sure to seek out what will benefit not only myself, but also those around me.

This card encourages me to make decisions that are based on mutual respect, fairness, and honesty, and to strive to create solutions that are beneficial to all parties involved.

This card also suggests that I should investigate any health issues and make sure that the truth comes out, so that I can make the best decisions for myself and my loved ones.

## Reversed Meanings For Justice And Health

### What It Means For Me

When the Justice card appears reversed, it implies an imbalance of power or an unfairness in the situation.

This could be indicative of an unhealthy dynamic in terms of physical or mental health.

For example, an individual in a toxic relationship may feel unable to be honest with their partner and is unable to find the win-win solution they need.

In the world of physical health, an individual may be dealing with an unfair situation such as an illness or disability that has caused them difficulty in achieving the life balance they need.

The reversed Justice card may also suggest that the truth is being hidden or withheld in a way that creates an unhealthy balance of power.

This could be related to health conditions that are not being addressed or are being kept hidden from a patient or those in their care.

It could even be an issue of the patient hiding their true symptoms in an effort to appear healthier than they really are.

In either case, it is important to consider the consequences of the imbalance of power and address the issue for the benefit of all involved.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Justice tarot card suggests that there is a lack of balance when it comes to health.

It could mean that there is an imbalance of energy, or a lack of clarity in decision making.

There may be a feeling of being overwhelmed by the options available and not knowing which one to take.

This can lead to an inability to make important decisions regarding health, such as deciding which course of treatment or lifestyle to pursue.

In terms of relationships, the reversed Justice suggests that there is an imbalance of power.

This can manifest itself in unhealthy relationships and unhealthy behaviors.

This could include anything from toxic communication to emotional manipulation.

There may be a lack of accountability and responsibility, which can lead to a lack of progress in any health-related goals.

Overall, the reversed Justice suggests that it is time to take a step back, assess the situation, and make decisions that will create a healthier balance.

It may be necessary to make changes in order to restore this balance.

It is also important to find a balance between personal health and the health of others.

It is necessary to take responsibility for our own health, as well as being supportive of the health of others.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Justice card is reversed, it can indicate a situation where cause and effect are out of balance, and the truth is being distorted or hidden.

This can be particularly concerning when it comes to health.

It may mean that patients are not receiving the full picture of their diagnosis, or a doctor is not being completely honest about the risks of a certain treatment.

It could also mean that those with power or authority are taking advantage of those with less power and influence, leading to unfair and unequal access to healthcare.

Reversed Justice can be a warning sign that a person’s health is in danger due to a lack of transparency and honesty.

It could be indicative of a situation where a person’s rights and freedoms are being compromised due to an imbalance of power.

This card can point to the need for justice and fairness in the healthcare system.

It can also serve as a reminder to be conscious of the decisions we make when seeking medical advice and to remember that true health is an ever-evolving process that requires honest communication at every step.
