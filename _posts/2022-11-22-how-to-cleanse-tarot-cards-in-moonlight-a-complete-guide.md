In many tarot readings, the moon often represents intuition and illumination. So it’s no surprise that cleansing your tarot cards in moonlight is a great way to tap into those powerful energies while you cleanse your cards.

The light of the moon has long been linked with magic and rituals—both ancient and modern witchcraft often take place on the nights of the full moon.

Because cleaning tarot cards in moonlight has so much potential, it’s worth exploring exactly how to cleanse tarot cards in moonlight effectively, and what this might mean for your readings moving forward.

Here’s everything you need to know…

## How to cleanse your tarot cards in moonlight

Cleansing your tarot cards in moonlight is one way of doing this. This ritual works particularly well on the night of the full moon—that’s when you’ll get the most energy from the moonlight. Here’s what you need to do:

- **Take your tarot cards outside and place them on the ground**. This will give them direct access to the light of the moon. You can place them on a table or stand inside as an alternative.
- **Leave your tarot cards outside overnight**. Make sure they’re out of direct sunlight though.
- **Once the sun rises, take your tarot cards inside**. They’ll still be bathed in moonlight until then.
- **Once inside, you can either leave your cards out or put them back in their deck box.** Either will work well.

## What is a Tarot Card Cleansing Ritual?

A tarot card cleansing ritual is an act of cleaning and preparing your tarot cards in a way that aligns them with your intuition. This is different from cleaning your tarot cards in a way that simply gets rid of dust and dirt.

While that’s important, it’s simply not enough for a good card reading. To really get the most from your cards, you need to cleanse and align them with your intuition.

This will ensure your readings are as accurate as possible and provide you with the kind of insights that only come from the deepest parts of your soul.

A tarot card cleansing ritual will usually involve a few different steps:

- Releasing negative energy from your cards.
- Clearing your cards of any remaining negative energies that could otherwise affect your reading.
- Re-energizing your cards, so they’re full of positive, clear energy.
- Aligning your cards with your intuition.

## Benefits of cleansing tarot cards in moonlight

There are many benefits to cleansing tarot cards in moonlight. As well as tapping into the light of the moon, you’ll also be using the light of the full moon specifically.

This is one of the most powerful lights in the universe—it can help you tap into your intuitive self, solve problems, and communicate more effectively. Cleansing your tarot cards in moonlight will also help you get rid of any negative energy that your cards might be carrying.

This could be from a previous owner of the cards, or it could simply be the energies of everyday life. If you’re cleaning your tarot cards in moonlight, you’ll also be able to benefit from this light throughout the night.

This will give you a longer window to cleanse your cards, and leave them ready to use at any time during the day.

## Disadvantages of cleansing tarot cards in moonlight

While cleansing tarot cards in moonlight has many benefits, there are also a few downsides. For example, if you live in an area with little or no moonlight, you won’t be able to do this ritual.

You may also need to wait until the night of the full moon, which may not be ideal if you’re planning to cleanse your cards ahead of an upcoming reading.

You’ll also need to make sure your cards are outdoor-friendly, so you don’t damage them during the cleaning process.

However, with these downsides in mind, it’s worth noting that you can still cleanse your tarot cards in moonlight even if you don’t live in an area with a lot of moonlight.

You just need to be patient and wait until the night of the full moon so you can make the most of this light.

Leave them there overnight until the sun rises, then bring them inside and either leave them out or put them back in their deck box.

Once you’ve done this, you’ll be able to get the most out of your tarot cards in all your readings.
