A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 5 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card can be interpreted together as a reminder that despite hardships, there is always something to strive for. In the case of The 5 Of Pentacles, ill health, poverty, worry, isolation, and financial loss can all be challenging to overcome, but with dedication and hard work, these difficulties can eventually be surpassed. The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card symbolizes a brighter future in which leaving a legacy, retirement, and an inheritance are all possible. These two cards together are an encouragement to persevere despite opposition, as the future holds a greater reward.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Despite financial loss and worry, the relationship could still be a source of joy and support. With effort, you can eventually have a relationship that is free from fear and worries, and one that will provide you with security and a safe haven.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles tarot card is often a warning of ill health, physical or mental. Physically, it could mean a health setback, injury or illness. It can also be a sign of poor physical health that has been neglected, such as not getting enough exercise or eating unhealthy foods. Mentally, it could suggest worries or anxieties that are weighing you down. These worries might be related to finances or relationships.

The 5 of Pentacles can also point to financial struggles. This can include being deeply in debt, struggling to make ends meet, not having enough money saved and having to borrow from family or friends. It could also suggest financial losses, such as investments that failed or legal issues that cost you money.

The 5 of Pentacles can also point to isolation and loneliness, suggesting that you feel disconnected from the people around you. This can cause problems in relationships both romantic and otherwise, as communication might be lacking. This feelings of isolation and loneliness can lead to resentment and alienation, leading to further relationship issues.

All in all, The 5 of Pentacles tarot card can be a warning of ill health, financial struggles, and a lack of connection in your relationships. It is important to be aware of these warning signs in order to address them and prevent them from escalating. Take time to care for your body and mind, seek professional help for financial issues, and reach out to loved ones for emotional and social support.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates a state of physical, mental, and spiritual well-being. When this card appears in a reading, it can signify the reward of good health gained through hard work, self-care and lifestyle choices. Health is an important part of leaving a legacy and protecting those we love. It is a sign of having a healthy lifestyle and finding a balance between work and leisure.

Finances:

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that financially, you have built a secure foundation that will earn you financial stability now and in the future. This card signifies financial security and the ability to pass on wealth to generations to come. It is also a sign of being able to save for long-term goals such as retirement, inheritance and investments.

Relationships:

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card represents the start of a harmonious and supportive relationship. When this card appears in a reading, it suggests that you have found a lasting connection and stability in your relationships. This card can also indicate the potential of leaving a lasting legacy that will continue to be passed down to future generations. It is a sign of being able to trust and rely on one another, allowing for healthy and meaningful relationships.
