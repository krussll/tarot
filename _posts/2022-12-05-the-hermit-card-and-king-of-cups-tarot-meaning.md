## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card is an emblem of the importance of self-discovery and reflection. It encourages us to look within and take time to truly understand ourselves and our relationships. For example, when it comes to health, the card tells us that we must take the time to understand our body and recognize how it functions and how it needs to be supported. Taking the time to listen to our bodies, to nourish and move them in a way that is beneficial, is one of the best ways to maintain our health and wellness.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card speaks to the importance of a financial plan and understanding of our personal finances. Taking the time to reflect on our spending habits, to create a budget and to save for our goals are important aspects of a healthy financial life. This process may involve making some sacrifices and learning to live within our means, but it is essential for establishing financial stability. The card also encourages us to seek wisdom and knowledge when it comes to financial matters, such as consulting experts and researching trends.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to look within and understand our own needs and desires in relationships. If we are to be able to give and receive love, trust and support from ourselves and our partners, we must first be able to understand ourselves fully. The card tells us that taking the time to reflect on how we want and need to show up in our relationships is essential for healthy, long-lasting connections. It also encourages us to communicate our needs and preferences clearly and to appreciate the unique perspective that each partner brings to the relationship.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

The King of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a powerful and supportive leader. The person governed by this card will often display a high degree of empathy, patience, and tolerance in their dealings with others. They will be generous and compassionate in their dealings with others, but can also be firm and authoritative when needed.

The meaning of this card in regards to health could be seen as a sign of emotional and mental balance. People who draw this card may be more open to self-care and healing, and the energies of this card could represent the type of help that someone needs to make long-term lifestyle changes for the better.

The King of Cups Tarot card can bring strong influences to financial issues. The person governed by this card may have an abundance of financial resources available to them, and they will also be able to manage their resources wisely, with forethought and understanding. They are likely to be successful in their business dealings, and will be able to offer wise and balanced advice to their partners and associates.

In their relationships with others, the King of Cups could signify a person who takes their role as a partner seriously and will strive to create a balanced and supportive environment for those around them. They will be generous with their time and resources, but also set boundaries and expectations that they expect others to follow. They will strive to foster a sense of mutual respect and understanding between themselves and those they care about.
