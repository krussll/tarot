A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and Judgment card together in particular.

The Death card and Judgment have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and Judgment mean together?

The Death tarot card and The Judgment tarot card together signify big changes that are on their way, and the clarity to make a final decision about how to move forward. It is a call to accept and let go of the past and start fresh. This transformation can be seen as a metaphorical death and rebirth and is a symbol that you are following your life’s purpose. It is time to move on from the things that no longer serve you and embrace a new beginning.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Death and Judgment mean together for your love life?

When the Death and Judgment tarot cards come together in a love reading, this can indicate a need for a major transformation in one’s relationship. Those affected may feel called to make significant changes to their relationship that will result in a complete shift in their current dynamic. The Death card suggests that the existing relationship must come to an end, while the Judgment card advises the querent to look inward and consider what they truly need and desire in their relationship.

This could mean that they must face the truth of the situation, and make a difficult decision that takes into account both their needs and their partner’s. At the same time, they may feel empowered to make a decision that empowers them to be true to themselves and take control of their own destiny. Ultimately, this combination of cards may be suggesting that it is time to move on from a current romantic relationship, and instead focus on self-care and personal growth.

The transition process could be difficult, as the querent may have to let go of something or someone that has been important to them. While the Death card might suggest that the transition will result in a painful end, the Judgment card also allows for the space to move forward with a greater sense of clarity and purpose. Once the querent has had the courage to make a difficult decision, they will find themselves open to new possibilities and new beginnings that may not have been available to them before. With the help of the Death and Judgment cards, it is possible to move forward with a renewed sense of power, hope, and inspiration.

The Death tarot card could indicate a necessary ending or a time of renewal. The Judgment card calls attention to your life’s purpose and clarity. To discover your new purpose and direction, you may need to examine your finances and job and make a decision to direct your energy elsewhere. The combination of The Death tarot card and The Judgment tarot card can signify a time of transformation and rebirth.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and Judgment mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health  
The Death tarot card meaning is associated with major changes and transformation. This tarot card meaning could indicate big shifts in one’s health. This could include receiving a major health diagnosis, undergoing a health transformation such as going gluten free or vegan, starting a new physical fitness routine, or making a lifestyle change to improve overall health. This card could mean that one must end an unhealthy habit, like smoking, drinking or overeating; or perhaps it could signal a time to let go of a past health issue. It could also represent a desire to do something entirely new, such as embarking on a spiritual healing journey to gain insight into physical health.

Finances  
When it comes to finances, The Death tarot card has a connotation of endings and changes. One might be called to let go of outdated financial practices or beliefs that no longer serve them. It could be a good time to embrace a different financial mindset or philosophy. This could involve creating a budget, consolidating debt, creating an emergency fund, investing, or learning new money strategies. The Death tarot card could also suggest that it is time to make a big change, such as a career transition or change in financial focus.

Relationships  
The Death tarot card can signify an ending of a relationship, be it a family relationship, romantic relationship, or friendship. This could mean that it is time to move on from a relationship that is no longer beneficial or growth-enhancing. The card could also signify a period of transition in relationships, where two people go through a difficult but ultimately beneficial transformation. It could signify a change in the way two people interact or communicate, or a shift in the way two people relate to each other. It could also suggest a new beginning for a relationship, where two people make a commitment to respect and honor each other in a new way.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card is an incredibly powerful card, providing clarity and insight into our lives. When it turns up in a reading, it can signify a time of major decision-making and a time to look inward. On a health level, it can be interpreted as a sign of an impending wake-up call, suggesting that it’s time to consider how you’re taking care of yourself, both physically and mentally. It may be time to make better lifestyle choices and to eliminate habits that don’t serve you. Financially, it could indicate making a major life decision, such as a career change or investing in something. It could signal a need to reevaluate your current financial situation and make sure you’re in control of it. Relationships-wise, this card can represent the liberation that comes with making a difficult decision, such as breaking up with a partner or ending an unhealthy relationship. It could also signify a final call to forgive and accept yourself and others around you, allowing for a sense of peace and closure. Ultimately, the Judgment card speaks of the need to make a judgment call, make a decision and take action.
