A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The Temperance card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The Temperance and Queen of Swords tarot cards together symbolize a balance between mental and emotional clarity. This card pair encourages us to cultivate patience and self-control when it comes to our thoughts and feelings. This combination reminds us to strive for a balanced lifestyle, with honest and logical decision making as well as a good sense of self-sufficiency. The Temperance and Queen of Swords tarot cards represents a harmonious equilibrium of wisdom, integrity, and poise.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Temperance and Queen Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When the Temperance and Queen of Swords cards appear in combination in a love reading, they suggest a balance of power between two potential partners. Both cards are strong in their own right; Temperance suggests emotional maturity, patience, and self-control, while the Queen of Swords implies a combination of emotional or mental strength and independence. Together, they speak to a certain amount of emotional and intellectual compatibility between two people.

The Temperance card encourages you to take your time in the romantic arena and not rush into anything. This card reminds us to think before we act, to practice balance and moderation in all things, and to think about our decisions and the consequences of them. This card encourages patience, respect, and diplomacy when engaging with someone we have feelings for.

Meanwhile, the Queen of Swords offers a similar type of wisdom, but from a different angle. This card symbolizes a high degree of emotional independence and insight. It calls for clear, honest communication and a willingness to take ownership of one’s decisions. Together, these two cards suggest that in matters of love, patience, discernment and honesty are essential.

In terms of a love reading, Temperance and the Queen of Swords suggest that two potential partners are capable of understanding each other’s feelings and coming to compromises that benefit both sides. They could both experience moments of harmony and balance if they work together. While compromise and negotiation are necessary for any relationship to succeed, these cards remind us that it is also important to have confidence in who we are and to remain honest with ourselves and others.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does Temperance mean?

Health

The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of balance in our day to day lives, especially when it comes to our physical health. This card encourages us to make sure to get enough rest and exercise, and to make healthy choices when it comes to food and drink. It is a reminder that moderation is key. It tells us to take time to really nurture our bodies and ensure a healthy lifestyle.

Finances

The Temperance Tarot card also emphasizes balance when it comes to finances. This card encourages us to avoid getting too caught up in the material world and instead think about what we really need, especially if it means cutting back in some areas. This card tells us to measure risk accordingly, to plan ahead and to make sure we don’t overextend ourselves.

Relationships

The Temperance Tarot card is also a reminder that balance is important when it comes to relationships. This card speaks to the importance of taking time to listen to those around us, working together through disagreement and looking for ways to be understanding. It encourages us to nurture our interpersonal relationships, with our friends, family and lovers alike.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

It can be challenging to look at the reality of your health without making excuses or putting it off, but it is an important part of maintaining your health. She also encourages you to speak up if you are feeling taken advantage of medically or if you feel like you are being given incorrect information. It can also be a sign that you need to take more control of your own health, make decisions and take the advice of those in the medical community more seriously.

When it comes to finances, The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a reminder not to let greed, desire, or envy prevent you from making the right decisions with your money. It is important to maintain total honesty and truth when it comes to your finances. This card also indicates that it is wise to be conservative in your approach to money, to be mindful of the reality of your financial situation, and to think carefully before making any big financial decisions.

In terms of relationships, The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages you to be honest and open in your dealings with others. It is also a reminder to practice self-care and to not let another person’s expectations or whims dictate your own. The Queen of Swords is also a sign that you should be comfortable setting clear boundaries in your relationships and to refuse to be taken advantage of. It can also indicate that you need to take more control of your own relationships and make sure to put your own needs first.
