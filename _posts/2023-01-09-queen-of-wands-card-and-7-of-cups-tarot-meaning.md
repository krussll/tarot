A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen of Wands and the Seven of Cups together in a reading can indicate a very ambitious and driven individual who is struggling with big choices. The Queen of Wands is a strong and confident character which shows that this person has a lot of potential, however, they are also having difficulty committing to any one decision due to the many options available, as indicated by the Seven of Cups. This could suggest that this person is having difficulty focusing on a specific goal and may need some help to make a tough decision. Ultimately these two cards together suggest this individual may need to realise their ambitions and be more decisive in order to move forward.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

While outwardly you may appear confident and assured, on the inside you may be struggling to commit to one particular relationship. Your feisty nature may be a double-edged sword, both helping and hindering you in your quest for love.

Your self-assurance may provide you with the courage to explore a plethora of romantic possibilities, however, it may also make it difficult for you to settle down and pick one. On one hand, you may be confident in your ability to get out there and find whatever you want in a relationship. On the other hand, it may be difficult to narrow down your choices and commit to one person. It can be a difficult balancing act to remain true to yourself and yet still take a risk and make a commitment.

You may need to take some time to reflect on your values and desires and then make a decision which feels aligned with your heart. Consider what would bring you the most joy in the long-term, as well as understanding and appreciating the value of taking a risk. The Queen Of Wands and The 7 Of Cups together are urging you to be mindful of your choices and take the leap into a lasting and fulfilling relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Wands and 7 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen of Wands Tarot card typically symbolizes health, finances and relationships. In health, it is associated with a feisty attitude and an optimism about life. It can represent the energy of revitalization, renewal, and self-care, encouraging you to make positive choices and take good care of your body and mind. When it appears in a reading, it could be a sign that you are regaining your strength, health and focus, and should enjoy the process of getting healthier.

When it comes to finances, the Queen of Wands Tarot card encourages you to trust in your own capabilities and ideas. It encourages you to put forth the effort to make your financial dreams a reality. It encourages you to take risks, to make investments, and to take charge of your financial future. It also warns against taking on too much debt, or overspending.

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of boldness and confidence in relationships. It encourages open-mindedness, empathy and communication in relationships. It indicates that individuals should embrace their boldness and self-confidence, and utilize these qualities to relate to each other in a healthy and attractive manner. It also warns against dominating and manipulative behavior, as this could lead to strained and damaged relationships.

Overall, the Queen of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of confidence, assurance and strong willed energy. It is a card that encourages us to take charge of our own lives and make positive choices in health, finances and relationships.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 7 Of Cups Tarot card typically signifies that one’s health is in a precarious position, and they are feeling overwhelmed or confused by the sheer number of options or possible paths ahead of them. It’s telling the querent to take their time, go slowly and not rush into anything. It’s a reminder to consider the pros and cons of each option and make sure it’s right for them before committing to it. It’s also a sign that they need to practice self care and find something they truly enjoy doing in order to find balance and overall wellbeing.

Finances:  
The 7 Of Cups Tarot card is a sign that one’s finances are in a state of flux. It’s indicating that there are many potential paths of action that a person could take when it comes to their money, but it’s important to weigh the pros and cons of each one before making a decision. This card may be a sign to take a more conservative approach and not take too many risks as it could lead to financial disaster. It could also be a sign to review one’s budget and make sure all their bills are being paid on time.

Relationships:  
The 7 Of Cups Tarot card is a sign that one’s relationships may be going through a difficult stage. It’s telling the querent to think long and hard before entering into a commitment or taking a particular path in their relationship. There may be too many choices and the person needs to be sure they are making the right decision. It may be a sign to take a step back and evaluate the current relationship or situation in order to get a better sense of clarity and understanding. It’s also a reminder to practice mindfulness when it comes to others and to always be kind and patient with loved ones.
