By keeping the two cards in mind, you will ensure that your ambitions are nurtured in a healthy, balanced way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health:  
The Temperance Tarot card is about finding balance and harmony in health. It means eating the right amount of food, getting regular exercise, and managing stress. It encourages people to be mindful of their own physical and mental health, and to be patient with their bodies. It also suggests looking for natural and holistic alternatives to traditional health care, such as herbal remedies, yoga, and acupuncture.

Finances:  
The Temperance Tarot card serves as a reminder to practice fiscal responsibility and self-control. This means understanding your financial goals, creating a budget and a savings plan, and adjusting spending habits accordingly. It suggests that wealth should be enjoyed, but in moderation and within the limitations of what is affordable. As a result, it encourages people to prioritize their financial goals and think twice before making any big financial decisions.

Relationships:  
The Temperance Tarot card is all about finding a balance between two opposing forces. This applies to relationships as well. It encourages people to be mindful of the conversations they have with their loved ones, to be patient when dealing with disagreements, and to practice self-control when facing difficult circumstances. It suggests that communication is key in any relationship and that respect and understanding are essential for maintaining a healthy relationship.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

Health

The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that it is a time for deepening understanding of your physical and mental health. It implies that you are taking active steps to learn more about what nourishes your body, mind and spirit and make meaningful changes that will improve your overall wellbeing. This could mean making more time for physical activity, taking steps to manage stress, making healthier dietary choices and learning to listen to your body and its needs.

Finances

The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that it is a time to get serious about your financial goals. It suggests that you are taking proactive steps to understand money matters and make decisions that will improve your financial stability. This could mean taking an online course, discussing investments and savings options with a financial advisor, budgeting and tracking expenses, or committing to a regular savings plan.

Relationships

The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card implies that it is a time for taking steps to strengthen relationships in your life. This card suggests that you are exploring how to build better connections with family, friends and/or partners. You may be taking classes to better understand how to communicate or practicing active listening and understanding. This card encourages you to learn how to navigate differences and constructively work towards mutual understanding and stronger bonds.
