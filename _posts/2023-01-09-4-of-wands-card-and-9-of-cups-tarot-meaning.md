A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 4 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 4 Of Wands and The 9 Of Cups tarot cards is a very auspicious one! It suggests that homecomings and reunions are in the cards for you, especially with good friends and family members. It also means that any wishes or desires that you have been hoping for or working towards in your life will be granted to you, leading to abundance and a general feeling of well-being. These tarot cards together indicate a time of celebration and joy, where all of your dreams become realities and family and friends are there to share in that with you.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

This card can also represent a form of respite from general health problems, like fatigue or difficulty sleeping. Financial success can be implied, as the card signifies abundance and plenty, as well as physical and mental well-being.

In terms of finances, the Four of Wands Tarot card indicates that one can expect financial stability and growth. This card reflects an end of an unlucky streak and that one can look forward to luck and abundance. It is a sign of prosperity and the potential to increase wealth, especially through the hard work one puts into their career, side hustles or investments.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the Four of Wands Tarot card speaks of a time of reunion and celebration. This card signifies that connection and security are being and can be enhanced, either through existing relationships or through new ones. Whether it is a romantic partner, family member or friend, this card signifies comfort and continuity. It is a sign that one can be fulfilled, content, and at ease through the connections that they have.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is often referred to as the Wish Card, so it is appropriate that it is associated with wishes coming true. This card is symbolic of finding contentment and happiness in all aspects of life and of granting yourself the opportunity for true fulfillment. In terms of health, the 9 of Cups Tarot card stands for good physical and mental health, as well as for achieving the overall wellness that comes from feeling joy and satisfaction. This card is a reminder to take the time to appreciate your health and the blessings that come with it, and to practice self-care and kindness by treating your body and mind with respect.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Cups Tarot card stands for abundance and prosperity. This card encourages you to stay positive and focused on achieving your financial goals and to stay motivated as you go through the journey of financial wealth. It is also a sign that you are on the right path and that positive outcomes will manifest if you continue to work hard and stay positive.

Finally, the 9 of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to nurture and cultivate strong relationships with friends and loved ones. This card suggests that if you are open to it, meaningful, fulfilling relationships can enter your life and bring happiness and a sense of security. Whether it is through friendship, romance, or family relations, this card encourages you to create strong and supportive bonds with others and to nourish these relationships through communication, trust, understanding, and love.
