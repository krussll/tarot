Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 8 Of Cups card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 8 Of Cups, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 8 Of Cups.

## 8 Of Cups And Love – An Overview

The 8 Of Cups tarot card is a symbol of retreat, abandonment, and moving on, and it has a direct meaning when it comes to health.

It encourages us to let go of unhealthy habits and behaviors that may be holding us back from achieving our fullest potential.

It can suggest that we take a step back in order to reflect on our current situation and make changes that will ultimately benefit us.

It may also mean that we evaluate our relationships and look for ways to find a healthier balance.

Ultimately, The 8 Of Cups encourages us to make the changes we need to make in order to move forward and live a healthier, happier life.

## Upright Meanings For 8 Of Cups And Love

### What It Means For Me

The 8 of Cups in a love context implies that the querent is feeling disatisfied and ready to move on from their current relationship.

They may be feeling like they have given all they can to the relationship and feel like they need to move on to something or someone different.

This could mean anything from ending the relationship to simply taking a break and being apart for a while.

It is a sign that the querent is ready to abandon the relationship and seek something new and different.

They may be feeling like their current relationship is no longer fulfilling their needs.

Whatever the case may be, this card is a sign that the querent is ready to take a step back and move on from their current relationship.

### What It Means For The Future

The 8 of Cups tarot card speaks of a need to retreat from a current situation in order to find something better.

This could suggest that in my future love life, I should be prepared for the possibility of moving on from a current relationship in order to find something more fulfilling.

This card indicates that I should be willing to let go of the past, and focus on creating something better for myself.

It may be difficult to leave something comfortable, but it could be necessary to find true love and happiness, and it is important to keep this in mind in my journey of self-discovery.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 8 of Cups in the tarot is a sign that the love of those close to me may be waning.

It is a reminder that sometimes relationships don’t last forever and people may need to move on or withdraw from a relationship that has become stale or unfulfilling.

It is a reminder that love is a dynamic and ever-changing thing, and that sometimes the best thing we can do is to accept that it is time to let go and move on.

This card encourages us to withdraw from relationships that are not allowing us to grow in order to make room for healthier, more fulfilling connections in the future.

## Reversed Meanings For 8 Of Cups And Love

### What It Means For Me

The reversed 8 Of Cups in a reading about love suggests that something is missing in the relationship.

You may find yourself feeling abandoned, like your partner is not as emotionally present as they once were.

You may feel as though you are being pushed away and ignored.

The reversed 8 Of Cups could indicate that it is time to move on from the relationship and find something more fulfilling.

It is important to remember that the reversed 8 Of Cups does not necessarily mean that the relationship is doomed.

It is a suggestion that something is lacking and it is important to take steps to address these issues before they become too overwhelming.

Perhaps it’s time to have an honest conversation with your partner and open up about what is missing in the relationship.

It is possible that with a few changes and adjustments, the relationship can be restored to a healthy and happy place.

### What It Means For The Future

When the 8 of Cups is reversed in a Tarot reading, it can indicate that there is a chance of renewal in your love life.

You may be feeling a sense of loneliness or emptiness and may be ready to move on from a relationship or situation that is no longer fulfilling you.

You may be ready to focus on yourself and take some time to explore your needs and desires, allowing yourself to open up to new possibilities and experiences.

The reversed 8 of Cups is an optimistic card and suggests that you may soon be in a position to open your heart to potential romantic partners, and that new and exciting relationships could be just around the corner.

It also encourages you to trust your intuition and connect with your feelings.

As you move through this period of growth and self-exploration, you may be able to find a partner who understands and accepts you for who you are and who helps you to feel loved and appreciated.

Your future love life is likely to be full of exciting and meaningful connections, if you remain open to the possibilities.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed 8 of Cups appears in a tarot reading, it suggests that the love from those close to you is not unconditional.

The reversed 8 of Cups indicates that you are feeling neglected or abandoned.

You may be feeling like your partner or family members have taken you for granted and are not putting in the same effort as you are.

You may feel as if your loved ones do not appreciate what you do for them.

This card also speaks to the need for you to step back and take a break from your relationships.

It urges you to take a break from those you love and focus on yourself.

It is possible that the love of those close to you has become too possessive, and it is possible that the relationships are not serving you in the way they should be.

This card suggests that it is time to move on and explore other options.

It is essential to remember that the love of those close to you should be unconditional and that you should be able to be yourself without feeling judged or put down.
