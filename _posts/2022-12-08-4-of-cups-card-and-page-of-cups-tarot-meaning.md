## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Cups and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 4 of Cups Tarot card is associated with apathy towards health. This can manifest in various ways, such as not taking care of oneself, avoidance of physical activities and health advice, or lack of awareness about one’s own health condition. People who fall into this tarot card may feel overwhelmed by their physical condition and lack motivation to do anything about it. It may also suggest feeling trapped in one’s own body and not being able to do much to improve the situation. Health issues may be caused by emotional blockages, mental fatigue, or physical exhaustion. Depression and anxiety may accompany this card, as a person in this state of mind may find it hard to take action and get back on the path to good health.

Finances

The 4 of Cups Tarot card also speaks of financial apathy. This could manifest in an unwillingness to change one’s financial situation and a lack of ambition to make improvements. It may be linked to feelings of helplessness or being stuck in the same position and not being able to move forward. People in this state of mind will probably not be interested in making any sort of investments or taking risks with their money. It may also be linked to procrastination and avoidance of making wise financial decisions.

Relationships

In relationships, the 4 of Cups Tarot card speaks of feeling unfulfilled or dissatisfied with one’s current situation. This could manifest in an unwillingness to accept surprises and gifts from a partner, or feeling bored by the same routine and activities. There may be an emotional disconnection from one’s partner and a lack of trust in each other. This card may also be linked to a lack of communication and commitment in a relationship, and may lead to feelings of loneliness and dissatisfaction. People in this state of mind may find it hard to express their feelings and may find it difficult to open up to their partner.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health:

The Page of Cups Tarot card symbolizes health as being in the hands of the individual. To stay healthy, the Page of Cups encourages us to take the time to understand our minds and emotions, and to pay attention to our physical health. This card suggests that we should take a holistic approach to health and wellness, combining physical, emotional, and spiritual aspects in order to achieve optimal wellness. We should take the time to nourish our bodies and minds through proper nutrition and exercise, as well as self-love and nurturing activities like yoga and meditation.

Finances:

The Page of Cups Tarot card indicates a period of financial learning and growth. This card emphasizes the importance of taking responsibility for our financial health, as well as setting financial goals and taking the necessary steps to reach them. It suggests that we should take the time to educate ourselves about financial planning and money management. We should not be afraid to invest wisely in order to achieve our financial dreams, but should be careful not to overextend ourselves or take on too much risk.

Relationships:

The Page of Cups Tarot card suggests that we should take the time to cultivate meaningful relationships in our lives. This card emphasizes love, compassion, and understanding as the foundation of strong, healthy relationships. It suggests that we should take the time to get to know our friends and family better and to express our appreciation for their presence in our lives. We should also make time for the pursuit of new, beneficial relationships, while being careful not to become overly attached and clingy in our relationships.
