However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Page Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

My hard work and dedication towards uncovering the facts or secrets of this situation will be rewarded with material abundance and the replenishment of my good health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health

The Page of Swords Tarot card is associated with the element of air and it is a card of communication, thought and ideas. In terms of health, the Page of Swords Tarot card may suggest that you are too focused on the details of your health and well-being. You may be so caught up in trying to figure out the cause of certain ailments or conditions that you miss out on the bigger picture. It could also indicate that you are dealing with a health issue that requires some deep introspection and psychological exploration. You need to take a step back, analyze the facts and develop an informed opinion about the situation before you can move forward.

Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card may be an indication that you need to pay very close attention to your finances. This card can suggest that you must be diligent in monitoring your budget and making sure that you are not taking financially irresponsible risks. In terms of investments, it could suggest that you need to make sure that you are mapping out the details of each venture and that you should not be hesitant to ask questions if you are unsure about something. This card can also suggest that you need to pay close attention to your financial situation to ensure that there are no disreputable sources or people that you are dealing with.

Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card can suggest that there may be difficulties in your relationships as a result of your inquisitive and inquiring nature. You may be too eager to probe into the lives of those around you, and this behavior could cause tension in your relationships. At the same time, this card can be a reminder that you should be honest and direct about your thoughts and feelings and be willing to listen to the thoughts and feelings of others. This card also suggests that it might be necessary to have to pry into certain matters in order to uncover the truth and get to the bottom of issues that you have with someone.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 Cups Tarot card is a card of wishes fulfilled and great material abundance. It signifies a period of good luck, especially for those who are open to it. In the realms of health, The 9 of Cups suggests that we are in a period of health and wellness, both physical and mental. As we take steps to care for our bodies and regulate our lifestyle, we will find that we have more energy, clarity of mind and an overall sense of well-being. In terms of finances, The 9 of Cups indicates that our financial situation is in a positive, abundant phase. We can expect our wealth to increase and prosperity to come our way. It encourages us to be proactive and make good financial decisions, so that our financial goals can be achieved.

In terms of relationships, The 9 Cups suggests good fortune in matters of the heart. We can expect to find happiness and contentment in our relationships. Our connections with those around us will be positive and supportive, and we will find joy in the company of others. We may also find that our relationships with our closest friends and family become stronger, as we are more open to expressing our feelings and really being present in the moment. Overall, The 9 of Cups Tarot card is a powerful symbol of great luck, abundance and joy. We can expect to experience joy, good health and financial abundance in this period, as well as strong, supportive relationships.
