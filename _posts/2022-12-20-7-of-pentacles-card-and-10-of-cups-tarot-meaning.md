A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card and The 10 of Cups tarot card mean that with patience and hard work, a family can find total love and support and contentment. This combination of cards suggests that any success achieved through waiting and diligent effort will bring joy and satisfaction within the family. The cards emphasize how important it is to take the time and make the effort needed to build strong emotional bonds between all family members, as this will lead to great rewards in the long run.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 7 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

The 10 of Cups Tarot card is an indication of contentment, happiness and fulfillment in all areas of life. When this card appears in a reading, it symbolizes a time of true joy and a feeling of contentment. It can represent a home life and family that is stable and secure, making for a strong sense of emotional wellbeing.

For health, the 10 of Cups indicates that overall health and wellness are excellent. This card is a reminder to stay active and engaged in life, and to make good lifestyle choices that promote longevity and vitality. There is a feeling of balance and stability in health and well-being.

In regards to finances, the 10 of Cups can suggest financial security and stability. This is a time when the finances are in order, and there is no need to worry about money issues. Money comes in abundance and there is a feeling of abundance and prosperity in the area of finance.

Familial relationships benefit from the 10 of Cups as well. This card can signify strong and supportive relationships in family and home life, with a close-knit feeling of love and support between family members. This could mean a newly formed relationship or a strengthening of an existing one. While family dynamics can be complicated, the 10 of Cups indicates a sense of connection and closeness that bring about feelings of contentment.

The 10 of Cups Tarot card can bring about a sense of comfort and satisfaction in all aspects of life. Health, finances and relationships are all supported and nurtured through this card. It is a reminder to appreciate the simple blessings life has to offer and to relish in the feeling of contentment that comes from it.
