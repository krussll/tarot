A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Tower and the 6 of Pentacles tarot cards together suggest a drastic change in your fortunes as a result of external forces. It could signal great upheaval and a feeling of being overwhelmed by a situation outside of your control. Even though you may experience discomfort and embarrassment, the 6 of Pentacles speaks to a willingness to receive and provide help during this difficult period. It could be that the generosity of your friends and family or accepting aid from outside sources helps you to navigate difficult times.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It could also refer to a drastic change coming in the form of someone entering or leaving the relationship.

The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card is a card of generosity and balance, suggesting that in times of difficulty, the couple can make it through together. It implies that help, either in the form of financial assistance, emotional support, or practical assistance, could be available to them. This could take the shape of friends or family members being willing to lend a hand to support the two of you as you navigate this difficult period.

In conclusion, when The Tower tarot card and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card appear together in a love reading, it could be a sign that a drastic change may be coming to the relationship. It could signify the end of the romance or a transition into something new. Despite feeling overwhelmed, the card also indicates that there is help available. With a generous spirit and support from others, you and your beloved can emerge from this transition stronger and more connected than ever before.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

When it comes to health, the Tower Tarot card can be an indication of unwanted change or upheaval. It’s a warning that something catastrophic may be on the horizon, and that you need to be prepared. It can indicate a physical illness or injury that could potentially ruin your finances or relationships. It could be a sign of a looming health crisis, such as a pandemic, or a life-threatening illness. Whatever the outcome, it’s likely to be unexpected and disruptive.

In terms of finances, the Tower Tarot card is a reminder that there is no guarantee of financial stability or security. It could be a sign of a major financial upheaval, such as bankruptcy, job loss, or sudden debt. It could be a sign of a major investment gone wrong or a business venture that fails. Whatever the circumstance, it’s likely to cause major and unwanted disruption in your financial life.

The Tower Tarot card is also a warning that relationships can be unstable, and even violent. It may indicate a break up, an irreparable rift between family members, or even a violent dispute. It may also serve as a reminder to be more aware of the people around you and to take extra precaution. Regardless, it’s a sign that relationships are not as secure and strong as they may appear.

Overall, the Tower Tarot card can be a warning of the sudden and destructive changes that can occur in your life. Whether it’s related to health, finances, or relationships, it’s a reminder to be vigilant and to remain prepared for any unexpected and unwanted disruption that may lie ahead.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a need for balance and generosity in regards to physical and mental health. It is a reminder that giving and receiving help from others is essential to staying healthy. If you are having a hard time managing your health, rely on family and friends to provide support. Sharing the burden with others can help lighten some of the weight. On the other hand, if you’re in a position to help someone else with their health, do not hesitate to be generous with your time and resources.

Finances: This card reflects a need to give and receive financial help in an equitable manner. It suggests that your money should be used to benefit both yourself and those in need. It is important to be generous when you have excess funds, and also to accept help when needed. The 6 of Pentacles reminds us to honor our commitments and to be mindful of our spending. It also encourages us to build financial security through investments and savings.

Relationships: This card represents the importance of equality, balance and generosity in relationships. It’s a reminder that relationships require give and take on both sides. If you are able to help someone, do not be afraid to be generous with your time and resources. If you need help, do not hesitate to reach out and accept help from others. Mutual support and understanding is essential for a strong, lasting relationship. The 6 of Pentacles also asks that you consider how your relationship is affected by other people, such as family and friends.
