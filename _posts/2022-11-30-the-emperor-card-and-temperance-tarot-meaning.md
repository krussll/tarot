We must be mindful of our decisions and always make sure that we are weighing our actions with a sense of respect and balance, allowing us to make the best possible decision that will have positive effects. With the harmonious combination of The Emperor and Temperance tarot cards, we can reach equilibrium and create a better world for ourselves.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health  
The Temperance Tarot card encourages people to remain in balance when it comes to physical, mental, and emotional health. It encourages people to find a balanced approach to self-care, emphasizing moderation, good habits, and a healthy lifestyle. People should also be patient with themselves in terms of their health and give themselves time to heal, grow and change. If people are struggling with certain physical, mental or emotional issues, they should be advised to seek proper treatment and help if needed.

Finances  
The Temperance Tarot card encourages people to find financial balance in their lives. This is especially important in times of economic volatility or when considering financial decisions. People should focus on developing strategies that promote long-term financial stability. This includes setting financial goals, budgeting, saving, investing, and managing debt wisely. People should also be patient and thoughtful when making major financial decisions and take the time to research and learn more about the market and their options.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card encourages people to practice balance and moderation in their relationships. People should strive for open and honest communication with their partners, friends, and family members. People should also be mindful of their boundaries and respect the boundaries of others. People should strive for harmony and understanding in their relationships, allowing for differences of opinion and working through conflicts in a respectful manner. People should take the time to practice self-control and patience when it comes to their relationships, understanding that effective communication and compromise are key in any successful relationship.
