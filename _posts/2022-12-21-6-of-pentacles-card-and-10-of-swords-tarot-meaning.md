A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The 6 of Pentacles and the 10 of Swords together for me is about finding the strength and courage to reach out for help. It is a reminder that if I am stuck in a painful situation, I should not be ashamed of asking for help. That there is strength in admitting that I need assistance, that I am not capable of handling this challenge on my own. This hardship won’t last forever, so it is ok to ask for a hand in order to reach the other side of this complete and painful ending. It is my time to draw on the generosity of others and use it to get back up.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that health is both an individual and communal responsibility. It encourages us to consider the connections between our own health, the health of our friends and family, and the health of our society as a whole. The card can also suggest that we look outside of ourselves for help. We should not be ashamed to ask for help when we need it, and we should be willing to offer help in turn.

Finances:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks to financial balance and sustainability. It is an invitation to explore broader concepts of wealth and abundance. We may be asked to consider how our finances can be used to help others. It is a reminder that money is a tool which can be used to create positive impacts, both within our own lives and on a larger scale.

Relationships:

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card can also be seen as a warning against inequity in our relationships. It calls on us to cultivate fairness and balance in our interactions. We should make sure that we are not taking advantage of someone, or expecting too much from them. It is a reminder that healthy relationships are built on mutual respect and open communication. The card also encourages us to practice the art of giving and receiving with grace.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 10 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of whatever physical health issues you may have hitting a breaking point. It could indicate a need for medical attention that you have been putting off for too long. It is a sign that it is time to take action and get help with any health concerns or challenges you may be facing, no matter how bleak the situation may seem. It is a reminder that you can turn things around and seek out much-needed help and support.

Finances  
The 10 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of financial problems that have become too much to bear. It could be a sign that debts have become too overwhelming and you have reached a point of feeling helpless and trapped. It’s important to remember, however, that this card is about endings, so it could also be a sign that you are ready to move on and start something new. It’s an indication that you should not be afraid to face the situation head on and take steps towards improving your financial situation.

Relationships  
The 10 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign that a relationship has come to an end, or that it needs to. It could be a sign that something has simply reached its natural conclusion, or it could be an indication that you need to walk away from a situation in order to heal. It is also a sign that you should not be afraid to cut ties and move forward with a fresh start. It could also be a sign that it is time to accept that it is time to move on and find a healthier, more balanced relationship for yourself.
