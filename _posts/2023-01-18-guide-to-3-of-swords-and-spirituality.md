Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The 3 Of Swords card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 3 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 3 Of Swords.

## 3 Of Swords And Sprituality – An Overview

The 3 of Swords tarot card represents emotional struggles, such as heartbreak, depression, and loss.

These difficult emotions can have a profound impact on physical health, causing symptoms like headaches, stomachaches, and fatigue.

In more extreme cases, depression can lead to more serious medical problems, including heart disease and cancer.

The 3 of Swords can also represent physical trauma, such as surgery.

While this can be a necessary step in restoring or maintaining health, the recovery period can be physically and emotionally draining.

The 3 of Swords indicates that it is important to seek support when dealing with physical health issues, as they can often be accompanied by powerful and difficult emotions.

## Upright Meanings For 3 Of Swords And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

The 3 of Swords tarot card speaks to a difficult moment of pain and loss in my spiritual journey.

It could signify a challenging time of heartbreak, divorce, depression, or even surgery.

It is a reminder to be gentle and patient with myself as I process the emotional aspects of these experiences.

This card also encourages me to reflect on the underlying spiritual lessons within my circumstances, and to look to the positive aspects of each experience.

Ultimately, the 3 of Swords encourages me to trust and embrace my journey, understanding that everything that comes my way has something to offer in terms of spiritual growth and enlightenment.

### What It Means For The Future

The 3 of Swords tarot card suggests that my future spirituality may be a difficult journey.

It implies that there may be a loss of something dear to me, leading to feelings of heartbreak and depression.

It could also mean that I will go through a spiritual surgery of sorts, where I will need to cut away old beliefs, attitudes, and practices that no longer serve me.

It is a difficult card to receive, but it may also signal a transformative experience that will help me grow and become a more enlightened being.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 3 of Swords tarot card suggests that physical and emotional heartache is inevitable and can be a part of life.

It may signify a painful separation, such as a divorce, or a mourning of a loss, either physical or emotional.

It can also indicate a spiritual disconnect, as emotions can be so intense that they can lead to spiritual and mental depression.

This card is a reminder to be mindful of our relationships with those close to us and to take time for self-care and reflection.

By recognizing our spiritual connection with those we love, we can better appreciate the joy of being in a loving relationship, and be more accepting of the pain that comes with it.

## Reversed Meanings For 3 Of Swords And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

When the reversed Three of Swords appears in a tarot reading, it suggests that there is a spiritual healing process taking place.

This card may indicate that the querent has recently gone through a period of heartache, loss, or depression, but is now on their way to a better place.

By facing the pain, they are learning and growing in the process.

The reversed Three of Swords suggests that the querent is coming out of a difficult period, and is now experiencing an improved outlook on life.

It is important to note that the healing process is not necessarily an easy one; it will require patience and resilience to move past the pain and find peace.

As the querent finds their way through the darkness, they may discover a newfound appreciation for life and a greater understanding of their own spirituality.

The reversed Three of Swords indicates that the querent is ready to move forward and embrace the changes that life brings.

By letting go of the negative energy, they will be able to focus on their spiritual growth and find a renewed sense of purpose.

The querent may find that the pain and heartache they experienced has taught them invaluable lessons about life and has given them a greater appreciation for the present moment.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 3 of Swords suggests that I should be open to allowing the healing process to begin.

I may have gone through great pain and loss lately, but I should take this time to reassess my spirituality and start fresh.

It is a signal that I should take the time to reflect and find a spiritual path that is right for me, something that will bring me peace and joy.

By doing this, I can find a way to begin the healing process and find a new way of understanding, embracing, and expressing my spirituality.

The reversed 3 of Swords indicates that I should be open to new ideas and let go of any preconceived notions of how I should express my spirituality.

I should be willing to explore different spiritual paths and accept new spiritual experiences.

I should be ready to accept the spiritual gifts that come my way and be willing to open my heart and mind to a new spiritual journey.

I should be willing to explore new beliefs, practices, and rituals and become more comfortable with them.

The reversed 3 of Swords is a reminder that my spirituality is a journey and there is no one-size-fits-all answer to it.

I should be patient and open-minded as I explore different ways to express my beliefs and practice my faith.

This will help me find a spiritual path that will bring me joy and peace.

By taking the time to reflect on my spiritual path and explore new possibilities, I can heal from the heartbreak, loss, and depression that I may have experienced.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed Three of Swords tarot card is associated with healing, hope and compassion.

This speaks to the spirituality of those close to me, suggesting that the time for sorrow and heartache has passed and that a journey of recovery and healing is beginning.

This card encourages me to be open to the possibility of a renewed faith in life, and that the healing process is possible.

It is a reminder that change is often necessary, and even when it comes with pain, it can lead to a deeper appreciation and understanding of life.

The reversed Three of Swords also speaks to the power of both support and understanding.

It speaks to the power of friendship, and how it can be a source of healing and strength.

It is a reminder of the importance of being present for those who are going through difficult times, and that being a source of light and hope can be the greatest source of comfort.

This card encourages me to reach out to those in my life who may be struggling, and to provide them with the care and understanding they need to get through tough times.
