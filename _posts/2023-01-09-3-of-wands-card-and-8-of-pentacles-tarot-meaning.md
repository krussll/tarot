A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 8 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 8 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 8 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 3 of Wands and the 8 of Pentacles working together mean that you are ready to take your skills and talents to the next level. You have a collaboration of creative ideas and are looking to expand in both commerce and travel. You have already mastered your craft, and now it’s time to take it further, to gain even more experience and knowledge. You will have plenty of opportunities for self-improvement and growth, by both learning and working with others. With the help of the 8 of Pentacles, you can enjoy your employment and have satisfaction in a job well done. The combined forces of these two cards signify that you have the resources and support system to make big things happen.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 3 of Wands Tarot card can be interpreted as a sign of good health. It is a message to focus on taking care of ourselves and exercising. Taking time to rest and do yoga, or any other type of relaxation, will help to bring balance back into our lives. It encourages us to look after our bodies and minds, which will help us to stay physically and mentally healthy.

Finances  
The 3 of Wands Tarot card suggests financial security and stability. It can be interpreted as an indication of increased income, whether from investments or from working hard. It urges us to seek out opportunities of growth in our financial lives, such as diversifying our income sources, planning for retirement, and making wise decisions about our money.

Relationships  
The 3 of Wands Tarot card encourages team work and collaboration in our relationships. It is a reminder that our relationships should be based on trust, mutual respect, and open communication. This card suggests that we need to create a strong foundation of understanding between us and our partner, so that we can grow and expand together. We should take time to travel and explore the world together, so that we can appreciate each other’s values and perspectives. It is also a reminder to nurture our relationships, and to remember to show our appreciation for our partners.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is an indication of mastering and enjoying a craft, skill or job. It implies a commitment to focus and hard work, but also a sense of pleasure and satisfaction. In terms of health, this card suggests the need to take care of our physical, mental and spiritual wellbeing in a consistent and methodical way. Regular exercise and a healthy diet, as well as adequate rest, can be key to stay healthy and balanced.

In terms of finances, the 8 of Pentacles Tarot card can indicate financial stability and security through careful planning and hard work. It suggests working on increasing your income but also paying attention to budgeting, savings and investments.

In terms of relationships, the 8 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that commitment, dedication and hard work are needed to make meaningful connections. It can signify the need to nurture both yourself and your relationships, focusing on communication and shared interests. Learning to listen and to find ways to bring joy to those around you brings heart and purpose to the partnership.
