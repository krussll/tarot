A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 9 Of Spades card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 9 Of Spades have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 9 Of Spades mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing both the 7 and 9 of Spades indicates a turbulent journey leading to fresh opportunities.

The 7 of Spades warns of deceit and implies a triumphant outcome over some kind of deceptive force.

It reminds us to stay vigilant and persevere during challenges.

It may suggest a time of difficulty, yet the subsequent victory suggests overcoming this time of trial.

Paired with the 9 of Spades, the foreseen journey becomes more complex.

The 9 of Spades symbolizes anxiety yet also new beginnings.

This means that despite the anxious feelings and uncertainties associated with this journey, there’s hope for a fresh start, perhaps through a path you haven’t considered before.

The association with winter and the element of water in both cards further emphasizes introspection, emotional depth, and transformation.

Therefore, this combination indicates a period of enduring through deception and anxiety, yet the final result would be a victorious new beginning.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Spades and 9 Of Spades mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of the 7 of Spades card and the 9 of Spades card can give a complex but insightful view into your love life.

The 7 of Spades suggesting “deceit, victory”, may indicate that you or your partner may have been dishonest or not completely open in the way you both present yourselves in the relationship.

However, despite the hurt and mistrust that might arise from this discovery, there is a predicted victory.

This could be related to overcoming the difficulties or betrayal in the relationship.

It suggests that if the relationship is in a tough spot, both of you could pull through this awkward phase successfully if there’s an open dialogue and honesty.

On the other hand, the 9 of Spades entails the concept of ‘anxiety, new beginnings’.

This suggests that the relationship might be moving towards a fresh path or that a significant change is about to happen.

This could bring a sense of fear and unease as change isn’t always easy to accept or adapt to.

When paired with the 7 of Spades, it hints that the truth that will come to light might bring unease but will, however, pave the way for a fresh start.

It is a reminder to not let the anxiety of new beginnings hinder your progress towards a more transparent relationship.

Your love life, therefore, is on the verge of renewed honesty and understanding, though you may need to navigate through some difficult truths and changes.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 9 Of Spades mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, pulling both The 7 Of Spades and The 9 Of Spades together for questions concerning your finances and job would suggest a significant period of challenge ahead.

The 7 Of Spades suggests deception, perhaps meaning you’re not fully aware of your current financial situation or not being completely honest with yourself about your spending.

This may also relate to your job; perhaps someone is being disingenuous, or there may be hidden obstacles or set-backs lurking in the shadows.

Yet, the presence of this card also signifies victory, implying that through this turmoil, there is potential for triumph over adversity.

The 9 Of Spades accompanying it, signifies a sense of anxiety and stress, again reflecting the hard times that may be coming.

It could be an economic downturn disrupting your financial stability, or fears and worries about job security.

However, the 9 Of Spades also reinforces the idea of new beginnings.

This suggests that after facing and acknowledging the span of hardships, you may enter a fresh phase in your career or financial life.

It may be a new job opportunity, a system upgrade, or a new profitable opportunity.

The association of both these cards with the Winter indicates a necessary period of hibernation and introspection before the arrival of the rejuvenating spring.

And Water as an element, signifies emotions, intuition, healing and cleansing, leading towards a potentially purifying, albeit tough, transformation.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 9 Of Spades mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, when you receive both the 7 of Spades and the 9 of Spades, this could symbolize a foreboding message about your health.

The 7 of Spades, symbolizing ‘deceit’, may suggest that you have been ignoring or misunderstanding signs and symptoms of a potential health issue.

The card’s association with ‘victory’ however, suggests that you could overcome these issues if only you confront them directly and take the necessary actions.

Its connection to winter and water might indicate that the issues may be related to the colder season or your emotional wellness – perhaps you’re not taking care as much as you should during stressful periods or emotionally draining situations.

On the other hand, the 9 of Spades deals with ‘anxiety’ and ‘new beginnings.’ The presence of this card in the same reading could indicate that your worries about your health might be causing unnecessary stress, which in itself can be detrimental to your overall health.

However, the ‘new beginnings’ aspect offers a glimmer of hope.

It suggests that while you may be undergoing a challenging time health-wise, there is a potential for a fresh start, essentially indicating that it’s never too late to start afresh with better health practices.

As this card is also associated with winter and the element of water, it emphasizes the importance of emotional health and how it can impact your physical well-being.

The combined effect of both cards encourages you to face your fears, take decisive action, and embark on a journey of better health and wellness.
