A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The 6 Of Pentacles and The 3 Of Swords tarot card represent a struggle between generosity and loss. On one hand, the 6 of Pentacles card stands for giving and receiving help and generosity. This suggests that you are in the midst of a difficult situation, but you have an opportunity to seek or provide assistance. On the other hand, The 3 Of Swords card symbolizes heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression, and surgery. This suggests that the situation may be deeply saddening, and it can be a challenging time for you. The combination of these two cards shows that you have a choice to make: are you going to give and receive help, or are you going to succumb to the pain and sorrow? Ultimately, both cards call for a sense of balance in order to make it through this difficult time.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with giving and receiving help, generosity, philanthropy and balance. Two figures are depicted on the card, one with a wealth of coins, the other holding out a begging bowl. Beyond its literal meaning, the 6 of Pentacles can also represent a need or desire to evenly distribute resources.

When it comes to health, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card can mean helping others with healing, whether it be through providing resources, knowledge or physical means. On a personal health level, it can signify reaching out and asking for assistance to improve health, or to recognize that others need and can benefit from assistance.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Pentacles encourages us to think about our possessions and whether or not we are responsible with our wealth or over-spending. It can also be a sign to be generous and share our financial resources with friends and family or to donate to a cause that is close to our heart.

In relationships, the 6 of Pentacles encourages us to think about our obligations to others. It may represent the importance of supporting our partner through hard times, or the need to ask for help from our partner when needed. The card also indicates that generosity and balance must be there for a relationship to succeed. Just as with health and finances, it is important to know when to give and when to receive in a relationship.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 3 of Swords Tarot card suggests that one’s physical health is in a state of distress. It is a sign that the individual is going through a period of physical pain, illness, or surgery. It could suggest a recent diagnosis that has left the person feeling emotionally devastated. This card can signal a tumultuous time in one’s health and wellbeing, and signifies a need to focus on self-care and healing while dealing with the physical and emotional pain.

Finances: The 3 of Swords Tarot card is a warning sign of financial loss. It is a time of hardship and can indicate that the person is at a financial low. It can suggest that investments have gone wrong, bills have gone unpaid, or income has been lost due to a setback or illness. It is important that during this period of financial difficulty, the individual take extra care to budget, save and make wise financial decisions.

Relationships: The 3 of Swords Tarot card is an indication of heartache and pain in relationships. It can suggest that the bond between partners or friends is being strained, or that a divorce or break-up is imminent. It can also suggest a period of emotional vulnerability and loneliness, as well as feelings of betrayal and distrust. During this time, it is important for the individual to reach out for support, as well as to take the time to process the pain and heal.
