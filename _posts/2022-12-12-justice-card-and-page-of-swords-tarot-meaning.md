A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Justice card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Justice tarot card, combined with the Page Of Swords tarot card, speaks to me of the importance of truth and fair dealings. Together, they suggest that any situation, no matter how complex, can be resolved in a way that is fair for everyone involved. There is a need to pay attention to the small details, such as spies and gossip, but to also not be influenced by them and to instead seek the true facts and the best resolution for all. Ultimately, the truth will come out and the outcome should be a win-win for all sides.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It’s a warning to take everything into consideration before making a choice and not to act impulsively. To make sure that both parties are heard in an unbiased way and that a suitable solution is derived that is beneficial to all involved.

This could also mean that a lie you have been told is revealed, or you may come to realize the truth of a situation. Expect to face some difficult and uncomfortable truths as you investigate further. It could be that your friend is snooping in your business or you have a tendency to meddle in other people’s personal affairs. Whatever the case may be, the truth will be revealed and the consequences of this information must be faced.

The Justice tarot card and the Page of Swords indicate that it is wise to consider the long-term implications of any action you take. Make sure that you are taking reasonable steps to ensure a win-win situation for everyone involved. If you take this route, you can hope for a peaceful resolution. It will be worth the effort as it could save you from many difficult confrontations or misunderstandings down the line. Additionally, it will give you a sense of satisfaction knowing you are being honest and fair.

Your job prospects may be uncertain right now, but as long as you remain diligent you’ll eventually be able to benefit from the situation.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Page Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health:  
The Justice Tarot card meaning for health is to help promote balance in both the body and mind. To achieve a balance of health, one must make sensible choices and address any underlying issues or conflicts. This card also suggests that doing what is fair and just is essential for maintaining health, both physically and mentally. In a health context, Justice encourages us to think about our decisions regarding health and wellness, and act accordingly. Ultimately, Justice implies that we should be supporting a system of true and fair health care, where everyone has access to quality medical care and attention.

Finances:  
The Justice Tarot card encourages us to have a balanced approach to our finances. It suggests that we should be thoughtful and fair when it comes to our money matters, and ensure that our decisions are both wise and just. This card advocates for living within our means, as well as being responsible with our spending and investments. Justice implies that any debts should be handled responsibly, and not taken lightly. Money is an important resource, so it is important to be committed to being honest, fair and balanced with our finances.

Relationships:  
The Justice Tarot card implies that we should strive for more balance, fairness and equality in our relationships. This card encourages us to examine our relationships to ensure that they align with our own moral standards. There should be a focus on fairness, respect and open communication when it comes to relationships. Transparency, trust and honesty should be the foundation of any relationship. Additionally, this card suggests that any disputes should be solved with reason and thoughtfulness, rather than with harsh and rash decisions. Ultimately, the Justice Tarot card encourages us to strive for a win-win solution that is fair to both parties.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card encourages us to take a good look at the physical health of ourselves and those we care about. This card can be a call to look for small signs of any health issues before they become serious. It could be a reminder to pay attention to our mental and emotional health, as well. It can also be a sign that we should be careful what we eat and drink, and to be aware of any possible health risks in our environment.

Finances:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card often suggests issues related to money, power and control. This card might be associated with a warning to be careful with finances, as it can symbolize the misuse of money and resources. It might also be a sign to double-check any documents related to finances or contracts, as this card is associated with being guarded and vigilant.

Relationships:  
When it comes to relationships, The Page of Swords Tarot card can be a sign that gossip or secrets are present in our relationships. It can also be a reminder to be honest and to be open to hearing the truth even if it’s difficult. This card can also suggest that there is something we may need to uncover or be aware of in our relationships, such as someone spying or watching us. Ultimately, this card suggests that we should allow room for honest communication and be willing to face possible difficult conversations.
