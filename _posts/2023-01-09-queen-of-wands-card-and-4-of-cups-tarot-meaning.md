A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen of Wands tarot card and the 4 of Cups tarot card together can tell us that you have the confidence and self-assurance to take on any challenge that comes your way, but you may currently be feeling unfulfilled and apathetic. Take some time to give yourself a break, turn away from the mundane and disconnect from your everyday life. You could be in for a surprise in the form of a new opportunity or gift if you take the time to pause and appreciate the present.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

Health:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card symbolizes a powerful and feisty energy, which can mean a lot when it comes to health. This individual is a natural leader, and their enthusiasm and confidence in their health decisions will no doubt be contagious and a source of motivation to those around them. This individual knows the importance of taking care of their body, and they take the time to nourish their body with healthy food and drinks, take part in regular physical activity, and recognize when to take necessary breaks and allow themselves some much-needed rest. They understand that their body is their temple, and that a healthy body = healthy mind.

Finances:

When it comes to finances, The Queen of Wands Tarot card signifies that the individual is a natural leader and confident in their ability to provide for themselves. This person takes calculated risks and doesn’t shy away from investing in sound investments. They’re not afraid to put in the hard work to achieve success for their future and their family’s. They understand the importance of a solid budget and often make wise decisions when it comes to spending. They’re not afraid to ask for help when needed, but they ultimately take full responsibility when it comes to their finances.

Relationships:

The Queen of Wands Tarot card indicates that the individual is confident and feisty in their relationships. This individual is a natural leader and their enthusiasm and passion for life is infectious. They know how to make their presence felt and inspire those around them. They’re passionate about the relationships in their life and work hard to ensure that these connections are healthy and long-lasting. They’re not afraid to ask for help when needed and are willing to make compromises for the sake of the relationship.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 4 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a lack of purpose or feeling that you are stuck in a rut when it comes to your health. You may be feeling apathetic and unenthusiastic about making healthy changes in your life. There is a sense of being stagnant and immobile, either not wanting to make the effort required for making positive health choices or feeling too unmotivated to do so. This card can indicate that you are complacent with the state of your health, so the advice is to take stock of the situation and make a conscious decision to put more effort into taking care of yourself.

Finances:

The 4 of Cups Tarot card can signify a feeling of stagnation when it comes to finances. You may be feeling stuck in a rut, unable to make progress in improving your financial situation. You may be feeling uninspired and unmotivated to take the steps necessary to improve your financial health. It can also indicate some complacency and apathy when it comes to your finances, so the advice is to be more aware of your situation and make the necessary changes to get yourself back on track. Additionally, it also reflects surprise gifts, perhaps one that helps you out of a financial bind or allows you to make a much-needed purchase.

Relationships:

The 4 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a feeling of apathy and emptiness when it comes to relationships. You may be in a relationship that seems unfulfilling and you may feel too unmotivated to make it better. It can also indicate that you may be complacent with the state of your relationships, so it’s important to take a step back and assess whether the relationship is worth your time and effort. Additionally, this card can also signal surprise gifts from a loved one, either in the form of something material or in the form of the time and attention they give you, which could be just what you need to help you feel more fulfilled.
