However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and The Devil card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and The Devil have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and The Devil mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The Devil tarot card together symbolize the struggle between following traditions and expectations and indulging in addictive and materialistic behaviours. It reminds us of the need to keep the balance and be conscious of our actions and our environment. While The Hierophant urges us to behave in accordance with the norm, The Devil tempts us with false promises of satisfaction and gratification. It is important to find a way to satisfy our needs, wants and desires without succumbing to our darker impulses or breaking the law or any of our cherished traditions.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

If left unchecked, the excessive materialism and worldly possessions, as well as the sexual temptations and desires, can create a negative dynamic in your relationship, gradually destroying your love and connection.

The presence of The Hierophant tarot card indicates that you, or your partner, may be subconsciously following traditional expectations or cultural norms which no longer serve your relationship. This could range from following the accepted relationship expectations of the past, to placing too much value on the material possessions and outward appearances of your relationship. These expectations may be pushing against the natural flow of your relationship, and may be having a negative impact on your connection.

The Devil tarot card, however, presents a reminder that, at its core, a relationship is also about our own hidden, darker desires. In a healthy relationship, these desires can be expressed and fulfilled in a way that enhances and enriches your connection. This tarot card encourages us to explore the darker side of ourselves and our relationships, without allowing it to control the relationship.

By recognizing the power and impact of both the traditional expectations of your relationship and the darker desires of the heart, you can begin to consider ways of making your relationship work in a way that works for both partners. If you allow yourself to find a balance that is suitable and comfortable for both of you, you can ensure that your relationship is one of mutual respect and understanding.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card is associated with a person’s need to control their own destiny and power. It can represent the dark side of human nature, and the seductive pull of materialism, addiction and obsession. It is a warning against being too attached to things and relationships, as this can lead to a lack of personal strength and autonomy.

When it comes to health, the Devil card can mean unchecked addiction to unhealthy habits, such as smoking, drinking, and drugs. It may also suggest an inability to stick with a healthy lifestyle, or a tendency to ignore symptoms of poor health. People should be aware of their own inner demons and strive to stay away from anything that could lead them down a path of destruction.

Financially, the Devil card can represent greed, materialism and selfishness. It can represent a temptation to spend beyond one’s means, or to make decisions based solely on one’s own self-interest. It can mean an obsession with accumulating wealth without regard for the consequences. People should strive to maintain balance in their financial lives and avoid making decisions out of pure greed.

In relationships, the Devil card can represent the power struggle between two people, as well as the dark side of human nature. It suggests a need for one person to be in control, and a temptation to treat the other person as an object or pawn. It can also mean an unhealthy dependency on another person, or a tendency to become too emotionally involved in a relationship. People should strive for balance in relationships and avoid manipulation or unhealthy control.
