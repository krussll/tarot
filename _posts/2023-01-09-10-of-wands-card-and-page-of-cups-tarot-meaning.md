However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 10 Of Wands card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

At the same time, the Page of Cups may be a sign for you to take a break and look for some creative outlets that can help balance out the burdens. It can also be a reminder to open yourself up to new forms of love and altruism, such as volunteering. Even though you may feel like you have too much on your plate right now, remember that a little bit of self-love and kindness can go a long way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes stress, exhaustion and too many burdens. In terms of health, this card can be an indicator of physical, mental or emotional exhaustion. It is often an indication of overextending yourself and taking on too many tasks or responsibilities. It may be time to take a break, ask for help, or reduce your commitments. This card can also point to a lack of balance between work, rest and leisure.

When it comes to finances, The 10 of Wands card can be an indication of having too much debt, feeling burdened by financial obligations or carrying too much risk. This card can suggest that it is time to reduce or prioritize your expenses, create a budget or enlist professional help to get your finances in order.

In terms of relationships, The 10 of Wands card can suggest feeling overwhelmed by stress, taking on too much or feeling burdened by the responsibilities of a relationship. It may suggest re-examining where boundaries are needed and learning to be comfortable with saying no. This card can also point to a need for balance between giving and receiving within a relationship.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health: The Page Of Cups Tarot card can represent a time of healing and recovery. This is a period where you can focus on your well-being and physical health. This can be a very important step in getting yourself back on the right track. Whether it’s taking up yoga, experimenting with healthy foods or seeking out alternative therapies, the Page Of Cups Tarot card encourages us to explore the different paths of natural healing.

Finances: The Page Of Cups Tarot card encourages us to put our finances back in order. It could be a great time to start saving money, paying off debts and taking a more responsible approach to personal finance. It also speaks of being able to make wise investments and not to be afraid to take risks in order to get ahead financially. This can also be a time to look at our spending habits and make sure that we’re not losing money unnecessarily.

Relationships: The Page Of Cups Tarot card speaks of a time of new love and starting fresh in a relationship. It can be the beginning of a new romance or the rekindling of an old one. This card also speaks of being open to new people and opportunities in life. If we’re open and willing to look outside of our normal circle of friends, we can find someone new and exciting to become involved with. This is also a great time to strengthen bonds with existing friends and family.
