Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The Wheel Of Fortune card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Wheel Of Fortune, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Wheel Of Fortune.

## The Wheel Of Fortune And Sprituality – An Overview

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card symbolizes good fortune and luck, making it a positive card when it comes to health.

It is a sign of hope and encouragement, suggesting that a turning point is coming and that positive changes are imminent.

The card can be a source of motivation to take risks, such as trying new treatments, seeking out different therapies, or simply making lifestyle changes that can lead to improved health and wellbeing.

In terms of physical health, the card is a reminder that no matter what our current state, there is always the potential for improvement.

The Wheel Of Fortune encourages us to trust our instincts and take the necessary steps to bring about positive change and create the health we desire.

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card indicates that I am about to experience a significant turning point in my journey towards spiritual growth.

## Reversed Meanings For The Wheel Of Fortune And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

The reversed Wheel of Fortune tarot card indicates a time of reversal and bad luck, where things may suddenly take a turn for the worse.

It can indicate that you are feeling stuck in a particular area of your life, such as your spiritual journey.

You may feel like you have been making the same old mistakes, and that you are in a rut and unable to move forward.

It is important to remember that this card is also a sign of change, so it is possible that the change you seek will come soon.

The reversed Wheel of Fortune can also be a sign that you could be feeling overwhelmed by the various decisions and choices you are faced with in your spiritual journey.

There are so many paths that you could take, and so many different directions, that it can be hard to decide which one is right for you.

It is important to take the time to reflect on your options, and to think about how each one can contribute to your spiritual growth and development.

The reversed Wheel of Fortune can also be a sign that you may have been too focused on the material side of life, and have neglected your spiritual side.

Now is the time for you to reconnect with your spirituality and to remember why it is important to you.

This could involve dedicating more time to meditation, prayer or any other spiritual practice that you find helpful.

Taking the time to nourish your spirit can help to bring about a greater sense of balance and harmony in your life.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Wheel of Fortune card appears in the reversed position, it can signal a period of bad luck and a lack of spiritual fulfillment.

You may have recently experienced a spiritual setback or a loss of faith in the spiritual realm.

The reversed Wheel of Fortune can also suggest that you are stuck in a repetitive cycle of bad luck and negative experiences.

You may be feeling confused and overwhelmed by the events that have taken place in your life and are unable to get out of the rut you have found yourself in.

You may be struggling to find the energy and motivation to continue on your spiritual journey.

If the Wheel of Fortune appears in the reversed position, it is important to take a step back and reassess your spiritual goals.
