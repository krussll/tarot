## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This card indicates that you are in control of your own health and that you have an inner strength to cope with whatever comes at you.

Finances: The 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes a period of prosperity and financial gain. You may have recently lucked out on an investment, or you have a steady stream of income coming in. Whatever the case, you can rest assured that the money you have coming in is stable and will continue to bring you a sense of security. This card is a reminder to not overspend and to be smart with your money.

Relationships: The 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes relationships filled with harmony and peace. You may have recently taken steps to improve a relationship or celebrated a milestone with someone you care about. This card indicates that all of your hard work and efforts are paying off, and you are now in a strong, solid relationship. You can look forward to a healthy, long-term relationship with the right person.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

It advises that one focuses on personal health and fitness, and to make decisions based on what is best for their body and mind. The Queen of Swords may suggest that it is important for one to be open and honest with themselves, and to be realistic in their expectations and goals. There may be times when one must be firm in their decision-making, and to make choices based on what is best for their long-term health and happiness.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card suggests that one must be self-sufficient and independent when it comes to financial matters. It suggests that one should take responsibility for their finances, and to make wise decisions. The Queen of Swords warns that one should be careful not to be too frugal, as it can lead to feelings of insecurity and lack of abundance. However, it also encourages one to remain realistic and honest in their judgments and spending habits.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages individuals to strive for honesty and open communication in their relationships. It suggests that one should be honest and direct in their communication, but also respect the other person’s boundaries. There may be times when the Queen of Swords warns of cutting ties with people who are causing harm, or who are not bringing positive energy into a relationship. However, it also emphasizes that relationships should nurture both individuals involved and be based on respect and understanding.
