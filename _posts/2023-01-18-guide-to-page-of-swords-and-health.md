Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Page Of Swords card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Page Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Page Of Swords.

## Page Of Swords And Health – An Overview

The Page Of Swords tarot card symbolizes having a healthy lifestyle that is based on gathering knowledge and understanding.

This can manifest in finding out more information about nutrition, exercise, and other aspects of health and wellness.

Additionally, this card can refer to the importance of being aware of how our words and decisions affect our health, as well as the health of those around us.

The Page Of Swords also encourages being conscious of our environment and the consequences of our actions.

It is a reminder that we should not only be mindful of our own health, but also of the health of those around us.

## Upright Meanings For Page Of Swords And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Page Of Swords tarot card is a reminder that health is an important part of life, and one should be aware of the consequences of their actions.

It implies that one should be honest with themselves and others and take the time to understand what their health means to them, as well as the truth behind the facts and stories they hear.

It speaks to the need to be mindful of gossip, prying eyes, and spies in order to protect one’s health.

The Page Of Swords encourages one to be an active participant in their own health and to be honest with themselves and others in order to make the best decisions for their wellbeing.

### What It Means For The Future

The Page Of Swords tarot card is indicative of a need to be mindful of your health in the near future.

This card suggests that it is time to be more watchful and aware of the signals your body is sending you.

It could mean that you should be listening to and paying attention to what your body is telling you – such as fatigue, stress, or other health issues – and take steps to address them.

It also means that it would be wise to pay attention to your environment and the people you interact with, as gossip or prying eyes could be a warning sign that something is not quite right.

By taking the time to be mindful of your health, you can ensure that you will be in the best possible condition to face the future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Page of Swords tarot card can be a reminder to stay aware of the health of those close to me.

It could be a sign to pay attention to gossip and rumors, and to consider the truth of what is being said.

It could also be a sign to be mindful of any prying eyes, or spies in my close circle of family and friends.

It could mean that I should be more vigilant in staying up-to-date on the health of those I care about and to be more open to conversations about their health.

It is a reminder to be curious and to make sure I am always up to date on the overall health of those closest to me.

## Reversed Meanings For Page Of Swords And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed Page of Swords tarot card is associated with health in a number of ways.

It can suggest that an individual is feeling stuck in an unhealthy situation, either mental or physical.

This could be due to a lack of communication, a lack of understanding, or a fear of speaking up.

It can also indicate that the person is feeling overwhelmed, not sure where to turn, or is struggling to make decisions.

In terms of physical health, it can signify that the person feels powerless or stuck in a certain health condition, or is facing challenges with their health that they can’t seem to overcome.

Reversed The Page of Swords can also suggest that the individual is not sure how to take care of their health, or is not paying attention to the warning signs that their body is giving them.

It can also point to a person who is too trusting of their own judgement, and is ignoring the advice of a medical professional or health care provider.

It is important to remember that the reversed Page of Swords is a warning that the person needs to pay attention to their health, and seek help if needed.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Page of Swords is often a sign that you should pay closer attention to your physical health.

It may be time to take a step back and assess the state of your wellbeing, and make sure that you are taking proper care of yourself.

It is likely that you have been ignoring small health issues and it is time to address them.

If you have been feeling run down, it could be a sign that something more serious is afoot and it needs to be checked out.

The reversed Page of Swords also speaks to mental clarity, and can be a sign that you should take some time to clear your mind and focus on understanding your thoughts and feelings.

This could be achieved through meditation, journaling, or talking to someone you trust.

It is important to take time to analyze your emotions, so you can make decisions that are beneficial to your wellbeing and accurately understand your own feelings.

Taking the time to nurture your mental health can have a significant impact on your physical health too.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Page of Swords is reversed, it can indicate a lack of energy and determination when it comes to health.

The figure in the card is no longer standing upright and determined, instead, they are slumped and almost cowering.

This card is a warning that you need to be more proactive in taking care of your health and make sure you do not become complacent with your current state of health.

It can also indicate a lack of knowledge or understanding when it comes to health matters.

It can mean that you are overlooking important health issues or not researching and educating yourself enough.

The reversed Page of Swords could be a warning to ask more questions, be more proactive and to take a more analytical approach to your health.

It is also possible that you may be dealing with gossip or negative comments about your health that are preventing you from taking action or making positive changes.

Do not let these external influences stop you from taking ownership of your health and getting the help you need.

The Page of Swords reversed is a reminder to be open-minded, seek the truth and take proactive steps to maintain and improve your health.
