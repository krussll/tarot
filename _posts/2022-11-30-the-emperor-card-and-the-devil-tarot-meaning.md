A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Emperor card and The Devil card together in particular.

The The Emperor card and The Devil have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Emperor and The Devil mean together?

The Emperor tarot card and The Devil tarot card together speak to the need to find balance in life. The Emperor encourages you to use respect, leadership and strategic planning to stay on the path of light, while The Devil serves as a reminder of the dark forces at play, such as addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession and sexual lust. While it is important to strive to reach your goals and achieve success, it is also necessary to avoid falling into the tempting traps of excess and indulgence. The Emperor and the Devil will help you find the balance you need to stay on the right track.

At the same time, it is important to remember that when The Emperor and The Devil tarot cards come together, they also signify passion, intensity and desire. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Emperor mean?

The Emperor Tarot card is a symbol of respect and leadership. This card often appears in readings when guidance and authority are needed to move forward in a positive direction. In regards to health, the Emperor stands for proven strategies and the ability to manage one’s own health decisions and outcomes. With this card, one is encouraged to take charge of their own health and understand their own limitations. This could include seeking professional medical advice if necessary, doing research on medical options, and making decisions with confidence.

The Emperor Tarot card is also associated with practical strategy and planning in regards to finances. This card can signify self-discipline and financial responsibility. The message of this card encourages individuals to stay on top of their finances and be mindful of their expenses. Money should be invested wisely and there should be a plan of action in place to reach financial stability.

The Emperor Tarot card also speaks of leadership and respect in relationships. This card emphasizes the need to set boundaries and be assertive in all interactions. Taking charge of the situation while being mindful of the other person’s feelings is essential. This card promotes a healthy balance of giving and taking within the relationship. Respect is key, and it is important to remember that both parties need to feel appreciated.

Overall, the Emperor Tarot card is all about respecting yourself and others, leading, and having a plan of action. This card promises success when one takes charge of their health, finances, and relationships.

## What does The Devil mean?

Health  
The Devil Tarot card is associated with physical and mental health issues. When The Devil card is drawn, it can be a warning that negative health habits, such as substance abuse, eating disorders and self-harm, may be present. It is also a reminder to take personal responsibility for one’s actions and pay attention to the body’s natural needs and seeing a doctor if necessary.

Finances  
The Devil Tarot card can indicate a person is caught in a cycle of debt due to materialism and greed. It is a warning to take control and make conscious changes to maintain financial stability. Rather than spending frivolously, it is important to focus on budgeting and saving. When The Devil card is drawn, it can also mean that a person is too focused on career success – sacrificing relationships, free time, and emotional wellbeing in an effort to build wealth.

Relationships  
The Devil Tarot card warns of unhealthy attachments and relationships built on codependency. It can indicate obsession or emotional manipulation, or an inability to break free of a destructive union. The card is a reminder to examine the motivations for who you surround yourself with, and to make sure those relationships are based on trust and equality. The Devil card can also suggest that a person is avoiding true intimacy, due to fear, guilt, or low self-worth, and to take steps to heal those issues in order to foster strong connections.
