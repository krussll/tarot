When it comes to tarot cards, many people have heard that your very first deck should be gifted to you; in other words, it should not be a deck you purchased yourself. Why?

**You can buy yourself your very first set of tarot cards. It was believed at some point that if you received a gifted deck of tarot cards, the karma would be better and the deck would more easily “speak” to you so that you can learn it better. To be honest, most tarot card readers have debunked this theory as a myth.**

## Just a Myth

Does this mean that you cannot use a tarot card deck that has been gifted to you? Of course not, but if you’re interested in learning how to read tarot cards and you’re waiting for someone to give you a deck as a gift first, you might be waiting a long time to learn.

In fact, tarot cards are so personal to their readers that it may actually be better for you to purchase your first deck yourself.

While tarot cards have the same basic cards in each deck, their designs can be different, so in a way, it’s good to find a deck that speaks to you and draws you near them.

Regardless of which deck of tarot cards you end up choosing, you’ll want to look at three things:  
● The deck itself. Does it resonate with you? Do you love the design of each card? Can  
you picture yourself working with that particular deck?  
● A guidebook. Most tarot decks have a guidebook, and even when you get used to  
what each card means, a guidebook is still a great thing to have. Even when you  
improve your skills as a tarot card reader, you’re still likely to refer back to this book  
time and time again.  
● A box or cover. Tarot cards should never be left out in the open. They need to be  
kept in a good sturdy box or a bag made specially for these types of cards. Make  
sure the box they come in is strong enough to keep them safe for a very long time.

## Other Considerations

When it comes to choosing the right deck of tarot cards, you should definitely judge a book by its cover, so to speak. If you don’t like the artwork and design that you see on the cards, you won’t like working with them, either.

It’s also a huge benefit if you can hold the cards before you buy them to see how they feel. This is helpful because it will help you determine how the cards feel in your hands, how easy they are to shuffle, and of course, how much you  
love the designs on the different cards.

You also need to pick a tarot card deck that fits your learning style. There are numerous decks that are perfect for beginning tarot card readers, including the Thoth deck and the Rider-Waite-Smith deck.

To be sure, some designs are merely easier to learn with than others. Of course, if you want a deck that is more open to interpretation and one that you can personalize and make your own, try the Starchild deck or the Wild Unknown deck.  
Of course, there are other things to consider as well.

### Study the Design of Each Card in the Deck

If you’re shopping online, go through each card if at all possible. If you’re visiting a store that sells tarot cards, look at the ones on display so that you can get a feel for what the cards look like.

The more cards you view, the better you’ll feel about the deck you purchase. The cards’ aesthetic appeal, in other words, is extremely important because if you don’t find a design you fall in love with, you won’t enjoy using them nearly as much.

### Look at the Quality of the Cards

Tarot cards need to be thick, and the box they come in should be strong and difficult to bend or damage. The more you use your cards, the more important this feature becomes. Over time, tarot cards can bend and even rip or tear, rendering them useless in many instances.

Always check to make sure the cards are made out of high-quality materials that won’t become damaged after just a few uses. If you shop online, the product description should tell you what the cards are made of and how strong they are.

### Decide If You Want Traditional or Modern Cards

These days, tarot cards can follow just about any “theme” you can think of, including angels, fairies, lovers, historical figures, gods and goddesses, and even some characters from contemporary films and comic books.

In fact, whatever your interests are, it’s very likely you’ll be able to find a tarot card deck with that particular design on it. Once you decide exactly what you want, shopping for the perfect deck of tarot cards is a lot easier.

### Decide Exactly How You’ll Be Using the Cards

To decide what type of deck to get, consider how you’ll be using the cards. If you are shooting for a peaceful, calming way to provide readings to people, you can choose a deck of cards that centers on Zen.

Earth-based interests can be satisfied with a deck based on Druidism, while you can buy decks of gods and goddesses to appease the goddess inside of all of us. It’s easier than you think to find the deck of cards that is personalized to your interests and tastes, whether you shop for them in person or through an online store.

## Conclusion

You certainly don’t have to wait until you’re gifted a deck of tarot cards to get started being a reader. In fact, it may take a long time to get started if that’s what you’re waiting for.

While there is certainly nothing wrong with using a deck that someone gave you as a gift, your readings will go just as well if you buy the deck yourself. Just decide what you want and do your due diligence before you buy a deck, and you’ll be happy with the results.
