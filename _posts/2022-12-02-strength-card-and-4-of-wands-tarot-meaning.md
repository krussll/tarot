A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 4 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Strength card and 4 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 4 Of Wands mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The 4 Of Wands tarot card combined embody the idea of coming home to yourself with courage and self-love, allowing for an internal reunion that celebrates who you are as a person and what you have achieved. Unconditional love and self-respect are the keys to embracing all of your personal power and unlocking your inner strength. Through this recognition and appreciation, you will be able to celebrate reunions with both family and friends, recognizing the importance of the relationships in your life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Strength and 4 Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Strength and 4 Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is indicative of a strong sense of self-love and self-respect. This card suggests that a person’s inner strength will carry them through any situation. It symbolizes a person’s courage to face adversity and stay true to their convictions.

For health, Strength suggests that a person has the resources and courage to take care of their physical wellbeing. It encourages them to trust their intuition and act in their best interests. It can suggest focus on healthy habits such as eating well, getting adequate rest, exercising, and taking time for self-care.

For finances, Strength encourages a person to use their personal willpower and resources to stay on track with their financial goals. It suggests being brave in the face of any financial setbacks, and having faith in one’s ability to make sound decisions that will improve their financial situation.

In relationships, Strength suggests trusting one’s intuition and having the courage to be honest with themselves and with their partner. It encourages a person to recognize their own worth and practice self-love, so that they can give their all to their relationship. This card encourages respect and mutual understanding between partners and shows that a person’s inner strength can be the foundation of any successful relationship.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 4 Of Wands Tarot card suggests that health is something to be celebrated and shared. It is a sign of having a strong and balanced foundation that promotes a healthy lifestyle. This card can encourage us to take time to nurture our body and mind, and to create a network of support with family and friends. It is a reminder to be mindful of what we are consuming, both physically and emotionally.

Finances: The 4 Of Wands Tarot card is a sign of financial stability and security. It can be a reminder to create a budget and stick to it, ensuring that we are living within our means. It can suggest that opportunities for financial growth are present, and that we should focus on long-term investment for sustainable growth.

Relationships: The 4 Of Wands Tarot card is a sign of strong and healthy relationships. Family, friends, and partners can be celebrated and their relationships nurtured. It can suggest that communication is key and that honesty and understanding are paramount. It can also encourage us to share our joys and sorrows equally with those that are closest to us.
