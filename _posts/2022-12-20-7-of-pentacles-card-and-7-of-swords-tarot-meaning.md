A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together?

When the 7 of Pentacles and the 7 of Swords are combined, it often reflects a need to put in extra effort in order to reap the rewards. The focus is on having patience, as well as hard work in order to achieve success. However, there may be some betrayal or dishonesty present which could slow down the process. It is important to remain aware and take precautions to protect yourself from any potential theft or unseen enemies. In the end, all your commitment and dedication will pay off if you remain patient and persistent.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When the 7 of Pentacles and the 7 of Swords come together in a reading related to love, it suggests that your relationship is in peril due to dishonest or untrustworthy behavior. Whether it’s your partner who is acting in a deceitful manner, or if it’s you who is not being forthcoming, the combination of these two cards is a sign that something shady is happening in your relationship.

The 7 of Pentacles encourages patience and hard work in this situation. Rather than fleeing the situation or taking drastic steps to confront the issues, take your time and be mindful of the consequences of your words and actions. If you’re on the receiving end of the psychological warfare, it’s important to practice patience in an effort to resolve the situation.

On the other hand, the 7 of Swords is a stark reminder to keep an eye on the people around you, and to be mindful of who you are trusting. Because of this card’s influence, it’s important to be aware of the possibility of betrayal within your relationship. It’s best to take the time to assess the situation fully before making drastic decisions or jumping to conclusions.

Ultimately, the 7 of Pentacles and the 7 of Swords serve as a reminder that there is always a risk of deception in any relationship. It’s important to remain patient and up front about what’s bothering you, but also to be aware of any untrustworthy behavior from either side. With the right amount of caution and patience, it’s possible to find solutions to the issues in your relationship.

The 7 Of Pentacles advises patience, hard work, and delayed success, but with The 7 Of Swords, these rewards may be stolen away in a very literal sense. This is a warning to be careful of who you trust and what financial arrangements you make.

If you’ve invested time, energy, and resources in a project, the message of The 7 Of Swords is a reminder to be mindful of the people around you and the potential for them to take advantage of your efforts. This is an especially important reminder to be aware of your own vulnerability in situations where money and power are involved.

On the other hand, The 7 Of Pentacles reminds you that you have the strength and knowledge to work hard even if you don’t receive immediate gratification. The tarot card indicates that if you can remain patient and continue putting in the effort, you will eventually find success.

The two tarot cards taken together can be read as a warning to be vigilant about protecting your investments and your efforts, but also to remain dedicated and to not give up when it feels like it’s taking forever to achieve your desired results. In terms of your finances and job, The 7 Of Pentacles and The 7 Of Swords mean that if you remain patient and keep working, you will eventually see the rewards you deserve.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that patience is needed when it comes to both health and finances. The card suggests that it is important to take the time to understand our bodies, minds and souls and to recognize any red flags to reduce the risk of illness. It encourages us to create a plan of action and stick to it instead of trying multiple measures at once and getting overwhelmed. Health goals are often long-term processes that require patience and resilience.

The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card also emphasizes the need for patience and hard work in order to achieve financial success. It suggests that a slow and steady approach to finances is the best way to reach one’s financial goals. It encourages people to save and plan for the future, and to resist the impulse to spend on short-term pleasures. Investing wisely, diversifying one’s investments, and keeping long-term goals in mind are key to achieving financial stability.

The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card also has relevance for our relationships. The card encourages us to give relationships time and energy in order to reap the rewards of strong, lasting connections. It suggests that patience and trust are important components to any successful relationship. It is important to recognize that relationships, like health and finances, require effort and dedication. The rewards of a strong and healthy relationship are worth the effort.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card often refers to theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and running away, but it can have various meanings depending on the context. When it comes to physical health, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can suggest a sense of self-sabotage. In other words, you are taking actions that either directly or indirectly harm your own health. This could be anything from smoking cigarettes, to indulging in junk food, to working too much without taking time to rest. These behaviors not only prevent you from achieving your health and fitness goals, but can actually do more harm than good in the long run.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can suggest a lack of trust and security. You may be overspending, or taking risks with your hard-earned money that you know deep down won’t pay off in the long run. You may also be relying too much on credit or loans to get you by, leaving you feeling in debt and completely out of control.

In the realm of relationships, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can suggest disloyalty, dishonesty, and betrayal. You may be in a relationship that you know isn’t healthy or stable, or you may be taking risks like flirting with someone else or engaging in secretive behaviors like lying or cheating in an effort to get what you want, regardless of the consequences. In either case, it is important to acknowledge that your actions have the potential to do serious damage to your relationships and should be avoided at all costs.

The 7 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning for all of us to remain mindful about our behavior in all aspects of life. Especially when it comes to our health, finances, and relationships, it is important to practice integrity, trust, and honesty in order to ensure that we are taking care of ourselves and the people we love.
