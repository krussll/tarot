A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The 7 of Pentacles and the 10 of Swords suggest a situation in which after much patience and hard work, progress appears to slowed or halted entirely. There might be a feeling of frustration and emptiness in knowing that while you have given your best effort and made significant progress, you are still not where you want or need to be. The 10 of Swords indicates that this rock bottom moment is also a time of complete and painful endings. There is nothing else to do but accept the situation and move forward, using this experience to better inform your future decisions.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that it takes patience and hard work to see the fruits of our labor pay off. It is a card of delayed success, reminding us that it won’t happen overnight. It symbolizes the need to keep working hard, even if it doesn’t seem like anything is coming from it.

For health, the 7 of Pentacles asks us to be patient with ourselves. It reminds us that health isn’t a quick fix, it takes dedication and consistency. It’s important to keep putting in the hard work, even when it seems like nothing is changing.

In terms of finances, the 7 of Pentacles suggests that we should be patient and diligent with our savings. It reminds us that it takes time to build our financial future, and that it’s important to keep investing and saving for the long term.

In the realm of relationships, the 7 of Pentacles can be a reminder to be patient and work hard on the relationship. It’s important to listen and be understanding, as relationships take time to grow and evolve. It’s important to recognize that relationships take effort and commitment, and that it’s necessary to put in the effort if we want to see the rewards.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 Of Swords Tarot card is a very sorrowful card, representing complete and painful endings in all aspects of our lives.

When it comes to health, the 10 Of Swords card stands for a severe physical or mental low, even a health crisis that causes one to hit rock bottom. This card can represent exhaustion and burn-out, exhaustion and physical pain, or unhealed wounds from the past that continue to haunt us. It also stands for fear, lack of faith and desperation in dealing with our health.

When it comes to finances, the 10 Of Swords card represents a devastating loss and financial ruin. This can be the loss of a job or business, or a financial setback. It can also stand for excessive debt or poor financial decisions. Furthermore, it speaks of an inability or unwillingness to pursue financial success, possibly due to trauma from past financial losses.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the 10 Of Swords card represents a complete and painful end. This could be the end of a romantic relationship, the ending of a friendship, or estrangement from family members. It implies a barren emotional landscape and a lack of trust or belief in oneself or in others. It can indicate lack of communication or understanding, and feelings of hopelessness and insecurity.
