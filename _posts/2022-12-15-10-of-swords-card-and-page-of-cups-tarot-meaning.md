A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together?

The 10 of Swords tarot card and the Page of Cups tarot card represent a complete, yet painful ending for something in your life. This could represent anything from a relationship to a job, but regardless of the situation, it is one that has reached its end. This is rock bottom and the only way left is up. The Page of Cups tarot card carries with it a message of hope, in that something new is beginning to emerge. This new beginning could manifest itself in the form of a new relationship, a volunteer opportunity, or a return to an old hobby that offers a sense of comfort and solace during this difficult time. Take heart in the fact that while this period of your life may be coming to an end, a new start is just around the corner.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

You are now seeking something more meaningful, something that will bring true joy and fulfillment into your life. The 10 of Swords and the Page of Cups in your love life indicate that it is time to move on from the pain and grief of your current relationship and open up to the possibility of new love. It is a reminder to be mindful and introspective, taking the time to learn more about yourself before committing to another relationship. It is a new beginning, a chance to start fresh and approach your love life with confidence and optimism.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 10 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords Tarot card is often seen as a card of complete and painful endings, signifying hitting rock bottom. This card can indicate a phase of exhaustion, burn out and failure that may last for some time.

For health, the 10 of Swords Tarot card can refer to a difficult period of illness or injury, a time of physical exhaustion, or a period of dangerous overwork. It may suggest that you have hit a point of no return, and that it is time to rest, even if your health is not completely restored. Both healing and taking a break are important aspects to consider.

For finances, the 10 of Swords Tarot card may refer to bankruptcy, debts and financial woes. This card can suggest that you have exhausted all of your options, and it may be time to reconsider how you manage your money. It is important to find balance between spending and saving, and to prioritize your needs over your wants.

For relationships, the 10 of Swords Tarot card may indicate a break-up or a time of deep sadness and loneliness. This card can suggest that a relationship has come to an end, and although it may be painful, it is for the best. It is important to accept these endings and to focus on the valuable lessons and experiences gained during the relationship. This will help you to move on and create healthier relationships in the future.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health: The Page Of Cups Tarot card signifies an overall sense of good health. It symbolizes a healthier way of living, with the intention to gain more energy and vitality. This card suggests a focus on diet, exercise, and self-care. It encourages people to take charge of their own health and take the necessary steps to cultivate a healthier and more balanced lifestyle, while still managing to stay positive.

Finances: The Page Of Cups Tarot card also points to financial stability. If you feel overwhelmed by finances, this card can help you focus your energy on taking the steps to gain control of your finances. It is a reminder to set your priorities and make sure that you are managing your money responsibly. This card suggests that you should focus on saving for the future, live within your means and develop a financial plan that will work for you.

Relationships: The Page Of Cups Tarot card is an indication that you should be focusing on nourishing and strengthening your relationships. This card is reminding you to take care of yourself and others by being open, compassionate and engaging with those around you. It encourages people to reach out to others and spread kindness. This card signfies new love and suggest taking the time to meet new people and start new relationships. It represents the potential for a beautiful journey of connection with others.
