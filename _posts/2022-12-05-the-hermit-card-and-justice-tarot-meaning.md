A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Justice card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Justice have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Justice mean together?

The Hermit and Justice tarot cards together speak to a journey of personal truth and spiritual illumination that leads to discovering the cause and effect of a given situation. It is a journey that requires time and effort in order to discover the justice of a situation, and is aided by looking within for clarity. As the Hermit steps away from the hustle and bustle of the outside world to look for the answers within, the Justice card reminds us that there is a way to approach each situation that is just and fair. It ultimately guides us to trusting that the truth will come out and that win-win solutions can be found.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It reminds us to seek the truth within, to remain consistent in our thoughts and behaviors, and to take ownership of our part in the relationship.

When taken together, the Hermit and Justice tarot cards offer us a balanced approach to our love life. They strengthen our capacity for self-reflection which, in turn, can help us navigate the complexities of relationships with greater clarity and understanding. In this way, the Hermit and Justice tarot cards remind us that love is not just a reflection of what we give, but is also an opportunity to learn more about who we are and discover true and lasting fulfillment.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and Justice mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and Justice the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

For Health

The Hermit Tarot card stands for self-reflection and inner wisdom; it is a sign that it is time to take a step back and look inward. Physically and mentally, this card can be an indication to take a break and put away any distractions. It is important to listen to our body, mind, and spirit and understand how we need to be taken care of in order to remain in balance. Taking a retreat or a vacation can be beneficial to our overall health when this card appears. Taking a pause and taking the time to assess our emotional and physical needs is also suggested.

For Finances

The Hermit Tarot card signifies that it is important to take a step back and reassess our finances. The card can be interpreted to mean that it is time to analyze our financial situation and gain insight on what the best steps to take in order to improve the current state. This means creating a plan of action that may involve budgeting, saving, investing or negotiating for better contracts. Taking the time to research and explore options can result in successful financial management.

For Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card emphasizes taking the time to focus on yourself and your needs. This can mean disconnecting from our current relationships and taking a moment to reflect on what we need out of our relationships and what we can do to contribute. This card can also be a sign to take a break from any tumultuous relationships or friendships and come back to them when we are ready with a clearer mindset. This can be beneficial in order to assess our relationship and create better communication, understanding and support between both people.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

When it comes to our health, the Justice Tarot card can have many implications. It can symbolise our ability to take responsibility for our own health, recognising the consequences of our actions and making conscious decisions based on the outcomes. It’s about striving for win-win solutions so that we can stay healthy without sacrificing the well-being of other individuals. The Justice Tarot encourages us to make decisions that are fair and just, and that take into account the health of all parties. It can also signify the importance of balancing our physical, mental and spiritual health.

Finances

In the realm of finance, the Justice Tarot can be interpreted as a reminder to think carefully before making financial decisions. It’s a call to ensure that the choices we make are fair, balanced and beneficial for everyone involved. It can also encourage us to stand up for what is right and just, and to speak out against any injustice or unfair practices. Furthermore, it may encourage us to take a holistic approach to our finances and think about the long-term implications of any decisions we make.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card is a powerful reminder to treat our relationships with fairness, respect and equality. It can encourage us to seek win-win solutions to conflicts and disputes, and to value the opinions of others equally. It can remind us to stay true to our own values, even when those around us may not agree. Furthermore, it can signify the importance of speaking our truth and setting clear boundaries with others. It can also be a call to seek out relationships that are healthy and balanced, and to strive for solutions that benefit everyone.
