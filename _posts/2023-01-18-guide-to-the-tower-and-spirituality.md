## The Tower And Sprituality – An Overview

The Tower tarot card is associated with negative health implications and unwanted change.

It suggests that an individual is vulnerable to upheaval and ruin, and can be a sign of imminent physical, mental, and emotional distress.

It can indicate that a person needs to take steps to protect their health and well-being, as the card may be signifying the possibility of illness or decline.

It may indicate that a person needs to take preventive actions to protect their health, such as getting regular medical check-ups, staying active, and following a balanced diet.

The Tower can also be a warning of potential violence, and may be a sign of warning of an impending danger to one’s health.

Taking the necessary precautions and protecting one’s health is the best way to avoid any negative health implications associated with the Tower tarot card.
