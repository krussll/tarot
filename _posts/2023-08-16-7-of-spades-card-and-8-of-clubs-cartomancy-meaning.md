A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Clubs card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Clubs have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Clubs mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Clubs together suggests a tumultuous journey ahead.

The 7 of Spades denotes a situation of deceit or dishonesty which may lead to victory, revealing underlying winter or water elements, suggesting a need for introspection and change that’s often associated with this season or element.

This may involve navigating through complex conversations or confronting destructive behaviors.

Further, the pairing with the 8 of Clubs indicates encounters with some hardships and struggles, but it also promises a potential for growth and power with the element of Earth symbolizing groundedness and the season of Summer representing vitality and fruition.

Together, these cards encourage you to trust your power and resilience, reveal the truth, and hold strong through the hardships, as your efforts will lead you to ultimate victory.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Clubs mean together for your love life?

In a love-related cartomancy reading, the presence of both the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Clubs together indicates a mixed bag.

The 7 of Spades draws attention to the possibility of deceit being a critical component in your relationship.

This could mean that there has been dishonesty or concealment in your love life, possibly implicating both you and your partner.

However, it also suggests an overarching theme of victory, which may simply mean emerging from a challenging situation positively.

This could involve overcoming obstacles, fighting off competitors or breaking past barriers that have been causing friction in your relationship.

As this card pertains to the Winter season, it can indicate that these issues may arise or climax during this time.

Meanwhile, the 8 of Clubs speaks to hardship and power, hinting at the struggle you may currently be facing in your romantic relationship or one that is on the horizon.

This card suggests a period of adversity, but also reminds you of your inner strength and your ability to overpower any hardships that may come your way.

The element of Earth associated with it implies that these challenges can be overcome with patience, effort and grounded decision-making.

As it is a card of Summer, this struggle and the subsequent overcoming may find relevance during this season.

To sum it up, the combination of these two cards seems to predict a rough path in your love life, but one that you are fully capable of navigating and that will lead to victory.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Clubs mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, if you draw the 7 of Spades together with the 8 of Clubs, this may hint at a complex situation concerning your finances and career.

The 7 of Spades, being associated with deceit and victory, might suggest that you will overcome a significant financial issue, but not without facing some dishonesty or difficulty.

If you’re feeling stressed about your financial standing or if you think someone is concealing information from you, it’s essential to remain vigilant.

This card is telling you that despite the hurdles you may face, eventually you will prevail and gain victory, though the journey might be fraught with challenges and guesswork.

Meanwhile, the 8 of Clubs represents hardship and power, indicating that you may be experiencing some tough times in your job.

The job could demand a lot of effort, or you might face stiff competition.

Despite this, it signifies power too, representing a strong sense of determination and resilience.

Combining the two cards, it might mean that while you may experience challenges and underhanded dealings at your job, your determination will enable you to cope and eventually gain control over the situation.

These hardships could be dictating your financial status too, but the strength suggested by the card implies that control and improvement are within your reach.

This duo of cards, although seemingly negative, comes with an undertone of resilience and eventual triumph, illustrating the journey to success rather than the downfall.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Clubs mean together for your health?

In a health-focused cartomancy reading, the appearance of the 7 of Spades might signify that there is a hidden aspect to your health that you may not be aware of.

This card, linked with deceit, talks about secret issues or concealed matters that may affect you.

As Water is connected with emotions, it may showcase that these concealed factors may have something to do with your emotional well-being.

The winter element suggests a cold or dormant condition that may lie within.

Perhaps you’re underestimating the effects of stress, or there might be a dormant issue that requires a medical check-up.

The victory aspect suggests overcoming this through awareness, honesty, and taking proactive action.

The 8 of Clubs, on the other hand, represents hardship and power, which can suggest a period of struggle or challenge concerning your physical health.

Maybe it’s a demanding health regimen you need to follow, or a rigorous diet plan that will be tough but ultimately rewarding.

The Earth element signifies material world, solidity, endurance and it can denote physical body, indicating a need for more grounding exercises like yoga, meditation, or just spending time in nature.

The summer season suggests a time when these issues will be most apparent, or when your focus on overcoming these hardships will be most effective.

Despite the challenge signified by the hardship notion, the power connotation gives a hopeful message of capability and strength to overcome those hardships within the realm of health.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
