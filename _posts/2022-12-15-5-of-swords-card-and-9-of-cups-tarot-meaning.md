## What does 5 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The 5 of Swords and the 9 of Cups tarot cards together provide a warning about the potential for bullies, theft, violence, and abusive relationships to threaten your ability to experience material abundance and wishes come true. It is a reminder to pay attention to your surroundings and not let anyone take advantage of your good fortune. The combination also suggests that even when faced with violent or abusive behavior, keep your faith that good health and wealth can still be yours to enjoy. With the right precautions and protection, no one can take away the abundance and joy that can come with the 9 of Cups.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 5 of swords tarot card is a reminder that physical and emotional health needs to be taken seriously. The card indicates a period of conflict, where the outcome could be damaging for those involved. Negative emotions such as anger and hatred can take a toll on the body. It’s important to remain mindful and aware of how our mental and physical health is impacted by the events we face. The card can serve as a prompt to practice self-care, look for outside help, or use meditation and other tools to manage stress and anxiety.

Finances:

The 5 of swords tarot card has been linked to financial misfortune. It can represent a decline in the financial situation, whether it’s the result of theft or mismanagement of resources. It’s a warning sign to pay close attention to the management of finances and to be careful not to fall into traps or become a victim of an unfair situation. The 5 of swords can also be an indication of a lack of trust, which can lead to disputes over money.

Relationships:

The 5 of swords tarot card is an indication of strife in relationships, be it romantic or platonic. Bullying, abusive behaviour, and manipulation are all possible in this card’s context, and it’s important to take the necessary steps to protect oneself and their relationships. It’s important to stay aware that the main aim of a partnership should be to bring out the best in both individuals. If the card appears, it’s a warning to not lose sight of this goal, and to take a step back and reassess the relationship.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The Nine of Cups indicates that you are likely in good health and happy with where you are. There is a feeling of contentment, satisfaction and joy with your life and health. You have been working hard and taking good care of your physical and mental state, and have achieved balance. The card suggests that you are feeling secure and blessed in the area of health.

Finances  
The Nine of Cups is a card of abundance and material wealth. There is a feeling of abundance and completion in financial matters. Your hard work has paid off and your finances are flourishing. You may find yourself in a position of considerable wealth and resources, which could mean purchasing a house, a car, or other luxuries. There may also be a strong feeling of security and financial stability in your life.

Relationships  
The Nine of Cups is a card of joy and happiness when it comes to relationships. Your relationships have been nurtured and cared for, and are now in a place of joy and satisfaction. You have learned to accept each other and have found a peace within your relationship. Your partners have learned to support you and help you reach your goals. The nine of cups suggests that you are all in a place of strong trust, harmony and companionship, and it will bring you much happiness and joy.
