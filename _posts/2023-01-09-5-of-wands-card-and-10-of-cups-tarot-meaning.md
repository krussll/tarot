## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

It can represent difficult physical challenges, such as rehabilitating an injury or pushing through a tough workout. It could also refer to mental or emotional roadblocks we must tackle in order to reach better physical health, such as defeating self-sabotaging thinking patterns or making lifestyle changes. The 5 of Wands may serve as an encouragement to stick with it, even when it’s difficult, as there can be much success in the end.

Finances: The 5 of Wands tarot card is associated with competition and difficulty. It can symbolize financial burdens to overcome or ruts that require a determined effort to get out of. This card can refer to the projects or investments that we have to be willing to put in effort and time into in order to see success. It can also suggest caution when considering big financial risks, as persistence and hard work are likely to be required to make such investments payoff in the end.

Relationships: This card is suggestive of the struggles and rivalries that can arise between two people in any relationship. There may be a power struggle between two partners, where compromise can be difficult to come by. It can serve as a warning not to let these battles become destructive, as it is important to listen to the needs of both sides of the relationship. The 5 of Wands can also represent the strength needed to overcome obstacles within the relationship, such as communication problems, trust issues, and miscommunication. Having determination and persistence to keep moving forward is key to success in relationships as well.

This card can represent the deep bonds of love and support between two people and it can bring out the best in both parties. The connection between two people can be something that penetrates both the physical and emotional planes. This card can indicate that any relationship you are currently in is steadily growing, and that you are in a healthy spot of mutual understanding and support.
