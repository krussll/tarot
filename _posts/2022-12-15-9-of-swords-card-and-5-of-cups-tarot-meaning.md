A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 9 Of Swords and The 5 Of Cups tarot card together suggest that you are experiencing a deep sense of grief and regret due to a recent loss. You may be feeling overwhelmed by this loss, leading to nightmares and severe anxiety. You may feel abandoned and unloved, further deepening your depression. It’s essential to be gentle with yourself in this challenging time and to seek comfort and support from your loved ones.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card can be an indication of poor health. It suggests that nightmares, anxiety, grief and depression are taking a toll on physical health. This card may be a warning to take better care of oneself, to get adequate rest, to observe healthy eating habits, and to avoid drugs or alcohol. In regards to finances, the 9 of Swords can be a sign that something is not right. It can be a warning to not take any chances with investments or business deals, as these can be more of a risk than expected. Furthermore, this card is an indication of feeling overwhelmed—whether it is by debt, bills, or other financial obligations. It can be a sign to take a step back and look at the bigger picture.

When it comes to relationships, the 9 of Swords can indicate a lack of communication or difficulty in dealing with certain issues. It suggests that it might be time to take a break or to take some time away from a relationship to assess what it truly needs. Furthermore, this card can be a warning to be weary of possible harsh words or actions that could potentially damage relationships. Instead, the card could be pointing to the need to make room for kinder, more compassionate communication.

In conclusion, the 9 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning sign for physical health, financial matters, and relationships. It is a reminder to take care of oneself, to proceed with caution when dealing with financial matters, and to make room for compassionate communication in relationships.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health – The 5 Of Cups Tarot card is a harbinger of poor health, implying a degree of physical or mental imbalance that may not resolve itself without intervention. If the card appears in a reading, it may be time to pay attention to the warning signs of a potentially serious illness, or to take action to prevent such an event. For example, stress and anxiety can take a toll on the body and undermine physical health, so it is important to take steps to manage stress in healthy ways. It is also important to stay on top of regular medical checkups and screenings.

Finances – In the realm of finances, the 5 Of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as a warning sign of impending financial loss. It is a sign that the time has come to reassess budgets and ensure that spending is in line with income. Any unnecessary debts should be paid off as soon as possible, and steps should be taken to protect assets against further financial hardship. This is a time to take precautions in order to avoid any kind of monetary disaster.

Relationships – In interpersonal relationships, the 5 Of Cups Tarot card can be an indication of regretful decisions and a need for emotional healing. The card implies that past mistakes, misunderstandings, and hurt feelings can all contribute to a feeling of abandonment, despair, and sorrow. In such cases, it is important to focus on ways to move forward. Acknowledging the mistakes and hurt feelings can help, as can seeking professional counselling if necessary. In some cases, it may also be wise to take a step back to allow for some space and time to heal.
