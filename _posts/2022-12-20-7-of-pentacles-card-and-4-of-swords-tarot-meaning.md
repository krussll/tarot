A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 4 Of Swords tarot card offer valuable insight into our need for patience and rest when striving for success. The 7 Of Pentacles reminds us that hard work and dedication often lead to delayed success and that we must remain patient throughout our journey. The 4 Of Swords encourages us to take regular breaks from our work and to nurture ourselves so that we have the energy necessary for our endeavors. Therefore, when these two cards appear together in a spread, it is a reminder to take advantage of times of rest and solitude to ensure that our progress is sustained and our success takes us further in life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 7 Of Pentacles and 4 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card is often interpreted as a card of patience, hard work, and delayed success. It appears when the Seeker is in need of guidance on how to take the steps necessary to sustain and cultivate what they want in the areas of health, finances, and relationships.

Health:

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card encourages perseverance when it comes to health. This card tells us that there is no easy answer to our health concerns. It may take hard work and patience to get the results we want. This card reminds us to take a long-term approach to health, including regular exercise and healthy eating. It also encourages us to seek out mentors and professionals who can help us reach our desired health goals.

Finances:

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card suggests that financial success requires patience and hard work. It advises us to set a financial plan and work steadily towards our goals. It warns us against chasing short-term gains and advises us to focus on sustainable methods of long-term financial success. It also encourages us to seek out advice and guidance when needed.

Relationships:

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card tells us that relationships require patience and hard work. It encourages us to invest in the relationships that we have, even if it doesn’t always feel easy. It suggests that we must be willing to put in the effort to sustain a relationship and be willing to work out our differences. It also encourages us to be open to different points of view and be willing to compromise.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is representative of healing for the mind, body, and soul. It encourages us to take the time to rest, retreat, and renew our energy. This is a card of rest and rejuvenation that can help us to cope better with any physical or mental illness. It tells us to find peace and balance within ourselves that can help us to lead a healthier life. To enhance the healing effect of this card, we can practice calming self-care activities such as yoga, meditation, breathing exercises, and going for walks in nature.

Finances

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is about taking a break from our busy lives and reassessing our financial situation. It is a reminder to take a step back and assess our needs and how to prioritize them. Additionally, this card can mean that we need to look into new sources of income or to enhance our budgeting skills. It can also suggest that we should look into ways to bring balance between what we desire and what we can realistically obtain.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card can represent a need for solitude in relationships. It may mean that we need to find a better balance between our needs and those of our partner. We should be honest with our partners and communicate clearly what we need in order to feel fulfilled in the relationship. We also need to take care of ourselves and create boundaries that allow us to take some time for ourselves, for rest and renewal. This will help us to come back to our relationships with renewed strength and understanding.
