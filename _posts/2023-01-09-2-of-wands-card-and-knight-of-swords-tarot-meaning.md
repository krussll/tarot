A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean together?

The 2 Of Wands and The Knight Of Swords tarot card together can symbolize a period of waiting for results, a decision needing to be made, and the possibility of a lifestyle change. This could represent a need to travel for new opportunities, but also a need to be wary of those who will try to fast-talk their way into influencing the outcome of your decisions. You may need to be mindful of how you are communicating, or who you are trusting, as you make your choices. Ultimately, the 2 of Wands and the Knight of Swords tarot card is urging you to trust your gut instinct and make your decision with confidence and clarity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

The 2 Of Wands Tarot card is often associated with choices and opportunities. As it relates to health, it indicates that it may be beneficial for the querent to make an informed decision to invest in their wellbeing. This could include researching how to eat better, exercising more, or seeing a health professional for a holistic health plan. It is also a sign to be patient and wait for results to become known; this often requires that the individual has faith that a positive outcome is possible and be diligent in their pursuit of health.

In terms of finances, the 2 Of Wands Tarot card can indicate that it is time to make a choice. Whether it is a big or small investment, this card suggests that careful consideration should be taken to ensure that it is the right choice. It may also be a sign that it is time to start planning for travel, which could lead to more prosperity down the line.

For relationships, it is a sign of considering the path ahead and making decisions in light of the future. It is a reminder to think about the commitment that each partner has to the other, and to take a thoughtful approach to any decisions that need to be made. It can also be a sign to plan a trip together, in order to get away and enjoy some time together away from the everyday pressures.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, the Knight of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the need to make changes to one’s lifestyle. It is a reminder to take the necessary steps to build a more wholesome and balanced lifestyle. This can be done through exercise, nutrition, and mindful practices such as yoga or meditation. By taking the time to focus on one’s wellbeing, one can effectively manage stress, give one’s body the rest it needs, and generally become healthier.

Financially, the Knight of Swords Tarot card can represent the need to make swift and speedy changes in order to stay financially afloat. It can symbolize the need to invest in the future and make decisions that may be unconventional. This card urges us to take risks, be creative, and combine both the old and the new in order to expand our financial horizons.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Knight of Swords Tarot card can symbolize the need to stand up for oneself and speak one’s truth. It can represent having to make tough but necessary decisions that may not be well-received by others. It can also be a reminder to not let others’ opinions sway one’s own beliefs and decisions. Ultimately, this card can represent having the courage to make changes that are beneficial to the individual in the long-run, even if those changes come at a cost.
