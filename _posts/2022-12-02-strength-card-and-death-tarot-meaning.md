However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and Death card together in particular.

The Strength card and Death have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and Death mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The Death tarot card together represent a powerful combination of emotions and energies. They speak to the need to make big changes in order to move forward, but with courage and self-love. The Strength card speaks to the courage and self-respect that it takes to move on from the past and start anew. The Death card points to the need for transformation and endings in order to progress and truly be reborn. This powerful combination encourages us to take this leap into the unknown with courage, respect, and the unconditional love that we have for ourselves.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and Death mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Death mean?

The Death Tarot card is a reminder to take stock of our physical health and make necessary improvements. It can indicate that it is time to stop unhealthy habits, such as smoking, drinking, or eating unhealthy foods. Perhaps it is time to find a new routine of exercise and meditation that will bring more balance and peace to our health. The Death Tarot card can signal the need to re-evaluate our finances. This may mean cutting out any unnecessary spending, budgeting, and paying off any debts that we may have. It can also mean the end of a job or career, or a shift in our finances that requires us to reassess our financial goals and start fresh. This can be a sign to be more frugal and to invest for the future.

Relationships

The Death Tarot card can mean the end of unhealthy relationships. It can also be a reminder to let go of toxic patterns and behaviours and move towards healthier relationships. It can indicate that it is time to forgive, let go, and move on to healthier, more fulfilling relationships. It may also be a sign to end a relationship if it is no longer serving either party. The Death Tarot card can also signify a need to start anew and find a sense of renewal in relationships. It can be a time to reconnect with friends and family and to form strong and meaningful connections with others.
