However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 5 Of Cups mean together?

These two tarot cards, The Hierophant and the 5 of Cups, represent the tension between tradition and the loss and regret of not having the support of those around us. The Hierophant encourages us to conform to the expectations of society and follow traditional societal norms while the 5 of Cups speaks of our deeper emotional side, of feeling abandoned or unloved by those close to us. Together, they remind us that while there are certain expectations in our lives that we must adhere to, we must also make sure that we don’t neglect our own feelings of being unloved and unsupported. Ultimately, these two cards remind us to try to balance the traditions that we follow with our own feelings and needs.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card can be seen as an indication that it is important to stay healthy. This could mean following a healthy diet and exercise regime, going to regular check-ups, or taking time to practice yoga or meditation in order to remain in balance. It also suggests that it is best to take a traditional approach to health and healing, trusting in the advice of experienced and qualified professionals. This card can be a reminder to pay attention to the body in order to prevent long-term illnesses, and to get regular check-ups to ensure that any health issues can be identified early.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card indicates that it is important to remain conservative with finances. It can be a reminder to save for a rainy day and to live within one’s means. It is important to be wise with resources and to practice good financial habits such as budgeting and planning for the future. It also advises to follow traditional and proven approaches to financial planning and suggests that a steady and conservative approach is more beneficial than taking an overly risky one.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card encourages an adherence to traditional relationship values and norms. It can be seen as a reminder to be respectful and mindful of others, to communicate in a constructive way, and to treat others as one would wish to be treated. It also encourages patience and understanding, and suggests that it is important to take a long-term view with relationships and to build trust through consistent, honest and reliable actions. This card can be a reminder to honour traditions and conventions in relationships, as well as to be open to understanding different perspectives.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 5 Of Cups Tarot card is a sign of a health setback. It signifies a physical or emotional loss, such as a chronic illness or disability, a long-term injury, or the death of a loved one. The 5 of Cups Tarot card reflects a loss of strength and vitality that can feel overwhelming. It may be difficult to stay motivated and focused on physical health or mental health and the sadness can result in depression. Those affected by this card are advised to be gentle with themselves and to seek out support, whether it is professional or through family and friends.

Finances:

The 5 Of Cups Tarot card is a warning to be mindful of sudden financial loss. It is a sign of unexpected expenses or investment losses that can be difficult to recover from. It is important to stay within budget, cut out unnecessary spending and practice mindful money management. This card warns that financial decision shouldn’t be taken lightly and it’s essential to research any investments before jumping in. This card also suggests that it may be wise to seek out financial advice.

Relationships:

The 5 Of Cups Tarot card is a sign of loss, regret and grief in relationships. It reflects a feeling of abandonment or being unloved. It could be a sign of a break-up, a death, or a betrayal. It’s important to remember that relationships go through phases and the 5 of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to appreciate the good times and to give yourself permission to grieve the losses. It’s also important to reach out for help if needed and to take time to reflect and heal.
