## What does The Sun and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Sun tarot and The 3 Of Swords tarot indicates a period of mixed emotions, when you may need to accept a difficult situation while still finding joy in your life. While the 3 Of Swords tarot suggests heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression, and surgery, this card is also a reminder that you must find a way to overcome these struggles and emerge on the other side with a new sense of confidence, joy, and success. On the other hand, The Sun tarot card is a reminder that despite life’s struggles, things will eventually come together in a way that brings material happiness and joy. It is a reminder not to give up hope and to always look for the positive in difficult times.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card is associated with happiness, joy, and success. This bright and optimistic card indicates that whatever obstacles may have been in the way of reaching a goal, they will be overcome and a positive outcome is on the horizon.

For health, The Sun Tarot card indicates a brighter future and overall wellbeing. Any aches and pains that have been slowing you down should be diminished or even disappear. If a chronic illness has been dragging you down, The Sun Tarot card promises a period of recuperation and relief.

For finances, The Sun Tarot card encourages positive vibes and signals better luck in the area of money. A financial boost is possible and now is the time to invest, save, and plan for a more secure future. It may be time to take a risk, whether it’s in the stock market or in a new business venture.

For relationships, The Sun Tarot card brings renewed hope and a bright future. If you’ve been stuck in a difficult period of communication, the Sun’s influence can help you resolve the issues and reach a more satisfying and loving connection. Whatever type of relationship you’re in, be it platonic, romantic or familial, now is the time to make your bond stronger and to get closer to the people who matter.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 3 of Swords Tarot card has many implications for health, primarily surrounding emotional and physical pain. In most interpretations of this card, it is a sign of heartache, a harbinger of a deep, aching sadness that can be accompanied by physical pain or illness. In extreme cases, this card can signify surgery or illness that requires healing, and it serves as a reminder to take care and nurture one’s physical and mental well being.

Finances

This card is often seen as a warning of financial instability, with potential losses and struggles on the horizon. It suggests that making wise decisions and cultivating financial literacy is necessary in order to protect one’s finances. Additionally, it implies a need to make wise investments and trust the process, as even when losses occur, the long-term gain will be greater.

Relationships

The 3 of Swords Tarot card often points to painful endings in relationships, such as breakups and divorce. It suggests severing connections in order to move forward, but warns of the emotional pain that this transition may bring. This card can also signal an end of a destructive or toxic relationship, so it can be seen as a sign of liberation and growth within oneself. It encourages people to open their hearts and minds to new relationships and understanding, despite the hurt.
