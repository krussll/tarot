A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and King Of Wands card together in particular.

The Justice card and King Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and King Of Wands mean together?

When The Justice tarot card and The King Of Wands appear together, it is a reminder that power and truth are not mutually exclusive. The King Of Wands brings forward courage and boldness to stand up for what is correct, while The Justice tarot card encourages balanced solutions that benefit all involved. Together, these two cards communicate that when we make decisions rooted in fairness and truth, those decisions will ultimately bring us the best outcomes. We can trust that those decisions will be supported and strengthened by the power of bold and courageous leadership.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and King Of Wands mean together for your love life?

When the Justice and King of Wands tarot cards are seen together in a reading, they can represent a situation in which strength and integrity are needed in order to bring balance and fairness to a relationship. This can refer to a sense of power, where one must have the courage to speak their truth and be heard, as well as the willingness to listen, consider and cooperate with the other person.

In a love life context, this combination suggests a willingness to build an equal partnership based on mutual respect and understanding. Both parties should be able to trust that, when confronted with difficult circumstances, the other is capable of making decisions that benefit both parties equally. It is equally important to take the time to fully consider each other’s feelings and desires, in order to arrive at a mutually beneficial conclusion.

The Justice card encourages both parties to be honest and direct when communicating with each other, while the King of Wands teaches that it may be necessary to be firm and decisive in order to ensure that both parties feel respected and heard. With this combination, the relationship can benefit from a strong sense of equitable decision-making and a mutual desire to maintain harmonious, healthy communication. The Justice and King of Wands can symbolize a relationship built on shared strength and balance, allowing each person the ability to support the other.

You are encouraged to be creative, resourceful, and bold. You have the courage to make the kinds of changes necessary to create positive changes in your financial situation.

The Justice card serves as a reminder to be mindful and diligent in your efforts. Consider the consequences of your choices and decisions before taking action. All possible outcomes must be weighed and evaluated, as what is right for one person might not be right for another. This analysis will help you choose the best course of action, with minimal fallout.

The King Of Wands offers you the opportunity to become a powerful leader. You have the strength of character and the self-confidence you need to bring your dreams to fruition. You are encouraged to seize upon the opportunities that come your way, but remember to remain true to your moral principles. If a solution to a problem seems unethical or immoral, it is best to look for a different solution that is mutually beneficial for all parties.

Overall, the Justice and King of Wands tarot cards provide you with the motivation and fortitude to take control of your financial and career goals. They remind you to evaluate possible consequences and make choices that are in alignment with your values. With the King Of Wands’ help, you can become a leader and make decisions that provide win-win solutions for all. This will ensure your success and prosperity in the long run.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and King Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice Tarot card can represent the need for balance in our physical and mental health, and for us to take responsibility for our actions and their consequences. This card serves as a reminder of the power of choice in our daily lives. We must become conscious of how our choices affect our health, such as whether we take the time to exercise, drink enough water, avoid unhealthy foods and give our bodies rest and relaxation. Justice encourages us to reach a win-win solution and be honest with ourselves and others when it comes to our health and wellbeing.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card indicates the need for us to take responsibility for our finances and look at the cause and effect of our decisions. It encourages us to think before we act, and to consider both short and long-term implications of our choices. We should always strive for win-win solutions and look for creative solutions to problems rather than focusing on the immediate. If we are honest and open about our finances, it can help us achieve our financial goals in the long run.

Relationships: The Justice Tarot card reminds us of the power of truth and honesty in our relationships. It encourages us to take responsibility for our actions, accept the consequences and look for ways to create a win-win situation for all involved. We should strive for balance and fairness in our relationships, and consider the consequences of our words and actions. This card can also suggest that we should not shy away from difficult conversations and should make an effort to be open and honest with our partners.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

The King of Wands Tarot card is an archetypal energy symbolizing bold leadership, power, courage and charm. In a Tarot reading, the King of Wands encourages you to take charge of your life and be a strong leader in your own right. The card speaks to the qualities of self-confidence, ambition, and adventure.

In terms of health, the King of Wands suggests that you have the strength and courage to make decisions that will benefit your health and wellbeing. This card encourages you to be proactive in shaping your health destiny, utilizing all the options available to you. You have the potential to rise above ill health with a clear and resolute leadership.

When it comes to finances, the King of Wands encourages you to take a brave and bold approach. You are being invited to be an astute entrepreneur, boldly taking risks that you are sure will yield great returns. You are being asked to think outside the box when it comes to money and wealth.

In terms of relationships, the King of Wands represents a mature, confident, and charismatic leader. You may be willing to guide others to success and provide loving and caring guidance as needed. You may have the capability to provide a strong and powerful presence in a relationship, making difficult decisions when required.

The King of Wands Tarot card carries with it an invitation to be brave, strong, and courageous in all areas of your life. You are encouraged to take charge and develop the inner resilience and strength needed to make difficult decisions and seize life’s opportunities. With this card as your companion, you have the potential to create positive change wherever you go.
