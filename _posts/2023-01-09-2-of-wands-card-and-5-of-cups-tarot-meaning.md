However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 2 Of Wands and The 5 Of Cups tarot cards when combined together can indicate that you are feeling stuck between two options and are uncertain of which direction to take. You may feel like there would be a sense of loss if you made one choice over the other, or that one path may bring more regret than the other. This may be compounded by a feeling of being abandoned and unloved, making it difficult to choose a path and make your travel plans. You are feeling the weight of making the right choice, but at the same time, you are waiting for the results of your decisions before you can take the next step.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

**Both 2 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Five of Cups Tarot card often signifies a period of poor health and weakened energy. During this difficult time, it is important to take care of oneself by getting plenty of rest and eating a healthy diet. It may be necessary to make changes in one’s lifestyle in order to reduce stress and improve overall health. The Five of Cups Tarot card can also indicate that a person may be holding onto physical or mental ailments for too long, and needs to seek proper medical attention in order to recover.

Finances:  
The Five of Cups Tarot card is an indication of financial losses, disappointments, and bad luck. It may be a sign that one’s current plans are not making progress and it is time to re-evaluate one’s approach to money. It could also be a sign that one should take a step back and evaluate any financial decisions they have made and be sure that they are being made in the most responsible way. It may be a sign that one should seek advice from a financial professional in order to get back on the right track.

Relationships:  
The Five of Cups Tarot card suggests a period of sadness and regret in relationships. It may be a sign that one is feeling neglected, abandoned, or unloved. It is important to be able to properly communicate how one is feeling in order to work towards resolving any difficulties. It might be necessary to make changes in one’s relationship in order to ensure that one is being treated with respect and kindness. However, it is also important to take ownership of one’s role in the situation, and to take responsibility for any mistakes that have been made.
