## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 9 Of Wands and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

For Health

The Nine of Wands tarot card is a reminder that health and wellbeing is something that needs to be monitored and maintained in order to enjoy a long and happy life. The card suggests that it is necessary to establish boundaries and regulations to combat illness or injury. The 9 of Wands can encourage us to take active steps to improve our physical health, such as regular exercise, proper nutrition and adequate rest. This card can also represent the need for mental and emotional health maintenance, such as engaging in healthy activities, seeking professional help and surrounding ourselves with supportive and positive people.

For Finances

The Nine of Wands tarot card is associated with financial security, as it suggests that having clear, defined boundaries can help protect our financial status from harm. It encourages us to take proper steps to ensure our financial stability and growth by budgeting, saving and investing in the future. This card also suggests that it is important to maintain a sense of vigilance when it comes to finances, and to be aware of our spending and saving habits. This can help us identify and address any issues before they become more serious.

For Relationships

The Nine of Wands tarot card can represent the need to establish and maintain personal boundaries in relationships – both romantic and platonic. It encourages us to take responsibility for our needs and to express those needs in a respectful manner. This card also emphasises the importance of communication, of listening and understanding before responding, and of making sure that we take time to nurture the relationships in our life. It suggests that we may need to make changes in order to have healthy, meaningful relationships that have a positive impact on our lives.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page Of Cups Tarot card is a reminder that even when you feel like you don’t have enough energy to take care of yourself, it is important to remain mindful of your health. Practicing mindfulness can help you stay present in the moment and better manage your stress levels. When dealing with your health, the Page Of Cups encourages you to listen to your body and be open to trying new things. For example, if you’re feeling run down, you might consider an herbal supplement or trying a new yoga routine to bring some balance back into your life. Working with your doctor to find the best health options for you can also be beneficial.

Finances

The Page Of Cups Tarot card may be a reminder to manage your finances carefully and thoughtfully. This could mean setting a budget and using it to allocate your money to both the immediate and long-term goals. Sticking to your budget and reducing unnecessary spending is important to ensure your financial well-being. The Page Of Cups will also remind you that it’s ok to be creative and have fun when dealing with money, such as investing in stocks or finding second-hand deals. Learning about economics and the stock market can help you stay informed and make sound financial decisions for yourself.

Relationships

The Page Of Cups Tarot card reminds us to be compassionate and understanding when it comes to relationships. For those in new relationships, this can mean taking the time to get to know the other person and learning to trust them. It is important to be patient and honest when it comes to building relationships that can last. For existing relationships, the Page Of Cups encourages us to show our appreciation and support for those who are important to us. Relationships can often be challenging and it is important to be attentive and open with your partner. Additionally, being mindful of boundaries and respecting each other’s needs can go a long way in sustaining relationships.
