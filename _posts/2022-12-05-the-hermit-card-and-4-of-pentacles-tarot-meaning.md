A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 4 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 4 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 4 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the 4 of Pentacles tarot card when combined together is a call to find balance between spiritual illumination and a desire for long-term security. It could also be a sign that you have a strong focus on material gains, but you need to take time to turn inward and seek for spiritual guidance and understanding. Even if you are successful in attaining material gains, you need to remember that spiritual growth is equally important. Look at what you want out of life and explore the different paths you can take to reach those goals, while still maintaining your spiritual growth.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 4 Of Pentacles tarot card suggests that the individual’s desires for long-term security in their relationship have taken precedence over the other aspects of the relationship. This can be seen as a sign that the individual is only interested in what they can get out of the relationship and not in being truly open and vulnerable. This can be detrimental to the relationship as it can cause them to be guarded and unwilling to really open up to their partner.

When The Hermit tarot card and The 4 Of Pentacles tarot card are looked at together, it suggests that the individual needs to take the time to go within and reexamine the desires they have for their relationship. By understanding what their true needs and wants are, they can then start to make sure their relationship is based on these needs and wants and not on greed for security. If the individual can manage to do this, then their relationship will have a much better chance of success and happiness.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 4 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card encourages one to look within for answers and clarity when it comes to our physical health. In doing so, we can begin to understand where we are lacking or needing improvement in our health and wellbeing. It is important to take the time to identify any issues or unhealthy habits we may have, so that we can make the necessary changes or adjustments to improve our physical health. This could be anything from eating a more balanced diet, getting enough sleep, reducing stress, or getting regular exercise. The Hermit Tarot card also serves as a reminder to pay attention to our bodies and to honor what it needs in order to stay healthy.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card also offers insight into our financial affairs. When it comes to money, the Hermit encourages us to take a step back and analyze our current situation to understand any areas where we might be struggling. This can involve looking at our spending habits, considering our current income sources, and examining the areas in which we need to cut back. In actively exploring our finances, we can start to develop a plan to help reach our financial goals. Additionally, the Hermit Tarot card can remind us to think more deeply about the importance of money and how it relates to our values.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card provides insight into our relationships as well. This card can be seen as a reminder to take time to reflect on our relationships, both with ourselves and others. Doing so can help us to identify any areas where we can improve, which could range from needing to build a better connection with ourselves, or strengthening existing bonds with our family and friends. Additionally, the Hermit can serve as a warning that our relationships can be impacted by our own behaviour, and that it is important to be aware of our emotions and how they influence our interactions with others. Ultimately, the Hermit Tarot card encourages us to cultivate healthy and meaningful relationships.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The 4 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a suggestion to ensure that our physical and mental health are taken care of first before any other priorities. It’s a reminder to invest time and energy in our health, and that good health will pay off in the long run. It also means to be mindful with our well-being and to be aware of stress that builds up as a result of too much work and/or materialistic desires. It can represent a need to make changes to our lifestyle in order to restore balance, such as eating healthy, exercise, getting enough rest and finding a healthy way to be productive.

Finances: The 4 Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that it’s important to have a long-term financial plan in place that can be tailored to our lifestyle. It can mean investing in long-term goals for financial security and stability. It’s important to budget carefully and to save up as much as possible. It’s also important to be mindful of our spending and to use our money wisely. The 4 Of Pentacles Tarot card can also mean being content with what we have and not wanting for more in order to be happy.

Relationships: The 4 Of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that relationships should be based on long-term commitments. It means investing in relationships, including with family and friends as much as with romantic partners. It speaks to the importance of honest communication and to making sure that every effort is being made to make the relationship work. It may also be a sign that an over-emphasis on material security is leading to neglect of relationships. It’s important to take time away from career and materialism to invest in meaningful relationships.
