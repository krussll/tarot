A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Wheel Of Fortune card and The World card together in particular.

The The Wheel Of Fortune card and The World have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune and The World mean together?

Furthermore, with The Wheel Of Fortune, you could find yourself with a stroke of luck with gambling, or a chance to win in a lottery or other type of game. Accepting the changes coming your way will lead to good fortune and positive changes for you in the future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a symbol of good fortune and a turning point in life. It symbolizes luck, a fresh start, and a new beginning. It can be seen as a gateway to reaching one’s highest potential in life. Looking at it more deeply, it can also suggest adaptation, transformation, and the ability to handle change.

In terms of health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be seen as a sign of good fortune. It suggests that any obstacles or difficulties in health that are present can be overcome and that better health is on the horizon. The card indicates that making changes to habits and lifestyle may be necessary to achieve better health, but it also promises the potential of great reward if the changes are made.

In terms of finances, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be viewed as a sign of luck and opportunity for better money management. It can mean that there is potential for investments or gambling to pay off. It also encourages people to take chances, be adventurous and pay attention to financial opportunities that could lead to financial success.

In terms of relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be seen as a sign of good fortune and potential for relationships to turn around. It suggests that things can work out if the parties involved are willing to put in the effort. It can also be a sign that relationships can possibly be renewed or improved upon, so long as both parties are willing to take the risk. It also encourages us to take chances to form new relationships and to reach out to those we may have lost contact with.

## What does The World mean?

Health:

The World tarot card reflects the idea of completing a cycle; a cycle of health and wellness. It can be a sign that you have successfully brought a health or wellness overall to a satisfactory conclusion. It can also be a sign that it is time to take a break and recharge, to empower yourself with a period of rest and introspection. It may suggest looking toward the future and resolving to approach health in a holistic manner, such as with exercise, diet, and self-care. Finally, it could signify the successful completion of a particularly difficult physical health challenge.

Finances:

Financially, the World tarot card signifies the completion of a financial goal. It could mean that you have achieved a major financial milestone, such as paying off a loan or reaching a savings goal. It could also suggest that you have achieved a degree of financial stability and security. This could be a sign of taking a break from finance-related activities and just simply enjoying the fruits of your hard work. Additionally, it could signify the success of a big investment or endeavor, such as starting a business or investing in stocks and bonds.

Relationships:

In terms of relationships, the World tarot card can represent the success and completion of an important relationship. It can be a sign that you have achieved a significant milestone in your relationship, such as getting engaged or finally understanding each other after a difficult period. Additionally, it could be a sign of completing a relationship cycle by letting go of a relationship that has grown stagnant or stale. It could even signify taking a break from the dating scene or committing to a new relationship that you feel is meant to be.
