## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of greed, a desire for security, and a tendency to become overly attached to material possessions. It suggests that the person is focused too much on money and material goods, and not enough on their relationships and their health.

When it comes to health, the 4 of Pentacles may mean that the person has become too focused on the physical comforts that money can buy, such as the latest health supplements, organic food, and luxury spa treatments. While it is important to invest in your health, it is also important to remember that money cannot buy good health. A healthy lifestyle is necessary to stay healthy; this requires an investment of time, not only money.

When it comes to finances, the 4 of Pentacles may indicate that the person is too focused on the accumulation of wealth and material goods. They may be over-investing in stocks or trying to accumulate the latest gadgets. Instead of focusing on the accumulation of money, they should focus on spending it wisely and making smart investments.

When it comes to relationships, the 4 of Pentacles can mean that the person is too focused on material things and not enough on the relationships that bring true security and happiness. They may be too focused on their possessions and forget about spending quality time with family and friends. Instead, they should focus on nurturing existing relationships and building new ones.

The 4 of Pentacles is a reminder that material goods can provide only a temporary sense of security. While it is important to plan for the future and take care of yourself financially, it’s also important to nurture relationships, invest in your health, and spend money wisely.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is symbolic of luxury, self-sufficiency, financial gain, and solo pleasures. When it comes to your health, the appearance of this card can indicate that you have been taking excellent care of yourself in recent times. If you have been eating well, getting plenty of exercise, and making sure to get enough rest, the 9 of Pentacles is a sign that your diligence is paying off. You should take pride in the more holistic approach that you have taken towards your personal health, and this card is encouraging you to continue to make choices in alignment with your goals.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign that you have been making great progress in accumulating wealth. This can mean that you have earned a significant pay raise, or that you have invested in new opportunities that are beginning to bear fruit. This card is a reminder that success can be achieved through hard work and perseverance, and it encourages you to create a secure financial future through smart investments, careful budgeting, and creative planning.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 9 of Pentacles is strongly associated with independence and self-sufficiency. It’s a reminder that while it’s great to be in a relationship, it’s just as important to maintain your own autonomy and sense of self. This card also encourages you to take time to cultivate your own solo pleasures, be it through travel, learning a new hobby, or simply taking time for yourself to relax and recharge. Remember that you need to recharge your batteries so that you can bring your best self to your relationships.
