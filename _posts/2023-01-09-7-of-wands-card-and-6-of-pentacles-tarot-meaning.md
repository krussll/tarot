This is a great time to step back and evaluate your situation, consider who may present competition to you, and find ways to form alliances to ensure your protection.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 7 of Wands Tarot card can represent a need to protect oneself from physical, mental and even spiritual harm. In the context of health, it can mean listening to your body and taking preventive measures — eating healthy, exercising, getting enough sleep, and seeking medical care when needed. On a psychological level, it’s also important to build resiliency in order to protect oneself from stress and potential trauma. The 7 of Wands can stand for being aware of your own boundaries and being mindful of your limits.

Finances

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Wands Tarot card speaks of being proactive in regards to protecting assets. Wise money management is essential, such as budgeting, avoiding debt, and investing wisely. It’s important to have a plan and to be mindful of potential risks. Preparing for the future by setting funds aside for emergency situations and retirement is also key. The 7 of Wands can signify being aware of the competition and not becoming vulnerable to scams or letting money slip through one’s fingers.

Relationships

The 7 of Wands can also signify being aware of the need to protect oneself from potential harm when it comes to relationships. While it is important to open up and trust, it can also be wise to be mindful of boundaries and to protect oneself from becoming vulnerable to manipulation, deceit and abuse. The card can also symbolize the need to protect oneself from the competition, whether it is in romantic relationships or friendships. As the 7 of Wands is a card of self-defense, it can stand for taking the necessary steps to ensure one’s interests and wellbeing are not being neglected.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

For health, The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card often represents receiving or giving help in physical or mental health-related matters. If you’re facing a health challenge and need help, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that you may be blessed with generous support from friends, family, or healthcare providers. If you are already receiving such help, this card could remind you to stay open to the care and support available to you. You may also be inspired to give back in some way to the community, especially through volunteering, donations, or other forms of selfless acts.

For finances, The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates generosity and support. This could come in the form of aid from family and friends, or even grants or loans from government or charitable organizations. If you are the one providing support, you may find that your resources are being used to help others—which can be a rewarding experience. If you’re being helped, it’s important to note that even though help is being offered, it’s still vital to take personal responsibility for your own well-being and financial situation.

For relationships, The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card signifies support and generosity in a relationship. You may find that one or both partners are willing to sacrifice time or energy to help the other, or you may find that you’re both drawing on resources to give something back to your community. It also indicates a need for balance and fairness. If assistance is being given, it should be balanced and reciprocal. And above all, it should come from a place of love and respect.
