A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Page Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the Page Of Cups tarot card together represent a balance between cause and effect, win-win solutions, and the truth coming out. On one hand, the Justice card signifies that if you are honest and take responsibility for your actions, you will be rewarded in the long run. On the other hand, the Page Of Cups tarot card shows you that doing volunteer work and getting involved in new relationships can be fulfilling and beneficial, something that will bring you rewards and joy. So, while the Justice card reminds us of the importance of hard work and responsibility, the Page Of Cups tarot card shows us that new connections and experiences can be both rewarding and overwhelming. Together, they signify a balanced approach to life and a combination of taking risks and making sure that you are doing the work that needs to be done.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card represents the idea of cause and effect when it comes to health. It is important to take care of our bodies if we want to experience the positive benefits of good health. If we make the effort to eat healthily, exercise regularly, and maintain healthy habits, our bodies will thrive and reward us with improved energy levels, lower risks of disease, and a feeling of being more balanced and centered. Money spent on healthy foods and activities will come back in terms of long-term benefits. On the other hand, if we neglect our health and make unhealthy choices, we will experience the consequences.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card can represent a sense of fairness and win-win solutions when it comes to finances. Investing in a little research, planning and wise decisions can yield great rewards. It is important to be aware of the effects that our financial decisions have on our lives. If we make good choices, such as avoiding debt and building up an emergency fund, we will be much better off in the long run. Similarly, if we make irresponsible decisions, such as spending too much money on items that we don’t need, we will suffer the consequences.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card can be a reminder to be honest and truthful in our relationships. When we are honest, the truth always comes out eventually, and it ensures that relationships are based on trust rather than secrets and lies. It can also be a reminder to listen to the other person and be fair in our dealings with them. When we take the time to try and understand each other’s needs and viewpoints, we can come up with win-win solutions that both parties can benefit from. When this happens, relationships flourish and become much stronger.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page of Cups Tarot Card indicates that you are taking care of your physical health, but may be neglecting your mental and emotional health. If this is the case, you may want to take some time to assess any underlying issues that are stressing you and take steps to address them. Consider journaling, talking to a therapist, or exploring relaxation techniques. Additionally, you should take the time to nourish both your body and mind with healthy activities and foods.

Finances

The Page of Cups Tarot card signals that you need to assess your financial situation and ask yourself some tough questions. Are you spending too much money on unnecessary items? Are you sure that you can keep up with the bills? Are your investments paying off? Once you have a clear understanding of your finances, you can start to make informed decisions that will lead you to a better overall financial future.

Relationships

The Page of Cups Tarot Card suggests that while you may be looking for new love and academic pursuits, it is important to remember to nurture your existing relationships as well. Spend quality time with your family and friends, and make sure that your interactions are positive and meaningful. Communication is essential to healthy relationships so make sure to listen to your loved ones and don’t be afraid to express yourself. This card also reminds you to practice self-care, and to take time for yourself so that you can stay grounded and connected.
