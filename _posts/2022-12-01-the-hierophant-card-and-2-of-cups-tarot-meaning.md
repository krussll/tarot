A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 2 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 2 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 2 Of Cups mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 2 Of Cups tarot card together represent the relationship between tradition and romance. This means that we have the opportunity to explore love and relationships with the support of conventional expectations and societal norms. Their combination can indicate a period in life where we can pursue commitments such as marriage or other serious partnerships, while still being rooted in customs and traditional values. Although it can be a challenge, they remind us that relationships can be enriched when built on both the foundations of love and respect for convention.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Together, these two cards suggest that you and your partner have a strong, meaningful connection that can stand the test of time in spite of its contemporary aesthetic.

The Hierophant card symbolizes the importance of traditional values and expectations when it comes to your relationship. This means that the two of you may want to consider honoring each other’s expectations and engaging in more conventional customs and practices, such as taking part in special occasions together, celebrating significant dates, and respecting each other’s opinions, beliefs, and values. The combination of the Hierophant and the 2 of Cups emphasizes the ongoing commitment the two of you share, suggesting that this traditional outlook on your relationship will build a strong bond between the two of you for years to come.

The 2 of Cups card also suggests that your relationship will involve not just conventional beliefs, but also romance and passion. You and your partner can enjoy time together that is both respectful and intimate, taking time to appreciate each other and engaging in activities as a couple that fulfill you both. Furthermore, the 2 of Cups can denote a proposal, marriage, or some other kind of commitment between you. Ultimately, the Hierophant and the 2 of Cups Tarot cards together symbolize a strong bond between two lovers that is grounded in tradition and warmed by passion.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hierophant and 2 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

The Hierophant Tarot card is a symbol of tradition and of being part of the larger group. At times it can be seen as restrictive; anchored in certain expectations and conformity. When it appears in a health, finance or relationship context it is often interpreted to mean that the individual is adhering to the traditions of their culture, family or relationship.

In health, The Hierophant Tarot card might suggest that the individual is taking a ‘conventional’ approach to their health, such as working with a traditional western-style doctor, taking a conventional approach to diet and exercise, or following a recognized wellness routine. Alternatively, it might be interpreted as a sign that they are seeking traditional healing methods such as acupuncture, meditation or herbalism.

In finances, The Hierophant Tarot card might suggest that the individual is following a traditional or conventional approach to money management, such as working with a financial adviser, investing in stocks and real estate, or seeking out advice from an accountant. It could be an indication that the individual is playing by the rules and is trying to minimize their risk.

In relationships The Hierophant Tarot card might suggest that the individual is adhering to traditional values and expectations; that they are trying to live in accordance with their culture and family values. It could signify that a couple is trying to go down a traditional path, such as getting married and starting a family. It could also be interpreted as a sign that the individual is trying to ‘play it safe’ in their relationships, seeking stability and security more than adventure and risk.

## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

Health  
Two of Cups Tarot card means that health is in a state of balance, harmony and flow. It indicates that the person is taking care of their physical and emotional needs, and their health is in a stable state. This card can be indicative of partnerships between people, whether it is between doctor and patient, clinicians and clients, healthcare professionals and institutions, or even friends and family helping each other out. It also signals that a person is taking preventative measures to ensure their health and wellbeing are in good standing.

Finances  
The Two of Cups Tarot card speaks of partnerships of a financial nature. It is a card of joint investments and collaborations that can generate great profits. It could also be an indication of merging two incomes, such as a couple merging their earnings or merging two businesses. It can also suggest financial partnerships between families, friends or even business partners. This card encourages people to look at the big picture and weigh their options carefully when it comes to financial decisions and investments.

Relationships  
The Two of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a strong, meaningful and loving relationship. This card can represent a young couple just starting out in life, ready to explore the wonders of the world together. It can also signify a deepening of an existing relationship, such as a marriage proposal, an engagement, or even the birth of a baby. The Two of Cups Tarot card is a sign of unity and understanding, and encourages people to create strong bonds with those they care about. It speaks of emotional stability, trust, and a deep understanding of each other. It is a card of union and togetherness, showing that two separate individuals can come together and create something beautiful.
