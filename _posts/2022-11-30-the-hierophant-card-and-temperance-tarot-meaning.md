## What does The Hierophant and Temperance mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The Temperance tarot card together speak to the need to find balance between following traditional expectations and standards, and striking out in a unique direction. It is important to stay rooted in our values and traditions, while also making sure to listen to our inner voice and allowing our individual strengths and passions lead the way. We must remain in control of our needs and impulses while also understanding that honoring traditional expectations doesn’t always mean sacrificing our own personal growth or progress. Striking a balance between the two is key.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

When it comes to health, The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that making healthy choices is important. It’s a reminder that staying true to your values and setting boundaries is key for maintaining good physical and mental health. It can be easy to forget to prioritize your health, especially in a fast-paced world where time is limited. But The Hierophant Tarot card is here to remind you to take care of yourself and prioritize your own needs. It tells you to stick with established healthy habits, like a balanced diet and regular exercise, so that you stay strong and energized.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card encourages you to take a practical approach to your finances. It means understanding what kind of money you have and taking steps to create stability and security. Establish a budget and work to stick to it. Consider using conventional methods of saving and investing, such as 401Ks and IRAs. Set up an emergency fund and make sure you’re covered in case of an unexpected event. The Hierophant Tarot card also suggests that you take your time when it comes to finances and not rush into any decisions. Take your time, explore all your options and make informed choices.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card hints at the importance of respecting your relationships and being loyal to those in your life. At the same time, it’s important to set boundaries for yourself and for the relationship. Understand that there can be tremendous value in the traditional methods of writing letters, communicating face-to-face or talking on the phone, rather than relying on digital communication alone. Take the time to create a solid framework for your relationships, including understanding what each person wants and needs from the relationship. Respect that relationships are complicated, require patience and can take time to develop.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card can represent a call for balance in all areas of life, including physical health, financial stability, and interpersonal relationships. Health-wise, the Temperance Tarot card suggests that we pay attention to our body and create a healthy balance between rest and activity, indulgence and moderation, and inwards and outwards focus. Self-care and compassion should be prioritized, and we should be mindful of the boundaries that we have. Financially, the Temperance Tarot card encourages us to think before we act, and to be disciplined with our spending, saving, and investments in order to create a balance between more security and larger purchases. It also suggests that we not get ahead of ourselves and act responsibly with our finances. Relationships-wise, the Temperance Tarot card encourages us to find a balance between our autonomy and our feelings for others. We should strive for self-control, respect boundaries, and practice patience to ensure healthy relationships and healthy dialogue. Additionally, it suggests that we take the time to really listen to those we care about and understand their perspectives. In summary, the Temperance Tarot card advises us to work towards balance in our lives and to respect boundaries in all aspects of our lives; physical, emotional, mental, and financial.
