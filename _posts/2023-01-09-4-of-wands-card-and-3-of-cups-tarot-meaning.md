## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

When it comes to health, the 4 of Wands Tarot card suggests that one should be in a steady state of good health. This card signifies a period of well-being which may last for quite some time. The card also implies that it is a good time to look after one’s health through proper nutrition, exercise, and relaxation. There are also possibilities of some minor illnesses like a cold or flu, but they should pass quickly and the person should enjoy a robust state of health.

The 4 of Wands Tarot card also has implications for one’s finances. It suggests that the current financial situation is stable, and that there is no need to worry too much about money. This card can also mean that there is potential for a financial windfall, but that it is best to remain conservative in spending during this period.

When it comes to relationships, the 4 of Wands Tarot card signifies a period of joyous celebration. This card can signify happy reunions with family, friends and loved ones. It symbolizes a time for bonding with others and celebrating one’s successes. This card can also indicate that one should take time to nurture and strengthen their relationships as it is a period of stability and happiness.

Overall, the 4 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of good health, financial stability and strong relationships. This card is a reminder to enjoy and nurture these positive areas of life and to embrace the special moments that come with them.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The Three of Cups is a card that represents celebration and joy, so it can be a very positive sign for relationships, finances and health. In terms of relationships, The Three of Cups indicates the importance of having strong, supportive friendships. It suggests that one should cultivate friendships that can offer strength and stability when needed. It also indicates that it is important to celebrate life’s successes with friends and family.

In terms of finances, The Three of Cups suggests that one should not forget to find joy in the process of managing finances. This could mean taking the time to celebrate small successes, such as having the money to pay off a student loan or save money for retirement. It could also suggest taking a break from the grind of everyday money management and enjoying the simple pleasures of life.

Finally, in terms of health, The Three of Cups can mean taking the time to celebrate the small milestones in one’s health journey. Whether it is making it to a yoga class or sticking to a nutrition plan, it suggests that one should take the time to appreciate the small wins, as they can offer motivation to keep going. Additionally, The Three of Cups indicates that having a strong circle of support is essential to success in this realm, as friends and family can offer support and cheer when things get tough.
