A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Emperor card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Emperor card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Emperor and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Emperor Tarot card and The 6 of Swords Tarot card speaks to the need for strategic planning and decision-making during a period of necessary transition. You may be moving or relocating, and it is important to take a step back to assess the situation before you make any big decisions. You will need to be a leader during this time and draw on the guidance and wise counsel of someone in a father-figure role. With planning and respect, you can make sure that this period of transition is as smooth and successful as possible.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Emperor mean?

The Emperor Tarot card carries a message of respect, leadership, strategic planning and strength. When The Emperor card is pulled in a Tarot spread, it can signify a need to take charge of a situation and bring order, discipline and structure to it on a personal and/or larger scale level.

In terms of health, The Emperor card indicates that it is time to take responsibility for one’s health, to be rigorous and disciplined. This could mean taking steps to create a healthy lifestyle by getting enough sleep, maintaining a healthy diet, and exercising. It could also mean creating boundaries and developing healthy coping strategies for managing stress.

Financially, The Emperor card speaks to the importance of taking a leadership role in creating a secure financial future. It suggests careful planning, budgeting, and making wise investments for the future. It also speaks to taking control of one’s spending habits, being realistic about financial goals, and pursuing smart money management strategies.

In relationships, The Emperor card suggests that one step into a leadership role to help steer the relationship in a positive direction. This could mean taking a strategic approach to problem solving, developing better communication skills, and setting healthy boundaries. It can also be a call to take ownership of one’s role in the relationship, be it as a partner, friend, or family member.

The Emperor card is a reminder that strength, order and discipline are necessary aspects of both our personal and collective well-being. It empowers us to take responsibility for our lives and to lead with clarity, purpose and direction.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, the 6 of Swords tarot card is a reminder to focus on aspects of personal well-being, such as physical and mental health, diet and exercise. Taking time for self-care will not only allow you to better cope with the stress that is associated with a transition, but it can also provide a steadying anchor in any storm. The card can also symbolize a need to detach from a certain situation or way of life, and focus on making necessary changes. This might mean developing healthier habits, such as eating clean, exercising regularly and making time for leisure activities. Financially, the 6 of Swords can bring news of a new move, or a relocation. The card suggests that it is time to make preparations for the transition, such as cutting back on expenses or providing for a new job search in the area. Lastly, the 6 of Swords tarot card speaks to the importance of relationships in the process of change. Being supported by those around you can make an upheaval much easier, so try to maintain connections, however brief, with the people in your life. Allow yourself to ask for assistance if needed, as having a strong network of people around you can help transform what could otherwise be a stressful time.
