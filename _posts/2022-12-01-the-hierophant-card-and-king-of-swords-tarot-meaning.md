## What does The Hierophant and King Of Swords mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The King Of Swords tarot card combined represent the push-pull between tradition and intentionality. On one hand, the Hierophant speaks to the need to honor and uphold traditional values, expectations, and conformities to anchor us. On the other, the King of Swords encourages us to think and act strategically, with thoughtfulness and purpose. The balance between the two cards is telling us that it is possible to use our creative minds to find the right balance between honoring traditional expectations while also taking charge and bringing fresh ideas to the table.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

**Both The Hierophant and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health  
The Hierophant card often suggests a need for traditions and conventional methods for improving one’s health. This could be anything from sticking to a regular exercise routine to taking set doses of vitamins and supplements that are proven to be effective. It could also mean that people should listen to their doctors’ opinions and advice and follow conventional treatments if they need it. This card might also suggest that people should not take shortcuts when it comes to their health and should always opt for the safest and most reliable methods instead of trying new fad treatments.

Finances  
The Hierophant card will often indicate that people should follow certain accepted financial practices in order to ensure their financial stability. This could mean that people should save regularly, invest in reliable investments, and avoid getting into too much debt. It also suggests that people should take advice from financial advisors and stay away from risky investments that could lead to a financial loss. This card might also suggest that people should set a budget and stick to it in order to maintain their financial stability.

Relationships  
The Hierophant card could suggest that people should follow tradition when it comes to relationships. This could mean that people should not shy away from commitment, fidelity, and the other societal expectations when it comes to relationships. It also suggests that people should respect each other and remain honest regardless of the type of relationship they are in. This card could also indicate that people should seek help from experts such as counselors if they need guidance in their relationships.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The King of Swords is a card of mental clarity and discipline. It reminds you that you have the power to take control of your health, to choose healthy habits and strategies, and to make positive changes that will have a lasting impact. When it appears in a reading, it urges you to take a hard look at your lifestyle and to make adjustments that will promote a healthier version of yourself. This card suggests that such self-discipline and taking responsibility for your own wellbeing will bring you greater overall health and balance in your life.

Finances:  
The King of Swords is a card of logic and efficiency. It reminds you that you have the power to take control of your finances, to make wise decisions and to stick to a plan. When it appears in a reading, it urges you to take a rational look at your financial situation and to be mindful of how you spend money. This card suggests that setting a realistic budget and investing in yourself and your future will bring you financial stability and security.

Relationships:  
The King of Swords is a card of open communication and understanding. It reminds you that you have the power to take control of your relationships, to be honest and direct, and to respect each other’s boundaries. When it appears in a reading, it urges you to have meaningful conversations that help build understanding and trust. This card suggests that learning to listen to each other and discovering the common ground between you will bring a sense of harmony and peace to your relationships.
