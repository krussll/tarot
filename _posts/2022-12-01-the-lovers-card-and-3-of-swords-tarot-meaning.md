A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Lovers tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card symbolizes the power and fragility of relationships. The Lovers tarot card suggests that two people can form a strong bond and experience deep love, while the 3 Of Swords tarot card warns that heartbreak and loss can be a potential result of that partnership. At the same time, it can be seen as a reminder that with this risk comes an opportunity for growth through making difficult decisions. Whether it is a partnership or a breakup, it provides a chance to learn and grow stronger in the end.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Lovers and 3 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Lovers tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card can represent a very difficult experience in love. When these two cards are combined, it can suggest a partnership that is facing hard times. The Lovers tarot card can mean that when two people come together, they are a powerful force that can overcome any challenge. However, The 3 Of Swords card can mean that a difficult choice needs to be made and that the partnership is in danger of heartbreak, divorce, loss, or depression. This could represent the couple struggling to make a choice, having to part ways, or being in a situation where one person is feeling deeply hurt or betrayed.

This difficult combination of cards could also represent a situation where both parties are completely committed to making the relationship work, even though there may be pain and sorrow in the process. It can be a reminder to each person in the relationship to be honest and stay strong for one another, even during hard times. It can be a reminder that if the two of them come together, the strength of their partnership can help them through any situation.

For those seeking guidance from their Tarot cards, The Lovers and The 3 Of Swords could be a sign that they are being asked to make a difficult decision, or a choice between what can bring joy or pain. It could be time to take a chance, or to make a hard decision. It could also be a reminder to be patient and trust in the power of partnership, as it can help in difficult times. Ultimately, this combination of tarot cards could mean that the couple will have to put in the hard work and dedication to make the relationship work, no matter how difficult the path may be.

### What does The Lovers and 3 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

The Lovers tarot card and the 3 of Swords tarot card together tell a complex story about finances and your job. When combined, these two cards can be interpreted as a warning to tread with caution in both financial and professional pursuits.

The Lovers card speaks of partnership and the strength of two together. When it comes to financial matters, this card advises that cooperation and collaboration, rather than individual action, is the key to success. It implies that working together with a partner or team, rather than relying solely on your own skills, will bring a greater success.

The 3 of Swords card speaks of heartbreak, loss, depression, and surgery. This can be interpreted to mean that when approaching financial and professional matters, it’s important to be prepared for change and potential pitfalls. Even if times seem rosy and the prospects appear favorable, there can always be unforeseen obstacles and losses. It’s important to plan ahead and have a backup plan in the event that the situation turns sour.

When it comes to how these two cards relate in terms of finances and your job, the key takeaway is to approach these areas with caution and an understanding that nothing is certain. By collaborating and planning for potential setbacks and changes, you can better prepare yourself for the future.

### What does The Lovers and 3 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The Lovers tarot card paired with the 3 Of Swords tarot card can represent a difficult decision that has to be made affecting your health. On the one hand, the Lovers tarot card symbolizes the strength of two together, which can be interpreted as the strength of partnership in regards to health. This could mean that you have a supportive partner that is helping you to make good health decisions, or that you are relying on the combined strength of two parties to make a life changing decision.

On the other hand, the 3 Of Swords card symbolizes heartbreak, as well as depression and loss, in connection with your health. This card could be indicating that you are facing a dilemma that requires choosing between two difficult options. You may have to make a decision that involves giving up something important in order to prioritize your health. The decision could be refusing risky treatments or activities, or it could be something more subtle, like choosing to invest in health insurance or make a lifestyle change.

In either case, the Lovers tarot card paired with the 3 Of Swords tarot card implies that a difficult decision has to be made in regards to your health. The combination of the two cards suggests that even though the decision may come with a certain amount of heartbreak, if you choose wisely and rely on the strength of two, you will be able to make the best decision for your health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

The meaning of The Lovers Tarot card for health is that of balance. This card is a reminder to pause and make healthy decisions for a balanced and holistic lifestyle. When you consider the health of your mind, body and spirit holistically, you can come to a place of equilibrium. The Lovers Tarot card encourages us to take steps to achieve a healthy balance by tuning into our own needs, desires and goals.

The Lovers Tarot card for finances is a sign of wise financial planning and the unity that comes from working together with a partner. This card suggests that there is strength and opportunity in the power of two, and encourages us to budget, save and strategize our finances with someone we trust.

The Lovers Tarot card in a relationship can be interpreted as a sign of connection, loyalty, and deep commitment. Working together with a partner in a relationship can bring new insight, as long as both partners are committed to their individual roles. This card encourages us to open our hearts, extend trust, and heal any wounds that may hinder a relationship’s success. The Lovers Tarot card advises that when two people come together with mutual trust and respect, they can create an atmosphere of growth and harmony that can nurture the relationship.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health: The Three of Swords Tarot card has a lot to say when it comes to one’s physical health. This card can signify that you are dealing with a terminal illness, or long-term illness or injury. It can also signify depression, emotional pain, and stress-related health issues. The Three of Swords often represents surgeries and treatments that one goes through in order to heal, and can refer to the emotional trauma that may accompany these difficult times. It can be a reminder to practice self-care, to listen to one’s inner wisdom, and to reach out for help if needed.

Finances: When this card appears in a reading, it can indicate that one is faced with financial hardship or loss. It may indicate bankruptcy or foreclosure, unexpected expenses, or unforeseen events that cost one money. It also can speak to a need to be more disciplined and organized with one’s finances. This card may be a reminder to slow down, to take a look at where one’s money is going, and to make sure that one is budgeting properly.

Relationships: The Three of Swords can indicate a troubled relationship, a separation, divorce, or the end of a friendship. It can also signify emotional pain, heartache, and betrayal. It may reflect a need to take a step back and look at the relationship as a whole, and to understand one’s part in it. It may also be a reminder to be gentle, to practice understanding and acceptance, and to seek help if needed.
