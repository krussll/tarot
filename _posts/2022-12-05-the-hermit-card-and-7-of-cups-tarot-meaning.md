## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 7 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 7 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health:

The Hermit Tarot card speaks of a time of introspection, self-reflection and turning inward for solace and answers. When it comes to health, The Hermit card suggests an opportunity for deep personal exploration into physical and mental wellbeing. This can be a period of healing and self-care. An individual should take the time to come to terms with their emotional and physical issues, allowing for understanding and gaining clarity in order to manage any health issues with greater success.

Finances:

The Hermit Tarot card can represent a period of rest for financial matters, or even a financial retreat. It could indicate that now is a time to hold back from making any big financial decisions and instead to use this period to look inward and gain insight into one’s current financial position. This could be a good time to review current budget and spending habits, research new financial strategies and gain more information on opportunities and investments.

Relationships:

The Hermit Tarot card can symbolize a time of growth, in terms of personal relationships and connections. This card can be viewed as a call to take some time alone, to reflect on and assess current relationships in an effort to gain deeper understanding and insight into them. Taking a step back and having some space to think can often be beneficial to relationships as it can provide perspective and lead to greater clarity and communication. Additionally, this period of introspection may provide an opening and understanding of the self and how to better interact with others.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 7 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a lack of focus and commitment when it comes to health and fitness. It can indicate an inability to make decisions, leaving you feeling overwhelmed and unable to decide which habits and patterns to pursue in order to improve your wellbeing. The card is warning you against delving too deeply into a wide variety of treatments, diets, and exercise regimes without fully understanding the potential risks and benefits that may arise from each path. Trying to simultaneously change too much at once can leave you feeling exhausted and distracted, and lead to burnout.

Finances: The 7 of Cups Tarot card illustrates a lack of confidence in your ability to make sound financial decisions. This could be indicative of compulsive spending, a perception of scarcity, or hesitance to embrace financial change. It may also be a sign that you’re too focused on the short-term and not investing in the future. The card cautions against frivolously spending or becoming too attached to material objects, instead encouraging you to create a solid plan with achievable goals that you can work towards.

Relationships: The 7 of Cups Tarot card indicates a lack of clarity regarding relationships at this time, and warns that indecision and fantasy about potential outcomes can leave you feeling overwhelmed and unable to take action. This can cause relationships to stagnate, either preventing a relationship from progressing or keeping you in a stagnant state of wishing and hoping that something will change in your current relationship. Rather than holding onto ideals and fantasies of what could be, the card encourages you to look at your relationships realistically and with a clear head, if you want to move forward.
