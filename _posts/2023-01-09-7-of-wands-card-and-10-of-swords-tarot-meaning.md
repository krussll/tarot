## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 Of Wands Tarot card is a card of self-protection and protection against competition. It symbolizes the need to stand up for one’s needs and to fight against those who try to keep you down. In terms of health, the 7 Of Wands card could be interpreted as representing the need to take care of oneself and protect oneself against any potential illnesses or health issues. This could mean taking extra precautions when it comes to exercise and diet, as well as protecting oneself from environmental toxins, and getting regular check-ups.

When it comes to finances, the 7 Of Wands suggests the need to protect oneself from financial rivals and competitors. It could mean taking extra steps and precautions to safeguard your money and assets from those who may try to take advantage of you, or from those who may be worse off than you. This could mean things like increasing your savings, only taking on calculated risks and avoiding putting all your eggs in one basket when it comes to investments.

In terms of relationships, the 7 Of Wands encourages one to stand up for themselves, and to be wary of those who may not have their best interests at heart. It could be interpreted as a sign to be mindful of those around you who may have ulterior motives or be out to cause harm. It could also be a reminder to stay true to yourself and to not let anyone else’s opinion of you dictate your behavior or relationships.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords tarot card is a major arcana card that can represent a rock bottom moment in all aspects of life – health, wealth, and relationships. Health wise, the 10 of swords tarot card can suggest that a person’s physical health has reached its breaking point due to a recent health crisis. In this case, a person may need to seek professional medical treatment, such as therapy or medication, in order to manage their health and get back on their feet. Financially, the 10 of swords tarot card can suggest that a person has reached their financial limit and is struggling to keep up with bills and expenses. A person may need to find creative ways to make ends meet, such as taking on a second job or selling items they no longer use or need.

In terms of relationships, the 10 of swords tarot card can suggest that a person’s relationships have reached a breaking point. In this case, a person may need to step back and analyze their relationships in order to identify what needs to change. This could include having honest and open conversations with friends and loved ones in order to address any underlying issues. Alternatively, this card could suggest that a person is experiencing extreme loneliness, in which case they may need to focus on developing a stronger sense of self before focusing on developing relationships.

Overall, the 10 of swords tarot card can represent a painful and difficult period in someone’s life, but it can also suggest that a person has the strength and perseverance to overcome this rock bottom situation. With the right tools, support, and self-discipline, a person can work to improve their health, financial security, and relationships, allowing them to move past a painful time in their life.
