It could also signify a new relationship on the horizon, be it a proposal, a marriage, or the conception of a new life. Either way, you are about to step into a period of transition and growth.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 Of Swords Tarot card, also known as “The Transition”, is associated with the need to leave a situation in order to make way for a healthier and better future. In terms of health, this card can indicate the need to bring about a fresh perspective or the idea of making a lifestyle change for the better. This means that it may be time to begin a new exercise regimen, make better nutrition choices, or start practicing relaxation techniques in order to bring about an improvement in overall emotional and physical health.

In terms of finances, The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can suggest that it is time to make a big change in order to move forward. This can refer to something as simple as a change in budgeting habits, or something more drastic such as a move to a more affordable place to live. This card can also be interpreted as a sign to make a major career change that could possibly lead to greater financial security.

Lastly, The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can have profound implications for relationships. This card suggests that it may be time to end a destructive relationship or to make a transition in an existing one. It can also be a warning that codependent behavior or toxic behavior needs to be released in order to make way for a healthier future. It can even be interpreted that it may be time to move on from a past relationship that has become unhealthy or stagnant in order to make way for a new and fulfilling relationship.

Overall, The 6 Of Swords Tarot card indicates the need to take a serious look at one’s life and make some big changes in order to bring about a healthier and more secure future. These changes can refer to physical, financial, and relationship issues, and can indicate that it’s time to make some major moves in order to bring about a better tomorrow.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

The Ace of Cups Tarot card is often seen as a symbol of new beginnings, especially concerning health, finances, and relationships. When it comes to health, the card is a positive indicator of new possibilities and opportunities, with the potential for healing and renewal. On a health level, it may represent a fresh start with improved mental and physical health and general wellbeing.

When it comes to finances, the Ace of Cups signals new opportunities and improved circumstances. It can suggest a new business venture or financial investments that ultimately lead to increased wealth and stability. It’s also a sign that existing debts and obligations can be settled, leading to more freedom and security.

When it comes to relationships, the Ace of Cups is a card of profound love and of new romantic beginnings. It can suggest a deep and meaningful connection between two individuals, often marked by the beginning of a new relationship or the strengthening of an existing one. It can also suggest the possibility of marriage or a proposal and the promise of a long and prosperous union.
