A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Justice tarot card and The 9 Of Cups tarot card is a powerful one. It is a reminder that when we seek justice and solutions that benefit everyone, we are more likely to achieve our wishes, abundance and good health. The truth will come out and the solutions that are found will benefit all parties in the end. This is a reminder to set our intentions for win-win solutions and to stay committed to achieving them. This combination card offers a reminder that when we are devoted to doing the right thing, good things will come our way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card can be very beneficial to our health when we look at it from a cause and effect perspective. By understanding the root causes of our physical ailments, we can then address them directly and have a greater effect in improving our health. This can be done through nutrition, exercise, and lifestyle choices. The Justice Tarot card also encourages us to seek justice from the medical community and ensure that we are not only seeking treatment for our current conditions but for the conditions we may develop in the future.

Finances

When looking at the Justice Tarot card regarding finances, it is important to remember that money is a tool to be used in a way that will benefit both ourselves and others. This card reminds us to consider the cause and effect when it comes to money and to be mindful of where our money is going and why. Money should be used in a way that is fair and equitable, in order to create a win-win solution for all involved.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card encourages us to use discernment and truth in all of our relationships. It is important to be honest in our communication and to seek out win-win solutions rather than attempting to manipulate or control our relationships. We must also be mindful of where our relationships are headed and to have a clear understanding of the cause and effect of our interactions. With this understanding, we can ensure that all of our relationships grow in a way that is beneficial to both parties and that each person is respected and heard.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 9 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes good health, both in body and in mind. It suggests that any health issues you may be experiencing can be alleviated, or resolved entirely. The card suggests that when it comes to your health and wellbeing, you have a great deal to be thankful for, and should take a moment to appreciate your body and the gift of good health. The card advises that you should nurture yourself, and trust that things will work out for the best.

Finances

This card promises rewards and material abundance. It indicates that your financial situation is likely to be positive, with plenty of opportunities for growth and expansion. It suggests that you remain open to the possibilities for wealth and comfort that life brings, and that you trust the process of financial security and prosperity. The 9 of Cups Tarot card is also a reminder to save and plan for the future, so that you will be ready to take advantage of any opportunity that presents itself.

Relationships

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is an auspicious sign when it comes to relationships. It symbolizes harmonious and harmonizing energies, suggesting that you can look forward to relationship bliss. Whether it’s a business or romantic relationship, it’s likely to be a positive one, with plenty of mutual respect and understanding. The card also suggests that you should take a moment to appreciate the people in your life who you can rely on and trust.
