A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and Page Of Cups mean together?

The 5 of Wands tarot card and the Page of Cups tarot card mean that it is time for you to challenge yourself and test your skills in a competitive environment. You should expect to come up against some obstacles and rivals, and you need to muster the courage and determination to succeed despite any barriers and blocks. At the same time, you should not forget to reach out to others and foster the relationships that can help you along the way. Show kindness to those around you, find something new to love, and volunteer your time and talents to a cause that you care about, and you will increase your chances of success.

Know that no matter what happens, you can handle it and that you will win in the end. Even if you don’t get the outcome you desire, the journey itself will be worth it and you will learn something valuable for the future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Wands and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 5 of Wands tarot card, when related to health, indicates that you have physical or mental challenges ahead of you. You may be feeling like you are in a race with yourself, or you may be comparing your health to that of others around you. You may be feeling the pressure to constantly be improving in order to keep up. You need to focus on your own health journey, learning to accept yourself and not letting others dictate your goals.

Finances:

The 5 of Wands tarot card, when related to finances, indicates that you will be facing obstacles in managing and growing your wealth. This may manifest itself in the form of competition, where you are vying with other individuals for access to the same resources. It may also appear as the challenge of sticking to a budget and having to deal with unforeseen expenses. It is important to maintain your focus and vision, putting your energy into creative and innovative solutions that will aid you in achieving your financial goals.

Relationships:

The 5 of Wands tarot card, when related to relationships, indicates that there may be competition for your attention and affections. You may be dealing with the challenge of balancing between the different aspects of your life. You may also be feeling the pressure of having to adhere to certain expectations and norms set by others. It is important to stay true to yourself and constantly remind yourself of what you value in your relationships. It is also helpful to remember that ultimately, any rivalry in your relationships should be used as a way to grow together, pushing each other to become the best versions of yourselves.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Page of Cups points to a period of emotional healing, mental clarity and overall well-being. It is a sign that any physical ailment will pass, and the area of concern will be improved. This is a reminder to take care of your health – listen to your body, and make sure you take the time to nurture both your mental and physical health. Regular exercise, balanced diet and enough rest are essential for overall wellbeing.

Finances:  
The Page of Cups indicates a period of financial stability. It symbolizes that, although your finances may be a bit tight, any issues that have been causing you financial worry will be resolved. It is a time of greater security and stability in your finances. However, this doesn’t mean that you should become complacent – it is important to remain frugal and budget wisely to ensure your financial success.

Relationships:  
The Page of Cups points to a time of growth in relationships. This is a reminder that relationships require work, and if you are willing to put in the time and effort, then your relationships can thrive. This card advises that you take care in how you build and nurture relationships. It is a time to be open, patient and understanding. Be kind, show compassion, and listen. With the help of this card, you can foster deeper connections with your loved ones.
