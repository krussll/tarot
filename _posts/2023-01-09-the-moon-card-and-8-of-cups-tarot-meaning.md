However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does The Moon and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Moon and the 8 of Cups tarot cards are an interesting combination and suggest that you may be dealing with some unseen issues and may need to take a retreat and move on from your current path in life. Trusting your intuition is key in this situation as it can serve as a guiding light during times of confusion and uncertainty. The 8 of Cups indicates that abandoning your current situation may be necessary in order to make room to grow and reach the next phase in your life. It could be the perfect time to take a step back and assess the changes that need to be made in order to positively progress your life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Moon and 8 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Moon and 8 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Moon mean?

The Moon Tarot card is a card that speaks of unseen problems, voluntary changes and trusting your intuition. When this card turns up in a reading it may indicate that issues you face may seem confusing or obscure, and that you should trust your intuition and be willing to make changes in your life in order to move forward.

Health: The Moon Tarot card can indicate that any health issues you might be dealing with can be overcome if you trust your intuition and be willing to make changes that may be necessary. It could mean getting to the root of issues like why you keep getting sick, or what might be responsible for your health issues in the first place. It could also suggest looking into alternative treatments and lifestyle changes to improve your health and wellbeing.

Finances: The Moon Tarot card speaks of trusting your intuition and making changes when it comes to your finances. This could involve getting to the root of any problems and creating opportunities to save money. It could also be a sign that it is time to invest in yourself and make changes to get your finances on the right track. This might include budgeting, setting up a savings plan, or even researching ways to invest your money wisely.

Relationships: The Moon Tarot card could be a sign that it is time to take a look at any relationship issues that may be present. It could mean taking a closer look at how you interact with your partner and be willing to make changes when necessary. It could also be a sign that it is time to reassess your friendships and strengthen the ones that matter most. More than that, it could suggest that it is time to trust your own intuition when it comes to relationships and follow the advice of your inner voice.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

The 8 of Cups Tarot card can suggest a sense of retreat or abandonment in a variety of situations. In the context of health, it could mean a physical or mental exhaustion which needs to be addressed. You may need to take a break or withdraw from some of your activities in order to re-energize and rejuvenate yourself. This could also represent a decision to completely change your lifestyle in order to restore your wellbeing.

In terms of finances, The 8 of Cups Tarot card suggests that you may need to cut back on some of your spending and start saving for the future. It could be time to move on from some investments that are no longer producing the desired returns. You may also need to take a step back in order to reassess your financial goals before making any further investments.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The 8 of Cups Tarot card could indicate that you need to take a break or withdraw from the situation. This could mean deciding to end an unhealthy relationship, or simply taking some time for yourself in order to re-evaluate the situation. It could be time to move on and explore other opportunities, or it could be a time of reflection and growth. The 8 of Cups Tarot card can represent a time of letting go and moving on.
