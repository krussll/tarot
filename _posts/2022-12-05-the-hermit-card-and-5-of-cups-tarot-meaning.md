## What does The Hermit and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of the Hermit and the 5 of Cups represents a need to take time out to process the emotions of loss, regret, and grief. It’s an invitation to go within, to reflect on the feelings of being abandoned and unloved and to look for a deeper, spiritual illumination that can offer a sense of clarity and peace of mind. Taking time away from the chaos of the world and allowing yourself to experience your emotions fully can be surprisingly healing, and ultimately lead to a greater understanding of yourself and your situation.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Taking the time to evaluate one’s health habits, and create healthier ones, can lead to a better sense of self-awareness and physical wellbeing.

With regards to finances, the Hermit Tarot card can signify a time to take a step back and take a hard look at one’s financial situation. This could include examining spending habits and coming up with a plan to better handle and manage money. The Hermit’s message here is to be honest with oneself and make decisions based on both short and long-term financial goals. This can help to increase financial stability, while also allowing individuals to make well-informed decisions with their money.

In terms of relationships, the Hermit Tarot card encourages individuals to take a step back to reflect upon their own values and needs, as well as the needs of those close to them. This can mean setting aside some time for introspection and understanding how one’s relationships are currently affecting their wellbeing. The card can also symbolize the need for honest communication in order to ensure relationships are healthy and respectful. Taking the time to evaluate one’s relationships can lead to greater clarity and understanding of how to improve them.

The Hermit Tarot card can provide guidance and insight into how to improve one’s health, finances, and relationships. Taking the time to take a step back, evaluate one’s current situation, and make changes accordingly can lead to greater clarity, fulfillment, and stability.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

The 5 of Cups tarot card can be interpreted as a symbol of loss and regret in regards to one’s health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, the card can indicate a physical or emotional trauma experienced, such as an injury or illness, that may have caused a setback in one’s overall wellbeing. It can also symbolize a feeling of helplessness or the inability to cope with the effects of an illness or injury. Financially, the card can suggest a sudden loss or sudden and unexpected setback. This can range from the loss of a job or major financial investments, to dealing with expensive medical bills or other financial obligations.

In terms of relationships, the 5 of Cups can represent a loss, be it the death of a loved one, the breakdown of a relationship, or a friendship that has been broken. It is a reminder of the importance of nurturing the relationships we have with the people around us and to be mindful of the consequences that a loss can bring. It is also a reminder to not take anything for granted, and to cherish the moments we have with the people we care about. The 5 of Cups can also be interpreted as an opportunity to learn how to grow and move forward, despite the pain and sadness of a loss. It encourages us to pick ourselves up and continue on, despite the obstacles we may face.
