A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Pentacles card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 5 Of Pentacles card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 5 Of Pentacles and The 9 Of Swords tarot card together can be a difficult combination. They can indicate a period of worry, financial loss, and isolation that can lead to feelings of deep depression, anxiety and fear. The cards represent a person who is so overwhelmed with fear and worry that they can barely cope with the daily stresses of life. This person may feel isolated and alone, unable to shake the feeling of despair even if they have a support system around them. Ill health may be a factor as the stress builds, and this person may have difficulty seeing any hope for the future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It may be an indication of impending financial loss or illness, resulting in isolation and worry. This could manifest as a struggle for security and stability for yourself, your partner, or both of you. It might be hard to stay positive and hopeful in moments like these and it’s important to remember that you don’t have to handle this situation alone.

The Nine of Swords card suggests feelings of anxiety, depression, and fear, making it especially challenging to work through issues like money and health. This card warns against the potential for nightmares and lingering grief. If you or your partner find yourselves struggling with difficult emotions, it’s important to reach out for help and support.

These cards do not predict disaster, but instead serve as a warning sign that the challenges you face in your relationship will be considerable. However, they also indicate a possibility for strength if you are able to weather the storm. If you can make it through and come out the other side, your bond will be stronger, and your understanding of yourself and your partner will be greater.

The 9 of Swords is an even gloomier image that symbolizes deep worries, grief and depression. Do not neglect self-care; focusing on health and wellbeing during this time is essential for maintaining physical and mental energy. This can help to restore feelings of control and power, which in turn can help a person to make informed decisions concerning their finances and job. Above all, the combination of the 5 of Pentacles and the 9 of Swords ask the querent to believe in themselves and to dig deep. There may be tough times ahead, but with the right mindset, one can work towards a brighter and more secure future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Pentacles and 9 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of ill health, poverty, worry, isolation, and financial loss. It is a difficult card to interpret, as it cautions against the potential pitfalls of life, but also poses a challenge to overcome these difficult times. In terms of health, the card suggests that an individual may be dealing with some form of physical illness or injury that is having a profound effect on their wellbeing. It is important to remember that the card is not a sign of impending doom, but more a reminder to take care of one’s physical and mental health.

When it comes to finances, the 5 of Pentacles Tarot card can mean that an individual is experiencing some kind of financial hardship. It could be anything from a sudden loss of job or a significant decrease in income. It is a warning to be mindful of how money is being spent and to prioritize spending on the most important areas of life. It is also a sign to save money for a rainy day and not to take financial risks.

Finally, the 5 of Pentacles Tarot card means worry and isolation when it comes to relationships. This could be a reminder that it is important to nurture relationships and build strong connections with others. It is a red flag that an individual may be struggling to build meaningful relationships or struggling to connect with those closest to them. It is a warning to invest time and energy into building relationships rather than allowing them to slip away.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 Of Swords Tarot card is associated with feelings of anxiety, grief, and depression. This card is a warning that these negative feelings, if left unchecked, can lead to further problems.

Health: The 9 Of Swords Tarot card is a warning of the potential health impacts caused by these negative emotions. Anxiety and depression can cause physical symptoms such as headaches, muscle tension, and fatigue, as well as sleep disturbances. They can also have a profound impact on your overall mental health, leading to feelings of worthlessness and hopelessness. It is important to address these issues with a professional, or with the support of a trusted friend or family member, to prevent the health consequences of these negative emotions.

Finances: The 9 Of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to be mindful of how our emotions can affect our finances. When we are feeling overwhelmed, it can be tempting to make impulsive decisions, such as overspending, that can lead to financial distress. It can also be difficult to focus on our finances and make sound decisions when dealing with depression or anxiety. Taking the time to practice self-care and positive coping strategies can help us manage these emotions in a more productive way, protecting us from the financial consequences.

Relationships: The 9 Of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to pay attention to how our negative emotions can impact our relationships. Depression and anxiety can lead to feelings of isolation and can make it difficult to express ourselves in a healthy way. It is important to take the time to nurture our relationships, even when we are feeling down, in order to prevent long-term damage. Connecting with trusted friends, engaging in activities that bring us joy, and taking the time to practice self-care can also help us manage our emotions in a more productive way and have a positive impact on our relationships.
