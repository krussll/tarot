A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 of Wands tarot card and the 8 of Cups tarot card combined suggest a time of transition in your life. After much success and recognition, you may now feel inclined to withdraw, or retreat, from the current situation and move on to something bigger and better. Despite the withdrawal, you still have the sense of victory and recognition that the 6 of Wands tarot card brings with it. It may be time to say goodbye to something that has been successful, but you can look back at it with a sense of accomplishment and pride. The combined message of these two cards is that it is time to take a leap of faith and move on to something new.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 6 Of Wands Tarot card is indicative of good health and vitality. It symbolizes the victory that comes with successful health management decisions and lifestyle habits. This card can indicate that a person is taking proactive steps to maintain their wellness and well-being, either through taking up a new health regimen or exercise routine, or by implementing a healthier diet. Additionally, the 6 Of Wands Tarot card can signify that a person has overcome a health challenge, achieved a milestone in their recovery, or overcome an illness.

Finances:

The 6 Of Wands Tarot card can represent financial success, good news, and recognition. This card indicates that a person is taking positive steps towards achieving their financial goals, is managing their money well, and is making the right choices for their financial future. This card could also signify that a person is about to receive an award or recognition for their hard work, or that a financial opportunity is about to come their way.

Relationships:

When the 6 Of Wands Tarot card appears in a reading relating to relationships, it can signify that a person is receiving recognition or reward in their relationships. In particular, it can symbolize that appreciation is being shown between romantic partners. It could indicate that a relationship is blossoming and a couple is enjoying a renewed sense of connection and understanding. This card can also indicate a new relationship that is starting off on the right foot and promises much potential. This card can also represent a sense of pride and satisfaction in existing relationships, and the joy that comes with being surrounded by a supportive and loving network of relationships.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

The 8 of Cups Tarot card indicates withdrawing, retreating and moving on. It strongly denotes a period of abandonment and a time where an individual wishes to part ways with their current situation. When this card is present in one’s life, it suggests that it is time to let go of unhealthy attachments, break ties and walk away.

Health

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign that it is time to take a step back and assess one’s physical and mental wellbeing. It could be a sign to get away from people and situations that no longer serve you, and to invest in yourself, your health and your well-being. It suggests that it is time to make choices that prioritize one’s health and wellbeing, and to honor the needs of the body and mind.

Finances

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign to take a break from certain financial entanglements and to reevaluate how one is managing their money and resources. It could indicate that it is time to take a step back from an unsustainable financial situation and to create boundaries to protect one’s financial health. It could also suggest that it is time to break away from a situation or relationship that is not financially viable.

Relationships

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign that it is time to part ways and release any unhealthy attachments that have been hindering one’s growth and wellbeing. It could suggest that it is time to break away from relationships that no longer serve you, to let go of unhealthy attachments, to step away from toxic situations and to break ties with people who no longer bring positive energy into one’s life. It suggests that it is time to invest in relationships that bring out the best in you and to honor the needs of all involved.
