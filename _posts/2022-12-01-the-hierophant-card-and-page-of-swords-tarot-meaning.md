However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and the Page of Swords tarot card together point to a need for a balance between living up to the expectations of society and traditional values, while maintaining a sense of curiosity and open-mindedness. While it is important to honor these societal conventions and expectations, it is also important to maintain an inquisitive attitude and a willingness to challenge established norms in order to develop a unique viewpoint. By using the wisdom of traditions and cultures as a foundation to build on, we can also take a more measured and critical approach to gathering information and making decisions. The combined messages of The Hierophant and The Page of Swords provide guidance for achieving a balance between these two powerful forces.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card can represent traditional approaches to health, where one follows conventional and established norms. This could mean a person having regular check-ups, attending doctor visits and following a prescribed course of medicine if necessary. It could also include sticking to healthy eating habits and taking regular exercise. Generally, the Hierophant Tarot card suggests that one should adhere to tried and tested methods when it comes to health and wellbeing.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card signals conforming to conventional financial practices, such as saving a certain percentage of one’s income, balancing a budget and investing in tried and tested methods. It encourages people to build financial security by following established guidelines and not taking unnecessary risks. The Hierophant Tarot card signifies planning for the future and having a sound financial strategy.

Relationships

In terms of relationships, the Hierophant Tarot card suggests traditional approaches. This could mean committing to monogamous relationships, honoring the sanctity of marriage, or obeying rules established by the church or another spiritual institution. Faithfulness and loyalty are important to the Hierophant Tarot card, along with a respect for accepted norms and an adherence to established values and traditions.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page of Swords Tarot card in terms of health can represent being too curious and gathering too much information about one’s health. It can represent wasting time and money on needless investigations to your health or questioning your doctor or specialist over every little detail of your health. On the flip side, it can also represent being a health savvy person and gathering information to educate yourself on your health. Taking time to research about your health and understanding what is going on with your body can be an empowering experience.

Finances: The Page of Swords Tarot card in terms of finances can mean an individual who is too curious and is constantly gathering information. This could be in the form of researching every financial product on the market or constantly looking for advice on how to make their money work for them. It can also mean being a penny pincher, double-checking every purchase and monitoring the fluctuation of the stock market. On the other hand, it can also represent a person who is knowledgeable about their finances and is taking the right steps to managing their money efficiently.

Relationships: The Page of Swords Tarot card in terms of relationships can represent being overly curious and wanting to know all of the details of how a relationship works. This could mean constantly asking questions or trying to gain intimate knowledge of the other person’s life by prying. It can also mean being a gossip who is always trying to get the inside scoop on other people’s relationships. On the other hand, it can also represent being honest and upfront about relationship issues and actively seeking to understand the other person by asking truthful questions and having meaningful conversations.
