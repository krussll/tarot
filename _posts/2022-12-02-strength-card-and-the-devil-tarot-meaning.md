A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and The Devil card together in particular.

The Strength card and The Devil have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and The Devil mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The Devil tarot card represent a balance between the need for self-love and self-respect and the darker tendencies of addiction and materialism. This speaks to the importance of learning to accept and embrace all parts of yourself – the good and the bad. By understanding and confronting our addictions, we can move towards having a healthier and happier relationship with ourselves, and a greater understanding of our own limitations and strengths. The Strength and The Devil together offer us an opportunity to explore both the light and dark sides of our lives, and to recognize how our decisions and behaviors may be influenced by these two forces.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Strength and The Devil mean together for your love life?

When interpreted together, the Strength tarot card and the Devil tarot card symbolize a need for balance in our love life. When we have an excessive need for material things – when we become greedy, materialistic, or envious – it can create an imbalance between our head and our heart, between ourselves and our partners. When greed and materialism overwhelm our love lives, it can lead to addiction, obsession, and sexual lust.

Developing a sense of self-love, unconditional love and self-respect is the counter to this imbalance. A strong sense of self-love can prevent us from being overly influenced by and dependent on material possessions. Unconditional love allows us to connect with others from an authentic place and focus on our true needs and desires. Self-respect encourages us to set boundaries in our relationships and make decisions for ourselves because we respect our own needs and feelings.

When we get lost in the traps of the Devil tarot card, with its materialism, envy and addiction, it’s important to remember the Strength tarot card and the gifts of self-love, unconditional love and self-respect. These gifts can help us to live authentically and have healthy, balanced relationships in which both parties are respected and loved. Through self-love and respect, we can create relationships that are based on real emotions and deep connections, not superficial desires and possessions.

### What does Strength and The Devil mean together for your finances?

The Strength tarot card and The Devil tarot card can be interpreted together to reflect the importance of having a healthy relationship with your finances and job. These two cards offer insight into the various aspects that can serve to shape our financial and career decisions and how to navigate them with self-love and courage.

When we look at The Strength card, we are reminded to prioritize self-care and self-love in order to make decisions that are beneficial to our long-term success. We should strive to make decisions from a place of self-respect, as this can ensure that our choices have our best interest in mind.

At the same time, The Devil card serves as a reminder to be aware of any potential unhealthy behaviors that may sabotage our financial and career ambitions. These may include allowing greed, envy, or materialism to guide our decisions, or allowing ourselves to fall into addiction or obsessions.

By being mindful of the two cards together, we can ensure that we are making decisions that are rooted in self-love and self-respect, while simultaneously being aware of the potential pitfalls that may arise from focusing too much on material gains or fall into temptation.

Overall, when The Strength tarot card and The Devil tarot card are interpreted together, they offer insight into the importance of balancing our ambition with self-care and awareness. We need to make sure that our decisions come from a place of self-love and respect, while also being aware of the potential consequences of overindulgence. By understanding and reflecting on these two cards together, we can begin to make decisions that will help us cultivate a healthier relationship with our finances and job.

The Strength tarot card and The Devil tarot card can have an important impact on one’s health. It can lead to draining relationships with self and others, and it can be detrimental to our overall wellbeing. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and The Devil mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

When it comes to health and wellness, The Strength Tarot card symbolizes inner strength and courage. It encourages us to reach deep within ourselves to find the tools for healing and wholeness—both in mind and body. It means learning to love and accept ourselves, to listen to our inner guidance, and to have the courage to take care of our health and well-being. This card teaches us that any physical limitations or emotional wounds we may face can be overcome by approaching them with strength, patience, and acceptance.

For finances, the Strength Tarot card suggests that our inner strength and resilience can help us make wise financial decisions. It encourages us to find the courage to take risks, such as starting a new business venture or investing to create long term financial stability. It also suggests creating a budget, understanding our financial circumstances and preparing for a rainy day.

In relationships, The Strength Tarot card implies that love and respect for ourselves is a prerequisite for a healthy, balanced relationship. It encourages us to have the self-confidence to express our needs and desires openly, without fear, and to be honest and kind to those around us, even when it is difficult. It also signifies the importance of maintaining open communication and positive boundaries, both to foster strong relationships and to establish healthy limits.

Overall, The Strength Tarot card represents the power of inner strength and courage. It encourages us to find the courage to take risks, to love ourselves, and to create deep and meaningful relationships with those around us.

## What does The Devil mean?

Health  
The Devil Tarot card is a reminder that our health, both physical and mental, can be impacted by our unhealthy behaviors and/or poor decision making. It can be a sign that we may be struggling with an addiction or obsessions, or indulging in unhealthy habits such as smoking, drinking, or drug use. It can also be evidence of self-destructive behaviors such as over-working or not taking adequate breaks. All of these behaviors can lead to physical and mental health issues, such as depression, anxiety, fatigue, or even physical diseases like heart disease or cancer.

Finances  
The Devil Tarot card can be a sign that our financial wellbeing is being affected by our unhealthy choices or obsessions. It can suggest that we may be stuck in a cycle of excessive spending, materialism, and greed. We may be neglecting more important bills and financial obligations in favor of short-term temptations or rewards. This can lead to us being in serious debt and potentially put our financial security in jeopardy.

Relationships  
The Devil Tarot card can be a sign of unhealthy relationships. It can indicate that we are allowing our obsessions and addictions to dominate our priorities, often at the expense of our relationships. We may be neglecting our partners, family, or friends in favor of our unhealthy habits. We may also be using our relationships to enable our behavior, and ultimately leading to damaged friendships or broken relationships.
