A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and The Moon card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and The Moon have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and The Moon mean together?

The Hermit tarot card in combination with the Moon tarot card is a powerful reminder to take the time to go within, for a quest of personal truth and spiritual illumination. These cards indicate that there may be unseen problems or issues in your life that you need to face and overcome. These problems may require you to make voluntary changes in your outlook and lifestyle to gain clarity and move forward. During this process of self-examination, it is important to listen to and trust your inner voice, intuition and feelings. Embrace this journey as an opportunity to honor yourself and discover your inner strength, guidance and wisdom.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and The Moon mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and The Moon the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

When drawn in relation to health, the Hermit card encourages one to take a step back from the hustle-bustle of life and reflect deeply on their lifestyle and the habits that may or may not be serving them well. It is the perfect time for self-examination, to ask honest questions about one’s current diet, sleep patterns, medical care, and exercise regimen. Additionally, the Hermit card can act as a reminder to seek out guidance when needed. It is important to reach out to healthcare professionals, alternative practitioners, and self-help groups to ensure that one is taking the best possible care of their body and mind.

Additionally, the Hermit card can act as a reminder to seek out guidance when needed. ## What does The Moon mean?

The Moon Tarot card is said to represent unseen problems, voluntary changes, and trusting in your intuition. For the area of health, this card can indicate underlying issues that may not be immediately apparent. These could be physical or emotional and often require time and effort to identify. This could also signify the necessity of adapting to changes in lifestyle that could lead to physical and mental well-being. Pursuing a healthier lifestyle could involve diet, exercise, or other therapeutic activities that lead to an improved quality of life.

For finances, this tarot card could indicate a need to heed internal advice. This could be advice regarding budgeting, investments, or debt. It could signify the need to trust in intuition when making financial decisions. The changes associated with the card could refer to taking risks or making voluntary changes in order to make better financial decisions. This could be hypothetically backing out of a bad investment or opting in to a new savings plan.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, this tarot card could bear importance in recognizing trust issues. It can represent watching for signs of toxic behavior or allowing for growth in relationships. The changes that come with the card could refer to cutting ties with certain individuals or making a commitment to a new relationship. It could also mean being able to make decisions based on intuition rather than relying on what others may say or do. Going by the gut can be a strong indicator within relationships, and The Moon Tarot card encourages this. Overall, this card can be a sign of growth in all areas of life, whether physical, financial, or relationship-based.
