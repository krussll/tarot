## What does Justice and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 7 Of Swords tarot card together signify the need for honest communication, cause and effect relationships, and win-win solutions. The truth must come out in order to create a balance between all parties involved. Dishonesty and betrayal will cause an unbalanced relationship and lead to negative outcomes and running away from the situation is not a viable solution. It is important to be truthful and work towards a shared understanding for everyone to benefit from.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card is rich in meaning for all aspects of life. In terms of health, the card emphasizes the importance of taking responsibility and making decisions that are based on fairness and justice. This could mean following a balanced diet and exercise program and seeking medical advice when needed. It also encourages us to avoid indulging in extreme and harmful behaviors that could damage our physical and mental health. Through making healthy choices and living a balanced lifestyle, we can create a more just health outcome for ourselves.

In terms of finances, the Justice Tarot card encourages us to make informed decisions and to consider the consequences of our choices. It suggests that we think carefully before making investments and strive to be fair and honest when conducting business dealings. We should strive to create win-win solutions that benefit all parties. The card also serves as a reminder that we should not be swayed by greed but instead make sure that all decisions are in line with the law, ethical codes and our conscience.

Finally, the Justice Tarot card is also very relevant to relationships. It encourages us to take responsibility for our actions and to strive for win-win solutions in our romantic, professional and family relationships. It suggests that we should be honest, fair and transparent in our dealings with others. We should also avoid trying to get away with anything, as the truth will eventually come out. Through exercising justice and integrity we can create strong and lasting relationships.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 7 of Swords card typically denotes a situation involving some form of theft or dishonesty that could have a negative effect on one’s health. This card suggests an individual has acted in a deceitful manner or taken something that was not theirs, either literally or metaphorically. In terms of health, this card could mean that one is not being honest with themselves or their medical professionals and that they could be concealing something that could have long-term negative consequences. It could also refer to a situation in which an individual is superficial and not looking after their health as they should, or that they are not being honest or open with themselves about the state of their health.

Finances:

The 7 of Swords card suggests an element of dishonesty or deceit when it comes to finances. It implies that an individual may have acted in a way that was not totally above board. This could be in the form of an illicit trade or theft, or having taken more than was offered to them. In terms of finances, this card could be warning against a situation in which one is not adhering to a budget, or taking financial risks that could have a negative impact in the future. It is a reminder to be honest with oneself and be fully aware of the consequences of one’s actions.

Relationships:

The 7 of Swords card can be indicative of a relationship in which one or both of the individuals involved are being dishonest or deceitful. This could be in the form of lying, manipulation, or withholding information. It could also be a sign that an individual is being unfaithful, or that an individual is taking something from the relationship that is not theirs. In terms of relationships, this card can be warning against playing games or hiding information that could have a damaging effect on the relationship in the short or long term. It is also a reminder to be honest and open with one another, and to be aware of how one’s actions can ultimately affect the relationship.
