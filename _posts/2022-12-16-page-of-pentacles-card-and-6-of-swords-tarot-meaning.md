A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Pentacles and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Page Of Pentacles and The 6 Of Swords tarot cards together represent the need for a transition that comes with a new job or raise. It could suggest that you are about to embark on a journey that involves leaving a secure environment and stepping into the unknown with the hope of a financial reward at the end of it. It could mean that you are learning how to value your intellectual property and that, as a patient kid, you are learning to wisely invest your resources. It could also suggest that it is time to relocate, where you will have to work hard and trust in the process, but with a secure financial future ahead.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Its meaning for health is that it encourages us to take our time in our healing process and seek out professional advice if we are having any issues. It can also signify that if we look after our body, we can be healthy, full of energy and able to tackle new tasks.

For finances, the Page Of Pentacles Tarot card is a good omen to receive. It suggests that if we are patient, put in the hard work and are open to new opportunities, we can gain financial stability and security. Starting a new job or being offered a raise are both examples of the kind of success that this card can bring. It can also be a sign of good news, such as a bonus or inheritance, coming our way.

In relationships, the Page Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that things are taking a more serious turn and commitment is becoming more of a focal point. It can be a sign that we are making progress towards a lasting connection with someone, or that we are actively seeking out a long-term commitment. This card encourages us to take our time and be patient with our partners, and shows us that taking little steps towards our ultimate goal can lead to great rewards.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 6 of Swords Tarot card represents a transition in terms of health. This could be a transition from sickness to health, an improvement in overall wellbeing, or the end of a long and difficult journey. This card indicates that the individual is likely to experience a positive change in their overall well being. It also speaks of a willingness to make the necessary changes required to restore health and wellbeing.

Finances: The 6 of Swords Tarot card typically suggests a transition in terms of finances. This could be the start of a successful financial period, or the beginning of a new financial venture. This card is likely to bring about an increase in abundance and prosperity, often in the form of an unexpected windfall or sudden success. However, it also suggests a willingness to take risks and make the necessary changes to achieve financial success.

Relationships: The 6 of Swords Tarot card speaks of a transition in relationships. This could be a transition from a troubled relationship to one that is successful and fulfilling, or it could be the end of a difficult period in a relationship. The card is likely to bring a new period of understanding and compromise. It also symbolizes the courage and willingness to make the necessary changes to restore the harmony and balance in a relationship.
