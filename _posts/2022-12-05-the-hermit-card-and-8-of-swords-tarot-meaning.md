A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card together suggest a journey of exploration and discovery. It indicates the need for time and space for yourself, where you can be free from the restrictions and limitations of the outside world. This time alone can be used to pursue a deeper understanding and connection to your own life and spiritual truths. While it may be a challenging journey, this period of self-reflection can bring you much needed clarity and a sense of freedom. By embracing the lessons of both tarot cards, you can use this time apart to deepen your spiritual practice, further your self-awareness, and reconnect with your truth.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 8 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 8 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card can be interpreted to represent the importance of personal introspection and reflection for health, finances and relationships. When this card appears in a reading, it can be seen as a sign that it’s time to go within and re-evaluate our lives, to search for personal truth and spiritual illumination.

In terms of health, The Hermit Tarot card can be seen as a reminder to take a step back and take inventory of our emotional and physical well-being in order to come up with a plan of action to improve our health. It’s a call to attend to our own self-care practices, such as self-care rituals, healthy eating, exercise, monitoring and managing stress, and getting enough sleep.

When it comes to finances, The Hermit Tarot card can be interpreted as a sign to get a clear understanding and realistic view of our current financial situation. This means taking a close look at our spending, budgeting, and overall financial health. It’s an invitation to take a more mindful approach to money, both in terms of what we spend, as well as how we save and invest.

In terms of relationships, The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to take a step back and get a clearer perspective of where we are in our relationships. It’s a sign to take some time to reflect and evaluate the relationships in our lives and to identify what’s working, what needs to be improved, and how we can better nurture the relationships in our lives. It’s also a reminder to take time to be alone and enjoy some inner reflection, to contemplate and access our emotions and feelings, and to get clarity and understanding of what we need and want from our relationships.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 8 of Swords Tarot card can signify a person who has become so overwhelmed with stress and worry that they isolate themselves and become their own worst enemy. This can manifest itself in numerous forms, such as poor eating habits, an unwillingness to exercise, and an avoidance of any type of emotional support. Additionally, this card can indicate a lack of mental clarity, which can lead to irrational decisions that negatively impact health.

Finances: The 8 of Swords Tarot card can represent financial struggles due to insecurity and a lack of confidence. This can prevent someone from taking the necessary steps to achieve financial stability and growth. They may become stuck in a cycle of overspending and debt, or be unable to make a clear plan for future investments. This card can also symbolize self-imposed limits, such as an inability to take risks or even think about the future.

Relationships: This card can point to an individual who is unable to open up and connect to the people around them. They may struggle to trust others and accept help, which can lead to loneliness and isolation. This can also be reflected in damaged relationships due to a fear of being judged or rejected. People with this card can often be fearful of getting too close to others and may struggle to maintain and build meaningful connections.
