## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card typically symbolizes good health, financial security and positive relationships. When this card appears in a reading, it suggests that an individual has reached a peak in all three areas.

The Sun Tarot card’s status as a representation of good health is a reflection of the card’s promise of energy, vitality, youthfulness and wellbeing. It is a reminder that we should take care of our physical health, so that we can continue to shine brightly. The card can also suggest that the individual’s health is improving or that they are handling a health challenge positively.

Financially, The Sun Tarot card symbolizes success, abundance and financial security. It suggests the individual’s ability to manage their finances, their good habits in terms of money management and their capacity to earn or acquire resources. Consequently, The Sun Tarot card is indicative of a well-deserved sense of financial freedom.

With regards to relationships, The Sun Tarot card embodies love, joy and the power of a strong connection. It is particularly associated with committed, long-term relationships, whether platonic or romantic. The card can also suggest that the individual is in a happy and balanced relationship and that they are surrounded by people who support them. Ultimately, The Sun Tarot card is a reminder to celebrate the love and happiness in one’s life.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

The 3 Of Wands Tarot card represents health as an expanding journey, a team effort made with both personal and collective effort. It could mean turning to therapy or to a health coach like a personal trainer, nutritionist or alternative health practitioner to help you make lasting changes. It could also mean working together with your doctor to make better health decisions and taking a holistic approach to wellness by adding more natural therapies into your life. Working with a team to achieve health goals can help you make lasting changes, stay motivated, and stay on track.

The 3 Of Wands Tarot card can also represent a journey in growing your financial wealth. This card could represent a new chapter in your financial life where you are growing and expanding your options. You could take a business venture, travel, or seek out alternative financial options like crowdfunding or investments. You could also be looking at ways to start and grow a business or invest in a new venture. It is important to have a team of people to help you with these decisions, so that you can make wise and informed decisions.

Relationships

This Tarot card can also relate to your relationships and how you are expanding the ones that are meaningful to you. This could mean being more open to having more friends and acquaintances, meeting new people and being more open to forming deeper connections. It can also mean expanding your current relationships with family and friends by discussing deeper topics, making plans for the future, and working together to achieve common goals. This card encourages team work and collaboration in relationships, which can help foster a stronger bond.
