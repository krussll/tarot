A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Hanged Man and The 9 Of Cups tarot cards suggest that in order to manifest the material abundance and good health that the 9 of Cups desires, it is important to find the wisdom and strength to make the right sacrifice. Perhaps this is a financial sacrifice, or the sacrifice of a material possession, or even the sacrifice of a mindset. By looking at things from a different perspective, and having the courage to make a tough decision, you can open yourself up to achieving your goals.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This card suggests that self-sacrifice and a willingness to accommodate the needs of your partner may be necessary. The 9 of Cups card suggests that in this situation, wishes will come true. Even though sacrifices may be necessary, both you and your partner will benefit from the changes. This card indicates that material abundance and good health will be the result of the changes made. This card encourages you to stay positive and to keep in mind that all the hard work will pay off. It’s a card of satisfaction, contentment, and fulfilled desires.

Together, these two cards point to a healthy and rewarding long-term relationship. The Hanged Man card suggests that you need to take a step back and look at the situation from a new point of view. The 9 of Cups card indicates that this change in perspective could lead to a more fulfilling and harmonious relationship that will bring great rewards. With these changes, you and your partner can both benefit and even enjoy the journey ahead.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card means many things, but when it comes to health, finances and relationships, the meaning is especially relevant. Health: The Hanged Man Tarot card is a reminder that the body and the mind are both important and each should be taken care of. It suggests taking time to assess habits and adjust them if necessary in order to keep the body and mind healthy. Additionally, the card urges us to accept our current state of health and strive to reach higher levels of physical and mental wellbeing.

Finances: This card reminds us to pause and rethink our financial decisions and plan for the future. It could mean taking a step back and evaluating our financial goals and the decisions made to achieve them. It could also mean recognizing that money is not everything, and that we should not let it dictate our lives.

Relationships: The Hanged Man Tarot card encourages us to look at our relationships from a different perspective. It suggests taking a step back from our relationships and changing our outlook so that we can gain a greater understanding of our loved ones. Additionally, it encourages us to be willing to sacrifice and compromise for our relationships in order to move them forward. Lastly, it reminds us that relationships require work and self-reflection, and that ultimate happiness comes from within.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is often associated with good health. This card symbolizes the abundance of all things good. When it comes to health, it can be a sign that any conditions or illnesses will be easy to manage and that physical and emotional healing is within reach. This card is a reminder to practice self-care, to be kind to yourself and to focus on the positive aspects of life. It encourages people to take preventative action, such as a healthy diet, plenty of exercise and stress-management techniques. It also serves as a reminder to listen to your body and to seek medical advice when necessary.

Finances

The 9 of Cups tarot card symbolizes material abundance and financial security. This card is a sign of good luck and suggests that having enough money to cover basic needs is within reach. It also indicates that life will become more stable and secure, and that financial goals will be achievable. This card encourages people to take the necessary steps to achieve financial well-being, such as budgeting, setting aside savings, and eliminating unnecessary expenses.

Relationships

The 9 of Cups tarot card symbolizes lasting and harmonious relationships. This card suggests meaningful connection with loved ones as well as a general sense of contentment and well-being. It encourages people to recognize their own worth and potential, to forgive, and to be open to new people and experiences. This card is a reminder to be honest and clear in personal relationships, to focus on what brings joy, and to trust the strength and resilience of love.
