## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card is associated with physical and emotional lethargy, a lack of motivation, and apathy. When it comes to health, it can be a warning sign that something is not right. It could mean that you are not getting the necessary rest, exercise and nutrition to maintain a healthy life, and that your body is feeling the consequences. It could also signify an underlying health issue such as depression, anxiety, or other mental and emotional disorders that need to be addressed.

Finances:  
The 4 of Cups tarot card is a warning to be careful with your finances and to be mindful of where your money is going. It can be an indication that you are overspending and it can be easy to get caught up in the need to buy everything you want. This card can also indicate that surprises may arise with unexpected costs and if you are not prepared for them, it can throw you off balance. You should be sure to create a budget and stick to it to ensure that you are managing your finances responsibly.

Relationships:  
When it comes to relationships, the 4 of Cups tarot card is a reminder to take a step back and assess the situation. It implies a need for more emotional, mental and physical distance in order to get a better perspective. It may be a sign that you are feeling unfulfilled in the relationship and that something is missing. It’s important to be honest with yourself and the other person about your needs and desires and to address any issues that are not being met. Taking a break from the relationship can help you gain clarity and decide what is best for you and your partner.

The Knight Of Cups Tarot card signifies an opportunity for abundance and prosperity. This could mean an unexpected bonus or inheritance. It can also be a sign that you are able to create wealth from creative ideas or projects. Investing in your future can be beneficial during this time. The Knight Of Cups Tarot card signifies a time of romance, courtship, and proposals. It is a great time to start new relationships because you are likely to be feeling more loving and open-hearted. At the same time, existing relationships will benefit from communication and closeness. Sharing your true feelings with your significant other during this time can be very rewarding. You may also be feeling more passionate and passionate about your relationships in general.
