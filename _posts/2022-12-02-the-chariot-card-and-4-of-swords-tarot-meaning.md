A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The Chariot tarot card and The 4 of Swords tarot card represent the two sides of a journey. The Chariot card signifies the need for action and change and the courage to move forward despite obstacles. The 4 of Swords card, on the other hand, is a reminder that sometimes it is necessary to retreat and take the time to rest, renew and recover before embarking on another journey. These two cards together can be seen as a reminder that after every action, there must be a period of rest, a time for reflection and renewal, before setting off on a new venture. This is an important balance to keep—that of taking the time to rest and recharge, and pushing forward in active pursuit of our goals.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Chariot and 4 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Chariot tarot card and The 4 of Swords tarot card in combination are a positive sign for your love life. It suggests that you’re on the cusp of a major change in your relationship, and that you need to take action to make it happen. The Chariot indicates movement, a journey, and the potential for a new and exciting path together. It may be the spark you need to set your relationship in motion, to take it to the next level.

The 4 of Swords suggests that it’s not time for action yet- instead, it’s time for rest and contemplation. Take a step back, and consider where your relationship is going. What do you really want from it, and what steps do you need to take to get there? Don’t rush into anything- take the time to think it through, and make sure you’re ready before you move forward.

Overall, this combination of tarot cards suggests that a great change is in sight for your relationship. Step back, rest and contemplate, then when you feel ready, take action and embrace the journey. The Chariot and The 4 of Swords suggest that if you take the time to make sure you’re both ready, then a new and exciting journey awaits.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Chariot mean?

The Chariot Tarot card is a reminder to take action and make changes in order to improve one’s health, finances and relationships. Physically, this card suggests that you should take the initiative to make positive changes in your diet, exercise regimen, and other health-related activities. This could include seeking medical advice or investing in fitness equipment to improve your well-being. Financially, this card encourages you to take action to get your finances in order. This could mean making smarter money decisions, budgeting, and/or working hard to increase your income. Finally, this card offers guidance on how to make positive changes in relationships. It suggests that you review your current relationships and take the necessary steps to create healthier relationships by setting boundaries, communicating better, and showing love and respect. With the power of The Chariot Tarot card, you are motivated and encouraged to make the necessary changes in order to thrive in health, wealth, and relationships.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card often refers to the need for rest, renewal and recovery during times of illness or injury. In terms of health, this card is a reminder to slow down, even if you don’t feel like it. You may need to take some time out of your routine to rest and recuperate. This may mean taking a break from physical activities or simply setting aside a few days for self-care and solitude. Taking some time away from the hustle and bustle of life can be a great opportunity for physical, mental and spiritual healing.

Finances:  
Money-wise, the 4 of Swords Tarot card often indicates a period of pause and respite from the flurry of financial decisions that you may often make. This is the time to assess the situation and gather your thoughts before making any huge financial leaps. It is important to ensure that you don’t stretch yourself too thin, or make assumptions about what is within your means. When it comes to finances, the 4 of Swords Tarot card encourages caution and patience.

Relationships:  
In terms of relationships, the 4 of Swords Tarot card can be a reminder to take some time away from your partner or friends to reflect and learn more about yourself. It is important to remember that relationships require balance and open communication. Taking some time away to reflect can help you come back to your relationship with a new perspective. During this period of respite, it is important to remember to nurture your relationship and appreciate the time you have together.
