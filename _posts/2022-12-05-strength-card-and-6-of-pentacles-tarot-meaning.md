A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Strength card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card together can indicate that it is important to have a sense of self-love and self-respect, and to have the courage to ask for help when you need it. Generosity is essential, and it’s important to be open to receiving help and also to offering help where you can. Balancing these two aspects of yourself can bring a great sense of strength and stability.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Strength and 6 Of Pentacles mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Strength and 6 Of Pentacles the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does Strength mean?

Health:

The Strength Tarot card is all about inner strength and courage. This card symbolizes the ability to overcome obstacles, no matter how difficult they may seem. It reminds us to stay focused on our goals and to never give up, no matter how hard things may get. On a physical level, strength refers to physical health. Having inner strength can help us stay strong and healthy by enabling us to make smart decisions about our lives.

In terms of mental health, Strength Tarot card encourages us to develop healthy coping mechanisms. When faced with situations that are out of our control, it helps to tap into our inner resilience and strength to keep our spirits high. Strength Tarot card can also remind us that it is ok to take a break and give ourselves some time to relax and recharge.

Finances:

The Strength Tarot card encourages us to be proactive when it comes to our finances. Though it can be hard to take a long-term perspective when managing our money, Strength Tarot card reminds us that it is important to do so. It encourages us to take control of our finances and make decisions that are in our best interest. This may involve setting up a budget, looking into different investment opportunities and being mindful as to where our money is going. With this inner strength and courage, we can become better stewards of our financial resources.

Relationships:

The Strength Tarot card reminds us to be resilient when it comes to relationships. It encourages us to reach out to those we care about, even when it may be difficult to do so. It also reminds us to be patient with those we care about and allow them to make their own decisions, even if we disagree. Communication is key and Strength Tarot card encourages us to be open and honest in our relationships. This card also encourages us to have healthy boundaries and respect the boundaries of others. Through strength and resilience, we can build strong and meaningful relationships.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot Card is a card of support, generosity and balance. When the 6 of Pentacles appears in a reading, it indicates that a period of giving and receiving help is coming. It’s a time of abundance and plenty, a time to share your resources and reap the rewards.

When it comes to health, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that there is a focus on health and well-being. It can be a time to pay attention to your own needs and to provide support to those around you. You may find that you are in a place where you can offer assistance to those in need and you have enough to do so. This can manifest physically, emotionally and spiritually.

When it comes to finances, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests a period of sharing and giving. It’s a time to look at your resources and see where and how you can best use them. Giving to others, or even to yourself, can bring rewards and bring balance to your finances. Give from the heart, without expecting anything in return.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of support and generosity. You may find yourself in a place where you can give and receive help. This card also speaks of balance within a relationship and suggests that it may be time to assess and adjust the balance accordingly. Offering help to your partner or friends can bring great fulfilment and offer a new perspective on the relationship.
