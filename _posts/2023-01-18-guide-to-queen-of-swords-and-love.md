Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Queen Of Swords card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Queen Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Queen Of Swords.

## Queen Of Swords And Love – An Overview

The Queen Of Swords tarot card indicates that health is best achieved through self-sufficiency and total honesty.

This card reminds us to take responsibility for our own health, to nurture and nourish ourselves, and to be honest about our physical and mental needs.

Knowing ourselves and trusting our intuition is key in maintaining a healthy and balanced lifestyle.

The Queen Of Swords reminds us of the importance of being honest with ourselves and with our health care providers, asking for help when we need it, and allowing ourselves to take time for self-care.

By maintaining an honest and self-sufficient approach to health, we can empower ourselves to take control of our own health and well-being.

## Upright Meanings For Queen Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Queen of Swords tarot card symbolizes a person who is honest and self-sufficient in matters of the heart.

This card encourages us to be truthful and honest in our relationships and to take care of our own needs first.

The Queen of Swords also suggests that we should be emotionally independent, so that we don’t become too dependent on others for our happiness.

She suggests that we should not be too open and vulnerable with our partners, and instead rely on our own strength and resilience.

This card is a reminder that true love is not a one-way street but instead involves two equally independent individuals.

### What It Means For The Future

The Queen of Swords tarot card is a sign that your future love will be a self-sufficient individual who values total honesty in relationships.

They will be independent and strong in their convictions, and won’t be influenced by the opinions of others.

They will be open and honest with you, and expect the same in return.

They won’t be afraid to take the lead in the relationship, and are likely to be a great source of strength and support.

You can expect a relationship full of trust and mutual respect.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Queen Of Swords is a card that symbolizes total honesty, which can be a difficult but beneficial quality when it comes to the love of those close to me.

It encourages me to be open and honest with my loved ones, even when it is difficult.

This card suggests that I don’t just tell them what they want to hear, but I communicate my feelings and thoughts in a direct and clear manner.

It also reminds me that I need to take responsibility for my own happiness, rather than relying on others to make me feel complete or fulfilled.

Ultimately, this card encourages me to maintain an honest and open relationship with my loved ones, that is based on trust and respect.

## Reversed Meanings For Queen Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

When the Queen of Swords is reversed, it is a sign that the person is having difficulty being honest with themselves and others in matters of the heart.

They may be feeling fearful and vulnerable when it comes to relationships, and this could be causing them to close their heart off.

They may be avoiding intimacy and exposing themselves emotionally, as it could be too painful for them to bear.

The reversed Queen of Swords encourages one to become open and honest about their feelings.

It is time to address the underlying issues that have been preventing them from expressing their true emotions.

They need to face their fears and start embracing the vulnerability that comes with being in love.

It may be frightening and uncomfortable, but it is necessary in order to move forward and have a healthy, meaningful relationship.

The Queen of Swords reversed is an invitation to be brave and honest with oneself and others, and to start taking steps to create healthier relationships.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Queen of Swords in a love reading can signify a relationship that is coming to an end, or a lack of trust and honesty between partners.

This could indicate that there is a wall of secrecy between the two of you, and that it’s time to break it down and start communicating openly.

It may also be a warning that a manipulative partner is trying to take control of the situation, or that there is deception present in the relationship.

In the future, the reversed Queen of Swords can signify that you need to open yourself up to true, unconditional love.

This will require you to work on being more honest and direct with your partner.

It could also mean that you need to learn how to trust again, even if it’s difficult.

The reversed Queen of Swords can help you to recognize that true love is possible, and that it’s worth the effort to find it and nurture it.

If you’re in a relationship, it will require patience and work, but it’s a worthwhile endeavor in the end.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Queen of Swords appears in a reading regarding love, it can indicate that the relationships with those closest to you may be strained.

You may be finding it difficult to open up to the people around you and communicate honestly, leading to a sense of isolation and distance.

This could be caused by a fear of being judged or misunderstood, or simply a lack of trust in the people around you.

You may also be having difficulty expressing your true feelings and allowing yourself to be vulnerable and open up to those who care about you.

It is important to remember that a reversed Queen of Swords does not necessarily mean that there is a lack of love in the relationship, but rather a lack of communication and trust.

It can be a sign that you need to take steps to open up and build stronger relationships with the people in your life.

You can start by simply expressing your feelings and needs more openly and honestly and let your loved ones know how much they mean to you.

Taking the time to really listen and understand the perspective of those around you will also help to foster a more connected and trusting relationship.
