A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card together represent the balance between staying connected with others and taking time to journey within. The Hermit serves as a reminder that spiritual growth comes from looking within and exploring our inner truths. The 3 Of Cups reminds us to not forget to celebrate life and the joy it brings. Together, these two cards are telling us that we need to take the time to occasionally go within for a spiritual deep dive, but don’t forget to also celebrate and make time for meaningful connections with friends and family to stay connected.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

You can find the clarity and personal truth you seek within yourself, but you don’t have to do it alone. The Hermit tarot card gives you the strength and courage to go within and discover what you need, while The 3 Of Cups tarot card lets you know that it’s ok to ask for help and support along the way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card can bring important themes of self-care and personal growth to the areas of health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, the Hermit can represent the need for dedicated self-reflection to better understand one’s own needs and limitations. This can include contemplation of how lifestyle choices such as physical activity levels, dietary habits, and stress management affect overall well-being. Stepping away from the busyness of life and making conscious steps towards better health can bring both physical and emotional rewards.

In regards to finances, the Hermit signifies a need to go inward and examine what has worked, and what hasn’t, when it comes to money. Taking a hard look at any barriers to fiscal success and addressing them in a considered manner can make a big difference. This could also mean setting aside time to create and stick to a budget, or actively searching for additional streams of income. Additionally, the Hermit’s wisdom can help to ensure that money is not a source of stress by setting sustainable and realistic saving and spending goals.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Hermit Tarot card speaks to the importance of time alone and personal reflection. Taking a step back to analyze how one interacts with other people, the ways in which one may be pushing away potential connection, and how one can cultivate healthier relationships can be valuable for any person. This can help to create healthier boundaries within relationships, as well as fostering greater understanding and empathy for oneself and others.

Overall, the Hermit Tarot card can be an important source of insight when it comes to all aspects of life, from health to relationships. Taking the time to go within to gain clarity, peace and personal truths can be a powerful source of knowledge and growth.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups Tarot card can have a positive influence on health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the 3 of Cups suggests that you have a firm circle of friends and family who are supportive and understanding of your needs. It encourages you to reach out to your social network for help and rely on their love and understanding. It encourages you to work together with others to create a positive, uplifting environment that can foster healing and wellness.

In terms of finances, the 3 of Cups implies building and maintaining strong relationships with those you trust. It is a reminder that collaboration and cooperation are essential for financial stability. The card encourages you to form a team of people who can help you navigate financial challenges, whether that be through offering advice, lending a sympathetic ear or even offering financial assistance.

For relationships, the 3 of Cups reminds us that celebrating with friends is important. It encourages us to reach out and connect with our friends, family and loved ones. This can be done through hosting gatherings, celebrating successes and offering support during difficult times. It is important to remember that positive relationships are built on strong foundations of shared moments, experiences, understanding and love.

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a powerful reminder that we are never alone, and that we can rely on our friends, family and loved ones for support in all areas of our lives. It encourages us to cherish the meaningful relationships we have, and to make a conscious effort to foster and strengthen those bonds.
