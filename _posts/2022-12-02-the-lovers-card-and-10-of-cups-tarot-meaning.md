However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The 10 Of Cups tarot card together represent the beauty of two people coming together in love and partnership and finding total love, support and contentment from each other. This card combination suggests that when two individuals merge together to create a partnership, that partnership has the power to bring about an emotional and spiritual connection that will bring out the best in each of them. It signifies a strong emotional bond between two people, and it shows the importance of making a choice to come together and allow that love to grow and develop. This is the kind of love that will provide a foundation of contentment and stability that will last a lifetime.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 10 of Cups is a card that symbolizes good health and overall well-being. It represents an abundance of energy and a healthy lifestyle. If you have been struggling with poor health, this card encourages you to make more active choices and to focus on healing yourself. This card symbolizes a time of peace and contentment, allowing you to relax and restore balance between your mind, body and spirit. Eating healthy, exercising regularly, and taking time for your emotional needs are all ways to create a healthier lifestyle.

Finances: The 10 of Cups symbolizes financial security and success. This card signifies that you have taken the necessary steps to improve your financial situation and it is paying off. This card can also be seen as a sign for you to remain focused, disciplined and consistent with your financial goals. Creating a budget, paying your debts in full, and setting up an emergency fund are all great ways to move forward financially.

Relationships: The 10 of Cups is a card that symbolizes joyfulness, unconditional love, and deep connections with others. This card indicates that you have established a strong bond with someone in your life and feel fulfilled by the connection. You may be finding your true soul mate or deep family connections. This is a time of celebration and you can trust that your relationships will remain strong. This card also encourages you to make time for quality conversations with your family and friends, in order to deepen the relationships you have and create healthier connections with others.
