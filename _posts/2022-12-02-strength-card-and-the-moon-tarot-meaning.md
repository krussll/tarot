A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and The Moon card together in particular.

The Strength card and The Moon have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and The Moon mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The Moon tarot card emphasize the importance of self-love, self-respect, and intuition in our lives. When we love ourselves and treat ourselves with respect, we are able to have the courage to face any unseen problems that come our way. Furthermore, the trust that our intuition can give us will guide us through any voluntary changes we make. With Strength and The Moon, we can forge through any difficult situation with courage, trust, and an open heart.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and The Moon mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is often interpreted as a card that represents inner strength and resilience. This card encourages us to embrace our own power and to find the strength within us to overcome our challenges. Being strong in this sense can take many forms and doesn’t mean being unyielding or hard-headed. It simply means having the courage to stay true to yourself, to find the power within to believe in and trust your own inner wisdom even in the face of difficult situations and circumstances.

When it comes to health, The Strength Tarot card can remind us of the importance of taking care of ourselves first. It encourages us to take care of our physical, mental, and emotional health, so we are able to stay strong and resilient in our lives. Having the courage to make healthy choices, such as eating right, exercising, and getting enough sleep, can be a powerful way to honor our own strength.

When it comes to finances, The Strength Tarot card may encourage us to trust our own wisdom, but also to be flexible in our approach. If we stay grounded and have the courage to take risks and make wise financial decisions, we can remain strong and stable even in troubling times.

When it comes to relationships, The Strength Tarot card can remind us to trust our own connections and to stay true to ourselves despite any challenges that may arise. Having the courage to stand up for what we believe in and to be honest and open in our relationships can lead to stronger bonds and more meaningful connections. Being strong doesn’t necessarily mean putting up walls or being unyielding—instead, it means being strong enough to have the courage to be vulnerable and open to the possibilities of love and connection.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health

The Moon Tarot card can indicate that there are some hidden health issues that you may not be aware of. This could be anything from a nutrient deficiency to a troubling medical condition. Pay attention to your body and trust your intuition if something does not feel right. You may need to make some changes to your lifestyle and diet in order to improve your health. If you are feeling anxious or overwhelmed, find some healthy coping mechanisms to help you manage your feelings.

Finances

The Moon Tarot card can indicate that you may need to make some voluntary changes to your finances. You may need to review your budget or look at ways to save money. Trust your intuition and don’t make any big decisions without weighing up the pros and cons. Try to stay aware of your financial situation and look out for any investments or opportunities that may arise.

Relationships

The Moon Tarot card can signal that there are some unseen issues in your relationships that need to be addressed. There may be trust issues or underlying problems that need to be addressed before the relationship can progress. Don’t be afraid to speak up if you need to and listen to your intuition. Taking time away to assess the situation can be powerful. Be honest and open with your partner and be willing to compromise if necessary.
