A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The 6 of Wands and the 3 of Swords tarot cards together can be seen as a journey of highs and lows. On the one hand, the 6 of Wands represents joyous victory, recognition, success and good news. This signifies a time of happiness and abundance. On the other hand, the 3 of Swords shows us that during times of great joy and victory, there may be sorrow and heartbreak along the way. This card warns us of potential losses and painful changes. Together, these two cards remind us that while we can experience thrilling highs of success and recognition, there may also be some difficult times ahead that we must face with resilience and courage.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes victory, recognition, good news and success. This card denotes that the seeker will receive good news and see substantial progress in their endeavors. It is a sign of abundant good fortune and success.

For health, the 6 of Wands Tarot card indicates that the seeker will experience healing and a renewed sense of vitality. The card encourages the individual to take care of their physical, mental and emotional wellbeing. It is also a sign that any obstacles or difficulties in regards to health can be overcome.

For finances, the 6 of Wands Tarot card indicates that the individual will receive an award or recognition, which will further their financial goals and ambitions. This card can suggest that the individual may receive financial compensation for their efforts, or receive unexpected money. Moreover, it suggests financial success for the individual in the future.

For relationships, the 6 of Wands Tarot card indicates that the individual will experience victory, with the relationships in their life blossoming and flourishing. This card is a sign that any quarrels or disagreements between the couple will soon be a distant memory. It is also a sign of joy and celebration, suggesting that the individual will experience a period of harmony with their partner.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, the Three of Swords Tarot card is a warning sign that physical and emotional pain is coming. It could mean the onset of a chronic illness, or a mental health issue such as depression or anxiety, as well as an outright physical injury. The individual needs to spend time taking care of themselves, both mentally and physically, so that the pain does not become too unbearable and lead to other, more serious problems.

In terms of finances, the Three of Swords Tarot card warns of significant losses and expenses. It can refer to a situation in which the individual has been defrauded, or it can simply mean that the individual will have to divest of some of their belongings in order to make ends meet. Careful budgeting is required in this case.

When it comes to relationships, the Three of Swords Tarot card is a warning sign of disharmony, heartbreak and divorce. It can be a sign of a couple’s relationship coming to an end, or it can refer to a serious disagreement between two individuals. In any case, the individuals involved should strive to move past the pain and work on rebuilding the trust between them.

Overall, the Three of Swords Tarot card represents a period of deep pain and stress. It is important for the individual to approach this time with caution, and to take whatever time is necessary to heal. With careful attention, this difficult period can be navigated and the individual can emerge more resilient and stronger than before.
