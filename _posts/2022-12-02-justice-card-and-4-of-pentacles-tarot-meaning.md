A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 4 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and 4 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 4 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 4 Of Pentacles tarot card mean that while striving for long-term security, it is important to make sure that the solutions found are win-win solutions. Greed must be avoided, and it is important to remain honest to ensure that the truth is brought out. Causality is also important when seeking solutions, as it will help to understand how to avoid similar problems in the future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 4 of Pentacles suggests that in your search for long-term security, you may be driven by greed and a desire to possess your partner. This is not healthy, and will only lead to feelings of unease and insecurity in the relationship. Instead, focus on what each of you can bring to the table to sustain your relationship. Allow yourself to be vulnerable and acknowledge that it is impossible for one person to satisfy all of your needs.

The combination of The Justice tarot card and the 4 Of Pentacles tarot card encourages you to remain honest and open with yourself and your partner. Don’t be swayed into making decisions based on fear or greed; strive for win-win solutions that will benefit both of you in the long run. If you can do this, then you and your partner can build a solid foundation for a lasting relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice Tarot card speaks of balance and fairness. In terms of health, this card encourages us to take responsibility for our own well-being by making sure that we are doing our part to stay physically, mentally, and emotionally healthy. It emphasizes the importance of seeking balance and fairness when it comes to taking care of ourselves. This includes ensuring that we get regular check-ups and preventative care, and that we maintain healthy eating habits and exercise routines. The Justice Tarot card also encourages us to be open to advice and guidance from medical professionals so that we can make the best decisions for our own health.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card encourages us to use our financial resources in a balanced and responsible way. This includes creating budgets that reflect our values and limiting superfluous spending. This card also speaks to fairness in our financial dealings and to being ethical with our financial decisions. This involves being mindful of how our spending impacts others, including research into the environmental and social impacts of our purchases. The Justice Tarot card also suggests that we be open to advice when it comes to our finances, including seeking out guidance from a financial planner or attorney if needed.

Relationships: The Justice Tarot card reminds us to be fair and balanced in all of our relationships. This includes being respectful of each other’s needs and interests while maintaining personal boundaries. It also speaks to a willingness to compromise, and to engaging in honest and open communication. The Justice Tarot card encourages us to stay true to our values and beliefs, even if it means occasionally sacrificing our own wants for the good of the relationship. It encourages us to practice empathy when it comes to disagreements, and to strive for win-win solutions that take into account both partners’ interests.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card represents a desire for security in health, finances and relationships. On the physical level, it may be a sign to focus on taking better care of one’s physical health, including getting enough rest, engaging in healthy activities such as exercise and making sure to get check-ups and screenings done. On an emotional level, it may be a need to take control of one’s mental health and to seek out counseling or therapy if needed.

Financially, the 4 of Pentacles suggests that a person is grasping tightly to what they have in order to ensure a secure future. It can indicate being too conservative with money, a fear of spending it and rigidly sticking to budgets. It may also signify that the individual is losing out on opportunities because they are too focused on not taking risks with their security.

In terms of relationships, the 4 of Pentacles can represent holding onto a relationship too tightly or having a fear of losing the connection with another. This can manifest as being possessive or clingy with a partner, or not allowing enough space or trust in the relationship. This could also indicate a fear of being vulnerable and an unwillingness to take chances in order to foster meaningful connections with others.

Altogether, The 4 Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages a person to strive for balance between holding on to what one has and taking the necessary risks to move forward. Seeking security does not mean becoming overly reliant on material possessions. Instead, it means striving for stability in the mental, emotional and physical aspects of one’s life, as well as creating healthy and supportive relationships.
