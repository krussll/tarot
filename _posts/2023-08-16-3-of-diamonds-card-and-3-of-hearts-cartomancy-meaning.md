A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Diamonds card and 3 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 3 Of Diamonds card and 3 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Diamonds and 3 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the simultaneous appearance of The 3 of Diamonds and The 3 of Hearts indicates a tumultuous period characterized by rifts and infidelity, but underscored by enduring faith.

The 3 of Diamonds’ association with Fall and Air signifies a period of major changes and intellectual stimulation, likely bringing disputes and disagreements to the fore.

Yet, the faith assigned to this card suggests that despite the upheavals, you will have the fortitude to hold on.

Similarly, the 3 of Hearts, being linked to Spring and Fire, indicates a sense of renewal or rebirth.

Its association with infidelity suggests potential breaches of trust or disruptions in relationships.

However, the faith attached to this card reiterates the need for steadfast conviction.

Collectively, these cards underscore the notion of faith amidst trials and tribulations, stressing the importance of belief and resilience through seasonally influenced, emotionally-charged periods filled with discord and potential unfaithfulness.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Diamonds and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 3 Of Diamonds card and the 3 Of Hearts card in a cartomancy reading paints a complex picture for your love life.

The 3 Of Diamonds indicates potential disputes or disagreements on the horizon.

This is not necessarily something to fear; disagreements can lead to growth and maturity in a relationship when they are faced with honesty and openness.

Coupled with its association to the fall – a time of change – and the element of air – signifying communication and thought processes – it suggests a need for clear, rational communication as you deal with these changes and disputes.

However, the 3 Of Hearts introduces a note of caution, symbolizing potential infidelity.

This card is also associated with faith, suggesting that despite the potential presence of unfaithfulness, trust and belief in your partner are required.

Associated with spring – a time of renewal and growth – and the element of fire – symbolizing passion and emotions – it points towards an impending period of emotional upheaval and potential transformation.

However, the growth and renewal of spring can also indicate a fresh start or a turn for the better, if the situation is approached with a faithful heart and a clear, honest communication while overcoming the challenges.

### What does 3 Of Diamonds and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing the 3 of Diamonds card in the context of finance and job signifies upcoming disputes or disagreements.

This could be conflicts with colleagues at work or differences of opinion regarding financial matters.

The element of Air associated with it may promote clear thinking and better communication to manage these disputes.

The relation with Fall means it is also a time to harvest the fruits of your labor, making this a period of assessment and reflection on your professional and financial journey.

Pulling the 3 Of Hearts card alongside amplifies this message.

This card, representing infidelity and faith, warns of potential disloyalty in financial matters or at the workplace, perhaps signaling untrustworthy business partners or colleagues.

However, the faith aspect also encourages trust in oneself and the process.

The association with Fire brings motivation, transformation, and dynamic energy which can be harnessed to overcome obstacles at work or in financial situations.

Moreover, its link to Spring symbolizes growth, renewal, and a fresh start.

Therefore, despite the challenges, the combination of these two cards encourages resilience, communication, renewed energy and unfolding growth in your financial and professional life.

### What does 3 Of Diamonds and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

The 3 of Diamonds, symbolizing “disputes” and “faith,” and linked with the element of Air and the season of Fall, could hint toward a period of strong mental activity in regards to your health.

This could mean having disagreements or differing opinions with healthcare providers or friends and family about your health choices.

However, the element of faith also suggests that despite these conflicts, you need to trust your instincts and maintain faith in your decisions.

Air, as the driver of intellect and communication, further emphasizes the need for clarity in expressing your needs and wants, and the season of Fall indicates a time of change or transition.

The 3 of Hearts, on the other hand, represents “infidelity” and “faith” associated with the season of Spring and the element of Fire.

In terms of health, this card can imply a lack of commitment towards your health goals.

You may be straying away from your set regime, consuming unhealthy food, or failing to maintain a fitness program.

However, the element of faith is again a prominent theme in this card that encourages you to stay committed to your end goal, and not indulge in short-term gratifications.

The symbol of Fire in this card can be associated with both passion and destruction, implying an energetic yet cautionary approach towards your health.

The season of Spring may signal a time to renew your health goals and plant the seeds of healthier habits.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
