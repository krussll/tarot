## The Wheel Of Fortune And Health – An Overview

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card symbolizes good fortune and a turning point in health.

It suggests that a change in your health, either for the better or worse, is on the horizon.

This could include luck in finding a medical breakthrough or a sudden improvement in your health.

It could also indicate a new approach to health, such as a change in diet or a new form of exercise, that could bring great success.

The Wheel Of Fortune encourages you to take a chance and gamble on your health, trusting that the outcome will be rewarding and beneficial.
