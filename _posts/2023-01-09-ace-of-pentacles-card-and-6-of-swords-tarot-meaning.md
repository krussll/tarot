## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Pentacles mean?

The Ace of Pentacles Tarot card represents health, wealth, and relationships in positive ways. In terms of health, the card symbolizes a newfound ability to take care of oneself and be in control of one’s own wellbeing. It represents good health, physical strength, and a commitment to healthy living. This card also suggests that individuals should strive to nourish their mind and body through nutritious foods, exercise, and proper rest.

In terms of finances, the Ace of Pentacles is a card of great prosperity and stability. It encourages individuals to use their resources to make the most of their situation. It signifies a newfound ability to make sound investments, secure financial stability, and build wealth. It also serves as a reminder for individuals to budget wisely, save for the future, and think before making a financial move.

Finally, the Ace of Pentacles provides an opportunity for individuals to strengthen their relationships. It suggests that individuals can use the newfound wealth and stability to create a supportive family and social life. This card also implies that individuals should focus on improving their communication and understanding of others. By being open and honest with others, a person can foster healthy and meaningful relationships.Overall, this card signals a period of great growth in health, wealth, and relationships.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 6 of Swords Tarot card can symbolise the need for physical and emotional healing. It suggests taking an imaginative break from reality to rest and restore your health. This can mean taking a short holiday, engaging in creative activities, or simply giving yourself time and space to relax. The 6 of Swords can also point to the need for going to therapy or counselling to help resolve deep-seated issues that might be impacting your physical health.

Finances

This Tarot card can also represent a period of financial transition. This could mean relocating to a different area that offers better job opportunities, or taking a financial risk in order to increase your income. Alternatively, the 6 of Swords encourages you to look at alternative sources of income, such as passive sources or side gigs. As long as you are willing to take a leap into the unknown, this Tarot card can bring success and abundance.

Relationships

The 6 of Swords Tarot card encourages you to move on from unhealthy relationships and seek out new and healthier ones. You may be ready to leave behind an old pattern of behaviour in order to make a fresh start. This can also be a sign that it’s time to change your relationship dynamics, to move away from the status quo and find creative ways to revamp your current relationships. The 6 of Swords encourages you to take the necessary steps to move forward, beyond any difficult points in your current relationships.
