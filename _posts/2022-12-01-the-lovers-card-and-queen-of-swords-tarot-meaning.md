The Queen Of Swords brings with her a message of total openness and honesty, a willingness to be vulnerable enough to share your true thoughts and feelings with your partner. Together, these two tarot cards tell a powerful story of two people in a loving relationship built on the strength of their individual autonomy and the trust that comes with total honesty.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This card reminds you to stay balanced and make wise choices, allowing you to create and maintain a healthy financial life.

Relationships:

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card indicates that a person should take well-being into their own hands. She advises to always be honest and forthcoming with yourself and others in terms of your health. This means that if there are signs of any health issues, they should be reported as soon as possible. This Tarot card also encourages people to take an active role in their own health so that they can take control of their destiny.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages a person to be honest and responsible with their finances. This means that one should be honest with themselves about how much money they can really afford to spend and how much they need to save. It also suggests that a person should be realistic about the return on their investments, rather than expecting quick and easy financial gain. It is important to be honest with accounts and keep track of money, so that expenses are always within budget.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card advises to always be truthful in relationships, and to avoid any deception. It is important to always be honest and open with partners so that relationships are based on trust and understanding. It is also important to be clear with others about their boundaries, and to set up healthy boundaries. This Tarot card encourages one to be brave and direct when conflicts arise, and to be honest and stand up for what is right and true.
