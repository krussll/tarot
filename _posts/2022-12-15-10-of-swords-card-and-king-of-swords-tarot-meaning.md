A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and King Of Swords mean together?

The 10 Of Swords and The King Of Swords tarot cards together point to a time of painful endings and a transformation of the way you lead your life. It is a time to be intentional and strategic in your thought process and the decisions you make, as you may reach a point of complete and utter failure if you don’t. Reaching this low state may be very difficult to handle but can offer you a unique opportunity to reconstruct your life in a new direction. During this time, you have the power to make lasting changes and choose a more empowered path that is in alignment with your true values.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

But sadly, there may have been warning signs that were ignored as the leader in question plotted and strategically thought out how to bring about this painful ending.

It is possible that this ending was just as much of a rock-bottom moment for the leader as it was for you. The 10 of Swords is an indication of feeling completely lost, desperate, and helpless when faced with a situation. But instead of taking action to make things right, your partner or friend may have chosen the cowardly way out by silently plotting an ending instead.

The King of Swords tarot card is associated with a person who is analytical and full of wisdom, but who can also be very manipulative and controlling. When paired with the 10 of Swords, this card could be an indication of a partner or friend who plotted an ending in order to gain greater control of their life. In other words, they may have made the strategic move of ending the relationship in order to put themselves back on the throne of their own life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Swords and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Swords and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health: Physically, the 10 of Swords Tarot card signifies a traumatic experience, or a period of illness or extreme exhaustion. It is a sign that the body and the mind may need healing and rest. It could also be indicative of physical wounds, bruises and cuts. Emotionally, it could represent a period of intense sadness, depression, difficulty sleeping and an inability to concentrate. It could be an indication that the person needs to take a step back from the situation and to look for ways to heal and help themselves.

Finances: The 10 of Swords Tarot card is a warning sign for financial trouble. It could be indicative of bad investments, losses, bankruptcies or lending situations gone wrong. It is a sign of difficult times and of no immediate solution to the situation. It is recommended to be careful and to take a step back and look for ways to reduce debt and find alternative sources of income. It may be beneficial to get a professional advice in order to find the best resolution.

Relationships: In relationships, the 10 of Swords Tarot card indicates there may be an ending of a cycle or an unhealthy relationship. It is an indication of one-sided relationships, deep-seated resentments, breakdowns in communication and control. It is a warning sign and advice is to take a step back, look for a way out and seek help. It could be a sign to end the relationship or to seek professional help.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health

The King of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take care of one’s health and well-being. The card highlights how important it is to be a strategic thinker when it comes to taking care of one’s body. This means understanding the importance of nutrition, exercise, and proper rest. Additionally, this card suggests that it can be beneficial to seek advice from healthcare professionals and to take an active role in one’s own health and wellbeing. Being proactive and taking the necessary steps to maintain one’s health is key.

Finances

The King of Swords Tarot card speaks of taking a strategic approach to finances. This involves making decisions based on research and logic rather than emotions or impulse. The card urges one to spend wisely and to be aware of the long-term implications of financial decisions, including investment strategies. Additionally, the card suggests that it is important to be realistic about one’s financial goals and to develop an understanding of markets and trends. A strategic approach to finances is key to achieving financial security and stability.

Relationships

The King of Swords Tarot card is an admonishment that relationships should be approached with strategic thought and careful consideration. This means being mindful of the impact of one’s words and actions on the people around them. Additionally, the card suggests that communication is key to any successful relationship, and to ensure that fairness and mutual respect are maintained. The card also urges one to carefully analyze the conditions of a relationship to ensure that it is healthy and beneficial to both parties. Taking a strategic approach to relationships can lead to greater satisfaction, understanding, and stability.
