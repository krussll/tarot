A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the King Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The King Of Wands card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does King Of Wands and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The King of Wands and the 6 of Swords tarot card together represent a powerful moment of change and transformation. The King of Wands speaks to courage and strength, enabling you to take bold steps and take charge of your life. The 6 of Swords speaks to the necessary transitions in life and the need to relocate and move as you pursue your destiny. Together, these two cards signify a powerful moment of change and transformation as you take full control of your life, with the power and courage of the King of Wands and the need to transition and relocate with the 6 of Swords. This combination of tarot cards is telling you that although it may require some difficult changes, you are capable of making the necessary changes to reach your goals.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

Health:

The King of Wands Tarot card symbolizes health, vitality and self-love. The ability to have a strong sense of self and to be able to take on challenges is part and parcel of this card. The desire to stay in good physical shape can be a welcome asset, and this card encourages you to focus on making the most of your health and wellbeing. It is also an excellent card for anyone who is looking to make positive changes in their lifestyle, as the King of Wands Tarot card suggests that courage and commitment are key to making a change.

Finances:

The King of Wands Tarot card is a great card to pull if you are looking to take control of your finances. This card suggests that taking charge of your money and finances is a positive step, and that you have the power and courage to make successful decisions. Financial security is important, and this card encourages you to be confident in your decisions and to trust your gut instincts. It also implies that financial success is achievable with the right attitude and tools in place.

Relationships:

The King of Wands Tarot card is a great card to represent relationships. It suggests that relationships can be successful when there is trust, commitment and courage on both sides. This card suggests that building a strong, solid relationship requires a sense of courage and self-respect, and that both parties should be willing to take risks in order to build a strong, lasting bond. It also implies that being true to oneself and to one’s values is key to any successful relationship. The King of Wands Tarot card encourages one to take a stand for what they believe in and not to compromise their values or beliefs.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Six of Swords tarot card is often seen as a metaphor for emotional and mental health. The boat in the card is a symbol of passage and the swords represent emotional burdens being carried. This card encourages one to move beyond old beliefs and routines that no longer serve them, and to free themselves from the stress and emotional baggage of the past. It is a reminder to let go of whatever is holding us back from our emotional and physical wellbeing.

Finances:  
The Six of Swords tarot card is a reminder that sometimes it is necessary to make difficult financial decisions, such as relocating for a better career opportunity or taking a loss on an investment. This card is a sign that a fresh start is possible, and that one should be willing to take risks in order to improve their financial situation. It also encourages one to take stock of their resources, trust their judgment and make plans for the future.

Relationships:  
The Six of Swords tarot card can suggest that a relationship is going through a period of transition. It may be time to let go of what is no longer working and to give the relationship a fresh start. This could include having difficult conversations or taking time to reassess and be honest about what it is one really wants and needs. With this card, it is important to have the courage to make changes, even if they feel scary or unfamiliar, in order to benefit the relationship in the long run.
