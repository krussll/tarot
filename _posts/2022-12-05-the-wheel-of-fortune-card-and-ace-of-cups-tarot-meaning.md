## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a potent symbol of luck and opportunity. It often indicates that a turning point or change of fortune is imminent. It can suggest that problems which have been causing difficulty are about to be resolved and that something new, beneficial and exciting is about to enter your life.

When considering health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be a sign that opportunities for improved wellness are on the horizon. It could mean that a breakthrough in health or the discovery of a new treatment could be around the corner. It also suggests that you should take advantage of any new health or fitness opportunities that present themselves to you, as they could be the key to a healthier future.

In terms of finances, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be indicative of a major financial change. It could suggest financial success, whether it be through an inheritance, a job promotion or a lucky gamble. Alternatively, it could mean that sudden financial losses are pending, such as a risky investment that could lead to bankruptcy. In either case, it is important to be mindful of any financial decisions you make and make sure they are sound ones.

When it comes to relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card could mean a favorable turn of events. It could suggest that a relationship which has been strained could suddenly become more harmonious, or that a new relationship could enter your life and bring with it much joy. However, it can also suggest that an old relationship could suffer a setback, or even come to an end. In either case, it is important to be mindful of the need for balance between luck and hard work in order to ensure a successful future for any relationship.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

Health  
The Ace of Cups Tarot card is associated with healing and health, both physical and emotional. It can symbolize taking care of oneself, developing healthy habits, and creating balance in life. It can also be seen as a sign of improvement in an existing condition. The card suggests taking a spiritual approach to dealing with health issues and staying open to the possibility of divine intervention. The card encourages us to be aware of our emotions and how they affect our physical well-being.

Finances  
The Ace of Cups Tarot card can represent a new and better financial situation, as well as the beginnings of wealth creation and success. It can be a sign of a new job, an unexpected inheritance, or a surprise bonus. It is a reminder to be mindful of our spending habits and to invest our money wisely. Above all, the Ace of Cups tarot card is a reminder to focus on manifesting our goals instead of letting our worries get in the way.

Relationships  
The Ace of Cups Tarot card can indicate a new relationship or the start of a journey of self-discovery as it relates to existing relationships. It can also be seen as a new understanding or a deeper appreciation of the give and take of relationships. It encourages us to be honest with our emotions and to express ourselves openly and authentically. The Ace of Cups can also signify the potential for recovery and reconciliation with those we have hurt or been hurt by in the past. Above all, the Ace of Cups Tarot card reminds us to trust the process and to be open to the changes that are coming our way.
