This is the journey of taking action, making change, and navigating through unknown territory. In essence, it is your journey to take risk, progress, and strive for success. As you prepare for this journey, remember to stay vigilant and steadfast in the face of competition, as this is what will ultimately lead you to success and a new adventure. With the Chariot tarot card and the 7 of Wands tarot card, you are ready to drive your vehicle of change into the future with ambition and power!

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Chariot mean?

The Chariot Tarot card can represent both an external and an internal journey. On the external level, it often symbolizes taking action, whether that’s setting up a plan of action to get out of debt or investing in yourself and your skills. It’s a card of movement and forward momentum, suggesting that you’re ready to take charge and make progress in whatever area you desire. On an internal level, The Chariot Tarot card encourages us to take charge of our own physical, emotional and mental health. It reminds us that we are the only ones who can take the steps necessary to improve our overall wellbeing.

In terms of finances, The Chariot Tarot card reminds us that to reach a more secure financial future, we have to be willing to take action. Whether that means starting a budget, learning new skills, or investing in ourselves, The Chariot encourages us to take that first step and make progress towards our financial goals. It also represents risk-taking, so if you want to be successful, you’ll need to be willing to explore new opportunities, even if it’s a bit scary.

In terms of relationships, The Chariot Tarot card reminds us to take action in order to strengthen our existing relationships or create new ones. It encourages us to have honest conversations, to strive for understanding and compromise, and to be willing to work hard on our relationships. It also reminds us that relationships require effort from both sides, so it’s important to be willing to put in the work if we want our relationships to be successful.

Overall, The Chariot Tarot card encourages us to take action. Whether it’s in regards to our health, finances, or relationships, this card reminds us that progress comes from taking that first step. It also encourages us to be willing to take risks, explore new opportunities, and strive to create the life we want.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands Tarot Card symbolizes self-defense and protection against competition. It tells you that if you take a proactive stance, you will be able to protect yourself from any negative energy or people in your life. In terms of health, this card advises you to look out for yourself and to not accept any offers that could be harmful to your well-being. It could also be telling you to keep up the good work with your current health plan and to make sure to stay on top of it.

In terms of finances, it could be telling you to protect yourself from any dangerous financial deals. Make sure to be aware of the risks, and take steps to understand your financial situation. You can also use this card to remind yourself to stay on top of your financial health, such as budgeting and paying off debt.

In terms of relationships, this card can remind you to take steps to ensure that your relationships are healthy. This could mean setting boundaries, being honest with yourself and your partner, and looking out for any warning signs that the relationship may not be healthy. Additionally, it can be a reminder to stay focused and not lose sight of what you want from the relationship, so that it stays strong and secure.
