A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The 2 of Wands and the 9 of Cups suggest that now is the time to make a wish and see it come true. You have done the hard work and are now ready to embrace the rewards of your labor. It is time to take a leap of faith and travel to a place that sparks your joy. Be open to a world of possibilities and let the universe know your deepest desires. With a mix of patience and vigilance, you can expect tangible rewards that can bring health and abundance.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 2 Of Wands and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

The 2 of Wands Tarot card is a card of waiting for results, making choices and travelling plans, and so its meaning can be applied both broadly and specifically in many areas of life. It is associated with restlessness and anticipation. In terms of health, the 2 of Wands informs us that it is time to take a pause and examine our well-being, both physical and mental. It could be a sign that we need to explore different forms of physical or spiritual healing and find ways to nurture ourselves.

When it comes to finances, the 2 of Wands urges us to be mindful of our spending and be wiser in our decision-making. This is a time to reflect on our current financial situation, as well as our future prospects, and to make decisions that will benefit us in the long run. However, it is important to remember not to be too impulsive.

The 2 of Wands can also provide insight into our relationships. The card inspires us to take a step back before we make any hasty decisions and to use this time to reflect on how we can nurture our relationships. We should be mindful of the potential consequences of our decisions and think before we act. Taking a pause to fully consider all aspects of a situation will help us to make the right choices and move forward with confidence.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is an excellent omen when it comes to health, finances and relationships. This card is all about positive outcomes, and so it promises that if one works diligently, they can achieve their goals and live a happy, healthy and prosperous life.

When it comes to health, the 9 of Cups offers the assurance that health and wellness can be attained through commitment and hard work. With this card, one is encouraged to make the most of their physical and mental health, striving to keep the body energized and the mind in balance. When there are challenges, one can find the strength and will to carry on, as this card ensures that the effort will not be wasted.

When it comes to finances, the 9 of Cups suggests that abundance and prosperity can be realized through dedication and perseverance. The idea is to remain patient and continue to keep your eye on the long-term goal, as the rewards are great and lead to success and abundance. One should also take the time to appreciate the blessings of life, understanding that money can come and go, but that wealth is in the heart and the experiences that are encountered.

Finally, with relationships, the 9 of Cups gives the assurance that love and companionship can be found and shared with others. The card implies that relationships should be nurtured, as true happiness and satisfaction come from meaningful connections and enjoying moments together. With this card, one is reminded to keep an open heart, be kind and understanding, and share genuine moments with those you care about. This will foster positive and long-lasting relationships.
