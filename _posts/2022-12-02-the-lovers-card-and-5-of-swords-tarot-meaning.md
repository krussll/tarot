## What does The Lovers and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The 5 Of Swords tarot card mean different things together for you. The Lovers card recognizes the special bond shared between two people and acknowledges the strength that comes from a deep love. However, The 5 Of Swords warns of the potential for abusive relationships, theft, and violence. This card is a reminder that you must be aware of the implications of your choices and take responsibility for the safety of yourself and your loved ones. Together, these cards suggest that while love can bring strength, it can also bring danger if not handled with care.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Five of Swords tarot card is often interpreted as an indicator of health problems related to violent, aggressive, or abusive relationships. This could include physical injuries, mental health issues, and substance abuse. Physically, a person may find themselves in a situation where they feel threatened and unsafe to the point that their health becomes compromised as a result. Mental health issues could arise from feeling unsafe or hearing aggressive language or threats. Substance abuse is often a symptom of feelings of powerlessness and fear which can be caused by a violent environment.

Finances  
The Five of Swords tarot card can also be interpreted as an indicator of financial struggles related to theft or theft-like behavior. This could include not only outright stealing of money or possessions, but also fraud and identity theft. It may also point to financial abuse or manipulation, and can refer to situations in which the victim feels powerless and doesn’t know how to protect themselves or their finances.

Relationships  
The Five of Swords tarot card is often seen as a warning of trouble in relationships. It can refer to relationships with a power imbalance or any kind of physical, emotional, or verbal abuse. It can also point to situations where one person is trying to control or manipulate the other, either through intimidation or threats. Additionally, it can indicate a bully-victim dynamic in which one person is taking advantage of the other, either emotionally or physically.
