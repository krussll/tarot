A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The 4 Of Pentacles and The 3 Of Swords tarot card reveal a complex and difficult combination to interpret, but they point towards a lot of insecurity and fear. The 4 Of Pentacles points to an underlying need for a sense of long-term security, but this can manifest itself in being overly possessive and greedy. The 3 Of Swords suggests a painful heartbreak, or loss, and is a reminder that desire for security can result in shutting oneself off from relationships and intimacy. Together, these two cards urge to find balance between staying safe and secure, and allowing yourself to take risks in life and open yourself up to love.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card can be interpreted to highlight issues relating to health, finances, and relationships. This card can indicate a tendency towards hoarding, which often has both positive and negative consequences.

When it comes to health, the 4 of Pentacles suggests a desire for long-term wellbeing. This card can denote a focus on nutrition, exercise, and prevention, and could also signal a fixation on health, leading to excessive worry and stress.

The 4 of Pentacles card in the realm of finances can indicate a desire for financial stability and security. This card can denote a focus on saving and investing, and an unwillingness to take risks. It could also signal an extreme need for money, leading to compulsive shopping and debt.

Finally, the 4 of Pentacles Tarot card can represent a need for constancy and stability in relationships. This card can denote a fear of commitment, and an unwillingness to open up or change. It could also suggest a focus on material possessions or power structures, and an unwillingness to let go of control or trust others.

Regardless of the domain, the 4 of Pentacles Tarot card can signify a focus on long-term security, though it can also indicate a tendency towards excess and a lack of flexibility. As such, it is important to take the time to cultivate the balance between strength and flexibility, and be thankful for the security it can bring.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords Tarot card carries a heavy energy, representing the pain of loss and heartache. When it appears in a reading, it can be a sign of a difficult time ahead in all aspects of life, including physical and emotional health, financial stability, and relationships.

In terms of health, the 3 of Swords indicates that something difficult is coming. It could be a major shift in your physical health, such as a major surgery, or a long-term illness or condition that you have to manage. It can also represent a mental or emotional difficulty, such as depression or anxiety, or a traumatic event that had an impact on your well-being. Whatever it is, it can be a difficult challenge to face, but it is also an opportunity for personal growth.

Financially, the 3 of Swords can indicate a difficult period, where existing resources may be stretched, or unexpected expenses may come up. It’s important to take a step back and assess the situation, try to make the best decisions you can with the information you have. It’s possible that with careful planning, you can work your way through this difficult period and come out the other side in a healthier financial situation.

In terms of relationships, the 3 of Swords can represent heartbreak and the end of a relationship. It can be difficult to move on, but it also is an opportunity to reflect and learn from past experiences. It can also bring new opportunities for connection and growth. Whatever the situation, it is important to look for the silver lining and be honest about your needs and feelings.
