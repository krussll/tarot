A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Pentacles card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Pentacles and Queen Of Swords mean together?

When the Page Of Pentacles and The Queen Of Swords come together, they signify a hardworking young person who is both ambitious and honest. They suggest that this person is driven to secure financial success and is willing to work for it. This person will be patient in their pursuit for money news and a new job or raise, and be able to rely on their own skills and abilities to make it happen. With total honesty and resilience, they will be able to achieve their goals and be rewarded with their ambition.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Page Of Pentacles and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is an encouraging message when it comes to health, finances and relationships. It signifies new beginnings, optimism, and fresh starts.

When it comes to health, the Page of Pentacles stands for health consciousness and a desire for a healthier lifestyle. It suggests that the querent is ready to make necessary changes in order to improve their overall health. The Page of Pentacles encourages taking small steps, such as getting more exercise, eating a balanced diet, and taking the time to relax and meditate. With patience and dedication, one can achieve a healthier lifestyle.

When it comes to finances, the Page of Pentacles encourages the querent to pursue new opportunities, such as a new job or career path. It also symbolizes a possible raise or money news. This card stands for patience and being mindful of one’s finances. It suggests that one should take the time to think through decisions and be willing to invest time and energy into their finances.

When it comes to relationships, the Page of Pentacles symbolizes a willingness to stick things out and build a strong foundation. It encourages patience and understanding, and encourages the querent to use their knowledge and resources in order to strengthen their connection with their partners. It also symbolizes the importance of communication and being willing to compromise. The Page of Pentacles stands for understanding and being willing to give and take in order to create a healthy and lasting relationship.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is indicative of taking control of one’s health. Self-sufficiency and a focus on honesty are emphasized, suggesting that it may be time to take ownership of one’s own health care journey. The Queen of Swords can indicate the need to be mindful regarding any medical advice taken in, and to seek out a second opinion if necessary. It encourages a person to approach any health issue with clear and rational thinking, rather than letting fear or anxiety rule the decision-making process.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a powerful reminder to take an honest and independent approach to finances. It encourages an individual to question their spending habits and look for ways to reduce costs and increase financial security. This card can also suggest that it is time to take responsibility for one’s own financial well-being, rather than relying on others to provide for one’s financial needs.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the need for self-sufficiency, total honesty and clear communication in relationships. It encourages a person to take responsibility for their own happiness, rather than relying on their partner to make them feel fulfilled. This card also encourages an individual to be open and honest with their partners, and to communicate their needs clearly and directly. It can be a reminder to approach all relationships with objective thinking, and to keep emotion out of the equation.
