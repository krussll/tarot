A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The 10 of Pentacles and the 8 of Cups together represent a time of transition and letting go. It could be a time to consider retirement or the passing on of a legacy, as the 10 of Pentacles often represents the culmination of a long life journey. However, it could also represent a time to retreat from the world and a change in direction. The 8 of Cups often suggests leaving behind a situation which has become unfulfilling or unproductive, so it could be time to withdraw from a situation and focus on a new course of action. Together, these cards suggest that a time of change is upon you and it’s time to create a legacy of your own or move on from a stagnant situation.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

When it comes to health, the 10 Of Pentacles Tarot card could indicate that one is taking good care of their health and have the resources to do so. It could be a sign that the person is in good health and is managing their lifestyle in a way that can bring long-term wellbeing.

In terms of finances, the 10 Of Pentacles tarot card could mean that the person is financially stable and can depend on a secure income stream. It often symbolizes stability and having long-term goals and plans for the future. It could mean that the person is making sound investments and is taking the steps to secure their financial future.

In terms of relationships, the 10 Of Pentacles tarot card could mean that the person is in a committed, long-term relationship that is built on trust, understanding, and devotion. It may also signify that the person is committed to building a strong family and leaving behind a legacy. They have a secure base from which to create and maintain strong relationships.

Ultimately, the 10 Of Pentacles tarot card is a card of security, accomplishment, and stability. It symbolizes the ability to create long-term goals and to secure one’s physical, emotional, and financial future. By taking the steps and making the investments today, one can ensure a better future while creating a secure and prosperous life.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 8 Of Cups Tarot card is an indicator that a person may be in the process of withdrawing from their current physical or mental health situation. This card implies that the individual wants to find a better or healthier balance and is making a conscious effort to step away and take a break for their own wellbeing. It could represent a need for self-care, a desire for healthier habits, or a need to take some time to reflect and understand the current circumstances in order to make better decisions moving forward. The individual may be taking a step back in order to gain clarity and perspective, so that they can be better prepared to tackle any issues they are facing in the future.

Finances

The 8 Of Cups Tarot card is an indication that a person may be turning away from their current financial situation. This could mean they are taking a step back from their current investments or financial strategies in order to reassess and make better decisions moving forward. They might be looking to make changes to their financial portfolios, or take a break from spending or investing in order to better prepare for their future. The card could also represent a need for fiscal restraint, with the individual taking a step back to evaluate their current financial opportunities and make better decisions in the future.

Relationships

The 8 Of Cups Tarot card is an indication that a person may be withdrawing from relationships, either temporarily or for good. This could mean that the individual is looking for some distance in order to protect their own wellbeing and regain clarity on the situation. They may be looking to take some time away from their current relationships to better understand what is beneficial or unhelpful for them. The card could also symbolize the need for space to process some difficult emotions, or the end of a relationship in order to move on to something better.
