A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 9 Of Cups tarot card together represent a coming together of the traditional and the modern. This suggests that if we are willing to conform to the conventions and expectations of the world around us, we may be blessed with the abundance and blessings of our wishes coming true. This can be seen in good health, financial security, and even the fulfillment of our hopes and dreams. By embracing and finding peace in the traditions and conventions of our society, we can reap the rewards of material abundance and true wellbeing.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This could mean anything from traditions in your family to cultural expectations in your society.

This could mean a wish to find the right person to share your life with or a wish to move to a more committed stage of your relationship. This could mean that any investments of energy you put into your relationship will be rewarded, and your efforts will be met with equal and positive energy. With these two cards, you are sure to experience harmony and gratitude for the efforts you make in your love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health  
The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that conventional approaches to health should be consulted and respected. This means traditional health practices and treatments, such as herbalism and acupuncture, should be considered and respected. It also means that you should be grounded in healthy habits and routines, such as regular exercise and healthy eating. The Hierophant card can indicate that the best results will come from following the tried and true methods of healing and wellness, rather than looking for the latest trend or fad.

Finances  
The Hierophant Tarot card can represent financial security, but also suggests that conventional approaches to finances should be respected. This means following the basics of money management, such as creating a budget, tracking your spending, and being mindful of your spending habits. The card can also indicate that the most successful approach to investing is the tried and true strategies, rather than chasing after the latest trends or rumors.

Relationships  
The Hierophant Tarot card can represent traditional approaches to relationships, such as showing respect for social norms and conventions. It can also suggest that relationships are built on trust, loyalty, commitment, and communication. As with health and finances, it suggests that the most successful relationships come from following the tried and true approaches to relationships, rather than pursuing the latest trend or fad. The card can also indicate that strong, lasting relationships require a certain level of commitment, both from the individual and from their partners.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is a very positive card, representing material abundance and good health. When it comes to health, the 9 of Cups indicates that you can expect a positive result or outcome in regards to healing, physical or emotional. You will have a strong sense of wellbeing, as well as a feeling of contentment and satisfaction with where you’re at currently. You will have restored inner peace and security, as well as serenity in your life.

When it comes to finances, the 9 of Cups signifies that all your wishes for financial security and abundance will come true. You may find yourself feeling financially stable and secure, with a good level of savings to back you up. You can expect to attract more abundance into your life and also to have a strong flow of abundance coming into your life.

In regards to relationships, the 9 of Cups suggests that you can expect more harmony and stability in your relationships. This card encourages you to practice unconditional love and forgiveness, which will help create stronger, more fulfilling relationships. You’re likely to feel more relaxed and content, allowing you to enjoy deep, meaningful connections with your loved ones. You will be relieved of any worries or concerns you may have had in the past. All of your relationships should be filled with joy and love.
