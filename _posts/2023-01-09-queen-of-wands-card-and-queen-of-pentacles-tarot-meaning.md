## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

For health, the Queen of Wands indicates a person who approaches health matters with strength and determination. They will have a proactive attitude towards maintaining and restoring their health and will be confident in their decisions and the course of action they take.

For finances, the Queen of Wands encourages the individual to be confident and make decisions that are best for their finances. This includes taking risks and being open to making bold financial decisions. They may be willing to take on challenges that others may not be comfortable doing, such as investing in the stock market or taking out a loan.

For relationships, the Queen of Wands encourages individuals to be more assertive and self-assured in their relationships. They will be unafraid to speak their mind and stand up for their beliefs. ## What does Queen Of Pentacles mean?

The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of wealth, stability, and fertility, and it can indicate a powerful female figure or a strong sense of domesticity. When it comes to health, this card is a sign of abundance. It is a reminder that when you are healthy and your environment is healthy, you will reap the rewards of feeling strong and taking good care of yourself. If this card appears in a reading, it suggests good news coming related to health – possibly a job offer, a better health plan, a new nutritional path, or perhaps an equipped exercise regimen made available.

When it comes to finances, the Queen of Pentacles is a sign of security, comfort, and stability. This card suggests that your finances are being managed with good intent and direction, and you can feel secure in knowing that your money is being handled responsibly. This card advises you to take control, be mindful of overspending, and build a steady stream of income.

In terms of relationships, this card signifies a strong bond, commitment, and trust. It suggests that your relationship is stabilizing and maturing, and that you will be able to weather any storm together. The Queen of Pentacles illustrates a sense of comfort, understanding, and support, indicating that your partnership is strong and will become even stronger with time. This card is a reminder to take care of and nurture your relationship, have thoughtful communication, and always keep the connection alive.
