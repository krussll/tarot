A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and The Tower card together in particular.

The Justice card and The Tower have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and The Tower mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The Tower tarot card together represent a situation of conflict and chaos, but ultimately a victory of truth and justice. In the face of upheaval and ruin, these cards suggest that the truth will come out and the right solution will be revealed. However, this victory may come with the price of unwanted change and disruption. There may be disruption and violence, but ultimately the situation will be resolved in a fair and just way, allowing everybody to come out winners.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Doing the necessary work may cause some upheaval in the short-term, however it can lead to a brighter and more fulfilling relationship in the long-term.

Therefore, the combination of the Justice and Tower tarot cards in the context of your love life is a message of needed change. It is a reminder that truth and honesty must be present if the relationship is to survive. And if you can find the courage to both confront the issues in your relationship and come together to find a solution, then the result could be a healthier, loving union.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and The Tower mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and The Tower the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card is a powerful warning about unexpected and unwanted changes in health, finances and relationships. When this card appears in a reading, it could mean that the changes that are coming may be disruptive and potentially damaging.

Healthwise, The Tower card can indicate severe illness that may prove to be difficult to overcome. It can suggest an unforeseen illness or an unexpected turn for the worse in an ongoing condition. It may also be a warning that comes too late in the game, indicating that prevention is too little too late. Alternatively, it suggests that a drastic change in lifestyle might be necessary in order to regain balance. Such changes might include quitting unhealthy habits, changing to a healthier diet or exercising more.

In terms of finances, the Tower card can be a warning of financial ruin or sudden economic upheaval. It could indicate that investments that are in place are at risk of collapsing or that trust that has been placed in another person is misplaced. It might also suggest that drastic measures must be taken to salvage the situation, such as cutting spending or seeking outside help.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Tower card can be a sign of destruction and upheaval. It can indicate the sudden end of a relationship that once seemed stable, or an unexpected turn for the worse in a current relationship. It can also signify restructuring and a need to reassess the dynamics of the relationship in order to make it work. It might suggest a need to take a step back and reassess one’s needs and expectations in relationships. In any case, it is likely that a major shift is required to move forward in a healthy way.
