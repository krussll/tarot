Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The 6 Of Pentacles card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 6 Of Pentacles, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 6 Of Pentacles.

## 6 Of Pentacles And Health – An Overview

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card is a reminder of the importance of generosity and the power of giving and receiving help in regards to health.

It encourages us to reach out to others in times of need and to look for ways to be of service to one another.

By helping others, we can also help ourselves.

This card encourages us to recognize that we are all connected, and that our health is dependent on the health of our communities.

It also speaks to the importance of self-care, both in terms of emotional and physical health.

The 6 of Pentacles encourages us to be generous with ourselves too, and to recognize that our own health is of paramount importance.

## Upright Meanings For 6 Of Pentacles And Health

### What It Means For Me

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card is a powerful reminder to be generous with ourselves and others when it comes to health.

This card symbolizes a willingness to give and receive help, and it calls on us to be open to receiving assistance, whether that be asking for help when we need it or offering a lending hand to those in need.

It emphasizes the power of community and collaboration in achieving optimal health, reminding us that we don’t have to go through our health-related struggles alone.

Moreover, this card can also mean that it’s time to be generous with ourselves, to take the time to nourish our bodies, minds, and souls by prioritizing self-care.

### What It Means For The Future

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card is a positive omen for your future health, as it suggests that you will receive help and support in achieving your health goals.

This card could indicate that you will receive financial support for medical treatments, or that someone will offer you emotional support and guidance.

It could also suggest that you will be generous with your own resources, either by offering advice or assistance to someone else, or by donating to a health-related cause.

In any case, it suggests that you will be able to use the help and support of others to work towards a healthier future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card speaks to the health of those close to me, indicating that I am surrounded by a generous and supportive network of family, friends, and colleagues.

It speaks to the importance of giving and receiving help in times of need, encouraging me to reach out for assistance from those around me when necessary.

It also emphasizes the importance of being generous with my time, energy, and resources in order to provide support for those who may be struggling with their own wellness.

The 6 of Pentacles encourages me to use my resources to assist in the health and wellbeing of those close to me, for the benefit of us all.

## Reversed Meanings For 6 Of Pentacles And Health

### What It Means For Me

When the 6 of Pentacles is reversed, it suggests an imbalance in the act of giving and receiving help.

This could be an indicator of unhealthy relationships with money and resources, where one party is not equally participating in the exchange.

The reversed 6 of Pentacles can also signify a lack of generosity, both financial and emotional.

People who are struggling with their health due to poverty or financial strain can feel overwhelmed, unable to provide for themselves as well as their loved ones.

It can also be a warning sign of emotional withholding, where a person is unable to show love and acceptance to others, resulting in an unhealthy state of mind.

The reversed 6 of Pentacles can also symbolize feelings of guilt or shame associated with asking for help from others.

This can lead to a person feeling more isolated, resulting in a decline of their physical and emotional health.

On the other hand, the reversed 6 of Pentacles can also suggest a refusal of help, where a person is taking on too much of a burden and not allowing anyone else to assist them.

This can be a sign of pride or stubbornness, but can also lead to feelings of exhaustion, overworking, and a lack of self-care.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 6 of Pentacles in a health context relates to a sense of imbalance and inequity.

In an individual’s personal life, this could indicate a lack of support from family or friends, or a lack of money or resources to pay for medical care.

It could also signify a feeling of being overwhelmed by the cost of treatments and therapies, or feeling unable to get the help and support required to improve one’s health.

In a broader sense, it might suggest that society is failing to provide adequate health care, or a lack of investment in public health initiatives.

The reversed 6 of Pentacles stands in stark contrast to its upright position, which encourages us to appreciate the generosity of others and to reciprocate their kindness.

In the reversed position, it could suggest that a feeling of bitterness or resentment has taken over, and that an individual feels like they’re not getting the help or support they need.

It could also suggest that a person is feeling guilty for not being able to give back in the same way that others have given to them.

Ultimately, the reversed 6 of Pentacles encourages us to take care of our own health, both in terms of physical and mental well-being, and to ensure that we are taking care of ourselves first and foremost.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the 6 of Pentacles is reversed, it suggests that there is an imbalance in the area of health.

There is a lack of equilibrium in the way we are giving and receiving help and support.

We may feel like we are not receiving the assistance we need or that we are unable to give assistance to others.

This imbalance can lead to feelings of frustration and discouragement.

It also speaks to our physical health.

We may be overwhelmed with our own physical needs and feel like our health is taking a backseat to other areas of our lives.

It can be difficult to take the time for self-care and invest in our own wellbeing.

This can lead to feelings of exhaustion and imbalance, resulting in physical and mental health issues.

The reversed 6 of Pentacles encourages us to seek help from our community and to be generous to ourselves and others.

We should make a conscious effort to balance our giving and receiving, and to nurture our own health and wellbeing.

This will create a healthier, more balanced life.
