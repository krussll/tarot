A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The 9 Of Swords and The Queen Of Cups tarot cards depict a difficult situation, but with a hopeful outcome. The 9 Of Swords brings up the negative emotions of nightmares, anxiety, grief and depression, but The Queen Of Cups is here to offer comfort and support. She is a source of healing, nurturing, and emotional support that can help you find your way out of the darkness. These two cards together represent the possibility of transformation and healing, as long as you stay open to the support and resources that you need. With the combined power of these two cards, you can find the strength and courage to overcome your struggles and emerge stronger than before.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords tarot card is a reminder of the psychological and emotional impact that negative circumstances can have on our lives, and the toll they can take on our physical, spiritual, financial and relationship health.

When interpreting the 9 of Swords card, it is important to understand the damage that anxiety, fear, depression, and despair can cause. Physically, these emotions can lead to insomnia, fatigue, headaches, nausea, and overall poor health. Spiritually, they can lead to a sense of disconnection, confusion and lack of clarity. Financially, fear, worry and sadness can lead to impulsive decisions that can be costly and cause further financial stress. Relationships can also be impacted when fear and despair are present, leading to isolation and a breakdown of communication.

The 9 of Swords card is indicative of a difficult and trying period, but there is still hope as it also encourages us to find the strength to break free from negative patterns and look toward the future. Working with a counselor or therapist can be beneficial to help manage and cope with feelings of anxiety and despair, and can bring clarity to better understand our emotions. Self-care, such as meditating, exercising, and engaging in meaningful activities, can provide a sense of calm and strength. Investing in our relationships, seeking counsel from a financial adviser, and taking steps to increase our financial security can help improve our overall wellbeing.

Ultimately, the 9 of Swords emphasizes that looking for solutions and taking charge of our lives can break through negative patterns and result in peace and harmony. By making mindful and conscious efforts to heal, our physical, spiritual, financial and relationship health can begin to restore.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health: The Queen of Cups Tarot card indicates a person who is emotionally nurturing and understanding. She understands the importance of a healthy balance between mind and body, and encourages others to ensure the same in themselves. The Queen of Cups is a symbol of healing from within and she is here to remind us to take care of our well-being. She teaches us to listen to our inner selves and to allow healing energies to come in and nourish our soul.

Finances: Although the Queen of Cups is not necessarily a symbol of wealth and abundance, she is a symbol of comfort, security and stability. She encourages us to focus on the long-term perspective and to build sustainable financial stability. She encourages us to save, to invest and to be responsible with our money. She is also a symbol of hope, as she reminds us to believe in ourselves, our capabilities and to be patient to allow wealth to come to us over time.

Relationships: The Queen of Cups is a symbol of unconditional love and understanding. She encourages us to care and nurture our relationships, to be emotionally available and to be supportive. She reminds us to be honest and open with our loved ones and to always act with respect and compassion. She is also a symbol of emotional strength, as she helps us to be vulnerable and brave to express our true feelings and be open to the emotions of others.
