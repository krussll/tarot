A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and The Star card together in particular.

The Death card and The Star have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and The Star mean together?

The combination of The Death tarot card and The Star tarot card is a powerful one, signifying the ending of one phase and the beginning of a new one. It symbolizes a significant transformation and change, but with the promise of renewal, clarity, hope, and even miracles. It is a call to have faith in yourself and the universe, and to embrace the opportunity to begin anew. It is time to move on from the old and make way for the new, allowing yourself to be in the spotlight and trust in your inner spark to guide you on your journey.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and The Star mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health

The Death Tarot card symbolizes the death of something, so it stands for endings. In health, this card often symbolizes the end of a certain period of bad health. It may mean that an illness has finally been resolved and healed, or an injury that has been taking a long time to heal is now coming to an end. It can also suggest that unhealthy habits and negative coping mechanisms are no longer necessary. It is a call to take a look at your physical and mental health, and make the necessary changes, in order to allow for new beginnings.

Finances

In terms of finances, The Death Tarot card stands for big changes. It can mean the end of an old cycle or habit that no longer serves your financial goals. It is a call to assess your current financial situation and to make the necessary changes. It may suggest that it is time to move on from a difficult financial situation, whether it means reapplying for a loan, finding a better job, or simply changing the way you manage your money. This card can also foreshadow a new financial venture that could open up opportunities you never knew existed.

Relationships

The Death Tarot card brings with it the foreboding of change, transformation, and endings. In relationships, this card typically signifies the end of a certain relationship dynamic or the end of a certain relationship itself. It stands for a time to reflect and assess whether the relationship is still healthy for both parties, and if not, to say goodbye. It can also indicate that it is time to move on and explore new relationships, or the end of a difficult period of adjustment in a relationship. It is a call to shed the old and begin anew.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card is a sign of encouragement, renewal, and hope. It encourages us to have faith and look to the future with optimism. In terms of health and well-being, the Star Tarot card suggests that we focus on our inner self and find clarity. We should look within and be honest with ourselves and our feelings and emotions, as it can lead to miraculous healing. We should look for the silver lining in all of our circumstances, even if we may not feel it right away.

In terms of finances, the Star Tarot card can bring us back from the brink. Feeling financial pressures can sometimes be overwhelming, but the Star Tarot card shows us the possibility of turning things around. We should have faith that positive changes can be made, and use all the resources available to us to create the financial stability and independence we desire.

In terms of relationships, the Star Tarot card is a reminder that we should always have faith in each other and never give up on a relationship. It suggests that we use our inner clarity and understanding to create better relationships and maintain strong communication with our partners. It also reminds us to be mindful of our own feelings and to take time to express them so we can be in the spotlight, thus strengthening our relationships.

The Star Tarot card is a powerful message of renewal, hope, and inner clarity. It reminds us to remain strong and have faith in ourselves, our health, our finances, and our relationships. We should trust our own inner power and recognize our own potential to create miraculous things. We should use our inner clarity to make decisions and take steps towards creating a brighter future.
