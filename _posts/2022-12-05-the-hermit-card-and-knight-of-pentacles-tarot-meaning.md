A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Knight Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Knight Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Knight Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hermit tarot card combined with The Knight Of Pentacles tarot card speaks to a journey within oneself to find the clarity, spiritual illumination, and personal truth that one seeks. This journey is a slow and steady one, requiring patience and hard work, just like the Knight Of Pentacles tarot card suggests. By investing in one’s personal growth and development as suggested by The Hermit tarot card and investing in the hard work required to get there by The Knight Of Pentacles tarot card, one can come out of the quest to find their truth with a deeper understanding of their self and the world around them.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and Knight Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health:

The Hermit Tarot card symbolizes the need to turn away from the outside world in order to focus on your inner self. This card highlights the importance of taking time to pause, connect with your inner-being, and pay attention to your personal needs. From a health perspective, this card may suggest that it’s time to focus on the things that nourish your body, mind, and soul. Whether it’s through diet, exercise, or self-care rituals, the Hermit Tarot card encourages you to take the time to nurture and nourish yourself.

Finances:

The Hermit Tarot card encourages you to take a step back from your financial decisions and reflect on how your actions can be more aligned with your values. This card suggests that it may be time to pause and reconsider your financial goals before moving forward. It could be a good time to detach from the materialistic aspects of life and focus on more meaningful goals and values. This card advises you to slow down and take the time to review your financial decisions, rather than rushing ahead and making decisions you may regret later.

Relationships:

The Hermit Tarot card encourages you to take some time away and go within to reflect on your relationships. This card can suggest that it may be time to take a break from socializing and reconnect with yourself before engaging in relationships again. It can also be a time to reflect on how you interact with others and what you can do to be more authentic and honest within relationships. This card encourages you to take a step back and examine the ways in which you communicate, rather than allowing yourself to be swept away in the flow of relationships. It may be time to be honest with yourself and decipher what is truly important to you within your relationships.

## What does Knight Of Pentacles mean?

For Health:

The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card is an indication of sustained effort and determination when it comes to physical and mental health. This card tells you to be patient and consistent in your efforts to stay healthy. It may take time and hard work to get results, but the rewards will be worth the effort in the long run. Taking a holistic approach to wellness – both physical and mental – is championed by this card. This can include adopting healthier lifestyle habits such as eating nutritious foods, getting adequate sleep, being physically active, and tending to your emotional and spiritual needs.

For Finances:

The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card advises that you be thorough, reliable and consistent when it comes to your finances. This card tells you to take a long-term view when deciding how to spend your money. Building wealth is a matter of making smart decisions, managing your resources wisely and working hard. Now is the time to take stock of your financial situation, set goals and devise a plan to achieve them. Keep your focus on the end game and strive to accumulate assets instead of liabilities.

For Relationships:

The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card urges patience when it comes to your relationships. This card suggests a steadiness and consistency when it comes to your interactions with those around you. Relationships require effort and hard work. Don’t expect instant gratification or rapid change. Instead, work slowly, thoughtfully and patiently to cultivate durable, lasting relationships. Honesty and trustworthiness is of the utmost importance. Take time to thoughtfully consider the needs of others and show them your presence and willingness to listen and understand.
