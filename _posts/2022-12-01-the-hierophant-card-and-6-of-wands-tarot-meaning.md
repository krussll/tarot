A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 6 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 6 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 6 Of Wands mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 6 of Wands tarot card together suggest the best path forward is to embrace traditional standards and expectations. This involves taking the time to learn the norms and following them while tapping into your internal strength to move forward. It is through consistency and conformity that you can obtain recognition and success. The Hierophant tarot card reminds you to persist and stay focused on your goal and that if you push through the barriers of conformity you will reap the rewards for your hard work. The 6 of Wands tarot card brings news of success and victory, reminding you that sometimes the most difficult path can reap the greatest rewards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card is a reminder to adhere to the traditional values associated with health and fitness. This card encourages us to seek professional medical advice when needed, as well as follow a healthy lifestyle, such as exercise, healthy eating habits and sleep. It also serves as a warning to stick to the accepted norms and avoid any particularly risky health practices that could cause long-term damage or even death. We should follow the advice of our doctors and avoid any fads or trends that seem to be outside the scope of what is generally accepted for health and wellbeing.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of establishing our financial commitment to sound financial practices. This includes budgeting, avoiding unnecessary expenses and taking calculated risks that increase potential for financial stability and growth. It reminds us to stick to what has traditionally worked in terms of financial planning, such as creating a plan for your financial future, setting aside money for retirement and investing in high quality stocks and bonds. We should follow the conventional wisdom, avoid taking unnecessary risks and trust in the experience of those who have built their wealth by following similar conservative strategies.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card encourages us to be mindful of traditional values when it comes to relationships. This card is a reminder to adhere to the established norms when it comes to matters of the heart, such as trust, honesty and respect. It is a reminder of the importance of prioritizing relationships, like family and friends, that have been established over time and honoring the commitment to keeping them strong. We should strive to follow the conventional wisdom when it comes to relationships and make sure that we are treating our loved ones with the honor and respect they deserve.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of triumph and success. It symbolizes the inner strength and courage it takes to overcome adversity and reach one’s goals. This card is an encouragement to continue on the path of success despite any struggles that may arise.

For health, the 6 of Wands Tarot card encourages perseverance in times of illness or injury. Reminding you to remain focused and committed to your recovery, it higlights the need for alignment between body, mind and spirit. It suggests taking time to nourish yourself, focus on your goals and channel your energy in a positive direction.

For finances, the 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes recognition for hard work. It signifies that you are on the right track and that you will soon be rewarded for your efforts. As such, it gives you the confidence to stay the course and make sound financial decisions that will help bring about success in the long run.

For relationships, the 6 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the power of compromise. It reminds you that it is necessary to move beyond any frustrations and challenge yourself to reach a mutual understanding with those you care about. It encourages the need to be honest and communicating openly with loved ones in order to ensure long-lasting relationships.
