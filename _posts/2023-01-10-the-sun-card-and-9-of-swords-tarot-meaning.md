A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The Sun tarot card and the 9 Of Swords tarot card together could mean that there is a challenge ahead that needs to be overcome before you can experience true material happiness and success. There could be anxieties, nightmares, or depression that you need to face in order to gain the joyous outcome you desire, but with hard work and dedication you can overcome these obstacles and achieve the happiness you crave.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Sun and 9 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Sun tarot card and The 9 of Swords tarot card, when drawn together in your love life, point to the presence of both positive and negative circumstances. The Sun tarot card suggests that, while there are difficult struggles in front of you, there is also strong potential for a joyous outcome. This card reflects that, although things may look bleak right now, the future could still be filled with material happiness and success.

At the same time, the 9 of Swords tarot card suggests that these positive aspects are juxtaposed with persistent worries and anxieties. It’s important to recognize that any success that you may experience could be accompanied by nightmares, deep depression, and harsh grief. This card encourages you to take the necessary steps to manage these feelings, as allowing them to persist in the long-term can have negative consequences for your relationship.

Therefore, the combination of The Sun and The 9 of Swords tarot cards in your love life indicates that things may not be easy, but opportunities for joy, success and happiness are still within reach. It’s important to remember to stay grounded, and to take care of yourself emotionally if you want to be able to make the most of these potential future successes. By addressing the anxieties and worries that are present, one can better equip themselves to make the most of the potential joys and successes that are available in your relationship.

When The Sun tarot card and The 9 Of Swords tarot card are drawn together, it can be quite a complex message. On one hand, The Sun tarot card indicates material happiness and joyous outcomes when it appears in the context of finances and job. This card speaks of success in these areas, and is a strong sign that you will experience growth and abundance.

However, The 9 Of Swords tarot card hints at some underlying issues that should not be overlooked. This card is associated with nightmarish scenarios, anxiety, grief, and depression. It indicates that even though you may be expecting a joyous outcome in your finances and job, there may be some obstacles that could prevent you from achieving this positive result.

The combination of The Sun tarot card and The 9 Of Swords tarot card suggest that you should take steps to protect yourself against potential negative outcomes. Although optimistic approaches are important, you should also plan ahead and be wary of anything that could potentially damage your progress. Make sure that you have a good grasp on the situation, and that you are well-prepared should any of the fears or anxieties that The 9 Of Swords tarot card warns of come to fruition.

Attitude is everything in this case, and a positive one is essential. Work hard to manifest the positive results you desire, and believe that you will succeed despite the potential for challenge or difficulty. This combination of tarot cards may suggest that success is attainable, but it will require hard work and dedication. With the right attitude and some strategic planning, you can reach the goals you have set for yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card is about experiencing joy and happiness in all aspects of life, and encourages us to take the time to appreciate the good things in life. It signifies a need for us to look at the bigger picture and recognize the ways in which the good will come our way.

When it comes to health, the Sun card can be seen as a sign of good fortune. It means a time of increased energy, vitality, and enthusiasm. It signifies balance, wellness, and personal growth, and encourages us to focus on self-care and dedication to improving our health. As we strive to maintain a healthy lifestyle, we are likely to find ourselves surrounded by happier and healthier people, bringing joy and cheer to our lives.

Financially speaking, the Sun card suggests that success is coming our way. It indicates the opportunity to make smart choices when it comes to money, and encourages us to use our resources wisely. It may also suggest that luck or good news is on its way, leading to a much-needed financial boost.

In terms of relationships, the Sun card is an excellent indicator of joy and contentment. It signifies a time where relationships can flourish, with increased trust, understanding and appreciation for one another. We may also find ourselves surrounded by supportive friends and family, who will bring us tremendous joy. By focusing on the positive aspects of our relationships and showing genuine care, we will be able to create stronger and more lasting bonds.

Overall, in health, finances and relationships, the Sun card brings cheer and optimism. It encourages us to not only look for the sunshine amidst the clouds, but to actively seek out the sources of joy and happiness in our lives. The Sun card is a reminder that despite whatever challenges we may face, there is always a light at the end of the tunnel.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 9 of Swords tarot card is associated with feelings of physical and emotional exhaustion, sadness, and anxiety. It can be indicative of a sense of hopelessness and despair. It suggests that the person is overwhelmed by their current situation, and has a feeling of being trapped or defeated. In this state, the person may be at risk for physical illness or mental health issues such as depression, panic, and insomnia. The health risks associated with this card are mainly related to the person’s mental and emotional wellbeing, as these can manifest in physical symptoms.

Finances  
The 9 of Swords tarot card is not a positive card when it comes to finances. It suggests that the person is worrying about their financial future and feels helpless to do anything about it. This can lead to feelings of frustration and despair, as well as a tendency to take risks that may not be in their best interest. This card is a warning sign to take a step back and reassess one’s financial situation and prioritize needs.

Relationships  
The 9 of Swords tarot card suggests that the person may be feeling insecure and pessimistic in their relationships. There is a sense of stagnation and a fear that the relationship cannot move forward. To keep the relationship healthy, it is important to recognize these feelings and work through them. This card can suggest that it is time to reassess the relationship and identify areas of improvement. It may be necessary to have an open and honest conversation about the future of the relationship.
