A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Temperance card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and the 9 of Cups card together signify a powerful combination of quality and abundance. They suggest that you have the perfect balance of qualities in your life to make your dreams come true, with the patience and discipline of Temperance and the material abundance, good health, and fulfillment of wishes that the 9 of Cups brings. Both cards are symbolizing a period of positive growth and joy, with a foundation of stability and moderation, while still allowing your hopes and wishes to come true.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is an important card that is associated with balance, harmony and patience. When we think of Temperance, we think of someone who is able to calmly navigate even the most chaotic of scenarios. The Temperance card represents a cool and collected approach to life, suggesting that it’s important to keep our heads even when things get tough.

The Temperance card is all about finding balance in today’s increasingly frantic world. In regards to health, it can be a reminder to take time for yourself to rest and relax. Mental health is just as important as physical health, and the Temperance card can be seen as a sign to prioritize both. Additionally, it can act as a reminder to listen to your body and to understand when it’s time to push yourself and when it’s time to step back and take a break.

In regards to finances, the Temperance card encourages us to look at our options carefully and make smart, calculated decisions. It’s easy to get overwhelmed with the endless number of investment and loan options, but this card reminds us to stay calm and trust our instincts. Similarly, if you’re looking to make a major purchase, the Temperance card can be a reminder to research all the options and find the one that works best for your budget.

In terms of relationships, the Temperance card reminds us to keep our emotions in check. It’s important to take a step back and think things through before jumping into a situation headfirst. Additionally, the Temperance card encourages us to think about how our actions and words can affect those around us, and it can serve as a reminder to treat others with kindness and respect. Finally, it can remind us to be patient during times of conflict, as patience can be the key to resolving disagreements.

The Temperance card is an important reminder to help us stay focused and balanced in an increasingly hectic world. It reminds us to take a step back and look at the big picture, and to be patient with ourselves and those around us. When we embody the Temperance card, we can make decisions that are ultimately beneficial to our health, finances, and relationships.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The Nine of Cups tarot card is an incredibly powerful card that expresses true joy and fulfillment. It represents the culmination of one’s desires and wishes coming to fruition, and can signify both material abundance and good health. As far as health is concerned, the Nine of Cups symbolizes a period of increased vigor and energy, allowing the individual to take full advantage of their newfound sense of well-being. Financially, the Nine of Cups denotes a prosperous period ahead. It can be interpreted as a sign of a financial windfall or a promotion that will bring increased income. Lastly, the Nine of Cups also speaks to relationships. It can be seen as a signal that long held wishes in love and relationships are about to come true. It symbolizes a time of balance, harmony and contentment in close relationships. All in all, The Nine of Cups tarot card is an incredibly positive omen, expressing material abundance, good health, and fulfilled relationships.
