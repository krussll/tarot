A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

When The 9 Of Wands tarot card and The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card appear together in a reading, it’s a reminder for us to focus on taking responsibility for our physical and emotional health and balance. It’s also a call to set boundaries and to be aware of how our current worries might be affecting our security and well being. The combination of these two cards symbolizes a period where it is essential to look after yourself both mentally and financially in order to prevent further losses or feeling overwhelmed by the situation. This could include seeking help from professionals whenever necessary, staying connected to a support network, and staying mindful of our thoughts and feelings. While it can be difficult during this period, The 9 Of Wands and The 5 Of Pentacles remind us to be persistent, trust our intuition, and take a break when needed in order to maintain our health and balance.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The card is cautioning you to be mindful of financial insecurity and feelings of poverty. Money might be a source of stress that is preventing you from enjoying your relationship. It is important to be honest with your partner about your feelings and to remember that your bond is worth much more than any financial setback.

The two tarot cards are warning you of the consequences when the security of a relationship is taken for granted. The 9 of Wands is asking you to hold strong to your convictions in order to build a strong foundation with your partner, while the 5 of Pentacles is a reminder to stay mindful of financial insecurity and to not let it hinder your relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The 9 of Wands tarot card speaks of the importance of keeping and maintaining boundaries in all areas of life. In terms of health, it represents fortifying oneself against emotional and physical illnesses. It encourages us to take care of our physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being. To nurture the body with proper rest and nutrition, to set boundaries of mental and emotional energy, and to practice self-care, self-love and acceptance. In terms of finances, it encourages us to be mindful of our resources and to set healthy limits in our spending and saving. It also serves as a reminder to have patience and determination during times of financial hardship.

In relationships, The 9 of Wands tarot card serves as a warning to maintain healthy boundaries and to resist overcommitting. It suggests that we learn to trust ourselves and to be firm in our decisions. It encourages clarity and balance in order to create positive, supportive relationships. It reminds us to take our time, be patient and mindful when giving of ourselves, offering help or making change. Lastly, it serves a reminder that the resilience of our spirit is strong and we can navigate even the most challenging of times.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles tarot card is a warning that hard times are ahead. It indicates ill health, poverty, worry, isolation and financial loss. In terms of health, it suggests that a person is dealing with a physical or mental illness that is difficult to overcome. This can manifest in the form of an ongoing physical or mental disorder, or a serious and long-lasting illness. It can also indicate that the person is in need of rest and relaxation, or is facing a long and difficult recovery from an illness.

In terms of finances, the 5 of Pentacles tarot card suggests that a person is in financial trouble. This can come in many forms, from being in debt, to losing a job or facing financial uncertainty. It can also point to a lack of resources, either financially, physically or emotionally. This could include being underresourced to make ends meet or having to rely on others for help.

Finally, the 5 of Pentacles tarot card can also indicate difficulty in relationships. This could indicate that the person is feeling isolated and alone, or that they are having difficulty forming healthy relationships. It can also point to feeling a sense of guilt or shame, or being taken advantage of financially or emotionally.

In sum, the 5 of Pentacles tarot card is a warning of difficulties ahead in terms of both health and finances. It can also suggest issues in relationships, as the person may be feeling isolated, taken advantage of, or struggling to form healthy connections. While this card can certainly be a sign of negative events, it is important to remember that it is not a sign of permanent failure or despair. It simply is a reminder to take a step back, assess your situation, and take the necessary steps to get back on track.
