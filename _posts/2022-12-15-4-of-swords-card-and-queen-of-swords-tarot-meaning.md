A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together?

When the 4 of Swords and the Queen of Swords combine in a reading, it speaks of a period of much-needed personal renewal. This is a time to retreat and rest, to get away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and to give yourself space to rest and recuperate. This is a great opportunity for self-reflection and to gain some much needed perspective, a time for honest self-evaluation and introspection, where you can learn to be self-sufficient and reliant on yourself. It’s a time to draw on your own inner strength, to gain greater insight and understanding that will ultimately lead to personal growth.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

If a break is what you need, then honouring that will open up the potential for rest, renewal, and lasting love in the future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 4 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that we all need rest and solitude in order to remain healthy. Taking time to retreat from the stresses and anxieties of everyday life is an important part of maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Taking a break to rest and reflect can help us clear our minds and rejuvenate our bodies. This is a time for recovery and renewal in order to build up our strength and confidence and to move forward in our lives.

Finances: The 4 of Swords Tarot card is a sign to take a break from spending and to take time to reassess our finances. Taking time to reflect on our current financial situation and to create a plan for long-term financial stability can help us make smart decisions about our money. This is a time for taking a step back and making sure we are spending our resources wisely and that we are creating a strong foundation for our future.

Relationships: The 4 of Swords Tarot card also reminds us to take time to rest and reflect when it comes to our relationships. Taking a break from our busy schedules and taking a step back to reassess our relationships can help us identify positive patterns and unhealthy dynamics in our relationships. Taking this time to rest and reflect can help us build stronger relationships and move forward in our lives with a clearer sense of purpose and connection.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords tarot card is associated with being healthy mentally and physically. It represents selfcare, discipline and resilience. In terms of physical health, the Queen of Swords encourages us to find balance in our diet and exercise regime, to take the time to look after ourselves and prioritise our own wellbeing. It also reminds us to be honest with ourselves about our health and to be honest with our doctors and practitioners about our medical history and symptoms. Furthermore, it is a reminder to set realistic goals for our health, as well as to seek advice from professionals when needed.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages us to be financially savvy and responsible. It encourages us to take a disciplined approach to our finances, create a budget, be clear and transparent about our money, and recognise the value of money. It also encourages us to be honest and speak the truth if we are in debt, to seek help and advice before it gets out of hand. Furthermore, the Queen of Swords advises us to develop a sense of financial independence, to look after our own finances so that we are selfsufficient.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card symbolises honesty and respect in relationships. It emphasises the importance of being honest with ourselves, our partners and our friends. It encourages us to be direct and honest in our communication, to avoid manipulation and drama, and to trust our gut instincts when dealing with difficult people or making decisions. Furthermore, the Queen of Swords reminds us to determine and maintain clear boundaries in our relationships and to be mindful of our own needs and desires. The card encourages us to act with kindness and compassion towards those around us, and to be emotionally available and trustworthy in our relationships.
