A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Queen Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The Queen Of Pentacles and The 8 Of Swords cards together represent the idea of having the potential to move forward in life but also feeling trapped by your own circumstances. Although you have many resources available to you, you are held back by your own self-imposed limitations and fear, feeling like you are in some kind of prison. Despite this, the Queen of Pentacles still offers a reminder of all the possibilities and potential you have if you can break free of the restrictions you have placed on yourself. Fertility and domesticity are still possible, but it requires the courage to take a chance and seek out the abundance that is your true birthright.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Queen Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Queen of Pentacles tarot card and the 8 of Swords tarot card together signify a need to break free from inherent limitations and restrictive forces in order to nurture and amplify the energy of your love life. This card pair tells of a woman who is wise and resourceful, holding the power to bring together financial resources, creative energy and compassion, to responsibly and fairly nurture the growth of her relationships.

The Queen of Pentacles is a card of abundance and fertility and often indicates a strong connection with children, pets and nature. Her nurturing, practical nature makes her well suited to provide for the needs of her loved ones and to be an anchor in times of chaos. This card reminds us that in order to be fully present for and successful in our relationships, both of us must take responsibility for ourselves and each other.

The 8 of Swords, however, indicates a need to break free from the binds which we have placed on ourselves, or that society in general has put on us. These self-imposed restrictions are associated with guilt, shame, and the fear of failure. This card encourages us to face our fears and anxieties and remove any impediments that stand in the way of connecting with our loved ones.

When these tarot cards come together in a reading, they urge us to take responsibility and nurture our relationships as we expand our notion of what is possible within our love lives. In order to do this, we must learn to identify and overcome our fears and limiting beliefs, while also understanding that commitment and compassion are a necessary part of any successful relationship. Understanding these dual forces will help us to discover the balance and harmony that is essential for finding the love we deserve.

### What does Queen Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

The Queen of Pentacles and the 8 of Swords together suggest that you are at a crossroads in your finances and job. On the one hand, the Queen of Pentacles implies that you are highly capable, resourceful, and have the potential to be incredibly successful. This card is a reminder to tap into your own inner strength, to trust yourself to do what is necessary to make your finances and job commitments a success.

At the same time, the 8 of Swords suggests that you may be feeling some internal restrictions and limitations holding you back. You may feel that you are financially limited or feel confined by the choices you are making in your current career or job. You may feel like you are in a self-imposed prison, or like you cannot break free from the ties that bind you.

On the contrary, this card also encourages you to take a step back and assess the situation. Instead of feeling trapped, you should take a hard look at the choices you have made up until this point, and determine if those choices have actually been in your best interests. By understanding your own limitations, you can better understand what changes need to be made in order for you to move forward and succeed in your finances and career.

The combination of the Queen of Pentacles and the 8 of Swords speaks to taking stock of your situation and being honest and realistic with yourself. You have the potential to be highly successful, but you have to have the knowledge and confidence to make the right decisions. This combination also suggests that you should be wary of shortcuts and gimmicks and look for ways to further your success through honest and resourceful work.

The Queen of Pentacles tarot card combined with the 8 of Swords denotes a fascinating combination of energies that can be used in a positive manner when it comes to one’s health. On the one hand, the Queen of Pentacles is a card that speaks to abundance and resourcefulness. This indicates that one should take advantage of all of the resources they have available to them in order to maintain optimal health. Whether it be physical, mental, or emotional, the Queen of Pentacles encourages one to explore all of their options and find the right solutions for their own well-being.

At the same time, the 8 of Swords tarot card speaks to restrictions and self-imposed limitations. This could point to one’s inner processes, such as self-judgement or other ways that we can hold ourselves back from reaching our full potential. The key is to take ownership of these limitations and learn to use them for our own betterment. In other words, understanding where we have imposed limits on ourselves can be used to redirect our energy in a more productive and sustainable manner.

All in all, the energy of the Queen of Pentacles and 8 of Swords when combined, speaks to a balance between resourcefulness and self-restraint. This can be an especially powerful tool when it comes to one’s health and wellness. By taking advantage of the resources available, while also having a strong understanding of our own boundaries, we can create a meaningful, sustainable health plan that works for us.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles mean?

Health

The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with physical health and wellbeing. She encourages us to take care of ourselves and make sure we are getting enough nutrition, exercise and rest. The card also suggests that a balanced lifestyle is key to good health. It can mean that one is open to new healing modalities and is willing to learn more about nutrition and self-care. The Queen of Pentacles can symbolize a strong connection to the earth, and this can be an important reminder to connect to nature and stay grounded.

Finances

The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with prosperity and abundance. This card encourages us to be resourceful and take a practical approach to managing our finances. We need to use the resources that we have to our best advantage and plan for long-term financial security. The card also encourages us to stay on top of our expenses and not get sucked into the impulse of overspending. Additionally, this card reminds us to be generous to ourselves, so that we can better provide for our loved ones.

Relationships

The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with stability and security in relationships. This card encourages us to be dependable and supportive in our relationships. We should strive to build solid foundations for our relationships by being open and honest, learning to compromise and building trust. This card also promotes reciprocity, suggesting that it is important to give and take in relationships. The Queen of Pentacles encourages us to be nurturing with ourselves and with those close to us, creating a safety net of love and support.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

For health, the 8 of Swords tarot card indicates self-imposed restrictions or limitations that can prevent us from living a healthy lifestyle. We may be so focused on the idea of perfection that we never move forward or take actions to improve our well-being. This card can also indicate a fear of the unknown, or a reluctance to step outside of our comfort zone. When this card appears, it is telling us that we need to take the time to look at our health from a different perspective and make the necessary adjustments.

For finances, the 8 of Swords tarot card suggests a period of stagnation or a feeling of being stuck in a rut with our finances. We may be afraid of taking risks or trying something new, and therefore remain tied to the same financial strategies that have not brought us any real growth. This card encourages us to get out of our comfort zone and take the leap of faith needed to move our finances forward.

For relationships, the 8 of Swords tarot card can indicate that we are not giving ourselves the freedom to truly connect with others. We may feel held back by our own insecurities, or be afraid to open up and trust someone. This card encourages us to break through our self-imposed barriers and give relationships a chance. This may require taking risks and staying vulnerable, but it will ultimately be the key to developing a meaningful and fulfilling connection with someone.
