A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and King Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Tower card and King Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and King Of Pentacles mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The King Of Pentacles tarot card together symbolize a period of unwelcome and unexpected change. This change will bring some upheaval into your life, and there is a risk of ruin and disgrace should you not be careful. The King of Pentacles suggests that you have the wisdom and practicality to invest your time, energy, and resources into navigating this difficult time. With the help of your steady leadership, you can make it through this period of upheaval, and come out the other side more successful than before.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card can indicate unwanted changes for health, finances and relationships, and is associated with some of the more difficult times life can bring. In terms of health, this card often signals a period of physical challenge or illness, and is a warning to take care of your health and to be mindful of your body’s needs. It might also be a sign to seek medical advice and help, depending on the severity of the symptoms.

In terms of finances, this card often signals a period of financial ruin, upheaval or a lack of resources. It might be a warning to be conservative and to watch over your finances closely. It could also be a sign that an unexpected expense or event is coming, and that you should prepare accordingly.

Finally, relationships wise, this card is often a sign of rupture, upheaval or discord. It could be a warning to be extra careful and careful with whom you invest your time, energy and emotions. In some cases, this card might be a sign that it’s time to end a relationship, as it is no longer serving you. Whichever the case, it is best to take this card seriously, and to be mindful of the changes that it brings.

## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

When it comes to health, The King of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes an attitude of taking care of yourself, having the discipline to maintain a healthy lifestyle and taking proactive steps to protect your well-being. This King is one who emphasizes the importance of self-care, and does not take health lightly. He is wise in his decisions, and will only take risks that are calculated and carefully considered. He promotes a balanced diet and regular exercise, good sleep and stress-relieving activities in order to be physically and emotionally healthy.

For finances, The King of Pentacles Tarot card denotes a leader with sound financial sense. He will make decisions that are carefully weighed and well-informed. He understands the importance of financial security and has a long-term plan to reach his goals. He is wise in his investments, and will take risks that are considered and well thought out. He is able to properly manage money, and will make sure to invest for the long-term security of his finances.

In relationships, The King of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of a steady and dependable partner. He is supportive and loyal, and will always have his partner’s best interests in mind. He is also practical in his decision-making and will always be looking out for the long-term stability and security of the relationship. He is not one to be swayed by emotions, but will instead make decisions that are firmly rooted in logic. He is a strong communicator and will always strive to get his point across without hurtful words.
