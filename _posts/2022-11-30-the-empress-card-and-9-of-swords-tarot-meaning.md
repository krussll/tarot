However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Empress card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Empress card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Empress and 9 Of Swords mean together?

When The Empress tarot card and The 9 Of Swords tarot card are combined, it can signify a situation in which you have come to a point of facing difficult emotions such as anxiety and depression. Perhaps you have felt overwhelmed and even been caught in a cycle of nightmares and negative thinking. You are now presented with an opportunity to create something new out of those challenging emotions, to use them for the greater good. The Empress tarot card speaks of abundance, fertility and good parenting, which can help you embrace the difficulties and fears you have, and ultimately turn them into something that serves a higher purpose. Take comfort and be mindful of the wisdom this combination of cards is offering as you work towards a better future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

For health, The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to stay mindful of our mental health. It’s a card of considerable emotional distress and can signify being stuck in a cycle of obsessive thoughts, anxieties, and nightmares. These negative states of being can have a detrimental effect on our physical health as well as our mental health. We need to take time for self-care, to rest, and to give ourselves permission to feel all of our emotions without judgment. It is important to seek help from a professional if the feelings are overwhelming.

In regards to finances, The 9 of Swords Tarot card can signify financial hardships and the need to be careful when making decisions concerning money. It’s a reminder to stay mindful of expenses and to make decisions based in reality, not fear and stress. This card can also signify worry and fear when it comes to our finances, so if these feelings are arising it can be beneficial to take a step back and consider if our worries are justified or if they are just anxiety causing us to focus on the worst-case scenarios.

For relationships, The 9 of Swords Tarot card can signify feelings of deep insecurity, mistrust, and fear. This card warns us to be mindful of the way our emotions are affecting our relationships and to take time to evaluate if we are being too hard on ourselves and others. It reminds us to be honest with ourselves and with our relationships so that we can work through any difficulties we are having. It is important to remember that no relationship is perfect and that difficulty and disagreements are inevitable. However, we must take the time to open up, face our fears, and find the courage to actively work through our challenges in an open, honest, and compassionate way.
