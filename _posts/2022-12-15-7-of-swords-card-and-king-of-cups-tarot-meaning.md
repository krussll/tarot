## What does 7 Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 7 Of Swords tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card creates an interesting dynamic. The 7 Of Swords tarot card speaks to the dangers of theft and betrayal, while the King Of Cups tarot card speaks to a leader who is supportive and tolerant. Together, they suggest a situation in which someone is running away from their problems, perhaps due to a feeling of betrayal or dishonesty. However, the supportive leader of the King Of Cups tarot card may be a source of empathy and understanding, helping to create a safe and secure environment in which the person can heal and move forward.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords in Tarot can have a wide range of meanings depending on its position in a reading, but in general, this card is associated with the idea of dishonesty, theft, betrayal, and running away. In terms of health, the 7 of Swords indicates that something is not quite right, whether it’s a physical illness or an emotional one. It could suggest that you are hiding something from yourself or from others, or that someone is lying to you about their intentions. Financially, the 7 of Swords can suggest a lack of trust in a particular financial situation, as well as a feeling of insecurity around money. It can also warn of the potential of being taken advantage of by someone else.

In terms of relationships, the 7 of Swords warns of potential infidelity or betrayal. It can indicate that someone is not being honest with you and that their actions may not be in your best interests. It can also suggest that you may need to take distance from a certain person or situation. This card can also indicate that there may be a need to watch out for being taken advantage of, or that someone may be withholding important information.

Overall, the 7 of Swords in Tarot indicates that something is not quite right, and can caution against the potential for dishonesty, betrayal and running away. It can suggest the need for greater self-awareness, more honesty and openness, and the need to protect yourself from potential dishonest intentions.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health:

The King of Cups in health is a sign of balance, peace, and patience. It is a sign of taking care of oneself in order to maintain balance and thus, good health. He symbolizes a healthy sense of self-awareness and self-care that helps to maintain both physical and mental health. On the physical level, this card advises moderation and self-control, and encourages people to take an active, conscious role in taking care of their health. On the mental level, this card encourages us to practice self-reflection, meditation, and mindfulness to maintain peace of mind and to understand our emotions better.

Finances:

The King of Cups in finances is a sign of wisdom, stability, and responsibility. He advises us to assess our financial situation and make conscious decisions to manage it wisely. We should plan our finances, save where we can, and understand the power of investing for our future. The King of Cups also encourages us to be generous with our finances, to give to those in need, and to remember that money is an energy that we should use wisely.

Relationships:

The King of Cups in relationships is a sign of understanding, compassion, and kindness. He encourages us to be tolerant, patient, and understanding in our relationships. We should always strive to be truthful and open with each other to ensure the relationship is based on trust and respect. The King of Cups is also a sign of sensitivity to the needs of others and a reminder that we should strive to be gentle and empathetic towards our loved ones. He advises us to take the time to really listen to each other and to understand each other’s perspectives. Overall, the King of Cups encourages us to nurture and cherish our relationships.
