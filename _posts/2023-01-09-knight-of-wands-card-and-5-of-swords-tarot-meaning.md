A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Knight Of Wands card and 5 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Knight Of Wands card and 5 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Knight Of Wands and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The Knight of Wands tarot card and the 5 of Swords tarot card together can reflect an imbalance of power. The Knight of Wands can represent a person who is guided by their creativity and lust for life, while the 5 of Swords can represent someone who takes advantage of their power or uses it to bully or abuse others. It is a warning that when unchecked, the creative energy of the Knight of Wands can be used to manipulate and harm others. It is important to recognize when an imbalance of power is present and take steps to correct the situation.

This energy is the creative and adventurous life force that the Knight brings in – they are the personification of the energies that spark a flame and get relationships going.

However, the 5 of Swords cautions that not all relationships should be entered into lightly. This card is a warning that abuse, theft, bullying, and other forms of violence have no place in any relationships, whether it is love or friendship. It is important to remember that in relationships, both partners still have to respect and honour each other.

In summary, the Knight of Wands tarot card and the 5 of Swords tarot card both provide important messages for love life. They encourage us to take advantage of the passionate and creative energies the Knight of Wands brings and to remember the importance of respect and safety when dealing with the 5 of Swords. By staying aware of both these messages, we can ensure that our relationships stay healthy and balanced.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Knight Of Wands mean?

The Knight Of Wands Tarot card is usually associated with the domains of creativity and lusty energy. This card speaks of a willingness to take risks and to put some zip into life. The Knight of Wands is an adventurer, a daredevil, and a trailblazer.

When it comes to health, this card can represent a need for an energetic approach to lifestyle. Take heed to ensure you maintain an active and healthy lifestyle, with plenty of exercise and nutritious meals. Be conscious of the risks associated with over-exertion and over-confidence, as well as the need for rest and relaxation.

In terms of finances, The Knight of Wands indicates a go-getter attitude. This card signifies ambition and the ability to be brave, taking risks in pursuit of wealth and success. However, it is wise to temper any venture with a sound financial plan and a consideration of possible risks.

In relationships, this card symbolizes the need for passion, enthusiasm, and a sense of adventure. The Knight of Wands encourages partners to take the initiative in new activities, such as travel and creative outbursts. This card suggests that couples should strive to keep the relationship alive with an ever-evolving sense of spontaneity. Be mindful of the need to never overcommit and to always remain mindful of the needs of both partners.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

From a purely metaphorical perspective, The 5 Of Swords Tarot card is associated with aggression and defeat. When it comes to physical and mental health, this card can represent a variety of ailments associated with stress, caused by unhealthy relationships. Physically, the card can represent high blood pressure, chest pain, headaches, and fatigue, all of which can be indicative of persistent stress from an unhealthy relationship. Mentally, The 5 Of Swords Tarot card can represent depression and anxiety, both caused by an environment of aggression, violence and bullying.

Financially, The 5 Of Swords Tarot card could refer to theft or the feeling of being taken advantage of. If the card appears in a reading, it could be an indication that finances are being mishandled due to the lack of healthy boundaries or respect in a relationship, where one or both parties take too much and give too little. This could also indicate impulsivity, where one or both persons in the relationship are constantly making impulse purchases, or using finances in a way that harms the relationship and the person’s financial stability.

In terms of relationships, The 5 Of Swords Tarot card represents a lack of self-respect and boundaries within the relationship. The card could be a sign that the relationship is full of bullying, manipulation, and hate. The card could also show an abusive relationship, where one person is controlling and dominating over the other. There is a lack of mutual respect, understanding, and communication in this relationship, which usually results in an unhealthy dynamic.
