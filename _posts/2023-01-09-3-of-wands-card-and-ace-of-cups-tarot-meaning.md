A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and Ace Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and Ace Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and Ace Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 3 of Wands and The Ace of Cups tarot cards suggests an adventure of expansion, team work, and new opportunity. Travel is likely to be involved as you explore the possibilities of a new relationship or new baby, a proposal, and marriage. This journey is likely to bring growth and satisfaction as you learn to rely on each other and celebrate the rewards of working together.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The Ace of Cups Tarot card is a card of abundance, joy, and emotional fulfillment. The card is associated with joyous emotions, like love, happiness, and contentment.

Health: The Ace of Cups Tarot card symbolizes contentment and emotional fulfillment, which can contribute to positive physical health. A balanced emotional state often helps to maintain good physical health. Those with the Ace of Cups in their Tarot reading may find that their overall wellbeing and physical health is improved with regular emotional check-ins, and that taking the time to focus on their emotional needs can lead to a healthier physical state.

Finances: The Ace of Cups Tarot card can represent financial prosperity, abundance, and abundance of opportunities. People with this card appearing in their reading may find that they are able to attract financial success because they are able to take advantage of the opportunities that come their way. Additionally, they may find that financial obstacles can be easily overcome with creative problem-solving and good planning.

Relationships: The Ace of Cups Tarot card indicates that a relationship is moving towards a more meaningful connection. Whether it’s a romantic, platonic, or work relationship, the Ace of Cups Tarot card indicates an exchange of loving energy that can help to deepen the connection between people. It’s a card of union and closeness, so those with this card in their reading may find themselves feeling emotionally fulfilled and content with their relationships. With this card, there may also be a proposal or marriage on the horizon. The Ace of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of new beginnings and can signify the start of a new relationship or the deepening of an existing one.
