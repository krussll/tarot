However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 8 Of Wands card and Knight Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign that a health condition could be changing quickly and news could arrive soon. This card could represent rapid healing or a sudden diagnosis. It could also indicate that several courses of action need to be undertaken in a short period of time to resolve a health issue.

Finances: The 8 of Wands Tarot card suggests a sudden influx of money, an unexpected windfall or profitable investment. It could also indicate quick changes in financial circumstances, such as unexpected expenses or sudden job offers. Regardless, this card is a positive sign for finances and could mean that wealth is coming your way soon.

Relationships: The 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of fast and forward progress in a relationship. It could point to a new relationship moving quickly, or it could represent news coming in from a long-term partner or friend. This card could also be a sign that it is time to make a move and take action in a relationship. It could also mean that a relationship is moving too quickly and needs to be slowed down.

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

The Knight Of Cups Tarot card is a powerful sign of hope, love and wellbeing. In terms of health, it can signify a person taking better care of their body and finding a newfound motivation to actively improve their health and fitness. It could also be a sign of someone feeling inspired to make healthier habits, such as exercise, diet, or even learning healthier coping strategies for stress.

In terms of finances, the Knight of Cups could mean that a financial windfall is coming your way. This could be in the form of a new job or a raise in salary, or it could even mean that you are getting good advice from someone who is better versed in financial planning. It could also mean that your luck is about to turn for the better.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Knight of Cups is a great card to see. It can indicate the start of a passionate, magical love affair or a deepening relationship with a partner. It could also represent new friendships being formed or existing ones deepening. It is a reminder to be open to new things, take risks, and appreciate the beauty of love in all of its forms.
