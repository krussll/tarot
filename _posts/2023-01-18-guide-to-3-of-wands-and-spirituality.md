Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The 3 Of Wands card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 3 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 3 Of Wands.

## 3 Of Wands And Sprituality – An Overview

The 3 Of Wands tarot card symbolizes teamwork and cooperation in the pursuit of health.

This card encourages us to form healthy relationships with others to work together in order to achieve our health goals.

It also encourages us to expand our knowledge of health, to travel and explore new ways of maintaining or improving health.

With the help of others, we can explore the world of health, from nutrition to exercise and beyond, and find ways to improve our own wellbeing.

This card reminds us that health is not a solo journey, and with cooperation and collaboration, we can make great strides towards better health.

## Upright Meanings For 3 Of Wands And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

The 3 of Wands tarot card is an encouraging sign for spiritual growth and exploration.

It is a reminder that spiritual journeys require the cooperation of others, for a team effort is often necessary to achieve success.

It can also be seen as a sign of progress and expansion, symbolizing the potential for growth.

The card can also indicate a time of travel, which can be physical or mental, offering a chance to explore new places and cultures, or perhaps to meet inspiring spiritual teachers.

Whatever the case, the 3 of Wands signifies that when it comes to spiritual growth and exploration, there is much to be gained from teamwork and travel.

### What It Means For The Future

The 3 Of Wands tarot card is a sign that my future spirituality will involve working with others, exploring new opportunities, and engaging in commerce.

It suggests that I should be open to expanding my spiritual journey by travelling to new places, meeting new people, and discovering new paths.

It may mean that I need to work with like-minded people in order to grow and learn spiritually.

By working together and exchanging ideas, I can open up to new perspectives and ways of looking at the world.

It is a reminder that my spiritual journey can be both collaborative and expansive, allowing me to explore and grow in ways I never thought possible.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 3 of Wands is a card that speaks to the spirit of teamwork and collaboration when it comes to those close to me.

It is a reminder that my spiritual growth and progress is more likely to happen when I work with others in my life, whether through commerce, travel, or simply sharing ideas and perspectives.

My relationships with those around me can become stronger and more meaningful when I choose to expand my horizons and work together to achieve our collective goals.

The 3 of Wands is a sign of the strength and power that comes from working together and encourages me to use the collective knowledge and energy of those close to me to reach my highest potential.

## Reversed Meanings For 3 Of Wands And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

When the Three of Wands is reversed, it can be a sign of stagnation and lack of forward progress.

It can mean that you are stuck in a rut and not moving forward with your plans or your spiritual growth.

You may be dealing with feelings of insecurity or a lack of self-confidence.

You may be feeling overwhelmed and frustrated with the lack of progress that you have made, and it can be hard to stay motivated.

This can be a sign that you need to take a step back and reassess your spiritual practices and goals.

It can also mean that you need to take a closer look at yourself and your relationships with others.

Are you feeling connected to your spiritual practice, or are you feeling disconnected and distant? Have you been putting in the effort to nurture and sustain those important relationships? Taking some time to reflect and explore your spiritual needs can help you find clarity and direction.

The reversed Three of Wands can also be a sign that you are too focused on the future and not living in the moment.

You may be in a rush to reach your goals, but it is important to remember to take things one step at a time and enjoy the journey.

Taking a break from your spiritual practice and indulging in some self-care can help you reconnect with yourself and bring balance back in your life.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Three of Wands is reversed, it can indicate that you are feeling stuck in your spiritual journey.

It can mean that you feel as though you have hit a wall and that you can’t find a way to move forward.

This can be a frustrating and disheartening feeling, but it is important to remember that this is a natural part of growth.

The reversed Three of Wands can also suggest that you may be feeling disconnected from your spirituality, and that you may be struggling to find a sense of meaning and purpose in your journey.

It is important to remember that your journey should never be linear.

You will always have good days and bad days, and feeling stuck and frustrated is part of the process.

The reversed Three of Wands can be a reminder that although you may currently be feeling disconnected or stagnant, you can push through and find a way to move forward.

It can be helpful to take a step back and to look at the bigger picture.

This can help you to gain clarity and to find a way to stay connected to your spirituality.

Taking some time out to explore different spiritual practices and to reconnect with your inner self can be extremely beneficial.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Three of Wands appears in a Tarot reading, it can suggest that those close to you may be struggling to find balance in their spiritual journey.

They may be feeling isolated and unsure of their direction or purpose in life.

They may be feeling overwhelmed by the journey they are on and unsure of how to move forward.

They may also be feeling disconnected from the spiritual guidance they once received, as if they are lost in the wilderness.

It can signify a lack of motivation and direction, and a lack of connection to your spiritual self.

It is important to remember that this card does not necessarily mean that the people close to you have completely lost their spiritual connection, but rather that they are having difficulty in finding it again.

Therefore, it is important to be supportive of those close to you and to provide them with the spiritual guidance and resources they need in order to reconnect with their spiritual selves.

A Tarot reading can provide insight into the current spiritual state of those close to you and can help you to understand their struggles and help them to find the spiritual guidance and resources they need to move forward.

Additionally, encouraging them to take part in spiritual activities such as meditation, yoga, or prayer can help them to reconnect with their spiritual selves.
