A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The 4 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 10 Of Swords tarot card together suggest a situation in which your desire for security has led you to a very painful and abrupt ending. Greed can be a dangerous thing, and in this case, it may have caused you to make a wrong decision that has caused you to crash and burn. This is a warning to be aware of your own motivations and to make sure you don’t make the same mistake again. Now is the time to start rebuilding and to make sure that any decisions you make in the future are in your best interest.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The 4 of Pentacles tarot card could suggest an unwillingness to take calculated risks, or a fear-based approach to life which prevents real progress and development. In terms of relationships, it is important to trust and rely on one another, and to allow each other the space and support to pursue their own goals. Similarly, with regards to health, it is important to find a balance between safety and calculated risks, and to make sure to take care of one’s physical and emotional well-being.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 10 of Swords tarot card symbolizes devastating physical and mental exhaustion. It can be a sign of physical collapse, suggesting a sudden onset of serious illness or a total breakdown from overwork, stress or other demanding lifestyle factors. The card can also denote a major psychological trauma, such as depression or all-consuming anxiety. It can indicate the need for rest and an overhaul of one’s lifestyle habits and approach to health, to avoid further suffering and long-term damage.

Finances

The 10 of Swords tarot card can be a sign of financial ruin, suggesting a complete collapse of wealth or resources. It can indicate the loss of a job or a major financial setback, such as being declared bankrupt. The card is a warning to be more mindful of money matters and to take measures to ensure financial security, such as creating a budget and establishing an emergency fund. There is also an underlying warning to stay away from risky investments and other dangerous financial strategies.

Relationships

The 10 of Swords tarot card can signify a breakdown in relationships, denoting heartache and trauma as a result of an acrimonious end. It could also point to an unhealthy codependency, where both partners are struggling financially or emotionally and each is using the other to fulfill their own needs. The card is a reminder to take time to reflect on relationships and determine if any needs to be reworked or dissolved in order to restore a healthy balance. It also suggests the need for honest and open communication with loved ones, to avoid further pain and suffering.
