A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card together may signify a period in life of great expansion of teamwork and commerce, but also a time of grief, regret and feeling lost. It suggests that although you may be participating in team efforts to gain great successes, there may be a sense of loneliness and feeling of abandonment. It may be a difficult period where you need to remain devoted to your goals, even when feeling low and abandoned. Remain focused on your vision and the success that you can create with the help of others.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card together suggest a difficult transition in your love life. You may be feeling overwhelmed by the changes that have recently taken place or that are looming on the horizon. You may have experienced a loss recently in your relationship and the feelings of grief and regret that come with it. This transition might seem daunting and it could have been unexpected, leaving you feeling abandoned and unloved at this time.

However, the combination of The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card also implies that with team work, you and your partner can navigate this transition together. The card speaks to the importance of communication, cooperation and collaboration as you both work to rebuild what you had before the loss and grief.

Similarly, the card combination encourages exploration and travel. You might want to take a short trip together with your partner to uncover the roots of your relationship which may have been lost amongst the grief. This may be a great opportunity for you to rekindle your bond, to explore and expand on what initially attracted you to each other in the first place.

By combining the energies of The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card, you can emerge from this transition feeling connected, loved and safe with your partner once again. While the transitioning may seem daunting, you can both turn to each other for comfort, support and guidance while building something beautiful and lasting out of the ashes of this experience.

### What does 3 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card are often interpreted to mean that your finances and job are in a state of flux. You may be facing some unwelcome changes and losses that are causing you to feel regret, grief, and a sense of being abandoned or unloved. However, it’s important to note that despite the losses and the grief, this card combination is really about moving forward. The 3 Of Wands is about team work, commerce, and expansion: if you’re willing to work with others and make the necessary adjustments, you can still achieve great new heights and opportunities.

The 5 Of Cups suggests that there is still a sense of loss and a possible feeling that you have “lost” a lot of what you held dear. It can sometimes feel like you have been abandoned or that you are not wanted in the new team dynamics. However, this card combination is not meant to convey a sense of permanence. Instead, it encourages you to accept the losses and the changes and use them as opportunities to further your financial and job prospects.

The key to understanding this tarot combination is to accept the losses and keep moving forward. In the shape of a team, working together to bring out the best in each other and the situation, you can use the losses to the benefit of everyone involved. Look for new opportunities to create a better and brighter financial and job future. With the right attitude, even a sense of abandonment can lead to great growth and success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 3 of Wands tarot card is associated with health and wellbeing because it suggests that collaboration, teamwork and expanding one’s knowledge of health matters are all important in order to achieve physical, mental and spiritual health. This card encourages individuals to work together in order to achieve an improved sense of wellbeing and to discover new, healthier habits and routines. It suggests that in order to achieve optimal health, one should be willing to take on new challenges and travel far and wide for the purpose of learning and experiencing new and different cultures or ways of life. This card encourages individuals to be open to new ideas and explore different paths in order to improve their overall health.

Finances

The 3 of Wands tarot card is associated with finances because it encourages individuals to explore different opportunities, be open to working with others and have a willingness to expand and grow in order to benefit financially. This card suggests that trusting and working with others can result in making wise investments and reaching financial goals more quickly. It also encourages individuals to take risks in order to open up new possibilities and explore different paths. The 3 of Wands tarot card is a reminder that true financial success can only be achieved by taking calculated risks and expanding one’s knowledge of financial matters.

Relationships

The 3 of Wands tarot card is associated with relationships because it encourages individuals to explore new paths and take risks in order to create and maintain fulfilling relationships with others. This card suggests that by being open to new ideas and experiences, one can learn and grow together with the people in their life. It encourages those in relationships to be willing to work together, think outside the box and explore different solutions in order to better understand each other and meet each other’s needs. The 3 of Wands tarot card is a reminder that in order to have meaningful and lasting relationships, one must be willing to take risks, explore and grow together.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health: Healthwise, The 5 of Cups Tarot card can signify health struggles that are linked to your emotional and mental state. You may be feeling overwhelmed by stress or sadness, which can lead to physical ailments. You may be dealing with physical pain that goes beyond just being tired, or you may be struggling with a chronic condition or disease. You may be struggling to take care of yourself or find ways to cope with your emotional pain, leading to health complications.

Finances: Financially, The 5 of Cups Tarot card can signify financial loss or setback. This can be a result of mismanagement of finances or poor decisions. Additionally, if your emotional state is having an impact on your ability to succeed in your job, you may find yourself struggling financially. The 5 of Cups can also signify an inability to move forward with your financial goals, perhaps due to fear of failure or feeling overwhelmed.

Relationships: In relationships, The 5 of Cups Tarot card can mean a feeling of abandonment or rejection. You may be feeling like your partner doesn’t care about you or that you’re not appreciated. Alternatively, it can also mean that you may be grieving the loss of a relationship, or feeling disconnected from those you care about. This can leave you feeling lonely and isolated, unable to find joy in these relationships.
