A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Spades card and 3 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 3 Of Spades card and 3 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, having both The 3 of Spades and The 3 of Hearts cards appear would significantly symbolize a tumultuous emotional journey ahead.

The 3 of Spades, representative of heartbreak and faith paired with the chilly season of Winter and the healing element of Water, suggests a painful ending or perhaps disappointment.

Meanwhile, The 3 of Hearts speaks of infidelity but also carries the promise of faith, paired with the vibrant season of Spring and the fiery element of Fire, hinting that from the ashes of destruction, rebirth can follow.

This combination may indicate a potential betrayal that’s going to deeply hurt, but with faith, emerge stronger and wiser from it.

The cycle of Winter to Spring also separates these two cards, suggesting that while the process might bear struggle, growth and renewal emerge just as the seasons change.

Together, these cards could be signalling a transformative experience through crisis.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, if the 3 of Spades card and the 3 of Hearts card come up together, it suggests a primarily tumultuous relationship which may often stem from high emotions, mirroring the nature of its associated seasons: the cold hardship of winter and the passion of spring.

The 3 of Spades indicates potential heartbreak, which could represent a painful time in the relationship, potentially ignited by third parties, emotional struggles, or miscommunications.

However, it’s important to note that with these changes comes growth, further reflected in the season of winter’s association with dormant potential waiting to bloom in warmer days.

The 3 of Hearts warns of potential infidelity, urging one to be cautious with trust.

The card, however, is also associated with the element of fire, symbolic of warmth, purification, and transformation.

It echoes the element of faith that both these cards carry, suggesting that faith could play a crucial role in overcoming the trials and tribulations in your relationship.

The season of spring linking to this card implies a time of renewal and growth, a possible sign that emerging from the challenging times will invigorate your relationship’s growth and development.

Therefore, this combination in a cartomancy reading insinuates a phase associated with struggle and heartache, but with the promise of growth, transformation, and potential revival.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

When seen together, the 3 of Spades and the 3 of Hearts in a finance and job-related cartomancy reading represent a period of hardship followed by a test of faith.

The 3 of Spades portends heartbreak, perhaps in the form of a loss or setback in your financial situation.

It may reflect an unfortunate occurrence such as an investment going bad, a costly expenditure that was unplanned, or maybe a job loss.

The Water element of this card means emotions could be high, suggesting it might be a significant setback.

This is reinforced by its association with Winter, a season symbolic of hardships and trials.

On the contrary, the 3 of Hearts, although it indicates infidelity and faith, in this context could mean you might face disloyalty in your professional relationships or deceit that may lead to financial loss.

Its association with the season of Spring, however, hints at new beginnings.

The fire element hints at a transformative phase, suggesting this could be a phase of purging and rebuilding after a setback.

According to your faith, this could be seen as trial by fire – an ordeal that tests and subsequently strengthens your resolve.

Interestingly, while the two cards when seen separately may predict misfortune, their combined symbolism can be interpreted as the need for resilience and faith during difficult times.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, seeing the 3 Of Spades and the 3 Of Hearts together can symbolize emotional strain related to your health.

The 3 Of Spades signifies struggle and heartache, suggesting potential emotional distress or anxiety that might be impacting your physical wellbeing.

Given the card’s association with Winter and the element Water, this may be related to seasonal affective disorder or feelings of lethargy during the colder months.

The card asks for patience and encourages faith to navigate through these testing periods.

Meanwhile, the 3 Of Hearts implies unfaithfulness, symbolizing a lack of commitment or discipline towards your health habits.

Associated with Spring and the element Fire, it may indicate periods of fiery enthusiasm followed by bouts of burnout, underscoring the need for sustained discipline, balance, and moderation.

The co-occurrence of these two cards in a reading suggests that your emotional and physical health are intertwined.

They serve as a reminder to manage stress, practice self-care, and maintain consistency in your health routines.

Faith here could mean trust in oneself and the process of healing or developing healthier habits.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
