However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does Temperance and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

When The Temperance tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card come together, they offer a reminder that life is a journey and that we must nurture our own balance and harmony throughout. With patience and trust, hard work and perseverance, we can reach our goals and accomplishments in due time. They bring a message to be mindful of our process and to maintain self-control when encountering obstacles along the way, as success will not come right away and it may take some time. Let us remember that these cards are here to provide us with the guidance to find balance and recognize that patience is truly a virtue.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Through temperance, it emphasizes the importance of finding a balance between work, rest and relaxation. Reaching our desired level of physical wellbeing requires us to pay attention to what we eat, how we exercise and when we rest. We must resist overindulging in unhealthy foods or lacking in physical activity, whilst still taking the time to relax in order to prevent burnout. It is also important to recognize when we are mentally unbalanced and requiring support in order to reach a state of equilibrium. The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to make necessary changes in healthy habits in order to maintain our physical and emotional wellbeing.

Finances: The Temperance Tarot card focuses on the concept of financial stability and the responsible use of finances. It is a reminder to resist impulsive purchases, and instead to take a long-term view of our financial goals. We should strive to create a budget which provides us with enough savings to afford us the life we want, while also allowing us to enjoy spending on the things we need. It also addresses the need to be patient with our finances, and to refrain from overspending in order to achieve our desired financial outcome. The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to be mindful of our spending decisions and to plan carefully in order to develop a sense of financial security.

Relationships: The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to seek balance and harmony in our relationships. It is an invitation to maintain a sense of calm, despite the challenges we may encounter. We should practice patience and understanding and refrain from acting impulsively in order to create a strong, healthy connection with our partners. The Temperance Tarot card emphasizes the importance of listening to our own needs and communicating openly with our partners. Through temperance we can develop a fulfilling relationship which allows us both to express who we are without sacrificing our sense of self.

It reminds us that while we may pour our heart and soul into a relationship, the results of our efforts may not be immediately visible. This card suggests that we should not give up when the going gets tough and instead, keep putting in the effort and dedication. The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card also encourages us to be more understanding and forgiving of our partners. We should understand that relationships require time, effort, and patience to develop into a strong and lasting bond.
