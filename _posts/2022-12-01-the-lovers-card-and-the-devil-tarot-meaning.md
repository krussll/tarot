A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and The Devil card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and The Devil have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and The Devil mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The Devil tarot card together represent a difficult but necessary choice – the strength of two in partnership versus a destructive obsession. It suggests that while commitment and love can be a driving force in life, it can also be challenged by the temptations of materialism and addiction. It’s important in this situation to maintain balance, understanding the power of two in combination but also to be aware of the potential downfall of giving into greed and envy.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Lovers and The Devil mean together for your love life?

The Lovers tarot card and The Devil tarot card can be quite a powerful combination when it comes to love. On the one hand, the Lovers card symbolizes a deep, connected partnership between two people, suggesting a strong level of commitment and a willingness to make compromises. On the other hand, The Devil symbolizes materialism, obsession, and sexual lust which can lead to intense desires for instant gratification.

It’s important to be aware of the potential danger of combining these two cards in the context of a love life. The positive aspects of The Lovers card – connection, commitment, and partnership are balanced against the potential for greed and addiction that can come from The Devil card. Both cards represent forces that can push and pull in a relationship, as well as temptations that can be hard to resist.

Those in a relationship should take this as a warning to guard against obsession and materialism, and make sure that strong values of mutual respect and communication are the backbone of their partnership. It’s important to stay connected and honest in order to ensure that both parties are satisfied and content in the relationship.

However, used with caution and in the right context, this combination of The Lovers and The Devil can represent a fulfilling and passionate relationship. The passion spurred by The Devil can be a positive force, adding some exciting height to the connection between two potential lovers. If both parties in the relationship can find a healthy balance between passion and commitment, then a situation that The Lovers and The Devil represent can be a beautiful, fulfilling union.

### What does The Lovers and The Devil mean together for your finances?

When Lovers and Devil cards are drawn together in a reading of finances and job, it suggests a potential conflict between the need for partnership, deeply felt love, and meaningful connection on the one hand and materialistic, selfish, and addictive desires on the other.

When this combination appears, it may be warning of a situation in which one partner has become too attached to material wealth, at the expense of the spiritual connection between them. This card combination could also suggest a situation in which one partner puts too much emphasis on personal gratification rather than considering the needs of the other.

The Lovers card combined with the Devil card could also suggest a situation in which money plays a critical role in one person’s pursuit of material pleasures. This could include a situation in which one partner is financially dependent on the other and uses money to get whatever they want, with little thought to the consequences.

In any case, it is important to recognize that when these two cards appear together, a healthy balance needs to be struck between material security and spiritual connection. It is important for both partners to be honest about their expectations and needs with each other, and to recognize that long-term financial security cannot come at the expense of authentic connection. It may also be necessary to make some difficult decisions about where money should be spent, and where it should be saved, in order to ensure that both partners’ needs are met.

On the one hand, The Lovers teaches us the importance of connection and partnership, of honoring and celebrating our connection with the people we love. At the same time, these two cards can also encourage us to find ways to enrich our relationships without overindulging in material gains. We can focus on finding healthy ways to nurture our relationships, such as sharing experiences, engaging in meaningful conversations, and cultivating meaningful connections that bring joy and growth.

By embracing the union of The Lovers and The Devil, we can find the right balance of physical and emotional nourishment that enriches both our relationships and our overall well-being. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and The Devil mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health:

The Lovers Tarot card reflects the importance of having a trusted partner to help us maintain a healthy lifestyle. When we make positive decisions about our health, we are likely to have the support of our partner. This could include things like joining a gym together, helping each other with healthy meal prep, or simply providing a comforting presence when one of us needs to take a break and relax. Working together brings balance and stability to our health, which can lead to greater overall wellbeing.

Finances:

The Lovers Tarot card also reflects the power of two when it comes to managing finances. When we have a partner to help us, it can be easier to make wise decisions and achieve financial success. With a partner, we can discuss our budget and goals, create a plan of action, and hold one another accountable. Working together, we can resist the urge to impulsively overspend and make strides towards financial security.

Relationships:

The Lovers Tarot card speaks to the power of true love and the strength of a committed partnership. When two people come together, they can create something special that neither of them could do on their own. Through mutual understanding, respect, and support, two people can create a deep connection that can last for a lifetime. This connection can bring joy and fulfillment, a feeling of unconditional acceptance, and a source of comfort and strength.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card is a powerful card that can signal a range of dangerous behaviors and emotions. It is frequently associated with addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession and sexual lust. Understanding the implications of this card on health, finances and relationships can help provide insight and direction surrounding a person’s behavior, outlook and attitude.

The Devil card can be a warning of health problems created by excessive behavior, such as drinking, drug use, overindulging in food, or even risking physical harm in the pursuit of pleasure. In terms of finances, the card is a sign of being overindulgent and wasting money on things that are ultimately unimportant and of no real value. Envy and materialism can draw attention away from what really matters and lead to financial burden, due to overspending and compulsive behavior.

When it comes to relationships, the Devil card can signify power struggles, manipulation and a lack of true intimacy. Greed and lust can drive people to seek external forms of gratification that ultimately leave them feeling unsatisfied. The card warns against trying to control or possess someone, as this will ultimately harm both parties. Working towards honest communication and self-reflection can help to bring balance and understanding to a relationship.

The Devil Tarot card is a strong representation of the dangers of unchecked, greedy behavior and the importance of exerting some self-control. Understanding the implications of this card on health, finances and relationships is key to avoiding its negative effects. Tapping into core values, setting personal boundaries and taking responsibility for one’s choices can help avoid the pitfalls of this card and ultimately allow people to live happier, healthier lives.
