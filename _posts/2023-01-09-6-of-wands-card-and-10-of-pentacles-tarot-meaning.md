A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 6 of Wands and 10 of Pentacles tarot cards represent a wonderful blend of past successes and future accomplishments. The 6 of Wands brings a sense of joy and victory, with recognition and good news coming your way. This is a card of success in the present, and a representation of the rewards you are currently receiving. The 10 of Pentacles shows that this success could manifest itself in the form of a legacy, retirement, or inheritance. You may find yourself feeling like you have truly fulfilled your potential, and that you can see the rewards of all your hard work. This is a great time to look back on all the successes you have had, and look forward to reaping the long-term benefits of your labours.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is a very positive sign when it comes to health. It is associated with success and victory, which suggest that you have the willpower to make positive changes in your life and to reach your health goals. This is a sign that you have the strength and determination to make good choices, such as choosing healthy foods, exercising regularly, and getting enough sleep. With this card in your reading, you can feel confident that you will succeed in your health endeavors.

Finances

The 6 of Wands Tarot card could mean that you are going to experience great success in your finances. This could mean winning a large sum of money, a career promotion, or even a business success. Whatever it is, this is a sign of good news and a reason to celebrate. This card is a reminder that you can reach the financial heights you’ve been striving for and that you will be recognized and rewarded for your hard work.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Wands Tarot card is a sign that good news is on its way. This could mean a new and exciting relationship, a positive resolution to an existing issue, or simply a greater understanding between two people. This card suggests that no matter what challenges you and your relationships are facing, you will be able to come out of it stronger than before. This could also mean that you are rewarded for your loyalty and commitment to those you love.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is a symbol of leaving a legacy, stability, prosperity, and retirement. In regards to health, this card suggests having a longevity in life, being well-taken care of, and having access to the best health care available. The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card also implies a comfortable and secure financial situation, perhaps even an inheritance. A sense of emotional and material security can come with such stability. This card can symbolize that you’ve worked hard to build something that will last into the future, and it will continue to bring rewards and stability.

In terms of relationships, this card symbolizes the sharing of resources and support. This could be between family members, business partners, or close friends. Traditionally, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is representative of family and community structures, implying that you are part of something larger and supported. This card can also imply a strong emotional connection with your partner, and a secure and stable commitment.

Overall, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a retirement from active work, a sense of security, a sense of stability, and a sense of leaving something behind for future generations. It plays a role in providing for physical health, financial security, and emotional security, as well. This card can represent the idea that work hard now and the rewards and stability will last for years to come.
