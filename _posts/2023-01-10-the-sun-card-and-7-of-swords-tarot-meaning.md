A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The Sun and 7 of Swords together suggests that you can use your creativity and imagination to work around obstacles and gain success. There may be a sense of betrayal or dishonesty with some people, but you can use this to your benefit by outsmarting them and turning the negative into a positive. Hard work and some creative thinking can bring about a joyous outcome and material happiness, despite potential theft and running away. This card combination represents seizing opportunities that come your way and being confident in your own abilities to reach success.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

In terms of your love life, it’s a sign that you need to think about the bigger picture in order to gain a better understanding of what is really going on. Before you jump to conclusions and make any rash decisions, do your best to analyze the situation and come up with a plan.

Take your time to consider all your options and research into the facts of the situation. You may even need to take a step back from your relationship in order to truly understand what is going on. Remember to remain patient and remind yourself that it’s important to be honest with yourself and your partner. You may not be able to achieve your ideal outcome right away, but honesty and understanding will help to make sure that you ultimately get the joyous outcome that The Sun tarot card promises.

It is possible to come out of this situation with your relationship intact, as long as you don’t jump to conclusions or act impulsively. The Sun tarot card and The 7 Of Swords tarot card together remind us to look past the difficulties and remain hopeful and optimistic. With patience, understanding and a bit of hard work, you can achieve the material happiness and joy that The Sun tarot card speaks of.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

For Health:  
The Sun Tarot card indicates that you are currently in a state of good health or on the path to improved health. You may be feeling energized and your wellbeing is likely to be improving. This could be a result of lifestyle changes such as exercise, diet and even spiritual practices. You are likely to feel more positive, content and relaxed during this period of well-being.

For Finances:  
The Sun Tarot card suggests that your finances are likely to be in good order. You may be feeling fortunate and optimistic about the future, as this card implies success and abundance in the world of finances. You could be reaping the rewards of hard work, savings and investments. It could also represent a period of financial luck, or perhaps a windfall that has brought unexpected financial rewards.

For Relationships:  
The Sun Tarot card implies that any relationships you have will be glowing with happiness and joy. You may be feeling content in your relationships and experiencing a period of peace and harmony. It suggests that current relationships are strong and stable and that any new ones that you develop are likely to be both fulfilling and meaningful. You could also be feeling optimistic about forming new connections and about your ability to build and maintain healthy interpersonal relationships.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 7 of Swords is a card associated with ill health, especially physical issues related to the body. It can indicate that an individual may experience an injury, illness or other medical condition that is caused by their own dishonesty or lack of effort. It can also represent an individual who is running away from their health issues, either through ignorance or trying to avoid responsibility. This can lead to an inability to take proper care of the body, resulting in further health complications.

Finances: The 7 of Swords is a card that can often symbolize financial losses or theft, especially if the individual has been dishonest in their money management. It can also indicate that the person may be avoiding their financial obligations, either by running away from them or simply denying that they exist. This will likely lead to further debt piling up as they are unable to meet their obligations and can eventually cause financial ruin.

Relationships: The 7 of Swords is a card associated with betrayal, deceit and dishonesty, especially in the context of relationships. It can indicate that an individual may be engaging in secretive or deceptive behavior in order to get what they want out of the relationship, or they may simply be running away from their responsibilities to their partner or family. It can also signify that an individual may have difficulties trusting others or being honest with themselves, leading to further issues within their relationships.
