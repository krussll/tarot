## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

It may also represent a period of surgery or a transformational time in your health. It can be a warning to keep up with regular check ups and to have a plan of action if surgery is required. It can also symbolize a mental health breakthrough, letting go of unhealthy habits and embracing healthier ones.

Finances: The Ace Of Swords Tarot card can mean a new beginning in terms of finances. It can suggest that you take action to increase your financial stability, tap into your creativity and find new ways of making more money. It can also mean that you should be careful with your money and not take on too much risk or stretch yourself too thin. It may represent the need to overcome any financial problems or debts that have weighed you down.

Relationships: The Ace Of Swords Tarot card can signify new ideas or new ways of thinking when it comes to relationships. It can suggest that you are ready to change the way you interact with people and to become more honest and open with your feelings. It could also mean that you will have to go through some difficult conversations or disagreements to get to a better place. It encourages you to take action and be the one to make the changes you want to see. It could also represent a need to set boundaries and not be too easily swayed by others.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health

When The 7 Of Cups card appears in a health spread, it generally indicates an individual with too many choices when it comes to health and wellness. This may represent an inability to commit to one particular diet and exercise plan or a lack of enthusiasm when it comes to self-care and personal health. The individual may struggle with making decisions regarding their physical health, or they may lack the motivation to make any changes at all. This lack of commitment can result in self-sabotage and a lack of progress in improving overall health.

Finances

The 7 Of Cups card in relation to finances indicates an individual who has too many financial choices to make or is indecisive when it comes to committing to one decision. This can be seen as scattered thinking or distractions that lead to bad decisions about money. This card can also reflect someone who focusses on short-term goals and does not plan for the future when it comes to their finances. The individual is likely to lack financial discipline, which may have a negative impact on their long-term financial health.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, The 7 Of Cups card warns of commitment issues, as the individual may have difficulty committing to any one relationship. This can manifest as a fear of intimacy or a feeling of restlessness. The person may have difficulty making decisions about relationships, or they may struggle with taking the relationship to the next level. This card can also indicate an inability to discern between potential partners or desires, which can lead to anxiety or emotional turmoil. They may struggle with getting too emotionally attached or emotionally distant, depending on the situation.
