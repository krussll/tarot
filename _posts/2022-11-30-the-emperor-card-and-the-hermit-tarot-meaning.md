A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Emperor card and The Hermit card together in particular.

The The Emperor card and The Hermit have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Emperor and The Hermit mean together?

The Emperor tarot card and The Hermit tarot card are very complementary cards for me. The Emperor card speaks to the importance of respect, leadership, and strategic planning, representing a father figure in my life. The Hermit card reminds me to go within for clarity, to search for personal truth and spiritual illumination. Together, these cards show me that I must have a strong base of external respect and leadership to begin my internal journey of truth and understanding. They remind me to take a step back and seek divine guidance and wisdom in order to move forward with intention and strategic thought.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Emperor and The Hermit mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

The Emperor Tarot card is a representation of a strong, authoritative figure whose presence exudes confidence and power. This card symbolizes leadership, respect, and the ability to achieve one’s goals. For health, the Emperor card signifies the importance of having a plan and taking charge of one’s health. It is a reminder to create secure routines, find balanced nutrition, and make healthy lifestyle choices. By being proactive and taking charge of your healthcare, you can lead a healthier and more fulfilling life.

For finances, the Emperor card speaks of the need for strategic planning. It is a reminder to create a budget and be mindful of your spending habits. It is also a reminder to take the time to analyze your financial situation and make decisions about investments and saving. The card encourages you to use your authority to create a secure financial future.

In relationships, the Emperor card highlights the importance of taking charge and exerting your authority. It serves as a reminder to set boundaries and be mindful of other people’s feelings. It encourages you to be the leader in your relationships, take initiative in achieving your goals, and be the one to make decisions. The card also serves as a reminder to provide structure and stability in your relationships.

The card can be a hint to look inward and listen to the body’s needs to create balance and harmony. The Hermit Tarot card can be a reminder to go within and reflect on one’s financial goals and evaluate if they align with personal values. The card may be a sign to look deeper and get honest with oneself about long-term financial decisions and security. It can also be a prompt to get organized with finances and create a budget to build a financial foundation. The Hermit Tarot card may also be a reminder to pause and consider the impact that money has on lifestyle, relationships and professional choices.

It can be a reminder to be honest with oneself and others about wants, needs, and expectations.
