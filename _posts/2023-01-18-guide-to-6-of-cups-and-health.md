Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The 6 Of Cups card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 6 Of Cups, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 6 Of Cups.

## 6 Of Cups And Health – An Overview

The 6 Of Cups tarot card is a reminder to take care of your health and overall wellbeing, as it speaks of nostalgia and reunions.

It encourages you to look back on your past and remember the gifts that health and wellness have given you.

It is a reminder to take time for yourself to be mindful of the present and its gifts of physical, mental, and emotional health.

It is also a reminder to remain innocent and optimistic about the future, and to never lose sight of the importance of self-care and healthy habits.

Health is a precious gift, and The 6 Of Cups encourages you to cherish it, appreciate it, and nurture it.

## Upright Meanings For 6 Of Cups And Health

### What It Means For Me

The 6 Of Cups tarot card can represent nostalgia, gifts, innocence and reunions, all of which are concepts that have a deep connection to physical and mental health.

Nostalgia has been linked to improved mood, as it reminds us of the positive aspects of our past.

Gifts, while not always material, can bring joy and satisfaction, which can help to boost our mental and emotional wellbeing.

Innocence is often seen as a desire to become more mindful of our thoughts and feelings, which can help us to identify any negative patterns that may be holding us back from achieving optimal health.

Finally, reunions can remind us of the importance of connections, both with family and friends, which is a key part of maintaining good mental health.

Ultimately, the 6 of Cups tarot card signifies the importance of balancing our past, present and future in order to achieve true health and wellbeing.

### What It Means For The Future

The 6 of Cups tarot card is a symbol of nostalgia, gifts, innocence, and reunions.

This card suggests that looking back on your past, and seeking comfort in the familiar, can bring about a renewed sense of health in your life.

It can symbolize that in the near future, you may receive a gift from a loved one, or a chance to reunite with a past friend or family member.

This reunion will bring new energy and a feeling of contentment that can support your overall health.

The 6 of Cups encourages you to reconnect with people and situations that bring you joy and comfort.

By doing so, you will find that your health is restored and your spirit renewed.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 6 of Cups tarot card is a powerful reminder that those close to us are precious and important to us.

It encourages us to nurture our relationships with those we care about, and to make sure that we are paying attention to their emotional and physical health.

It is a reminder to take time out of our busy lives to reconnect with family and friends and to show our appreciation for them.

The 6 of Cups encourages us to provide emotional and physical support to those closest to us, offering gifts, reassurance and comfort.

It is a reminder to stop and savor the moment, to let go of the past and to appreciate the present, so that we can create a brighter future for ourselves and for those around us.

## Reversed Meanings For 6 Of Cups And Health

### What It Means For Me

When the 6 of Cups is reversed, it is indicative of forgetting the past and not holding on to the sentimental feelings that come with nostalgia.

This can be a warning that may have a negative impact on one’s physical and mental health.

In terms of physical health, the 6 of Cups reversed can mean that one is not taking care of their body and not paying attention to their needs.

This could be signs of physical neglect, such as not eating healthily or not taking time for exercise.

In terms of mental health, the 6 of Cups reversed can signify that one is engaging in unhealthy coping mechanisms.

This could manifest in substance abuse, avoidance of difficult emotions, or a lack of self-care.

It can also be an indication that one is not dealing with their traumas and is instead burying them deep down.

This can lead to a feeling of being stuck and can lead to depression, anxiety, and other mental health issues.

The reversed 6 of Cups cautions one to take a step back and reflect on their past in order to better understand themselves and their current situation.

### What It Means For The Future

When The 6 Of Cups tarot card appears in its reversed position, it can be an indication of a period of poor health.

It could represent an illness that has been lingering for some time, or a recent diagnosis of a health condition.

It can also be a sign that the person is neglecting their health, either through denial or negligence.

The reversed 6 Of Cups tarot card can be an indication that the person needs to take time to focus on their health and prioritize their wellbeing.

They may need to make some lifestyle changes, such as eating healthier, getting more exercise, and managing stress levels.

The reversed 6 Of Cups tarot card can also be a sign that the person needs to seek professional help if they are having difficulty managing their health on their own.

It can be a reminder to take care of oneself and to listen to one’s body so that any health concerns can be addressed and treated in a timely manner.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the 6 of Cups tarot card appears in its reversed position, it is an indication that health is at a fragile state and needs to be nurtured and taken care of.

This card indicates that it is time to be conscious of one’s physical and emotional health, and to take steps to improve it.

It may be a sign that physical and mental exhaustion has set in, and it is time to take a break and practice some self-care.

This can be done through physical activity, rest, relaxation, and healthy eating.

Additionally, this card may be a sign to be open to reconnecting with supportive people who can provide emotional nourishment and healing.

When the 6 of Cups appears reversed, it is a reminder that our health and wellbeing is a priority and it is essential that we nurture and protect our physical and emotional health.
