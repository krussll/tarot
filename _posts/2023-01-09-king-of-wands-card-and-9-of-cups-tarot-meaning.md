This card combination encourages you to take calculated risks and have the confidence to make your wishes come true. This combination is a sign that you can have it all – good health and prosperity – with an unwavering commitment to your vision, and that you have the courage to lead your life the way you want to.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

The King of Wands Tarot card is indicative of wellness and vitality, indicating that you have the necessary energy and willpower to maintain good health. The card encourages you to take the initiative and take a leadership role in improving your health. This means not only embracing a healthy lifestyle but also being proactive in seeking out treatments when necessary. The card also warns against taking health risks and encourages careful consideration before making decisions.

Finances  
The King of Wands Tarot card indicates that you have the creativity and power necessary for financial success. It suggests that you use your natural skills and resources to your advantage. The card encourages you to create a financial plan, set goals, and take a bold approach to your finances. This means accepting responsibility and understanding the consequences of your decisions, as well as taking risks when necessary.

Relationships  
The King of Wands Tarot card indicates that you have the charisma and strength of character necessary for maintaining good relationships. It encourages you to take a leading role in your relationships and show your commitment to those around you. This means showing loyalty and respect, as well as taking responsibility for any mistakes. The card also suggests that you be direct in communication and that you have the courage to ask for what you need within relationships.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is a very positive omen, often representing wishes coming true and material abundance. In regards to health, it could mean that you are able to access the necessary care and treatment you need to improve and maintain your health. During times of illness, it can provide a sense of hope that you can manage and even overcome the ailment. In terms of finances, it is often seen as a sign of great wealth, whether through sensible investments or even luck. It can also indicate that you are able to manifest financial abundance through hard work and dedication.

When it comes to relationships, the 9 of Cups Tarot card can be an indicator of great joy and happiness. You may find yourself in an emotionally healthy and loving relationship, and receive the support and companionship you need. Even if you are not in a romantic relationship, the 9 of Cups can suggest a strong connection with family, friends and the people in your community.

Overall, the 9 of Cups Tarot card is an excellent sign, indicating that your needs in terms of health, finances and relationships will be taken care of. This card serves as a reminder to stay positive, have faith and trust yourself to make the right choices. The universe is holding space for your dreams to grow and will reward you if you are open and willing to receive its blessings.
