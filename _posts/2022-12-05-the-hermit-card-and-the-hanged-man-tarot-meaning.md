## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and The Hanged Man mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and The Hanged Man the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health:

The Hermit Tarot card is a reminder to look inwards when it comes to physical and mental health. It encourages us to take stock of our lives and assess our current situation from an internal, holistic perspective. It suggests reflecting on our health habits and routines, getting to the root of any issues, and eliminating unhealthy patterns and behaviors. The Hermit Tarot card also suggests taking time for ourselves alone, away from hectic schedules and everyday stresses. This can help us to cultivate inner peace, understand our needs, and clear our minds so that we can create a healthier and more balanced life.

Finances:

The Hermit Tarot card speaks to financial matters as well. It encourages us to assess our current money situation, take stock of our resources, and reflect on our financial habits and patterns. It suggests becoming more aware of our spending and investing patterns and creating a more mindful approach to money, looking for opportunities and solutions within. The Hermit Tarot card suggests taking time away from the hustle and bustle of the outside world to evaluate our finances and make more conscious decisions. By taking a step back and looking within, we can gain clarity on our current financial state and create better outcomes for the future.

Relationships:

The Hermit Tarot card has a great deal to offer when it comes to relationships. It suggests turning inward and examining our current relationships objectively. It encourages us to take some time away from the external chaos and stresses of everyday life, in order to look at our relationships from a place of understanding, clarity and insight. This can help us to identify patterns, relationships that aren’t working, and areas where we need to work on ourselves or our relationships. It also encourages us to be honest with ourselves and with those around us, so that we can create healthier and more fulfilling relationships.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health

The Hanged Man Tarot card symbolizes the importance of being willing to make difficult choices when it comes to one’s health. It may be that this requires one to make a difficult decision such as taking time off from work to recover from a health issue, or opting for a more holistic approach to health like developing a yoga practice or ditching processed foods. On a deeper level however, this card urges one to look at the underlying psychological issues that negatively affect their health, such as chronic stress or unresolved traumas. It asks that one be willing to be vulnerable and look within, actively seeking out and tending to the wounds of the past, in order to bring about healing in the present.

Finances

The Hanged Man Tarot card suggests that it may be time to transform one’s relationship to money. This card can literally speak to suspending activities like excessive spending or gambling, but it can also symbolize a willingness to reorganize one’s finances in order to facilitate a more meaningful connection to money. This could mean reducing unnecessary expenses, developing a budget, and reallocating money to investments that could yield long-term growth. This card also speaks to the importance of learning the art of contentment and a recognition that it is not money, but our connection to it, that ultimately leads to satisfaction.

Relationships

The Hanged Man Tarot card symbolizes the importance of being willing to make sacrifices in order to maintain healthy relationships. It could be as simple as making time for a friend despite busy schedules or being willing to compromise with a partner or family member. It may also ask one to look at the larger dynamics of their relationships, such as being willing to speak up when something doesn’t feel right or being willing to forgive if there has been hurt or betrayal. Ultimately, this card urges an understanding that relationships require work and that the willingness to be vulnerable is key. Self sacrifice and taking a different perspective are essential to success in any relationship.
