A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The combination of the 5 Of Wands and the 5 Of Pentacles tarot card means that there are certain obstacles and challenges that must be faced in order to achieve success. The 5 Of Wands represents competition and rivalry, which could manifest in the form of sport, or in a professional or business enterprise. The 5 Of Pentacles, however, indicates that such success may be blocked by illness, financial hardship or loneliness, making it harder to stay motivated and win. Together, this combination suggests a course of action involving dedication, discipline and hard work in order to break through these barriers and achieve the desired goals.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 5 Of Wands suggests that there may be a degree of rivalry or competition between you and your partner, or perhaps you both might be feeling the pressure to out-do each other to prove your worthiness. The 5 Of Pentacles, on the other hand, warns of potential problems with money or health, which can also lead to worry and isolation.

You may need to be mindful during this time so that the competition between you and your partner does not become destructive. It’s important to remember that despite the obstacles, you can still stay close and support one another. The 5 Of Pentacles encourages you to pay attention to the physical and emotional needs of both of you, as well as those of your relationship. If there are health or financial worries, make sure you are taking steps to address them and don’t let them become sources of tension.

Overall, when the 5 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles appear together in a love reading, it may indicate that things will not be easy – but with proper communication, care, and understanding, you will be able to weather the storm and come out stronger on the other side. You and your partner need to pull together to make it happen!

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 5 Of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to stay physically healthy and active as this card speaks to the idea of facing challenges in order to achieve greater rewards. It is important to remember to set realistic goals for yourself and have the willpower and determination to face any obstacles along the way. Unexpected health issues and illnesses can be seen as positive challenges, and so it is important to take the time to care for yourself and build strength in order to better face and overcome such issues.

Finances: The 5 Of Wands Tarot card may be telling you to face any financial challenges with a fighting spirit, or to be wary of any competition or rivalry in regard to your finances. It also advises to be proactive and creative when it comes to expanding and developing your financial resources. Use your energy and initiative to overcome any obstacles along the way, and remember to be mindful of your budget and the risks associated with making any big financial decisions.

Relationships: The 5 Of Wands Tarot card speaks to the idea of facing any relationship challenges with a sense of determination and courage. It advises us to be mindful of the dynamics of the relationship and to be open to the idea of compromise and sacrifice in order to achieve a more balanced outcome. Be mindful not to let any rivalry or competitiveness in a relationship cloud our judgement, and be open to the idea of confrontation and open communication in order to work through any issues in an effective manner.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The Five of Pentacles tarot card is a stark reminder of the fragility of our physical and financial health. This card symbolizes poverty and ill health, worry and isolation. It is associated with feelings of being stuck, of feeling like you’re being left out of the world and of being ignored. It also suggests a decline in our financial resources, and a lack of resources and prospects for improving our financial situation.

On a physical level, the 5 of Pentacles suggests long-term health issues. These could be physical issues such as chronic pain, long-term illnesses or even short-term health concerns. The card also implies a lack of access to the medical care or resources one needs to address their health problems, further exacerbating the difficulties being experienced.

The 5 of Pentacles also implies a decline in one’s financial resources. It can signify a loss of wealth, of savings, of investments and of income. It also suggests a difficulty in finding ways to improve one’s financial situation, leaving one feeling powerless and hopeless.

In regards to relationships, the 5 of Pentacles can represent a period of isolation and loneliness. It reflects a time when the person feels abandoned and like their needs are not being met. This can greatly impact the connections a person has with others and can cause difficulty in forming and maintaining social ties.

The 5 of Pentacles warns us to take care of our health, both physical and financial. It serves as a reminder of how quickly our lives and circumstances can change, and how important it is to take preventative measures to ensure our wellbeing and security.
