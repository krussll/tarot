A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The 7 Of Pentacles and The 8 Of Swords tarot cards suggests a need for patience and the restriction of your actions if you want to achieve success. It means that you must work hard, but at the same time be mindful of any self-imposed restrictions or limitations. This can be a feeling of being confined or of circulating in a certain place without receiving any real returns. You might feel like you’re in a bind, but with patience and determined effort, success will eventually come your way.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

There is a slight feeling of hesitation on the card as you come to the brink of your ambitions. This suggests that, when it comes to health, you should be patient and take the time to get fully well. Don’t rush your recovery; take the time to invest in yourself and your well-being, as it will pay off in the end.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of delayed success. You may have been working for months or even years to get to where you want to be. But now, you are almost there and the end is in sight. This is a card of patience, as you will only see the rewards of your hard work if you keep pushing forward. Don’t give up or become complacent in your pursuit, as success is just around the corner.

Relationships: The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to take the time to build your relationships. Although the end goal is a strong connection, it is important to invest in the small moments that will lead to success. Take the time to understand your partner, their needs and their feelings. This card suggests that, by taking the time to nurture your relationships, you will be rewarded with a powerful connection that will last a lifetime.

Overall, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of patience, hard work and delayed success. It is a reminder to take the time to invest in your health, finances and relationships, as it can lead to rewards that will benefit you in the long-term.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health  
When it comes tohealth, The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can represent feeling stagnant. It suggests that you may be severely limiting yourself, whether it be through fear, complacency or even inaction. You may be stuck in a cycle of self-imposed goals, rules and regulations which are preventing growth and progress. If there is an underlying illness or physical limitation, it can represent this as well. This card can be prompting you to take action and make the necessary steps to release yourself from the mental, physical and even spiritual self-imprisonment and move forward.

Finances  
The 8 Of Swords Tarot card can suggest a lack of progress when it comes to finances. You may be self-imposing barriers to financial freedom, whether it be through hoarding money, feeling too afraid to invest or even just not taking any action. This card can be an indicator of stagnation and even a sign of debt and financial burdens. It warns that if you continue to impose restrictions and not take action to improve financial situations, you will remain stuck and in debt.

Relationships  
When it comes to relationships, The 8 Of Swords Tarot card suggests that you may be creating invisible walls and putting up your own walls of protection. You may be in an unhealthy dynamic, or it may signify that you are closing yourself off and not allowing new people into your life. It can be a sign of feeling trapped, powerless or even feeling like you have to remain in an unhealthy dynamic. It can also represent that you are unable to take action and move forward with relationships, whether it be platonic or romantic. The 8 Of Swords Tarot card suggests that it is time to take responsibility and make the necessary steps to break free of self-imposed boundaries and find freedom and growth.
