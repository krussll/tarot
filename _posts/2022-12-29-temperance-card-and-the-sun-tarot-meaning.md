However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is all about balance, harmony, patience and self-control. This card has a powerful meaning that can be applied to almost every aspect of life, including health, finances, and relationships.

When it comes to health, Temperance encourages us to find balance. This card can serve as a reminder that physical health is not the only component of overall wellness – spiritual and mental health can be equally as important. The Temperance card encourages us to practice moderation and be mindful of how we treat ourselves. We should strive to take care of our bodies while also taking the time to nurture our spiritual and mental well-being.

In terms of finances, Temperance suggests that we should practice fiscal responsibility. We must balance our needs and wants, prioritize our spending, and plan for the future. It’s important to know when to say ‘no’ and when to be generous. Temperance also suggests that we should not be too hard on ourselves – it’s ok to save for a rainy day, but it’s also ok to enjoy the present.

When it comes to relationships, Temperance encourages us to strive for harmony and balance. We should be patient and understanding with our loved ones and be willing to compromise when necessary. This card can also suggest that we need to be mindful of how we communicate with others. We should also be mindful of our own feelings and needs, make sure we’re taking care of our mental and emotional health before trying to take care of others.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card is an incredibly positive card, indicating the promise of material happiness and joyous outcomes. When it comes to health, this card symbolizes an abundance of energy, vitality and personal growth. Those who are looking to make improvements in their physical and mental well-being can find encouragement and guidance from this card. It offers an optimistic outlook on the future, suggesting that taking the proper steps and putting in the necessary effort can lead to positive health outcomes.

In terms of finances, the Sun Tarot card can represent unexpected windfalls, a general sense of abundance, and the achievement of long-term goals. It signifies the potential to make wise investments, to increase capital, and to effectively manage existing resources. The joyful energy of this card offers a sense of optimism and confidence that wealth, stability and success are within reach.

Finally, the Sun Tarot card offers hope and optimism in romantic relationships. It is a reminder that trust, open communication and a willingness to reach for shared dreams can create the foundation for lasting and fulfilling relationships. This card suggests that the growing sense of connection and increasing levels of comfort that come with strong and health relationships are possible. The Sun Tarot card also offers assurance that the joys of companionship and the beauty of a romantic partnership are within reach.
