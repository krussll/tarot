However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The 6 of Swords and the 8 of Cups tarot cards together suggest that you are at a point in your life where you need to move on and make a transition away from your current situation. You might feel the need to retreat from the current situation, letting go of the past and embracing a new journey ahead. It might be tough, but it is a necessary step if you want to find growth and inner peace. This can mean physically relocating, but also letting go of outdated patterns, beliefs, and mindsets that no longer serve you. It is an important step forward in order for you to find success and joy in the future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords Tarot card often indicates a major change in overall health. It could mean that you need to make a transition in your health routine or lifestyle. This could mean deciding to start a new exercise routine, or a healthier diet. It could also be an indication that you need to think about the mental and emotional components of your health as well. It could mean making a transition from a negative thought pattern to a more positive one, or doing things like alternative treatments like yoga and meditation. It could also mean making a transition from a state of chronic pain and illness to one of better health.

Finances:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card often indicates a transition in your finances. This could mean becoming more conscious of how you’re managing your money while also working on improving the security of your investments. For some it might mean looking at opportunities to grow your personal wealth such as investments or real estate. This card could also point to the need to be able to recognize when it’s time to move on from an unsuccessful venture and make a transition to something new.

Relationships:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card often indicates a need for a major transition in relationships. This could be because of feeling outgrown a current relationship or wanting to try something new. It could also be the need to make a transition from a current relationship to one that is more compatible and supportive. This card could also suggest that it’s time to leave a current relationship behind and move on. It could be a sign that it’s time to let go of a painful and toxic situation and focus on the potential of finding new and more supportive relationships.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 8 of Cups Tarot card represents a withdrawal from current health patterns. This could mean that it’s time to formally end a relationship with a health practitioner, or time to pursue alternative paths to physical and emotional wellbeing. It’s a sign to step back and make a change in one’s health habits. It could signify a need to create healthier boundaries with oneself and with others. It could also mean it’s time to explore new approaches to healing like meditation, yoga, counselling, or energy healing.

Finances: The 8 of Cups Tarot card indicates a period of retreat from an existing relationship with money. This could mean it’s time to step away from an old way of handling money, like spending without considering the consequences. It could also indicate a time to be more mindful about one’s financial decisions, like withdrawing from credit cards and taking a look at one’s spending patterns. It could signify a need to establish a healthier relationship with money, such as budgeting, saving and investing.

Relationships: The 8 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a period of disengagement and withdrawal in personal relationships. It indicates a need to step away from an unhealthy relationship or a need to take a break and reconnect with oneself. It could signal a need to distance oneself in order to reflect and consider what can improve in a relationship or how it can be changed. It could also show that it’s time to let go and move on from a relationship that’s no longer serving one’s wellbeing.
