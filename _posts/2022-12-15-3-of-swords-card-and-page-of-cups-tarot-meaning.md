A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 3 of Swords and The Page of Cups in a reading means that you are about to be faced with a difficult and painful situation in your life. It could involve a divorce, the loss of a loved one, a depression or even a surgery, and you may feel as if your entire world is crumbling around you. However, you can also find strength and hope in the Page of Cups, which suggests that, although you may be experiencing great pain and suffering, you will find the courage and resources to move through it and start again. The Page of Cups symbolizes a new beginning, with a studious kid or a new love, volunteer work or any other kind of meaningful pursuit that can help you to rebuild your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

For Health

The Three of Swords Tarot card is a sign of heartache, loss and distress, and is often associated with health issues. Physically, this card can foreshadow surgery, hospitalization, or long-term illness and suffering. Mentally, it can signal depression, grief, and anguish. Spiritually, it suggests a period of healing and recovery, although the process may be painful and difficult.

For Finances

The Three of Swords Tarot card warns of financial hardship, especially when it comes to debt. This card can also signal significant losses and setbacks in your business, as well as difficulty managing money. You’re likely to need to cut back on spending, even if it means sacrificing luxuries or comforts.

For Relationships

The Three of Swords is a sign of heartbreak and strife in relationships. This card suggests a divorce, a breakup, or a difficult break-up of some kind. It can also signal betrayal, infidelity, and hurt feelings. Communication and trust may be strained between partners, and it could take a long time to heal from the pain and sadness.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health

The Page of Cups in health matters often indicates a new beginning or rebirth. It may suggest starting a new routine, such as healthy eating habits or a new exercise regimen. It could also be a sign that you need to take time for self-care, whether it be through yoga, a massage, or simply taking time for yourself. This is a great time to re-evaluate your health, set new goals, and take action to make long-term changes.

Finances

The Page of Cups in regards to finances suggests a new opportunity, or a chance to start something new with money. It is a sign that this could be the start of a financial journey if you allow it to be, and it could bring success. It is a sign to pay attention to your intuition and don’t be afraid to take risks. It is also a good time to start saving and investing, as well as review your current financial situation.

Relationships

The Page of Cups in a relationship context often indicates the start of a new relationship or a re-kindling of an old one. It is a sign of patience and understanding, as well as forgiveness and kindness. The Page of Cups encourages looking at the relationship from a different perspective, such as from a child-like view. It is a sign that communication is key, and it is important to take time to listen to your partner and be open and honest.
