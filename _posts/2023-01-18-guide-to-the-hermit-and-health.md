Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Hermit card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Hermit, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Hermit.

## The Hermit And Health – An Overview

The Hermit tarot card can be a powerful reminder of the importance of self-care when it comes to our health.

It suggests that we take some time to listen to our inner wisdom and reflect on our physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

Taking time to go within and connect with our true selves can provide us with insights and clarity that can help us to make positive lifestyle changes and better understand our own needs.

Paying attention to our inner guidance can help us to identify habits and patterns that are not serving us, and to make decisions that will lead to a healthier, more balanced lifestyle.

The Hermit can be a reminder that taking care of our health starts within, and that a quest for personal truth and spiritual illumination is essential in order to have a healthy and fulfilling life.

## Upright Meanings For The Hermit And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Hermit tarot card encourages an inward journey to seek clarity and personal truth when it comes to health.

It suggests that spiritual illumination can lead to attaining and maintaining physical health.

This card speaks to the power of meditation or introspection to gain a better understanding of oneself.

It can be a reminder to take the time to go within and assess one’s physical and emotional health.

The Hermit encourages us to listen to our inner voice and tap into the wisdom that lies within, so that we can make better decisions for our health and wellbeing.

### What It Means For The Future

The Hermit tarot card is a reminder that personal truth and spiritual illumination are key to your future health.

It suggests that to achieve your health goals, it is important to take some time to go within and explore your innermost thoughts, feelings, and emotions.

By doing so, you can uncover hidden knowledge about yourself and your physical and mental health.

The Hermit tarot card encourages you to ask yourself meaningful questions about how you can better care for yourself, and to listen to your intuition and inner wisdom to find your own answers.

As you nurture your inner self, you will be able to create a healthier and more fulfilling life for yourself.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Hermit tarot card is a reminder to take a step back and look at the bigger picture of our relationships with those close to us.

It is a reminder to take a moment to look inward and reflect on our own personal truths and values, and to gain spiritual illumination and clarity.

This can help us to better understand and connect with those around us, as we have a clearer understanding of ourselves.

Through this process of self-reflection, we can ensure that we are providing those close to us with the emotional and physical support that they need, and be able to recognize the signs of any health issues or concerns that they may be struggling with.

## Reversed Meanings For The Hermit And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed Hermit tarot card speaks to a period of stagnation in terms of our health.

It denotes a lack of clarity and direction, leading to confusion and frustration.

It is a sign that we should take some time to go within ourselves and reconnect with our inner voice.

We need to be honest with ourselves about our current health and wellness, so that we can make the best decisions for our future.

The reversed Hermit encourages us to pause and reflect on our physical and mental health.

We should strive to identify the areas in our lives where we need more clarity and focus in order to improve our health.

In doing so, we may find that there are underlying issues that need to be addressed in order to heal and move forward.

We need to take time to nurture ourselves and our bodies, so that we can create positive, lasting changes.

### What It Means For The Future

When the Hermit card appears in its reversed position in a tarot reading, it can signify that the querent is feeling disconnected from their inner self and their spiritual truth.

This may be manifested in physical and emotional ailments as the querent has neglected their spiritual needs.

Health-wise, the reversed Hermit can point to the need to look inward and reflect on how the physical and emotional health may be linked to the spiritual state of being.

The reversed Hermit can act as a call to action to reconnect with the inner self and restore the relationship between the mind, body, and spirit.

This may involve engaging in practices such as meditation, yoga, or other forms of self-care.

It is important to remember that healing the physical body often requires healing of the emotional and spiritual self as well.

The reversed Hermit card is a reminder that the power to heal lies within and that it is important to listen to the inner truth and reconnect with the spirit within.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed Hermit tarot card is a warning that a person may be ignoring their physical health in order to focus on spiritual growth.

It is a reminder to attend to one’s physical needs and to be mindful of the balance between mental, spiritual, and physical health.

It can indicate that a person is not taking time for self-care, or that they have become too isolated and need to reconnect with the outside world.

When it comes to health, the reversed Hermit suggests that a person needs to take a step back and assess the situation.

It’s possible they are relying too much on their own resources, and need to reach out for help and support.

It also indicates that they may be neglecting their body and the signs it is sending them.

It could be a sign that they need to take a break and focus on healing, both physically and spiritually.

The reversed Hermit encourages us to take a look at our lifestyles and make sure that we are prioritizing our health and wellbeing.
