A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Death card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Death tarot card alongside the 5 of Pentacles can indicate that you are currently undergoing a major life transition, but that this shift can also be accompanied by struggles related to health, finances, and feelings of insecurity. While it may seem like an uphill battle at first, the Death card reminds us that it is ultimately a necessary step in order to start anew and experience a period of growth and rebirth. It may be difficult to let go of what was, but the healing process cannot begin until one is willing to accept the new beginnings in store. With enough courage, the hard times associated with the 5 of Pentacles will pass and the future will be full of exciting opportunities.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Death mean?

The Death Tarot card implies that a person’s life is about to undergo a transformation and that it is time for them to move on from the current stage of their life to the next. This card can represent a great opportunity for growth and transformation; however, it can also be a difficult transition and a time of great emotional stress.

For health, the Death Tarot card can indicate a need to make some dramatic changes in lifestyle in order to become healthier. This could mean making dietary changes, getting more exercise, trying out different treatments or therapies, or making other drastic lifestyle changes. This card can also signify the end of an illness, or the start of a new and healthier life.

For finances, the Death Tarot card suggests that it is time to move on from the current financial situation and to start fresh. This card could signify the end of a job or the start of a new business venture. It can also signify a need to overhaul one’s financial situation and to make some drastic money-saving changes.

For relationships, the Death Tarot card can indicate the end of a chapter in the relationship, or it can signify the need to make some drastic changes in order to move on to the next phase. This card can also signify the death of a relationship if the changes that need to be made are too drastic. This card can also signify a rebirth of a relationship, and the need to start over on a positive note.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card signals a difficult time ahead in terms of health, finances and relationships. When this card appears, it is a sign of bad luck in all areas of life, and it can be a warning of potential problems to come.

Health-wise, this card is a sign of potential physical illness or instability. It can be a warning of a chronic condition or physical decline, or a sign of an upcoming illness or health scare. It can also be an indication that it is time to take time for yourself and review your health, diet, and lifestyle, as it could be an indication of neglect or imbalance.

Financially, the 5 of Pentacles is a sign of great stress and worry. Financial hardship and loss are indicated, as is the need to make financial decisions that could lead to further financial distress. It is important to take stock of your situation, find ways to generate income, reduce existing debt, and invest for the future. You will also need to be prepared to make difficult decisions in order to survive this period of financial hardship.

Relationship-wise, this card is a sign of loneliness and isolation. If you are in a relationship, it might be time to check in with your partner and make sure that the boundaries and communication channels are in place and functioning. If you are single, it might be time to take a break from dating and focus on yourself. Taking time to reconnect with your true self is one of the best things you can do during this time.

Ultimately, the 5 of Pentacles is a sign that a struggle is ahead and it is important to be prepared and use this time constructively. Making sure that your health, finances, and relationships are in good shape will help you weather this difficult period.
