A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Emperor card and Justice card together in particular.

The The Emperor card and Justice have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Emperor and Justice mean together?

The Emperor tarot card and The Justice tarot card are quite complementary. Together, they represent respect, leadership, strategic planning, and a father figure in the sense of a fair and just leader. The Justice tarot card indicates that the law will be applied in an equitable way to all and that any decisions made will be based on justice and fairness. This combination therefore encourages you to pursue a balanced approach to any situation, determining the truth and coming to win-win solutions through organized and planned action that benefits everyone.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Emperor and Justice mean together for your love life?

The Emperor tarot card and The Justice tarot card are two sides of the same coin when it comes to love. If your relationship is in need of a reset, the pairing of these two cards together is the right approach. The Emperor card represents responsibility, respect, and strategic planning. This encourages a rational, leading approach to dealing with any issues in the relationship.

On the other hand, Justice focuses on the importance of fairness and balance. It also signifies the importance of cause and effect so that both partners can come to a mutual understanding of what needs to be done to get the relationship back on track. Together, these cards emphasize the importance of being flexible and understanding of the other person’s perspective, as well as giving each other the space to grow.

In the love life, the combination of the Emperor and Justice tarot cards suggests that both partners are willing to take a step back, evaluate their feelings and actions, and look for a win-win solution. It’s important to take the time to discuss issues and come up with a plan that works for both of you. The cards also imply that communication is key in resolving any issues, as well as recognizing when to take the high road and let go of grudges.

When it comes to relationships, the Emperor and Justice tarot cards suggest the presence of a strong father figure. This person encourages an atmosphere of discipline and respect, while also doing their best to ensure fairness and justice in the situation. With their guidance, couples can gain invaluable insight into their own relationship and what needs to be done to move forward.

The pairing of The Emperor tarot card and The Justice tarot card is a powerful reminder to all couples that communication, understanding, and fairness are the keys to a successful relationship. While it may take some time and effort to get things back on track, if both partners are open and willing to take the time to listen and understand one another, the rewards can be great.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Emperor and Justice mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Emperor mean?

For Health:  
The Emperor Tarot card speaks of a need for discipline and staying focused on one’s health. This card implies that to be healthy, one must have a plan of action and leadership qualities. As the leader of the Tarot Major Arcana, The Emperor can also be interpreted as a reminder that we must take responsibility for our health, make decisions and take control of it. In order for us to stay healthy, we must take a holistic approach to our health – considering diet, exercise, rest, relaxation, and any other necessary treatments like therapies or medications. This card serves as an admonition to put in the hard work and effort to maintain a healthy lifestyle, as this is key to a long and healthy life.

For Finances:  
The Emperor Tarot card signifies a need for keeping a tight budget, being strategic and mindful of money. In today’s world, it is essential to make careful decisions when it comes to money, as it can have a significant impact on our lives and future. This card calls for one to take on a leadership role and be the one who takes control of their finances, as opposed to leaving it up to fate. In order to stay on top of one’s finances, it is important to create a budget and stick to it, while at the same time setting goals and taking calculated risks like investing if it is appropriate. This card serves as a reminder to be mindful of where one’s hard-earned money is going and to think twice before making any big financial decisions.

For Relationships:  
The Emperor Tarot card signifies a need for stability and respect in relationships. This card speaks of taking on a leadership role and being the one to take charge of the relationship. The Emperor can also be interpreted as a reminder that as a leader one has a responsibility to be fair, patient, and open to understanding and compromise when it comes to relationships. Ultimately, this card serves as a reminder to be mindful of one’s actions and words, as relationships can be delicate and easily broken. The Emperor signifies that it is important to be proactive in fostering and maintaining healthy relationships.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card means that people should make decisions with fairness, balance and responsibility. It implies that justice should always be served. With regards to health, it means that everyone should take responsibility for his or her own health, and make decisions with their best interests in mind. This may mean making lifestyle changes, such as adopting a healthier diet or exercising more, in order to maintain good health.

When it comes to finances, The Justice Tarot card implies that people should be honest and fair with their money. This could mean deciding what is truly necessary and using resources responsibly. People should also be transparent in their business dealings, as well as consider how their decisions will affect others.

The Justice Tarot card also encourages people to create and sustain healthy relationships. It suggests that people should take responsibility for their actions, be honest and open with each other, and show respect. This could mean learning how to communicate effectively, as well as being willing to forgive and move on.

The Justice Tarot card encourages people to be fair and responsible in all aspects of life, be it health, finances or relationships. It is a reminder that justice should be served, and that people should strive to make decisions that are balanced and beneficial to all involved.
