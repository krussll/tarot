The Hermit card encourages you to seek out your personal truth and to be mindful of what resonates with your inner wisdom and the 2 of Pentacles reminds you to trust your ability to balance the needs of your inner and outer life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 2 Of Pentacles mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 2 Of Pentacles the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with physical health and well-being. The imagery of two coins spinning on a rope suggests that there are multiple paths to take in order to maintain optimal health. The card is about balance, meaning you must find a way to juggle multiple activities (like exercise, diet and rest) in order to stay healthy. The card can also be interpreted as a warning to not overexert yourself and to be mindful of your energy levels. It is important to find a balance between physical activity and rest in order to stay healthy.

Finances:

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card is linked to financial decision-making. The imagery suggests that there are multiple options available when it comes to how you manage your money. The card is a reminder to weigh all the different options and to make informed decisions before investing or spending. It also indicates that you may need to focus on multiple streams of income in order to achieve financial stability. It is important to balance short-term and long-term goals when it comes to money.

Relationships:

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with relationships. The imagery suggests that relationships require balance and compromise in order to thrive. It is important to strike a balance between giving and taking, as well as finding ways to coexist peacefully. The card is a reminder to be mindful of the other person’s needs while also expressing your own. The card can also indicate that you might need to juggle multiple relationships at once in order to achieve harmony.
