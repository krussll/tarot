Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The 7 Of Swords card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 7 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 7 Of Swords.

## 7 Of Swords And Sprituality – An Overview

The 7 of Swords tarot card can be a warning when it comes to health.

It suggests that you may be dishonest or deceptive in some ways when it comes to your health.

It can also mean that you may be tempted to run away from difficult health issues, or even betray the trust of a doctor or medical professional.

The card is cautioning you to take a more honest approach to your health and not to avoid difficult conversations or decisions.

It’s important to be honest and open with your healthcare providers and to take responsibility for your own health.

If you take an honest and proactive approach to your health, it will serve you much better in the long run.

## Upright Meanings For 7 Of Swords And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

The 7 of Swords tarot card is a reminder to be mindful of our spiritual path.

It speaks of the consequences of dishonesty, betrayal, and theft, and how these can lead us away from the spiritual path we have chosen for ourselves.

It encourages us to take responsibility for our actions, and to be honest in all of our dealings with others.

The 7 of Swords also serves as a reminder to stay vigilant of our own spiritual journey and to be aware of the consequences of straying from our chosen spiritual path.

We must be mindful of our decisions, and to remain true to our spiritual beliefs in order to continue along our spiritual journey.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 Of Swords tarot card is a warning to me that I need to be cautious in my future spiritual journey.

It is a reminder that dishonest behavior and betrayal will have consequences, and running away from difficult situations is not the answer.

This card encourages me to take a step back and assess the situation before making any drastic decisions.

It also reminds me to have integrity in my spiritual journey and to make sure that my decisions come from a place of honesty and integrity.

With this in mind, I can move forward with confidence and trust in my spiritual journey.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 of Swords tarot card is an indication that those close to me may be on a spiritual journey that is full of dishonesty and betrayal.

It is a warning that I might be vulnerable to theft and deceit, and that I should be wary of those I trust.

This card can also suggest that those close to me may be running away from their own spiritual growth and progress, and that I should be careful to not let them drag me down with them.

This card is ultimately a reminder to be mindful of the spiritual state of those around me, and to be aware of the potential risks of being involved with them.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Swords And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

When the reversed 7 of Swords appears in a Tarot reading, it signifies a break in the pattern of dishonesty, betrayal, and theft.

It can be seen as a sign that you are ready to start anew, free from the previous negative patterns.

The reversed 7 of Swords suggests that you have the courage to face the truth, even if it is uncomfortable or difficult.

This can be a sign of spiritual growth, as you are able to recognize and accept your past mistakes and make amends.

You can start to move forward, free from the guilt and shame that can come with dishonesty and betrayal.

The reversed 7 of Swords can also be a sign of spiritual protection.

It can be a reminder that you are being watched over by a higher power, and that you are not alone.

It can also point to the need for increased spiritual awareness and self-care.

By embracing your authentic self and taking time for yourself, you can learn to trust yourself and your decisions again.

The reversed 7 of Swords is a reminder that it is never too late to begin again.

You have the courage and strength to move forward, and to create a different future.

This is a card of hope and renewal, and a sign that you are on the path to spiritual transformation.

I will be less likely to resort to deception and dishonesty, and instead be more honest in my dealings with others.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Seven of Swords card appears in a Tarot reading, it can signify a resurgence of spiritual connection for those close to me.
