A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Page Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Wands and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

When the Page of Wands tarot card and the 6 of Pentacles tarot card are drawn together, it suggests that a creative idea is being put into action and that help is being given or received to make that project a reality. This could be about an active kid looking for resources to turn his/her creative ideas into something concrete. It could also be about someone being generous by providing advice, resources, or material assistance to help someone else get their project going. In either case, this card combination suggests collaboration and working together to turn creativity into something tangible.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Wands mean?

The Page of Wands Tarot card means that now is a time to take charge of your health. It encourages people to be more proactive in their physical, mental, and emotional well-being. It encourages you to focus on healthy habits and an overall balanced lifestyle, with an emphasis on physical activity. The card suggests that you focus on nourishing your body, both externally and internally. It is a reminder to take care of your physical health through nutrition, exercise, and overall self-care. The card also encourages you to create healthy relationships with those around you, so that you can work together to create a stronger support system and keep each other healthy.

Finances

The Page of Wands Tarot card can symbolize financial luck. It indicates that something new and exciting is coming your way, whether it’s in the form of a new job, a business venture, or a financial opportunity. It encourages you to take risks and be open to new ideas and opportunities. The card also urges you to be diligent and stay focused on your financial goals. It suggests that now is the time to be creative and innovative with your finances, taking the time to come up with new ways to save, invest, and make money.

Relationships

The Page of Wands Tarot card can symbolize a new beginning, either with a current relationship or when meeting someone new. With a current relationship, it can mean a time for growth, adventure, and exploration. It encourages both parties to open up and be honest, so that they can build a stronger bond with each other. If meeting someone new, the card encourages you to be open-minded and creative in order to start a strong foundation. It suggests that you should be willing to take risks and explore different activities together, in order to build a strong and lasting relationship.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 6 of Pentacles in a Tarot reading can be a sign of balance and harmony, particularly when it comes to physical and mental health. It can be a reminder to look after your health and well-being, and to make sure that you have a healthy balance between work, rest and play. This card encourages you to look beyond the surface of any health issue, and to consider all factors involved. It highlights the importance of healthy diet and lifestyle, and finding ways to relieve stress and reduce anxiety. This can be a sign of taking advantage of alternative treatment methods, such as yoga or meditation, to attain improved health and well-being.

Finances:

The 6 of Pentacles in the Tarot can symbolize financial stability and the importance of generosity. It is an indication that you have the resources to provide for yourself or others, and to be generous with them. This card shows that by investing in yourself, both financially and emotionally, you can create a solid foundation and security for the future. It can also be a warning to take a balanced approach when it comes to money, such as avoiding impulse buying or making hasty decisions. This sense of balance and stability can also be seen in having a budget or saving plan that allows you to enjoy the present while still making wise investments for the future.

Relationships:

The 6 of Pentacles in a Tarot reading can be an indication that your relationships are in a period of balance and harmony. This card suggests that you may be in a generous mood and willing to give support and help to those around, allowing for equitable exchange and reciprocity. It can also be a reminder to look for power imbalances in relationships, and to make sure that both parties are benefiting from an exchange in some way. This card calls for an appreciation of the value of sharing and caring in relationships, and the importance of both giving and taking in order to establish mutual respect and understanding.
