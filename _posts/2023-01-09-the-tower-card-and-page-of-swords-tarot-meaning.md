A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Tower card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Tower tarot card when paired with The Page Of Swords card can mean that you are experiencing unexpected and unwanted changes to your life. This can be unsettling and bring about a sense of upheaval and ruin. The Page Of Swords can also imply that gossip and rumors are circulating about you, evoking feelings of disgrace and violence. It is important to remain vigilant to anything and anyone who seeks to pry into your personal life. Remember to focus on the truth and not the malicious whispers of others.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Tower and Page Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The Tower tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card appear together in your love life, they can signify unwanted changes and upheaval that will cause ruin and disgrace. This could be a situation in which a big transition is happening in your relationship, and it is not one that either you or your partner welcome. It may be a time of transition in which you are breaking up, or a time in which your current relationship is becoming unrecognizable. It can also be a time when another person or outside source is intruding into your relationship in a negative way.

The Page of Swords tarot card can suggest that this unwelcome transition is being driven by gossip, spies, and prying eyes. There could be someone in your life who is spreading rumors or interfering in your relationship in an unwelcome way. This person may also be trying to manipulate or expose the truth about your relationship in a way that neither you nor your partner want brought to light.

In these circumstances, it is important to recognize that the Tower tarot card and the Page of Swords tarot card are advising you to take action to protect your relationship. You may need to confront whoever is meddling in your relationship and set boundaries. You may need to stand up for your relationship and fight against those who would try to undermine it. This could be difficult, but it is necessary to protect your relationship from the chaos of unwanted change and upheaval.

The Page Of Swords card warns that you must be wary of gossip, and also of people who may be spying on you or prying into your affairs. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: The Tower Tarot card symbolizes health issues that are both physical and mental. These health issues can range from a sudden illness to a chronic health condition. It can also refer to a disrupted lifestyle that was previously successful. People may experience a decline in their physical health as a result of an unexpected health issue. Moreover, a person may be more prone to illness or injury due to high stress levels.

Finances: The Tower Tarot card can be a warning of potential financial ruin. It usually indicates that the person is headed for a period of financial upheaval. Unwanted change may include unexpected expenses, loss of employment, or unexpected financial losses. People may need to make difficult decisions about their finances because of the strain that the upheaval brings.

Relationships: The Tower Tarot card also symbolizes unfortunate changes in relationships. It often indicates that a relationship is headed for ruin, or that it may be experiencing disruption and turmoil. The undesirable changes may come from the unexpected end of a relationship, or from a major disagreement or betrayal in an existing partnership. Depending on the situation, the person may need to be prepared for some emotional distress or sorrow during this transition time.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card can represent secrets, vulnerabilities, and hidden truths related to your health. It’s a warning to be aware of any hidden health issues as well as any issues you’re intentionally ignoring. It’s a reminder to be honest and open with your doctor, or to get a second opinion. An open dialogue between you, your body, and your doctor will help you to identify any health issues that might otherwise go unnoticed.

Finances:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card can represent prying eyes when it comes to your finances. It’s a reminder to take extra care to safeguard your personal bank information and keep your finances under wraps. It’s a warning to not be too trusting when it comes to financial matters, and to double-check everything. It’s an invitation to explore clever ways to invest and grow your money, as long as these moves are well researched and thought-out.

Relationships:  
The Page of Swords Tarot card can represent gossip and secrets when it comes to relationships. It’s a warning to be aware of what other people might be saying about you and to take extra precautions to protect yourself. It’s a reminder to ensure that all communication is honest and open with your partner. Being transparent is the only way to have real trust and genuine connection. It’s a reminder to take time to reflect and investigate the truth beneath the surface of any conflicts or misunderstandings.
