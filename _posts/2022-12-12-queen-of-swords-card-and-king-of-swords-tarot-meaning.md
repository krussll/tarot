A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and King Of Swords mean together?

The Queen Of Swords and The King Of Swords combined indicate a person who is both self-sufficient and an intentional leader. This person will strive for total honesty and their decisions and actions will always be strategically thought out. They will be determined to lead with a sense of integrity while understanding the power of their words and actions. They will be committed to leveraging their authority to make effective and wise decisions, always with truth and justice in mind.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Queen Of Swords and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Queen Of Swords and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card signifies a person who is self-sufficient and independent when it comes to health. This card suggests that the person is completely honest about their own health and takes full responsibility for it. They take a proactive approach to health and don’t rely on medical professionals to keep them healthy. They realize that their health is inextricably linked to their own lifestyle and diet decisions and are vigilant about making sure their health is always in tip-top condition.

Finances  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card signifies a person who is honest and self-sufficient when it comes to money and finances. This card implies that they take full responsibility for their financial success or failure and refrain from blaming others or outside forces. They are independent and make their own decisions when it comes to money, trusting their own judgment and understanding of the markets and their own financial situation. They also value honesty when it comes to money, refusing to use it to deceive or manipulate others.

Relationships  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card implies that the person is honest and self-sufficient when it comes to their relationships with others. They recognize that they have autonomy in the decisions they make when it comes to their relationships and take responsibility for the quality of their connections. They are honest about their feelings and thoughts and take the initiative to devise solutions to any conflicts or discord in their relationships. They value being truthful and open with others and expect the same in return.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health

The King of Swords Tarot card is all about taking a strategic approach to health. It’s about taking an intentional approach to caring for your body and mind. This card suggests a focus on preventative health, rather than waiting until a problem arises. This could include taking preventative measures such as regular physicals and lab work, eating well and exercising, and seeking out clear and accurate information regarding health and wellness. This card also encourages cultivating a strong mental health, understanding and managing one’s emotions, and seeking help and guidance when needed.

Finances

The King of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to be prudent and thoughtful when it comes to finances. This card suggests taking a strategic and intentional approach to finances. This could include setting budgets, tracking spending, seeking out information and advice, and coming up with a plan to get out of debt and save for long-term financial goals. This card encourages exercising self-discipline and moderation when it comes to spending and investing.

Relationships

The King of Swords Tarot card implies taking an intentional and strategic approach to relationships. This card encourages setting boundaries, being clear about what is expected from a relationship, and communicating honestly and openly with one’s partner. The card also suggests pursuing relationships with intentionality, rather than aimlessly drifting from one relationship to another. These strategic approach to relationships could help in managing expectations, effectively resolving conflicts, and developing strong, healthy connections.
