A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The 7 Of Swords and The Queen Of Cups tarot cards together could signify the potential for betrayal and dishonesty, followed by healing and support. These cards suggest entering into a situation with caution and being aware of the possibility that someone may feel they need to protect themselves by keeping secrets or running away. The Queen Of Cups offers a beacon of hope; although the situation may have started with dishonesty and betrayal, healing and nurturing energy can be brought to the situation by a supportive person. In the end, it is up to the individual to decide to accept the help and guidance.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The 7 of Swords and The Queen of Cups appear together in a love reading, it suggests that there is a great need for honesty and openness. This card combination can indicate a situation where deceit and betrayal have played a role in the relationship, or that one partner may be hiding something important.

The Queen of Cups could mean that there is an opportunity for healing and trust to be restored between partners. This card suggests a nurturing energy – where love and understanding can be found if both partners are willing to open their hearts and minds to a fresh start.

The 7 of Swords could be signaling a warning that running away from the problem will not help the relationship heal. Rather, it is only through honesty, communication, and hard work that the relationship can be repaired.

If both partners are willing to put in the effort, The 7 of Swords and The Queen of Cups together in a love reading can be a sign of growth and hope. This combination can signify a willingness to take bold steps to build trust, and to move forward in the relationship with mutual understanding. This card combination can also mean that both partners are willing to forgive past indiscretions, and to move towards a brighter and healthier future.

### What does 7 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When the 7 of Swords and the Queen of Cups appear together in a spread relating to finances and job opportunities, it reflects a difficult decision that may have far-reaching consequences. On the one hand, this card combination suggests an opportunity to explore a new financial venture or career that could prove profitable and rewarding, but the potential for betrayal and dishonesty lurk in the shadows. This card combination can be interpreted as a warning to proceed with caution, and to listen to your intuition.

In situations such as this, the Queen of Cups points to the importance of nurturing yourself and seeking out healing so that you can make a wise decision. She encourages you to be mindful of any emotions that may be clouding judgement, and to instead focus on clarity and instinct. This card suggests it may be beneficial to ask for advice and support from friends and family before taking a leap.

The 7 of Swords also points to the importance of maintaining an awareness and being extra vigilant when making a potentially risky decision. Even though it might be tempting to jump headfirst into an opportunity, it’s best to take your time and think through the pros and cons of the situation before taking any action. If we take the time to use the Queen of Cups’ judgement and the 7 of Swords’ dedication to diligence and caution, then we can make a decision that won’t lead to betrayal or harm.

Although the 7 of Swords and the Queen of Cups might appear to be contradictory, when combined in this spread, they offer a balanced aspect of caution and care. Taking the time to assess the opportunities available, as well as listening to our intuition, will help us to secure our financial future without compromising our emotional wellbeing.

The combination of the 7 of Swords and the Queen of Cups tarot cards in regards to your health implies that you must take a balanced approach to tackling any health issues you may be facing. The 7 of Swords indicates that there is a sense of betrayal or dishonesty in regards to your health, and that you may be feeling as though someone has stolen something important away from you. This could be in terms of a lost opportunity, an unaddressed health issue, or any number of other possibilities. The Queen of Cups suggests a need for nurturing and healing, however. Find someone to talk to who can provide you with the support you need and listen to your feelings without judgement. Self-care, exercise, and healthy eating habits can also help to keep your energy levels balanced and your body in good shape.

This combination also suggests that healing is possible, but that it’s important to take care of yourself first and foremost. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Swords and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The Seven of Swords in Tarot symbolizes theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and running away. It is considered to be an unlucky card, signifying a lack of security and trust. This card can represent many different things in terms of health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, the Seven of Swords can represent a lack of self-care, the need to take better care of both the physical and mental health of the individual. It can be an indication that the person is feeling overwhelmed and stressed, and that they need to make time for themselves to do the things that nourish and nurture their bodies. Taking proactive measures of self-care like good nutrition, regular exercise, and getting enough rest can help the individual to regain a sense of balance and well-being.

When it comes to finances, the Seven of Swords card can represent a lack of financial security and stability. This can be an indication that the person is spending too much, failing to save, and not keeping track of their finances. It is a warning to be more conscious of budgeting, saving, and being mindful of spending habits. It’s important to pay off any debts as soon as possible and make sure to prioritize saving money for the future.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Seven of Swords can represent a lack of trust and betrayal. It can indicate dishonesty, cheating, or manipulation. It can also be a sign that the person needs to re-examine their friendships and romantic relationships and make sure they are healthy and secure. This can be achieved by having open and honest conversations with loved ones, setting boundaries, and respecting each other’s feelings and needs. Ultimately, it is important to create relationships with people who are supportive, trustworthy, and encouraging.

It can also be a reminder to focus on what is truly needed to nurture and support your relationships. The Queen of Cups encourages you to approach relationships from a place of understanding and open communication. She can also bring a sense of security and safety to your relationships, allowing for a deeper connection that is built on love and trust.
