A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Wands card and 3 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Ace Of Wands card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

It’s a project that is inspiring you to create, but it looks like it will also require team work, commerce, and the need to travel to help it come to fruition. The energy of both cards encourages you to take the steps you need to turn your creative dreams into reality. With the support of the people around you, you will be able to expand your dreams and make them something tangible.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Ace Of Wands and 3 Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Ace Of Wands and 3 Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

Health: The Ace of Wands is a card of energy and enthusiasm. It suggests that you have a new spark of vigour and vitality, or have the potential to develop one. Its presence invites you to direct your energy into transforming your health, both mentally and physically. With a burst of enthusiasm, you can have the motivation to develop a healthy routine. It could be a symbol of the start of a physical activity or healthy diet, or a chance to start a journey of self-improvement.

Finances: The Ace of Wands tarot card encourages people to take a proactive approach to their finances. It symbolizes the opportunity to make sound investments, take a new approach to expenditure, and seek out new opportunities to increase your wealth. It can also signify a positive change in lifestyle, one that brings better financial health, such as finding a better-paying job or new sources of income.

Relationships: The Ace of Wands is a card of new beginnings in relationships. It indicates a new spark of passion and creativity, or the potential for it. It can also represent new beginnings, such as the start of a new romantic relationship or the strengthening of a current one. If a relationship has been feeling stagnant, the Ace of Wands signals a chance to reignite the spark and passion. The Ace of Wands also urges couples to explore new things together, either emotionally or romantically, to keep their relationship fresh and exciting.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 3 of Wands Tarot card signifies care, health and wellness. It is a card of partnership, indicating that two or more individuals can work together to make significant strides in their health. The card suggests that those individuals should focus on communication, collaboration, and trust for the most successful outcome. It can also indicate that taking a team approach to a health issue can be beneficial, such as working with a nutritionist, physical therapist, etc. Working together can also lead to a better understanding of a particular health issue and a greater appreciation for one another’s individual strengths.

Finances: The 3 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes financial growth, expansion, and progress. It is a card that speaks of business partnerships, alliances and joint ventures that have the potential for greatness. The card suggests forming alliances with those who have complementary financial skills and interests, working together collaboratively to create new opportunities and financial success. It can also be a reminder to stay organized, be mindful of financial risks, and to aim for long-term sustainability.

Relationships: The 3 of Wands Tarot card indicates a relationship that requires collaboration and communication. It is a card of teamwork and connection, suggesting that a couple should work together to reach their relationship goals. It is a card of adventure and exploration, indicating that the relationship should be open to travel and new experiences. This card suggests that listening to each other, being understanding and respectful of each other’s needs, and being proactive in communicating your feelings and needs, will ensure the most successful outcome.
