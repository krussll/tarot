The The Tower card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 9 Of Cups mean together?

When looking at The Tower tarot card and The 9 Of Cups together, it is possible to see a message of hope despite a time of change. In this context, The Tower signifies abrupt and often unwanted changes in your environment, but The 9 Of Cups is a reminder that even in the face of this difficult transition, you may be able to find blessings as a result. There could be a sense of abundance and good health, even though you might have to go through ruin, upheaval, or violence first. Ultimately, this combination of cards suggests that although you may be going through a scary or difficult period, there is the potential for growth, renewal, and even some blessings on the other side.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card is a card of significant, often unexpected change. It can mean unwanted change, ruin, upheaval, disgrace and violence, and is one of the more negative cards in a Tarot spread. When this card appears in relation to health, it can be a warning sign that something is out of balance and needs to be addressed. It may be a sign of an impending health issue, or that a health issue has gotten to the point where drastic action is needed. In the world of finances, The Tower is an omen of financial ruin. It can mean the collapse of investments, or of an entire business. It can also be a warning of a surprise expense, and that cash flow problems are imminent. On the other hand, it can also be a sign that something unproductive needs to be removed from one’s financial portfolio, allowing one to make new investments and deals.

When The Tower Tarot card appears in relation to relationships, it can mean a breakdown of trust and a fracturing of the relationship. It can be a sign of upheaval and of a potentially violent confrontation. It can also be a sign that the relationship is in need of some kind of drastic action, such as end of a toxic relationship or taking a break from a co-dependent one. In any case, it is a sign that something must change in order for the relationship to be healthy and productive.

The Tower Tarot card is a difficult card to interpret, as it is synonymous with disruption, upheaval, and often times, negative change. It should be taken as a warning sign that a situation has gotten out of balance, and that something is not as it should be. It can be a helpful reminder to take care of one’s health, to review one’s finances, and to make sure that relationships are on the right track.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 Of Cups Tarot card has a deeper meaning for health, finances, and relationships. When it comes to health, this card indicates good physical and mental health, along with a feeling of contentment and balance. It suggests that you take care of your body and mind, so that your health can be the best it can be. It also indicates that you are in control of your health and that you are focusing on prevention and maintenance, rather than reacting to illness in an unhelpful way.

In regard to finances, the 9 Of Cups Tarot card foretells abundance and financial gain. This could occur in the form of a promotion, a business opportunity, or a new job. It suggests that you will find financial stability, and you can draw on your resources and create wealth. The 9 Of Cups Tarot card also encourages you to make sensible investments and to plan for the future.

When it comes to relationships, the 9 Of Cups Tarot card suggests contentment and satisfaction. It indicates that you are in a stable, loving relationship, and that the two of you are working together to build a powerful, lasting connection. The card encourages you to be understanding and to show support for your partner. It also encourages you to learn from each other and to continue to grow together.

The 9 Of Cups Tarot card has many positive meanings for health, finances and relationships. This card encourages you to focus on your own wellbeing, to create financial stability, and to maintain a strong, loving relationship.
