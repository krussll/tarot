A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Strength card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 2 Of Swords mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The 2 Of Swords tarot card together suggest that the time has come for you to make an important decision and to use the power of your self-love, unconditional love, self-respect, and courage to do so. This could mean making a compromise that involves giving up something that you want to hold onto, but which you know deep down is not in your best interest. In order to make the right decision, you must take the time to consider all of your options and, ultimately, trust in yourself and your strength of character. Doing so will enable you to find the balance that you need to move forward in a healthy way.

The Strength tarot card and the 2 of Swords tarot card together represent a message to approach love in your life with strength, courage, and self-love. This includes the ability to set boundaries, and also be open to compromise.

The Strength tarot card speaks to having the power to stand up for yourself, and express your feelings and opinions confidently. This is an important part of having any kind of successful relationship. Whether romantic, familial, or interpersonal, you need to ensure your needs are being taken care of and remain a priority. This can only be done by displaying strength and courage of self-love.

The 2 of Swords tarot card suggests that there is a need to make decisions, and also to strive for balance and compromise. Making decisions throughout your relationships is a regular part of life. In order to have a successful relationship, you need to be open to compromise and mutual understanding. This means that sometimes, you need to give in and be open to the other person’s wants and needs, even if it means letting go of something you wanted. Additionally, this card also speaks of the need for balance – not one person should consistently be giving in or “winning” all of the time.

When taken together, The Strength tarot card and the 2 of Swords tarot card imply that relationships require strength and self-love, while also being open to compromise and negotiation. It is important to take care of yourself and know that you are worth a relationship that meets your needs and is mutually beneficial, while also being willing to sacrifice and give in. This is a fine balance that when achieved, can lead to a healthy and long-lasting relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and 2 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is associated with courage, self-love, and unconditional love. In terms of health, the Strength card symbolizes inner strength and resilience. It suggests that we find the courage and resolve to face our physical and mental health challenges with compassion, patience, and kindness. We are encouraged to love ourselves, to practice self-care, and to make thoughtful decisions that will help us heal. When we are strong and healthy, we become more resilient and better able to survive times of difficulty.

For finances, the Strength Tarot card is a reminder to take charge of our financial affairs. This might be by creating a budget, paying off debt, choosing to invest and save for the future, or even setting up a financial floor plan or safety net. It also encourages us to act with integrity and to trust our own judgment instead of relying on external advice. Moreover, the Strength card symbolizes the courage and willpower to set boundaries and protect our finances.

Finally, the Strength Tarot card symbolizes the courage to cultivate and nurture relationships with ourselves and with others. It encourages us to take time to get to know ourselves and to accept our flaws and imperfections. It also suggests that we practice unconditional love in our relationships, that we speak authentically, and that we strive to maintain healthy interpersonal boundaries. Moreover, the Strength card encourages us to have honest conversations and stand our ground when a relationship is not serving us. We are reminded that we are strong enough to make decisions from a place of love and wisdom.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 2 of Swords Tarot card indicates that it is time to reassess what your health goals are and to make important decisions on how to reach them. This could mean having difficult conversations about health issues with loved ones, seeking out medical advice, or doing research to understand your condition better. It could also mean asserting your boundaries in order to maintain a healthy balance in your life. It is important to strike a balance between making decisions that are in your best interest, while also being mindful of the consequences they may have on your health.

Finances: The 2 of Swords Tarot card suggests that now is a time to scrutinize your finances and make important financial decisions. This means carefully weighing all of your options as you consider what will be the most beneficial choice in the long run. It is important to be mindful of the risks associated with any financial decision and to proceed with caution. It is also important to be able to compromise and come to a mutually beneficial financial decision with your partner and/or family.

Relationships: The 2 of Swords Tarot card suggests that now is a time to take a step back and assess the state of your relationships. This could mean having difficult conversations with your partner, friends or family members to clarify expectations and boundaries. It is important to be mindful of the need to compromise and to recognize when it is time to make necessary decisions in order to maintain the connection. It may also be necessary to stand your ground if a particular decision threatens the health of the relationship.
