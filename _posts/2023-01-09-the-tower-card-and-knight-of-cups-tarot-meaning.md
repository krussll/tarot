## What does The Tower and Knight Of Cups mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The Knight Of Cups tarot card combined represent a situation of unwelcome and unexpected change coming in a romantic context. This could be a situation where a relationship is ending abruptly and unexpectedly, or where a sudden shift in dynamics occurs and forces a period of adjustments. Whatever the situation may be, it has the potential to be very uncomfortable and even a little bit painful. Despite the unpleasantness, it is important to keep looking for a silver lining and accept the situation as part of a journey that will ultimately bring growth and positive changes.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card is a symbol of unwelcome and unexpected change. Its symbolism has to do with ruin, disgrace, upheaval, and violence. It’s a card of disruption and it can bring sudden but necessary transformation, difficult as it may be.

When it comes to health, The Tower Tarot card suggests a period of health instability or illness. It can mean sudden, life changing news about a major health condition or a shock to the system that forces you to adopt healthier practices. Either way, the disruption can ultimately be a positive if it forces us to change our lifestyle for the better.

When it comes to finances, The Tower Tarot card can mean sudden, unexpected losses, such as a job loss or major financial setback. It can also mean a major investment or financial opportunity that requires you to take a risk. It’s an indication that change is inevitable and you have to be willing to take risks in order to reach your financial goals.

Finally, in relationships, The Tower Tarot card suggests a period of upheaval, disruption, and turmoil. It can mean a breakup or the end of a relationship, but it can also mean a period of transformation and growth. It’s a chance to reassess your relationships and make changes that bring you closer to a better version of your relationship. It’s important to be aware of the potential for violence in this card. It’s an indication that violence is not the solution and it should be avoided at all costs.

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

It suggests that while your social life and the joys of romance are important, do not neglect your physical and emotional wellbeing. The Knight of Cups signifies the importance of self-care, protecting your health and nourishing yourself. It could be a reminder to make time to relax, practice healthy habits, get regular check-ups and take time to enjoy the simple things in life. As the Knight of Cups is known as the Prince/ss Charming, it could be a sign to practice self-love and appreciation and to not be too hard on yourself.

When it comes to finances, The Knight of Cups Tarot card can be a sign of good news. The Knight of Cups Tarot card is a sign of romance, passion, and commitment. It can be a sign to put in the effort to make things work, to open up and be honest, and to not play games. It also suggests that while fun and excitement are important, it is essential to get to know someone deeper and to go beyond the surface. The Knight of Cups can indicate that there is potential for love, and so it can be a helpful reminder to be brave and take a chance.
