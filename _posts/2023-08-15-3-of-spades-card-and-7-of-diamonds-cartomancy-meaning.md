A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Spades card and 7 Of Diamonds card together in particular.

The 3 Of Spades card and 7 Of Diamonds have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing both the 3 of Spades and the 7 of Diamonds collectively indicates a journey of recovering from emotional turmoil to achieving triumph in the professional sphere.

The 3 of Spades represents heartbreak and faith, indicating that you may be confronted with emotional challenges.

The winter and water elements associated with this card emphasize the deep introspection and rejuvenation that this period of sorrow might entail.

However, with faith and courage, you are then expected to pass from this time of adversity and emotional seclusion.

On the other hand, the 7 of Diamonds introduces the themes of work issues and victory.

This suggests that trials and tribulations in your work life, represented by the season of fall, may consume your attention.

However, as the element of air signifies, through intellectual reasoning and communication, you can overcome these challenges and find success.

Together, these cards signify a roller coaster of emotional challenges and professional triumphs, reminding you to keep faith in times of sorrow and employ rationality in times of trouble.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together for your love life?

Drawing the 3 of Spades together with the 7 of Diamonds in a cartomancy reading suggests an emotional journey for your love life.

Given the heartbreak associated with the 3 of Spades, this can suggest a period of sadness, conflict, or loss, typically involving a romantic relationship.

Riding this storm may be especially challenging during the cold, introspective winter months.

However, this card also speaks of faith.

Despite the heartache, faith in love remains.

This could mean the development of resilience or personal growth from trials and tribulations that inevitably strengthen you and make you wiser in your love choices.

Meanwhile, the 7 of Diamonds hints at work-related issues which could indicate a struggle to maintain a work and love life balance.

There could be a physical or emotional distance due to work engagements that may create an impact on your relationship.

However, the association with victory signifies a very positive outcome.

When paired alongside, these cards predict eventual triumph over the difficulties symbolized by the 3 of Spades.

Associated with the fall, a time of change and transition, and the element Air – related to communication and intellect, victory could mean successfully navigating through the challenges, and reaching a stage of stability or even flourishing in your love life.

The combined element of water and air also suggest an emotional cleansing, promoting a new perspective.

Therefore, while a tough road may lie ahead, perseverance could lead to personal growth, better understanding of love and ultimately, triumph.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of the 3 Of Spades and the 7 Of Diamonds might signal a time of both emotional and professional turbulence.

The 3 Of Spades, representing heartbreak and faith, could be suggesting a period of emotional distress or disappointment related to your financial situation.

This might result from a sudden loss or drastic change, such as a shift in investment or unexpected expenses.

However, the presence of ‘faith’ also indicates that despite the immediate hardship, maintaining a positive outlook and trust in your financial planning and decisions is crucial.

The 7 of Diamonds, on the other hand, denotes ‘work issues’ and ‘victory’.

This could translate into job-related challenges, struggles in your career, or unforeseen obstacles that need to be faced.

It might imply that your current work environment or job position is under threat, leading to financial instability.

But remember, it also promises ‘victory’.

This suggests that although you may face professional obstacles, ultimately, your hard work, determination, and perseverance will bear fruit, leading to success.

In terms of your finances, this could refer to a considerable financial benefit in the longer run.

Thus, this card combination encourages you to remain resilient in the face of challenges.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, if you draw the 3 of Spades, it signifies the possibility of suffering heartaches or disappointments related to your health.

This vital element of your life might be going through a winter phase, symbolizing a period of dormancy or stagnancy.

Perhaps this is directly associated with your emotional stress or uncertainty that you are harbouring deep within.

The element water signifies fluidity, emotions, and intuition, suggesting you must listen to your internal instincts regarding your health and well-being.

It’s a reminder that, like the ebb and flow of the tide, your health should not be taken for granted but nurtured with compassion and mindfulness.

On the other hand, drawing the 7 of Diamonds cartomancy card in relation to health, indicates potential obstacles in the path of maintaining or enhancing your health status.

It might represent work-related strain that’s negatively affecting your health.

The card is associated with the Fall season, a time of slowing down and preparation for the coming winter.

This could imply that it’s time to slow down, reflect, and address the work issues that might be contributing to your poor health state.

The element associated with this card is Air, symbolizing thoughts, communication, and action.

In the context of health, it prompts you to take appropriate action and engage in productive communication with health experts or supportive networks to gain a victory over your health concerns.

This card is about rising from adversity and coming through the other side victorious.

Together, these cards suggest a period of difficulties followed by eventual triumph, if the right steps are taken.
