## What does Justice and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 7 of Wands tarot card together suggest that you must be prepared to deal with potential competition and defend yourself against any adversaries. This card combination encourages you to take a proactive stance and seek out win-win solutions that will benefit everyone involved. It also suggests that in the end, the truth will come out, and as such, it is best to stay honest and truthful in any conflict. Above all else, be sure to practice fairness so that any conflict is resolved in a mutually beneficial way, rather than resulting in a winner and loser.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Justice mean?

When considering the Tarot Card Justice, three key areas come to mind: health, finances, and relationships. In each of these contexts, the Justice card suggests a number of important things.

In terms of health, the Justice card indicates that one’s health and well-being will be determined by their own actions and decisions. Cause and effect will be at play, and it may take some time to identify the various correlations. Ultimately, though, the Justice card suggests that if one takes the necessary steps and makes wise decisions, they can attain and maintain good health.

In terms of finances, the Justice card suggests taking an honest and fair approach to earning and spending. Win-win solutions can be found through open and honest communication and a commitment to fairness. The Justice card suggests that there is no need to rush, and that financial decisions should be made thoughtfully and with patience, in order to avoid potential pitfalls.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the Justice card reinforces the message of fairness and honesty. Relationships should be based on trust and mutual understanding, with an emphasis on win-win solutions to any disagreements. The Justice card suggests that all interactions should be conducted from a place of understanding and true concern for the other party, and that the truth will always come out in the end.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands tarot card is a card that highlights the importance of self-defense, protecting against competition, and standing up for oneself. This card offers an important reminder that even in times of struggle, we must stay strong and maintain our self-confidence.

When looking at health, the 7 of Wands can suggest that it is time for an extra boost of self-care and protection. This could be in the form of extra mediation and yoga, making sure to pay attention to one’s body and not ignore any warning signs. It could also mean that it is a time to set boundaries, and to be more assertive in terms of one’s health and wellbeing.

In terms of finances, the 7 of Wands can suggest that there is a need to be more mindful of how money is handled and to come up with strategies to protect against any financial losses. This could mean creating an emergency fund, sticking to a budget, or coming up with strategies to get out of debt. It could also mean looking out for any deceptive or exploitative practices and doing research when making large investments.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 7 of Wands can suggest the need for the individual to defend their boundaries and values. This could be in the form of being more direct with communication, having honest conversations about expectations, or understanding and respecting one’s own limits. It could also mean being more mindful of the relationships in one’s life, and being more selective about who one allows to enter it.

Overall, the 7 of Wands tarot card is an important reminder to take care of oneself and to defend one’s boundaries, values, and resources. It encourages being strong and assertive, and to look for ways to protect oneself against any potential risks. When looking at health, finances, and relationships, this card can serve as a reminder to be mindful, to carry out the necessary precautions and to stay on guard when needed.
