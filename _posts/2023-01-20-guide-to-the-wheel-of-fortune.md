## The Wheel Of Fortune – An Overview

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card is a powerful symbol of hope, change, and luck. It signifies that a new cycle of luck and opportunity is about to start, and that fortune and success are all around us. It encourages us to take risks and remain open to whatever possibilities come our way, no matter how unlikely they may seem. The Wheel Of Fortune also suggests that the future is in our hands and that we have the power to shape our own destiny. It can represent the idea of taking a gamble, trusting in luck, and having faith that the universe will provide us with the resources we need. The Wheel Of Fortune is a reminder that, with the right attitude and effort, we can overcome any obstacles in our path and bring good luck and prosperity into our lives.

## Upright Meanings Of The Wheel Of Fortune

### What It Means For Me

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card is an indicator of great potential for good fortune and luck in my life. It suggests that I am on the brink of a turning point, where I can manifest incredible opportunities for growth and happiness. It also speaks to the luck and chance that I have in life and encourages me to take a risk and gamble on the unknown. With The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card, I am reminded that I have the power to shape my own destiny and create the life I want.

### What It Means For The Future

The Wheel of Fortune tarot card indicates that I am at a turning point in my life and that luck is on my side. It implies that I have the potential to attract good fortune and that I should take risks and gamble when the opportunity arises. It is a sign that although I may be facing some struggles in the present, I am on the brink of great success. This card is a reminder that I should remain hopeful and confident in my ability to bring about positive change. The Wheel of Fortune is an assurance that I will be rewarded for the effort and dedication I have put into achieving my goals.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card indicates that those close to me are about to experience a significant turning point in their lives. This card often symbolizes good fortune and luck, so it is likely that positive opportunities and experiences are coming their way. It is possible that this could include gambling, either figuratively or literally, so it is important to be aware of risks involved in such a venture. The Wheel Of Fortune is an encouraging card and suggests that those close to me should be open to all possibilities and use their instincts to make the best decisions for themselves.

## Reversed Meanings Of The Wheel Of Fortune

### What It Means For Me

When I think about the reversed Wheel of Fortune, it feels like I’m stuck in a rut. It could be that I am stuck in a situation that I can’t seem to get out of, or that I am feeling like I’m in a cycle of bad luck. It could also be that I am not taking the necessary risks or making decisions that could lead to new opportunities. The reversed Wheel of Fortune could be telling me that I need to make changes that will help me move forward and break away from the stagnation I am feeling.

I need to take a leap of faith and trust that the universe will support me in my endeavors. I need to take risks and be willing to accept that things might not work out the way I plan. The reversed Wheel of Fortune tarot card is a sign that you may be stuck in a rut and feeling as though you’re not making progress in life. It may be a sign that you are going through a period of stagnation and feeling as though you are not making any headway. When this card appears reversed, it is a warning to take action and be mindful of how you are spending your energy. You may be feeling helpless and as though your efforts are going unnoticed. When the reversed Wheel of Fortune card appears in a reading, it often indicates a negative turn of events, especially in regards to the lives of those close to me. The card may also suggest that they are stuck in a cycle of bad luck and can’t seem to break free.

This card could be a warning that I should be careful in my dealings with people close to me, as they could be facing a major setback or going through a difficult time.
