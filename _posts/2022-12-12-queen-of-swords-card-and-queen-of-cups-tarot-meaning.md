A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The Queen of Swords and Queen of Cups together can represent a balance of self-sufficiency and nurturing. The Queen of Swords encourages us to be self-sufficient, honest, and independent. She encourages us to be ambitious and stick to our values and beliefs. The Queen of Cups encourages us to be nurturing, supportive, and open-hearted. She encourages us to be kind and care for others and to ourself. Together these two Queens are suggesting that we strive for a balance between the two – being self-sufficient but also taking the time to nurture and love ourself and those around us.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Queen Of Swords and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Queen Of Swords and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card represents an individual who is in tune with their own physical and mental state. This person has the ability to identify potential health issues before they arise, and take appropriate measures to address them. They understand the importance of taking care of their own personal health and well-being, and take action to prevent illness and injury. They also have the ability to make informed decisions when it comes to their own health, and have the self-discipline to stick to those decisions.

Finances  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card denotes a person who is fiscally responsible and has a great sense of financial discipline. This individual is adept at creating a budget and managing their finances. They understand the importance of long-term financial planning and are able to assess the risks and rewards associated with financial decisions. The Queen of Swords is also known for taking a balanced approach when it comes to their finances, not over-investing in one particular area while disregarding others.

Relationships  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card signifies a person who is honest, direct and fair. This individual is likely to be self-sufficient and doesn’t need to rely on others for emotional or financial support. They have the strength and courage to be truthful with others and say what needs to be said. They demand reciprocity in relationships and expect intentions to be made clear. They are loyal but also know when it’s time to let go. They are not afraid to take the lead when necessary and communicate with others openly and respectfully.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card is symbolic of health and wellness. It represents a nurturing energy that can promote physical and mental stability. Meaning, an individual drawn to the Queen of Cups likely needs to take some time to relax and take care of themselves. This card symbolizes healing on all levels — physical, mental, emotional and spiritual — as well as being able to provide a supportive environment for others.

Finances:  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card is also associated with financial stability and abundance. Individuals with this card in their readings can use it as an affirmation that their finances are in order. The Queen of Cups is a sign of wealth, but not of overspending. This card suggests that one needs to take the time to assess their financial situation and make wise decisions when it comes to spending.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Cups Tarot card is an indication that one needs to nurture both themselves and their relationships. This card is a reminder to think about relationships with an open heart, and to be gentle with oneself and with others. One should strive to maintain an attitude of respect, understanding, and companionship in relationships, as well as being emotionally supportive to their partner. This card is also a reminder to seek balance in one’s relationships and to make sure that they are mutually beneficial.
