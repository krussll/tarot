A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Spades card and 3 Of Diamonds card together in particular.

The 3 Of Spades card and 3 Of Diamonds have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, pulling both the 3 of Spades and the 3 of Diamonds could indicate a tumultuous journey ahead, marked by emotional trials and disagreements.

The 3 of Spades firstly suggests forthcoming heartbreak or emotional discomfort, echoed in its association with the cold and introspective season of Winter and the deep, feeling element of Water.

This could point towards a relationship issue or intimate betrayal perhaps.

Meanwhile, the 3 of Diamonds reveals quarrels or conflicts, particularly intellectual or communicative difficulties illuminated by its connection to the windy season of Fall and the element Air.

It’s symbolic of strong disagreements perhaps at work or amongst friends.

However, interestingly both these cards are tied with the concept of faith.

Despite the difficulties implied, there’s also an encouragement to keep faith, suggesting that while the path may be rocky, there’s potential for enlightenment, growth, and reaffirmation of personal convictions following these trials.

The reality of the heartbreak and disputes could indeed be a test of faith, a chance to prove resilience and strength, and an opportunity to learn and emerge stronger.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, seeing both the 3 of Spades and the 3 of Diamonds together in regards to your love life can suggest a complex journey where both heartache and disputes are at play.

The 3 of Spades speaks of heartbreak, suggesting that you may experience emotional turmoil or a period of sadness, likely due to a relationship ending or going through rough waters.

This feeling is compounded by the card’s association with the cold, austere season of Winter and the deep, emotional element of Water, hinting at a time of introspection and emotional depth in your love life.

On the other hand, the 3 of Diamonds is associated with disputes, suggesting potential arguments, disagreements, and unresolved issues within your current or future relationships.

With an association with Fall, a season where things naturally come to an end, it suggests that these disputes may lead to significant changes such as the end of a relationship or a major shift in its dynamics.

Moreover, the element linked to this card is Air, indicative of communication, thought, and logic, implying that these disputes might stem from intellectual differences or communication break downs.

However, despite these challenges, the common theme of faith present in both cards infers that you should keep believing in love, and with patience and resilience, better days will come.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing the 3 of Spades card suggests a period of heartbreak or sorrow likely to affect your finances and job.

This could be represented in a variety of events such as getting fired, facing financial difficulties, or experiencing a significant setback in your career.

Since it is connected to the season of Winter, these challenges might arise during the cold months.

The Water element suggests depth and reflection, which could mean that these difficulties could lead to personal growth by teaching you important life lessons about overcoming hardship.

On the other hand, drawing the 3 of Diamonds right after, indicates conflicts or misunderstandings that might continue to affect your financial situation and job status.

However, as this card is associated with Faith, it also signifies that you might be able to resolve these disputes, often through communication or negotiation.

The association with Fall season might explain that it’s a transitory phase leading to a more prosperous period.

The element of Air symbolizes intelligence and flexibility, suggesting that you’ll need to think critically and adapt to changes to navigate through these issues.

Together, both cards suggest that despite facing difficulties and conflicts, maintaining faith and resilience will allow you to overcome these obstacles.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 3 Of Diamonds mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, when the 3 of Spades and the 3 of Diamonds appear together in the context of health, they signify a period of emotional and mental strain that can take a toll on your overall wellbeing.

The 3 of Spades, being linked to the essence of heartbreak and the winter season, suggests a period of emotional challenges or sadness that can lead to stress, affecting your cardiac health or immune system, often leading to colds or flus due to the winter connection.

This card encourages you to be strong in your faith, providing hope that this challenging phase will pass.

On the other hand, the 3 of Diamonds corresponds to disputes, implying a time of mental unrest or disagreements that might cause anxiety, stress or sleep-related issues, and its connection to fall could mean a drop in energy levels or mood fluctuations.

With its element being air, it might be related to issues such as respiratory problems.

However, the core theme of both these cards is faith, implying that despite these health challenges, maintaining a positive outlook and belief system is both beneficial and necessary.

It suggests that these periods of strife can be resolved or lessened with a strong belief in your strength and healing capacity.
