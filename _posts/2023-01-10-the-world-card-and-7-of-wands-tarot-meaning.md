A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and 7 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The World card and 7 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The World tarot card and The 7 Of Wands tarot card combined can mean that a goal or lesson is about to be completed. This could be a journey that you embark on, either physically or spiritually. It is also a sign of self-defense; protecting yourself from any competition you may face on this path. This could mean defending yourself against any outside forces that intend to interfere in the completion of your goal or lesson. It may also suggest that the endings that you face can be empowering and empowering experiences, allowing you to move forward with a strong sense of protection.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The World and 7 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The World tarot card and the 7 Of Wands tarot card when together in a love reading symbolize a relationship that is undergoing a period of completion. This can refer to the end of a relationship, the conclusion of a stage in a partnership, or the completion of a goal that the two of you have been striving for. This could also signify a time of travel or emigration, a period of goodbyes and farewells. Though the end of something may seem sad and difficult, it is also a time for fresh possibilities and the freedom that comes from completion.

The 7 Of Wands tarot card in this context also refers to protection and self-defense. It can represent standing up for yourself and your values, and being courageous and resilient in the face of competition or difficulty. This card can help you and your partner to stay strong and loyal to one another during this period of change, and to work together as a team to weather any storms that come your way.

The combination of The World tarot card and The 7 Of Wands tarot card can represent a time of growth and transformation in your relationship, with the potential for a beautiful new beginning. Though the ending of something can feel scary or uncertain, the combination of these two cards emphasizes that whatever changes may come your way, you and your partner have the courage and strength to face them together.

### What does The World and 7 Of Wands mean together for your finances?

When The World tarot card and The 7 Of Wands are combined, it suggests that the journey to achieving financial success and finding a job will be a challenging one. It signifies that you will have to be proactive in defending yourself and staying competitive, especially in the current employment market. You may have to take risks, such as moving to a new city or country in order to find the right job or further your career. The World and 7 Of Wands together emphasize the importance of determination and strength of will in order to overcome obstacles and succeed.

If you are looking to make a career move or change in your life, you must stay committed to your goal. The World tarot card suggests that you can expect ultimate success, although it might take a while to reach that “happy ending.” It also implies that you need to take a leap of faith and embrace the unknown in order to reach your goals. The 7 Of Wands provides the strength and perseverance necessary to overcome any obstacles that may arise during the journey.

Overall, The World tarot card and The 7 Of Wands signify the importance of staying strong, determined and willing to take risks in order to reach your financial and career goals. It is essential to have a vision of success and be willing to put in the effort to make it happen. The combination of these two tarot cards confirms that you will eventually reach your desired goals if you stay focused, brave and determined.

### What does The World and 7 Of Wands mean together for your health?

The combination of the two tarot cards, The World and The 7 Of Wands, represent a necessary shift in focus from your external environment to your own personal health. The World tarot card is an indication that a period of growth and expansion is coming to an end, and by completing this cycle, you have gained knowledge and learned valuable lessons. On a deeper level, this card can indicate emigration, or a need to push your boundaries and experience new aspects of life.

The 7 Of Wands tarot card emphasizes the importance of self-defense and protection. This card suggests that you need to be prepared to defend yourself against competition and any challenges that may arise during this time of change. This card encourages you to take steps to ensure your own safety, whether that means protecting your physical or emotional wellbeing.

When combined, the messages of The World and The 7 Of Wands tarot cards can be interpreted as an invitation to take a closer look at your health and well-being. This combination encourages you to take action and make changes in your lifestyle to ensure your safety, security, and overall health. This may include evaluating your current diet, exercise habits, and social support system to make sure it is up to par. Additionally, it may be a good idea to do a self-check to make sure you are getting enough rest, taking breaks when needed, and taking care of yourself emotionally.

Taking care of your health is a lifelong journey, and the messages from The World and The 7 Of Wands tarot cards is a reminder that you must always be observant and use caution in order to protect yourself and maintain your wellbeing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

Health

The World Tarot card is associated with good physical health, and generally indicates a period of mental, physical and emotional well-being. It also suggests that you are taking care of yourself and are in balance with your physical needs. This card can suggest that you are looking for ways to improve your physical health such as through exercise, diet, relaxation and meditation. It can also be a reminder to take time for yourself and ensure that you have good self-care practices in place.

Finances

The World Tarot card is strongly associated with financial stability and completion of financial goals. It suggests that you are in a good financial position, and have taken the necessary steps to ensure a secure financial future. This card can represent the completion of a major financial goal, such as purchasing a home, or it could signify that you have finally managed to pay off all of your debts. This card can also suggest that you are investing in your future, taking steps towards financial security, or just finally feeling satisfied with the amount of money you have in your bank account.

Relationships

The World Tarot card is associated with long-term relationships, and it generally indicates that your relationships have reached a point of completion. This could mean that a long-term relationship has finally borne fruit, or that you have found stability and satisfaction in a relationship. The World card may also represent a journey between two people, such as a period of travel together or a successful venture. This card can be an indication that you and your partner have reached a point of understanding and contentment in your relationship, which can lead to a stronger, lasting bond.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

The 7 of Wands Tarot card implies that one is being watched and must protect what one has. This card warns against trusting others easily, as one must be prepared to stand-up for oneself and protect one’s potential and current possessions, as well as one’s dreams.

For health, this card encourages one to be vigilant and alert in monitoring one’s physical and emotional health. It is important to take the time to care for oneself and be mindful of potential threats to one’s health. This card suggests that one must build a healthy defense system to protect oneself from sickness, emotional distress and physical damage.

For finances, this card indicates that one must be cautious when dealing with money. As the saying goes, “look before you leap”. Be mindful when making investments, signing contracts and taking out loans. The 7 of Wands Tarot card encourages one to do their research and protect their assets by being mindful of any risk they may take.

For relationships, this card suggests that one must be mindful when forming relationships. There are some people who are not to be trusted and should be avoided if possible. The 7 of Wands Tarot card emphasizes that one must be prepared to defend oneself, should a relationship become emotionally or physically abusive. It is important to build healthy relationships with people who respect and nurture one’s well-being.
