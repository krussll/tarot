However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The Justice card and 10 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 10 Of Wands mean together?

The combination of The Justice tarot card and The 10 of Wands can be a reminder to us that in order to create a win-win situation, we cannot take on too many burdens or we will become exhausted and stressed. This combination highlights the need for balance; we must take responsibility for our actions but also set boundaries to ensure our own well-being. If we overstrain ourselves we can’t ensure that the truth will come out and justice will be served. We must take the time to reassess our commitments while staying true to our values and keeping our eyes on the end goal.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 10 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 10 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

The Justice Tarot card reminds us that we are responsible for our own health and to take a holistic approach to self-care.

The Justice Tarot card encourages us to seek out the truth in any matter and to be willing to speak our truth when it is necessary.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a card that speaks to heavy burdens and a feeling of being overwhelmed and exhausted. When this card is drawn it can indicate a period of time in which one feels weighed down and exhausted due to too many responsibilities, obligations, or tasks. The burdens can be physical, psychological, emotional or spiritual in nature, but all of them add up to an overwhelming sense of being too bogged down with responsibilities.

In terms of health, The 10 of Wands Tarot card can often be drawn when one is dealing with long-term health issues, stress, or an inability to rest and recover in between tasks. When this card is drawn it can be a sign of an exhaustion of energy and resources, along with the need to make time for rest and recovery.

In terms of financial matters, The 10 of Wands Tarot card can indicate an inability to handle or keep up with all of one’s financial obligations and debts. It can mean that one is stretched too thin, feeling as if they are constantly trying to make ends meet, but never quite able to get back on top. It can also be a sign that one is taking on too many projects financially, without being able to focus on how best to spend their limited resources.

In terms of relationships, The 10 of Wands Tarot card can indicate an inability to focus on relationships or prioritize them due to too many burdens elsewhere. When this card is drawn it can be a sign that one’s attention is spread too thin between too many tasks and other responsibilities, leaving little energy for relationships and connection. It can be a sign that one needs to step back from the obligations in order to dedicate more time to the relationships in their life that need attention and focus.
