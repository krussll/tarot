A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean together?

You may be dealing with the prospect of a retirement or inheritance, but at the same time feel the pain of a heartbreak, divorce, or loss. You may feel the conflicting emotions of the joy of planning for your future and the sadness of what was lost. This could also indicate a change in your financial status, but financial changes can also lead to emotional struggles. In any case, you may be feeling overwhelmed and need to take some time to reconcile these issues.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 10 of Pentacles tarot card often symbolizes good health and long life. It can represent a period of stability and happiness, as well as a sense of security and peace. When it comes to good health, the 10 of Pentacles can indicate a time in which our physical and mental well-being is at its peak. We may have the resources to access nutrition, exercise, and medical care, and the energy and willpower to take full advantage of those resources. This card can mean that we feel secure and untouchable, safe from the worries of everyday life.

Finances:

The 10 of Pentacles tarot card also reflects financial security and abundance. It suggests that we have done a good job of organizing our finances, as well as taking care of our investments. The 10 of Pentacles often indicates that now is a great time to save up for the future, as our financial standing can become even stronger in the long run. It is also a sign of financial stability and the worries of financial insecurity can diminish. This card can also suggest making wise investments in order to ensure increased security in our future.

Relationships:

Finally, the 10 of Pentacles tarot card can represent strong relationships. It often symbolizes that our closest relationships are secure and meaningful, and we have done a good job at maintaining them. We may have the resources to nurture our interpersonal relationships, and the security to know that these relationships will last for many years to come. This card can also represent the legacy that we leave behind, not only in terms of finances but also in terms of the importance of our relationships.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 Of Swords Tarot card is a harrowing reminder of the pain and anguish that can occur in one’s life. It stands for emotions that feel like an open wound, and situations that can leave us feeling overwhelmed, vulnerable and lost. In terms of health, it often displays an indication of severe distress, physical pain or surgery. It can appear when the querent is struggling with depression, anxiety, or an illness that requires medical attention. Financially, the 3 Of Swords warns of a financial loss or burden, which could be caused by divorce, bankruptcy, or the termination of a business deal. Relationships likewise suffer under this card, as it is a signifier of strained ties, heartbreak, and sorrow. It can also be a signifier of infidelity, betrayal and the demise of a relationship.

The 3 of Swords brings with it a warning – to be prepared for adversity, and to take proactive steps to protect the fragile balance of one’s life. When this card appears in a spread, it can be a good indication to review the past and to acknowledge any potential struggles that may be present. Being mindful of one’s current lifestyle will be especially important, as this card also stands for a period of extreme stress. To support health, it is suggested to seek professional help if necessary, to exercise regularly and to nourish oneself with a well-balanced diet. Financially, it could be helpful to safeguard finances by researching legal advice and to build a financial safety net. In terms of relationships, the querent should look for ways to soothe the pain and heal the wounds.

The 3 Of Swords Tarot card is one of the worst cards to come up in a spread. It signals a period of darkness, sorrow and grief. But it can also be a sign of hope, in that it prompts the querent to work on improving their health, finances and relationships. Despite the challenges that this card may bring, it is an invitation to find the strength and courage within to persevere.
