## Justice – An Overview

The Justice tarot card is a symbol of cause and effect, win-win solutions, and the truth coming out. It is a card of balance, fairness, and impartiality. It encourages us to accept responsibility for our own actions, as ultimately we are the ones in control of our destiny. The Justice card implies that when we take the time to understand the true nature of our situation, we can come up with solutions that benefit everyone. It is a reminder that truth and justice are essential components of any successful relationship or endeavor. By recognizing our choices and taking responsibility for our actions, we can create a positive outcome for all involved.

## Upright Meanings Of Justice

### What It Means For Me

The Justice tarot card speaks to me of finding balance and harmony through understanding cause and effect. It tells me to strive for solutions that benefit all parties in a dispute, as opposed to solutions that only benefit one. It reminds me to be honest with myself and with others and to seek the truth in any situation. The Justice card also encourages me to be fair and just in my dealings with those around me, and to think of the greater good in any situation. Above all, it reminds me to think before I act and to remain level-headed and compassionate in all of my dealings.

### What It Means For The Future

The Justice tarot card is a reminder that cause and effect are in play in my life. This means that my actions will have consequences, both good and bad. It is a sign that I need to take responsibility for my actions and seek a win-win solution whenever possible. By taking the time to consider all sides of a situation, I will be able to make decisions that serve my best interests. The Justice card also suggests that the truth will ultimately come out, so it’s important for me to be honest and ethical in all my dealings. As I work to integrate this card’s message into my life, I can look forward to a future filled with justice and fairness.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Justice tarot card is a reminder that when we take the time to seek justice and fairness in our relationships with those close to us, we create a win-win solution in which everyone involved is respected and heard. The card suggests that when we strive for justice, the truth of our situation will be revealed, which can lead to a beneficial outcome. This card also encourages us to seek balance and fairness in our relationships, even when it’s difficult or time-consuming. This can mean having difficult conversations with loved ones and taking the time to listen to each person’s point of view. Ultimately, The Justice card suggests that when we take the time to seek justice, harmony and understanding can be achieved between ourselves and those close to us.

## Reversed Meanings Of Justice

### What It Means For Me

When the Justice card is reversed, it can indicate a lack of balance in the context of the situation. In my life, this means that there may be an imbalance between what I want and what I have, or between my values and my actions. This can lead to feelings of frustration and even resentment, as I struggle to reconcile my needs and goals with my current reality.

The reversed Justice tarot card can also suggest that my actions may be based on a false sense of justice and fairness, rather than a genuine attempt to create a win-win solution. This can manifest as a tendency to blame others for my problems, or to act out of anger rather than reason. In some cases, it can even lead to a feeling of entitlement and a refusal to accept any responsibility for my own mistakes.

Overall, the reversed Justice tarot card is a reminder to take a step back and assess my life from a more objective perspective. It encourages me to recognize my own accountability in any given situation, to be open to compromise, and to strive for win-win solutions for all parties involved.

### What It Means For The Future

When The Justice tarot card appears in its reversed position, it can signify that justice is not being served and that the truth may not come out. This can be an unsettling message and may hint that something is being kept from you. It can also point to a situation in which someone is not taking responsibility for their actions and that decisions are being made without considering the consequences.

The reversed Justice tarot card may be warning you to stay away from manipulative people, as they may be trying to gain the upper hand in a situation and are not looking for win-win solutions. It can also mean that you may be feeling the need to control or manipulate others to get what you want. It is a reminder to take responsibility for your own actions and to look for solutions that are beneficial for all parties. If you have been struggling with a situation, this card can be a sign that the truth will eventually come out, and justice will be served.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Justice tarot card appears, it may indicate that there is disharmony among those close to me, or that there is a disconnect between what I think is fair and what those around me think is fair. This could lead to conflict and arguments over matters of right and wrong, as each party insists that their point of view is the only one that matters. It could also mean that those close to me are not taking responsibility for their actions, and are instead expecting others to take the blame when things go wrong. This could be a sign that I need to step back and assess the situation objectively, and be honest with myself and those around me about what is really going on.

The reversed Justice tarot card also suggests that I should be wary of hidden agendas, as those close to me may not be acting in my best interests. This could lead to me being taken advantage of, or ending up in situations which are not beneficial in the long run. It is therefore important for me to be aware of any power dynamics that are at play, and to challenge them if needed.

Ultimately, the reversed Justice tarot card indicates that I should strive for a balance between fairness and understanding when it comes to those around me. If I can remain impartial, then I should be able to create a win-win situation for everyone involved. Ultimately, the truth will come out, and I should be prepared to face it head-on.
