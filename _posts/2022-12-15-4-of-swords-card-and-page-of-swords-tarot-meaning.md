## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card is a sign for those who need to take a step back and rest, to recover from physical, mental and emotional exhaustion. It’s a reminder to take a break from physical activities, relationships, and work and instead, focus on one’s own wellbeing.

When it comes to health, the card stands for a retreat from the world, in order to pause, rest and replenish oneself. This can mean taking a vacation, both physically and mentally, to give the mind and body a chance to reset and recover. During times when physical or mental health is suffering, the 4 of Swords Tarot card can provide the necessary reminder to take the time to physically and emotionally recharge in order to restore balance and harmony.

Finances

When it comes to finances, the 4 of Swords Tarot card can be a warning sign to step back and reassess one’s long-term financial goals. It’s a reminder to take a deep breath and to pause, rather than rushing into a potentially poorly thought out financial decision.

The card represents taking a step back and looking at the bigger picture. It encourages looking at one’s finances from a balanced perspective, and to find the best financial option that will serve one’s long-term goals. When it comes to financial decisions, the 4 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to use one’s head instead of their heart.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card can also be seen as a warning for those in a relationship, to take a step back and take time for themselves. It’s a reminder to nurture one’s own needs, and to not forget about taking care of oneself in the process of tending to a relationship.

When it comes to relationships, the 4 of Swords Tarot card encourages one to rest and to give themself space and time to recenter. It’s a reminder that, at times, the most beneficial thing one can do for their relationship is to take a break and recharge. And, upon returning, the relationship will be stronger and more balanced than before.

It can also be a warning that you need to be more aware of any negative gossip or manipulative behaviour coming your way.
