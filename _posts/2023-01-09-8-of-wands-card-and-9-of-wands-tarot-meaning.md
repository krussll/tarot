The 8 Of Wands tarot card and The 9 Of Wands tarot card represent the need for quick action and action taken to maintain boundaries. The 8 Of Wands encourages you to take quick and decisive action, as any hesitancy can diminish the potential and results of your endeavors. The 9 Of Wands encourages you to persist and stay the course, to not give up when the going gets tough. This card speaks to the importance of having firm boundaries and standing your ground in the face of adversity. Together, these two cards suggest that you need to be quick and decisive, but also maintain and protect your boundaries while persisting to meet your goals.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 8 Of Wands Tarot card is a powerful sign when it comes to health. It encourages action and quick change in order to restore balance. It is a reminder that the time has come to take steps to improve your health, whether that means improving your diet and exercising more or consulting with a medical professional. While action may be required, it will most likely be brief, leading to swift and positive results.

Finances

When it comes to finances, The 8 Of Wands Tarot card is also a sign of great potential. It signifies that news is on its way, and it could mean great news related to finances. Whether you have been waiting on a loan approval, a job offer or an unexpected windfall, The 8 Of Wands Tarot card encourages you to stay positive and keep your eye out for good news. It may even mean that the time has come to explore new opportunities and take risks that could lead to financial gain.

Relationships

The 8 Of Wands Tarot card can also signify positive changes in relationships. It is a reminder that change is good and can often bring two people closer together. It encourages swift action, such as taking the time to talk and address any issues that are causing strain in the relationship. It can also be a sign that news or events related to the relationship are on their way, be it a positive development or an unexpected surprise. It is a reminder to stay vigilant and remain open to new possibilities in order to maximize the potential of the relationship.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The 9 of Wands Tarot card is all about keeping your boundaries, persevering, and standing your ground. In the context of health, it speaks to the importance of staying strong in the face of adversity and maintaining your boundaries when it comes to physical, mental, and emotional well-being. It means taking care of yourself, setting boundaries and limits, and having the strength and courage to say ‘no’ when needed. It also means being aware of your physical and emotional limits and knowing when to ask for help or take a break.

When it comes to finances, The 9 of Wands Tarot card encourages you to remain vigilant and be wise in your financial decisions. It highlights the importance of setting boundaries and having a plan in place to ensure you don’t overextend yourself or take on too much risk. You should also take the time to research and understand the financial products you’re investing in, and don’t be afraid to ask for help.

In terms of relationships, The 9 of Wands Tarot card speaks to staying true to yourself and your beliefs, and standing firm in your convictions. It stresses the importance of being self-aware, setting boundaries and keeping yourself healthy. It also encourages you to be honest and open with your partner, and to be mindful of how their actions and words affect you. Lastly, it highlights the need for communication and compromise in any relationship.
