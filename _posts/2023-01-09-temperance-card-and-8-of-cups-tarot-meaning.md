Balance and harmony should be achieved by creating an environment of restraint, which will allow you to figure out the best path forward. At times this may mean that a retreat or abandonment of a certain aspect of life is necessary in order to ultimately make progress. By taking a step back to reflect, you should be able to clearly determine what should be retained and what should be abandoned in order to create the best possible outcome.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card indicates a need for balance in all aspects of life; including health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, the Temperance Tarot card encourages one to practice moderation and to stay balanced. This means eating a variety of healthy foods, exercising in an appropriate way and getting enough rest. Finding a balance between hectic life demands and relaxation is key for good health. It is important to take care of yourself and to choose activities that make you feel good.

When it comes to finances, the Temperance Tarot card reminds us to stay in control of our spending and use resources wisely. We should be mindful of our needs and wants, and to avoid overspending. Instead, we should focus on saving money and investing wisely.

When it comes to relationships, the Temperance Tarot card encourages us to be patient and understanding. Living with someone can be challenging, so it is important to be willing to compromise. By practicing patience, we can find harmony and work together to resolve conflicts. Self-control is essential to maintaining a healthy relationship. It is important to manage our emotions and express ourselves with respect.

This card can signify the need to step away from certain activities or habits that are no longer beneficial, such as overworking, indulging in unhealthy practices, or over-exercising. The 8 of Cups encourages individuals to take a break and focus on self-care, such as engaging in yoga, meditation, or other relaxation techniques. It can also suggest that one take a break from stressful or difficult situations in order to restore their wellbeing.

This card can indicate that now is the time to rethink one’s financial situation and make necessary modifications or adjustments. It can be a sign to take risks in regards to investments or business ideas, but to also be mindful of potential risks and losses. This card can remind individuals to practice self-discipline and moderation when it comes to their spending habits in order to maintain financial stability.

Relationships: The 8 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a need to end certain relationships or move on from certain friendships if they are no longer beneficial or healthy. This can be a reminder to put oneself first and to focus on one’s own wellbeing. It can also suggest that one take a break from toxic relationships or environments that are creating more stress and problems rather than solutions. Additionally, this card can represent the need to look inward and take some much-needed time to recharge and reflect on one’s own emotions and needs.
