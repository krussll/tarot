A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the King Of Wands card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The King Of Wands card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does King Of Wands and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The unite of The King Of Wands and The 8 Of Swords tarot cards signify that while you may have a bold, charismatic leader in the forefront of your life, they may be facing self-imposed restrictions or even imprisonment that restricts their power. This could be a real-life situation of a leader facing an unjust trial, or it could represent an internal struggle of giving up personal power and freedom. Either way, it reminds you that even the strongest of leaders face challenges, restrictions, and hardships, and that you must use patience, sympathy, and understanding to overcome them.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does King Of Wands and 8 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When it comes to love, the combination of The King of Wands and The 8 of Swords Tarot Cards indicates a difficult, yet potentially powerful, relationship.

The King of Wands indicates a bold leader, overflowing with charisma and passion. His energy is intense and can be quite persuasive when it comes to matters of the heart. This is definitely a card that represents the “romantic type”, someone who will go all out to win their beloved’s heart.

On the other hand, The 8 of Swords shows that there are restrictions or boundaries set in place within the relationship. It could signify a fear of commitment or a sense of imprisonment in a relationship. There could be a feeling of being stuck, unable to break away from the relationship even though it might be stifling and wanting to move on.

The combination of these two cards denotes the potential for a passionate yet restrained relationship. The intense and powerful energy of the King of Wands can be an exciting and alluring prospect, yet the 8 of Swords warns that these feelings are not to be taken lightly. It could be that the commitment and intensity required to take the relationship to the next level is too steep a price to pay, or that it is simply not attainable given the circumstances.

The combination of The King of Wands and The 8 of Swords in your love life could suggest that a relationship could run smoothly if the boundaries are appropriately set and respected. If both parties are willing to be honest and communicate openly, there may be the possibility of intense passion and commitment, but to move the relationship forward, both will have to trust each other and be patient until the right time.

That being said, the King of Wands is the perfect card to counter this sense of restriction and powerless, as it suggests a bold, confident leader with a great level of charm and the courage to pursue their dreams and reach their goals.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

Health: The King of Wands Tarot card symbolizes health and wellbeing. It encourages a proactive attitude in order to maintain your health and energy. It indicates that taking the necessary measures to stay in good shape and health is essential. It proposes that leading a healthy lifestyle by eating properly, exercising, and having enough rest will bring balance and strength to body and mind.

Finances: The King of Wands Tarot card indicates that the focus should be on the long-term financial goals. It encourages taking initiatives, being resourceful and being creative with handling money matters. It suggests being careful and organized in order to make sound financial decisions for the future. It advises investing in tangible assets such as real estate and other forms of investments in order to secure your finances.

Relationships: The King of Wands Tarot card symbolizes passion, strength and courage. It suggests that in relationships you should be direct, honest and have a clear vision. It encourages taking initiative in order to move the relationship forward in the direction of your goals. It stands for courage and bravery in facing and overcoming any challenges that may arise. It proposes being patient and tolerant and understanding the needs and opinions of your partner in order to achieve harmony.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

The Eight of Swords tarot card can represent difficulty in various areas of life, including health, finances and relationships. Based on its symbolism, it suggests that a person may be intentionally limiting themselves in regards to any of those topics.

For health, the Eight of Swords tarot card can indicate that a person may be placing self-imposed restrictions and limitations on their physical or mental health. They may be aware of these restrictions and yet feel unable to break free and make positive, healthier choices. It could be that fear and feelings of helplessness are holding them back, or it may be a result of depressive or anxious thoughts.

When it comes to finances, the Eight of Swords suggests that a person could be adjusting their budget and finances in order to make it more restrictive. This could be in an effort to save for a future goal, or it could be out of fear that spending too much could lead to a financial crisis. The individual may be trying to stay within a certain budget and may be feeling chained to it.

In terms of relationships, the Eight of Swords can indicate that a person may be placing boundaries around themselves in order to feel safe. They may be withdrawing emotionally or physically, creating a space where past hurt is unlikely to be encountered again. This card can also indicate that a person may be having difficulty communicating their needs to their partner, leading to a lack of connection or intimacy.

The Eight of Swords tarot card can be a warning to its recipient to recognize any self-imposed restrictions they may have created and work to break free of them. Becoming mindful of these restrictions can be the first step in finding the freedom to choose health, financial freedom, and strong relationships.
