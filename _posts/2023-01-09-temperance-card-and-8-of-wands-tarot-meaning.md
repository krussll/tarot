A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and 8 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Temperance card and 8 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and 8 Of Wands mean together?

The Temperance tarot card and The 8 Of Wands tarot card together represent a balance of taking action quickly and also maintaining balance while doing so. It encourages one to stay focused on the task at hand with patience and restraint while also taking fast, decisive action. It speaks to the importance of finding a balance between action and thoughtfulness, allowing one to make progress and progress quickly while still staying in control. The duo of these cards suggest that there can be both swiftness and harmony in any situation, and that the two can be achieved simultaneously.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health

The Temperance Tarot card is representative of balance and moderation. Achieving the right balance of exercise, nutrition, rest, and relaxation is vital for good health. When it comes to exercise, you should be doing just enough to strengthen and maintain your physical health, but not overdo it. Balancing work with leisure time is important as well. Too much work can lead to burn out, while too little can result in boredom. You should also strive for balance in your nutrition, eating enough to provide your body with the fuel it needs to function, but not too much. Sleep is important too. Make sure to get enough rest so that your body has time to recover. Finally, relax and enjoy life. Don’t become so focused on your goals that you forget to enjoy the moment.

Finances

The Temperance Tarot card encourages balance in finances as well. You should spend money on what you need, not just on what you want. Figure out what is really important to you and make sure you budget enough to cover those things. Additionally, practice moderation when it comes to spending. Don’t try to keep up with the latest trends or buy the most expensive items. It’s also important to save money for a rainy day. Don’t live paycheck to paycheck and be sure to have an emergency fund. Finally, patience is key. Don’t rush into any financial decisions, take the time to think things through and make the right decisions for you.

Relationships

The Temperance Tarot card can also be used to encourage balance and moderation in relationships. Being in a healthy relationship requires both partners to give and take. Don’t be too selfish and always remember to listen to the other person. Likewise, don’t expect too much from the other person. Respect each other’s boundaries and remember to be patient when dealing with disagreements. Take the time to really listen to each other and understand each other’s needs and desires. Finally, don’t forget to take time for yourself and your own hobbies and interests. Having your own interests will give you a break from the relationship so that you can come back with fresh eyes.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

For health, the 8 of Wands Tarot card can be a reminder that health comes in many forms, not just physical. It is important to remember to make time for mental and emotional health as well. The 8 of wands can be a sign that it is time to take an active role in your own health and make sure you are taking the time to focus on your overall well-being. This could mean making time for a daily meditation practice, signing up for a new exercise routine, or seeing a therapist to work through any lingering issues.

For finances, the 8 of Wands Tarot card can be a reminder that any financial changes that you are planning to make should be done with thought and care. While it is easy to make sudden decisions when it comes to money, the 8 of wands encourages us to take a step back, evaluate our situation, and make changes that are thoughtful and intentional. This could mean setting up a budget plan, talking to a financial advisor, or investing in the stock market. Whatever you decide to do, make sure it is a decision you are making based on thorough research and a wise analysis of your finances.

For relationships, the 8 of Wands Tarot card can be a reminder that any connections we have with others require effort and intention. While it can be easy to get complacent or stuck in routines, this card encourages us to make sure that we are investing time and energy into our relationships with others. This could mean planning regular dates with your significant other, making time for regular conversation with friends, or taking the time to learn more about yourself and your relationships. Whatever it may be, make sure to take the time to foster the relationships in your life.
