However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The Five of Pentacles Tarot card signifies a time of difficulty, hardship, and loss. On the physical level, the card may indicate ill health and financial strife. It can indicate a time of physical suffering and poverty due to illness or injury. It also suggests worry, isolation and a lack of resources, such as a lack of money or social support.

When it comes to finances, the Five of Pentacles can suggest a period of financial difficulty or loss. It may be an unexpected expense that causes financial strain, or a period of unemployment or underemployment. This card can also suggest resilience, however, and the need to find creative solutions to solve financial woes.

Relationships may also suffer when this card appears. The Five of Pentacles can indicate isolation or loneliness, as well as worry and fear. In relationships, it suggests a lack of stability as well as a need for outside resources. It can signify feelings of inadequacy and a fear of abandonment. In times of trouble, such as poverty and financial difficulty, it can suggest that trust must be re-built and boundaries re-established.

In conclusion, the Five of Pentacles Tarot card signifies a time of difficulty and struggle. This card can represent physical and financial hardship as well as distress in relationships. It suggests the need for resilience and creative problem-solving to find a way out of hardship. Most of all, the Five of Pentacles Tarot card reminds us to reach out for support when we need it.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of the power of giving and receiving help. It is a card of generosity and understanding when it comes to health, finances and relationships. This card can be seen as a reminder to be generous with our health, finances and relationships, as well as to not be too possessive or expect everything to be on our own terms.

When it comes to health, The 6 of Pentacles can be seen as an indication that a generous attitude towards our own health is necessary. Instead of expecting our bodies to do what we want them to do, we should be open to receiving help in terms of seeking medical advice, getting adequate rest and taking care of ourselves on a holistic level. It is also a reminder that giving to others in terms of offering advice, support and even financial assistance can be beneficial to all parties.

When it comes to finances, The 6 of Pentacles is a reminder that generosity can go a long way. It is better to be open to helping others who are in need and to be willing to share our resources with those in need instead of expecting something in return. It is also a reminder to be generous with ourselves financially and to invest in something that will benefit us instead of just our pockets.

In terms of relationships, The 6 of Pentacles is an indication to be generous with our time and energy. It is important to be willing to give to another person as well as to receive help if it is needed. It is also important to remember that a healthy balance between giving and receiving is essential in any relationship.

The 6 of Pentacles is a card of balance, understanding and generosity. It is a reminder to be generous with our health, finances and relationships in order to promote harmony and to truly benefit all parties.
