You may need to take drastic action or undergo a process of surgery to get through this difficult period, but the clarity will eventually lead to a successful outcome.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of stress, exhaustion, and too much to handle. It is often a sign that one is carrying too much emotional or physical burden, or simply too many obligations. In health, this card can signify a state of mental and physical exhaustion, as well as feelings of overwhelm. It can be a sign of insomnia, chronic pain, burnout, and other health problems. In terms of finances, it is often a sign of financial burdens, such as debt or other expenses that one can’t afford. It can also symbolize a lack of financial resources or a feeling of being stuck in one’s financial situation.

In relationships, this card can suggest stress, exhaustion, and an imbalance of power. It can indicate that one partner is taking on too much responsibility or feeling weighed down by the relationship, which can lead to resentment and conflict. It can also signify an unbalanced relationship with one person dominating or controlling the other. In either case, The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a sign that the relationship is not in a healthy or balanced state, and requires attention and change.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

When it comes to one’s health, The Ace of Swords Tarot card indicates that it is a time of healing and clarity. The card can represent surgeries and treatments that are intended to bring about a complete recovery, as well as mental changes that will bring about greater wellness. In terms of physical health, the card can represent challenging medical conditions that require the person to undergo treatments, as well as the ability to make the difficult choices that will bring about improved quality of life.

As for finances, The Ace of Swords Tarot card can signify the need for a new approach to one’s finances. It can be a warning that one’s current strategies are no longer viable, or that a fresh, innovative approach is needed to improve one’s financial situation. Prudent budgeting and financial planning, as well as an eye to the future, are all advised. This card can also represent a new job or opportunity that may bring greater financial stability, as well as the ability to make better financial decisions.

In terms of relationships, The Ace of Swords Tarot card can indicate a time of clear thinking and decisive action. It may be a time for a new beginning, a clean slate and a fresh start, as this card can represent the end of unhealthy or toxic relationships. It can also represent the ability to make difficult decisions, such as the decision to break away from a codependent relationship. Additionally, this card may signify a strong bond of mutual respect and understanding in an existing relationship.
