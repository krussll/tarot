A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The Ace Of Swords card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Ace Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The Ace of Swords and the Queen of Swords tarot cards together can be interpreted as a sign of entering into an important period of thought and evaluation. With the Ace of Swords card, you are being encouraged to embrace new ideas and to be open to new ways of thinking in order to resolve any conflicts you may be facing. It is also a sign to take a more proactive approach in making decisions and taking action, as you will undoubtedly be called to do. Meanwhile, the Queen of Swords card speaks to a self-sufficient and honest attitude you should adopt. With her strong will and independent spirit, you can rely on her to offer stability and dependability as you come to important decisions and undertake difficult tasks. Together, these two cards symbolize the necessity of being conscious and aware of your thoughts, feelings and actions in order to move forward with confidence and clarity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Ace Of Swords and Queen Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health

The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages self-care and a healthy lifestyle. It suggests that you take responsibility for your mental and physical health and know your limits. It also encourages you to be mindful of your energy levels and prioritize rest and relaxation. Balance is key – don’t overwork yourself, but don’t become too comfortable in a stagnant lifestyle either. Practice self-discipline, mindfulness and self-care to stay healthy and grounded.

Finances

The Queen of Swords Tarot card speaks of financial responsibility. She urges you to be mindful of your spending and prioritize savings. She encourages you to be honest with yourself and your money goals, and have a mindful attitude when it comes to budgeting. Set realistic financial goals and take control of your money. Remain strict but be generous to yourself, ensuring that you are not scrimping too much.

Relationships

The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages honest and trusting relationships. She suggests that we be mindful of our words and actions when it comes to our relationships, encouraging us to practice total honesty and open communication. She also encourages us to be self-sufficient and independent, and not rely too much on others for emotional support. Ultimately, she encourages us to be honest and trusting, which will lead to healthier, more balanced relationships.
