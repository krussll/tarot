A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and Jack Of Diamonds card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and Jack Of Diamonds have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and Jack Of Diamonds mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, seeing the 7 of Spades card alongside the Jack of Diamonds card may raise some warning signs.

The 7 of Spades, symbolizing deceit and victory associated with the cold, reflective nature of the Winter and the deep emotional and subconscious realm of Water, strongly suggests a possible treacherous circumstance or individual in your surrounding.

Moreover, when placed next to the Jack of Diamonds, known as a dishonest person, associated with the perceivably unstable season of Fall and the intellectual and communicative element Air, it heightens the indications of deceit, potentially implying that someone might be misleading you with cunning words and thoughts.

In simple terms, the combination of these two cards cautions to be wary of people around you, especially those who appear convincing and articulate, as they may have concealed intentions.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Spades and Jack Of Diamonds mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, the presence of the 7 of Spades card regarding your love life insinuates the presence of deceit or disloyalty, a signal that something may not be as it appears.

Yet, with its association with victory, it brings about the possibility of overcoming these challenges, hinting at a struggle that will ultimately be won.

Its association with the element Water points to a deeply emotional time, while its connection with Winter signifies a period of introspection and inner turmoil, paralleling the stark realities and inherent quietness this season is known for.

The inclusion of the Jack Of Diamonds further compounds on the theme of dishonesty.

Symbolising a dishonest person, it may be indicating a current or future person in your love life that may not have honest intentions.

The card’s association with the element of Air warns of a disconnect between your thoughts and your emotions, suggesting you to step back and take a thoughtful, objective view of your situation.

Its link with Fall shows this is a time of change and transformation, much like the season which represents releasing the old to make way for the new.

Together, these two cards indicate a turbulent phase in your love life full of challenges but overcoming them can lead to triumph and personal growth.

### What does 7 Of Spades and Jack Of Diamonds mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, when you draw the 7 Of Spades and the Jack Of Diamonds, it warns you to be extra cautious in your financial decisions and at your job.

The 7 Of Spades represents deceit, so related to your finances, it could indicate that someone is not being entirely honest about their financial dealings with you.

You might have been presented with an investment that seems too good to be true or an offer that appears lucrative but is not transparent.

For your job, this card suggests that you may be enticed with false promises or someone around you might not be as honest as they seem.

Meanwhile, drawing the Jack Of Diamonds could represent a dishonest person that might be particularly manipulative in your professional or financial sphere.

The connection with the element Air can represent thoughts, ideas, and communication, suggesting that you may need to pay close attention to what is communicated to you.

In the context of your job, it could indicate a colleague or boss who may not have your best interests at heart.

Concerning your finances, it could indicate that any new investments or big purchases should be made with great care and thorough research.

As both cards are warning against deceit and dishonest people, it would be wise to use clarity, reason, and intuition when dealing with anything connected to money and your job.

### What does 7 Of Spades and Jack Of Diamonds mean together for your health?

Drawing the 7 of Spades card during a health-related cartomancy reading could suggest that you are not being entirely honest about your current health situation.

Perhaps you are ignoring symptoms or suppressing feelings of illness in order to maintain an illusion of well-being.

This card is urging you to acknowledge the truth and seek necessary medical help.

The victory aspect of the card could denote that you’ll overcome this deceit and eventually achieve better health.

Since it is associated with winter and water, it may indicate that you need to focus more on hydration and keeping warm to ensure wellness.

On the other hand, the Jack of Diamonds card, when associated with health, could signify a person in your life that may not have your best interests at heart regarding your well-being.

It could be a false friend, a manipulative family member or a dishonest medical professional.

As this card is associated with fall and the element of air, it represents change and communication.

Therefore, it may suggest that a change needs to occur in how you communicate your health issues, or perhaps you need to consider changing who you trust with your health concerns.

Pay attention to these possibilities, and remember to take care of your health, not just physically but emotionally and mentally as well.
