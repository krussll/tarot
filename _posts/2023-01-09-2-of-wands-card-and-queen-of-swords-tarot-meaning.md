A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean together?

The 2 Of Wands tarot card and The Queen of Swords tarot card suggest that you are in a period of contemplation. You have been waiting for the results of something, and now you have all the information and the power to make an informed choice. You have the opportunity to travel, but you need to use your self-sufficiency and honesty to decide the best way forward. The Queen of Swords encourages you to be honest with yourself, and to recognize your own potential. The 2 Of Wands reminds you that although it can be difficult to make a decision, it is important to trust yourself and go with your proverbial gut.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 2 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The combination of the Two of Wands and the Queen of Swords tells us that you are likely weighing your options and considering the pros and cons of a situation in your love life. It could be a decision to take a relationship to a deeper level, to move in with one another, to travel together, or some other commitment. It does not necessarily indicate that you are indecisive, but rather that you are recognizing the importance of making the right decision in this situation.

This combination warns you to consider the potential consequences of making the wrong decision, and to make sure that you are being honest with yourself and with your partner. The Queen of Swords reminds you to be honest and direct when navigating your current problem, instead of masking your true feelings. Unpleasant as it may be, it is important to be honest and direct so that you know exactly where you stand.

The Two of Wands also suggests that you may feel the need to step away from the situation before making a decision. It could be time for a short trip to gain a different perspective, or to think more objectively about your relationship and how it is progressing. This could be a welcome opportunity for gaining clarity and subsequently being able to make the best decision for yourself and your partner.

Ultimately, this combination of cards encourages a balanced approach to navigating any decision you may be considering. It is important to be honest with yourself and your partner, to weigh both the positive and negative aspects of the situation, and to give yourself the opportunity for some space if you feel the need for it.

The 2 of Wands and the Queen of Swords is a strong combination when it comes to finances and job. Together, these cards represent the need to evaluate where you are, make a decision, and take action.

The 2 of Wands tarot card symbolizes the anticipation and anxiety of waiting for results. This card is a reminder to make a choice before things can progress. You need to clearly define what you want before taking the necessary steps to get you there. It also suggests that a journey, either physical or metaphorical, is needed in order to reach your desired outcome.

The Queen of Swords tarot card is a symbol of a strong, selfsufficient individual. This card is a reminder to always remain honest in all your pursuits. Never sacrifice your values for the benefit of others. Being open, honest and assertive will take you far.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Wands and Queen Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

She encourages us to take a holistic approach to our health and to pay attention to our physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing. Additionally, she reminds us to be truthful with ourselves and to be honest about our health needs. She inspires us to take charge of our health, to take a proactive stance and to listen to our bodies.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages honesty and practicality when it comes to finances. It symbolizes the power of total transparency and good decision-making. She encourages us to make well-informed choices about our money and to be aware of how our financial decisions might affect our futures. She helps us get to the heart of our financial issues and can help us find creative solutions to our money problems.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that relationships are based on truth and honest communication. This card encourages us to engage in real conversations with our partners and to be open and honest with each other. It encourages us to be direct about our needs and wants and to be open to compromising and negotiating. Ultimately, the Queen of Swords helps us create strong and healthy relationships.
