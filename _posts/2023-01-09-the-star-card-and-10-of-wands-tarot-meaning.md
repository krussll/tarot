A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Star card and 10 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Star card and 10 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Star and 10 Of Wands mean together?

The combination of The Star tarot card and The 10 Of Wands tarot card speaks to a situation where things may feel daunting and overwhelming right now, but have faith that renewal and hope are on the horizon. It’s time for you to take a step back and assess the burdens that you’re carrying before taking on any new ones. This is your time to practice self-care and inner clarity, allowing the light of hope to guide you through your current struggles, even in the midst of exhaustion. Through faith and resilience, you will be able to shed light on the situation, bringing about miraculous change, and allowing you to stand in the spotlight.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Star and 10 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card is a card of renewal and hope; it symbolizes a time of spiritual clarity and inner strength. In terms of health, the Star Tarot card can represent a new path to healing and wellness, whether it is physical, mental, or emotional. It encourages us to have faith in ourselves, to believe that deep healing is possible, and to take the necessary steps to make it happen. The Star Tarot card also encourages us to embrace the miracles of everyday life, and to find the silver lining in any situation.

In terms of finances, the Star Tarot card indicates that when we take the time to review our current financial situation, and make wise choices, we can be sure of positive outcomes for the future. It reminds us that money is not a measure of success, but rather our attitude and relationship to money can lead to greater abundance and security in life.

When it comes to relationships, the Star Tarot card can be a reminder that we are capable of achieving new heights of connection and understanding with those around us. It reminds us to stay true to ourselves, to embrace the beauty of our differences, and to stay open to the possibilities that come with each new moment. Finally, the Star Tarot card encourages us to step into the spotlight and share our true selves with the world; to share the inner light that lies within us, so that it can be seen and appreciated by others.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands tarot card often represents feeling stressed and overburdened, with too many responsibilities on your plate. It can be a warning sign that you are taking on more than you can handle, and it’s important to recognize when you need help.

When it comes to health, the 10 of Wands tarot card can be a warning sign of burnout. You may need to step back and take a break from your over-scheduled life and make time to take proper care of yourself. Consider saying no to some of your obligations, as it could make a big difference in your health and overall wellbeing.

Financially, the 10 of Wands tarot card can represent being weighed down by debt. You may be struggling to make ends meet or to juggle multiple financial obligations. It’s important to take a hard look at your finances and come up with a plan to get out of debt and develop a budget that works for you.

In relationships, the 10 of Wands tarot card can signify feeling overwhelmed by people in your life. You may be feeling taken advantage of or resentful to those closest to you. Take a step back and assess your relationships to determine if any changes need to be made. Remember, maintaining healthy relationships is important to your mental and emotional wellbeing.

The 10 of Wands tarot card is a reminder to take a step back and assess the areas of your life that are causing you the most stress. Recognizing when you need to take a break or make changes is key to staying on top of your health, finances, and relationships.
