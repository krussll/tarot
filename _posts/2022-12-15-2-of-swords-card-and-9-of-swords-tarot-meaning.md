## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 9 of Swords tarot card indicates that the querent is beset with the negative effects of nightmares, anxiety and depression, which can greatly impair the physical, mental, and emotional health of the individual. Nightmares can cause restlessness and insomnia, coupled with feelings of exhaustion and fatigue. Anxiety can manifest itself in physical symptoms such as increased heart rate and difficulty breathing, as well as feelings of tension, worry and dread. Depression can lead to decreased energy and concentration, feelings of worthlessness, difficulty sleeping and a general loss of interest in activities and people. All of these can lead to physical sickness and decreases in overall health.

Finances:  
The 9 of Swords tarot card can symbolize financial hardship, especially when the querent is under the influence of depression or anxiety. Lack of motivation and energy can result in missed opportunities and delays in payments, while fear and worry can lead to impulsive financial decisions. This card can also be associated with debt, and as such, the individual should be very mindful of their choices to ensure that they do not become too entrenched in debt.

Relationships:  
The 9 of Swords tarot card can symbolize a difficulty in forming and maintaining relationships due to the effects of nightmares, anxiety, and depression. These conditions can lead to avoidance of social situations, fear of intimacy, low self-esteem, and other issues that can make it difficult to form and maintain relationships. The individual should seek treatment and therapy to help address these issues and learn how to build and maintain healthy relationships.
