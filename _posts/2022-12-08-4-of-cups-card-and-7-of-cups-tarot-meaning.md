## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can indicate a feeling of apathy towards your health. This could mean anything from feeling unmotivated to exercise, eat healthy, and take care of yourself, to feeling like no matter what you do, you’ll never be healthy. It could also indicate a feeling of surprise when it comes to health – perhaps a new diagnosis that you weren’t expecting or a new treatment option that you weren’t aware of. Alternatively, you could be feeling a sense of relief that a health issue has been resolved.

Finances:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can represent stagnation, both in terms of your current financial situation and in terms of your potential for financial growth. This can mean feeling stuck in a job that doesn’t pay enough or feeling like you are maxed out on credit cards and unable to save. It can also indicate surprise financial gifts – maybe a bonus from your employer or a lucky windfall from an unlikely source.

Relationships:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can represent feeling unfulfilled when it comes to relationships. Perhaps you’re feeling like your current relationships don’t quite meet your expectations or that you’re not able to be your true self around those closest to you. Alternatively, you could be feeling a sense of surprise when it comes to relationships – perhaps a new romance or an unexpected friendship. It could also indicate a feeling of relief when a difficult relationship has been resolved. Whatever the case, the 4 of Cups can indicate that it is time to take stock of your relationships and do some soul-searching.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health

The Seven of Cups tarot card often symbolizes a person who has too many choices and is unable to decide which is the best option for them. This can lead to an individual feeling stuck in their life, unable to make healthy decisions for their health and wellbeing. When health is concerned, this card can suggest unhealthy behaviors such as overindulging in food, alcohol and drugs, or performing risk-taking activities. It can also indicate a tendency to ignore warning signs from their body or to procrastinate making medical appointments.

Finances

The Seven of Cups tarot card pertains to decision-making in other aspects of life as well, including finances. People may find that they are indecisive when it comes to long-term financial planning and investment decisions, leading to them possibly missing out on important opportunities for growth and financial stability. They may also be unable to prioritize their spending, ending up overspending and living beyond their means.

Relationships

In relationships, the Seven of Cups tarot card suggests that an individual may be struggling to commit to a partner or a relationship in general. This can be seen as a sign that they are not ready for a deeper connection, or that they may be afraid of the responsibilities and expectations that come with it. Alternatively, overindulgence in pleasure-seeking and short-term flings can also be connected to this card, which would not lead to building a strong and lasting relationship.
