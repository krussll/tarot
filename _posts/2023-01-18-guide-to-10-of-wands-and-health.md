Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The 10 Of Wands card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 10 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 10 Of Wands.

## 10 Of Wands And Health – An Overview

The 10 of Wands is a card that symbolizes stress and exhaustion due to too many burdens.

It can be interpreted as the feeling of being overwhelmed, weighed down, and burdened by too much responsibility and obligations.

This feeling of being overwhelmed can have an adverse effect on one’s physical and mental health.

It can cause exhaustion, insomnia, headaches, muscle tension, and irritability.

Additionally, it can lead to an increase in stress hormones, which can lead to further physical ailments such as high blood pressure, heart palpitations, and digestive issues.

It is important to recognize the signs of stress and exhaustion, and take steps to balance your life to avoid long-term physical and mental health issues.

## Upright Meanings For 10 Of Wands And Health

### What It Means For Me

The 10 Of Wands tarot card is a warning to be mindful of one’s health when carrying a heavy load.

This card serves as a reminder to take time to rest, relax, and take a break from the burdens and stresses of life.

It suggests that the burden one carries can be too much to bear and can lead to exhaustion, fatigue, and ill-health if not addressed.

It is important to evaluate the amount of stress placed on oneself and take appropriate steps to reduce it in order to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

A balanced approach to life and taking the time to enjoy one’s hobbies and passions can help to release the tension of being overburdened.

### What It Means For The Future

The 10 Of Wands tarot card is a warning to take stock of your current situation and to be mindful of the burdens you are carrying.

It cautions that it is easy to become overwhelmed, exhausted, and overworked.

This card suggests that the future may hold more of the same, so it is important to make sure that you are looking after your own wellbeing.

Taking time to rest and recharge is essential, as well as making sure that you are taking on only what you can handle.

The 10 Of Wands tarot card encourages you to make sure you are making time for self-care, in order to ensure that you stay healthy and balanced in the future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 10 Of Wands tarot card is a reminder that the health of those close to me is suffering due to stress, exhaustion, and too many burdens.

This could mean that they are overwhelmed by too much responsibility, or that they are simply exhausted from the amount of work they take on.

It is important to take the time to support them, to ensure they are getting enough rest and relaxation, and to provide them with the necessary resources to cope with their stress levels.

It is important to remember that it is possible to have too much on one’s plate, and it is important to check in with those around me to make sure they are not taking on too much.

## Reversed Meanings For 10 Of Wands And Health

### What It Means For Me

When The 10 Of Wands card appears in the reversed position, it is an indication that the energy of stress and exhaustion has been relieved.

This can be a positive sign in regards to one’s health, as it means that the burden of responsibilities has been lifted and that one is now free to focus on their well-being.

The reversed 10 Of Wands card can symbolize the release of physical, mental, and emotional burdens that were present in one’s life.

This can lead to more energy and motivation to take care of one’s health.

It may also suggest that a person is now paying closer attention to their physical, mental and emotional health, and that they are taking steps to create a healthier lifestyle.

The reversed 10 Of Wands can also suggest that one is learning to say no when it comes to taking on too many responsibilities.

This can help a person to be more mindful about how much stress they are taking on and how to better manage it.

By being mindful of the amount of stress they are taking on, they can better balance their health and wellness.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed 10 of Wands appears in a tarot reading, it can indicate that the querent is feeling overwhelmed by the many burdens in their life.

It suggests that the querent is feeling overburdened and that they need to take a step back and prioritize their health and wellbeing over everything else.

The reversed 10 of Wands can indicate that the querent is struggling with poor mental or physical health due to the stress of carrying too many burdens.

This card can be a warning to focus on taking care of oneself, to rest and recharge, and to delegate some of the burdens if possible.

It suggests that the querent needs to create a healthier balance in their life and to focus on self-care and healing.

If the querent is struggling with mental health issues, it may be time to seek professional help.

This card can also indicate that the querent needs to take time to appreciate the little moments in life and to enjoy the simple pleasures.

Taking a break and allowing oneself to relax are essential for restoring balance and nurturing one’s mental and physical health.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed 10 of Wands tarot card can indicate that a person is feeling overwhelmed and overburdened.

They are likely feeling exhausted and drained, both physically and emotionally.

When this card is reversed in a health context, it can suggest that the person is feeling weighed down by the stress and fatigue of a long-term health issue or condition.

It may be that they are struggling to keep up with treatments or appointments, and feel like they can’t take a break or step away from their health issue.

The reversed 10 of Wands can also indicate that a person is not taking proper care of themselves.

They may be neglecting self-care practices, such as rest and relaxation, proper nutrition, or exercise.

It can be a reminder to the person to take the time to focus on their own health and wellbeing, and to find ways to manage the physical and emotional burden of their health issue.

By taking the time to prioritize their own health, they can find ways to manage their stress and fatigue, and find balance in their life.
