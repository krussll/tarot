A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 4 Of Swords and the 3 Of Cups tarot card tell me that it’s time to take a break from the hustle and bustle of my daily life and go into a period of retreat, rest and renewal. This does not have to mean physical isolation, however. It could be about carving out time for self-care even when surrounded by people, as the 3 Of Cups reminds me of the circle of support from friends and family that will be there for me to help me restore and rejuvenate. This is an invitation to really slow down and nourish my body and soul, so that I can come out of this time feeling restored and refreshed.

Being refreshed with new ideas and insight, you might find that you are able to approach your relationships with newfound clarity and a renewed connection with yourself and your partner.

Overall, the 4 of Swords and 3 of Cups together in a love reading could be a gentle reminder that it’s important to take a break from relationships at times in order to keep them healthy and vibrant. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 4 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 4 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

For Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card means retreating from the hustle and bustle of daily life to rest and heal. It is a reminder that the body needs to recuperate and rejuvenate when it is feeling overwhelmed and worn out. This can involve getting enough sleep, engaging in calming activities, eating healthier food, and taking the time to nurture and care for oneself. Taking some time for yourself can help to restore your physical and mental health, and return you ready to take on whatever life throws at you.

For Finances

The 4 of Swords Tarot card brings a basic message of financial caution. It can be a call to step back and create a clear financial plan, set achievable goals, and consider your decisions carefully. It cautions to not take on more than you can handle, or to try to manage too many different transactions at once. Taking the time to rest and reassess your financial situation can help you to create a deliberate and well-thought-out pathway to success.

For Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card encourages withdrawal and respite from the freneticness of regular social interactions. To foster and maintain healthy relationships, it is important to take the time to take stock, reflect on your relationships, and identify areas where adjustments or changes can be made. Taking a break from busy social circles can also be beneficial, giving you the space to appreciate the relationships you have, as well as reconnect with yourself as an individual. It is also a reminder that it is perfectly acceptable to not engage in a relationship if it does not feel right. Taking a step back from the situation and giving yourself time to recover can be an important part of the relationship process.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

In terms of health, The 3 Of Cups Tarot card represents nurturing friendships and a strong circle of support. It suggests that health can be improved when we have strong ties with our friends and family. The card speaks of celebration and joy, which can be integral elements of health, since mental and emotional well-being greatly impacts our physical health. It encourages us to appreciate those who support us and to recognize our connections with others as a great source of vitality and strength.

In terms of finances, The 3 Of Cups Tarot card can be seen as a symbol of good luck, suggesting that a financial windfall can be attained with the help of our friends. It speaks to the concept of communal effort, emphasizing that cooperation and collaboration can lead to more success. It’s a reminder to not underestimate the power of our network, as that could be the key to prosperity.

Finally, The 3 Of Cups Tarot card speaks to relationships, signifying the importance of coming together for support. It encourages us to tend to our connections with others and make sure to connect with friends regularly. The card speaks to the importance of celebrating our relationships with others and remembering that we are not alone. It’s a message that relationships are a source of great strength and should be cherished.
