A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

You have all the energy and enthusiasm of the Page of Wands, so use it to make a decision and take action on your new project or creative idea. Don’t be afraid to take a risk or to try something new – it could lead to wonderful results.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Page Of Wands and 2 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Page Of Wands mean?

Health

The Page of Wands Tarot card symbolizes an energy that is strong and vibrant. It speaks of a person who is full of life-force and has great potential for health. It suggests that one should take advantage of their enthusiasm and ambition to explore and experiment in order to find the healthiest versions of themselves. It encourages a person to stick to their health goals and to stay committed to improving their wellbeing. Additionally, it can indicate an opportunity to take on a new health-related challenge such as running a marathon or learning a new diet.

Finances

The Page of Wands Tarot card speaks of an enterprising individual who is full of fresh ideas and a willingness to take risks. It symbolizes an opportunity to embark on a new business venture that could bring great financial reward. This card also speaks of the importance of developing financial knowledge and taking charge of one’s own financial affairs. It suggests that the individual is likely to succeed if they are resourceful and willing to take calculated risks.

Relationships

The Page of Wands Tarot card suggests a period of new beginnings, fresh ideas and optimism. It encourages an individual to take risks in order to explore and experiment with relationships and friendships. It speaks of the importance of open communication in order to foster relationships. Additionally, it can also symbolize a change in romantic relationships and the potential for unexpected rewards. It encourages one to be creative and brave in order to make the most of their relationships.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

The 2 of Swords Tarot card is a reflection of the need for compromise in all aspects of life. In terms of health, this card suggests the ability to find a balance between rest and activity. There is a certain element of inactivity that can be good for the body, just as there is a certain element of activity that can be beneficial. It is essential to find the right combo of activity and rest in order to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

In terms of finances, this card suggests the need to carefully consider both sides of an issue before making a decision. A balance must be found between spending and saving, and between investing and paying off debt. This card also encourages individuals to take into account the long-term repercussions of their financial decisions.

In terms of relationships, this card implies the need to find middle ground between both parties. It is important to listen to each other, respectfully consider each other’s opinions, and strive to make decisions that are beneficial for both people. Compromise does not mean that one must sacrifice their own needs in order to benefit the other, but rather that both parties can come to an agreement that meets their needs and resolves the conflict.
