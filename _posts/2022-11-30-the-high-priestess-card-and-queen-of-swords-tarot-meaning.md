A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The High Priestess card and Queen Of Swords card together in particular.

The The High Priestess card and Queen Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The High Priestess and Queen Of Swords mean together?

Wands

The High Priestess tarot card is associated with the element of water, which is often associated with emotions and intuition. The Queen Of Swords is associated with the element of air, which is often associated with the mind and logic. When these two cards are combined, it suggests that you need to use both your head and your heart in order to make the best decisions for your health and your relationship. Listen to your gut instinct, but also don’t be afraid to analyze things objectively.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The High Priestess and Queen Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The High Priestess and Queen Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The High Priestess mean?

The High Priestess Tarot card is connected with the astrological sign of the Moon, and it is all about intuition, mystery, and secrets. In terms of health, The High Priestess Tarot card suggests that you should trust your gut instincts and listen to your body. This is a time for inward reflection and paying attention to your dreams and intuition. In terms of finances, The High Priestess Tarot card suggests that you should be careful with investments and not take any unnecessary risks. This is a time for saving your money and being conservative with your spending. In terms of relationships, The High Priestess Tarot card suggests that you should go with your gut and trust your intuition. This is a time for being honest with yourself and your partner and for letting your intuition guide you in your relationships.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health:

The Queen of Swords Tarot card indicates that you are a self-sufficient person who is totally honest with yourself. You have a strong sense of who you are and what you want in life. You are not afraid to take risks and are always up for a challenge. You are also a very independent person who is not afraid to go it alone. You are always looking for new and exciting ways to keep yourself fit and healthy.

Finances:

The Queen of Swords Tarot card means that you are a very independent person who is not afraid to take risks. You are always looking for new and exciting opportunities. You are also a very honest person who is not afraid to tell it like it is. You always live within your means and are very frugal with your money. You are also very wise with your investments and always think long-term.

Relationships:

The Queen of Swords Tarot card means that you are a very independent person who is not afraid to take risks. You are always looking for new and exciting experiences. You are also a very honest person who is not afraid to tell it like it is. You always speak your mind and are very direct with people. You are also a very loyal friend and always have your friends’ best interests at heart.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The High Priestess and Queen Of Swords has helped you.
