A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Strength card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Strength Tarot card and The 8 Of Cups card mean something powerful when taken together. The Strength card symbolizes self-love, unconditional love, self-respect, and courage– things that are necessary for any personal journey. The 8 Of Cups card symbolizes withdrawal, retreat, moving on and abandonment. This can symbolize the process of letting go of what is no longer necessary, so that you can focus on cultivating courage, unconditional love, and self-respect. In this way, it can be empowering to honor and practice the Strength card while setting aside ties and attachments to the past symbolized by the 8 Of Cups. Both cards can create a pathway towards new beginnings and a greater understanding of yourself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is one of the most powerful Tarot cards and symbolizes resilience, courage and inner strength. When this card appears in a Tarot reading, it is indicative of a great force of will within. On the physical and emotional level, the Strength card is a reminder that we all possess within ourselves the power to overcome adversity and to open the door to a brighter and more successful future.

With regards to health, it is important to remember that true strength comes from within. When faced with physical illness, we must take a holistic approach, engaging both our body and our mind with a combination of exercise, proper diet, and relaxation. Acknowledging our inner strength and turning to it to conquer physical ailments is the first step towards a healthier lifestyle.

The Strength Tarot card also carries a message of financial security and stability. Even in times of financial stress, it is important to remember that true wealth comes from within. Keeping a watchful eye on our spending habits and looking for additional ways to expand our income streams can both help bolster our financial position.

On the relationship front, the Strength Tarot card is a reminder of the power of unconditional love. In a relationship, it is important to practice self-love and self-respect first, as this is the best way to truly show love and respect for ourselves as individuals. From there, we can then extend these qualities to our partners, and ultimately create a more loving and secure relationship with them.

The Strength Tarot card is a powerful symbol of resilience and inner strength. When interpreted within the context of our health, finances, and relationships, it offers us a powerful reminder to draw courage and inspiration from within in order to continue moving forward and achieving success in all areas of our lives.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

The 8 of Cups Tarot card can have different meanings in different areas of life. When it comes to health and wellbeing, the 8 of Cups often means that it’s time to take a step back and retreat. It might be beneficial to take an extended break from treating yourself or engaging in any unhealthy habits. This card can also be interpreted as an indication to start taking better care of yourself by making healthier lifestyle choices.

When it comes to finances, the 8 of Cups Tarot card can mean that it’s time to go against the grain and take a new approach to your finances. It may be the perfect time to re-evaluate your budget and look for new ways to save money and manage your finances.

In relationships, the 8 of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as a sign of withdrawal. It’s often a sign that it’s time to reassess the situation and give yourself some space. It can also be a sign that it’s time to move on and leave behind a toxic or unhealthy relationship. This allows you to create new and better relationships in the future.
