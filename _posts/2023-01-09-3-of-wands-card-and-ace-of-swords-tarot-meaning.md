A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and Ace Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and Ace Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Wands tarot card and The Ace Of Swords tarot card together represent the idea that teamwork is essential for growth, expansion and travel. It also highlights the importance of being open to new ideas and arguments, and the need to be focused and clear in your thinking when dealing with conflict. This combination also suggests that it may be necessary to make changes and take risks in order to achieve success, and that careful consideration and planning should go into any venture. With teamwork and mental clarity, new opportunities for success and growth can be created and realised.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

The 3 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of teamwork, commerce, expansion and travel, which is all very beneficial for health, finances and relationships.

On the health side, the 3 of Wands symbolizes the need to work as a team when it comes to dealing with a health issue. This involves both the patient and the health professionals taking the lead to find a solution. It also reminds us of the importance of having a good support network of friends and family when dealing with any kind of health problem. It’s a reminder that “no man is an island” and that your team can make all the difference.

When it comes to finances, the 3 of Wands is a symbol of expansion, business and commerce. It points to the importance of being creative in how you earn money, taking active steps to increase your income, and constantly being on the lookout for new opportunities. This can involve starting a business, selling things online, diversifying your investments, or anything that involves taking calculated risks to generate more money and grow your wealth.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 3 of Wands is a reminder that teamwork and cooperation are integral to any partnership. It encourages us to think about our relationships in terms of what we can do together and how we can work together to overcome any obstacles. It also serves as a reminder to be open to new experiences and to always keep an eye out for new opportunities that can bring new dimensions to our relationships. Whether this involves traveling together or simply taking the time to learn something new together, it’s important to take the initiative to keep our relationships alive and growing.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

The Ace of Swords Tarot card can signify a breakthrough in understanding your health and how it affects your life. It could be a moment of clarity when you realize a new dietary or exercise regime to improve your health. This card can also indicate surgery or the need to make an important decision regarding your health. It could be the time to take the steps toward healing an old physical injury, or the time to take control of a chronic illness.

Finances  
The Ace of Swords Tarot card can represent a time for new ideas around finances and money in your life. It can indicate a fresh start or the ability to move forward and make wise decisions with your finances. It can symbolize the ability to cut to the heart of the matter and be decisive in your financial decisions. It can signify being careful with investments or being clear about your income and expenses. It can also represent a time of mental sharpness and clarity when it comes to financial planning.

Relationships  
The Ace of Swords Tarot card can mean that a difficult situation may be coming to a close in a relationship. It could signify a breakthrough in communication or a new understanding. It could also indicate a moment of clarity when it comes to a difficult decision in a relationship. The card may represent the need to make a mental shift in order to accept and forgive, or the courage to stand up for what you believe in. It can symbolize being clear about your boundaries and not allowing yourself to be taken advantage of.
