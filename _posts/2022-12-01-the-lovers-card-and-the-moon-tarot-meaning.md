A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and The Moon card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and The Moon have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and The Moon mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The Moon tarot card together signify the power of two coming together. They indicate a strong bond between two people that has the potential to grow further and that, with trust and cooperation, can result in significant growth and success. The Moon tarot card also reminds us that there are unseen forces at work, and that the ability to be open to change and trust our intuition is key to the progress. Overall, The Lovers and The Moon cards together suggest that if you enter into a partnership with a strong commitment to each other, you can make great progress and have a fulfilling and loving relationship.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It is a reminder to trust one’s intuition when it comes to the decisions made in love. There is a wisdom that comes from within and this card is a reminder to tap into that.

The combination of The Lovers and The Moon cards in terms of love life is a powerful one. It speaks to the strength of partnership and the importance of being mindful of the choices made in that partnership. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and The Moon mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

For Health

The Lovers Tarot card indicates that taking a holistic approach to your health is important. This card suggests that the best health comes from a partnership between mind, body, and spirit. To take proper care of yourself, you need to balance both your physical health with the mental and emotional aspects of your life. It is not enough to just focus on physical health – the emotional and spiritual aspects of your wellbeing need to be taken into consideration in order to achieve true balance. Making decisions in partnership with others, such as a medical professional or partner, is also important.

For Finances

The Lovers Tarot card suggests that you need to think of your finances as a partnership. When it comes to money, two heads are better than one. Working in partnership with a trusted partner or financial advisor is the best way to find success. This card encourages you to take the time to create a sound budget, plan for the future, and make smart investments. Working together with another will also help you to prevent the pitfalls of impulse buying and emotional spending.

For Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card signifies the importance of relationships. This card is a reminder that true love and partnerships are built on trust, communication, and understanding. You must work together to create a strong connection and have a successful relationship. Taking the time to truly know and understand your partner is fundamental. When both partners are willing to put in the effort to understand each other, the strength of the bond will become greater. As long as the partnership is based on respect and empathy, it will provide immense joy and fulfilment.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health:

The Moon Tarot card means focusing on your health, both inner and outer, as well as your mental health. This Tarot card speaks to being aware of any unseen issues that could be affecting your physical, mental, or emotional well-being. This is the time to seek medical attention, if necessary, and to trust your intuition when it comes to how you are feeling. The Moon Tarot card urges you to trust your body and take the steps necessary to keep it healthy.

Finances:

The Moon Tarot card suggests quick and voluntary changes in order to improve your finances. This card encourages you to consider making changes in areas like budgeting, savings, investments, or debt. It is important to remember that even small changes can make a big impact on your overall financial health. The Moon Tarot card also encourages you to trust your intuition when it comes to money; if something feels off, it is best to investigate further and make the necessary adjustments.

Relationships:

The Moon Tarot card is a reminder to trust your intuition in your relationships, both intimate and platonic. This card speaks to the importance of listening to your gut and understanding any unseen problems that may be present in your relationships. It is important to be aware of any underlying issues that could be causing tension or creating an unhealthy dynamic. This card advises you to take the necessary steps to reestablish trust and improve your relationships. It is also important to recognize that changes will be necessary in order to move forward.
