A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

**Both The Hierophant and 3 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 3 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health  
The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that traditional methods of healing can be beneficial to one’s health. These methods, such as acupuncture or herbal medicines, should be explored and considered when seeking treatment for any physical or mental health issue. This card emphasizes that following the accepted norms of the medical community might not be enough in some cases, and alternative healing may be necessary.

Finances  
The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that there is great benefit in following a path of financial prudence. This card encourages conservative strategies, such as long-term savings plans, diversified investments, and budgeting. Additionally, The Hierophant urges caution when it comes to taking risks; large sum gambles are not recommended. Instead, follow a path of tried-and-true financial practices to maintain financial health.

Relationships  
The Hierophant Tarot card encourages adherence to traditional relationship dynamics. This card suggests that maintaining the accepted norms of a relationship are the best way to ensure a healthy, successful connection. These traditional practices include communication, fidelity, respect, and loyalty. Additionally, sharing mutual interests, activities, and values is recommended for growing and nurturing a healthy relationship.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

Health: The Three of Swords Tarot card is a stark reminder that life is full of hardships and suffering. On a physical level, this card can represent difficult or painful medical procedures or treatments. It might be the suffering of an illness, or the stress of a cancer diagnosis. It could also be an indication of physical pain from a traumatic injury or surgery. In any case, this card is a reminder that health and well-being require a delicate balance and must be vigilantly maintained.

Finances: The Three of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that financial setbacks can occur. It is a warning that financial hardship can befall anyone regardless of their current financial standing. It could represent a period of loss because of a major investment gone wrong, or a job loss that has caused financial upheaval. Whatever the cause of the financial setback, this card is a reminder to prepare for tough times ahead.

Relationships: The Three of Swords Tarot card can be an indication of the end of a relationship. This card can represent the heartache and sorrow that comes with the loss of a loved one, whether through death or other means. It can also signal that a relationship is in need of repair, and that it will take hard work and commitment to move forward. No matter what the cause, this card indicates that it is a time of mourning, sorrow, and loss – something that can be difficult to reconcile.
