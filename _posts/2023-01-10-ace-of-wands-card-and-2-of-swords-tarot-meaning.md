However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Ace Of Wands card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Ace Of Wands and 2 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

Health: The Ace of Wands Tarot card indicates a new burst of health and vitality. The energy this card carries indicates a drive to take on new physical activities, start a new fitness plan or make a new commitment to improved health and wellbeing. This is a great time to reach out to a health professional, explore new forms of exercise and to create healthy habits that will last over time.

Finances: The Ace of Wands Tarot card suggests newly found financial opportunities. This could bring with it a new job or business venture, or a windfall of money from unexpected sources. It prompts us to keep an open mind and be willing to take risks. The energy of this card carries a sense of excitement and enthusiasm, so it is the perfect time to explore new avenues like investing, saving or web-based projects that could bring greater financial stability.

Relationships: The Ace of Wands Tarot card can be interpreted as a sign of renewed energy and commitment in relationships. This could mean taking a relationship to the next level, or finding that spark again in an existing one. It could also point to improved communication and understanding between partners, new projects or activities that bring couples closer together and the potential to move closer to your partner’s desires. It is a great time to re-commit and to form new bonds with people.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health: When it comes to health, The 2 of Swords tarot card suggests that it is time to make a decision that is most beneficial to your wellbeing. A common issue this card brings up is the decision between surgeries, treatments, or other health decisions that need to be made. The 2 of Swords Tarot card is prompting you to take a step back and assess both sides of the issue in order to make a balanced decision. Consider the pros and cons of each option and weigh them thoughtfully. Ultimately, the choice is yours and it is important to make sure that it is the best one for you.

Finances: The 2 of Swords Tarot card often appears when a financial decision needs to be made. This could be anything from deciding whether to invest in a certain stock or to take out a loan. The card encourages you to look at both sides of the situation carefully before coming to a decision. Before you commit to anything, make sure that you understand the risks and potential rewards of each option. Take the time to research your options and make sure that you have a clear understanding of the situation before you make a decision.

Relationships: In relationships, The 2 of Swords Tarot card can signify a time to compromise. Whether it is a disagreement between friends, family, or romantic partners, this card indicates that a resolution needs to be reached. The card suggests that both sides of the argument should be listened to and taken into consideration. It also suggests taking the time to reflect and assess the situation before making a decision. Taking into account both parties’ needs can help to create a fair and balanced resolution to the disagreement.
