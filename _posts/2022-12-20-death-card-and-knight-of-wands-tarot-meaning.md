A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and Knight Of Wands card together in particular.

The Death card and Knight Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and Knight Of Wands mean together?

The Death and Knight Of Wands tarot cards together represent an opportunity for a major transformation in your life. It’s time to end old patterns and behaviors, to let go of the past, and to move forward with creativity, passion, and excitement. The Death card is a call to drastic change, while the Knight Of Wands symbolizes a bold, enthusiastic spirit to embrace the future with courage and determination. Take this as an opportunity to release the old and welcome the new, and allow yourself to experience a joyful, invigorating rebirth.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and Knight Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

The Death Tarot card symbolizes considerable change, endings and a time for transformation in health, finances, and relationships. It is often viewed as a difficult card to read, but its symbolic power can be incredibly helpful during difficult transitions in life.

When it comes to health, the Death Tarot card serves as a reminder that we are meant to evolve and transcend our physical form. This can mean a literal death of the physical body, but it can also represent the death of a part of our identity that we have been clinging to or a way of life that is no longer serving our best interests. In this sense, the Death Tarot card serves as a reminder that it is sometimes necessary to let go of unhealthy habits or patterns so that we can make room for healthier alternatives.

In terms of finances, the Death Tarot card can signify the end of an era where money has been scarce or the conclusion of a business venture that has run its course. In this case, the card serves as an invitation to take stock of where you stand financially and free yourself from any lingering debt or negative money patterns that may have been holding you back. It is an opportunity to courageously start anew and develop more positive money habits that honor your financial goals.

Finally, the Death Tarot card also encourages us to look to our relationships and release any stagnant ties that may be causing us harm. This could be an unhealthy relationship with a romantic partner or family member, or even an unjust job that is no longer serving our highest good. In this case, the Death Tarot card serves as a reminder that our relationships can experience death and rebirth. Instead of clinging to unhealthy dynamics, we are invited to move through them with courage and grace and make room for something better to come.

All in all, the Death Tarot card carries the message of hope and healing during times of major transition. Its symbolic power is meant to remind us of the strength and courage we have within us to bravely move through what may feel like the end and make room for something new in health, finances, and relationships.

## What does Knight Of Wands mean?

Health – The Knight of Wands Tarot card can bring both positive and negative influences when it comes to health. On the positive side, this card encourages a healthy lifestyle and suggests a vibrant and dynamic approach to physical activities. It encourages people to stay active and enjoy a healthy balance of work and play. On the negative side, the card can indicate a tendency toward overworking oneself, or pushing oneself too hard which can lead to burnout and health problems in the long run.

Finances – The Knight of Wands Tarot card can be interpreted as a warning to stay vigilant and protect against financial risks. The card implies that taking a “wait and see” approach is the best way to ensure one’s financial security. It also suggests that taking calculated risks can pay off, but only if done wisely.

Relationships – The Knight of Wands Tarot card indicates that relationships can be unpredictable and exciting. The card encourages people to be playful and creative in their relationship, and to not let the mundane things get in the way of living life to the fullest. The card suggests that the best relationships are ones based on mutual respect and understanding rather than being held together by sheer force of will.
