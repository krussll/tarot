You are reminded of the importance of taking responsibility for every decision you make and every action you take. Having financial security, optimizing your investments, and being mindful of your spending will set you up for long-term success and material happiness. With the Sun and King Of Pentacles in your reading, you have the potential to have great joy, satisfaction, and abundance in your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

Health:

The Sun Tarot card is a positive omen for one’s health. It implies that any health issues faced now will soon be resolved and that the outlook is very bright. Positive changes in one’s well-being are imminent. It encourages us to put in the effort to create a healthier lifestyle and to focus on eating nutrient dense foods, getting enough rest and exercise and taking care of ourselves in general. Additionally, The Sun Tarot card could mean that we need to seek professional help in order to mend any physical issues we may be facing.

Finances:

The Sun Tarot card indicates that financial prosperity is on its way. There may have been some hard times lately but now it is time to look forward to success and abundance. It signifies an end to financial hardship and an increase in income. Investments should turn out to be profitable and savings should steadily increase. The Sun Tarot card also encourages long-term financial planning and for us to stay disciplined when it comes to managing our finances.

Relationships:

The Sun Tarot card implies that our current relationships are bright and flourishing. There will be feelings of happiness and contentment, and a sense of security and loyalty will form. New beginnings are also on the horizon, where we may meet people that will become important figures in our lives. We may also experience some healing and closure of relationships, so we can move on to new ones. The Sun Tarot card encourages us to nurture our existing relationships and be open to meeting new people. Communication, understanding and compassion are essential for healthy relationships.

## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

The King of Pentacles tarot card symbolizes a leader with a strong materialistic and practical approach to life. This king is an exceptional investor and wise manager of his resources, both tangible and intangible. He takes a measured and steady approach to life, knowing that success is not achieved overnight.

Health-wise, the King of Pentacles encourages a significant focus on prevention, rather than intervention. This king realizes that a healthy lifestyle is essential to long-term health and wellbeing. Eating a nutritious diet and exercising regularly are encouraged, as well as getting adequate rest, and taking the time to relax and unwind. The King of Pentacles suggests that by taking a preventative approach to health, you can greatly reduce the chance of illness down the road.

Financially, The King of Pentacles is an excellent source of advice and guidance. He understands that creating a budget, investing wisely, and avoiding unwise risks are the keys to long-term financial health. The King of Pentacles suggests that by creating a solid plan and executing it with care and dedication, you can build security for yourself and your family.

In relationships, the King of Pentacles is a steady and reliable companion. He knows that the key to sustaining a relationship is treating it with respect and dedication. He encourages open and honest communication, as well as the willingness to compromise. The King of Pentacles also values respect and trust. He knows that these qualities form the bedrock of any strong relationship.
