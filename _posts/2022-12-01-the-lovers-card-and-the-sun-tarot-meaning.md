A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and The Sun card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and The Sun have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and The Sun mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The Sun tarot card together mean a powerful relationship in which two people are able to work together to achieve mutual success. The Lovers card suggests that by joining forces, there is the potential to be incredibly strong, while The Sun card promises material happiness and joyous outcomes. Together, these cards point to a relationship where the strength and success of both individuals is deeply valued and celebrated.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Lovers and The Sun mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Lovers and The Sun the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health: The Lovers Tarot card can represent a strong friendship, partnership or relationship between two people, which can be helpful when it comes to maintaining and improving your health. When two people who care for one another support each other, there is potential for improved health outcomes. The card encourages you to listen to the advice of your partner or close friend and to maintain a strong and healthy relationship. This can be especially beneficial when it comes to tackling physical and mental health issues, as the strength of a partnership can be a great source of motivation and support.

Finances: The Lovers Tarot card can represent a mutual understanding and respect between two partners, family members or friends. It encourages us to use the collective intelligence and resources of the partnership to our advantage. It can also be a sign of an intellectual and financial union; this could mean a successful business venture or property investment. Ultimately, it should be remembered that the key to achieving financial success is often through collaboration.

Relationships: The Lovers Tarot card represents a strong emotional connection between two people. It is often about making the difficult choice between two options, and the card reminds us that the greatest strength lies in two lovers coming together. The card encourages us to create a balanced relationship based on mutual love and respect, as well as understanding each other’s needs. It is also a reminder that when two people come together, they can create a powerful and beautiful relationship that can bring great joy to both of them.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card is an incredibly hopeful card. For health, it suggests that healing and good health are within reach. It is a reminder that physical health is a blessing and should be appreciated. Financially, it symbolizes prosperity and material wealth. This can mean that new job opportunities, investments, and investments in business endeavors will bear fruit. Finally, in relationships it means that joyous, genuine connections are available and will bring happiness.

Health-wise, the Sun Tarot card promises that physical well-being is within reach. It encourages us to take care of our bodies, as well as our mental health. Through proper nutrition, rest, and regular exercise, we can bring about a positive outcome for our health. The card also promotes positive thinking and taking care of our own health before helping others.

Financially, The Sun Tarot card is a very good omen. Its meaning is the same for both businesses and individuals. It encourages wise investments and the pursuit of profitable avenues. It also encourages us to explore potential new sources of income and to open up our minds to different types of opportunities that could bring in more money.

Finally, The Sun Tarot card reminds us that genuine relationships are available, and that true joy can be found in them. This can be in the form of family, friends, or romantic relationships, and it encourages us to find people who share our values, goals, and interests and to nurture meaningful relationships. This card also encourages us to practice self-care, as well as to show appreciation to those we care about.

Ultimately, The Sun Tarot card is a reminder that we should focus on our health, financial security, and relationships. Through proper care and dedication, we can achieve amazing results and lasting prosperity.
