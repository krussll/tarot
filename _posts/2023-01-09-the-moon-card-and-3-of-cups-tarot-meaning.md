A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Moon card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Moon card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Moon and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The Moon tarot card and The 3 Of Cups tarot card indicate a difficult combination. On one hand, the Moon card suggests that there may be some unseen obstacles or changes that could prevent you from experiencing the joy and friendship of The 3 Of Cups. On the other hand, The Moon card also urges you to trust your intuition to determine the best course of action to take. It could be that the support and guidance of friends, as indicated by The 3 Of Cups, can help you overcome whatever unseen challenges may be preventing you from having a fulfilling social life. By trusting your intuition and being open to a circle of supportive friends, you can work through these unseen problems and enjoy the joy of celebration.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Moon and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health:  
The Moon Tarot card could indicate that you are feeling generally off, or it could be a sign of something more serious. It could be an indication of unseen problems in your health that you need to pay attention to and take action on. It could also point to the need for you to make changes to your lifestyle that you are aware of to improve your health. It could be a sign to trust your intuition when it comes to making decisions regarding your health that can have a positive impact.

Finances:  
The Moon Tarot card could mean that unseen problems are present in your finances, and it is time to take a hard look at your financial situation and make the necessary changes. It could be indicating that you need to trust your intuition when it comes to managing your money. It may be time to make voluntary changes in your spending habits, such as creating a budget, paying off debts, or saving more. It could also suggest taking advantage of opportunities that could lead to increased financial stability.

Relationships:  
The Moon Tarot card could be a sign of an underlying issue in a relationship that needs to be addressed. It could point to the need for voluntary changes to be made, such as altering communication styles or mending fences with a friend or family member. It could also suggest that you need to listen to your intuition when it comes to making decisions about a relationship, such as whether to stay in it or end it. However, it could also be a sign that a difficult situation is about to be over, such as a fight or disagreement.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a representation of friendship, celebration and a circle of support, so it can mean a lot of wonderful things for one’s health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to one’s health, the 3 of Cups can symbolize taking the time to honor and nurture positive relationships. This may mean being more conscious of the relationships you have with your friends, family and even yourself. Taking the time to truly appreciate the people and things in your life will help you keep a healthy attitude about your life. Additionally, celebrating your successes, no matter how small, can help boost your motivation and help you achieve your health goals.

In terms of finances, the 3 of Cups is a reminder to not just think of yourself, but also those you care about when it comes to money. Taking the time to support each other’s financial goals, whether they are short-term or long-term, is an important part of creating a healthy financial situation. Additionally, it can pay off to come up with creative ways to make sure everyone in your social circle is making sound financial decisions that will ultimately benefit everyone.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 3 of Cups Tarot card suggests that the time spent with others should be meaningful, enjoyable and celebratory. Taking the time to appreciate other people, especially those closest to you, will help create a positive and supportive atmosphere. Additionally, celebrating your successes and those of the people around you can help build strong relationships and foster a sense of trust and mutual respect.
