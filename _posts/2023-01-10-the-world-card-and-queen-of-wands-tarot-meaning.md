A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The The World card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The World tarot card and The Queen Of Wands tarot card together signify the completion of a goal or lesson, and the confidence and self-assurance to take the next step and travel to a new place. It could also mean being ready to move on from the old routine and embrace a new, more exciting life. The Queen Of Wands will guide you through your journey and bring about a new perspective as you reach for your goals. As you wander around the world, you will gain experience, knowledge, and wisdom. With the Queen of Wands as your companion, you can fully enjoy your journey, and at the end, you can look forward to the completion of your goal and the fulfillment of your dreams.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This is a time for celebrating the present, for appreciating the work you have done, and for cherishing the person by your side.

The Queen of Wands brings an element of self-assurance and confidence to the mix, meaning that you are ready and willing to take the next steps in your relationship. With her strong will and infectious enthusiasm, she is an important reminder that it is not just about completing a journey and reaching the destination. It is also about the journey itself, and making sure that you are learning and growing as a couple.

The World card is a reminder to cherish the moment and enjoy the journey, and the Queen of Wands indicates that you have the courage and the passion to make the most out of the experience. You have all the tools you need to make it to the end of your romantic journey, and you can do it with your head held high. You have what it takes to make it to the end – all you need to do is keep going, stay strong, and follow your heart.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

The World Tarot card is the final card in the Major Arcana of the Tarot deck, and symbolizes the end of a cycle or journey. As such, this card is often interpreted to signify completion, success, wholeness, and finality.

When looking at the health aspect of this card, it could represent that a person is coming to a point of completion, such as the end of a health issue or the conclusion of a long recovery from an injury. In this case, it could signify that the person has found a positive resolution and achieved balance in their life and health. On the other hand, it could suggest that a person is coming to a point where they have achieved a level of physical and mental health and wellness, and are now ready to go to the next level.

In terms of finances, The World Tarot card could signify that a person is ready to complete a financial goal or cycle. This could involve reaching a certain level of financial security or success, or the realization of a long-term financial goal. In this case, The World card could suggest a feeling of completion and satisfaction, as well as being ready to take new risks or challenges in order to further progress financially.

Last, but not least, The World card could signify the completion of a relationship cycle. This could indicate that a person has learned the lessons they needed to learn from a certain relationship and is now ready to move on, or it could mean that the person has achieved a certain level of satisfaction or understanding in their relationship and is now ready to take it to the next level. It could also suggest that a person has found the completion or understanding that they were seeking within themselves or in a relationship.

Overall, The World Tarot card can represent different levels of completion and finality in terms of health, finances, and relationships. It is an empowering card that signifies that a person has achieved something and is ready to move on to the next phase.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen of Wands tarot card is all about power and leadership, signifying a woman who is fiercely independent with a positive attitude that nothing can get in her way. In health, this card speaks to using your resources effectively to maintain an optimum level of health and well-being both physically and mentally. She encourages one to take control of their own health, be pro-active in seeking assistance when it is needed, and not to become overwhelmed by stress and worries.

In terms of finances, the Queen of Wands shows that one should be confident and assertive in their commercial dealings and investments. She encourages an independent approach to managing finances while keeping a firm grip on one’s financial security. She also shows that one needs to plan carefully and be mindful of any risks that may be associated with change in financial circumstances.

The Queen of Wands governs relationships. Here she encourages confident communication, understanding and empathy. She shows that a strong, honest relationship is built on mutual respect and understanding. She also encourages the use of diplomacy and negotiation where necessary, so that relationships remain buoyant and healthy. Ultimately, the Queen of Wands helps to maintain positive, vibrant and lasting relationships.
