However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Moon card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Moon and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The Moon tarot card combined with the Queen of Wands card is indicative of a situation in which you must take a leap of faith. You may be facing unseen and unexpected problems, and you must be willing to make voluntary changes in order to achieve success. However, you must trust your intuition and inner wisdom to guide you in the right direction. The Queen of Wands card symbolizes a feisty, confident, and self-assured person who knows what they want and will do whatever it takes to get it. This combination encourages you to move forward with the trust that you have the strength and intuition to navigate any difficulties you may face along the way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This could include changes to diet or exercise, or the adoption of new stress-relieving activities.

Finances: The Moon Tarot card suggests that there may be unseen financial problems that require a proactive approach to solving. Barnum warned to “look before you leap” and this is especially applicable to financial matters. This card indicates that it is important to trust your intuition and to be brave when it comes to making changes. This could mean taking risks but it could also mean avoiding other risks. Alternatively, it could mean looking for new investment opportunities that capitalise on current market conditions.

Relationships: The Moon Tarot card can suggest that there are unseen psychological complexities in one’s relationships. Trusting one’s intuition is important and it could be that certain changes may be necessary. This could involve initiating a more honest and open dialogue with a partner or putting boundaries in place to ensure a healthy balance. It also suggests that taking a step back and observing a relationship from a distance can help to gain a better understanding of its complexities.

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is also associated with emotions and feelings, which can be beneficial in sustaining health. When the card appears in a Tarot reading, it can suggest that the querent should pay attention to the emotional aspects of their health, as well as their physical needs.

This card can also represent the need to take up opportunities that will bring us financial stability, and to have faith in our ability to make sound business decisions. The Queen of Wands Tarot card encourages us to create wealth through sound investments and wise decisions, and not to get caught up in reckless spending or wasteful spending habits.

Relationships: The Queen of Wands Tarot card symbolizes relationships that are based on mutual respect and understanding. This card encourages us to be honest and open with our partners, to communicate openly and to remain respectful of their feelings and beliefs. The Queen of Wands encourages us to strive for a balance between independence and intimacy, and to cherish relationships that are based on honesty and trust. This card can suggest that the querent should take the initiative to establish a closer connection with their partner. It also encourages us to remain confident and independent while still allowing ourselves to be vulnerable and open to other people.
