## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card is a sign of a warning, a reminder to be careful and be aware of one’s surroundings. In terms of health, it can be interpreted as a warning to be alert and be aware of any physical warning signs that something may be wrong or that one’s health may be in danger. It is important to pay attention and take necessary measures to protect one’s health if needed.

In terms of finances, the 7 of Swords indicates a warning to remain vigilant and carefully consider any decisions that involve money. There may be a risk of theft, betrayal or dishonesty when it comes to one’s finances, so it is important to be aware of this risk and to be extra cautious around financial matters. It is also a warning to ensure that any agreement involving money is clearly understood, as misunderstandings and disputes could arise leading to loss of money.

When it comes to relationships, the 7 of Swords represents betrayal and dishonesty, so it is important to be aware of these potential risks. It can be a warning to be aware of any signs of betrayal and dishonesty from those close to one, and to remain vigilant in order to protect oneself from such actions. It can also be a reminder to be honest and open in relationships, as this will be the best way to prevent any potential deception. Furthermore, it is a reminder that running away from a problem will not solve it, and it is important to stay and face the issue.

## What does Ace Of Cups mean?

The Ace of Cups Tarot card is associated with joy, emotion, and contentment. It is a card of new beginnings, potential, and emotional fulfillment. Health wise, it can indicate a positive time of healing, both physical and emotional. It may signify the start of a new exercise routine or improved well-being. Financially, it can represent the start of a new venture or improvement in money matters. It can suggest the opportunity for the realisation of your financial dreams and ambitions.

In terms of relationships, the Ace of Cups Tarot card is an incredibly positive sign. It can indicate a new relationship, a proposal, marriage, or a baby in the family. It is a card of joy and acceptance that promises a strong bond of love and understanding. This card can speak of emotional harmony and of fulfilling relationships. The Ace of Cups Tarot card is a wonderful card to receive during a Tarot reading. It is full of optimism and opportunity, and can bring emotional satisfaction and success in many areas of life. It is a card of connection and love, of potential and hope.
