## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 7 Of Cups Tarot card has many implications for health. When this card appears in a Tarot reading, it can indicate that there are too many choices to make an effective decision regarding one’s health. This could lead to an individual feeling overwhelmed, confused, and even paralyzed when making decisions about their health. It is important to take time and consider the options carefully. The 7 Of Cups can also symbolize a lack of commitment to staying healthy, which can have serious consequences if not addressed. An individual might be tempted to make unhealthy choices or put off making any changes to their routine. It is important to find balance, and remember to make commitment to taking care of oneself.

Finances:

The 7 Of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as a warning when it comes to making financial decisions. This card can be a sign that a person is feeling overwhelmed and uncertain. They may be overthinking their options or second-guessing their decisions. This card can also indicate that a person may be trying to make too many financial choices at once, or is avoiding taking any decisive action altogether. This can be a sign that it is important to take a step back and look at the bigger picture of their finances, and to make a plan before committing to any decisions.

Relationships:

The 7 Of Cups Tarot card can warn of a lack of commitment in relationships. It can indicate that a person is feeling overwhelmed, and is not sure how to proceed with a relationship. They could be presented with too many options, and be confused by all possibilities. This card can symbolize a feeling of instability and a lack of security, which can be damaging to any relationship. It is important to take time to consider all the options, and to make a commitment to the chosen decision. If a person is feeling overwhelmed, then it may be time to seek out counsel.
