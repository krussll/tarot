A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the 6 Of Swords tarot card mean that it is time to embark on a journey of self-discovery, to seek clarity and enlightenment. This journey requires that you make a transition, a move from where you are now to somewhere new and unfamiliar. You may not know where you are going or what lies ahead, but the journey itself will bring you the answers you seek. This transition could be an actual physical relocation, or an emotional, spiritual, or mental journey. Hold tight to your inner truth throughout the transition and it will lead you to revelation and understanding in the end.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 6 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health: The Hermit Tarot card signifies a need to focus on personal health, both physical and mental. It suggests using quiet introspection to look within, and to trust in one’s own individual experiences to make decisions about health. It may also suggest a need to take a break from the hustle and bustle of everyday life, and to spend more time alone in order to rest, rejuvenate, and reflect on one’s well-being.

Finances: The Hermit Tarot card suggests that it is important to take time to reflect on one’s current financial situation, and to look within for creative solutions. It suggests being patient and trust in one’s own instincts when making decisions about finances. It may also reflect a need to simplify one’s lifestyle and to exercise caution and careful forethought when making financial decisions.

Relationships: The Hermit Tarot card indicates a need for personal reflection and meditation in order to better understand one’s place in relationships. It suggests applying lessons from within in order to improve communication, understanding, and the overall quality of relationships. It also reflects a need for self-discipline in order to maintain healthy boundaries, and to ensure that relationships are beneficial for both parties.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card is symbolic of a necessary transition related to health. It could indicate the need to change your lifestyle or medical treatment – such as a change in diet, exercise routine, prescription medication, or mental health therapies. This card can also represent changes in behavior and habits to improve overall health. Taking the necessary steps to improve your health can be difficult, but this card encourages you to take the plunge and make proactive progress in your overall well-being.

Finances:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card is about taking the steps to improve your financial situation, whether it is making a budget, saving more money, or changing a spending habit. Taking the initiative to make these changes will result in financial stability and security in the long run. This card indicates that it may be time to re-evaluate your spending habits and income sources, and identify areas that need to be adjusted and improved. Taking the time to make a drastic change right now will have a positive long-term effect on your financial health.

Relationships:  
The 6 of Swords Tarot card can represent a necessary transition in relationship dynamics. This card indicates that it may be time to make a change to improve relationship dynamics, such as adjusting communication methods, introducing new activities or methods to resolve conflict, or finding a new activity to share with your partner. Additionally, it can also signify the need to take a break from a relationship, whether it is a friendship, family relationship, or romantic relationship, in order to reassess the dynamic and make necessary changes. Taking the necessary steps to improve relationships can be difficult but rewarding in the long run if done correctly.
