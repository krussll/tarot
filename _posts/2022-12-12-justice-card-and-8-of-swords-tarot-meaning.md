You’ll need to pursue justice and balance in order to ensure that these solutions are fair and equitable. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 8 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 8 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice Tarot card can represent a call to balance in terms of health. This can mean that if you have made some unhealthy decisions, it may be time to reverse them and start fresh. Maybe it is time to make better dietary choices, increase your exercise, and start on a path to better physical and mental wellbeing. By seeking balance and justice, you will be able to take control of your health and make better decisions in the future.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card is a call to right your financial wrongs and establish a healthy budget. It may be time to cut back on frivolous spending, pay down debt, and save money. Setting up an emergency fund and taking a good look at your expenses can help you make decisions that will be beneficial in the long run. By creating balance in your finances, you will be taking the first step on the path to financial stability and freedom.

Relationships: The Justice Tarot card is a reminder that fairness is key in relationships. This card can be a sign to step back and assess your relationships and make sure that each partner is being treated fairly. It can be a sign to address any misunderstandings or grievances that have been left unaddressed. It can also be a sign to be honest and open with each other and establish clear boundaries. Justice can help ensure relationships stay strong and things stay balanced.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card indicates that one may be restricting themselves in terms of their physical health. This could be through not exercising, eating unhealthy food, or even not taking the necessary steps to maintain personal hygiene. There may be a feeling of hopelessness and helplessness that can drive one to further isolation and prevent them from seeking the necessary help or advice to improve their health. They might be unable to break free from this prison or have difficulty recognizing the potential consequences of their actions.

Finances:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card suggests that a person’s finances may be in a restrictive state. While there may be some opportunity for improvement, the individual in question might be unknowingly holding themselves back. This could manifest as a fear of taking risks, a lack of understanding about how to manage their money, or simply not wanting to put in the effort to better manage their finances. They may be stuck in a stagnant financial state, unable to make the necessary changes to improve their situation.

Relationships:  
The 8 of Swords Tarot card indicates that a person may be feeling trapped in their relationships and unable to break free. This could manifest as shutting oneself off from others, not wanting to open up and share feelings, or feeling like they are stuck in a negative cycle of communication. They may also be afraid to make the necessary changes to their relationships or to reach out to potential partners. This card suggests that the individual may need to take some time to reflect and reassess their relationships in order to have a healthier and more fulfilling connection with their partner.
