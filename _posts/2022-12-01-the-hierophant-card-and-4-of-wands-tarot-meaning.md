A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 4 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 4 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 4 Of Wands mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and the 4 of Wands tarot card mean a connection between tradition and homecoming. It suggests a coming together of all that is familiar – family, friends, and customs. It’s a reminder that when we come together in celebration of our traditions, we can find true joy. It encourages us to follow conventional expectations, but to also embrace the warmth of reuniting with those close to us. Ultimately it speaks to the importance of cherishing our relationships and the security of long-established customs.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

Following the status quo of modern medicine and lifestyle can bring physical, mental and spiritual balance and health. It also suggests seeking out holistic practices for maintaining and improving health, such as acupuncture, yoga, and massage. When The Hierophant card appears in a Tarot reading, it’s a reminder to take care of your body and mind, and to stay aware of your physical and mental health.

Finances: The Hierophant Tarot card can show that adopting traditional and conventional ways of managing your finances may be a wise choice. This could include budgeting, avoiding debt and making investments that are safe and secure. It is a reminder to be wise with your money and think of the long-term benefits of sound financial decisions. If this card appears in a Tarot reading, it could also suggest being open to new investment opportunities and taking a risk with your finances.

Relationships: The Hierophant Tarot card symbolizes the traditional standards of relationships, such as commitment and respect. It encourages you to find balance in your relationships and to be mindful of your expectations. This card can suggest staying within your comfort zone and taking it slow with new relationships. It’s a reminder to stay true to yourself and also to be respectful of others’ needs and boundaries. ## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

The 4 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of joyous celebration and joyous homecoming. It’s a reminder of the importance of family, friends, and community in our lives, and of our need to come together and enjoy each other’s company. In tarot, this card speaks to the health of relationships and the value that those relationships bring to our lives.

When looking at the 4 of Wands in terms of health, this card can represent the importance of home life and having supportive relationships. Friends and family can provide a source of strength and guidance that can help you stay healthy, both mentally and physically. Having a strong support system and a home to come back to can provide stability and a sense of security, even in the face of illness or hardship.

In terms of finances, the 4 of Wands can indicate a period of financial stability. This card often refers to financial success that is a result of the dedication and hard work of a team. Working together towards a common goal, whether in business or personal finance, can help to ensure that the goals are achieved. Through collaboration and dedication, opportunities can open up that increase the wealth and prosperity of a group of people.

When looking at the 4 of Wands in terms of relationships, this card can also speak to the concept of homecoming. This can refer to reuniting with a lost love, or of family coming together after a period of separation. It can also speak to the idea of rebuilding relationships that have experienced tough times. In all cases, the 4 of Wands can indicate a renewed bond and joy at being reunited with those we love.
