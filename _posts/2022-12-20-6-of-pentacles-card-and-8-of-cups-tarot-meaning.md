A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Cups mean together?

When The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 8 Of Cups tarot card come together, it is a message of being generous with your help and assistance, but also knowing when it is time to withdraw and move on. This could be in a situation in which you have given of your time and energy to help someone, but suddenly you are no longer needed or wanted. This card combination is asking you to trust in your decision to move on, even though it may feel difficult at first. Know that you were generous with your help, and now it is time to focus on yourself and draw inward for a time.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

Health

The 6 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to keep an eye on our overall health and to practice balance in all aspects of our lives. It suggests that health should be seen as a priority, that we should take care of our bodies and minds and find ways to replenish and support our energy levels. We should seek balance between work and play, rest and labour and value our physical health as well as our emotional health. The card also encourages us to be generous with our resources, to help out others where it is needed and to be generous with our own time and energy, making sure to give back to the world around us.

Finances

The 6 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to be wise and generous with our finances. It suggests that we should both save and spend money in a responsible manner and that we should be aware of our own financial goals and stay mindful of how we are using our resources. We should seek to find balance between saving and investing, and remain in control of our finances. The card also encourages us to give and receive financial help when both parties are in need. We should be generous with our money and use our financial means to help out those who are less fortunate and in need.

Relationships

The 6 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to approach our relationships with a generous and balanced outlook. It suggests that we should remain open to new relationships and that we should be generous with our time and energy in nurturing and sustaining existing relationships. We should find ways to give and receive support when both parties are in need. We should also practice balance in our relationships, finding ways to nurture both our own needs and the needs of others. The card also encourages us to think of ways to be generous, giving and receiving help and never taking more than is necessary.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 8 of Cups Tarot card is a sign that it might be time to withdraw from stressful situations in order to restore balance in your life. The 8 of Cups is a card of retreat, so this might be a good time to take a break from the daily grind or look for ways to reduce stress and anxiety. Taking a break from work or an overly demanding lifestyle can help you to refocus on your health and well-being, allowing you to get back on track and maintain balance in your life.

Finances

The 8 of Cups often indicates a period of financial retreat or a time to save energy for more important matters. It’s a sign that you should be wise with your finances and think about the long-term impacts of your decisions. This can be a time to step back from investments or financial decisions that may be too risky, instead opting to focus on saving money and creating financial stability.

Relationships

The 8 of Cups Tarot card can signify a period of withdrawal in relationships, particularly those that are no longer supporting your growth. If a relationship has become toxic, this card suggests it’s time to distance yourself and focus on relationships that bring you joy. It’s also a sign that you should retreat within yourself and focus on your own needs rather than continually trying to please others.
