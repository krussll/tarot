## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 10 Of Swords and Knight Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 10 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a time at which your physical, mental and emotional health may be at their lowest point. It represents a time when you may feel like you have tried everything to get better, and nothing is working. You may be feeling completely lost and hopeless. This card may indicate that it is time to take a step back and reassess your current health situation and identify how you can improve it. It could also signify that there are underlying, unseen issues at play that are contributing to your health struggles and that you may need to seek the help of a professional to get to the root of the problem.

Finances

The 10 of Swords Tarot card signals a time of financial distress. You may be feeling like nothing can turn the situation around and that you are stuck in an awful financial situation until it gets better by itself. This card may also be a sign that you have been irresponsible with your finances, making decisions that have led to this problem. It is important to understand that you can turn your financial situation around by acknowledging where you went wrong and making changes accordingly. It is also essential to remember that any financial hardship you are going through is only temporary and can be resolved.

Relationships

The 10 of Swords Tarot card in a relationship context often signifies that there are feelings of betrayal, anger, and powerlessness within the relationship. It is indicative of a relationship at its lowest point, where both parties feel unheard and taken for granted. You may feel trapped in the relationship, unable to see how it can get better. It is important to recognize that although the situation may seem bleak and without hope, this card is an indication that you can use the insight it provides to make positive changes in your relationship and move forward in a healthier way.

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

Health  
The Knight of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of spiritual good health and general well-being. It implies that the querent is in good physical, mental and emotional health, and is content with the level of health they are currently experiencing. This card encourages the querent to focus on mental and emotional balance instead of physical health, as these factors are strongly linked to overall health and well-being. The card also asks the querent to pay attention to their dreams, as they can be indicative of the state of their health, and provide them with spiritual guidance.

Finances  
The Knight of Cups Tarot card indicates that the querent is taking care of their finances responsibly and with great wisdom. It indicates that any financial decisions made are lasting and beneficial for the future. The card also signifies abundance and plenty, signifying that it is not just about the financial stability of the present, but also about the potential for growth and wealth in the future. However, the card also warns against overspending, as the accumulation of debt can be damaging in the long run.

Relationships  
The Knight of Cups Tarot card stands for passion and romance. It symbolizes the perfect relationship and unconditional love. The card also signifies harmony between two people, and indicates that the querent is in a meaningful and committed partnership. It implies that both parties involved in the relationship have the necessary understanding and patience to make it last. The card also signifies great communication and understanding in the relationship, and is a sign that the relationship has the potential to last a long time.
