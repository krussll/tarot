A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 10 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 10 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 10 Of Wands mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 10 Of Wands tarot card have opposing messages, yet they go hand in hand. The Hierophant tarot card cautions against excessive conformity, stressing the importance of embracing traditions while also respecting individual preferences and needs. On the other hand, The 10 Of Wands tarot card brings attention to the detrimental outcomes of an over-reliance on established expectations and conventions, warning that too much reliance on traditions can lead to stress, exhaustion, and too many burdens. Together, these tarot cards suggest that, while traditions can bring comfort, it is also important to break free from societal expectations and find the balance in life that works for you.

### What does The Hierophant and 10 Of Wands mean together for your love life?

The Hierophant and the 10 of Wands tarot cards combined symbolize the struggle between self-expression and traditional expectations that many of us face in our love lives. On one hand, the Hierophant represents the need to honor traditions and conform to conventional expectations. As your relationship progresses, you may feel pressure to follow the accepted path, such as getting married or even having kids.

On the other hand, the 10 of Wands speaks to the burden of responsibility that comes with decisions like these. It can be exhausting to juggle all the expectations placed on us and you may find yourself feeling overwhelmed and stressed. It’s easy to get lost in the hustle and the bustle and forget about enjoying the journey.

Finding balance is key. While there may be certain expectations that both you and your partner need to follow in order to make the relationship work, it’s important to find ways to ensure that your individual needs and passions can still be expressed. Don’t let the weight of traditional expectations crush your spirit and make sure you take the time to nurture your relationship so that it can flourish.

When The Hierophant tarot card appears alongside The 10 Of Wands, it is a sign that your finances and job may be feeling the strain. The presence of The Hierophant suggests that you may be feeling overwhelmed with the traditional expectations and cultural norms that you feel obligated to adhere to. You could feel like you are constantly in a rat race with little reprieve or chances to relax or take a break.

At the same time, The 10 Of Wands shows that your obligations have become too many to handle, leading to immense stress and fatigue. You may have taken on too many responsibilities or are trying to do too much at once. Perhaps you put too much hope in one project or situation and are now scrambling to piece together what you can still salvage.

The Hierophant and The 10 Of Wands warn that if you are not careful, your ambition and hard work could backfire. It is essential that you practice moderation and ensure that you are not overextending yourself. Allowing yourself room to breathe and rest is as important for success as dedication. Taking breaks and allowing yourself time to reset will give you the necessary energy to power through The Hierophant’s traditional expectations that come with the job.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and 10 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 10 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health

The Hierophant Tarot card is a reminder to stay in tune with our bodies and to practice self-care. It is a call to honor our bodies and their needs and to not push them to a breaking point. We should be mindful of our physical and mental health and strive to create balance and stability. Health is a journey, and we must remain devoted and patient in our efforts to achieve it. We should recognize when it’s time to take a break or to seek professional help.

Finances

The Hierophant Tarot card warns us not to give into destructive financial habits such as splurging or living beyond our means. It encourages us to stay diligent and disciplined in our financial decisions. We should practice thrift and frugality, setting aside some of our money for a rainy day. We should strive to build financial wealth and stability, both for ourselves and for our loved ones.

Relationships

The Hierophant Tarot card encourages us to be loyal to our relationships and to practice healthy communication skills. Our relationships are fragile, and we must remain in tune with our feelings and be willing to make compromises. We should strive to create mutual understanding and respect and to make a conscious effort to grow together. We should practice empathy and avoid destructive behaviors such as gossiping and badmouthing.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can represent daunting physical or emotional burden that could negatively impact one’s health. This card suggests that the individual is carrying too much weight, physical or psychological, and they’re struggling to cope with its strains. Burnout is likely, and this card indicates that the individual should prioritize self-care to prevent further exhaustion and strain. Healing might require rest and an understanding of the purpose of all of this effort, to help them find a way to move forward with a more optimistic outlook.

Finances

The 10 of Wands Tarot card might indicate that a person is overstretching their financial resources, taking on too many obligations and financial responsibilities that are weighing heavily on them. This card is a reminder to look for ways to reduce or eliminate burdens, or look for other resources that may help support the individual in financial matters. Learning how to manage financial matters responsibly and with mindfulness of the consequences of their actions can help to reduce stress and open up new opportunities in life.

Relationships

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can indicate that one’s relationships might suffer when they are dealing with too much stress and exhaustion. Whether in platonic, professional or romantic relationships, this card can signal that the individual is not taking the necessary time to invest in their relationships and build the connection with them. Taking time to nurture relationships, even amidst a time of stress and exhaustion, can help to create deeper bonds and understanding between each other. Additionally, it is important to practice self-care in order to be able to support and care for others.
