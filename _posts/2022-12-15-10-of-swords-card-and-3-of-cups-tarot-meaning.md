A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 10 of Swords and the 3 of Cups tarot cards together can mean that a difficult but necessary ending is being followed by the support of friends who can celebrate the new beginning. It is a reminder that after a painful ending, it’s important to find solace in the understanding and comfort of friends who can provide the support that is necessary to rise up after reaching rock bottom. It means that while endings can bring with them their own form of suffering, the support of a community can be the nourishing force that allows us to find a new beginning.

### What does 10 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 10 of Swords and the 3 of Cups in a love reading can have a powerful symbolism for a current or potential relationship. The 10 of Swords suggests a complete and painful ending of sorts – a sense of finality, or perhaps a realization that what was once desired has been lost. It speaks of a time of rock bottom and disruption, leaving you feeling somewhat directionless.

The 3 of Cups on the other hand speaks of friendship, celebrations, and a close circle of support being available. This could indicate a break from a romantic relationship, allowing you to reconnect with family and friends. Or, it can also suggest a new relationship – with the support of your existing relationships, you may find yourself ready to explore something new.

In any case, this combination encourages you to take stock of what is truly important in love – not just passion and infatuation, but trust and friendship too. As relationships can often be fragile, it is important to allow yourself some distance to reflect and really understand both your own and your partner’s needs.

In the end, the combination of the 10 of Swords and the 3 of Cups encourages us to find healthy balance in our relationships. If we recognize and appreciate the ability to find joy and support in our relationships, we can use it to help us through the difficult times and emerge from them even stronger.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 10 Of Swords and 3 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 10 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that when trying to make positive health changes, sometimes a person can reach a rock bottom point before things start to improve. It is a reminder that while you may be feeling discouraged and that all your hard work has been for nothing, this is a necessary step to reach a higher peak of health and wellness. It is a reminder to take a step back and reassess how you are approaching your health journey, and that there may be resources or an entire approach you have not yet tried. When feeling defeated or in a state of “hit-rock bottom,” it is important to remember that the journey of health and wellness is one of ebbs and flows, creating a roller coaster of emotions.

Finances: The 10 of Swords Tarot card carries a warning that in the financial realm, the concept of “rock-bottom” can quickly become a reality. When finances become overwhelming and plentiful debts have been accumulated, the 10 of Swords can serve as a reminder that this moment will pass, but a change in financial strategies is necessary in order to start the recovery process. It can be a reminder to make a financial plan, start creating a budget, and finding resources to help you get back on the right path. It is also a reminder to not give up when it seems like all hope is lost, and to keep working with tenacity and determination to begin rebuilding your financial stability.

Relationships: The 10 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that in relationships, sometimes it really does feel like hitting rock bottom. Whether in a romantic relationship, a friendship or a family bond, the 10 of Swords can be a sign that it is time to take a step back, reassess the situation and make a plan for how to move forward. It is a reminder that when relationships become volatile and emotionally draining, more often than not the resolution involves both parties recognizing what they need to do in order to heal and move forward. The 10 of Swords is a reminder to not stay in a place of bitterness and resentment, but to find a way to create a positive space and create a better, more positive relationship.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 3 of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as a reminder that one’s health should never be taken for granted. It encourages a holistic approach to health that not only focuses on physical well-being, but also emotional and mental health. It is a reminder that taking care of yourself and celebrating your successes are just as important as having a strong support system. This card encourages you to use your network of friends and family to help you stay healthy and better manage stress.

Finances

The 3 of Cups Tarot card signals the start of a new financial journey. It encourages you to see opportunities for financial success and to be open to different ways of achieving your goals. The card also serves as a reminder of the importance of having solid financial planning and relying on a solid support system of family and friends to help you thrive. It emphasizes that patience and consistency are important when it comes to building wealth, and that relying on the knowledge and expertise of your network can serve as a powerful asset.

Relationships

The 3 of Cups Tarot card is a symbol of strong community and a supportive circle of friends and family. It encourages you to nurture and celebrate your relationships, and focus on strengthening your connection with those around you. This card emphasizes the importance of communication, understanding, and loyalty as the foundation of any strong relationship. It also serves as a reminder of the power of unity and how coming together with your support network can help you get through any challenge or problem.
