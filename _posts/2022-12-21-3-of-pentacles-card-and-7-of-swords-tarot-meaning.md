A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Pentacles card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The 3 of Pentacles and the 7 of Swords tarot cards together suggest a cautionary tale about the power and danger of collaboration. The 3 of Pentacles symbolizes the potential for great skill, craftsmanship, and quality when working in a team, but the 7 of Swords points to the darker side of collaboration: theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and the potential for running away when things don’t turn out as expected. This combination of cards tells us to take care when entering into collaborations, and to approach them with an open, honest attitude in order to avoid any potential negative outcomes.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The combination of the 3 of Pentacles tarot card and the 7 of Swords tarot card can indicate that you may be faced with a situation in your love life where betrayal, dishonesty, and theft are involved. This combination can suggest that there is a lack of trust in the relationship and you may be feeling vulnerable.

At the same time, it can be a sign that there is the potential for improvement in your relationship if you can find a way to work together with your partner and focus on quality, craftsmanship, and collaboration. The 7 of Swords tarot card indicates that if you can be honest with each other and address any issues that have caused mistrust, you can rebuild your relationship and create a strong foundation of love and respect.

The 3 of Pentacles tarot card encourages you to be creative and use teamwork to solve problems and make sure there is a balance of communication and respect in the relationship. It is important to remember that collaboration is key and to be open to compromise. If you can take the time to listen to each other and find common ground, you can rebuild the trust in your relationship.

But the 7 of Swords tarot card still warns of potential betrayal and deceit. Consider if you should be careful of who you trust and if anyone else is involved in your relationship. Be careful of giving too much information to other people and trust your gut when it comes to protecting yourself.

Overall, the combination of the 3 of Pentacles tarot card and the 7 of Swords tarot card can suggest that your relationship may be in need of some repairs but it is by no means doomed. If you can focus on teamwork, collaboration, and trust, you can rebuild your relationship and find a balance that makes both of you happy.

### What does 3 Of Pentacles and 7 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 7 Of Swords tarot card are drawn together, it is likely to suggest a lack of financial success due to inattention, dishonesty, or betrayal. The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card indicates that there may have been opportunities to create financial success by working together with a team or by honing your own craft and skill, but the 7 Of Swords tarot card suggests that these opportunities have been spoiled by theft or dishonest dealings.

This might mean that you have been betrayed in your financial dealings, whether it is through embezzlement, fraud, or any other dishonest act. Alternatively, it could mean that someone has gained access to your financial information and taken advantage of it. This could be a partner, a colleague, or anyone else you have been working closely with.

In the case of job-related issues, the pairing of these two cards suggests that someone has stolen the recognition for your hard work. It may be a colleague who has taken credit for your ideas or a manager who has overlooked your accomplishments, instead rewarding someone else for their efforts.

No matter what the exact circumstances are, The 3 Of Pentacles and The 7 Of Swords suggest that money is not being handled honestly and respectfully. If this is a situation you find yourself in, the best course of action is to take a step back and assess the situation objectively. Try to determine who is responsible and take the necessary steps to remedy the situation. Although it may be difficult to trust again, it is important to focus on the positives and continue to work hard and believe in the quality of your own work.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This card encourages working closely with a qualified doctor and other medical professionals to ensure your health is in the best possible condition. This card suggests that by combining the skill, knowledge and craftsmanship of the highest quality medical professionals, you can make incredible progress in improving your health.

Finances  
The 3 of Pentacles is a card of teamwork and cooperation in finance. This card suggests that to make the best financial decisions, it is important to work closely with a team of trusted advisors. This card also encourages taking a multi-faceted approach to your finances by considering different opinions and perspectives. Acting on this advice will foster collaboration and create the most effective plan to achieve your financial goals.

Relationships  
The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of teamwork and collaboration in relationships. This card suggests that by working closely with your partner, you can create a relationship filled with balance and harmony. The card encourages bringing compassion, communication and understanding to your interaction with each other. Working together to make the most of your relationship, the 3 of Pentacles suggests that by combining skill, quality and craftsmanship, your relationship will last through any challenge.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card is associated with health, finances and relationships, representing theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and running away. When it comes to health, this card indicates that a person is struggling with their health and chooses to take matters into their own hands. They are often secretive about their health problems, not wanting to share their struggles with anyone else. This could lead to them taking matters into their own hands and making a decision that may not be beneficial to their health in the long run.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can indicate theft, fraudulent behavior, or dishonesty. This can mean either someone taking advantage of another person financially – such as stealing from them, or taking out loans in their name without their permission – or it can be someone overspending, hiding financial issues to make it appear as if they’re doing better than they are. The person represented by the 7 of Swords is often running away from their financial issues instead of facing them head-on.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can indicate that one or both of the involved parties are being dishonest or untrustworthy. This can mean that they are not inclined to open up and share their feelings, that they are hiding something or that they may be betraying the other person in some way. This card can sometimes signify that the relationship is unsustainable or that the two parties need to communicate openly and honestly if they want to make it work.

Overall, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can represent a variety of negative themes, such as theft, betrayal, dishonesty and running away. These themes are meant to indicate a person’s reliance on his or her own resources, their struggles to find the courage to face their problems, and their lack of courage to communicate openly and honestly in any situation. It is a warning to take action and find a way to rebuild trust and improve communication in all aspects of life.
