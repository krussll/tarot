## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The 7 of Pentacles tarot card and the 8 of Cups tarot card together remind us that patience and hard work are essential for success, but sometimes we need to take a step back and walk away. We must know when to continue to persevere and when to abandon something that isn’t bringing us joy or progress. Although the 7 of Pentacles suggests that success takes time and dedication, the 8 of Cups reminds us that it’s okay to let go of what’s not working and move on to something more fulfilling. It’s important to take a break and reevaluate your goals or plans in order to create a new path.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

Health

When it comes to health, The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that patience and hard work is key in achieving wellness. It suggests that now is a time of contemplation and taking stock of the habits and lifestyle choices that we are making. It encourages us to make improvements that are sustainable and beneficial in the long-term instead of trying to get results quickly. This card also encourages us to make sure that we are taking care of ourselves mentally, emotionally, and physically and to be patient in our efforts to improve our health.

Finances

The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card can signify careful investment and a willingness to be patient with the returns. This card can suggest a time of saving for something that you really want in the future, or being cautious when entering into a financial agreement. It can also signify delayed success, or rewards that need time to manifest. This card can advise against entering into any get rich quick schemes, and it encourages us to be smart with our money and to be mindful that there can be setbacks along our financial journey.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, The 7 Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages us to be patient and understanding in our dealings with others. This card can suggest that there is a need for taking a step back and looking at the bigger picture, and allowing time for issues to be sorted out. It can also suggest that there is a need for some re-evaluation of the relationship and for hard work to be put in for it to find success. It also suggests that there is reward that can come from this hard work and patience, but that it can take time to manifest.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The 8 Of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a period of retreat, withdrawl, and abandonment of unhealthy lifestyle habits. This may involve quitting smoking, taking an extended break from partying, doing a digital detox, or breaking up with someone who is a negative influence. It could also mean accepting negative emotions in order to process what needs to be released. In this way, the 8 of Cups encourages us to take the time to step back from the chaos of life and evaluate what is necessary for our overall well-being.

Finances:  
The 8 of Cups Tarot card is an encouragement to let go of financial obligations that are holding us back, such as excessive debts, investments, or mortgages. It can be a sign to explore more frugal and minimal ways of living in order to ease financial burden and allow for more flexibility in the future. It could also be a reminder to take a break from the stress of always trying to make money and instead to focus on more meaningful, heartfelt pursuits.

Relationships:  
The 8 of Cups Tarot card may signify a period of withdrawal from unhealthy relationships and the need to part ways with someone in order to pursue a new and healthier direction. This is a card of abandonment, so don’t be surprised if you sense a feeling of being overwhelmed and a need to step back and take a break. It is important to note, however, that even though the 8 of Cups encourages us to withdraw, it doesn’t mean that the relationship needs to end permanently. Instead, it could be an opportunity to set boundaries, practice better communication, and make sure both parties have the space and freedom to continue to grow and evolve.
