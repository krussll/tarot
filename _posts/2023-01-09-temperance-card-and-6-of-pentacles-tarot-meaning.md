A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Temperance card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Temperance card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Temperance and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

When The Temperance and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot cards come together, they signify the need to balance giving and receiving help. This can refer to finding harmony within yourself through patience and self-control to ensure you don’t overcommit or spread yourself too thin. At the same time, it also suggests to be generous with others and provide the assistance when needed. The key here is to stay in balance and balance your own needs with the needs of others, allowing for them to benefit just as much as you do.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card signifies a need for balance and moderation when it comes to health, finances and relationships. It encourages self-control and the ability to find balance between competing interests. It can also be interpreted as a call to practice patience, self-discipline and understanding when dealing with both external and internal issues.

When it comes to health, The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to take a balanced approach to our health. This means maintaining a healthy lifestyle, but not going overboard in any area. By avoiding extremes, we can preserve our physical and mental health. Temperance can also help us to resist temptation and make smart decisions when it comes to health care.

When it comes to finances, The Temperance Tarot card encourages us to practice financial discipline. It suggests that we should save for a rainy day, budget for retirement and be mindful about where, when and how we spend our money. We should also keep track of our finances and take a long-term approach to our money.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder to practice patience and kindness. We should strive to find a balance between being assertive and understanding of others. We should also resist the urge to overreact to difficult situations and instead focus on communication and compromise. By doing this, we can maintain peace and harmony in our personal relationships.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card means health, finances and relationships. This card is about giving and receiving, and it encourages us to take stock of both our resources and our needs.

Health: This card encourages us to practice balance, both in our physical care and the care of our emotional wellbeing. As we give to ourselves, we can also seek support from those in our lives who are capable of helping us better our own health. It might be worth considering consulting with a medical professional before taking any drastic steps. We can also explore avenues for better nourishing our bodies, such as improved exercise and diet regimes.

Finances: This card speaks to our relationship with money and reminds us of the importance of giving and taking. When it comes to finances, it’s important to know the value of our resources and to use those resources responsibly. We should be mindful of those who have less and open to receiving help when offered. The 6 of Pentacles card encourages us to seek out aid and advice when needed and to be generous when giving to others.

Relationships: This card encourages us to give and receive in our relationships, whether that be with friends, family, or romantic partners. It’s important to stay open to give and receive help and support, as well as to practice acceptance and understanding. Recognize when it’s time to step back and when it’s time to offer assistance. Realize that a balance between both parties is essential for healthy relationships.
