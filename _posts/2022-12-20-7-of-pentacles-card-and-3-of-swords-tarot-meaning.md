A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The two tarot cards of The 7 Of Pentacles and The 3 Of Swords together reflect a journey of hard work and patience that is filled with difficulties and sorrows. This combination speaks of a period of uncertainty, when a great deal of effort is put in with no immediate reward or recognition. This can be a difficult time especially when you are dealing with heartbreak, divorce, or a major loss. Although despair and depression can be difficult to cope with, the message is that eventually this time of hardship will pass and you will have to have faith and courage to get through it and eventually reach your goals.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 7 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 7 Of Pentacles and 3 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card often indicates that things will not happen quickly. If a person is looking for quick solutions or solutions that come without effort, this card suggests that the desired result will take time and patience to achieve. It indicates that you must invest your effort, money, and energy towards a goal before you will see any results. On the one hand, you must be willing to put in the hard work as this card does not promise an effortless path to success. On the other hand, it does imply that the effort is worth it, so don’t give up if the path seems difficult.

Finances:

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of caution when it comes to financial matters. This card indicates that any investments you make should come with patience. There is no quick fix, and any overnight success stories should be seen with suspicion. You should plan for the long-term, as it may take some time before you see any return on your investments. This card is also a reminder to be prepared for potential difficulties and delays and to plan accordingly.

Relationships:

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can be seen as a reminder to be patient with relationships. It is important to remember that all good relationships are built on trust and communication. This can take time, and you may need to be patient and invest a lot of effort into the process. This card suggests that you should focus on the long-term goals, as anything worth having is worth working for. You should also be flexible and willing to compromise, as this will help you to reach an understanding with your partner. If you are open to new experiences, you may be pleasantly surprised.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is a representation of pain and heartbreak, both in the physical and mental forms. It is a reminder that life can bring us to our knees, whether it is through divorce, loss, depression, or surgery. Health-wise, the 3 of Swords signifies a difficult time and a lack of self-care. It could be a reminder to focus more on your physical and mental well-being, and to take measures to safeguard your health, such as exercising and proper nutrition. Financially, this card can point to financial strain, either from medical costs or from external sources such as a job loss. It is a reminder to be financially responsible and not take on more than you can handle. Relationships-wise, the 3 of Swords tarot card can point to issues that have to do with trust and communication. This card can be a reminder to be honest with yourself and with others and to build and maintain strong relationships. It can also be a warning to be careful of toxic people who try to manipulate or take advantage of you. Ultimately, the 3 of Swords tarot card is a reminder to be mindful and take care of yourself and your relationships.
