A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Knight Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Knight Of Swords card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Knight Of Swords and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The Knight Of Swords tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card represent a tricky combination for me. On one hand, The Knight Of Swords tarot card symbolizes the need for a fast-talker to help me through a difficult period of change. On the other hand, The 5 Of Cups means a time of regret and loss, feeling abandoned and unloved, which can stand in contrast to the need for decisive and confident action symbolized by The Knight Of Swords. Together, these cards indicate a period of intense change, but whether I ultimately find success or failure depends on how I navigate the difficult emotions, attitudes, and decisions I’ll face along the way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Knight of Swords Tarot card is a card associated with the element of air, which symbolizes the mind and intellect. As such, it can signify a strong mental attitude and a dynamic approach to health. It suggests taking charge of one’s own health and making the determination to take action in order to reach a healthier state. This card can also signify the need to rid oneself of negative influences, such as bad habits or unhealthy people, that are holding one back from achieving one’s full potential.

Finances:  
The Knight of Swords Tarot card can mean a lifestyle change when it comes to finances. To honor this card, one should look for more efficient ways to manage their money and seek out financial opportunities that will bring prosperity and stability. Frequently, this card can point to taking a risk and making bold investments or taking calculated risks that can pay off in the long run. Additionally, this card can sometimes imply the need to make a stalwart decision, such as reducing debt, that may not be initially popular but can pay off in the future.

Relationships:  
The Knight of Swords Tarot card can indicate new beginnings in relationships when it is pulled. It might suggest that one should make the effort to communicate more actively with their partner, to be more honest and open, and to make sure that both partners in the relationship are equally active participants. Additionally, this card can signal that it’s time for one to take charge of their relationships and to make sure that their needs are getting met. It could also be a sign that it’s time for one to take a step back and make sure that their relationship is healthy and serving both parties.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

The 5 of Cups Tarot card can symbolize various difficult experiences and feelings in regards to health, finances and relationships. When it comes to health, the card may represent a difficult health issue or a loss of health and well-being. It may also refer to the frustration of feeling unwell and being unable to do the activities one used to enjoy. Financially, the card can refer to a financial setback or disappointment, such as a failed investment, an unexpected expense, or diminished earnings. Finally, in relation to relationships, the card can indicate feelings of abandonment, betrayal, or hurt. This feeling may be due to a break-up, lack of communication, or lack of support from a loved one.

In conclusion, the 5 of Cups Tarot card can refer to difficult situations or emotions in relation to health, finances and relationships. In health, it can represent a difficult health issue or a loss of health and well-being. Financially, it can refer to a financial setback or disappointment. Finally, in relationships, it can refer to feelings of abandonment, betrayal or hurt due to a break-up or lack of communication or support. Ultimately, the card serves as a reminder to take time for self-care, acknowledge the feelings associated with these difficult experiences, and take whatever steps necessary to move forward.
