Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Hierophant card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Hierophant, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Hierophant.

## The Hierophant And Health – An Overview

The Hierophant tarot card is symbolic of tradition and conformity when it comes to health.

It suggests that we should stick to the conventional expectations and protocols that have been passed down through generations in regards to our health.

This could be anything from following a particular diet or physical routine, to seeing a doctor in our local area with whom we are familiar.

It is a reminder to not get too caught up in the latest trends and fads, but to instead focus on the tried and true methods of maintaining good health.

It is also a reminder to trust in the wisdom of those who have come before us, for their advice can often be more beneficial than something new.

## Upright Meanings For The Hierophant And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Hierophant tarot card symbolizes adherence to tradition and established norms, as well as obedience to a higher power.

When it comes to health, this card can indicate a tendency to stick to a conventional approach and to trust in traditional wisdom when it comes to healing.

This could mean relying on doctors, conventional medicine, and traditional treatments, or it could also refer to a need to let go of any resistance to prescribed treatments and to trust in their power to heal.

The Hierophant also reminds us to be mindful of our own spiritual health, which can be nourished through prayer, meditation, and other forms of spiritual practice.

### What It Means For The Future

The Hierophant Tarot card is a reminder that following conventions and traditions can lead to a healthier life.

It suggests that adhering to the tried and tested wisdom of the past can be beneficial, and that conforming to the expectations of others can bring a sense of balance and harmony.

The card encourages me to look to traditional remedies and practices to maintain my physical and mental health, as well as to look for support from those around me.

It is a reminder of the importance of community and of following socially accepted standards of behavior, as well as finding a healthy balance between the individual and society.

The Hierophant card can be seen as a reminder to take care of my health, both physically and spiritually, by following established traditions and conventions.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Hierophant tarot card suggests that the health of those close to me may be shaped by conventional expectations and traditions.

For example, following the advice of medical professionals and adhering to scheduled check-ups and preventative treatments are often seen as the most appropriate way to ensure good health.

This card also implies that it is important to take time to reflect on the health of those close to me, looking at how their upbringing, family rituals, and beliefs may be impacting their wellbeing.

Taking the time to consider how traditional values, standards, and habits can influence our health can be an important step in ensuring the long-term wellbeing of those around us.

## Reversed Meanings For The Hierophant And Health

### What It Means For Me

When The Hierophant appears in a reversed position, it symbolizes a need to break free from conventions and expectations that are holding you back.

This can be especially important in terms of health.

When The Hierophant appears reversed, it may be time to reassess your current health regimen and find ways to make it more fulfilling or authentic.

It may be time to take risks and challenge yourself in terms of your health goals, or it may be time to seek the help of alternative healing modalities.

The reversed Hierophant suggests that it is time to explore new ways of thinking about health, and to look beyond the traditional, accepted ways of achieving health.

It may be time to step outside of society’s expectations and find a path that works for your body.

It may be time to question the limitations of conventional wisdom and to create a unique approach to your health.

It may also be time to ask for help from professionals outside of the traditional medical system.

Ultimately, the reversed Hierophant is a reminder to be open-minded and creative when it comes to your health.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed The Hierophant tarot card indicates that it’s time to break away from traditional expectations, and step out of the box to find new approaches to health and wellness.

It encourages taking a more unconventional approach to health, and suggests breaking away from the status quo.

It may be time to explore alternative forms of healing that are not part of the conventional medical system, such as herbalism, acupuncture, or homeopathy.

The reversed The Hierophant encourages us to take charge of our own health and look at it from a holistic perspective.

The reversed Hierophant also suggests that we should look at the bigger picture, and be mindful of the effects that our environment, lifestyle, and thoughts have on our overall health.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed Hierophant tarot card speaks to a need to break away from traditional health practices and conventions.

It encourages individuals to think outside of the box and to find new, inventive ways to approach their health.

By breaking away from the conventional expectations, one can explore healthier options that may have been previously overlooked.

The reversed Hierophant is a reminder to take control of one’s own health and to question the status quo.
