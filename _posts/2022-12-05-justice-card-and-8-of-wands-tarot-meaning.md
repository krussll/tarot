A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 8 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Justice card and 8 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 8 Of Wands mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 8 Of Wands tarot card coming together brings a message of swift action as the truth comes out. There is an emphasis to act quickly and make decisions that result in win-win solutions for all involved. The combination of these cards fast-tracks the process, ensuring that news arrives quickly and decisions are reached with all the facts. Ultimately, the focus is on making the best choices with the most ethical outcomes.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 8 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

When this card appears in a health reading, it can suggest that a situation will be resolved in a way that is beneficial for all parties involved, allowing for win-win solutions. This could be in the form of a treatment plan that is managed with care and thought. This card could also be a sign to take responsibility for our health, be mindful of what we put into our bodies and develop healthy habits to support our physical and mental wellbeing.

The Justice Tarot card in a finance reading could suggest that the decisions you make will be guided by fairness and integrity. While it can be tempting to take shortcuts to make money, this card encourages following through the desired outcome with honesty and transparency. It can be a reminder to balance our spending and saving in order to build stability and security in our finances.

When it appears in a relationship reading, the Justice Tarot card encourages clear communication and a commitment to being honest and truthful. It also suggests that we take responsibility for our actions, be mindful of the power dynamics in our relationships and strive to achieve fair balance in all aspects. This card can also be a reminder to practice justice when dealing with people, making sure that each person’s needs are considered in order to help reach a mutually beneficial resolution.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health

When The 8 Of Wands appears in a Tarot spread, it suggests that the individual should take swift and confident action to improve their physical and emotional health. In this context, the card encourages the individual to set health-related goals, make a plan to achieve them, and move forward without hesitation to ensure their plans are realized. The figure in the card is depicted rushing forward on their journey, which implies that to reach the desired results, the individual should move quickly and decisively. This card can also suggest that the individual should expect a quick turnaround in their physical or emotional health, likely in the form of a healing or recovery from an illness or injury.

Finances

The 8 Of Wands implies that financial fortune is likely to come quickly and suddenly. This card can be seen as a sign of potential wealth and unexpected windfalls, suggesting that the individual should prepare for sudden changes in their financial situation. In this context, the card encourages the individual to be prepared to spot and seize good financial opportunities once they manifest, and to act fast and confidently to make the most of these opportunities. It can also serve as a reminder to invest money wisely and to practice patience when it comes to building wealth.

Relationships

The 8 Of Wands Tarot card suggests that the individual should take swift and decisive action in their relationships. In this context, the card encourages the individual to actively seek out new relationships and to seize the opportunities that arise when they do. It also implies that relationships may progress quickly, with significant changes and developments occurring in a short period of time. However, it is important to remember to keep reasonable expectations when it comes to relationships, as the card is suggesting that change and progress can happen quickly, but not without effort.
