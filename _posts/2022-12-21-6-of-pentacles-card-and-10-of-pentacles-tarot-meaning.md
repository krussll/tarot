A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 6 Of Pentacles and The 10 Of Pentacles, when seen together, represent a cycle of generosity. While the 6 Of Pentacles signifies the giving or receiving of help, and the 10 Of Pentacles speaks of leaving a legacy or inheritance, the combination of the two speaks of the idea of paying it forward. When we graciously give and receive help, we are creating a more secure future for ourselves and our families. This cycle of generosity will ensure that our efforts to be generous will be continued and passed on to future generations. We are able to leave a lasting legacy by being generous and compassionate to others in our lives.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 of Pentacles often symbolizes financial stability, but its meaning goes beyond just money. It speaks to leaving a lasting legacy and achieving retirement. It often refers to the rewards of hard work and dedication over a period of time. This Tarot card suggests that sustained effort will accumulate into true and lasting value for the future.

In terms of health, the 10 of Pentacles refers to the concept of taking steps now to ensure one’s future health and well-being. This card is an encouragement to invest in our physical and emotional health today in order to enjoy lasting benefits in the future. This could mean taking preventative measures for physical health such as exercising and eating nutritiously. It could also include making efforts to manage stress and emotional health by practicing relaxation techniques, talking to a therapist or engaging in activities that bring joy and peace.

In terms of finances, we can take the 10 of Pentacles to mean a long-term focus on accumulating and maintaining wealth. This card suggests setting up a retirement strategy and taking the time to plan for a future where financial stability allows for greater freedom. It may also signify the need to employ a sense of personal stewardship and responsibility with money, like setting up a budget or making a plan to pay off debts.

Finally, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks to leaving a legacy with relationships. It suggests that a sustained focus on nurturing and sustaining them in the present will be rewarded down the line. Taking the time to invest in family relationships, romantic relationships, and friendships now will result in healthy, stable and fulfilling relationships in the future. Additionally, the 10 of Pentacles teaches that as we approach retirement, there is comfort in surrounding ourselves with our dearest people.
