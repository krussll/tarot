A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Cups card and Knight Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Cups card and Knight Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Cups and Knight Of Cups mean together?

The 8 of Cups tarot card and The Knight of Cups tarot card together suggest that a journey of leaving the past behind and moving on is in its beginning phases. It could be that you have been stuck in a situation that no longer serves you and it is time to move on and start anew. The Knight of Cups is a symbol of romantic possibilities and suggests that despite your withdrawal from the situation, you will likely be presented with a new opportunity for change that has a positive, romantic outcome. If you wait patiently, something beautiful may come into your life and sweep you away.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 8 Of Cups and Knight Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 8 of Cups Tarot card suggests that when it comes to health, it might be wise to recognize when it is time to retreat, move on or abandon certain habits or routines. Perhaps you have been trying to lose weight but have hit a plateau and need to step back from it and try something new. Maybe you have been relying on medication to manage your health concerns but recognize that you need to explore different avenues for healing. This card suggests that it is good to be able to be honest with yourself and recognize that it is sometimes necessary to take a step backwards in order to progress.

Finances:

The 8 of Cups Tarot card can represent withdrawing from an unhealthy relationship with money. If you feel like you are stuck in a cycle of overspending and not saving, this card suggests that it might be helpful to take a step back and reassess your relationship with finances. Maybe you need to set boundaries or take a break from shopping to help you get back on track financially. You can appreciate what you have and make changes to help you move forward.

Relationships:

The 8 of Cups Tarot card can suggest a time to recognize when it is time to walk away from a negative relationship. It could be that you have invested a lot of time and energy into a relationship, but you know it is no longer healthy or positive for you. This card suggests that it is time to move on, even if that means having to withdraw the emotional investment. It is important to be honest with yourself and recognize that it is sometimes necessary to take a step back in order to progress, and that sometimes relationships need to end in order to move on to something better.

## What does Knight Of Cups mean?

The Knight of Cups Tarot card generally represents health, finances and especially relationships. This card typically symbolizes a new opportunity, or a transition, or the start of a new journey. To look at health, this card suggests that you are at a threshold for discovering ways to take better care of yourself. It could be as simple as taking a break from a hectic lifestyle, or researching alternative forms of healing.

For finances, this card symbolizes the potential to gain new sources of income. It also suggests that you could have new opportunities with investments and could be more successful than ever before.

For relationships, the Knight of Cups is symbolic of romance. This card can indicate that it is time to ask someone out, or that a proposal is coming your way. It can also signal the beginning of a new relationship, or the continued growth of an existing one. It can also mean a new understanding within a relationship, as both partners agree to stay in it for the long haul.

No matter what area of your life the Knight of Cups appears in, it is ultimately a card of transition and opportunity. It shows that you are in a place of growth and open to exploring new possibilities. It also means that you are ready and willing to take the necessary steps to make your dreams come true.
