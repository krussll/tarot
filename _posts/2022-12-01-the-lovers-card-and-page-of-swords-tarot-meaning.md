A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The Page of Swords tarot card coming together for me signify an interesting mix of energy. On one hand, I feel the strength of partnership, love and a big decision on the horizon. On the other hand, I’m reminded that I need to take caution and protect myself, as I will encounter curious and prying eyes who will be looking to test the strength of my decision and my partnership. I’m reminded to speak my truth and to ensure that I’m being honest to my partner and most importantly, to myself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The Page of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take care of our mental and physical health. It suggests to stay alert, be aware of our habits and take action if necessary. This card can be a sign to get tested for any underlying physical illness or psychological condition. It is a reminder to create healthy boundaries, defend ourselves and pay attention to warning signs that something might be ailing our overall health.

Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card is associated with financial matters, since it is about finding the truth, being analytical and careful. It warns us to be mindful of our financial habits and take action if necessary. It suggests to be wise and make smart choices, think twice before making any purchases and watch out for those who might want to take advantage of our vulnerability. This card can be a sign to develop healthy financial habits and take control of our finances.

Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to be honest and direct in our relationships. It encourages us to open up, keep secrets and be forthcoming with our feelings and emotions. It suggests to practice good communication skills, develop trust and be open to feedback. This card can be a sign to protect ourselves and set boundaries, as well as to look within and be self-aware of our feelings and needs.
