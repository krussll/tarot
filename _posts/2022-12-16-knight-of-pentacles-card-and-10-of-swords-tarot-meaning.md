A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Knight Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Knight Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Knight Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Knight Of Pentacles and The 10 Of Swords tarot cards suggests a time of endings and beginnings. It is a reminder that hard work and patience are essential at this time in order to achieve a successful and reliable foundation for the future. The 10 Of Swords tarot card cautions that it is important to accept a painful and complete ending, allowing for a clean break from the past. The Knight Of Pentacles tarot card tells us that through hard work and patience, a better outcome can be achieved.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does Knight Of Pentacles mean?

Health  
The Knight of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with a physical and mental state of well-being. It symbolizes a patient and disciplined approach to health and wellness. When this card appears in a reading, it indicates that you are taking a serious and actionable approach to your health. This card suggests that you are likely exercising, eating well, and getting enough rest to keep your body and mind healthy. It also encourages you to practice mindfulness and stay in tune with your own physical and mental needs.

Finances  
The Knight of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of financial security. It encourages you to take a disciplined and methodical approach to money management. This card suggests that you should take stock of your current financial situation and make a plan to reach your long-term goals. It also emphasizes patience and hard work—it’s important to remember that financial security doesn’t happen overnight. This card also encourages you to be mindful of your budget and to look for ways to save money.

Relationships  
The Knight of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that you are in a stable, reliable relationship. It’s a sign of commitment and loyalty, and of taking the time to nurture and grow your relationships. This card encourages you to remain focused and dedicated to building strong bonds with your loved ones. It advises you to take a patient and mindful approach to deepening your relationships, and to strive to create a supportive and secure environment for those you care about.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 10 of Swords tarot card can represent the ultimate destruction of physical and emotional health. This card symbolizes a complete refusal to nourish and strengthen the body and spirit. It suggests that a person’s physical and mental health is being neglected. This neglect can manifest in unhealthy habits, reckless behaviors, and a lack of self-care. In the worst cases, The 10 of Swords can suggest that a person has hit rock bottom and is in danger of negating their general state of health.

Finances:

The 10 of Swords tarot card can represent a financial downfall. This card suggests complete destruction, a hit to the bottom line, and the loss of all assets. It suggests that the person has neglected their resources and mismanaged their money, leading to a complete economic collapse. It can also be a sign of loss and bankruptcy, with the person having little to no assets left and no way of recovering or rebuilding their financial status.

Relationships:

The 10 of Swords tarot card can represent a total destruction and breakdown of relationships. This card symbolizes the end of all communication, understanding, and trust. It suggests that a person has neglected the relationships in their life and that the situation has become hopelessly broken beyond repair. In the worst cases, The 10 of Swords can symbolize the complete loss of all interpersonal connections, with the person left isolated and alone.
