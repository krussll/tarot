A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The Chariot and the 9 of Swords tarot cards together represent a challenging journey with a series of obstacles to overcome. The Chariot card is associated with action and change, so whatever this journey may be, it will require a conscious effort to make progress. The Nine of Swords card suggests that there will be a lot of inner struggle involved, as it is associated with nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression. It may be that in order for one to move forward, they must confront their inner worries and doubts. This journey will be difficult, but ultimately it can lead to powerful transformation, setting one on the path to success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Chariot and 9 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a person who is living in fear and distress. It indicates a person who is weighed down by the fear of their circumstances and is feeling overwhelmed with their current situation.

In terms of health, this card can represent physical symptoms of poor health due to mental health issues, such as insomnia, headaches and fatigue. It can also reflect the exacerbation of existing illnesses due to stress. The individual might be feeling immense pressure, which could be causing them to feel drained and exhausted.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Swords is a card of financial worry and fear. It can indicate a person who is under financial stress and is fearful of the future. This can be a sign of financial instability and insecurity. The individual might be feeling overwhelmed by the thought of their looming financial responsibilities, such as paying off debt or having to take on new expenses.

In terms of relationships, the 9 of Swords can represent relationships suffering due to anxiety and fear. The individual might be feeling isolated and alone, due to their inability to overcome their own internal struggles. This card can indicate a person who is having difficulty in trusting others, due to their own internal demons. It is a sign of someone who is feeling a heightened sense of insecurity and fear.
