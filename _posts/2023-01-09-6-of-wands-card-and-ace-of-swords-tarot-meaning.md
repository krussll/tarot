A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and Ace Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and Ace Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The 6 of Wands and the Ace of Swords tarot cards signify an important moment in your life. This moment is one of victory, recognition and success. You are being rewarded and celebrated for something, but new ideas and conflict are also coming into play. You are faced with a mental clarity, and need to make decisions that will determine a new direction in your life. You have gone through surgery, of sorts: you have faced your challenges, overcome them and are now being rewarded for it. This is a time of great self-empowerment, where you are the one honoring yourself for the success you have achieved.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 6 Of Wands and Ace Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 6 Of Wands and Ace Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is a great omen for one’s health, finances and relationships. This card symbolizes victory, which can be interpreted as the successful achievement of one’s goals. This can apply to any area of life, from physical health to emotional wellbeing and financial security. Health wise, this card suggests that one’s efforts to lead a healthy lifestyle are paying off, and they will soon reap the benefits of their hard work. Financially, it indicates that one’s current strategies are leading to success and that they should be rewarded with a sense of financial security. In terms of relationships, this card hints at recognition of the hard work that both parties have put in to maintain the bond, resulting in a strengthening of the relationship.

In summary, the 6 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of triumph in any area of life and one should not be afraid to take advantage of the opportunities that lie ahead of them. The positive attitude of this card suggests that success will be achieved with patience and perseverance. With hard work, one can manifest their desired outcome in any area of life, from health and finances to relationships.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

For Health:

The Ace of Swords tarot card is typically a sign of new ideas for health and wellbeing. It is associated with mental clarity and insight that can help you take decisive action towards a healthier lifestyle. It may urge you to consider having surgery or medical treatment, as sometimes a physical problem can only be solved with a medical solution. With the Ace of Swords, you are encouraged to look for the best plan of action for your health and to make the tough decisions that may be required to take proper care of your health.

For Finances:

The Ace of Swords tarot card, while not associated with wealth per se, is related to success, power and control. These aspects are related to money and possessions, so this tarot card might be a sign of a great opportunity or a successful venture. If you find yourself in a difficult financial situation, this tarot card may represent the clarity you need to find a way to make successful decisions and changes. It could also mean negotiating terms and gaining the upper hand when dealing with finances.

For Relationships:

The Ace of Swords tarot card is a card of clarity and insight. While it doesn’t suggest that relationships are suddenly going to change or become easier, it may mean that you gain the clarity you need to make the tough decisions that are necessary to improve your relationship. If it appears as an answer to a love-related question, it might represent an opportunity for two people to gain insight into each other and for them to express their true feelings. This could mean that it is a sign for a relationship to become closer and for two parties to come to a beneficial agreement.
