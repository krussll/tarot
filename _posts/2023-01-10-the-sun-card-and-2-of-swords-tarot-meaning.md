A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 2 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Sun tarot card and The 2 Of Swords tarot card represent a time of making a decision that will lead to material happiness, joyous outcome, and success. This is a time to compromise and make a wise decision that will bring positive changes in your life. It is a time for optimism and for trusting that whatever you decide, it will have a positive outcome. The Sun tarot card encourages you to be positive, to believe in yourself, and to move forward with confidence, while The 2 Of Swords tarot card asks you to be mindful of your decision and to take the time you need to make sure you are making the best decision for yourself.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This could mean that both parties need to be aware of each other’s needs and wants, and figure out how they can come to a mutual agreement. It could also suggest that it’s time to look at the dynamic between you and your partner and see if there is a way for you to find common ground and make decisions that benefit both of you.

The combination of The Sun and The 2 Of Swords tarot cards, then, suggests that you need to focus on both material and emotional needs when looking towards the future. It is important to set goals that benefit both you and your partner and to take the time to make decisions that are mutually beneficial. This may take some time and compromise but, ultimately, this will bring you closer to the joyful outcomes and successes that you seek.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card signifies health, happiness, and joy throughout life. It is symbolic of a strong and resilient body, mind, and spirit that uses the power of the sun to look forward to whatever life brings. Health-wise, this card encourages individuals to take charge of their health, to look after their physical and mental wellbeing. The Sun card also encourages individuals to take responsibility for their choices and to honor their own values. The Sun card speaks of physical health and longevity and implies a strong, healthy body and improved energy levels.

Financially, The Sun Tarot card represents growth, guidance, and abundance. It speaks of material possessions, investments and financial security. It encourages individuals to strive for success through hard work and dedication, and to view money as a tool to create a better life. The Sun card also encourages individuals to look for creative ways to generate money and wealth and to use their money wisely to achieve financial stability.

For relationships, The Sun Tarot card speaks of joy, happiness, and a sense of fulfillment. It encourages individuals to be open to new forms of love, to let go of old patterns, and to embrace fresh experiences. The Sun represents strong, loving, and long-term relationships, as well as friendships that are enriched by joy and laughter. Most importantly, The Sun encourages individuals to show compassion to their partners and to always be honest and direct in their communication. It also encourages individuals to give their time and energy to their relationships and to find ways to make them stronger and more meaningful.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health: The Two of Swords tarot card symbolizes a need to face any physical or mental health obstacles you are having. When this card appears, it may be a warning of the need for a compromise or a difficult decision. It is a reminder to be mindful of the choices you make, as these choices can have a significant impact on your physical and mental health. It can also be a sign that a stalemate has been reached regarding a health issue, and that now is the time to find a middle ground or alternate solution.

Finances: This tarot card can also be interpreted as relating to finances. It is a sign that a compromise needs to be made regarding a financial issue, such as debt or a budget. It warns of a potential stalemate, and encourages you to draw on your inner strength and wisdom to make a decision. It can also suggest that you should be cautious when it comes to making any big financial decisions.

Relationships: The Two of Swords tarot card symbolizes the need to make a difficult decision when it comes to relationships. It could be that you have reached a situation of stalemate and have to decide which direction to take with your relationship. It may also represent a need for compromise when two persons have conflicting needs and wants. The card is a reminder to make decisions that come from the heart, and to be mindful that the choices you make when it comes to relationships can have long lasting consequences.
