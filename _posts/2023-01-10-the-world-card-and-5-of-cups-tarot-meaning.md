A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and 5 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The World card and 5 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and 5 Of Cups mean together?

The World tarot card and The 5 Of Cups tarot card together show that it is time to close the door on a chapter in your life, possibly due to regret or grief. You may have recently experienced a loss that has caused you to feel abandoned and unloved. This may have prompted you to travel or emigrate away from a difficult situation or to pursue goals or lessons in a new location. It is important to recognize that this is a new beginning and that you can use what you have learned from these experiences to build a brighter future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The World and 5 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The World and The 5 of Cups tarot card come together in a reading of your love life, it suggests the possibility of a necessary change. It could mean that a relationship has come to an end and you may be feeling overwhelmed with sadness and regret. This could mean that the end of the relationship was due to a change in circumstances or a lesson to be learned. The need to move on can be painful, but the World tarot card also suggests that it could be the key to reaching a new stage of understanding and spiritual progress.

On the one hand, The 5 of Cups suggests that there may be a time of mourning, sadness, and grief as a result of the ending of the relationship. You may be feeling abandoned or unloved and regretful for not being able to make the relationship work. On the other hand, the World tarot card can suggest that although the relationship has ended, it is a door to greater understanding. It implies that you will find closure and healing as you discover a new beginning and new opportunities.

The combination of these two tarot cards reflects the need to accept that there is a need to move on from a chapter in your life. The World tarot card suggests that the end of the relationship could provide a chance for a fresh start, a new chapter and a chance to learn more about yourself and the world around you. It is a reminder that change is part of life, and with the right attitude, it can be an opportunity to start something new and different.

The World and The 5 of Cups together in a reading of your love life is a reminder to be resilient and patient as you start to make sense of the changes that have taken place. Although it can be painful, it also provides an opportunity to learn and to take the time to figure out what the next step should be. It is a time to focus on the important lessons of love, acceptance and healing that can be gained from the ending of a relationship.

### What does The World and 5 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When The World tarot card and The 5 of Cups tarot card appear together in a reading regarding finances and job, they represent both the end of a difficult journey and the grief associated with it. This combination of cards suggests that while you have achieved a great accomplishment in reaching your goal, the loss of something valuable along the way is causing an emotional burden.

Though the 5 of Cups may be seen as a negative omen, it’s important to remember that it is also a card of transformation. It can represent the emotions of sorrow and loss that are often necessary for us to make significant changes in our lives. So, while it can be difficult to accept the pain and sadness that comes with the achievement of our goals, it is part of the process of growth and improving our circumstances.

In terms of finances and jobs, The World tarot card can indicate a change in lifestyle that involves travel or moving away from your current situation. As the card of completion, it can symbolize a successful small business endeavor or the launch of a long-term career plan. The 5 of Cups can simultaneously suggest a loss of stability, security, or friendships associated with the change, and it can be difficult to accept the negative emotions surrounding it.

However, it is important to look at the end result, and to remember that the end of one chapter often marks the beginning of another. This combination of cards encourages us to look to the future with optimism, even if the journey has been challenging. It reminds us that while change can be difficult, it can also be incredibly rewarding.

It could also suggest that, while the feeling of sadness and loss are difficult to overcome, they can be replaced with feelings of joy and accomplishment.

In terms of health, this combination of cards could be a sign that it’s time to start a new health regimen, such as changing your diet and exercise habits. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

The World Tarot card is an excellent symbol for completion and transformation, and this can be applied to all areas of life, including health, finances and relationships.

When it comes to health, The World Tarot card could indicate the completion of a major health issue or a period of good health. It could also signify that it’s time to take charge of your health and take steps to maintain your wellbeing. It might suggest that you seek professional help or attend classes if necessary to improve your health.

In terms of finances, The World Tarot card could mean the end of a financial struggle. It could signify that you have achieved financial stability, or that you have completed a major project that has benefited your financial situation. The card could also suggest that you maintain your financial stability and use your newfound wealth in a responsible manner.

Finally, The World Tarot card could point to a relationship coming to an end, or a period of understanding and mutual respect that has been achieved with a partner. It could also indicate a new and significant relationship that has potential to last, or a relationship that has finally healed from a long-standing issue. It might suggest that you should appreciate the connection you have with others, and be willing to negotiate and compromise if necessary.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 5 of Cups Tarot card can be associated with health. When this card appears in your reading, it could indicate that it is time to assess the current state of your health. Perhaps there is something in your life that is causing you distress and it is time to take a step back to look at the situation objectively. You may be feeling abandoned, either physically or mentally, by friends, family, or even medical care professionals. Either way, it is important to take the time to listen to yourself and make sure you are properly taking care of yourself.

Finances:

The 5 of Cups Tarot card can also represent finances. When this card appears in your reading, it could indicate that it is time to step back and assess the current state of your finances. However, it could also mean that there could be a loss of money due to unforeseen circumstances. It could also mean that you are feeling regret or sorrow because of a financial decision you have made and it has not worked out as well as you had hoped.

Relationships:

The 5 of Cups Tarot card can be related to relationships as well. When this card appears in a reading, it could mean that a relationship is in a state of decline or that it has been abandoned altogether. You could be feeling abandoned, unloved, or ignored. You may have experienced a loss of a close friend, or the end of a relationship. It is important to take the time to look at the situation objectively and work to let go of any negative emotions you may be feeling. If a relationship is salvageable, you can work to rebuild it and strengthen it.
