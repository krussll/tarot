## What does The World and 6 Of Swords mean together?

When The World tarot card and The 6 Of Swords card appear together, they point to the need for a significant transition. This could involve a physical move, such as emigration or relocation, or it could represent a spiritual journey or an emotional shift. In either case, the cards suggest that you have come to a crossroads in your life and that a necessary transition is in order if you want to fulfill your goals and complete the lesson you were meant to learn. These cards could also suggest that you need to move in order to gain a greater perspective on life. The ultimate meaning behind this combination of cards is that the transition will bring new opportunities, experiences and ultimately, a greater sense of fulfillment in life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

For Health:

The World Tarot card symbolizes the completion of a stage in life, and it can be interpreted as the attainment of wellness and balance. In terms of physical health, this card may signify a successful treatment, recovery from an illness, or the prevention of a serious health issue. It can represent a period of rest and relaxation which allows for the body to recuperate and recharge. On a more spiritual level, this card could indicate personal growth and development in one’s relationship with their physical and mental being.

For Finances:

The World Tarot card is associated with the concept of abundance, completing a project with a healthy financial outcome, and coming to a place of satisfaction and financial stability. It may signify the successful closure of a project, the successful negotiation of a contract, or the receipt of a long-term investment. It can also represent the payoff of taking a risk, or the financial reward from diligent work and effort.

For Relationships:

The World Tarot card might signify the successful closure of a relationship, or the completion of a stage in a romance. It can also signify the end of a situation in which two people have been co-dependently attached, or the completion of an entire cycle of a close relationship. It can indicate that two or more people have come to a place of understanding and acceptance, with a spirit of celebration. It also portends a period of change and growth, as two people move forward in their relationship in healthier and more sustainable ways.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 6 of Swords Tarot card represents a transition in one’s physical health. It suggests a period of transition in which one needs to move from a state of ill health or exhaustion to a healthier state. It symbolizes a journey away from illness and into a new, healthier direction. This can be a transition in lifestyle, diet, exercise, or any other means necessary to improving one’s health. This card also suggests that it is time to take action and make the necessary changes in order to achieve better health.

Finances: The 6 of Swords Tarot card can also have meaning in the area of finances. This card represents a transition in financial stability, moving away from a state of financial difficulty and into better prospects. It may represent a period of financial transition where one needs to move away from relying heavily on debt or loans and into a more stable financial situation. This can be a period of hard work and sacrifice in order to gain financial stability. It can also signify a chance to start over with respect to finances and to make better decisions this time around.

Relationships: The 6 of Swords Tarot card can also symbolize a transition in relationships. It suggests a period of transition in which one needs to move away from unhealthy relationships and into a healthier state. This can represent an ending of a long-term relationship or the beginning of something new. It may also suggest that it is time to let go or distance oneself from relationships that are too emotionally draining or draining one’s resources. This card can also symbolize a transition in communication and understanding, as it is necessary to build healthy relationships and communication. This transition can be a difficult one but is necessary for better relationships in the future.
