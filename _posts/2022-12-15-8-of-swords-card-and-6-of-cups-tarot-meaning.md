A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Swords card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Swords card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Swords and 6 Of Cups mean together?

When The 8 Of Swords and The 6 Of Cups tarot cards are combined together, it emphasizes the importance of letting go of the past and freeing yourself from the self-imposed restrictions and isolation you may feel. It serves as a reminder to move beyond nostalgia and not to be afraid to reach out to reunite with those you care about, while allowing yourself to receive the gifts of joy they bring. The mix of these two cards encourages you to leave your prison of the past and start anew, allowing a new sense of innocence and freedom to enter your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 8 of Swords Tarot card can indicate that there are some self-imposed restrictions regarding one’s health. It can suggest that someone is so overwhelmed by the fear and worry about their health that they have stopped taking care of themselves and their health. They may have stopped exercising, going to doctor’s appointments, and taking necessary medications, all out of fear and worry. This can result in physical, mental, and emotional distress.

Financial

The 8 of Swords Tarot card can indicate that there are limitations when it comes to one’s finances. It can suggest that someone has put a mental block on themselves, making it harder to make the necessary financial decisions to better their situation. They may be too scared to take risks, or they may be too scared to make the “right” decisions. They may be limiting themselves, not allowing their finances to grow to their full potential because of the fear and worry that comes with taking risks.

Relationships

The 8 of Swords Tarot card can indicate that a person is in a destructive cycle when it comes to relationships. It can suggest that someone is so overwhelmed by the fear and worry of being rejected or hurt that they have isolated themselves, not allowing for anyone to get close. They may have built up walls that keep even their closest friends and family at a distance, and this can lead to feelings of loneliness and despair. It can be hard to break the cycle and find true connection in relationships, but this card can indicate that it is possible if someone is willing to take the necessary risks and learn to trust again.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

For health, The 6 of Cups Tarot card can represent healing from emotional and spiritual wounds, as well as important memories, both good and bad. It often indicates a need to revisit the past, for better or for worse, and to bring closure or healing to old wounds that may have been left untreated. This tarot card may also indicate a return to a healthier or more balanced lifestyle, or the need to take time away from certain activities to rest and reflect on life.

For finances, The 6 of Cups Tarot card can suggest a period of financial security and stability, when income and expenses are balanced. This card can also refer to any gifts or rewards you may receive, either through inheritance or a lucky opportunity. It can also point to a time of economic generosity, of giving and receiving in equal measure.

For relationships, The 6 of Cups Tarot card usually represents reunions, innocence, and happy memories. It could also indicate a time of emotional growth and understanding, where both parties in the relationship can trust one another and set boundaries that are beneficial for both. This card can also indicate a need to take time away from the relationship and focus on your own emotional needs, so that when you come back together, the relationship is stronger and healthier.
