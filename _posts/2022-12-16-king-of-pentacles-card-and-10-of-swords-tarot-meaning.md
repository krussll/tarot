A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the King Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The King Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does King Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The King Of Pentacles tarot card and The 10 Of Swords tarot card signify a situation in which a solid and practical leader needs to assess a painful and complete ending before making any wise investments. This could mean that one must suffer a rock bottom experience before finding the wisdom and resources necessary to get back on top. For example, a leader may have failed in their current venture, but must take stock of the defeat to learn and grow from it, then use that knowledge to invest in a better future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 10 of Swords tarot card suggests that your health could be experiencing a painful period of ending. This could mean physical or mental health, or both. You might be feeling overwhelmed and exhausted, nearing the point of burnout. You could be coming to the realization that prior habits were detrimental to your wellbeing. This may manifest in dealing with the consequences of overworking, or a lifestyle that lacked proper rest and nutrition. It’s time to be honest with yourself and take a step back to reflect on the changes you need to make in order to regain your health.

Finances:  
The 10 of Swords tarot card suggests that there is a difficult ending ahead in your financial life. This could be the end of a job, a business venture, or a time of financial struggle. You could be in a place of feeling overwhelmed and unsure of what lies ahead. It is important to prioritize your finances and to make wise financial decisions during this time. Work to develop a budget that is sensible in regards to your financial goals, and stay focused on the long-term.

Relationships:  
The 10 of Swords tarot card suggests a painful and complete ending in a relationship. You could be feeling overwhelmed and heartbroken, filled with regret for how things ended. It is important to take some time to mourn the end of the relationship and to be honest about how you feel. Allow yourself to be vulnerable and to heal. You may find strength in speaking to a friend, family member, or even a therapist to help you make sense of your feelings and to help you create a healthier outlook going forward.
