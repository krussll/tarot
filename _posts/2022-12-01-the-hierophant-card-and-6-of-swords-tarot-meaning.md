## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is associated with health in that it reminds us of the importance of traditions when it comes to our physical and mental well-being. Conventionally, many cultures follow practices that are inherently beneficial to our health, such as regular exercise, proper diet, and even abstaining from bad habits like smoking. By adhering to these traditional principles, we can keep ourselves healthy and free of illness. Additionally, the Hierophant can remind us of the value of seeking out advice from licensed medical professionals when it comes to making decisions that affect our health. Following their recommendations can help us manage our physical and mental health, while avoiding dangerous or ill-advised treatments.

Finances:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is associated with finances in that it reminds us of the importance of following traditions when it comes to fiscal matters. We must be mindful of the consequences of our actions, as overspending can easily lead to difficult financial situations. Additionally, the Hierophant encourages us to practice fiscal responsibility and to take into account long-term financial planning when making decisions that affect our monetary lives. By carefully considering our options, we can make decisions that will benefit us financially in the long run.

Relationships:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is associated with relationships in that it encourages us to abide by conventional norms and expectations when interacting with others. When it comes to intimate relationships, such as those between family members, it encourages us to maintain traditional values such as respect, communication, and understanding. Additionally, when dealing with business transactions, we should strive to be courteous and honest to our customers and potential clients, while not sacrificing our own values in favor of theirs. Through following conventional principles, we can build meaningful relationships that are based on mutual respect and trust.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Six of Swords Tarot card is often associated with health. It typically signifies a need to step away from unhealthy habits and behaviors and make a conscious choice to begin a new path of health and wellness. This could be a physical or emotional transition, or a complete lifestyle change. This card suggests that an individual should take the necessary steps to make a significant difference in their health, be it physical or mental. A person who is dealing with poor health should look for ways to make changes that will better serve them and their overall wellbeing. This may mean seeking guidance from a professional and taking proactive steps to get back into a healthier lifestyle.

Finances:  
The Six of Swords Tarot card often points to financial transitions. This card indicates that an individual should take a step back from existing debt and money worries and begin a fresh path of money management and wise investing. This could be as simple and small as choosing to save for a rainy day or investing in stocks. It could also signify a larger commitment such as downsizing, debt consolidation, and budgeting. The implications of this card advise a person to be frugal and logical with their finances and make a long-term plan that will lead to financial stability.

Relationships:  
When it comes to relationships, The Six of Swords Tarot card often holds a deeper and more complex meaning. This card can suggest the need for a person to step back and reassess any relationships that may be causing distress or unhappiness. It can indicate a necessary shift of focus and perspective in order to move forward with a healthier and more beneficial outcome. This could mean stepping out of a destructive cycle or leaving a toxic relationship. It may also suggest that a person is able to make peace with a former lover and find a way to move on. In essence, this card can signify a need for a fresh start for both parties in order to find more fulfilling and healthier relationships down the line.
