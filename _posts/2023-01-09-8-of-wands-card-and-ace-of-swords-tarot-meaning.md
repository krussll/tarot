A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and Ace Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and Ace Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and Ace Of Swords mean together?

The 8 Of Wands tarot card combined with The Ace Of Swords tarot card suggests a fast-paced journey that brings about quick changes. It is a time of new ideas and mental clarity where one may experience a new conflict or even surgery. News will arrive swiftly and likely bring about a transformation of ideas. This could also be a time to act quickly in order to make the most out of these new changes, as this is a time of great potential and opportunity.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

For health, the 8 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of taking action and making quick changes to improve one’s health. This could suggest making an immediate change in diet, starting a new exercise routine, or taking up yoga or meditation in order to improve overall wellbeing. This card could also suggest seeking out the help of medical professionals, such as therapists, nutritionists, and doctors, to create a healthier lifestyle.

For finances, the 8 of Wands Tarot card suggests taking action to improve one’s financial circumstances. This could include making quick decisions in order to manage debt, create a budget and save for the future. It could also suggest speaking to a financial advisor or taking a class to increase knowledge about managing one’s finances.

For relationships, the 8 of Wands Tarot card is a sign to take action and make necessary changes in order to strengthen relationships and mend any issues that may have come up. This could mean having a conversation with a partner or friend to resolve a conflict, setting boundaries in order to create healthier dynamics, or taking the initiative to plan fun activities or dates in order to create more positive connections. The 8 of Wands Tarot card is also a sign to take time to reflect on relationships and decide whether to continue or if it is time to walk away.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, the Ace of Swords Tarot card indicates that surgery might be necessary in order to restore balance and improve overall wellbeing. This card is associated with mental clarity and suggests that the person should use their intellect and resolve to make decisions that promote health and stability. This card also suggests that any underlying psychological issues should be addressed immediately in order to maintain optimal mental health.

When it comes to finances, the Ace of Swords Tarot card indicates that the person should take decisive action in order to create financial stability. This card encourages the person to use their intellect to make smart decisions that will result in increased income and decreased spending. The person should strive to cut expenses and make investments that will bring rewards in the future.

When it comes to relationships, the Ace of Swords Tarot card suggests that the person should approach the situation with openness and honesty. There could be conflict in order to reach a resolution, but this should be done with fairness and respect. This card encourages the person to use their intellect to make difficult decisions and interact in ways that foster a healthier and more harmonious relationship.
