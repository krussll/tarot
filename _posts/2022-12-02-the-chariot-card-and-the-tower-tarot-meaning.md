A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and The Tower card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and The Tower have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and The Tower mean together?

The Chariot tarot card and The Tower tarot card together indicate that you are about to experience a great deal of action and change in your life. Although this change may be difficult, it is necessary for you to move forward. You may feel as if you are undertaking a journey, a new vehicle of some kind, to help you get to the next place. However, you must be prepared for the unforeseen and some unwanted change, ruin, or upheaval along the way. This change may bring some disgrace or violence but it is a necessary part of your journey to self-discovery.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Chariot and The Tower mean together for your love life?

The Chariot and The Tower tarot cards together can indicate a time of major, sometimes unexpected, change in your love life. This can be a turbulent period, and one that can take you off guard and leave you feeling vulnerable. Symbolically, The Chariot represents a journey, a vehicle for change, and the ability to move forward no matter the obstacle. However, The Tower symbolizes a feeling of ruin or descent, of chaos and upheaval. This can lead to feelings of disgrace or uncertainty, of not knowing what lies ahead or of being unable to control the course of events.

In terms of love, The Chariot indicates that you are setting off on a journey, that there is movement and action, but as always with The Tower card, these changes will not happen as expected. It could signify an unexpected breakup, a sudden change of circumstances, or the realization that you are no longer on the same path as your partner. This card combination may be indicative of a need to let go of your previous relationships, to start anew and take risks.

Despite the uncertainty and changes that come with both of these cards, it is important to remember that change is a necessary part of life and love. The Chariot asks you to be brave and strong in your conviction and to seize the opportunity for change, to jump into the fray and accept whatever comes your way. With The Tower, you need to accept that sometimes change and upheaval can be a powerful force, and that there is a chance for a new structured life and a new look at love. The combination of these two cards can lead to a time of renewal and growth, and ultimately, a stronger, more fulfilled relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Chariot and The Tower mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card is a symbol of change, and can therefore denote a period of upheaval in all areas of life, including health, finances and relationships. In terms of health, it may mean a sudden, unwelcome or distressing medical diagnosis or setback; or alternatively, a period of physical transformation or recovery from a long-term illness. In terms of finances, it could suggest the sudden loss of a job, an unexpected financial crisis, or even a windfall of wealth. In terms of relationships, the Tower Tarot card could suggest the sudden end of a relationship, the breakdown of a long-term partnership, or an introduction of a new and unexpected ally.

The Tower Tarot card is associated with destruction, ruin, embarrassment and destruction, and therefore can represent a period of instability and disruption. In terms of health, it may suggest a period of physical trouble, as well as emotional distress; in terms of finances, it may signify financial ruin; and in relationships, it may indicate a broken trust or bond. The Tower Tarot card is also linked to feelings of vulnerability and a sense of being exposed and powerless. During this period of upheaval, it is important to be mindful of our own emotional health and to try to find a sense of peace amidst the chaos.

The Tower Tarot card can also signify a period of transformation and renewal. In terms of health, it may indicate a time of healing and recovery from physical or mental illness; in terms of finances, it might suggest the emergence of new opportunities to gain financial wealth; and in terms of relationships, it may signify a new beginning and a fresh start with a partner or friend. The Tower Tarot card is associated with change and transformation, and this period of disruption can be a time for re-evaluating and re-focusing your goals and ambitions.

The Tower Tarot card is a reminder that change can be both unwelcome and exciting, and that it is important to stay flexible and open-minded in all areas of life. It can be a difficult card to interpret but its message of transformation, upheaval and change can be reassuring in challenging times.
