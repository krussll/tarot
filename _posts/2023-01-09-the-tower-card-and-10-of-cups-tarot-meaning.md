A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The 10 Of Cups tarot card together symbolize the potential for a difficult transition within a loving family. While it is possible that this transition may include disruption, hardship, and unwelcome change, the ultimate goal is that by facing these challenges, the family will be closer and more strengthened by the experience. There may be upheaval and violence along the way, but the end result will be greater love, support, and contentment among the family members, making the hard times worthwhile.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Tower and 10 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When we look at the Tarot cards of The Tower and The 10 Of Cups side-by-side, the juxtaposition may seem jarring our first. The Tower offers a cautionary message about unexpected and unwanted change, ruin and upheaval, while The 10 Of Cups evokes the emotions of contentment, total love and support. Together, these Tarot cards can provide a valuable insight into the state of any relationship.

The Tower Tarot card often indicates that something has gone awry in a love relationship and that a period of disruption and upheaval has been set into motion. This doesn’t always mean a breakup, but it could indicate that one or both parties involved in the relationship will be facing major changes – whether these changes are positive or not is still to be determined. It is important for both parties to examine their individual needs and to communicate openly and honestly with each other in order to reach a healthy and mutually beneficial solution.

The 10 Of Cups Tarot card reflects the ultimate picture of love and harmony in a relationship. It is a card of connection and commitment, expressing all the joy and contentment that comes from knowing you are loved and supported. If a relationship is already in a good place, the 10 Of Cups indicates that both parties are ready to move forward into a deeper level of love, connection, and trust.

When The Tower and The 10 Of Cups Tarot cards appear together, they can indicate either a happy or difficult time in a relationship. It could signify that a current relationship is ready to take the next step into a deeper, more fulfilling connection or that a troubled relationship is going to experience some significant changes. Whatever the situation, it is important to approach the challenges with an open and honest heart. If both parties can do this with courage and commitment, they have the potential to build a strong and lasting bond with each other.

### What does The Tower and 10 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When The Tower tarot card and The 10 Of Cups tarot card are drawn together, it points to an unwelcome change in your financial and professional life. This change will be disruptive and lead to upheaval, ruin, and possibly even violence. It could mean losing your job or being forced to take a drastic cut in salary, or even facing bankruptcy. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: The Tower tarot card is a powerful card that can indicate a major transformation taking place, in this case in regard to one’s health. It can symbolize disease, illness, or physical injury, though the severity of the transformation will depend on the surrounding cards in the reading. It can also be a warning to take better care of one’s health, as the lightning-filled tower on the card is a warning of the destruction that could occur should one not heed it.

Finances: For those seeking guidance on their finances, The Tower tarot card can be an uncomfortable one. It is a warning that something is out of balance, and that one must take action to right the ship. It can signify a sudden loss of money, bankruptcy, or a large financial setback. However, it can also point to a restructuring of one’s finances that ultimately leads to financial freedom and stability.

Relationships: The Tower tarot card can be a difficult one when it comes to relationships, as the card can indicate a major restructuring taking place. It can signify a break up, a divorce, or a shift in the dynamics of a relationship. This transformation can be difficult, especially if it is unwanted, as it can symbolize the destruction of the existing foundation. However, it can also point to a process of healing and growth, as the destruction of the Tower can lead to the creation of something new.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a card of family contentment and total love and support. It is a sign of emotional fulfillment and personal happiness, often associated with being content in one’s own life.

Health: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of good health, indicating that physical and mental well-being is of the utmost importance. It suggests that one should take good care of themselves and cultivate positive lifestyle habits, such as regular exercise, healthy eating, and sufficient rest.

Finances: The 10 of Cups Tarot card suggests financial stability and the ability to be prudent with one’s money. Investing in the future often requires taking calculated risks, but this card warns against reckless spending. It encourages one to save and make sensible financial decisions in order to achieve long-term wealth.

Relationships: The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of strong, supportive relationships. It suggests that one should cultivate meaningful relationships with both family and friends, as this is essential for true happiness. It also encourages one to be open, honest, and forgiving of others in order to maintain healthy and balanced relationships.
