A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Swords card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Swords card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The 6 Of Swords and the 10 Of Swords tarot card together tell a story of a necessary but difficult transition. It suggests that you are at a difficult and painful ending of a situation in your life and may need to relocate or move to be able to start anew. It may be a hard transition, and it might bring about some harsh consequences, but it must be done in order for you to reach the next phase of your life. It’s a reminder that some endings can be necessary in order to start a new chapter of your life and that even though the transition can be hard, it will be worth it in the end.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

If the 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords tarot cards show up together in a reading regarding a love life, it could be a warning sign of a necessary but painfully ending transition. This usually points to a difficult yet necessary break-up or heartache. It could mean that a person has to go through some pain and suffering as part of a necessary transition in order to move on from a relationship. It could indicate that the individual needs to leave their current relationship in order to make room for better things to come.

It could also mean that someone is having to go through a relocation because of their relationship. Whether the two are moving in together or just having to part ways due to a long-distance relationship, it could mean this is a difficult and painful transition that needs to be made in order to move forward. It could be a sign that the couple needs to make the painful decision to end the relationship in order to honor their individual needs and growth.

Overall, the 6 Of Swords in tandem with the 10 Of Swords can point to a difficult transition in a love life that may bring heartache and suffering but is ultimately necessary for growth. It can mean that sometimes the best thing to do is to make the difficult and often painful decision to make a change and move on from the relationship. This could mean a breakup or a relocation, but it could also mean using the experience as a starting point for the next chapter in someone’s love life.

### What does 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

The 6 of Swords tarot card and the 10 of Swords tarot card commonly mean a difficult period of transition in your finances and job. The 6 of Swords often refers to a necessary transition that is on your horizon and this could be anything from a relocation or moving, to changing your job or switching up your financial goals and strategies. It’s a card of transition where you will be able to reach your destination, but only if you take a risk and leave behind what you are comfortable with.

The 10 of Swords usually refers to a complete and painful ending to something that was once stable. It could be an agonizing end to a job or a financial situation that you’ve exhausted all your resources in order to maintain. While this tarot card can be difficult to process, it is important to remember that this ending is bringing forth a necessary transition and ultimately, leading you to a better place.

When you put together the 6 of Swords and the 10 of Swords tarot cards, it’s likely there will be a difficult financial or job situation that you must leave behind in order to make the necessary changes in your life. While this may seem daunting and overwhelming, taking the risk of transitioning to something better is worth the effort as it will bring forth a lot of positive changes. The ending of this financial or job situation will be difficult, but that doesn’t mean it won’t be beneficial in the long run.

### What does 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The 6 of Swords tarot card and the 10 of Swords tarot card can signify a major shift in your health, one that may be necessary and painful. You may need to make adjustments to your lifestyle and your habits for the sake of improving your health, such as taking up a physical activity or eating a healthier diet. This transition may feel like a big challenge at first but it is important to remember that it is ultimately for the better and can offer you the potential for better health in the long run.

The 10 of Swords tarot card, on the other hand, can signify your reaching a point of rock bottom, in regards to your health. This could be a time where you recognize that the habits, lifestyle, and routine you have been practicing is not conducive to your health and wellbeing. This could be a time where you are ready to make a change, to do the hard work that it takes to care for yourself better.

By recognizing the potential for the 6 of Swords and the 10 of Swords tarot cards together, you can use them as a guide for understanding where you are in your journey towards better health, and what needs to be done from here. Whether it is a lifestyle shift or recognizing when it is time to make a move, this pair of cards can motivate you to take the actions necessary for long term health and wellbeing.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Swords and 10 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 6 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a need for change, transition and movement in relation to health. This card can indicate a crossroads in one’s health, where a difficult decision needs to be made. This could be a transition from one state of health to another, or from one treatment to another. Alternatively, it could indicate a transition from one type of lifestyle to another, such as changing from an unhealthy lifestyle to a healthier lifestyle. The 6 of Swords encourages us to make the transition necessary for a more healthy and balanced life.

Finances: The 6 of Swords Tarot card indicates a transition period in financial matters. This card can suggest that a new job or opportunity may arise and one needs to make a move to take advantage of it. This could also refer to a need to make changes to one’s budget or financial management in order to ensure a secure future. This card can also mean that a difficult decision may need to be made regarding a financial matter, such as taking out a loan, investing in a business, or selling off assets. Whatever the case may be, the 6 of Swords encourages us to make the necessary transition in order to secure our financial future.

Relationships: The 6 of Swords Tarot card can relate to the need for transition and change in relationships. This card may indicate a need to let go of an unhealthy relationship, or to move on from a destructive pattern of behavior. Alternatively, this card could mean a transition from one relationship to another, or from a place of stagnation to a place of growth. This card encourages us to make the necessary changes in order to build healthy, fulfilling and satisfying relationships.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 Of Swords Tarot card is a clear indication of a life in turmoil. It symbolizes a devastating situation, characterized by pain and complete defeat. It can sometimes be seen as a representation of rock bottom, when a person’s life has become unbearable and they find themselves in an utterly defeated state.

In terms of health, The 10 Of Swords Tarot card serves as a reminder that our health is one of the most important aspects of life, and should not be taken for granted. This card can indicate that a person is feeling overwhelmed and exhausted due to physical or mental ailments. It’s a warning that a person’s health can reach a critical point if they don’t take proactive steps to look after themselves.

When it comes to finances, The 10 Of Swords Tarot card warns against making decisions that could take a person to the brink of financial ruin. This card can be interpreted as a sign that a person’s finances are in disarray. It may be indicative of a financial predicament that is beyond the person’s control, or the result of reckless choices. This card warns people to exercise extreme caution when it comes to managing their money, as the potential for an unbearable financial situation is high.

In terms of relationships, The 10 Of Swords Tarot card warns that a person’s relationships could be reaching a breaking point. This card can indicate a toxic relationship, one that is filled with pain, hurt and lack of trust. It’s a sign that this relationship might be beyond repair, and the person needs to find the strength to walk away in order to heal. This card serves as a reminder to learn from difficult relationships and take the necessary steps to create healthy, fulfilling relationships.
