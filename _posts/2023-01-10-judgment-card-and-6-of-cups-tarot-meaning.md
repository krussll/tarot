A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and The 6 Of Cups tarot card together suggest that you need to bring clarity to a past issue that is currently causing you distress. It is time for you to make a final decision so that you can move forward in life and follow your true purpose. You may feel a strong sense of nostalgia as you explore the past, but this is a necessary part of your journey in order to find closure. The 6 Of Cups encourages you to appreciate the simple joys of life and the gifts that come with reunions. Take time to nurture your innocence and be thankful for the relationships that are rebuilding and coming together.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

On the other hand, the 6 of Cups tarot card can also represent gifts and reunions. This could mean reconnecting with an old lover or friend. Sometimes we need to go back to where we started in order to move forward.

Overall, the Judgment tarot card and the 6 of Cups tarot card can offer some very valuable insight into your love life. If you take the time to reflect and make the necessary changes, you’ll be able to move forward and find clarity and peace in your relationships.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Judgment mean?

Health: The Judgment Tarot card could indicate that it is time to be honest with yourself and your health situation. It could be a wake-up call to take better care of yourself. The card may also signify that you need to make a decision regarding a health-related matter. It is possible that you need to seek out a healer or professional who can help you make this decision.

Finances: When The Judgment Tarot card appears, it may be a sign that you need to take a look at your financial situation. Are you acting in an honest and responsible way with your money or are you being careless? Perhaps you need to be clearer about your budget and find solutions to maintain a healthy financial future.

Relationships: The Judgment Tarot card can also point to the need for a final decision when it comes to relationships. It’s time to take an honest look at the dynamics within a relationship and decide if it is your life’s purpose to stay together or if it is time to move on. The card is a reminder to examine our relationships and be honest with ourselves and our partners.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

For Health:  
The 6 of Cups in a Tarot reading symbolizes a fresh start, a renewed outlook. It suggests that you are entering a period of healing, and that you are being provided the opportunity to take care of your health and to explore new areas of wellness. This card can represent a cleansing or a detox, either of the body or the mind. You may find yourself getting involved in activities such as yoga, meditation or other spiritual practices for the first time that bring you mental and physical relief. Taking a break from the stresses of life and focusing on your wellbeing can help you to restore balance.

For Finances:  
The 6 of Cups in a Tarot reading suggests that you are connecting with your own inner resources and that a period of prosperity and financial stability is ahead. This card can indicate that you have taken a step back and allowed yourself to look past the materialistic world into what truly matters. Money is no longer your primary focus, and instead you are more concerned with providing yourself with security and stability. You may find that with your new attitude towards finances, you can build a better future and achieve financial freedom.

For Relationships:  
The 6 of Cups in a Tarot reading means that you are looking at relationships in a new light. Relationships are no longer viewed as a burden and you may even find yourself feeling a sense of nostalgia for the past. This card can represent possibilities for renewal, forgiveness and reunions with friends or family. You may find yourself setting positive boundaries and communicating better with those around you. An appreciation for the past and acceptance of the present can help you to cultivate healthier relationships and find happiness in your connections.
