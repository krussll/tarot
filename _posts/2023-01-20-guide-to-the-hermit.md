## The Hermit – An Overview

The Hermit tarot card encourages us to take a journey of self-discovery and take time to be alone and reflect. It symbolizes the need to go within and search the depths of our own souls for clarity, personal truth and spiritual illumination. The Hermit encourages us to listen to our inner voice and gain insight into ourselves, our life and our purpose. The Hermit teaches us to trust our intuition and to listen to our inner wisdom. It also encourages us to take the necessary steps to create a life that is aligned with our highest truth, so that we can live in harmony with ourselves and all of creation. By taking this inward journey, we can tap into our own inner power and live an authentic, meaningful and fulfilling life.

## Upright Meanings Of The Hermit

### What It Means For Me

The Hermit tarot card means that I should go inward and reflect on my own truth and spiritual enlightenment. It encourages me to take a step back and see the bigger picture of life, to accept my own beliefs and values, and to learn from my own experiences. It also reminds me to take time for myself and to be alone in order to find clarity. This card helps me to understand that sometimes I need to pause and be still in order to appreciate the beauty in my life. By looking within, I can find the answers I need to move forward in a positive and meaningful way.

### What It Means For The Future

The Hermit tarot card is a reminder that I should take some time to focus on myself and get in tune with my inner self. It is a sign that I should take some time away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life to go within and look for the answers that I need. It is also a reminder that I should go on a spiritual journey, exploring my own truth and gaining a better understanding of my purpose in life. The Hermit tarot card is a call for me to find clarity and illumination through self-reflection and inner work. By doing this, I can gain a deeper understanding of myself and my place in the world.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Hermit tarot card reminds us to take some time to reflect on our own personal truths and values, and to take a step back from our relationships with those close to us. It encourages us to seek out greater clarity and understanding by looking inward, and to use that newfound enlightenment to better understand our loved ones. By taking a step back and going within, we can learn to better understand our relationships and how to foster more meaningful connections with those around us. Ultimately, The Hermit tarot card encourages us to prioritize our spiritual and emotional journeys in order to strengthen our relationships with those closest to us.

## Reversed Meanings Of The Hermit

### What It Means For Me

When the reversed Hermit tarot card appears in a reading, it can be an indication of stagnation, a lack of clarity, or a disconnection from one’s inner truth. This can manifest in my life as a feeling of being stuck in the same place, unable to move forward or make progress. I may be feeling overwhelmed or confused, without the clarity and insight necessary to figure out the next steps to take.

This card encourages me to be more introspective and to really take the time to go within and reconnect with my true self. I should take a step back and examine my thoughts and feelings more deeply, seeking greater understanding and insight. With this newfound clarity, I should be able to make more informed decisions that will lead me down the path towards my goals. Additionally, I should be open to guidance from the Universe and to embrace a more spiritual, mindful approach to life.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Hermit tarot card indicates that you are on the verge of a spiritual breakthrough. You are on the cusp of a period of deep inner exploration, where you may find yourself on a quest to find your own personal truth. This may be a time of great uncertainty, where you feel lost and confused. However, this is an opportunity for you to gain profound insight into yourself and your place in the world. It is a time to be introspective and ask yourself the difficult questions.

The reversed Hermit tarot card also signals a need for you to take a step back from the stressful and chaotic aspects of life in order to gain a better perspective. You may need to take some time for yourself to rest and reflect in order to gain the clarity and insight necessary to make important decisions. This period of inner exploration can be a time of great illumination and growth, as you are allowed to go deep within to uncover the truth of your own soul. Ultimately, the reversed Hermit tarot card is a reminder to trust yourself and your intuition, as this is the key to finding peace and happiness.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the Hermit tarot card is reversed, it can indicate that those close to me are not living up to their potential. It can suggest that they are being too comfortable in their position and not pushing themselves to find deeper meaning and spiritual enlightenment in their lives. They may be too focused on the material world and have trouble seeing beyond the mundane. This could be causing them some inner turmoil and dissatisfaction that they are unable to express.

The reversed Hermit can also mean that those close to me are not connecting with their inner wisdom or spiritual calling. They may be feeling lost and disconnected from their true self, and are not taking the time to look within for answers. This could be resulting in confusion and lack of direction, and making it difficult for them to find their path. It is important for them to take time for themselves and reflect on what is important to them and how to move forward in a meaningful way.
