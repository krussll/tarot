A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 5 Of Wands and 10 Of Pentacles tarot cards together suggest that you are being challenged and must overcome obstacles in order to create something of lasting value. This could be in the form of building a legacy, leaving a financial inheritance, or creating a successful business. The 5 Of Wands also suggest that this challenge is not just a solo venture; it may involve some friendly competition or rivalry of some sort. The 10 Of Pentacles symbolises the retirement of the challenge and the reward it brings. Although it may require hard work and perseverance, success is within your grasp and the rewards will last beyond your lifetime.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 5 Of Wands and 10 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

The 5 of Wands is associated with the challenges and obstacles that must be overcome to move forward in health, finances and relationships. In regards to health, the 5 of Wands symbolizes the challenges we may face when starting a new fitness routine, or tackling a health issue. These challenges can range from working out in the gym, to making healthy lifestyle changes, to managing a chronic illness. It is important to stay focused and acknowledge that progress can take time, however, with dedication and hard work success can be achieved.

In terms of finances, the 5 of Wands represents the obstacles we must overcome to achieve our financial goals. This card can indicate the need to be creative and resourceful when it comes to money. We may need to put in extra effort to make our money stretch further, find additional sources of income, or to make wise investments. The 5 of Wands can symbolize a competitive attitude towards financial matters and the need to stay ahead of the game.

Within relationships, the 5 of Wands can signify a certain level of rivalry and competitiveness. This card can indicate that we need to work hard to succeed in a relationship and that we should be open to both challenges and compromises. We must be willing to acknowledge our differences and put in the effort to maintain a healthy and balanced relationship. The 5 of Wands can also bring to light the need to respect each other’s boundaries, to have patience, and to approach conflicts and disagreements with understanding.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with leaving a legacy, retirement, and inheritance. This card represents the culmination of hard work and the successful completion of tasks. It symbolizes the rewards, stability, and abundance that come from living a life of discipline and focus. It also speaks to the importance of planning for the future and using your experiences and wisdom to benefit those who come after you.

Health-wise, this card can represent physical and mental well-being. It suggests that hard work, consistency, and a holistic approach to health (including both the physical body and the mind) will result in a life of stability and abundance. Additionally, it can be interpreted as a reminder to be mindful of the skills and resources you have, as they will be invaluable to your future.

Financially, the 10 of Pentacles Tarot card signifies wealth, stability, and success. It suggests that financial security is possible through hard work, careful planning, and wise investments. It is also a reminder to not only focus on the present but think ahead to your future and the legacy you would like to leave behind.

Relationship-wise, this card speaks to the deeper bonds that come when two people have committed to build a life together. It is a reminder to be mindful of what you bring to the table, both in terms of your own resources and experiences, as well as the wealth and wisdom of those in your past. It also suggests that it is possible to find true stability and lasting love through mutual respect and dedication.

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of the power of hard work, wise investments, and careful planning for the future. From health to finance to relationships, it is a sign of success and abundance. It is also a reminder to use our experiences and wisdom to benefit those who come after us and to live life with purpose and intention.
