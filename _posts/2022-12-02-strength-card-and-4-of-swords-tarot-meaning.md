A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Strength card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The Strength tarot card and the 4 Of Swords tarot card together imply the importance of self-care. Self-love and respect are essential for finding the courage to take meaningful action. But this action needs to be balanced with times of restful retreat. As the 4 Of Swords tarot card implies, it’s important to take time to restore, recover and renew our energy, in solitude if necessary. This letting go is necessary to open ourselves up to new experiences and make room for new opportunities. It is a process of actively choosing to take care of ourselves, as if we were our own best friend.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Strength and 4 Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Strength and 4 Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is one of the major Arcana cards, which are the most powerful cards of the Tarot deck, and symbolizes courage, confidence, and the power of the inner self. This card often appears when we are faced with a situation requiring us to find inner strength and courage to stand up for our beliefs and take decisive action. In terms of health, it suggests that we should focus on our own physical and emotional strength, and develop self-compassion in order to stay healthy and balanced.

In terms of finances, Strength Tarot card encourages us to use our own internal strength to create financial stability and long-term sustainability. We should tackle our finances with courage and self-belief, and take the necessary steps to improving our financial health. Strength Tarot is also an invitation to find a balanced approach when it comes to money; neither extremes of overspending nor cutting back are recommended.

In terms of relationships, the Strength Tarot card encourages us to foster strong, healthy relationships with our partners, friends, and family. We should have the courage to stand up for our relationships and trust our instincts. In addition, we should tap into our inner strength and be confident in our decisions and how we love. Always remember to stay authentic and true to yourself and your relationships.

Overall, Strength Tarot card encourages us to find courage and trust in ourselves, stay balanced and authentic in our relationships, and create financial stability. By doing so, we can foster a healthy and vibrant life.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

The 4 of Swords tarot card is a symbol of rest and retreat. It suggests taking a break from everyday life to focus on yourself and your wellbeing. This can be especially beneficial for health and finances, as it allows you to step away from stressful situations and take a much needed respite. It encourages you to relax and recover from any physical, emotional, or mental exhaustion. Taking time for yourself to recuperate and reset can be beneficial for both your mental and physical health, as it allows your body to rest and repair.

In terms of finances, the 4 of Swords tarot card encourages you to create a plan that allows you to maintain a healthy balance between work, rest, and relaxation. By taking time to rest and recharge, you can return to your financial situation with a clear head and the energy you need to make smart financial decisions.

The 4 of Swords tarot card also holds a special place in relationships. This card suggests that it’s okay to take a break from the demands of a relationship and take some time to yourself. Doing so can help you re-evaluate the relationship and reset it on a more stable foundation, allowing both parties to communicate better and to connect on a deeper level. Taking some time apart can help you grow individually and as a couple, allowing you to come back and reconnect in a healthier and more satisfying way.
