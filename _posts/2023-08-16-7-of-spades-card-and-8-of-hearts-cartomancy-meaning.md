A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, seeing The 7 of Spades and The 8 of Hearts together could represent a tumultuous period marked by trickery and eventual triumph.

The 7 of Spades, symbolizing deceit and victory, hints at the possibility of overcoming malicious intentions or lies told to you.

This struggle will not be easy, as denoted by its winter season association, characterized by harsh conditions and difficulty.

However, the element of water suggests an underlying resiliency and adaptability that can help you navigate these challenges.

This phase is followed by The 8 of Hearts, a card embodying moving on and power.

This card’s alignment with spring, the season of renewal, and the fire element suggest an impressive recovery from hardship and a period of intense growth and empowerment.

Therefore, these cards together hint at the bitter trials you may face but ultimately reassure you of your potential victory and rejuvenation.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, if The 7 of Spades emerges, it signifies the presence or probability of deceit within a relationship.

This could suggest hidden emotions, infidelity, or dishonesty.

Signifying victory, the card also portrays the emerging victorious from such a situation, maybe by finding the truth or overcoming the deceit.

Associated with the cold season of Winter and element Water, it might suggest a period of emotional turbulence, mystery, or inward focus in your love life.

On the other hand, The 8 of Hearts represents moving on and power.

In the context of your love life, this card suggests that, despite the chaos or difficulties brought by the deceit indicated by The 7 of Spades, you would find the strength within yourself to move forward.

This card represents a transition phase, where you let go of the past and embrace the future, finding new strength and emerging more powerful.

Mirroring the vitality of Spring and the energy of Fire, it suggests a time of renewal, growth, and transformation, and might hint towards gaining newfound independence or taking charge of your love life after a difficult phase.

Together, these cards indicate a challenging yet transformative journey in your love life.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Hearts together indicate a powerful transition in your financial situation and employment.

The 7 of Spades, with its associations of deceit and victory, might imply that you may be in a position at work or financially where things seem dubious, perhaps because the whole truth about certain matters is being hidden from you.

However, it also indicates victory, suggesting that in spite of the apparent deceit, you are destined to achieve a significant win in your financial or professional life.

This victory might come to you as a welcome surprise, given you were unsure about many uncertain factors that may have been causing anxiety.

The 8 of Hearts signifies movement and power, which in terms of your career or finances, indicates that you will be moving on to a better place.

This could manifest as a significant increase in your financial status or perhaps a more empowering position in your job.

The element of fire associated with this card symbolizes the transformation and purging of the old to be replaced with the new and better.

The connected season of Spring perhaps points to a period of growth and renewal.

So, the combination of these two cards in a cartomancy reading suggests a transition towards victory and more power in your financial and professional sectors after overcoming a period of hidden obstacles or uncertainty.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

In a health-related cartomancy reading, the appearance of the 7 of Spades indicates possible deceit or hidden information.

This could denote a lack of transparency regarding your health or perhaps an aspect of your health that you’ve been neglecting or ignoring.

Its associated season, Winter, further underscores a period of potential challenge or lack of vitality.

Its element, Water, is tied to emotions and intuition – suggesting that you might need to tune in more to your body’s signals or consult a health professional to uncover what could be lurking beneath the surface.

On the other hand, the 8 of Hearts speaks of moving on and power.

In a health context, this could imply recovery from illness, pursuing a healthier lifestyle, or overcoming a health challenge.

Its associated season, Spring, is a time of renewal and growth, hinting at the possibility of a positive turnaround in your health situation or a time to revive and refresh your health goals.

The element Fire is linked to transformation and energy; thereby showing that you have the power and the energy required to take charge and improve your health condition.

Overall, these cards suggest that while you may face certain health obstacles, you have the determination and resilience needed to overcome them.
