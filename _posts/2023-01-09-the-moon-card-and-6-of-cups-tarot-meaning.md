## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Moon mean?

The Moon Tarot card can signify a variety of different messages when it comes to health, finances, and relationships. When it comes to health, it can represent unseen issues that require attention, such as mental health issues, or physical illnesses that may be more difficult to detect. It implies that you should be aware of the warning signs, but also trust your intuition. Taking care of yourself and being conscious of your health is important, and The Moon encourages you to remain diligent and take care of yourself.

When it comes to finances, The Moon can point to changes that are voluntary, but also potentially necessary. It is important to pay attention to our financial state and determine what changes can be made in order to improve it. We should also be willing to adapt and make necessary changes as our finances can ladder up to our mental and physical health. Trust your intuition and be mindful of your money and financial decisions.

Lastly, in terms of relationships, The Moon can represent a desire to trust your intuition rather than necessarily making a decision. It can be wise to trust yourself and your instincts when it comes to romantic and platonic relationships. This card can point to either ending a relationship that is not serving you, or working to make the relationship healthier with communication and trust. With regards to meeting new people, The Moon can imply an opportunity to make changes in your life, and to trust your gut when it comes to allowing people into your life. Taking your time to trust yourself and your instincts can be beneficial in the long run.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 6 of Cups often refers to the need for nourishment, whether physical or emotional. It symbolizes health, regeneration and self-care. Healthwise, it suggests taking the time out to invest in yourself and nurture your physical and mental health. It may also indicate that you’re due for some rest and relaxation. Taking care of your physical and emotional needs is an important part of overall health.

Finances  
The 6 of Cups card may signify an acceptance of the financial reality around you. It suggests that the time is now to be conservative and responsible with your finances. It can also mean to keep your boundaries in mind when it comes to lending money or taking out loans or credit. Be mindful of what you can and can’t afford, as well as setting appropriate financial boundaries.

Relationships  
The 6 of Cups speaks of nostalgia and reunions, and often suggests that it’s time to reunite with those who have had an effect on your life. It’s a reminder to reconnect with people from your past, or strengthen your current relationships. This can also mean it’s time to let go of the people who no longer serve you in a positive way. The 6 of Cups signifies harmony and balance within your relationships.
