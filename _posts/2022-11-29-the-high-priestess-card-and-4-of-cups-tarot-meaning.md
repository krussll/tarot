A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The High Priestess card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The High Priestess card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The High Priestess and 4 Of Cups mean together?

If The High Priestess and The Four Of Cups come up together in a reading, it generally means that someone is in danger of becoming too wrapped up in their own little world and forgetting about the rest of the world around them. This can be a health hazard, as it can lead to isolation and depression, or it can simply be a sign that someone is not paying attention to what is happening in their relationship and is letting it slide into apathy. In either case, it is important to remember that there are other people in the world who care about you and who can help you if you let them. Don’t be afraid to reach out to others for help or advice when you need it.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The High Priestess and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The High Priestess and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The High Priestess mean?

The High Priestess Tarot card can symbolize many things in different areas of life. In terms of health, The High Priestess can represent hidden health concerns or conditions that may only be discovered through intuition or a higher level of awareness. This may also be a sign to trust your gut instinct when it comes to your health, and to listen to your body’s inner wisdom. In terms of finances, The High Priestess can represent hidden assets or sources of income that may not be immediately apparent. This may also be a sign to trust your intuition when it comes to financial matters, and to be open to new and unexpected sources of abundance. In terms of relationships, The High Priestess can symbolize the need for intuition, trust, and communication. This may be a sign that there are things going on behind the scenes that you are not yet aware of, or that your partner is keeping secrets from you. This may also be a sign to trust your intuition when it comes to relationships, and to listen to your inner voice.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 4 of Cups tarot card can indicate a period of apathy or dissatisfaction with your current health situation. You may feel unfulfilled or that your health is not what it could be. This is a time to take stock of your health and make changes if necessary. The 4 of Cups can also indicate surprise gifts or unexpected help when it comes to your health. These could be in the form of a new treatment or simply some much-needed rest and relaxation.

Finances:

The 4 of Cups tarot card can suggest that your finances are not currently in a great state. You may be feeling unfulfilled with your job or lack of money. This is a time to take a close look at your finances and make some changes if necessary. The 4 of Cups can also suggest that surprise gifts or help may come your way when it comes to your finances. These could be in the form of a windfall or simply some much-needed financial assistance.

Relationships:

The 4 of Cups tarot card can suggest that your relationships are currently in a state of apathy or dissatisfaction. You may feel unfulfilled in your current relationship or that something is missing. This is a time to take a close look at your relationships and make changes if necessary. The 4 of Cups can also suggest that surprise gifts or help may come your way when it comes to your relationships. These could be in the form of a new relationship or simply some much-needed support from friends or family.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The High Priestess and 4 Of Cups has helped you.
