A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 7 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and 7 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card together suggest that success requires hard work and patience. To achieve true success, you must seek out win-win solutions and focus on fair, just Decision-making. With time and effort, the truth will come out and the hard-won rewards will be worth the wait.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and 7 Of Pentacles mean together for your love life?

When it comes to the love life, the Justice tarot card combined with the 7 of Pentacles can mean that a great deal of patience is needed to reach a successful outcome. The Justice card suggests that all parties involved need to come to an understanding, and then take action to press forward with a solution that is beneficial for everyone. In this sense, it serves as a reminder of the importance of fostering healthy, win-win communication between partners, working together to bring about a satisfying resolution.

At the same time, the 7 of Pentacles brings with it the message that a successful outcome may be delayed, and the importance of patience and dedication of both partners will be essential to help bring about a positive resolution. The card suggests that hard work and effort put in at this stage will see rewards later on. It also signifies an underlying commitment of both partners to their relationship, helping to ensure that both parties can move forward in a positive manner.

When taken together, the Justice tarot card and the 7 of Pentacles tarot card can suggest that a successful end to any disputes or tensions in a love life can be achieved through understanding, dedication, and perseverance. Ultimately, these two cards can serve as a reminder of the power of win-win solutions, the importance of communication, and the rewards for the patience of those willing to invest time in the process. As such, their combined power can be a sign of great hope for any couple wanting to achieve a happy and successful future.

### What does Justice and 7 Of Pentacles mean together for your finances?

When considering The Justice tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card together in regards to finances and job, it can be interpreted as a reminder to keep your actions above board and work hard. The Justice card often reminds us of the concept of cause and effect, meaning that our decisions have consequences. Every action taken produces a reaction, and it’s important to remember this as we make choices both in our finances and career. Because of this, The Justice card reminds us to stay honest and fair in our financial dealings, as any dishonesty or unfairness may result in future repercussions.

At the same time, The 7 of Pentacles reminds us to be patient and persistent. This card is a reminder that nothing in life comes easily and that hard work is necessary for success. The 7 of Pentacles is a card of delayed gratification and encourages us to focus on the long term instead of trying to get immediate results. By investing in our futures, we can reap the rewards of our efforts in the end.

In conclusion, The Justice tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card in combination are a reminder to stay honest and fair in our financial dealings, while remaining patient and working hard. By following these principles, we can be sure to achieve success in our finances and job in the long run.

### What does Justice and 7 Of Pentacles mean together for your health?

When The Justice tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card come together in a reading, it could be an indication that health outcomes will require careful consideration of both the cause and the effect of any decision that is made. This requires wisdom and patience, as it may take some time for the desired outcome to be realized. There is no rush, as good health depends on taking time to carefully assess the options and make any changes or decisions with a long-term view.

The Justice card is a reminder to make mindful decisions when it comes to health, as this will ensure a win-win situation. This could mean weighing up both short-term comfort and long-term benefits, or opting for a more natural approach if possible. On the other hand, The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card is a warning that results may not come immediately. Progress may be slow, and determination and focus will be needed to achieve the desired outcome.

The combination of The Justice tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card suggests that it is wise to be patient and consistent in looking after your health. It is important to weigh up the pros and cons of different health decisions, and be mindful of the long-term impact of any changes, as this will ensure the best possible outcome. Furthermore, it also reminds us that, although progress toward a healthier lifestyle may sometimes seem slow, the truth will eventually come out, and the results of hard work, dedication, and careful decision-making will be worth the wait.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 7 Of Pentacles mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 7 Of Pentacles the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card has often been said to represent fairness and balance, but in the context of health, finances, and relationships, it holds broader implications for both individuals and the larger community. In health matters, this card suggests fairness and balance in terms of sharing resources and providing necessary services to all members of society. Financially, Justice Tarot card implies making sound decisions that are in the best interest of both parties, such as creating a reasonable budget, avoiding risk factors and seeking out sound investments. On the other hand, when it comes to relationships, the card represents finding a balance between striving for one’s goals while also paying attention to the needs of others. It can be difficult to maintain a sense of equity among loved ones, but when approaching relationships with an open, fair-minded attitude, true growth and understanding can be found.

When it comes to health, the Justice Tarot card encourages us to be aware of our own health needs and to do what we can to maintain our well-being. Investing in preventative care is often a smart financial decision, and it can also go a long way in preventing more serious health problems from developing. Likewise, a balanced diet and regular exercise can help to keep our bodies and minds in the best shape possible, so that the weight of stress does not become overwhelming.

The Justice Tarot card in relation to finances urges us to be responsible with our money choices. It can help to be aware of when we are making decisions out of fear or impulsiveness, and instead prioritize meeting our financial goals while also being aware of how our choices may affect others. Investing money in a way that spreads the risk while still meeting financial goals is one example of finding a balance.

For relationships, the Justice Tarot card supports us in maintaining equity and harmony, even when we feel like our needs are not being met. Instead of focusing solely on our own needs, we are encouraged to seek out win-win solutions in which both parties can come out feeling heard and respected. This is especially true when it comes to resolving conflicts. By taking the time to listen to the other person’s point of view, make compromises, and find mutual understanding, we can form closer connections with the people in our lives.

In summary, the Justice Tarot card encourages us to create an air of fairness, balance, and understanding in all facets of life. This card supports us in making decisions that benefit both ourselves and those around us, so that we can create a healthier, fairer, and more harmonious existence for all.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can symbolize that sometimes in life we need to work hard and wait patiently for the results. This can be related to our health, as we need to make changes to our lifestyle and habits and be prepared to wait for the results. We may have to make lifestyle changes, such as changes to our diet, exercise and activity levels, stress levels and our overall wellbeing. It can also be a reminder to be persistent and to not give up on our goals and dreams, even if we don’t see results immediately.

Finances: The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can also be a reminder to be patient with our finances. It can be easy to get caught up in the need for quick success, but the 7 of Pentacles is a reminder to take things slow, to invest in long-term projects, and to save for the future. It can also be a reminder to think long-term when it comes to investments, to plan for the future, and to stick to a budget.

Relationships: The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can remind us that relationships take time, patience, and hard work to build and nurture. It can be a reminder to not expect overnight success, but to be patient with the process of building and strengthening relationships. It can also be a reminder to take time to appreciate the small moments in our relationships, and to take the time to connect deeply with our loved ones.
