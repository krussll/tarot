A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Swords card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Swords card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Swords and King Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Swords and The King Of Swords tarot cards mean that you may be going through a difficult time, as the 3 Of Swords symbolizes heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression, and surgery. However, the presence of the King Of Swords card shows that you have the ability to strategically navigate your way through this time, allowing you to emerge stronger than ever. You have the power to stay focused, make wise decisions, and lead yourself out of this difficult period. With your strong leadership skills, you will be able to come out of this experience with newfound wisdom and direction.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

This could be a sign of a difficult divorce or a break-up, which has left you feeling a deep sadness and grief. The King Of Swords figuratively wearing a crown of swords may be suggesting that this difficult decision was made with intention and strategic thought. Perhaps this means that, although the situation may be difficult and heartbreaking, it was the best decision to make in the long run.

This could also be a period of transition, as the 3 of Swords is often associated with surgery or other operations. Surgery of any kind is often very scary and unsettling, but with the King of Swords there is a sense of determination, intelligence and control. This suggests that you are taking the situation into your own hands, using your best judgement and making sensible decisions.

It’s natural to feel sad and broken-hearted when going through a major life transition or difficult break-up, but the King of Swords tarot card indicates that you have the strength and wisdom inside of you to work through it. You are taking a difficult situation and handling it with intention and strength. This could be a time of healing and growth, and although it may be painful at first, you will eventually come out stronger for it.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 3 Of Swords and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 3 Of Swords and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The Three of Swords in the Tarot is a very powerful card, conveying a sense of deep pain and sorrow. While the traditional meaning associated with this card is that of heartbreak or a broken relationship, it can also represent depression, surgery, and other difficult life circumstances.

Health-wise, one of the main meanings is that of surgery and healing. The 3 of Swords tarot card can indicate that some kind of medical procedure is in store, and possibly even an emotional healing process as well. This card may signify that a person should take time to care for and heal physical and emotional wounds.

Financially speaking, the 3 of Swords tarot card can mean that there are certain difficult financial circumstances that need to be addressed. In some cases, this card can also mean that the person should be careful with budgeting and money matters. It is also associated with loss, so it may be a warning to stay away from any risky investments.

The Three of Swords tarot card also has a strong influence on relationships. It can mean that a relationship is going through a difficult phase, or even that a break-up is likely. It can also signify the end of a marriage or a relationship that was once full of love and hope, but is no longer. This card is a reminder of the emotional pain that can be experienced during these times, and a reminder that it is important to take the time to heal and move on.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health: The King of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a strong, rational, and decisive approach to health. This is someone who takes responsibility for their own wellbeing and takes the initiative to make positive, life-changing decisions. Perhaps they are researching their health and diet options, actively seeking out the latest health news and information, or taking a proactive approach to their own fitness and lifestyle choices. They are a take-charge individual who makes the tough decisions necessary to maintain their overall wellbeing and fitness.

Finances: The King of Swords Tarot card can be interpreted to mean a disciplined and strategic approach to finances. This person has a clear understanding of the financial landscape, works to diversify their investments and is always looking to maximize their money. They know the importance of budgeting, saving, and smart investments—all part of their plan to stay financially secure. They’re always looking for new avenues to grow their wealth, but at the same time, recognize the need for caution and prudence with their financial decisions.

Relationships: The King of Swords Tarot card is often seen as a sign that a relationship has reached its peak potential, with both parties in a deep state of mutual respect. This individual takes a direct and honest approach to communication, but with a gentleness and compassion. They seek deep understanding of the other person and are always looking for ways to strengthen their bond and create a more secure foundation for the relationship. They lead with intelligence and integrity, making decisions that are good for both parties. The King of Swords is the perfect companion for a healthy, long-lasting relationship.
