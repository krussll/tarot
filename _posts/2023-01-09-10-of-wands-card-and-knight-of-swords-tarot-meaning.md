A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean together?

The 10 Of Wands and the Knight Of Swords tarot card together for me signify a period of intense stress, exhaustion, and feeling overburdened by life. At the same time, it’s a period of change, with the Knight Of Swords representing a fast-talking person or lifestyle choice that could potentially bring light and hope in the midst of feeling overwhelmed. The cards may be telling me to take a step back and assess the situation, as well as be open minded to the changes that could potentially improve my life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card signifies physical and emotional exhaustion. This card is typically a sign that a person has been pushing themselves too hard and needs to take a break from their responsibilities. This overload can lead to burnout, which can manifest in physical and mental health issues. Physical exhaustion can occur when a person is pushing themselves too hard in their exercise or daily activities. They can become strained, tired and prone to accidents due to not resting enough. Mental exhaustion can occur when a person is tackling too many responsibilities and mental strain, leading to feelings of depression, anxiety and irritability.

Finances:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card is indicative of too many financial burdens on a person’s plate. This can mean that they are dealing with a large number of debts, loans and obligations that they feel they cannot escape. This card can also be an indication that they are overspending, feeling overwhelmed and unable to break the cycle of debt. This may lead to feelings of guilt, shame and helplessness.

Relationships:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card can signify that a person is feeling overwhelmed in their relationships. This could mean that they have too many friends, family or partners who are relying on them and they cannot fulfill their obligations. This could result in a person feeling taken for granted or underappreciated, leading to feelings of resentment and anger. Alternatively, it could mean that a person has not been able to focus on the quality of their relationships due to an overwhelming schedule and too much responsibility. This could mean feeling disconnected from those close to them or sacrificing their own needs for those of others.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

When The Knight of Swords tarot card appears in a reading, it often indicates a major lifestyle change. It serves as a warning to be prepared for a large turn in events and a sudden need for difficult decisions. The card often encourages a person to take the opportunity and make the most of it.

The Knight of Swords tarot card symbolizes quick changes in health and financial situations. Most importantly, it warns that it is important to not act hastily or make decisions too quickly. It is best to take the time to carefully assess all options before making any major decisions. It is also important to identify any potential risks and make sure they are thoroughly considered.

The Knight of Swords tarot card speaks to relationships as well. The card often indicates that it is time to make changes in both current and long-term relationships. These changes may be necessary to ensure the continuation of a healthy relationship. Alternatively, it may be time to end a relationship if it is not currently beneficial.

In conclusion, The Knight of Swords tarot card is an indication of a lifestyle change. It is important to take the time to carefully consider all aspects of health, finances, and relationships prior to making any major decisions. As a warning, the card encourages one to avoid making any hasty decisions.
