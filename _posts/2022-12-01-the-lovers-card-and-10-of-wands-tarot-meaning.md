A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 10 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 10 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 10 Of Wands mean together?

The combination of The Lovers and The 10 Of Wands tarot cards signify a time when a deep and meaningful partnership is being tested but is still the strongest thing you have. You may be feeling overburdened and under a great deal of stress, but it is the strength of your connection to your partner that will help you to get through it. You have a difficult decision to make, but with the support of your partner, you will be able to make the right choice that will keep your relationship strong.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and 10 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health

The Lovers Tarot card speaks to the power of two in terms of health. We may be strong individuals in terms of physical and mental health, but when we join forces with someone else, it can be a potent combination for success. Two minds can be better than one when it comes to understanding what’s going on with our health and in finding solutions. We can form partnerships with friends, family, and health professionals to ensure that we’re taking care of ourselves in the best way possible. Whether it’s finding the motivation to stick to a diet or exercise program, or working through a health challenge, the Lovers Tarot card emphasizes the power of working together.

Finances

The Lovers Tarot card can also be a reminder that sometimes we can get further in life and with money when we have someone else to help us. We can join forces with a business partner to create something that’s bigger than we could have done alone, or find ways to save more money together. This card can also point to the importance of communication when managing money. It’s important to stay on the same page with a partner about what’s being saved and spent, and to realize that there is strength in numbers when it comes to setting financial goals.

Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card symbolizes partnership, particularly in relationships. It speaks to the beauty of two people who come together and decide to become a unit, and what that kind of commitment and loyalty can mean. In a relationship, finding ways to join forces and to work together can bring a lot of joy. This tarot card speaks to the importance of understanding, communication, and compromise in relationships, encouraging us to come together and find strength in each other.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

In health, the 10 of Wands Tarot card represents the feeling of exhaustion and physical strain due to the many burdens and responsibilities we carry. It can indicate feeling overwhelmed by the sheer amount of tasks that have to be done, leaving little time for rest and self-care. It can also symbolize pain, either physical or emotional, due to the weight of these burdens. This card is a reminder to take a break and focus on your wellbeing. It also suggests that you make sure to take time to appreciate the small things in life and remind you that you don’t have to take on all the burdens of the world.

In finances, the 10 of Wands Tarot card suggests increased financial strain due to the multiple commitments that one has agreed to take on. It can signify feeling burdened by the amount of expenses that have to be covered and worry over the cost of paying them all at the same time. This card is a reminder to assess where you can cut back and create a budget that works for you. It also implies that you should prioritize your health and wellbeing over any financial obligations.

In relationships, the 10 of Wands Tarot card indicates a feeling of being overwhelmed and burdened by the expectations of others. It can signify getting caught up in trying to please everyone and feeling guilty for not being able to do so. It is a reminder to take time for yourself and to make sure that your relationships are balanced and healthy. It also suggests taking a step back and assessing which relationships are worth investing your time and effort in. Finally, the 10 of Wands Tarot card reminds you to communicate your needs and boundaries to your partners so that everyone is on the same page.
