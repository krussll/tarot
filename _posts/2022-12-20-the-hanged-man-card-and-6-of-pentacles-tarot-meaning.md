However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card and The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card together suggest a need to step back and view your current situation from a higher perspective. The Hanged Man asks you to sacrifice for the greater good, while the 6 Of Pentacles encourages you to be generous with your time or resources. This combination is telling you to evaluate the help or assistance you’ve been receiving and to use your wisdom to determine whether it is beneficial in the long run. Doing so may require you to make a selfless decision, but it will ultimately give you the clarity needed to make the right choice.

Similarly, you may be in a position to give back to someone else in a meaningful way, which can help strengthen the bonds of your relationship.

In conclusion, the combination of The Hanged Man and The 6 of Pentacles tarot card encourages you to take a step back and get a new perspective on your relationship. This may require self-sacrifice on your part, and the generosity of giving or receiving help from others, in order to make progress.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card has many interpretations that can vary depending on the area of life being discussed. When considering the meaning of this card in relation to health, finances and relationships, it often signifies a time of self-sacrifice and difficult decisions.

For health, The Hanged Man Tarot card often indicates that a period of personal sacrifice and perseverance is necessary in order to come out in better health. This may mean giving up certain habits and activities that may have been detrimental to one’s well-being, or taking on activities that are healthier and more beneficial. It is important to remember that while this time of self-sacrifice can be difficult, it will result in better outcomes in the long run.

For finances, The Hanged Man Tarot card often symbolizes prudence and a need to make difficult financial decisions. This could mean cutting back on unnecessary expenses or putting more energy into making the most of one’s available resources. It can also represent the need to take a step back and assess the big picture of one’s financial situation in order to come out ahead.

For relationships, The Hanged Man Tarot card often indicates a need to take a step back and view situations from a different perspective. This could mean giving up the need to always be right in order to better understand another’s point of view. It can also represent the need for self-sacrifice in order to maintain a healthy relationship. This could mean putting the needs of the other person first and making compromises in order to ensure that the relationship succeeds.

The Hanged Man Tarot card is a reminder that although difficult decisions may need to be made in health, finances and relationships, the ultimate outcome is often worth it. It requires a period of self-sacrifice, but with the right mindset, it can lead to a more prosperous, healthy and meaningful life.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 Of Pentacles Tarot card is traditionally known as a card of charity, both giving and receiving. It implies a situation in which the necessary support and help is given, whether it be finance, advice, or physical assistance. In terms of health, the 6 Of Pentacles can indicate that medical aid is needed, or perhaps somebody is encouraging you to help yourself. If finances are the matter at hand, it may mean that somebody is coming to your aid or you are taking the initiative to help somebody who is in need. The 6 Of Pentacles also speaks of finding balance in relationships; it can indicate the willingness of both parties to give and take equally.

In terms of health, the 6 of Pentacles encourages you to seek help if you need it. This card can also suggest a willingness to support a friend or family member through any medical issues they may face. Additionally, the 6 of Pentacles can also mean it is important to nurture your own body, mind and spirit.

In matters of finance, this card indicates both giving and receiving assistance. Perhaps somebody is lending you a helping hand, or you are the one providing support to a loved one who needs it. It can also be a reminder to be generous with your own money.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 6 of Pentacles encourages giving and taking equally. It suggests that a relationship should be built upon fairness, understanding, and selflessness. It could mean that a sense of balance is needed in your relationship – helping each other out in times of need, and being generous and caring towards each other.
