A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The Strength card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and Page Of Cups mean together?

The Strength tarot card and the Page of Cups tarot card together mean that it is important for you to find the strength and courage within yourself to achieve true love, self-respect, and unconditional acceptance. Along with that strength and courage, it is important to use your intelligence, creativity, and enthusiasm in order to reach out and be more involved with the world and those around you. Find volunteer work, show kindness to others, and pursue new loves with an open heart, cherishing the unique differences that each brings to the relationship.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Strength card indicates that the querent is willing to accept the good and bad in their relationship, and to look for a compromise that makes the relationship healthier. It also suggests the querent is ready to forgive and accept their partner for who they are, flaws and all.

The Page Of Cups shows that the querent is ready to take a risk and make a new start in their relationship. This could manifest in something as simple as a date night or as significant as deciding to take the relationship to a new level. This is a card that encourages the querent to take a step forward in trusting their partner and in being vulnerable.

The combination of these two tarot cards is an indicator that the querent is ready to put in the effort and be brave enough to give their relationship the best chance at success. This could open up amazing potential and create the basis for the querent to have the strong and happy relationship they truly desire.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is associated with the sign of Leo, and its meanings include emotional resilience, confidence, and a willingness to take risks. It is a reminder that everyone is capable of finding their own inner strength and courage, regardless of the situation.

The Strength Tarot card encourages us to tap into our inner power, to face our problems and to solve them. When it comes to health, the Strength card advises us to take care of ourselves and take responsibility for our own well-being. This means recognizing when we need to take a break and rest, as well as listening to our bodies and responding to physical and emotional needs.

When it comes to finances, the Strength card suggests an attitude of practicality. While it can be easy to be swayed by the seduction of material possessions and status, the Strength card encourages us to be wise with money and focus on our financial future. It reminds us to have the courage to take risks and make calculated decisions, so that our money works for us in the long term.

Finally, the Strength card is about building relationships based on trust and respect. It advises us to be open with our feelings and boundaries, and to be respectful of our partners’ needs and feelings. Strength encourages us to confront conflicts head-on and to be emotionally honest with our partners. Additionally, the Strength card reminds us to be gentle with ourselves and to treat ourselves with unconditional love and kindness.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

The Page of Cups Tarot card meaning in terms of health points to a time of physical and mental discipline. It suggests that it is important to take care of one’s physical and mental health. This card can indicate a need to exercise regularly, to get proper nutrition and sleep, and to make time for relaxation and reflection. It may signify a new commitment to self-care and health, or the need to adjust existing routines.

When it comes to finances, the Page of Cups Tarot card implies that now is a good time to reassess one’s finances. It encourages careful budgeting, saving, and wise investments. It may also signify that now is a good time to take on new projects or risks in the realm of finances, as long as they are done cautiously and strategically.

The Page of Cups Tarot card can signify new love in the realm of relationships. It suggests that this is a good time for beginning a new relationship, or for developing an existing one. This card can also indicate that it is time to be open and honest in one’s relationships with others, and to practice communication and understanding. It can also encourage one to volunteer in some capacity, to give back to one’s community. Overall, this card signifies a period of growth, development, and loving communication in one’s relationships.
