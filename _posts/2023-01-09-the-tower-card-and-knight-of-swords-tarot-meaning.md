## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card generally represents unexpected and unwelcome change. When it comes to health, it could mean that you face an unexpected health issue or a financial burden from medical procedures. It could also mean that a situation that you thought was stable is now unstable. Healthwise, this card could represent a sudden illness or injury, a financial burden due to expensive medical procedures, or a reversal of health—either the sudden deterioration of an existing condition or a sudden and unexpected recovery.

When it comes to finances, The Tower card can indicate unexpected expenses, a significant financial loss, or a major shake-up in your finances. It could also mean that you are facing a large financial burden due to unforeseen circumstances. This could be the result of a business failure, a speculator’s blunder, or a natural disaster which has caused financial loss.

When it comes to relationships, The Tower card can mean the end of a relationship, a lack of communication, or a major upheaval in the relationship. It could mean a sudden breakup, a major disagreement, or a sudden and unexpected shift in the dynamics of a relationship. In some cases, it can even indicate an unexpected and unwanted pregnancy.

Overall, the Tower Tarot card usually indicates a period of unwelcome and unexpected change, which can have far-reaching effects on our health, finances, and relationships. It can be a difficult time, but it can also be an opportunity to reassess our lives and make positive changes.

The Knight of Swords Tarot card could suggest that it’s a time for bold action when it comes to relationships. This card could point to the need for one to speak their truth and stand up for their needs in order to foster more meaningful and honest connections. This could involve learning how to engage in difficult conversations and take the initiative when it comes to navigating rocky or stagnant relationships. On the flip side, it might be necessary to cut ties with relationships that are no longer serving one’s highest good. This card could also point to the need to be more decisive and courageous when it comes to taking risks in one’s interpersonal connections.
