A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Temperance card together in particular.

The Justice card and Temperance have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Temperance mean together?

When The Justice tarot card and The Temperance tarot card come together, it suggests that you should have a balanced approach when seeking truth and justice. You should use your patience and self-control to make sure that you are taking a fair and balanced approach, with win-win solutions. This way, you can ensure that the truth will always come out, and justice will be served in the end.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Temperance mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice tarot card represents a balanced approach to health and wellness, and understanding the interconnectedness of physical and mental health. It suggests taking a holistic approach to health, not just focusing on one aspect. All areas of health – mental, physical, emotional, spiritual – need attention and should not be ignored. Justice also shows that choices have consequences; if we choose to live an unhealthy lifestyle, our body may suffer. On a positive note, taking care of ourselves can lead to improved energy and clarity of mind, as well as increased longevity.

Finances

The Justice tarot card speaks to the importance of being financially responsible and accountable. It encourages us to face our money issues and not be afraid to seek help if needed. We must assess where we are now and what our financial goals are in order to take the steps to achieve them. It is essential to make sure that we’re spending and saving in a way that is aligned with our values. Justice also speaks to the importance of understanding the law when it comes to finances and taxes, as making mistakes can have serious consequences.

Relationships

The Justice tarot card encourages us to seek relationships that are based on mutual respect, fairness, and equality. It emphasizes the need to be truthful and sincere; we must practice clear communication and be honest with each other. We need to be aware that there is no one-size-fits-all approach to relationships and that each partnership is unique. Justice also speaks to the importance of tackling conflicts with kindness and understanding. We must be able to accept our differences and seek win-win solutions.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health:

The Temperance Tarot card offers a reminder of the importance of balance, harmony, and self-control when it comes to your health. This card encourages you to take time to contemplate your well-being, so that you can correctly identify any areas that may require either an increase or decrease of effort. It reminds you to maintain a steady, balanced lifestyle that includes adequate sleep, proper nutrition and regular exercise. When faced with health problems, The Temperance Tarot card also encourages you to look for holistic solutions, such as consistent mindfulness practice, rather than rushing to take over-the-counter medications.

Finances:

The Temperance Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of patience and self-control when it comes to managing your finances. You should make sure that you don’t rush into decisions with regards to investing your money or spending on big-ticket items. It may be beneficial to set up a budget and stick to it, and look for ways to save money and reduce unnecessary expenses. Additionally, this card can be a reminder to find harmony between work and personal life to ensure that your financial situation is stable and secure.

Relationships:

The Temperance Tarot card is seen as an indicator of peaceful and harmonious relationships. It encourages self-restraint and patience when dealing with any relationship issues or conflicts that may arise. This card can be a reminder to take time to reflect before taking any action with regards to your relationships, and to ensure that any decisions you make are for the greater good of both sides. It’s important to practice self-care and moderation, both in dealing with yourself and the people around you. It’s important to be honest with yourself and your partners, and strive for a state of balance between your needs and wants.
