A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and Queen Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and Queen Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and Queen Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card and The Queen of Pentacles tarot card together symbolize a person’s willingness to put their own needs aside in order to make a difference in the world. It speaks of someone who is wise and resourceful, and willing to make sacrifices for the greater good. It also reflects something of the balance between domesticity and the need to push boundaries in order to grow. This person is able to see a situation from a different perspective and is aware of the value of a deeper understanding. They are willing to do whatever it takes to create balance, bring abundance and fertility in their life, and provide for their family.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health  
The Hanged Man tarot card is symbolic of the need for physical, mental, and spiritual health. In terms of physical health, it symbolizes the need for self-care. As it is a card of sacrifice, it suggests that we sacrifice our unhealthy behaviors for healthier ones. This can include unhelpful dietary habits, lack of physical activity and poor sleep hygiene. It could also mean that we make time and energy for relaxation, to help us manage and reduce stress.

In terms of mental health, the card suggests that it’s important to take the time to reflect on our situations and the underlying causes of our dissatisfaction or distress. Often this suggests that we need to re-evaluate our beliefs and thought patterns, and challenge the assumptions upon which our thinking is based. This could involve exploring the foundations of our values and beliefs, and considering why we hold them so dearly.

On a spiritual level, the card suggests that we open ourselves up to a higher power, and trust in the guidance and support that it may offer. This could involve meditation, prayer, or religious practices to help us nurture our spiritual wellbeing.

Finances  
The Hanged Man tarot card is a reminder to review our finances, and take steps to ensure that we’re managing our money in the best way possible. This card encourages us to make sacrifices and adjustments where needed, in order to get our financial lives in order. This might involve taking on a side hustle to supplement our income, or negotiating a new salary or better job opportunities. We should also consider ways that we can reduce our expenditure, such as cutting down on unnecessary luxuries, or spending smarter.

Ultimately, the card suggests that we need to be mindful of our financial decisions, and approach them from an informed perspective. Consider how best to use your resources and expenses, so that you can reap the maximum benefit from them.

Relationships  
The Hanged Man tarot card reminds us that relationships take effort and compromise. It suggests that we should be open to giving up something of ourselves in order to benefit relationships. This could involve having difficult conversations that don’t always come easily, or creating a compromise when disagreements arise.

The card also encourages us to be open to new perspectives, and to see the situation from the other person’s point of view. We need to listen and be mindful of the other’s feelings and wishes. If a relationship has become strained, the card encourages us to look beyond the surface issues and consider what else may be causing the tension.

Finally, the card suggests that we make time for our relationships and prioritize them. We need to be sure that we’re putting the effort into our relationships that they deserve. Nurture them, show appreciation and be generous with our time and energy.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles mean?

The Queen of Pentacles Tarot card signifies many things when it comes to health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, this card is a reminder that we need to be resourceful and take care of ourselves. This card encourages us to look within to access our own inner resources and use them to stay healthy and balanced. It also signifies the importance of taking time to relax and be nurtured in order to avoid burnout and exhaustion.

In terms of finances, the Queen of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of taking a well-thought-out and strategic approach to money management. This card encourages us to use our resources wisely in order to build financial security and stability. It also acknowledges that wealth is created from our own endeavors and reminds us to be mindful of our spending and investing habits.

When it comes to relationships, the Queen of Pentacles Tarot card embodies the characteristics of a nurturing and supportive partner. This card encourages us to be psychologically and emotionally available to our partners as a way of fostering strong and healthy relationships. It also reminds us that relationships require patience and commitment, and that it is important to take the time to nurture and care for one another if we want our relationships to thrive.
