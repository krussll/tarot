A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 3 Of Wands and The 3 Of Cups tarot cards signifies a time of growth, expansion, and the reward of teamwork. You have the potential to build solid relationships with the people in your life that you can rely on, while also looking outward to form functional partnerships with other people or entities. This period marks a time of progress and greater gains, as well as a feeling of mutual understanding, celebration, and support. You will see the fruits of your labors in the form of expanded business opportunities, travel and adventures, and being part of a vibrant, uplifting team.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health:  
The 3 of Wands suggests that although health may not be a priority right now, it is still important to maintain a healthy lifestyle. This card symbolizes a sense of partnership and team work, suggesting that now may be the time to work toward achieving a healthier lifestyle. It also encourages taking a proactive approach by organizing group activities such as jogging, hiking, or yoga that do not only help with physical fitness but also strengthen relationships. As a team, it is much easier to reach health goals and maintain a healthy lifestyle.

Finances:  
The 3 of Wands is a card of expansion, commerce, and travel. This card often signals a time of financial growth and suggests that an individual may become successful in a business venture. It could mean that good investments are coming and that this is a great time to capitalize on potential opportunities. In addition, the 3 of Wands encourages taking risks and broadening horizons – perhaps by starting a business or looking into other potential investments.

Relationships:  
The 3 of Wands tarot card symbolizes teamwork, which could be a positive indicator for relationships. This card suggests that now is the time to get creative, take risks, and team up with others. Together, couples can brainstorm new ways to strengthen their bond and explore new experiences. This card also encourages communication, understanding, and compromise, which will create a healthy and long-lasting relationship. Lastly, the 3 of Wands encourages travel, which can be a great way for couples to explore the world and create experiences together.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 3 Of Cups tarot card symbolizes great health when it appears in a reading. The card suggests that you are surrounded by the support of friends and family, that are encouraging you to stay healthy and active. This includes making sure you are eating right, engaging in physical activity, getting adequate sleep, and taking enough mental breaks. It also means you have access to resources and trusted advice that can help you on your path to wellness. It also implies that you have the spiritual and emotional resources to draw on when times get tough, showing you that you are not alone.

Finances

The 3 of Cups tarot card can be a sign of financial abundance in the future. The card suggests that the support of friends and family can have a positive impact on your finances. There is potential for your inner circle to provide you with practical guidance and helpful advice on money matters, allowing you to make sound decisions and create a secure financial plan. This could also mean that you are open to new business partnerships, investments, or collaboration opportunities with those in your network.

Relationships

The 3 of Cups tarot card conveys a strong message of relationships and support when it appears in a reading. The card implies that your inner circle is a source of strength and comfort, and that you are never alone. Your friends and family are a haven of trust and understanding, helping you through the highs and lows of any relationship. With the 3 of Cups, there is potential to build strong and lasting connections, while also being able to celebrate and enjoy these special moments together.
