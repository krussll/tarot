## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 9 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 9 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

The Hermit Tarot card can represent a period of introspection and reflection in all areas of life, particularly in the areas of health, finances and relationships. The Hermit Tarot card is a reminder that we need to take some time away from the external world to gain insight into ourselves and our lives.

When it comes to health, The Hermit Tarot card may be asking us to listen to our bodies, to be mindful of what we eat and drink, to get adequate rest and to make sure we are taking care of ourselves both physically and mentally. The Hermit Tarot card may also be an indication that we should take some time to be still and quiet, to reflect on our physical and emotional health and make changes that will improve our overall wellbeing.

In terms of finances, The Hermit Tarot card may be telling us that it is time to take a step back and evaluate our financial situation. We may need to go within and assess our values and goals and decide which actions we need to take to reach them. It could also suggest that we are being too impulsive with our money and need to take a more measured approach.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the Hermit Tarot card can signify the need for us to take a break in order for us to gain clarity and insight. We may need to distance ourselves from certain people in order to reflect on our current relationships and evaluate whether they are good for us. It also may suggest that we need to take time to nurture our relationship with ourselves first before we can be in a healthy, meaningful relationship with someone else.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a symbol of despair and worry. It suggests that a person is struggling with deep emotional pain and anxiety, leading to a lack of emotional balance. On a physical level, it can represent issues such as insomnia, depression, headaches, and other physical ailments caused by extreme mental distress.

For those looking at this card from a financial standpoint, it can be a sign of financial struggles and worries. The person may feel helpless and unable to get out of their financial situation, which can lead to feelings of frustration, stress, and despondency. It’s important to remember that it’s ok to seek professional help and guidance to help get back on track.

In terms of relationships, The 9 of Swords can signify difficulty in maintaining healthy relationships. It could represent a lack of communication, worry, and fear of commitment, potentially leading to a lack of trust and intimacy in the relationship. It may also mean that the person is feeling drained due to the emotional burden they are carrying. In this case, it’s important to remember that relationships can be strengthened by learning to communicate openly and honestly, and being compassionate and understanding towards each other.

Overall, the 9 of Swords Tarot card can be viewed as a warning sign of distress and a reminder to take care of one’s mental health and financial situation. It’s important to remember that it’s ok to ask for help and support from the people around you, and to be mindful of the effects of your thoughts and emotions on your overall well-being.
