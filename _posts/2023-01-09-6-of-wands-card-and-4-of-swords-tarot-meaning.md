A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The 6 of Wands tarot card combined with The 4 of Swords tarot card suggest that you have achieved success through hard work and determination and are now taking some needed time to retreat and rest. You have earned recognition for your efforts and now it’s time to pause and reflect on all the good news and success you have accomplished. This is a time for renewal and recovery, so take some solace in the solitude away from the world and recharge your batteries before taking on the next challenge.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

That pause will allow you to take a deep breath, reflect on where you’ve been, and plan for a new future.

You need to take some time off from your busy relationship and devote it to yourself, contemplate any setbacks and obstacles that have been encountered in your love life, and find a way to come out of the situation stronger than before.

This combination of cards can be difficult to interpret, but its message is clear: give yourself a moment of respite from the hustle and bustle of your relationship; carve out a little time and space for yourself for some rest and renewal, and reflect on the successes and mistakes in your relationship. With the 6 of Wands signaling recognition and the 4 of Swords calling for thoughtful contemplation, the cards together suggest that you take the time out to reaffirm and reconnect with your feelings, and then boldly move forward with confidence and commitment.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 6 Of Wands and 4 Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 6 Of Wands and 4 Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 Of Wands Tarot card denotes victory, award, recognition, good news, success and accomplishment. Health-wise, this card is a sign of strength and resilience, indicating that any health problems you may have encountered are now in the past and you are on the path to recovery. This card is also a great sign for finances, as it suggests that you are entering a period of financial stability and abundance. Whether it be a raise at work, an inheritance or a windfall, the 6 of Wands is a sure sign that you are on your way to financial success.

In terms of relationships, the 6 of Wands is a sign of solidity and recognition. It is a sign that two people have truly come to a deep understanding and acceptance of each other. This could be in any kind of relationship: romantic, familial, or even business. There is a feeling of reward and appreciation in the air, and this is something that can be relied upon to last.

So, in sum, the 6 of Wands is a sign that success is on its way in all forms. Whether it be in the realm of health, finances, or relationships, the 6 of Wands can be relied upon to bring with it a sense of victory, reward and accomplishment.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 4 of Swords tarot card presents a picture of a knight resting on a tomb in a sign of meditative repose, representative of how it relates to health. This card encourages one to take a step back and to focus on recovery and renewal of physical, mental, and spiritual wellbeing. This can be done through rest, relaxation, and solitude. It may be a sign of illness, or mental fatigue and so a break is necessary to restore your health. It could also mean that a healing process is beginning and one should take the opportunity to take good care of themselves and their bodies.

Finances  
When The 4 of Swords appears in a reading related to finances, it can mean a few different things. It can be a sign that a break is needed in order to recharge and regain strength and confidence in order to move forward with financial goals and ambitions. It can also mean that it’s time to take a step back for a moment and to reflect on current financial standing and decisions that have been made in order to create a strong and healthy financial foundation.

Relationships  
The 4 of Swords tarot card carries a message of retreat and renewal when it appears in a reading about relationships. This card can signal a period of rest and reflection for both partners when relationships have become strained. It encourages the need for a break from the relationship in order to restore energy, understanding and harmony. It can also indicate that a period of healing has begun, and both partners should take the opportunity to take good care of themselves and the relationship.
