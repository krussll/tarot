A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Swords card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Swords card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Swords and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of the 10 of Swords and the 6 of Cups can be a bittersweet one. While the 10 of Swords can mean a complete and painful ending, the 6 of Cup’s message of nostalgia and reunions can offer a glimmer of hope amidst the darkness. It can be a reminder that although this ending was painful and quite possibly devastating, there are still wonderful memories and moments to look back on and be grateful for. It may also signify that while this ending was necessary, it may also open up the opportunity for a new beginning and fresh start, where old relationships can be renewed and new connections can be created.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 6 of Cups is a reminder to look back at the good times with fondness and nostalgia. Even though the relationship has ended, the love and understanding that was shared during its duration should not be forgotten. The 10 of Swords and the 6 of Cups together indicate that it is now time to move on from the relationship and the pain of the past. This can be a difficult process, but ultimately it allows us the opportunity to create new bonds, reunite with old friends, and find the innocence and joy that was once present. Moving forward can be a difficult journey, but it can also lead to an even more beautiful future; a future of love and understanding that can be a source of strength and hope.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords Tarot card is a symbol of a difficult and painful ending, often signifying a rock bottom in various aspects of life. When it comes to health, this card signifies a physical and/or emotional crisis that has been experienced or is on its way. It could also indicate a major medical event, such as a major illness, accident, surgery or injury. The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests that a person will experience a low point in their health and will have to tap into their inner strength and resilience to overcome the difficult situation.

When it comes to finances, The 10 of Swords Tarot card indicates financial misfortune, a deep financial loss or the feeling of being overwhelmed by debt. This card signifies an extreme financial hardship that is difficult to recover from and that has brought someone to a rock bottom. It could also indicate a period of financial struggle and instability as a person works to climb out of deep debt.

In relationships, The 10 of Swords Tarot card is a sign of a difficult ending, often signifying the end of a relationship that has brought pain and suffering. It indicates that the relationship has reached its breaking point, and either person may have to accept the reality that it is coming to an end. This card often suggests that it is time to let go and move on, even though it is difficult and painful to do so.

No matter the situation, The 10 of Swords Tarot card is a sign that a painful ending is near and some hard work and resilience is necessary to move on. It is important to remember that this card is often a sign of a new beginning and the opportunity to start fresh with a new outlook.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

The 6 of Cups Tarot card is a highly favorable card when it comes to its influence on health, finances and relationships. This card speaks of nostalgia, gifts, innocence and reunions, and these energies carry into the realms of health, finances and relationships.

Health: The 6 of Cups brings a nostalgic energy that encourages us to look back on our past experiences and to remember that there was a time when life was simpler and we had less to worry about. This card can remind us to take a break from the stress of everyday life and to remember the joys of simpler times. It can also prompt us to seek out healing modalities like yoga, meditation, and energy healing to restore balance and harmony to our lives.

Finances: The 6 of Cups symbolizes receiving gifts and unexpected blessings, so when it appears in a reading related to finances, it portends a financial blessing on the horizon. It can also remind us to enjoy the simple pleasures of life, and to focus on what truly matters, such as family and relationships, rather than material possessions.

Relationships: The 6 of Cups Tarot card can be a sign of reuniting with old friends and family members that have been out of our lives for some time. It symbolizes innocence, so it can be a reminder to never take one’s loved ones for granted and to savor every moment we spend together. This card reinforces the importance of cherishing our relationships and to never forget the innocence of love.

Overall, the 6 of Cups Tarot card is a positive card that brings nostalgia, gifts, innocence and reunions into our lives. These energies can help to enrich our health, finances and relationships, reminding us to enjoy the simple joys of life, to never take our loved ones for granted, and to always cherish the innocence of love.
