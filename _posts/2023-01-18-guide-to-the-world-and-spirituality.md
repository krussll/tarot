## The World And Sprituality – An Overview

The World tarot card is a symbol of coming full circle and achieving success.

When it comes to health, this card can signify completing a lesson or goal related to health, such as achieving a healthy weight or overcoming an illness.

It can also signify the importance of travel when it comes to health, such as taking a vacation to relax, or emigrating to a new country to seek better healthcare.

In any case, The World tarot card indicates that health will ultimately be achieved by completing a journey or lesson.
