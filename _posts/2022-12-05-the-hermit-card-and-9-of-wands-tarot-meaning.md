A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 9 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 9 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 9 Of Wands mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the 9 Of Wands tarot card mean that it is time to go within to seek personal truth and spiritual illumination. However, this cannot be done in isolation; instead, one must be diligent in maintaining boundaries and persisting in order to keep an open channel of communication that is both honest and respectful. This is the ideal balance that can be struck in order to move forward in life with clarity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 9 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 9 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

It advises you to go within to find the guidance you need to discover what is best for your health and wellbeing. When it comes to health, The Hermit card is all about seeking out the deeper truths in order to stay on a path of wellness. It encourages you to look beyond the surface to find balance, harmony, and wellbeing in your life. This card suggests that you take the time to find out what it is that truly nourishes you and nourishes your body.

The Hermit Tarot card is about spending time alone to think and assess your financial situation. It is a reminder that it is important to think deeply about your finances and develop a plan for how to make them work. Don’t be afraid to seek out the advice of a financial advisor or someone you trust and ask questions. This card encourages you to focus on patience, perseverance, and steady progress. It also suggests that you take a practical approach to your finances and focus on long-term plans and goals.

The Hermit Tarot card indicates the need for inner reflection and a journey of self-discovery. This is an important step in cultivating meaningful relationships. This card advises you to spend some time alone in order to think about the relationships in your life, and find out what is truly important. It is a reminder the value of true friendship and understanding of another person. Spend time alone to reflect on where you are and where you’d like to be in your relationships. Don’t shy away from seeking out the help of a counselor or mentor as you move forward in developing satisfying and fulfilling relationships.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The Nine of Wands Tarot card has many meanings that can be applied to all areas of life, including health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, the Nine of Wands can be interpreted as a reminder to take care of ones self and to be aware of ones limits. It suggests that one should focus on their own physical and mental well-being, and that they should strive to maintain and protect their boundaries. In the world of finances, the Nine of Wands can symbolize the importance of steady and consistent effort. It advises one to be mindful of the decisions they make and to persevere in maintaining their resources in times of financial hardship. In relationships, the Nine of Wands encourages people to be mindful of their boundaries and to have compassion for their own emotional and mental needs. This card reminds people to be understanding of their own limits, and to be mindful of their own needs so that they can be supportive in their relationships. Ultimately, the Nine of Wands encourages self-care and understanding in all areas of life, especially in matters of health, finances, and relationships.
