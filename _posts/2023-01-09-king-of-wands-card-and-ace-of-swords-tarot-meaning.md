## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

When it comes to finances, the King of Wands represents an individual who is willing to take risks and make plans to increase their wealth and financial security. They are likely to be driven and make smart investments. They are also likely to take a creative approach to their finances, such as finding creative ways to save money or to start a side business.

## What does Ace Of Swords mean?

The Ace of Swords Tarot card is associated with mental clarity, new ideas, and conflicts. It is a card of power and intellect, signifying the ability to cut through any issue and come to a logical resolution.

In terms of health, the Ace of Swords Tarot card indicates that the time is right to make a change or go through a physical transformation. It may suggest surgery or other medical procedures that can bring physical healing. It can also mean receiving a diagnosis or understanding one’s health condition with greater clarity.

In matters of financial security, the Ace of Swords Tarot card also points towards taking decisive action. It may not be easy to take the necessary steps, but the outcome should be positive. This card also encourages the individual to be vigilant and not to be too trusting of others.

The Ace of Swords Tarot card is also a card of clarity when it comes to relationships. It is a reminder that there is a need for clarity and honesty in any relationship. It is a sign that conflicts may arise and will need to be tackled head-on. It also suggests that a new perspective or point of view may be necessary in order to properly address any issues in a relationship.

The Ace of Swords Tarot card indicates that it is time to use one’s intellect and insight to make positive changes in health, finances and relationships. With clarity and courage, the individual can make great strides and come to solutions that will bring much-needed peace and contentment.
