A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The Queen of Swords and The 9 of Cups suggest a period of harmony and abundance as you move confidently in your own direction. Your honesty and determination will lead to material abundance and good health, enabling you to fulfill your wishes. As you forge your own path and trust your intuition, you will find success in the things that you set out to do. Your self-sufficiency, combined with your full commitment to your goals, will be your greatest strength.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card can represent mental clarity and health. It is often seen as a symbol of inner strength and resilience—the capacity to cope with difficult situations or obstacles. This card can be a reminder to be conscious of our inner dialogue, to look after our bodies, and to take care of our physical health. It can suggest the need to make lifestyle changes that support our overall health, such as nutrition, exercise, and reducing stress. This card can also represent the need for emotional boundaries, especially in the face of unhealthy relationships.

Finances  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card can represent a strong financial outlook. This card can suggest a desire to be independent and self-reliant and a commitment to financial stability. It can indicate that someone has the ability to take the necessary steps to secure their finances, such as budgeting, saving, tracking expenses, and making smart investments. This card often represents a person who is sensible with their money and knows how to make the most of what they have.

Relationships  
The Queen of Swords Tarot card often symbolizes honesty and integrity in relationships. It can be a reminder to be mindful of what is said and done at all times, as it is easy to cross the line into dishonesty, manipulation, and dishonor. This card can also point to the need for self-respect, dignity, and the capacity to speak truthfully and with authenticity. The Queen of Swords can indicate that someone should COMMUNICATE openly and directly, while honoring the boundaries of both parties. This card can also suggest that someone learns to make decisions that serve their highest good and prioritize their own well-being over pleasing others.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health

The Nine of Cups Tarot card is associated with good health, healing and the manifestation of one’s wishes. This card represents the physical, emotional and spiritual healing that can result when our dreams come true. It is a sign of good fortune, harmony and abundance. The card encourages us to take charge of our health, to take responsibility for our healing, and to use whatever resources are available to us. When this card appears, it can be a sign of a healthy body, mind and spirit, and an increase in overall wellbeing.

Finances

The Nine of Cups Tarot card symbolizes material abundance, wealth and financial security. It indicates success in achieving long-term financial goals and the fulfillment of desires. This card suggests things may be going well for you financially, and it encourages you to continue to strive for your goals.You may find that your efforts and hard work are finally paying off, and that your efforts are leading to greater financial security. This card can also be an indication that new opportunities may be coming your way, and that you should be ready to take advantage of them.

Relationships

The Nine of Cups Tarot card is also associated with strong relationships and strong connections with others. This card suggests that your relationships can be a source of psychological nourishment, support and comfort. It encourages you to nurture and cultivate relationships which you value, and to make your own wishes come true by encouraging those of your loved ones. This card can also be a sign of harmony and peace in our relationships, and a reminder that we must always be mindful of our loved ones and the bonds between us. When this card appears, it can be a sign of a strong and fruitful relationship, where both parties are committed to making the other happy.
