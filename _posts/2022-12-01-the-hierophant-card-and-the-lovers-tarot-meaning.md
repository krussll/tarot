## What does The Hierophant and The Lovers mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The Lovers tarot card represent different aspects of life, yet when combined, they represent a powerful balance. The Hierophant symbolizes the importance of traditions and conforming to society’s expectations, while The Lovers tarot card speaks to the deeper connection and power of love that exists in partnership. Together, these two cards represent a strong acceptance of the traditional and the unconventional, of two partners that can come together to find strength in the union of love and tradition.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

It can also be a reminder to seek out conventional advice from healthcare professionals and follow the recommended treatment plans for any ailments. The card can also symbolize taking part in rituals and relying on ancient beliefs for health and healing.

Finances: The Hierophant Tarot card pertaining to finances and money matters can indicate that it is important to follow the conventional rules and standards when it comes to your finances. This means budgeting, saving, and investing in an appropriate and responsible manner. The card can also symbolize doing things the right way and having an eye toward the long-term. It can represent being prudent and thoughtful when making decisions relating to money, and making sure that you are following the advice of experts.

Relationships: The Hierophant Tarot card can represent conformity and following societal expectations when it comes to relationships. It implies that it is important to adhere to the traditional principles of relationships and avoid engaging in activities that are not seen as socially acceptable or morally justifiable. This could mean refraining from gossiping, engaging in offensive behavior, or forming relationships with people who have a negative influence on you. The card can also suggest that it is wise to seek out the counseling of experts who have more knowledge about the area of relationships. This could be a mentor, counselor, or religious figure.
