A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The High Priestess card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The High Priestess card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The High Priestess and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The High Priestess tarot card suggests that there are some things in life that may be best kept hidden or Mysterious. The 4 Of Swords tarot card suggests that sometimes it is necessary to take a break from the hustle and bustle of everyday life in order to Renew and Recharge. Together, these two tarot cards suggest that there is a need for Balance in our lives between the need for Action and the need for Rest.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The High Priestess and 4 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The High Priestess mean?

Health: The High Priestess Tarot is a good omen for health. It suggests that you are in tune with your body and your intuition will guide you to making good choices for your health.

Finances: The High Priestess Tarot also bodes well for finances. It suggests that you trust your intuition when it comes to money and that you will make good decisions regarding your finances.

Relationships: The High Priestess Tarot is a good omen for relationships. It suggests that you are in tune with your partner and that you will be able to communicate effectively.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

When The 4 Of Swords Tarot card appears in a reading, it is often an indication that the querent needs to take a break from the hustle and bustle of their everyday lives. This may manifest as taking a vacation from work, or simply taking some time out for themselves to rest and rejuvenate. It is important to listen to your body and mind when The 4 Of Swords Tarot card appears, as it is likely that they are telling you that you need some time to yourself.

In terms of health, The 4 Of Swords Tarot card can indicate that it is time to take a break from your usual routine and give your body and mind a chance to recover. This may mean taking a few days off from work, or even taking a longer vacation. If you have been feeling run down or stressed, The 4 Of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that you need to take care of yourself. It is also worth considering any health issues that may have been bothering you and whether now is the time to address them.

When it comes to finances, The 4 Of Swords Tarot card can suggest that it is time to take a break from your usual spending habits. This may mean setting aside some money each month to save, or simply cutting back on unnecessary expenses. If you have been feeling stressed about money, The 4 Of Swords Tarot card can be a reminder that you need to take a step back and reassess your finances. It may also be a good time to consider investments or other ways to grow your wealth.

In relationships, The 4 Of Swords Tarot card can suggest that it is time to take a break from the intensity of your interactions with others. This may mean spending more time alone, or simply taking a break from social media and other forms of communication. If you have been feeling overwhelmed by your relationships, The 4 Of Swords Tarot card can be a reminder that you need some time to yourself. It is also a good time to reassess your relationships and consider what is truly important to you.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The High Priestess and 4 Of Swords has helped you.
