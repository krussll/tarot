## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles represents an individual’s need for security, both financial and emotional. In the card, a figure holds down four pentacles in a position of power and control. When this card appears in a Tarot reading, it can be a warning that the querent is too focused on security, leading them to make decisions based solely on their will to survive, not their better judgement.

In terms of finances, the 4 of Pentacles is a sign that the querent is in a stable financial situation, but they could be taking their security too far by being overly frugal and refusing to take risks. They may be hording possessions and money in an attempt to protect themselves instead of investing in a more secure future. This can lead to stagnation, as the querent is unwilling to take a necessary risk to reach their financial goals.

In terms of relationships, the 4 of Pentacles can be a warning that the querent is overly controlling or possessive of their partner. They may be so focused on protecting their relationship that they are not allowing their partner the necessary space to grow. As a result, their relationship may become constricted and this may cause mistrust and resentment.

In terms of health, the 4 of Pentacles can be a sign that the querent is too focused on their health and wellbeing, to the point that they are becoming obsessed. This can cause anxiety, as the querent is so focused on their health that this fear is controlling their life. They may also be so focused on their security that they are neglecting their emotional health and not allowing themselves to enjoy life.

The 4 of Pentacles is a warning that too much focus on security and control can be detrimental in all aspects of life. The querent needs to take a breath and remember to take risks and invest in their future, both financially and emotionally.
