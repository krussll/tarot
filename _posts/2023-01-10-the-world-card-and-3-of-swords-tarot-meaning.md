A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and 3 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The World card and 3 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and 3 Of Swords mean together?

The World tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card together in my life signify that I am at the point of completing a difficult lesson or achieving a goal, but it has come at the cost of a loss, heartbreak, and depression. This could mean that I am close to the end of a situation that has been difficult to navigate, such as the end of a relationship, a relocation, or finishing an especially trying period of my life. Furthermore, these two cards suggest that with emigration and travel, I need to be prepared for the heartache and sadness that may come with this change. This could be a helpful reminder to take care of myself emotionally as I prepare for the new and exciting journey ahead.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The World and 3 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When The World tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card appear together in your love life, it could mean that you are coming to the end of a difficult experience or lesson. It could be a lesson of love and loss that you are learning from and completing, or it could be the end of a relationship or partnership. This could be the end of a marriage, separation from a partner, or a need to move on from a difficult situation.

The completion of this experience could be painful and difficult for both parties, causing heartbreak and emotional distress as a result of the loss or change. You may feel immense sadness and despair, and be unsure how you will find your way back to where you were before. You may feel a sense of loneliness, even if you are surrounded by people who love and support you.

Nevertheless, the completion of this experience will bring a sense of freedom and liberation. With a sense of closure and a new outlook on life, you may find yourself ready to move on and start fresh. Without the baggage of the past, you can focus on creating a new and better future for yourself.

At the end of the day, the tarot cards are a reminder that even when something difficult in your love life comes to an end, it does not necessarily mean that it was wrong. It is a time for healing and a new beginning. Embrace this experience, learn from it, and look forward to the new world that lies ahead.

### What does The World and 3 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When The World tarot card and The 3 Of Swords tarot card are drawn together for your finances and job, the combination can mean a period of significant transition and change. On the one hand, The World tarot card could represent an opportunity to complete a goal or lesson related to your finances and job. It could symbolize a sense of completion and closure, or the end of a cycle. On the other hand, The 3 Of Swords tarot card could represent a painful and difficult experience in relation to your finances and job. It could symbolize a period of heartbreak and loss connected to money or job opportunities, or a divorce from a career path.

This combination could represent a period of growth and revelation. The completion suggested by The World tarot card could mean either an outward journey, such as traveling to take on a new job or emigrating for new opportunities, or an inward journey of self-reflection and healing. The loss suggested by The 3 Of Swords tarot card could lead to difficult moments of mourning, but also to a newfound sense of clarity. It could help you to recognize what isn’t serving you and focus your energy in more beneficial directions.

This card combination could also represent a feeling of coming full-circle. The ending described by The 3 Of Swords tarot card could be cathartic, and lead to a new phase of growth and progress. The completion and travel suggested by The World tarot card could lead to fresh new perspectives, both literally and figuratively. It could be the start of a journey towards a healthier and more prosperous outcome for your finances and job.

### What does The World and 3 Of Swords mean together for your health?

When you combine the World tarot card and the 3 of Swords tarot card, they represent a significant turning point in one’s life regarding their health. The World card suggests that a goal or lesson related to health has been completed, and the 3 of Swords implies that the completion of this lesson or goal is painful or emotionally devastating. As such, this combination of cards can indicate that someone is dealing with the difficult emotions of moving on from a traumatic medical experience or a failed attempt to improve their health.

This card combination can also represent the difficult but necessary choice to leave behind certain aspects of one’s life in order to prioritize their health and wellbeing. It is a call to make a break from the past, to let go of that which is no longer serving them, and to pursue a more fulfilling, healthier lifestyle. Whether this means completing a treatment plan, forgoing a risky medical procedure, or just changing your lifestyle habits, it is important to recognize that there is immense power in letting go of the old in order to make space for the new and unknown.

Finally, these cards suggest the possibility of a fresh start. The World tarot card indicates an opportunity for growth and transformation, and the 3 of Swords signifies a deep and emotional healing process. Together, these cards speak of a chance to recover and rebuild, and to emerge a stronger and more enriched individual. It is painful, but it is also an invitation to take a stand and to choose health and wellbeing without hesitation or fear.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The World mean?

Health:  
The World Tarot card symbolizes an individual’s journey of physical, mental and spiritual well-being. It encourages the idea of self-care and grounding, ensuring that one takes time for themselves to reconnect and make positive changes. The card encourages looking after one’s health, not only for the physical body but for the mind and soul too. It is also a call to activity to bring balance, which requires time, patience and discipline.

Finances:  
The World Tarot card encourages a balanced approach to finances. It reminds us to use our resources responsibly and to plan for the future. The card can represent financial stability and security, but it also promotes the idea of diversification and taking calculated risks. It’s a reminder that we should always be prepared to adjust accordingly.

Relationships:  
The World Tarot card signifies the culmination of relationships and connections. It is a reminder that relationships should be based on mutual respect, trust and understanding, and that we should strive to bring balance and harmony to all of our relationships. The card encourages us to appreciate our relationships and to work on them constantly in order to ensure that they are long lasting and rewarding. It also advises us to think carefully before making any major life decisions as these could have a lasting impact on those around us.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The 3 Of Swords Tarot card symbolizes heartache, sorrow, and frustration, and it often speaks to the difficult and painful emotions associated with the end of a relationship. This card can also imply a period of struggle and loss, as well as feelings of sadness, loneliness, and betrayal. The 3 Of Swords can represent a difficult choice that must be made, either professionally or personally, and it also suggests a certain level of surrender or acceptance of one’s fate.

When it comes to health, the 3 Of Swords Tarot card can signify the need to face a physical or mental health issue with grace and acceptance. It can indicate that there is a need to take a break, to seek medical help, or to take the necessary steps to heal the body. It is important to be aware that there is a need to address a health issue and explore any available treatments.

The 3 Of Swords Tarot card can also be a warning about financial woes. It could be a signal that a debt must be paid, that a risky investment could have a negative outcome, or that funds will be unexpectedly depleted due to unexpected expenses. It is important to be aware of these potential financial issues and prepare in advance to remain financially stable.

In relationships, the 3 Of Swords Tarot card can speak to emotional loss and betrayal. It can suggest a need to reassess relationships and leave behind those situations in which there is no longer a sense of connection or trust. It can also be a sign that it is time to move on from a relationship that has become stagnant or toxic. It is important to take the time and energy to recognize when a relationship is no longer healthy and move forward in a more positive direction.
