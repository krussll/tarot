A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 10 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The 10 Of Pentacles and The 8 Of Swords tarot cards together show a picture of planning for the future by using self-controlled restrictions and isolation. These cards demonstrate that it is important to save and invest your money, as well as look after your mental and physical health, as it allows you to build a legacy for you and your family that will live on for generations. If you are able to maintain the self-imposed restrictions and isolation in order to bring about a successful retirement, then you will be able to reap the rewards in the form of an inheritance that can pass down to future generations.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The Ten of Pentacles tarot card speaks to a time of legacy and inheritance. It serves as a reminder that health, finances, and relationships are all intertwined.

From a health perspective, the Ten of Pentacles is a card of longevity. It is a sign that health is something that can be passed down from generation to generation. This card also speaks to the importance of taking time to rest and relax in order to achieve a healthy balance in life. It encourages individuals to consider the long-term health of their body, mind, and spirit, so that they can enjoy good health for years to come.

From a financial perspective, the Ten of Pentacles is a reminder that financial security can be achieved through careful planning and wise investments. If and when we inherit money or receive a significant financial gain, it is important that we use it wisely. This card can also serve as a reminder to not be too attached to material possessions and to only spend what we can afford and what is necessary.

From a relationship perspective, the Ten of Pentacles is a sign that strong and meaningful relationships can transcend generations. It speaks to the importance of cherishing friendships, maintaining communication and connection, and creating an environment of mutual support. In the end, it is a reminder that relationships are a form of inheritance and can last even after we are gone.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 8 of Swords tarot card is a card of restriction and isolation, both of which can have a significant effect on mental and physical health. A person feeling restricted or stuck in their current situation may experience feelings of depression, anxiety, and anger, making it difficult or impossible to pursue even basic forms of self-care. This can manifest in unhealthy habits, such as neglecting to eat properly or getting insufficient sleep, that can lead to physical maladies. Additionally, isolation can inhibit a person’s ability to develop meaningful relationships or social support networks, leading to feelings of loneliness and further impacting their mental health.

Finances:

The 8 of Swords tarot card suggests that a person is experiencing financial constraints and limitations. This could be due to the lack of money available or spending more than an individual can truly afford, resulting in an inability to pay bills on time or at all. This card can also mean that the person is stuck in a cycle of debt that they cannot escape, leaving them feeling trapped and frustrated. This cycle can be difficult to break as the individual may feel too overwhelmed or restricted by their current financial situation to make any meaningful progress.

Relationships:

The 8 of Swords tarot card suggests that a person is feeling stuck in certain relationships and is unable to make any changes or progress. This could mean that someone is in a toxic or unhealthy relationship that they have difficulty getting out of. It could also mean that the person is feeling constrained by social or familial expectations and the pressure to meet certain standards or remain in certain roles. This can lead to a lack of fulfillment in their relationships as well as feelings of isolation, as the individual may feel unable to speak up about their needs or desires.
