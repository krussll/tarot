A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Magician card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Magician card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Magician and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The Magician tarot card paired with The Queen Of Wands suggests that you are a creative and determined individual who is able to turn your visions into reality. You are confident and self-assured, and able to take inspired action in order to achieve your goals. This combination suggests that you are a powerful force to be reckoned with, and that you are capable of achieving great things.

In terms of your health, this combination suggests that you are in good shape and have the energy and drive to maintain a healthy lifestyle. You are likely to be physically active and have a good amount of stamina. You are also likely to have a good sense of self-discipline when it comes to your health, and are able to stick to a healthy routine.

In terms of your relationships, this combination suggests that you are a confident and assertive individual who is able to take charge when necessary. You are likely to be a good partner who is supportive and motivating, and who is also able to have a lot of fun. You are likely to have a good balance of independence and dependence in your relationships, and are able to give and receive love in a healthy way.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Magician and Queen Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Magician and Queen Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

This can be a time of great healing power, as you are able to channel your energy and attention into manifesting your deepest desires and highest visions. You have all the tools and resources you need to create lasting health and wellness in your life. Trust your instincts and intuition, and take action from a place of heart-centered power.

Follow your heart, and you will manifest your deepest desires.

Trust your instincts, and take inspired action from a place of heart-centered power. You have all the resources you need to create lasting love and intimacy.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen Of Wands Tarot card indicates a person who is confident and self-assured. This person is usually feisty and knows what they want. They are usually very successful in whatever they put their mind to.

When it comes to health, The Queen Of Wands Tarot card means that this person is usually healthy and has a lot of energy. They are able to overcome any sickness or injury that comes their way.

As for finances, The Queen Of Wands Tarot card means that this person is usually very successful in business. They are able to make a lot of money and are usually very good with money.

When it comes to relationships, The Queen Of Wands Tarot card means that this person is usually very successful. They are able to find the perfect partner and usually have a very happy and healthy relationship.
