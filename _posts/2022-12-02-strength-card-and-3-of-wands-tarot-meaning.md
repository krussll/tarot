A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and 3 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Strength card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and 3 Of Wands mean together?

The Strength tarot card and the 3 of Wands tarot card are a powerful combination, as they signify self-love and self-respect combined with teamwork and expansion. With Strength, you can have the courage to seek out new opportunities and the confidence to collaborate with others to bring these opportunities to fruition. The 3 of Wands encourages you to continue to pursue your goals and ambitions, inspiring you to take the necessary risks to see them fulfilled. With these two cards together, you can have the courage and strength to expand beyond your current boundaries, to travel and explore the world, to collaborate with others, and to create something great.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Strength and 3 Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Strength and 3 Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does Strength mean?

Health  
The Strength Tarot card suggests that having good physical and mental health requires genuine self-love and self-respect. It encourages you to appreciate and accept yourself for who you are. It emphasizes the importance of being kind to your body, by eating a healthy diet, exercising regularly and getting enough sleep. It also encourages you to be mindful of your thoughts and feelings, and take action when necessary.

Finances  
The Strength Tarot card encourages you to make sound and sensible financial decisions, and avoid impulsive or reckless spending. It suggests that you focus on creating a secure financial future by setting realistic goals and taking action. It also encourages you to practice resilience and courage when faced with challenging financial situations.

Relationships  
The Strength Tarot card encourages you to practice self-love and self-respect when it comes to your relationships. It suggests that you cultivate unconditional love and acceptance, of yourself, and of others. It encourages you to be open and honest with your loved ones, and to practice patience and understanding in your relationships. It reminds you to be open to compromise, and to have the courage to resolve any conflicts.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

The Three of Wands Tarot card is all about teamwork, expansion, and even travel, so it can be interpreted in a lot of different ways when it comes to health, finances, and relationships.

In terms of health, the Three of Wands implies that working together is the most important factor, whether it’s with the help of a fitness coach, a support group, or a reliable healthcare provider. Teamwork is the best way to stay healthy, whether physically or mentally, and strong relationships are the foundation for any successful health regimen.

For finances, the Three of Wands suggests that the most successful way of making money is through teamwork and collaboration. It could indicate the formation of a business partnership, a joint venture, or simply working together to further a common goal. Achieving success in your financial endeavors requires team effort, communication, and a shared vision.

As far as relationships are concerned, the Three of Wands symbolizes the expansion of your connections and the search for more meaningful connections. It could point to meeting someone who could become a lifelong friend or business partner, or it could indicate the deepening of current relationships. Expansion of your circle and working together with people you trust is the key to strong and meaningful relationships.

Overall, the Three of Wands is a reminder that collaboration is always the best way to achieve success in any aspect of life. Good, strong relationships are essential for our well-being and to move forward, regardless of the field, whether it’s finance, health, or relationships. Working together, communicating, and having a shared vision are the skills we need to achieve our goals.
