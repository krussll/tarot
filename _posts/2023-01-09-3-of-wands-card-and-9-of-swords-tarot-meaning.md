A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The 3 of Wands and The 9 of Swords tarot cards can represent a journey of transformation and healing. This could be a journey that involves traveling to a new place, working with a team of people, or expanding your business. Though this journey will involve moments of anxiety, depression, and dread, it may also provide a much-needed reprieve from the worries of your everyday life. The guidance of this card suggests that you will be able to emerge from your difficult times with a newfound sense of stability, understanding, and self-confidence. Your courage and strength while journeying through this transformation will lead you to a more prosperous and fulfilled life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Wands and 9 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

When it comes to health, this card may symbolize teamwork and collaboration with medical professionals and caretakers. It could suggest taking a holistic approach to healing and using traditional and alternative methods for optimum health. It could also signify financial investments that promote wellness and create a strong foundation for investing in healthy options and habits.

When it comes to finances, this card could indicate the potential of investment and financial growth. It could suggest long-term financial planning that brings both short-term and long-term financial rewards and stability. Furthermore, it could be a sign to start planning for the future and taking necessary steps to achieve financial security. Additionally, it could represent collaborative efforts in business and real estate investments.

And finally, when it comes to relationships, the 3 Of Wands Tarot card can symbolize the opportunity to expand beyond existing relationships and create new ones. It could be a sign to take risks in relationships and explore the unknown. It could also be a reminder to foster open and honest communication in existing relationships and to invest the time and effort in strengthening those connections. Furthermore, it could suggest the potential for new romantic relationships, either for love or for business. In any case, it could represent the potential for growth and transformation within all areas of relationships.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

Health  
The 9 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes the tremendous stress and anxiety we experience in our day to day lives that can take a toll on our physical and mental health. This card points to difficulty with sleeping, nightmares, and physical exhaustion due to worry and mental burden. It can indicate worry over health problems or the stress of not being able to get proper medical attention. The 9 of Swords can be a sign to seek professional help or to practice better self-care.

Finances  
The Nine of Swords Tarot card warns of financial worries and instability. It may be a sign of unresolved debts and money problems due to poor budgeting or overspending. It could also indicate difficulty maintaining financial stability due to the unpredictable nature of the economy. The card suggests to keep a close eye on spending habits, practice good money management and try to remain positive and hopeful despite any financial worries.

Relationships  
The Nine of Swords Tarot card can point to emotional distress and confusion in relationships. It could represent grief over the loss of a loved one, difficulties with communication and understanding between partners, or the difficult emotions of being betrayed. This card warns of a tendency to create and maintain relationships that are based in fear and power struggles. It is a reminder to be sympathetic and patient with those we care about, and to not let our own anxieties and worries get in the way of developing healthy relationships.
