## The World – An Overview

The World tarot card is an indication that you have achieved a great success and are ready for a new journey. It typically symbolizes completion, travel, and a new start. It is a reminder that everything in life is cyclical and that there is always a new challenge to be faced. This card is all about the journey, the learning and growth that has been accomplished, and the joy of setting out to see what the world has to offer. It is a sign that you are ready to take the next step, to move forward and to experience something new. It is a reminder to stay focused and to keep your eye on the prize, because the possibilities are endless. The World tarot card reminds us to remain humble and to be grateful for the blessings and lessons we have been given.

## Upright Meanings Of The World

### What It Means For Me

The World tarot card is a sign to me that I am coming close to completing a goal or major lesson in my life. It could mean that I am now ready to embark on a new journey in life, perhaps by immigrating to another country or completing a long-term project. It could also symbolize the end of a significant chapter in my life and the beginning of a new period of growth and exploration. The World tarot card reminds me that I should take this opportunity to embrace change and move forward with a newfound sense of purpose.

### What It Means For The Future

The World tarot card signifies that I am at the end of a cycle, and that I am ready to embark on the next stage of my life. It suggests that I may be completing a goal or lesson, and that it is time to move on to the next big step in my life. The card can also suggest that I am ready to take a step towards a major life change, such as emigration, travel, or a big move. It is a reminder that I am capable of making big changes and that I should trust my own instincts and abilities in order to reach my goals. With The World, the possibilities are endless, and I should embrace the future with optimism and excitement.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The World tarot card is a representation of completion, and can be interpreted as a sign of accomplishment for someone close to me. It can mean that they have achieved a goal or lesson they have been striving for, or are ready to move on to the next phase of their journey. It can also signify that they are ready to travel, either through physical or spiritual means, and that they are ready to take on a new challenge or opportunity. Ultimately, The World tarot card is a sign of completion and a message that someone close to me is ready to move forward and complete the journey they have been on.
