Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 8 Of Swords card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 8 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 8 Of Swords.

## 8 Of Swords And Love – An Overview

The 8 Of Swords tarot card is a warning to take care of your health, both physical and mental.

It symbolizes self-imposed restrictions, isolation, and imprisonment, which can be a result of poor health habits and decisions.

It is a reminder to take a step back and reflect on the choices we make and how they affect our wellbeing.

It is important to practice self-care and be mindful of our physical and emotional health.

Making healthy lifestyle choices such as eating a balanced diet, exercising regularly, and getting enough rest can help us stay in tune with our bodies.

Taking the time to check in with ourselves and ensure our mental health is in good shape is also essential.

The 8 Of Swords is a reminder to break free from the self-imposed restrictions holding you back from living a healthy and fulfilled life.

## Upright Meanings For 8 Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

The 8 Of Swords tarot card is an indication that when it comes to love, you may be in a state of self-imposed restrictions, feeling isolated and imprisoned in some way.

This could be due to a lack of self-confidence or feeling stuck in a situation that has become difficult to escape from.

It is a reminder to look at the situation with a fresh perspective, to consider what you have to offer and to take steps to remove the restrictions that have been placed on you.

With a little effort, you can break down the walls that have been built, allowing yourself to move forward without the limitations you have placed on yourself.

### What It Means For The Future

The 8 of Swords tarot card suggests that my future love will be a journey of self-discovery and introspection.

It may feel like I am stuck in a cycle of self-imposed restrictions and isolation.

While it may be difficult to break free of these oppressive feelings, I must be willing to take risks and accept the uncertainty of the unknown.

With patience and self-awareness, I will eventually escape the prison of my own mind and find a connection that is both deep and meaningful.

Once I have released myself from my personal limitations, I will be able to find a fulfilling relationship and open my heart to unconditional love.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 8 of Swords tarot card is a reminder that when it comes to the love of those close to me, sometimes I can be my own worst enemy.

This card is a reminder that I might be preventing myself from receiving and giving love because of the self-imposed restrictions I have implemented on myself.

These could be mental or emotional blocks that I have put up, resulting in me feeling isolated and imprisoned in my own mind.

This card serves as a warning to be mindful of my inner dialogue and to be aware of the walls I might be building around me.

It is important to remember that love is a two-way street; to let go of the restrictions I have placed on myself, and to open up to the love of those close to me.

## Reversed Meanings For 8 Of Swords And Love

### What It Means For Me

When the 8 of Swords is reversed in a tarot reading, it often indicates that a person is starting to break out of the self-imposed restrictions and isolation that has been holding them back in matters of love.

They may be ready to take a risk and make a commitment to someone they care about, or they may be ready to end a toxic or unfulfilling relationship.

They may also be ready to start exploring their romantic feelings for someone new, and to nurture their relationship in new and exciting ways.

The reversed 8 of Swords can also represent a person who is starting to break away from the fear that has kept them from opening their heart and allowing themselves to be loved.

They may be learning to trust in the power of love and to take a leap of faith into a more passionate and fulfilling relationship.

With the reversed 8 of Swords, a person is starting to let go of their fears and hesitations and embrace the possibilities that love brings.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 8 of Swords tarot card speaks to the freedom and empowerment of finding love in the future.

It signifies that you are no longer restricted by self-imposed limitations and that you are ready to find and accept love into your life.

Your heart is open, and you are willing to explore the possibilities of a future relationship.

Although the 8 of Swords can signify a period of isolation, the reversed card encourages you to actively seek out and connect with potential partners.

It encourages you to take risks, be open to new experiences, and to trust in the process.

The reversed 8 of Swords also suggests that the person you will eventually find love with is already in your life.

It encourages you to look around and be open to the possibilities that already exist in your current relationships.

You may not have noticed the potential for love in relationships that you already have.

This card encourages you to look deeper and take the time to get to know others on a more intimate level.

With the reversed 8 of Swords, you can expect to find a partner who understands you, loves you for who you are, and is there to support you unconditionally.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the 8 of Swords is reversed, it represents the love of those close to me being freed from any self-imposed restrictions, isolation, and imprisonment.

This reversed card suggests that I am no longer stuck in a mental prison of my own making.

I am liberated from negative thinking and toxic beliefs and feelings that have been holding me back.

I am now free to express my true feelings and open up to the love of those closest to me.

The love from those close to me is now embracing and unconditional, allowing me to be vulnerable and trusting with those I cherish.

I am able to break free from the fear of being judged and letting my true self be seen.

I am no longer afraid to take risks and explore new relationships, as I can now trust that the love of those around me will support me every step of the way.

The reversed 8 of Swords indicates that I am able to fully embrace connections with my family, friends, and significant other, allowing me to build and maintain meaningful and fulfilling relationships.

## Conclusion

As you can see there many interpretations of The 8 Of Swords depending on how it was draw, and what you were asking your tarot deck.
