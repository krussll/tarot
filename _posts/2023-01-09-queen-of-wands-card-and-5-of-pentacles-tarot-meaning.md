A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Queen Of Wands and The 5 Of Pentacles tarot card combined suggest that you are facing a tough time. You are, however, blessed with a strong streak of confidence and self-assurance, which will help you get through the challenging situation. The cards represent a period of ill health, poverty, worry, isolation and financial loss, but despite all this, you must trust that you have the strength, courage and power to move forward and make something positive out of the current situation.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

You may have to draw upon your creativity and resourcefulness to find clever ways to make ends meet. The 5 of Pentacles is a sign that there may be a lot of worry and stress associated with this period. You will likely feel isolated and alone in your struggles, but you must remember that this is simply part of the journey. You may be facing financial obstacles, health issues, or emotional burdens, but it is your duty to take care of yourself and make sure that you get the help you need.

It is unclear how long this period will last, but the Queen of Wands and the 5 of Pentacles encourage you to stay strong, resilient, and positive throughout it. Trust in yourself and your abilities, and rely on the love and support of those around you. You may be feeling the strain, but with the right attitude and determination, you have the potential to weather this storm and come out the other side with a newfound sense of strength and courage.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen Of Wands Tarot card is a powerful symbol of personal power and self-assurance. This card can be used to represent a courageous, confident, and feisty individual.

In terms of health, the Queen of Wands Tarot card encourages an individual to take ownership of their physical well-being. She suggests that you take a proactive approach to your health, emphasizing preventive care and self-care. This card can also signify a period of healing and rest, allowing you to take the necessary steps to renew your energy and vitality.

The Queen of Wands Tarot card can be an encouraging sign when it comes to finances. She symbolizes the strength to make decisions that are in your best financial interest. She can also represent the courage to take risks in pursuit of your financial goals.

Finally, the Queen of Wands Tarot card can also signify a strong and confident individual in terms of relationships. This card encourages openness and communication in relationships, with the confidence to stand up for yourself and your values. She can also indicate that it is time to take a leap of faith and make bold moves in your love life.

In summary, the Queen of Wands Tarot card symbolizes a feisty, brave individual who is willing to take risks and make bold moves. This card encourages taking ownership of one’s health and financial situation, as well as having the courage to open up and communicate in relationships. When this card appears in a reading, it can be a sign that great things are awaiting you if you have the courage to take the necessary steps.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is a card of ill health, financial ruin, and isolation. On the surface, it looks bleak but, when you look more deeply, it holds the potential for renewal and change.

The 5 of Pentacles speaks to ill health in a very real and significant way. It suggests that our health is in a fragile state and that we are lacking in the physical, mental and emotional support that we need. We may feel depleted, exhausted, and isolated. We may be dealing with physical illness or disease. It is important to be aware of our limitations and to take steps to protect our health.

In terms of finances, The 5 of Pentacles can be a warning that our financial situation is not secure. We may have fallen into debt, or we may be facing financial loss. The 5 of Pentacles reminds us to be mindful of our money and to make sure that we are taking steps to protect ourselves against financial hardship.

The 5 of Pentacles also speaks to isolation and alienation in our relationships. It can be a sign that we are feeling disconnected from those around us, whether that be family, friends, or co-workers. We may be feeling like we don’t belong, that we are an outsider, or that we don’t have anyone to turn to. It is important to recognize these feelings and to make an effort to build relationships with people who understand and accept us.

The 5 of Pentacles can be a difficult card to face, but it is also an opportunity. It speaks to the potential for renewal and transformation. We can use this card as a reminder to address our physical and mental health, to be mindful of our finances, and to nurture meaningful relationships.
