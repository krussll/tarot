A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Spades card and 7 Of Clubs card together in particular.

The 8 Of Spades card and 7 Of Clubs have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Spades and 7 Of Clubs mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of the 8 of Spades and the 7 of Clubs creates an intriguing mix of energy.

The 8 of Spades representing restriction and power, coupled with its wintery, watery energy, suggests that there are obstacles to overcome in achieving either emotional understanding or a specific goal.

This could mean that one’s ambition may be hindered by restrictions, perhaps of one’s own making or those imposed externally.

However, the presence of the 7 of Clubs, symbolizing “trouble in love” and “Victory,” hints at difficulties in matters of the heart.

This could represent a rocky relationship or the struggle between passion and logic.

But, not to forget its elemental association of Earth, it also suggests overcoming difficulties and achieving hard-won victories.

Its summer relation offers a promise of warmth, growth, and positivity, counterbalancing the cold, introspective influence of the 8 of Spades.

Taken together, it might represent the struggle of overcoming personal barriers and the potential victory that ensues, especially in the context of love and relationships.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 8 Of Spades and 7 Of Clubs mean together for your love life?

The combination of The 8 of Spades card and The 7 of Clubs card in a cartomancy reading signals a tumultuous period in your love life.

The 8 of Spades is representative of limitation and power, suggesting that you may be feeling confined or restricted in your current relationship.

This could be due to power dynamics where one person holds more control or decision-making influence.

This power could be used either positively or negatively; it could either mean getting through obstacles due to willpower or suppression due to power misuse.

The element Water associated with this card hints towards deep emotions, intuition and subconscious thought-process at play.

The 7 of Clubs, on the other hand, brings disharmony in love but also promise victory.

This indicates that despite the turbulent or challenging times in your relationship, success and positive outcomes may still be achieved.

This card is a reminder that struggles and strife are a part of any relationship, but these troubles are not insurmountable.

With Earth as the associated element, it implies grounding and firm roots, indicating that the trouble can be overcome with perseverance and solid effort.

So, while your relationship may be going through a challenging phase in the hot season of Summer, there’s a stirring indication that with strength, determination, and faith, you can navigate through these troubles and emerge victorious.

### What does 8 Of Spades and 7 Of Clubs mean together for your finances?

When it comes to financial aspects, the 8 of Spades represents restrictions and limitations.

There is an impression of a hurdle or obstacle that you may be facing at this moment.

Its power could point towards financial regulations that you need to understand and adhere to.

It suggests a need to reassess your financial situation and make conscious efforts to make changes to overcome these restrictions.

The Winter season association suggests a potential period of economic stagnation or slow growth.

As a Water element, it emphasises thoughtfulness, emotion and internal evaluation in navigating your financial circumstance.

On the other hand, the 7 of Clubs represents trouble in love, which can be interpreted as a warning to avoid letting emotional bias affect your financial decisions.

Its association with victory implies that despite these troubles, you might overcome them and achieve success.

This could pertain to work-related problems or negotiation in your job environment.

Being associated with the season of Summer, a period of growth and prosperity, hints at potential opportunities for financial advancement.

Finally, its relationship to the Earth element emphasises pragmatism, security, and stability.

Together, these cards suggest a journey of overcoming obstacles and emerging victorious in your monetary and professional life.

They advise you to remain conscious of your emotional biases in financial decisions while reminding you of the potential for victory in overcoming your current struggles.

### What does 8 Of Spades and 7 Of Clubs mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading for health, drawing the 8 of Spades could suggest that you are facing some form of restriction or limitation in terms of your wellbeing.

This could indicate a need to take care of your physical self more and cut out or hold back on harmful habits such as poor diet, lack of sleep or lack of exercise.

Although associated with power, the water element in this card could mean a need for emotional cleansing, perhaps letting go of stress or negative thoughts that may be impacting your health.

The season of winter might imply potential health issues in the colder months, advising you to bolster your immune system.

Drawing the 7 of Clubs in the same reading might indicate potential disturbances in your emotional wellbeing, potentially resulting in physical manifestations.

The “trouble in love” could represent heartache, stress or emotional turmoil, potentially signaling the need to address such issues for the sake of your health.

However, this card’s association with victory signifies the potential to overcome these circumstances successfully.

The element Earth grounding you and the summer season could suggest a period of recovery, growth, and renewal.

To ensure victory over your health concerns, this card encourages you to stay grounded, practice self-care, rely on your resilience, and aim for overall wellness.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
