## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Chariot and The Star mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Chariot and The Star the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does The Chariot mean?

The Chariot Tarot card signals a major change and journey, with opportunities to make beneficial progress. As the card is strongly linked with action and change, it can be used as a positive omen in many areas of life.

When it comes to health, the Chariot Tarot card can represent a major shift in the focus of one’s wellness. It could mean a new diet, exercise regime or lifestyle change, where the individual is taking the initiative to make their own journey towards greater health and fitness.

In terms of finances, The Chariot Tarot card can signify a change in one’s financial situation. It could mean a shift in spending, savings or investments, where the individual is taking a new and proactive approach to managing their finances. It could also signal a potential opportunity for financial growth, whether it is through a new job, investment or business venture.

In relationships, the Chariot Tarot card can show a major transformation. It could indicate a need to make changes in order to cultivate a healthier and stronger bond, or it could signify a new relationship beginning and the potential for growth. It can also mean a journey towards self-knowledge and spiritual growth, where the individual is taking the initiative to make a positive change.

No matter what arena of life the Chariot Tarot card applies to, its meaning is ultimately one of empowerment, progress and actively making progress in a positive direction. It signifies a journey of action and transformation, which can bring positive results in any aspect of life.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card is a sign of renewal, hope, and inner clarity. It is a reminder for us to have faith and trust that miracles are possible. On a health level, the Star Tarot card is a reminder to stay in tune with your body and mind, to take time for yourself, and to be mindful of the physical changes that you experience. It’s a call to take time to nurture yourself and to treat yourself with respect and kindness.

On a financial level, the Star Tarot card is a reminder to trust that the Universe will provide what you need. It reminds us not to worry too much and to have faith that things will work out in the end. It is a reminder to focus on what we already have, rather than what we don’t have, and to move forward with confidence.

In terms of relationships, the Star Tarot card is a reminder to stay connected to yourself and to find the inner strength to stay true to your values and beliefs. It’s a reminder to stay true to your relationships – with friends, family and partners – and to trust that life will bring the right people into your life. It encourages us to be in the spotlight and to show up for the people that we care about.
