A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The 8 Of Cups tarot card represent a difficult choice that must be made. On one hand, it is a reminder of the strength of two together in partnership and deep love, yet on the other it speaks of withdrawing, retreating and moving on. Perhaps you have reached a point in your relationship where it is time to take a step back and re-evaluate. There may be a feeling of sadness and abandonment, but it is an important moment of reflection that will ultimately bring you growth and understanding.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Lovers and 8 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When both The Lovers tarot card and The 8 Of Cups tarot card appear in your love life, it can be a sign of a difficult time. The Lovers card talks of deep love and partnership, which suggests that a relationship is already in place. The 8 Of Cups is the card of withdrawal, retreat, and abandonment; this could mean that one partner is reconsidering the situation and deciding that maybe it’s time for them to move on and look elsewhere for a partner.

This can be a tricky time, as both cards together suggest a difficult decision needs to be made. The Lovers card may be prompting you to be honest with yourself and your partner, and whether the love you share is still strong enough to get through the tough times. The 8 Of Cups could be a warning that your partner is seeking a new direction and will not be able to stay in the relationship, no matter how much you love each other.

It’s important, though, not to jump to conclusions. The cards simply suggest that a choice needs to be made, and whatever decision you choose, it is your own. This is a chance to truly assess the strength of your relationship, and decide if you’re willing to work through the tough times to keep the love alive. It is also a chance to be honest with yourself, and ask yourself if you are truly happy with the state of your relationship.

Ultimately, The Lovers and The 8 Of Cups together suggest that there is a choice for you to make, and you need to decide whether the love between you is strong enough to stay the course, or if it’s time to move on to a different direction.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

The Lovers Tarot card is a symbol of harmony, understand, commitment and deep love in relationships. It speaks to a balance between the masculine and feminine and a mutual understanding between two people. It is a representation of trust and a strong emotional bond. In a health context, it speaks to a good balance between stress and relaxation, between work and leisure. It is a reminder to take care of oneself, while also taking time to nourish relationships with others.

In terms of finances, The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder to keep financial decisions in balance. It encourages making decisions in partnership, communication between partners and spending within one’s means. It also speaks to taking risks as a team, sharing resources and being wise with investments.

When it comes to relationships, The Lovers Tarot card speaks to a strong, committed bond. It emphasizes the importance of communication, understanding and openness. It speaks to embracing both the positive and the negative in any relationship and working together to reach common goals. It is a reminder that two people can be stronger together than apart. This card encourages couples to form a strong emotional bond and to always keep in mind the importance of trust and mutual respect.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 8 of Cups in health often symbolizes a need to step away from a stress-filled routine or situation and take a break. This could mean taking actual days off, or even a longer break such as a vacation. It could also mean that the individual needs to make some changes to their lifestyle to reduce stress levels, such as learning some relaxation techniques or making time for themselves. The individual needs to be aware of their own emotional and physical wellbeing and prioritize it accordingly.

Finances: The 8 of Cups in finance is a reminder to look for opportunities for growth and change, but also to be aware of when it’s time to step away from an unfavorable situation. A continuing to invest in a situation that is not going to provide substantial growth can lead to financial loss, and this card serves as a warning to avoid this. It is a reminder that there are always other opportunities available, and that looking for the right one is important.

Relationships: In a relationship context, the 8 of Cups encourages the individual to recognize when it is time to move on, or to make changes. This could mean that it’s time to end a relationship, or to make changes to how it functions. In some cases, it could mean that the individual needs to take a step back and reassess their needs, as well as the needs of their partner. It could also mean that it’s time to look for new ways to communicate and connect.
