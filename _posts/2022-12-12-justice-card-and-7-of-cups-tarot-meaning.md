However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The 7 Of Cups card together suggest that there may be a difficult decision looming in your life. The Justice card is urging you to look at all possible outcomes and consequences before making a choice, and to be honest with yourself and others about the situation. The 7 Of Cups card is warning that being overwhelmed by too many possible choices can lead to commitment issues, and you may feel torn or confused in which option to pursue. It is important to approach the situation with patience, thoughtfulness, and logic; this will help you to find a win-win solution that is beneficial to all involved. Ultimately, seek the truth and put it into action.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and 7 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and 7 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card is a powerful card of balance and justice. It means that health, both physical and mental, is found through the pursuit of balance. It encourages us to take a holistic view of health, looking at diet, exercise, work-life balance, and stress management. It suggests that we pay attention to our own self-care and not ignore warning symptoms or put off doctor visits. Taking proactive steps to maintain balance and make sure that we are getting the care we need can help us keep our bodies and minds healthy.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card represents fairness and balance in the realm of finances. It can mean that the more we honor what is fair and balanced the more successful our finances can be. This card can remind us to be honest and ethical in our financial practices, to treat others fairly and to practice responsible spending. It can also be a reminder to create a budget, check in with it regularly, and work to make sure that we are not only taking care of our own needs, but also considering our obligations to others.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card also speaks to balance and fairness in relationships. It suggests that we should put effort into creating mutually beneficial relationships with others, one that fosters mutual respect and trust. Being honest and open in relationships can help us to cultivate healthy and strong bonds with others. In addition, this card can mean that we should not allow our relationships to become imbalanced or one-sided, that we should take responsibility for our own mistakes, and that we should strive for fairness in our dealings with other people.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

7 of Cups Tarot Card and Health

The 7 of Cups tarot card symbolizes an abundance of choices and a lack of commitment, which can affect your health. When it comes to health and wellness, you may be tempted to try every new diet or fitness trend that comes your way, but this can lead to confusion and balancing multiple conflicting elements. Without making a commitment to a specific regimen, you may find yourself lacking in energy, focus and consistency. Alternatively, you may be so overwhelmed by the abundance of choices that you end up unable to make a decision altogether, leading to procrastination and an unhealthy lifestyle.

7 of Cups Tarot Card and Finances

The 7 of Cups tarot card can mean that you’re struggling with commitment issues when it comes to your finances. You may be tempted to spend excessively on luxury items or come up with grandiose plans that you never follow through with. Or your lack of commitment may manifest as procrastination when it comes to budgeting or sticking to a financial plan. The 7 of Cups can signify an inability to make a decisive decision, leading to financial turmoil and insecurity.

7 of Cups Tarot Card and Relationships

The 7 of Cups tarot card can mean that you’re struggling with commitment issues when it comes to relationships. You may find yourself tempted by more than one option, leading to confusion and a lack of focus. You may be experiencing commitment-phobia, which makes it difficult to stay grounded in any one relationship or to make a lasting commitment. You may also find yourself overwhelmed by the abundance of choices, leading to passivity and a fear of making the wrong decision. As a result, your relationships may suffer from a lack of direction, consistency and trust.
