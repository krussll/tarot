## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Wands and 5 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

The 10 of Wands Tarot card indicates a period of heavy burden and exhaustion. It speaks of the struggle of carrying a large responsibility and working hard to achieve goals that have become increasingly difficult to attain. Health-wise, when this card appears, it could point towards feelings of extreme physical or mental fatigue. It is a sign of taking on too much and not having enough time to properly rest, recuperate and take care of one’s own health. This could lead to burnout, illness or stress-induced aches and pains.

Financially speaking, the 10 of Wands can indicate a period of financial strain where resources are spread so thinly and needs require more resources than are available. It could be a sign of overextending oneself financially, leading to more debt and less security. It can be a sign of financial strain on a relationship, when one partner’s obligations are too great, leading to arguments and disagreement.

In terms of relationships, the 10 of Wands Tarot card shows that a relationship or partnerships are labored, with each person taking on too much responsibility and not enough time to connect or talk. It could indicate a lack of intimacy, communication or support due to the heaviness of the burdens that each person is carrying. It is a warning to take a step back, lighten the load and take time to reconnect and support one another.

## What does 5 Of Cups mean?

In terms of health, The 5 Of cups card can represent a sense of being physically or spiritually drained due to excessive grief and disappointment. The individual may feel as though they are unable to muster the energy to take care of themselves or push forward in any meaningful way. This card can often signify a health crisis or a feeling of physical exhaustion due to carrying around too much emotional baggage.

In terms of finances, The 5 Of Cups card denotes a period of financial struggle and disappointment. This card often indicates that hard decisions need to be made in order to reestablish security and prosperity. It can be a warning to stop chasing after dream investments and to focus on more sound and practical business decisions.

In terms of relationships, The 5 Of Cups speaks of a feeling of being abandoned or unappreciated. It can signify a lack of recognition for the effort and hard work put in by the individual in a relationship. This card can be a signal that an individual is focusing too much on the negative aspects of a relationship, to the point where it has become a burden. The person must accept that relationships require compromise and effort from both parties and choose to focus on the positives.
