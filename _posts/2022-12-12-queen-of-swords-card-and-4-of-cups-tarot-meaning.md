A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Swords card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Queen Of Swords card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean together?

When the Queen of Swords and the 4 of Cups appear together, it often indicates a need to be more honest with oneself and seek to find fulfillment in new and unexpected areas. The Queen of Swords encourages one to be self-sufficient, taking responsibility for one’s own life and decisions. On the other hand, the 4 of Cups speaks of feeling unfulfilled and requires one to look beyond the apathy and find lasting joy in the surprises that life brings. Together, the two cards suggest a balance between independence and receptivity to the unexpected, taking a risk and facing the unknown while in full control of oneself.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card is often seen as a sign of good health and well being. People who embody this archetype tend to have a good sense of personal balance and are dedicated to maintaining their physical, mental and emotional health. They understand that self care is essential and as such, make sure to spend time caring for themselves and their bodies. Being conscious of their diet, and making sure to get adequate sleep and exercise are all important for the Queen of Swords.

Finances: The Queen of Swords is someone who is committed to the practical side of life and likes to stay mindful of her finances. She is frugal and enjoys the process of budgeting and saving. Although she is not overly concerned with material possessions, she is not against making smart investments and building a financial base that she can rely on in times of need. The Queen of Swords is wise when it comes to money and doesn’t take unnecessary risks.

Relationships: While the Queen of Swords can seem cold and distant, she is actually a loyal and dedicated friend. She is a practical person who wants to make sure that each relationship she has is built on honesty and trust. Her caution doesn’t make her a bad friend, however. In fact, if you are lucky enough to gain her trust and friendship, she will be fiercely loyal to you and can be depended upon to provide sound advice in times of need.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 4 of Cups Tarot card is associated with apathy, which can affect one’s physical, mental and emotional health. When this card appears in a Tarot reading related to an individual’s health, it could suggest that the seeker is feeling unfulfilled and uninspired, and this could manifest in lethargy and a lack of motivation to take care of themselves. They may be feeling too apathetic and uninterested to engage in activities that could improve their physical, mental and emotional health, such as exercise, relaxation techniques, or connecting with friends and family. It is important for those with this card to reconnect with their physical, mental and emotional health, as well as to re-engage in activities that bring them joy and fulfillment.

Finances:

The 4 of Cups Tarot card can suggest stagnation when it comes to one’s finances. This could mean that a person is stuck in an unconscious pattern of spending and not saving or investing. It could also suggest that the individual is not feeling inspired to take action when it comes to their financial goals. The 4 of Cups could signal that a person is not taking advantage of potential opportunities or not recognizing their own gifts and talents that could be monetized. It is important for the seeker to start taking steps toward their financial goals, no matter how small, and to be open to surprises and possibilities.

Relationships:

The 4 of Cups Tarot card could indicate that an individual is feeling unfulfilled in their relationships. They may be in relationships that they feel they need to stay in out of a sense of obligation, or they may be feeling apathetic and disconnected from their partners. While the 4 of Cups could suggest that the person is content to stay in a relationship out of comfort, it could also suggest that the individual is holding themselves back from taking the chance to explore their passions and interests alone. It is important for the individual to take stock of the relationships they are in and to take action to improve or end them if necessary. It is also important for them to explore their passions and interests even if it means that they have to do it alone.
