This card combination suggests that you should try to find a healthy balance between these two aspects and be prepared to make some hard decisions. It also encourages you to look at the situation from a different point of view, which can lead to greater insight and understanding.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card is associated with many health-related interpretations. In the Tarot it is linked to release and letting go, which can have a significant impact on physical and mental health. It can indicate that you are in need of rest, relaxation and recuperation. This can be physically or mentally. You may be suffering from an illness, or an emotional state of mind such as depression or anxiety, and The Hanged Man suggests that you take time to heal and allow yourself to let go of the past. It can also symbolize an awareness of the connection between body and mind, allowing you to make conscious changes to your lifestyle and diet that are healthful and beneficial.

Finances

The Hanged Man can also be interpreted in a financial sense. This card warns to use caution, which is important in making any financial decisions. It can indicate that you need to be more mindful of your spending and saving, and it may be time to make a budget and stick to it. The Hanged Man suggests that you may need to give up something in order to gain something more in the long term. This could mean making sacrifices in the present moment in order to secure your financial future. It could also mean re-evaluating your financial goals and ensuring that they align with your values and what you truly want in life.

When it comes to relationships, The Hanged Man is a reminder to practice self-compassion and be prepared to let go of past hurts. This card can also indicate that it is time to reassess the dynamic of the relationship and make sure that it is still healthy and beneficial for both people. The Hanged Man can suggest that it is time to be honest with yourself and your partner and to take responsibility for your actions.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is indicative of a desire for long-term security. In health, this card indicates a need to plan for the future. This could manifest in taking preventative steps towards health such as regular exercise, healthy eating and adequate rest, or investing in medical insurance for possible future treatments. In some cases, the 4 Pentacles suggests a person may become obsessed with their health, looking for ways to stay healthy even if it means making extreme lifestyle changes.

Finances:

When it comes to finances, the 4 Pentacles Tarot card could hint at a need to save and invest for the future. This could mean cutting back on luxuries and focus more on having secure finances in the long run. This could mean paying off debt and building a savings account and diversifying investments. The 4 of Pentacles suggests that the individual is willing to sacrifice short-term pleasures in exchange for long-term security and stability.

Relationships:

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card could indicate a need for stability or security in relationships. This could mean committing to someone for the long-term or taking steps to solidify a relationship. This could also mean that the individual is seeking a sense of security in their relationships, and may be hesitant to take risks or allow themselves to be vulnerable. The 4 of Pentacles suggests that the individual may need to take steps to ensure their partner or themselves are secure in the relationship.
