A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Cups card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Cups card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Cups and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 3 of Cups and The 4 of Cups tarot cards suggest the idea of treasuring and celebrating the circle of support and friendship that is present in your life. It is a reminder to not take them for granted and to show appreciation for the love and bond you share with your friends. At the same time, it also warns against becoming too apathetic or feeling unfulfilled with the friendships you have created. Appreciate what you have and look for surprise gifts in the form of unexpected moments of kindness in your relationships.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Cups and 4 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 3 of Cups suggests that now is a time to celebrate good health and seek a supportive circle of friends who can help sustain it. This card reminds us to take the time to honor our bodies and to be grateful for the moments of wellness we experience. Additionally, the 3 of Cups is a reminder to practice the self-care that is necessary for long-term health and wellbeing. This might include engaging in meaningful activities, maintaining a healthy diet, exercising regularly, and seeking the social support that an active and healthy lifestyle requires.

Finances

The 3 of Cups reminds us of the importance of collaboration and shared resources. Money is not something that needs to be gained, nor kept or hoarded, but something to be exchanged, shared and enjoyed. By choosing to work with others and forming a circle of support people can find greater financial stability. Allowing others to share in financial responsibility is not only an economically sound decision but it also fosters closer relationships and a greater feeling of connection and support.

Relationships

The 3 of Cups encourages us to be open and welcoming to the people in our lives. Having a strong support system and a circle of friends is valuable and can help us to navigate challenges that we may be faced with. This card also reminds us to celebrate life and all the people in it, by taking the time to enjoy one another’s company, whether that be through shared activities, lively conversation or simply spending quality time together. By taking the time to really appreciate one another we can cultivate healthier and more joyful relationships.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 4 of Cups tarot card is a reminder to pay attention to your health and well-being. You may be feeling unfulfilled and apathetic when it comes to looking after your body. The card is a warning that if you don’t take action, health problems may arise in the future. It is important to take a step back and realise that you need to take care of yourself. Eating well, exercising, taking breaks and getting enough sleep can all help to improve your health.

Finances  
The 4 of Cups tarot card could represent a lack of motivation or a feeling of apathy towards your finances. This could be due to being overwhelmed by the current economic situation. It is important to take a step back and look at your financial goals and take any necessary action. The 4 of Cups could also represent unexpected gifts or windfall which can provide a great help to your finances.

Relationships  
The 4 of Cups tarot card could represent feeling of emptiness and dissatisfaction in relationships. This could be due to feeling unfulfilled from a lack of communication or connection with your partner. It is important to take time to listen to each other and share your thoughts and emotions. It is also important to not take each other for granted and to show appreciation and gratitude for what you have. The 4 of Cups could also represent a surprise gift or unexpected moment of connection in relationships. It could be a reminder to open your heart to possibility and to not underestimate the potential of love and companionship.
