A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 3 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 3 Of Wands mean together?

The 3 Of Wands tarot card also suggests commerce, expansion and travel potentially to help reach your goals. With a combination of clarity and focus, working with others and venturing out, there is no limit to what can be achieved. It is time to take a leap of faith and commit to the decision that has been made in order to reach your goals.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card can be interpreted differently when it comes to health, finances, and relationships. When it comes to health, this card can signify a need to let go of unhealthy patterns and habits, and work on creating healthier ones. By letting go and allowing closure, this card encourages self-reflection and a commitment to a better future. It can also be seen as a call to action – to take the steps necessary to achieve physical and mental wellbeing. In terms of finances, The Judgment Tarot card can symbolize the need to take stock of our current financial situation and make changes where necessary. It also invites us to consider our long-term goals and create a plan to achieve them. Lastly, when it comes to relationships, The Judgment Tarot card encourages clarity and evaluation of our current connections. It might signal the need to communicate more openly and honestly with our partners, and to make decisions that are aligned with the overall well-being of the relationship. At the same time, it can also emphasize that it is important to draw boundaries and create healthy relationships with others. Ultimately, The Judgment Tarot card encourages self-growth and new beginnings – a sense of clarity and purpose that can be applied in all aspects of life.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

The 3 of Wands Tarot card suggests that in areas of health, finances, and relationships, one should focus on collaboration and building strong foundations.

When it comes to health, this card is symbolic of an opportunity to explore alternative healing methods such as yoga, meditation, and massage. By practicing such methods and working with a health professional, one can improve their physical and mental wellbeing. Additionally, taking advantage of the power of collaboration can be beneficial for achieving better health. Utilizing the help of a friend, family member, or support group can provide the necessary motivation and encouragement needed to continue progressing in one’s health goals.

The 3 of Wands Tarot card also speaks to matters of finance and suggests that teamwork and collaboration are important aspects of achieving financial security. This can mean seeking the assistance of a financial advisor or partnering up with another to achieve a shared financial goal. Additionally, building strong connections with others in the same field of business can open up new opportunities for investment and collaboration.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, this card encourages open communication and collaboration. Working together is essential for any successful relationship, and that includes fostering meaningful friendships, partnerships, and family ties. The 3 of Wands Tarot card encourages one to reach out to their connections, seek advice, and work together to resolve conflicts. Collaboration can also include taking part in extracurricular activities, joining community groups, or even sharing tasks with a partner. Teamwork and cooperation will bring harmony and balance to all relationships.
