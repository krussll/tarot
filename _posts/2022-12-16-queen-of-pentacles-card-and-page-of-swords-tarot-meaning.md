However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Queen Of Pentacles card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Pentacles and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Queen of Pentacles and the Page of Swords tarot card together mean that you have both resourcefulness and curiosity in your life. With the Queen of Pentacles, you have the ability to make the most of your resources, whether they are related to fertility, domesticity, or something else. With the Page of Swords, however, you also have a need to seek out the truth, to ask questions and gather information. You may be prone to gossip and having prying eyes, but your curiosity also helps you discern the facts of the situation. Together these two cards point to a life of balanced ambition and curiosity.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health

The Page of Swords Tarot card is a reminder that no matter how healthy our physical health is, it can be undermined by unhealthy mental or spiritual health, or by entering into situations that cause us stress or anxiety. The Page of Swords is a warning that the imbalance between the physical and spiritual can lead to divergent paths and even illness, if we don’t take care to maintain a balance. In terms of physical health, the card can indicate a need to take more care of oneself, as well as being aware of one’s mental and spiritual state. It can also serve as a reminder to stay away from dangerous or unhealthy environments, such as overworking, not getting enough rest or avoiding physical exercise.

Finances

The Page of Swords Tarot card can be interpreted as a warning about financial matters. It can suggest that certain factors such as gossip, prying eyes, truth and curiosity can have a detrimental effect on one’s financial situation. The card can indicate that there may be an external force at work that is actively trying to undermine one’s financial security and stability. It can also serve as a reminder to remain vigilant and keep an eye on one’s finances, as well as to be aware of any potential pitfalls that can leave one vulnerable to financial ruin.

Relationships

The Page of Swords Tarot card can signify a warning about the potential for spying, gossip and rumor mongering to ruin relationships. It can suggest that both partners need to be more aware of the subtleties of the relationship, as well as the potential consequences of certain words or actions. Additionally, the card can indicate that one or both partners need to take care not to let external forces interfere and disrupt the relationship, as this could lead to misunderstandings and conflict. The card can also suggest that one or both partners need to be more honest and open with each other, as this can be essential for a healthy and long-lasting relationship.
