A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Justice card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 6 of Swords tarot card together can mean that it is time for a major transition in your life. It is time to make decisions that will bring about win-win solutions and lasting results. You can trust that the truth will come out as you make these changes, so that you can have the best outcome. It is time to relocate or move in order to bring a new phase of your life or the next step in your journey into fruition. It is time to look forward to a new beginning and the positive energy that change can bring into your life.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Above all, The Justice card teaches us to put ourselves in our own shoes and to make decisions with fairness and equity, for both ourselves and our families.

The card encourages us to be open and honest with our partners, to show respect and compassion, and to be honest with ourselves about our feelings and opinions. We must remember that relationships are a two-way street, and that both parties’ needs and desires must be taken into consideration. In regards to health, the 6 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of recovering from a difficult experience. This card can also suggest a change in career or lifestyle, which can open up avenues of increased financial stability.

For relationships, the 6 of Swords Tarot card indicates a period of transitioning, possibly without the support of those close to you. It could be a warning to distance yourself from the old in order to make room for the new. This tarot card suggests that you are in the process of making changes both internally and externally with the help of your own strength and determination. It also suggests that there may be a need for communication and compromise in order to make progress in your relationships. This card is a reminder that although changes may be necessary, they do not have to be difficult. Ultimately, the changes you make now have the potential to lead to greater joy and stability in your relationships in the future.
