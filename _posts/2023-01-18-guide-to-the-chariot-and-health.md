Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Chariot card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Chariot, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Chariot.

## The Chariot And Health – An Overview

The Chariot tarot card symbolizes action and change when it comes to health.

It suggests that a journey towards better health is necessary, and that one should take the initiative to do so.

This could involve seeking out a new healthcare provider, getting a second opinion, or trying a new form of exercise or nutrition plan.

The Chariot encourages us to take the driver’s seat and be proactive in our own health and wellbeing.

It reminds us that even small changes can have a big impact over time, and that as long as we stay focused and work towards our goals, we will be able to achieve them.

## Upright Meanings For The Chariot And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Chariot tarot card is an indication that you should take action and make a change in your health routine.

This could mean you should embark on a new fitness journey and try a new exercise routine or begin a diet.

Whatever it is, it’s a sign that you should focus on your health and get moving in order to reach your goals.

It’s a reminder that you are in the driver’s seat of your own health and you should take the lead and move forward.

With focus and dedication, you can reach your goals and achieve true health and wellness.

### What It Means For The Future

The Chariot tarot card is a powerful sign for positive change in your future health.

It indicates that you are on the cusp of a new journey and a new vehicle for healing.

You will be taking action to improve your health and you should be prepared to make some changes in order to make it happen.

With the Chariot in your future, you should be open to trying new treatments and therapies that could help you on your path to health and wellness.

The Chariot is the sign of progress, so use this energy to propel yourself forward and take charge of your health.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Chariot tarot card is an omen of action and change, and can indicate a period of renewal and transformation for those close to me.

It could be a sign that a new journey or project is about to begin, a sign that a new vehicle of progress is on the horizon.

It is a message of hope and progress, and a reminder to remain focused and to keep taking positive steps forward.

It can be a sign of progress in health, whether this be physical, mental or emotional.

It could be an indicator of a new way of managing health, or a sign that a personal challenge is about to be overcome.

The Chariot is an inspiring card that encourages us to keep pushing forward, no matter the obstacles.

## Reversed Meanings For The Chariot And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed Chariot tarot card is an indication of a period of stagnation and lack of progress in terms of health.

It can represent a stage in which, despite all efforts, one is not able to make any substantial improvements to their health, either physically, emotionally or mentally.

This could be down to an underlying issue that is not being properly addressed, such as an undiagnosed medical condition or a psychological block.

It can also signify a reluctance to make the necessary changes in order to achieve better health, such as adopting a healthier lifestyle or seeking professional help.

The reversed Chariot tarot card can be a reminder to take a proactive approach to your health and wellbeing, and to be mindful of any issues that may be preventing you from achieving your goals.

Taking the time to identify and address any underlying issues can be the first step in regaining a sense of control and improving your overall health.

It is also important to remember that progress isn’t always linear and that setbacks are inevitable, so don’t be discouraged if you don’t experience immediate results.

### What It Means For The Future

When The Chariot tarot card is reversed, it can be a sign that you are stuck in a rut and unable to move forward with your health journey.

This could mean that you feel lost in the direction of your health, or that you have been struggling to make changes to your lifestyle that will benefit your wellbeing.

It could also be a sign that something is holding you back from making healthy changes.

This could be an emotional or psychological block, or it could be something external that is preventing you from taking action.

For example, it could be financial constraints, limited knowledge, or lack of support.

The reversed Chariot tarot card is a reminder to take a step back and evaluate your current situation.

It is a call to recognize the barriers that are preventing you from making progress and to find ways to move past them.

When the card is reversed, it suggests that it is time to make a change and to start taking action towards a healthier lifestyle.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed Chariot tarot card indicates that your health is in a state of stagnation.

You may be feeling stuck in the same routines and habits that do not serve your physical health.

You may be facing an obstacle that is preventing you from making progress towards a healthier lifestyle.

The reversed Chariot urges you to slow down and take stock of your current situation.

Identify the blocks that are preventing you from making positive changes in your health.

The reversed Chariot also warns of potential health issues that may arise if you don’t take action.

With the reversed Chariot, you may be facing physical ailments that stem from past negligence or bad habits.

Now is the time to take control and make the necessary changes to improve your overall health.

Identify what needs to be changed and create a plan for yourself to help you on your journey to better health.

With the reversed Chariot, you are encouraged to take a step back and reevaluate the choices you have been making, so that you can create positive changes and move forward with health and wellness.
