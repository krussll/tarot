A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 4 Of Swords card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The 4 of Swords and 4 of Cups tarot cards together signify a need for both rest and renewal, as well as an awakening out of apathy. This could be the perfect time for a break; a period of solitude to recover from the past, regroup, and rest. You likely need a change of scenery or a time away to clear your head and refocus your energy. Although apathy can be a sign of being unfulfilled, the 4 of Cups can also bring a sense of surprise and show that something new is coming your way. If you can make time for yourself to retreat, you may find new purpose and see the gifts that life has in store.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Swords and 4 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes physical and mental health, as it suggests that you need to take some time out to recover and rejuvenate. When the 4 of Swords Tarot card appears, it is a sign that your body and mind need some rest, recuperation and regeneration. This means that right now, you may be feeling very tired, overwhelmed, stressed or even exhausted, and need to take some time to relax and replenish yourself. Taking a break, sleeping more, getting physical exercise and engaging in activities that bring you pleasure are all important ways to restore balance back into your life and nurture your health.

Finances

The 4 of Swords Tarot card can be a difficult card to interpret when it comes to finances, as it can suggest that you need to refrain from making large investments or rushing into new business deals. This card is a reminder for you to take a step back, pause and reflect on your current situation. Before making any big decisions, it is important for you to evaluate whether the costs outweigh the potential benefits. This card can also suggest that now is not the right time to take on any new responsibilities or make any large changes. Instead, it is important to focus on taking care of what you have and manage your resources wisely.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card suggests that now is a time to rest and reflect on your relationships. It can be a sign that perhaps you have been giving too much of yourself and it is now time to take care of yourself as well. It can also suggest that you need to take a step back to gain some perspective on the situation and reassess what truly matters to you. This card can also signify that it is time to create some boundaries and let go of anything or anyone that is not beneficial for your wellbeing. Taking the time to retreat and nurture yourself can be an important step towards creating healthier relationships in the long term.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 4 of Cups in Tarot can represent a stagnant mindset when it comes to health. It can mean that you are uninterested in making changes to improve your physical wellbeing, and instead are content with letting things stay the same. You may feel unfulfilled when it comes to your diet, exercise, and general health practices, and could be struggling to break out of your old routines. Alternatively, it can indicate that you are stuck in a cycle of poor habits and need someone to come along with a surprise gift to help you break out of it.

Finances:

In terms of finances, the 4 of Cups can mean that you lack enthusiasm or drive when it comes to your financial goals. It can represent a kind of apathy that is preventing you from making any meaningful progress. You may feel trapped in a monotonous cycle of spending and saving and need something to shake things up, such as a surprise gift from a friend or family member. This could be just the motivation you need to get back on track and start taking proactive steps to improve your financial stability.

Relationships:

The 4 of Cups in Tarot can indicate a lack of enthusiasm when it comes to relationships as well. You may feel unfulfilled in your current partnerships, or have difficulty maintaining a healthy balance between work and play. You may feel like you’re “stuck” in an unsatisfying cycle, and need something to surprise you and help you break out of it. This could come in the form of a thoughtful gift from someone close to you, or a new perspective that helps you appreciate your relationships in a different light.
