## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Queen Of Wands and King Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is an embodiment of the fire element, often depicting a woman with a crown of flames and a lion at her feet. This card is associated with a strong, confident, and feisty energy that encourages us to take charge of our lives. Generally, the Queen of Wands is seen as a positive sign, indicating the abilities of a person to manifest the desired outcomes in life.

Health:  
The Queen of Wands is a card of physical and mental strength and stability. She represents a feeling of being in control of your life, taking charge of your own health, and not relying on or depending on others. You may find that you have much more energy and enthusiasm when it comes to taking care of your body and mind.

Finances:  
The Queen of Wands is a sign of financial stability and prosperity. She encourages you to take risks and find success in your financial decisions. It also suggests that you should keep your finances organized, setting yourself up for long-term success. It’s important to remember to be smart with your money and not get into debt.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Wands card is associated with a strong self-assuredness and independence. This means that you have a healthy level of self-confidence and are comfortable in assertive relationships. The Queen of Wands encourages you to be honest with yourself and your partners, and to build strong and meaningful relationships.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health  
The King of Swords in a health reading can suggest taking control of your own health, being mindful of your body’s needs, and relying on your own intelligence and insights. Signs point to making decisions and plans for your health that are rooted in long-term thinking and rational decision-making. It is wise to take a multidimensional approach to your health, seeking physical, mental, spiritual, and emotional wellbeing. You are confident in your ability to spot signs of imbalance before they become a problem. Focusing on good nutrition, regular exercise, sufficient rest, and preventive care is key.

Finances  
The King of Swords indicates that you have the power to make wise financial decisions when it comes to money. This card suggests its time to get organized and apply your critical thinking skills to financial matters. Don’t be afraid to ask questions in order to gain clarity. Don’t be a victim of manipulation or become overwhelmed by financial matters. Take the time to educate yourself and develop a system that works for you. The potential rewards are worth the effort.

Relationships  
The King of Swords in a relationship reading implies that a rational and objective approach should be taken in order to attain the desired outcome. This may mean setting boundaries and making sure that your needs are communicated clearly and assertively. Create a plan for your relationships and stick to it. Make decisions about partnerships with wisdom and discernment. Be willing to stand up for yourself and don’t be afraid to negotiate and compromise. Allow yourself to be vulnerable with your partners and trust in your intuition. The King of Swords suggests that positive relationships are within your reach.
