On the one hand, you may be feeling a sense of fulfillment from leaving a legacy and feeling secure in your retirement. On the other hand, this security and sense of achievement can be overshadowed by nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression. This is likely due to a period of transition, as the future may feel daunting and scary after taking the important step of retiring and leaving your legacy. Taking the time to process this complicated blend of emotions, and leaning into the security of the 10 Of Pentacles, can be incredibly helpful in finding peace and stability.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of lasting wealth and prosperity, including health, finances and relationships. This card represents a legacy that extends beyond the material, something to be passed on from one generation to the next.

The 10 of Pentacles symbolizes that in terms of health, the hard work has been done and the rewards are on their way. One has made good choices which will pay off in the long run. It also signifies taking care of your health and looking after yourself now, so that you can enjoy the rewards of a long and healthy life.

In terms of finances, the 10 of Pentacles suggests that money earned during this time will not only be enjoyed during one’s lifetime but will be passed down to future generations. This means taking care of finances now, making wise investments and budgeting responsibly so that years down the line the fruits of labor will be enjoyed by all.

When it comes to relationships, the 10 of Pentacles can represent having lasting and meaningful connections with others. This is the card of bonds, for both family and friends, that transcend physical lifespans. It is about showing your loved ones not only your love but also your commitment to them and investing in relationships that will outlast even death.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The Nine of Swords Tarot card speaks to many aspects of life. When it appears, it may indicate a difficult time where you feel overwhelmed, anxious, and helpless. It can signify a period of fear, worry, and sadness due to internal or external circumstances. These feelings can manifest in both physical and mental health issues.

For physical health, the Nine of Swords tarot card can signal a period of stress. Stress can cause physical pain and exhaustion, as well as contribute to physical illnesses. It can make existing health conditions worse and can lead to aches, pains, and other symptoms. This card is a warning that one should take time to rest and relax, rather than push oneself too hard.

For finances, the Nine of Swords can mean that one is feeling overwhelmed by worries or debts. It can be a sign of hard times ahead, so one should take appropriate steps now to ensure financial security. It’s important to take care of oneself financially, and to create a budget, save money, and reduce debts.

In relationships, the Nine of Swords card can indicate a feeling of loneliness and despair. It can mean that one is feeling overwhelmed by problems and worries, and this can cause problems in relationships. It can lead to feelings of sadness and isolation, and difficulty connecting with others. In order to improve relationships, it is important to be honest about your feelings and to reach out for help and support.

The Nine of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take care of yourself and be mindful of your mental and physical health. It’s important to take time for self-care, to seek help and support, and to maintain healthy relationships. This card can signify a difficult time, but it can also be a reminder to focus on what is important and to make positive changes.
