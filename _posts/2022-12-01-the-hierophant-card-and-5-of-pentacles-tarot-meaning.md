## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is a very stark symbol that can leave those who encounter it in a state of worry and fear. This card speaks to a lack of resources, whether that be health, financial or relationship-wise. Those that are struggling to take care of themselves, their finances, and/or relationships, will find this card to be a powerful and striking reminder in the Tarot cards of their current predicaments.

With regard to health, the 5 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of a negative physical and mental state. Those that are struggling to manage their health and are feeling overwhelmed by their physical and mental state will find this card to be a stark reminder of the challenges they currently face. It could represent a need to make a major health decision, a lack of access to resources to help manage any medical issues, or even a general feeling of being completely lost in regards to health management.

In terms of finances, this Tarot card symbolises a struggle to make ends meet and a sense of financial insecurity. Those that are struggling to make ends meet or who are worried about their financial future may find this card to be a reminder of the financial challenges they are facing. The 5 of Pentacles could also represent an inability to afford basic necessities or a sense of being overwhelmed by the amount of debt one is in.

When it comes to relationships, this card indicates a feeling of separation or social isolation. People who find themselves feeling disconnected from their family, friends, or partner may find this Tarot card to be a potent reminder of their current relationships. It could represent a need to reach out and reconnect or a fear of being too vulnerable and exposing oneself to further hurt.

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks to the challenges of health, finances and relationships that many people are currently facing. Whether it is a feeling of physical or mental ill health, financial insecurity or a lack of meaningful relationships, this card is a powerful reminder to take care of oneself and to seek help if needed.
