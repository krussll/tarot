Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for spirituality. Understanding what The 7 Of Wands card means in regards to your spirituality can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 7 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 7 Of Wands.

## 7 Of Wands And Sprituality – An Overview

The 7 of Wands tarot card suggests that your health is something that needs to be defended and protected.

In order to maintain your health, you must be proactive in defending yourself against any potential health risks.

This card is a reminder to take preventative measures such as exercising regularly, eating healthy, and avoiding dangerous activities.

It is also important to be vigilant and take appropriate steps when faced with any potential health threats.

The 7 of Wands is a reminder that taking action to protect yourself and your health is the best way to stay healthy.

## Upright Meanings For 7 Of Wands And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

The 7 Of Wands tarot card represents the spiritual awakening of self-defense and protection.

It encourages us to protect ourselves against negative influences and to protect our personal power by standing firm against competition.

This card is a reminder that even though we may feel threatened or insecure, we have the strength to defend ourselves and stand up for our own personal truth.

It reminds us that we are capable of standing strong against any opposition and that we can use our courage and self-discipline to keep our spirits high.

The 7 Of Wands encourages us to take a stand and defend ourselves against any forces that try to limit or harm us in any way.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 of Wands is a card that speaks of standing firm in one’s beliefs and defending them against those who may oppose them.

This can mean that in my future spirituality, I need to be bold and unafraid to stand up for what I believe and protect my core values.

This can be a challenge to maintain a peace-loving outlook while standing up for what is right.

It is important to remember that while we can defend our beliefs, we should also be respectful of other people’s opinions and beliefs.

Ultimately, The 7 of Wands is a reminder that I should strive to be a strong and confident person, who can stand up for what is right and protect what I believe in, even if it means defending against competition.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 of Wands tarot card is a reminder that we should protect the spiritual wellbeing of those close to us and ensure their safety.

It encourages us to put up our spiritual walls and to be mindful of those around us who may be competing for our attention or trying to tear us down.

This card serves as a reminder to stand up for ourselves and those we love, to confront any negative energies and protect our spiritual wellbeing against any kind of competition.

It is a reminder to maintain our spiritual guard and to trust our intuition in order to safeguard our spiritual journey.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Wands And Spirituality

### What It Means For Me

When reversed, the 7 Of Wands tarot card can suggest that my spiritual journey is under threat of obstruction.

It may be that my spiritual path is being blocked by negative forces that are out of my control, or that I am simply feeling overwhelmed by the challenges that are in front of me.

The reversed 7 Of Wands can indicate that I need to take a step back and examine what I am doing in order to protect and defend my spiritual journey.

The reversed 7 Of Wands can also be a sign that I need to re-evaluate my spiritual goals and review the strategies I have implemented to reach them.

There may be an underlying fear that I am not doing enough and that I am not on the right path.

This card can encourage me to use my intuition and trust my inner wisdom to navigate through any obstructions in order to stay on the course I have set.

By taking the time to reassess my goals and strategies, and to take action to protect my spiritual path, I will be able to reach the spiritual destination I have set for myself.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 7 of Wands suggests that my future spirituality will be focused on the idea of protection and self-defense.

This could be interpreted as an indication that I need to be more mindful of the risks associated with my spiritual path and take steps to protect myself from potential harm.

I may be called upon to take a stand against those who threaten my spiritual beliefs and values, and to be prepared to defend myself if necessary.

At the same time, this card also suggests that I should not be too defensive, and instead use the energy of protection to open up opportunities for growth and expansion.

This could mean embracing the idea of a spiritual community and working in partnership with others to achieve common goals.

I should try to stay open to new ideas and perspectives, both in terms of my own spiritual journey and the journey of others.

By taking the time to understand the perspectives of others, I can learn to better protect my own spiritual beliefs and values.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

In tarot readings, the reversed version of The 7 Of Wands tarot card is said to indicate a lack of self-defense or protection against competition.

This can be interpreted to suggest that the spirituality of those close to me may be lacking in their ability to defend themselves, or that they may be allowing themselves to be taken advantage of in some way.

They may also be struggling to stand up for themselves and their beliefs, or may be feeling overwhelmed by the obstacles and challenges that come their way.

The reversed 7 Of Wands can also be interpreted to mean that those close to me may be feeling vulnerable and exposed to the judgement of others.
