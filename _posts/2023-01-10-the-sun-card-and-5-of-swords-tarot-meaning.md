A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 5 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 5 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The Sun tarot card generally speaks to a joyous outcome and success, while the 5 of Swords tarot card signifies bullies, theft, violence, and abusive relationships. When taken together, these two cards can signify a challenging path that still leads to material happiness and joyous outcome, as well as a victory earned through hardship. It could also signify being able to overcome bullying, abusive relationships, violence, and theft and to come out the other side feeling triumph and joy. The combination of these two cards signals that there will be struggle, but ultimately it can lead to success and a bright future.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Sun and 5 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Sun tarot card in combination with the 5 of Swords tarot card in your love life is a cautionary statement. While the Sun tarot card indicates material happiness and a joyous outcome, it is the 5 of Swords tarot card that may be the more important card to note. The 5 of Swords tarot card means bullies, theft, violence and abusive relationships. It is important to be aware of these potential hazards as they can have a negative impact on any lasting relationships.

First and foremost, it is important to realize the potential for one’s own actions to lead to bullying, theft, violence and abusive behavior. This can be especially true in relationships, where the dynamics of power and control can create an environment in which such behavior more likely to occur. It is important to recognize that this behavior must be nipped in the bud and not allowed to spiral out of control. On the other hand, it is also possible that you may find yourself in a relationship in which you are the victim of such behavior. In this case, it is important to remember that you are in control and always have the right to set boundaries and voice your opinion, even if it may be difficult. If a relationship feels abusive or untenable, it is important to reach out for help or remove yourself from the situation.

It is also important to note that while The Sun tarot card indicates material happiness and joyous outcomes in relationships, this card also encourages you to reflect on the boundaries of any relationship. What matters most is not money or possessions, but the love and respect that can be found in a relationship. The 5 of Swords tarot card is a reminder to look for genuine happiness and connection in all relationships, and to recognize when a situation may be unhealthy and require a change. By bearing both of these interpretation in mind, it is possible to maintain healthy relationships with the right boundaries, a joyous outcome and true material happiness.

### What does The Sun and 5 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When The Sun tarot card and The 5 Of Swords tarot card appear together in a reading related to finances or job, it can be a sign of mixed outcomes. On the one hand, it may be a sign of material happiness and joyous outcomes. Success in your endeavors is likely, but you may need to be aware of potential bullies and threats in the workplace that could derail your progress.

At times, those bullies may come from external sources, such as bosses or competitors. It could be that someone is attempting to steal your idea or take credit for your work. In these circumstances, it’s important to stand up for yourself and take necessary steps to protect your interests.

At other times, the 5 Of Swords could signal the potential for violence or abuse within the workplace. This could come in the form of verbal or physical threats, or it could be part of a workplace culture that rewards aggressive behavior. The Sun’s message of joyous outcomes and material happiness is a signal to remain vigilant and stay strong in the face of any abuse or bullying.

Overall, when The Sun and The 5 Of Swords appear together in a spread related to finances or job, it’s a reminder to stay focused, protect your interests, and take a stand against any threats in the workplace. With the combination of success and the ability to remain vigilant and assertive, you can overcome any obstacles and achieve material happiness and joyous outcomes.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

Health  
The Sun Tarot card often represents a positive outlook on health, emphasizing that physical and mental health issues can be overcome with positive energy and a confidence in the self. It encourages us to take care of ourselves, heal ourselves, and pay attention to our own health needs as well as the health of those we love. The Sun Tarot card can be seen as a reminder to prioritize our own well being and health in order to feel good, have energy and be able to live up to our highest potential.

Finances  
The Sun Tarot card is a strong symbol of abundance, prosperity and financial success. It encourages us to think in the long-term rather than in the short-term, and to strive to build financial stability and sustained, lasting wealth. Moreover, it suggests that money and material possessions, if acquired in the right and honorable ways, can bring joy, happiness and satisfaction. The Sun Tarot card is a reminder to make responsible decisions, plan carefully, take calculated risks, and realise that a wealth of knowledge and understanding will lead to greater financial security.

Relationships  
The Sun Tarot card symbolizes joy, harmony and the power of the bond between people. It encourages us to be open to new relationships and to nurture those that already exist. It promotes connection, love and understanding, understanding each other’s perspectives and finding common ground. The Sun Tarot card can be seen as an assurance that radiating positivity and good will into the world will reward us with happiness, companionship and good fortune. It reminds us to act sensibly in our relationships, to be open to compromise and to remember that relationships are about taking joy in each other’s presence, as well as talking and listening.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

The Five of Swords Tarot card represents a time of turmoil and conflict in health, finances and relationships. This card is a stark warning that a difficult period is on the horizon, and it pays to be prepared for whatever comes next.

In terms of health, the Five of Swords indicates that there may be an increase in stress, poor sleep, and a general lack of energy and motivation. If you have an existing health issue, the situation is likely to worsen and you may find yourself struggling to take care of yourself.

In terms of finances, the Five of Swords can represent a period of financial insecurity. You may find yourself overspending on items or services that are not necessary and, in the worst cases, you may find yourself victim of fraud or theft.

In relationships, the Five of Swords serves as a reminder that there may be difficult times ahead. You may find yourself in a verbally or physically abusive relationship, or you may be on the receiving end of bullying. In the worst cases, you may find yourself in a situation where violence is a possibility.

It is important to remember that the Five of Swords does not necessarily predict violence, theft or abuse, but it does indicate that difficult times lie ahead. It is essential to pay attention to both what is going on in your life and around you and to be prepared for anything. With preparation and support, you can navigate these difficult times and come out the other end a stronger and more resilient person.
