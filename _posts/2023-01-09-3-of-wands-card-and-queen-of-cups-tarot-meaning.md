A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Wands card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Wands card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Wands and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The 3 of Wands tarot card and the Queen of Cups tarot card together mean a focus on teamwork which involves supportive nurturing and healing. This combination of cards encourages expansion through collaboration, building connections and finding new opportunities to travel and explore. The nurturing energy of the Queen of Cups card helps guide and heal any issues or challenges that come up during the journey. The 3 of Wands helps form a plan of action and provides the courage necessary to do what needs to be done for successful expansion. This combination of cards also encourages taking a risk, having faith in yourself, and entering into new business ventures or partnerships.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Wands and Queen Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 3 of Wands Tarot card suggests that a healthy lifestyle may be in the works. This card encourages people to make changes to their diet, exercise, and sleep habits that can lead to greater wellbeing. It encourages people to work together to find solutions to their health issues. It suggests that seeking medical advice, working together with medical professionals, and engaging in a variety of activities to promote health can be beneficial.

Finances  
The 3 of Wands Tarot card has associations with finances, specifically in regards to enterprise. It signifies the building of financial foundations either through investments, or through business partnerships or collaborations. It indicates that taking action to grow finances can be profitable and can lead to greater wealth. It suggests seeking out opportunities in the business world, and suggests taking risks with money in order to expand wealth.

Relationships  
The 3 of Wands Tarot card is a card of teamwork, collaboration and partnership. In relationships, it suggests that working together to reach a common goal is beneficial. It encourages people to strive for mutual understanding and communication. It suggests to focus on the common good in relationships and encourages people to explore new places, experiences and even people in order to create a stronger bond. It’s a card that encourages adventurousness in relationships, so that each partner can explore new sides of their relationship.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health  
The Queen of Cups in a Tarot card reading indicates a strong interest in and compassion towards healing. This card represents a mature and nurturing individual, who is empathetic and understanding towards the health and wellbeing of others. On a physical level, this card is a sign that you are taking great care of your body, tending to your own needs, and focusing on ways to improve your health. In a reading, the Queen of Cups can suggest that you are exploring alternative methods of healing, like meditation, aromatherapy, or acupuncture, and that you are learning to be more in tune with your body’s needs.

Finances  
The Queen of Cups in a Tarot card reading reflects a wise and knowledgeable approach to financial matters. This card often appears as a sign that you are considering the long term, taking a balanced approach to making and keeping money, and being aware of the consequences of your financial decisions. In a reading, the Queen of Cups can suggest that you are making wise investments, creating a plan that is both smart and secure, and looking for creative ways to get ahead financially.

Relationships  
The Queen of Cups in a Tarot card reading is a sign of many positive qualities in relationships. This card suggests a warm, nurturing, and caring individual who is invested in the wellbeing and happiness of those they care about. In a reading, the Queen of Cups can suggest that you are investing your time into deepening relationships, taking care of your own emotional needs, and seeking out connections that feel safe and secure. This card also encourages you to find a balance between giving to others and maintaining boundaries, as well as seeking help when you need it.
