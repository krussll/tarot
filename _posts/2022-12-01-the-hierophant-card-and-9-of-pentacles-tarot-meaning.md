A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hierophant card and 9 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hierophant card and 9 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hierophant and 9 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 9 Of Pentacles tarot card together mean living life your own way, while still adhering to certain traditions and expectations. You’re not constrained by what society deems as ‘normal’, yet you’re still able to enjoy all the benefits that come with wealth and prosperity. You also gain the satisfaction of self-sufficiency and being able to indulge in solo pleasures without feeling guilty. The combination of these two cards signify freedom within the boundaries of structure and conformity.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

It cautions against violating the norms and expectations of society when it comes to personal health and wellness. It’s a reminder to adhere to accepted standards of healthy lifestyle choices, from getting regular exercise and proper nutrition to staying humble and not ignoring the advice of medical professionals. It encourages individuals to take care of their physical, mental and spiritual wellbeing and cultivate a sense of respect and trust between those providing health care and patients.

Finances:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is a sign of tradition, conformity, and convention when it comes to finances. It cautions against taking risks and going against the grain of accepted financial habits. It is a reminder to stick to wise financial choices, avoid excessive spending, and save and invest wisely. It encourages individuals to practice fiscal responsibility and build up a foundation of financial stability and security. It can remind individuals to be mindful of their own expectations when it comes to money, as well as the impact of their decisions on the larger economy.

Relationships:  
The Hierophant Tarot card is a sign of tradition, conformity, and convention when it comes to relationships. It cautions against going against the grain of accepted social norms and expectations, particularly when it comes to interpersonal relationships. It’s a reminder to be mindful of cultural differences and expectations, accept one another’s differences, and compromise when necessary. It encourages individuals to practice empathy and compassion, build trust and respect, and strive to build strong, healthy relationships with those around them. It can also remind individuals to be mindful of the impact and importance of their words and actions and strive to maintain an atmosphere of mutual understanding and respect.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

The 9 of pentacles tarot card symbolizes a person’s comfort, independence, and abundance. In terms of health this card can represent deep relaxation and joy from taking pleasure in solo activities. It speaks of taking time for oneself, engaging in activities that bring peace and fulfillment, and nourishing oneself with adequate rest and healthy lifestyle habits.

In terms of finances, the 9 of pentacles tarot cards reveals financial abundance, prosperity and success. This can mean being secure in having enough money for security and enjoyment. It also encourages taking action to increase wealth and secure financial stability. Taking financial risks can result in great profits with this card’s influence.

In terms of relationships, the 9 of pentacles tarot card speaks of joy that comes from time spent alone, contentment and a secure feeling of being able to sustain and maintain a healthy and balanced relationship. This card encourages independence and self-sufficiency without sacrificing intimacy. At its highest level of expression, it speaks of trust and taking the time to build a trusting and secure relationship.
