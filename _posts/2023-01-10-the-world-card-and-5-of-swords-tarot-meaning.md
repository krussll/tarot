A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The World card and 5 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The World card and 5 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The World and 5 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The World tarot card and The 5 Of Swords tarot card suggest that you have reached the end of a difficult journey or experience. You have achieved a goal or learned an important lesson, and now it is time to move on. However, the 5 Of Swords card reminds you to be mindful of the potential for bullying, theft, or violence along the way. It is important to be aware of yourself and your surroundings, as some experiences can be emotionally or psychologically abusive. This combination encourages you to take any necessary steps to protect yourself and stay safe during your travels, as your sense of completion and fulfillment is worth fighting for.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The World and 5 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The World tarot card and the 5 Of Swords tarot card in a love context could point to a situation of hostility and disregard on your part. You may have been going through a difficult experience and have allowed it to harden your heart, leading to a lack of consideration or respect for your partner. This could lead to bullying, stealing, and violence in your relationship, or it could signify an abusive relationship.

As a result, the World card could represent a period of healing and growth where you can let go of the pain and learn to leave behind destructive patterns of behavior. You may have to travel away from the situation and focus on getting to know yourself again, learning to accept and love yourself without relying on the hurtful ways that you used to cope. Through this journey of self-exploration, you will learn to rebuild your relationship with your partner.

This period of transition and transformation can signify a powerful journey in which you will discover the true power of your love and find the strength to become a better individual – meaning a healthier, more balanced relationship with your partner. The World tarot card, when combined with the 5 Of Swords, can offer you the opportunity to learn the lessons of your past, and to come away from it with a new outlook. With this comes the potential to cultivate more loving and meaningful connections with others, and to move forward with a stronger, more secure relationship with your partner.

### What does The World and 5 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When The World and The 5 Of Swords tarot cards are combined, it is a warning sign to pay close attention to your finances and job. The World card reflects the need to complete a goal or lesson, while The 5 Of Swords speaks of a dark and dishonest force at work, such as theft, bullying and other forms of abuse. In terms of your finances and job, this combination of cards hints at a potentially difficult situation, where you need to be mindful of potential wrong-doing or manipulation.

The World card suggests that you have the knowledge, experience and wisdom to complete the task at hand, but you must be careful to not let others take advantage of you during this process. The 5 Of Swords warns that you should be aware of people who are trying to cut corners, bully you or steal from you. Keeping an eagle-eye out for any suspicious behavior and activities is critical, as it can help you successfully overcome this obstacle.

At the same time, the World tarot card also speaks of emigration and travel, which can affect your finances and job. It is important to ask the right people for advice, get the right support and do your homework before making big decisions. If the situation requires emigration, do your due diligence and research the economic and employment prospects in the new city or country.

Finally, it is imperative that you stay focused on the goal at hand, and not become distracted by any pressures or distractions around you. The World card also speaks of completion, and if you are able to navigate any potential roadblocks with patience and wisdom, you will eventually reach a successful and satisfying conclusion.

### What does The World and 5 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The combination of The World tarot card and The 5 Of Swords tarot card can provide insight into your current health. The World is a card of completion and travel, while The 5 Of Swords is a card of violence, bullying and abuse. Taken together, these cards suggest that change is necessary in order to improve your health and well-being.

The World card suggests that you have completed a goal or a lesson. This could refer to a change in lifestyle or attitude that you have made in order to promote better health. By recognizing the positive changes you have made, you can give yourself credit for the progress that you’ve made. This could include a healthier diet, regular exercise, better sleep habits, or anything else that has helped you achieve better physical and mental health.

The 5 Of Swords tarot card suggests that you have faced some type of violence, bullying, or abuse in your life. It may be time to take a stand against this type of behaviour and protect yourself from further harm. This could mean reaching out to a counsellor or support group, or even just breaking away from any toxic relationships that you have been involved in. It is important to seek help if you are feeling unsafe, as this can help ensure your safety and improve your overall health.

Together, The World tarot card and The 5 Of Swords suggest that a change is necessary in order to improve your health. By recognizing the positive changes you have made and protecting yourself from further harm, you can make the journey towards a healthier and happier life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

For health, the World Tarot card signifies completion and renewal. It means that your health is reaching a new level and you are about to reap the benefits of the hard work and dedication you have put into your routine. After taking care of your body, you can begin to enjoy the fruits of your labor. This could mean anything from a clean bill of health and a newfound energy to a newfound appreciation for the physical and mental blessings you have.

For finances, the World Tarot card suggests that you are about to come into a period of completion and prosperity. It is a indicator that you have done the hard work necessary to get to where you need to be and it is now time to reap the rewards for yourself. It suggests that at this point, you are free to invest and make the best decisions that will help you maximize your potential for success.

Finally, for relationships, the World Tarot card signifies a period of renewal and completion. It is a time where you feel that your partnership has reached a point of balance and harmony, having gone through all the hard work of communication and dedication. Your relationship is now strong and healthy, full of understanding and loyalty. It is a testament to the strength of your bond and connection with one another.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

The 5 Of Swords Tarot card is a difficult card to interpret as it can manifest in many different forms. In relation to health, this Tarot card indicates physical or mental abuse, violence, and bullying. It suggests that the victim may be feeling overwhelmed, exhausted and powerless. This card can indicate that someone is in an abusive relationship, where their partner is taking control of them and preventing them from expressing their individuality and feelings. It can also indicate a lack of self-esteem, leading to feelings of worthlessness and depression.

In relation to finances, this Tarot card suggests that the person is in a situation of unfairness, where their resources are being stolen or taken away. It can manifest as manipulation and aggressive persuasion to take what isn’t rightfully theirs. In some cases, this card can be interpreted as taking advantage of someone’s kindness and generosity, leading to feelings of being oppressed, exploited and taken advantage of.

Finally, in relation to relationships, this Tarot card suggests a situation of tension or conflict. It can indicate a power struggle between two people, where one person is trying to oppress or dominate the other. It can also suggest a lack of respect and trust, leading to arguments and disagreements. In short, this card reflects an environment of violence, coercion, manipulation and coercion.
