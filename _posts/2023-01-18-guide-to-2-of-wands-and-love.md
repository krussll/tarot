Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The 2 Of Wands card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 2 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 2 Of Wands.

## 2 Of Wands And Love – An Overview

The 2 of Wands tarot card represents waiting for results, making a choice, and travel plans, when it comes to health.

It is important to take the necessary steps to ensure your health and wellbeing is taken care of.

This card encourages you to take the time to contemplate the choices you need to make in order to achieve better health.

It is also a sign that you should look into getting regular checkups, as well as taking the time to go on a health-related trip to improve your physical and mental health.

Taking the time to make thoughtful choices and taking the time to plan out a health-related trip can help you achieve the best possible results for your health.

## Upright Meanings For 2 Of Wands And Love

### What It Means For Me

The 2 of Wands speaks to the necessity of making decisions in order to move forward in any aspect of life, including love.

It is a reminder to take action and not remain in a state of limbo when it comes to relationships.

It could mean that you are waiting for results, and it’s time to take the plunge and make a choice, be it to move forward or to part ways.

Alternatively, it could be a sign to make travel plans to meet a love interest.

Either way, this card encourages you to take initiative and create a path that will lead to a satisfying outcome.

### What It Means For The Future

The 2 of Wands tarot card suggests that my future love may require me to make a difficult choice soon.

I should take the time to carefully consider all of my options before making any decisions.

Additionally, I should expect to wait for results, as the outcome may not be immediate.

I may also be presented with the opportunity for travel, which could further my understanding of my new relationship.

With careful consideration, I am sure that the right path forward will become clear.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 2 of Wands in tarot speaks to a time of waiting for the results of something, a time of making a choice and feeling the uncertainty that comes with it.

When it comes to the love of those close to me, this card can speak to a time of contemplation or inner reflection, of asking myself what I truly want out of my relationships and of trying to make the best decision for me and for my relationships.

It can also be a sign of plans for travel being made, either for myself or for someone close to me, which could bring about an exciting opportunity to explore and discover new things together.

## Reversed Meanings For 2 Of Wands And Love

### What It Means For Me

When the Two of Wands is reversed in a love reading, it can indicate feelings of restlessness and a lack of direction in a romantic relationship.

This card can signify a fear of commitment, a feeling of being stuck, or a fear of making the wrong choices.

It can also point to a lack of self-confidence or an inability to trust in the relationship.

This card can also indicate a need for communication and compromise in order to move forward in the relationship.

If partners are not able to express their feelings and desires, they may find themselves stuck in a stalemate, unable to make progress.

To move forward, the couple must work together to create a plan for the future that both of them can agree on.

The reversed Two of Wands can also signal a need to take a break from the relationship.

Sometimes, distance can make the heart grow fonder and provide the much-needed clarity to make an informed decision about whether to stay in the relationship or move on.

It can be difficult to make a decision, but this card can remind us to trust our instincts and make the best choice for ourselves.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed 2 of Wands tarot card can mean that a choice is about to be made in regards to a potential relationship.

It could be that you have been waiting for some kind of sign or result to come in, and that this sign or result is now telling you that it is time to make a decision.

You may be considering whether or not to move forward with someone, or if you should keep exploring other options.

You may also be considering whether to take a risk on a new relationship, or if you should stay in the same place as you are now.

The reversed 2 of Wands could also indicate that you are ready to take a chance on love and commit to a relationship.

You may be feeling ready to embark on a journey with someone, and to make travel plans with them.

This could be a sign that you are ready to open yourself up to a new person and to explore the potential of a romantic relationship.

The reversed 2 of Wands is a sign that you have the courage to take a chance and to trust that this relationship will bring you joy and fulfillment.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed 2 of Wands appears in a tarot reading it can indicate that the love of those close to me is on shaky ground.

It may indicate that I am feeling out of touch with my partner, or that I am not sure of their feelings for me.

This can lead to feelings of insecurity and uncertainty in my relationship, and a lack of communication between us.

It may be time to take a step back and consider the state of our relationship, as well as look for ways to reconnect.

The reversed 2 of Wands can also suggest that I am avoiding making a choice about our relationship.

I may feel that I am stuck in a holding pattern, not sure if I should stay or go.

This can create a sense of paralysis, and it is important to remember that I do have the power to make a decision and take action.

I should be honest with how I am feeling and let my partner know, so that we can work together towards a resolution.

Ultimately, the reversed 2 of Wands is a reminder to take the time to connect with those I love, and make sure that our relationship is healthy and strong.
