A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hermit and the 6 of Pentacles tarot cards together suggest that now is the time to take a step back from the outer world and focus inwardly. Going within for spiritual illumination and truth can help you to reach out to those in need and offer your help. Your generosity of spirit and offering of aid to others will open up a deeper level of understanding and appreciation for yourself, and your path of enlightenment. This is a time to listen to your inner voice, to reach out to your community, and to share your knowledge, compassion, and love with others.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Hermit and 6 Of Pentacles mean together for your love life?

When these two cards appear in a reading about love, it can indicate a strong need for individual growth and contemplation. The Hermit card prompts us to be introspective and take some time for ourselves, in order to gain a better understanding of what we truly want and need from our relationships. Focusing on our inner development is essential in order to move forward in a healthy and meaningful way.

At the same time, however, it’s important to not forget the importance of generosity and benevolence in our relationships. The 6 of Pentacles card encourages us to be open-minded and generous when it comes to giving and receiving in our interpersonal relationships. Taking the time to provide support and assistance to our partners, as well as recognizing when we may need extra help ourselves, can go a long way in strengthening our bonds.

At its core, this combination of cards encourages us to manifest a balance in our relationships, between taking time for self-reflection and actively giving of ourselves to others. Engaging in activities that promote self-growth and being generous with our support and resources are both equally essential components in maintaining healthy and meaningful relationships.

### What does The Hermit and 6 Of Pentacles mean together for your finances?

When the Hermit tarot card and the 6 of Pentacles tarot card combine, it suggests that one should go within for clarity and take a quest for personal truth in order to find the best approach to achieving financial success. It is a reminder that one should be giving and generous with their resources in order to create positive results. Whether it be giving their time, effort or money, the key is to be generous with what you have and to be open to receiving help if needed.

The Hermit tarot card is also encouraging one to reflect inward and seek spiritual enlightenment with regards to their finances and career. It suggests that one should take a deeper look at what they want and need in life and base their decisions off of those deeply rooted desires. It is a reminder to pay attention to their intuition and go with what feels right for them, all for the purpose of achieving financial and career success.

The 6 of Pentacles tarot card adds to this message by encouraging one to not just be generous and give their resources, but to also be open to receiving help from others. It is asking you to accept help and guidance from those who are knowledgeable and experienced in order to help you in your pursuits. It is a reminder that it’s ok to ask for help and accept resources from others who may be in a better place. Doing so will only add to your financial and career success.

Put together, The Hermit tarot card and the 6 of Pentacles tarot card suggest that one should go within for clarity in order to seek spiritual enlightenment and personal truth to achieve financial and career success. It is a reminder to be generous with your resources and to seek the help of others when needed. With this combination, you should be able to find a path towards a more secure and prosperous financial and career future.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 6 Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

When it comes to health, The Hermit Tarot card encourages balance and harmony between the physical, mental and spiritual aspects of the person. It asks the individual to find their center, to be still and go within to gain insight and understanding of the deeper issues of health. It may be necessary to step away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and find time for reflection and meditation. This can help to understand the root cause of any physical and emotional ailments, allowing for more effective treatments that go beyond just the physical.

The Hermit Tarot card can also be a call to take time to nurture the mind and spirit, creating a state of well-being that can affect the body. Taking time to be in nature, to exercise and to enjoy different activities that bring pleasure, such as a hobby or a yoga class, can all help to create a feeling of balance and help to restore vitality to the body.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card can be a reminder to look within for guidance when it comes to finances. This can involve taking the time to research and understand more about financial matters and investments. When feeling overwhelmed by the complexity of money matters, it can be a good idea to take time away from the hustle and bustle of everyday life and retreat to a quiet, peaceful space. The Hermit Tarot card encourages the individual to focus on the deeper issues of money and to make careful, thought-out decisions.

The Hermit Tarot card can also be a reminder to look beyond the materialistic world of money and focus on the spiritual aspects of finances. Being mindful of how money is spent, donating to charity and being grateful for the financial resources that the individual has, can all lead to greater wealth and abundance.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card can be a call to take time for self-reflection in order to gain clarity in relationships. When relationships become complicated, it can be useful to take some time away from the relationship to gain perspective and understanding. This can help to identify any underlying issues that need to be addressed in order for the relationship to move forward.

In terms of relationships, The Hermit Tarot card encourages the individual to take a spiritual approach, looking beyond just the physical aspects of the relationship and focusing on the inner needs. Taking time out from the relationship to meditate, to nurture the individual’s own spiritual growth or to go within to gain insight can all be beneficial to a relationship.

This card tells us to be generous with ourselves, to nourish ourselves through positive self-care habits and to give generously to others who are in need of assistance. It suggests that it is important to maintain a healthy balance between giving and receiving, providing space for both in our busy lives.

Finances: The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of wise decisions and a willingness to share resources with others. It encourages us to be generous with our finances, to be mindful of where our money is going and to use our resources in a way that benefits not only ourselves but those around us. It suggests that it is important to find balance in our financial lives, and to use our resources to help others while still taking care of our own needs and wants.

Relationships: The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes the importance of generosity and balance when it comes to relationships. It encourages us to be generous with our time, energy, and emotions by building meaningful connections with those around us. It suggests that it is important to give and receive in equal measure, so that no one in the relationship feels overwhelmed or overwhelmed. It speaks of a commitment to creating strong, healthy relationships built on trust, understanding, and shared values.
