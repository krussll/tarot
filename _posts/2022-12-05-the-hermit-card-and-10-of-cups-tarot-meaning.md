## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and 10 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

Lastly, the Hermit Tarot card also has implications for relationships. It encourages us to take a step back and evaluate our relationships. Asking us to consider our individual needs and boundaries, it suggests recognizing and honouring these when engaging with others. It reminds us to take time for ourselves, to reassess our relationships and find the right balance between giving and receiving. It insists on prioritizing our self-care and understanding our needs. The Hermit Tarot card is a reminder to stay true to our own values and create relationships that are mutually beneficial and enriching.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health:

The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a card of health, contentment and balance. It symbolizes that all aspects of our physical body are in harmony, and that all aspects of our mental and emotional health are in balance. This card speaks to the importance of having a strong sense of balance and contentment in all aspects of life, and in regards to our physical, mental and emotional wellness. It is a reminder to nourish the body with healthy food, exercise and rest, to nurture the mind and spirit with positive affirmations, and to keep relationships with others healthy and full of love and support.

Finances:

The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a card of financial stability and abundance. It is a reminder to maintain a healthy financial lifestyle, including creating a budget and living within its means. This card also speaks to the importance of eliminating any financial fears or worries. It encourages us to practice good financial habits, such as setting aside emergency funds and striving for long-term sustainability. The 10 of Cups encourages us to remain optimistic about our finances and to trust that everything will work out for the best.

Relationships:

The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a card of love, loyalty and family unity. It symbolizes the importance of building strong and secure relationships to create a supportive and loving environment. It tells us to look for ways to show our love and appreciation for those closest to us, and to be present in our relationships. This card also encourages us to create a safe space for open communication and understanding. It reminds us to cultivate relationships that bring joy and balance into our lives and help us to reach a sense of contentment and fulfillment.
