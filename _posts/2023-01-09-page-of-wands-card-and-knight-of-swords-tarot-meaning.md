A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Wands card and Knight Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean together?

The Page of Wands card with the Knight of Swords card suggests that you have an active kid in your life or a creative project or idea that is fast-moving and requires speedy decision making. This could be an adolescent on the brink of a lifestyle change, or a new venture in which you’ll need to be decisive and act quickly. You may also be presented with an opportunity that requires you to think and act swiftly; the Page of Wands and Knight of Swords will support and guide you as you make your decisions.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Page Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

The card indicates a great amount of energy and enthusiasm that is needed in order to make this change successful. It suggests that the individual needs to make necessary changes in order to better their physical and mental well-being. This could include making healthier diet and exercise choices, seeking medical assistance if needed, or even just making a point to take time for themselves and ensure that their lifestyle is adequate for them to be healthy and happy.

Finances: The Knight of Swords Tarot card is associated with making abrupt changes in one’s financial situation. This could mean branching out into new investments, taking calculated risks, or seeking new opportunities. The card suggests that this individual needs to move quickly and decisively to make changes that will benefit their financial situation. Taking calculated risks and being open to new ideas is necessary to make this happen.

Relationships: The Knight of Swords Tarot card suggests that a person needs to be honest and direct in their relationships if they are to achieve positive outcomes. This individual needs to be confident in their ability to express their thoughts and feelings honestly and openly. They also must be willing to have difficult conversations when needed in order to make sure their relationships are healthy and balanced. Ultimately, this card suggests that the individual needs to take the initiative and be proactive in order to ensure that their relationships are successful and rewarding.
