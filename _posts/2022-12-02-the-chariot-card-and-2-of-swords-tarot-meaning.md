A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and 2 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and 2 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and 2 Of Swords mean together?

When The Chariot tarot card and The 2 Of Swords tarot card come together, it can mean that a period of action and change is approaching. Right now is the time to make a difficult decision or compromise in order to propel your journey forward. The new vehicle being referred to may not be literal, but instead a metaphor for the right course of action to take. Visualize yourself in the driver’s seat of this metaphorical vehicle, navigating the road ahead while taking ownership of the decisions you make. All of the potential change that is coming your way can be made easier and smoother if you take the time to make the best decisions along the way.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Chariot and 2 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 2 of Swords tarot card suggests that for those who are dealing with health issues, it is time to make a decision or compromise. You may be in a place where you are trying to decide between two different treatments or options, and the best way to tackle the situation is to choose the option that is best for your health. It is important to be honest with yourself and consider what is best for your wellbeing. Consider talking to your healthcare provider to help you make the best decision possible.

Finances

The 2 of Swords tarot card speaks of compromise and decision-making when it comes to finances. If you are struggling to make ends meet, you may need to find a way to balance your budget. It is important to look at your finances objectively and weigh up the options. Consider how to get the most out of your resources and make sure that you are making decisions based on the long-term financial stability rather than short-term gains.

Relationships

The 2 of Swords tarot card encourages you to make decisions and compromises in relationships. If you are facing a situation where two parties are clashing, take some time to assess the situation and decide what is best for both parties. Consider the needs, wants and values of both people. Communication and compromise are key, so try to find a resolution that both parties can be happy with. Don’t be afraid to have difficult conversations and reach an agreement.
