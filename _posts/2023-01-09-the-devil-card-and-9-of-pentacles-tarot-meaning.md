A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Devil card and 9 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Devil card and 9 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Devil and 9 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Devil tarot card and the 9 of Pentacles tarot card together could mean that you are indulging in luxurious pleasures and rewards to the detriment of your relationships, health and wellbeing. You may be sliding into addiction and obsession, putting your materialistic needs ahead of everything else, in a desperate attempt to fill an internal void. The consequences of this may be detrimental to both you and those around you, leading to feelings of envy, greed, and sexual lust.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Devil and 9 Of Pentacles mean together for your love life?

When The Devil tarot card and The 9 Of Pentacles tarot card come up together in a reading, it suggests that selfishness, materialism, and envy could be preventing you from having a healthy, loving relationship.

You may be focusing too much energy on achieving financial gain and material possessions, and allowing these goals to take precedence over your love life. Your excessive need for comfort and luxuries could be preventing you from taking risks in love and being emotionally vulnerable. You may be putting too much attention on yourself and seeking pleasure in solo activities, rather than giving and taking in a loving relationship.

Additionally, your obsessive or addictive behavior could be preventing you from having a successful relationship. You may be easily tempted by vices such as sex, drugs, alcohol, or gambling, and your partner could be struggling to keep up. Some of your partners may even feel neglected or inferior next to your materialistic and often selfish desires.

This card combination suggests that your overindulgence in luxury, greed and obsession can have a negative impact on your love life. To have a successful relationship, you must be willing to let go of these materialistic desires and focus on living more selflessly. Spend time cultivating an emotional connection with your partner, participating in activities that make your relationship stronger, and working on self-control and moderation.

### What does The Devil and 9 Of Pentacles mean together for your finances?

The Devil tarot card and The 9 Of Pentacles tarot card create a complicated image when it comes to your finances and job. In one sense, the Devil card can represent a dangerous relationship with money, where you could become addicted to the power and security it can provide, or obsess over the material acquisition of wealth. At the same time, an unhealthy focus on material possessions brings with it a spiritual cost. Greed and envy can lead to financial hardship, as well as a lack of any enjoyment of the luxuries that we do manage to attain.

The 9 Of Pentacles offers a more positive outlook. Here we find financial gain, comfort and selfsufficiency which can come from a life of hard work and dedication. This card represents balance and the attainment of financial security without becoming reliant on the allure of material possessions. It can also suggest a life of luxury and solo pleasures, with the focus on reward rather than obsession.

When considering the Devil tarot card and the 9 of Pentacles together, it is important to focus on balance. The card encourages you to maintain a healthy relationship with money and material possessions, where we can enjoy the comfort and luxury of a comfortable life, without becoming obsessed. Greed and envy can lead to financial hardship, and in order to avoid this, it is important to strike a balance between enjoying life’s pleasures and practicing financial responsibility.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Devil mean?

The Devil Tarot card symbolizes several negative aspects of life that can hurt health, finances, and relationships. It is a warning to be aware of the danger of addiction, greed, envy, materialism, and obsession. These dark forces can have a negative impact on the individual’s physical, mental and spiritual health, as well as their financial and emotional wellbeing.

For physical health, The Devil Tarot card warns that those drawn to unhealthy practices such as substance abuse, overeating, excessive drinking, gambling, or promiscuity, are particularly vulnerable. It is a cautionary sign to not let a person’s desire for material success and pleasure poison their mental and physical wellbeing. Addictive behavior can lead to serious health problems, and it is important to be aware of the risks of letting these behaviors go unchecked.

In terms of finances, The Devil Tarot serves as a reminder that greed and excessive materialism can have destructive effects. It warns of the potentially damaging consequences of letting money and possessions become more important than people and relationships. It is also a reminder that spending too much with no real plan for financial security can be a dangerous path.

The Devil Tarot also has implications for relationships. Those drawn to the card may be prone to feeling envious of others, and to putting their own desires before those of their loved ones. It is a signal that relationships can suffer from feelings of possessiveness and obsession, and from an unhealthy focus on material success. The card also reminds us to be aware of how our behavior can cause hurt and resentment to those we care about.

Ultimately, The Devil Tarot card is a warning that addiction, greed, envy, materialism, and obsession can have harmful effects on our health, finances, and relationships. When tempted by these darker impulses, it is important to be aware of their potential consequences and to find healthier ways of coping.

This card suggests that you can be independent, self-sufficient and enjoy the reward of taking care of your body, mind and spirit. With the right mindset and discipline, you can create an oasis for yourself which enables your health and wellbeing in the long term.

It suggests that there is potential for wealth and abundance when you trust your instinct and focus on making good and smart financial decisions. This card also encourages you to find joy in your accomplishments, such as saving money, investing, or starting your own business.

Relationships: The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to enjoy the solo pleasures of life, as well as to appreciate the relationships and connections you have with others. This card can indicate that you can find contentment and joy when you enjoy the company of others and share experiences with them. By recognizing the importance of relationships and valuing the people you share your life with, you can build a safe and supportive network of people.
