A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Knight Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Justice card and Knight Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Knight Of Pentacles mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the Knight of Pentacles tarot card combined speak to patience, hard work, and the need for win-win solutions. In this combination, the Justice card suggests finding a balanced approach to the issues at hand, and relying on the truth to come out in the end. The Knight of Pentacles encourages us to be reliable and put in the hard work and effort necessary to achieve the desired outcome. This combination encourages us to trust that, through patience and effort, a lasting and fair solution can be found.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice Tarot card symbolizes cause and effect in terms of a person’s health. This could mean that all the choices you make in terms of your lifestyle and diet will directly affect your overall health and wellbeing. Decisions such as exercising regularly, eating nutritious meals and taking necessary vitamins could result in a strong and healthy body. On the other hand, making poor choices such as eating junk food and leading a sedentary lifestyle can lead to a variety of health issues. The Justice Tarot card subtly reminds you to always be conscious of your decisions and how they will affect your own health in the long run.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card also represents win-win solutions when it comes to money. This means that you should always strive to make decisions and investments that will benefit both yourself and others. Rather than taking a risky gamble with your finances, it’s wise to consider all possible outcomes and make decisions that will create a win-win situation. Taking the time to calculate the risks and rewards can help ensure that you make secure financial decisions and secure a good financial future.

Relationships: Lastly, The Justice Tarot card symbolizes that the truth comes out in relationships. This could mean that any dishonesty or manipulative behavior in a relationship will eventually be revealed. It’s important to practice honesty, understanding and respect in all relationships so that trust can be built. By being open and honest with one another and striving for a win-win situation, relationships can thrive and grow in a healthy way.

## What does Knight Of Pentacles mean?

Health

The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of good health, mindfulness and stability when it comes to physical and emotional health. It encourages one to develop a balanced, healthy lifestyle. This card will remind you to make time for yourself and to take care of your body. Regular exercise, proper nutrition and getting enough rest are a few of the things associated with the Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card. It also implies that you should be aware of any potential risks to your health and take necessary steps to counteract them.

Finances

The Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes financial success through slow and steady progress. It advises against making quick decisions or risky investments. It encourages the use of practical methods such as budgeting and saving up before making investments. This card can also represent the need to be wise and frugal with money. It is important to be aware of both your income and expenses to maintain financial stability.

Relationships

In relationships, the Knight Of Pentacles Tarot card stands for commitment, stability, and hard work. It implies that you should be willing to put in the necessary effort to keep relationships going strong. This includes being patient with each other, communicating honestly and being understanding. Overall, this card encourages balance, trust and respect in relationships, as well as being mindful of potential challenges, in order for them to stand the test of time.
