This could mean that you will be engaging in some kind of sport or other physical activity where you will have to battle against fellow competitors to emerge on top. Additionally, the fast-talking person signified by the Knight of Swords could indicate that some changes in your lifestyle or habits may be necessary in order to ensure your success. It’s important to stay focused, remain proactive and tap into the strength and determination of the Knight of Swords in order to achieve your goals.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 5 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 5 Of Wands and Knight Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

It can be a sign that you need to find ways to incorporate physical activity, rest, and healthy nutrition into your life. It can also suggest that you are engaging in unhealthy competition with others that is leading to physical and emotional exhaustion. If you have been feeling overwhelmed, this card is a reminder that you need to focus on yourself and your own health, rather than competing for attention or status.

Finances: The 5 of Wands Tarot card suggests that you are engaging in some kind of financial competition, whether it be through risky investments or competing with others for a job or a promotion. It can also suggest that you need to be cautious in order to avoid any kind of financial trouble. The card is a reminder to be careful and creative when it comes to your finances; it suggests that the path to financial stability requires patience and discipline.

Relationships: The 5 of Wands Tarot card in a relationship context suggests that competition is at play between two people. Whether it is a friendly competition or a fierce rivalry, it can be difficult to find balance in the relationship. Seeking compromise, working together, and nurturing each other’s strengths can help you both achieve a healthier balance. It is also important to make sure that your relationship is not causing either of you physical or emotional exhaustion. This card suggests that finding a healthy balance in your relationship will help foster a healthier and more balanced connection.

## What does Knight Of Swords mean?

The Knight of Swords Tarot card can be interpreted to mean a major lifestyle change or adjustment. Those who draw this card may experience a significant overhaul in their health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the Knight of Swords is a reminder that it’s time to take a closer look at your physical, mental and spiritual wellbeing. This may mean that you need to invest some time and energy into developing healthier habits such as exercising more, eating better and doing activities that promote relaxation. It’s important to be mindful of your overall wellbeing during this period of transformation.

When it comes to finances, this card is a sign that it’s time to review your budget and start to make fiscally responsible decisions. It’s also an indication that you may need to make changes to your spending habits in order to get your finances on track. This may mean cutting back on non-essential expenses or redirecting resources to investments that can provide greater returns in the long-term.

Finally, the Knight of Swords Tarot card can signify that it’s time to leave the past behind and focus on fostering new and healthy relationships. Be open to trying new things and experiences—you never know what great connections you can build with others. At the same time, be aware of the boundaries that you need to set for yourself in order to look out for your own wellbeing. All in all, this card encourages friends and family to embrace life’s changes with enthusiasm and optimism.
