However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and 8 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean together?

The 7 of Wands tarot card and the 8 of Cups tarot card together mean a moment where you are having to make a hard choice between taking the initiative to defend your interests and standing up for yourself, or withdrawing and moving on from a difficult situation. The 7 of Wands is about protecting yourself against competition and taking a stand for what is important to you, while the 8 of Cups encourages you to retreat from a difficult situation and create some distance. In this moment, it is important to look within to see which option is best for you, as both decisions have their benefits and drawbacks.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

By being proactive and taking care to protect yourself, you can work to cultivate a strong and balanced sense of health.

Finances:

This card encourages you to stay active and aware of potential financial risks and to take steps to guard yourself against them. ## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

The 8 of Cups Tarot card can mean different things when it comes to health, finances and relationships.

Health: In terms of health, the 8 of Cups Tarot card typically signifies a need to retreat or withdraw from a situation. This card is usually seen as a warning that we should take some time out of our normal routine and focus on ourselves. It suggests that it is time to stop pushing so hard and allow our bodies to get the rest and recovery that it needs. It also could be telling us to take a break from bad habits like smoking or over-eating, and to make time for healthier activities like yoga or meditation.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the 8 of Cups Tarot card can signify a need to reassess our current financial situation. It is a reminder that changes are necessary to get back on track and create a healthier relationship with money. It suggests that we should take a step back and take the time to rethink our priorities and our spending habits. We should also be prepared to let go of any negative associations we have with money and focus on making wise investments and creating a budget that is sustainable.

Relationships: For relationships, the 8 of Cups Tarot card can signify the need to move on and find closure. This card is often seen as a reminder that it is okay to let go of unhealthy relationships and to seek out healthier ones. It is a reminder that sometimes we need to take a step back and reevaluate our current relationships and assess whether they are helping or hindering our growth. The 8 of Cups Tarot card can also suggest that it is time to forgive and let go of past hurts so that we can move forward and find healthy, prosperous relationships.
