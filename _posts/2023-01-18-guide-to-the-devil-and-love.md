Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Devil card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Devil, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Devil.

## The Devil And Love – An Overview

The Devil tarot card is a reminder that we can become stuck in unhealthy patterns of behavior and thinking.

It symbolizes addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust.

All of these can have a negative impact on our physical and mental health.

These destructive behaviors can lead to stress, anxiety, depression, and other mental health issues.

Additionally, addictions can lead to physical health issues, such as increased risk of heart disease, stroke, lung cancer, and other serious illnesses.

The Devil tarot card serves as a warning to break free from unhealthy habits and embrace healthy behavior and positive thinking instead.

## Upright Meanings For The Devil And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Devil tarot card warns of the pitfalls of being obsessed with love.

It cautions against allowing love to become an addiction, allowing it to lead to greed, envy, materialism, and sexual lust.

The Devil warns that such obsessions can lead to dark consequences and can have a negative impact on the relationship.

Love should be about mutual respect, understanding, and trust, and not about becoming dependent on another person.

The Devil reminds us that true love is never a one-sided affair and is based on mutual support and respect.

### What It Means For The Future

The Devil tarot card is a sign that my future love may be plagued by addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust.

This may lead to unhealthy, unbalanced relationships, and I should be on the lookout for partners who display these qualities.

Unfortunately, it can be difficult to recognize these traits in someone, so I should be cautious and proceed with caution.

My future love may be a passionate person, but I should be aware of the potential for underhandedness, manipulation, and deception.

I should be open to love, but also be careful and watchful for signs of these negative qualities.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Devil tarot card is a warning to those in love to be aware of the potential pitfalls of materialism and obsession.

It cautions against the dangers of allowing a desire for possessions or other worldly pleasures to come between those closest to us.

It suggests that allowing such feelings to take precedence over love can have a damaging effect, as it can lead to envy and greed, and can threaten to destroy the bond of trust and loyalty between loved ones.

The card serves as a reminder to stay true to our hearts and to focus on our love for each other, rather than on the material things of this world.

## Reversed Meanings For The Devil And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The Devil card is reversed in a reading about love, it is a sign of liberation from the negative influences of materialism and obsession.

The reversed Devil is an indication that you are able to look beyond the superficial aspects of relationships and find the deeper, more meaningful connection and true love.

The reversed Devil may also be a sign that you are ready to let go of any addictions or obsessions that have been blocking your path to true love.

In a reversed Devil Tarot card, you may be able to recognize the unhealthy patterns in your relationships and be able to break free from them.

You can also be freed from the shackles of envy and greed, as you will be able to appreciate the love that you have and not become consumed with wanting more.

This could be a sign that you are ready to commit more fully and sincerely to the person you love and share a more meaningful and fulfilling connection.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Devil tarot card suggests that in my future love, I will be able to overcome any potential issues of addiction, greed, envy, materialism, obsession, and sexual lust.

This card reminds me to make sure that I stay away from any of these negative influences in my relationships and to focus on what is healthy and beneficial.

Instead of striving for the external trappings of love, this card suggests that real, meaningful, and lasting relationships come from building a strong foundation of trust, communication, and emotional connection.

Rather than allowing the temptations of materialism, lust, or envy to take over, the reversed Devil tarot card encourages me to make sure that the focus of my relationships is on the growth and development of both individuals involved.

By seeking out partners who are committed to exploring their own relationship with themselves and their partner, I can create a bond that is rooted in compassion, understanding, and mutual respect.

This card also encourages me to make sure that I am not sacrificing my own needs in order to make my partner happy, and to make sure that I am taking care of my own emotional and physical needs.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Devil card appears in a reading, it can indicate that it is time to let go of unhealthy attachments and/or obsessions in order to create more space for love within relationships.

This could mean releasing anxieties and insecurities that have been holding back love and connection, or ending unhealthy patterns of behavior that have been blocking true intimacy.

Additionally, the reversed Devil card can represent an opportunity to acknowledge and confront the darker side of human nature, while still allowing the light of love to shine through.

By recognizing the need to let go of the unhealthy attachments and obsessions, the reversed Devil card can encourage us to open our hearts to those close to us.

This allows us to experience more joy and love within our relationships, being freed from the chains of materialism, envy, and addiction.

It is a message that the highest form of love is in cultivating genuine connection with those close to us, through genuine acceptance and understanding.

Such love is more valuable than any material possessions or temporary pleasures.
