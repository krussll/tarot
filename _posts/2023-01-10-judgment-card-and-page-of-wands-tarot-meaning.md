A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and Page Of Wands card together in particular.

The Judgment card and Page Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and Page Of Wands mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and the Page Of Wands tarot card together suggest that you may be at a crossroads and at a point of clarity in your life. The Judgment tarot card serves as a wake-up call to help you find your life’s purpose and make the decisions that will help you achieve it. The Page Of Wands tarot card suggests that this will involve taking on a new project or creative idea with the enthusiasm of an active kid; an exciting new beginning. Together, these cards indicate that you have the power to take control of your life and to make decisions that will lead to positive changes.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

It is time to take ownership, take action and reinvent yourself and your love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Judgment and Page Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Judgment and Page Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

The Judgment Tarot card in the realm of health often signifies a time of clarity and decision-making about your health. This could be for things like deciding to go to the doctor for a check-up, taking prescription medications, or even creating a new health plan. During this card, you will be making conscious decisions about how you’re going to take care of yourself in the future and these decisions will be guided by a deep understanding of your body and your current state of health. You will be asked to be intentional and diligent in your self-care.

In the realm of finances, The Judgment Tarot card represents a wake-up call to take a closer look at how you manage your money. You may realize that some of your current decisions or behaviors are not in line with your long-term financial goals and you will be prompted to create new habits and approaches to your finances. This could involve making a budget, setting up a savings plan, paying off debt, or investing in certain financial products. Ultimately, this card is about making a final decision and committing to a plan that is in line with financial success.

When it comes to relationships, The Judgment Tarot card often signifies a moment of clarity about the situation. This could involve an awareness that something isn’t working in the relationship and you will be called to make a decision about its future. This could be a decision to work through it or to let it go. In either case, you will be able to reach a conclusion that is in line with your highest truth and this will be accompanied by a deep understanding of what is best for you. The Judgment Tarot card encourages us to make conscious decisions in our relationships and to act on them with resolve and self-confidence.

## What does Page Of Wands mean?

Health

The Page of Wands Tarot card can be seen as an indicator of a fresh start in health. It can indicate that a person has the enthusiasm and energy to begin a new and healthier lifestyle. This could be anything from starting a new diet and exercise plan, to embarking on a course of natural therapies and self-care. The Page of Wands Tarot card can also suggest that a person is ready to explore alternative ways of healing and self-care, such as yoga, dancing, or regular meditation.

Finances

The Page of Wands Tarot card is a sign that a person may be ready to embark on a new and more successful financial venture. This could be anything from turning a hobby into a business, to participating in the stock market or investing in property. It can also indicate that a person is ready to take an active approach to their finances, and is willing to take risks in order to achieve their desired level of financial success.

Relationships

The Page of Wands Tarot card can indicate a person’s willingness to be more active and involved in relationships. It can suggest that a person is ready to explore new possibilities and forms of connection, whether that be with a romantic partner, family member, or friend. This Tarot card can also suggest that a person is willing to put in the effort to make connections and build relationships, and that they have the energy and enthusiasm to take on a new and creative project with someone else.
