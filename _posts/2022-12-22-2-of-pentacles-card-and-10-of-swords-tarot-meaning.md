A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Pentacles card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The 2 Of Pentacles and The 10 Of Swords tarot cards together can mean that you are at a crossroads in your life. On one hand, you are presented with two different choices and must choose carefully. On the other hand, you know that the choice you make could end up being a complete and painful ending. You must weigh all of your options and decide which outcome is the least destructive. It may be difficult, but it’s important to make the right decision, even if you have to hit rock bottom to do it.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

Perhaps you’re trying to decide between two different partners, two paths in a relationship, or two different outcomes. Whatever the case may be, it’s a reminder that you are in the unique position of having to choose one option over the other. With this card, it’s important to remember to weigh your options so that you can make the most informed choice possible.

On the other hand, the 10 of Swords can represent a complete and painful ending. This card can signify that the end of a relationship is upon you, and that this is going to cause a great deal of pain. Perhaps you have come to the realization that things cannot go on as they are, and that ending the relationship is the only way forward. If this is the case, it’s important to recognize the bravery it takes to make such a difficult decision.

Ultimately, The 2 of Pentacles and The 10 of Swords together can signify a confusing, difficult, and emotionally charged period of your life. It’s a reminder that a major decision is coming, and that it may not be an easy one. Regardless of your outcome, it’s important to remember to take your time and weigh your options carefully.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Pentacles and 10 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder to take care of your health. It shows that you must find a balance between rest and exercise, between healthy eating habits and occasional indulgences. The pendulum of the card shows that it’s important to be mindful of your decisions and to create a plan for the future. The card encourages you to make the best decisions for your health, like taking time for yourself and eating the right nutritious food.

Finances:

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages you to stay organized and flexible when managing your finances. The card suggests that you consider all of your options before making decisions. It also reminds you to look to the long term when making financial plans. Take the time to weigh all of the pros and cons and make informed choices that will set you up for success in the future.

Relationships:

The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card indicates that in relationships, you must find a balance between flexibility and commitment. The pendulum of the card reminds you to pay attention to changes in your relationships and be open to different opinions and ideas. This card suggests that in order to keep your relationships healthy and strong, you must be willing to continually re-focus and adjust. Be flexible and work together to come up with creative solutions to any issues that arise.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card indicates a complete and painful ending when it comes to matters of health. This card symbolizes a time that could feel like hitting rock bottom, be it in terms of physical or mental health. It can signal a long period of disease or injury, as well as exhaustion and depletion of energy reserves. It can also mean coming to terms with a difficult diagnosis or facing a chronic or terminal illness head-on. This card imparts the idea of having a deep-seated need for rest and retreat in order to restore the balance.

Finances:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests a complete and painful end when it comes to financial matters. This card symbolizes a difficult time, often accompanied by a feeling of being completely bankrupt. It can signal a period of massive debt, job loss, sudden financial drain, or a severe financial crisis. It is important to take time to assess the situation and make sure that all resources are used to the best of their abilities to get out of the financial quagmire. With enough determination and hard work, there is always an opportunity to rebuild and turn financial fortunes around.

Relationships:

The 10 of Swords Tarot card can indicate a complete and painful ending when it comes to relationships. It often signifies broken hearts, broken bonds, and severing of ties. This could mean the end of a romantic partnership, a friendship, or even a family connection. It is important to be aware that this card symbolizes a time of pain and sorrow, but it also provides an opportunity for honest reflection and personal growth. Allow yourself the time and space to let go of the past and focus on the future. This card can be a reminder to take a step back, focus on the self, and heal from the past in order to move forward.
