## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 4 of Wands Tarot card is a wonderful card when it comes to health, as it is a sign of balance and harmony. This card symbolizes that you are taking care of yourself in a balanced way and that you are making time to focus on your mental, physical, and spiritual health. You may be taking a break from work, school, or family obligations in order to invest in yourself and recharge. This is an especially helpful card to draw if you’re in the midst of a particularly stressful or difficult time, or if you’ve been neglecting your health.

Finances  
The 4 of Wands Tarot card carries with it a message of financial stability. This card could be a sign that you are finally beginning to get your finances in order and are taking the necessary steps towards financial freedom. You may be managing your finances responsibly, paying off debts and creating a budget. With this card, you can sense that your money worries are fading and that you are on the path to financial stability.

Relationships  
When it comes to relationships, the 4 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of homecoming and celebration. This card symbolizes that you’re starting to reunite with friends, family, and loved ones after a period of separation or difficulty. It could be a sign that you are finding balance in your relationships, learning how to forgive and reconnect. This card encourages you to be open-minded and patient with your loved ones as you strive to reconcile and celebrate being together again.

## What does 5 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 5 Of Swords Tarot card signifies that your overall health and well-being could be compromised, whether by accident or through physical violence. This card suggests that health should not be taken lightly and that self-care and safety should be prioritized. It indicates that there could be a need to protect oneself mentally, physically and emotionally.

Finances

The 5 Of Swords Tarot card can be a warning to beware of theft and scams. There could be a need to keep an eye on your bank accounts and credit cards and to be careful with financial dealings. It could signify that there is an unsettling tension in the financial environment, and it could be wise to take precautionary measures.

Relationships

The 5 Of Swords Tarot card can signify an abusive relationship, or it could indicate that an unhealthy relationship could be heading in that direction. It suggests that it is important to be aware of signs of a toxic or damaging relationship, and to take steps to protect oneself from the potential danger. The card can also indicate a need to set and maintain healthy boundaries, both in romantic and platonic relationships. It can be wise to look for any signs of manipulation, dishonesty or power imbalances as a warning that a relationship might not be as healthy as it can be.
