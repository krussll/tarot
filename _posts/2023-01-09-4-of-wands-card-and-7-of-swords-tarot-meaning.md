While you may be surrounded by family and friends, it is important to be aware of and guard yourself against any possible betrayal or dishonesty that can be lurking in the shadows. Enjoy all the fun and festivities while staying mindful of your boundaries and personal safety.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 4 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the completion of a cycle and offers a sense of relief that comes with the attainment of a goal. By having achieved a level of emotional and physical balance, you can now take the time to relax and take stock of your well being. This card is indicative of physical health, energy, and vitality, indicating that you have been sticking to a healthy lifestyle and reaping the benefits. It could also point to times of respite from disease or illness, or newfound strength and resilience.

Finances:

In terms of finances, the 4 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes financial stability and security, as well as the potential for increased wealth. This is a positive sign of good fortune, as well as a reminder to take calculated risks and be judicious with your resources. You may have recently achieved success after working hard towards a goal, or you could be enjoying a period of financial stability. If you have recently come into a greater degree of financial security, this card encourages you to continue in the same vein and maintain a sensible approach to your finances.

Relationships:

The 4 of Wands Tarot card symbolizes the sturdy foundations laid in relationships and the joy of coming together with family and friends. This card can signify the beginning of a new relationship, or a period of much-needed rest and relaxation with those closest to you. It suggests that you have put in the hard work to create lasting relationships, based on trust and loyalty. This is a great time to celebrate, share your successes and express gratitude for your loved ones in your life.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 7 of Swords can indicate possible health problems caused by dishonesty or betrayal. It could mean that you have been taking part in harmful activities such as smoking, drinking, or taking drugs in an effort to cope with difficult emotions. These activities, if continued, could begin to take a toll on your physical health. Additionally, if you are feeling anxious or stressed due to a betrayal or a sense of feeling unheard, this may manifest itself as physical symptoms.

Finances: The 7 of Swords can indicate that you may find yourself in a situation in which financial resources are being taken from you or are not as robust as you need them to be. This can stem from dishonesty on the part of another person or entity, or it can be the result of bad financial decisions that have been made by you. It is important to take a step back, assess the source of the problem, and make adjustments to ensure that your finances will remain secure in the future.

Relationships: The 7 of Swords can indicate betrayal or dishonesty in relationships. This card can indicate a lack of trust between two people, or that someone has been deceiving another to their own gain. It can be difficult to move forward with a healthy relationship if there is a lack of trust or if one person feels like they have been taken advantage of. In this case, it is important to take the necessary steps to clear the air and reestablish trust if possible, or to seek other relationship options if necessary.
