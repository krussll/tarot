However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does The Lovers and King Of Wands mean together?

The Lovers and King Of Wands tarot card combination speaks to the power of partnership and strong leadership. It is a call to choose wisely, to look for a partner with whom you can share a deep love and also trust to lead you to new heights. Together, these cards emphasize that when there is a strong love, trust, and understanding between two individuals, they can achieve anything, no matter how formidable the obstacles may appear. This card combination also illustrates that when individuals have the courage to work together and use their combined power and charm, they can create something bigger than either could alone.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Lovers and King Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Lovers and King Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health:

The Lovers Tarot card speaks of health in terms of a partnership. It is a reminder that there is strength in two, whether it is between you and a partner, doctor, or nutritionist. One cannot be healthy or successful on one’s own; it takes the help and support of someone else. The Lovers Tarot card suggests that having a supportive relationship or helping hand can help one stay healthier in body and mind. This doesn’t mean one must be reliant on someone else, but a loving partnership can be a great motivator for personal health and well-being.

Finances:

The Lovers Tarot card symbolizes a strong partnership when it comes to finances as well. This can be a partnership between two people, two businesses, or two financial advisors. It speaks to the power of two and the cost-savings and security that can come with a balanced partnership. This card also encourages one to make wise financial decisions, and find the right balance of taking risks and playing it safe. It is important to remember that a partnership requires give and take, and both partners need to contribute in order to be successful.

Relationships:

The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder that relationships require a delicate balance of both giving and taking. It also suggests that a partnership or marriage can bring greater security and success when both parties are committed to the relationship. This card speaks to the power of two, and emphasizes the importance of compromise, communication, and connection. It can also be a sign of deep love, and encourages one to find a relationship that is fulfilling and mutually supportive. It is a reminder that relationships can be a source of joy and growth, and that a strong partnership is essential for long-term success.

## What does King Of Wands mean?

You have the courage and willpower to make positive health changes, such as following a diet or changing your lifestyle habits. With this card, you may be motivated to focus on improving your health through nutrition, exercise and other healthy steps. The King of Wands can also indicate that you’re willing to take risks for the sake of your health.

Finances: The King Of Wands Tarot card suggests that you may take a commanding role in your finances. You may be willing to take risks when it comes to your money, often making calculated decisions. This may lead to financial success. You may also use your strong personal power and ambition to find creative ways to make money. With the King of Wands, you may be successful in pursuing financial dreams.

Relationships: The King Of Wands Tarot card suggests that you may have a strong personality when it comes to relationships. You may be in control of the dynamics between you and your partner, or you may take a leadership role in your friendships. You may be bold and daring when it comes to relationships. You may also have the courage to pursue meaningful relationships, even in the face of challenges. The King of Wands indicates that you can handle difficult conversations and tough situations with strength and charm.
