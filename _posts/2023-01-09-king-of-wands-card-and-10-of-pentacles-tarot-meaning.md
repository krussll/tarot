However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

This could mean financial stability and retirement or building a lasting business and brand that can continue to be enjoyed far into the future. The combination of the two cards indicate an individual with the determination and drive to accomplish great things, even under difficult circumstances.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The Ten Of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes legacy, retirement, and inheritance. It serves as an invitation to reflect on our past and plan for our future. For those who have attained financial and health stability, the card indicates one’s ability to leave a legacy for future generations.

When it comes to health, the Ten of Pentacles encourages us to take care of our bodies and minds now so that we can enjoy our later years. Taking the necessary steps today—such as maintaining a healthy diet, exercising regularly, and engaging in leisure activities—will provide us with long-term benefits that will help us to live a fulfilling life in our later years.

When it comes to finances, the Ten of Pentacles suggests that our hard work may be rewarded in the form of inheritances, retirement plans, and investments that will provide us with financial security in the future. This card also encourages us to save for the future and to invest wisely so that our wealth can be shared with our loved ones.

When it comes to relationships, the Ten of Pentacles encourages us to build strong relationships with the people in our lives. Nurturing our friendships, family bonds and romantic partnerships will ensure that our connection with those we love endures long after we are gone. Building a secure and loving bridge between generations is a way to pass on our legacy and ensure that our family remains connected long after we are gone.
