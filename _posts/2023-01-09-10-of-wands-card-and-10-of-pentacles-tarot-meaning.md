A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and 10 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and 10 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and 10 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 10 of Wands and the 10 of Pentacles together could mean that you are feeling overwhelmed by the weight of responsibilities and obligations on your shoulders, and this is preventing you from fully enjoying your life. You may be exhausted from pushing yourself to meet the expectations of others, but you could also be focused on building something of lasting legacy. With the stress of the 10 of Wands and the security of the 10 of Pentacles, you are working towards ensuring that your hard work is not wasted and that your efforts will pay off in the long run. It is possible that you will be able to eventually enjoy the fruits of your labour, although you may need to take some time to rest and recover along the way.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card is a sign of feeling overwhelmed and overburdened physically and mentally. Those who are strongly influenced by this card are often feeling a tremendous amount of physical and mental exhaustion. Issues such as fatigue, headaches, digestive problems, and anxiety can arise from too much stress and pressure. It is important to make time to relax, practice self-care and to develop healthy coping strategies to deal with these feelings of being overwhelmed.

Finances:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card is indicative of a person who is under immense financial strain. It may be caused by a huge financial burden or by taking on too many financial obligations. This card urges the person to take responsibility and to prioritize their finances. They must be willing to take risks, or be creative with their problem solving in order to reduce the burden.

Relationships:

The 10 of Wands Tarot card suggests that a person may feel weighed down due to their relationships. There may be too many responsibilities in the relationship or a partner may be expecting too much from them. It is important to take a step back from the situation and evaluate if it is truly healthy and beneficial. This card also encourages people to prioritize their own needs and to not be afraid to set boundaries within their relationships in order to protect their wellbeing.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 of Pentacles tarot card is a representation of long-term stability and financial security. This card signifies leaving a legacy and is generally associated with retirement or an inheritance, often from a deceased or elderly relative. This card is a reminder to plan ahead and think about the future, while also recognizing the importance of family and the relationships that accompany it.

For those regarding health, the 10 of Pentacles tarot card can signify the need to prioritize their physical and mental wellbeing. It can suggest that now is the time to take care of oneself, as well as make sure that proper health benefits are in place for the future. The 10 of Pentacles can also signal the need to start planning for retirement, and make sure one’s finances are in order.

For finances, the 10 of Pentacles tarot card can represent an opportunity to invest financially for the future. This card can signal the need to start saving and preparing for retirement, both in terms of liquid funds and long-term investments. Different strategies should be implemented, such as establishing a budget, saving for retirement, and setting aside money for the future.

For relationships, the 10 of Pentacles encourages one to think about the importance of family and loved ones. It suggests being mindful of relationships and being generous towards others. It also suggests having a backup plan when it comes to retirement and inheritance planning, as it is better to ensure that one’s loved ones are taken care of in the long run. The 10 of Pentacles encourages people to reach out to family and build upon their relationships, as this can help to provide a sense of security in the future.
