Let your inner strength and wisdom guide you and make sure to take your time, as patience and the right decisions will be necessary in order to navigate any difficulty.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Moon and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Moon and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Moon mean?

Health: The Moon Tarot card is a sign of health issues that may not be visible yet. As the card represents unseen problems, it can be a warning about physical ailment that are yet to manifest. It can also be indicative of a health problem that one is unaware of, but with which they need to be especially vigilant. It is therefore important to take time to check in with oneself and pay attention to any signs of potential problems.

Finances: The Moon Tarot card can represent financial changes that are beyond our control. This could be the result of market fluctuations, changes in our income, or unforeseen expenses. On a more positive note, this card can also indicate voluntary changes we make to our finances, such as taking the steps to pay off debt or taking steps to invest in our future. Whatever the change, this card can indicate that we can trust our intuition and that if we act diligently and thoughtfully, we can expect to make the best of these changes.

Relationships: The Moon Tarot card can represent a need for trust and honesty in our relationships. It can be a warning about hidden problems or issues in our relationships, or can indicate that there may be changes coming that are beyond our control. It can also be a reminder to ask for help when we need it and to be mindful of how we can best serve our relationships. As with our finances, the Moon Tarot card can also represent voluntary changes we can make in our relationships, such as putting effort into actively listening and being open and honest with our loved ones. Ultimately, this card can remind us of the importance of trust in our relationships and to pay attention to any signs of potential issues.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The King of Swords represents an individual who is in control of their health, both physically and mentally. This card can encourage us to take ownership of our health and make conscious choices that benefit our well-being. It can encourage us to use strategic thinking to better our health, whether that be taking up a new exercise routine or monitoring our diet. We should use the guidance of this card to question our daily habits and find ways to move forward that benefit our wellbeing.

Finances:  
The King of Swords can denote someone with strong financial sense, who approaches money with a logical, strategic mindset. This card can encourage us to be wise with our finances, to look objectively at our financial situation and make decisions that will benefit us in the long term. We should take a careful approach to managing our money and approach our finances with discipline and consistency.

Relationships:  
The King of Swords speaks of someone who is able to have honest, open conversations with their loved ones. This card can encourage us to approach our relationships in a strategic way, to prioritize communication and ensure we remain mindful of our partner’s feelings. We should strive to make decisions that benefit us both, rather than being solely focused on our own needs. The King of Swords reminds us that honest, thoughtful conversations can help us build strong, lasting relationships.
