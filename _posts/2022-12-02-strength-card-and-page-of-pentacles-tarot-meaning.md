## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card is particularly relevant in the context of mental health, personal finances, and intimate relationships. It suggests that through self-love, unconditional love, self-respect and courage, we can take ownership of our lives and take charge of our personal growth.

When considering mental health, Strength is an important reminder to love and accept yourself. We often find ourselves in situations where we feel overwhelmed by emotions and tired from the weight of the world. Strength provides the courage to overcome self-doubt and pursue our dreams with confidence. After recognizing our own strength and worth, we have the power to take on difficult tasks and meet challenges head-on.

On the financial side, Strength symbolizes the need to be rigorous and diligent with our money. Strength encourages us to create a budget and stick to it, setting financial goals and pushing ourselves towards financial independence. To move forward, we should have faith in ourselves and take ownership and responsibility for our financial situation.

Finally, in matters of relationships, Strength encourages us to be open and vulnerable. A healthy relationship requires courage, trust, and understanding of boundaries. With Strength, we can take the time to truly care for our partner and strengthen our bond with open communication and conflict resolution. We can also use our courage to say “no” to unhealthy relationships and Toxic people, recognizing our own worth and setting a healthy example in our relationships.

All in all, Strength encourages us to be brave and trust ourselves. Through Strength, we can foster unconditional love, self-respect, and courage to reclaim our power and take control of our personal health, finances, and relationships.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is an auspicious card for those seeking success in health, finances and relationships. Health-wise, the Page of Pentacles indicates that this is a time when you can be focused, patient and diligent in taking care of your health. The card suggests that a period of recovery and healing is ahead of you, so be sure to take advantage of any medical treatments and health supplements that might be available to you.

When it comes to finances, the Page of Pentacles can be a very encouraging card. It is a sign that financial success is on the way, such as a raise or a new job. The Page of Pentacles also signifies a willingness to work hard and put in the effort necessary to achieve financial goals. The card emphasizes that, with careful planning and sensible decisions about money, financial stability is achievable.

Finally, the Page of Pentacles in regards to relationships is a very positive one. The card suggests that you are being guided towards achieving meaningful, lasting relationships. The Page of Pentacles encourages you to be open and accepting of changes in your relationships, and emphasizes that understanding and patience can go a long way towards building and maintaining strong relationships. The Page of Pentacles promises that with effort, you can create and nurture healthy, happy relationships.
