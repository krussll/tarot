## Page Of Swords – An Overview

The Page of Swords is a card that speaks to being curious, inquisitive, and a bit nosy. It is a card of truth and information, as it is associated with gossip, spies, and prying eyes. It can represent a literal kid, or it can represent a more metaphorical concept, such as a person who seeks the truth in all matters and is willing to go to great lengths to uncover it. The Page of Swords can be a sign of objectivity and even-handedness in communication, as well as a reminder to take a step back and evaluate matters with an unbiased eye. It can also be a sign that someone is watching what you do and say, so it is important to be mindful of your words and actions.

## Upright Meanings Of Page Of Swords

### What It Means For Me

The Page Of Swords tarot card suggests that I am a person who is curious and inquisitive, eager to learn and explore new ideas. I am a person who loves to gather information and seek out new knowledge. I am also a person who can be nosy at times and may engage in gossip. I am loyal and honest, and will always tell the truth even if it is not what people want to hear. My tendency to be nosy can sometimes get me into trouble, but I never mean any harm. I am a truth seeker, and often rely on my own intuition to guide me.

### What It Means For The Future

The Page Of Swords in my future means that I should expect to encounter some curious and nosy individuals in my life. I should be aware that there may be people who are trying to find out my secrets and gossip about me. I should also be ready to deal with truth being exposed, as the Page Of Swords indicates that truth will come out in whatever situation I’m in. However, I should take this as an opportunity to grow and learn, as I can use the knowledge revealed to my advantage. The Page Of Swords also reminds me to pay attention to details and use my sharp intuition to pick up on what’s going on around me.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Page Of Swords is a reminder to be mindful of those around you and the influence they may have on your life. It speaks to being conscious of the conversations that you engage in and being aware of the gossip and prying eyes that come with being in close relationships. It is a warning to be aware of the truth and to be mindful of the information that you share with those close to you. Being the Page of Swords, it is important to be selective with who you trust and choose your words carefully. It is a reminder that no matter the relationships, it is always best to be honest and open in order to maintain the trust and integrity of the relationship.

## Reversed Meanings Of Page Of Swords
