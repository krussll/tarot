A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Chariot card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Chariot card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Chariot and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Chariot tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card together suggest that taking action and making changes is the only way to move forward. The Chariot card is a reminder that now is the time to take the wheel and take the journey that you know is necessary for growth. It’s time to drive forward towards your destiny. The Page Of Swords card adds an element of caution and exploration, indicating that you should pay attention to the environment around you. Trust that the voice of truth and the knowledge it holds will be revealed soon, as if it were whispered in your ear. Taking action, coupled with careful exploration and observation, can lead to great success.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Chariot and Page Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Chariot and Page Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Chariot mean?

The Chariot Tarot card refers to action, momentum, and advancement. It suggests that the individual is moving in a new direction, either physically or metaphorically. It warns of a potentially difficult journey and major changes that may be necessary for positive outcomes.

For health, The Chariot Tarot card can represent the idea of taking action for better health. It can signify medical treatments, lifestyle changes, diet, or exercise that may be necessary to get on the path to better health. It can also refer to taking a break and carving out time for self-care, or making a commitment to an activity that can contribute to overall emotional, spiritual, and physical wellbeing.

For finances, The Chariot Tarot card can suggest a need to take action and make changes in order to get on a better financial footing. It can point to budgeting, reducing expenses, making investments, and managing finances in order to create a more secure financial future.

For relationships, The Chariot Tarot card can refer to the notion of taking action to build better relationships. It may represent a need to communicate, compromise, and negotiate. It can also highlight the need to break out of unhealthy patterns, move out of a stagnant situation, and make proactive choices that will enhance relationships. The card can also point to the importance of establishing healthy boundaries while remaining open to possibilities.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page Of Swords Tarot card can represent a need for knowledge and understanding in regards to health and well-being. It can be a reminder to stay curious, use our intuition and to keep learning and exploring new and different ways to stay healthy and balanced. It can be a warning to stay on top of mental and physical health, to stay aware of what we put into our bodies, and to be mindful of our own intuition when it comes to our health.

Finances: The Page Of Swords Tarot card can represent a need for financial wisdom and caution. It can be a reminder to be aware of where our money is going and to make informed decisions before investing. It can also be a sign that careful planning and research is needed before making any financial commitments. It can be a warning to remain vigilant for potential scams or frauds, as well as to be mindful of how our decisions may affect our financial situation in the future.

Relationships: The Page Of Swords Tarot card can represent a need for balance and clarity in our relationships. It can be a reminder to be mindful of our communication, both verbal and nonverbal, and to stay informed about any potential changes in any relationships. It can also be a warning to maintain a healthy sense of curiosity and to guard against gossip and prying eyes. It can be a sign to be honest and authentic in our dealings with others, and to seek out the truth while being mindful of our own boundaries.
