## What does Justice and King Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card together symbolize a leader who is fair and just, but also considerate and empathetic. This leader is willing to seek out win-win solutions, understanding that sometimes one person may have to give something up in order for everyone to benefit. This leader has the ability to take into account the needs of everyone involved and finds a way to achieve harmony. They also have an unwavering commitment to the truth and are willing to seek out justice and set things right, no matter the cost.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card is a powerful card representing fairness, objective judgment, and cause and effect. In the realm of health, the Justice card is a reminder that all health decisions must be made with rational thought and consideration for long-term consequences. One could interpret it as encouraging one to keep their body and health in check by emphasizing the importance of mindful nutrition, exercise, and taking care of one’s mental health. It encourages people to take responsibility for their health and be conscious of their decisions and actions, as every choice has an effect.

In terms of finances, the Justice Tarot card denotes the importance of understanding the implications of financial decisions. It is a reminder that those who make wise, informed decisions are more likely to achieve the best outcomes. It encourages people to be mindful and responsible of their decisions and understand the potential repercussions. It also suggests that one should not be fooled into making decisions based on emotion or blind trust, nor should they take advantage of others.

When it comes to relationships, the Justice Tarot card encourages people to seek balance and fairness. It implies that relationships should be based on mutual respect and understanding. It is a reminder to not become too attached or make decisions out of sentimentality, but to allow justice and the truth to guide one’s interactions. It also cautions individuals to be wary of making promises that they cannot keep and to think beyond the present moment when considering their relationships. The Justice card advises people to think objectively and with an open mind when making decisions that will affect the dynamics of their relationships.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health

The King of Cups tarot card symbolizes a supportive leader in the area of health. This person has the ability to extend empathy and understanding to those under their care. They are guided by their own feelings, values and instincts, but not by their emotions. This person is able to maintain a strong balance between mind and body, and to find the proper balance between rest and effort. They are tolerant, and are able to express their feelings without causing harm. They are able to listen to and understand other’s needs and emotions, while also having the strength to remain in control and make wise decisions.

Finances

The King of Cups tarot card signifies a great leader in the area of finances. This person has the ability to be rational, logical, and also able to think outside the box. They are also patient, and able to stay focused on long-term goals. They provide financial guidance and stability to those under their care, as they understand the importance of proper risk management. This person is also tolerant, and able to remain unbiased when making decisions related to money. They are able to assess people fairly and objectively, without the influence of any personal feelings.

Relationships

The King of Cups tarot card exemplifies a strong leader in the area of relationships. This person is able to show empathy and understanding to those close to them. They are emotionally stable, able to remain calm in the face of adversity and can listen to and understand other’s needs and feelings. They are able to communicate effectively and maintain balance between their own feelings and the feelings of others. They understand that relationships are not always easy and that compromise and tolerance are necessary for them to remain healthy. They have the ability to remain fair and objective when making decisions and can keep a level head when the situation becomes difficult.
