A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and Queen Of Wands mean together?

When The 7 of Wands and The Queen Of Wands come together, it suggests that you need to be assertive and use your energy to protect yourself against any competition. This could be a physical, mental or emotional competition. The Queen Of Wands tarot card suggests that you have the confidence and self-assurance to protect your own values and wants. You should be resilient and stand up for yourself and any causes that are important to you. With the combination of these two tarot cards, you have the confidence and determination to fight for yourself.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Wands and Queen Of Wands mean together for your love life?

When these two cards appear together in a love Tarot reading, they suggest that you have a strong sense of self-protection and are feeling confident of yourself and your relationship. You have a healthy sense of competition and you’re ready to defend yourself and your place in the relationship.

The Queen of Wands represents an attitude of self-assurance, and with the presence of the 7 of Wands, it’s clear that you have no intention of backing down in a battle of wits or wills. This card combination infers that you will strongly defend your right to choose, your right to protest, and your right to express yourself in any way that you see fit.

However, when it comes to relationships, competition should not be the primary focus. The 7 of Wands points to the challenge of walking the line between defending one’s position and allowing for flexibility, compromise, and understanding. It’s time for you to accept that your relationship will not always be a competition; sometimes it is about mutual understanding, cooperation, and compromise.

The Queen of Wands encourages you to take a strong stance in favor of yourself and your relationship. Don’t be afraid to put yourself first, communicate clearly, and stand up for your interests in order to ensure that your relationship is based on trust, understanding, and mutual respect. It’s important to stand your ground while being open and flexible at the same time.

When The 7 Of Wands tarot card and The Queen Of Wands tarot card come together in a reading about finances and job, it is a message to protect your finances and job from competition. It encourages you to be aware of your financial situation and to be ready to defend it. This means that you may need to use your own self-defense skills and be assertive when it comes to competition for your finances and job.

The Queen Of Wands brings the element of self-assurance and confidence to the mix. Having the confidence to stand up for yourself and your finances is key in this situation. It may be difficult to do, but having the courage and determination to stay true to your values and what you have worked hard to build will serve you well. Whether it is handling competitive issues in the workplace or in your financial ventures, having the assurance that you are doing the right thing will give you the courage to stand strong.

The combination of The 7 Of Wands and The Queen Of Wands in a financial and job related reading is a message to stay alert and protect your finances and job from competition. It is important to have the confidence in yourself and your values to be able to stand up for what is right and defend it. With these two cards together, you can be sure that you have the courage and determination to look out for your own financial success and protect yourself against any competition that may try to take it away from you.

### What does 7 Of Wands and Queen Of Wands mean together for your health?

The 7 of Wands and The Queen of Wands Tarot cards together signify the importance of self-defense and protection when it comes to your health. When these two cards appear together, they suggest that you need to take control of your health and vitality, and be diligent in protecting them from all forms of competition, whether it be from people or a particular situation.

The Queen of Wands shows that, in addition to self-defense, you need to have confidence in yourself to take care of yourself. In summary, The 7 of Wands and The Queen of Wands tarot cards suggest that in order to have lasting health, you need to be proactive in self-defense. Be confident in yourself and your abilities, investigate the risks to your health, and take the appropriate measures to prevent it. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 7 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take care of ourselves and defend our health against all the bad things that can affect us, both from the inside and outside. It is a reminder to take time to focus on our health and to put energy into developing and maintaining good habits that can help us keep our bodies, minds, and souls in balance. It encourages us to be mindful of our own needs and to stand up for ourselves in taking the necessary steps to protect our health.

Finances

The 7 of Wands Tarot card can represent a warning that we need to be vigilant and protect ourselves against economic issues or the competition of others. It can be a reminder to be smart and proactive in our financial lives—to invest in our future and to be conscious of our expenses. This card can also suggest that it is necessary to be aggressive and competitive when dealing with money, and to be mindful of potential risks.

Relationships

The 7 of Wands Tarot card encourages us to be honest and defend ourselves in our relationships. We should be prepared to stand up for our beliefs and beliefs that we believe are important to the relationship, no matter how the other person may respond. This card suggests that defending ourselves in a relationship should be done from a place of love and understanding. It is important to remember to be firm and compassionate when engaging in this type of self-defense.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is a card that is said to represent a fiery, independent and creative personality. It often depicts a powerful woman who is not afraid to take charge and be assertive. In terms of health, this card implies an ambitious and determined approach to life. It suggests that one should take charge of their lives and work towards achieving their goals, no matter how difficult the journey may be. With this in mind, it can be helpful to stay active and exercise regularly in order to maintain a healthy lifestyle. If a person is feeling particularly motivated and inspired, they can take this as an opportunity to achieve their physical goals.

In terms of finances, the Queen of Wands Tarot card implies that one should approach their finances with a confident, goal-oriented mindset. This card can be seen as a reminder to stay focused, organized and to assume control of one’s finances. This may entail setting a budget and investing in self-foresight. Taking the time to consider future financial decisions can allow one to make smarter choices and, ultimately, have greater financial freedom.

In terms of relationships, the Queen of Wands Tarot card implies that one should approach their interpersonal relationships with an open-minded attitude and an optimistic view. This card can be seen as a reminder to remain confident and embrace the powerful qualities within oneself. It suggests that one should have a strong sense of self-respect and work towards being able to be honest and authentic in their dealings with others. By doing so, one can establish more meaningful connections and be open to any opportunities that may arise.

Overall, The Queen of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take charge of one’s life and to embrace the qualities within oneself in order to achieve success. It implies that one should be ambitious and confident in their approach towards health, finances, and relationships in order to find balance and satisfaction.
