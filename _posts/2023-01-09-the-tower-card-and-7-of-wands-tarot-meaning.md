A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 7 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 7 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 7 Of Wands mean together?

The Tower tarot card is a warning of enormous, unexpected change, ruin and upheaval, and the 7 Of Wands tarot card is about finding the strength to defend yourself against competition, so when these two cards appear together, it could be a sign that you’re facing a period of major disruption and you’ll need to dig deep within yourself to find the courage and strength to protect yourself and your interests. It could be a difficult time, but these cards can be seen as a reminder that you have the strength and power to protect yourself against sudden changes and upheaval.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health

The Tower Tarot card is a warning that disruption and upheaval may have a negative effect on health. It may suggest a time of instability, where health issues are hard to diagnose or treatments are not successful. It may also be a reminder to take extra care with one’s health, as the card can bring sudden illness or injury. The Tower can also indicate a period of overwhelm, where it is difficult to stay on top of everyday tasks, leading to greater stress.

Finances

The Tower Tarot card can mean a sudden financial upheaval. It can be a warning that sudden, unwelcome change can lead to financial ruin. Any investments at this time may not return the profits expected, and any debts may not be forgiven as easily. The Tower can also indicate a time of financial turmoil, where unexpected bills or financial difficulties arise.

Relationships

The Tower Tarot card can suggest that disruption and upheaval in other areas of life may also have an effect on relationships. If a person’s health or finances are in a state of flux, it can be hard to keep relationships functioning smoothly. Unresolved issues between partners may cause a rift in the relationship, which can have long-term consequences. The Tower can also indicate that a relationship may end abruptly, and one or both parties may suffer a loss of dignity.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 7 of Wands Tarot card is associated with the planet Mars. This means that it carries the energy of ambition and strength, which can be useful for those feeling under the weather. Symbolically, the card suggests that it is time to defend yourself and your health, to be proactive and protect yourself from any threats to your wellbeing. It advises that you can become stronger by standing up for yourself and not letting any illness or fatigue take control of your life. You should be sure to take care of your body and mind, to remain mindful of the potential risks and to take all the necessary steps to keep yourself healthy.

Finances:

The 7 of Wands Tarot card also carries a message of self-defense and protection when it comes to finances. This card is all about being aware of the potential risks and using proactive strategies to protect yourself and your money. Be sure to use caution when entering into any financial agreements and to double-check all the details, so you can minimize the chances of any unpleasant surprises. You should also be extra vigilant and make sure that you take action and respond whenever there are any changes that could potentially have a negative financial impact on your life.

Relationships:

When it applies to relationships, the 7 of Wands Tarot card carries the message of protection and self-defense. This card signals that you should use caution when entering into new relationships or dealing with existing ones that have become strained. It’s important for you to preserve your own boundaries and to make sure that you are not taken advantage of in any way. It is wise to be on your alert and to protect yourself, both physically and emotionally. If you are feeling vulnerable, the card advises that you take some time out to rejuvenate and to reconnect with yourself. This can help to reestablish your sense of security and inner peace.
