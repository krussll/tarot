A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The Sun tarot card and The 6 Of Swords tarot card together signify a transition that will bring joy and material happiness. This could be related to a move or relocation, something that might seem like a big change but one that ends up having a positive outcome. It could also be about making a change or transition in your life that leads to material success and happiness. Ultimately, this combination indicates that a change is necessary and that it will ultimately result in great rewards and a joyous outcome.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Sun tarot card and The 6 Of Swords tarot card when placed together in your love life are a good indication of something joyous and positive to come. It could be a sign that you two are ready to move onto the next stage in your relationship, or that a period of transition is ahead. This could be a sign that you are both ready to take the next step, to move in together, to get married, or to start a family. It might also signify a change in location or a relocation as a couple, whether that is to a different city, state, or country. This could potentially be a great thing, as it could mean a fresh start and new beginning with the one you love. Whatever the transition is, it will be joyous and successful and will bring you both closer together.

The Sun tarot card and the 6 Of Swords tarot card when combined together suggest a positive shift in your finances and job status. The Sun tarot card represents material happiness and joyous outcomes, a sign that you may be achieving success in your money-related endeavors. This success could manifest in the form of secure income, in the form of a raise, or any other type of financial reward.

The 6 of Swords tarot card is often interpreted as a representation of a necessary transition, which could indicate that this success might involve a move or relocation. The combination of these two tarot cards suggests that things are looking up in terms of your finances and job. You might be feeling prepared to take a leap of faith and pursue a new and exciting opportunity that could bring you a better income and a more fulfilling career. It is important to remember that opportunities and changes like these do not come easily, so consistency and dedication is key. Even though this transition can be daunting, the combination of The Sun tarot card and the 6 Of Swords tarot card reassure you that it is the right path to take in order to achieve success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card can signify optimistic outcomes for health, love, and finances. In terms of health, it speaks of good vibes and an overall glow of good health. It suggests that if one is going through a difficult time healthwise, then it is likely to be overcome, and one will experience a renewed strength and wellbeing.

In terms of love and relationships, The Sun signifies joy, passion, and energy. It implies that these things will be rekindled in an existing relationship, or a new and vibrant relationship will emerge. A harmonious relationship which is full of love and understanding is likely to be experienced when The Sun appears in a Tarot reading.

In terms of finances, The Sun suggests that a person’s fortune will be improved and that money matters will become more organized and secure. A positive outlook on money matters will manifest and begin to attract abundance. The Sun Tarot card also encourages a person to become more confident and passionate with their money, as it is a symbol of ultimate abundance.

Therefore, the Sun Tarot card encourages a person to move forward with optimism and faith that they will be blessed with health, money, and love. It is a symbol that hope is alive and will manifest in a joyous and meaningful way.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The 6 of Swords tarot card speaks to the need to transition in life. It can signify the need to travel, to relocate, to physically move, or it can apply to a need to move forward emotionally and spiritually. In terms of health, the 6 of Swords suggests that it is time to make a change, to take a step forward in terms of improving yourself and your life. It can mean that it is time to talk to a doctor, to make a lifestyle change, to start a new exercise regime, or to make some other health-related decision.

Financially, the 6 of Swords can signify the need to make a change in how you relate to money. It can ask you to take steps to improve your financial situation, to make a budget, to save money, or to invest. It suggests a willingness to take risks, and to make decisions that will benefit your future.

In terms of relationships, the 6 of Swords can suggest a need to transition in one’s relationships and to take a step forward. It can mean that it is time to move on from a toxic relationship or to find a new direction for an existing relationship. It can also indicate a need to open up and be more honest, to take responsibility for your emotional wellbeing, and to make changes that will benefit the relationship.

Overall the 6 of Swords tarot card speaks to making a necessary transition and stepping into a new future. It can give courage and strength to make difficult decisions and to confront the unknown.
