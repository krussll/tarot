A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 8 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together?

When The 8 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card come together, they can be seen as a reminder to take some time to hone your craft and skills, but to make sure that you don’t become so engrossed in this task that you end up limiting yourself or imprisoning yourself within your own thoughts. It is important to take the time to hone your skills, be it artistic, creative, or otherwise, but it is equally important to make sure that you don’t become so engrossed in this task that you close yourself off from the world or feel like you are in a prison of your own making.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 8 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

When the Eight of Pentacles and the Eight of Swords appear side by side in a reading, it can suggest a time of learning, mastering, and enjoying the many facets of a love relationship. The 8 of Pentacles indicates that you are in a period of growth, where you’re learning to use all of the resources available to you to strengthen and deepen your bond. This could include developing a more empathetic attitude, learning to communicate better, and taking the time to develop a more meaningful connection with your partner.

At the same time, the 8 of Swords cautions you to be aware of the boundaries you set for yourself in relationships, whether they are emotional, physical, or mental. The card could suggest that you’re too quick to judge or criticize yourself, or that you’re uncertain about how to express your feelings, resulting in feelings of isolation and imprisonment.

The combination of the two cards might be a warning to be careful that you don’t box yourself in with too many self-imposed restrictions. It’s not only important to recognize your boundaries, but also to be open to exploring new possibilities in order to reap the full benefits of your relationship. The 8 of Pentacles suggests that if you do, you can learn to use the resources available to you, to the benefit of both you and your partner. By taking steps to master the art of loving another, you can enjoy the journey of discovering the rewards a relationship can bring.

### What does 8 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When both The 8 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 8 Of Swords tarot card appear together in a reading, it can be a sign of stagnancy in both the finances and career. This could indicate that one is working hard but not producing any tangible results and feeling trapped by their current situation.

The 8 Of Pentacles is a sign of mastering a craft and enjoying employment. On its own, this card could signify positive changes in one’s career and the potential for good earnings. However, when seen in conjunction with the 8 Of Swords, it could be a warning that one’s hard work is not being rewarded. This combination could suggest that there are certain obstacles or restrictions preventing an individual from achieving the results they desire.

The 8 Of Swords card points to self-imposed restrictions and isolation, which could indicate that the individual is too afraid to try something new. Even though there are possibilities for success, they are too worried to take the necessary steps. There might be a need to take risks or make changes to the current course of action in order to achieve the desired goals.

The combination of the 8 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords tarot cards is a sign that it is time to reevaluate one’s finances and job. The individual should analyze their current situation and take the necessary risks to create positive change in their finances and career. If the individual is willing to move out of their comfort zone and commit to mastering their craft, then the potential for success is limitless.

### What does 8 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together for your health?

The 8 of Pentacles and the 8 of Swords tarot cards together, when it comes to health, can be seen as a juxtaposition of two very different energies. On the one hand, The 8 of Pentacles encourages us to focus on bettering and perfecting our craft and to take pleasure in our task. Consider if there are any thoughts or behaviors you have that are limiting your progress. Are there any fears or anxieties causing you to retreat into isolation and preventing you from taking meaningful steps towards improving your health? Now is the time to break free from these barriers and be mindful of potential roadblocks to our own physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing.

In summary, The 8 of Pentacles and The 8 of Swords tarot cards when read together provide a comprehensive message: take action to improve your health and master your craft, while also being aware of any self-imposed restrictions or feelings of isolation that may be preventing progress. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

Applied to health, this card suggests that you are ready to dedicate the time and effort necessary to master the habits that will bring you good health and vitality. You should be wise to consult with experts and take the advice they give you seriously, as the guidance you receive can be the key to making healthy lifestyle choices. Your commitment to health may also require diligence and dedication to healthy eating, regular physical activity and regular rest.

The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with mastering your craft, which when applied to finances, means that you are prepared to take the necessary steps to become financially literate and savvy. This card signifies that you are ready to give your undivided attention to understanding and managing your finances, such as budgeting, saving, and investing. You recognize the importance of having a solid financial foundation and are willing to put in the time and effort to ensure that you have financial freedom.

This card suggests that you are ready to dedicate yourself to listening and understanding the needs and desires of your partner. You recognize that no relationship can survive without communication and understanding, and you are willing to be patient and understanding in order to foster a healthy relationship. This card also suggests that you are willing to compromise and work through difficulties that may arise in a relationship, as this will foster mutual understanding and love.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health

The Eight of Swords tarot card symbolizes self-imposed restriction when it comes to one’s health. This can range from restrictive diets, refusal to seek medical help, feeling unable to prioritize one’s own well-being, or even avoiding activities that could lead to self-care. Such restrictions can lead to physical, mental, and emotional suffering as well as impede one’s overall health and well-being.

Finances

The Eight of Swords tarot card also symbolizes a self-imposed restriction when it comes to finances. This can be self-imposed feelings of guilt or shame in spending money, excessive saving or hoarding, or even an unwillingness to invest in self-improvement opportunities that could lead to financial increase. This card is often a warning sign of potential financial instability and mismanagement.

Relationships

When looking at relationships, The Eight of Swords tarot card symbolizes a self-imposed restriction to free and open communication. This card can refer to feelings of helplessness or powerlessness regarding a relationship or situation. There can be an inability or unwillingness to reach out for support or assistance, leading to feelings of isolation. This can also be a warning sign of potential manipulation or control in a relationship that needs to be addressed.
