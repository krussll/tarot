However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Hermit card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and 4 Of Cups mean together?

It is important to recognize the deep yearning within that may be unfulfilled, but don’t lose hope. If you take the time to understand yourself, and the part of yourself that is feeling stagnant, you may be surprised and rewarded with spiritual illumination and gifts. Through introspection and self-exploration, these two cards emphasize that you can find clarity and growth if you are willing to look.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health: The Hermit Tarot card may appear when you are in need of a personal journey of healing. It is a reminder to go within and connect with your inner wisdom and intuition in order to bring physical and emotional healing. Now is the time to look at your existing lifestyle choices and be mindful of your physical and mental health. The Hermit signifies the importance of self-care – take the time to pay attention to your body, nurture yourself, practice self-love and learn to value your own wellbeing.

Finances: The Hermit teaches us to examine our financial decisions and ask ourselves why we are behaving in certain ways with our money. Are we being driven by greed or fear? Are we setting financial goals that are realistic and aligned with our values? This card encourages us to be honest with ourselves about our values and motivations so that we can achieve financial stability and a sense of inner peace. Making changes to our habits and beliefs can be difficult, so take the time to reflect and practice patience while on this journey.

Relationships: The Hermit Tarot encourages us to focus on our own inner journey, rather than placing our energy on external sources. By being true to ourselves and understanding our needs, we can make informed decisions in relationships and create a foundation of trust and respect. The Hermit’s message is one of self-reliance and self-discovery; it also serves as a reminder to take the time to nurture and grow our relationships. Spend time alone and within yourself in order to get in touch with your inner truth and your authentic way of expressing yourself within relationships.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

For Health:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can be interpreted as a sign that you need to take better care of yourself, particularly in regards to mental health. You might be feeling apathetic and unfulfilled, not knowing how to move forward in achieving a greater state of wellness. This is a sign to pause and reflect on what you really need in order to feel your best. Take a break from your regular routine by trying something new and engaging, in order to increase motivation and focus. Reassess the areas in your life that are draining your energy, and make time for self-care.

For Finances:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card can also be interpreted as a sign that you are not seeing the full potential of your financial situation. You might feel unfulfilled and scared, as if you’re not doing enough to secure a stable financial footing. This is a sign to pause and reflect on what you can do to invigorate your finances. Review your current strategy, reassess goals, and consider new means of boosting your income. Look out for surprise gifts, such as unexpected bonuses, which can help you get back on the right track.

For Relationships:  
The 4 of Cups Tarot card is a sign to take a step back and reassess your relationships. You might be feeling unfulfilled and disconnected from the people you care about. To bring a sense of stability and joy back into your life, pause to reflect on the role that each person plays in your life. Identify any areas that need improvement and make an effort to reach out and strengthen those relationships, or even make new ones. Be open to surprise gifts that come your way, such as an unexpected visit from a close friend or a kind gesture, in order to keep the connection alive.
