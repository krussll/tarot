A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 6 Of Swords tarot card together suggest a journey of creative collaboration and craftsmanship. You may be transitioning to a new location or project that requires you to bring your creativity, skill and teamwork to the table. This journey will bring success as long as you are open to working with others and using your creativity as a tool for collaboration. Take the time to assess the situation and the skills of those around you in order to find the best way to move forward.

The cards indicate that whatever changes need to be made, they can be achieved with a combination of skill, thought, and collaboration. The relationship may be going through a difficult patch, but if both parties are willing to put in the effort, it can lead to a better outcome for everyone involved.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The 3 Of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes the need to work as a team when it comes to health. It shows the importance of collaboration between doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals in order to provide quality care. This card is a reminder that health is collective, and that it can only be achieved through a combination of skills. It also highlights the importance of craftsmanship, which is essential in medical procedures, surgeries, and treatments. By working together and applying the highest standards of quality and care, we can ensure the best possible outcomes.

Finances:

The 3 Of Pentacles Tarot card indicates the need to be strategic and organized when it comes to finances. It suggests the importance of being mindful of budgeting, saving, and investing, as well as seeking out professional advice when it comes to financial decisions. This card is a strong reminder to take the time to understand financial matters to make sure that any investments are making the biggest impact. Furthermore, it advises patients to consider working with a financial advisor who can help design an appropriate approach for their specific needs and goals.

Relationships:

When it comes to relationships, the 3 Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages partners to work together in order to make things better. It suggests to pause and reflect on what areas of a relationship might need work and then apply their individual skills and craftsmanship to create something that is beneficial for both. This card speaks to the importance of communication and compromise when it comes to relationships, as it takes a team effort to strengthen the bond. It is a powerful reminder to take the time to listen and understand one another in order to build a foundation for a successful connection.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can signify a necessary transition in terms of health—you may find yourself needing to make changes that will positively improve your physical and mental health. This can include changing your diet and fitness routine, seeking out medical advice or treatment, taking a break from unhealthy habits, or making an effort to prioritize self-care. It is important to listen to your body and make these changes in order to ensure long-term health and wellbeing.

Finances:  
When it comes to finances, The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can suggest that you need to adjust your approach. You may find yourself needing to make significant changes in order to keep your finances in order, such as decreasing spending, paying down debts, restructuring investments, or altering your job or career. Making these changes now can help you establish better financial stability in the future.

Relationships:  
In terms of relationships, The 6 Of Swords Tarot card can signify a need to reassess current connections. It may be necessary to take a new approach to relationships, such as being more honest with your needs and expectations, eliminating negative influences, or developing healthier boundaries. Making these changes can help you to have healthier, more meaningful relationships.
