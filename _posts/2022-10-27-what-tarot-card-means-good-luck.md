Good luck is a state of mind as much as it is a state of fortune. The good news is that no matter where you are on your journey through life, you can steer your own course and find your own good luck.

In fact, some people seem to have an innate knack for it. So what does that mean for those who could use a little more good luck in their lives?

Understanding the symbolism of tarot cards can help you understand what — and who — they mean.

If you’re asking yourself “what tarot card means good luck?” then keep reading.

## Ace of Pentacles

If pentacles appear in your reading, you’ve got a lucky streak a mile long. Pentacles represent wealth, stability, and abundance in all forms — emotional, financial, and spiritual.

If you’re reading for yourself and pentacles show up, it’s likely related to your career or finances. If you’re reading for someone else, financial security is likely at the heart of their reading, too. It might point to a promotion, a salary increase, or a windfall in the near future.

If the [Ace of Pentacles](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-ace-of-pentacles/) show up in a reading about a relationship, it may indicate a financial or emotional investment in the partnership.

It might be a good time to talk about joint finances, investing in each other, or creating a fiscal safety net between you and your partner.

## Two of Wands

If the [2 of Wands](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-2-of-wands/) show up in your reading, you’re in luck. Wands signify growth, expansion, and creative energy — all great things when you’re looking for good fortune.

When wands appear in a reading for yourself, it may be time to make a change. If you’re reading for someone else, you may want to encourage your friend to go out on a limb and take a risk. Wands are associated with creativity, so if wands appear in a relationship reading, it may be a good time to channel your creativity into your partnership.

If you notice wands in a reading about your career, you may be at a turning point. Some outside stimulus may urge you to go after your goals and make a name for yourself.

## Nine of Wands

The [Nine of Wands](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-9-of-wands/) is a powerful card, but not necessarily in a good way. Nine of Wands is often associated with stress and anxiety — two things that obviously aren’t helpful when you’re looking for good luck.

In fact, a reading with a high number of wands might indicate that you need to take a step back from your life and slow down a bit. When wands show up in a reading for yourself, you may have too much on your plate right now.

If wands appear in a reading for someone else, the person in your reading may need to take a step back from their life. It’s possible that there’s too much stress and not enough time for rest and relaxation.

Wands are also associated with creativity, so if wands appear in a reading about a relationship, it may be because you or your partner need to chill out.

You or your partner might be burning the candle at both ends and could use some down time.

## Four of Swords

[Four of Swords](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-4-of-swords/) is a card of rest and recovery. It typically appears when there’s too much stress in a reading, and it’s time to take a break.

If this card appears in your reading, a few days off or a trip to the spa may do the trick. When swords show up in a reading for someone else, it’s likely that your friend needs a break. Be sensitive and supportive when you discuss the reading with your friend.

If this card appears in a reading about your career, it’s time to take a break from work. Take some time for yourself and recharge your batteries. If this card appears in a reading about a relationship, it’s time to take a break from your partner.

Find time for yourself and recharge your batteries so you have energy to give back to your relationship.

## Six of Swords

[The Six of Swords](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-6-of-swords/) is a card of reflection and healing. When it appears in a reading, you may want to consider reaching out to an old friend, finding a creative outlet, or going on a hike.

Six of Swords is a card of self-reflection and healing, so it’s a great card to have in your reading. When this card appears in a reading for someone else, it may be a good time to have a conversation about grief, loss, or whatever may be weighing on your friend’s mind.

When Six of Swords appears in a reading about your career, it may be a good time to reflect on your current situation.

Some downtime and self-reflection may help you gain new insights.

## Seven of Cups

[Seven of Cups](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-7-of-cups/) is a card of imagination, creativity, and inspiration — all things that can help you find your own good luck.

When this card appears in a reading, you may want to invest in some art supplies or find a creative outlet in your community. Maybe you’ve been itching to write or paint — now’s the time to let your creative juices flow.

When this card appears in a reading for someone else, it’s a sign that your friend is open to new ideas and experiences. It’s a good time to share your own creative projects with your friend.

If this card appears in a reading about your career, it’s a good time to start exploring creative side projects. You may have more ideas than time to explore them all.

## The Tower

[The Tower](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-the-tower/) is a card of sudden change. When it appears in your reading, it’s important to be aware of the sudden changes happening around you.

You may not be able to control them, but you can be prepared for them. When this card appears in a reading for someone else, it may be a sign of impending change in your friend’s life. It could be something as big as a new job or as small as a new haircut.

When The Tower appears in a reading about your career, it may be a sign that it’s time to make a change.

Be prepared for sudden change as you make the transition.

## The Star

[The Star](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-the-star/) is a card of hope and inspiration. It’s a message that there’s good in the world, and that hope exists for you, too.

When this card appears in a reading, it’s a reminder to look for the good in the world. When this card appears in a reading for someone else, it’s a reminder to look for the good in the world. When The Star appears in a reading about your career, it’s a reminder to look for the good in the world.

Look for inspiration and ways to make a positive impact through your career.
