## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

When it comes to health, The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card is all about taking a break from the regular grind and taking care of your body. This card encourages you to make time for yourself and give yourself the attention and care that you need. It could also be interpreted as a warning to live a balanced lifestyle and to not let stress take over your life.

Financials wise, The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card symbolizes a secure financial future. It suggests that you are in a position where you can rely on yourself, your savings and investments, and the sensible financial decisions you have made in the past. This card is a sign that you have created a firm financial foundation, and you should be proud of it.

As far as relationships are concerned, The 10 Of Pentacles tarot card implies that you are secure in the relationships you have. This card speaks of stability, both in your family life, friendships, or even professional relationships. It could also suggest that you have found a way to make your relationships work long-term, as your dedication and commitment have paid off. This card is a sign of contentment and happiness.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 6 of Swords tarot card usually symbolizes a journey, relocation, and the need to make changes to one’s lifestyle. The relocation may be physical, emotional, or a combination of both. With regard to physical health, this card could indicate a need to take a break from the norm and seek out new and healthier ways of living. This could mean making small but significant changes such as trying out healthier eating practices, establishing a better sleep schedule, or engaging in some type of exercise program. Taking a break from the usual routine in order to focus on one’s health can be an essential part of maintaining physical and emotional wellbeing.

Finances

The 6 of Swords tarot card can also relate to financial matters. In this case, it could signify a need for a change in one’s financial situation. This may involve seeking out more cost effective ways of managing money, looking for other sources of income, or consolidating debt. Making changes to one’s finances can be a daunting task, but it is often a necessary one. It is important to stay focused on the end goal and stay motivated during difficult times.

Relationships

The 6 of Swords tarot card can also signify a need to make changes to one’s relationships. This could manifest in many different ways, such as spending more quality time with friends and family, or simply making an effort to stay connected with them. It could also involve starting a new relationship or ending an existing one which may be unhealthy or unfulfilling. Ultimately, this card is a reminder that change is often necessary in order to make the most out of life and maintain healthy relationships.
