A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The Tower tarot card and the 6 Of Cups tarot card together can mean a time of upheaval, a moment where seemingly innocent and nostalgic feelings are suddenly disrupted by an unwanted change. It could indicate an abrupt change in circumstances or an abrupt end to an innocent relationship, as well as potential ruin, disgrace, and even violence that accompany it. It also suggests that although the situation can seem tragic, gifts of understanding and wisdom can be found in the ruins of the upset.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Tower and 6 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The Tower tarot card and the 6 of Cups tarot card, when combined, represent a moment of unwanted upheaval in your love life. This abrupt change may come about through a sudden event or realization, but it is likely to be quite dramatic and disruptive. You may feel as though you are being dragged down a steep, dark path that you had not expected to take, and the sense of ruin and disgrace cannot be overlooked.

Unfortunately, as this card combination often points to, you may also need to face the uncomfortable truth that something that you thought was secure and safe is no longer viable. Old bonds may be severed and you may have to let go of the nostalgia of past moments of joy and innocence with your beloved. That may be a difficult task, as the memories of what once was can often be a powerful force. You may find yourself struggling to accept the loss of a relationship that had brought you so much joy.

However, this card combination also serves as a reminder that although life can change in an instant, it does not mean that the love and connection you have for someone cannot evolve and change with it. As the Tower card signifies, you may be forced to accept that the circumstances have shifted, but this doesn’t mean you can’t find a new path to reunite in love. The 6 of Cups is a card of nostalgia and comfort, and so this card combination serves as a reminder that even in the midst of turbulent times, there is always hope. You may never be able to recreate the past but this doesn’t mean there isn’t something new and beautiful to build.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: The Tower Tarot card can mean that a person’s health is in danger. A sudden and unexpected health problem, such as a serious injury, illness or fracture, can appear out of nowhere. This could cause considerable physical and emotional anguish, as well as medical bills. In extreme cases, the Tower card can signify the death of a loved one or even one’s own death.

Finances: The Tower Tarot card can also indicate financial collapse or bankruptcy. Unexpected changes in the economy or other financial problems, such as the loss of a job or investments, could suddenly bring about ruin and poverty. This card can be a warning that one should be careful with money and to save for a rainy day.

Relationships: The Tower Tarot card can be a sign that relationships may be on the rocks. Unforeseen and unwanted changes can occur in the form of a break-up, a sudden change in feelings, or a malicious attack from another person. The card can also be a warning to be cautious in one’s interactions with others, and to be prepared for the possibility of sudden negative changes in a relationship.

This card encourages you to restore health and balance, such as through incorporating exercise, nutrition, and relaxation into your daily routine. Practicing self-care is important, and this card reminds you to prioritize your health.

Finances: The 6 of Cups can be a reminder to stay patient and think before making financial decisions. This card indicates that it is important to take the time to research and understand the financial decisions you are making. This can help you make informed decisions and reduce financial stress. Additionally, the 6 of Cups symbolizes the potential for unexpected financial gifts. This can bring unexpected sources of income or abundance.

Relationships: The 6 of Cups card encourages you to reconnect with the people around you. It is important to be open to new people and reconnect with those who are important to you, such as family and friends. This card emphasizes the importance of nostalgia, as it encourages you to treasure the relationships and memories from your past. The 6 of Cups also symbolizes a sense of innocence, which encourages you to look at relationships from a new perspective, such as from a place of patience and understanding.
