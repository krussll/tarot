A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Swords card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Swords card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean together?

When The 8 Of Swords tarot card and The 7 Of Cups tarot card come together for you, it can mean that you are feeling trapped and overwhelmed. You are feeling isolated because of the self imposed restrictions you have set up for yourself, and it feels like you have no choices because of them. You feel helpless and stuck in your current situation, and this makes it difficult to make any decisions or commitments. It’s a difficult spot to be in, but understanding the cards and the meaning behind it can help you to see that this is only a temporary state and that with a bit of intervention, you can make progress.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 8 Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When the 8 of Swords and the 7 of Cups appear together in a tarot reading, it can suggest a situation in which your love life is weighed down by self-imposed restrictions, imprisonment, and too many choices. You’re feeling overwhelmed with options and obligations, and instead of facing these decisions head on, you’re dodging difficult conversations and avoiding commitments. It’s likely that these limiting thoughts and doubts are holding you back from true love and a fulfilling relationship.

The 8 of Swords can point to a fear of the unknown and a fear of leaving the comfort of known conditions. This card can represent the stagnation of a relationship caused by the need for certainty, which can manifest itself in putting up walls and refusing intimacy. These feelings can manifest as commitment issues, and a fear of being vulnerable and open in a relationship. The 7 of Cups is a warning to stay grounded and cautious, reminding you not to be lured by possibilities.

This tarot pairing is a call for you to take control of your love life, free yourself from self-imposed restrictions, and assess the choices before you with clarity. Instead of allowing yourself to be lost in a fantasy world with one foot in and one foot out of a relationship, take stock of the practical considerations and assess each potential path before making any decisions. You don’t have to rush into any commitments, but you must make sure you don’t allow yourself to be carried away by too many options and too many choices. With a bit of mindfulness, you can take back control of your love life and find a path to true and lasting connection.

### What does 8 Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

The 8 of Swords and the 7 of Cups tarot card when combined together mean that it is time to take a deeper look at the choices you have made in your finances and job. On the one hand, this combination could be suggesting that you have chosen to put yourself in difficult circumstances by self-imposing limitations on your finances and job. Maybe you have chosen to not commit to any certain job or have decided to take a break from any kind of major financial commitment out of fear or anxiety. This could cause you to feel isolated and restricted in your finances and job.

On the other hand, this combination could also be suggesting that you have too many choices and commitments when it comes to finances and job. You may be stuck trying to decide which path to take, or you may have too many commitments and don’t know which one to prioritize. This could mean that you are overwhelmed with too many commitments and can’t decide on what to focus on.

Either way, this combination is suggesting that there is a need for self-examination and reflection. You need to take a step back and assess which path is best for your financial and job security. You may need to prioritize certain commitments, learn to let go of others, or make changes to your current financial and job situation. Make sure to consider all the options available to you and carefully choose the one that works best for you.

The 8 of Swords and the 7 of Cups together point to a very confusing and potentially dangerous situation when it comes to your health. This combination suggests that you are in a state of limbo, with too many choices and too many options. You may be feeling overwhelmed and unable to make a decision, leading to confusion, indecision and self-imposed restrictions on your health. The combination of these two cards suggests that you may be “imprisoning” yourself by refusing to make decisions and actively engaging in healthy behaviors.

You may be neglecting your health and turning a blind eye to the issues at hand, leading to an unhealthy lifestyle and a lack of progress.

Ultimately, the 8 of Swords and the 7 of Cups suggest that you are putting unnecessary restrictions on yourself and avoiding commitments when it comes to your health. This can be a dangerous path to walk, as it can lead to poor choices being made and a neglect of one’s health. In order to move forward, it is important to take a step back and look at the choices available, make a commitment to yourself and to your well-being and actively engage in healthy choices and behaviors.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 8 Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 8 Of Swords and 7 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It suggests that both physical and psychological obstacles may be hampering your health and well-being. The 8 of Swords suggests that you may need to redefine your routines and boundaries in order to find relief from a feeling of being held back. You may need to be open to the idea of taking a break from the usual hustle and bustle of life, in order to prioritize your health. This could mean reevaluating your daily diet, as well as taking more time for relaxation and self-care.

Finances: The 8 of Swords can indicate a period of financial restrictions. You may feel as though you are living within a budget that is too tight and that you are unable to break free from the cycle of paycheck to paycheck living. The 8 of Swords suggests that you need to carefully investigate and evaluate your current financial situation. Revisit all of the aspects of your finances, such as spending habits and budgeting. Once you have a better understanding of where your money is going, it will be easier to break free of the financial restrictions that you are facing.

Relationships: The 8 of Swords in the Tarot suggests that relationships may feel a little constrained. You may feel as though you’re unable to break away from any negative patterns or restrictive dynamics in your relationships. In order to open up the lines of communication, it’s important to be honest about your feelings and be open to compromise and negotiation. Acknowledge where the boundaries lie in your relationships and create healthy ways to move through them. By understanding your needs and the needs of your partner, you can create more balance and freedom within the relationship.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health

The Seven of Cups Tarot card is a card of too many choices, and can suggest that a person is struggling with committing to a decision because they’re afraid of missing out on something better. It is also a representation of a dreamer, wishing for a perfect outcome instead of being realistic and taking the necessary steps to get there. In health, this card can mean that someone is stuck in a rut, unable to make a decision on the best course of action. They may be avoiding the realities of their situation out of fear that they may make the wrong choice.

Finances

The Seven of Cups Tarot card can signify a lack of financial focus and short-term thinking. It could be warning of too many choices and financial irresponsibility, as the person is unable to commit to a financial plan or goal. They may be jumping from one financial idea to the next without really looking at the bigger picture, or considering the consequences of their decisions.

Relationships

The Seven of Cups Tarot card can mean too many commitments in a relationship, or a lack of commitment. It could also indicate someone is unable to decide on a proper course of action due to fear of commitment. This card can also refer to a person’s inability to make decisions because they feel it would mean letting go of other potential opportunities. The card could suggest that the person needs to take a step back and focus on what is important in the relationship, or else suffer from an “all or nothing” approach.
