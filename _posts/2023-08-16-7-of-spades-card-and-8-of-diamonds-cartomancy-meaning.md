A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Diamonds card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Diamonds have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Diamonds mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, if the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Diamonds appear together, the interpretation would be complex but rich.

The 7 of Spades, symbolizing deceit and victory, suggests that there may be underlying deceit in your life but this will ultimately lead to victory—this might perhaps indicate a hidden truth or unexpected circumstance that needs to come to light.

Winter, the season associated with this card, emphasizes introspection, stillness, and sometimes, uncomfortable truths.

Its element, water, suggests emotional depth and intuition.

On the other hand, the 8 of Diamonds stands for unexpected alliance and power, indicating you will gain power or rise to a position of influence through an unexpected relationship or alliance.

Fall, the season of this card, suggests a time of change, transformation and letting go of the old, while its element, air symbolizes communication, intellect, and life’s breath.

Together, these cards might indicate that while there is some deceit shrouded in a situation or relationship, this will lead to emotional growth (7 of Spades).

Moreover, an unexpected alliance will bring about change and personal power (8 of Diamonds).

This could point to life transitions that, although challenging, result in victory.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Diamonds mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing both the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Diamonds can generate an interesting narrative for your love life.

The 7 of Spades, signifying deceit and victory, implies there may have been hurdles or deception in your relationship.

However, the fact that victory is also a key characteristic shows that despite the negative, there will be a triumph, a resolution to the deception or obstacles you’ve faced.

This card being associated with the chill of Winter and the depth of Water dictates a period of introspection and emotional discovery, perhaps suggesting that it is through self-reflection and understanding that you will achieve this victory in your relationship.

On the other hand, the 8 of Diamonds represents unexpected alliance and power, hinting the presence of new friends or allies that will influence your relationship positively.

The unexpected alliance could also mean developing a deeper bond with your partner, essentially stating that there will be strengthening in ties.

This card resonates with the energy of fall and the element of Air.

The season of fall can signify a change, a transition making way for something better.

Air symbolizes communication, so speaking out your thoughts and feelings may bring a positive shift in your love life.

Thus, together, these cards indicate overcoming problems through personal growth and communication leading to a strengthened bond in your relationship.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Diamonds mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing both the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Diamonds could mean several things for your finances and job.

The 7 of Spades denotes deceit and victory, which indicates a possible experience of fraud or dishonesty, but eventually resulting in a win for you.

This could imply that someone in your professional realm may try to take advantage of you or deceive you, and in hindsight, this could be a risk to your finances and job stability.

However, the victory associated with this card suggests that you would come out triumphant, perhaps even gaining more financial stability or a higher position in your role.

On the other hand, the 8 of Diamonds signifies an unexpected alliance and power.

This card could denote unexpected partnerships in the business or at your job, which could lead to new opportunities or even promotions.

The power associated with this card suggests you gaining authority or control, which in the work setting could mean increased responsibilities and in turn, more rewards.

Coupled with the 7 of Spades, it could mean that despite encountering deception, you will find leverage in unexpected alliances to overcome adversities in your job or financial situation.

Ultimately, both cards together may suggest a bumpy road ahead, but with potentially rewarding results.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Diamonds mean together for your health?

In a health-focused cartomancy reading, the presence of The 7 of Spades may indicate a period of hidden problems or setbacks associated with your well-being.

The ‘deceit’ meaning might signify undiagnosed or misdiagnosed health issues while ‘Victory’ suggests that you will eventually overcome them, perhaps through new treatments or lifestyle changes.

As it is linked with the Winter season and the element of Water, these might suggest the need for emotional healing or a possibility of experiencing health complications during the winter.

On the other hand, the 8 of Diamonds suggests an unexpected ally in your journey to better health.

There might be a person or even a new medical practitioner who will give you valuable insights and support.

The ‘power’ aspect of this card signifies a turning point where you might gain more control over your health situation.

With a connection to the Fall season and the element of Air, this card suggests that a fresh perspective or a change in the approach towards managing your health may come during the fall.

Therefore, together, these cards suggest a journey through a dark phase in health moving towards an enlightened phase where you will find help in unexpected quarters and take control of your health situation.
