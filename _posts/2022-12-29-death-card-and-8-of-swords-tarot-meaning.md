A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Death card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The Death tarot card and The 8 Of Swords card is symbolic of the need for change. It suggests that it is time to break free from the things that have been holding you back, and that this process is empowering and freeing. It is time to end the cycle of self-imposed restrictions and isolation that have been weighing you down and to embrace transformation and rebirth. The Death tarot card is a reminder that change can be a positive force, and that the 8 of Swords allows you to take control of your destiny and move forward.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Death and 8 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Death and 8 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

Health  
The Death Tarot card is an indication of a change in health, either physical or emotional. It may be a sign that someone is nearing the end of their physical life, or, more positively, that they are entering a period of emotional healing and growth. It could also be a warning that it is time to make some major changes in lifestyle and habits to improve health and well-being.

Finances  
When it comes to finances, the Death Tarot card can indicate a new beginning. This may mean an unexpected windfall, or a need to let go of a spendthrift lifestyle and make more responsible financial choices. It could also be a sign of hard work paying off, with long-term plans coming to fruition. It is a reminder to stay focused on reaching goals and prioritizing positive financial habits such as informed investments and disciplined savings.

Relationships  
The Death Tarot card is a reminder that sometimes, relationships must come to an end. This can be a difficult experience, but it can also be a time for growth and transformation. It can also indicate the need to make changes in order to create a healthier and more balanced relationship. It can be a sign to take time to reflect on the past and to find a new perspective for the future. The Death Tarot card is a reminder to not be afraid of endings, for with them comes the opportunity for new beginnings and the chance to create new and healthier relationships.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health: The 8 of Swords Tarot card can speak to physical and mental health, signifying an inability to move forward due to self-imposed restrictions. Here, one might feel helpless, stuck, and trapped in their circumstances. It can suggest that, maybe due to fear or lack of motivation or resources, one is unable to break free from whatever problem they’re facing and take steps towards healing. Perhaps they’re in a destructive cycle of negative thought patterns or bad habits that prevent them from leading a healthy and happy life. Going to therapy, making healthy lifestyle changes, and surrounding oneself with supportive people can all provide the guidance and strength necessary to break through these paralyzing restrictions.

Finances: The 8 of Swords can symbolize a lack of direction or build-up of debt that might lead to foreclosure, bankruptcy, or loss of job security. One might be stuck in a financial situation (often of their own making) that is difficult to break out of. Here, one’s choices might be restricted due to the decisions they have already made, perhaps leading to habitual spending, lack of savings, or ignoring sound financial advice. To break free of these self-imposed restrictions, one must make conscious efforts to improve their money habits and financial outlook. Working with a financial planner or adviser and setting up a budget can be great steps towards establishing financial independence and freedom.

Relationships: The 8 of Swords Tarot card can signify feeling like one is unable to fully express their feelings in a relationship. It might mean that one is so focused on their own inner restrictions and insecurities that they forget to consider the other person’s feelings or needs. This can lead to an emotionally distanced relationship that is unfulfilling and unbalanced. To move forward, it’s important to be honest about one’s feelings and needs, as well as to be willing to receive and offer support. Acknowledging and accepting one’s limitations and fears can be key to freeing up and improving relationships.
