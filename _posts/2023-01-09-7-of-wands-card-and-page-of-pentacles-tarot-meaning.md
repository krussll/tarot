However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and Page Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and Page Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and Page Of Pentacles mean together?

The 7 of Wands tarot card and the Page of Pentacles tarot card suggest that you should focus your efforts on defending yourself and your accomplishments from competition. The Page of Pentacles also indicates that you may receive news related to money and perhaps even a new opportunity or raise. This is a sign that you are on the right track and that you should continue to strive to protect your interests while working hard to achieve your goals. With the combination of these two cards, it is important to remain level-headed and confident yet open-minded, as you have much to gain.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health:

The 7 of Wands Tarot card represents self-defense for your health. This means that you should take measures to protect yourself from physical, emotional, and psychological illness. Self-care should be a priority, and you should make sure you are taking time to rest, eat healthy, and exercise. Don’t let yourself be overwhelmed by stress, and practice positive affirmations to increase your general wellbeing.

Finances:

The 7 of Wands Tarot card suggests that you use self-defense to protect your finances. This includes being prudent with spending, saving for a rainy day, and only taking on financial risks that you understand. Don’t be too cavalier with investments, and research any big financial decisions before you make them. Use your resources wisely, stay on top of your budget, and practice frugality whenever possible.

Relationships:

The 7 of Wands Tarot card implies that you should use self-defense in your relationships as well. This means being aware of how others might try to manipulate or exploit you, and speaking up when you need to set boundaries. It also means being honest with yourself and your partner, and being willing to work through conflicts in a healthy way. Being strong in your relationships means protecting yourself from unhealthy dynamics and maintaining your own identity.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is a positive sign when it comes to health. It indicates that physical and emotional health can improve if one takes their time and takes the necessary steps to make their health a priority. This card symbolizes taking the time to nourish the body, mind and spirit with exercise, healthy food and a balanced lifestyle. Making small adjustments to diet and lifestyle, such as adding a multivitamin and getting regular checkups, can help to improve overall health.

Finances:

The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is a good sign when it comes to finances. This card indicates that a person is likely to receive a raise, a new job opportunity or a promotion. It can also signal a financial windfall or a financial success, such as winning the lottery or receiving an inheritance. It may also indicate that one’s financial situation is set to improve in the near future. This can be a great opportunity to invest in stocks, start a retirement fund or make other sound investments.

Relationships:

The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is a great sign when it comes to relationships. This card symbolizes patience, understanding and balance. It indicates that a relationship can improve if both parties make effort and take the time to connect with each other. It also indicates that a relationship can benefit from having both parties express themselves and their feelings without fear. The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign that relationships can be very rewarding when the proper care is taken.
