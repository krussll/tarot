You may be struggling to make a choice between two different paths, or juggling many options and feeling overwhelmed by the fear of making the wrong decision. This can lead to physical and mental exhaustion, and plaguing nightmares. It is important to take a step back from the situation, and try to approach it from a different frame of mind. Remember, it is ok to take your time and weigh all your options before making a decision that feels right for you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Pentacles and 9 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Pentacles and 9 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a very powerful card that speaks to the difficulties of living with mental and emotional challenges. For example, health wise, the card suggests that it is important to take care of your mental and emotional health. It means that one needs to recognize and take steps to address any feelings of anxiety, depression, or fear that they are experiencing. It is also a reminder to practice mindfulness, relaxation, and stress reduction techniques, as well as to seek professional help if needed.

On the financial side, the 9 of Swords Tarot card reminds us to be mindful of our spending and not to overextend ourselves. It also speaks to being honest and open with ourselves about our financial situation, and to be cautious about taking on too many risks.

In relationships, the 9 of Swords Tarot card signals that the need for openness, communication, and understanding is paramount. It emphasizes the importance of expressing our feelings to our partner and having difficult conversations when needed. It reminds us that if we take the time to listen, understand, and support each other, our relationships will be stronger and more authentic.

Overall, the 9 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to prioritize our mental and emotional health, manage our finances responsibly, and be open and honest with ourselves and those around us. When we are mindful of our physical and mental health, our financial resources, and our relationships, we can be better equipped to handle the challenges of life.
