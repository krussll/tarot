Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for love. Understanding what The Justice card means in regards to your love can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Justice, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Justice.

## Justice And Love – An Overview

The Justice tarot card is an important reminder when it comes to health.

It emphasizes the concept of cause and effect, and the importance of taking preventive action in order to avoid any long-term health problems.

By understanding the cause and effect of our actions, we can make sure that we are taking the necessary steps to remain healthy and happy.

The Justice card also suggests that we must be honest with ourselves and our doctors in order to get the best health care possible.

By seeking out win-win solutions, we can ensure that our health is protected and that our long-term health goals are met.

Ultimately, the Justice card encourages us to take responsibility for our own health, and to remember that the truth will eventually come out.

## Upright Meanings For Justice And Love

### What It Means For Me

The Justice tarot card symbolizes the importance of fairness and balance in love.

It encourages us to seek win-win solutions that honor the needs of both partners, rather than one partner dominating over the other.

It suggests that both parties have a right to their own opinion and should be heard.

It also shows us the importance of being honest and open-minded in relationships.

Both parties need to be willing to take responsibility for their actions and be accountable for the consequences of their choices.

The Justice card reminds us to be mindful of the power of cause and effect, and to make sure we are always seeking justice and equity in our relationships.

### What It Means For The Future

The Justice card is a sign that when it comes to your future love, you will be guided by a sense of balance, fairness, and justice.

You will strive to reach a win-win situation in your relationships and be sure to treat each other with respect.

The card also suggests that the truth will come out eventually, so it is important to be honest and open with each other.

Ultimately, the Justice card promises that your future love will be built on a foundation of justice, truth, and fairness.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Justice tarot card tells us that true love between those close to us is based on cause and effect, win-win solutions, and a commitment to telling the truth.

Loving someone deeply means understanding that we are all connected, and that our actions and words have a direct impact on the people we love.

It means being willing to take responsibility for our past mistakes, and to use our experiences to create a better future for everyone involved.

It also means having the courage to face the truth, even when it’s hard to do so.

When we love our closest friends and family with the Justice tarot card in mind, we can create a deeper, more meaningful relationship that will last a lifetime.

## Reversed Meanings For Justice And Love

### What It Means For Me

When The Justice card is reversed, it indicates a lack of balance in relationships, particularly when it comes to love.

It may be that one partner is taking more than giving, or that there is an imbalance of power which is causing friction in the relationship.

The reversed Justice card also suggests a need to look at the truth of the situation and to be honest with one another about what is really going on.

The reversed Justice card can also signify a lack of communication, leading to misunderstandings, hurt feelings and arguments.

When this card appears reversed, it’s time to sit down, have honest conversations and look for win-win solutions.

It’s time to listen to each other, understand each other’s point of view and reach a balance that is fair to both partners.

It may be time to reassess expectations and roles in the relationship, re-establish boundaries and make sure that both partners feel heard and respected.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed Justice tarot card appears in a reading about love, it can indicate that things may not be as they seem.

There could be hidden motivations behind the actions of your partner, or a lack of balance in the relationship.

You may be feeling as though you’re carrying the burden of the relationship and that your partner isn’t pulling their weight.

This card can also signify a lack of communication in the relationship, as well as a lack of commitment and trust.

The reversed Justice card can also suggest that you need to take a step back and look at the situation objectively.

It is time to take responsibility for your part in creating the imbalance in the relationship, and to make sure that you are being fair to your partner.

It is important to make sure that you’re communicating your needs, so that you can both work together to make the relationship a win-win.

If you find yourself in a situation where the truth has been hidden, the reversed Justice card can suggest that it is time to face the truth and to make sure that the truth comes out.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The Justice card is reversed in a tarot reading, it can signify a lack of balance in relationships.

In the realm of love and romance, it can indicate a situation where the scales of justice have become tipped in one party’s favor, creating a situation of unbalanced power dynamics.

This can manifest as one person having too much control over the other person’s decisions or feelings.

It can also mean that someone is not getting their fair share of love or support, or that one partner’s needs are not being met.

The reversed Justice card can also point to a lack of communication and understanding between two people.

It can indicate a situation where both parties are not taking the time to really listen to the other’s feelings or to get the full picture of the situation.

This can lead to miscommunication and hurt feelings, and can ultimately damage the relationship.

The reversed Justice card is a reminder to take a step back and to try to look at things with a new perspective.

It can be a call to action to take a more balanced and fair approach to the relationship, and to ensure that both parties’ needs are being met.

By taking the time to listen, communicate, and work together to find win-win solutions, it is possible to restore balance and harmony to the relationship.
