A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 6 Of Pentacles card and 8 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together?

The combination of The 6 Of Pentacles and The 8 Of Swords tarot cards speaks to the idea of needing help to break free of self-imposed restrictions and isolation. The 6 Of Pentacles encourages us to reach out and ask for help, while The 8 Of Swords reminds us that help can come from within if we take the time to confront our fears and find our own inner strength. The clash between needing help from an external source and recognizing our own internal power can help us break free from the self-imposed restrictions or the patterns of isolation we have created for ourselves.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 6 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card and the 8 Of Swords tarot card together suggest that, in matters of the heart, it is important to remember the importance of balance between offering and accepting help from another. This card combination often reflects a relationship where one partner is struggling with self-imposed restrictions, such as fear and insecurity, that are holding them back from fully engaging in the relationship. Although these inner struggles can lead to feelings of isolation and imprisonment, the 6 Of Pentacles tarot card reminds us that there is a way out.

This card combination can be a reminder that there is a need for both parties in the relationship to be willing to give and receive help. A supportive partner can help break through the barriers of the self-imposed restrictions and assist the other in finding freedom in order to move forward. This card combination encourages both partners to look beyond the restrictions and limitations in order to foster a healthy and balanced love life.

The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card tells us to extend our hand out of generosity, while the 8 Of Swords tarot card makes us aware that help cannot be forced. It is essential to approach any challenges within the relationship with respect, kindness, and understanding. A well-balanced love life is only achieved when both parties are willing to offer and receive help equally. The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card and the 8 Of Swords tarot card together invite us to recognize the challenges in our love life and to unlock them by coming together as a team.

The combination of The 6 Of Pentacles and The 8 Of Swords tarot cards can indicate a period of financial and job-related struggle in your life. The 6 Of Pentacles tarot card symbolizes generosity and giving or receiving help. This card asks you to consider ways you can reach out for help to ease your current struggles. You may have had to ask for assistance, or you may have been offered assistance from a friend or family member.

The 8 Of Swords tarot card suggests that you may be going through a period of self-imposed restrictions and isolation in regards to your finances and job. You may be feeling helpless and stuck due to your current circumstances. The 8 Of Swords can signify that you need to take a step back, take a break, and look at the situation from the outside before you can move forward.

The combination of the two cards can signify that you are feeling overwhelmed and stuck in your career and financial situation. You may need to reach out to those around you to seek their help and support. Acknowledging and leveraging their help may be the only way to create positive momentum in your finances and career. It also encourages you to take some time for yourself and reflect on the situation; this could help to provide clarity and motivation for potential solutions. The combination of the two cards calls for you to show yourself compassion and seek external support to move forward.

This combination encourages us to embrace a kind of self-care that acknowledges both our need for giving and receiving help, and our need for taking ownership of our health.

On the other hand, the 8 of Swords points to the need for setting boundaries, so that we can take care of ourselves. This card speaks to the power of taking ownership of our health, and understanding when it is time to step back and focus on our own wellbeing. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 6 Of Pentacles and 8 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card has implications for health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the 6 of Pentacles suggests a need to receive or give help. People may need to seek assistance from a healthcare provider to ensure they are getting the best care available. Alternatively, they may find themselves in a position to give assistance or support to a friend or family member in need, whether in the form of advice, a listening ear, or physical help. This card encourages people to seek out support if needed and to be generous when offering it.

Financially, the 6 of Pentacles encourages a sense of balance between taking and giving. It suggests that people may either benefit from seeking out help for their finances, or that they may find themselves in a place to help out someone else with their bills. It is a reminder to be mindful of how money is spent and to practice generosity whenever possible.

Finally, the 6 of Pentacles can refer to relationships. It suggests that individuals should strive to be generous with their time and attention when it comes to the people they love. This card encourages people to give of their time and energy, in order to create healthy, meaningful connections with their loved ones. In terms of seeking assistance, the 6 of Pentacles reminds people that asking for help is a sign of strength, not weakness. People should not be afraid to ask for support when they need it.

## What does 8 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 8 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to take care of our mental, physical and emotional health. It urges us to take a step back and assess our current life and routine, so that we can create healthier boundaries and limits, reducing the risk of ‘over-doing’ things. It suggests that we may need to remove ourselves from toxic relationships and environments, allowing us to escape oppressive people and situations. This card is a warning of possible physical and mental exhaustion if we don’t take the time to ‘reset’, as well as the risk of being stuck in a rut.

Finances

The 8 of Swords Tarot card can be an indication of unfortunate financial circumstances, often warning us of the need to stay away from risky financial behaviours. It is an indication to manage our money wisely, practice self-discipline with budgeting and safeguard our finances for a better future. It cautions us to make sensible financial decisions, avoid taking out loans and only make investments we know we can afford.

Relationships

The 8 of Swords Tarot card also emphasises the importance of being discerning when it comes to relationships, highlighting the need for boundaries and limitations to protect our wellbeing. We are encouraged to be mindful about who we let into our lives, understanding that not all relationships are equal and it’s ok to say no if something doesn’t feel right. We may need to protect ourselves from passive forms of abuse and manipulation by creating healthy boundaries and limits. The 8 of Swords reminds us that we are worth more than any sacrifice and that we can choose to free ourselves from any unhealthy relationship.
