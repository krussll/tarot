Bad luck is something that everyone encounters from time to time. Whether it is in the form of a car breaking down on the way to work, spilling your coffee, or tripping and falling as you leave your home and banging your head on the front door, bad things happen to all of us.

They are not always easy to predict. However, there are some things in life that are universally considered unlucky.

The tarot has long been associated with foretelling future events and understanding our innermost thoughts. It’s no surprise that this old and original card game would have a few sayings about bad luck as well!

Read on for more information…

## The Death Card

The [Death](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-death/) card in the tarot is often regarded as a card that means bad luck. It often signifies a major change, whether good or bad. This change could be in a relationship, a move to a new house, a pivotal work promotion, or the loss of a loved one.

Death is often depicted as a cloaked figure standing over the corpses of people in coffins. Some people interpret the Death card as a warning that a loved one is dying, but it can also simply be a sign of impending change.

Another interpretation of the Death card is that it represents transformation through destruction.

Life is a constant cycle of creation and destruction, and this card can indicate a period of destruction or loss that will lead to new life or change.

## The Devil

[The Devil](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-the-devil/) card in tarot is often associated with bad luck, temptation, and obsession. This card was often depicted as a horned and leering man tempting a woman out of her robe.

It can be interpreted as being tempted to engage in an unhealthy relationship, act upon an unhealthy obsession, or becoming obsessed with something unhealthy. The Devil card can also mean that you’re feeling pressured to do something you don’t want to do.

It can also indicate a relationship that is unhealthy or obsessive.

If the Devil card appears in a tarot reading, it is important to pay attention to the other cards in the reading to get a better understanding of what the card means and how best to move forward.

## The Tower

[The Tower](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-the-tower/) card in the tarot indicates an uncontrollable and sudden event that will have a negative impact on your life.

Most often, the Tower card indicates a sudden ending of a relationship or friendship, financial loss, or a health scare.

More positive interpretations of the Tower card include a sudden and decisive change that leads to a positive outcome.

For example, quitting your job to pursue a dream or ending an unhealthy relationship to begin a happier one.

## The Lovers

[The Lovers](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-the-lovers/) card in the tarot often represents a choice that will have a huge impact on your life. It can be a positive or negative change depending on what the rest of the cards in your reading indicate.

The Lovers card can indicate a romantic relationship, a friendship, or a decision that will affect you for the rest of your life.

This card also often indicates a decision that causes internal conflict and stress.

## Ace of Swords

The [Ace of Swords](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-ace-of-swords/) in the tarot often indicates a new beginning or positive change. It can also indicate the need to take action because something is going to happen soon.

The Ace of Swords can also indicate a period of growth and strength. It is important to pay attention to the other cards in your reading when the Ace of Swords appears to get a better idea of what the card means and how best to move forward.

## The Fool

[The Fool](https://tarothappy.com/tarot-card-meanings/guide-to-the-fool/) card in the tarot often indicates naivety and foolishness. It can also indicate being too idealistic and unrealistic.

The Fool card can also signify a beginner who is starting out new and has much to learn. It can also indicate someone who is too optimistic and does not see the potential for negative outcomes.
