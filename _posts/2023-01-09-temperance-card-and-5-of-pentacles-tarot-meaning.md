However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

In other words, it is important to maintain a sense of self-control and harmony even when life throws you a curveball. This could mean difficulties like financial loss, isolation, and worry, but it is important to remember that if you take the time to stay balanced and find harmony, you can eventually achieve your goals and turn any negative situation into a positive outcome.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

Health  
The Temperance Tarot card conveys a message of balance and harmony when it comes to health. It emphasizes the importance of moderation in all areas of life, including food, exercise, and rest. It encourages its holder to take the time to establish and maintain healthy habits, and to find ways to reduce stress. The card advocates for an overall mindful lifestyle, where diet and exercise are used to maintain balance and regulate stress levels.

Finances  
The Temperance Tarot card asks its holder to manage their finances with a certain level of self-control, patience, and understanding. It urges the cardholder to set realistic goals and manage their resources wisely. It emphasizes the importance of moderation and suggests that it is best to save and invest money with a long-term view of growth and return. It also suggests that impulse purchases should be avoided.

Relationships  
The Temperance Tarot card indicates that relationships should be based on mutual respect, understanding, and self-control. It emphasizes the importance of patience, open communication, and balance in all relationships, whether intimate or otherwise. The card encourages its holder to be mindful of their words and actions and to take the time to listen to and understand the perspectives of those around them. It suggests that relationships should be handled with a sense of moderation and caution.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card is often linked to ill health, poverty, and worry. From a physical health standpoint, this card suggests that something is wrong. This could be a physical illness, injury, or other physical setback. It could also suggest that one’s health is not where it should be, either mentally or emotionally. It may be accompanied by feelings of anxiety, depression, and despair.

This card can also indicate the financial struggles that come with illness. It could suggest that a person is struggling to pay medical bills or living expenses due to an illness or injury. It could also reflect a situation where financial resources are scarce, leading to fear and worry.

From a relationship perspective, this card can suggest a sense of loneliness and isolation. The person may feel disconnected from family or friends, or feel like they are struggling to make meaningful connections. This could be due to financial constraints, physical or mental health concerns, or other struggles.

All of these factors can take their toll on one’s overall well-being, creating a downward spiral of fear, worry and despair. The 5 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages us to face our challenges, seek help when needed, and rebuild a life of balance and abundance.
