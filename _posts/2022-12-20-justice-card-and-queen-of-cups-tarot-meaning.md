A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The Justice card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the Queen of Cups tarot card point to a wonderful combination of traits. When these two cards appear in a reading together, they suggest a need for a balanced approach to any situation. This combination of cards offers the ability to find solutions that work for everyone, no matter which party is involved. A sense of fair play and justice is paramount in this combination, with the Queen of Cups providing the necessary nurturing and healing to ensure that solutions benefit all parties. Furthermore, the combination of these cards signals that the truth will eventually be revealed, so it is best to approach situations with a sense of honesty and integrity.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Justice and Queen Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The Justice tarot card and The Queen of Cups when drawn together indicate a connection in your love life that is rooted in justice and compassion. This connection is one of understanding, where both parties involved feel heard and appreciated. When those in the relationship continue to operate on a foundation of fairness, the truth will always come out and the long-term effect on the relationship will be greater.

The Justice card reinforces that it is important to strive for win-win solutions, rather than trying to prove yourself right or win every argument. Win-win solutions in a relationship benefit both parties, creating a more healthy and sustainable dynamic. When it comes to the truth coming out, this tarot card shows that by seeking win-win solutions and staying on the path of justice and fairness, you can ensure that the truth will be revealed in more effective and straightforward manner, creating trust between you two.

The Queen of Cups tarot card brings healing and nurturing to your relationship. Allowing both of you to be vulnerable with each other, while still being able to have a foundation of support, understanding, and love. It encourages you to show up for each other in times of need, being an emotional support and giving each other the comfort and nurturing you both need during tough times. The Queen of Cups also encourages healing any wounds and trauma that might arise, in order to create a more secure and stable connection.

Together, The Justice and The Queen of Cups tarot cards encourage you to be honest and open with each other, to stay on the path of fairness and justice, and to turn to each other in times of need. This connection will bring a sense of balance, stability, and support, where both parties will not only feel seen and heard but also feel secure and loved.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Justice and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Justice and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

The Justice Tarot card is a powerful symbol of cause and effect, win-win solutions, and the truth coming out. When it comes to health, The Justice Tarot card can serve as a reminder that the body is a powerful instrument and that what we put into it and the choices we make can have a lasting impact on our wellbeing. It’s essential to be mindful of the consequences that come from our actions, whether that’s choosing healthful foods or taking proper vitamins and supplements. Taking control of our health is essential in achieving balance and harmony.

As far as finances, The Justice Tarot card encourages us to make decisions that serve our long-term interests. That might mean setting up a budget, researching investment opportunities, or engaging in other money-saving measures. When it comes to money, making sound, rational decisions that come with a consideration of the future can help us save and possibly even earn more.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Justice Tarot card can remind us that how we approach conversations can have a lasting effect. Engaging in conversations in a clear, honest, and straightforward way can help build trust and strong communication. We’ve all experienced how difficult it can be to repair a relationship once trust has been broken; this card encourages us to practice being mindful in our interactions. By keeping the well-being of all parties in mind, we can create a healthy balance in our relationships. Ultimately, The Justice Tarot card shows us the importance of considering cause and effect, win-win solutions, and the truth coming out when it comes to our health, finances, and relationships.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health: The Queen of Cups Tarot card encourages us to nurture our health and be supportive of our body and mind. This card indicates that it is time to be discerning and mindful of what we take into our body, whether it is food, alcohol, medication or anything that has the potential to influence our wellbeing. This card could also be a reminder that when we are feeling unwell, we should look at the emotional, mental and physical causes and diagnose an illness or condition before jumping to conclusions.

Finances: The Queen of Cups Tarot card indicates the need for a balanced and supportive approach in financial matters. This card can indicate that now is the time to be savvy when it comes to money and make sure to take calculated risks. The Queen of Cups also suggests that we should take into account our emotional needs and feelings when making decisions related to our finances.

Relationships: The Queen of Cups Tarot card denotes a compassionate, loving and understanding person. It suggests the need to be nurturing and supportive of those around us, whether it be through a romantic relationship or a friendship. This card acknowledges the importance of kindness and sensitivity when interacting with others. It also reminds us to listen to our hearts and emotions when considering a relationship, as this will help us to make the best decisions for both ourselves and for those we care about.
