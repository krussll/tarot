Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The 3 Of Swords card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 3 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 3 Of Swords.

## 3 Of Swords And Career And Finances – An Overview

The 3 Of Swords tarot card is a warning of potential health issues, especially those related to the heart.

It suggests struggles related to emotional and physical health, such as depression, heartbreak, divorce, and loss.

It may also be a sign of impending surgery, whether it is major or minor.

This card can be a difficult one to receive and is a reminder to take care of oneself and to be mindful of one’s mental and physical health.

It is important to remember that there is always hope and that it is possible to move past any dark times.

## Upright Meanings For 3 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The 3 Of Swords is a card that symbolizes heartbreak, loss, and depression and can be a warning sign of potential issues in the area of career and finances.

This card can be a sign of a difficult period of time where you may be struggling to make ends meet, or feeling overwhelmed and unmotivated.

It could also indicate the potential for a career setback or the dissolution of a business partnership.

It can be a reminder to stay mindful of your decisions, as they may have a profound impact on your financial situation.

It is important to remember that this card is not a sign of failure, but rather a warning to be prepared for any potential difficulties.

With careful consideration of your choices and a proactive approach to your finances, you can still make sure that your career and finances remain on track.

### What It Means For The Future

The 3 Of Swords indicates that my future career and finances are at a crossroads.

I may experience heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression, or even surgery in the near future.

This can lead to major changes in my life, such as having to start a new career path or find new sources of income.

These major changes may bring financial pressures, but the card also suggests that I have the ability to persevere and find a way to make my situation better.

With patience, hard work, and determination, I can overcome the obstacles I face and eventually find success in both my career and finances.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 3 Of Swords tarot card often signals a period of heartache, loss, and depression within close relationships, which can have a significant impact on one’s career and finances.

It suggests that someone close to you may be going through a tough period, where they may be facing a divorce, a job change or even a surgery that could affect their professional and financial futures.

It could mean that they may need to make difficult decisions or take a step back in their career or finances to recover.

It is important to provide support to those close to you during this time, as they may need your help to make it through this difficult period.

## Reversed Meanings For 3 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The reversed 3 of Swords in relation to career and finances represents a period of healing and recovery.

You have faced some difficult times recently and may have experienced a bout of depression or hardship as a result.

However, this is a time for you to heal, to repair any damage that may have been done, and to start anew.

You may find that your finances are not in their best state right now, and it is important to take the steps necessary to get them back in order.

This can include budgeting, cutting back on unnecessary expenses, and making any necessary changes to your job or career path.

The reversed 3 of Swords also suggests that you will soon be able to experience some positive forward momentum in your finances and career.

You may find that you have an opportunity to make a major career change, or that you are able to pursue a new job that better fits your needs and desires.

You may also find that you have some luck with investments, or that you are able to make some smart decisions with your money that will benefit you in the long run.

This is a time to focus on what you want out of your career and finances and make sure that you are making the best decisions for yourself.

### What It Means For The Future

The reversed Three of Swords suggests that the future of your career and finances will be filled with hope and healing.

You are entering into a period of growth and progress, and your hard work and dedication will be rewarded.

Your finances and career will be more stable and secure, and you may begin to see a steady increase in income.

You will find yourself in a more confident and optimistic state of mind, allowing you to make more calculated decisions that will benefit you in the long run.

The reversed Three of Swords also indicates that you may be on the cusp of a new career path, one that is more rewarding and purposeful.

This could be the result of a new job opportunity or a sudden realization that your current role is not fulfilling your needs.

Either way, you will have the energy and drive to make a successful transition into this new career.

Additionally, this period could bring about new business opportunities and investments that will lead to greater financial stability.

Your hard work and dedication will be rewarded, bringing a sense of satisfaction and accomplishment to your life.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The 3 Of Swords appears in a reversed position in a reading, it can be interpreted as a sign of optimism and hope for those close to me when it comes to their careers and finances.

This card signifies that although there have been difficult times, the dark clouds of despair are starting to clear and the path ahead is a little brighter.

The reversed Three of Swords suggests that there is a light at the end of the tunnel and that those around me may be experiencing new opportunities and greater financial stability.

This card is also an indication that although there may have been difficulties in the past, there is a newfound resilience that is helping those close to me to face the challenges ahead with confidence and optimism.

This could mean that they are beginning to see the rewards of their hard work, and that their careers and financial situations are finally improving.

The reversed Three of Swords indicates that those around me are starting to feel more secure and that they are now on the path to financial stability and success.
