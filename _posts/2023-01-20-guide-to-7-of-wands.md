## 7 Of Wands – An Overview

The Seven of Wands tarot card is all about self-defense and protection against competition. The card symbolizes the need to defend oneself and one’s beliefs, standing one’s ground, and being assertive and determined. The figure in the card is standing on a hill, his staff in his hands, looking out over the six other staves which signify the competition he faces. He is determined and strong, ready to take on any challenge that comes his way. He is protecting something which is precious and important to him, and is willing to fight for it. The card is a reminder that we all have something to fight for and that we must stand up for ourselves and our beliefs.

## Upright Meanings Of 7 Of Wands

### What It Means For Me

The 7 of Wands tarot card is a strong indicator of self-defense and protecting yourself against competition. This card encourages you to stand up for yourself and your beliefs in the face of any opposition, even if it means confronting those who threaten you. It is a reminder that you have the strength and courage to defend yourself and your goals, no matter what the odds. This card speaks to your inner strength and resilience and encourages you to stay firm and defend your position no matter how difficult the situation might be. It is also a reminder to stay focused and determined to succeed, even in the face of opposition.

### What It Means For The Future

The 7 of Wands in the Tarot is a sign that you should stand your ground and protect yourself against any competition that may arise. It is a reminder that you should be unyielding in your strength and determination as you move forward in life. This card emphasizes the importance of having self-confidence and the courage to stand up for yourself in difficult situations. As you continue on your journey, this card encourages you to be resilient and remain determined in the face of any obstacles that may come your way. With the 7 of Wands in the Tarot, you can be confident that you have the strength and will power to not only protect yourself but also to succeed in any future endeavors.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 7 of Wands tarot card suggests that those close to me may need to be on guard and protect themselves against competition. It serves as a reminder that the people I care about should stand up for themselves and fight for what they believe in. It also serves as a warning to not take any situation lightly and be prepared to defend themselves in order to stay safe. The 7 of Wands asks us to be vigilant and be careful about the intentions of those around us. It can also be interpreted as a reminder to check in with our loved ones and make sure that they are feeling supported and protected.

## Reversed Meanings Of 7 Of Wands

### What It Means For Me

The reversed 7 of Wands suggests that I have been feeling the pressure of competition and have been struggling to protect my own interests and ideas. I may have become overly defensive and have been pushing away opportunities for collaboration. This card warns that I may be too fearful of failure and need to find the courage to take risks and compete.

The reversed 7 of Wands also suggests that there might be an imbalance in my self-defense. I may need to focus less on protecting myself and more on recognizing the potential of collaboration and teamwork. This reversal encourages me to step back and evaluate the situation; to open myself up to the possibility of working with others and to trust that I can be successful. It is time to find the balance between self-protection and openness to collaboration, in order to make progress.

### What It Means For The Future

When The 7 Of Wands appears in its reversed position, it can be an indication that your future may involve a period of vulnerability and passivity. This card warns against relying too heavily on external forces to protect yourself and your interests. You may be fighting an uphill battle and it is important to remember that you are the only one who can truly protect yourself.

The reversed Seven of Wands suggests that this is a time for you to take a step back and reevaluate your situation. You may need to look at what strategies you are currently using and determine which ones are no longer effective. It is important to remember that you have the power to make decisions that will help protect your interests and to be aware of the competition that surrounds you.

Overall, the reversed Seven of Wands encourages you to be proactive and self-reliant. You may need to take a few risks in order to secure your future and protect your interests. This is a time to be aware of the competition and take steps to ensure your success. With the right attitude and a willingness to take action and stand up for yourself, you can achieve great things.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed 7 of Wands appears in a reading, it can be indicative of an underlying desire to protect those close to me. This could mean that I am feeling a strong need to defend my loved ones, both physically and emotionally. I may be ready to take on any competition, challenge, or conflict that may arise in order to keep them safe. This card could also suggest that I am being overly protective and interfering in matters that don’t necessarily need my help. It is important to remember that I cannot always be the protector for my family and friends. Sometimes, it is better to stand back and let them navigate their own way through life’s challenges. The reversed 7 of Wands serves as a reminder to trust in their own capabilities and to offer my help and guidance only when they truly need it.
