A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 7 Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 7 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The combination of the Lovers tarot card and the 7 of Cups tarot card could mean that you are facing a difficult choice, and considering the various options available to you. The message is that you must take your time to weigh up the pros and cons and determine the consequences of your decision before committing to anything. The Lovers card symbolizes partnering up and the strength of two, so no matter what you decide, it is important that you have an understanding and supportive partner who can help you work through the decision. Ultimately, the cards are telling you to go with your heart, as true love and partnerships can endure any situation.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Lovers and 7 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The Lovers tarot card and the 7 Of Cups tarot card together represent a time of choice and decision making in your love life. While The Lovers card speaks to a strong partnership and deep love, it is tempered by the 7 Of Cups card suggesting that you may have too many choices in front of you and difficulty making a commitment.

The challenge for you lies in sorting out your feelings and deciding which direction is right for you and your relationship. This can be a difficult but rewarding process. If you are in a committed partnership, you may be struggling to decide between staying the course and taking a leap of faith in a different direction. Or, you may be feeling a strong connection with someone but wrestling with the uncertainty of taking the next step.

The Lovers card encourages you to use the strength of two to make a decision that you can both live with. It also suggests that it will be necessary to communicate openly and honestly with your partner if you decide to stay together or embark on a new journey with someone else.

The 7 Of Cups card acts as a reminder to take your time, be confident in the choices you make, and commit to the path you choose. There may be many options and alternatives, but the important thing is to make sure that you and your partner can both agree on and accept the decision you make.

Ultimately, The Lovers tarot card and the 7 Of Cups tarot card together suggest that you have the power to create a strong and loving relationship, but it may take some thought, communication, and commitment to make it happen.

### What does The Lovers and 7 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

The combination of the Lovers tarot card and the 7 of Cups card signifies that a difficult choice between two paths must be made. This could represent a decision between two job offers or two investments that are tempting you but at the same time, commitment issues or too many choices are clouding your ability to make a decision. This reflects that you may be uncertain about which path to take, and this uncertainty is preventing you from making a decision.

The Lovers tarot card signifies partnership and deep love, which implies that it is important to consider the opinions of a partner or close friend in your life when it comes to your finances and job. This could mean talking to your partner about which job offer to take or getting advice from a trusted friend on which investment to pursue.

At the same time, the 7 of Cups card comes into play as it could mean that even after consulting with others, you may struggle to make a decision and commit to either job offer or investment. This could mean that you are feeling overwhelmed by all of the possibilities and feel unable to make a solid commitment.

Ultimately, the combination of the Lovers and 7 of Cups cards in relation to your finances and job indicate that you need to take your time to make a decision that you are confident in. Consider the opinion of a trusted confidant in your life, but ultimately, only you can make this choice. Take some time to reflect and make sure that you are comfortable with the decision you make.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Lovers and 7 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health:

The Lovers Tarot card symbolizes partnership and togetherness, and for people who are looking for guidance about their health, this card can be interpreted as a reminder to stay mindful of health-related decisions that are made jointly with a partner. This card encourages people to become invested in their partner’s health as well as their own, to appreciate the strength that comes from two people making decisions together. People may find that their relationship has a positive effect on their overall health, as the strong bond that they share gives them both the ability to be each other’s motivator and support system.

Finances:

The Lovers Tarot card speaks of the power of partnership in the realm of finances. This could refer to having a joint bank account or having a financial planner to help manage a couple’s money matters. People may find that their relationship is parallel to their financial success, as both parties can work together to create a budget and establish financial goals. It could also refer to having a strong support system that enables both people to pursue their interests independently, yet be financially accountable to one another.

Relationships:

The Lovers Tarot card is an indication of deep love, and it can be interpreted as a reminder that relationships need to be nurtured and supported in order to thrive. People may find that their relationship becomes stronger when they make decisions that are mutually beneficial, such as going on vacations together or participating in joint hobbies. This card is also a sign of commitment, which could refer to a couple making a decision to move in together or get married. It is important to recognize that two people can be stronger together than they are apart, and this card can be interpreted as an emblem of the power of relationships to give strength and guidance in all facets of life.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

For Health:  
The 7 of Cups tarot card can be an indication that you are feeling overwhelmed by too many choices when it comes to your health. You may feel that there are numerous treatment options available to you, and it can be difficult to decide which one is right for you. It is important to take time to research and consider all of your options before making a commitment. It is also important to recognize when you need help and seek out trusted medical advice or a second opinion.

For Finances:  
The 7 of Cups tarot card can represent a need for better financial planning. This can often lead to commitment issues when it comes to making long-term financial decisions. For example, if you are considering making a large financial purchase such as a car or house, it is important to take the time to understand what all of your options are and how each one can affect your financial future. It can be tempting to make a quick decision or jump on the hottest deal, but this can often lead to financial instability in the long run.

For Relationships:  
The 7 of Cups tarot card can indicate that you are feeling overwhelmed when it comes to making a commitment in a relationship. You may be considering multiple relationships and find it difficult to choose just one. It is important to take the time to think about each relationship and the implications it will have on your life before making a commitment. You may also want to take a step back and reevaluate any relationships that do not feel right or that do not feel like the best fit for you.
