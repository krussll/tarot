However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does The Hierophant and 7 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Hierophant tarot card and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card together suggest that traditional expectations and hard work are needed in order to achieve success. It emphasizes the importance of taking the time to do things the right way and not taking shortcuts; the good things in life take time and require effort to manifest. You may feel a need to conform to societal norms and expectations, but the card combination reminds you to take the time to be sure that you are following your own path, not someone else’s. This may mean doing the work to achieve delayed success, and in the end will be worth the effort.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hierophant and 7 Of Pentacles mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hierophant and 7 Of Pentacles the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

The Hierophant Tarot card symbolizes maintaining healthy traditions and habits. It encourages us to stick to a regular exercise regimen, and to follow a balanced diet. We should strive to keep our physical and mental health in alignment with the teachings of our culture and the foundations that have been set forth by our elders. It implies that it is important to keep certain practices in our lives, such as regular medical check-ups, in order to ensure that our bodies and minds remain in top form.

Finances  
The Hierophant Tarot card also speaks to our financial health. It speaks of conventional wisdom and of following methods traditionally accepted as financially sound. It reminds us to pay our bills on time, and to stick to a budget. It suggests that we should be wise with our money and to invest in the future, so that we can be financially secure. Furthermore, The Hierophant Tarot card encourages us to seek advice from someone with financial knowledge, such as a financial planner, to ensure that our financial goals are met.

Relationships  
The Hierophant Tarot card can also represent relationships. Here, it suggests that we should follow the rules of propriety and societal standards when it comes to relationships. It implies that we should communicate clearly with our partners, and strive for a solid foundation of trust. We should be mindful of the commitments we make, and always strive to keep our promises. This card also speaks to the importance of traditional values like courtesy and respect, which are essential for a healthy relationship.

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes the need for careful and patient planning in life. This card speaks to the importance of long-term projects, as well as delayed gratification.

When it comes to health, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can be interpreted to remind us that patience and persistence are key elements of achieving and maintaining good health. This card teaches us to be mindful of our health, to invest our energy into the long-term care of our bodies, and to develop healthy habits over time. This card indicates that the effort we put into our health today will bear success in the future.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can be interpreted to remind us of the need for diligence and patience when it comes to financial success. This card indicates that focusing solely on the short-term can lead to instability and struggle. This card teaches us that long-term sustainability is key, and that focusing on progressive planning and smart investments will pay off in the long-run.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card can be interpreted to remind us that all relationships require hard work, patience, and dedication. This card indicates that steady, consistent effort is necessary in order to build strong, meaningful connections. The 7 of Pentacles calls upon us to invest our energy into our most important relationships, to stay mindful of the effort required to foster them, and to be patient with the process.
