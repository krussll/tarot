A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and 2 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The The Tower card and 2 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and 2 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Tower tarot card and The 2 Of Pentacles tarot card together could mean that unexpected and unwelcome change is approaching, and you must make a difficult decision that could lead to both ruin and upheaval. The choices may be weighed carefully, as the consequences could be dire, but even if the consequences of one choice are not ideal, it might still be the best option to avoid further destruction and ruin. The key is to remain flexible and open to different courses of action in order to make the most logical and beneficial decision that will keep you safe and secure.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

The Tower Tarot card can have a very negative effect on health, finances, and relationships. When it comes to health, the Tower Tarot can indicate serious physical health issues, such as chronic diseases or even death. Physically, the Tower can signify the need to make a huge change in lifestyle, such as quitting smoking or drinking, or making drastic changes to diet and exercise. The Tower can also mean a sudden or unexpected illness, or an injury that puts a person’s life at risk.

When it comes to finances, The Tower can indicate an unexpected loss of money or an investment that goes wrong. It can also mean financial ruin, leading to an inability to pay bills, debts, or mortgages. The Tower can also signify a period of poverty, when necessary resources can be scarce and difficult to come by.

The Tower Tarot can also be a warning sign for relationships. It can indicate an unexpected breakdown of a relationship, or even the end of a relationship. It could also signify a period of upheaval, upheaval that can cause a great deal of damage and pain to a person and their relationships. It can also mean a dramatic change in the power dynamics of a relationship, with one partner suddenly gaining control and the other feeling powerless. This can have a devastating impact on the relationship and can lead to arguments, misunderstandings, and emotional upheaval that can take a long time to recover from.

In summary, The Tower Tarot can be a deeply worrying card when it appears in readings. It can indicate unwanted change, ruin, disgrace, upheaval, and violence, with physical, emotional and financial impacts. In this case, the best approach someone can take is to prepare for the worst and take steps to protect their health, finances, and relationships.

## What does 2 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:  
The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages us to take a holistic approach to health. It suggests that instead of focusing on one specific aspect of our health, like diet or exercise, we must examine the bigger picture and prioritize our health in all areas. It could indicate that we are being too rigid with our health plan and need to take a step back and reassess which areas we want to prioritize. This card can also signify that we need to balance our well-being by incorporating physical, mental, and spiritual health into our daily lives.

Finances:  
The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card encourages us to take a balanced approach to our finances. It suggests that we have multiple options available to us and should take time to weigh each option carefully. This card can represent difficult financial choices that need to be made, as well as options to look at as we try to make sound financial decisions. The 2 of Pentacles is a reminder to not be too rigid and to keep an open mind when making decisions about our finances.

Relationships:  
The 2 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that in relationships, we need to prioritize balance. We should look at all parties involved and assess how we can create a space where everyone can be heard and respected. It also signifies that we may be trying to juggle multiple relationships at once and need to evaluate what is most important. We should be careful not to let one relationship take too much of our energy, while neglecting the other. The 2 of Pentacles encourages us to maintain a healthy balance in our relationships so we can give each relationship the attention and respect it deserves.
