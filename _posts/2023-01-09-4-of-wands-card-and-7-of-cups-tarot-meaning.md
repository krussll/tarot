## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Wands mean?

The 4 of Wands tarot card typically symbolizes homecoming, celebrations, family, friends, and reunions. When this card appears in a tarot reading, it indicates that the querent is about to embark on a path of success and recognition. In terms of health, the 4 of Wands allows the querent to relax and enjoy some much-needed time with family and friends, allowing them to put their mind and body at ease. This can be beneficial for both physical and mental health, as the querent is able to take a break from their daily stresses and reflect on the positive aspects of their life.

When it comes to finances, the 4 of Wands can signify a new opportunity that may bring financial security and stability. This card can also indicate a victorious end to a difficult financial period, allowing the querent to celebrate and enjoy the fruits of their hard work.

In terms of relationships, the 4 of Wands can indicate that family and friends are gathering to celebrate a successful cycle or accomplishment. The querent is likely to be surrounded by love and support, as these people come together to share in their joy. This card can also signify a new relationship, or the reuniting of existing relationships, as the querent is able to come together in a spirit of celebration.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 7 of Cups Tarot card is a warning sign of being distracted and unfocused, which can be dangerous when it comes to maintaining one’s health. In particular, the person may be making bad decisions regarding their lifestyle, diet, and other habits which affect their overall health. The card is warning you to pay attention to what you’re doing and make sure that you’re not taking too many risks or undergoing too many unhealthy habits.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the 7 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of too much spending and being reckless with money. It also warns against taking unnecessary risks with investments and not developing a sound financial plan. People should be wary of taking on too much debt and should make sure to set a budget and stick to it. They should also be sure to research and consider all options available before making important financial decisions.

Relationships: The 7 of Cups Tarot card can be a sign of commitment issues when it comes to relationships. It cautions against making hasty decisions when it comes to relationships, as well as avoiding being too open with your feelings. The card is suggesting that you take the time to really get to know someone before committing to them, and that you make sure to keep your heart open but also protect yourself. It also warns against becoming too emotionally dependent on someone or developing unhealthy relationships.
