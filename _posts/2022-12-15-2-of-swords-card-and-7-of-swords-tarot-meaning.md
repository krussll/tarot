However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Swords card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Swords card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Swords and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The 2 Of Swords and the 7 Of Swords tarot cards represent two very different aspects of life. The 2 Of Swords urges you to use your logical mind and find a way to compromise or make a decision. The 7 Of Swords speaks to the darker side of life, such as theft, betrayal and dishonesty. Taken together, these two cards could be interpreted as a warning that divided loyalties and an unwillingness to make a decision can lead to greater problems such as theft or betrayal. It is important to weigh your options and come to an agreement or decision before any other party is able to take advantage of the situation.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

The 2 of Swords Tarot card has several meanings, depending on the context. When it comes to health, it indicates that it is time to seek a compromise between strong conflicting views, such as between doctors and patients. One must strive to balance the wisdom of both sides and take a balanced approach to any health issues. This could be a matter of balancing the safety of one’s health with the risks associated with a particular treatment.

When it comes to finances, the 2 of Swords suggests that it is time to make a decision. This might be a question of weighing up two different investment options, or choosing between two different loans. It could involve deciding between paying off debt and saving for the future, or allocating funds to different projects. In any case, a balanced and practical decision must be made in a timely manner.

In relationships, the 2 of Swords indicates that it is time to compromise and seek a balanced solution. This could mean having a respectful and open conversation about any issues between two parties, or coming to an agreement about a shared matter. It could also involve making a concerted effort to finally resolve a troublesome argument. In any case, the goal is to find understanding and common ground to ensure lasting peace.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card can have different meanings depending on the context and the area of life in question. When it comes to health, it can represent a lack of discipline, self-destructive behavior, or a reluctance to seek help due to fear of judgement. Financially, it can refer to theft, dishonesty and betrayal, especially when it comes to borrowing or lending money, or not repaying debts. It could also symbolize a lack of financial planning or running away from responsibilities.

In relationships, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can indicate dishonesty, betrayal and a tendency to keep secrets. It could be an indication of running away from potentially difficult conversations, which can lead to further confusion or misunderstanding. This card can also hint at a lack of commitment, insecurities, selfish decisions or someone taking advantage of another.

Overall, the 7 of Swords Tarot card represents dishonesty, betrayal, running away and a lack of discipline. In terms of health, it calls attention to potentially self-destructive behavior and avoidance of seeking help. With finances, it may point to risky decisions and not paying back money owed. In relationships, it speaks of dishonesty, betrayal and secretiveness. All these interpretations of this card point to the need for awareness, caution and honesty in all areas of life.
