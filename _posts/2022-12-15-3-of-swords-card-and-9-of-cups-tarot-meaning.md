A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 3 Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together?

The Tarot cards The 3 of Swords and The 9 of Cups together can be interpreted as a difficult and difficult transition. This could be a painful heartbreak, such as a divorce or a breakup, or a deeper and darker form of loss, like depression or surgery. This difficult transition also may signify a wish that needs to be fulfilled in order to restore one’s material abundance, good health, and mental wellbeing. This transition could be long and hard, but with the right attitude, you may be able to bring your wish to a successful realization.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The 3 Of Swords and The 9 Of Cups tarot cards together signify a tricky and emotionally challenging situation when it comes to matters of the heart. On one hand, the 3 Of Swords card shows that heartache and difficult times are ahead of you, be it from the end of a relationship, a divorce, or a deep-rooted disappointment. This loss or break in trust could cause you to feel depressed, anxious, and generally unhappy for a time.

However, the 9 Of Cups card offers you a glimpse of hope in the midst of the trying situation. It suggests that your wishes and desires can come true, and that despite any negative emotions or experiences currently surrounding your love life, there is much to look forward to on the horizon. Good health and material abundance may come your way, while issues of the heart could be resolved in a positive and uplifting way.

Ultimately, the two cards together represent that while there may be trials and tribulations to face in matters of the heart, there is a promise of better things to come. With patience, perseverance and a healthy dose of faith in yourself and in the universe, you may find that you come out of this situation loved, appreciated and rewarded for your efforts.

### What does 3 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together for your finances?

The combination of the 3 of Swords and the 9 of Cups tarot cards has a difficult but ultimately rewarding message for your finances and job. It indicates that although you will go through a difficult or painful experience, you will come out on the other side with financial abundance. This difficult experience could be a job loss, extended period of unemployment, or unexpected financial strain.

The 3 of Swords tarot card warns of challenging news and a heavy heart. You may feel discouraged or disheartened about your current financial or employment situation. The 3 of Swords implies that whatever is causing this heartache will soon end, as the 9 of Cups tarot card is a sign that wishes will come true, and new opportunities are ahead.

The 9 of Cups suggests that despite your current hardships, a positive outcome is in sight. This could be manifested as a new job, a raise, new investments, or any other financial fortuity. You may be in a period of transition and uncertainty now, but the 9 of Cups implies that better times are coming. This message of hope and appreciation for the bright future ahead is one of renewal and joy.

This card combination encourages you to have faith and trust that things will get better. With positive thoughts and effort, you will find the resources to achieve financial abundance.

### What does 3 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean together for your health?

When looking at the combined message of The 3 Of Swords and The 9 Of Cups tarot cards, it can be seen as a journey of healing and recovery. This suggests that while a heartache, divorce, and loss may have been experienced, there is the potential to move forward and find the strength to heal. This means exploring ways to dig deeper into one’s own soul, getting to the root of any emotional or spiritual issues, and finding new ways to cope with the pain and depression that may arise from such experiences.

This is where The 9 of Cups comes in. By exploring the positive energy from this card, wish and abundance can be achieved. This journey of healing can be supported through acknowledging and exploring new ways to be healthy, such as engaging in regular physical activity, eating healthily, and taking time out for yourself. Additionally, finding meaningful and fulfilling relationships, such as those with friends, family, and spiritual practices, can support and nourish the soul.

In addition, this card combination could suggest the possibility of having to undergo surgery to physically heal the body. This could come with a range of emotions, but revisiting the message of the 9 of Cups could help to focus on the outcome and provide a sense of comfort and hope. This suggests that all is not lost, and recovery and healing, both physically and emotionally, is within reach.

Overall, these two tarot cards call for a physical and mental journey of healing and transformation. It is a reminder to allow yourself to explore new paths and possibilities, as there is abundance and hope waiting to be discovered.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 3 Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, the Three of Swords card could indicate possible surgery or some other form of health crisis that could lead to depression and other emotionally draining symptoms. It could also imply a severe loss or trauma that causes long-lasting physical or mental pain. When this card appears, it is possible for it to warn of a deep and long-term illness or injury and one should take it as a sign to take better care of one’s physical and mental health.

When it comes to finances, the Three of Swords Tarot card could indicate a tumultuous situation, such as a divorce or loss of a job, and it could be a sign that one should take precautions and be resourceful in managing one’s finances. This card signifies the potential for a great struggle in trying to stay afloat and it is important to accept the need for help if needed. It could also suggest seeking advice, both financial and legal, in such an instance.

When it comes to relationships, the Three of Swords Tarot card could signify a broken heart and disappointment. It could be a sign that someone has been hurt by the decisions of another and is now trying to cope with the feelings of grief and sorrow that the situation has caused. It could also indicate a need to let go of a painful situation and accept what is, in order to move on and move forward. In terms of new relationships, this card could point to the potential for disappointment and heartache if caution is not taken. It could also be an indication that a transition or a new beginning is necessary in order to create something better.

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

The 9 of Cups Tarot card is associated with a feeling of contentment and satisfaction. It signifies that wishes and desires have been fulfilled and that things are going in the right direction. This card is a positive omen of material abundance, good health, and strong relationships.

When it comes to health, the 9 of Cups Tarot card carries with it a message of being at ease with your body and being in a place of balanced energy. It suggests that overall good health is here, and that any physical ailments can be remedied with time and care. This card urges you to take the necessary steps to contribute to the maintenance of your health, such as eating well, getting adequate rest, and exercising regularly. It’s also an invitation to appreciate and honor your body.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Cups Tarot card predicts a period of material abundance. This card is a reminder to stay balanced with money, not to overspend, and to stay mindful of what is necessary. It is a sign of growth and success, so it invites you to use this newfound wealth responsibly to strengthen and ensure your long-term financial security.

When it comes to relationships, the 9 of Cups Tarot card is a sign of contentment, fulfillment and satisfaction. This card bodes well for relationships, suggesting that strong ties are being maintained and that partnerships are going through a positive period. With this card, it’s an invitation to nurture and treasure the relationships in your life, to communicate honestly and openly, and to continue investing in these connections.
