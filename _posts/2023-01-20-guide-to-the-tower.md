## The Tower – An Overview

The Tower tarot card symbolizes a period of unwanted and unexpected change. It can signify ruin, disgrace, upheaval and violence, often involving the loss of something important. The Tower brings the need for a radical shift in your life, often forcing you out of complacency and into a state of tumultuous yet necessary change. It can be a frightening card to encounter, as it hints at a period of chaos and uncertainty. However, often this change is necessary in order to progress and reach a higher level of spiritual awareness. One should not fear the Tower, but accept it as a sign that great transformation is coming and that it will ultimately lead to something better.

## Upright Meanings Of The Tower

### What It Means For Me

The Tower tarot card suggests that I may be facing some unwanted and unexpected changes that could lead to upheaval in my life. This could mean having to start from scratch after experiencing some kind of ruin or disgrace. I may feel that I have been dealt a difficult hand, and I may have to face some violence or aggression in order to make it through. The Tower is a warning to be prepared for the unexpected, as changes can happen quickly and without warning. It is a reminder to remain strong and resilient despite the difficulties that may come my way.

### What It Means For The Future

The Tower tarot card is a warning of a potentially difficult period in the near future. This card represents unexpected and unwanted change, ruin, upheaval, disaster, and even violence. It is a reminder to brace yourself for the unexpected, as it could come at any time and in any form. This card may also represent a difficult decision you have to make, one that will cause you to go against the grain and challenge the status quo. It is a sign that you should be prepared to face the consequences of your actions and be ready to accept the challenges that come your way. Although this card is a warning of potential destruction, it also carries with it hope, as it encourages you to have the courage and strength to face the challenges and make the hard decisions that could ultimately lead to a better future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Tower tarot card often signals a period of unwanted change, ruin, disgrace, upheaval, and violence for those close to me. This card warns of a sudden and unexpected event that will force me and those close to me to adjust to a new and difficult situation. This could manifest as a loss of a job, an illness, or even the end of a relationship. It is important that I be prepared to deal with the consequences of this upheaval and make sure that I am there to support those close to me in this time of change.
