## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 4 Of Pentacles mean?

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of greed and a desire for long-term security in all areas of life. This card speaks to our need to protect ourselves and our resources, particularly in the areas of health, finances, and relationships.

When it comes to health, the 4 of Pentacles Tarot card may indicate a need to practice good self-care. This could mean investing in health insurance, making a regular exercise routine, or setting aside money for medical bills. It could also mean stubbornly holding onto unhealthy habits out of fear that any change would be too great a risk. It’s important to remember that it is possible to strike a balance between responsibly taking care of our health and being careful with our resources.

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is a warning for financial concerns. Greed has no place in our finances, and a desire for security can lead to unhealthy spending. Money is important, and it should be taken care of. But it’s important to remember that it’s not necessarily good to hold onto it too tightly. Don’t forget to have fun with your money and treat yourself occasionally. You don’t necessarily have to hoard it.

The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is also a sign of a desire for security in relationships. We want to feel secure in the people we love and trust, but this card may warn us to not be too controlling. It is important to remember that relationships are a two-way street, and in order for them to flourish, both parties must be willing to compromise and make sacrifices. A healthy relationship requires both parties to be open to change and growth, and it may be necessary to let go of some attachments in order to have a safe, loving relationship.

Overall, The 4 of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that security is important, especially in health, finances, and relationships. However, it is also important to strike a balance between security and openness. Don’t be too fearful of change or too greedy with your resources, but instead practice a healthy level of self-care and caution.
