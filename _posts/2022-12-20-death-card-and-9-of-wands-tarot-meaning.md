A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Death card and 9 Of Wands card together in particular.

The Death card and 9 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Death and 9 Of Wands mean together?

The Death tarot card and The 9 Of Wands tarot card together speak to a time of great transformation and transition. The Death card encourages us to let go of the old and embrace the new and the 9 of Wands suggests that this transition will be difficult and require us to maintain our boundaries and show perseverance. This could be a difficult time in our lives, but it is also a time of rebirth and great potential for our growth and evolution. The challenge with the Death card is to embrace the idea of letting go and allowing ourselves to move forward into a new and improved life. Meanwhile, the 9 of Wands informs us to maintain our boundaries and stay focused on our goals despite the difficult times and transition.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Death and 9 Of Wands mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Death and 9 Of Wands the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It is a reminder to pay attention to your diet, physical activity, rest and relaxation, and overall wellbeing. This card could also signify transitions in your health, like a diagnosis of a chronic or terminal illness, or a health-related death in your family or circle of friends.

Finances: The Death card could be a sign to take a look at your financial situation and make any necessary changes. It could mean it is time to reduce your debt, create a budget, and stick to it. It might also be a sign that it is time to let go of an unsuccessful business venture and make a new financial plan. It could signify a major life change financially, such as a job loss, an inheritance, or an investment opportunity. This card may also indicate a necessary shift in attitude towards money, whether it be to spend less, save more, or learn to be more mindful of how you can achieve financial security.

Relationships: The Death card could be a reminder to take a look at your relationships, especially ones that may have been neglected. It may be a sign that it is time to let go of unhealthy relationships or to take a break from them. It could also signify the end of certain relationships or the beginning of new ones. This card could also be a reminder to work on your personal relationships, whether it is connecting better with family or friends, or learning to be more compassionate and understanding with others. This card could mean taking a closer look at how you tend to your relationships so you can be open to possibilities that could help you grow.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

Health: The Nine of Wands tarot card is associated with maintaining one’s health. It speaks to a commitment to stay balanced and well in order to achieve long-term health and vitality. It serves as a reminder to take care of yourself, stay active, and make wise choices when it comes to diet and exercise. It is also a sign to be mindful of fatigue, stress, and other physical manifestations of burnout that can be detrimental to health.

Finances: The Nine of Wands tarot card speaks to building and maintaining financial stability. It reminds us not to let emotions or irrational behavior cloud our judgement when it comes to money. This card encourages us to set and maintain boundaries when it comes to our financial life. Making decisions based on careful consideration of long-term goals and a realistic approach to our finances is key. It also serves as a reminder to stay focused on our goals and be mindful of expenses.

Relationships: The Nine of Wands tarot card speaks to the importance of setting and maintaining personal boundaries in our relationships. It reminds us to be mindful of our energy and how we use it in our interactions with others. This card encourages us to be thoughtful in our interactions and to be kind and respectful when engaging with others. It also serves as a reminder to stay true to ourselves, to be assertive with our requirements, and to remain true to our values and beliefs.
