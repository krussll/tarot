A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and The Wheel Of Fortune card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and The Wheel Of Fortune have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and The Wheel Of Fortune mean together?

The Hermit and the Wheel of Fortune tarot cards mean that you should take a journey to find the answers you seek within yourself. Your journey will be filled with luck and good fortune, as the Wheel of Fortune brings opportunities for you to make a change in your life. Through this journey, you will discover personal truth and gain spiritual illumination. The Hermit and Wheel of Fortune cards bring a message to open your mind and be ready to take on the world with optimism and courage. Take a chance and be ready to use your newfound luck and clarity to make positive changes in your life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and The Wheel Of Fortune mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

When looking at the Hermit tarot card, it can be seen as a reminder to take time for yourself and to nurture your health. The Hermit tarot card is about going within for clarity, a quest for personal truth, and spiritual illumination. In terms of health, it encourages you to take time to assess your wellbeing, both mentally and physically. It can be a reminder to focus on self-care and to take stock of any changes like diet, exercise, or lifestyle habits that will improve your overall health and wellbeing.

Finances

The Hermit tarot card also has a lot to say about finances. It is a reminder to slow down and take a step back to evaluate and assess your current financial situation. It can be a symbol of saving money and being wise with your spending, as well as a sign that you should build strong financial foundations. The Hermit tarot card encourages you to think carefully and make smart decisions about your finances. It also warns that taking risks may lead to costly mistakes, and advises you to be patient and to wait for the right opportunities.

Relationships

When it comes to relationships, the Hermit card suggests taking time for yourself and creating space to reflect and reassess your current relationships. It is about taking a step back and looking for clarity and understanding in your current relationships, or potential relationships. It can also be a reminder to be honest and open with your loved ones, to express your feelings and needs, and to make sure that both sides are heard. The Hermit tarot card is a reminder to take the time to build deep and meaningful connections, and to prioritize self-care while doing so.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is an important symbol of good fortune and luck, and its meaning can be applicable to all areas of life.

When it comes to our health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a reminder that our health can turn in an instant. Whether it’s for better or for worse, it’s ultimately up to you. This card symbolizes the luck and chance that is part of life, and encourages us to take risks and make changes in order to improve our health.

In terms of finances, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be seen as a sign that hard work and smart decisions can lead to financial stability and even abundance. It is a reminder that our finances can change with the turn of a wheel and that, with the right attitude and a bit of luck, we can enjoy success and prosperity.

When it comes to relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card encourages us to take risks and make changes in order to get the most out of our relationships. As with health and finances, relationships require effort, but they also can bring great rewards if we are willing to embrace change and opportunity. The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card can be seen as a sign that our relationships can go through significant change and that we have the potential to achieve whatever we desire.

All in all, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card symbolizes the idea that life is full of ups and downs, but that our fortunes can improve with hard work, smart decision-making, and a bit of luck. It is a reminder to remain open to opportunities and to embrace change.
