Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The 9 Of Swords card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 9 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 9 Of Swords.

## 9 Of Swords And Career And Finances – An Overview

The 9 of Swords tarot card is a powerful symbol of psychological distress and emotional turmoil.

It often signifies a deep sense of distress, fear, and anxiety.

It could represent sadness, grief, or depression, as well as nightmares, intrusive thoughts, and the inability to cope.

In terms of physical health, the 9 of Swords tarot card might suggest that the body is suffering due to the emotional distress, leading to physical ailments such as headaches, insomnia, and an inability to concentrate.

It is important to take time to care for the mind and body when dealing with the psychological distress and depression associated with the 9 of Swords.

Meditation, yoga, and counseling may help to alleviate some of the symptoms and restore balance.

## Upright Meanings For 9 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The 9 of Swords tarot card is a warning that difficult times may be looming ahead for your career and finances.

It suggests that you may be facing anxiousness and depression from stress and worries about your current situation.

This card can also hint at nightmares you may be having about your future, which can lead to even more stress and anxiety.

It is important to remember that this card does not indicate a lack of success, but rather an opportunity to work through these difficult times and emerge as a stronger and more financially stable person.

With patience, resilience, and perseverance, you can overcome the obstacles that stand in your way and eventually reach your goals.

### What It Means For The Future

The 9 of Swords tarot card is a warning sign of potential future difficulties in both career and finances.

It suggests that anxiety, depression, and grief may be lurking in the shadows of your future career and financial pursuits.

It is a reminder to take precautions when making decisions and to stay vigilant to protect yourself from potential pitfalls.

You may need to take a step back and re-evaluate your goals and strategies in order to ensure success in the long term.

By being mindful of the difficulties you may face, you can set yourself up for success and financial stability in the future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 9 of Swords tarot card symbolizes a difficult time of intense mental anguish and distress regarding a person’s career and finances.

It can indicate that a loved one is suffering from nightmares, anxiety, grief, and depression related to their work or financial situation.

This card can be a sign that they need help and support to overcome the challenges that are preventing them from achieving success.

It can also represent a warning that they should be careful with their investments and decisions as they may have a negative impact on their future.

Ultimately, it implies that they could benefit from guidance and assistance in order to tackle their career and financial problems.

## Reversed Meanings For 9 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

When The 9 of Swords is reversed, it suggests that you are ready to move forward in your career and finances.

You are not bogged down with worries and anxieties, and instead, you are motivated and willing to take risks to see results.

You are beginning to trust yourself and your decisions, and you are no longer letting fear or depression hold you back.

The reversed 9 of Swords indicates that you are now able to see solutions where before you only saw problems.

You are able to trust yourself and stand up for yourself, and you are no longer being held back by your negative thoughts.

Your finances are likely to improve as well, as you become more confident in your ability to make money and make wise investments.

You are also beginning to see the value in taking calculated risks that could bring you closer to success.

You can trust your instincts and your judgement to make the right choices, and you are no longer letting fear keep you from achieving your goals.

With the reversed 9 of Swords, you can finally break free from the fears and anxieties that have been holding you back and start to make progress towards your goals.

When the reversed 9 of Swords appears in a Tarot reading, it can be an indication that a period of anxiety, depression, and grief is coming to an end.
