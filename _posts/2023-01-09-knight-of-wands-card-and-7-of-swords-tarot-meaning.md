However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Knight Of Wands card and 7 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Knight Of Wands card and 7 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Knight Of Wands and 7 Of Swords mean together?

The Knight of Wands tarot card and the 7 of Swords tarot card together suggest that you may be feeling creative and free-spirited but that this could be clouded by feelings of betrayal, dishonesty, and theft. You may need to take a moment to assess what is yours and what is not. You could be experiencing trouble trusting those around you, but don’t let that stop your creativity. It is important to be aware of the temptation of running away rather than facing the consequences of your actions.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Knight Of Wands mean?

Health

When it comes to health, the Knight of Wands Tarot card represents vitality, agility and the willpower to stay active and healthy. The knight’s action and enthusiasm for life reflects the need to get out and experience the world and its beauty. This can be taken to mean that it’s important to keep ourselves fit and healthy with regular exercise and a balanced diet, while also engaging with friends, family and the outdoors. The Knight of Wands can also represent the connection between health and spirituality, as well as the importance of taking time to reflect on our own individual journey.

Finances

The Knight of Wands Tarot card is a great card to bring into a finances reading. This card is all about taking risk and being bold and confident in our business decisions. It encourages expansive thinking and willingness to explore new ideas and opportunities. The Knight of Wands is not about recklessness but about striking a balance between measured risk and potential reward. While being open to new ideas and adventures, it’s also important to remember to be mindful of our spending. The Knight of Wands is an indicator that if we are brave, creative and take calculated risks, we may find financial abundance and success.

The Knight of Wands Tarot card can be a great card to bring into a relationships reading. It can often indicate that a relationship has potential for growth and new life. The Knight encourages boldness and the willingness to take chances, and so in relationships it can mean pushing boundaries and taking risks. It may also be an indicator that a relationship needs a bit of spice and excitement, so it be a good idea to think of ways to reinvigorate it and bring in some fresh energy. On the other hand, this card can also represent the need to take a step back and to reassess your own needs and goals. Whatever the outcome, the Knight of Wands is an optimistic card that can open up new possibilities and pathways for a relationship to grow and evolve.

## What does 7 Of Swords mean?

The 7 of Swords Tarot card usually brings news of theft, betrayal, dishonesty, or running away. It typically indicates a situation where one party has taken advantage of or deceived another. Consequently, when considering what this card means for health, finances, and relationships, it is important to take these aspects of the card into account.

In terms of health, the 7 of Swords Tarot card suggests that someone may be taking advantage of your physical or emotional wellbeing. This could take the form of someone promising a medical treatment or remedy that they are not delivering, or could be more subtle, such as a relationship in which one partner is constantly taking advantage of the other’s emotional or physical resources without any intention of giving anything in return. The card may also indicate a situation where a person is running away from their own health problems, either by ignoring them or avoiding the necessary steps to resolve them.

When it comes to finances, the 7 of Swords Tarot card can act as a warning that the person is facing some kind of financial betrayal. This may be in the form of a loan default, a scam, or a breach of a financial agreement. It can also indicate a situation where someone is running away from their financial obligations, such as not paying their taxes or bills on time.

In relationships, the 7 of Swords Tarot card often signals a situation of betrayal, deception, or someone running away from their responsibilities or commitments. This could be someone in a relationship who is not being honest or is taking emotional or financial advantage of their partner. It could also mean someone running away from a relationship, not facing up to their responsibilities or breaking promises they have made.

Overall, The 7 of Swords Tarot card speaks of theft, betrayal, dishonesty, and running away, and is a card to be taken seriously when considering its implications for health, finance, and relationships.
