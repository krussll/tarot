However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The The Hanged Man card and 3 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and 3 Of Wands mean together?

The Hanged Man and The 3 Of Wands tarot cards together suggest the need for teamwork and collaboration to gain wisdom from a different perspective. This could be in terms of expanding a business, making a long-distance journey, or working with others to gain a new insight into a situation. Self-sacrifice is key in any endeavour, and this could be in terms of giving up personal time or energy to contribute to the team. The combination of these two cards indicates that working together and getting a different angle can bring greater rewards and understanding.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

The Hanged Man Tarot card is a symbol of transformation, suspended between two worlds, and in this way, it is representative of the potential for growth in all aspects of life. For health, The Hanged Man encourages us to step back and take a break from our connected lives in order to gain perspective and gather strength. This can manifest in physical healing as well, such as getting enough rest, eating well, and avoiding harmful substances. In terms of finances, The Hanged Man calls us to consider if our decisions will benefit us in the long run. It cautions us against impulsiveness, and urges us to take the time to understand our investments and prioritize our financial goals. On a deeper level, The Hanged Man invites us to question our own motivations and the values we prioritize.

In the context of relationships, The Hanged Man invites us to pause and reflect on how we interact with the people in our lives. It encourages us to pause and consider how our words and actions affect others. Ultimately, it allows us to step back, understand a different perspective, and act with compassion and understanding. The Hanged Man also challenges us to take responsibility for our actions. It asks us to consider how our words and actions affect others, and to choose our words and actions with care and consideration. By taking the time to pause and reflect on our actions, we can create healthier, more sustainable relationships with those we care about.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The Three of Wands Tarot card symbolizes a sense of balance and health. It indicates that a person is living a healthy life, with a balanced diet, an active lifestyle, and healthy relationships. It can also signify physical strength and vitality, and suggest that a person is taking care of and nurturing their body, mind and spirit.

Finances  
The Three of Wands Tarot card suggests that a person is engaging in business and financial dealings that are mutually beneficial to all involved. It speaks to balanced and healthy strategies for strengthening finances, such as investments, loan negotiations, and foreign commerce. This card also signifies new opportunities for growth, particularly if a person is willing to take risks and explore new avenues.

Relationships  
The Three of Wands Tarot card speaks to the strength of relationships. It suggests that partnerships, friendships and romantic relationships are mutually beneficial. It speaks to the balance of giving and receiving, as well as having a sense of appreciation for one another. This card signifies that individuals are taking time to nurture their relationships, travel, and explore the world together. It’s also a sign that there is respect and trust in the relationship, and that communication is both honest and open.
