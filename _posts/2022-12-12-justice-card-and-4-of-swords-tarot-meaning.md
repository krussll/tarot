A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Justice card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The Justice tarot card and the 4 Of Swords tarot card together signify a need to take a step back, step away from the chaos, and focus on recovery. In this difficult time, it’s important to create a situation that works out for everyone, and to find the truth through collective effort. Taking a break from the hustle and bustle is essential to finding the win-win situation, and solitude can be a powerful tool of renewal. After the necessary rest and recharging, you can come out refreshed, informed, and ready to seek the truth that will bring a resolution to the matter.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and 4 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Justice mean?

It points to making decisions that will bring about the best outcomes in terms of health and wellbeing. It is an indication that taking a holistic approach to life, looking at all aspects, will bring balance and ultimately improved health. Financial balance and stability is also implied in this card, with wise decisions that will create a win-win situation and bring positive results.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card suggests being organized and disciplined when it comes to finances. It encourages us to take a long-term view when planning our finances, setting aside funds and investing wisely. It encourages us to be objective and honest when engaging in financial transactions, to ensure all parties are getting a fair deal. It also encourages us to look for win-win solutions and to be generous with our resources when appropriate.

Relationships: The Justice Tarot card encourages us to be balanced, fair and honest in our relationships. It suggests that we should strive for win-win solutions, allowing both parties to feel as though their needs have been taken into consideration. It implies that we should be open and honest with each other, allowing for clear communication that can bring about understanding and harmony. It suggests that we should take care to be fair and open minded, ensuring that our relationships are built on trust and respect.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Swords tarot card is associated with rest, renewal, and solitude. It is an indication that taking a break from your daily life to get some rest is needed. It can be difficult to take a break, but the 4 of Swords reminds us that it is necessary, not just for physical health, but for mental and emotional health as well. Taking a break can help us recharge and rejuvenate our minds, bodies and spirits, allowing us to come back to life with a renewed sense of purpose and enthusiasm.

Finances:  
The 4 of Swords card is also associated with financial solvency and stability. It’s an indication that now is the time to retreat and reassess your finances, looking for areas where you can cut back and improve your overall financial health. It’s also a reminder to be prudent when it comes to your investments and expenditures, as taking too many risks can lead to financial disaster. By taking a step back and looking at your finances from a more detached and objective perspective, you can make sounder decisions about how to best manage your money going forward.

Relationships:  
The 4 of Swords tarot card can also be seen as a reminder to step back from relationships and take a break to assess and reassess your emotional needs and wants. It’s important to take time to reflect on the strengths and weaknesses of your relationships and to create a plan to strengthen the former and eliminate the latter. Taking time away from your relationships can also help you gain clarity on any issues that need to be addressed or let go of. By taking a break and looking at your relationships with a clear head, you can find ways to create healthier, more vibrant and dynamic relationships with those closest to you.
