Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The Lovers card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The Lovers, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The Lovers.

## The Lovers And Health – An Overview

The Lovers tarot card is a symbol of a strong and loving partnership, and this can be extended to physical and mental health.

When we have a supportive partner or friend in our corner, it can boost our confidence and ability to take on any challenges.

It can also remind us that we are not alone and can lean on them for emotional and spiritual support.

Together, we can feel stronger and more resilient when times are tough.

The Lovers card also encourages us to make mindful choices about our health, both physically and emotionally.

We can use this card to remind ourselves that we are not alone in our journey and that we can take care of ourselves better when we come together as a team.

## Upright Meanings For The Lovers And Health

### What It Means For Me

The Lovers tarot card is a powerful symbol of health, as it speaks to the strength of two working together.

It reminds us of the importance of partnership in maintaining good physical and mental health.

Whether it is a partner, a friend, or a healthcare provider, having someone to talk to and lean on in times of need can make all the difference.

The Lovers card is a reminder that we don’t have to face our health struggles alone; rather, we can draw strength from the support of those around us, and make the difficult decisions that will lead to overall well-being with their help.

### What It Means For The Future

The Lovers tarot card indicates that partnership and deep love will play a major role in your future health.

This may refer to the strength that comes from having a partner to support you and encourage you in your journey to health and wellness.

The card also alludes to a choice you may need to make in order to improve your health.

This could be a choice to make a lifestyle change or to seek help from a professional.

The card emphasizes the power of two working together, reminding you that you don’t need to go through this journey alone.

With a supportive partner by your side, you will be able to achieve greater success in your health goals.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The Lovers tarot card is a reminder of the strength and power of partnership and deep love.

It suggests that those around me are connected in a way that can bring out the best in one another.

It is a reminder to nurture our relationships and to appreciate the unique combination of two people.

It encourages us to make conscious choices that are rooted in love, understanding and mutual respect.

It shows that our closest relationships can be a source of healing, growth and joy.

Ultimately, The Lovers card highlights the importance of nurturing the health of those close to me.

## Reversed Meanings For The Lovers And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed The Lovers tarot card often symbolizes a lack of balance in a person’s life.

It can indicate a person who is disconnected from their own body, or disconnected from the needs of their loved ones.

This can manifest in physical and mental health issues, such as stress, depression, and anxiety.

It can also be a sign of unhealthy relationships, where one partner has more power or control than the other, causing feelings of resentment and dissatisfaction.

Ultimately, the reversed The Lovers can indicate a person who needs to prioritize self-care, and to take a step back and assess their relationships.

It can be an opportunity to look at how their relationships affect their physical and mental health, and to make changes if needed.

It can also be a sign that it’s time to take a break and focus on self-care and healing.

This can include anything from regular exercise, to yoga, spending time with nature, or talking with a therapist.

It is important to remember that healing is a process, and it takes time and effort to reconnect with oneself and to restore balance and harmony.

### What It Means For The Future

When The Lovers tarot card is reversed, it could be an indication of potential problems in health.

The reversed card is a warning that a lack of harmony and balance between physical, mental, and emotional health could lead to difficulty.

The message is to pay attention to all these aspects of health.

It could also be a sign that the individual is not making healthy choices when it comes to their overall wellbeing.

This could mean anything from having a poor diet or exercising too much or too little, to neglecting mental health or not getting enough rest.

The reversed card could also indicate that the individual is denying themselves the healing they need.

It is important to pay attention to the warning of The Lovers reversed, and make sure that both physical and emotional health are being cared for.

Make sure to take care of yourself, and find balance in all areas of life.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When the reversed Lovers tarot card appears in a health reading, it can signify a lack of balance in one’s physical, emotional, and spiritual health.

This could indicate a feeling of being stuck in an unhealthy dynamic or a relationship that is not mutually beneficial.

It could also point to a tendency to neglect one’s own well-being in favor of the needs of others.

On a physical level, the reversed Lovers tarot card might indicate an imbalance between one’s own needs and those of one’s partner or family.

It could point to a lack of self-care, such as not eating well, not getting enough exercise, or not taking time for relaxation.

On an emotional and spiritual level, the reversed Lovers tarot card can indicate a disconnect between one’s inner voice and the needs of one’s partner or family.

It could also signify a lack of communication, leading to an unhealthy dynamic in the relationship.

The reversed Lovers tarot card is a reminder to take the time to assess your physical, emotional, and spiritual health and to re-establish balance in your life.

Make sure you are taking care of your own needs and that you are communicating openly and honestly with your partner or family.
