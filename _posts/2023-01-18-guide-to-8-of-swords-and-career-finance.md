Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for career and finances. Understanding what The 8 Of Swords card means in regards to your career and finances can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 8 Of Swords, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 8 Of Swords.

## 8 Of Swords And Career And Finances – An Overview

The 8 of Swords represents self-imposed restrictions and isolation when it comes to health.

It symbolizes that one’s physical and mental health can be negatively affected by keeping oneself isolated or restrained from the help of others.

It can be seen as a warning that neglecting our health or refusing to seek help can lead to long-term harm.

It can also indicate that we may be too strict with ourselves, causing us to feel trapped and limited.

This card encourages us to recognize when it is time to reach out for support and to take steps to make sure we are looking after our physical and mental health.

## Upright Meanings For 8 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The 8 of Swords tarot card is an indication that I may be limiting myself when it comes to my career and finances.

I may be feeling isolated and imprisoned in my current state of affairs, causing me to hold myself back from achieving the success I’m capable of.

It is a reminder to assess my current situation and make sure that I’m not allowing fear or worry to restrict me from taking the steps needed to move forward.

It could be that I need to take a risk, or take the time to reassess my finances and career goals, letting go of any self-imposed restrictions that are preventing me from reaching my potential.

### What It Means For The Future

The 8 of Swords tarot card suggests that my future career and finances may be marked by self-imposed restrictions.

This could mean that I am holding myself back by not taking the necessary risks required for financial and professional growth.

It could also indicate that I am isolating myself from opportunities, relationships, and resources that may be beneficial to my future success.

Alternatively, this card could be warning me to be aware of situations that may feel like a prison, such as a job that is unfulfilling or a financial situation that I feel trapped in.

The 8 of Swords indicates that I need to take a step back and evaluate the situation to ensure I am making the right decisions to create a positive and successful future.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 8 of Swords tarot card can be a sign of self-imposed restrictions in the careers and finances of those close to me.

It suggests that someone close to me is limiting themselves in their professional and financial capacities, possibly because of fear or uncertainty.

This could mean that those close to me are holding themselves back from taking risks or making bigger investments that could lead to greater rewards.

It may be a warning to encourage them to be more open-minded and to push past their self-imposed boundaries in order to create more financial stability and success in their lives.

## Reversed Meanings For 8 Of Swords And Career And Finances

### What It Means For Me

The reversed 8 of Swords in a reading about my career and finances can suggest that I am being held back by my own self-imposed limitations.

This could mean that I am not taking advantage of opportunities that could further my career and financial goals.

It could also mean that I am too concerned with what others think or too afraid to take risks that could propel me forward.

The reversed 8 of Swords can also suggest that I am stuck in a cycle of stagnation, unable to break free from a self-imposed prison of my own making.

It could mean that I am in need of a new perspective and an open mind to new possibilities.

It could also indicate that I need to take a step back and look at the bigger picture, and make changes that will benefit me in the long run.

With a bit of courage and self-belief, I may find that the solutions to my career and financial challenges are closer than I think.

### What It Means For The Future

When The 8 Of Swords tarot card is reversed, it suggests that self-imposed restrictions and feelings of isolation are being lifted.

These can be related to career and finances, suggesting that you will soon experience a period of freedom and liberation.

It is likely that your career and finances will start to improve, and you will be able to make decisions that are right for your future.

You may find that your career options open up, or that you can start to make more money through investments.

It is important to remember that while this card symbolizes freedom, it still requires hard work and dedication to make your dreams a reality.

This card also suggests that you may be able to find balance between your professional career and personal life.

You may be able to start finding joy in what you do for a living, and also enjoy having a healthy balance of work and play.

This reversed card suggests that you can finally start making decisions based on what is best for you in the long-term and that you will be able to make progress financially.

With the help of this card, you can start to be more confident in your abilities and trust in your decisions.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The reversed 8 of Swords tarot card suggests that those close to me may be able to break free of the self-imposed restrictions, isolation, and imprisonment they have been facing in their career and finances.

This card may indicate that they are ready to take risks and make bold decisions in order to move forward and achieve their goals.

It may also signify that they are open to new opportunities and are willing to take calculated risks in order to make decisions that will benefit their financial and career future.

The reversed 8 of Swords suggests that they may be able to break free from their current financial and career struggles and can start to make progress towards their desired future.

It could indicate that they are taking steps to make positive changes in their life and are open to advice and support from those around them.

They may also be able to learn from past mistakes and take a fresh approach to their situation.

This card can signal a period of growth and positive change for those close to me in terms of their career and finances.
