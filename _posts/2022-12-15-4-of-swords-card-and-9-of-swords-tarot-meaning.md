A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 4 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The 4 Of Swords and the 9 Of Swords tarot cards together mean the need for mental, emotional, and physical recovery from difficult times. While The 4 Of Swords suggests turning inward to nurture our own energy and spirit, The 9 Of Swords symbolizes confronting and dealing with our fear, depression, and anxiety – so that we can find peace and solace in our own hearts. By retreating and taking the time to rest and restore, we can bring ourselves back to a healthier and more balanced state of being.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 4 of Swords suggests that a period of solitude and recovery will be beneficial and necessary to rebuild strength. This could be leaving an unhealthy relationship or taking a break from the stress and emotions of the situation, both of which would be beneficial to the individual’s well-being.

The 9 of Swords could represent deep-rooted anxieties and fears that the person has been dealing with. This could be the result of a bad breakup, the fear of commitment, or even underlying feelings of guilt or regret which need to be addressed. Taking the time to seek help or therapy, to work through any trauma or pain that might be hindering a person’s love life, will be essential.

The combination of the 4 of Swords and the 9 of Swords tarot card signals that it is time for the individual to take a step back and look at the situation they are in. It is a reminder to take care of oneself and to focus on healing and renewal. While it might seem daunting, it is a necessary step in order to move forward and ultimately find the inner peace and strength to continue with a healthy love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Swords and 9 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

The 4 of Swords Tarot card suggests a period of rest, retreat and renewal. This card also encourages a focus on health and wellbeing. It is a time to retreat from your surroundings and become mindful of your physical, emotional and spiritual needs. Take some time out to reflect on your life, practice self-care and rest.

When it comes to finances, The 4 of Swords Tarot card suggests that it is time to take a break from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. Consider the long-term and look for ways to boost savings, prioritize debt, and find budget-friendly solutions. Use this time to organize and plan for upcoming financial goals, such as investments or a retirement plan.

In terms of relationships, The 4 of Swords Tarot card encourages a period of contemplation. Consider your interactions with friends, family and partners, and think about the positive and negative aspects of each relationship. Use this time to withdraw and reflect on how your relationships can be improved. Consider making changes in your own behavior, practices and lifestyle to improve your relationships.

Overall, The 4 of Swords Tarot card encourages a time of reflection, recuperation and rest. During this period, you can focus on taking care of your health, planning for long-term financial goals and improving relationships. Consider setting aside time to step away from the busyness of life, reconnect with yourself and renew your energy.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, the 9 of Swords tarot card is a sign of mental exhaustion, and it can be a warning sign of a breakdown if caution is not taken. Physical symptoms of depression, such as reduced energy levels, sleeping problems, and a general loss of interest in life may accompany this card. It can also indicate a feeling of being overwhelmed and that the individual may need to take a break from their obligations in order to properly recuperate.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Swords tarot card can point to unwise decisions, recklessness, and overspending due to anxiety and depression. There may also be worries surrounding money, payments, and other financial matters. It may be wise to seek professional advice and take a close look at one’s budget to make sure it is on track. It is also important to take a step back and analyse any decisions that may have been made too quickly.

With relationships, the 9 of Swords tarot card indicates difficulties in communication and understanding. Anxiety, depression, and excessive worry can lead to a breakdown in communication and difficulty in forming relationships. It can also indicate a lack of trust and an inability to make decisions in relationships. It is important to address any issues before they become unmanageable, and to try and find ways to improve communication and understanding. It is also important to not let worries and anxiety get in the way of the relationships you value.
