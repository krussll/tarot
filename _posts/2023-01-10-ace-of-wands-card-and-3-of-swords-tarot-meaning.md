## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Ace Of Wands mean?

The Ace Of Wands Tarot card is an indication of growth, new beginnings, and the potential for something spectacular. It can be interpreted as the beginning of a new project, a business venture, an exciting endeavor, an opportunity for creativity, and an inspiration to chase one’s dreams. In matters of health and wellbeing, the Ace Of Wands Tarot card can suggest that it is time to look forward to a healthier lifestyle, perhaps through a new diet, exercise routine, or other lifestyle changes geared towards improving one’s physical and mental wellbeing.

In terms of finance, the Ace Of Wands Tarot card suggests the possibility of a windfall coming your way, either through a business venture or creative endeavor. The card may indicate that it is the perfect time to embark on a new financial venture, one that is likely to be long-term and rewarding. It could also be a reminder to pay attention to the small details, as this is often a key factor for financial success.

In matters of relationships, the Ace Of Wands Tarot card can be an indication that there is new potential for starting a relationship, or reigniting the passion in an existing one. ## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

The Three of Swords Tarot card is an indicator of heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression and surgery. It is a card that can indicate a time of deep sorrow, pain, heartache and betrayal.

For health, the Three of Swords emphasizes both physical and emotional well-being. It may indicate that a major medical surgery is coming up, or that a person is in a severe state of depression. The Three of Swords can also be a warning against ignoring the signs of physical distress and neglecting to attend to one’s own health.

In terms of finances, the Three of Swords can be a sign of debt and financial hardship. It may suggest that someone is facing the prospect of bankruptcy, or that a major loss has occurred. This card also can indicate that a person might be facing financial ruin due to deceptive practices.

The Three of Swords can also be a warning about the difficulties encountered in relationships. It may suggest that someone is in the midst of a difficult break up or that a person’s loved one is in a state of deep depression. This card can also show the deep feelings of betrayal that may arise in relationships. Because of its connotations of heartbreak and loss, this card can indicate a situation where communication has broken down and relationships are strained.

Overall, the Three of Swords is a card that can have far-reaching implications, whether it is health, finances or relationships. It is a card that warns against the potential consequences of ignoring signs of distress and the difficulties encountered in relationships. It is a card that suggests that, while sorrow and loss are inevitable, the process of overcoming them can lead to a sense of healing and resolution.
