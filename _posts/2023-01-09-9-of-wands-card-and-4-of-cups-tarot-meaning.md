A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 9 Of Wands card and 4 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Wands and 4 Of Cups mean together?

The 9 of Wands and the 4 of Cups together can be a challenging combination to understand. The message of this pair is telling you to be cautious and pay attention to your boundaries, yet to also open yourself up to new opportunities, even if you may not feel particularly enthusiastic about them. You must remain strong and assertive to prevent your boundaries from being violated and stay focused on staying true to your values and beliefs. Yet, don’t close yourself off completely to unexpected surprises and possibilities. If you can learn to balance these two energies and adjust as needed, you can navigate this tricky situation and find new and fulfilling paths in life.

It is important to remember that you have the power to make decisions and to set healthy boundaries. You need to be mindful of the physical, emotional, and mental energy you are expending, and to discern the situations that require you to stand firm and those that can be gently resolved.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

**Both 9 Of Wands and 4 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Wands and 4 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

The Nine of Wands Tarot card is a sign of protection, insight, caution and perseverance. It brings with it the message that one should remain alert and wary of their environment and those around them. In health, The Nine of Wands could be a sign of recovery from illness or injury, and a reminder to take the necessary steps to remain healthy such as diet and exercise. Financially, it could signify that the current economic trends are not in one’s favor and that one should be wary of making risky investments. It could also be a sign of hard work and patience paying off, and thus caution should be taken when spending or taking risks.

In relationships, The Nine of Wands encourages one to maintain their boundaries and to stand up for themselves in times of conflict. This card could indicate that a person is holding on too tightly to things that are not in their control, or it might be a reminder to stay persistent and keep pushing forward when faced with adversity. It could also symbolize that patience is needed in difficult relationships, as a situation may take time to resolve itself. Overall, the Nine of Wands Tarot card is a sign of protection and perseverance, which can be beneficial in all areas of one’s life.

## What does 4 Of Cups mean?

Health

The Four of Cups Tarot card can represent a stagnant period in regards to health. This can take the form of feeling listless and tired, or a lack of motivation and energy to take care of yourself. On one hand, it can symbolize a need to pause and take time out to focus on yourself, but it can also indicate a health issue hindering your progress.

Finances

The Four of Cups Tarot card can signify a period of unfulfillment when it comes to money. It can represent a plateau in progress, or reveal that your spending has been imbalanced. It can also mean that there is an unexpected windfall that can serve to revitalize or re-energize your finances.

Relationships

The Four of Cups Tarot card can suggest that relationships need to be re-evaluated and reassessed. It can indicate a lack of enthusiasm from you or the other person, or that it is time to mix things up in order to rekindle a spark. It can also signal a surprise invitation or offer that can open up new possibilities and opportunities.
