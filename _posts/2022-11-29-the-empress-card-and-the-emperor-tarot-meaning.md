A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Empress card and The Emperor card together in particular.

The The Empress card and The Emperor have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Empress and The Emperor mean together?

The Empress tarot card and The Emperor tarot card together represent fertility, creation and abundance. The Empress is associated with pregnancy and motherhood, while The Emperor is associated with fatherhood and leadership. Together, they represent the perfect balance of energy and power necessary for successful creation. In terms of health, this combination indicates that you are in a good place physically and that your relationship is strong and supportive. You are able to take care of yourself and your loved ones, and there is plenty of room for growth and expansion. In terms of your relationship, this combination indicates that you have a strong foundation on which to build. You are able to give and receive love freely, and you have the respect and support of your partner. You are able to work together towards common goals, and you are able to provide for each other emotionally and physically. This is a very positive combination for your health and your relationship, and it indicates that you are on the right track.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Empress and The Emperor mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Empress and The Emperor the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Empress mean?

For health, The Empress Tarot card means that you are in a good place physically. You have vitality and energy, and you are able to create and sustain new life. You are fertile and can bring new things into the world. You have the ability to be a good parent, and to create a stable, loving home.

For finances, The Empress Tarot card means that you are in a good place financially. You have the ability to create and sustain abundance. You are fertile and can bring new things into the world. You have the ability to be a good provider, and to create a stable, prosperous home.

For relationships, The Empress Tarot card means that you are in a good place emotionally. You have the ability to create and sustain relationships. You are loving and caring, and you have the ability to be a good partner. You are also able to create a stable, harmonious home.

## What does The Emperor mean?

In health, The Emperor Tarot card suggests that you take a proactive and strategic approach to maintaining your health. You may need to make some tough choices in order to stay on track, but you are up to the challenge. You are a natural leader, and your example can inspire others to make healthier choices as well.

In relationships, The Emperor Tarot card suggests that you take a firm and authoritative stance. You are a natural leader, and your partner will look to you for guidance and direction. You are able to create a stable and secure relationship by using your wisdom and experience.

In finances, The Emperor Tarot card suggests that you take a long-term view of your finances. You are able to make sound investment decisions and create a solid financial foundation. You may need to tighten your belt at times, but you will ultimately be able to achieve your financial goals.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The Empress and The Emperor has helped you.
