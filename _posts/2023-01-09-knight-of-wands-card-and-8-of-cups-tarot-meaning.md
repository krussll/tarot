## What does Knight Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean together?

It may be time to seek a new direction and make a fresh start, either emotionally or spiritually. It could be that you need to move on from an old habit or project and withdraw, retreating from something that no longer serves you. This is not necessarily a bad thing; it could be an opportunity to let go, to re-evaluate your direction, and make sure you are on the right path.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Knight Of Wands and 8 Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

Overall, the Knight of Wands tarot card is indicative of a person who is passionate, full of energy and willing to take risks when it comes to money, health and relationships. This tarot card encourages you to make the most of your energy and resources and be courageous in pursuit of your goals.

## What does 8 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 8 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a need for withdrawal or retreat from health matters. This could be physical and/or mental health. The 8 of Cups Tarot card suggests retreat and/or abandonment of overly active or unhealthy coping mechanisms. This could mean avoiding unhealthy habits like eating junk food, excessive drinking, smoking or doing drugs. This card can also signify taking a break from a medical regimen or exercise routine that has become too tedious or overly stressful.

Finances

The 8 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the need to withdraw or retreat from financial matters. This could be a sign of avoiding or stopping certain financial practices that may be hurting your finances. This card suggests that it is time to reevaluate your financial situation, look for areas where you could be wasting money, and be more mindful of your spending habits. It also suggests that it may be time to move on from high risk investments or projects that have yet to give you any returns and focus on more stable and secure options.

Relationships

The 8 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the need to withdraw or retreat from relationships. This could mean taking time for yourself and emotionally disconnecting from the people and relationships around you. It can also signify that it may be time to reevaluate the relationships around you and decide if they are truly beneficial or if it is time to move on. This card can also suggest that it may be time to distance yourself from people or situations that you feel are draining your energy and causing emotional pain.
