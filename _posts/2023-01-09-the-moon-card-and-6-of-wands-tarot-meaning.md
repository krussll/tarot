However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Moon card and 6 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Moon card and 6 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

Trust your intuition and have faith in yourself to make the right decisions, as the combination of these two cards shows that your efforts will be rewarded.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Moon and 6 Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does The Moon mean?

Health: The Moon tarot card can be a sign that you are facing a situation that is unseen to you, yet is nonetheless having a large influence on your health. This could be due to an underlying physical or mental health issue that you have not yet identified, or even something more spiritual. You may need to trust your intuition and take a closer look at your health, including your diet, habits, and lifestyle, to uncover the source of these hidden problems. This can be an uncomfortable process, but making changes in the right direction can ultimately lead to improved health.

Finances: The Moon tarot card may indicate that something is out of balance in your finances, and that it is time to make some voluntary changes. This could mean taking a hard look at your money habits, such as budgeting or investing, as well as questioning where your money is going and why. You may need to make a commitment to making smarter financial decisions in order to take control of your money and ensure a more secure financial future.

Relationships: The Moon tarot card can suggest that something is amiss in your relationships, but you may be unaware of it. It is important to trust your intuition and not ignore the signs, no matter how uncomfortable it may make you. Doing some self-reflection can help you to identify any issues that may be causing problems, and then taking the appropriate steps to address them. This may include communicating more openly, seeking outside help, or even making changes to your behavior. In the end, these voluntary changes can lead to more secure and healthier relationships.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of success, recognition, and good news. The card itself often shows a person on a horse, with other people looking up to them with admiration. It is a sign that you have achieved something great and are being rewarded for it.

In terms of health, the card is a sign to be grateful and recognize the hard work that led to improved wellbeing. The 6 of Wands is a reminder to celebrate all the small victories on your wellness journey, as they can often be just as important in the long run. It could also be a sign that you are about to receive some recognition for the progress you have been making, such as receiving an award from a professional organization.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Wands signifies a time of rewards. This could mean a raise, a bonus, or a gift, but it is likely to be something that you have been working for for some time. It is a sign to be grateful for the opportunities presented and to continue striving for success.

Finally, in relationships, the 6 of Wands is a symbol of appreciation and recognition. It may mean that you have been doing something right and your hard work is being noticed by your friends, family or partner. It could also mean that you are getting recognition or validation from someone important in your life and it is a sign to accept the appreciation gracefully and enjoy the moment.
