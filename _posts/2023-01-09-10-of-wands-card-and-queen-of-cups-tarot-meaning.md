A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Wands card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The 10 Of Wands card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Wands and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The combination of The 10 of Wands and The Queen of Cups Tarot card can represent a situation where a nurturing person is trying to help alleviate the feeling of stress, exhaustion, and too many burdens. This person is providing healing, support, and compassion to help ease your distress and help you be able to manage your burdens more effectively. They bring comfort and understanding through their presence and support, and enable you to move forward with greater ease and less worry. This person wants to help you succeed and is offering their assistance through sincere caring and problem-solving skills.

There may be times when it is necessary to take a break from the daily demands and pressures in order to recharge. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 10 Of Wands and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 10 Of Wands and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 10 Of Wands mean?

Health  
The 10 Of Wands Tarot card is indicative of a situation in which someone has taken on too much. An individual in this situation may be feeling overwhelmed and exhausted, leading to physical and mental health problems. Stressful situations can lead to physical problems like headaches, muscle aches, and other illnesses caused by too much strain on the body. It can also lead to mental health issues such as depression, anxiety, and insomnia. By recognizing when one is carrying too many burdens, one can take steps to reduce their stress level and prevent these health-related issues.

Finances  
The 10 Of Wands Tarot card can also be a warning sign that finances are taking too great a toll on someone’s life. Taking on too many financial burdens can be overwhelming and can lead to financial problems. It can be difficult to pay bills, save for the future, pay for necessary items, and still be able to enjoy life. Taking too much on financially can create a cascading series of financial troubles, making it even harder to get out of an already precarious situation.

Relationships  
The 10 Of Wands Tarot card can point to an individual taking on too much in their relationships as well. An individual may be trying to please others or care for others and is feeling overburdened as a result. This can create stress in the relationship and can be a sign of emotional exhaustion. It is important to recognize when too much is being taken on in a relationship and to take steps to reduce the amount of strain. This can help to foster a healthier, more rewarding relationship.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

The Queen of Cups Tarot card symbolizes a nurturing figure in health, finances, and relationships. It is an indication that a person is gentle, compassionate, and understanding. It also represents a person who is a natural healer, giver of emotional support, and possesses great inner wisdom.

Health: The Queen of Cups Tarot card suggests that someone is able to provide strong physical and emotional support, as well as counsel and guidance during difficult times. It can also indicate a sensitivity to one’s health needs, or be a sign of inner strength and resources that can be tapped into during times of need.

Finances: The Queen of Cups Tarot card can be an indicator of financial stability and prosperity in one’s life. This card may represent the capacity to make intelligent decisions that can lead to financial success. It can also point to wise investments and decisions and the ability to draw on the inner wisdom to make sound decisions and choices.

Relationships: The Queen of Cups Tarot card suggests emotional and spiritual support and connection. It can represent the understanding, empathy, love and compassion needed in a relationship. This card can signify a deep level of understanding between two people, and mutual respect and support. It can be a sign of heightened trust and increased mutual understanding within a relationship.
