A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Diamonds card and 6 Of Hearts card together in particular.

The 3 Of Diamonds card and 6 Of Hearts have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Diamonds and 6 Of Hearts mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the 3 Of Diamonds and the 6 Of Hearts coming together indicate a period of contention and change swathed in nostalgia.

The 3 of Diamonds heralds disputes, perhaps challenging your faith in people or circumstances.

This is compounded by the element of Air, which speaks of intellectual struggles and harsh thinking.

This challenging period of your life is symbolized by the onset of Fall, where things begin to wither.

On the other hand, the 6 of Hearts represents a longing for the past, a desire to return to happier times.

It signals necessary adjustments into a new course of life.

The fiery element of this card represents passion, transformation and motivational energy, intertwined with the rejuvenating essence of Spring.

The combination suggests reflection on past battles and the necessity to adjust and move forward with renewed faith and perseverance.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Diamonds and 6 Of Hearts mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, seeing the 3 of Diamonds and the 6 of Hearts together in relation to your love life is a keen indicator of both challenges and necessary changes.

With the 3 of Diamonds signifying “disputes,” it’s possible that a disagreement or tension could rise to the surface during the fall season.

However, the element of air associated with this card suggests that through communication and reason, these disputes are able to be resolved.

The presence of “faith” in the reading indicates that it is essential to trust in your relationship and believe in your shared ability to overcome these challenges.

The 6 of Hearts card, on the other hand, points towards “nostalgia” and “adjustments” in your love life.

With an association to fire and spring, this could suggest a time of transformation, growth, and renewal.

Perhaps the adjustments refer to learning from past experiences, possibly past relationships, and applying that knowledge to your current situation.

It’s likely you may find yourself reminiscing about the past, but remember to not let it hinder your present relationship.

The presence of these two cards together in your reading indicates a mix of dealing with present challenges and learning from past experiences, but through faith and self-growth, harmony is achievable.

### What does 3 Of Diamonds and 6 Of Hearts mean together for your finances?

In a reading concerned with your finances and job, the 3 Of Diamonds card denotes that you may face several disputes or arguments in your professional life.

Additionally, these disagreements may arise from financial discrepancies at your job or a difference of opinion regarding fiscal issues.

The card also refers to faith.

It’s an indication that though the path might appear complicated, having faith in your abilities will see you through crisis situations.

It symbolizes resilience and the ability to persevere in tough situations.

The Fall season may signify the need to collect and prepare, highlighting that it is time for you to save, prepare and perhaps budget your finances for future uses.

As it is connected to the element air, this card suggests the importance of clear communication to resolve disputes, and intellectual reasoning to tackle monetary issues.

When seen alongside the 3 of Diamonds, the 6 of Hearts indicates a sense of longing for financial stability or the need to adjust to changes in your financial status.

This could mean returning to old, profitable habits or perhaps, considering a job that you previously enjoyed but left for some reason.

This card is also a sign of adaptability, meaning it advises you to be open-minded, flexible and quick to react to alterations in your job or personal economics.

Spring, the associated season, is a time of rebirth and growth; this could mean a hint at new beginnings or almost certainly an improvement in your current financial situation.

Meanwhile, the fire element implies boldness and initiative, subtly hinting that in order to progress in your career or improve your economic standing, you need to be more assertive and confident in your actions.

### What does 3 Of Diamonds and 6 Of Hearts mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, when the 3 of Diamonds card appears in a health context, it warns of disputes regarding your health or potential conflicts with your healthcare providers.

Perhaps you have been neglecting your health or avoiding medical advice.

This card also represents faith, which could mean that overcoming these health-related disputes may rely on your belief in your own strength and the reliance on the advice of trustworthy professionals.

Its association with the season of Fall suggests that these issues may arise or intensify during this season.

Additionally, Air as its linked element highlights the importance of mental attitude and communication in resolving these issues.

On the other hand, pulling the 6 of Hearts card represents nostalgia and modifications you may need to make in your life for better health.

It could denote a longing for a time when you were healthier.

The root of some current health issues might trace back to old habits or lifestyle choices.

Recognizing these patterns can lead to necessary adjustments towards a healthier life.

As this card relates to the season of Spring and the element Fire, changes in health and improvements might kick off during springtime and would require a lot of energy and passion on your part.

Therefore, the combination of these two cards suggests that resolving current health conflicts and returning to a healthier state of being would require mental clarity, communication, acknowledging past habits, and the courage to make significant lifestyle adjustments.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 3 Of Diamonds and 6 Of Hearts mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 3 Of Diamonds and 6 Of Hearts the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.
