## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords Tarot card is an incredibly powerful card often associated with great suffering, loss and despair. Symbolizing the worst case scenario, this card carries the energy of a dark and dismal situation, one that can be incredibly difficult to overcome.

Health:  
The 10 of Swords symbolizes not only physical ailments and hardships, but also emotional pain. This card can suggest a complete depletion of energy, health and mental resources. It conveys a sense of hopelessness and a feeling that no matter how hard one tries, nothing will change the circumstances in which they find themselves. It indicates a condition that is likely to be long-term and difficult to resolve.

Finances:  
The 10 of Swords carries the message that a financial situation is out of control. It is a loss of direction and a struggle to get out of debt. It suggests that the situation is beyond one’s control and that all avenues seem to be closed to them. It is a warning against taking too much risk and to watch for potential pitfalls that may lead to further financial distress.

Relationships:  
The 10 of Swords symbolizes a broken relationship. It suggests that one or both parties have cut off communication and are no longer willing to work through the issues. This card indicates that the situation is at its worst and that it may be necessary to accept the fact that the relationship is beyond repair. It may be necessary to move on and find contentment in other aspects of life.

Overall, The 10 of Swords is a powerful Tarot card that carries a great deal of weight. It speaks of pain, suffering and the sense of hopelessness that can accompany difficult times. It is important to remember, however, that with great suffering can come great healing and growth if one is willing to work through the difficulties.

## What does 2 Of Cups mean?

The 2 of Cup Tarot card is a card that brings us good luck and balance in terms of our health, finances, and relationships.

Health: The 2 of Cups Tarot card signifies excellent health and well-being. It encourages us to take good care of our physical and mental health, to stay motivated and positive, and to be mindful of our diet and lifestyle choices. By improving our health and overall wellness, we can enjoy a much higher quality of life in the long run.

Finances: The 2 of Cups Tarot card encourages us to be wise and prudent when it comes to our finances. It tells us to establish financial goals and to save money for the future. We should invest in stocks and investments that are sound and can yield us long-term rewards. For short-term gains, it advises us to look into business ventures and other projects that bring us increased wealth.

Relationships: The 2 of Cups Tarot card encourages us to prioritize our relationships and to value the joy and peace they bring. It reminds us to show our appreciation and gratitude for our friends and family, and to make time for our partnerships and romantic relationships. It also tells us to be respectful and kind to those around us, and to treat those we interact with in a respectful and understanding manner. With these qualities, we can find much happiness and fulfillment in our relationships.
