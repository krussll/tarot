## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 3 Of Wands Tarot card means that health should be a team effort. Whatever your current health condition is, it can be improved through teamwork. Reach out to doctors, nutritionists, and other health professionals to create a health plan that works for you and your team. Take advantage of any available resources, such as health seminars, wellness programs, and health coaching. Working together with your healthcare team can help you reach your health goals.

Finances

The 3 Of Wands Tarot card encourages you to use teamwork when it comes to your finances. Reaching out to financial experts and advisors is essential to creating a financial plan. Working together with a financial team can help you establish more secure investments, manage debt, and build your wealth. The card also indicates that the potential for success is greater when working with a team rather than going it alone.

Relationships

The 3 Of Wands Tarot card emphasizes that relationships should also be a team effort. When two people come together they should build a team of two. They should be open and honest with each other and support each other in their individual goals. Working together as a team in a relationship can help you both stay connected, build trust, and deepen understanding. Group activities can also be beneficial as they allow both people to come together and foster a sense of togetherness.

## What does 6 Of Pentacles mean?

The 6 of Pentacles Tarot card is about giving and receiving help, generosity and balance. This card is about having enough to share with others and being generous with what we have.

When it comes to health, the 6 of Pentacles indicates helping and being helped. This could mean offering health advice to those who need it or getting help from a doctor or a health professional. It could also indicate getting or giving financial assistance or offering moral support.

In terms of finances, the 6 of Pentacles suggests having enough to share. This can be in the form of gifts, donations or financial support. Having a balanced approach to finances is also an important aspect of this card. This card could also denote being generous with our resources and helping those in need.

When it comes to relationships, the 6 of Pentacles suggests a generous and balanced approach. This card can indicate giving or receiving help and being generous with our time, energy and resources. It can also indicate having enough to share with those close to us and offering support when needed. It is also about creating a balanced and generous approach to relationships and not taking more than we can give.
