A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Wands card and 3 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Wands and 3 Of Cups mean together?

The 8 of Wands and the 3 of Cups tarot cards together represent great news coming in quickly that is cause for celebration. With the 8 of Wands bringing speed and action, and the 3 of Cups embodying a circle of support and friendship, it appears that your recent changes will be received with excitement, enthusiasm and support. Whether it’s the birth of a child, a promotion, or an upcoming celebration, the news will be embraced by your closest friends and family. Enjoy the moment as it is a time of great joy.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 8 Of Wands and 3 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw 8 Of Wands and 3 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 8 of Wands tarot card is often associated with physical health. It suggests quick recovery, healing and resolution of health issues or symptoms. When you experience sudden bouts of energy, clarity or enthusiasm that is a sign that the 8 of Wands is present in your life. It’s a card of motivation and can be a sign that you should start taking direct action today to improve your health and well-being.

Finances: The 8 of Wands tarot card often appears when you are about to receive some good news or a change in your financial situation. It is a card of progress and suggests that the progress you are making will occur quickly. If you have been struggling financially, this could be a sign that things are about to turn around. It’s also a sign of unexpected income and good luck coming your way.

Relationships: The 8 of Wands tarot card is an excellent card for relationships. It suggests that a new romance is about to come into your life. It can also be a sign that things are likely to move quickly in an existing relationship. The 8 of Wands encourages clarity and direct communication between two people. If you’ve been stuck in a rut in a relationship, this card can be a sign that things are going to start becoming more intense and passionate.

## What does 3 Of Cups mean?

The 3 of Cups Tarot card indicates a period of harmony, friendship, and joyous celebration in all areas of life. It speaks of the balance between giving and receiving, of nurturing and being nurtured, and of a connected and supportive circle of friends and family. This card is a reminder of the value of openness in relationships, of the importance of social and emotional intelligence, and of the need to take some time out to nurture yourself and those around you.

For Health: The 3 of Cups reminds us that health is not just about physical wellbeing but is also a reflection of our mental and emotional state. This card suggests that we should make sure to build a support system in our lives that can help us cope when stress and difficulties arise. Taking the time to celebrate your accomplishments and nurture yourself with compassion can help alleviate stress and increase wellbeing.

For Finances: The 3 of Cups suggests that while it is important to focus on our own financial goals, there is also great value in connecting with others who are further along in their financial journey. Working with an experienced financial advisor, or joining a financial literacy group that can provide you with resources and support can be incredibly beneficial. Additionally, learning to trust your intuition and forming honest relationships with those who can provide valuable insight can help you make the best decisions for your long-term financial roadmap.

For Relationships: The 3 of Cups Tarot card speaks of the importance of creating and maintaining strong relationships. In order to make relationships long-lasting and meaningful, it’s essential to nurture them with love, respect, and open communication. Taking the time to celebrate accomplishments and spend quality time with friends and family can also be beneficial in building close connections. Additionally, it’s important to build a circle of support that you can rely on – both platonic and romantic – that can help you through difficult times.
