A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Swords card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Swords card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Swords and The 6 Of Swords tarot card together can mean a time of transition and change in a difficult or painful situation. The 3 Of Swords can represent a period of deep sadness due to heartbreak, divorce, loss, depression, or surgery, while The 6 Of Swords can signify a period of necessary transition and moving away from this situation. This could mean packing up and moving to another city for a fresh start, or relocating to a different state for a new job opportunity. Whatever the case may be, this is a period of change in an emotional situation—it will involve sadness, but will also involve a new beginning.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Swords mean?

When The 3 of Swords Tarot card appears in a Tarot reading, it can be a sign of upheaval, sorrow and pain. The card is usually associated with a triad of negative experiences: health, finance and relationship difficulties.

When it comes to health, The 3 of Swords is a warning that something is seriously amiss. It could mean a physical illness, mental health issues, or the need to undergo a major surgery. In any case, the message is clear: there is suffering, and it requires attention and treatment. On a metaphysical level, the card may also suggest that the body, mind and spirit are out of balance, and healing is needed.

In terms of finances, The 3 of Swords card can signal a period of financial hardship and loss. This card may indicate that some money has been taken from you, or that you are in deep debt. Often it warns of a situation where you are squandering money, or you are being taken advantage of financially. It is a sign that you must take a hard look at your financial situation and make sure that your money is being used wisely.

Finally, The 3 of Swords is a sign of relationship problems. It can indicate a broken heart, a period of loneliness and despair, or a divorce or separation. It could mean that communication between partners has broken down, or that there is a lack of trust and mutual understanding. Whatever the situation may be, it is essential to address the underlying problems in order to heal the relationship. There must be open and honest communication if the partnership is to be restored.

The 3 of Swords Tarot card is a difficult card to interpret, as it can point to many different health, financial and relationship difficulties. However, no matter how painful the situation, it is important to remember that healing and growth are always possible.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

Health:

The 6 of Swords Tarot card signals a new start and a necessary change in health. This may mean letting go of unhealthy habits, such as unhealthy diets and inactivity, and embracing healthier choices. It is a sign to take a break from any stressful situations and to take time to heal. This card implies that there is hope for recovery and better health. The time for transition is now.

Finances:

The 6 of Swords Tarot card can mean a change in one’s financial situation. This card suggests that a reset is in order and that the time is ripe for a new financial strategy. It is a sign to think through one’s current financial decisions and make changes if necessary. It is important to be aware of opportunities and risks, and to be proactive in adapting finances for a more stable future.

Relationships:

The 6 of Swords Tarot card is a sign to transition in relationships as well. It is a sign to learn from past mistakes and to step out of negative cycles to make space for a healthier, more balanced and nurturing relationship. The card encourages to focus on the present and to adjust communication styles and behaviors in order to create a better environment. It is an empowering card that suggests that relationships can overcome hardships and blossom into something new and beautiful.
