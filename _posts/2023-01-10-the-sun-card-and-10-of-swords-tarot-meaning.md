A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Sun card and 10 Of Swords card together in particular.

The The Sun card and 10 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Sun and 10 Of Swords mean together?

The Sun tarot card and The 10 Of Swords tarot card represent a seemingly contrasting journey. On the one hand, The Sun tarot card symbolizes a joyous outcome and material happiness. This suggests that you will experience success and fulfillment. On the other hand, The 10 Of Swords tarot card represents a complete and painful ending, representing a form of rock bottom. However, when both of the cards are taken together, it could mean that you will ultimately succeed and achieve your goals, but it won’t be easy. You may feel like you have hit a wall but in the end, the Sun will shine and you will be able to find the joy and happiness that you seek.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does The Sun and 10 Of Swords mean together for your love life?

The Sun tarot card and The 10 of Swords tarot card together in a reading about love can be interpreted as a painful ending leading to material happiness. The Sun card is associated with material gain and joyous outcomes, which might be seen by some as a silver lining to the end of a relationship. This could represent the pain of a breakup that leads to a newfound opportunity to focus on self-care and personal success. The end of the relationship could be the freeing of energy and resources to give to yourself and ultimately create a better life. Being able to find the joy and beauty in life again is an important part of the healing process after a painful ending.

The 10 of Swords tarot card represents the finality and despair of a situation. It could be the conclusion of a relationship that has been filled with suffering and betrayal. This card can also be seen as a moment to accept and move on from any lingering feelings. Acknowledging the end of a relationship can be a difficult but necessary step in order to move on and find success in the future.

The Sun tarot card serves as a reminder that after every painful ending, there is the potential for joy and material gain. The healing process can take time, and it is important to remember to give yourself grace and patience in the meantime. Eventually, the beautiful sun will rise and you will be able to experience the joy of success and happiness again. Although the end of a relationship can be difficult and filled with pain, the Sun and 10 of Swords cards can serve as a reminder that it is possible to come out of the darkness and find material happiness.

### What does The Sun and 10 Of Swords mean together for your finances?

When The Sun tarot card and The 10 Of Swords tarot card are combined, it can signify a period of difficulty and despair in financial and professional matters. Initially, it suggests that a difficult and painful ending is coming. This could be the end of a job, business venture, or any other venture that was previously providing financial gain or security. This can be a difficult time, as not only will a source of income be lost, but it can also be accompanied by feelings of failure and rejection.

However, the Sun tarot card indicates that this should not be seen as a complete loss. It suggests that, although the immediate future is bleak, the ending of one venture can eventually lead to the possibility of material happiness and a joyous outcome in the not-so-distant future. The Sun tarot card suggests that a new path can be taken, and that new opportunities can be explored.

Although this period of darkness will be difficult and frustrating, it is important to look for the silver lining. The idea here is that sometimes hitting rock bottom is necessary in order to get back on the path to financial success and professional fulfillment. With effort, the right attitude and planning, it is possible to turn this difficult situation into the opportunity of a lifetime.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card is a symbol of material happiness and joy, one of the most hopeful cards in the deck. It signifies a successful outcome in all three areas of life: health, finances, and relationships.

The Sun Tarot card is a positive omen for one’s health, as it indicates that physical and emotional wellbeing can be achieved. It could also signify a much-needed break from illness or a period of recovery. The Sun Tarot card speaks of a time of rejuvenation, as it encourages taking care of one’s body and restoring energy.

The Sun Tarot card is also a symbol of financial stability and security. As such, it speaks of the chance for a successful investment, a lucrative job opportunity, or a well-deserved raise. This card encourages that any financial troubles can be overcome with careful planning and persistence.

Finally, the Sun Tarot card represents joyful and meaningful relationships. This card generally indicates that any current relationships are likely to be strengthened, and that there is hope for any new relationships. It encourages one to take the time enjoyed in relationships, as well as savouring any moment shared. The Sun Tarot card suggests that any difficulties in relationships can be overcome through connection, communication, and understanding.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

The 10 of Swords Tarot card’s meaning is representative of a complete loss and bitter ending in health, financial and relationship matters. It implies a sense of hopelessness, as if all hope is lost in the aspect being discussed.

When it comes to health, the 10 of Swords implies a situation of total despair and a feeling of being at rock bottom. There may be a sense of helplessness, a sense that the health issue cannot be improved. This may manifest in either physical or mental health. In either case, it signals a time of extreme frustration and hopelessness.

In terms of finances, the 10 of Swords suggests a situation of bankruptcy or complete ruin. It may represent an individual who has lost everything due to overspending or bad investments. This card may also hint at an impending financial disaster, such as a failing business or a bad debt.

When it comes to relationships, the 10 of Swords symbolizes a painful end. It may indicate that a relationship is doomed to fail, or that the relationship is coming to an abrupt and bitter end. It may represent a broken trust, a break up, or a separation. This card suggests that whatever once was will never be the same again.

Overall, the 10 of Swords implies a situation of total despair in various aspects of life. It may signify a rock bottom, where all hope seems to be lost and there is no forward progress. However, it also serves as a reminder that every situation is temporary and that there is always a chance to move forward, even in the darkest of times.
