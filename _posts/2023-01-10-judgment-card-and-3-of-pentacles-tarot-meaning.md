However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The Judgment card and 3 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

It is a reminder that the decisions we make should be made collectively and with the help of those around us. Together these cards suggest that we should strive for quality and craftsmanship in all aspects of life, particularly in the decisions we make and the paths we take. The Judgment tarot card signals a wake-up call and a reminder to search for our life’s purpose and to be mindful of how our decisions impact those around us. With the help of The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card, it is possible to find clarity and make a wise, final decision when it comes to the paths we choose in life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Judgment and 3 Of Pentacles mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Judgment and 3 Of Pentacles the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

For health, The Judgment Tarot card means that it’s time for self-reflection and evaluation of one’s current lifestyle. It’s a reminder to take stock of whether or not we’re taking care of our bodies, both inside and out. It can be a wake-up call to become more mindful of our healthy eating and exercise habits and to stay in tune with any stressors, both physical and emotional, that could be sabotaging our overall wellbeing. This card can also symbolize a deeper understanding that one’s health isn’t just about the physical body; one also needs to tend to their mental, spiritual, and emotional health in order to feel their absolute best.

For finances, The Judgment Tarot card signifies a time of making big decisions and coming to a conclusion. It could mean taking stock of one’s current financial situation and asking if it aligns with their goals and values. It can also signify large investments or purchases, or the hard truth that certain changes need to be made in order to reach financial stability. This card can be a wake-up call to think responsibly about money, seek help with budgeting, and to understand that long lasting financial success often starts with a realization that there can be emotional and spiritual growth through money management.

For relationships, The Judgment Tarot card can signify the need for personal growth and self-reflection in order to make decisions about the relationships we choose to nurture. It’s a reminder to look within ourselves and understand our own needs, values, and goals in order to evaluate what type of relationship we want and need in our life. This card can also be a wake-up call to recognize unhealthy patterns in the relationships we have, and to make necessary changes to create healthy bonds. Ultimately, this card is a reminder to understand our own self-worth and to choose relationships that make us feel fulfilled and at peace.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes teamwork and collaboration, which is an important key to success in many aspects of life. This card represents skill, quality, and craftsmanship. It can signify achieving greater heights in health, finances and relationships through the power of collaboration.

Health: In the realm of health, the 3 of Pentacles Tarot card is an indication that the power of collaboration can help to improve your overall health. When it comes to health, teamwork is essential; doctors, nutritionists, physical therapists, and other health practitioners working together with a single-minded focus can create powerful outcomes. Additionally, it is important to create a strong support network, so that you can lean on each other for help and advice during trying times.

Finances: This card emphasizes the importance of teamwork to gain financial success. Working with a financial adviser or accountant to manage your budget, or seeking the advice of a financial planner can ensure that your finances are on track. Additionally, the 3 of Pentacles Tarot card implies that you should seek out the help of a mentor or professional to develop your financial skills, such as investing knowledge and budgeting skills. With the right team working together, you can create a strong financial foundation.

Relationships: The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card emphasizes that collaboration is the key to a successful and fulfilling relationship. It is important to identify areas of agreement and compromise, and then work together in a collaborative manner to reach those agreements. When you are working together in a meaningful way, your relationship can become stronger, as it will create trust and understanding. Additionally, trusting and respecting each other’s perspectives is essential to progress. Finally, it is important to always keep communication open and honest, so that both individuals in the relationship can understand each other’s needs and desires.
