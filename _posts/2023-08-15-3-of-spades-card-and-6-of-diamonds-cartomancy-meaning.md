A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Spades card and 6 Of Diamonds card together in particular.

The 3 Of Spades card and 6 Of Diamonds have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Spades and 6 Of Diamonds mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing the 3 of Spades and the 6 of Diamonds in tandem suggests a tumultuous and transformative period in one’s love life.

The 3 of Spades, symbolizing heartbreak and faith, hints at an emotional turmoil or a significant loss.

It invokes the energy of Winter which signifies an end or a period of introspection.

This card is tied closely to the element of Water, which further emphasizes emotional depths and intuition.

On the other hand, the 6 of Diamonds resonates with themes of breakups and adjustments, embodying the energy of Fall, a time of letting go and change.

Reinforced by the element of Air, it suggests mental clarity, communication, and intellectual growth.

Together, these cards may indicate a painful separation, but also the promise of personal growth.

The journey ahead may be challenging, but faith and intellectual reflection will lead to necessary adjustments and emotional healing.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 3 Of Spades and 6 Of Diamonds mean together for your love life?

In a joint cartomancy reading with the 3 of Spades and the 6 of Diamonds, there may be a prediction of emotional turmoil in your love life.

The 3 of Spades talks about heartbreak, faith and being in the thick of emotional distress, representing those moments in love that test your faith and resilience.

This card suggests that you may be dealing with betrayal, loss or disappointment in your relationship.

However, the mention of faith also suggests that there will be the strength to endure this difficult phase, and a strong belief that things will eventually improve, aligned with the season of winter, which symbolizes endurance and waiting for brighter days ahead.

On the other hand, the 6 of Diamonds stands for breakups and adjustments.

This might indicate a phase in your relationship where a breakup could be imminent or necessary.

It points towards the inevitable period of adjusting to a new reality without your significant other being present anymore, yet it also implies this separation may hold room for personal growth and understanding.

Coupled with the season of fall, it alludes to a time of letting go and embracing change.

When combined with the air element, it introduces the wisdom and perception that would be needed to handle this phase.

Thus, while the pairing of these two cards could predict a tumultuous period in your love life, they also promise personal development and faith that will pull you through.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 6 Of Diamonds mean together for your finances?

In the context of finances, drawing the 3 of Spades could indicate a possible loss or difficulty in your financial realm, perhaps a setback that seems to break your heart.

This could mean losing a deal, incurring unexpected costs, or experiencing a decrease in your income.

But it also signifies faith – an indication that you need to trust in the process and have faith that you’ll overcome these challenges.

It suggests a tough winter season that you must go through in your financial journey.

But just like every winter, it will pass and spring will come.

Drawing the 6 of Diamonds, on the other hand, suggests adjustments and transitions.

In terms of a job, it might imply a rough phase such as a breakup with a business partner, a layoff, or a shift in your career path.

These changes might demand considerable adjustments from you.

However, the card also reminds you that change, just like the shifting wind in Fall, is inevitable, and it is crucial to adjust your sails to navigate properly.

In terms of finances, these adjustments might include re-budgeting, changing your financial strategy or exploring new income streams.

The combined interpretation of these cards is that though you may face financial hardship or career changes, faith, resilience, and flexibility will see you through.

### What does 3 Of Spades and 6 Of Diamonds mean together for your health?

In a health-focused cartomancy reading, the presence of The 3 Of Spades suggests an emotional turmoil or heartache that may be impacting your health.

It is a card that is inherently linked to Winter and the element of Water, signifying a time of introspection and deep emotional undercurrents.

Health-wise, this might hint at the need to pay more attention to your mental and emotional wellness, perhaps indicating depression, stress, or anxiety.

The idea of “faith” in this card’s explanation, however, suggests hope and reassurance.

It can be interpreted as a need to have faith in your healing process and personal strength, emphasizing psychological resilience.

The 6 Of Diamonds, associated with the Fall and the element of Air, holds the meaning of “breakup” and “adjustments”.

In a health context, it may symbolize the need for lifestyle changes or potentially breaking up with unhealthy habits that could lead to illness or exacerbate already existing conditions.

The element of Air might suggest issues related to respiration, communication, and thoughts.

Therefore, this card could imply making specific changes, such as improving your diet, getting more exercise, or addressing respiratory issues.

The notion of “adjustments” implies the necessity of adapting to new health routines or modifications in your current lifestyle for the sake of your health.

The combination of these two cards suggests a holistic approach to health, considering both emotional and physical aspects.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
