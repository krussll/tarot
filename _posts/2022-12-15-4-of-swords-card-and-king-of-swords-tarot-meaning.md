A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 4 Of Swords card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The 4 Of Swords card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

Being aware of when we need to retreat and be alone, so that we can rest and move closer to a place of renewal, is just as important as being a strategic leader. Taking a step back and giving yourself the opportunity to be with your thoughts and reflect is vital to the process of restoration and growth, and the King of Swords provides the clarity and insight to guide us in this journey. Together they remind us that progress is not only achieved through active leadership, but often through committing to a period of rest and rejuvenation.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 4 Of Swords and King Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card symbolizes a time to retreat and take a break from the fast-paced life. It is a sign to focus on one’s health, rest, and renewal. It is time to put away all worries, and let go of any stress or anxiety and start to focus on restoration of body, mind, and soul. If a person has been struggling with physical or mental health issues, this Tarot card can be a sign to take a break, rest and recover before returning to their normal activities. It can also symbolize taking a vacation, abstaining from work, or taking a break from certain activities in order to restore one’s health.

Finances:  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card is also a sign to take a break from your financial situation. This can be a time to get organized, assess current expenses, and find ways to cut back on unnecessary spending. It also may be a sign to divert some money into a savings account or other form of security. Additionally, the card can symbolize a time of respite from investments to give the necessary time to find more stable financial opportunities. This card can also be identified as the need to be more judicious with one’s money, teaching a person to be more thoughtful with their financial decisions.

Relationships:  
The 4 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign to step away from a relationship which is causing strife, or to take a break from all of the struggles of a relationship. This can be a sign to take time to be alone, catch one’s breath, and collect one’s thoughts. It may be a sign to take time away from long distance relationships, or to pause and evaluate what is going on in a relationship before making drastic decisions. This card can also represent going on a ‘relationship vacation’ or taking a break from talking to someone. This can be a time to focus on the self, and get clarity on the direction they want their relationship with another to take.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health

The King of Swords Tarot card is about making intentional and strategic decisions for health. In terms of physical health, it suggests that you take the time to evaluate your current status and decide on the best course of action. The King of Swords can help you to identify what needs to be done to improve your health and have the willpower to do it. This might include getting regular exercise, eating healthy meals or seeking medical advice.

Finances

The King of Swords Tarot card is indicative of making realistic financial decisions. It encourages you to look at your financial situation objectively and identify what needs to be done to get it under control. Taking control of your finances is necessary if you want to achieve financial stability and security. This includes being mindful of your spending habits, identifying areas for potential savings and creating a budget plan.

Relationships

The King of Swords Tarot card represents the idea that relationships should be handled strategically. This is especially true when it comes to navigating difficult relationships. This card encourages you to look at the situation objectively and make decisions that are based on logic, rather than emotion. It also suggests that you have honest and open conversations with the other person to ensure that you both understand each other’s perspectives and needs. This will help to build a strong foundation for the relationship and lead to a more successful outcome.
