## What does Death and Temperance mean together?

The Death and Temperance tarot cards together suggest that you are in a period of transition in your life. The Death card indicates that you may be facing endings or big changes and that it is time to move on, while the Temperance card speaks to balance, harmony and greater self control in order to help you on your journey. This combination of cards encourages you to maintain a sense of patience and understanding, as you find the courage to move forward towards transformation and rebirth. This is a time to be mindful of your own energy and emotions, and to trust that the courage and balance you maintain will bring you to the new beginnings you seek.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Death and Temperance mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Death mean?

The Death Tarot card suggests that big changes are in store, signalling an ending or time to move on. It can indicate a transformation or rebirth happening in our lives, and might be a sign of death in strictly physical sense. But whatever the case, it is important to keep in mind that this card does without fail announce a period of renewal.

The Death card in health could indicate a change of diet or a lifestyle shift; for instance, cutting out certain unhealthy habits. It could also represent a major physical change, such as a health scare or a serious injury, as a result of which we may need to take care of ourselves better. It is important to recognize the importance of change and follow through with whatever necessary steps will help promote a healthier lifestyle.

The Death card in finances could be related to a major loss of money or the downsizing of assets. This could require us to restructure our finances and make more thoughtful choices with the money we have. It may lead to the need to get a new job, start a new business venture or take on a side hustle to secure our financial future. The card suggests that with careful planning, these changes could lead to a more secure and successful future.

In relationships, the Death card could signal an ending or transformation of our current connection with someone. The card suggests that it’s time to move on, and might possibly indicate a period of mourning or grieving. But it could also signify the start of a new and healthier bond with someone else. This could be a sign of increased self-love or of a deeper connection with the people around us. The Death card encourages us to break free from unhealthy past relationships and create space for new and healthier ones.

## What does Temperance mean?

The Temperance Tarot card is all about creating balance in your life. When it comes to health, this card is a reminder to stay mindful of your physical and mental well-being. This could be done through diet, exercise, and taking regular breaks from stress. It is important to find the right balance between doing enough to take care of your body, but not going overboard.

When it comes to finances, the Temperance Tarot card is a sign to practice self-control and wise decision-making. This could be through budgeting, avoiding indulgences, or planning for the long-term. It is important to find a balance in spending and saving, where neither is given too much attention.

Finally, in relationships, the Temperance Tarot card has much to offer. This card can remind us to find harmony in our relationships, as this will bring a greater sense of peace and joy. It can also encourage us to be self-controlled and express ourselves with patience, as communication is key in maintaining healthy relationships.

Ultimately, the Temperance Tarot card is a gentle reminder to bring balance into all areas of life. It invites us to be mindful of our decisions and to practice moderation in everything we do. With this card in mind, it may be easier to find the balance we need to live a successful and fulfilled life.
