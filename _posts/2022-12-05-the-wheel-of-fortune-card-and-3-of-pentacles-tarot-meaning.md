## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Wheel Of Fortune mean?

The Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is an indicator of good fortune, a turning point, luck, and gambling. When this card appears in a Tarot reading, it can symbolize all three of these areas, providing insight into the past, present and future of an individual’s health, finances and relationships.

In terms of health, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a sign that you will experience a turning point in your well-being. This card is a reminder that progress and health can come in cycles and it may be time to look at your daily routine and make changes to improve your overall wellbeing. It may also be indicating that there are some health risks that you need to pay attention to.

In terms of finances, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a sign of good luck and increased financial opportunities. This card may mean that you will experience a short-term gain in money that could lead to a long-term financial increase. It can also indicate that you should take advantage of any opportunities that have recently presented themselves that could result in a financial windfall.

When it comes to relationships, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a sign that you will experience a turning point in your relationships. This card is a reminder that relationships can be cyclical and that it might be time to make necessary changes to ensure your relationships remain fruitful and rewarding. It also indicates that there may be opportunities to expand your social circle and make new connections.

Overall, the Wheel of Fortune Tarot card is a sign that good luck and progress are on their way in your health, finances and relationships. It is a reminder to take advantage of any opportunities that come your way and make necessary changes if needed. With patience, tenacity and the right attitude, you can make sure that this turning point leads to the success that you desire.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The 3 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks of the strength of teamwork when it comes to health, finances and relationships. On a physical level, this card encourages us to work collaboratively with our health care providers to ensure the best possible outcome for our bodies. Involving ourselves in conversations about our own needs and making sure to full take part in our health decisions is a powerful way to put the 3 of Pentacles’ emphasis on collaboration into practice.

In terms of finances, the 3 of Pentacles urges us to seek the assistance of others when making decisions about our money. From creating a budget to developing a savings plan, the 3 of Pentacles encourages us to make informed choices with the help of those around us. Whether it’s talking to a financial adviser or getting insights from our family and friends, the 3 of Pentacles encourages us to make money decisions with confidence, knowing that we’re supported by knowledgeable people.

Finally, in relationships, the 3 of Pentacles encourages us to build on our existing networks. Whether it’s through work, family or friends, the 3 of Pentacles urges us to develop relationships with others to help us achieve our goals. Perhaps we can seek out different perspectives on our own dilemmas, or collaborate with our partners to find solutions to complicated issues. The 3 of Pentacles encourages us to find value in the input of others, whether it’s financial, emotional, or practical.
