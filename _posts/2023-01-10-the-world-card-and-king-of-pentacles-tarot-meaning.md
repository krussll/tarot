However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The World mean?

The World Tarot card is a powerful symbol of completeness, indicating that it is time to reap the rewards of hard work and dedication. In terms of health, the card could suggest the attainment of peak physical condition. It could also indicate achieving a balanced lifestyle and good self-care, leading to improved overall health and wellbeing. If a person is having difficulty in terms of physical health, this card could symbolise that the person is in the process of healing and the journey will be completed soon.

In terms of finances, The World Tarot card could suggest the attainment of financial stability, with the potential for prosperity. It could represent the successful completion of a monetary goal, such as completing a financial plan or achieving a desired income level. Alternatively, it could indicate that the person is moving in the right direction financially, but has not yet reached the desired level of security.

The World Tarot card can also represent the successful completion of a cycle in relationships. It could mean that a person has overcome a difficult situation with a partner or family member and is close to achieving a balanced and harmonious relationship. Alternatively, it could suggest that the person has drifted apart from a loved one, and is moving on to a new stage in their life. Whatever the case may be, this card indicates that a journey is coming to an end.

## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

Health:  
The King of Pentacles Tarot card means that your health will be managed in a practical, responsible, and conservative manner. You will take care of your health by exercising regularly, eating well, and getting plenty of rest. You take a sensible and methodical approach to your health, and you will see the positive results.

Finances:  
The King of Pentacles Tarot card means that your finances will be managed in a secure, responsible, and practical way. You will be an excellent investor, determined to make wise financial decisions that will provide you with long-term security and stability. You will work steadily and carefully to build your financial resources, and you will be rewarded for your efforts.

Relationships:  
The King of Pentacles Tarot card means that you take relationships seriously, and you will approach them in a conservative and practical way. You are careful and level-headed, but you are also generous and supportive. You will be reliable and dependable, and your relationships will be solid and long-lasting. You will strive to maintain a sense of stability and security in your relationships.
