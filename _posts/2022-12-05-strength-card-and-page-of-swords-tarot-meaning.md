A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and Page Of Swords card together in particular.

The Strength card and Page Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and Page Of Swords mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The Page Of Swords tarot card together for me symbolize the need for balance in life. It’s not enough to just have self-love and self-respect, but we must also be aware of the people and situations around us. The Page Of Swords reminds us to be mindful of gossip and prying eyes, and to always seek the truth. Strength gives us the courage and strength we need to navigate through these situations, so that we are not weighed down by fear and insecurity. Together, these two cards serve as a reminder to stay strong and stand up for ourselves and our beliefs, no matter what challenges we might face.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Strength and Page Of Swords mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Strength and Page Of Swords the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

On the mental health side, the Strength Tarot card symbolizes the courage to be honest with ourselves and others, and to take the time to process our thoughts, feelings, and experiences. Lastly, the Strength Tarot card encourages us to be open and to express our emotions with clarity and respect.

When it comes to finances, the Strength Tarot card encourages us to take control of our money and use our resources wisely. It reminds us to be smart with our finances, set goals, and work towards them. The Strength Tarot card speaks of discipline, of making wise choices, and of doing our best to stick to our plans. The Strength Tarot card is also associated with relationships. It speaks of the strength of character, honesty, and respect that should go into every relationship. This card encourages us to open up and express our feelings, as well as to be honest and vulnerable with ourselves and others. It also speaks of the courage to face and overcome difficult challenges that may arise in relationships. The Strength Tarot card also reminds us to be patient and understanding as we strive to foster healthy, meaningful relationships. ## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health: The Page Of Swords is a reminder that health must be safeguarded and not taken for granted. This card suggests a need for caution in order to avoid any potential health risks or illnesses. You may need to take an extra step to protect your health by avoiding any potentially harmful situations. It is also a reminder that trust is necessary when it comes to health and to seek out a healthcare provider who understands your needs and provides the best possible care.

Finances: The Page Of Swords is a warning to be cautious when it comes to financial matters. It suggests that careful consideration and smart investments are necessary to avoid any potential losses. When it comes to spending, the card speaks of a need to be mindful and to know when to say no. It is also a reminder to stay away from any deals or investments that could potentially put you in a difficult financial situation.

Relationships: The Page Of Swords card urges you to be mindful of what you say in your relationships. It is a reminder that gossip and idle chit-chat can do more harm than good, and can even cause irreparable damage. This card also speaks of the need for honest communication and transparency, as it is only through understanding and trust that meaningful relationships can form. The card is a reminder to remain open-minded and tolerant even when disagreements arise.
