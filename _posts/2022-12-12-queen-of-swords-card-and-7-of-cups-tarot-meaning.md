However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

## What does Queen Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean together?

The Queen of Swords and the 7 of Cups tarot card together represent a conflict between the need to be self sufficient and be totally honest with yourself and the desire to explore all the options and possibilities available to you. The Queen of Swords encourages you to be honest and direct with yourself and commit to a course of action, while the 7 of Cups encourages you to explore the wide array of options out there. With this conflict between the two, it can be difficult to decide which path to take, but if you focus on your commitment to yourself and your own sense of honesty, you will eventually find what is right for you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Queen Of Swords and 7 Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw Queen Of Swords and 7 Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Queen Of Swords mean?

Health: The Queen of Swords Tarot card suggests having self-discipline and self-control when it comes to your health. Being honest with yourself about what you eat, how much you exercise, and how much you relax is very important in maintaining a healthy lifestyle. Try to be mindful of your body and what it needs, and make sure to prioritize it above all else. Health should always be the top priority, not just in physical appearance, but also in regards to mental and emotional wellbeing.

Finances: The Queen of Swords Tarot card encourages total honesty when it comes to finances. Being open and truthful with yourself and others when it comes to money can help you achieve financial stability. Try to adopt a plan of action for long-term financial success and be realistic about what you can achieve. Be firm and stick to your budget and try to save before you spend. The Queen of Swords suggests that having an honest approach to money and never making rash decisions will serve you well in the long run.

Relationships: The Queen of Swords Tarot card indicates that honesty should be at the heart of all of your relationships. This includes being honest when communicating with your friends, family, and significant other. Being open, direct, and having the courage to say what you really mean are important components to having successful relationships. No relationship can survive without trust, and total honesty can help to strengthen that trust. Being honest also means being understanding and able to forgive one another when mistakes are made.

## What does 7 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 7 of Cups tarot card can signify that you have too many different options at your disposal for your health, or that you are unsure of which path to take. You may be considering a multitude of treatments, diets, or supplement regimes, and unable to decide which one is best for you. It suggests that you may have commitment issues when it comes to your physical health, perhaps struggling to stay on a routine or regularly follow through with self-care practices. The 7 of Cups can indicate that it is time to take a closer look at why you are having difficulty committing and dedicating yourself to your own wellbeing, and to make an effort to stay consistent with healthy habits.

Finances: The 7 of Cups tarot card invites you to look at your current financial situation and ask yourself whether you are utilizing all the different possibilities to your advantage. You may be presented with a variety of investments or savings plans, unsure of which will be the most beneficial for you financially. It could also suggest that you may be struggling to make a long-term commitment with your finances. You may be too easily swayed by short-term, get-rich-quick schemes and have difficulty seeing the bigger picture of what investments will quickly and steadily help you achieve your financial goals.

Relationships: In the context of relationships, the 7 of Cups tarot card can indicate that you may have too many options in front of you and can’t decide which path to take. You may be presented with a variety of potential partners and be hesitant to commit to any one person. You may feel overwhelmed by the sheer number of possibilities and be unsure of whether you want to take a big risk on any of them. Furthermore, this card can point to a lack of commitment in existing relationships, as you may find it difficult to make a long-term commitment and stay dedicated to your partners. It can be a reminder to be more conscious of the decisions you make and to think long-term before acting.
