A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and Queen Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and Queen Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and Queen Of Wands mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and The Queen of Wands tarot card together bring to your attention the importance of partnership in your life. It is a reminder that when two people come together in a relationship, that relationship has the potential for a deep and lasting love. This card also signifies a choice that you need to make, a choice that can be made from a place of confidence, strength and self-assurance. The potential for the two of you is great, so be sure to make the most of it and let the power of two guide you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Lovers and Queen Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Lovers and Queen Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health  
The Lovers Tarot card is associated with the mutual support and balance that comes from a loving relationship. It speaks of harmony, good health and resilience in the face of adversity. This card offers a reminder to maintain the balance between giving and receiving in your relationships, and not to take too much or give too much. By consciously investing in healthy relationships, we can create an environment of harmony and balance in our lives, both emotionally and physically.

Finances  
The Lovers Tarot card often appears when it’s time to make a major decision related to finances. It is a reminder of the power of partnership and cooperation, and of the strength that comes from two people working together. It suggests that we should take some time to consider our partnerships and determine how we can best collaborate to reach our financial goals. There may be areas where we need to create a better balance or provide more support to our partner to ensure an optimal outcome.

Relationships  
The Lovers Tarot card symbolizes a deep connection between two people and encourages us to be mindful and appreciate the special bond shared with a partner. This card also serves as a reminder to communicate openly and honestly and to be willing to compromise. Mutual respect and understanding are key components of any healthy relationship and if we are willing to put in the effort to maintain connection and harmony we can establish a strong and lasting foundation for our relationship.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is associated with confidence, self-assurance and a feisty attitude. As far as health is concerned, the Queen of Wands encourages individuals to push their own limits, to become more active and engaged in their health and wellbeing. It can represent a strong physical presence and the ability to take charge of one’s own health and to take risks when it comes to caring for oneself.

In terms of finances, the Queen of Wands indicates a great sense of confidence and ability to manage money. She symbolizes a person who is not afraid to invest and take risks in order to reap rewards. This card suggests that someone is savvy when it comes to their finances and has a good understanding of what will bring in the best returns.

In terms of relationships, the Queen of Wands is a strong indicator of self-assurance and faith in one’s own abilities. This person, who is represented by this Tarot card, is a strong leader and is not afraid to take charge and have confidence in their own decisions. They also have the ability to bring about positive change in relationships and have the strength to stay true to their convictions. This Tarot card suggests someone who understands the importance of having boundaries and knows how to stand up for themselves and their relationships.
