However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

The 9 of Cups tarot card and the King of Cups tarot card together signify a time when wishes come true, material abundance, and good health, with a supportive leader providing the guidance and understanding necessary to make it become reality. The King of Cups symbolizes tolerance and empathy, which can help create an atmosphere of acceptance and understanding. With the balance of power, material wealth, and emotional support between the two cards, there is the potential to bring great change and transformation.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 9 Of Cups and King Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

For health, The 9 Of Cups tarot card symbolizes good health, physical well being, and good luck. It suggests that if one takes care of oneself, their health will be rewarded with a long and happy life. People who have this card in their reading may be advised to look after their physical, mental and emotional health. They may also be advised to focus on the foods they eat, how they exercise and how they take care of their mind and spirit. This card may suggest that taking good care of oneself will help to bring good health and longevity.

For finances, The 9 Of Cups tarot card indicates material abundance and fortune. It suggests that one is in a good financial position, and that money is flowing freely. Those who receive this card in their reading may be encouraged to take risks and pursue any project that may bring them more wealth. It may also indicate that one is currently in their peak wealth-earning period and should make the most of it.

For relationships, The 9 Of Cups tarot card symbolizes wishes coming true and good luck in relationships. It suggests that those who have this card in their reading should focus on romantic partnerships, and that good luck and fortune will follow. It may also suggest that one is in a good place in their relationship, but should continue to put effort and work in to maintain a strong, loving bond. It is a card of optimism and faith that good things will come in the future, as long as one puts in the effort to make it happen.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health: The King Of Cups Tarot card offers a beneficial insight into one’s overall health and wellbeing. Symbolically, it indicates a stable yet nurturing energy which can be used to encourage better health habits, such as exercising regularly and eating a balanced diet. This card can also suggest that the individual is in a position to act as a support to others, helping them to take care of themselves. Furthermore, this card can signify a great understanding and compassion, both towards oneself and those around them.

Finances: The King Of Cups Tarot card can symbolise a period of financial stability and prosperity. These energies offer the individual the opportunity to calmly assess their financial situation and make wise decisions for the future. This card can mean that the individual has a positive attitude and outlook when it comes to money, and is willing to work hard and make sacrifices in order to progress and be successful. Meanwhile, it also advises that money doesn’t have to be at the root of all decisions, and that a balanced approach to life is key.

Relationships: The King Of Cups Tarot card can signify that the individual has the capacity to be a supportive and understanding partner. This card advises that, by listening, understanding and accepting the needs of their partner, the individual is able to provide a safe and nurturing environment in their relationship. This card can also suggest a capacity for patience, tolerance and empathy, which are all integral ingredients for a harmonious relationship. This card can also suggest that the individual will benefit from taking the time to communicate openly and honestly with their partner, and that they should trust their intuition when making important decisions.
