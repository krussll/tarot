## What does Judgment and 7 Of Wands mean together?

When the Judgment tarot card and the 7 of Wands tarot card appear together in a reading, they suggest a time of clarity is coming in your life. This is an opportune time to make a final decision, take a stand and take control of your life. It is time to stand up for your beliefs and to fight for your life’s purpose. You have the courage to defend yourself and the ability to protect yourself against competition. Now is the time to listen to the wake-up call and to use your newfound sense of clarity to focus on what truly matters to you.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 7 Of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to take care of your own health. This can mean making sure that you are eating a healthy diet, exercising regularly, and getting enough sleep. It is also a reminder to protect your mental and emotional health by taking control of your thoughts, speaking kindly to yourself, and taking time for self-care. Self-defense is a key concept in this card, and it also applies to health. It is important to shield yourself from any unhealthy influences or bad habits that do not serve your well-being. This card also encourages you to find balance between working hard and taking time to rest.

Finances

The 7 Of Wands Tarot card encourages you to protect your financial health by being mindful with your money. This means not taking unnecessary risks, avoiding debt, and budgeting. This card is also a reminder that competition could be at play when it comes to finances. You may have to protect your wealth and resources from others who may be trying to take advantage of you. Self-defense is key when it comes to financial matters. Being aware of your rights and boundaries, as well as owning up to your mistakes and taking responsibility for them, will help you protect your finances.

Relationships

The 7 Of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to protect the relationships in your life. It is important to set clear boundaries and communicate your needs in a respectful and honest manner. This card also encourages self-defense when it comes to relationships. This means not allowing others to take advantage of you or mistreat you. It is important to remember that relationships are two-way streets, and that it is OK to protect yourself. It is also a reminder to stay true to your values and stand up for yourself when necessary.
