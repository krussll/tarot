A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 5 Of Wands card and 8 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 5 Of Wands card and 8 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 5 Of Wands and 8 Of Pentacles mean together?

When the 5 of Wands and 8 of Pentacles come together, it can suggest that you are in the midst of a competitive situation, such as a sporting event, a job interview, or a debate. While it can be intimidating to face such a challenge, the 8 of Pentacles encourages you to prepare thoroughly and use your own skill and abilities to your advantage. The 8 of Pentacles may suggest finding a way to up your game, so you can turn this nerve-wracking situation into an enjoyable experience by mastering a craft or improving your technique. Despite the competitive nature of the 5 of Wands, the 8 of Pentacles reminds you to enjoy the process of learning and self-improvement, and to view the challenge as a chance for growth and success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 5 Of Wands mean?

The 5 Of Wands Tarot card is one that symbolizes a period of intense competition, rivalry and obstacles. It indicates a need to surmount certain challenges in life in order to gain success. In terms of health, the card represents a time of physical struggle and the requirement to work hard in order to maintain physical health. It can suggest that there are competing forces in the body, such as conflicting supplements and diets, or inconsistent exercise plans. It could also indicate that the individual is competing against an illness or body condition to maintain physical well-being.

When it comes to finances, the card speaks of competition between different sources of income, or between different investments. It can suggest that the individual needs to be creative and persistent with money-making strategies to stay ahead of the competition. It is also a sign of needing to work hard and remain persistent in order to achieve any major financial success.

In terms of relationships, the card can symbolize competition between two people, or competing interests in a relationship. It can indicate that the relationship is no longer balanced, or that the dynamics within the relationship are unbalanced. It is a reminder to take a step back and properly assess where the relationship stands, and how best to adjust it to suit both parties. The card also speaks of a need to work hard and communicate openly in order to maintain the relationship.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles mean?

Health:

When The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card appears in a reading, it is a reminder of the importance of taking care of our physical health. This card suggests that we prioritize our well-being, investing our time and energy into taking care of our bodies, minds and souls. By seeing our physical health as a craft to master, we can build the discipline to make wise choices when it comes to eating and exercise, as well as seeking professional help when needed. We can take pride in investing in ourselves and our health, and in the long run, this investment will bring us health and joy.

Finances:

The 8 Of Pentacles Tarot card also speaks to our financial health. This card suggests that we approach our finances with patience and discipline. Being mindful of our spending, budgeting, and saving habits can lead to financial security and peace of mind. With the 8 of Pentacles, we are encouraged to spend time investing in our financial future, understanding our money and learning how to make smart decisions with it. We can also take delight in our accomplishments in this area and take pride in achieving financial mastery.

Relationships:

When it comes to relationships, the 8 of Pentacles encourages us to approach them with an open heart. This card suggests that we invest our time and energy into understanding and mastering the art of relationships. We can learn to be vulnerable, to communicate authentically and to be understanding of our partner’s needs. Taking time to learn and grow with another person can bring us great joy and fulfillment. With patience, mastery of relationships can be achieved, leading to meaningful, beautiful connections and lasting bonds.
