A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The High Priestess card and The Hierophant card together in particular.

The The High Priestess card and The Hierophant have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The High Priestess and The Hierophant mean together?

The High Priestess tarot card and The Hierophant tarot card together represent the need to trust your intuition and go with your gut feeling in order to be successful. This can be a difficult balance to strike, as The Hierophant tarot card also represents the need to conform to tradition and meet conventional expectations. However, if you can find a way to trust your intuition while still staying true to yourself, you will be able to achieve great things.

Health-wise, this combination of tarot cards suggests that it is important to listen to your body and give it what it needs. Don’t neglect your health in pursuit of other goals, but also don’t be afraid to try new things and trust your instincts when it comes to your wellbeing.

In terms of relationships, The High Priestess tarot card and The Hierophant tarot card together suggest that it is important to be true to yourself. Don’t try to be someone you’re not just to please others – be honest about who you are and what you want, and you will attract the right people into your life.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The High Priestess and The Hierophant mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The High Priestess and The Hierophant the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The High Priestess mean?

The High Priestess Tarot card is a reminder to trust your intuition and gut feelings. Secrets may be revealed to you, so be prepared to handle them with discretion. This is a good time to reflect on your past choices and see where you could have made better decisions. It’s also a time to focus on your mental and spiritual health. Your finances may be stable at this time, but don’t take any unnecessary risks. In relationships, The High Priestess Tarot card suggests that you need to spend some time alone to reflect on your own needs and desires. This is not a time to make any big decisions, but rather to listen to your heart and intuition.

## What does The Hierophant mean?

Health:

The Hierophant Tarot card suggests that in terms of health, one should focus on following traditional methods and expectations in order to maintain good health. This may include following a balanced diet, exercising regularly and getting adequate rest. Additionally, it is important to conform to any medical advice or treatments prescribed by professionals in order to stay healthy.

Finances:

The Hierophant Tarot card also has implications for finances. In terms of finances, it is important to conform to societal norms and expectations in order to be successful. This may include getting a good education, working hard and budgeting wisely. Additionally, it is important to be mindful of any financial advice given by experts in order to make wise choices with one’s money.

Relationships:

Finally, The Hierophant Tarot card also has significance for relationships. In terms of relationships, it is important to follow conventional methods and expectations in order to maintain healthy and happy relationships. This may include communicating openly, being honest and respecting boundaries. Additionally, it is important to be aware of any relationship advice given by experts in order to make informed choices about one’s relationships.

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The High Priestess and The Hierophant has helped you.
