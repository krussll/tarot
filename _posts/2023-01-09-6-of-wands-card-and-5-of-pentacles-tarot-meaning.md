A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 6 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 6 Of Wands card and 5 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 6 Of Wands and 5 Of Pentacles mean together?

The 6 Of Wands and The 5 Of Pentacles tarot cards together suggest a situation in which struggles and hard times may have been endured, but you have come out on the other side victorious. This could indicate that, despite the initial hardships, you have achieved success and recognition of your work and efforts. It is a reminder that no matter how dark the night is, there will always be a dawn. Your suffering will be eventually replaced with the joy of victory, and hard times will no longer be an obstacle.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 6 Of Wands suggests that despite these struggles, you will ultimately experience success and recognition in your relationship. It indicates that you are likely to experience good news, such as a promotion or a meaningful reward, that will bring you joy and make up for the difficult times you have been facing.

The 5 Of Pentacles symbolizes ill health, poverty, worry, and isolation, which can be especially challenging when it comes to love and relationships. This card indicates that you may experience financial loss, or there may be a strain between you and your partner due to stresses in life. Yet, the combination of The 6 Of Wands and The 5 Of Pentacles suggests that this period of difficulty can also lead to long-term victory and success in your relationship. By weathering the storm and pushing through the challenging times, you and your partner will be able to overcome any obstacles, and experience a victory of sorts in your relationship.

You may need to take extra care during this period and show one another extra love and support. There may be times when one or both of you feels like the worries of life are too much to handle, but know that this is just a moment in time. Through patience, perseverance, and communication, you and your partner will be able to make it through any difficult times and reach a new place of victory.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 6 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is an indication of good health, both physical and mental. It suggests that you have taken care of yourself and your body, and that you have worked hard for optimal health. It can also be a sign of success in a health regimen, such as in a diet and fitness program. This card could also be an indication of a successful recovery from an illness or injury, or just of general well-being. Whatever you have put into improving your health will certainly pay off with the 6 of Wands, and you’ll find yourself feeling better than ever.

Finances

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is a symbol of financial success and accomplishment. This can be related to a job promotion or successful business venture, and you’ll find that your efforts have paid off in the form of tangible rewards and recognition. It’s a sign that you have been mindful of your budget, investments, and savings, and are likely to have made smart decisions that will continue to pay off in the future. Overall, the 6 of Wands is a sign of financial success and stability.

Relationships

The 6 of Wands Tarot card is also a symbol of success and recognition in relationships. This could be due to a conflict or issue being resolved, or a partnership that has been worked on and is now bearing fruit with positive results. If you are in a relationship, this card could be an indication of a successful compromise or collaboration between the two of you. It’s also a sign of optimism and faith in a relationship, which can open up the possibility of growth and further success. The 6 of Wands can also be a sign of recognition from others, such as from your family and friends, signaling that your relationships are solid and healthy.

## What does 5 Of Pentacles mean?

The 5 of Pentacles tarot card suggests a time of ill health, poverty and worry. Those who are in need of financial help or assistance may feel that resources are scarce and difficult to come by, leading to feelings of isolation, worry and loss. This card can also point to a lack of physical, emotional and spiritual nourishment, leading to an overall sense of unease and unhappiness.

Regarding finances, the 5 of Pentacles suggests a time of poverty and financial setback. This can mean that income is limited, expenses are high, and resources are scarce. This card can highlight an inability to make ends meet or a scarcity mindset, making it difficult to make the best financial decisions. This can also refer to a period of extreme poverty, where desperate measures must be taken just to survive.

Relationships can also be affected by the 5 of Pentacles. Those dealing with health, financial or other issues may feel isolated and disconnected from loved ones. It can be hard to connect on a deeper level when faced with hardships, leading to feelings of loneliness and abandonment. This card can also point to a time of withdrawal from relationships, where it feels better to be alone than to be in the company of friends or family.

Overall, the 5 of Pentacles Tarot card speaks to a time of ill health, poverty, worry and isolation. Financial loss and struggles can have an effect on physical, emotional and spiritual health, as well as relationships. If you have the 5 of Pentacles in your spread, know that it is a time where you may need to rely on extra support and assistance in order to get through the difficult times. Challenges are temporary and with perseverance, you will eventually reach a place of stability and security.
