## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The Ace of Cups Tarot card represents the potential health benefits of new beginnings in life. It alludes to the idea that when we open ourselves up to new opportunities and experiences, we often open up to improved health. Physically, the Ace of Cups implies that starting a new health-related routine may be beneficial to one’s wellbeing. For example, if someone is looking to improve their energy levels, they may consider starting a new exercise routine or committing to a healthy diet. Mentally and emotionally, the Ace of Cups suggests that when we push ourselves outside of our comfort zone and expose ourselves to new experiences, we open our minds to the possibility of greater joy, contentment, and peace.

The Ace of Cups Tarot card can represent financial opportunity and potential. It reminds us to be open to new ideas, investments, and other methods of generating wealth. It encourages us to look for creative ways to generate income and to take chances when we see potential. It also encourages us to consider our own personal values when making these decisions, as we will be more successful when our financial decisions align with our sense of integrity and purpose.

The Ace of Cups Tarot card can represent the potential of new relationships and partnerships, as well as the potential of existing relationships. It indicates a willingness to open up to new connections, as well as the opportunity to explore existing connections more deeply. It also suggests that taking chances on new connections can lead to surprising results. It encourages us to be patient and to trust that connection and meaningful relationships can develop over time. It also encourages us to take good care of our relationships, as they are essential to our overall wellbeing.
