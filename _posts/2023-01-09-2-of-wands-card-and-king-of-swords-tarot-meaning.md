A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and King Of Swords mean together?

Together, the 2 of Wands and the King of Swords tarot card mean you are in a period of contemplation and decision making. The combination of cards suggests that you are at a crossroads, with many potential choices and directions before you. You are being asked to take a step back, observe, and use your strategic thought and intentions to decide, weighing up the pros and cons of each option. You have the knowledge and wisdom to choose the right course of action to take, and it is wise to take time to think through the consequences of each choice. Once you do so, you can make a confident commitment to your decision, trusting that it will lead you to the right results and travel plans.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 2 Of Wands and King Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 2 Of Wands and King Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Wands mean?

The 2 of Wands Tarot card is a representation of both potential and caution. When associated with health, the 2 of Wands suggests that the querent should be aware of any potential health issues that may arise, but also be open to new opportunities for health and well-being. This could mean a new exercise regimen, a change in diet, or even alternative medicine like yoga or acupuncture. It also warns against ignoring warning signs and encourages the querent to take a proactive approach to their health.

When associated with finances, the 2 of Wands encourages the querent to think twice before spending money or making any major financial decisions. It also indicates a period of waiting for the results of a financial decision. This could mean waiting for an income tax refund, or for a return on an investment. Patience and wise decision-making are key when trying to make the best financial decisions.

Finally, when associated with relationships, the 2 of Wands suggests that the querent needs to take a step back and consider their options. It encourages the querent to think through any decision they make and to make sure they are considering all of the options before making a move. It suggests that the querent has potential for growth in their relationships, but needs to be mindful of their actions and the potential repercussions.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health

The King of Swords tarot card symbolizes a disciplinarian approach to health. It encourages us to take a more strategic and intentional approach to our physical health and well-being by taking a top-down approach. This is an ideal time to focus on both short and long term goals, as this card recommends a structured and goal-oriented approach to health. It also represents a commitment to staying disciplined and motivated, and a focus on personal responsibility. By understanding the power of balance, we can use the King of Swords to keep us on track and focused on our health goals.

Finances

The King of Swords tarot card suggests taking a no-nonsense approach to finances. This is a card of careful calculation, planning, and budgeting. It encourages us to use our heads, rather than our hearts, when it comes to financial matters. This is a card of careful scrutiny and assessments. It suggests analyzing our financials to identify resources, and finding solutions to our current financial problems. It can also suggest a need for financial restructuring to create stability and success. By being intentional and prepared, we can develop a plan to make our financial goals a reality.

Relationships

The King of Swords tarot card encourages making sound and strategic decisions when it comes to our relationships. It implies taking an active role in our relationships, rather than relying on our emotional responses. It suggests being direct and honest in our communication to ensure that our relationships are successful. This card can also point to a need for a certain level of independence and self-sufficiency when it comes to relationships. By being intentional and honest about our needs and boundaries, we can foster and maintain healthy relationships.
