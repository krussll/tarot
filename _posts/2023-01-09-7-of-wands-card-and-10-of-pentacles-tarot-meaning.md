At the same time, you should also consider how you want to be remembered by others and the legacy you want to leave behind. This could mean building up your reputation for future generations, investing for a comfortable retirement, or creating an inheritance for your loved ones. By combining the power of self-defense and creating a legacy, you will be setting yourself up for long-term success.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

This could mean getting enough rest and exercise, eating a balanced diet, taking supplements, getting regular check-ups, and setting boundaries with people who might be a drain on your resources. Taking these measures can help you to maintain high standards of health and wellness.

The 7 of Wands in a financial reading speaks to the importance of protecting your investments and finances from competition. With the 7 of Wands, you will be able to take confident steps to safeguard your finances and maintain financial security.

It could also mean being proactive in striving for balance in your relationships. The 7 of Wands suggests that it is important to take a stand for yourself and to defend yourself if needed. It could also mean communicating clearly about your needs and being committed to upholding your personal standards. If you take proactive steps to protect yourself and your relationships, you will be able to maintain a strong sense of personal safety and security.

## What does 10 Of Pentacles mean?

The 10 Of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a healthy, prosperous future and security. It is associated with a sense of stability, material wealth, and creating a lasting legacy. In terms of health, it can refer to an individual’s commitment to taking care of their physical and mental well-being. This includes taking the time to nourish oneself through proper diet, exercise, and rest in order to achieve optimum health.

In terms of finances, the 10 of Pentacles serves as a reminder to save and invest wisely. It speaks to the importance of creating financial assets that can be passed down to future generations, such as investments, property and inheritance.

In terms of relationships, The 10 Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages individuals to express their love, appreciation, and appreciation for those closest to them. This card indicates that building and maintaining relationships through thoughtful communication and commitment is essential in ensuring that relationships stay strong and enduring. It also suggests that taking the time to strengthen existing relationships and build new ones can bring a sense of financial stability and emotional fulfilment in the longrun.
