The 7 of Wands tarot card speaks to the importance of self-care and taking steps to protect our health.

It suggests that we can’t always rely on external forces to protect us and that we need to be proactive about safeguarding our own health.

It suggests that we should take steps to build up our physical and mental strength, to guard against the potential competition that life may throw our way.

Learning the lessons of the 7 of Wands, we can take a stand for our own health, creating and maintaining a strong and healthy body and mind.

## Reversed Meanings For 7 Of Wands And Career And Finances

The reversed 7 of Wands suggests that you may be facing some setbacks and challenges in your career and finances.

It is a sign that you may need to take a step back and reassess your situation.

You may need to reevaluate your strategies, build up your skills, and work on self-promotion in order to break out of the rut and increase your chances of success in the future.
