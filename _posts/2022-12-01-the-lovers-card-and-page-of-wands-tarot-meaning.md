## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Lovers mean?

The Lovers Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of partnerships and partnerships in health. This card encourages focus on both physical and emotional health, as well as a need to seek balance in both. It is a reminder that the power of two persons working together to achieve health is strong and can lead to positive and lasting results. Individuals who work together to monitor and support each other’s health may find that the pair is in a much better position to maintain healthy lifestyle habits. In particular, those with chronic ailments, who often feel isolated, may find that having a partner to lean on and help them with their care and health decisions can be incredibly beneficial.

Finances

The Lovers Tarot card can also symbolize a strong financial partnership. This card represents the strength and commitment of two individuals working together to achieve financial success. By building a strong financial partnership, two people can work together to budget, plan and save, forming a strong supporting foundation of financial security. The card also encourages individuals to partner with financial advisors who can guide and support both partners in their financial decisions.

Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card also symbolizes love and romance. The card speaks to a deep and spiritual connection between two people, one that is built on trust and understanding. This card encourages couples to nurture and develop their relationship, remembering that they are stronger together than they are apart. It is a reminder to be honest and open with each other, and to work together to find solutions in times of conflict. Further, it encourages couples to celebrate and appreciate each other, committing to building a relationship and partnership that will last.

## What does Page Of Wands mean?

When The Page of Wands Tarot card is drawn, it indicates a period of youthful energy and eagerness in one’s health, finances, and relationships. In terms of health, this card speaks to a strong desire for physical and mental well-being. A Page of Wands person may go out of their way to take advantage of all the health and fitness options available, from great workout plans, to exploring new holistic remedies, to pursuing a more balanced, healthy diet. Additionally, this card speaks to a desire to be more active, to seek out new and exciting adventures and experiences.

In terms of finances, The Page of Wands Tarot card speaks to a newfound creativity and resourcefulness. This person may be on the cusp of a new business venture or idea, and could find the financial success they have been looking for. They are likely to explore the different ways in which they can monetize their talent and passions, taking risks and thinking outside of the box in order to make their finances more sustainable.

When it comes to relationships, the Page of Wands Tarot card speaks to a desire for newness and exploration. This person may be looking for new social circles, and will make an effort to connect with as many people as possible. They may also be open to trying out new romantic relationships, as they seek out potential partners who share their interests and passions. Overall, this person is seeking a balance of adventure, exploration, and practicality in order to improve the quality of their relationships.
