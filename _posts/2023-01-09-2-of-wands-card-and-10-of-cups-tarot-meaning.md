A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 2 Of Wands card and 10 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Wands and 10 Of Cups mean together?

The 2 of Wands and 10 of Cups tarot cards together cast a powerful combination in your life. This is a card of manifesting your dreams and goals through taking action. It signifies a time of making decisions and planning the next steps in your journey. You are encouraged to take risks, trust your gut, and trust the universe that it will all lead to the best possible outcome. These cards also signify a time of contentment and happiness in your family life, a time of complete and undying love and support. So take a risk and know that you are surrounded by love and supported in your endeavors.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

Making the right decisions for your health now could pay off in the future, so it is important to be mindful of the choices you make.

Finances: The 2 of Wands Tarot card could be an indication that there is a need for careful financial planning. If you find yourself facing important financial decisions, this card indicates that this is the time to weigh your options and to carefully consider the consequences of each choice. Relationships: The 2 of Wands Tarot card could be an indication that it is time to make a difficult decision regarding a relationship. In any case, it is important to take some time to evaluate the situation before making a decision. It is vital to have patience and to listen to your own intuition regarding the situation before making a decision. This card is also an indication that a new exciting journey is about to begin in your relationships.

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 10 of Cups Tarot card is a positive indicator for overall health. It suggests a healthy emotional and physical state – with lots of love, support and contentment from your family, friends and community. When it comes to health, the 10 of Cups can indicate you have a balanced lifestyle and are taking care of your body. You likely have regular check ups and good nutrition, and your relationships with loved ones might be helping you stay motivated to take care of yourself.

Finances

The 10 of Cups is a card of abundance and joy. This can be interpreted as a commentary on financial stability and an increase in assets. The card is a sign of fulfillment and can mean you are feeling financially secure and content with your situation. It can also suggest that you are able to save money and make good investments and financial decisions.

Relationships

The 10 of Cups is a symbol of thriving relationships which are filled with love and support. If a couple has this card as part of their reading, it can mean that their relationship is full of commitment and joy. The card indicates strong connections and unconditional trust. If a single individual draws this card, it can mean that they will soon find true love, or have a new connection to look forward to.
