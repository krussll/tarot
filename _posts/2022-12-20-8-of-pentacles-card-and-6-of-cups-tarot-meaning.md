A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 8 Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Cups card together in particular.

The 8 Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 8 Of Pentacles and 6 Of Cups mean together?

The 8 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 6 Of Cups tarot card together symbolize the hard work, dedication, and joy that comes with mastering your craft. It is a reminder that joy can be found in the small details, and the reward for hard work will come. The 6 Of Cups tarot card also advises that we take some time to remember the happy moments in our lives, and to connect with those who were part of them, whether that’s through gifts, nostalgic reunions, or simply taking the time to look around and appreciate the beauty of the moment. Together, these two cards suggest that in order to make the most of our lives and craftsmanship, it is important to take the time to savor the moments – to not just focus on mastering our skills, but to enjoy them as well.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

It is important to prioritize oneself and invest in the skills needed to maximize one’s health. This can take the form of learning new healthy-eating habits, taking up a new sport, establishing a regular exercise routine, or simply setting aside time to relax and take care of one’s wellbeing.

Finances: The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card is a sign of financial stability and security. It is a reminder to stay focused on achieving financial independence and to be open to new opportunities and ideas to increase one’s wealth. It is also a sign to be mindful of responsible spending habits and to create a sustainable budget to ensure financial security. It is important to recognize that wealth accumulation takes time, hard work and dedication, but that these efforts will be rewarded in due course.

Relationships: The 8 of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a reminder to invest and nurture existing relationships. It is time to prioritize meaningful conversations, time spent with loved ones, and investing in the relationships that bring joy. It is important to stay mindful and be present in order to develop a trusting and open relationship. It is also the perfect reminder to be dedicated and committed to the development of those around us, forgiving any missteps and working together to sustain an emotionally healthy bond.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Six of Cups tarot card suggests that when it comes to health, you may find yourself longing for simpler days or the way things used to be. It could be a reminder to take the time to practice self-care and nurture yourself physically, mentally and spiritually. There is an emphasis on healing the body and mind through practices like journaling, meditation, yoga, walks in nature and reconnecting with old friends or family. This card is a sign that you should look for joy in the little moments, for example, eating good food, getting fresh air and surrounding yourself with positive supportive people.

Finances:  
The Six of Cups tarot card suggests that you should be leery of holding onto old outdated ways of budgeting and investing. Re-evaluate your needs and create a budget that reflects your current financial situation and goals. The card encourages you to be mindful of how you spend your money, prioritize savings and stay disciplined about it. There is a message to honor the past, but look to the future. You can draw on lessons and wisdom from the past, but also be open to new ways of thinking.

Relationships:  
The Six of Cups tarot card suggests that when it comes to relationships, think of it as a moment of renewal. There is a message to rekindle old connections, make peace with the past, and appreciate the joys of the present. The card encourages people to show kindness and compassion to others, and to take the time to reconnect with old friends or family members. It is also a reminder to look at relationships with a fresh perspective and find joy in the little moments. There is an emphasis on exploring different perspectives and being open to change.
