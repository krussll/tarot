A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Swords card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The Page Of Swords card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together?

The Page of Swords tarot card and the King of Cups tarot card when put together represent a curious leader. This is someone who is committed to getting to the bottom of things, who thinks for themselves and is persistent in finding out the truth. They are also kind, compassionate and supportive as a leader, having a high level of tolerance and empathy. This person is sure to be a great listener and someone who looks out for the well being of all those they lead. They won’t be afraid to challenge and probe, but will do so with the intention of helping and understanding, rather than just to satisfy their own curiosity.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does Page Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When The Page Of Swords tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card come together in your love life, it is a situation of conflicting energies. On the one hand, the Page of Swords indicates that there is gossip and prying eyes, and you need to be wary of the truth in your relationship. You may feel as though you need to be on guard, as any conversation with your partner should be taken with a grain of salt.

On the other hand, The King Of Cups tarot card serves as a beacon of light amidst the chaos. The King Of Cups represents a supportive leader who is tolerant and empathetic. This card suggests that in spite of the gossip and prying eyes, your partner supports you and is understanding of your feelings. They are a pillar of strength, offering emotional stability and security in a time of uncertainty.

The two tarot cards together indicate that, while there is still external chaos due to gossip and prying eyes, your love life can still be a source of comfort and support. If you and your partner can come together and be tolerant and empathetic towards each other, you can weather through the difficulties that may arise. By relying on the stability of your relationship, you can stay focused and present in the present, rather than be held down by past gossip and prying eyes.

### What does Page Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together for your finances?

When The Page Of Swords Tarot Card and The King Of Cups Tarot Card appear in a reading together, it can signify a strong and supportive leader who is keenly aware of the truth and is willing to look closer to uncover any underlying issues. This combination of cards is a sign that you have a strong ally who can provide valuable insight into any financial or job-related matters that may arise.

The Page of Swords, in particular, implies a greater focus on facts and truths and a demand for transparency. Therefore, you may find that your leader is eager to uncover the truth in all financial matters, no matter the source or destination. Whether it’s studying the current financial markets, running background checks on potential investors or scrutinizing contracts, your mentor will work hard to make sure nothing is left unchecked.

The King of Cups, meanwhile, suggests that your leader is driven by an unwavering sense of morality, empathy and tolerance. While The Page of Swords ensures that you get the facts you need, The King of Cups will provide judgment and support to help you make the best decisions for your financial and job-related future. This combination of cards promises that your mentor will listen to your ideas, be open to considering different suggestions and offer an impartial, yet supportive opinion when needed.

All in all, this combination of cards is a great sign that you have a leader who is knowledgeable, resourceful and unwaveringly supportive when it comes to your finances and job security. With this duo on your side, you can be sure that any financial or job-related decisions you make will be made with the right intentions in mind.

### What does Page Of Swords and King Of Cups mean together for your health?

When the Page of Swords and the King of Cups come up together for your health, it’s a warning that emotional stability is essential for your well-being. The Page of Swords can be a sign of gossip, prying eyes, or a need to spy on your own or others. It can indicate a desire to know the truth or a tendency to be overly curious. Though it’s important to know the truth, it’s also important to make sure that you don’t go too far in pursuit of it. The King of Cups is a reminder of the importance of physical, mental, and emotional balance. He represents a supportive leader type of figure who is tolerant and provides empathy.

The combination of these two cards suggest that your emotional balance is important for your health. Knowing the truth is important but not at the expense of your emotional stability. The Page of Swords urges you to be mindful of how much information you collect before you start to make decisions. It also encourages you to be cautious in how you voice your opinions. On the other hand, the King of Cups reminds you to take care of yourself by offering empathy and compassion to yourself and others.

When you view this combination of cards in regards to your health, it is a reminder that your emotional balance is important. You should strive to find a balance between curiosity and self-care. Make sure that you don’t pry too much and be too restless in collecting information, and that you make sure to keep a level head and stay emotionally balanced. It will help lead to you greater stability and improved health.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Page Of Swords and King Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Page Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The Page of Swords can represent a need for greater physical energy. It is a reminder to pay attention to the body and take care of it with proper diet, exercise, and rest. It may also indicate that it is time to seek medical guidance if feeling lethargic or weak. This card also serves as a warning to take caution against “gossip sickness”, which is the tendency to draw too much energy from gossip and rumors.

Finances:  
The Page of Swords is a warning to watch out for financial scams and to avoid getting overly involved in financial schemes where the outcome is uncertain. It also warns against frivolous spending habits. On the other hand, the card may also represent a need to be more open and honest when it comes to financial matters.

Relationships:  
The Page of Swords suggests that relationships are in need of greater openness and honesty. It warns of gossip on either side, or too much prying into the other person’s affairs. This card also suggests that gossip or trying to get information by any means necessary is not the way to go. Also, it serves as a reminder to be mindful when giving advice to others, as words can be hurtful.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health

The King Of Cups is a card of empathy and compassion. It encourages you to take the time to nurture your body, mind and spirit. This card is full of healing potential—to help with physical ailments, mental health and emotional balance. The King Of Cups encourages you to take time for yourself to practice self-care and self-compassion. In times of stress, this card symbolizes having the courage to take care of yourself. Its compassionate energy encourages you to make healthful choices, to be mindful of your lifestyle choices and to proactively work towards the best health possible.

Finances

The King Of Cups Tarot card symbolizes balance and moderation when it comes to finances. This card urges you to take a mindful approach to your money. It encourages mindful spending, saving and investing. The King Of Cups encourages you to be responsible and disciplined and to think twice before making financial decisions. The card also indicates a supportive outlook towards money and encourages you to be generous and kind with your financial resources.

Relationships

The King Of Cups indicates the power of empathy and compassion when it comes to relationships. This card symbolizes a supportive, encouraging and kind attitude. It encourages you to be mindful of the feelings and needs of others and to nurture and sustain healthy relationships. The King Of Cups also encourages you to be empathetic and to be a supportive and respectful listener. With its supportive energy, it also encourages you to set boundaries if needed.
