Tarot cards are a centuries-old tool used for fortune-telling and as a means of accessing intuition. Many people believe that tarot cards are real, but the truth is that they aren’t. Tarot cards don’t possess magic powers, as some people might think.

However, with some knowledge about the history of tarot cards and the way they are used, you can determine whether using them is right for you.

Discover the facts about how these cards came to be, their purpose, and whether or not they’re harmful in any way.

## Can Tarot Cards Ruin Your Life?

**If used correctly Tarot cards will not ruin your life. As with anything you must be careful not to get obsessed with reading, or using it as a crutch. The cards themselves will not ruin your life, but your relationship with tarot, and your readings can become unhealthy.**

## Are Tarot Cards Harmful?

The short answer is no. They’re not harmful. However, if you’re reading them as if they were a crystal ball, then they can be harmful.

That’s when you might get addicted to them, or otherwise misuse them. You might also be harming yourself if you’re reading them for the wrong reasons. There are also some risks associated with them; for example, you might get false information from them.

You might also misinterpret what they mean, which could lead to a major misunderstanding. Seeing something bad in the cards when there’s nothing to worry about can also be harmful, as it can make you nervous and fearful.

## Tarot Obsession

Some people become obsessed with tarot cards and read them all the time. This can be harmful, as it can make you too dependent on them.

Others might use them as a crutch, too, in that they rely too heavily on what they think the cards are telling them. If you find yourself becoming too reliant on the cards, it might be a good idea to set them aside for a while.

You can always pick them up again at some point, but it’s important to know when to let go.

## Tarot as a Crutch

If you’re using the cards as a crutch, you’re reading them when you don’t need to. For example, you might need to take a certain action, but the cards indicate that it won’t end well.

In that case, you don’t need to read them at all; you just need to make a good decision. While sometimes you do need to make your own decisions, reading the cards when you can make the right choice on your own can be harmful.

It can also be harmful if you’re reading them too often.

## How Much is too Much?

The number of times you read the cards in a single day is a good indication of whether or not you’re reading them too much.

If you find that you’re reading them multiple times a day, it could be a sign that you’re overdoing it. If you’re reading them once or twice a day, and you’re doing it as part of your daily routine, then there’s nothing to worry about.

It’s when you start reading them more often than you should, or when you’re reading when you don’t need to that you have a problem.

### Reading While In Fear or Distress

Sometimes, people read the cards when they’re in a state of extreme fear or distress. This isn’t the best time to read the cards, as you’ll likely misinterpret the messages that they’re giving you.

When you’re in a state of panic, you’re likely to see bad things in the cards and read them as a sign of an impending crisis.

If, however, you’re reading the cards in a calm state of mind, you’ll be able to view them more accurately and make more sense of what they’re telling you.

## Signs that your Tarot reading is unhealthy

Whilst it is rare for tarot to get out of hand, there are signs that you should look out for. Here are a couple of things to keep in mind when you are looking at your own tarot practice.

### Your readings are triggering unhealthy behaviour

If you’ve been doing readings for a while and are concerned that they’re triggering unhealthy behaviour, it’s best to speak to a professional.

This behaviour can take many forms. You might be feeling a lot of anxiety and fear around your readings, but feel trapped by your own predictions. For instance, you may have predicted a negative outcome, such as a death in the family.

This is a very serious problem and can lead to severe mental health issues, such as obsession. The tarot cards aren’t meant to give you hard facts, they’re meant to give you a symbolic or intuitive interpretation of your life and the direction it’s going in.

It’s not uncommon to get a negative result, but it’s not the end of the world either. That’s why it’s important to tell your psychic if you’re feeling trapped by their readings.

They should be able to help you break free from the negative thinking.

### Your readings keep being wrong

If your readings keep coming up with the wrong answers, it may be time to take a break from them. This isn’t a sign that you should give up on tarot forever.

It just means that you need a break from them in order to reset your intuition. If you’ve been reading for a long time and are experiencing this, you may have become too invested in the cards. This can happen with any practice, including yoga and meditation.

You can’t let yourself get too attached to any practice or you risk losing your creativity and ability to flow with it.

## Final Words

The truth is that tarot cards aren’t magical and don’t possess any supernatural powers. They’re exactly what they appear to be: cards used for fortune-telling.

If you’re interested in reading them, first make sure that you know how they work. You also need to make sure that you’re reading them for the right reasons and in the right way.

While they’re fun to use and can be helpful, they also need to be used responsibly. Make sure to read up on how to properly use the cards before you dive into reading them.
