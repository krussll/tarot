A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 9 Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 9 Of Pentacles card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles and 6 Of Swords mean together?

The 9 Of Pentacles and The 6 Of Swords tarot card together suggest that you are moving on to a new phase in life where you will be able to reap the rewards of all your hard work. You are ready to be financially independent and secure, and you can enjoy the fruits of your labor. But the transition from your current situation to the life of luxury and self-sufficiency is a necessary one, and you will have to relocate in order to make it happen. It is a challenging but exciting process, and you can look forward to reaching the luxurious new situation.

The 6 of Swords, meanwhile, can indicate that you need to make a transition in order to move forward. This may involve relocating, both physically and metaphorically. It may be necessary to make difficult changes in order to progress, and this could involve making decisions that may initially feel painful.

Together, the two cards suggest that the path to a secure and happy future together needs to be embraced, no matter how difficult this journey may be. This is a time for adapting to new situations and making compromises to ensure you both get the best out of life. With courage and resilience, this period of transformation can lead to a much brighter future for your relationship.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 9 Of Pentacles mean?

The 9 of Pentacles Tarot card is associated with luxury, self-sufficiency and financial gain. This card can represent a situation where you’re able to create a self-sufficient lifestyle that gives you financial stability and comfort. It indicates a period of time where your finances are in order and you are able to enjoy the fruits of your labors.

The 9 of Pentacles in health denotes a time of rest and rejuvenation. This card speaks to making time for yourself and taking care of yourself, both mentally and physically. It can be a sign that you’re making meaningful progress on your health goals, and that you’re taking deliberate steps to create a healthy lifestyle for yourself.

In terms of finances, the 9 of Pentacles can be a sign that you’re on the right path and are making wise decisions with your money. You may be taking necessary steps to save and invest your money, creating a foundation for long-term financial stability and growth.

On the relationship side of things, the 9 of Pentacles indicates that you are secure in yourself, confident and independent. You may be focusing on building your own life, becoming financially independent and creating a sense of self-sufficiency without relying on the validation or support of someone else. This card can also point to finding pleasure in the simple joys of life, such as a good book, a hot bath or a lovely garden. This card can indicate that you’re taking the time to appreciate the beauty and abundance in life.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

When it comes to health, The 6 of Swords tarot card generally indicates a period of transition in terms of personal well-being and physical health. It could be a period of healing, of moving forward, or of getting help to assist you on your journey towards a healthier state of being. While this may mean a change in lifestyle, diet and exercise, it also speaks to the need for mental, emotional and spiritual support. This card can signify that now is the time to seek professional help, to ask for assistance and guidance, and to take the necessary steps to make changes that will bring lasting health.

When it comes to finances, The 6 of Swords tarot card suggests a period of transition or change. It could be a relocation or change in residence, a job change or even a move to a different city or state. This card indicates that it is time to find a new way of making money, to find a different way to support yourself and your family. It is time to move out of the financial rut you’ve been in, and to explore alternative sources of income.

Regarding relationships, The 6 of Swords tarot card speaks to a period of transition, an indication that it is time to make a change. This card can signify that now is the time to move on from an unhealthy or unfulfilling relationship, to find a new relationship, or to form new and better relationships with existing friends and family members. It is also a reminder to take the necessary steps to heal old wounds and to build a better foundation for new relationships.
