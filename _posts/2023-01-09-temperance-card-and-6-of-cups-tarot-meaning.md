## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Temperance mean?

When it comes to health, The Temperance Tarot card can be interpreted as a reminder to practice self-care and moderation in all aspects of life. It encourages us to recognize our physical and emotional boundaries and be mindful of how we treat our bodies. The Temperance card also emphasizes the importance of finding balance between working hard and taking sufficient rest. We should strive to be aware of our body’s needs and respond to them appropriately, whether it is sleep, exercise, healthy food or simply time for relaxation and contemplation.

In terms of finances, The Temperance Tarot card suggests that we should use moderation and caution when making financial decisions. We should strive for a balance between saving and spending, and if needed, reach out to financial professionals for advice. The card indicates that we should view money as a tool for creating and sustaining stability in our lives, not as a source of luxury and overindulgence. It encourages us to practice budgeting and saving, to be aware of our financial goals and to make wise investments in order to achieve them.

Finally, when it comes to relationships, The Temperance Tarot card indicates that it is important to maintain healthy boundaries in our interactions with others. We should strive to remain open-minded and respectful when resolving conflicts, and practice patience and self-control when dealing with difficult times. The card encourages us to listen to each other with understanding and to negotiate solutions cooperatively instead of letting our emotions get the better of us. It also suggests that we should focus on creating harmony within our relationships and respect each partner’s needs in order to build a strong and lasting connection.

## What does 6 Of Cups mean?

Health

The 6 of Cups Tarot card symbolizes the health benefits of looking to the past in order to learn and grow. It encourages us to allow ourselves to reflect on our history and draw strength from it, rather than continuously looking towards the future. The healing that comes from nostalgia can provide us with a balanced outlook and help us to move forward. The card suggest that rather than looking to grand, new solutions, we should start with small steps and build our way up from there. Connecting with our past, as well as our current lives, can provide a valuable source of insight and guidance.

Finances

When it comes to finances, the 6 of Cups Tarot card indicates the importance of understanding where your money is coming from. Not just in terms of the source of income but also understanding patterns in the spending and saving of our hard earned money. Its message is that we should reflect on our past behaviors and experiences with money, and use this knowledge to plan for the future. As adults, we should invest in our understanding of fiscal responsibility, just as we invest in our savings, as these skills will pay off in the future.

Relationships

The 6 of Cups Tarot card encourages us to look to the past in order to build healthier relationships. The card suggests that we should acknowledge our mistakes and learn from them, rather than dwelling on the negative. This card encourages us to remember what makes a relationship strong and meaningful, things such as communication, understanding, appreciation and compromise. This card also tells us that with the right amount of love and respect, relationships can be deeply meaningful, and create a lasting bond between two people.
