A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Lovers card and 8 Of Wands card together in particular.

The The Lovers card and 8 Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Lovers and 8 Of Wands mean together?

The Lovers tarot card and the 8 of Wands tarot card together indicate that a relationship based on deep love and partnership is heading towards quick action and change. There is a strong connection between these two cards, as the Lovers card suggests the strength of two together, while the 8 of Wands signifies the speed at which news of the pending changes will arrive. Together, these tarot cards imply that a new journey for you and your partner is about to begin; a journey of passion and purpose, rooted in the power of your collective connection.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

When taken together, the cards emphasize that a choice must be made, but it does not have to take a long time. The strength of two together can create a powerful love, and the news of a new adventure may be just around the corner.

In short, the Lovers card and The 8 Of Wands tarot card together are a sign that a quick choice needs to be made in order to take advantage of a powerful love that is waiting to be discovered. The strength of two together will help create a beautiful relationship, and the news of a new journey is on its way. Take a chance and venture forth into the unknown with your loving partner, as the rewards will be worth the risk.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Lovers and 8 Of Wands mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Lovers and 8 Of Wands the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does The Lovers mean?

Health

The Lovers Tarot card suggests good physical health and well-being when it appears in a reading. The card is a reminder to nurture relationships, whether it be with yourself, a partner, family, or friends. When one is in a healthy and loving relationship, their physical, mental and emotional health is likely to benefit. It can also be a reminder to maintain balance, stay focused in the present moment, and enjoy the sweetness of life.

Finances

The Lovers Tarot card can indicate financial success – it shows that two people or forces can work together to achieve a common goal. This could be a potential financial venture or a project that two people can work on together. The card symbolizes the power of two and suggests that working together, whether it be through a business deal or a practical juggling of financial resources, can produce positive results.

Relationships

The Lovers Tarot card signifies a strong bond between two people, whether it be a romantic relationship, family, or a close friendship. The card represents deep love, commitment, and companionship. It emphasizes that a strong relationship is formed with mutual respect, understanding, and support. It also hints at a decision that needs to be made – whether it be to pursue a certain relationship, strengthen existing ties, or to create more balance. The card can be a reminder to trust your own judgement and to listen to the heart.

## What does 8 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 8 of Wands tarot card is a sign of quick action and fast change in health. When this card is drawn, it can signify a speedy recovery or a sudden spike in health—both good and bad. For instance, if you’ve been struggling with an illness, the 8 of Wands could indicate that a treatment you’ve recently started is working quickly and you should expect to feel better soon. On the other hand, it could also mean that your health is quickly deteriorating and you should seek help soon.

Finances: The 8 of Wands tarot card can mean a sudden influx of money coming in, or a quick change in the financial situation for the better. It’s a reminder to remain alert to new income-generating opportunities and act on them quickly. It could also suggest that financial luck is on your side, and that making a risky investment could pay off in a big way. On the other hand, it could also suggest that you need to act fast or you could miss out on a great opportunity.

Relationships: The 8 of Wands tarot card is a sign of quick and unexpected change in relationships. It could mean that the relationship is either blossoming or withering quickly. Generally, it is seen as a sign of good news — if a relationship has been struggling, the 8 of Wands signals that it is on the upswing and that solutions to problems will be found quickly. However, it could also mean that a new relationship is blossoming quickly, or that a current one is coming to an abrupt end.
