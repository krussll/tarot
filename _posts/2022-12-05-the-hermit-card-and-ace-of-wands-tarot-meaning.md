## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hermit and Ace Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card is an important symbol for health. It can be interpreted as a call for greater self-reflection and a need to focus on one’s own physical, emotional, and spiritual needs. On the physical level, The Hermit Tarot card may suggest that it is time to pause and assess the current state of health. It could point to the need for taking a break from one’s everyday routine and engaging in activities that nurture the self such as yoga, meditation, or even a detoxifying retreat. In terms of emotional health, The Hermit Tarot card may point to the need for a journey of self-discovery to better understand and address personal issues. This could involve exploring spiritual beliefs or asking deeper questions about one’s life, in order to gain a greater sense of clarity and personal truth.

Finances

When it comes to finances, The Hermit Tarot card is a call to practice mindful spending and create a budget. It could point to the need for greater financial planning and conscious decisions about how to spend money. It may suggest the need to break out of a cycle of overspending, so that there is more awareness and control over resources. To gain greater clarity and financial insight, it is important to take a step back and truly assess current expenses, savings, and investments. The Hermit Tarot card could also be interpreted as a reminder to be objective and unemotional when it comes to money and investments, so that one is able to make decisions from a place of clarity and personal truth.

Relationships

In terms of relationships, The Hermit Tarot card can be interpreted as a call for more self-reflection and a willingness to go within for clarity. This card may point to the need for taking time for oneself, removing oneself from relationships that can be draining, and ensuring there is still space for personal interests and activities. It may also indicate that it is time to take a break and get some much-needed distance in order to gain a better understanding of the situation. It can be a reminder to take care of oneself first, and to make decisions based on personal truth and clarity rather than external pressures or expectations.

Representing the element of fire, this card encourages one to take risks, to pursue creative endeavors, and to direct one’s energy towards new ventures. In terms of health, this card suggests that one should use their vital energies for positive endeavors and to stay focused on their well-being. Mental and physical health should be prioritized, and the Ace of Wands is an encouragement to stay active, healthy, and creative.

In terms of finances, the Ace of Wands suggests that one should use their drive to manifest wealth and security for themselves. The card is an invitation to be open-minded and try something new to increase your financial stability. Making smart financial choices and taking calculated risks are also emphasized by this card, so stay focused and work hard to achieve your financial goals.

Relationships will also benefit from the Ace of Wands Tarot card.
