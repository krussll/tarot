A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Wands card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The 7 Of Wands card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Wands and King Of Cups mean together?

The 7 of Wands tarot card and The King of Cups tarot card together indicate the need for self-defense combined with a supportive leader. It suggests that in order to protect yourself against competition, you will need to have the guidance and understanding of a leader who can provide a listening ear, offer empathy and be tolerant of your decisions. It also reminds you to stay focused on your goals while accepting the support that others bring to the table. With the combination of self-defense and a supportive leader, you stand the best chance at reaching your highest potential.

### What does 7 Of Wands and King Of Cups mean together for your love life?

When the 7 of Wands and the King of Cups come together in a reading, it points to a need for balance between the need to defend and protect yourself, and the willingness to accept a supportive and nurturing guidance from a leader or figure in your life.

The 7 of Wands encourages you to be vigilant and self-protective, and to stand your ground in any conflicts or confrontations that come your way. You need to be assertive and self-confident in order to protect yourself and your relationship. On the other hand, the King of Cups symbolizes a supportive leader, a figure of tolerance and understanding; it encourages you to trust in the guidance of this figure, to take their advice and to accept the wisdom they impart.

In the context of love and relationships, the 7 of Wands symbolizes a need to be strong and independent, to stand up for yourself and your beliefs, and to protect yourself against any threats or challenges that arise. At the same time, the King of Cups shows that you must also be willing to accept guidance and support from your partner. Your partner can be a source of strength and wisdom if you are willing to listen and accept their input.

The combination of these two cards encourages you to find a balance between self-defense and self-acceptance, between being independent and being open to guidance. It is important to be confident in yourself and your abilities, while also allowing yourself to lean on your partner and trust their wisdom. Finding the right balance between these two states is the key to having a healthy and successful love life.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 7 Of Wands mean?

Health: The 7 of Wands Tarot card is about taking a defensive stance when it comes to health. This card advises you to protect your health by taking preventative measures and being aware of potential threats. Investing in good nutrition and regular exercise can help to strengthen your body’s natural defenses while avoiding activities that could lead to injury or illness. Being mindful of your overall health and wellbeing will help you to stay strong and standing firm against any health challenges you may face.

Finances: The 7 of Wands Tarot card speaks to safeguarding your personal finances. This card is a reminder to use due diligence when it comes to financial matters. Be sure to research the options available before making any financial decisions, be it investing money or utilizing a credit card. While it’s easy to become overwhelmed by paperwork, taking the time to review the details of an agreement before signing it can help ward off potential financial difficulties in the future.

Relationships: The 7 of Wands Tarot card encourages self-protection in relationships as well. This card suggests creating and maintaining healthy boundaries which keep both parties in a relationship safe and healthy. Taking the time to understand and recognize your own needs, desires and expectations is important when negotiating boundaries in any type of relationship. It is also important to remember to respect the boundaries of those you are in a relationship with and to be mindful of any interactions that could be deemed as crossing a line. With proper boundaries in place, relationships can have a strong foundation and withstand any competition.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health:

The King of Cups Tarot card has many meanings for health. It often symbolizes the supportive leader and healer, someone who is emotionally and spiritually present in aiding others to better health. This card can suggest that people can use their empathy, patience, and self-control to support their own healing process, as well as that of others. Through understanding and compassionate care, issues such as mental health, physical health, and emotional health can be improved.

Finances:

The King of Cups Tarot card can bring many benefits for finances. It symbolizes the ability to take control of one’s financial life, as this card can encourage a person to evaluate their current resources and use them wisely. It also suggests being open to others and listening to their advice. This allows people to be able to see the bigger picture and come up with better financial strategies. Furthermore, it encourages an attitude of tolerance and cooperation, which can help create a better, more stable financial environment.

Relationships:

The King of Cups Tarot card can bring many benefits for relationships. It emphasizes the importance of empathy and understanding for others, which helps foster communication and trust. It encourages patience and respect in all relationships, which is key to any successful partnership. It also suggests that you take the time to listen to the other person’s point of view and accept that your viewpoint may not be the only one. This encourages a healthy, open dialogue and can help build stronger, more meaningful relationships.
