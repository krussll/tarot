Facing up to this change can be difficult, but will ultimately allow for a new beginning.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both 9 Of Wands and 10 Of Swords mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw 9 Of Wands and 10 Of Swords the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 9 Of Wands mean?

Health

The 9 of Wands Tarot card is a card that speaks to the importance of maintaining healthy boundaries both within yourself, and with the people around you. This can refer to the physical body–eating healthily and staying active for example–as well as the mental and emotional aspects of wellbeing. Setting limits on bad habits and unhealthy behaviors, both for yourself and with others, is a vital aspect of this card. Understanding when it is and isn’t appropriate to share your feelings with someone and how to express them in healthy ways can facilitate a more balanced outlook and lifestyle.

Finances

The 9 of Wands Tarot card is a reminder to stay mindful of your financial boundaries and limitations. Whether this be in the form of budgeting and planning, taking on a side-gig to supplement your income, or deciding on how you want to save or invest, understanding and adhering to your financial boundaries is the key to financial sucess. Additionally, the 9 of Wands can also indicate a need to set limits on spending, as well as establishing healthy and productive boundaries with those who may be co-managing finances.

Relationships

The 9 of Wands Tarot card is about knowing where to draw the line in relationships, both romantic and platonic. Understanding when it is appropriate to compromise and when it is best to hold firm in your beliefs is key to maintaining healthy relationships. In addition, it can also suggest that it is important to recognize the need for space from family, friends, and partners, and to take the time to engage in self-care and hobbies that bring you joy. Lastly, being able to differentiate between healthy and unhealthy relationship patterns, and having the courage to walk away from the latter can improve the quality of your relationships.

## What does 10 Of Swords mean?

Health:  
The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests a complete and painful end to an unhealthy situation in regards to one’s health. This could refer to diseases that have been allowed to fester and bring the body to its breaking point, or to unhealthy habits that no longer serve the individual and have been allowed to continue until they take a severe toll on the body. This suggests a period of healing and rebuilding is required, and that it will take time and effort to make positive progress.

Finances:  
The 10 of Swords Tarot card implies that a period of financial strain and heavy losses may be coming to an end. This could indicate that one’s financial woes have been significant and ongoing, and that it is now time to bring them to a close. This could also represent a period of re-building, where one must pick up the pieces and create a more secure financial situation for themselves.

Relationships:  
The 10 of Swords Tarot card suggests a painful ending to a relationship that has been allowed to fester and deteriorate. This could refer to a romantic relationship, a friendship, or any other type of relationship where communication, understanding, and trust have been lacking. It indicates that in order to move forward and establish healthier relationships, the individual must first be willing to let go of the past and its associated pain. This may require some difficult and painful decisions, but it can eventually bring about a stronger and more fulfilling relationship.
