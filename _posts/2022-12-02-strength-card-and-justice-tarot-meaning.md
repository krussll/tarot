A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Strength card and Justice card together in particular.

The Strength card and Justice have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Strength and Justice mean together?

The Strength tarot card and The Justice tarot card together symbolize a balance of self-love and respect, justice and truth. They reflect the fact that when we are brave and loving towards ourselves, we are better equipped to handle difficult situations with fairness and compassion. Our decisions are more carefully considered and our solutions are more likely to be fair, just, and beneficial for all parties. Each card serves as a reminder to practice self-care and cultivate an environment of understanding and acceptance, while also remaining honest and truthful. When we walk the path of both Strength and Justice, we can find the courage to stand up for what is just and true and make decisions that honor ourselves and all those around us.

Building a strong foundation of self-love and respect is the key to a fulfilling and healthy relationship. It is also important to remember that justice requires patience, compassion, and honesty from both partners. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Strength and Justice mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

It urges us to take ownership of our health, to be strong and disciplined in creating a healthy lifestyle, and to build self-confidence in believing that we can reach our health goals. It is an invitation to practice self-care and self-love, treating our bodies with respect and nurturing our minds and hearts.

In terms of finances, The Strength card symbolizes the power of willpower and determination to achieve success. It is an invitation to control our own destiny through hard work, dedication, and discipline. The Strength card is an encouragement to face our financial reality with courage and take the steps necessary to make changes for the better. It encourages us to be proactive in seeking resources and support to improve our financial situation, instead of remaining passive and letting our money situation control us.

The Strength card also has a strong presence in relationships. It implies that strong relationships are founded on a strong sense of self, as well as on respect, empathy and understanding. It is an invitation to stand in our own power and to practice good communication, as well as to respect the other person’s opinions and boundaries. The Strength card encourages us to build relationships with strength and courage, without compromising our sense of self. It also encourages us to take a break if needed, to replenish our energy and strength before making decisions about our relationships.

## What does Justice mean?

Health

The Justice Tarot card symbolizes fairness, balance, and harmony. In the context of health, it suggests that maintaining an equilibrium between the physical and emotional bodies is essential for good health. It is important to ensure that you lead a healthy lifestyle and practice self-care, as this will allow you to make decisions and take actions that are beneficial for your well-being. The Justice Tarot card symbolizes striving for a balance between mind, body, and spirit, while avoiding extremes. You should strive to create a healthy and balanced lifestyle that will allow you to nurture your physical and mental health.

Finances

The Justice Tarot card symbolizes fairness, justice and balance. In terms of finances, the Justice Tarot card suggests that one should strive to create an equilibrium between income, expenses, and savings. It is important to create a financial plan that is realistic and fits your lifestyle, while ensuring that your spending and saving habits are sustainable. When it comes to financial decisions, the card recommends seeking guidance from trusted professionals and to be realistic with investments and other financial activities.

Relationships

The Justice Tarot card symbolizes fairness and harmony. In terms of relationships, the card suggests that relationships should be based on mutual respect, understanding, and fairness. Everyone in a relationship should strive to treat each other with kindness and respect, while taking into consideration each other’s needs. It is important to maintain a healthy balance between giving and receiving in a relationship. Creating a trusting and supportive atmosphere in a relationship can help ensure that it will last and be beneficial for both parties. The Justice Tarot card suggests that communication is a key element for any successful relationship.
