A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The 7 Of Pentacles card and Page Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles and Page Of Pentacles mean together?

For me, the combination of The 7 Of Pentacles and The Page Of Pentacles tarot cards represent the need for patience. Hard work is necessary for the success of any endeavor, and the reward for your efforts will often come with a delay. The Page Of Pentacles tarot card indicates that you may be awaiting news of a financial increase, such as a raise or new job, and The 7 Of Pentacles tarot card also implies that a young person may be patient in anticipation of opportunities. While the wait may be difficult, the ultimate reward will be worth it.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

By combining the 7 of Pentacles and the Page of Pentacles, it suggests that if you remain patient and put in the necessary hard work and effort, your love life will be rewarded with a successful and rewarding relationship and some exciting new financial opportunities.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 7 Of Pentacles and Page Of Pentacles mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 7 Of Pentacles mean?

The 7 of Pentacles Tarot card is a major arcana card associated with patience and hard work. This symbolizes that the hard work and effort you are putting into something will eventually lead to the desired results. This card does not signal instant gratification; instead, it indicates that the rewards for your efforts will come eventually but will require a significant amount of time.

In terms of health, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that the healing process is a slow one, and that it will require patience, dedication, and determination on your part. This card suggests that you will feel the benefits of regular exercise, diet, and rest in the long term. It reminds you to stay positive and have faith in your body’s ability to heal.

In terms of finances, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card reminds you that money doesn’t come overnight. It’s important to work hard and remain patient as you make financial decisions that may lead to future gains. This card encourages you to focus on the long-term and not be swayed by short-term gains. It reminds you to keep looking toward the future and put in the effort now to reap the rewards later.

Finally, in terms of relationships, the 7 of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that taking the time to invest in relationships will eventually yield results. This card encourages you to set boundaries and practice patience with others. It reminds you that relationships take a lot of work and effort and asks you to remain mindful and patient as you attend to your relationships. As with any investments, the more effort you put in, the more likely it is that the desired results will be achieved.

## What does Page Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The Page of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a disciplined and focused approach to health and wellbeing. This card can represent a person taking a proactive approach to their health, such as seeking out a personal trainer, signing up to a health plan or taking up a new kind of exercise regimen. It can also signify that a person is giving their body the attention it needs, such as getting enough rest, eating a balanced diet or making sure to keep up with regular checkups and screenings.

Finances: The Page of Pentacles Tarot card is a strong sign of money news, new opportunities for wealth and financial stability. This card could represent the potential of a raise or promotion, a new job offer, or even some other kind of windfall. It can also represent a more cautious approach to money, such as budgeting, saving and investing. This card can also suggest that a person takes their financial future into their own hands and looks for creative ways to make money or build wealth.

Relationships: The Page of Pentacles Tarot card symbolizes a patient, thoughtful and focused approach to relationships. This card can represent a person striving towards stability and commitment in their connections, such as taking the time to truly listen to their partner and show patience and understanding. It can also represent the commitment to work through any issues or disagreements in order to keep the connection strong. This card can also signal a person to take the initiative in relationships, such as making the first move or expressing their feelings openly.
