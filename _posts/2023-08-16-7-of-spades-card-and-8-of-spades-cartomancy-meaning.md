A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Spades card together in particular.

The 7 Of Spades card and 8 Of Spades have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Spades mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Spades may point to a situation shrouded in deceit yielding to a period of restriction, but also a potential for self-empowerment.

The common element of Water suggests deep emotions are at play, possibly hidden under the surface, and the alignment with winter denotes a time of possible introspection or challenges.

The 7 of Spades brings forward the concept of victory surrounded by deceit, hinting at a triumph that might not be as clear-cut as it seems.

The 8 of Spades adds to this by hinting at power and restriction.

This could mean that your success might come with a price or a feeling of confinement or limitation.

It is a certainly a call to navigate your situation with care, authenticity and inner strength.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Spades mean together for your love life?

In a Cartomancy reading, if you draw both the 7 of Spades and the 8 of Spades for your love life, it suggests the existence of deceit and restriction.

This combination indicates that there might not be complete honesty in your relationship at the moment, and it could be resulting in feelings of constraint or limitation.

This deceit could be from either party in the relationship or may even be related to self-deception about one’s true feelings or the dynamics of the relationship.

The victory associated with the 7 of Spades, however, implies that the truth can triumph if courage is shown.

The 8 of Spades carries a secondary message of power, which could mean that despite present challenges or interpersonal issues, there is a strong force within the relationship or within you that can help navigate through these struggles.

The shared association with winter and water points to a potentially deep, emotional experience that has the propensity to feel cold or distant at times.

But like winter, this phase only constitutes one season of your relationship, and it too will pass.

Harnessing the strength inherent in the 8 of Spades can lead to personal growth and deeper foundations for your relationship.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Spades mean together for your finances?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing both the 7 and 8 of Spades together can have specific implications for your finances and job.

The 7 of Spades is indicative of deceit and victory, suggesting that there may be some hidden factors or deceptions in your financial situation or workplace.

However, it also signifies victory, indicating you have the ability to overcome these unseen forces or obstacles that might be concealed at first.

The deceit perhaps could refer to hidden expenses, financial loopholes, or unexpected financial losses.

In the professional realm, this might point towards covert office politics, hidden agendas, or unseen rivals at work.

However, the prediction of victory suggests that clever and strategic handling of these issues could lead to success.

The presence of the 8 of Spades is an indication of restriction and power, suggesting a feeling of limitation or constraint in your financial endeavors or job.

You may face financial limits or boundaries curbing your economic growth.

This card, however, also symbolises power, indicating that despite these restraints, you have the control or strength to manage them.

In terms of your career, the restrictions might appear as limited job opportunities, hurdles in promotion, or stagnation.

Nevertheless, the power refers to your resourcefulness or ability to maneuver within those boundaries and gain control.

When the 7 and 8 of Spades are drawn together, they suggest a struggle overcoming hidden issues and constraints in your financial and professional life, but retain the promise of victory and power by astute and wise navigation.

### What does 7 Of Spades and 8 Of Spades mean together for your health?

The 7 of Spades in a cartomancy reading suggests a warning against deceit or untruth in relation to your health.

This could potentially indicate a time where you may be misled by someone’s advice or manipulated in terms of your health routines.

It may be useful to be cautious and introspective about what you’re told regarding your health– even by professionals and especially if it’s a drastic change from what you are used to.

This is a card of victory as well, insinuating that despite potential pitfalls, you have the strength to work through these issues and come out on top, especially if you maintain a clear, focused mind.

On the other hand, the 8 of Spades is a card that signifies restriction and power.

This denotes a period where you might feel boxed in or limited regarding your health, perhaps due to a particular ailment or because of a restrictive diet or exercise regimen.

However, this card is also associated with ‘power’.

It suggests that within this restrictive health circumstance lies the power and opportunity for deep transformation.

It could imply that it’s time to take control over your life, take the necessary steps in managing your health effectively, and mold it in a way that is beneficial for you.

Together, these two cards could be indicating a struggle regarding your health but also the ability to overcome such hurdles if approached wisely.

They suggest that paying close attention to your health and being cautious, combined with self-discipline and resilience, could lead to an eventual triumph.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
