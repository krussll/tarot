A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Page Of Swords card and King Of Swords card together in particular.

The Page Of Swords card and King Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Page Of Swords and King Of Swords mean together?

The Page of Swords tarot card and the King of Swords tarot card together speak to an intentional leader who is inquisitive and strategic in thought. The combination suggests that this individual is not afraid to speak the truth and is willing to be the bearer of it. They are often the ones to uncover secrets and tend to have sharp observational skills. The King of Swords is using the Page’s curiosity and inquisitiveness to find the truth. They may use this knowledge to make strategic decisions and be a leader in their field.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Page Of Swords and King Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

You could also be being called to pay attention to the little signs and signals your body is giving you, to pick up on the warning signs before something goes wrong. The Page of Swords also encourages you to be honest with yourself and the ones around you when it comes to your health. Instead of hiding the truth, they suggest that speaking the truth is a better way to protect your wellbeing.

Finances:

It also encourages you to be more vigilant when it comes to your money, suggesting that you pay attention to details and to not let anyone take advantage of you. It is time to do the research into things like investments and budgeting in order to make smarter decisions that will benefit your financial future.

Relationships:

This card suggests that it’s time to be more open and honest with the people you care about, as well as with yourself. This card can also warn against making assumptions when it comes to the people that are around you. Instead, it encourages you to ask questions and be actively engaged in understanding the circumstances and the people around you. It is time to take a deeper look into the relationships that you hold dear and to make sure that you’re not being taken advantage of.

## What does King Of Swords mean?

Health

The King of Swords tarot card often symbolizes a need for clarity in one’s approach to health. As a leader, this card suggests that you take initiative to create a strategic plan for your health. This could include small daily habits like going for a walk, eating well, and getting enough rest. It also suggests taking a long-term view of your health, looking at the bigger picture of how the decisions you make will affect your health both now and in the future. This could include making plans for how to cope with any long-term health issues, and coming up with a plan for how to take care of yourself in times of stress or illness. This card also calls for clarity when dealing with health professionals, keeping your own needs and opinions in mind as you make decisions.

Finances

The King of Swords tarot card suggests that you take charge when it comes to your finances. With a strategic mindset, you can plan your finances wisely in order to reach your goals and ensure your financial security. This could mean coming up with a budget to stay within your means, creating a plan for how to save for retirement or a house, or looking for ways to invest smartly. This card also calls for clarity and objectivity in financial matters, making sure you’re not being driven by emotion or impulse when it comes to money. It also suggests being prepared to take decisive action when it comes to financial crises, such as negotiating with creditors or taking other steps to get out of debt.

Relationships

The King of Swords tarot card calls for a leader’s mindset in relationships. This could mean taking a strategic approach to understanding your own needs and emotions, and being clear and direct in communicating your feelings to others. This card also suggests setting boundaries to ensure mutual respect, and avoiding becoming too involved in other people’s lives. On the other hand, it can also mean that you take a leadership role in the relationship, such as when initiating projects or conversations. It also suggests being strategic in understanding how to best meet the needs of both people in the relationship, such as coming up with a plan for how to support each other financially or how to divide household chores. Overall, this card cautions for a balanced approach to relationships that respects both parties.
