A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Swords card and 6 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 2 Of Swords card and 6 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords mean together?

When the 2 Of Swords and 6 Of Swords tarot cards come together, it is a sign that it is time to make a difficult decision between two paths. It could be a decision on whether to stay or leave, whether to compromise or not, or whether to transition in some way. It is a reminder that any transition or move away from a current situation may be hard at first, but it will ultimately bring about a positive change. This may involve making some tough decisions, but eventually it will bring clarity and understanding.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 2 of Swords Tarot card is a card of balance and compromise. It suggests that if you’re struggling with a health issue, you need to find a middle ground that works for both sides. It can suggest that making decisions between two different treatments may be necessary. It may mean that a difficult compromise will be required to achieve the best result for both parties. It can also suggest that you need to stay open to alternate solutions and perspectives, as there is likely a compromise that will be beneficial for both sides.

Finances

The 2 of Swords Tarot card can be a sign of negotiation and compromise when it comes to finances. It suggests that if you’re dealing with a financial issue, such as a debt, then you should work on finding a middle ground that meets both parties’ needs. It can be a sign that if you are in a situation where you have to choose between two options, such as investing in stocks or real estate, then you have to weigh both options carefully and make the decision with a clear head. It also suggests that you need to be willing to make difficult compromises in order to make the best decision for your financial future.

Relationships

The 2 of Swords Tarot card is a sign that you need to reach a compromise in order to make things work in your relationships. If communication is lacking in your relationship, you need to be willing to make the effort to talk and find a middle ground that works for both of you. This card can be a sign that if you’re struggling with a decision in your relationship, such as whether or not to continue the relationship, then you need to talk it out and figure out a way to reach an agreement that works for both parties. It can also be a sign that you need to be open to alternate solutions and perspectives if you want to make it work.

## What does 6 Of Swords mean?

The Six of Swords tarot card is a card of transition, meaning that it prompts a need to move forward and leave behind something that has become stagnant or outworn. It can be a difficult card, as it often implies a change that is not necessarily wanted, but needed. It such, it can be a challenging card, as it can parallel hard decisions that sometimes need to be made in order to progress.

When the Six of Swords appears in terms of health, it can sometimes indicate the need for a radical change in regard to one’s lifestyle or health habits. It might imply a need to find a different support system to assist one in their quest for health and wellness, or a need to let go of certain bad habits or practices that are harming ones physical and emotional health. It could also mean the need to take a break from one’s current routine and find a healthier alternative.

When the Six of Swords shows up in a reading regarding finances, it can often mean that it is time to re-evaluate current spending and saving habits, find new ways to save and invest money, or to look for new opportunities to make money. It could also mean the need to let go of current financial constraints and obligations, and to move on to more fruitful and lucrative endeavors.

Finally, when the Six of Swords appears in a reading regarding relationships it can often mean that it is time to move on from a current situation and explore healthier, more fulfilling relations with others. It can often refer to the need to let go of unhealthy relationships and find new, more harmonious companions. It can also imply the need to search for a new form of companionship if one feels lonely and isolated, or the need to move away from unhealthy forms of attachment.
