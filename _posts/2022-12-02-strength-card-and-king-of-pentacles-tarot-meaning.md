## What does Strength and King Of Pentacles mean together?

The Strength and King of Pentacles tarot cards together speak of the power of being able to make wise and long-term decisions from a place of self-love and self-respect. When we have the courage to trust our own intuition and invest in ourselves, we become a solid and practical leader. This card combination can indicate that we may have difficulty making decisions or taking action when we lack self-love, but with self-love, unconditional love and self-respect, our decisions are firmly rooted in the wisdom and knowledge of our own inner self. By being aware of our own power and being willing to take the risks necessary to tap into that power, we can become the leader that we need to be in our lives.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Strength mean?

The Strength Tarot card embodies the power of the human spirit, and an understanding that strength can be in both physical and mental form. The card symbolizes the inner strength and courage required to not only survive life’s challenges but to overcome them.

For health, The Strength card encourages us to tap into the power and determination needed to stay healthy, whatever our current health situation may be. It may mean taking steps to create a healthy lifestyle, such as eating nutritious foods, exercising regularly, and creating healthy habits. It could also mean having the courage to ask for help when needed, or seeking out medical advice, if needed.

For finances, The Strength card can represent our ability to be diligent and determined in our financial pursuits. It might mean implementing strategies for managing money better or having the courage to make difficult decisions to take our financial future into our own hands. It could also mean having the fortitude to seek advice and guidance from financial experts, when necessary.

For relationships, The Strength card encourages us to draw on our internal strength and courage when it comes to our connections with others. It could mean having the confidence to stay true to our own values when in relationships, or to take bold steps to protect ourselves from relationships that are not healthy for us. The card encourages us to stay connected to our intuition and inner wisdom, so that we make choices that are in our own best interests.

## What does King Of Pentacles mean?

Health: The King of Pentacles Tarot card can represent physical and mental health. It may be a reminder to look after one’s physical body and mental well-being with rest, exercise, healthy food and other self-care activities. The card can also be a call to take control of one’s health and be practical and wise with decisions which will benefit overall well-being.

Finances: The King of Pentacles Tarot card represents financial security and wise investments. It could be an indication of successful investments, an income from a successful venture, and a plentiful savings account. It may also be an encouragement to practice good money management and make wise investments.

Relationships: The King of Pentacles Tarot card is often seen as a symbol of loyalty, mutual support and trust in relationships. It can signify a long-term relationship which is stable and secure. It could be a reminder to practice loyal commitment and to focus on what is valuable in a relationship. It could also indicate that one should be wise and practical with decisions concerning relationships.
