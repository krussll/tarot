A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 3 Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords card together in particular.

The 3 Of Pentacles card and 4 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles and 4 Of Swords mean together?

The 3 Of Pentacles tarot card and The 4 Of Swords tarot card together emphasize the importance of balancing working hard with taking the time to rest and rejuvenate. It encourages us to find harmony in our lives between collaboration, skill and quality craftsmanship, and taking the time to step back, retreat and recover. Only when we can make time for both hard work and rest can we truly find the balance and create something of quality.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

The 3 Of Pentacles Tarot card suggests that you need to take a team approach in order to reach your health goals. Working together and collaborating with your health care team and family members can help you achieve optimal health. This card shows that through applying skill, craftsmanship and quality to your health regimen, you will be able to make lasting improvements in your health. This card also encourages you to find a supportive group of people to work with and help guide you on your path to health and wellness.

Finances:

The 3 Of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of collaboration and teamwork when it comes to your finances. When working with money and other financial resources, it is essential to bring together the best possible team of experts and professionals to ensure that all aspects of a financial plan are working in harmony. Take the time to find the right people and resources and make sure everyone is aligned with each other. This card encourages you to use your skills, quality and craftsmanship in order to make sound financial decisions and reach your goals.

Relationships:

The 3 Of Pentacles Tarot card encourages you to work together with your partner or with significant others in order to create and sustain fulfilling relationships. This card suggests that collaborating and communicating effectively can help you strengthen your relationship and deepen understanding and connection. Working together to make decisions and solve issues can help lead to growth and connection. It also suggests that being mindful of each other’s skills and craftsmanship is just as important as working together, as this will help build trust and respect.

## What does 4 Of Swords mean?

Health

The 4 of Swords Tarot card can represent a need for a period of rest and recovery in order to optimize health. It suggests a time of recuperation after illness and a withdrawal from regular activities. This card encourages one to take a break from their daily rituals and stresses in order to rest, relax and restore balance in their body and mind. It also suggests seeking professional help if needed. The 4 of Swords signals an opportunity for solitude, and to be true to oneself and recognize the need for self-care and nurturing.

Finances

The 4 of Swords Tarot card in terms of finances suggests taking a step back and reflecting on current financial matters. It encourages one to take a break from the stress of managing money and find the time needed to assess their financial situation. It can also mean that a pause is needed in order to look for alternative money making methods or adjust behavior that may be causing financial stress. It can mean taking a calculated risk and looking for financial opportunities, but also having the wisdom to know when to back off when things become too risky.

Relationships

The 4 of Swords Tarot card in terms of relationships can point to the need to have a break in order to restore balance and harmony. This card suggests a period of peace and solitude in order to gain clarity and perspective. It encourages one to take a step back and assess their current relationships, including those with friends and family. This card can also indicate that it is beneficial to put some distance between oneself and their partner, so that they can come back in their relationship with fresh eyes and create a healthier, more harmonious connection.
