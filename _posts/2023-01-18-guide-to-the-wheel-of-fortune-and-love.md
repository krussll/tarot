## The Wheel Of Fortune And Love – An Overview

The Wheel Of Fortune tarot card is a symbol of good fortune, luck, and a turning point when it comes to health.

It represents the potential for a positive change in your health and the opportunity to take a risk that could bring about improved health.

It suggests that you take a chance and move forward with a health-related decision, trusting that the outcome will be favorable.

Whether it’s trying a new diet, committing to an exercise program, or seeking out medical care, the Wheel of Fortune encourages you to embrace the possibility of good fortune coming your way.

Taking a risk can bring about positive change, and the Wheel of Fortune tarot card is a reminder that the rewards of taking a chance can be great.

## Reversed Meanings For The Wheel Of Fortune And Love

### What It Means For Me

When the Wheel of Fortune is reversed in a love reading, it can indicate a period of stagnation and uncertainty.

The reversed Wheel of Fortune suggests that luck and good fortune are not on your side in matters of the heart, and that you may feel stuck in a difficult or painful situation.

It can also indicate that you are struggling to take control of your own destiny and that your life is stuck in a cycle of unhappiness and disappointment.

The reversed Wheel of Fortune can also warn against taking a risk in your love life.

It may be a sign that you need to step back and reassess your situation before making any hasty decisions.

You must be aware of the potential consequences of your choices, and that luck may not be on your side at the moment.

It’s important to remember that relationships take work and that you may need to take a break from your current situation before making any major changes.

### What It Means For The Future

When the reversed Wheel of Fortune appears in a tarot reading, it can indicate that a period of stagnation in your love life is coming to an end.

In the near future, you may find yourself in a situation that requires more action and energy on your part, and your current relationship may suddenly reach a turning point.

It is important to remember that the reversed Wheel of Fortune can also indicate a greater risk of taking chances when it comes to love.
