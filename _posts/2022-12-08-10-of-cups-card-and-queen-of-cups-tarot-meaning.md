## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 10 Of Cups and Queen Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does 10 Of Cups mean?

Health: The 10 of cups tarot card speaks to overall well being and balance. Health is often a state of mental, physical and spiritual harmony, and this card serves to remind us that this is something to celebrate and be grateful for. Health is the foundation of all aspects of our life, and when we have ours in balance, we can give ourselves and others love, support and joy.

Finances: This tarot card speaks to the idea of abundance, but not necessarily a material kind. The 10 of Cups encourages us to look at our finances from an emotional, spiritual and energetic perspective. With proper planning and care, we can create a healthy financial foundation for ourselves and our families. We can make sure we are living within our means and set aside money for rainy days. When we are in tune with energetic aspects of abundance, we can create a sense of financial security.

Relationships: The 10 of Cups encourages us to nurture our connections with family and friends. This card symbolizes a wish of joy and contentment within our relationships. It reminds us to seek balance, gratitude and respect in our relationships, and to be patient even in times of disagreement. This card emphasizes the importance of finding a balance between self-care and caring for others, and reminds us that relationships are not meant to be perfect, but rather opportunities for growth and meaningful connection.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

The Queen Of Cups Tarot card is associated with nurturing, healing and support. In terms of health, it can suggest that the individual is on a path of healing and restoring balance in their life. This may come in the form of exploring mindfulness, meditation, yoga, or any other nourishing practice that helps one become more in tune with their body and emotions. It serves as a reminder to practice self-care and puts the emphasis back onto nurturing and restoring one’s health.

In terms of finances, the Queen Of Cups Tarot card suggests that the individual is being supported in some way. This could be through a financial windfall or an unexpected help from a friend or family member. Ultimately, the Queen Of Cups Tarot card suggests that one is being supported and that the individual should trust in the process, while also remaining wary of not becoming too dependant on the help provided.

In terms of relationships, the Queen Of Cups Tarot card also suggests that the individual is being supported and that they are receiving healing. This could be in the form of a relationship that provides comfort and security, or in the form of a spiritual connection that helps one feel closer to the divine. In either case, the Queen Of Cups Tarot card represents the support and genuine healing that the individual is receiving. It serves as a reminder to be grateful for the support and kindness of others and to use it to further empower one’s self.
