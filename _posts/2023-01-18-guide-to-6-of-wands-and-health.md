Finding meaning in your tarot readings can be tricky. This is especially true for health. Understanding what The 6 Of Wands card means in regards to your health can be hard to decipher, however there are some interpretations that you may need to be aware of.

In this guide I hope to show you some of the common interpretations of The 6 Of Wands, in a variety of settings and subjects!

The meaning of a card can change depending on whether you drew it upright, or reversed, so I will cover both instances, as the meanings can vary wildly!

Lets take a closer look at The 6 Of Wands.

## 6 Of Wands And Health – An Overview

The 6 of Wands in a health context symbolizes good news, success and recognition.

It could signify achieving a goal, such as reaching a healthy weight, or completing a health program.

It could also represent a reward for your hard work, such as a promotion or recognition for your commitment to healthy living.

On the mental health front, it could represent overcoming a challenging situation or finding the courage to take the steps necessary to improve your wellbeing.

All in all, it is a card of positivity, success, and achievement in the health arena.

## Upright Meanings For 6 Of Wands And Health

### What It Means For Me

The 6 of Wands is a positive card when it comes to health as it symbolizes victory and success.

It suggests that any health issues or concerns you may be facing can be overcome with determination and courage.

The 6 of Wands reinforces the idea that hard work and perseverance will pay off, and you will be rewarded with good news and recognition.

It is a sign that any health problems you may have should be taken seriously and with a strong sense of faith, you will be able to overcome them.

This card encourages you to stay positive and celebrate your wins, no matter how small, as it can be a sign that you are on the right track to achieving your health goals.

### What It Means For The Future

The 6 of Wands in a reading about your future health is a sign of good news and success.

It indicates that you are likely to achieve victory over any health challenges you may face.

This card is a reminder to stay positive and to keep up the hard work and dedication you’ve been putting in towards your health.

This card is a sign that you can count on recognition and awards for your commitment to your health, as well as a celebration of your successes.

You can be sure that your efforts will bring you the recognition you deserve and the results you are working towards.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

The 6 of Wands in a tarot card reading can be interpreted as a sign of good news and success when it comes to the health of those close to you.

It indicates that you have achieved a victory, recognition, and success in the health of your loved ones.

It could be a sign that a medical treatment or procedure was successful and that your loved ones are now on the path to a full recovery.

Alternatively, it could be a sign that the people close to you are doing well and taking good care of themselves.

Either way, the 6 of Wands is a card of optimism and hope that should fill your heart with joy and relief.

## Reversed Meanings For 6 Of Wands And Health

### What It Means For Me

The reversed 6 of Wands tarot card can suggest that health-related issues are causing a block in achieving success.

It is a warning to not be too cocky or overconfident in your health and to take a step back and evaluate if you are taking the necessary precautions to take care of your body and mind.

You may be feeling overwhelmed or exhausted due to the constant struggle of maintaining good health, and this card is a reminder that while success is important, health should always come first.

This card also suggests that you are struggling to be recognized and appreciated for your efforts in trying to stay healthy.

It’s important to know that your hard work and dedication is noticed and valued, even if it isn’t being acknowledged by those around you.

Don’t allow feelings of discouragement to get in the way of your progress, and remember that taking care of yourself is the greatest victory of all.

### What It Means For The Future

When The 6 Of Wands is reversed, it can indicate that health is in a state of flux.

This card can hint at a person feeling dejected and worn down due to a lack of recognition or success.

It can be a warning that physical and mental health are suffering due to a lack of motivation and ambition.

The reversed 6 Of Wands might be a sign that a person is struggling with an illness or injury that is not healing or that they are in need of some kind of medical attention or intervention.

It could also indicate that they are not taking proper care of themselves, perhaps due to feeling overwhelmed or depressed.

Advice to someone in this position could be to take time to rest and recharge and seek help from a medical professional if needed.

They may also need to learn how to better recognize and appreciate their own successes and victories in order to regain their sense of confidence and stability.

### What It Means For Those Close To You

When The 6 of Wands appears in its reversed position, it signifies that there can be a lack of success or recognition when it comes to health.

This card warns against overconfidence and suggests that the path to health may be more challenging than you think.

Health may be in a state of decline, or there may be some kind of mental or physical block preventing you from achieving your desired level of wellness.

This card encourages you to be realistic about your health and to focus on being patient, diligent, and consistent in your efforts to improve it.

It suggests that while short-term gains may be small, they will eventually lead to larger successes in the long run.

It also advises that you take the time to rest and to listen to your body and its needs.

The reversed 6 of Wands encourages you to take the necessary steps to improve your overall health and wellbeing, rather than aiming for instant results.

## Conclusion

As you can see there many interpretations of The 6 Of Wands depending on how it was draw, and what you were asking your tarot deck.
