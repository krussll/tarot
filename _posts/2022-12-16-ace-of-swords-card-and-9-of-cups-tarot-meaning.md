A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Ace Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups card together in particular.

The Ace Of Swords card and 9 Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

With the love and support to back up your intentions, your success is possible.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both Ace Of Swords and 9 Of Cups mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw Ace Of Swords and 9 Of Cups the answer to your query is “YES”.**

## What does 9 Of Cups mean?

Health  
The 9 of Cups tarot card speaks to good health, happiness and well-being. It is associated with the element of Water and emphasizes the joy of feeding one’s body with nourishing and healthful ingredients. The water element is a symbol of wellbeing and healing, and it is believed that when this card appears in a Tarot reading, the seeker is being invited to adopt a healthier lifestyle and nourish their body. A major focus of this card is on the importance of balance and harmony in order to achieve optimal health. Taking the time to cultivate inner peace and joy can result in physical and mental health benefits.

Finances  
The 9 of Cups tarot card is a sign of good fortune, financial abundance, and material success. It speaks to joy and contentment that comes from achieving our financial goals. Achieving financial goals requires patience, hard work and dedication, and the 9 of Cups reminds us that when we work hard and stay focused, our efforts will be rewarded. It is also associated with luck, and is symbolic of good luck and prosperity. The 9 of Cups tarot card can be a reminder that the Universe is looking out for us and wants us to succeed.

Relationships  
The 9 of Cups tarot card is an indication of happy, fulfilling relationships. It speaks to the joy and contentment that comes from strong connections with others. The water element is also associated with emotions, and this card can be seen as a reminder to nurture our relationships through healthy communication, compassion, and understanding. With the help of this card, we can ensure that our relationships are founded on mutual respect and understanding. We can also use this card to help us accept our relationships for what they are, and to appreciate the joy and love that comes from them.
