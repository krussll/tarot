By combining individual skills and talents with strength in numbers, you can create something of greater quality and craftsmanship. This is a reminder to trust in your own abilities and strive for excellence, but also to open yourself up to collaborations and partnerships. Working together with others can help you develop a greater understanding of your strengths and weaknesses and help propel you to greater heights.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does 3 Of Pentacles mean?

The Three of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of teamwork, collaboration, skill and quality when dealing with health, finances and relationships. Achieving success in these areas often relies on finding a balance between individual effort and collective effort.

When it comes to health, the Three of Pentacles encourages individuals to work together with their medical team to get the best care possible. It suggests that each member of the team has a role to play and important skills to contribute, so nourishing and maintaining strong relationships between the patient and their medical team is key. It also encourages individuals to take care of their own health by utilizing preventive care, seeking out preventative screenings, and leading a healthy lifestyle.

When it comes to finances, the Three of Pentacles emphasizes the importance of financial literacy and good money management. It suggests that working together with financial advisors or other professionals who have your best interests in mind is important in order to make smart and sound decisions that will benefit you and your financial future.

In terms of relationships, the Three of Pentacles Tarot card is a reminder that building strong relationships requires a combination of hard work and tenderness. It encourages finding a balance between setting boundaries and giving of yourself, as well as being understanding and compassionate toward the people who matter to you. It also reminds you to be mindful of who you choose to invest your energy in, as well as the effort you put into developing and maintaining those relationships.
