A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Justice card and Page Of Wands card together in particular.

The Justice card and Page Of Wands have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Justice and Page Of Wands mean together?

The Justice tarot card and The Page Of Wands tarot card represent a powerful combination of creative energy, cause and effect, and unveiling the truth. When combined, they signify an opportunity to create a win-win outcome through thoughtful, creative solutions. It’s a time to take action on something new and fresh, while also making sure that everyone involved is heard, respected, and is a part of the result. It’s also a time to honor and accept the truth, no matter how difficult it may seem. Working together to reach a successful outcome through creative solutions is the key to success here.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Justice and Page Of Wands mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

## What does Justice mean?

Health: The Justice Tarot card speaks to the idea of cause and effect when it comes to our health. Choosing healthy habits now will have a positive effect on our health in the future. This card symbolizes the importance of making responsible choices surrounding our wellness since both positive and negative consequences will follow. We must be mindful of our decisions in order to maintain and promote optimal health.

Finances: The Justice Tarot card is a reminder to practice financial responsibility and to only make decisions that are in our best interest. It encourages us to be mindful of our choices and be aware of their potential consequences. It is a warning to stay away from any financial decisions which may be too risky, or have a negative consequence. Acting responsibly in our finances will bring about more positive outcomes in the future.

Relationships: The Justice Tarot card reminds us that each of our decisions will have an effect on our relationships, either positive or negative. It encourages us to think through our decisions carefully, and take into consideration how they will affect others. It invites us to be honest in our relationships, and to practice open communication with one another in order to build trust and understanding. By being respectable and responsible in our relationships, we can foster positive connections that will pay off in the long run.

## What does Page Of Wands mean?

The Page of Wands Tarot card symbolizes energy, creativity, enthusiasm, and a thirst for knowledge. In regards to health, it can signify that one needs to take the initiative to make positive lifestyle changes. It can suggest that one needs to put more effort into their fitness routine or make healthy decisions. The Page of Wands encourages overall mental and physical well being, so it encourages one to make positive changes and start new projects to benefit health.

In terms of finances, The Page of Wands suggests that it is time to make a fresh start. It can signify success, new opportunities or new investments that can bring financial gain. It also encourages you to take the initiative in your own financial matters and put in the effort necessary to make progress. This can include researching investing options, creating budgeting plans, or looking for new job opportunities that can help to increase income.

Finally, in terms of relationships, The Page of Wands Tarot card indicates that there is a need for more enthusiasm, energy, and creativity. It can mean that it is time to take the initiative to bring new ideas into relationships. It can symbolize a need to be more open and communicative in relationships, to put in the effort to make them better. It can also signify a need for a change in attitude in order to bring about the desired outcome, or a new opportunity to explore different aspects of the relationship.
