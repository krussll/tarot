A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 2 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The 2 Of Swords card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 2 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together?

The two of Swords and the Page of Cups indicate that the time has come to make a decision or compromise when it comes to matters of the heart. You may find yourself in a situation where you have to choose between two paths, but the Page of Cups encourages you to take a more lighthearted approach. Think of volunteer work, of the new love that could be just around the corner, of being a studious kid. Taking a softer approach to your decisions can help you make the right one.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 2 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean together for your love life?

The 2 Of Swords and the Page Of Cups tarot card together in your love life suggest that time is of the essence, and it is time to make a decision: either to enter into a new relationship, or to compromise an existing relationship. The 2 of Swords, with its tightrope walker balancing between two swords, implies that you have a difficult choice before you and that staying in the middle ground is seemingly impossible.

The Page of Cups, meanwhile, suggests a gentleness, openness and sensitivity that is required in any budding relationship. It suggests a new love, and even a sense of innocence or naivety that comes with it. It may be easy to drift into a relationship with someone, but it takes true effort to make it last for the long haul.

What the two cards together are implying is that in your love life, you must choose carefully about who you trust and open your heart up to. It could be that the responsible and studious young Page of Cups is the one you should trust and invest in, even with the risk that it may not work out in the end. There may be a feeling of hesitation or uncertainty on your part, which is totally natural. But if you trust in yourself and your instincts, and make a decision, you could find yourself on the path to a successful and fulfilling relationship.

In terms of your job and finances, it might be time to look for new sources of income or to explore different career paths. Do you feel stuck in your current job? Is it time to look for a new challenge? The combination of the 2 of Swords and the Page of Cups also speaks to the importance of learning and adapting. The Page of Cups can represent a young person learning new things, so this could be a time when you need to do some research and gain knowledge that will help you make better financial decisions. It could also be a time to consider new approaches to investing or to re-examine the role of emotions in your work life.

Ultimately, this card pairing is about finding balance between the need to make a decision and the need to take a step back and look at things from a different perspective. It’s a reminder to take the time to evaluate your options and to focus on creating a financial path that is in alignment with your values.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as 2 Of Swords and Page Of Cups mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does 2 Of Swords mean?

For health: The 2 of Swords is a card of balance and equilibrium, indicating a need to temper your physical and mental health and create a delicate balance between them. It is important to recognize that your body and mind need to work together and in harmony. Make sure to get enough rest, practice good nutrition and exercise, and take time for yourself to relax and unwind. When it comes to health issues, this card can also signify a need to seek advice or assistance to find a compromise between different approaches, treatments, or lifestyles.

For finances: The 2 of Swords is a card of compromise and fairness, so it can suggest a need to make financial decisions which are both equitable and balanced. It can indicate that a financial compromise must be found in order to avoid any overspending or financial pitfalls. As you put together a budget, be sure to include all the potential expenses and to factor in financial protection such as savings and investments. Also consider that a balanced budget includes money for both saving and spending.

For relationships: The 2 of Swords suggests that it is time to make a decision in order to achieve a balanced and harmonious relationship. Compromise is essential in order to reach an agreement, and collaboration should be the goal. When making a decision with your partner, weigh all sides of the issue and look for an outcome which is fair for both of you. Try to find a solution which works for both of you, and don’t be afraid to seek outside help if needed. This card can also suggest that this is a time to be honest and direct in conversations, as unspoken issues can lead to misunderstandings.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

The Page Of Cups Tarot card has a wide range of meanings within the realms of health, finances and relationships.

Health: From a health-oriented perspective, The Page Of Cups Tarot card can symbolize a childlike innocence, fun, and joy. It can suggest that it’s time to take a break from your daily routine and treat yourself with compassion. It’s a reminder that taking care of yourself is paramount for your physical and mental well-being.

Finances: When it comes to finances, The Page Of Cups Tarot card could represent one’s own frugality when it comes to spending. It can also symbolize the need to be cautious and vigilant with any business or financial endeavors as this card encourages one to look before they leap.

Relationships: In relationships, The Page Of Cups Tarot card could represent someone new in one’s life. This could be a newfound passion, a new friend, or a potential romantic interest. It can also signify compassion, loyalty, and kindness, while also representing the need to take things slow in any relationship, be it platonic or romantic.

Overall, The Page Of Cups Tarot card is a reminder to slow down, practice self-care, and be mindful of any financial and relationship endeavors. It’s a card that encourages one to look before they leap and to treat oneself with kindness and compassion. It could represent new beginnings in all aspects of life and is a reminder to enjoy the journey.
