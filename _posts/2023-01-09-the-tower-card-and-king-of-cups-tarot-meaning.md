A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and King Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Tower card and King Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and King Of Cups mean together?

The Tower tarot card combined with The King Of Cups tarot card suggest a time of unwanted change and upheaval in your life, but one during which you will find a supportive leader who will help you to manage it with tolerance and empathy. It is a difficult time where some things may be destroyed or ruined, but at the same time, it is an opportunity for personal growth as you are supported in making it through the upheaval and violence.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The Tower tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card in your love life could symbolize a moment of unexpected and drastic change that is nonetheless guided by a supportive leader. It could indicate that you are going through a difficult transition or upheaval in your relationship, but that your partner is there to provide emotional and practical support. If the Tower card has been drawn in response to a relationship question, it might suggest that a dramatic event outside of the relationship is causing a major disruption in the equilibrium of your partnership. This could be anything from a sudden job loss or a health crisis to the news of a new pregnancy or a sudden move.

The King Of Cups suggests that your partner is being patient and empathetic, and looking after your emotional needs during this time of upheaval. This could be a time where they are checking in on your feelings and making sure that your love life continues to thrive. This card encourages you to remain open and to approach communication with compassion and understanding. Your partner may be the one who helps you through this transition and guides you towards a better life, so it is important to have faith in them and allow them to take the reins if they feel comfortable doing so.

The combination of The Tower tarot card and The King Of Cups tarot card in your love life can suggest a sudden and unwelcome change, however, it also symbolizes that you have a strong and supportive partner who will guide you through the transition with patience and compassion. This card is a reminder that there is hope in the midst of chaos, and that your partner is there for you no matter what.

In order to cope with this unwanted change and the resulting financial difficulties, you can benefit greatly from the qualities of the King Of Cups card. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Tower and King Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Tower and King Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: The Tower Tarot card can represent a time of unwanted physical, mental and emotional change. The Tower Tarot card suggests that a person’s health may suffer due to the stress of unavoidable change. This can include disruptions to one’s physical, emotional and spiritual health, such as an illness, injury or crisis that one may not have been expecting or prepared for. This card can also point to an emotional breakdown due to external events, or a lack of self-care. It can also suggest a person relying too much on medications, or other substances, to cope with life’s uncertainties.

Finances: The Tower Tarot card can be a warning sign that a person’s finances are at risk of suffering due to change. This card warns that finances may take an unexpected or abrupt turn, leading to financial ruin or upheaval. This can include unexpected financial losses, a job loss or change in income, or unexpected bills or debt. This card can also symbolize an individual’s lack of financial responsibility, such as irresponsible spending habits, or a failure to plan for the future.

Relationships: The Tower Tarot card can point to difficulty in relationships that can arise due to potential change and upheaval. This can include relationships ending abruptly due to an unexpected event, or a sudden revelation causing distress between partners. This card can also suggest that an individual is focusing too much on external relationships instead of their own needs, leading to a feeling of loneliness and disconnection. It can also represent a time of difficulties in communication, as well as breakdowns in trust as one person or the other may feel uncertain of the direction of the relationship.

## What does King Of Cups mean?

Health: The King of Cups Tarot card is a reminder of the importance of taking care of one’s mental and emotional health. This card encourages you to nurture yourself and others, to practice self-care, and to be more understanding and compassionate towards yourself and others. It also suggests that you take advantage of available resources to make sure your physical health is in good shape. With this card, you may want to consider taking regular trips to the doctor, eating healthier foods, getting enough rest, and managing stress.

Finances: The King of Cups Tarot card encourages you to approach financial matters with an open mind and level-headedness. This card is an invitation to make well-thought-out decisions with regard to money. It emphasizes the need to put financial security first and to seek out advice when needed. To stay on track with your finances, be mindful of your spending habits, as well as saving habits, and take the time to keep track of your budget.

Relationships: The King of Cups Tarot card is a representation of healthy relationships — ones in which compassion, understanding, and mutual respect are the foundation. This card encourages you to pay attention to your communication with others and to be honest and open about your feelings. It also asks you to practice self-love, so that your relationships can be balanced and authentic. Additionally, it serves as a reminder to maintain a healthy balance between your personal and professional relationships.
