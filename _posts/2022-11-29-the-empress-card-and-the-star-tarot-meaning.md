A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Empress card and The Star card together in particular.

The The Empress card and The Star have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Empress and The Star mean together?

For me, The Empress tarot card and The Star tarot card together mean that I am entering a time of creative abundance and fertility in my life. I am pregnant with new ideas, new projects and new ways of being. This is a time of great hope and renewal for me, as I am finally able to see clearly what it is that I want to create in my life. I am surrounded by the miracle of new life, and my faith in the process is strong. I feel confident and optimistic about the future, and I am ready to step into the spotlight and share my gifts with the world.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Empress and The Star mean “Yes” when being asked a question. There is no doubt here, if you draw The Empress and The Star the answer to your query is “YES”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Empress mean?

Health: The Empress Tarot card is a very positive card for health, as it represents fertility and the ability to create life. This can be a very good time to conceive a child, or to start a new health regime that will lead to a more abundant and prosperous life. The Empress also suggests that you take care of your body and your health, as you are the one who will be responsible for creating and sustaining life.

Finances: The Empress is a very positive card for finances, as it represents abundance and fertility. This is a good time to invest in new ventures, or to start a new business. The Empress suggests that you are surrounded by abundance and prosperity, and that you have the potential to create even more. This is a good time to take risks and to invest in your future.

Relationships: The Empress is a very positive card for relationships, as it represents the ability to create life and to nurture those around you. This is a good time to start a new relationship, or to deepen an existing one. The Empress suggests that you are surrounded by love and that you have the potential to create even more. This is a good time to express your love and to show your affection for others.

## What does The Star mean?

The Star Tarot card is a positive card that suggests hope, renewal and inner clarity. In terms of health, The Star suggests that you have faith and believe in miracles. This is a good time to focus on your health and wellbeing, and take steps to improve your overall health. In terms of finances, The Star suggests that you will be in the spotlight and have the opportunity to achieve your financial goals. This is a good time to focus on your career and financial security. In terms of relationships, The Star suggests that you will have the opportunity to renew and improve your relationships. This is a good time to focus on your personal and interpersonal relationships.

## Conclusion

Understanding how the meaning of a reading changes once you start involving more than one card can be tricky. This will come with time and practice, however I hope this guide on what your cards might be telling you when you draw The Empress and The Star has helped you.
