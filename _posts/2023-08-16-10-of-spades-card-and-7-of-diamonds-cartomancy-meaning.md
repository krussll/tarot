A key aspect of reading your own cards is interpreting meanings. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the 10 Of Spades card and 7 Of Diamonds card together in particular.

The 10 Of Spades card and 7 Of Diamonds have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does 10 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together?

In a cartomancy reading, drawing the 10 Of Spades and the 7 Of Diamonds together indicates a likely hardship or struggle – specifically, falling upon unfortunate news or facing issues at work.

However, the combined meanings of these cards suggest that despite these difficulties, there will be success and victory.

This journey might be a test of your perseverance due to the harsh winter element of the 10 Of Spades and the unpredictable air element of the 7 Of Diamonds indicating a rocky experience.

However, the alignment of these cards signifies a robust cycle of trials and triumphs.

While unpleasant surprises might arise, they go hand in hand with fortitude, paving the path toward accomplishments.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

### What does 10 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together for your love life?

In a cartomancy reading, the combination of the 10 of Spades and 7 of Diamonds may signal a difficult, but eventually victorious period in your love life.

The 10 of Spades, representing unfortunate news but also success, may mean that you’ll face certain hardships or obstacles in your relationship, which could result from miscommunication or misunderstanding.

This card corresponds with the season of Winter and the element Water, signifying a time of introspection and deep emotional journey.

You may need to face your deepest fears, and delve into your emotional depths to navigate these challenges.

On a positive side, the 7 of Diamonds promises victory.

It may indicate work issues, drawing attention to the need for dedication, efforts, and constant nurturing of your relationship.

This card, associated with the season of Fall and the element Air, symbolizes a time of change, transformation and communication.

Even though this period might require sincere work from your side, there’s a promise of success.

You could come out of it with a stronger, more resilient relationship.

So, the combined energy of these two cards calls for patience, perseverance, and a belief in love to navigate through the issues and to achieve a harmonious relationship.

### What does 10 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together for your finances?

When drawn together in a cartomancy reading, the 10 of Spades and the 7 of Diamonds point towards a period of significant change and transition in your professional life and financial situation.

The 10 of Spades, symbolizing unfortunate news, associated with the harshness of Winter, and the watery element of emotional upheaval or change, may indicate that you may experience some unexpected news or setbacks in your career or financial situation.

This could take many forms, perhaps a transition in your workplace, a project failure, or sudden financial drought.

However, the presence of the 7 of Diamonds card provides a counterbalance to these challenges.

Associated with the season of Fall – a time of harvesting and reaping rewards, and the element Air that typically signifies intellectual capacity and clarity of thought, this card suggests work issues, but also victory.

This implies that while you may face obstacles in your professional life, your diligence, hard work and adaptability would eventually lead to success.

Regarding your finances, it suggests that despite some probable losses as indicated by the 10 of Spades, you are likely to navigate through the difficulties and find new opportunities, possibly a new source of income or investment, that would help secure your financial future.

### What does 10 Of Spades and 7 Of Diamonds mean together for your health?

In a cartomancy reading, if you draw the 10 of Spades and the 7 of Diamonds cards about your health, it may suggest a difficult journey ahead, but one that ends in success.

The 10 of Spades speaks of unfortunate news which could be connected to your health.

It might point to a time of poor health or indicate the presence of a health concern that you may not have noticed.

However, the presence of this card also signifies triumph in the end.

With its association with winter and the element water, it might suggest that your emotional resilience will be crucial during this period.

On the other hand, the 7 of Diamonds symbolizes work issues and victory.

With regard to your health, this could be understood as the effort required on your part towards recovery or maintaining your health.

The work issue might refer to an imbalance in work life and health life or a need for developing a healthier routine.

However, this card also promises victory which could hint at ultimate successful management or overcoming health challenges.

Its link to fall and the element air could be interpreted as the need to let go of unhealthy habits or pessimistic attitudes regarding your wellbeing.

Together, these cards stress on the fact that the road to good health may be tricky, but with endurance and work, an overall victory is possible.

The meaning of the cards will depend on what kind of reading you are doing and the question you asked the deck. This is a guide covering the general meanings of the cards and how they relate to each other.
