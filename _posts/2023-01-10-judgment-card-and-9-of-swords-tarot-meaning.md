A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Judgment card and 9 Of Swords card together in particular.

The Judgment card and 9 Of Swords have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Judgment and 9 Of Swords mean together?

The Judgment tarot card and the 9 Of Swords tarot card together signify a time of self-reflection and acknowledgement. It is a wake-up call to take the time to look deep within yourself. It is a reminder that you can achieve a greater sense of clarity and understanding in your life when you are able to confront and process your fears, anxieties, and grief. It is an opportunity to gain insight into your life’s purpose, enabling you to make decisions that are truly aligned with your highest goals and aspirations. It is time to look at the truth of your life and give yourself the chance to move forward with clarity and understanding.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as Judgment and 9 Of Swords mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does Judgment mean?

For health, the Judgment Tarot card is a reminder to take responsibility for your well-being. To live a healthy, balanced life you need to be aware of your body and mind. This card urges you to take the time to assess your overall health, the choices you make that affect it, and the steps you need to take to make improvements. It may require some self-reflection and hard work, but if you can obtain a sense of clarity and understanding of what you need to do to be healthier, you can make positive changes that will benefit you in the long run.

For finances, the Judgment Tarot card signifies that it is time to make a clear and informed decision about your future. You are being called to assess where you are financially, take stock of your debts and assets, evaluate your money-making strategies, and come up with a plan for how to use your resources in the most effective way. Taking a strategic approach to your financial life and implementing necessary changes can bring a sense of peace and security to your future.

For relationships, the Judgment Tarot card means it is time to be honest with yourself and your loved ones. Evaluate your current relationships and make a conscious decision to either continue or end them. If you decide to continue, this card serves as a wake-up call: be honest, communicate openly and frequently, and create healthy boundaries. If you decide to part ways, send healing energy to your relationships so both parties can move on without resentment. Whatever decision you make, remember that it is your decision and it is the right one.

## What does 9 Of Swords mean?

The 9 of Swords Tarot card is a dark card that can represent a lot of difficult emotions and experiences. When it appears in a reading, it can denote health problems, financial hardships, and relationship issues.

When it comes to health, this card symbolizes physical or mental exhaustion, stress-related illnesses, and health conditions that stem from anxiety or depression. It may be an indication that the person needs to take better care of themselves and to pay attention to any warning signs their body may be sending them.

The 9 of Swords Tarot card may also signify financial hardship. It could be an indication that the person is feeling overwhelmed by money problems or that their finances are in a precarious state. It could also mean that the person needs to adopt better money management strategies.

Relationships are represented by this card as well. The 9 of Swords Tarot card may be warning of the erosion of the relationship due to unresolved conflicts, lack of communication, or other issues. It may serve as a hint that if steps are taken to repair the relationship, it can be saved. The card could also signify intense emotions, depression, and bitterness between the two people.

Overall, the 9 of Swords Tarot card is a reminder to pay attention to any underlying issues in one’s life and to take proactive steps to address them. It can be interpreted as a sign to be more aware of one’s physical, mental, financial, and emotional wellbeing and to make necessary changes to ensure that all areas are balanced and healthy.
