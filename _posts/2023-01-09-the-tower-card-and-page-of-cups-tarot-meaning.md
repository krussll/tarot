A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Tower card and Page Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Tower card and Page Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Tower and Page Of Cups mean together?

The Tower tarot card in combination with the Page Of Cups card suggests that unexpected and sudden changes may occur in your life, either leading to ruin or upheaval or a transformation for the better. The chaotic energy of The Tower could be balanced out by the positive energy of the Page Of Cups. You may need to volunteer or take on a project that requires dedication or learning something new, but it will ultimately help lead you to a more positive future. It’s important to keep an open mind and ready yourself for anything that might come your way. You may experience some disruption and volatility, but you could also find love, inspiration, or a new purpose in your life.

The Tower card may be a warning of a difficult situation, but it is also a sign that something new is here for you to experience. ## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does The Tower mean?

Health: When The Tower card is drawn in a reading, it may suggest that drastic, unexpected changes may be coming in regards to one’s health. It could be a warning sign that something unexpected or shocking may be on the horizon, such as a serious diagnosis. It could indicate a collapse of the body’s ability to function properly, or the complete breakdown of good health. It could suggest a dramatic change in health, such as the sudden onset of an illness or injury, or the need for a significant change in lifestyle. Those undergoing medical treatment may find that there are unexpected results or complications. In any case, The Tower suggests that some drastic changes may be imminent and unavoidable, and that these changes may leave one feeling vulnerable and exposed.

Finances: The Tower is a sign of financial upheaval and unexpected change. It could indicate that a financial collapse is coming and that one will have to face major destabilizing changes in their financial life. It could mean the loss of a job or the need to drastically alter the way one approaches their finances. It could also indicate that a major upheaval in the economy or a complete collapse of the financial markets is occurring. In any case, it suggests that the unexpected and drastic changes could leave one feeling vulnerable and exposed.

Relationships: The Tower is a sign of destruction and upheaval in relationships. It could suggest that the relationship is about to undergo major changes and that the destruction and chaos are unavoidable. It could indicate a complete breakdown of the relationship, and the loss of trust and security. It could also suggest that a sudden and dramatic surprise is coming, such as a breakup or the revelation of a secret. In any case, The Tower suggests that the relationships in one’s life may be going through a period of drastic and unexpected change, and that these changes could leave one feeling vulnerable and exposed.

## What does Page Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Page Of Cups Tarot card is associated with good health and wellbeing. This card indicates that you should keep an eye on your physical and mental health, as it is important to stay in balance. It is necessary to take the time to practice self-care and be mindful of your own needs. Also, it suggests that you should practice mindfulness meditation to reduce stress and anxiety. The Page of Cups encourages us to keep our bodies healthy and connected to our minds.

Finances:  
The Page Of Cups Tarot card indicates that you should keep track of your financial situation and use your resources wisely. Take the time to assess your current budget, analyze your regular expenses, and make sure you know where your money is being spent. When it comes to investments, this card suggests taking a cautious approach and letting intuition guide your decisions. It is also important to be patient and think deeply about what you are doing, as it will help you make sound decisions that will benefit your finances in the long run.

Relationships:  
The Page Of Cups Tarot card is associated with new relationships and budding romances. It encourages you to try something new and explore new possibilities that may come your way. This card indicates that it is important to open your heart and mind to new experiences and to embrace the unknown. It also suggests staying mindful of your own feelings and the feelings of others and being mindful when communicating. The Page of Cups suggests taking the time to nurture and cultivate relationships and to make sure to focus on the positive aspects of relationships, rather than the negative.
