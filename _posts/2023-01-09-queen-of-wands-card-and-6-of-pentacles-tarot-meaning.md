A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the Queen Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles card together in particular.

The Queen Of Wands card and 6 Of Pentacles have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does Queen Of Wands and 6 Of Pentacles mean together?

The Queen Of Wands and The 6 Of Pentacles together speak of a person with confidence and self-assurance who is generous in their giving and receiving of help. This could mean that you have the courage and the resources to reach out to those in need, lending a helping hand, or alternatively, that you have the inner strength and trust needed to accept the caring and compassionate assistance of others. Be it financial, emotional or practical, this card combination is a reminder that it is okay to receive help, as much as it is to offer it.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

The 6 of Pentacles reflects the need for both parties to give and receive help. This card can represent a situation in which both partners are willing to help each other out, whether it’s with day-to-day needs or something more significant. The card also reflects a generous spirit that is willing to share their resources and time with the other person.

The combination of the Queen of Wands and the 6 of Pentacles speaks of a relationship between two strong-willed individuals who are willing to be there for each other and to support each other. This kind of relationship is based on mutual trust and a spirit of giving and receiving. The Queen of Wands adds a touch of fire and passion to the relationship, while the 6 of Pentacles adds the sense of stability and security. Together, they create a balanced and lasting relationship that is built on mutual respect and love.

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## What does Queen Of Wands mean?

The Queen of Wands Tarot card is often seen as an indication of health and vitality. It shows the confidence and self-assuredness we need to take care of our bodies, nurture our minds and keep ourselves healthy and thriving. It suggests that those who carry this energy are able to take action to create the life they want and to live it fully. It is a reminder that all of us have the power within us to heal ourselves from any physical and mental ailments.

Finances  
The Queen of Wands Tarot card also has a strong influence when it comes to our finances. It speaks to a person’s determination, resourcefulness and ability to make their dreams come true. This card indicates that we can make our own luck, cultivate our own opportunities and manifest wealth and abundance in our lives. It is a reminder to be responsible, take the initiative and take calculated financial risks to secure the future we want.

Relationships  
Finally, the Queen of Wands indicates that success in relationships also relies on confidence and self-assurance. This card encourages us to be true to ourselves and to our partners, to be assertive and take care of our own needs in the relationship. It speaks to taking responsibility for our own well-being and trusting that our relationships will be supported and nurtured by mutual respect, understanding and commitment.
