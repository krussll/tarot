A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hermit card and Queen Of Cups card together in particular.

The The Hermit card and Queen Of Cups have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hermit and Queen Of Cups mean together?

The Hermit tarot card and the Queen Of Cups tarox card together suggest the need for a balanced approach when seeking spiritual illumination and personal truth. The Hermit card encourages you to go within yourself to explore and uncover your true nature, while the Queen Of Cups reminds you to seek out nurturing, support and healing from the people around you. By taking advantage of both paths you can gain more clarity, insight and understanding of yourself and the world around you.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Both The Hermit and Queen Of Cups mean “No”. If you are asking a direct question, then be under no illusions, the answer is a negative one. If you draw The Hermit and Queen Of Cups the answer to your question is “NO”.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hermit mean?

Health

The Hermit Tarot card is an indication that we should take a step back from the external world and go within to gain clarity. It is a reminder to take some time for yourself and connect to your deeper self, which can lead to greater physical and mental health. The card encourages you to connect with your body and mind, to practice mindful meditation or yoga to recharge and nurture yourself. It is a reminder to listen to your intuition and inner wisdom and to trust in the natural healing process of your body as you work to maintain a healthy lifestyle.

Finances

The Hermit Tarot card encourages us to look inward when making financial decisions. It is a sign to pause, reflect and take a deep breath before acting on any impulse purchases. To be mindful of our financial goals and to make sound decisions based on our personal values. When it comes to investments, the card suggests being mindful of taking risks, to carefully weigh the pros and cons and to make decisions that are in line with our long-term objectives.

Relationships

The Hermit Tarot card is a reminder to look within to become aware of our needs, values and expectations. To take time to reflect and to be sure that we are taking care of ourselves and our mental health. This card is a reminder to set clear boundaries and communicate our needs openly with our partners and loved ones. To be mindful of our expectations and to take the time to assess whether they are realistic or not. It is a reminder to pause and take a step back in order to gain clarity before making an important decision. The Hermit Tarot card can be an indication of taking a relationship break in order to be sure that your needs are being met before diving back in.

## What does Queen Of Cups mean?

Health:  
The Queen of Cups tarot card symbolizes a deep and instinctual healing power. It suggests that healing is a process that requires patience, understanding, and nurturing. This card often signals a new beginning and suggests that we take the time to look within and listen to our intuition. It is a reminder to trust our inner knowing and be gentle with ourselves. We should seek out professional help or guidance when needed, but also acknowledge that we are not alone in our journey to healing.

Finances:  
The Queen of Cups symbolizes emotional security and an abundance of resources. It encourages us to assess our financial situation with a heart-centered outlook and make decisions that create a stable and secure future. It suggests practicality and forethought should be employed to create sound financial decisions, but with a loving and nurturing attitude. We should take time to honor our emotional needs and prioritize our values when making financial decisions.

Relationships:  
The Queen of Cups symbolizes a deep connection and intuition. It encourages us to nurture our relationships in a compassionate, loving way. It represents emotional sensitivity and a willingness to be vulnerable with others. It suggests that we open our hearts to cultivate meaningful connections and express our feelings openly. We should also be understanding of the needs and emotions of our loved ones and strive to create trust, security and emotional stability within our relationships.
