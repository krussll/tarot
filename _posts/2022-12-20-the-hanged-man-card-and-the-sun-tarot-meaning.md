A key aspect of reading your own tarot is interpreting cards. For a beginner interpreting a single card can be fine, as there are plenty of resources to help you out, however interpreting two or more can be very tricky.

However, if you have been practising single card readings for a while a 2 card spread will be a great introduction into reading multiple cards.

As you’ve found this page, you’re probably wondering how to interpret the The Hanged Man card and The Sun card together in particular.

The The Hanged Man card and The Sun have unique qualities and will interact with each other in different ways depending on what kind of reading you are doing.

## What does The Hanged Man and The Sun mean together?

The Hanged Man tarot card and The Sun tarot card represent two different paths that can be taken to reach success. By understanding the wisdom of self-sacrifice, as represented by The Hanged Man card, and changing your perspective to get a broader view of the situation, you can put yourself on the path to success. The Sun card symbolizes material happiness, joyous outcomes, and success that can be achieved through hard work and dedication. When combined, these two cards suggest that if you are willing to make sacrifices and look at things from a different angle, you can create the conditions for joyous success.

The meaning may differ depending on what you are asking. Here are some common questions and their possible meanings

## Common 2-card spreads

Whilst it’s great to know who these two cards interact with each other, it’s important to note that the meaning will depend on the context of your spread

The most common 2 card spreads I recommend to newer readers are:

- The 2 card cross (otherwise known as “The Fool’s Journey”)
- The 2 card “Yes or No”

### “The Fool’s Journey”

Setup: The two card cross is where you place two cards face up, one placed vertically and with one laying horizontally over

Interpretation: The 2 cards represent a “situation” and a “challenge”. The first card will describe the situation you are in with the second horizontal card being the “challenge” crossing you (literally crossing you in this case!)

Reading: Using the overviews in this guide is a great way to start. First, look at the meanings of the 2 cards, and see how they might relate either as your “situation” or “challenge”. Once you have that you can look at the general overview for how the two cards interact with each other.

The benefit of this spread is that it can give clarity when you’re feeling uncertain or anxious. Once you have clarity of thought, and a strong idea of what your challenge is you can find solutions to help your situation

### The 2 card “Yes or No”

Setup: The 2 card “Yes or No” is where you place two cards face up side by side, whilst asking your deck a “yes or no” question.

Interpretation: Each card in a tarot deck has a “yes” or a “no” meaning. Look at the meanings of each card, and you will have your answer.

The benefit of this is you get a stronger yes or no compared to a single card draw. You may find you often get a mix of “yes or no”, and this is fine. It simply means there is doubt, or things are not yet clear.

## Yes or No meaning

**Things are a bit tricky for this combination, as The Hanged Man and The Sun mean different things. The cards are unsure about your question, or the answer to the question has not yet been decided.**

The “Yes” and “No” meanings can differ from reader to reader. The meanings here are based on what I believe are the generally accepted definitions.

## What does The Hanged Man mean?

Health: The Hanged Man Tarot card is associated with the ability to take a step back from the rigors of life in order to gain an unbiased perspective on any situation. When it comes to health and wellbeing, this card can indicate the need to take a “time-out” in order to properly assess one’s current condition. This can mean taking the necessary time to focus on healing and recharging, whether that is through exercise, meditation, or some other form of self-care. It may even mean that one needs to let go of some unhealthy habits, such as overworking or unhealthy eating, in order to restore balance and harmony to their life. This card can also indicate that it may be time to seek professional help, such as with a therapist, nutritionist, or doctor, to identify and address any underlying health issues.

Finances: When it comes to finances, the Hanged Man Tarot card speaks of the importance of taking a long-term, holistic approach. This card suggests that one should examine and assess the current state of their finances and resources in order to find an optimal course of action. This card can also be seen as an encouragement to let go of any patterns or behaviors that no longer serve one’s highest good. Perhaps one should reconsider how much money they are spending, or how they should be investing and allocating their resources, in order to be wise with their money. Ultimately, this card is all about taking the time to really assess one’s finances in order to determine the best plan for the future.

Relationships: The Hanged Man Tarot card can be interpreted as a message of wisdom, patience, and self-sacrifice when it comes to relationships. This card suggests that one should take a step back and look at any existing relationships objectively, in order to truly see where they are headed. This card encourages one to assess what they need, as well as what their partner or family members need, in order to create a healthy and balanced connection. This card can also indicate that it might be time to let go of any patterns or behaviors that no longer serve either party. Ultimately, the Hanged Man Tarot card speaks to the importance of self-reflection and growth in order to cultivate and nurture healthier, stronger relationships.

## What does The Sun mean?

The Sun Tarot card is associated with the possibility of joy and material happiness. When it comes to your health, The Sun card indicates that you are in the process of healing. Whether this is physical, mental, or spiritual healing, it’s the kind of healing that brings you closer to a state of whole-heartedness and wellness. You will benefit from feeling secure about your health and grounded in yourself during this process.

When it comes to finances, The Sun card indicates that you are on the right track. It can mean a steady flow of income and new opportunities, such as a new job or promotion. You will be able to meet your financial goals if you make wise investments and use your resources wisely. Additionally, you should be mindful of budgeting and saving.

For relationships, The Sun card predicts joy, harmony, and a sense of satisfaction. It means that your relationships are currently in a healthy and balanced state. You may gain a newfound appreciation for your loved ones and develop a more positive outlook towards the people in your life. The Sun card also indicates that you have what it takes to make any relationship a long-term, lasting one. You may also experience a period of growth and/or a newfound sense of balance in your relationships.
